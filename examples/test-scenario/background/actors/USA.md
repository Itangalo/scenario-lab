# United States of America

## Overview

The United States maintains global leadership in AI research and development through a combination of private sector innovation and government investment. With major technology companies and leading research institutions, the US seeks to balance technological advancement with democratic values and international cooperation.

## Strategic Goals

- Maintain AI technological leadership
- Establish international safety standards
- Protect national security interests
- Promote democratic AI governance frameworks

## Current Capabilities

The US AI ecosystem benefits from deep capital markets, world-class universities, and a culture of entrepreneurship. Government agencies are increasingly coordinating with private sector partners to address both opportunities and risks from advanced AI systems.
