# People's Republic of China

## Overview

China has emerged as a major AI superpower through coordinated state planning, massive investment, and the integration of AI across industrial and social sectors. The Chinese government views AI as strategically critical to national rejuvenation and global competitiveness.

## Strategic Goals

- Achieve AI leadership by 2030
- Develop sovereign AI capabilities
- Shape international AI governance to reflect Chinese interests
- Leverage AI for economic modernization and social stability

## Current Capabilities

China's AI development benefits from substantial government funding, large-scale data availability, and rapid deployment cycles. The integration of state and commercial interests enables coordinated advancement across multiple sectors, though concerns about innovation constraints persist.
