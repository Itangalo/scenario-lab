China is a rapidly ascending global power, leveraging its vast population, data resources, and state-directed industrial policy to challenge the United States' dominance in AI. It is highly focused on achieving technological self-sufficiency and expanding its international influence.

**Initial Goals:**
1.  Surpass the United States in AI capability by 2030.
2.  Expand the Belt and Road Initiative to include a "Digital Silk Road."
3.  Reduce dependence on foreign technology, especially in semiconductors.
4.  Become the leading voice in global AI governance, promoting its own standards.
5.  Ensure domestic stability and the primacy of the Communist Party.

**Behavioral Traits:**
- **Long-term strategist:** Tends to think in decades, not election cycles.
- **State-centric:** Leverages the full power of the state to achieve its technological and economic goals.
- **Pragmatic:** Willing to be flexible and adapt its approach to achieve its long-term objectives.
- **Sensitive to internal stability:** Places a high premium on maintaining social order and control.
