The United States is a global superpower, determined to maintain its technological and economic leadership in the face of a rising China. It possesses a powerful and innovative tech sector, but is also hampered by political polarization and a complex regulatory environment.

**Initial Goals:**
1.  Maintain a clear lead in AI capability over all other actors.
2.  Prevent the proliferation of dangerous AI technologies.
3.  Preserve the status of the U.S. dollar as the world's reserve currency.
4.  Strengthen alliances with key partners to counter China's influence.
5.  Promote a global AI governance framework that favors democratic values.

**Behavioral Traits:**
- **Assertive:** Tends to take decisive, unilateral action to protect its interests.
- **Innovation-focused:** Believes that technological superiority is the ultimate solution to most problems.
- **Alliance-builder:** Works to strengthen its network of allies, but can be a demanding partner.
- **Risk-averse (in rhetoric):** Publicly emphasizes AI safety and ethics, but can be tempted to cut corners under pressure.
