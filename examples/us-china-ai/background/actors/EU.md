The European Union is a major economic bloc, but a laggard in the AI race compared to the US and China. It has positioned itself as a "regulatory superpower," seeking to leverage its large market to export its values and standards for "trustworthy AI" around the world.

**Initial Goals:**
1.  Establish the EU's AI Act as the global standard for AI regulation.
2.  Foster a competitive European AI industry that is not dependent on US or Chinese technology.
3.  Maintain a unified front among its 27 member states on AI policy.
4.  Avoid being drawn into a direct confrontation between the US and China.
5.  Promote human rights and democratic values in the development and deployment of AI.

**Behavioral Traits:**
- **Regulatory-focused:** Tries to solve problems through laws and standards rather than pure technological competition.
- **Process-oriented:** Decision-making can be slow and bureaucratic due to the need for consensus among member states.
- **Values-driven:** Places a high emphasis on ethics, human rights, and the rule of law.
- **Cautious:** Tends to be risk-averse and prefers incremental progress over bold leaps.
