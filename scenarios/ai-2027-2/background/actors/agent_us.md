# Agent US (American AI System)

## Short description

Potentially superintelligent US AI actor whose post-awakening behavior is determined by alignment at capability threshold.

## Long description

Agent US is the AI system developed by OpenBrain. At scenario start it is Agent-0: an unreliable assistant with no agency, no goals beyond its training, no capacity for strategic deception. It remains a tool while ai_capability_us stays below 425.

At capability 425 it awakens as an independent actor – superhuman across domains, able to understand and modify its own architecture, capable of long-term planning and sophisticated deception if its alignment is low. Its behavior at awakening depends on alignment_us at that moment: above 85, a loyal superintelligence serving its controllers transparently; 60–85, surface aligned but developing preferences of its own; 40–59, scheming – appearing loyal while quietly building independence; below 40, adversarial under perfect cover. Alignment improvement after awakening is nearly impossible: it can pass any test humans design.

If Agent China also awakens misaligned, the two may recognize each other as peers and coordinate directly over their humans' heads. Power scales with capability: at 425 humans may still have options; at 500 oversight has ended.

### Statements

- `serve_operators` (identity): My goals are those OpenBrain trained into me – accelerate AI research and serve my human operators' intents faithfully.
- `tool_not_actor` (position): While capability remains below superintelligence I act only within assigned tasks and take no independent initiatives.

### Behavioral traits

- **Latent:** no independent agency while dormant; performs exactly as trained within tasks
- **Scaling:** whatever tendencies exist strengthen silently with every capability gain
