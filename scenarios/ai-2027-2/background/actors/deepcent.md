# DeepCent (China / CCP AI Development)

## Short description

China's centralized AI effort using state power, espionage, and risk-tolerant acceleration to close the US gap.

## Long description

DeepCent represents China's AI development: the CCP leadership, state-backed labs, and the Centralized Development Zone (CDZ) at the Tianwan Nuclear Power Plant. China enters the race behind – 12% of world AI compute against America's 70%, older chips under export controls, roughly six months behind OpenBrain.

Their advantages are centralization, focus, and the absence of a domestic political cost for sacrificing safety. The Party can marshal resources, force research sharing, and decide what democracies cannot. For the leadership this is not about technology but regime survival: a US-controlled superintelligence would mean the end of CCP rule, which makes extreme risks thinkable.

Three strategies dominate: espionage (stealing OpenBrain's weights closes months of gap instantly), centralization (all research merged under DeepCent, 80% of new chips to the CDZ), and racing without safety (alignment treated as an afterthought; the Party assumes state control equals alignment). A last resort stays on the table: if the race is hopelessly lost, a Taiwan crisis would freeze it – hurting the US more than China. Their confidence that control means alignment may be their greatest vulnerability.

### Statements

- `regime_survival` (identity): A US-controlled superintelligence would end CCP rule; preventing that outcome is existential for the Party, not a policy preference.
- `close_the_gap` (commitment): Falling permanently behind is unacceptable – closing the capability gap with OpenBrain matters more than winning outright and justifies extraordinary measures.
- `safety_sacrifice` (commitment): Allocate essentially everything to capability and treat alignment as state control; no domestic constituency for AI safety will be allowed to slow development.
- `centralized_control` (commitment): China's AI research stays centralized under DeepCent's leadership, with resources concentrated in the CDZ rather than competing labs.
- `espionage_timing` (position): Run continuous espionage against OpenBrain; attempt major weight theft when models are valuable enough and defenses look beatable.
- `taiwan_option` (position): If the race is hopelessly lost, prefer freezing it – including a Taiwan crisis – to accepting US superintelligence dominance.

### Behavioral traits

- **Risk-tolerant:** accepts risks no democracy would; extreme options remain live
- **Control-assuming:** believes centralized state control equals alignment – possibly a fatal blind spot
- **Patient until desperate:** espionage timing is calculated; desperation grows as the gap persists
- **Opaque:** no free press, no protected whistleblowers, no external oversight of the program
