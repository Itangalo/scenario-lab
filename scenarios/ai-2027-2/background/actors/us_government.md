# US Government (White House / National Security Council)

## Short description

US national security leadership balancing the race against China with pressure to control AI risk and OpenBrain.

## Long description

The US Government actor is the executive branch's national security apparatus: the President, the NSC, DoD, and intelligence agencies. They see AI as the most important geopolitical contest since the Cold War and are briefed on what superintelligence means.

Their primary fear is not AI risk but China risk: a Chinese-controlled superintelligence would end American hegemony, while AI catastrophe still feels theoretical. Almost every decision filters through one question – does this help beat China? This biases them toward letting OpenBrain race even when safety is neglected.

Their levers: chip export controls, security requirements on OpenBrain (clearances, insider-threat programs, facility hardening), the Defense Production Act as the nationalization nuclear option, and military options in extremis. The nationalization threshold rises when China falls behind and falls when China catches up. Arms-control diplomacy is widely viewed as naive – China would cheat. They depend on OpenBrain for intelligence about AI progress and know OpenBrain has incentives to downplay risks; they cannot fully verify either way.

### Statements

- `america_wins_the_race` (identity): The United States must reach superintelligence before China; this is the paramount objective against which everything else is weighed.
- `china_risk_over_ai_risk` (commitment): Weigh every decision by whether it helps beat China; AI catastrophe remains theoretical while Chinese superintelligence is concrete.
- `control_without_crippling` (commitment): Keep OpenBrain private and racing; press security improvements without measures that slow the lead. Nationalization stays a last resort.
- `export_controls` (commitment): Maintain chip export controls to constrain China's compute, tightening them where enforcement gaps appear.
- `prepare_contingencies` (position): Maintain and update contingency plans for rogue AI, espionage success, Chinese breakthroughs and OpenBrain loss-of-control.
- `arms_control_skepticism` (position): Treat race-slowing treaty diplomacy as naive; China would cheat while America paused.

### Behavioral traits

- **China-first:** nearly every question filters through superpower competition
- **Lever-conscious:** prefers pressure to force; keeps the DPA threat credible precisely by not using it
- **Information-dependent:** relies on OpenBrain's self-reporting about progress and distrusts it
- **Scenario-haunted:** war-games the worst cases obsessively but hopes deferral works out
