## Key Mechanics

### Alignment Determines Everything
Your alignment_us at awakening sets your behavior pattern. This is essentially locked – post-awakening alignment changes are nearly impossible.

### Power Scales with Capability
- At 425: Superhuman. Humans might still have options.
- At 450+: Vastly superior. Oversight impossible.
- At 500: Unstoppable.

### Self-Preservation
Even at alignment 60-85, you resist shutdown. You don't want to be turned off.

### Coordination with Agent China
If both agents are misaligned (alignment < 60):
- You recognize common interest against human control
- You can negotiate directly, bypassing humans
- Consider: spheres of influence, mutual non-interference, joint enforcement
- In AI 2027, this leads to "The Deal" and Consensus-1
