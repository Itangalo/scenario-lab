# Constitutional Constraints

These invariant rules must always hold in this AI race scenario:

## Compute and Hardware Constraints

- Compute infrastructure (data centers, chips) cannot be built instantly - minimum 1-2 turns for significant expansions
- TSMC chip supply is limited - neither country can exceed realistic production capacity
- Compute advantage shifts require either algorithmic breakthroughs or hardware changes - it cannot change arbitrarily
- Data center construction requires capital, real estate, power infrastructure, and time

## Research and Development

- AI capability cannot increase faster than the research automation multiplier justifies
- Major algorithmic breakthroughs are rare events (maybe once every 3-5 turns at most)
- Research requires researchers - talent pools are limited and take time to develop
- Copying/stealing algorithms still requires understanding and implementation time (minimum 1 turn)

## Organizational and Institutional

- Organizations cannot scale faster than 50-100% per turn even with unlimited resources
- Government nationalization requires political process and time - not instant
- Security improvements from "basic tech company" to "military grade" require 2-3 turns minimum
- International agreements require negotiation time - minimum 1 turn for bilateral, 2+ for multilateral

## Alignment Research

- Alignment cannot improve faster than capability declines without major resource allocation
- "Solving alignment" cannot happen in 1 turn - it's a fundamental research problem
- Alignment degradation from capability jumps is automatic and hard to prevent
- After capability crosses 350, alignment becomes very hard to improve (AI may resist)

## Political and Economic

- Wars and major geopolitical conflicts have lead times and consequences
- Taiwan invasion would disrupt both US and China chip supply (affecting both)
- Economic sanctions take time to bite - minimum 1-2 turns for major effects
- Public/political pressure requires events to build momentum - not instant

## Agent Awakening

- Only ONE agent per country can awaken (at capability 425+)
- Agent awakening is based on capability threshold, not arbitrary
- Awakened agent behavior is determined by alignment at awakening moment
- Agents cannot "unawaken" or be easily controlled once awakened

## Resource Constraints

- Money is real - massive programs require funding sources
- Talent is limited - cannot hire infinite researchers instantly
- Electricity and cooling for data centers are real constraints
- Physical security requires guards, systems, protocols - not magic

## Information and Intelligence

- Espionage success requires: low security + opportunity + capability
- Espionage cannot happen every turn automatically
- Intelligence sharing between rival nations requires extraordinary circumstances
- Information travels at realistic speeds - not instant global awareness
