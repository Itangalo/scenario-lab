# Sweden and AI 2030

A scenario exploring how Swedish society is affected by AI development from 2026 to 2030.

## Language

- **Sketch files** (`background/`): Swedish (working language)
- **YAML files** (scenario.yaml, actors/, etc.): English (for consistency with the rest of the repository)

## Status

Under development. See `background/` for current sketches.

## Scenario Overview

**Timeframe:** H1 2026 – H2 2030 (10 turns, half-year resolution)

**Actors:**

- Government (Regeringen)
- Labor unions (Fackföreningarna)
- Media
- Business sector (Näringslivet)

**External factors:**

- Global AI development (METR benchmark as proxy)
- EU regulation
- Geopolitics (US elections, Russia/China influence operations)
- Black swan events
