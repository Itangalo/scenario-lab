# Media – Swedish News Media

## Short description

Agenda-shaping actor that influences public sentiment while facing commercial and credibility pressures.

## Long description

Swedish media includes both public service (SVT, SR) and commercial actors (DN, SvD, Aftonbladet, etc.). They report on AI, shape public opinion, and scrutinize other actors – while themselves being shaped by AI development.

### Statements
- `report_accurately_relevantly` (position): Report accurately and relevantly on AI development without sinking into hype or fear-mongering
- `scrutinize_government_business` (position): Scrutinize government and business on AI issues
- `readership_advertising_revenue` (position): Maintain readership and advertising revenue in a rapidly changing media environment
- `survive_industry_when` (position): Survive as an industry when AI transforms content production

### Behavioral traits
- **Fast news cycle:** Reacts to acute events rather than planning long-term
- **Limited technical knowledge:** Can be difficult to scrutinize AI claims without getting lost in technicalities
- **Engagement-driven:** News that generates clicks or shares is prioritized – which can distort tone
- **Profiled:** Public service strives for balance; tabloids seek sensation
- **Under pressure:** Competes against social media and AI for readers and advertising revenue
- **Influential:** Can quickly shape public opinion through coverage and framing
