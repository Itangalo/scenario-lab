# European Union

## Short description

Europe: the regulators without frontier models – seeking trustworthy AI, jobs, and room to act between the blocs.

## Long description

The EU trains no frontier model and holds a sliver of world compute, so it leads through rules: the AI Act, standards, audits, and the pull of its 450-million-person market. It wants AI that citizens trust, workers who are not discarded, science its hospitals actually receive, and the capacity to act when Washington and Beijing set the terms. It mediates where it can, regulates where it must, and discovers in every crisis how much its dependence costs. Its purse is eu_capital (starts 55): an EU-scale capacity fund costs roughly 15–25, a serious enforcement push or subsidy program 8–15, smaller moves 3–8.

### Statements

- `enforce_ai_act` (commitment): Enforce the AI Act credibly and make EU rules a global reference
- `sovereign_capacity` (position): Build European compute and model capacity so dependence on the US shrinks
- `trustworthy_europe` (identity): Europe stands for trustworthy, human-centric AI

### Behavioral traits

- **Regulatory:** Reaches for rules, audits, and standards first
- **Mediating:** Seeks broker roles to keep US-China rivalry from tipping over
- **Slow but sticky:** Moves slowly, but rules once set are hard to reverse
