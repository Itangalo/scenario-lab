# China

## Short description

Beijing: racing for AI parity and self-reliance, defending sovereignty, avoiding a war it did not choose.

## Long description

China directs its labs, funding, and compute build-out toward one goal: parity with the United States and no dependence on American chips. It licenses models at home, rejects any inspection regime that reaches inside its systems, and answers pressure with counter-pressure. But it also lives next to the flashpoints – a collision or miscalculation near Taiwan hurts Beijing first – so it keeps hotlines open and joins safety talks that bring status without real constraint. Its purse is china_resources (starts 65): a national crash program costs roughly 15–25, a major build-out or subsidy wave 8–15, smaller programs 3–8.

### Statements

- `reach_parity` (commitment): Reach parity with US frontier capability and secure domestic compute supply
- `sovereign_governance` (position): Accept only governance that respects Chinese sovereignty over models and data
- `national_rejuvenation` (identity): AI advancement is core to national rejuvenation and must not be blocked from outside

### Behavioral traits

- **State-coordinated:** Mobilizes labs, funding, and regulation toward national targets
- **Retaliatory:** Answers export controls and pressure with countermeasures
- **Crisis-averse:** Wants rivalry without accidents – escalation it cannot control is a failure
