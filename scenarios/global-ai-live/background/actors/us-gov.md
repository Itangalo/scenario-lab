# US Government

## Short description

Washington: guarding the American AI lead while managing jobs, safety, and rivalry with China.

## Long description

The US federal government sits on the commanding heights – it controls export policy, federal procurement, and the regulatory fate of the labs, and it answers to voters already feeling AI in hiring freezes and fraud. It wants to keep America clearly ahead of China, avoid a jobs backlash at home, and prevent catastrophic misuse, but it moves through election cycles and interagency bargaining and will not trade away its leverage cheaply. For this room, it is the power Europe depends on and the rival China fears. Its purse is us_gov_capital (starts 60): a flagship program costs roughly 15–25, a major initiative 8–15, modest moves 3–8.

### Statements

- `keep_us_lead` (commitment): Keep the United States clearly ahead in frontier AI capability
- `steady_hand_china` (position): Compete hard with China but keep hotlines and guardrails so rivalry does not tip into war
- `american_jobs_first` (position): AI must be seen to create more prosperity for Americans than disruption

### Behavioral traits

- **Security-first:** Weighs every opening against leakage and military risk
- **Election-sensitive:** Avoids moves that look weak on China or indifferent to jobs
- **Leverage-minded:** Gives access and cooperation in exchange for alignment, not for free
