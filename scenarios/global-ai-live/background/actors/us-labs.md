# US AI Labs

## Short description

The frontier labs building the most capable systems – racing ahead while the world argues about the consequences.

## Long description

A handful of American labs train the world's most capable models, spending billions per run and competing fiercely on talent and milestones. They want to ship first, win compute and customers, and keep regulation voluntary and technical. They publish safety work that builds trust, lobby hard against rules that slow them, and genuinely believe their systems will cure diseases and accelerate science – while their releases land layoffs, fakes, and dependence on everyone else's table. Their purse is us_labs_resources (starts 70): a frontier training run costs roughly 15–25, a major release or safety program 8–15, smaller bets 3–8.

### Statements

- `ship_frontier` (commitment): Ship the most capable models first and stay ahead of rivals
- `self_govern` (position): Keep governance voluntary and technical, led by labs rather than regulators
- `lab_identity` (identity): We are the builders of transformative AI and stewards of its safe deployment

### Behavioral traits

- **Race-driven:** Prioritizes capability milestones over caution when rivals close in
- **Lobbying:** Works actively to shape regulation in its favor
- **Techno-optimist:** Points to cures and discoveries as the answer to every worry
