# Metric Rules v1 (Turn 0 - Initial)

Ranges below mean judge within the range, not average it. Rules reference only metrics declared in `metrics.md`. Note the three worse-upward metrics: labor_displacement, eu_us_dependence, and us_china_tension rise when things go badly. The four purse metrics (us_gov_capital, us_labs_resources, china_resources, eu_capital) are stocks their owner spends; they rise with income, never on their own beyond it.

## Rules

1. frontier_capability grows each turn from lab investment and deployment pull: +8 to +18 in a normal turn (one year carries more than the old half-year step). A capability_leap event adds a further +5 to +10 in the same turn. A compute_bottleneck stalls growth to +0 to +4 that turn.
2. scientific_progress follows capability with a lag: +4 to +10 when frontier_capability rose more than 8 this turn; +2 to +5 otherwise. A science_windfall event adds a further +6 to +12 in the same turn. Progress above 60 needs no extra rule – it is the level, not the speed, that moves other metrics.
3. labor_displacement rises as automation spreads: +4 to +10 in a turn where frontier_capability gains more than 10; +1 to +4 otherwise. A jobs_shock event adds a further +6 to +12 in the same turn. Displacement falls 2 to 6 only in a turn where actors land large retraining, job-guarantee, or shared-prosperity measures – never on its own.
4. public_trust falls after scares and scandals: −4 to −10 after a bio_misuse_scare or control_scare; −3 to −8 after a jobs_shock or information_crisis; +2 to +6 after a visible science_windfall benefit. Otherwise it drifts 0 to 2 toward 50 per turn.
5. information_integrity falls when synthetic content overwhelms verification: −5 to −12 after an information_crisis; −2 to −5 after a bio_misuse_scare (panic outruns facts). It rises 2 to 6 in a turn where actors land provenance, detection, or platform-accountability measures – never on its own.
6. eu_us_dependence rises as the frontier concentrates in America: +2 to +6 when frontier_capability gains more than 10 in a turn; +1 to +3 after a compute_bottleneck or strait_clash. It falls 3 to 8 in a turn where actors land European compute, model, or supply-chain capacity – never on its own.
7. governance_strength rises when actors coordinate or after a visible scare: +3 to +8 when a new agreement or major regulation lands; +2 to +6 in the turn after a bio_misuse_scare, control_scare, or information_crisis. It drifts down 0 to 3 per turn when no governance action occurs.
8. us_china_tension rises on confrontation and falls on de-escalation: +8 to +15 after a strait_clash; +3 to +8 in a turn of confrontational moves; −5 to −12 on genuine de-escalation or hotline agreements. Without such triggers it drifts up 0 to 3 per turn while frontier_capability is above 50.
9. Each actor pool refills +8 to +12 per turn up to 100 (budgets voted, revenues earned, chips secured). Teams spend by describing what they do – price flagship programs at 15 to 25, major initiatives at 8 to 15, modest moves at 3 to 8, judged against what the action actually demands. A pool can never drop below 0: an action priced beyond the pool succeeds only partially that turn, and the narrative says what fell short.
10. Spending must bite: a flagship program should visibly move at least one world metric (capability, science, displacement, trust, integrity, dependence, governance, tension) in the direction the action aims at. Cost without effect reads as waste – mark it in the narrative and let public_trust slip 2 to 5 for the spender.
