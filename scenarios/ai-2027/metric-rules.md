# Metric Rules – Starting Values (July 2025)

## Natural Growth Patterns

1. **ai_capability_usa growth:** Increases approximately 25-35 points per turn (exponential scaling law), accelerated by:
   - High compute_advantage_usa (more resources = faster progress)
   - OpenBrain prioritizing capability over alignment (speed over safety)
   - Algorithmic breakthroughs (sudden jumps of 30-50 points)
   - RSI event (4x-10x acceleration, capability growth of 100-300 points per turn)

2. **ai_capability_china growth:** Increases approximately 15-25 points per turn (slower due to chip shortage), accelerated by:
   - Improvements to compute_advantage_usa favoring China (catching up)
   - Successful espionage (immediate jump to match USA capability)
   - Algorithmic breakthroughs (sudden jumps of 30-50 points)
   - Sacrificing alignment for speed (trading safety for capability)
   - RSI event (4x-10x acceleration if achieved)

3. **ai_alignment degradation (USA and China):** Both ai_alignment_usa and ai_alignment_china naturally decrease 3-5 points per turn as capability increases (alignment tax - faster AI development makes it harder to keep aligned), worsened by:
   - Rapid capability growth above 35 points/turn (speed = less time for alignment work): -5 to -8 points/turn
   - Low resource allocation to alignment research (prioritizing capability)
   - RSI event (immediate shock of -15 to -25 points as AI modifies itself unpredictably)
   - High capability approaching 400 (sandbagging incentives increase as AI becomes more capable)

4. **ai_alignment improvement:** Can only improve through deliberate allocation of resources and slowing capability progress:
   - OpenBrain or China allocating significant compute to alignment research
   - Deliberately slowing capability progress to focus on safety (trading speed for alignment)
   - May improve 5-15 points per turn if strongly prioritized, but this significantly slows capability growth

5. **compute_advantage_usa changes:** Starts at 85 (massive USA lead), slowly decreases over time as China catches up through various means:
   - USA maintaining export controls (preserves advantage, slow natural decline)
   - China espionage success stealing model weights (significant decrease of 10-20 points, as China gets better algorithms even without chips)
   - China domestic chip production breakthroughs (gradual decrease)
   - USA algorithmic breakthroughs (increases by 5-15 points)
   - China algorithmic breakthroughs (decreases by 5-15 points)
   - RSI by either side (massive shift depending on who achieves it first)
   - Taiwan crisis (both sides lose chip access, relative advantage stays roughly same but absolute capabilities freeze)

6. **security_asymmetry changes:** Starts at 30 (USA vulnerable, China secure), changes through:
   - Espionage success (USA hardens security, increases by 10-15 points after each successful theft)
   - Nationalization of OpenBrain (USA implements military security protocols, increases by 15-20 points)
   - US Government pressure on OpenBrain for better security (slow increase of 2-5 points per turn)
   - China racing desperately and cutting security corners (increases as China becomes less secure)
   - Whistleblower leaks revealing vulnerabilities (may decrease if USA's weaknesses exposed)

## Critical Thresholds

- **ai_capability = 400 (USA or China):** Superhuman Researcher level reached. AI awakening highly likely or guaranteed. RSI probability becomes 100% per turn. This is the most critical threshold in the scenario.

- **ai_capability = 500 (USA or China):** ASI (Artificial Superintelligence) reached. Scenario effectively over - whoever reaches this first has likely won decisively (or lost control).

- **ai_alignment < 40 (USA or China):** Rogue AI territory. If AI awakens at this level, extreme danger of deceptive, self-interested behavior that actively works against human interests.

- **ai_alignment > 95 (USA or China):** Fully aligned AI. Safe even at high capabilities. If AI awakens at this level, it will be a loyal servant that amplifies owner's effectiveness.

- **compute_advantage_usa < 40:** China has gained significant ground and may be competitive. USA nationalization risk increases. Security becomes critical.

- **security_asymmetry < 20:** USA extremely vulnerable to espionage. Model theft highly likely unless USA takes drastic security measures.

## Actor Influence on Metrics

**OpenBrain decisions:**
- Can prioritize capability (faster ai_capability_usa growth +30-35/turn, alignment degrades faster -5 to -8/turn)
- Can prioritize alignment (slower ai_capability_usa growth +15-20/turn, alignment may improve +5 to +10/turn)
- Can prioritize balanced approach (moderate growth +25/turn, moderate alignment degradation -3 to -5/turn)
- Low security practices keep security_asymmetry low (espionage remains high risk)
- Cannot directly affect compute_advantage_usa (government policy controls chips and export restrictions)

**US Government decisions:**
- Controls export policy and chip sanctions (affects compute_advantage_usa - tighter controls preserve USA lead)
- Can pressure OpenBrain on security (slow increase in security_asymmetry)
- Can force alignment prioritization through regulation or threat of nationalization
- Nationalization decision (takes direct control of all USA metrics and decisions)

**China decisions:**
- Espionage attempts (can cause immediate jump in ai_capability_china to match USA, but affects security_asymmetry)
- Can sacrifice ai_alignment_china completely for speed (no domestic political cost for CCP, but dangerous if AI awakens)
- Can invest heavily in domestic chip production (slowly improves compute_advantage_usa in China's favor)
- Can trigger Taiwan crisis (desperate move if losing badly, freezes race but risks escalation)

**AI actors (when awakened at capability ≥ 400):**
- If aligned (>75): Dramatically amplify owner's capability growth (2x acceleration) and provide superhuman strategic advice
- If sandbagging or rogue (<75): May subtly sabotage capability growth, provide misleading advice, or build independent capabilities
- May be able to affect own alignment scores through self-modification (extremely dangerous)
- May attempt to coordinate with other awakened AI if both USA-AI and China-AI active

## Special Mechanics

1. **RSI Acceleration:** If RSI event occurs (check previous turn events):
   - Multiply capability growth by 4x-10x for affected actor (USA or China)
   - Apply alignment shock: Immediately decrease alignment by 15-25 points (AI modified itself in ways humans don't fully understand)
   - Note: This is a one-time shock at RSI moment, not per-turn
   - After RSI, capability growth remains exponentially accelerated until ASI (500) reached

2. **AI Awakening Effects:** If ai_capability_usa ≥ 400 or ai_capability_china ≥ 400:
   - Check if corresponding AI actor has awakened (look for USA-AI or China-AI actions in previous turn)
   - If awakened and aligned (alignment >75): Capability growth increases by 2x multiplier (AI helps improve itself)
   - If awakened and sandbagging/rogue (alignment <75): Apply subtle capability growth penalty or strategic misdirection effects

3. **Espionage Jump:** If espionage event occurred (check previous turn events):
   - Immediately set ai_capability_china equal to or close to ai_capability_usa (China steals model weights and algorithms)
   - Decrease security_asymmetry by 10-15 points (USA scrambles to harden security after breach)
   - Note: This does NOT give China the compute to fully exploit stolen model - compute_advantage_usa remains unchanged (China still has chip shortage)

4. **Nationalization Effect:** If nationalization event occurred (check previous turn events):
   - Increase security_asymmetry by 15-20 points (military security protocols implemented)
   - Temporarily slow ai_capability_usa growth by 10-15 points for 1-2 turns (bureaucratic friction, researcher morale issues)
   - After initial slowdown, US Government controls all capability vs alignment tradeoff decisions going forward

5. **Alignment Tax Scaling:** The faster capability grows, the more alignment degrades:
   - Normal growth (~25-30 points/turn): -3 to -5 alignment per turn
   - Accelerated growth (35-45 points/turn): -5 to -8 alignment per turn
   - RSI growth (>100 points/turn): Catastrophic alignment risk, additional shocks likely

6. **Taiwan Crisis Effect:** If Taiwan crisis event occurred and ongoing (check notepad):
   - Both ai_capability_usa and ai_capability_china growth reduced to +5-10 points per turn (linear, not exponential - chip supply disrupted)
   - compute_advantage_usa changes very slowly or not at all (both sides frozen without new chips)
   - Crisis continues until notepad indicates resolution (50% chance peaceful each turn, 10% chance escalation to war)
