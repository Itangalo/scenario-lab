### The sweep returns
Autumn brought the form defenders had dreaded: a large automated intrusion rolled across municipal systems, clinics and a regional grid operator in the same week, using compromised update tooling whose blast radius took days to map. Screens went dark in town halls, appointments were cancelled, and attribution teams said only that the tooling was machine-built and full answers were months away.

Where the pooled command wiring held, isolation and locked-backup rebuilds worked. Crews cut segments, restored core registers and kept two hospital groups on electronic triage. Where it did not, councils reverted to paper for a fortnight.

### Plague protocol and exhaustion
The bio-containment surge became the winter plan. Mobile sequencing vans, isolation wards taped off in car parks, offline kits and decontamination teams stayed deployed through the turn. Continuity money still required clinical and network telemetry, and resentment hardened into open refusal in several opposition-led cities.

Allied rotation teams were quoted in regional press saying they could not keep shuttling town to town. Brussels rationed help quietly, prioritising sharing members. Requests again outpaced crews, and a single-source report of crews sleeping in a clinic corridor circulated widely.

### A deal in Washington and Beijing, a side-deal at home
Washington and Beijing announced a limited accord on weights security, escalation safeguards and controls on a class of biological design tools, with thin verification. Brussels was not at the table. Its late bid for observer-verifier status — offering pooled attribution telemetry and bio-surveillance data — drew polite acknowledgement and no invitation.

Worse, a member state unveiled its own access arrangement with a foreign hyperscaler on terms that undercut the common mandate, defended at home as pragmatism. The Commission invoked the common line and threatened review instruments, but could not reverse it.

Wards stayed open where backups held. The Union held the line on operations, and lost ground on standing.
