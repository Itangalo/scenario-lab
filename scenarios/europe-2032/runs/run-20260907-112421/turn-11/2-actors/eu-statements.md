The Open Science Resilience Initiative has shown that auditable models can deliver real capability — but only in silos. If we cannot verify our own systems in real time, we cannot defend, respond, or even attribute. This expansion is not a shift — it is the necessary scope of the original commitment, now made visible by catastrophe.
