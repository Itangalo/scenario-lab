## In practice  
This turn, we are finalizing the closure of the Open Science Resilience Initiative. The three hubs in Germany, Sweden, and France have delivered their first auditable models for bio-risk forecasting and grid resilience — not frontier-smashing, but robust, inspectable, and already being stress-tested by national regulators. DG CONNECT and DG SANTE are signing off on the transition framework, ensuring that each hub maintains dual reporting lines to Brussels and national parliaments, embedding accountability into technical governance. These models will feed into the new directive, becoming the baseline standard for what counts as “auditable.”

The joint threat response integration is now operational, but incomplete. Secure pipelines run between ENISA and ECDC in Brussels and Stockholm, and six member states are fully connected. Italy and Poland remain partial — citing legacy IT exposure — so we are deploying rapid assessment teams with emergency modernization funds under the Digital Europe Programme, not as conditionality but as solidarity. This is not coercion; it is investment in collective visibility.

But the cyber_major_incident forces escalation. A model-generated ransomware sweep exploited poisoned dependencies with surgical precision — evolving its targeting mid-attack. We did not fail to defend; we failed to comprehend. And with ai_capability at 94.0, we will never catch up if we keep chasing ghosts in black boxes. So we are acting: drafting a Council Regulation under Article 114 TFEU, with derogations under Article 346 for defence-specific cases, to make auditable AI a mandatory condition for any system touching critical infrastructure. The European Defence Agency will get a carve-out, but civilian energy grids, hospitals, and transport networks will not run on models whose decisions cannot be traced. We are working with Legal Service to narrow scope to systems with access to essential data, avoiding blanket technology mandates. Industry resistance is fierce — firms warn of performance gaps — but the ai_investment_collapse has weakened the frontier labs’ bargaining power. We are offering fast-track certification and procurement advantages to auditable-first developers, turning compliance into competitive advantage. This is not just a rule — it is a reorientation of the playing field. If the world wants to sell here, it must show its work.
