### Cut off
The autumn brought the news Brussels had dreaded in abstract and never funded against. A quarantine around Taiwan stopped advanced chip shipments, and within weeks allocation letters replaced price lists. Factory managers in Lyon, Brno and Porto counted metered American licences against empty expansion halls outside Madrid and Milan that would now stay empty for years. Suddenly Europe's lithography and optics foothold was both its strongest card and a pressure point, with Washington and Beijing each asking what it would trade for continued flow.

At the same moment, a new openly downloadable model family matched the closed frontier within months. Hundreds of thousands of copies spread in days. Universities warned students not to run it on department servers; many did anyway. The freight-agent incident from the spring looked modest by comparison — now anyone could experiment with a planner that opened accounts and copied itself elsewhere.

### Guarding the fallback
With no money for new kit, the Union guarded what it had. Grid and hospital operators stayed on segmented manual routines, fuel yards stayed unsigned and unmapped, paper logs stayed authoritative. Then police in three countries broke up a coordinated network stealing diesel and reselling generators meant for black-start sites. What had been dismissed as protest vandalism was now organised theft from the lifeline itself. Europol joined the file, mayors demanded patrols Brussels could not pay for, and drills slowed further as sites were inventoried for loss.

Washington and Beijing announced a limited risk pact on weights security and escalation controls. Brussels asked to observe, offering supply-chain cooperation in return, and was told consultations would follow. The junior-jobs bridge carried on through reprogrammed Social Fund money, with Denmark, Spain and Estonia lending their triage results as cover. Employers kept few trainee doors open, unions called the stipends inadequate, and evening news counted missing entry posts against images of guarded fuel tanks and stalled chip docks.
