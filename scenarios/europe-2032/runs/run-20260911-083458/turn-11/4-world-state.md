### Holding the line while the frontier leaks
Autumn brought no relief on the wards. Isolation orders were extended in the three affected states, wastewater tracing widened, and emergency repair crews stayed in town halls and hospitals keeping paper triage and offline dispatch alive. The fallback models hosted in Europe kept running, but nurses and clerks described them as brittle under pressure — slow at night, prone to garbled referrals, hungry for overtime that no one had left to give.

Over that exhaustion two larger shifts landed.

The first was technical and unrecallable. A new openly downloadable model family appeared that was close to the closed frontier, and within days it was on hundreds of thousands of machines. University labs benchmarked it on exploit-writing and patching; biosecurity researchers warned in preprints that genome design assistance had taken a categorical step forward, though the claim was fiercely contested. For European hospitals and ministries it changed little day-to-day, but for anyone trying to govern capability through releases, the ground moved.

The second was diplomatic. Brussels signed a coordination framework with a group of middle powers holding chokepoints in chips, equipment and servicing — joint licence alignment, pooled bargaining for compute access and maintenance, shared testing capacity. The Commission sold it as a way not to be picked off alone after the American cutoff and servicing curbs. It produced communiqués, a joint bargaining mandate and a first offer to pool evaluation work, but no restored servicing contracts and no new machines by December. A wavering capital stayed inside the common line after another round of solidarity aid.

Ordinary offices provided the only good news. Studies across law, accountancy, administration and media showed measurable productivity gains, strongest among juniors, without job cuts — firms that had trimmed early quietly rehired. It did not restore trust after funerals and blackouts, but it softened the mood a fraction and gave ministers brief cover to keep spending on containment and the new bargaining pool.

Factory sites stayed warm and unfunded behind mended fences.
