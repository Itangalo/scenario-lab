## Priority
EU–Middle Power Bargaining and Evaluation Pool, because export-control escalation decides whether servicing, spare parts and therapy inputs flow at all, and without that joint line no restoration bridge can hold.

## In practice
We run the Council through Health and Telecom formations together: HERA/ECDC to license and procure the new therapies for EU-site manufacturing and EU-hosted model-guided prescribing, using Emergency Support Instrument and rescEU stockpiles, with EMA conditional rollout tied to our Fallback Care Corps wards first so the benefit is visibly EU-delivered. ENISA/CERT-EU repair cell teams stay embedded, now shifted to cyber-incident triage — clean backups, manual dispatch, prioritised restore of the three pneumonia states' hospitals.

We use M9 as the shield for both: Trade/Foreign Affairs Council mandates the Commission to bargain servicing, compute maintenance and pharma/biotech tooling as one pooled offer under the Anti-Coercion Instrument, offering shared evaluation work from our Safety Institute as the contribution. No new Gigafactory spend; sites stay warm while every euro of political capital goes to keeping lights, wards and the new treatments running.
