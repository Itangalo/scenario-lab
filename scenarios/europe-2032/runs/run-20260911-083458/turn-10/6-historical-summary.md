Spring brought twin shocks: engineered severe pneumonias in three member states triggered isolation, tracing and wastewater case-finding, while US cut European access to its leading model without reason, crippling hospital, ministry and logistics copilots.

A new code release sharply advanced machine-written exploits and automated patching, multiplying intrusions and signature speed.

Brussels acted without vote under health-emergency powers: repair teams stayed embedded with paper triage and offline backups, sequencing/sentinels turned to containment, and ministries/hospitals ordered onto cleared European-hosted open models with reprogrammed funds for EU inference.

Continuity kits and sentinel surge completed, keeping services degraded not stopped. But substitute models were weaker and failure-prone, staff burned out, and a wavering capital was held to joint licensing line with solidarity aid amid deeper US servicing curbs on ordinary chips.

Trust collapsed amid funerals and cutoff seen as proof of dependence on borrowed ground. No new Gigafactory money; sites stayed warm.
