### The Blind Strike

A wave of AI-powered ransomware cripples health and transport systems across three member states, adapting in real time to evade detection—a hallmark of autonomous evolution. The attack’s sophistication confirms fears: offensive AI has outpaced defences. With no sovereign evaluation capacity, the EU reacts blindly, relying on fragmented national responses.

### Sovereignty in Retreat

The Frontier Access Guarantee remains stalled—chip disputes and security flaws delay deployment. No sovereign inference comes online. As global AI capability rises by +2.5, EU dependence deepens, and *eu_ai_sovereignty* drops to 14.0. The Emergency Sovereignty Procurement Directive ends in failure, compounding the sense of strategic drift.

### A Measure Forged in Crisis

In response, the European Commission proposes the creation of a Sovereign Evaluation Authority—a new body under ENISA and the AI Office—to conduct real-time analysis of model weights on EU soil. The proposed mandate includes probing for goal persistence, self-modification, and unauthorised coordination using red-team agents in air-gapped environments. Feasibility studies begin immediately, and legal groundwork is discussed in the Internal Market Council, with Finland and Estonia expressing interest in hosting.

However, no implementation occurs this turn. The proposal enters early consultation, facing resistance: Germany demands veto rights over findings, smaller states fear exclusion, and developers push back against mandatory access, calling it a nationalisation of insight. The measure is formally logged as a new initiative but remains in design and negotiation, with full operational capacity at least one turn away. Legal fast-tracking is proposed but not enacted.

### Political Capital Crumbles

The portfolio charge remains high—InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, Labour Transition Accelerator Fund, Frontier Access Guarantee, and the priority—totaling −13. The cyberattack triggers public backlash: *public_sentiment* falls to 37.0. Political capital erodes, but not as severely as proposed; with the Sovereign Evaluation Authority not yet active or resourced, its cost is deferred. *eu_political_capital* declines to 22.0, reflecting crisis response and agenda strain, but avoids collapse due to delayed implementation.

### America Turns Inward

The US election is decided: a sweeping victory for the anti-AI platform. Data centre moratoriums, sector taxes, and hiring bans are promised. The frontier slows—not from shortage, but from policy. For the EU, the pressure eases, but the partner it relied on vanishes. The world watches as the second-most capable power gains ground.
