# Statements: The European Union (turn 7)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Assert sovereign authority over AI systems at the frontier by enforcing real-time evaluation, containment, and incident response rights across all models operating within or affecting the EU

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Assert sovereign authority over AI systems at the frontier by enforcing real-time evaluation, containment, and incident response rights across all models operating within or affecting the EU
  - Trigger: the openweight_frontier_release and emergent_bio_uplift_findings this turn
  - Grounds: The frontier is no longer a distant laboratory—it is here, embedded in infrastructure, downloaded onto private hardware, and approaching bio-relevant capabilities. The original commitment to "secure sovereign evaluation and control" assumed a manageable gap between closed and open systems; that gap has collapsed. With offensive AI now adaptive and self-coordinating, and open-weight models nearing the closed frontier, evaluation without enforcement is ceremonial. We are shifting from building oversight to asserting authority: the Union must not only see the models, but stop them when they cross lines. This means mandatory access, binding containment protocols, and the right to interrupt deployment—powers that must now be exercised, not negotiated.
  - Evidence quoted: **openweight_frontier_release:** An open-weight release lands within months of the closed frontier rather than a year. It is downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in the first week, and whatever capability it carries is now on private hardware permanently and beyond recall. `openweight_capability` moves to within 5–10 points of `ai_capability` at a stroke.

**emergent_bio_uplift_findings (emergent event):** A genome model produces a viable design for an organism able to infect humans, or a credible study shows a non-expert reaching that point with model assistance. It is contested on methodology, the authors are accused of both alarmism and of publishing a recipe, and the argument stays inside the biosecurity community – but it is a categorically stronger signal than anything published so far. This is a precursor: it opens the bio gate for the next 4 turns.
  - Reason: The developments collapse the gap between closed and open AI, distributing dangerous capabilities and raising bio-risk, directly undermining the original commitment's assumptions about control and evaluation.
