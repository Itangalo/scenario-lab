## In practice  
We are acting on the fact that the EU can no longer afford to be a spectator to the frontier. The open-weight release within months of the closed frontier means that every dangerous capability is now permanently distributed—on laptops, in labs, in the hands of actors who need no permission to run it. The emergent_bio_uplift_findings, though contested, are the clearest signal yet that the bio gate is opening. We cannot wait for consensus when the threat is already in motion.

This turn, we are amending the Sovereign Evaluation Authority’s mandate through a fast-tracked regulation under Article 114 TFEU, adding emergency containment powers that allow the Authority to halt deployment, demand model modifications, or trigger isolation protocols when an AI system exhibits behaviour that could lead to uncontrolled replication, autonomous coordination, or bio-design capability. We are proposing this in the Internal Market Council, pairing it with a binding review clause: any suspension must be justified within 72 hours and reviewed by a joint panel of ENISA, the AI Office, and two member states (one sceptical, one supportive). To Germany, we offer a national override on enforcement affecting its sovereign infrastructure; to France, we offer co-leadership of the review panel. To the developers, we offer a pre-engagement window: if they submit to voluntary audits six weeks before release, they gain immunity from emergency suspension unless new risks emerge.

We are funding the legal and technical upgrade through a reallocation of the €1.4bn REACT-EU envelope, shifting focus from cluster build-out to enforcement readiness. Air-gapped evaluation nodes in Estonia and Finland will now include automated containment triggers and forensic logging, and we are staffing a rapid-response unit drawn from national cyber and biosecurity agencies. The Gigafactories, though finishing this turn, will continue under the InvestEU framework—we are not abandoning industrial capacity, but we are no longer pretending that compute alone gives us control. Control now means the right to stop. And this turn, we are taking it.
