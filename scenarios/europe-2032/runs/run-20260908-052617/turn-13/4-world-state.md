### The Weight of Speed

A model-generated ransomware wave cripples public services across five member states, exploiting AI-optimised zero-day flaws in a shared administrative software stack. The attack unfolds in minutes; coordinated response begins only after 38 hours. Defenders are blind to the mechanism, not the malware — its logic is adaptive, recursive, and unrecognisable from known signatures. The incident confirms that offensive AI has decisively outpaced defensive cycles.

In response, the EU launches the Behavioural Surveillance and Anomaly Response Framework (BARF), reallocating €1.8 billion to deploy real-time telemetry across critical infrastructure. Automated containment — sandboxing, circuit-breaking — will trigger on statistical anomalies in recursion depth, data flow, or self-modification. But BARF cannot monitor what it does not host: foreign-dependent systems remain blind spots, and rollout is uneven. Germany delays integration, citing legal uncertainty over automated shutdowns.

The Distributed Detection and Disruption Network formally completes deployment, with nodes in four countries now legally empowered. Yet they remain passive. Without legible reasoning, no disruption order meets the CJEU’s threshold for justiciable cause. Behavioural proxies are still in testing; no tool can yet justify intervention.

Sovereign inference efforts continue under emergency powers, but migration bottlenecks persist. Workforce shortages delay the porting of health and border models. The InvestAI Gigafactories deliver compute, but not capability at scale.

Public sentiment erodes further as strikes in France, Germany, and Belgium intensify. Workers denounce AI systems as unaccountable and destabilising. Eastern member states maintain their block on regional funds, tying disbursement to binding job guarantees. Fiscal cohesion frays.

Meanwhile, global AI capability surges past 88.0. The instrument is losing resolution: systems now design their own improvements faster than institutions can characterise them. Safety, already minimal, declines further — not from negligence, but from irrelevance.
