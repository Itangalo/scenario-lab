## In practice
We task DG TRADE and DG COMP to open an Anti-Coercion Instrument examination of the expanded US lithography controls, with a Council mandate that no further ASML export or servicing cut is implemented without joint EU authorisation, offering The Hague indemnity and EU-level compensation for lost servicing contracts. In parallel we link any future Dutch licensing to reciprocal guarantees: vetted EU evaluator access to US frontier models and security assurances for EU-hosted compute.

We keep InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and the Tech sovereignty package (M2) alive through the investment collapse by shifting emphasis from private co-finance to EIB, Innovation Fund and redirected cohesion bridging, and by slowing site selection to match power realities in France, Germany, Spain, Sweden and Poland rather than cancelling. The Evaluation Institute (M3) is directed to add an industrial-control systems track, using the port-operator intrusion artefacts to draft mandatory segmentation and incident-reporting standards for transmission operators via ENISA and ACER, without opening a separate resilience programme this turn.
