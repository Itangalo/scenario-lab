## Priority
EU Grid and Public-Service Cyber Shield, because with ICS playbooks circulating and open weights at the frontier, a second cascade would finish both sovereignty and trust — redress matters but lights staying on outranks it this turn.

## In practice
We execute the Shield (M5) through ENISA, EU-CyCLONe and ACER: segmentation and backup control for the two transmission zones, NIS2 incident reports turned into detection rules, reprogrammed Digital Europe money held for operators still cleaning contaminated systems. The Evaluation Institute feeds the deployed control/interpretability tool into monitoring.

We open the redress track with the AI Office, Fundamental Rights Agency and national ombudsmen under AI Act Articles 61/65/77: suspend the offending system, order human-oversight and log-review remediation, publish findings, and fund case review for those wronged. We explicitly frame it as breach of high-risk obligations, not a gap in the Act, to avoid a rewrite fight.

We keep Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) alive on grid-plausible sites with security and compensation, spending no new push while capital is at 13 — survival means not starting what we cannot fund.
