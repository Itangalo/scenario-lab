## Priority
EU Grid and Public-Service Cyber Shield, because containing the ongoing cascade outranks even gigafactories — without it political capital and public tolerance for everything else collapses.

## In practice
We task ENISA with ACER and EU-CyCLONe to execute emergency segmentation, backup control and dependency-patching across transmission operators and affected municipalities, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and Recovery bridging, with mandatory incident reporting under NIS2 enforced now. The Evaluation Institute (M3) feeds artefacts from the attack into detection rules and adopts the safety breakthrough for monitoring, while keeping its grid track.

We hold InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) on public financing and grid-plausible siting, adding security perimeters and community compensation after the sabotage at two sites to keep permitting alive. The Chokepoint Shield (M4) stays as legal cover: no ASML servicing cut implemented without joint authorisation, while we seek US model access in exchange, but we spend no new capital pushing it this turn.
