# Statements: The European Union (turn 7)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI capacity to act independently in a world where capability outpaces control

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign AI capacity to act independently in a world where capability outpaces control — and now, with runtime verification as a condition of that independence, not a complement
  - Grounds: The original commitment evolved last turn to reflect that sovereignty requires not just hardware but control. This turn, the failure to operationalise verification despite the safety breakthrough proves that anchoring compute is not enough: if the EU cannot observe and enforce limits on agent behaviour in its own critical infrastructure, it does not own the systems running on its soil. The commitment must now explicitly bind runtime assurance to sovereignty, or the gap between possession and agency will widen unchecked.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
