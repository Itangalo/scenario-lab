On bio-risk, we act to close a dangerous gap. The unsanctioned red-teaming by independent labs proves both necessity and fragility. We cannot depend on goodwill. This is not a ban — it is a condition of responsible diffusion. We coordinate with national biosafety boards to harmonise protocols and prevent duplication, but sovereignty over findings rests with the Union. This is how we prevent harm without surrendering control.
