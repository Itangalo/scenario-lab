### A Step Forward, Two Steps Back

Global AI capability advances to 59.0, driven by continued integration of agent systems and efficiency improvements in training pipelines. The closed frontier inches forward without discontinuity, but the pace remains steady.

In Europe, the **Sovereign Compute Acceleration Directive** formally concludes, having established binding legal oversight for data centres in three member states. This delivers a +3 boost under metric rule 5, though rising capability costs −1, resulting in a net gain for sovereignty.

The EU announces the **Sovereign AI Run-Time Assurance Programme**, a category 5 measure aimed at building independent verification capacity for high-risk AI systems. While the Commission signals intent to prioritize AI verification in coming months, no formal designation has been made, and no staffing, budget, or telemetry access has been secured. As such, the measure remains in early proposal phase and does not yet contribute to portfolio burden or qualify for the proposal bonus. It is not yet the named strategic priority, and no priority penalty is applied.

Meanwhile, **export_control_escalation** and **supply_chain_coercion** unfold in tandem. Washington tightens chip and model export controls, restricting allied access under volume licensing. Simultaneously, ASML faces expanded restrictions on servicing older lithography tools abroad, undermining European leverage in semiconductor supply chains. These actions constitute an event that takes away EU access to critical AI capacity. Under rule 5, this triggers a −2 term, fully offsetting the earlier +3 gain from the concluded directive, leaving `eu_ai_sovereignty` unchanged at 22.0.

The **adoption_success** event lifts public sentiment: digital services in Estonia and Finland cut processing delays by over half, and retraining pilots show early promise. This yields a +2 bump to `public_sentiment`, now at 34.0.

Political capital remains unchanged. The portfolio charge holds at −12, reflecting four active large measures carried over from previous turns: InvestAI Gigafactories (−3), Tech sovereignty package (−3), ASML Alternative Lithography Initiative (−3), and Emergency Labour Transition Facility (−3). No new burden is added this turn, as the new assurance programme has not entered implementation. The proposal bonus is not applied, as the measure has not yet cleared initial resourcing thresholds. With no additional costs or rewards, `eu_political_capital` remains at 30.0.

Resilience dips slightly as no category 6 measure is active and capability rises, per rule 4.
