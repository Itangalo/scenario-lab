  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign AI capacity to act independently in a world where capability outpaces control — and now, with runtime verification as a condition of that independence, not a complement — and with the understanding that sovereignty must now withstand both external defection and internal fragmentation

The commitment must now reflect that sovereignty is not only about compute and telemetry, but about the authority to bind member states to a common standard. This is not a reversal, but a deepening forced by the failure of enforcement to follow intent.
