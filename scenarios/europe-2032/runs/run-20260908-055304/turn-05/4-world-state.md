### The Weight of Transition

Global AI capability advances steadily to 58.0, driven by efficiency gains and tighter integration of agent systems. No discontinuity occurs, but the closed frontier continues its incremental climb. In response to the **safety_breakthrough**, some leading labs begin piloting certified behaviour prediction frameworks in new model deployments. Adoption remains partial and concentrated in North America and Europe, with no standardized rollout in China or among frontier developers outside regulatory reach. As a result, `ai_safety` sees modest improvement to 26.0, reflecting early traction without global compliance.

### Sovereign Foundations Laid

The **Sovereign Compute Acceleration Directive**, proposed in Turn 4, enters partial effect this turn. Legal anchoring and audit rights for EU-supported AI data centres are now enforceable under ENISA oversight. First Sovereignty Zone designations progress in France, Germany, and Belgium, establishing institutional legitimacy. While no new compute is yet operational, the framework is binding. This delivers a +3 boost to `eu_ai_sovereignty`, countering the −1 decay from rising capability, resulting in a net increase to 22.0 as recorded in the notepad.

### Labour Crisis Meets Labour Measure

The **emergent_labour_displacement_wave** hardens into open political crisis. Union federations threaten pan-European strikes unless action is taken. In response, the European Commission formally tables the **Emergency Labour Transition Facility**, a category 7 measure proposing income support and retraining funded by mandatory contributions from automating firms. However, legal basis under Article 114 TFEU is immediately contested by several member states and industry groups. The European Parliament defers debate pending a legal opinion from the Court of Justice. As a result, the measure does not enter the active portfolio this turn, and no proposal bonus is accrued.

### Political Capital Adjusts Amid Partial Delivery

The partial realization of the Sovereign Compute Directive yields a +2 delivery bonus to `eu_political_capital`. However, portfolio costs remain high (−13), and no offsetting gains arise from stalled initiatives. The net change is −11, bringing `eu_political_capital` down to 30.0. Public sentiment rises slightly to 32.0 on early signals of institutional action, despite unresolved labour concerns.
