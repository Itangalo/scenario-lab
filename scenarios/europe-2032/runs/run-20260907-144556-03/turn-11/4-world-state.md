### The Weight of Absence

AI capability plateaus at 68.5, as frontier labs fail to deliver transformative gains and market prices for high-end inference collapse — a signal that last year’s peak is now widely replicable. Yet this stagnation brings no relief. A leaked training run from a defunct startup begins to circulate, enabling open-weight models to advance. Early replications and fine-tuned variants demonstrate unexpected robustness in cyber automation and expert task emulation, pushing openweight_capability to 59.0. While significant, the diffusion remains partial: deployment is fragmented, compute bottlenecks persist, and full replication requires rare expertise. The capability rise is real but incomplete — a wave still building, not yet crested.

The EU’s Openweight Containment Initiative collapses with its target, undone by technical infeasibility and member-state non-compliance. With no unified response, the spread accelerates in unregulated corners.

A major cyber incident strikes: a coordinated ransomware sweep disables public health and transport systems across three member states. The attack is largely automated, using AI-generated tooling that evades traditional detection. Response is fragmented, and attribution remains unclear months later. In its wake, the Critical Infrastructure Resilience Surge is credited with limiting cascading failures — a rare success — boosting resilience to 43.0.

Meanwhile, the Integrity Audit Expansion formally unravels as three major member states withdraw participation, citing its irrelevance in the face of opaque models and public distrust. With no audit regime capable of verifying safety, and no new safeguards in place, ai_safety plummets to 9.0 — a collapse accelerated by the gap between advancing capability and absent oversight.

The EU launches the Civilian AI Redeployment Corps, a large category 7 measure, aiming to restore legitimacy by re-employing displaced public-sector workers as algorithmic redress officers. The proposal is framed as a moral counterweight to the annulled election and automated decision scandal. It passes under Article 162 TFEU with narrow support, but implementation is delayed — no deployment occurs this turn, and full rollout will require at least one additional cycle. The gesture lifts public sentiment slightly to 20.0.

Political capital, though strained, sees a modest rebound to 5.0. The completion of eight initiatives yields no reward, but the new measure — responding directly to recent crises — earns a proposal bonus. Sovereignty holds at 10.0: no new capacity comes online, and the US posture of consolidation continues to restrict access.

### A Governance Vacuum

With EAAD, SMAF, and the audit expansion all defunct, the EU lacks any functioning mechanism to verify or enforce AI safety. The frontier moves on, but Europe watches in silence.
