### Stalled Momentum, Fragile Gains

AI capability remains stagnant at 68.5, as frontier progress stalls under the weight of diminishing returns and internal turbulence at leading labs. Yet the open frontier surges: a leaked training run from a defunct startup continues to circulate, enabling distributed fine-tuning efforts that push openweight_capability to 59.5. Defensive efforts cannot contain it — the Openweight Containment Initiative collapses under technical infeasibility and patchy enforcement, with hosting providers in two member states refusing to implement remote deactivation triggers on legal grounds.

The EU’s Critical Infrastructure Resilience Surge limits cascading failures during a major ransomware incident, reinforcing modest gains in resilience, which holds at 43.0. However, the Integrity Audit Expansion formally unravels as three major member states withdraw, citing irrelevance amid opaque models and eroding public trust. With no audit regime capable of verifying safety, ai_safety remains at a historic low of 9.0.

The Civilian AI Redeployment Corps launches under Article 162 TFEU, with front-loaded grants disbursed to early-adopter states. Implementation is slow, and no redress officers are deployed by June. The gesture lifts public sentiment marginally, but it remains at 20.0 amid widespread scepticism.

In a high-stakes bid to reclaim leverage, the Commission pushes the Emergency Compute Access Pact through the Competitiveness Council. Resistance is fierce: smaller member states fear losing bilateral advantages, and legal services in three capitals question the pact’s enforceability against US providers. A fragile political consensus emerges — but only for a watered-down version without mutual defence guarantees. The pact will proceed in name, but its core mechanism is deferred. No new sovereign compute capacity comes online.

### The Weight of Dependence

Sovereignty remains at 10.0. Despite the effort, the EU still lacks control over the infrastructure underpinning its own institutions. The governance vacuum deepens.
