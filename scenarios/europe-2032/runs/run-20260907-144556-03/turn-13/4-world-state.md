### Stalled Frontier, Open Floodgates

AI capability remains frozen at 68.5 as evidence mounts that the frontier has plateaued — top labs release models that underperform projections, and prices for high-end inference drop sharply, suggesting commoditization rather than breakthrough. Yet the open frontier surges: the leaked training run continues to propagate, and distributed fine-tuning efforts, now coordinated by a persistent cyber cartel, push openweight_capability to 64.0 — dangerously close to the threshold where autonomous agents become broadly deployable.

### The JCCC Gambit

The European Union activates the Joint Compute Coordination Cell (JCCC), attempting to operationalize the Emergency Compute Access Pact. Technical nodes are established in Brussels, Dublin, and Marseille, linked via satellite backhaul. But member-state resistance stalls full integration: Estonia and Luxembourg withhold configuration manifests, fearing retaliation from US cloud providers. The promised Dresden lithography access fails to sway them — the incentive is seen as distant, while the risk of exclusion is immediate.

Despite the delays, limited routing tests succeed during a simulated blackout, yielding modest resilience gains. The Civilian AI Redeployment Corps expands to ten municipalities, but deployment remains patchy, and public sentiment holds at 20.0 amid skepticism about both job displacement and dependency.

### Sovereignty on a Knife-Edge

The Taiwan blockade disrupts global semiconductor flows, turning compute access into a frontline security issue. Yet no new sovereign capacity comes online. The Sovereign AI Core Programme finishes, but its infrastructure remains underutilized due to access disputes. The JCCC, though launched, lacks enforcement teeth — a symbolic step, not a shield.

The emergent openweight cyber cartel demonstrates sustained attacks on French and Polish systems, exploiting leaked models to automate intrusion and evasion. With no effective containment regime, offensive AI is now organized, persistent, and distributed.
