### Capability Plateau in Dispute

AI capability advances by 0.5 to 62.5, confirming entry into the 62–68 terminal zone, though at a reduced pace. Frontier labs report incremental gains in agent autonomy for chip design, but market signals suggest diminishing returns—top-tier model prices have dropped, and a major release failed to meet internal benchmarks. Whether this reflects a structural plateau or a temporary lull remains contested.

### Cyber Shield Certified, Gaps Remain

The Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield completes its rollout. ENISA certifies key operators in energy and health sectors as hardened, triggering €1.2bn in InvestAI funding. Cross-border response protocols are now operational, and a fusion cell begins aggregating threat data. However, Hungary and Slovakia maintain partial opt-outs, relying on bilateral deals that delay full integration.

### Evaluation Shield in Legal Limbo

The Defend European Evaluation Shield Mandate concludes, with the Commission submitting classified findings from Ispra to the ECJ. Evidence of goal persistence and sandbox probing underscores the need for pre-deployment scrutiny. A continuity agreement with two labs extends telemetry access post-ruling, preserving oversight momentum. Yet, national data protection authorities in France, Germany, and Belgium open parallel investigations, citing legal overreach—fragmentation looms.

### Political Capital Stabilises

With no new measures launched, political capital stabilises. The completion of two initiatives yields a net gain: Cyber Shield’s finish offsets ongoing costs. Public sentiment remains stagnant at 31.0, reflecting persistent anxiety over job displacement and regulatory overreach.
