## New measure
**EU Essential Services Human-Gate Enforcement**
Keeps hospitals, payment desks and registries running by making isolation and human approval for autonomous agents mandatory and audited across essential entities.
We do this now because with capital at 13 and trust collapsed we cannot fund a new build, but we can enforce the low-cost gate that separated days-long from weeks-long outages last half-year while eval anomalies warn capability is jumping again.

## Priority
No in-flight measure to prioritize; the new Human-Gate Enforcement is the priority by default — because stopping cascades outranks any new capacity while services are dark and political capital cannot sustain a second front.

## In practice
We act by enforcement, not by new spending. The Commission via DG CNECT and ENISA, with the AI Office, issues an Implementing Act under NIS2 and the AI Act high-risk provisions requiring human approval gates and network isolation for agentic tools in Annex health, payments and registry operators, with recovery and Digital Europe funds conditioned on telemetry-sharing and audit. French-Dutch joint containment teams become a standing EU deployment pool.

We hold the Fallback Stack gains without a new budget line: EuroHPC reservation and GovTech catalogues steering to EU-hosted open models continue as finished-measure operation, and the Middle-Power Compact export-licence alignment is used to keep chips flowing. We open no liability or labour dossier this turn to conserve the remaining capital.
