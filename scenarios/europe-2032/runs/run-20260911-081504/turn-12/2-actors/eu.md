## New measure
**EU Local Resilience Repair Corps**
Puts funded technicians and approval staff into small municipalities and subcontracted registries to make the human-gate rules actually operable and visibly shorten outages.
We do this now because gates work where staffed and fail where not, and with capital at 15 and sentiment at 8 we cannot afford another build — only the cheap fix that turns paper law into shorter dark periods and buys back trust.

## Priority
The new Local Resilience Repair Corps is the priority by default — because without staffed gates the finished Shield and Human-Gate measures decay and no capacity project is fundable.

## In practice
We act through ENISA and DG REGIO via cohesion reprogramming, not new money: recovery funds already conditioned on telemetry are released to hire and second gate-approvers and field technicians to the weakest registries and municipal payment offices, with the French-Dutch standing pool as trainers. Council EPSCO and JHA formations are asked only for secondments, avoiding a fresh legislative fight.

We hold what finished: EuroHPC fallback reservations and EU-hosted catalogue steering stay as operating procedure, and the Middle-Power export-licence alignment is used quietly to keep spares and chips flowing. No siting or frontier-lab dossier is opened this turn to conserve capital while graffiti and siting-pause pressure continue.
