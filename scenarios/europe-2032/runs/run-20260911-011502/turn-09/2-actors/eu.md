## In practice
We keep the Fallback Corps as the Union's visible face: ENISA and the Joint Cyber Unit via Health and Transport Councils sustaining offline kits, manual procedures and daily coordination in hit municipalities, clinics and ports on existing Digital Europe emergency funds, with no new money asked.

We do not break ground or force grid connections at the blockaded gigafactory site; DG CNECT and the EIB keep all five reservations legally warm and paid, while Commission mediators and the rotating Presidency offer power-price relief dialogue to farmers and mayors to prevent a second blockade. The solo foreign cloud deal is contained, not cut, with further repair help itemised but continuing.
