US election winner pledged models as national asset with tiered foreign access/tighter exports, confirming revocable dependence.

EU pooled leverage around 5 unbuilt gigafactory sites (Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm, Warsaw): joint licence alignment with Netherlands/Japan/Korea, common compute ask in Washington, shared testing via evaluation institute. Permits preserved, power protests continued.

Winter brought machine-written ransomware/poisoned updates hitting municipalities, clinics, ports across states; services reverted to paper, attribution blurred. Brussels responded with joint repair cell under EU cybersecurity agency using existing teams/emergency funds, restoring backups without new money — visible help but re-installing exposed systems.

Collective front fractured as one capital signed solo privileged model/cloud deal, undercutting common bargaining line; Paris/The Hague tried to hold licensing front, offering share of Washington deal while warning solo deals risked solidarity repairs.

Sovereignty package formally closed: permits, frameworks, pledges worth hundreds of billions declared secured, but no factory ground broken. By June 2029 services partly restored, trust thinner: EU could send help, not prevent next wave nor keep members aligned; dependence managed not overcome.
