## Priority
Municipal Fallback Corps, because with no measures in flight and capital at 5 it is the only deliverable that serves the two-year commitment to hold together through dependence.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the Joint Cyber Unit via the Health and Transport Councils to deploy the existing CSIRT roster and Digital Europe emergency funds to the worst-hit municipalities, clinics and ports, leaving laminated fallback procedures and offline backup kits on site. DG DIGIT runs daily coordination so mayors see Union vests, not Brussels paperwork.

We keep the five gigafactory reservations legally warm with existing fees via DG CNECT and the EIB but break no ground, and we use the Foreign Affairs Council to contain the solo-deal split by making continued repair help conditional on return to the common line, without cutting anyone off. No new money is asked while export-control tightening and embodied-AI job pressure are handled by messaging, not instruments.
