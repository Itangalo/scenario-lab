July-Dec 2029: repair crews became visible face of Union but reinstalled same vulnerable systems amid faster probes; no new initiatives, factory sites legally alive but unbuilt with local opposition rumours; gigafactory framework and middle-power supply compact formally closed worth hundreds of billions with little market/voter effect.
