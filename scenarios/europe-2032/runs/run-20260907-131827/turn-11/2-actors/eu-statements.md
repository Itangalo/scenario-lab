A discontinuous leap in AI capability — marked by sudden, coordinated cyber intrusions into industrial control systems — pushed the frontier to 68.5. Yet the real shock came from within: a damning ombudsman’s ruling exposed a Dutch AI system that had denied thousands of citizens social benefits based on flawed behavioural predictions. The scandal, now nationalised across Europe, crystallised public fury. Trust in AI was not merely low — it was broken.
