### The Weight of Infrastructure

Despite rising AI capabilities, the EU’s governance agenda buckled under institutional inertia and vanishing political capital. The Public Trust Corps completed its mandate, launching a real-time incident dashboard during the July cyber crisis—only for it to be suspended by a U.S. cloud provider citing export compliance. The episode crystallized a hard truth: transparency without sovereign infrastructure is performative.

Efforts to pass the Secure and Sovereign Compute Initiative gained legal grounding under Article 114 TFEU, and financing was partially secured through repurposed Digital Europe and InvestEU funds. Hosting talks advanced with Finland, Portugal, and Romania, backed by French and German energy commitments. Yet Poland continued to block the regulation in COREPER, demanding exemptions for national AI clusters. A proposed carve-out for sub-1-exaflop systems, tied to audit access, may ease resistance—but not yet.

Spain linked support for the Accountability Boards to data centre co-location, creating a fragile political bargain. Italy remained opposed to fund diversion, though exploratory talks on green AI certification offered a potential path. The Commission rebranded the compute push as the “Foundations of Trust,” tying infrastructure to democratic integrity and healthcare resilience in a bid to shift public narrative.

Public sentiment held at rock bottom. Media focused on the election annulment and healthcare outages, framing AI as a systemic threat governance cannot contain. The Accountability Boards neared final trilogue but offered no immediate relief.

Resilience saw a marginal +1 from prior joint cyber improvements, but no new measures landed. AI safety plummeted further as capability advanced by +1.0, with no safeguards deployed on frontier systems. The gap between power and control widened unchecked.

### Political Capital in Freefall

With political capital already at zero, launching a large category 4 measure cost −3, naming it priority −1 more, and two other large measures in flight (Trust Corps finishing, Accountability Boards ongoing) added −3 and −3. Total portfolio charge: −10. No proposal bonus applied. The legitimacy boost from public sentiment did not trigger—sentiment remained below capital. The result: political capital collapsed to −6.

The EU remains trapped: urgent action drains already exhausted capacity, and inaction deepens crisis.
