### Patching faster, judged harder
The autumn brought one piece of genuinely good engineering news. New defensive tooling — automatic patching pushed at machine speed and detectors tuned to swarm behaviour rather than signatures — finally closed off a whole class of intrusion that had plagued grid operators and hospitals. ENISA folded it quietly into guidance for operators already on the continuity schemes, and for weeks incident logs were calmer. Technicians noticed; the public did not.

Any credit was drowned by two other shocks.

Washington tightened export licensing again. Under the country-tier system, European buyers kept their licences in name while volumes were withheld in fact. Dutch transit hubs complied, reluctantly and on camera, logging requests that never turned into shipments. With nothing to buy and no retaliation Brussels could sustain, the episode read at home as another demonstration that access was someone else's gift.

Then the benefits affair returned. An ombudsman finding, backed by court judgments, showed the suspended social-insurance system had systematically wronged claimants for years: conformity files passed on paper, human review reduced to a caseworker clearing a queue at forty seconds a file, logs generated correctly and never read. Brussels argued breach, not design — high-risk duties existed and were hollowed out — to save the AI Act itself. Lawyers and press drew the opposite moral: enforcement discredited, redress crawling through a few thousand re-examined files and back-pay in tranches.

The one thing that finished — the guarded lifeline pact for dispersed black-start stores and trusted administration — paid out modestly. Where towns trusted the flat reimbursements, spares stayed; where they did not, the two-books habit hardened, with a token stock for inspectors and the real reserve off-list. Winter break-ins, torched depots and drivers refusing night runs without escort were handled as national sabotage cases, logged in Brussels without a new operation.

By December, lights stayed on and collapse was again avoided. Nothing else could be claimed.
