## Priority
No priority measure in flight this turn, because M9 has finished and with zero capital we cannot credibly push a new instrument; holding the dispersed black-start practice and scandal redress by procedure outranks starting what we cannot fund.

## In practice
We hold by procedure, not by new law. DG ECHO through the Civil Protection Mechanism keeps flat reimbursements for dispersed unmapped micro-stores flowing, guards only on request, no public map and no re-listing. Municipalities, hospitals and DSOs stay on-scheme where trust holds; arson, break-ins and night-run refusals are handled as sabotage of essential services by national police and logged via EU-CyCLONe and ENISA, without a new operation. We quietly absorb the cyber-defence breakthrough into existing hardening — automated patching and swarm-detection pushed through ENISA guidance to operators already on the Grid Shield and Continuity Stack — rather than standing up a programme we cannot staff.

On the renewed automated-decision scandal we stay inside AI Act enforcement already opened: the contested system stays suspended, the AI Office with national supervisors and the Ombudsman continues log publication, case-by-case review and back-pay in tranches, and the two health tools remain on segmented manual operation under separate branding. We argue the breach reading — high-risk obligations passed on paper, human oversight reduced to forty seconds an item, logs unread — to keep the Act intact while conceding enforcement failure. On export-control escalation we make no external ask: with country-tier quotas biting and nothing to buy, Dutch transit complies reluctantly and we log volumes withheld without retaliation we cannot sustain.
