### Holding by hand
The first half of 2031 did not bring a new blackout. It brought something more corrosive: an attack on the workaround.

In several member states, generator pools, transformer stores and the sheds where breaker logs and segmentation kits were kept were broken into, drained, burned or smashed. Leaflets left behind called the stockpiles collaboration with the machine system. Police called it coordinated. Grid engineers called it the loss of the only restoration capacity they had left.

Brussels put the black-start network on a protection footing. Under existing civil-protection and security rules, ENISA, the EU crisis network and national police moved to bring the off-books pools on-scheme — dispersed, guarded, reimbursed, and publicly listed as a lifeline. It partly worked. Some municipalities reported fewer incidents once guards appeared. Others reported holders refusing to declare stocks for fear of being listed as targets.

At the same time the outside tightened again. Washington put allied buyers on volume quotas for advanced chips and model access. The Hague complied under protest. With private capital fleeing the AI sector, valuations collapsing and announced build-outs cancelled, there was nothing to buy even where licences existed. Clinics stayed on a European default with an American fallback now openly metered. Factory managers spoke of being fenced in twice over.

The one bright spot was administrative. In two health systems and a handful of municipalities, European-run scheduling and decision-support tools cut waiting lists and decision times from months to days. DG DIGIT and health ministries co-branded the deployments, paid the teams, and kept them running on segmented manual operation.

It did not shift the mood. Coverage folded the sabotage, the quotas and last year's intrusion wave into a single story of dependence and machines beyond their minders. The containment protocol completed this half-year helped mutual aid arrive faster, but restoration still crawled where parts were gone.

### Restoration by hand
Hospitals postponed care when spares ran out. Towns ran on what was guarded. The common line held, but only just.
