## In practice
We do not re-list depots. Under the Civil Protection Mechanism and NIS2/CER, DG ECHO/ENISA with EU-CyCLONe and national police fund dispersed, unmapped micro-stores held by municipalities, hospitals and DSOs on a flat reimbursement, with guards only on request and no public map. Holders who withdrew return off-books but on-scheme; intimidation and arson are treated as attacks on essential services.

We answer the scandal inside the AI Act frame. The Commission opens an Article 70 coordinated enforcement with the AI Office, national supervisors and the Ombudsman: suspend the contested social-insurance/policing system, publish logs, provide case-by-case review and back-pay, and admit where conformity and human oversight failed on paper. DG DIGIT keeps the two health-system scheduling tools running on segmented manual operation, separately branded, to separate useful administration from the wrongful system. No new fund, no new law, no Washington ask we cannot pay for.
