## Priority
Priority is M9, the Guarded Lifeline and Trusted Administration Pact, because it is the only instrument still in flight and it directly holds the black-start dispersal and scandal redress our two-year commitment depends on.

## In practice
We keep M9 alive by hand, not by money. Under the Civil Protection Mechanism, DG ECHO and ENISA keep the flat reimbursement for dispersed unmapped micro-stores running, guards only on request, no public map, no re-listing. Municipalities, hospitals and DSOs that returned stay on-scheme; arson and intimidation continue to be handled as attacks on essential services through national police and EU-CyCLONe, without new operations we cannot fund.

On the wrongful queue we stay inside the AI Act enforcement already opened: the contested system stays suspended, the AI Office with national supervisors and the Ombudsman continues log publication, case review and back-pay, and DG DIGIT keeps the two health-scheduling tools on segmented manual operation under separate branding. We issue no new law, no new fund, and no external ask this turn.
