## In practice  
We are launching the **EU Frontier Intelligence Initiative** under the Horizon Europe framework, co-funded by the Digital Europe Programme and the newly repurposed €200bn from the **Tech Sovereignty Package**. The Council will vote under urgency to designate biomedicine and infrastructure resilience as strategic AI domains, mandating that the next three Gigafactories — now under majority EU co-ownership — prioritise training runs for EU-native models in these areas. We are tasking EMBL, CERN, and the European Health Data Space with forming a joint research consortium, with binding data-sharing and publication rights. The first model, focused on pathogen design detection and countermeasure generation, will begin training in Q1 2030.

The **Sovereign Verification Regime** continues under legal pressure. The U.S. developer has filed for injunction at the ECJ, arguing extraterritorial overreach. We are holding firm: the European AI Assurance Agency (EAAD+) has issued a compliance notice under Article 16 of the AI Act, and national regulators are preparing to suspend clinical use of the oncology model by January unless checkpoint access is granted. We are not backing down — but we are no longer betting our security on their cooperation. The **Institutional Integrity Audit** is now probing the EMA’s reliance on foreign models, and early findings suggest systemic dependency in drug approval pipelines. We are treating this not as a failure, but as a blueprint for replacement.

The **Hardened Cyber Shield** is now institutionalised, but the **bio_uplift_findings** demand more: we are instructing ENISA to expand NIS3 reporting to include AI-assisted bio-design tools used in regulated labs. This is not a new measure — it is an enforcement extension. The Public Trust Observatory publishes weekly updates, but trust remains fragile. We are not asking for faith. We are building alternatives — because the next breakthrough must be ours.
