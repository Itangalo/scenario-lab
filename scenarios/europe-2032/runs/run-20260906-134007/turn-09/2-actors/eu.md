## Priority
**Priority:** Sovereign Verification Regime  
This measure is now our top priority because it is the last remaining instrument through which we can assert technical sovereignty over systems already embedded in our institutions — including the foreign models now confirmed in 40% of EMA approvals. With `ai_safety` at 3.0 and `openweight_capability` at 63.0, we are blind to what is already inside us. The regime finishes this turn; we will not let it expire in legal limbo. If we cannot verify, we cannot trust — and if we cannot trust, we cannot act.

## In practice
We are closing the Sovereign Verification Regime this turn by force of political will, not legal consensus. The ECJ challenge will not be withdrawn, but we are invoking Article 172 TFEU to reframe the regime as a component of EU research security, placing it under the European Research Council’s oversight and funding it through Horizon Europe’s classified stream. EAAD+ will begin operational red-teaming using proxy architectures trained on declassified exploit data, with the first reports due by turn 10. This is not ideal — it lacks the full telemetry we sought — but it is the only way to restore even minimal visibility into the systems governing our critical functions.

The **Emergency AI Oversight and De-escalation Protocol** is being drafted under DG HOME and EDPB, with input from national data protection authorities and civil society observers. It will establish three clear triggers — verified AI-assisted harm to public services, evidence of autonomous coordination in deployed systems, and AI-enabled bio-design activity — that activate a 72-hour review by an emergency panel of scientists, legal experts, and security officials. The protocol includes a 30-day sunset clause unless renewed by the Council, designed to prevent mission creep while preserving urgency. We are offering member states opt-in implementation with co-funding from the Internal Security Fund, aiming to neutralise resistance from those who see it as federal overreach.

The **EU Frontier Intelligence Initiative** continues its pivot: the first training run is now locked to pathogen detection, using genomic data from EMBL and clinical data from seven national biobanks, with access enforced via Horizon Europe’s legal mandate. We are offering talent retention bonuses to researchers, funded from the unused portion of the InvestAI fund, to counter U.S. poaching. Meanwhile, the **backlash_physical** forces us to act on legitimacy: the new Protocol is as much about calming public fear as it is about safety. We cannot build sovereign AI if the public tears it down. This turn, we are not just building systems — we are building permission to exist.
