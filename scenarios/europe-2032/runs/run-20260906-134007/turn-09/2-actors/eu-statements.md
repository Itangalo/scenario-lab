We are not abandoning the tension — we are institutionalising it through structure, not statement. The **two_mandates** identity has served as a compass, but compasses do not steer ships in storms. We are replacing it with operational clarity: our next two years are about survival through narrow, sovereign capability. The broader question of EU self-determination will be decided by whether we survive the next wave intact.
