### Emergency Activation, Legal Backlash

The European Union invoked Article 222 TFEU to activate the Sovereign Defensive AI Initiative (SDAI), overriding national objections and bringing defensive AI systems online at Luxembourg and Marseille. The move, framed as a response to sudden frontier access denial and the rapid open-weight capability surge, aimed to assert operational control over sovereign AI. However, the emergency activation triggered immediate legal and political resistance. Several member states challenged the invocation as disproportionate, arguing that mutual defence clauses were never intended for technological contingency planning. The Joint Oversight Board remains unconstituted, and transparency commitments are now under judicial review.

### Open Frontier, Real Risk

A leading lab released a powerful open-weight model within months of its closed counterpart, rapidly distributing agent-grade AI globally. This narrowed the open-closed gap dramatically, pushing `openweight_capability` to 57.0. While the EU’s DARCA authority began integrating interpretability tools and running synthetic red-team scenarios, no live agents have yet been deployed—let alone gone rogue—leaving its mandate untested.

### Labour and Legitimacy Erode

Job losses in law, accountancy, and public administration mounted, with entry-level roles vanishing. Though the AF-HCS-LTS framework finished, its transition mechanisms proved too slow to stem public anxiety. A new Labour Impact Observatory was launched, but seen as reactive. Public sentiment dipped as strikes spread, and trust in AI governance weakened.

### Sovereignty Stalls, Capital Shrinks

Despite physical readiness, SDAI’s activation did not translate into immediate capability gains. The legal dispute negated operational cohesion, and `eu_ai_sovereignty` fell to 17.0 due to `ai_capability` growth and no effective capacity gain. Political capital eroded further—now at 5.0—under the weight of a new large measure, a priority, and four ongoing initiatives. The emergency move failed to rally support, instead deepening fragmentation.
