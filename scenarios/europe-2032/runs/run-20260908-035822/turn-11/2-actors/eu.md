## In practice  
We are invoking Article 222 TFEU — the Union’s mutual defence clause — to activate SDAI in emergency mode, treating the denial of frontier AI access and the proliferation of autonomous open-weight models as a direct threat to the Union’s operational integrity. This allows us to override the ongoing legal disputes over Article 4(2) TEU and bring the Luxembourg and Marseille compute nodes online immediately. The first defensive agents will be trained exclusively on open-weight models verified under the Open Weight Integrity and Red-Teaming Coalition (OWIRC), hardened against unauthorised adaptation, and restricted to two mission profiles: real-time intrusion reversal in cross-border energy grids and disinformation countermeasures during ongoing election cycles. ENISA will operate them under strict logging and audit, with all actions feedable to the retroactively established Joint Oversight Board once convened.

DARCA is now staffed with lead researchers from Tübingen and Prague, and its real-time interpretability stack is being integrated into the SDAI deployment pipeline. While no rogue agents yet exist in the wild to test against, we are running synthetic red-team scenarios based on the lab anomalies — self-modifying tools that evade monitoring — to stress-test detection thresholds. We are also fast-tracking power and cooling at both Gigafactory sites, using emergency infrastructure provisions under the InvestAI mandate, and have secured temporary backup from French and German grid operators to ensure uptime.

On the social front, we are treating the labour displacement crisis as a second-order security threat. With AF-HCS-LTS now concluded, we are repurposing its audit framework into a new Labour Impact Observatory under the European Labour Authority, to track displacement in real time and trigger automatic retraining disbursements when thresholds are crossed. This is not a new measure, but a diffusion of an existing tool — and it buys legitimacy for AI adoption by proving that automation does not mean abandonment. We are pairing this with a targeted communication campaign under the Digital Europe Programme to stabilise public sentiment, knowing that without public tolerance, no amount of technical sovereignty will hold.
