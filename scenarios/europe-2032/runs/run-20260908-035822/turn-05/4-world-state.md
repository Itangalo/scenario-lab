### A Turning Point in Crisis and Control

Global AI capability inches forward to 56.5, as labs cautiously resume scaled-down training runs despite the ongoing investment slump. While progress is slow, it does not stop—advancements in efficiency and distributed compute sustain minimal gains.

The release of a powerful open-weight model earlier in the year continues to ripple outward. With widespread deployment on accessible hardware, `openweight_capability` rises to 48.0, nearing the threshold where misuse becomes both potent and untraceable. Security researchers report early signs of automated disinformation campaigns and targeted cyber intrusions using fine-tuned variants, underscoring the urgency of enforcement.

The EU completes two major initiatives: the **Secure Open-Weight Monitoring and Response (SOMR)** network and the **European AI Security Partnership (EASP)**. SOMR delivers real-time detection tools to national agencies, contributing to a +3 boost in `resilience`. EASP establishes joint evaluation protocols among aligned states, but its impact is limited by patchy participation—Germany and Poland delay ratification, citing sovereignty concerns.

In response to escalating misuse risks and public unrest, the EU launches the **Open-Weight Incident Response Corps (OWIRC)**, a large-scale measure under ENISA-Europol coordination. Designed to rapidly investigate and contain AI-enabled incidents, it earns a proposal bonus due to its direct response to recent threats. However, legal disagreements among member states delay full operational authority, and private-sector cooperation remains voluntary.

Labour protests in France and Spain intensify, triggered by AI-driven public-sector cuts and inflamed by the perceived technocratic overreach of projects like the Gigafactories. Demonstrators block supply routes to data centres, and trust in AI governance erodes further, dragging `public_sentiment` down to 31.0.

Despite completing two measures, `eu_political_capital` declines sharply under the weight of a bloated portfolio and social backlash. The completion bonus is outweighed by ongoing costs and the priority penalty.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 19.0, pressured by stalled domestic compute projects, export control tightening, and the absence of secured frontier access. The US election outcome—marked by retrenchment—confirms a shift in transatlantic cooperation, leaving the EU more isolated in its sovereignty ambitions.
