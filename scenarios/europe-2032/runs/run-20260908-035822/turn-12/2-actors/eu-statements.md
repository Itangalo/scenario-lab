# Statements: The European Union (turn 12)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Ensure that the EU retains sovereign access to AI systems capable of defending its critical infrastructure and democratic processes

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Ensure that the EU retains sovereign access to AI systems capable of defending its critical infrastructure and democratic processes — now explicitly including the ability to detect, contain, and terminate unauthorised agent behaviour in real time, under EU command and on EU-controlled infrastructure, without reliance on external legal goodwill or contested jurisdictional interpretations — **but under revised operational conditions: we now acknowledge that emergency activation has failed to deliver capability, legal cohesion remains absent, and the systems are not under effective control**
  - Grounds: The invocation of Article 222 TFEU did not activate sovereign capability; instead, it exposed the Union’s inability to enforce its own emergency decisions. The Joint Oversight Board remains unformed, power connections are still delayed, and no defensive agents are operational despite technical readiness. The legal challenges have not been overcome but deepened, and member states now treat SDAI as a contested overreach rather than a shield. Our commitment stands — the need for sovereign control is greater than ever — but the path through unilateral emergency action has collapsed. We must now secure the same objective through means that bind rather than bypass, or lose legitimacy entirely.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but now, we act on the assumption that unaligned optimisation is not only in motion but has already crossed into operational environments, and that our defences must be anticipatory, not reactive — **and we add the corollary: when action fails to land, we adjust course without waiting for a second collapse**
  - Grounds: We acted under uncertainty and were wrong in method, though not in diagnosis. The surge in openweight capability to 57.0 and the verification_widens event confirm that autonomous, uncontainable systems are already in motion across domains once thought to require human judgment. Yet our emergency activation did not produce operational sovereignty — it consumed political capital, fractured cohesion, and left us more exposed. To persist in the same mode would not be resolve; it would be denial. We maintain the duty to act early, but now bind it to a duty to recalibrate swiftly when action fails to materialise.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
