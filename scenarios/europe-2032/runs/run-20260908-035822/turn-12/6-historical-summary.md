The EU abandoned the emergency Sovereign Defensive AI Initiative after its failure to deploy operational capabilities and legal challenges stalled implementation. DARCA advanced interpretability integration but lacked live agents for testing. The Labour Impact Observatory was rebranded as the Social Foresight Unit amid ongoing strikes in legal, healthcare, and coding sectors, driven by visible job displacement.
