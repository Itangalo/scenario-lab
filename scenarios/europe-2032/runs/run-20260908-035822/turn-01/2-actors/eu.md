## Priority  
**Cyber Shield for Critical Operators (CS-CO)** — because the cyber_test_shot proved that existing detection failed against AI-coordinated intrusions, and without verified defences in place, the Union cannot absorb or deter the next incident, regardless of sovereignty or regulation.

## In practice  
We are mobilising under Article 114 TFEU to propose a directive that upgrades the NIS2 framework for energy, water and transport operators, mandating AI-specific intrusion detection systems, quarterly red-teaming with EU-certified agents, and real-time telemetry sharing with ENISA. The legal base allows qualified majority voting, but we must still secure Council approval in Competitiveness formation, where Germany and the Nordics resist new compliance burdens on utilities already strained by grid modernisation. We are offering co-financing through the InvestEU programme — 40% of compliance costs for the first three years — to secure their assent.  

The directive will designate ENISA as the central hub for AI incident telemetry, with a new mandate to publish anonymised attack patterns and coordinate cross-border response playbooks. This is not just about resilience: by forcing visibility into how AI-enabled attacks evolve, we create feedback into ai_safety at the developer level, even when labs themselves are opaque. The US incident reporting regime under the AI Safety Institute is too narrow and too slow; we are building something that operates in real time, across sectors, with teeth.  

Meanwhile, InvestAI Gigafactories is in early site selection — potential clusters in Finland, Spain and Poland are being evaluated for grid headroom and cooling capacity. We are fast-tracking state-aid clearance and negotiating with EIB to front-load €30bn in guarantees. The Tech sovereignty package is advancing permitting reform through the Infrastructure Council, but resistance remains in France and Italy, where local mayors see data centres as energy hogs. We are offering regional digital transition funds — tied to retraining and local AI adoption — to turn opposition into partnership. This turn, capital is tight, but we are holding the line: sovereignty without resilience is brittle, and resilience without sovereignty is temporary.
