### A Fragile Step Forward

Global AI capability advances to 57.0, sustained by algorithmic refinements and distributed training breakthroughs, even as investment remains constrained. The open frontier follows, with `openweight_capability` rising to 49.0 as new fine-tuned variants of powerful open models spread across consumer-grade hardware, enabling increasingly autonomous cyber intrusions.

The EU marks the completion of three measures: Cyber Shield for Critical Operators, SOMR, and EASP. CS-CO strengthens baseline defences in energy and transport sectors, contributing +4 to `resilience`. SOMR delivers enhanced telemetry but with patchy adoption; its full potential is diluted by national opt-outs. EASP establishes coordination protocols, though without full buy-in, limiting its strategic weight.

OWIRC, now a named priority, avoids collapse through compromise. By anchoring operations under Europol’s SIRIUS platform and requiring dual-state requests for deployment, it gains provisional activation. However, delayed powers and limited private-sector mandates reduce its effectiveness. Still, the effort prevents total disintegration of enforcement credibility.

The new Accountability Framework for High-Capability Systems (AF-HCS) is proposed as a large category 3 measure, aiming to enforce downstream accountability via financial and access sanctions. It earns modest public support for targeting abuse rather than technology itself.

Despite completions, political capital erodes. Labour unrest in France and Spain eases slightly as retraining funds are redirected, lifting `public_sentiment` by +1. However, member state defection — a central European country finalising a bilateral compute deal with a US hyperscaler — undermines cohesion, weakening trust in collective strategy.

### US Posture Confirmed

In Washington, the new administration takes office. Its approach is clear: strategic retrenchment. Access to frontier models tightens, with little transparency. The EU faces deeper isolation in shaping global AI governance.
