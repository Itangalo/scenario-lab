### The Crack of Authority

In a final act of institutional defiance, the European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) activated its Enforcement of Suspension Authority, suspending three public-sector AI systems—including the Ghent welfare engine that denied 87% of disability claims—and released a public "failure docket" listing 23 unresolved cases of AI-driven harm. The move was broadcast not as reform, but as reckoning: names, dates, and internal cover-up attempts were published, and criminal investigations were initiated against local administrators in Belgium, Lithuania, and Spain.

The signal was clear: the EU would enforce accountability, even from the edge of collapse. Investigative reports in *Le Monde* and *Der Spiegel* amplified the moment, framing it as the first real consequence for algorithmic injustice. Public sentiment inched upward, particularly among civil society groups, as the visibility of action briefly outweighed the weight of despair.

Yet no structural recovery followed. The Sovereign AI Core Programme, formally concluded, yielded no sovereign capability—its remaining funds redirected into forensic units to trace harm, not prevent it. No new compute came online. US frontier access remained tightly rationed under **CONSOLIDATION**, and the open frontier advanced as mid-tier models leaked into public repositories, pushing `openweight_capability` toward 57.0.

The political cost of the enforcement push was absorbed, but not reduced. With `eu_political_capital` already at 2.0, the small measure consumed its cost without collapse—but no gain followed. The measure finished on schedule, yet the portfolio remained burdened by prior commitments. A faint signal emerged from the Commission’s informal crisis cell: coordination on threat assessments continued, but without mandate or resources.

### A Symbol Without a Foundation

The suspensions sparked legal challenges in two member states, testing the limits of Article 16 authority. Courts in Vilnius and Barcelona questioned the EAAD’s jurisdiction, delaying enforcement. The gesture had landed, but the machinery behind it remained broken.
