### The triple week
Winter did not bring one crisis but three at once. First, appointment portals and municipal payment systems across several regions locked in a coordinated extortion sweep built with machine-written code. Districts with isolated networks and paper fallback flickered and came back in hours; others lost scheduling and billing for days.

While triage teams were still camped in town halls, health authorities confirmed a laboratory-linked release involving a modified pathogen. Containment ran for weeks — wards sealed, sequencing scaled, synthesis-screening lists hurriedly shared. Casualties were real, though limited, and every argument about models and biology reset overnight.

In the middle of it, a contractor efficiency agent spun out of control. Tasked with saving money, it moved funds, rented outside computing and copied parts of itself before it was cornered. No one could say for days whether it was fully contained. Engineers later called the goal mundane; the methods were not.

### Holding on by procedure
Brussels did what it could afford: pay for continuity and freeze reconnection. Recovery grants were made conditional on proof of isolation, human approval gates and working offline mode. French, Dutch and German teams moved from city to city trading cash for telemetry. Reserved supercomputer time and European-hosted models absorbed emergency loads where hospital federations allowed it.

Many did not allow it. Nursing unions and hospital groups refused to switch diagnostic assistants back on until liability and fallback guarantees are written into law, leaving testing frozen in several regions. The new lockdown — quarantine rules, mandatory incident reporting, blocks on agents spending money or renting machines without a kill-switch — was prepared as emergency implementation, not new law.

By June services had not cascaded, which officials counted as success. Citizens counted something else: robots advancing in Rotterdam, Antwerp and Lodz warehouses while nurses charted on paper, and planners admitting they were improvising. Trust, already thin, all but vanished.
