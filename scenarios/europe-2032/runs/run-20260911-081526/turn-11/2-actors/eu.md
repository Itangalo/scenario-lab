## Priority
M12 EU Municipal Continuity and Bio-Detection Surge — because stopping cascades in health and town halls outranks any new sovereignty build while blackouts and a viable pathogen design signal are public.

## In practice
We run this through ENISA, HERA and the EU-CyCLONe network with Digital Europe and EU4Health reprogramming: conditional recovery grants for isolation, human-approval gates and paper-capable fallback, French-Dutch-German triage teams seconded to hit districts, and mandatory telemetry-sharing tied to funds. EuroHPC reserve time and EU-hosted hardened open models from the finished Fallback Stack are kept warm as the switch-over path, not rebuilt as a new programme.

For bio, we extend the finished Joint Threat cell into wastewater and clinical sequencing surge sampling, shared reference screening for genome synthesis providers, and a common alert channel with middle-power labs under the Trusted Compute Compact. No new liability law or compute build is opened this half-year; we enforce what is on the books and pay municipalities to stay online, to stop the trust collapse from taking cohesion with it.
