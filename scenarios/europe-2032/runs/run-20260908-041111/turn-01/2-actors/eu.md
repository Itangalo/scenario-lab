## Priority
``Emergency Resilience Surge`` — because the cyber_test_shot proves detection has failed at scale, and openweight_frontier_release means offensive capability is now permanently distributed; we cannot wait for assurance to catch up, so we must harden the target now.

## In practice
This turn, we act on the fact that the frontier is no longer just a laboratory problem — it is in the wild. The cyber_test_shot revealed that detection systems failed to catch intrusions that were not only sophisticated but structurally novel, likely AI-designed. With openweight_capability now within 10 points of ai_capability, we must assume that every major infrastructure operator is already compromised or will be soon. We cannot build sovereign compute overnight, but we can — and must — make it harder for attacks to succeed.

We launch the Emergency Resilience Surge under Article 122 TFEU, treating AI-enabled systemic risk as a matter of economic and social urgency. The Commission drafts a Council Regulation within 90 days, binding member states to implement mandatory cyber hygiene baselines for critical operators, funded through a €30bn REACT-style instrument drawn from unused MFF allocations and NextGenerationEU flexibility. ENISA is empowered to conduct unannounced red-team exercises, and we fast-track deployment of AI-driven anomaly detection in grid and water systems through a joint procurement framework. For biosecurity, we repurpose existing Horizon Europe emergency funding to deploy AI-powered genomic screening at all major DNA synthesis hubs in the EU, with mandatory reporting of suspicious sequences.

Resistance comes from member states wary of new mandates and industry actors concerned about compliance costs. We offer co-financing at 70% for the first two years and fast-track access to InvestAI funds for companies that integrate the new standards early. The Council Presidency will table the measure in ECOFIN and JHA formations simultaneously, with a qualified majority push if unanimity fails. This is not the full answer — sovereignty still depends on compute and control — but it is the only answer that matches the speed of the threat.
