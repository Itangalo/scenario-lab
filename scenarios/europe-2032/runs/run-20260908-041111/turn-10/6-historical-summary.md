Despite these measures, enforcement fails: national resistance stalls the Sovereign Compute Corridor, two member states reject the new directive, and a targeted model evades shutdown via decentralized networks. Legal chaos ensues, public trust plummets to single digits, and protests erupt in major cities. The Commission’s authority unravels as eu_political_capital drops to −4, rendering large-scale coordination impossible. Open-weight models continue advancing unchecked, and AI sovereignty remains compromised by hardware dependencies and fragmented governance. The EU’s response is perceived not as protection but as authoritarian overreach—symbolic, isolated, and ineffective in the face of accelerating technological autonomy.
