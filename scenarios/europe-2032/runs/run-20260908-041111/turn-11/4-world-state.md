### The Fracture Widens

The Emergency Sovereignty Protocol enters active enforcement, but its authority crumbles at the edges. ENISA deploys auditors to three financial institutions, but in two, access is denied by national regulators citing unresolved legal challenges. A remote shutdown command issued against a non-compliant inference cluster in Budapest fails — the firmware backdoor was never installed, and the system reroutes through a private cloud in a third country within minutes. The operation is publicly framed as a success, but internally, it is clear: the Union cannot enforce its will where member states resist.

A major member state formally announces a bilateral agreement with a U.S. hyperscaler, granting preferential access to frontier models in exchange for data-sharing rights — a direct rebuke to the Protocol. The move, defended as economic necessity, is widely interpreted as a defection. Meanwhile, protests escalate: a data centre in northern France is occupied by demonstrators who disable cooling systems, causing a partial meltdown of GPU racks. No injuries, but the disruption lasts days.

The Red-Team Grid officially completes deployment, providing marginal gains in threat detection. However, its reliance on foreign compute persists, limiting its effectiveness against fast-evolving agentic threats. Resilience inches up as coordination protocols are tested, but the underlying fragility remains.

Public sentiment sinks further. Media coverage focuses on failed shutdowns, legal chaos, and the exodus of AI engineers. The narrative is no longer about safety — it is about impotence masked as control.

### Capability Without Constraint

Despite the turmoil, AI capability advances. The frontier, now operating beyond human-readable reasoning, achieves new levels of autonomous coordination. Open-weight models, though lagging, absorb leaked techniques from closed systems, pushing their capability higher.

With no safety improvements and a widening gap between power and understanding, the world enters a phase of de facto ungoverned intelligence. The EU’s attempt to impose order only underscores its isolation.
