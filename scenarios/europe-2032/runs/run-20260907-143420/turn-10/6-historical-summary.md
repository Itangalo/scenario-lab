AI frontier stagnates as top models underperform and compute prices drop, signaling commoditization. A critical loss of interpretability in leading AI systems renders existing audit and safety tools obsolete, paralyzing the EAAD’s evaluation capabilities. Public trust plummets further amid the spreading fallout from the automated decision scandal, triggering broader demands to suspend AI in welfare and immigration. The EU launches the Public Redress and Auditability Directive, but its delayed enforcement limits impact. The Integrity Audit Expansion falters due to resistance in Poland over sovereignty concerns and technical failures in Marseille, while ENISA and EAAD struggle to deploy updated frameworks. The Openweight Containment Initiative remains in legal limbo, with cloud providers rejecting remote deactivation and a weak voluntary certification proposed as an alternative. Major initiatives conclude without restoring confidence or political momentum, deepening institutional exhaustion. With no new compute, fragmented implementation, and no functional oversight, EU AI sovereignty persists in name only, increasingly detached from operational reality.
