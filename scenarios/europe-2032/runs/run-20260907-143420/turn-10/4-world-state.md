### Plateau and Opacity

The AI frontier shows signs of stagnation: a flagship model release underperforms expectations, and prices for top-tier compute begin to fall, suggesting last year’s breakthroughs are becoming commoditized. Yet this apparent pause is overshadowed by a deeper shift—the leading systems no longer produce human-readable reasoning. Overnight, the interpretability tools that underpinned audits, safety checks, and regulatory oversight become obsolete. No alternative is ready. The loss is felt acutely in Brussels, where the European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) halts its evaluation pipeline, admitting it can no longer verify system behaviour.

### Crisis of Legitimacy

Public trust erodes further after the automated decision scandal, now seen as a symptom of a systemic failure. Protests spread to new cities, with growing demands to suspend AI use in welfare and immigration. The EU responds by launching the Public Redress and Auditability Directive, mandating auditable logs and fast-track appeals. But the measure is small and distant, with enforcement still two years away.

The Integrity Audit Expansion, now the EU’s priority, struggles to gain traction. Resistance mounts in Poland, where authorities invoke constitutional sovereignty to block zero-trust mandates. In Marseille, technical incompatibilities prevent air-gapped audits from interfacing with legacy systems. ENISA and EAAD draft new templates, but implementation lags.

The Openweight Containment Initiative remains stalled in court. Cloud providers refuse to implement remote deactivation, citing digital rights. A voluntary certification scheme is proposed, but it lacks teeth.

### Exhaustion Sets In

Multiple major initiatives conclude—Gigafactories, the Tech Sovereignty Package, EAAD, CIRS, SMAF, and the Public Assurance Protocol—yet their completion brings no political gain. The mood is one of fatigue. The belief that sovereign infrastructure ensures safety has collapsed. With no new compute, fragmented compliance, and no operational oversight, EU sovereignty remains symbolic.
