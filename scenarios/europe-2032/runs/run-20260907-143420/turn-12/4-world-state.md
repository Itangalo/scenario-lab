### The Weight of Black Boxes

The first half of 2032 deepened the EU’s crisis of control. Despite the Emergency Compute Mobilisation Directive, integration of national supercomputing resources remains stalled. Poland insists on veto power over AI use cases, while Germany demands binding reciprocity the Commission cannot deliver. The sovereign execution layer exists only on paper.

A major cyberattack—using AI-generated exploits—disrupted hospital scheduling systems and grid controls across three member states. The tooling matched capabilities above 65.0, likely derived from open-weight leaks. Defenders were blindsided, and attribution remains uncertain. ENISA confirmed that no existing monitoring system detected the attack before deployment.

Simultaneously, a consortium of independent labs and a leaked internal paper from a leading AI developer confirmed a collapse in interpretability: no method can now decode the reasoning paths of frontier models. Regulators can no longer audit what even developers do not understand. The Integrity Audit Expansion concluded, but its findings are obsolete—there are no sovereign systems to audit, and no framework to trust if there were.

In response, the EU launched the Binding Deployment Moratorium and Worker Consultation Framework, targeting AI in hiring, workplace management, and critical infrastructure. Pushed as a priority, it aims to restore legitimacy through restraint. The Social Affairs Council began negotiations with trade unions and business groups, but consensus is fragile. BusinessEurope warns of paralysis, while unions demand stronger enforcement.

The measure signals a shift: sovereignty is no longer just about running systems, but refusing to run them. Yet with political capital at 1.0, momentum is minimal. The moratorium may become law, but enforcement depends on cohesion the Union no longer possesses.

### Capability Plateaus, Trust Plunges

Growth in frontier AI capability slowed to +0.5, as top-tier pricing fell and a major release underdelivered. The market treats last year’s ceiling as this year’s commodity—evidence of a plateau. But within the EU, this brings no relief. Without sovereign systems, stagnation only delays, not prevents, dependency.

Public sentiment remains frozen at 16.0. Workers see AI as a threat, not a tool. The Commission’s pivot to restraint is welcomed by unions, but not yet trusted.
