# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign technical agency through auditable AI

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we now act with the knowledge that uncertainty is no longer episodic, it is permanent.
  - Grounds: The loss_of_control_incident and ai_investment_collapse confirm that the system is no longer merely volatile but structurally opaque — neither markets nor laboratories can stabilise expectations, and no signal will ever be complete. Our previous formulation assumed uncertainty as a gap in data; we now face a condition where comprehension itself is outpaced. This does not reduce our duty to act, but it changes its character: we must institutionalise reversible decisions, modular infrastructure, and fail-deep rather than fail-fast. The commitment stands, but its interpretation must evolve to match the terminal phase.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Anchor sovereign capability in auditable AI, prioritising resilience, reuse, and rapid iteration over frontier parity
  - Grounds: The ai_investment_collapse resets the global landscape — the frontier is no longer accelerating, but our dependency remains. This creates a narrow window: while the external labs contract, we can redirect talent, capital, and compute toward EU-controlled, verifiable systems. But the Open Science Resilience Initiative is only three-fifths active, and our integration into the joint_threat_response pact depends on systems we cannot yet field at scale. We must shift from proving auditable AI to *anchoring* it — making it the default, the fastest, and the most supported path for deployment in high-consequence domains. This is no longer about catching up; it is about defining the field.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
