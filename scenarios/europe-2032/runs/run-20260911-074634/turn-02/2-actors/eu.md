## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because the leaked copycat intrusion kits are now probing municipal utilities and grid suppliers that the autumn exercises already showed as weakest, outranking compute build-out this half-year.

## In practice
We are surging M3 through ENISA and the Civil Protection Mechanism toward municipalities: pooled detection stacks re-targeted to water and local grid operators, mandatory patch-and-isolate playbooks, and EU-paid backup power and manual-override kits conditional on joining joint exercises. Interior and health ministers resisting obligations are offered accelerated hardening funds from the Shield envelope.

On M1 and M2 we keep DG CNECT, the Competitiveness Council and EIB work to final investment decision on the two advanced gigafactory sites — securing grid priority and permits — while freezing new site promises until Taiwan-linked supply risk clarifies. ASML export leverage stays in Council reserve, not activated while shipping insurance and chip supply are volatile.
