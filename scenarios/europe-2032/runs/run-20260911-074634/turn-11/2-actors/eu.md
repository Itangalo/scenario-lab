## In practice
We run on what has finished landing. The Critical Services Shield, Continuity Stack, Bio-Shield and Cyber Surge regimes stay in operation without new money: ENISA and NIS2 joint teams keep hospitals and ports degraded-not-stopped, civil protection maintains guarded sites and stockpile coordination as Strait premiums rise, and DG EMPL keeps the Entry-Level Corps placements and wage-insurance payouts flowing through ESF+ channels already cleared.

We treat the investment collapse as a constraint, not a rescue: cancelled hyperscaler build-out is not replaced, anchored capacity in Lyon, Porto and Gdansk is reserved for essential public workloads including the procured US medical planning use, and the lawful-wrong benefits system stays in ombudsman-led redress with logging review and a narrow rulebook patch. On eval anomalies and Taiwan exercises we trigger only existing contingency playbooks — incident reporting and blockade-continuity stockpiles — without opening a new legislative front we cannot fund or enforce.
