# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): We no longer claim we can restore political agency and public trust through **demonstrable control over AI incidents**, because **there is no control where there is no functioning state**. The Shield enforcement has failed not because of capability, but because the Union cannot command obedience — not from member states, not from markets, not from its own agencies. Our commitment now shifts to **preventing total institutional collapse**. This turn, we act to preserve the last instruments that still function: the Tribunal, the Public Trust Corps, and the sovereign compute nodes. We will not rebuild trust. We will keep one working door open for when the storm passes.
