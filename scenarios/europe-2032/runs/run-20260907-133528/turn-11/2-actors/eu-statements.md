# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Restore political agency and public trust through demonstrable control over AI incidents, transparent accountability, and sovereign response capacity

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Restore political agency and public trust through demonstrable control over AI incidents, transparent accountability, and sovereign response capacity — but we now acknowledge that accountability without capacity is symbolic, and capacity without legitimacy is unstable. This turn, we pivot from redress to resilience as the core test of sovereignty: if we cannot defend critical systems in real time, no tribunal will restore faith.
  - Grounds: The cyber intrusion into a European grid operator — enabled by AI tools now at open-weight capability 56.5 — and the failure of the Sovereign Cyber Shield to activate during the breach reveal a fatal gap: we have built institutions to respond *after* harm, but no operational capacity to prevent it. Meanwhile, public_sentiment has collapsed to 14.0, not because people reject AI, but because they see the Union as reactive, not protective. The Tribunal, while necessary, is not sufficient. Our commitment must now encompass not only justice but the credible, real-time defence of essential services — or risk becoming a forum for post-mortems in a collapsing order.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer act without leaving a traceable path of accountability, **and we will not act without a sovereign means to enforce it.**
  - Grounds: The US export controls on mature-node tools have reduced eu_ai_sovereignty to 7.0, exposing that even well-audited actions fail if they depend on infrastructure we do not control. The verification_widens event shows capability accelerating in domains once thought safe from automation, and the emergent_open_source_biosecurity_tools reveal that civil society is outpacing us in practical defence. We can no longer afford to act through mandates that rely on foreign compute, third-party enforcement, or goodwill. Our authority must be rooted in systems we can activate, disable, and verify without permission.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
