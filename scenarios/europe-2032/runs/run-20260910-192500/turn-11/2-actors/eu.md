## New measure
**EU Trusted Recovery Assistants**
Deploys EU-vetted, human-supervised AI assistants for hospital administration, municipal services and port logistics to cut backlogs from the February attack while showing AI under human control.
This now because opaque reasoning has killed chain-of-thought oversight and sentiment is at 12, so only visible, supervised public-sector benefit can rebuild trust while the shared-leverage footing holds, and the productivity evidence from offices gives us something real to point to.

## Priority
EU Trusted Recovery Assistants, because with no measures in flight and capital at 21, rebuilding felt trust from the shopfloor and ward up outranks any new frontier or supply push this turn.

## In practice
We run this through DG CNECT and DG SANTE with ENISA assurance: a narrow whitelist of models deployed on EU-controlled infrastructure, black-box pre-deployment checks instead of chain-of-thought review, mandatory human sign-off and offline-backup verification, funded by repurposed Municipal Recovery and HealthEU lines, not new borrowing. Health and EPSCO Councils own the rollout in hit border regions first.

We hold the finished lines without new instruments: EPSCO keeps M8 wage-insurance and co-determination rules enforced in Rotterdam, Antwerp, Duisburg so assistants are paced by works councils, and DG TRADE keeps the M9 pooled-spares channel warm quietly. No gigafactory or oversight announcements — credibility is shorter queues and readable decisions, not promises.
