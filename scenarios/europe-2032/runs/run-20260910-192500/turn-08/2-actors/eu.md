## New measure
**EU Shopfloor Transition Pact**
Pairs accelerated warehouse and logistics robot deployment rules with wage insurance, retraining guarantees and works-council co-determination funded through ESF+ and employer levies.
This now because embodied AI is landing from outside on our industrial base while sentiment is at 19 and capital at 9 — without a visible adjustment bargain, hostility will block both hardening and any sovereignty rebuild, and this directly serves the hardening half of our two-year commitment.

## Priority
EU Shopfloor Transition Pact, because at 9 capital and 19 sentiment nothing else — not leverage, not compute — can move until displacement stops reading as abandonment.

## In practice
We run this through EPSCO and the social partners, not DG CONNECT: Commission recommendation plus ESF+ reprogramming for wage insurance and rapid retraining pools in logistics, automotive suppliers and ports, with access conditioned on works-council agreements on deployment pace and on offline-backup and safety drills already required by M7. Employers who automate contribute to the transition fund; that is the price of flexibility.

We hold the existing line without new instruments: ENISA keeps M7 restoration teams and conditional aid running, DG TRADE keeps upstream leverage maintenance on ASML servicing, pooled spares and stockpile discipline, and gigafactory warm sites stay permitted and insured. No new compute promise while supply is frozen — we defend credibility by not announcing fields as fabs.
