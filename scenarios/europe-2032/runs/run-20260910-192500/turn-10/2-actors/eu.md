## In practice
We run this through ENISA and DG CNECT under the M3/M7 playbook: pre-mandated mutual-aid cells, EU-paid clean images and offline-backup verification via insurers, with Health and JHA Councils ordering isolation exercises in hit regions. Money comes from the Municipal Recovery and Isolation Fund line, topped by rescoped social funds, not new borrowing.

We hold the finished lines without new instruments: EPSCO keeps M8 shopfloor wage-insurance disbursing in ports to stop the attack being read as another job-loss shock, and DG TRADE keeps the M9 middle-power pooling trickle warm but makes no new supply promises until restoration is visibly underway. No gigafactory announcements this turn — credibility is restoration trucks, not empty sites.
