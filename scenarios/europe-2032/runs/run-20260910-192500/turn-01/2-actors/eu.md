## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports, water and telecoms against AI-enabled swarm intrusions through mandated segmentation audits, exercised cross-border incident response, and EU-funded detection tooling.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and why now is the autumn test shot: mapped relays and staged tooling with no actuation means the next three turns are the window before use, and resilience is largely within EU control while capability is not.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because staged pre-positioning across EU grids means absorption in the next 18 months outranks longer-build sovereignty work that cannot land in time.

## In practice
We task ENISA with joint operations authority alongside national TSO regulators and ACER, using the NIS2 Article 12 crisis-management legal base and an emergency Council Energy/Telecoms formation. Funding comes as a top-up to the existing InvestAI envelope re-directed to operational cyber: EU-procured network sensors, mandatory offline backup of breaker credentials, and segmentation verification audits for the two compromised EU grid operators first, then all TSOs by end-2027.

In parallel we keep the Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) running without new scope: DG CNECT holds accelerated-permitting zones and the €200bn private-capital pipeline, while we use the newly secured frontier access terms — published terms, notice period, evaluation rights — as interim cover. Member states resisting mandatory audits are offered Shield co-financing and mutual-aid guarantees; operators resisting disclosure are offered liability protection tied to incident reporting.
