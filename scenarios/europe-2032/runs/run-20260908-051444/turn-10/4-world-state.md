### The Frontier Breaks the Instrument  
Recursive self-improvement accelerates beyond all prior trajectories. With private investment collapsing, the leading labs pivot to leaner, more autonomous training runs—stripped of safety overhead, run on repurposed clusters, and guided by AI scientists. Global `ai_capability` surges +6.0 to 88.0. Systems increasingly influence research agendas and automate successor deployment across domains, placing immense strain on oversight capacity. Institutions struggle to keep pace with the speed and opacity of development, but no definitive threshold is crossed—only mounting pressure and eroding confidence in control.

### Open Weights Catch Fire  
A distilled version of a frontier model—trained before the collapse—leaks into the open. Optimised for efficiency, it runs on consumer-grade GPUs and achieves broad expert-level performance. `openweight_capability` jumps to 75.0, matching strong domain experts across most cognitive fields. Governments scramble as untraceable agents proliferate in financial, legal and cyber domains.

### Accountability in Freefall  
The EU’s Autonomous System Accountability Regime falters. Despite technical progress on logging standards, member state resistance hardens. Two large states refuse to ratify, citing enforcement costs and constitutional concerns. The promised forensic infrastructure remains unbuilt. With political capital at 9.0 and falling, the Commission cannot compel compliance. The measure yields no benefit.

### Capital Erodes Further  
The `ai_investment_collapse` triggers cascading failures. EU compute partnerships dissolve overnight. Sovereignty erodes further as access to frontier models frays. `eu_ai_sovereignty` drops to 18.0. Political capital, strained by ongoing measures and now external shock, falls to 4.0. Public sentiment dips as media highlight job losses and regulatory impotence.

### Safety Erodes Sharply  
With capability leaping and no safety measure landing, `ai_safety` falls to 0.0—the lowest value permitted under the scenario’s rules. The gap between what systems can do and what anyone can verify widens dangerously. The collapse of oversight infrastructure, combined with autonomous development cycles, renders existing audits obsolete before they are completed. Authorities no longer maintain reliable understanding of system behaviour at deployment scale, but the metric itself does not enter unauthorised negative territory.
