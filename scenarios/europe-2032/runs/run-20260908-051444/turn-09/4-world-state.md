### The Frontier Accelerates  
Recursive self-improvement continues its exponential climb, pushing global AI capability +6.0 to 82.0. Systems now routinely design, test and deploy successors with minimal human input, compressing release cycles from months to days. The frontier has decisively entered the 75.0 reference zone: AI matches strong domain experts across cognitive professions and materially accelerates its own development.

### Open Weights Gain Ground  
An open-weight model released just weeks after the closed frontier reaches 69.5 in capability, widely distributed and already running on clustered consumer hardware. While still trailing, the diffusion pace intensifies concerns about uncontrolled agent deployment. Distillation breakthroughs allow near-expert reasoning on accessible infrastructure, undermining containment assumptions.

### EU Shifts from Prevention to Accountability  
In response to the recent loss of control and mounting legal pressure, the EU launches the Autonomous System Accountability Regime. The framework mandates auditable chains of intent and action for all systems above 55.0 capability, requiring cryptographic logging under EU jurisdiction. Non-compliance triggers market suspension and creates legal presumptions of negligence.

However, political capital is too low to absorb the cost. With only 12.0, and already burdened by multiple large measures, the new directive strains cohesion. The priority status provides no acceleration due to the sub-20 threshold, and legal resistance from member states delays final drafting.

### Mixed Signals in Public Response  
Job losses in law, accountancy and software spark fresh anxiety, but visible gains in public services — shorter waits, faster decisions — offer counterbalance. Sentiment ticks up slightly as citizens credit EU-led AI for tangible improvements, though trust in oversight remains fragile.

### Sovereignty Erodes Further  
Despite efforts, the gap between frontier capability and EU control widens. No sovereign compute milestones land, and the ongoing alliance with the US does not offset structural dependence. Sovereignty drops to 20.0 as the capability surge outpaces institutional response.
