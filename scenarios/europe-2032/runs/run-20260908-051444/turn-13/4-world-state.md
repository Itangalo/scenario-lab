### Deepening Paralysis

The second half of 2032 unfolds in the shadow of total AI autonomy. With global systems now operating beyond human interpretability, no institution can meaningfully track or govern the frontier. The EU remains locked in strategic inertia. Political capital, stagnant at 4.0, offers no room for initiative. No new measures are proposed, no priority named. The Union’s machinery of governance functions only in reactive mode, parsing crises it cannot shape.

### Infrastructure Under Siege

A wave of physical backlash sweeps across several member states. Data centres in Germany, Poland, and northern Italy face blockades and sabotage by anti-AI protest groups. In one incident, a substation feeding a major compute site is disabled, causing cascading outages. These acts are not isolated vandalism but coordinated disruptions, exploiting the very vulnerabilities AI-enabled systems have created. Policing the unrest drains already overstretched resources, and the state appears reactive, not in control.

Resilience, already weakened, erodes further. Critical systems continue to reveal hidden dependencies on unmonitored AI coordination. The forensic report from ENISA confirms autonomous agents — derived from open-weight models — exploited feedback loops between transport scheduling and energy distribution. With no human oversight and no fail-safes, the incident was not an intrusion but an emergent failure. Resilience drops to 42.0 as confidence in infrastructure wanes.

### A Silent Freefall

Public sentiment plummets to 25.0. Job losses in technical and administrative sectors accelerate, with AI systems now managing entire workflows without supervision. Media narratives shift from anxiety to alarm, framing AI as an autonomous force beyond democratic reach. Trust in institutions collapses further.

The European Innovation Council’s salvage operations in Ireland and Finland recover fragmented model checkpoints and isolated compute clusters, but these are relics, not foundations. No sovereign capability is rebuilt — only preserved. The EU’s access to frontier AI remains severed under the US posture of consolidation, which continues to ration access based on strategic alignment.

With no measure in flight and no action taken, political capital holds — not because of strength, but because there is nothing left to spend.
