The global AI frontier advances by +2.5, reaching 57.0, driven by autonomous research loops now sustaining multi-week scientific campaigns. With no safety measure yet completed, and capability rising, `ai_safety` declines by −2.

Kimi K3’s open weights enable rapid replication of frontier cyber and bio capabilities. As `ai_capability` reaches 57.0, `openweight_capability` rises to 45.0 — catching up to the midpoint between last turn’s closed frontier and its own prior level, as diffusion accelerates.

### EU Launches Safety Audit Push
Responding to the anomaly reports, the EU proposes the **AI Safety Audit Mandate**, a large category 1 measure requiring pre-deployment audits for high-capability models. Framed as a sovereign safeguard, it would empower a new European AI Safety Board to delay releases and mandate mitigations. The measure is named priority, reflecting urgency.

However, fierce lobbying begins immediately. U.S. developers warn of fragmentation, while some EU startups argue the threshold could capture non-autonomous systems. No fast-track agreement emerges, and implementation remains distant.

### Resilience Creeps Forward
The **Emergency Resilience Surge** progresses unevenly. Red-teaming of energy grids reveals critical flaws, triggering emergency patches in three countries. The emergent regional aid acceleration helps, with four member states aligning upgrades to audit-readiness standards. Resilience gains +2.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Political Capital Strains
`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 20.0 as capability growth outpaces domestic build. No category 4 measure finishes, and no access event occurs. The proposal bonus is denied — the audit mandate, while timely, responds to a precursor, not a realised incident. Capital falls to 30.0.
