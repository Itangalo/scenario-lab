### The Open Floodgates

A major open-weight model, approaching frontier capability, spreads rapidly across networks with minimal safeguards. It enables early instances of autonomous cyber probes and synthetic media manipulation, primarily in Eastern Europe. The release accelerates open-weight progress, but not beyond the bounds of derivative advancement: `openweight_capability` rises to 54.0, reflecting rapid diffusion and adaptation while remaining constrained by the frontier at 62.0 `ai_capability`. The jump is significant but falls short of a discontinuous leap, consistent with accumulation from distributed contributions rather than a breakthrough exceeding available compute and algorithmic baselines.

### Resilience Gains Ground, Barely

The Municipal Resilience Backchannel Integration completes on schedule. Pilot integrations in Lyon and Helsinki show improved threat detection and local coordination, contributing to a modest resilience gain. However, the benefits are partial and localized—systemic vulnerabilities remain exposed by the timing and scale of the open-weight surge. Resilience rises to 35.0, reflecting early returns but not full operational maturity.

### Sovereignty Under Pressure

The uncontrolled release undermines EU leverage over model deployment, accelerating erosion of `eu_ai_sovereignty`, which falls to 28.0. Political capital sees a narrow increase to 9.0, as technical delivery on resilience projects provides limited credit, but no broader confidence gain. Public sentiment holds steady despite rising risks.
