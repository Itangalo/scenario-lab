### The Calm After the Storm, But No Recovery

The EU remains locked in reactive mode, its institutions reeling from the recent cyberattack and lacking the political capital to launch new initiatives. With eu_political_capital at 9.0—deep in the paralysis zone—no new measures are proposed, and no priority is declared. The window for decisive action remains closed.

### Capability Creeps Forward

Global AI development continues unabated. ai_capability rises to 63.0, reflecting steady progress in model efficiency and training infrastructure, particularly in the United States and China. This growth enables further automation of offensive cyber operations, feeding into the open diffusion of powerful tools.

Openweight_capability climbs to 57.0, driven by the wider availability of recently leaked model weights that retain strong autonomy in cyber and social engineering tasks. While still behind the closed frontier, these models are now capable of sustaining multi-stage attacks with minimal human oversight.

### Safety and Resilience Erode Further

ai_safety holds at 2.0. While the gap between capability and control widens, and the recent attack exposed critical opacity in model reasoning, no formal evaluation regime has been deactivated or suspended—those structures remain in place, though inactive. Interpretability research continues at a low level, preventing a full collapse in the metric. The conditions for a deeper drop are building, but no triggering event has formally resolved to justify a decline.

resilience drops to 31.0. Despite informal coordination among Trusted Resilience Nodes in Lyon and Helsinki, systemic fragility persists. The health sector’s slow recovery reveals inadequate redundancy and unexercised continuity plans. The lack of unified response deepens institutional mistrust.

### Sovereignty and Sentiment Under Pressure

eu_ai_sovereignty falls to 24.0. The failure to secure access to frontier models, combined with rising capability (+0.5) and no sovereign build in progress, accelerates dependence. The Union’s leverage erodes as member states pursue divergent strategies.

public_sentiment declines to 22.0. Public frustration grows over job losses, privacy breaches, and the lingering effects of the health system outage. Protests near data centres intensify, with blockades and symbolic occupations reported in several cities. While anger mounts and fringe actors call for sabotage, no confirmed acts of physical damage or injury have occurred—yet. Media discourse frames AI as a destabilising force, and trust in institutions continues to erode, but the backlash remains below the threshold of a resolved `backlash_physical` event. The risk of escalation is high, but the gate remains unopened.
