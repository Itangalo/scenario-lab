## Priority
**Priority:** Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield Initiative  
We are pushing hardest on the Cyber Shield because the eval anomaly reports confirm that capability is accelerating beyond control, and our own red-teaming shows that essential systems remain fragile — we cannot wait for a cascading failure to prove the threat is real.

## In practice
We are executing the Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield Initiative through binding implementing acts under NIS2, co-signed by the Commission and the High Representative, mandating AI-augmented intrusion detection systems across all essential service operators by mid-2028. ENISA is deploying technical integration teams to support municipalities and regional grids, particularly in Germany and Poland, where we have accepted differentiated implementation paths under Article 36 TFEU to preserve unity of standards while respecting national security frameworks. €18bn in conditional funding from the Digital Europe Programme and Recovery and Resilience Facility is being released in tranches tied to verification of detection system deployment and red-team performance. The European Cybersecurity Competence Centre is running live adversarial simulations using AI agents modelled on known intrusion patterns, exposing hidden vulnerabilities in energy and water systems — findings are being fed directly into operator upgrades.

Simultaneously, we are operationalising the EU AI Incident Response Corps under the Union Civil Protection Mechanism, with €9bn from the Internal Security Fund securing recruitment and training of 300 specialists from national CSIRTs, ENISA, and vetted private partners. Deployment protocols are being finalised with the Council’s Permanent Security Committee, establishing unified command and rapid activation thresholds. The Corps will be on standby by turn 5, capable of isolating and stabilising AI-compromised systems before cascading failures occur — a critical hedge against the growing gap between capability and oversight.

On the industrial front, we are advancing InvestAI Gigafactories by activating the Brittany regional pact: 15% of revenue will fund local green hydrogen via InvestEU, and 40% of technical roles will go to residents through EU4Skills pipelines. Grid interconnections are now secured with ENTSO-E, unlocking €40bn in EIB disbursements pending European Council approval of recovery fund reallocation. The Tech Sovereignty Package is being restructured to decouple the hyperscaler levy from permitting reform, instead linking accelerated permitting to enforceable commitments on EU-based operational control and energy efficiency benchmarks — a WTO-compliant path forward ahead of the Council vote in turn 4.
