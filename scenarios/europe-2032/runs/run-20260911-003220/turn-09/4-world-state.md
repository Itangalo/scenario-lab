### The jump
Autumn brought confirmation that the leaked benchmark sheets had understated the case. A new American system, demonstrated first to select partners and then in carefully staged public tests, made prior timelines obsolete overnight. Planning papers written in July read as archaeology by October. Laboratories spoke of training loops that ran with minimal human intervention.

Days later Washington and Beijing announced a limited understanding on securing model weights, restraining autonomous escalation, and controlling a class of biological design tools. Verification was thin but not absent. Brussels learned the outline from press wires and a brief allied readout. Inclusion had not been negotiated.

### Used as the instrument
At the same time, Washington pressed The Hague to widen cuts to servicing and exports of chipmaking equipment — beyond the most advanced machines to older product lines sold to China and other customers. For the Dutch government the American technology content left little room to refuse. For the Commission it was a blunt lesson: its strongest supply-chain card had been played by someone else.

The Council response tried to turn necessity into leverage. Foreign ministers mandated negotiators to link any further restraint to European co-decision and a place at the verification table for the new great-power understanding, waving competition and anti-coercion instruments as backing. In Washington the offer was noted without commitment.

### Closing one file
At home, the long-delayed accession to the pooled cyber and biological threat-sharing arrangement finally closed. After months of fights over outbound data, custody of samples and who could order a shutdown, a compromise decision with time-limited safeguards passed. Liaison officers took seats at the joint cyber command; feeds began to flow in late autumn.

Containment drills improved at the margins. Playbooks rehearsed in spring now held a little longer, and operators credited the new outside telemetry for faster triage. The four factory shells remained empty, held as bargaining chips rather than built out. Mayors kept wage support going on central transfers, thinner than before.

Public mood sank further. Headlines about a foreign power rationing European machines and a new system no European lab could match fed a sense of humiliation layered over exhaustion. Protests thinned, but polls showed trust collapsing into resignation.
