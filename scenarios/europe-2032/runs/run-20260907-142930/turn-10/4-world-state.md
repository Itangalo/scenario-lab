### A Directive Ends, Not a Build

The Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation formally concludes this turn with no new EU-controlled facilities operational. Germany and Ireland never ratified implementation agreements, leaving the directive ungrounded. No capacity was secured, and the event term under metric rule 5 applies: `ai_capability` rose by 0.5 — below the 2.0 threshold — so no decay from dependence, but sovereignty still falls due to stagnation.

Instead of new infrastructure, the Commission pivots to oversight. Invoking digital fairness, DG COMP launches a post-implementation review that reframes compute access as a market condition for dominant AI providers. The audit protocol for energy draw and spare capacity is in early drafting, but its reach is uncertain and enforcement would require national cooperation now in short supply.

### Bottom-Up Resilience Gains Ground

With top-down leverage exhausted, the Municipal Resilience Backchannel becomes the EU’s central resilience strategy. ENISA certifies Lyon, Barcelona, and Helsinki as Trusted Resilience Nodes, offering future funding advantages and emergency data flexibilities. Bologna, Ljubljana, and Tallinn are identified for integration using existing disaster envelopes — a low-cost expansion that avoids new budget requests.

Yet these gains are incremental. The measure remains in flight, yielding no full resilience bonus until completion next turn. Interoperability holds in testing, but systemic hardening remains uneven.

### Safety Erodes Further

The European Evaluation Shield fractures irreparably. Two member states reject its authority to impose remediation mandates, citing legal overreach. Enforcement of safety norms across the bloc collapses into disarray. With `ai_capability` advancing slightly and no safety measure landing on frontier systems, `ai_safety` drops sharply.

Public sentiment holds at 35.0 — anxiety persists, but so does quiet reliance on municipal services keeping pace. Political capital improves slightly, spared deeper loss by avoiding a failed proposal in this fragile state.
