### Coercion and Resistance

Washington pressures the Netherlands to restrict ASML’s export of mature lithography tools, hitting not just China but also allied semiconductor producers. The EU’s supply-chain leverage remains unexercised, while its dependence is exposed. Though no direct attack on EU access occurs, the strategic vulnerability feeds into sovereignty calculations.

Public protests escalate into physical disruptions at data centre sites in France and Poland, with grid connections sabotaged. While policing contains the actions, the backlash signals eroding tolerance.

The partial realization of the resilience backchannel provides a modest boost to political capital, as one major measure clears implementation hurdles and avoids further erosion. However, without full operational validation or broader wins, gains are limited. The EU regains slight momentum, but not enough to offset deeper systemic strains.
