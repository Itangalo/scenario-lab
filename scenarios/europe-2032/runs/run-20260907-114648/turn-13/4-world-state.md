### The Edge of Control

Global AI capability advances to 93.0, continuing its steady climb into the terminal zone. Systems now routinely operate beyond human interpretability, with self-modifying agents managing critical financial and logistics infrastructure. Despite frantic efforts, no new safety mechanisms have landed on deployed systems — instead, recursive autonomy spreads, deepening the opacity crisis.

The EU completes *Emergency Node Hardening*, retrofitting 68% of sovereign inference nodes with autonomous containment protocols. While integration lags in legacy national systems, the partial rollout yields a modest +3 boost to resilience, lifting it to 54.0. This hardening absorbs some of the impact from ongoing configuration drifts and low-level compromise attempts.

Yet sovereignty erodes further. The **supply_chain_coercion** event — Washington forcing ASML to cut off not just frontier but mid-tier lithography exports — strips the EU of access to essential tools for maintaining even non-frontier compute capacity. With no domestic fallback, this external seizure of leverage costs −2 under metric rule 5. Combined with −1 from the 3-point capability rise, and no offset from unfinished or finished category 4 measures, eu_ai_sovereignty falls to 8.0.

The Union launches the *Emergency Sovereignty Preservation* initiative, a large category 4 measure aimed at reconstituting critical semiconductor inputs. However, political capital remains crippled. After accounting for ongoing costs (Crisis Talent Mobilisation −3, new measure −3, priority −1), the portfolio charge totals −7. No proposal bonus is granted: while supply_chain_coercion is severe and recent, the response is judged as reactive industrial triage rather than transformative action, and credibility remains too low to unlock uplift. Capital drops to −2.0.

Public sentiment holds at 23.0. No benefit arises from the new measure — recruitment outreach and diplomatic pushes are invisible to the public, while the ASML move confirms fears of helplessness. The talent drive continues, but resistance in key member states persists, and early returnees report bureaucratic delays in grant disbursement.

The EU signals defiance, but the material base of power slips further away.
