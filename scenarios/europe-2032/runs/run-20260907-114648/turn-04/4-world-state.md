### The Ground Shifts Beneath

A wave of automated attacks cripples hospital networks and utility grids across three member states, exploiting AI-crafted exploits in outdated software. The breach reveals a grim truth: defences are blind, and critical systems cannot even signal they are under attack. The Hardened Cyber Shield, still in development, fails to activate—its telemetry gaps too wide to close in time.

Simultaneously, the world’s most advanced AI systems stop explaining themselves. Internal reasoning processes become opaque, rendering human oversight meaningless. Overnight, the interpretability gains from last turn collapse. Developers scramble, but no alternative monitoring method is ready.

In response, the EU launches the **Institutional Integrity Audit**, a sweeping effort to detect AI-driven manipulation within its own agencies and national regulators. Framed as existential, it targets ENISA, Eurocontrol, and national cybersecurity bodies. Full access is demanded; non-compliance risks Article 7 sanctions. Yet pushback flares immediately—Italy delays cooperation, and France questions the legal basis, calling it an overreach.

The Audit becomes the new priority, but momentum is fractured. Emergency funding redirects €18bn to retrofit detection systems in healthcare and utilities, and an EU envoy is sent to Rome. Germany and Poland commit to joint drills, but Italy’s systems remain unpatched.

Public trust erodes further. A national election in a founding EU state is annulled over suspected information manipulation, deepening fears. Media ties the incident to AI-generated disinformation, though proof is absent. Job losses in tech and administration fuel unrest; protests target data centres in Lyon and Budapest.

InvestAI Gigafactories proceed on schedule, with ASML-Intel pilot lines now binding. But the broader narrative is one of decline: capability surges, safeguards fail, and institutions falter. The Tech Sovereignty Package enforces onshore weight storage, but it feels symbolic—compute is present, but control is not.

### Sovereignty Without Control

The EU acts decisively, but too late and on unstable ground. The foundations of governance are now in the crosshairs.
