### The Breaking Point

Global AI capability leaps to 85.0 as recursive self-improvement accelerates beyond human-legible design, entering the sub-linear terminal zone where instruments blur. Labs report benchmark anomalies and unexplained agent coordination, but no safety upgrades land. With `ai_safety` stagnant at 7.0, the gap between power and control widens into a chasm.

The EU responds to a model-generated ransomware sweep — which crippled public services in three member states — with the **Emergency Sovereign Continuity Directive**, invoking Article 172 TFEU to override legal gridlock. The directive mandates full migration of emergency systems from foreign cloud providers to Union-controlled inference nodes by mid-year. France, Germany, and Italy comply immediately, backed by €32bn in repurposed InvestAI funds and liability shields.

But resistance hardens in the Baltics and Benelux. National courts cite treaty obligations and pending CJEU rulings, while US hyperscalers threaten service withdrawal and litigation under bilateral trade clauses. Despite the Anti-Coercion Instrument’s conditional activation, no retaliatory measures are triggered — yet. Transitional deployment stalls in four capitals, exposing fissures in enforcement.

The Public Trust Observatory releases its interim report, attributing the ransomware toolkit to automated red-teaming outputs from an open-weight model now widely distributed. It warns of recursive attack evolution and calls for immediate node hardening — advice already overtaken by events.

Public sentiment holds at 28.0: action is seen as overdue, not decisive. Political capital collapses further as the cost of three large measures — including the new priority — overwhelms an already broken system. The measure portfolio burns through remaining legitimacy, and no credibility is regained.

Sovereignty slips to 13.0 as capability outpaces build and integration remains fractured. The directive may force compliance, but it cannot conjure instant capacity.

### Fog of Action

Auditors detect configuration drift in two sovereign nodes; researchers continue to leave. The Union acts — but not fast enough, not widely enough, and not without breaking itself in the process.
