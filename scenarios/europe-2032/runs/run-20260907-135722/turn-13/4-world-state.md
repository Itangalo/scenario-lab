Closed labs continue slow but persistent progress, driven by efficiency gains and architectural refinement rather than breakthroughs. The plateau is not yet a wall.

Open-weight models benefit from distributed innovation and tooling improvements. `openweight_capability` climbs to 54.0, trailing the frontier as expected, sustained by community-driven deployment frameworks and incremental optimizations.
