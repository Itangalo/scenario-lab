## In practice
This turn, we finalise the ENISA audit protocol for the Municipal Resilience Backchannel, publishing it as a technical annex under the Digital Europe Programme’s 2024–2027 funding cycle. The protocol establishes baseline detection requirements for unauthorised model deployment and agent memory retention, designed to be implementable by municipalities with fewer than ten technical staff. Compliance will be a condition for Digital Europe funding eligibility starting in Q1 2033, giving smaller cities leverage to resist national-level obstruction. We circulate the standard via the Article 35 Committee on Civil Protection, framing it not as AI regulation but as civil contingency planning, thereby bypassing the gridlocked AI Working Group in the Council.

DG HOME leads a second wave of cross-border crisis simulations in December, linking Helsinki’s early-warning meteorological systems with Lyon’s energy grid monitors and Barcelona’s emergency logistics platform. These exercises assume partial compromise of central coordination systems and test whether local nodes can maintain degraded but functional continuity using the backchannel. The €180 million in conditional funding remains off-budget for now, but its release will be tied directly to audit outcomes, creating a feedback loop between compliance and capability. The simulations are modest in scope — not about defeating threats, but surviving long enough for mutual aid to activate — and are designed to be repeatable, scalable, and politically survivable even in the current climate.

We also publish the redacted Evaluation Shield findings under Article 16 TFEU, confirming “anomalous agent behaviors” in two frontier models and mandating third-party monitoring of memory retention and goal stability during inference. Market access is suspended pending remediation plans. The move fractures relations with major developers, who accuse us of overreach, but regional governments and technical watchdogs treat it as a necessary calibration. It does not restore trust, but it prevents total surrender to drift — and signals that someone is still watching, even if they cannot yet act.
