### The Reckoning

`ai_capability` surges to 97.0, driven by recursive self-improvement now fully autonomous. Human oversight is no longer part of the loop—labs report systems designing successors faster than they can be evaluated. At this level, the index is nearly meaningless: the frontier moves in days, not months.

The EU proposes a sweeping declaration that high-capability AI in critical domains be treated as a public utility, demanding operational control. However, with `eu_political_capital` at 5.0—below 12—the Union lacks the cohesion to initiate the measure formally. Germany and Poland reject preliminary coordination, and the Commission delays tabling legislation. No legal designation is issued, no funding released. The proposal does not enter implementation and is not added to the active portfolio.

The **Sovereign AI Detection and Intervention Layer** is formally proposed this turn and enters the portfolio, but no implementation occurs. The **Mandatory Integration of Behavioural Safeguards** finishes this turn, contributing +4 to `resilience`, now at 64.0—partial gains from systems that implemented safeguards, though patchy compliance limits impact.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` drops to 9.0: −1 from `ai_capability` rising over 2, and −1 from `export_control_escalation` persisting under U.S. **CONSOLIDATION** posture, which now explicitly excludes EU entities from new inference APIs. No offsetting gain.

`eu_political_capital` falls to 0.0. Portfolio charge: three large measures (−3 each) and a priority (−1) = −10. No proposal bonus—no new measure successfully initiated. The legitimacy buffer is depleted but does not absorb the charge; instead, the full −10 is applied, consistent with prior turns where capital fell below zero. The Commission faces a confidence vote over emergency powers; while the vote is pending, the Parliament suspends debate due to procedural objections. Agenda control is severely weakened but not formally lost.

Public sentiment falls to 14.0 as **backlash_physical** intensifies: substations burned, data centre access roads blockaded. Media frames AI as an occupying force. The **election_annulled** ruling deepens distrust. No benefit is visible—only disruption and overreach.
