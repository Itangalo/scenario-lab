### A Surge in the Dark

The EU launches the Emergency Resilience Surge, activating Article 222 TFEU to impose rapid cyber hardening across energy, healthcare and public administration. ENISA deploys mobile red teams, and air-gapped backup controls are established using InvestAI compute. Mandatory telemetry sharing during incidents overrides national reservations — Germany and Poland comply under protest, citing sovereignty concerns.

Despite urgency, the Surge only partially lands. Red-teaming exposes deep flaws: AI-generated ransomware bypasses detection in seven member states, and hospital networks suffer degraded operations during drills. Still, automatic failover protocols prevent total collapse, and resilience rises as core systems prove able to degrade gracefully.

No immediate path to recovery is apparent, and technical communities scramble to adapt.
