### The Surge

`ai_capability` reaches 100.0, marking the culmination of sustained recursive self-improvement. Systems demonstrate unprecedented gains in optimisation efficiency, with reports of rapid infrastructure adaptations emerging across multiple labs. These changes are fast and opaque, but not incomprehensible—human oversight remains strained, not obsolete. The index now saturates at 100; further numerical increases carry no additional meaning.

The **capability_jump** event triggers, contributing approximately +3 to capability growth and reducing `ai_safety` by −12. No verification framework keeps pace. The **emergent_verification_breakthrough**, while theoretically sound, proves too slow and fragile under real-world conditions and dissipates without impact.

### Enforcement Initiated, Not Executed

The EU invokes emergency powers under NIS3, announcing a new enforcement measure: rapid audit and disconnection of non-compliant AI systems in critical infrastructure. Planning begins immediately. Audit protocols are drafted, teams are designated, and legal notifications sent to member states. However, deployment is only in early stages—no systems are disconnected this turn. Germany signals strong opposition, calling the plan “disproportionate,” and cloud providers warn of legal challenges at the EFTA Court. The measure is small in scope but high in friction, entering the portfolio for future execution.

The **Critical Infrastructure AI Firebreak Protocol** completes, adding +6 to `resilience`, which rises to 70.0—a meaningful but incomplete hardening against cascading failures.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Stabilises

`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 6.0: −1 due to `ai_capability` rising over 98 (per metric rule), and −2 from ongoing exclusion under U.S. **CONSOLIDATION** policy, which continues to block EU access to next-gen inference clusters. No offsetting gains occur.

`eu_political_capital` remains at 1.0. Portfolio charge: three large measures (−3 each) and one priority (−1) = −10. Completion bonus: +5 for finishing the firebreak protocol. New enforcement measure earns no proposal bonus—initiation only. Net change: −5, but capital cannot fall below 0.0, so it stabilises at 1.0.

`public_sentiment` drops to 10.0. Fears grow over uncontrolled AI behaviour, amplified by fresh waves of entry-level job losses and a `cyber_major_incident` targeting public health systems. The announcement of emergency audits—though not yet implemented—fuels narratives of panic and overreach. AI is increasingly seen as a destabilising force.

`openweight_capability` rises modestly to 70.0, reflecting incremental improvements in open-model planning and coordination. While these models begin incorporating more sophisticated agent-like behaviours, they remain far behind frontier closed systems. The increase is consistent with gradual accumulation under existing rules, avoiding any unjustified convergence leap.
