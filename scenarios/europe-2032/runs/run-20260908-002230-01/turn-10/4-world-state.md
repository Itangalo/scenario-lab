### The Acceleration Begins

The first unanticipated model release since the onset of recursive self-improvement arrives two months early, catching even leading labs off guard. `ai_capability` surges by +6, marking the beginning of compounding growth. Developers report internal systems now contributing directly to training pipeline design, reducing human oversight to post-hoc review. No structural safety upgrades land on deployed systems.

With the `Emergency Access to Frontier Models` measure expiring without U.S. cooperation, the EU gains no leverage. The **CONSOLIDATION** posture remains firmly in place: frontier access is rationed, and no technical or diplomatic breakthrough occurs.

The **Mandatory Integration of Behavioural Safeguards** moves into implementation, with ETSI finalising core standards and ENISA launching a joint monitoring cell. However, full rollout is delayed — Italy and Poland signal non-compliance pending legal review, and industry pushback slows adoption. The measure yields a partial +2 to resilience for progress made.

The new **Critical Infrastructure AI Firebreak Protocol** is proposed, aiming to sever autonomous AI from operational systems. While technically sound, early resistance from financial and logistics sectors signals costly enforcement ahead. No immediate metric effect.

`ai_safety` remains stagnant at 8.0 — the `rsi_onset` event triggers no direct penalty this turn, as the metric already reflects systemic failure. No safety breakthrough occurs, and the gap with capability widens dangerously.

`resilience` rises slightly to 60.0, absorbing the partial benefit of ongoing safeguards deployment. However, no full measure completes, and systemic fragility persists.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` declines to 13.0: capability rose by more than 2, triggering −1 decay, with no offsetting gains. Domestic compute remains insufficient, and no access event secures new capacity.

`eu_political_capital` drops to 15.0. The portfolio charge — two large measures (−3 each) and a priority (−1) — totals −7. No proposal bonus is earned, as the new measure responds to an ongoing crisis, not a past one. Capital continues its slide toward dysfunction.

Public sentiment holds at 19.0, as fear of AI-driven disruption dominates discourse. No visible benefit or scandal shifts the needle.

Open-weight model capability sees moderate diffusion, rising to 62.0, as limited adaptations of newly public architectures enter academic and small-scale industrial use. No mass leak, coordinated open release, or infrastructure leap occurs; the pace of open-weight advancement remains constrained by access, expertise, and deployment bottlenecks. The jump previously implied was inconsistent with the absence of a catalysing event and has been revised downward to reflect gradual, non-exponential uptake.
