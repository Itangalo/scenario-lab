# Statements: The European Union (turn 10)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign control over AI systems operating in critical domains

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Prevent uncontrolled agentic AI from operating in or against EU critical infrastructure
  - Grounds: The original commitment to “secure sovereign control over AI systems operating in critical domains” has failed in practice: the Sovereign Control Layer is operationally fragmented, blind to U.S. models, and lacks enforcement teeth. The ransomware attack proves that capability without containment is vulnerability. We are shifting from attempting to control all frontier systems — which requires access and unity we do not have — to a narrower, more defensible objective: ensuring that no agentic AI, foreign or domestic, can act autonomously within or against essential services. This is still about self-determination, but grounded in what we can actually stop, not what we wish we could govern.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
