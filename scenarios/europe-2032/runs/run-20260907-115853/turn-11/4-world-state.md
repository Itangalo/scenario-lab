### Open-Weight Progress, Limited but Persistent

Despite blockages, openweight capability rises modestly to 68.0. Gains stem from incremental replication of older architectures and partial distillation successes outside the most restricted domains. These are not breakthroughs, but maintenance of momentum under duress—small-scale progress in academic and non-frontier settings, supported by residual data access and distributed volunteer compute. The gap with frontier capability widens, but the open ecosystem holds.
