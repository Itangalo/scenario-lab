## New measure
**EU Displaced Entry-Work Transition Guarantee**
Funds rapid retraining, hiring subsidies and income bridging for entry-level cohorts in law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration via ESF+ reprogramming and employer co-funding.
Category 7 Labour and social protection is the lever because graduate hiring has stopped while budgets are exhausted and sentiment is hostile, and without a visible adjustment path the continuity fallback loses its political license.
