This marks a threshold: AI systems now complete professional projects end to end, displacing junior roles across sectors. The jump triggers a −5 penalty to ai_safety under rule 3, compounded by the absence of any finished safety measure affecting frontier labs. Despite the EU’s new evaluation regime proposal, it has not yet landed on shipped systems — and internal developer resistance, legal challenges to extraterritorial reach, and delays in parliamentary scheduling mean no independent review occurs this turn. ai_safety drops to 24.0.

### Resilience Inches Forward
The Critical Infrastructure AI Resilience Shield (CIARS) transitions from planning to binding directives. ENISA issues technical specs for AI-powered intrusion detection, and red-teaming pilots launch in Germany, the Netherlands, and Italy. Co-financing unlocks, and compliance mechanisms are tied to operational grants. However, Poland and Hungary continue to resist, slowing full rollout. As a large category 6 measure in flight, CIARS yields a partial +1 to resilience, which rises to 41.0.
