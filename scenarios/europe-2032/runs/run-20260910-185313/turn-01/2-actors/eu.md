**EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against swarm AI cyber intrusions with EU-wide patching, detection and exercised response.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, because the precursor intrusion with staged destructive access outranks build-out that pays off only after 2029.

## In practice
We task ENISA jointly with the new EU Cybersecurity Reserve under the Cybersecurity Act and the NIS2 Cooperation Group to roll the defensive breakthrough — automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection — to the two affected transmission operators first, then to all ENTSO-E members, major port authorities and water utilities by mid-2027. Funding comes from reprogrammed Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility money plus accelerated procurement, with mandatory incident reporting and joint exercises.

At the same time we keep the InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty package moving through DG CNECT, the EIB and member-state permitting zones, offering grid-connection priority and co-financing to hold cohesion after the member-state defection, but without letting that defection divert this turn's operational focus from containment. We use the Energy and Transport Council formations to bind the defecting capital into common standards rather than a separate hyperscaler deal.
