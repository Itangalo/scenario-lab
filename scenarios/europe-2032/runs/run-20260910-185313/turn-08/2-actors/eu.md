## New measure
**EU Essential Services Continuity Switch**
It reroutes the hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the leading foreign model onto EU-hosted and allied fallback models with emergency licences, support desks and safety checks to keep essential services running.
This and why now because the cutoff has turned sovereignty dependence into an operational failure that threatens our two-year commitment to managed transition — a category 6. Preparedness and resilience small switch contains the harm now while blockades and low trust block new builds.
