## New measure
**Emergency Municipal Service Restoration Pact**
It surges ENISA-led restoration teams, offline backups and mutual-aid rerouting to the municipal utilities, hospitals and public services hit by the automated attack, to stop cascades and restore essential services.
This and now because the large model-generated attack lands on the lagging municipal layer our cyber shield had not yet hardened, and our two-year commitment to absorb shocks and keep services running demands restoration first; the capability jump means patching alone no longer suffices without exercised containment.

## In practice
We keep the new Pact under the NIS2 Cooperation Group and ENISA with Civil Protection Mechanism activation: pre-positioned restoration teams to the worst-hit water plants and municipal IT, mandatory isolation and clean-backup playbooks already drilled in autumn, funded by emergency reprogramming from Digital Europe and the Union Civil Protection reserve. Energy and Health Councils co-sign service-priority orders so grid operators and hospitals get clean systems and rerouted capacity first, with the half-out capital offered mutual-aid access if it plugs its side tools into common reporting.

We push M4 as the operational fallback: DG CNECT accelerates pooled pilot compute and EU-hosted models for the American-cut-off wards and ministries, while the joint testing cell triages the leaked anomaly only enough to gate which replacement models are safe to deploy. M1/M2 gigafactory site works continue on existing permits without new money this turn; we cannot fund build-out and restoration at once with capital at 16.
