## Priority
EU Safe Systems Patch and Critical Recovery – because it is the sole in-flight effort and directly serves holding absorption through retrenchment and diffusion.

## In practice
We act through ENISA and DG CNECT under the Critical Services Shield legal base, not a new law. ENISA issues a binding patch instruction mandating the validated control/monitoring property for certified hospital, utility, bank and ministry tenants, with Digital Europe and civil-protection funds paying for re-imaging, log rebuild, and failover to EU-hosted open-weight slots where US service is still suspended.

In Council Health and TTE formations we trade visible recovery for time: mayors and regions hit by blockades and the cyber sweep get recovery aid and mutual-aid crews first, in exchange for holding permitting injunctions in courts rather than street vetoes and for keeping Gigafactory legal-anchoring talks alive. No new compute fund is opened, no side hosting deal is signed; the Commission holds the line that frameworks already closed are the vehicle, now patched to be safe.
