### The Compute Threshold Crossed

`ai_capability` rose by +2.5 this turn, continuing its steady climb as automated verification unlocks self-reinforcing gains in clinical, legal, and engineering domains. Systems now reliably execute multi-day professional tasks in these fields, accelerating the frontier toward autonomous research. The closed labs pull ahead, but the open frontier widens too: `openweight_capability` jumps to 47.0, closing half the gap with last turn’s closed capability, as foundational verification techniques leak into open ecosystems.

### Sovereignty, Secured — For Now

The **Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive** formally enters force, a landmark in EU digital policy. The European Digital Infrastructure Consortium conducts its first auction, reserving 30% of frontier compute for public-interest use. Three major hyperscalers participate under revised liability terms, cementing operational access. Yet legal challenges from two northern member states remain before the Court of Justice, casting uncertainty over long-term enforceability. Still, the precedent stands: compute is no longer purely commercial infrastructure.

This delivers a +3 boost to `eu_ai_sovereignty` under rule 5, offsetting the −1 decay from capability growth. With no access-denial event, the net change is +2, lifting sovereignty to 22.0 — a fragile rebound, but a rebound nonetheless.

### Verification Gains, at a Cost

The **Critical Verification Access Framework** becomes law, enabling real-time oversight in high-risk domains. Building on this, the EU launches **Sovereign Verification Nodes** — small, targeted environments on sovereign compute where accredited teams can independently validate AI outputs. The move strengthens technical assurance and reduces reliance on developer self-reporting.

This small category 5 measure earns a +2 boost to `ai_safety` in flight (toward a final +2 to +4), while the broader capability advance without systemic safety upgrades would otherwise have cost −1 to −3. Net `ai_safety` rises to 36.0.

### Political Capital Erodes

Three large measures and one small remain in flight, costing −11 total. The new priority adds −1. No measure finishes to trigger a completion bonus. The proposal bonus for Sovereign Verification Nodes is +3 — timely, relevant, and directly addressing the verification shift — but insufficient to offset the strain. Political capital falls to 26.0.

Resilience dips to 35.0, as no category 6 measure counters growing systemic risks. Public sentiment holds at 42.0, but labour displacement pressures mount.
