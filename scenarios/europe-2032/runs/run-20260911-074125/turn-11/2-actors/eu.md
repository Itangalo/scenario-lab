## New measure
**EU Cyber Emergency Restoration and Defensive Patch Surge**
Stands up an EU-wide emergency restoration operation that pushes automated patching and swarm-detection defensive tooling into hospitals, municipalities and power-water operators hit by the major attack, with ENISA-led joint teams and mutual aid. Category 6 preparedness and resilience — why this and why now: the automated attack has degraded services publicly while a real defensive breakthrough offers the first chance in years to close the gap, and with capital at 4 and sentiment at 16 we must show restoration before any other promise counts.

## Priority
M11 EU Welfare AI Redress and Human Oversight Enforcement — because without visible back-payments and human re-decision on the welfare files, no cyber restoration will be trusted, and the two-year commitment to hold livelihoods and democratic agency fails first there.

## In practice
We run the Surge through ENISA and the NIS2 joint teams already guarding builds and ports, with DG CNECT and civil protection funding emergency patch deployment and backup restoration in the worst-hit health and municipal systems, prioritising continuity stocks and EU-anchored capacity where chips allow. Member states with stronger SOCs second staff under cross-border mutual aid, and operators adopting the new detection share incident logs to the common body.

We keep M11 as the political front: DG JUST, the AI Office and national ombudsmen push the frozen welfare queue into human re-decision, publish audit logs, and start arrears payments where systematic wronging is confirmed, while the redress office is staffed to clear months-long backlogs. Placement corps graduates are diverted to offline-backup, queue-support and redress-assistance roles so remedy is visibly staffed even where hospitals and ports remain degraded-not-stopped.
