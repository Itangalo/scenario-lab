## Priority
M12 EU Cyber Emergency Restoration and Defensive Patch Surge — because without finishing restoration of hospitals and municipalities hit in October, no wartime continuity claim is credible and the two-year commitment to hold essential services fails first there.

## In practice
We run both operations through ENISA, the NIS2 joint teams and the Civil Protection Mechanism, with emergency Council conclusions declaring EU hospitals, water-power operators, subsea cable landing stations and EU fabs protected civil infrastructure not to be targeted, and offering deconfliction channels to Washington and Beijing. Money is reprogrammed from communications and civil protection budgets to forced backup restoration, offline continuity stocks, and secondment of stronger national SOCs to weaker regions.

We push the safety breakthrough defensive tooling that held in early deployments — machine-speed patching and swarm-behaviour detection — into health, municipal and port IT first, with shared incident logs to the common body, while HERA and ECDC stand up passive-to-active bio detection around clinics and water systems after the genome-model uplift finding. The welfare redress files stay politically front: DG JUST and the AI Office keep publishing audit logs and paying arrears, staffed partly by placement corps graduates, because adoption is already politically impossible after the scandal and only visible remedy prevents obstruction of the continuity work.
