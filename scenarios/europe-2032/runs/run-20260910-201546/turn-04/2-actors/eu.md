## New measure
**European Public Results Programme**
Scales the proven public-sector AI wins — shorter waits, faster decisions — into a branded EU-wide rollout in health, administration and schools using EU-controlled infrastructure. Why this and why now: with adoption finally producing attributable European benefit amid export tightening and low trust, visible public gain is the cheapest way to rebuild sentiment and capital to sustain resilience.

## Priority
European Public Results Programme, because rebuilding public sentiment and political capital now outranks even bio-surge completion — without it no other instrument can be funded or enforced.

## In practice
We task DG DIGIT and DG SANTE with codifying the successful deployments into replicable kits procured under EU preference, funded by repurposed Digital Europe money, with EPSU and patient groups as validators in Council EPSCO to blunt opposition. ENISA and HERA keep M3/M5 tracks alive: forensic closure on the backdoor and sequencing/reporting rollout in the five states, but no new staff hires this turn.

On export controls, DG TRADE uses the finished Chokepoint Coalition to seek allied volume-licence access while keeping ASML leverage unspent, and DG COMP holds gigafactory state-aid clearances open with permitting zones as the US posture clarifies — signalling without spending capital we do not have.
