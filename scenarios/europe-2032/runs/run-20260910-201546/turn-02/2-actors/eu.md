## In practice
We keep the Hardening Pact as the main effort: ENISA and DG ENER push OT-monitoring joint procurement to contract, TSOs in the two hit states get funded secondees from the Cybersecurity Reserve, and we extend the Q1-Q2 2027 exercise series to water and port operators while folding the new bio-uplift signal into Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority detection work — no new bio instrument yet, just incident-learning inside M3.

On compute we do not open a new programme: DG COMP clears two gigafactory state-aid cases, DG CNECT holds the permitting-zone talks with host regions, and grid-connection queues are arbitrated with TSOs so hardening and build-out stop competing for the same engineers. On coercion we task DG TRADE to open the Anti-Coercion evidence file on servicing bans, Council Foreign Affairs to mandate a joint approach to Tokyo and Seoul on lithography servicing, and The Hague to delay compliance while that coalition forms — defensive, jurisdictional, and cheap in capital.
