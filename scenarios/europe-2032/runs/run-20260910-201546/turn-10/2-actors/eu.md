This is diffusion, adoption and public trust work, category 9, and why now is because the permit revolt has stalled sovereign power, sentiment is at 27, and the medical breakthrough plus safety result give us something trusted to deploy if access is on our terms.

## Priority
M11 EU Municipal Staffing and Care Access Guarantee, because without written staffing the connections stay blocked and without EU-terms access the medical benefit reads as dependence.

## In practice
We run it through EPSCO and Health Councils with ENISA/HERA and the Last-Mile Corps teams already in place: EU4Health and ESF+ top-up for multi-year municipal health/IT posts in writing, disbursed only alongside signed grid-connection permits with restoration priority made transparent, not used as leverage behind closed doors. The Commission mediates the two stalled sites directly with mayors, TSOs and the Jülich battery pilot line on jobs.

We route the new therapies via HERA joint procurement and EHDS hospitals using centrally assured models, adopting the new interpretability/control check as a procurement condition via the AI Office, and publish the capability-jump evaluation note so the frontier-watching unease is answered with method, not reassurance. No new compute build — permitted zones remain the vehicle, we only unblock power and staff the last mile.
