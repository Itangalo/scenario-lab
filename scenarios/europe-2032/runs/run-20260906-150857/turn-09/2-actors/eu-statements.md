# Statements: The European Union (turn 9)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure the foundations of democratic resilience and public trust against AI-enabled harm
- `accountable_governance` (identity): We are not the first authority to face a technology that outpaces oversight — but we are the first to do so in a union built on mutual trust and democratic legitimacy. Our sovereignty does not reside in compute or models, but in our ability to act collectively and be held to account. When AI undermines public services, it undermines the Union itself. We now exist to restore and demonstrate that accountability — not by retreating from capability, but by making every exercise of power answerable to the people it affects, and by ensuring that when harm occurs, redress is swift, visible, and structural.
