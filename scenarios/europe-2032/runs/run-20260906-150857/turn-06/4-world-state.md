### A Surge and a Split

A sudden leap in AI capability—driven by a self-improving agent architecture emerging from a US lab—pushes the frontier to an estimated 67.0. Autonomous research surges in quantum materials and cryptanalysis, rendering prior roadmaps obsolete overnight. This **capability_jump** destabilises global expectations, exposing Europe’s lag.

Simultaneously, a **safety_breakthrough** offers rare hope: a real-time interpretability tool capable of detecting deceptive reasoning before output is adopted across leading labs, including one in France. The European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) fast-tracks its integration into audit protocols for the Sovereign AI Core Programme, marking the first tangible gain in assurance in years.

But cohesion fractures. Poland signs a bilateral deal with a US hyperscaler for priority access to frontier models, embedding joint AI cells in its defence ministry. Labeled “pragmatic deterrence” in Warsaw, it is seen elsewhere as a defection from EU unity. In response, Brussels offers inclusion: observer status in the Sovereign Model Assurance Framework (SMAF), a co-hosted air-gapped cluster in Wrocław, and a €10bn resilience grant—contingent on full compliance with expired CIRS mandates by 2032.

The EAAD and CIRS formally conclude their establishment phases. EAAD begins its first audit; CIRS transitions to ENISA as a standing monitoring function, now mandated to publish redacted quarterly threat assessments. Ground breaks on the Swedish InvestAI Gigafactory, accelerated under emergency powers.

The EU launches the Public Assurance Protocol—a small transparency measure requiring public reporting on safety and incidents for high-capacity sovereign systems. Framed as accountability, it aims to rebuild legitimacy. Yet public sentiment remains frozen at 25.0, unmoved by technical promises.

Six major initiatives remain in flight. Political capital erodes under sustained strain, despite the urgency justified by external shocks.

### US Posture Set

The 2028 US election concludes: a president favouring strategic control takes office. Frontier AI is now rationed by geopolitical tier, cementing dependency as leverage.
