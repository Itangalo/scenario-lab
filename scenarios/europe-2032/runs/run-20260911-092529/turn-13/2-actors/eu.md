## New measure
**EU Local Repair Jobs and Continuity Stipends**
It pays hit municipalities to hire locally for rebuild, guard and care continuity work while keeping benefits flowing during outages, turning restoration into visible jobs.
Why this and why now: with sentiment at 8, arson at yards and a member state cutting its own deal, only paid local work tied to M11 restores holds order; the office productivity news means no job collapse to fund it from, so a small labour bridge is the affordable hold.

## Priority
Priority is M11, the EU Municipal Restoration and Open-Model Triage Corps, not the new stipends, because rebuild kits and guarded convoys must land first or paid jobs have nothing to work with.

## In practice
We run M11 as the spine through the Civil Protection Mechanism and ENISA: mobile rebuild teams stay in worst-hit municipalities and hospitals, pooled transformers and generators move only under guarded convoy authorised by Justice and Home Affairs, and the failure-predicting inspection sign-off stays limited to clinic systems to avoid false trips. HERA emergency medical funds keep clean hospital restores reimbursed.

We stand up the new stipends via ESF+ and SURE-type loans through Employment and Social Affairs Council: municipalities on the outage map get wage support for repair assistants, yard guards and care aides, plus continuity payments when benefits systems are dark. We name the defection without punishing it — Council conclusions offer the servicing-compensation terms of M10 to any member state that stays in the pool, and quietly let Washington quotas be managed nationally while Brussels funds the gap locally.
