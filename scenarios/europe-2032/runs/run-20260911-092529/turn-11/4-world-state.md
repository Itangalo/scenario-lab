### Guarded clinics, empty servicing bays
Autumn brought two shocks from opposite directions. In the labs, an interpretability result that actually worked on deployed assistants — predicting failure modes before they showed — spread fast because developers wanted it. In The Hague, a second American demand landed: stretch the servicing freeze beyond leading-edge tools to older machines and wider customer lists, using US parts jurisdiction to force compliance.

Brussels tried to weld the two together. The Commission kept money flowing to the two health systems and the justice pilot, put uniformed guard on generator yards and spares containers at those sites, and extended black-start manual-fallback pools to cover them with reimbursement. Waiting-list dashboards stayed up. At the same time it opened a compensation facility for servicing losses, filed examination under the anti-coercion instrument, and sent a joint demarche to Tokyo and Seoul for spares-for-servicing.

Only half of it landed. Certification of the new safety technique on EU-hosted systems did cut incident rates in the guarded showcase — fewer locked records, fewer false trips — and nurses noticed. But compensation payments stalled in Council working groups, Tokyo offered boxes of parts again rather than a line, and ASML engineers under protest stopped boarding planes. Dutch press called it paid humiliation; French press called it submission with receipts.

Sabotage did not stop. Two more transformer stores were torched in November, and mutual-aid fuel convoys kept three regions on manual procedures for weeks. The black-start network, now formally complete with drilled isolation procedures and stocked fallback kits, kept lights and care degrading rather than stopping — the reason the showcase survived at all.

Frontier models crept forward despite paused training runs; open weights absorbed the prior generation almost fully. Control improved on paper where the new method was adopted, nowhere else. Voters noticed the quotas, the empty servicing bays, and the guarded clinics in the same broadcast. A few local lists began campaigning explicitly on removing automation projects, so far as rumour and single leaflets.
