### Containment weeks
Autumn brought the alarm health ministers had rehearsed for. A modified pathogen, designed with assistance from a widely downloaded model, sickened dozens in two countries before hospital triage and pooled sequencing flagged the cluster. Wards isolated, stockpiles opened, and cross-border referral held for weeks while laboratories raced to characterise the agent. Containment worked, but slowly and unevenly — showcase regions with screening in place moved fast, others waited for kits.

Fear reset the public argument overnight. News cycles that had ignored genome papers now led with them. Trust in open models collapsed in polls, and councils that had blocked data-centre water permits cited the incident as vindication.

Brussels kept its focus narrow. Emergency health agencies were put on incident footing, mandatory referral extended in the strongest regions, and water-system controls tightened. The Commission deliberately refused to reopen the construction fights, hoping to keep municipalities inside the screening effort.

### Chokepoint used by another hand
While doctors contained, trade officials absorbed a second shock. Washington tightened chip and model controls again and pressed the Netherlands to extend servicing bans beyond leading-edge machines to older lithography lines. The company, exposed through American components, had little room to refuse. Allied buyers kept rationed access on licences; others were cut off.

The Union did not trigger its anti-coercion tool. Instead it used its recently concluded common-access table to align export-licence positions with Japan and Korea, bargaining jointly over compute access. The coordination was real but defensive — a bid to avoid being picked off one by one.

A late-summer heatwave added pressure, with rationing in the south slowing logistics and hardening local opposition to water-hungry builds. Concrete sites stayed silent.

One bright signal cut through: a Franco-German lab network, using AI-accelerated protein modelling, sharply shortened design time for countermeasures deployed in the affected zones — wards, kits delivered, queues held. It did not restore sovereignty, but gave Brussels something visible to point to.
