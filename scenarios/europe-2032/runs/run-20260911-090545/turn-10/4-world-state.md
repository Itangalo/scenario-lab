### Where the drills had failed
Spring 2031 gave Brussels something it had not had for years: a defensive tool that actually worked. Automated patching that kept pace with newly found flaws, and detection that spotted coordinated agent swarms rather than single signatures, was pushed by ENISA out to response teams, municipal utilities and hospitals.

In certified hospitals the rollout was fast. Unpatched systems fell sharply in weeks, and a coordinated probe against three city grids in April was caught and isolated before it spread. In uncertified water and power utilities the story was messier. Packages arrived but staff could not install them without taking billing and control systems offline, and two cities had to roll back after false positives froze legitimate procurement orders.

### A genome paper no one wanted
At the same time a contested genome-model study circulated among biosecurity specialists. Its authors claimed a non-expert with model help had reached a workable design for a human-infecting organism. Methodologists attacked the result, others accused the authors of publishing too much, but health officials treated it as qualitatively different from past alarms.

HERA contracted hospital labs for expanded wastewater and clinical sequencing, and linked samples to the incident swap with Japan, Korea, Canada and Gulf hosts. Coverage remained thin outside the big cities, and sequencing backlogs meant results came in days late.

### Concrete, certificates and empty desks
No new home build was funded. Backup compute shells and shield certificates continued on old budgets. Labour ministers finalised a retraining and income-support plan for graduate-entry law, accountancy, software and customer work, but left it unfunded for next year.

The middle-power compact formally closed this semester, giving Europe pooled hosting quotas and shared black-box test results. The holdout capital was offered priority access to the new defensive packages if it aligned export rules and unwound its separate supply arrangement. It took the tooling, delayed on the alignment, and cohesion stayed cracked. Relief at a defence gain mixed with anger over jobs that still had not returned.
