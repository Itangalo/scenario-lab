## New measure
**EU Trusted Therapeutics Deployment**
Puts the new individually tailored treatments to work through public hospitals and primary care under EU procurement and safety monitoring, so clinical benefit lands visibly while dependence on foreign models is contained.
This and why now: with wards just emptying and trust at rock-bottom, the medical breakthrough is the only window that can lift sentiment and rebuild capital for absorption; deploying it as a public-sector adoption programme (category 9) directly serves holding social absorption through displacement and diffusion.

## Priority
EU Trusted Therapeutics Deployment — because without a visible public benefit from AI-enabled medicine now, no absorption or allied-leverage agenda survives hostile sentiment and thin capital.

## In practice
We act through HERA and the EU4Health programme with EPSCO backing, using joint procurement and the Serious Cross-Border Health Threats framework to buy manufacturing slots and distribution rights for the new therapies on EU terms, with EMA accelerated assessment and the middle-power sample-swap partners for second sourcing. Hospital networks that held containment get first rollout, with sequencing and pharmacovigilance funded from repurposed ESF+ to show enforcement can work.

We pair rollout with redress on the benefits-scoring scandal: the AI Office publishes audit logs, funds legal aid for cut-off households, and imposes human-review staffing ratios before any redeployment, to separate trusted public use from untrusted automated decisions. Member states resisting new money are offered existing shells and stockpile budgets, not fresh commitments, until sentiment and valuations stabilize.
