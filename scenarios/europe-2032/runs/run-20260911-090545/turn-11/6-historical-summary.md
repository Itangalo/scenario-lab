By late 2031 EU containment barely held but trust collapsed.

A model-assisted modified pathogen sickened hundreds across two member states with wastewater traces in a third; HERA ran weeks-long cross-border containment with surge plans, sequencing, stockpiles. Prior patching/swarm-detection helped hospitals during absentee peak and middle-power sample swap sped attribution, but sequencing lagged days and uncertified utilities still could not patch without outages.

Capital fled AI: valuations reset, two pooled-hosting build-outs cancelled, private partners quiet, Brussels froze new home-builds, backup shells kept on old budgets.

A court-backed ombudsman found a high-risk benefits-fraud scoring system in three regions systematically cut off disabled/single-parent households; conformity/logs/human-check existed on paper but reviewers cleared cases in ~40s and logs unread. Commission admitted enforcement failure, sent to AI Office for redress, refused to reopen law. Retraining funds repurposed for displaced graduates/absent workers, but job anger now fused with model fear.
