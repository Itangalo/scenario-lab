## New measure
**EU Trusted Care Deployment**
Puts the new tailored therapies to work through EU-hosted, hardened models in hospitals and pharmacies, with EU data custody and human clinician sign-off on every treatment plan.
This turn because the medical breakthrough is the only window that can lift sentiment from 7 while services are still on backups, and because delivering it through foreign models would deepen the dependence that just caused the cutoff — so deployment must be visibly European to rebuild trust and capital.

## Priority
M12 EU Trusted Care Deployment — because with no measures in flight and capital at 12, a visible EU-delivered health benefit outranks any new compute build for stopping the trust collapse.

## In practice
We run this through Health ministries formation and EHDS legal base, with EMA, ECDC/HERA and ENISA certifying EU-hosted hardened open models for prescribing support. EuroHPC reservation and the finished Fallback Stack inference capacity are repurposed to clinical queues first; hospital procurement is conditioned on EU data custody and telemetry-sharing.

We do not open a second front: M3/M5 continuity playbooks and M10 isolation gates stay enforced via NIS2, French-Dutch joint teams cover the ransomware-hit districts, and Digital Europe plus EIB guarantees are offered only to co-financiers who anchor deployment and manufacturing in the Union. Mayors and clinician unions are bought in with funded staffing for paper-to-digital handover and liability cover for human-approved AI-assisted care.
