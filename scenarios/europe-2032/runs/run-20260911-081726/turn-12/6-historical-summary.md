By autumn 2031 EU fallback models restored hospital/municipal services in hardest-hit areas, with EuroHPC inference reserved for clinical/administrative use — first partial recovery in a year.

A largely automated ransomware wave via small booking/billing contractors knocked services offline; districts with telemetry-sharing and human-approval gates recovered in days, others stayed dark for weeks on paper. Attribution stalled.

Brussels created a conditional Municipal Restoration Corps with seconded French-Dutch teams and emergency-funded hardened builds — aid tied to telemetry sharing and maintenance. A second wave reopened quickly, giving first positive footage in a year, but east/south arrived late, handover staffing ran short, and regions criticized conditionality.

In parallel, foreign frontier models enabled tailored therapies; EU-hosted prescribing support with in-Union data and clinician sign-off moved to routine in several networks — triage shortened, first therapies delivered, but dependence on foreign compute/models remained.

Routine coding, drafting, tier-one support work thinned further without mass layoffs; entry hiring unrecovered. Use of restored services rose while trust fell to new lows; cascade stopped but confidence not restored.
