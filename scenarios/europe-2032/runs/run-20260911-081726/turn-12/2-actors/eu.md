## New measure
**EU Municipal Restoration Corps**
Deploys funded French-Dutch joint teams plus EU-hardened fallback builds to the ransomware-dark districts to restore booking, billing and payment desks in days, with enforced human-approval gates and shared telemetry as condition for aid.
This turn because the automated ransomware sweep is the active cascade that decides whether the fallback stack survives politically, and because districts left dark for weeks are what drove sentiment to 3 — so restoration must be visible and EU-delivered now; a preparedness and resilience instrument (category 6).

## Priority
M13 EU Municipal Restoration Corps — because with capital at 9 and services dark, stopping the visible cascade outranks any new compute build for holding the two-year commitment to keep essential services running.

## In practice
We run this through the Civil Protection Mechanism and NIS2 Article 11 crisis process, with ENISA coordinating and the finished Critical Services Shield playbooks and Incident Backstop as legal cover. Funding is Digital Europe emergency reallocation plus EIB guarantees, conditioned on telemetry-sharing and human-approval gates; French-Dutch cyber teams are seconded with per-diem EU cover.

We hold the clinical line without a second front: EuroHPC inference reservations and the Trusted Care Deployment stay enforced via Health Council and EHDS, hospital procurement stays tied to EU data custody, and mayors get funded handover staffing for paper-to-digital return. Co-financing holdouts are offered restoration only if they anchor maintenance in-Union.
