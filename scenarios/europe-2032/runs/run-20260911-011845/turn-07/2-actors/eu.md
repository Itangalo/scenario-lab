## New measure
None this turn, while political capital is at zero and public sentiment is at 11 after the open-weight frontier release.

We cannot fund or enforce a new instrument until capital recovers; any large build or licensing push launched now would fail on adoption and further fragment cohesion after the defection. This turn we husband the finished Shield, Evaluation Institute, and Compact for what they still deliver without cost, and work to rebuild legitimacy before starting the next two-year build.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority named this turn, because with no measures in flight and no new measure affordable, pushing a finished line as priority would misrepresent where effort goes; all executive effort is incident-containment and cohesion-repair described below.

## In practice
We run this turn through ENISA, CERT-EU and national CSIRTs under NIS2/CER on the open-weight fallout: the release now within points of the closed frontier means the ransomware tooling seen last winter is permanently distributable, so we push hardened configurations, offline backups and clinic/municipal triage playbooks from the finished Grid and Public Services Shield, plus daily technical notes. No new resilience fund is stood up; we execute on spares, mutual aid, and procurement guidance already in national hands.

We contain the defection politically, not financially. DG TRADE keeps the Anti-Coercion review open as leverage, the Competitiveness and Foreign Affairs Councils keep the joint licence/testing offer and the Middle-Power Supply-Chain Compact as the return path, and DG CNECT/EIB keep the five gigafactory site permits warm with no cash committed. With Washington tiering access and sentiment openly hostile, we do not ask the public for data-centre siting or new money this turn; we ask for time to show containment works.
