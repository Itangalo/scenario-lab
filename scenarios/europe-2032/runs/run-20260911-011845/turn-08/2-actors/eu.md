## In practice
We run this turn through ENISA, CERT-EU and national CSIRTs under NIS2/CER on the large automated attack: triage, hardened configs, offline-backup restoration and clinic/municipal playbooks from the finished Grid and Public Services Shield, on spares and mutual aid already in national hands, with daily technical notes. No new resilience fund is stood up; we prevent cascade, not control.

We treat the short-notice cut-off from the leading model as a foresight failure to contain, not to escalate. DG CNECT and Health/EuroHPC shift affected hospitals, ministries and firms to EU-hosted and middle-power models via the finished Middle-Power Compact, DG TRADE keeps the Anti-Coercion review open against the defecting capital but offers joint licensing and evaluation recognition as the return path, and we note the limited US-China risk agreement as the floor to align future EU evaluation work to once capital allows rebuilding.
