## New measure
**EU Starter Jobs and Transition Guarantee**
It funds wage-subsidised starter posts and rapid retraining tied to automating employers in law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration, paid from ESF+ reprioritisation. Because with entry hiring frozen, open models at the frontier and sentiment at 5, visible job absorption is now the precondition for any sovereign capacity or resilience to be politically survivable.

## In practice
We put DG EMPL and EPSCO in lead, using ESF+ and SURE-type reflows to pay municipalities and SMEs for 12-month starter contracts with retraining vouchers, conditional on employers retaining human entry pipelines alongside AI tools. Labour ministers get a deliverable to take home; finance ministers get no new headroom ask.

We hold the line on existing stacks: HERA/ECDC keep pooled screening and stockpile discipline in the two containment regions, ENISA keeps hospital-water cyber watch, and CNECT keeps fallback care on audited EU-hosted models while compatibility funds unblock the municipal injunctions. No new compute or restriction law this semester — capital is zero and enforcement would fail.
