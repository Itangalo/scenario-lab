From Feb US cut-off through 2026, Europe ran on continuity stacks and EU helpers with longer queues, strained by frozen AI factories, thin staffing, and sabotage.

January twin biosecurity shocks brought Brussels pooled screening, lab triage, stockpiles, cyber-hardening favoring large sites. In late winter a modified pathogen with partial model-assisted design spread in two regions, killing dozens before containment by early summer; a working interpretability control steadied triage and refusals. Municipal operators then refused screening modules over fit and funding, courts froze enforcement, surveillance stayed patchy, Brussels answered with negotiation, funds, and delayed deadlines. Care stayed open on blander models with longer queues; vigils at frozen builds grew angrier.

Autumn brought an openly downloadable near-frontier model to hundreds of thousands of machines, humiliating official stacks. Scarred regions kept screening discipline, pooled testing high, stockpiles replenished, no new chain restarted — detection shield worked — but maps stayed patchy and hospital cyber-watch routine. Law firms, accountants, software and customer centres stopped hiring juniors despite good productivity; Brussels funded small wage-subsidised starter posts and retraining vouchers from repurposed social funds, conditional on human pipelines, with no fresh headroom. Union held line on blander machines amid managed-decline anger.
