## In practice

Health and Telecoms Councils carry it as service continuity, not retaliation.

We do not open a court fight on freezes or a trade fight on the cut-off this semester; the Legal Service documents the cut-off for the Anti-Coercion file while DG ENER keeps water-power compacts alive, and ENISA teams stay on hospital/lab hardening under M9 so the fallback does not become the next intrusion path.
