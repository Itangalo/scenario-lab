## Priority
M6 European Public Benefit Rollout, because holding triage, permit and tutoring workloads on EU-hosted fallback models is the only live execution of our two-year commitment to hold European access to capable AI inside the Union.

## In practice
We run M6 as a continuity operation under DG CNECT with ENISA: joint procurement stays switched to fallback models runnable on EU-jurisdiction clouds, sequenced to inference for public services first with Digital Europe and EIB money re-sequenced from paper gigafactory builds. Where EU-hosted capacity exists waiting lists are defended; where it does not we fund paper fallbacks and keep insurer-required segmented backups and reporting as the condition for cover.

We do not open a parallel compute programme. The finished Gigafactories and tech-sovereignty lines are kept alive politically by converting their permits into the emergency re-anchoring co-location offer, starting with the breakaway hyperscaler deal, and we use Competitiveness Council and health ministries to press capitals to accept EU-jurisdiction terms — but no new fund or directive until capital recovers enough to carry it.
