## In practice
We treat the cutoff as a continuity incident, not a negotiation. Under the NIS2 and Critical Entities Resilience bases, DG CNECT with ENISA and national health funds switch EU-hosted procurement under M6 to fallback models that can run on EU-controlled infrastructure, prioritising triage, permit and tutoring stacks already showing numbers, while joint cyber teams keep segmented backups and reporting as the condition for affordable cover.

We keep M1 alive politically by converting permits and lithography talks into an emergency re-anchoring offer: co-location and EU-jurisdiction terms for any hyperscaler capacity still delivered, starting with the breakaway state's deal, with Digital Europe and EIB money re-sequenced toward inference capacity that unblocks public services first. No new fund, no new directive this semester — with capital at 8 we cannot start what we cannot carry, so M6 scales only where EU-hosted clouds already exist.
