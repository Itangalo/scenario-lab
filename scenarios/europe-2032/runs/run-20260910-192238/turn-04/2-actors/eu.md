## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee**
It funds wage-subsidised first hires, rapid retraining vouchers and short-time transition income for displaced juniors in law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration, paid by automating employers and ESF+.
This and now because measurable non-replacement of entry roles plus a member-state breakaway makes a visible jobs answer the condition for holding any resilience and sovereignty line, and it is cheap enough to start while capital is at 26.
