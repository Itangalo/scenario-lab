By spring 2031 a compromised IT-management update enabled AI-mutated ransomware across portals, clinics, two hospital groups and a grid-spares supplier; islanding kits, paper folders and wastewater sampling held power on but care degraded, attribution pending.

Meanwhile a US lab demonstrated a far faster coding/operations agent; distilled copycats from near-frontier open weights circulated on private hardware beyond recall. Washington tightened chip/model licences, rationing Europe; data-centre curtailments hardened into contracts.

Junior hiring froze. Commission wage-bridge/retraining fund passed after 3-month fight; payments late, enrolment thin. After Nov 2030 freeze blackouts, EU triage protected homes/hospitals while curtailing compute; voters saw Brussels as manager of decline.

Autumn 2031 brought no new blackout but no relief: slow portals, paper registries beside servers, graduates steered to unrecognised checker courses. Two papers shifted Brussels: a contested genomics claim that a model helped sketch a viable human-infecting design, making wastewater look like frontline infrastructure; and an interpretability result giving a testable, certifiable control property adopted by two large labs. Commission paired them in an implementing act with no new law/levy: screened model configs for hit hospitals/towns, PCR/syndromic kits for clinics, validation by EU evaluation institute. Delivery uneven — configs broke legacy records and rolled back, kits arrived Oct in some cities, not by Christmas in others. US rationing did not ease; Europe absorbed, did not catch up.
