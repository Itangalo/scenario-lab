## New measure
**European Public Benefit AI Deployment Scale-Up**
Puts the working public-sector AI use cases that just cut waiting lists and decision times onto EU procurement and funding rails to replicate them across health systems, administrations and schools.
This directly serves our two-year commitment to restore consent via jobs, bills and visible protection, and with political capital at 4.0 and sentiment at 0.0 we must amplify the one visible success attributed to a European decision rather than start a new contested build.

## Priority
M10 European Public Benefit AI Deployment Scale-Up, because only attributed public benefit landing in clinics and town halls can lift sentiment from zero and rebuild capital to carry anything else under our jobs-bills-protection commitment.

## In practice
We task DG DIGIT and DG SANTE with a joint scale-up facility under the Digital Europe Programme and EU4Health, using existing procurement frameworks to copy the sites where waiting lists fell: same screened configs from M9, same EU Evaluation Institute validation, no new law. Cohesion and RRF reflows fund deployment teams, with member-state health and interior ministries as co-owners.

We keep ENISA/CERT-EU telemetry sharing and HERA/ECDC biosurveillance running on Shield mandates to hold the April stop against a second sweep, and DG EMPL continues ESF+ advances for the late wage-bridge payments so benefit and protection arrive together. Communications names the European decision behind each site, to convert filmed blockades into filmed queues moving.
