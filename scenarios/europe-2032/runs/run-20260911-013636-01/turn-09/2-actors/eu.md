**EU Middle-Power Resilience and Jobs Pact**
What it actually does is lock the new middle-power coordination into joint compute bargaining, export-licence alignment and shared evaluation, tied to EU retraining and hiring support for displaced sectors. ## Priority
We name the EU Middle-Power Resilience and Jobs Pact as priority because with restoration stalled and capital at zero, joint external leverage plus domestic job relief is the only path that serves the incoming commitment.

We finish the old commitment by keeping the ENISA-led Restoration Cell on sustainment: ENISA with EC3 and health ministries hands cleaned backups to municipal IT and showcase hospitals, keeps islanding procedures, and stops forcing the failed foreign-workflow ban where evasion is universal.

We start the new direction in the Foreign Affairs and EPSCO Councils, using the Anti-Coercion Instrument review and the European Social Fund Plus reallocation to fund the Pact secretariat, joint evaluation pool, and wage-insurance pilots for law, accountancy, software and customer-operations graduates.
