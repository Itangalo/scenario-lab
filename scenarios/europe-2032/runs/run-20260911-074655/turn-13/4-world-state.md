### The floor falls out from under the deal

Autumn brought the sweep everyone feared and one announcement no one in Brussels had priced.

The attack came first. Automated ransomware moved through municipal registries, billing platforms and hospital IT in a dozen cities within hours, using a poisoned software component no scanner had flagged. Wards went back to paper. Attribution teams again found only machine-written tooling and said so on camera. Where the joint health-emergency and cyber teams had already installed automatic patching and swarm detection, the wave broke: protected hospitals in Lyon, Porto and Gdansk islanded their assistants and stayed up while neighbours went dark.

Then Washington took direct control of its frontier laboratories. Security officers, a state equity stake and classification orders replaced the old voluntary testing regime. Brussels discovered overnight that conformity assessments, market access threats and fines built for companies did not reach another state's security apparatus. Continuity talks for Rotterdam and Krakow's lost diagnostic tools were referred to state-to-state channels. At the same time Washington and Beijing announced a limited risk pact on weights security and autonomous escalation, thinly verified but real.

At home the labour numbers hardened. Law firms, accountants, software houses and customer centres confirmed entry posts simply not replaced; the graduate corps guarding clinics and fenced data-centre sites became the image of the season. A few productivity studies arguing work was being augmented, not removed, were overtaken before publication.

The one delivery was unglamorous and noticed. The hospitals keep-running regime completed: paper fallbacks drilled, backup power and staffing rotas funded from reprogrammed health and civil protection money, queues re-read by humans where logs had never been checked. Services degraded but did not stop during the December aftershocks. Councils still banned new builds, mayors still refused forced siting, and trust kept sinking — but the patches held where installed.

By December, Europe ran on patched systems it did not own, under rules it had not written.
