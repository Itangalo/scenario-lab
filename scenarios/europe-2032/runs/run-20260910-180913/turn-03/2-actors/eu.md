## New measure
**EU Continuity Stack for Essential Services**
Stands up EU-hosted fallback AI services for the hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the leading foreign model, using EU-controlled compute and vetted open-weight models under EU jurisdiction.
This is Diffusion, adoption and public trust work, because the cutoff makes sovereignty tangible in wards and offices now, and only a visible EU-run substitute restores function while answering the foresight failure without duplicating the Gigafactories compute line.

## Priority
EU Continuity Stack for Essential Services, because the access cutoff outranks even the defensive rollout — essential services without models cannot wait for gigafactories in 2030.

## In practice
We mandate via Health Council and Digital Council an emergency continuity mandate: DG CNECT with ENISA and the AI Office contracts EuroHPC and the M1 gigafactory pre-sites to host hardened open-weight models for triage, admin and industrial users hit by the cutoff, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health money, with procurement preference for EU-processed data residency.

M4 Defensive Shield Scale-Up continues as the audit backbone — ENISA certification, NIS2 rollout to the two compromised TSOs first, municipal monitoring costs covered — while M1/M2 are held to permitting/state-aid tracks and explicitly tasked to reserve capacity for the Continuity Stack, turning the cutoff into leverage to unblock siting in Spain/Germany. On the benefits scandal we hold to strict 2027 AI Act application plus redress, to stop trust falling further while substitution lands.
