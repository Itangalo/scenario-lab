## In practice

French, German and Nordic SOCs second staff to a scale-up cell that runs live loss-of-visibility drills in Q2.

M3 continues as the audit and exercise backbone underneath this deployment, while M1/M2 gigafactory and compute-zone work is held to permitting and state-aid tracks without new political spend. On the benefits/policing scandal we do not legislate anew: we order strict 2027 application of the AI Act high-risk duties, fund redress via ESF+ technical assistance, and direct the AI Office to publish enforcement benchmarks to answer the hollow-enforcement charge.
