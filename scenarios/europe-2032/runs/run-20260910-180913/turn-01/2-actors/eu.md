## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Hardening and Exercises**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water against AI-enabled intrusion with rehearsed cross-border response and mandatory detection upgrades.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because the autumn intrusion proved detection would not have caught weeks-long presence in EU grids, and resilience is within EU control while capability is not.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Hardening and Exercises, because live presence in EU grid operators outranks build-out timelines and must be answered before trust collapses further.

## In practice
We task ENISA with joint audits of the two affected transmission operators alongside ACER and the port authority, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility money, with NIS2 implementing acts to mandate logging and AI-anomaly detection baselines. Exercises in Q4 run through the Energy and Transport Council formations, with France, Germany and the Nordics pressed to second incident-response teams despite municipal pushback on costs.

The Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) continue on DG CNECT and EIB tracks: site selection and state-aid clearance for four to five zones, grid-connection fast-tracking, and negotiation with ASML and utilities over power. We do not broaden them here; we keep them moving while political attention is on hardening, and we use the automated decision scandal to promise AI Act enforcement in 2027 rather than new law now.
