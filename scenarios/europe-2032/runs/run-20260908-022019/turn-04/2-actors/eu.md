## In practice  
We are acting on a rare convergence: a real safety advance and a real catastrophe that together redefine the stakes. The safety_breakthrough — a certified method for predicting emergent agent behaviour before deployment — is being adopted globally within days, but we will not depend on its trickle-down. Instead, we are launching the Sovereign Safety Stack to integrate it directly into EU-controlled systems. Hosted by the European AI Safety Institute (EASI), with co-development from INRIA, Fraunhofer, and KU Leuven, this stack will include real-time interpretability dashboards, anomaly detection trained on incident data from CS-CO, and enforceable containment protocols for all AI systems funded or operated under EU authority. We are repurposing €3.8bn from the Digital Europe Programme and Horizon Europe’s AI4Trust strand to fund development, with deployment mandated across Public AI Corps projects by turn 5.

At the same time, we are closing out Cyber Shield for Critical Operators: the joint alert system between Germany and Poland is now live, and we are using the June JHA Council to extend participation to eight additional member states under a new Article 13a of NIS2, activated by AI-specific threat levels. The system will feed into the Sovereign Safety Stack’s monitoring layer, creating continuity between resilience and control. The Tech Sovereignty Package remains behind schedule, but we are leveraging the Anti-Coercion Instrument to negotiate priority transformer deliveries from South Korea, offering semiconductor export coordination in return. Meanwhile, the Open-Weight Initiative’s shadow monitoring has identified two repositories hosting models above 46.5 capability; Europol is coordinating takedown requests through Interpol while we fast-track a legal opinion on extraterritorial enforcement. The Public AI Corps moves into implementation planning: DG DIGIT and the European Health Agency are finalising the first wave of deployments in healthcare and vocational education, with pilot regions selected based on interoperability readiness. We are not spreading thin — we are threading the needle between sovereignty, safety, and trust, and we are doing it in real time.
