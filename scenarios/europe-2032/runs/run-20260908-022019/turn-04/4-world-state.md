### A Turn of Crises and Clarity  
Global AI capability advances by +2.5, reaching 62.0, as autonomous agents demonstrate sustained coordination in multi-day tasks. The **bio_incident**—a lab-escaped AI-designed pathogen causing localized outbreaks across two continents—shocks the world. Simultaneously, a **safety_breakthrough** emerges: a method to predict emergent agent behaviour before deployment is validated and rapidly adopted. The gap between risk and control narrows—but only for those who can deploy it.

### EU Responds Under Pressure  
The European Union accelerates work on the **Sovereign Safety Stack**, a large category 5 measure already in flight and reflected in the portfolio charge. The project, hosted by EASI and backed by €3.8bn in redirected funds, enters its second phase, expanding integration of the safety breakthrough into Public AI Corps systems and preparing mandates for interpretability tools and containment protocols across critical infrastructure. Full deployment will not be complete this turn, consistent with the two-turn minimum for high-cost measures.

**Cyber Shield for Critical Operators** concludes successfully, with the German-Polish threat-sharing system now feeding into eight additional member states via a new NIS2 mechanism. This delivers +3 to resilience, raising it to 43.0.

However, strain shows. The portfolio now carries five large measures (InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, EU Open-Weight Monitoring, Public AI Corps, Sovereign Safety Stack) and one named priority, costing −13 political capital this turn. Despite the +8 proposal bonus from earlier measures answering the bio_incident and safety_breakthrough, capital drops to 22.0. Public sentiment falls −3 due to anxiety over the bio_incident and growing unease about opaque AI in public services, despite earlier gains.

### Sovereignty Erodes  
Global capability rose by 2.5, costing −1 to eu_ai_sovereignty. No capacity event occurred, and no category 4 measure finished. The InvestAI Gigafactories project provides no momentum this turn. Sovereignty thus declines to 19.0.

Openweight capability rises to 49.0, tracking half the distance to the closed frontier, as spillover from safety tools enters open research.
