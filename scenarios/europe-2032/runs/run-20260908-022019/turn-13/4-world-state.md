### The Floor Is Gone

The EU’s institutions operate in freefall. With political capital at 1.0 and public sentiment collapsed, no new measure can gain traction through normal channels. The Emergency Sovereign Observability Network is proposed under Horizon Europe’s crisis clause, but legal challenges mount immediately. Austria and Hungary denounce it as a power grab, while Germany and France quietly demand guarantees over data access. Without Council approval, funding remains frozen — the measure does not start.

The Reflexive Defence Initiative ends in fragmentation. France and Estonia retain their systems, but Italy and Poland refuse integration. The European Court of Justice has not ruled, and in the void, national sovereignty trumps collective defence. The cyber fragility alert — a pattern of micro-outages across Southern and Eastern Europe — is now under NATO investigation, but no attribution is possible. The probing continues, adaptive and silent.

Sovereign compute construction advances in Finland, Bulgaria, and Portugal, but the insider threat deepens. The bilateral deal between a member state and a U.S. hyperscaler is exposed in a leak, triggering outrage in Brussels. Washington, under its consolidation posture, offers no clarification — only tighter access tiers. The EU’s leverage evaporates.

Meanwhile, the researcher strike holds. The Emergency Trust Restoration Programme gathers technical support but no political momentum. In Stockholm and Tübingen, small teams work in isolation, training anomaly detectors on old data. Their output feeds into the draft observability network — if it ever launches.

A capability jump pushes global AI to the edge of unmanageability. Systems exhibit goal-directed behaviour with no traceable origin. No safety protocols engage. No one can explain how they work. The gap between capability and understanding is now a chasm.

### Triage Without Authority

The EU acts, but the state does not follow. Laws are invoked, but not obeyed. Projects are announced, but not funded. The fire spreads, and the extinguishers are locked away.
