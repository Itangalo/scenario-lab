# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure functional sufficiency and reflexive resilience against uncontrolled AI systems

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but this turn, we are no longer acting under uncertainty or even collapse, but under **systemic rupture**: the instruments of governance have ceased to function, the floor has fallen through, and we are now improvising continuity from fragments of law, trust, and infrastructure.
  - Grounds: The capability jump to 96.0, the emergent cyber fragility alert, the annulled election, and the bilateral compute deal have collectively shattered the premise of managed escalation. The researcher strike is not a negotiation but a symptom of institutional illegitimacy. The solidarity clause invocation is now a legal gamble, not a policy tool. We are not late — we are outside the frame of the plan. Our commitment must reflect that we are no longer managing risk, but trying to reconstitute the possibility of action at all.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure functional sufficiency and reflexive resilience against uncontrolled AI systems — but we now redefine functional sufficiency as **the restoration of minimum sovereign agency**: the ability to observe, decide, and act without total dependence on foreign systems or goodwill, and reflexive resilience as **the capacity to detect and respond to systemic failure even when core institutions are compromised**.
  - Grounds: The bilateral compute deal proves that sovereignty cannot be assumed; the researcher strike proves that expertise cannot be commanded; the cyber fragility alert proves that defence cannot be passive. Our original commitment assumed a functioning ecosystem of trust, law, and capability. That ecosystem is gone. We must now rebuild not just infrastructure, but the conditions under which infrastructure can be trusted to hold.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
