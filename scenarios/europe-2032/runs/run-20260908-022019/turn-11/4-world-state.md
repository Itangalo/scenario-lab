In response, the EU invoked crisis powers to launch the **Sovereign Compute Redundancy and Insider Threat Shield**, redirecting funds to build hardened, air-gapped facilities in Finland, Bulgaria, and Portugal. However, the breach deepened public distrust. Protests erupted outside data centres, and media framed the incident as a failure of both security and legitimacy, dragging `public_sentiment` down to 10.0.
