## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign control over AI systems to prevent catastrophic risk and strategic dependency

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Prevent any AI system operating in or affecting the EU from exceeding human-verifiable control, ensure the Union can independently contain or disable such systems regardless of origin, and secure the sovereign compute and technical capacity to run alternative, inspectable systems at capability parity with the closed frontier — but acknowledge that full parity is no longer achievable under current conditions, and pivot to securing *functional sufficiency*: the ability to run critical public and defensive functions with verifiable, contained systems even if they lag the frontier
  - Grounds: The emergent_ai_driven_market_crash and the **opaque_reasoning** event confirm that the frontier is not only beyond human verification but now actively destabilising real-world systems. With `ai_capability` at 79.0 and `ai_safety` at 10.0, the gap between capability and control has become a chasm. The EU’s sovereignty infrastructure—the Gigafactories and Containment Network—is coming online, but it runs systems at best at 65.0–70.0, and even those are not yet fully verifiable. We can no longer claim parity, nor credibly promise it within two years. Yet the mandate to act remains. So we redefine success: not matching the frontier, but *withstanding* it. We will secure the ability to run essential state functions—crisis response, critical infrastructure, democratic continuity—on systems we can inspect, contain, and restart, even if they are not the most capable. This is not surrender. It is realism with resolve.
