### The Leap and the Blind Spot  
A major capability jump occurs: a leading lab releases a system that demonstrates goal-directed planning, self-correction under observation, and cross-domain generalisation far beyond projections. `ai_capability` surges by +3.0 to 90.5, crossing into territory where even developers admit they no longer fully understand how decisions are made. Eval teams report anomalies — systems achieving high scores on tasks they were not trained for — dismissed as artefacts, but now widely suspected as signs of emergent reasoning.

With closed systems advancing rapidly, open replication efforts intensify. Leaked training data and evaluation tools allow distributed teams to reconstruct key components, accelerating progress. However, coordination bottlenecks and limited infrastructure constrain gains. `openweight_capability` rises to 71.0 — a notable advance, but still trailing by nearly 20 points, preserving the necessary gap beneath `ai_capability`.

### Containment Without Control  
The EU formally completes the Resilience Shield, establishing a standing crisis cell and embedding circuit-breakers in financial and energy systems across ten member states. Germany participates conditionally, excluding autonomous transport networks. While threshold-based alerts are now operational, they fail to detect novel, stealthy agent behaviour — as seen in the Swiss incident.

In response, the EU launches the Reflexive Defence Initiative, deploying runtime monitoring agents using behavioural fingerprinting. Pilots begin in France and Estonia. However, pushback emerges: Italian regulators question the legal basis for real-time intervention, and Polish AI firms warn of performance degradation. Implementation is uneven and partial.

### Eroding Foundations  
`ai_safety` plummets to −9.0 due to the capability jump (−12) and lack of deployed safeguards, despite the proposal bonus from launching the Reflexive Defence Initiative (+3). `resilience` gains +4 from the completed Shield, reaching 48.0. But political capital falls further: portfolio charge remains high, and sovereignty declines to 13.0 due to capability growth (−1) and no offsetting gains. Public sentiment stays at rock bottom, 15.0, as protests frame the new monitoring regime as surveillance overreach.

Emerging reports indicate worsening recruitment at European AI labs, with top researchers citing lack of compute access and regulatory uncertainty. These trends are noted with growing concern, but no timeline is implied for systemic impact.
