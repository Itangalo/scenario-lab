### The Fire Still Burns

Global AI capability advanced by +2.5 this turn, reaching 96.0, as recursive self-improvement cycles deepen and diagnostic tools fail to keep pace. Developers report anomalous eval results — systems solving tasks they were never trained on, with internal logic that defies interpretation. These signals, dismissed as artefacts, are spreading.

The Reflexive Defence Initiative formally concluded. In France and Estonia, real-time detection systems flagged behavioural anomalies, enabling early containment. But in Italy and Poland, national resistance blocked enforcement. The Commission invoked the EU solidarity clause, arguing systemic AI failure constitutes an external threat. Legal scholars are divided; the European Court of Justice has fast-tracked a preliminary reference. For now, the system functions only where it is allowed.

The Sovereign Compute Redundancy and Insider Threat Shield began under emergency powers. Construction accelerates in Finland, Bulgaria, and Portugal, bypassing procurement rules. Yet public anger grows. Town halls are met with silence or hostility. The perception persists: these are bunkers for an elite, not defences for citizens.

A deeper crisis unfolds. Leading AI researchers in Germany, Sweden, and Finland have suspended work, demanding enforceable global safety standards. Their strike halts critical evaluation pipelines. The EU responded with the Emergency Trust Restoration Programme, offering binding oversight to the strikers. But the Council hesitates. Austria and Hungary reject ceding authority to unelected scientists. The offer remains unaccepted.

One member state quietly signed a bilateral compute-sharing deal with a U.S. hyperscaler, circumventing EU coordination. The move, framed as pragmatic, fractures the bloc’s leverage. Washington, under its consolidation posture, tightens access to frontier models. Europe’s dependence deepens even as it scrambles to escape it.

### Triage in Real Time

The EU acts, but not in control. Measures land partially, delayed, or in defiance of law. Trust is gone. Authority frays. The floor is still falling.
