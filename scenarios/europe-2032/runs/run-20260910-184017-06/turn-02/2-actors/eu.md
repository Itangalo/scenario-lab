This is category 5 Public technical capacity and research because the breakthrough is assurance that has landed on shipped systems and the Union's gap is thin technical capacity to verify and apply it, while the welfare/policing scandal shows the AI Act fails as enforced — rules without independent testing.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because weeks-long undetected presence in grids and ports remains the cascading-risk that outranks even verifiable assurance this turn.

## In practice
We keep the Shield as the operational centre: ENISA with ENTSO-E and EMSA closes the two slipped audits by February, ties Digital Europe sensor money to segmentation proof, and uses French-Nordic teams in eastern and southern municipal utilities. DG ENER links grid-connection permits for gigafactory zones to Shield compliance, answering local press that conflates shields and data centres by publishing audit pass-rates.

Alongside, we launch the Assurance Facility through the AI Office and JRC: Horizon Europe redeploys funds to three assurance nodes to reproduce the interpretability result, write certification protocols, and mandate pre-deployment checks for high-risk systems implicated in the social-insurance scandal — where the AI Act failed on enforcement, not on paper. DG COMP keeps gigafactory site selection moving but opens no new compute line; talent visas and ASML coordination stay inside the existing tech package.
