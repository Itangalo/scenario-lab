## In practice
We run the Shield as incident command: ENISA with CERT-EU and ENTSO-E triages the ransomware/dependency blast, ring-fences Digital Europe sensor money to segmented sites that passed winter audits, and publishes daily restoration rates to separate defenders-behind narrative from recovery. DG DIGIT fast-tracks the Assurance Facility checklists to hospitals and energy control rooms hit in the sweep, using the three JRC nodes for post-incident certification.

We stand up the Transition Fund through DG EMPL and ECOFIN: ESF+ repurposed for wage insurance pilots in five hardest-hit labour markets, with EPSCO agreement that firms deploying frontier agents co-fund retraining, while DG CNECT ties gigafactory zone permits to local hiring and apprenticeship quotas to answer councils blocking power. We bank the public-sector adoption win — waiting lists falling — via DG REFORM procurement showcase to prove European decisions deliver benefit, not just restriction.
