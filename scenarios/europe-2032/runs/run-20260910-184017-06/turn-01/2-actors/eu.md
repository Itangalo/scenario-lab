## In practice
We task ENISA with ENTSO-E, EMSA and member-state TSOs to roll emergency NIS2 implementing acts: 72-hour deep-audit of transmission operators, port authorities and water utilities, forced segmentation of remote access, and EU-funded sensor retrofits from the Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility. The Competitiveness and Telecoms Councils carry it jointly to avoid a single veto point, with France and Nordics providing incident-response teams to exposed eastern and southern operators in exchange for accelerated permitting credits.

Alongside, we keep the inherited InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty package moving: DG COMP and DG ENER shepherd the four-to-five site selection and the AI data-centre acceleration zones, using the June US switch-off scare to press member states on grid connection and ASML supply-chain leverage as a bargaining chip. No new compute line is opened — broadening happens through execution, not duplication.
