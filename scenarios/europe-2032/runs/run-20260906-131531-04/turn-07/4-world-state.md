### The Enforcement Gambit

The EU launched **Suspend Frontier Model Access for Non-Compliant Developers**, a large category 3 measure, as its new priority. Though legally contested, partial enforcement mechanisms were initiated: ENISA issued compliance directives to major cloud providers, and BEREC began drafting DNS filtering protocols. However, Germany and the Netherlands formally challenged the proportionality of extraterritorial enforcement under EU law, delaying full rollout. OpenAI and Anthropic dismissed the threat, citing minimal EU revenue exposure, while Meta maintained its non-cooperation stance. The credibility of the enforcement push remains unproven, but the act of attempting it marks a shift from symbolic to coercive regulation.

The **Resilience Surge Protocol** and **InvestAI Gigafactories** both concluded this turn. The former delivered hardened cyber defences across energy and health sectors, earning +6 resilience. The latter, despite delays, secured binding commitments for three gigafactory sites, contributing +5 to eu_ai_sovereignty. These completions freed political capital, offsetting some portfolio strain.

AI capability rose to 80.0 (+4.0), driven by self-improving training loops and the **ai_investment_collapse**, which redirected capital toward efficiency over scale but did not slow progress. Early signals from a nascent open-weight model ecosystem began to emerge, supported by distributed fine-tuning efforts and increased compute access under InvestAI. While no single release closed the capability gap, incremental improvements across multiple open models lifted `openweight_capability` to 60.0 (+4.0), reflecting steady accumulation rather than a sudden leap.

`ai_safety` inched up to 10.0, as the audit regime’s enforcement attempt created a nominal deterrence effect, though no actual audits were completed. `resilience` reached 48.0, reflecting sustained hardening. `eu_ai_sovereignty` rose to 17.0: +5 from Gigafactories completion, −1 from capability growth ≥2. `eu_political_capital` improved to 13.0: portfolio charge dropped to −7 (two large measures finished, two remain), priority cost −1, but +5 from measure completions and +4 proposal bonus for responding to bio-leak and investment collapse. Public sentiment fell to 25.0 amid fears of digital fragmentation and job losses in AI-dependent sectors.

The first signs of a broader public model advancement have appeared, but no decisive breakthrough has yet materialized. The **emergent_public_stack_breakthrough** gate is now active, reflecting growing momentum—but its full effect remains pending.
