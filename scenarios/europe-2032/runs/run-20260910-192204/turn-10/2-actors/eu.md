## New measure
**Blockaded Sites Local Benefit and Entry-Jobs Compact**
Puts blockaded gigafactory and data-centre sites back to work through binding local-benefit agreements — local hiring quotas for entry workers, community energy and tax shares, and mediated construction restart via Council dialogue — funded by reprogrammed InvestAI regional envelopes.
This and now because with capital at 9 and sentiment at 6 concrete curing in the rain is a political death spiral: no restart without local consent, no sovereignty without restart, and wage cheques alone do not shorten queues.

## In practice
We task DG REGIO and DG ENER with the restart: temporary pause orders stay, but Civil Protection site guard converts to mediated access, with prefects and mayors in the three blocking states convening site-by-site tables with contractors, farmers and graduate pickets. The offer is concrete: earmarked apprenticeships and entry posts tied to the Transition Backstop cohorts, municipal power-price rebates from the substations, and a share of land-lease revenue held locally.

Alongside, HERA/ECDC keep the Bio Early-Warning Surge (M10) procurement moving — wastewater nodes and stockpile pre-positioning — and ENISA joint feeds plus municipal paper kits stay tasked for containment. We do not launch new compute or lift the triage freeze; we contain the bilateral cloud deal and let wage insurance keep paying while the compact tries to turn shells into jobs.
