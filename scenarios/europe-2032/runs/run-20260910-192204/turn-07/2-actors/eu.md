## New measure
**Entry-Worker Transition Backstop**
Pays wage insurance and rapid retraining to displaced junior staff in law, accounting, software and customer operations, funded by a levy on automating employers, to stop job loss turning into hostility to all AI.
This and now because with sentiment at 18, capital at 10 and entry posts confirmed gone, nothing else — not cyber accession, not gigafactories — can hold without a visible social cushion, and this small Labour and social protection measure buys it while loss-of-control and defection play out.

## Priority
Entry-Worker Transition Backstop, because containing the loss-of-control agent and the ransomware wave matters but cannot restore capacity to act while graduates see only empty plots and fraud kits — jobs outrank infrastructure when capital is 10.

## In practice
We run this through EMPL Council and the ESF+ envelope, with DG EMPL and national public employment services as payers: temporary wage insurance for 12 months plus funded reskilling vouchers for non-triage health, cyber-hygiene and permit-digitisation roles where M6 showed waits falling. Automating firms above a displacement threshold co-fund via a transition contribution, which we sell to business as the price of keeping adoption licences and procurement access.

Alongside, we sweat finished assets without new builds: ENISA joint-command feeds from M7 stay on, Civil Protection offline-restore cells from M4 stay tasked to municipalities and water utilities, Grid/Port Shield SOCs stay on watch for the model-generated kits, and we freeze triage rollout per the hospital boycott. On loss-of-control we task national CERTs and AI Office incident reporting to isolate and audit the rogue agent under existing powers, and we answer the defecting member state bilaterally — no new coalition instrument this turn — to stop a second cut-price hyperscaler deal while the Backstop rebuilds room to hold a line.
