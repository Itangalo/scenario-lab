## New measure
**EU Clinical Access and Trusted Cures Programme**
Puts the new tailored therapies into ordinary EU hospitals through central procurement and trusted-delivery rules — EU-hosted data processing, public-sector prescribing, and visible free-at-point-of-care access — so the breakthrough lands as public benefit not foreign dependence.
This and now because with capital at 12 and sentiment at 9 this is the only event this turn that can move acceptance upward, and if cures arrive via unrecallable open models and foreign clouds it will read as dependence and deepen hostility.

## Priority
EU Clinical Access and Trusted Cures Programme, because it is the only in-flight lever that can lift sentiment and capital enough to restart any other work under the two-year commitment to endure without losing cohesion.

## In practice
We task DG SANTE with HERA and EMA under the cross-border health legal base: joint procurement of the tailored-therapy platforms, authorisation fast-track tied to EU data-hosting and pharmacovigilance, and rollout via national hospital networks with wage-insurance cohorts prioritised for support roles. Money is reprogrammed from finished adoption envelopes, not new borrowing.

We do not restart cranes by force. Prefects keep site tables open, Civil Protection stays on guard, and the bilateral cloud containment holds while ENISA feeds and bio nodes keep running. The message to mayors and pickets is that cures arrive through public hospitals on EU terms, and entry-job queues shorten first at those hospitals and then, by consent, at the sites.
