### The Plateau and the Flood

Evidence mounts that the AI frontier has plateaued: top-tier models underperform expectations, and prices for access drop sharply, suggesting yesterday’s cutting edge is now commoditised. Yet this apparent pause brings no relief. A powerful open-weight model, nearly matching the closed frontier in capability, is released and spreads globally within weeks. With no central control, it is now beyond any jurisdiction’s reach. However, the EU’s own open-weight ecosystem remains constrained by earlier underinvestment and fragmented coordination. The diffusion of this foreign-led model increases baseline capability awareness, but only marginally advances actual operational capacity within the Union’s sphere. Thus, `openweight_capability` rises modestly, reflecting slow absorption rather than sudden leap.

The EU completes the Integrity Audit Expansion, its final attempt to salvage accountability in an age of opaque systems. Auditors deploy new methods—tracking outcome anomalies and redress patterns—but find little to validate. The tools are clever, but they probe shadows. Still, the completion of a major oversight measure, even one fighting a losing battle, is recorded in the books.

Simultaneously, the Union launches the Societal Resilience Acceleration Programme, a large-scale effort to cushion AI-driven disruptions. Fast-tracked wage support, expanded cyber-response funding, and bio-detection R&D are approved and begin initial deployment. Member states respond unevenly, and bureaucratic inertia delays full rollout. Germany and the Nordic countries show early commitment, but disbursements and implementation will take time. The programme’s focus on people over systems resonates modestly, slightly lifting public sentiment despite ongoing anger over past failures.

Poland’s legal challenge to sovereignty initiatives continues to strain cohesion, and local resistance in Marseille and Wrocław slows implementation. The shift in tone—from control to care—begins to register, but institutional fatigue remains deep.

### Capital’s Narrow Reprieve — In Theory

Political capital, long in freefall, was expected to stabilise with the completion of the Integrity Audit Expansion and the launch of the new resilience programme. The proposal bonus of +6 was anticipated for addressing recent systemic failures with targeted measures. However, the heavy portfolio charge of −10—carried over from unresolved burdens across the Sovereign AI Core Programme, Openweight Containment Initiative, and other stalled efforts—outweighs these gains. With no legitimacy lends or other offsets, the net effect on political capital is −4. The symbolic shift toward societal protection provides narrative cover, but does not alter the arithmetic: political capital falls to 0, reflecting the EU’s eroded capacity to convert completed actions into usable credibility. The institutions remain at the edge of functional overload, sovereignty unchanged, access to frontier AI still rationed under the US **CONSOLIDATION** posture.
