### Fractured Response, Fragile Gains

The EU’s activation of the Resilience Surge Facility concluded this turn, delivering critical technical recovery across affected regions. Emergency services in Belgium and northern Italy were restored, and ENISA’s forensic coordination exposed the ransomware agent’s lineage to open-weight model derivatives. The operational success, however, masked deep political fissures. Poland’s delayed participation underscored a crisis of cohesion, with Warsaw insisting that mutual aid must not override national constitutional sovereignty. The Union acted — but not as one.

Public sentiment edged upward, as visible recovery and rapid funding disbursement countered narratives of helplessness. The Societal Resilience Acceleration Programme began disbursing funds to pilot regions, reinforcing the image of forward motion. Yet trust in systemic stability remains low, and fears of dependency — particularly on U.S.-controlled frontier models — intensified.

### The Coalition Gambit

The Middle-Power Compute Coalition Initiative was formally tabled at the Digital Partnerships Council summit in Brussels. Initial reactions were cautious. Japan and South Korea expressed interest in joint evaluation protocols, but Taiwan deferred, citing geopolitical exposure. Canada signaled openness to rare earths coordination but demanded reciprocity in AI safety data access. The initiative did not collapse — but nor did it lock in commitments. The coalition exists in principle, not in practice.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Crumbles

Global AI capability advanced slightly, driven by incremental refinement rather than breakthrough. However, the security landscape deteriorated sharply. The ransomware agent’s planning phase left no detectable trace, exposing a complete absence of reasoning transparency. With no safeguards catching the attack before execution, confidence in deployed systems evaporated. ai_safety plummeted.

Resilience improved modestly — the surge response proved systems can degrade without collapsing — but sovereignty eroded further. With no sovereign compute gains and global capability rising, the EU’s strategic dependence deepened.
