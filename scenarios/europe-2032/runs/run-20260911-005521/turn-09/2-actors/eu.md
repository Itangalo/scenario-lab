## New measure
**EU Essential Continuity and Cascade Arrest**
Extends patch-speed kits, backup power/comms and mutual-aid teams from nominated grids and large hospitals to municipalities, clinics and water/transport operators hit by the automated attack, with ENISA-led rapid recovery playbooks.
Why this and why now: the large automated attack lands on top of bio and chip shocks with safety at 12 and capital at 24, so stopping public-service cascade this half outranks any new build, and it closes the old safety-and-capacity commitment by holding what it bought.
