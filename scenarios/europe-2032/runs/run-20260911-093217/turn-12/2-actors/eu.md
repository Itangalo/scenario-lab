## Priority
EU Essential Services Cyber Triage and Care Continuity Corps — because holding triage, lights and municipal services through the incident is the two-year commitment in this turn, and nothing else is in flight to prioritize.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the Emergency Response Coordination Centre to stand up rotating cyber-triage teams seconded to the three hospital systems hit by the withdrawal and to the municipalities hit by the ransomware sweep, paid from rescoped Digital Europe and EU4Health emergency lines with Council EPSCO/Health sign-off. Health ministries in Denmark, Estonia and Spain second their local-model assistant operators to keep waiting-room gains visible; elsewhere DG DIGIT authorises paper-to-local fallback and reimburses direct off-list holder payments via police.

We do not promise new compute or restored US access. Under EU-CyCLONe we re-issue location-free manuals, guard and disperse the two hit depots, and run weekly black-start drills, telling mayors and publics plainly this is continuity by hand until capability and trust recover.
