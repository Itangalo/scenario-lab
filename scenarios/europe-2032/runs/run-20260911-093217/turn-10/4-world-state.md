### Holding by hand
The spring began with money leaving. Valuations in AI reset hard in February and March, cranes stopped over half-built data-centre shells in the US and the Gulf, and two cloud deals European planners had counted on for overflow compute quietly evaporated. Washington answered scarcity with rationing: volume quotas for allied buyers on advanced chips and model access, servicing licences case by case.

For Brussels, already fenced in and metered, the quota letter read as a second cut. The Hague complied under protest again. No new procurement was possible, and none was promised.

What remained to defend was what could run without new kit. The black-start and manual-fallback network — segmented grids, breaker logs, fuel and transformer pools — became the priority in name, and the target in fact. In April and May, coordinated groups broke into generator yards, transformer stores and telemetry cabinets in four member states, leaving slogans treating backup stocks as collaboration with the machine system. Police and gendarmerie, coordinated through the EU crisis network and civil protection channels, moved to bring off-books pools on-scheme, disperse them, guard them and reimburse holders.

It worked unevenly. Where pools were registered and guarded, restoration drills held. Where lists leaked, sites were hit twice. Grid operators stayed on manual operation; hospitals kept postponed schedules. The containment and mutual-aid protocol agreed two years earlier finally paid out — isolation was faster, cross-border spares moved on reimbursement — but restoration still crawled, and every guarded warehouse was photographed as proof the state protected machines, not people.

The one counter-story came from clinics and town halls. In Denmark, Estonia and parts of Spain, triage and permitting assistants cut waiting lists and decision times from months to days. Health ministries co-branded the deployments as a European choice, kept clinics on a European default, and paid the teams. Trust ticked up from open hostility to wary attention, but did not carry. Vandalism continued, and the Commission ended June with almost no standing left to spend.
