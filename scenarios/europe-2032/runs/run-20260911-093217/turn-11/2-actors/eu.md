## In practice
We treat the frontier cut-off as the contingency we planned for. Under the Continuity Stack and the Black-Start Network, now finished, hospitals, ministries and firms that built on the withdrawn US model are switched to segmented manual procedures and the EU-default assistants where they exist — DG DIGIT and health ministries keep the Danish, Estonian and Spanish triage/permitting deployments running and co-brand them as the European choice, because they are the only visible proof that something still works.

We answer the leak of drill manuals and fuel-pool locations through the Civil Protection Mechanism and EU-CyCLONe: relocate leaked sites, re-issue manuals without locations, move reimbursement off published lists to direct holder payments via national police/gendarmerie, and keep grid operators on manual isolation. We promise no new procurement Washington has now denied, and spend nothing we do not have.
