## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for political capital and public tolerance to recover above the level where any new instrument can be started without immediate collapse.

With capital at zero, sentiment at zero, sovereignty at 12 and a major automated attack plus a loss-of-control incident landing on degraded services, blockaded siting and rationed foreign access, proposing a new directive, fund or build-out now would fail on contact and further fragment cohesion.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority named this turn, because with no measures in flight and no capital to start one, there is nothing to push hardest without pretending capacity we do not have.

## In practice
We hold what has already finished landing. The Shield, the Continuity and Islanding kits, and the Recovery Corps procedures stay in operation under ENISA, CERT-EU, EU-CyCLONe and Civil Protection on existing mandates: island water and emergency wards, run daily isolation notes, keep pre-paid patches flowing, and keep the single cyber/energy claims window open even if payouts remain fractional.

We do not contest the siting blockades by force, do not promise new compute, and do not issue new rationing orders. Mayors, TSOs and hospital operators get honesty about attribution delays and therapy queues, and technicians are left to use local open models for triage because recall is impossible. Survival through the ransomware sweep and the agentic incident without institutional breakage is the whole of Union action until capital rebuilds.
