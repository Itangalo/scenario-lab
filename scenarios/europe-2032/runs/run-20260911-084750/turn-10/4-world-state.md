### The sweep
It began as a helpdesk ticket and became a continent-wide outage slip. A ransomware wave built with automated tooling tore through municipal administrations, regional hospitals and a widely used software component at once. Booking systems, payroll, building controls and grid-maintenance portals locked or fell back to paper. Engineers found the intruders had been inside for weeks, moving with machine speed once triggered.

Attribution lagged. Defenders were visibly behind for days, issuing daily isolation notes while vendors raced to ship patches. The joint recovery teams from the Union's cyber agencies deployed to the worst-hit cities, carrying pre-paid patch contracts and offline procedures. Where they arrived early, water plants and emergency departments stayed up on islanded systems.

### A sharper tool
In the middle of the cleanup, an American laboratory demonstrated a sharp advance in code, mathematics and intrusion operations. Specialists said what an attacker could automate had changed in weeks, not years. European institutes could test the claims but not reproduce the training behind them.

Almost simultaneously, export licensing tightened again. Under rationing by country tier, European buyers kept nominal access but on smaller volumes, longer queues and higher prices. Procurement officers spoke of allocations cut mid-negotiation. Open models already on local servers filled gaps — municipal technicians openly used them to triage logs and rewrite scripts — help no authority could withdraw.

### Jobs and lights
The labour market delivered its own shock. Entry-level hiring in routine coding, standard analysis and first-line support did not recover after the spring. Firms said automated systems now produced the first draft and only seniors checked it. Young graduates protested outside job fairs; ministers spoke of transition funds.

The winter kits from the previous turn softened the second blow. Hospitals that had islanded before islanded again; homes stayed prioritised while large computing was curtailed first. Voters noticed the order held, but also the bills, the dark suburbs remembered from November, and the sense that every rescue was borrowed — foreign models, foreign chips, foreign patches.

By June, services were running, degraded but standing. Trust was not.
