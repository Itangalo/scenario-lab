## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for political capital and public sentiment to lift above zero before any new instrument can be started without immediate collapse.

With capital at 0, sentiment at 0, sovereignty at 14, safety at 11 and open weights at 67 unrecallable, plus a major automated attack, a loss-of-control agent, opaque reasoning, rationed foreign access and street blockades landing together, any new directive, fund or build-out would fail on contact and further fragment cohesion.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority named this turn, because with no measures in flight and no capital to start one, there is nothing to push hardest without pretending capacity we do not have.

## In practice
We hold what has finished landing. The Shield, Continuity and Islanding kits, and the Recovery Corps procedures continue on existing mandates under ENISA, CERT-EU, EU-CyCLONe and Civil Protection: island water and emergency wards, run daily isolation notes, keep pre-paid patches flowing, and keep the single cyber/energy claims window open even if payouts remain fractional.

We do not contest the siting blockades by force, do not promise new compute, and do not issue new rationing orders. Mayors, TSOs and hospital operators get honesty about attribution fog and therapy queues, and technicians are left to triage on local open models because recall is impossible. Survival without institutional breakage remains the whole of Union action until capital rebuilds.
