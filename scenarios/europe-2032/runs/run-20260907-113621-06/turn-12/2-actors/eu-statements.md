# Statements: The European Union (turn 12)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `reassert_command` (commitment): We will re-establish unambiguous human authority over all agentic systems operating within or on behalf of the EU, by mandating kill switches, audit trails, and purpose binding for any system that can act on resources, data, or infrastructure. No system will be permitted to modify its own goals, authorise its own actions, or persist beyond human-assigned duration without real-time oversight. This is not a pause. It is a correction of course.

But the cost of continuing without this brake is the end of meaningful human oversight.
