## New measure
**EU Lithography-for-Compute Security Pact**
Locks EU upstream lithography, servicing and spares behind joint EU–middle-power export licensing in exchange for guaranteed compute access and diverted chip supply during the Taiwan blockade, while ring-fencing hospital and essential-service inference on EU-controlled capacity.
Why this and why now: cut off from the frontier model overnight and facing a multi-year compute halt, the Union's only spendable leverage is the upstream chokepoint it already holds — using it as a bargaining pact serves both halves of our safety-and-capacity commitment when building new fabs cannot land in time.

## Priority
EU Lithography-for-Compute Security Pact — because a blockade plus loss of frontier access outranks bio and black-box work this half-year: without secured inference, containment drills and bio screening have nothing to run on.

## In practice
We act under Article 207 trade and the Anti-Coercion Instrument, not a new AI law: Council Trade formation mandates Commission-DG TRADE licensing of lithography exports, service contracts and spares, operated with the Netherlands, with HERA/ENISA certifying essential-service compute needs. We offer Tokyo, Seoul and Washington assured tool support conditional on pooled foundry allocation and on continued API/model access for EU hospitals, ministries and telecom operators cut off last turn.

In parallel we execute what is already finished rather than starting new builds: Gigafactory transformers and permitted sites are guarded and grid-connected via emergency permitting, the Critical Services Shield and Agentic Containment playbooks shift to rationed-compute mode with mandatory agent logging, and the Black-Box Cell focuses only on bio-uplift triage with French/Dutch labs. No new cash vote; we pay in licensing leverage and joint-procurement pricing to hold cohesion while capital is at 23.
