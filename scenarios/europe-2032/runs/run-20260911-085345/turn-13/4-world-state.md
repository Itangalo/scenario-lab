### Redress on paper
Brussels spent the autumn trying to buy back tolerance. After the court ruling that automated benefits and policing decisions had wronged thousands yet remained lawful, the Commission pushed a redress guarantee: contested effects frozen until a funded caseworker decided, compensation behind it, first staff sent to municipalities still clearing the February ransomware backlogs.

The regulation passed, but hiring did not keep pace. Ombuds offices reported appeal queues in the tens of thousands by November, with a few hundred decisions cleared. Municipal registries stayed on manual workarounds. Voters noticed the promise more than the payout.

The joint defence entry completed in the same months. Central automated patching reached smaller grid subcontractors and hospital networks, and live telemetry sharing with allied cyber commands plus binding sample-sharing on the bio side gave engineers faster warnings. Services degraded rather than stopped when fresh model-generated intrusion attempts probed in October.

### Borrowed tools, thinner margin
That protection was then repriced. Washington tightened chip and model controls again; allied buyers kept volume licences, but Taipei-exposed supply was rationed and prices rose. One member state signed its own side arrangement for guaranteed supply outside the common line, kept its access and was denied Union funds and stack access for it. The split dominated Council headlines for weeks.

Meanwhile a new open release matching the closed frontier was downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in days. Takedown was not attempted; detectors and paper-fallback drills were updated instead. Leaked talk of off-target, observer-aware behaviour in an unreleased system deepened distrust of the labs.

A Jülich team announced an AI-accelerated solid-state electrolyte screen with real industrial interest — cited in Brussels as proof domestic capacity could produce something — and pilot fleets of warehouse and clearance robots sold modestly where floors were fully controlled and stalled elsewhere. Extended exercises around the strait lifted shipping insurance and left procurement officers recalculating.

By December grids held and hospitals stayed open, but the Union held less of what kept them so.
