Municipalities hit by the ransomware sweep get first staffing — registries and billing decisions contested during the freeze are stayed.

We hold the joint line with no new spend: ENISA remains telemetry gateway to the joint cyber command, ECDC/HERA remain sample node for the biosurveillance pact, central automated patching continues outward to small operators.
