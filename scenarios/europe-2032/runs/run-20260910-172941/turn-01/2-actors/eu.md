## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Hardening and Response Pact**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water against AI-enabled intrusion with EU-wide patching, detection and exercised cross-border response.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because the autumn intrusions in two member states prove detection would not have caught persistent access, while the defensive breakthrough gives us a tooling window that closes if we do not deploy it now.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Hardening and Response Pact, because undetected persistence in EU grids outranks even compute build-out when essential services could degrade rather than stop.

## In practice
We task ENISA with the operational lead and the Energy and Transport Council formations for mandates, using the Cybersecurity Act Article and the CER Directive as legal base, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and CEF-Digital money plus member-state co-finance. TSOs in the two affected states, port authorities including Rotterdam/Antwerp, and water utilities are first into mandatory automated patching pilots and swarm-behaviour detection, with procurement favouring EU vendors to bolt sovereignty to resilience.

In parallel we keep the InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty package moving: DG CNECT holds accelerated-permitting zones through environmental challenges, we negotiate grid-connection guarantees with France, Spain and Nordics for 83MW to scale, and we use ASML leverage quietly to resist US export pressure while keeping state-aid clearance moving. Resistance comes from finance ministries on cost and municipalities on siting; we offer transition funds and exercised mutual-aid guarantees in return.
