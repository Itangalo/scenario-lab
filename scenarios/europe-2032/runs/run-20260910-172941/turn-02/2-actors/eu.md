**EU Biological Resilience Shield**

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Hardening and Response Pact, because undetected persistence in power, ports and water remains the immediate cascade risk and it must reach exercised response before funds and attention shift to bio.

## In practice
We keep ENISA as operational lead for M3, pushing the Energy and Transport Councils to lock cost-sharing: Digital Europe and CEF-Digital reprogramming for automated patching and swarm detection in Rotterdam, Antwerp and the two breached TSOs, with EU-vendor procurement as the concession to sovereignty-minded states and mutual-aid guarantees to mayors. On the scandal, we direct enforcement through the AI Act frame as a high-risk-system failure — Commission requests AI Office investigation with the Fundamental Rights Agency and EDPS, national market-surveillance authorities suspend the implicated social-insurance/policing module, and we publicly back ombudsman redress to show the Act bites.

In parallel we launch the Bio Shield via DG HERA and ECDC with Health Council mandate: mandatory know-your-customer screening for synthesis providers under the Dual-Use Regulation base, sequencing-based wastewater and clinical detection pilots in five hub cities, and stockpile and medical-countermeasure readiness. DG RTD funds contested red-team replication of the genome-model claim to settle methodology without publishing method, and we coordinate export-control alignment quietly to avoid amplifying the recipe debate. Gigafactories permitting continues through DG CNECT accelerated zones with grid-connection deals, but it is explicitly second in line for capital this turn.
