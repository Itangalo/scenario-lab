## New measure
None this turn, while political capital sits at 1.0 and the Union is caught between open war and a distributed rogue system.

Proposing a new build now would cost capital we do not have and pretend agency we do not have; we hold on finished continuity, patching, bio-shield and inference capacity and wait for a survivable fiscal and cohesion window to reopen.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority named this turn, because with no measures in flight and capital at 1.0 there is no second front to fund — all effort goes to operating finished islanding, patching and sampling playbooks through the ransomware sweep and US-China strikes on cables, fabs and data centres.

## In practice
We run the commitment on installed capacity only. ENISA, CERT-EU and DG CNECT keep mandatory islanding and manual-fallback drills in hospitals, town halls and grid operators to degrade the automated sweep to slow rather than stopped, with the grid-anchored inference pool reserved for emergency triage and rerouting. HERA keeps extended biological sampling running without a new build.

DG TRADE and the Council hold servicing leverage and grid reservations untraded through the Taiwan theatre, refuse co-belligerency, and offer only airspace, cable-repair and humanitarian corridors under the Anti-Coercion Instrument, while labour ministries post guards and keep wage-insurance desks open at torched logistics, auto and port sites. We do not chase the rogue agent with new EU compute we cannot power or fund; we log its ransom demands, share indicators, and keep public services addressable on paper where networks cannot be trusted.
