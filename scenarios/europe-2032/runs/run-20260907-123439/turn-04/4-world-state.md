### A Surge in Resilience, Amid Rising Fears

The EU completes the **Sovereign Compute Implementation Acceleration**, activating physical compute enclaves in Finland and Romania, with France nearing integration. This delivers a +5 boost to **eu_ai_sovereignty** under rule 5, offset by −1 due to **ai_capability** rising 2.0 this turn. No external access event occurred, so sovereignty ends at 25.0 — a modest gain, but the first real step toward independent capacity.

Meanwhile, **ai_capability** climbs to 59.0, driven by algorithmic gains and the **verification_widens** event — automated checks now validate clinical coding and structural engineering outputs, accelerating deployment in those domains. **openweight_capability** rises to 46.0, reflecting incremental adaptation of existing exploit frameworks into more modular forms, aided by limited leakage of optimization techniques from frontier systems. However, without broad algorithmic breakthroughs or coordinated open development, the gain is contained, preserving the invariant that open-weight progress does not outpace frontier progress disproportionately. The gap between the two remains significant, and no surge in uncontrolled proliferation is observed.

The **cyber_major_incident** — a wave of AI-generated ransomware crippling regional health and energy systems — lands with severe disruption. In response, the EU proposes the **Critical Infrastructure Resilience Sprint**, a large category 6 measure, earning a +3 proposal bonus for directly addressing the crisis. However, Germany and Spain resist central mandates, and implementation cannot begin in full until governance alignment is achieved. As a result, only preliminary coordination and threat assessment occur this turn, contributing +1 to **resilience**, bringing it to 42.0 — a cautious step forward, not a leap.

**ai_safety** drops to 22.0 as no safeguards land on frontier systems, and opaque coordination in attacks confirms developers are losing visibility. The **campaign_backlash** swells public anxiety, with moratoriums on data centres gaining traction — a visible **labour displacement wave** and **dependency humiliation** after the cyberattack erode trust. **public_sentiment** falls to 32.0.

The portfolio charge is revised: only **Sovereign Compute Implementation Acceleration** (−3) and the standing **Tech sovereignty package** (−3) are active; the **Critical Infrastructure Resilience Sprint** is newly proposed but not yet counted as an active measure until next turn, when implementation may begin pending agreement. With no priority charge carried over (priority expired last turn), total portfolio cost is −6. The +3 proposal bonus applies, and legitimacy lends nothing (sentiment below capital), leaving **eu_political_capital** at 39.0 — slightly improved from last turn due to focused action under pressure.
