No priority measure to name this turn, because with no instruments in flight and capital at 2.0 we are holding existing shields by forbearance rather than pushing a build.

## In practice
We keep the biosurveillance pooling and joint cyber telemetry running on existing HERA/ECDC and ENISA mandates, with Health and Home Affairs Councils extending legal cover by conclusions rather than new law. Sequencing reads, test-kit flows and hospital continuity teams stay tasked to flag and isolate the next modified-pathogen scare and to keep core care up through the automated ransomware sweep.

We do not start builds, do not replace the cut-off US frontier service, and commit no new money. Showcase sites stay on fallback helpers and paper protocols, water-power compacts stay in draft, and we absorb the capability jump by warning rather than rebuilding, until cohesion and capital allow a funded commitment again.
