### The Union in Freefall

The EU does not act — it watches. The Emergency Cross-Border Containment Protocol, already stillborn, is formally abandoned in practice. Germany suspends all liaison, Sweden refers the measure to its constitutional court, and France demands veto powers incompatible with EU law. No data flows, no teams deploy, no authority is exercised.

Instead, the European AI Shield Initiative — a coalition of France, Poland, and several Central European states — becomes operational. Funded through emergency defence budgets and bypassing Commission oversight, it deploys AI red teams, hardens critical infrastructure with military-grade encryption, and reroutes sensitive communications via satellite networks outside civilian reach. This is not coordination: it is a sovereign revolt, fracturing the single market and establishing a parallel security architecture.

Simultaneously, a major cyber incident strikes. A self-propagating AI-generated exploit infiltrates public health and energy systems across three member states. The attack is fast, adaptive, and evades all known defences. Response is fragmented — some states invoke national emergency protocols, others wait for a Union lead that never comes. Damage is contained only after days of disruption, but the perception of helplessness spreads faster than the patch.

The Commission convenes an emergency Council session under Article 42.7, framing the attack as a collective security threat. But without leverage, the meeting dissolves in recriminations. Paris and Warsaw offer technical cooperation — not subordination. The EU’s credibility as a central actor erodes further.

Public sentiment plummets. Media frames the Shield not as defiance, but as necessity. The Commission is seen as obsolete, its tools broken, its authority hollow. Engineers quietly relocate. Trust evaporates.

### The Cost of Inaction

With no measure in flight, political capital does not drain from portfolio costs — but the collapse of unity, the major incident, and the rise of a shadow structure rooted in internal fracture trigger severe penalties. Sovereignty erodes as access to unified capacity is lost. Resilience weakens under unmitigated strain. Ai_safety remains untouched — no safeguards exist. The world moves on, and the EU is no longer at the table.
