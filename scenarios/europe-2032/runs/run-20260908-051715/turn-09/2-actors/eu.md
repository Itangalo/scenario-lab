The goal is clear — once a model crosses capability 50.0, its weights are infrastructure, not speech, and infrastructure must be governed. Legal challenges persist, but we are building precedent through enforcement, not waiting for consensus.

Without U.S. coordination, we are drafting unilateral market access conditions requiring telemetry and rollback for models above 60.0, but these remain declaratory without enforcement capacity. We are holding the line, but we are out of room to move.
