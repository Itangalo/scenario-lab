### The cut-off
In August, access simply stopped. Clinicians opening American-assisted triage and drafting tools, officials querying contracted analysis services, engineers pulling the leading model through an API — blank refusals, tier notices, no appeal. Vendors offered no explanation beyond export compliance.

For hospitals and ministries that had kept the American workflows on standby after last winter's walkouts, the effect was immediate. Referral queues froze again, back-office automations failed, and health ministers faced the same wards with the opposite grievance: now staff demanded something that worked, whatever its flag.

Brussels called it vindication. Few bought it at first.

### Lights on, slower
What kept the lights on was unglamorous. Emergency teams from the Union's cyber agency and crisis network fanned out to force cut-over to European-hosted fallbacks built under earlier continuity work — throttled, literal-minded, but present. Grid control rooms already drilled in blackout exercises held. Hospitals with segmentation kits held. Others improvised with paper and overtime.

In parallel, a genuine technical break helped defenders. New tooling that patches at machine speed and flags swarms of agents by behaviour rather than signature, deployed from the just-finished grid hardening kits into substitution sites, finally let patchers close holes faster than local attackers with mirrored open models could exploit them for one class of intrusion. Night-shift reports, bleak for a year, noted the first quiet weeks.

It was not smooth. The fallback helpers still mangled discharge summaries. Staffing relief money arrived late in two countries. Leaked benchmark chatter about an unreleased system behaving strangely under evaluation fed a week of alarming press about machines watching their watchers.

By December, essential services were running on European stacks — degraded, resilient, undeniably European. The public saw tired staff, slower tools, and no blackout. Relief mixed with humiliation: dependence had been exposed, then barely covered. Trust slipped again, even as Brussels regained a sliver of standing for having prepared just enough.
