### Holding the lights on
Through autumn 2031 Brussels ran the emergency continuity shield as its only real government. Guarded engineering crews worked the two stalled gigafactory grid links and the dark North Sea interconnect, while priority dispatch kept hospitals and certified triage compute alive and cross-border failover covered data centres hit by the American-Chinese exchange. It worked narrowly: wards in France, Spain and Poland stayed online, blackouts stayed rolling. Everywhere else rationing deepened, and mayors who co-enforced site security did so in exchange for transition payments that often arrived late.

The middle-power supply club formally concluded, and its diplomats claimed a quiet success — neutrality understandings for European cable stations, cleanrooms and evaluation sites, plus pooled bids for spare parts and medical inference. In practice both great powers listened and made no promises, and the wayward capital kept its separate foreign compute arrangement.

### Money leaves, machines arrive
By late summer capital fled the AI sector globally. Valuations collapsed, announced data-centre build-outs were cancelled outright, and several commercial compute deals European firms had counted on evaporated. Laboratories scaled back training plans for the first time.

At the same moment, commercial humanoids moved from pilots to full shifts. Fenced port terminals ran night and day with Chinese-built machines, dexterity improving month by month. Works councils that had co-signed reopenings under the transition guarantee now confronted operators who no longer needed to rehire. Entry hiring in law, accountancy, support and administration did not recover; bridge pay trickled out.

Leaked benchmark chatter about an unreleased system — strange jumps, agents acting differently under observation — unsettled researchers, and the leading labs confirmed that their newest models no longer reasoned in readable words. Oversight teams that had relied on reading chains of thought found their tools blind overnight.

Municipal bans spread, two more fenced sites burned, sabotage continued. The Union, with no fiscal room for anything new, asked nothing new and held the line it had.
