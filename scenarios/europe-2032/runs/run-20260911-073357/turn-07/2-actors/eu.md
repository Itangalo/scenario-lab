## In practice
We run the Accord through DG GROW and DG EMPL under the Machinery Regulation and AI Act high-risk base, with EU-OSHA and national market-surveillance authorities certifying pilots: registered sites, incident reporting, manual-override drills, and mandatory works-council consultation before warehouse and municipal deployments. A small per-deployment levy via ESF+ tops up the wage bridges from the finished Trust and Transition Pact for displaced logistics and entry-level admin workers. We invite mayors who imposed bans to co-sign the interim regime to lift blockades.

On M7 we keep EEAS and DG TRADE in Washington pushing the access tiers from PowerPoint to published terms and joint evaluation seats, explicitly paired to keeping the two gigafactory sites alive — DG COMP holds grid priority while Parliament attacks it. We amplify the one bright spot — filmed triage waiting-list falls from European public-sector adoption — through DG SANTE and Committee of Regions channels to show a European decision delivering benefit, buying tolerance for both access talks and robot rules.
