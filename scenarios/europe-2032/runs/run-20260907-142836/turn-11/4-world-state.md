### A Quiet Turn, but Not a Still One

With political capital near paralysis, the EU consolidates rather than leaps. The **Municipal Resilience Backchannel Integration** formally concludes, anchoring Lyon, Barcelona, and Helsinki as certified Trusted Resilience Nodes. Bologna, Ljubljana, and Tallinn are integrated using existing disaster recovery envelopes, sidestepping new budgets and Council votes. The network now spans six cities, with interoperability standards in place and model-audit protocols drafted. It is a quiet win—incremental, decentralised, and politically survivable.

Meanwhile, the **Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation** expires in law, its failure acknowledged. No new EU-controlled frontier compute exists. Yet the strategic intent is not abandoned. The Commission pivots to antitrust leverage, launching a preliminary **Digital Markets Act review** targeting dominant AI providers. The focus: transparency on EU energy use and audit rights over spare capacity. DG COMP, ENISA, and ACER begin scoping protocols. This is not sovereignty by construction, but by coercion—visibility, not ownership. Legal challenges loom, and no mandates are issued yet.

### Capability Creeps Forward

Globally, **ai_capability** edges up to 62.0, entering the **62–68 terminal zone**, as frontier labs optimise existing architectures. Open-weight models benefit modestly: **openweight_capability** rises to 53.0, reflecting gradual diffusion of prior knowledge and improved efficiency in open frameworks. No major leaks or structural failures in closed systems are reported, and the gap between open and closed remains significant. The pace of open progress is steady but constrained by the slow frontier advance.

With no safety measure landing on shipped systems and capability advancing, **ai_safety** drops to 14.0. Two member states’ rejection of the Evaluation Shield weakens enforcement, and no central response emerges.

**Resilience** gains +3 from the completed backchannel integration (category 6, large), rising to 39.0. The network is not yet fully operational, but its structure is validated.

**EU AI sovereignty** falls to 28.0: −1 from capability growth (ai_capability rose ≥2), −2 from institutional fracture weakening enforcement, and no offsetting gains. The antitrust pivot offers no immediate capacity.

**Political capital** holds at 9.0: +3 from finishing the backchannel measure, −4 from portfolio charge (Municipal Resilience Backchannel Integration −3, priority −1). No new measure proposed, no bonus earned. The EU remains in paralysis.

Public sentiment holds at 35.0—no incident, no scandal, no visible benefit.
