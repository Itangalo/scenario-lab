### A Strategic Reckoning

The EU closes two major initiatives on schedule: the **Tech Sovereignty Package** unlocks €28bn in private AI infrastructure investment and streamlines permitting across six member states, while the **Critical Infrastructure Resilience Sprint** achieves 78% audit coverage among Tier 1 operators and sets red-teaming in motion. Both finish as planned, delivering tangible gains.

With the Taiwan blockade cutting off advanced semiconductor exports, the EU activates its leverage under the Chips Act, coordinating emergency production shifts with ASML, STMicroelectronics, and Infineon. This secures preferential access to next-generation tools, anchoring future compute expansion and marking a rare moment of strategic autonomy.

Building on this momentum, the EU launches the **Systemic Integrity Watch**, a small Category 1 measure hosted at ENISA. It integrates real-time telemetry from energy grids, health systems, and digital civic platforms to detect runaway AI coordination. Though designed for speed, bureaucratic friction delays full staffing by two months, slowing initial deployment.
