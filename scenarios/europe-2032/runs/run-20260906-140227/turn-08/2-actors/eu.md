## In practice  
This turn, we launch **Cyber Shield for Public Services**, a large-scale resilience measure to counter the growing threat of automated cyberattacks on essential public functions. The recent **cyber_major_incident**, involving a model-generated ransomware sweep across regional health and tax platforms in three member states, revealed a critical gap: detection is no longer the bottleneck — response speed is. While the Systemic Integrity Watch flagged anomalies in AI-driven lateral movement, affected agencies lacked pre-approved fallback protocols, clean-room recovery environments, or AI-audited patching tools. Too many were forced into days-long outages, eroding public trust and exposing systemic fragility.

We act through the NIS2 Directive and the Digital Europe Programme, allocating €1.2bn to establish regional Cyber Shield Hubs in 12 member states, each equipped with air-gapped backup systems, AI-powered log reconstruction tools, and standing authority to override local IT during declared incidents. The legal basis is Article 13(4) of NIS2, activated for cross-border crisis response, and we are negotiating with the Council’s COREPER II to fast-track implementation via comitology — bypassing full legislative review to meet the six-month urgency. Resistance is strongest in smaller states fearing loss of autonomy, so we offer full EU funding and joint governance: no hub operates without a national co-director and encrypted telemetry sharing. ENISA will coordinate red-teaming every quarter, simulating AI-driven attacks that exploit openweight models now at 51.5 — a capability that can already automate intrusion chaining. This is not about stopping every breach; it is about ensuring no breach becomes a collapse. The measure costs 3 political capital per turn, justified by the joint_threat_response that just arrived: we are invited into a new NATO-EU cyber command with real-time telemetry sharing, but only if we can demonstrate sovereign response readiness by mid-2031. This is the price of the seat — and we will pay it.
