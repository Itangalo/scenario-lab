### A Surge in Cyber Threats, a Shield in Response

A wave of AI-generated ransomware cripples regional health and tax platforms across three member states, overwhelming incident response teams despite early warnings from the Systemic Integrity Watch. The attack, built using tools derived from openweight models now at 51.5, automates lateral movement and evades signature-based defences. Recovery takes days, not hours, exposing a critical gap: detection works, but response does not.

In response, the EU launches **Cyber Shield for Public Services**, a large-scale resilience initiative. Twelve regional hubs will host air-gapped backups, AI-audited recovery scripts, and standing override authority during crises. Funded at €1.2bn and coordinated through ENISA, the programme leverages NIS2’s emergency powers and fast-tracks approval via comitology. Smaller states resist, fearing loss of control, but full EU funding and joint governance secure buy-in. The measure is named priority, reflecting urgency.
