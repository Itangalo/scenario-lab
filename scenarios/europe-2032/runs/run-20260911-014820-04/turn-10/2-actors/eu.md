## New measure
None this turn — waiting for the Entry-Level Corps to finish landing and for capital to recover before staking another instrument.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
M10 EU Entry-Level Transition and Essential Service Corps — because with graduate hiring collapsed, sabotage routine, and an automated-decision scandal breaking, visible EU-paid work in hospitals and municipalities is the only thing holding consent for everything else.

## In practice
We run what we have. DG EMPL pushes ESF+ clearances through EPSCO to get Corps placements from thousands toward tens of thousands, prioritising Lyon, Porto, Gdansk Dividend sites and guarded build sites where sabotage threatens chips still arriving late. NIS2 joint teams and ENISA keep hospitals and ports degraded-not-stopped, and harden permit and therapy assistants on EU-anchored capacity only.

On the scandal — a benefits-scoring system in two member states found to have systematically cut entitlements, with caseworker oversight at seconds-per-case and logs never read — we do not open a new instrument. The Commission frames it as breach of Annex III high-risk obligations, not a gap in the Act itself, orders conformity-body review and suspends the deployments pending human-review remediation, to avoid making restriction cheap and adoption impossible for years. On Taiwan surcharges and the US medical and research breakthroughs arriving on terms we cannot access, we keep TTC allied-access warm, invoke Anti-Coercion monitoring without escalation, and direct procurement to EU-anchored capacity where it exists.
