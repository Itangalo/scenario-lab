## Priority
EU Visible Benefit Adoption Drive, because without sentiment and capital recovery the Distressed Site Hold cannot be sustained and Turin hardens further — attribution now outranks blocking later.

## In practice
We task DG DIGIT and DG SANTE with a joint procurement and deployment cell, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health money, to replicate the three sites where waiting lists fell: standard triage assistants, permit pre-checks, and tutored catch-up in vocational schools. Delivery is via municipal contracts with EU resellers on Continuity Guard power, with clear EU signage and weekly published waiting-time figures to lock attribution.

Around it we hold M12: College maintains the FDI screening and DG COMP/ENER freeze on the enclave auction while ECFIN/EIB keeps the bridge offer open conditional on no exclusive grid carve-out. ENISA and TSOs keep hospital inference nodes lit for the audited cure queries, and we explicitly protect clinic power from any enclave reservation in Council messaging to link sovereignty to cures flowing.
