## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee**
A Category 7 Labour and social protection backstop that funds paid retraining, wage-insurance and public-sector transition posts for displaced graduates and admin/customer-ops workers, tied to automating employers and delivered through PES and municipal corps.
This and now because measurable graduate and white-collar displacement plus sentiment at 18 threatens cohesion and any further capacity build, while investment collapse makes private hiring freeze durable; as social absorption it carries the second leg of our two-year commitment forward when resilience alone cannot.

## Priority
EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee, and not the Embodied-AI Shield, because without stemming graduate hiring collapse and hostility at 18 no sovereignty or hardening measure remains enforceable.

## In practice
We run the Guarantee through EPSCO and the ESF+ / SURE precedent: DG EMPL channels repurposed cohesion and unspent RRF loans to public employment services for 6-month paid reskilling plus municipal, health-admin and cyber-hygiene transition posts, with co-funding levied on large automating employers via social-partner agreements. Member states with graduate unemployment spikes get first tranches; unions get hiring guarantees in exchange for flexibility on restructuring.

We hold M8 alive on procurement and certification only — no new money, IPCEI track via DG GROW/CNECT and CEN-CENELEC for EU-hosted inference — while UCPM Recovery Corps teams stay on water/port manual fallback with pre-stocked spares and paper playbooks. On Taiwan and export controls we take no retaliatory stance this turn, quietly diversify spares and use volume-licence access where Washington still offers it, and let Gigafactory sites sit at permitting/grid-queue stage until capital recovers.
