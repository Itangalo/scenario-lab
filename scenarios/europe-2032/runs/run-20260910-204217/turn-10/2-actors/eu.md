## Priority
EU Clinical Benefit Access Programme, because with no measures in flight and the Guarantee expired it is the only instrument that can lift sentiment and buy capital for any later shield work.

## In practice
We run this through DG SANTE and HERA with EPSCO backing: EU4Health and rescEU-adjacent procurement for diagnostics and personalised-treatment pathways, EMA accelerated clinical guidance, and EHDS-based data access so hospitals in Leuven, Lyon, Turin, Warsaw and Utrecht see first deliveries.

We negotiate access as a bloc — volume licensing and EU-hosted inference where possible, audited foreign-API use where not — and pair rollout with retraining slots for displaced graduates as care assistants and cyber-hygiene staff, converting protest cities into first-benefit sites without reviving grid-conditionality.
