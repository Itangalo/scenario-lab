## New measure
**EU Automation Transition Fund**
Creates an EU-coordinated wage-insurance, retraining and hiring-support fund targeted at entry-level white-collar displacement, co-financed by ESF+ repurposing and a levy-linked contribution from large deploying employers.
This is Labour and social protection because measurable non-replacement of graduate hiring in law, accountancy, software and customer operations directly threatens public sentiment and political capital needed for the two-year commitment, and a Union transition instrument must be started now before anxiety hardens into hostility to all AI build-out.

## Priority
EU Automation Transition Fund, because the labour-displacement reports this turn outrank even the Shield and Compact — without visible income and retraining cover, resentment will block grid shutdowns, data-centre siting and any leverage negotiation.

## In practice
We launch the Fund via EPSCO Council with DG EMPL lead, repurposing uncommitted ESF+ and RRF loans for rapid wage-insurance pilots in the worst-hit graduate labour markets, paired with retraining vouchers delivered through national public employment services. Large deployers get contribution credits for retained retrained hires, to blunt employer resistance without opening a new treaty-level tax fight.

In parallel we keep the in-flight book alive without new money: ENISA pushes draft network codes toward delegated-act status while Energy Council brokers winter-after shutdown scheduling, and DG TRADE runs the joint licensing desk as the single servicing-decision venue to hold The Hague from further bilateral concessions. Gigafactory ground works continue tied to DSO cooperation, but no new disbursement is forced this winter.
