## New measure
**European Embodied-AI Industrial Shield**
Builds an EU-anchored robotics stack — pooled procurement for EU manufacturers, secured component supply, and EU-hosted control models with safety certification — to keep deployment from becoming pure dependence on Chinese hardware and American models.
This and now because embodied AI has reached commercial deployment on top of a capability jump and automated attacks, directly threatening the industrial base we still lead in while sovereignty sits at 15 and sentiment at 23; as Sovereignty and industrial capacity it carries the rebuild-capacity leg of our commitment forward without opening a parallel compute programme alongside the Gigafactories.

## Priority
European Embodied-AI Industrial Shield, because the robot deployment plus Taiwan precursor threatens irreversible loss of our industrial core while M1 gigafactories remain paper — if the factory floor flips now there will be no sovereign demand left to compute for.

## In practice
We push the Shield through DG GROW and DG CNECT under the Net-Zero Industry Act and Chips Act precedents: an IPCEI for robotics and actuators, coordinated procurement by automotive, logistics and hospital groups, and a certification track via EU standardization bodies requiring EU-hosted control-model inference for critical-site robots. Funding repurposes unspent InvestAI and Innovation Fund envelopes, not fresh fiscal asks, to spare political capital at 19.

In parallel we hold M1 alive on permitting only — Competitiveness Council keeps grid-connection queues moving for the four to five sites, using the US moratorium window to quietly court talent — while the Recovery Corps teams from last turn stay deployed via UCPM to restore municipal, water and port systems hit in the automated wave. No new export-control fight is opened while Taiwan insurance spikes; we pre-stock spares instead.
