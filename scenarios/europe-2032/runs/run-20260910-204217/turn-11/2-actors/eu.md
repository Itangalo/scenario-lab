## New measure
**EU Care-Inference Continuity Guard**
Keeps the thin clinical rollout alive by hardening power, grid connection and cyber-monitoring for the hospital inference nodes and cross-border remote-access links the cures depend on.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, because with export controls tightening and substation interference rumours spreading, the immediate risk is that first-benefit sites go dark — and with sentiment at 11 and capital at 8 we cannot afford to lose the one visible benefit we have.

## Priority
EU Clinical Benefit Access Programme, because it is the only instrument that can lift sentiment from 11 and buy any capital back — the Guard only matters if clinics are still delivering.

## In practice
We keep M10 as the front line: DG SANTE/HERA bloc procurement, EMA compassionate-use guidance, and EHDS-routed data flows, with per-query licences renegotiated as a Union rather than hospital-by-hospital, and first deliveries ring-fenced to Leuven, Lyon, Turin, Warsaw and Utrecht to show the bridge held.

Around it we stand up the Guard through the Critical Systems Shield operators and ENISA: priority grid-connection for hospital inference nodes, physical security and monitoring for feeding substations, backup routing for audited foreign-API links, and municipal recovery corps shifts to keep primary-care diagnostics online. We ask interior and energy Councils for fast-track protection orders, funded from rescEU and existing shield maintenance, offering wary regional operators liability cover and repair funding to restart slowed works without reviving the old grid-conditionality fight.
