## New measure
**EU Fallback Stack for Essential Services**
Puts EU-hosted open-weight models into hospitals, ministries and grid operators as a working fallback so the next US switch-off does not switch Europe off, via procurement rules and funded deployment.
This is category 9 Diffusion, adoption and public trust, and it must come now because the cut-off proved dependence is already operational while the gigafactory compute will not land for years, and because the lab-disclosure scandal makes a US-frontier-only stack politically indefensible.

## Priority
EU Fallback Stack for Essential Services, because being cut off from the leading model this turn outranks even our longer compute build in serving our un-switch-off-able commitment.

## In practice
We move through Health and Digital Councils with a procurement implementing act under the AI Act and NIS2/CER critical-entity base: any hospital, TSO, port and ministry workflow built on a non-EU frontier model must have a qualified EU-hosted fallback, funded by reprogrammed EU4Health and Digital Europe deployment credits and operated by ENISA-vetted national hosting pairs.

In parallel we keep the inherited lines alive without starting a parallel compute programme: DG CNECT and EIB push Gigafactories site selection to final grid-connection offers to break the permitting stall, the Tech sovereignty package stays in Competitiveness Council for zones and IPCEI clearance, and the finished Cyber Shield hunt-team playbooks become the security baseline the fallback stack must meet before connection. French-German cyber staff secondments are extended to accredit the fallback deployments, with Eastern municipalities prioritised for funded integration teams.
