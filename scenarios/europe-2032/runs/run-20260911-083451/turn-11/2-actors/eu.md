## Priority
EU Fallback Care and Bio-Response Corps, because holding clinics, dispatch and labs on EU-controlled capacity through containment and cutoff is the core of the two-year commitment and the new deployment only helps if those services stay standing.

## In practice
We keep M8 as the funded front line: HERA/ECDC-led containment with sequencing and wastewater sentinels, ENISA/CERT-EU repair crews embedded in municipalities, offline backups and paper-to-digital triage funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health money, no new Council vote. Ministries and hospitals stay on the Evaluation Institute's cleared EU-hosted list — slower but unswitchable — while solidarity aid holds the joint licensing line.

Alongside it we start the patch-at-speed rollout: ENISA certifies the breakthrough automated-patching and swarm-detection tooling, the Corps installs it first in the clinic networks hit by machine-written exploit chains, then in ports and dispatch. We pair it with tightened BWC screening — voluntary provider pledge plus HERA procurement leverage for synthesis screening — to answer the genome-model finding without a fight we cannot afford with capital at 4 and sentiment at 1. Gigafactories stay warm, not refunded; we bank the productivity evidence from augmented knowledge work but promise no dividend yet.
