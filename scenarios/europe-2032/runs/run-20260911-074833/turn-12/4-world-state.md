### Patching At Machine Speed
Spring 2032 brought no pause in the sea and orbit war, but two usable tailwinds. Researchers in allied labs demonstrated defensive tooling that finally closed a whole class of intrusion — automated patching pushed as fast as vulnerabilities were found, and detectors that caught swarm behaviour rather than signatures. ENISA teams rushed it to hospitals, grid operators and municipal registries. For the first time in years, operators reported attacks that simply did not cascade.

Brussels tried to lock that window into a wider bargain. With American labs now under direct state control and bilateral hardware deals dead, the Commission turned to other middle powers holding lithography spares, reagents and maintenance capacity. Joint export-licence lists were aligned, compute access was bargained jointly, evaluation of the patching stack was shared. As a bloc that could withhold inputs, Europe regained a hearing in Washington and Beijing it had lost dealing alone.

The gain was partial. A second capital cut its own supply arrangement on terms that undercut the common line, defended at home as pragmatism. Brussels chose quiet containment — cohesion funds and procurement leverage in bilateral channels — over a public fight. The split still leaked, and evening talk shows asked what a coalition was worth if members shopped alone.

Lifelines otherwise held. Reserve engineers stayed on substations and landing points, registries ran on dispersed backups, triage ran locally through link jitter. The bio-detection and trusted-care network completed its rollout: pooled reagents, stretched licences and faster detection kept tailored cures flowing week to week, cutting false triage flags. Pharmacists still counted stocks in weeks and hiring stayed frozen.

Faint single-source reports spoke of a neutral-flagged repair flotilla restoring an Atlantic landing station under observation, easing cloud and medical links for days at a time. Nothing was confirmed, and jitter returned.

Voters credited shelter and remissions on the news, but doubted neutrality without domestic models, machines or medicines.
