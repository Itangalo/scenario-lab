## In practice
We run what is on the books as wartime lifelines. Under the Council and HERA/EU4Health, reserve teams stay on grid switchgear, hospital power and landing points, clean backups stay dispersed off US-China paths, and triage assistants stay on local EU-run servers. Transition Shield top-ups, now finished as a build but sustained as payments, and the Bio-Detection and Trusted Care Network carry the load: pooled reagents, licensed therapy batches stretched week to week, and cyber-hardening where we control the stack.

We do not pretend to regulate a nationalised US frontier from Brussels, nor to recall open weights at 83.5, nor to match embodied AI with our empty factories. We deny offensive use of Union infrastructure, keep non-belligerency credible, offer Washington state-to-state safety and supply talks instead of company conformity, and contain the defection bilaterally rather than spending our last capital on a public fight we would lose. The safety breakthrough, where it lands on deployed care and registry systems, we adopt immediately through the Evaluation Institute legacy.
