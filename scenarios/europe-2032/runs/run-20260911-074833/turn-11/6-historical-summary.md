Autumn: no new Brussels programme, focus on holding lifelines amid sea/orbit war — reserve engineers guarded grid/hospitals/landing points, registries stayed open on dispersed backups, triage ran locally through link jitter.

Frontier model leap abroad obsoleted timelines; new interpretability breakthrough patched into EU care/registry tools, cut false flags and calmed automation debate.

Washington placed leading labs under direct state control with clearances, bans, state-chosen customers — Brussels lost leverage over firms now answering to a government. Markets seized, valuations collapsed, two hardware deals Europe counted on evaporated.

Chinese-built, American-modelled robots arrived commercially, armed elsewhere, stung empty EU factories. A member state broke ranks with separate supply deal undercutting common line; Brussels chose quiet containment.

Pooled reagents/stretched licences kept tailored cures flowing week-to-week with remissions in news, but stocks still counted in weeks, hiring frozen. Voters credited shelter but doubted neutrality without domestic models, machines, medicines.
