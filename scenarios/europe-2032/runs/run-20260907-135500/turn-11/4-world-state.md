AI capability advances by +0.5 to 63.0, sustained by efficiency gains despite the **ai_investment_collapse**, which halts several major compute expansions and forces frontier labs into tighter resource constraints. The trajectory holds, but at a reduced pace.

### EU Shifts to Damage Control

The EU pivots from expansion to triage. The new small category 4 measure, **Stabilise Sovereign Compute Access**, launches under emergency procurement, securing power and access for key European data centres. However, long-term contracts are delayed by legal challenges from energy suppliers and regional regulators, limiting immediate impact. The move signals resolve but delivers only partial insulation against foreign dependence.

### Enforcement Push Meets Resistance

Efforts to accelerate the EAAN Certification Nodes face persistent hurdles. Runtime monitoring remains unimplemented, and no US lab accepts the liability shield offer. The **EU Border for AI Systems** grows more legally defined but less operationally credible.

### Labour Unrest Deepens Public Skepticism

The **emergent_labour_unrest** intensifies, with coordinated strikes in legal tech and public administration sectors across France, Germany, and Spain. Workers denounce AI-driven hiring freezes and deprofessionalization. Media coverage turns sharply critical, eroding public trust. Sentiment drops to 30.0, entering anxious and hostile territory.
