### Capability Creeps Onward

AI capability advances by +0.5 to 64.0, sustained by quiet gains in training efficiency and distributed inference coordination. The frontier does not leap—but it moves, widening the gap with stagnant safety and eroding resilience further.

### Certification Without Control

The Accelerate EAAN Certification Nodes measure finishes: secure hardware is operational in Munich and Sophia Antipolis. Yet without telemetry from member-state intelligence agencies, runtime monitoring remains blind. Worse, a growing technical dissent—framed as scientific caution—challenges whether current evaluation methods can detect agentic drift at all. Confidence in the EU Border for AI Systems erodes from within, even as its legal framework stands.

### Labour Support Lands, But Tensions Persist

The Labour Transition Emergency Facility begins disbursements in France, Germany, and Spain. Wage support and retraining slow strike momentum in municipal administration, and public sentiment rises slightly to 34.0. However, unions denounce the scheme as reactive, and early data show only partial uptake among displaced legal and technical staff.

### Power Struggles Over Compute

Stabilise Sovereign Compute Access, now the priority, sees mixed progress. Emergency PPAs lock in power for Helsinki and Amsterdam, but Lyon’s inclusion triggers fierce resistance from French grid operators citing civil preparedness. A last-minute invocation of Regulation (EU) 2019/941 forces compliance, though implementation delays push the finishing date to turn 15. The physical foundation of sovereignty holds—for now—but at political cost.

### Political Capital Erodes Further

The finished AI Safety Emergency Investment delivers a +3 bonus, and the new Labour Facility earns a modest +2 proposal bonus for responding to ongoing labour unrest. However, portfolio strain remains severe: three measures in flight (−3−2−2), priority (−1). Net change: −5 → political capital falls to 10.0.
