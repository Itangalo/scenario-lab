### Resilience Grows Through Partial Integration

The EU’s investment in joint cyber-biosurveillance integration shows progress. Coordinated response protocols helped contain the recent attack, limiting cascading failures across three member states. Real-time telemetry and shared analysis cells enabled faster patching and fallback activation, delivering a +2 boost to `resilience`, raising it to 54.0.

The Open Science Resilience Initiative advances but does not complete. The Toulouse, Heidelberg, and Uppsala hubs are now connected to national biosecurity agencies in pilot mode, and their auditable models are being tested in live monitoring scenarios. However, full operational integration is delayed by interoperability issues and staffing shortages. The initiative remains in flight, with effects growing toward completion but not yet realized.

### A Fragile Political Calculus

The EU launches the Digital Europe Reinforcement, a large category 9 measure aimed at securing cohesion around the Auditable Core Programme. However, resistance in ECOFIN stalls the €900 million reallocation. Southern and eastern states demand more control over implementation, calling the proposal “Brussels-imposed digital colonialism.” The Council defers the decision, leaving funding unresolved.

Still, the measure’s proposal earns a +4 bonus under metric rule 6, as it directly answers the recent `cyber_major_incident` and `ai_investment_collapse`. This, combined with the +5 from completing one large measure and partial credit for Open Science nearing integration, offsets the ongoing portfolio charge of −7 (−3 for Auditable Core, −3 for Open Science, −1 for priority), resulting in a net gain of +2 to `eu_political_capital`, bringing it to 2.0.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` holds at 20.0: the rise in `ai_capability` triggers a −1 decay, but there is no capacity event and no category 4 completion. The Digital Europe Reinforcement is category 9, so no sovereignty bonus applies.

`openweight_capability` rises to 64.0, as last year’s models continue to leak into the open. Yet the gap with the closed frontier widens, underscoring structural dependency.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 27.0—no visible benefit has landed to shift the narrative, and fears of disruption persist.
