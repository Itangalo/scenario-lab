## Priority

**Openweight First Responder Initiative** — because the Auditable Core Programme is failing as a top-down mandate, but the same technology, when offered as crisis relief, may yet gain trust and uptake — turning sovereignty from an imposition into an evident advantage.

## In practice

This turn, we are pivoting from enforcement to demonstration. The Auditable Core Programme remains law in draft, but its credibility hinges on adoption, and adoption hinges on perceived value. With the Digital Europe Reinforcement dead in ECOFIN, we cannot subsidize compliance — but we can respond to failure with speed and visibility.

We are repurposing €420 million in unallocated Digital Europe Programme funds and redirecting technical teams from the stalled reinforcement effort toward emergency deployment. When a hospital system collapses under AI-driven fraud, when a regional grid fails due to compromised forecasting, we will arrive within 72 hours with pre-vetted, open-weight models from the Open Science Resilience Initiative — already integrated with EU telemetry, already auditable, already proven in Toulouse and Heidelberg.

This is not charity. It is proof of concept. We will operate under the Civil Protection Mechanism, invoking Article 5 of Decision 1313/2013/EU, to bypass national procurement delays. DG ECHO will coordinate, with ECDC and ENISA providing technical validation. The condition: the host state must publish performance data and allow independent review. If we succeed, we do not compel sovereignty — we make it desirable.
