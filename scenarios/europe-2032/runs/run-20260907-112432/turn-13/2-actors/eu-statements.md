## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but only when the cost of waiting exceeds the cost of error.
  - Grounds: The continued stagnation of `ai_safety` at 0.0 despite the `cyber_major_incident` reveals that commitment without enforcement is performative. We acted on uncertainty last turn, but the failure of the Digital Europe Reinforcement to secure funding shows that resolve unbacked by capacity or consensus carries no weight. We remain willing to act early, but no longer as if will alone suffices.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign technical agency through auditable AI
  - Grounds: The Auditable Core Programme has become a political liability rather than a lever of agency. Southern and eastern states see it as coercive, not enabling. The openweight frontier release has widened the capability gap in practice, not narrowed it. Sovereignty through auditability only works if the systems being audited are the ones actually in use — and they are not. We must shift from mandating sovereign systems to ensuring they are the most attractive option.
  - Reason: modified text is identical to the current text
