### The Frontier Leaps Ahead

Global AI capability advances by +2.0, reaching 98.0, as frontier systems deepen their lead in strategic reasoning and autonomous deployment. The opacity of their decision-making is now total—internal diagnostics resemble post-mortems. No safety frameworks have caught up; `ai_safety` remains at 0.0, a void where control should be.

### Open Release, Open Consequences

An unexpected open-weight release—allegedly a leaked training run from a Pacific Rim lab—pushes `openweight_capability` to 75.0. Overnight, highly capable models flood decentralized networks. While still behind the closed frontier, the jump narrows the gap dramatically. The EU’s auditable models are now outpaced by open alternatives it cannot govern.

### Crisis Response, Political Collapse

The EU launches the Openweight First Responder Initiative, repurposing funds and teams to deploy auditable AI in crisis-hit regions. The first mission—a hospital network in Bulgaria overwhelmed by AI-generated fraud—is executed in 68 hours. The model stabilizes triage systems and recovers encrypted logs. Initial reports are positive.

But the political cost is fatal. The measure is large and named priority, yet `eu_political_capital` is only 2.0. The portfolio charge—Auditable Core (−3), Openweight First Responder (−3), priority (−1)—totals −7. No proposal bonus offsets it: while the cyber incident was recent, the measure is diffusion-focused, not a direct corrective. The legitimacy lending term does not apply—`public_sentiment` is above capital, but the rule only adds when capital is below sentiment, not the reverse. The Union overextends.

ECOFIN’s rejection of the Digital Europe Reinforcement signals deeper fracture. With capital already spent, the new measure collapses into symbolic action. The Council questions the legality of invoking civil protection for AI deployment. Two member states refuse entry. `eu_political_capital` hits 0.

`resilience` rises to 57 as the Bulgarian deployment proves the model’s operational value. But `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 19—`ai_capability` rose by 2.0 (−1), no category 4 measure finished, and no capacity event occurred. The window for sovereign demonstration slams shut.

Public sentiment remains at 27. The public sees another AI rollout—this time during a crisis. Trust is not built; suspicion grows.
