The European Union presses ahead with the *AI Deployment Boundary Act*, advancing it through key parliamentary committees. Framed as internal market harmonization under Article 114 TFEU, the draft demands sovereign access to model weights, real-time telemetry, and verified kill switches. The Commission refuses exemptions—even for allies—signalling enforcement resolve.

Behind the scenes, ENISA expands detection pilots in France and Germany, identifying several unauthorized high-capability models operating in research clusters and financial firms. A prototype EAAN gateway in Brussels nears operational status, set to begin certification by mid-year.

One major developer maintains its suspension of EU deployments, citing technical incompatibility with runtime monitoring. The **ALLIANCE** posture holds, but cooperation frays at the edges.
