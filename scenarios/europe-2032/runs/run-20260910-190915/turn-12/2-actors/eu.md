We table the chokepoint hold under the Anti-Coercion Instrument and Dual-Use Regulation in Trade/Foreign Affairs Council: no further ASML service cut without Council authorisation, Commission as single negotiator with Washington, pooled DIGIT/HERA procurement of mature-node chips and spares for hospitals, energy and payments, and infringement warning to any separate hyperscaler or Beijing deal. We keep audited Gigafactory inference and the joint cyber-bio cell in degraded-hold through the automated ransomware sweep and loss-of-control containment, isolating hospital networks to paper fallback where re-infection recurs.
