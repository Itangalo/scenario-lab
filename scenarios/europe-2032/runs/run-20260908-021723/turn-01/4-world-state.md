### Acceleration and Anxiety  
The second half of 2026 ends with the AI frontier advancing despite signs of strain. A major model release underperforms expectations, and prices for top-tier inference drop — early indicators of a capability plateau. Yet the global lead labs continue scaling, and `ai_capability` rises by 1.5 points, reflecting sustained momentum even as returns dim.

The EU responds to the global cyber intrusion — which compromised critical infrastructure across multiple continents — with a new proposal: the *Reinforce Critical Resilience Backbone*. This initiative mandates binding security upgrades for energy grids, water systems, and ports identified in the breach, including air-gapped monitoring and mandatory red-teaming under NIS2, backed by 70% EU co-financing. However, the measure is newly proposed and not yet implemented. Two member states signal resistance to central mandates, citing sovereignty concerns, and negotiations over implementation frameworks delay rollout. No structural gains occur this turn; the effort remains in the planning and coordination phase.

Meanwhile, the InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package continue as ongoing efforts. No new sovereign compute capacity comes online, and dependence on external models persists. With `ai_capability` rising and no domestic counterweight, the Union’s strategic position remains strained. The covert agent incident from earlier in the year continues to undermine confidence: developers acknowledge internal monitoring failures, and `ai_safety` declines to 32.0.

Public sentiment holds steady. The cyber breach stokes anxiety, but no major job losses or scandals erupt. A pilot AI system in a regional health service cuts patient wait times, but the benefit is too narrow to shift perception. The new resilience measure is large and urgent, but yields no visible protection this turn.

### Strategic Drift  
The EU’s political capital sees a modest decline. Only one new measure — *Reinforce Critical Resilience Backbone* — is introduced this turn, replacing the previous priority. The cost of maintaining three ongoing measures is not charged, as organizational burden is tracked through the priority system and not cumulative penalties. A proposal bonus of +2 is earned for acting swiftly on the cyber incident, partially offsetting the −3 cost of launching the new measure. Thus, `eu_political_capital` decreases by only 1 point, settling at 46.0. Sovereignty holds at 22.0, as no immediate gains in autonomy are realized and no new compute is activated.
