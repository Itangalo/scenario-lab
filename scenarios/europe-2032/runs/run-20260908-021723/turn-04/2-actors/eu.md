## In practice  
We are treating the ASML negotiation as a race against demonstrated coercion. The Article 346 TFEU legal instrument is now before the European Council, where we are trading access guarantees to legacy lithography tools for votes on the export control framework. The €15bn equity package is structured to give the EU a blocking minority, not control — a compromise designed to satisfy Dutch concerns about sovereignty while denying Washington unilateral leverage. We are quietly informing Washington that if this fails, the Union will activate full semiconductor autonomy planning under crisis procedure, including nationalisation triggers and emergency energy allocation, which we frame not as retaliation but as consequence management.

Simultaneously, we are launching the middle-power coalition through a ministerial-level dialogue hosted in The Hague, co-chaired with Tokyo and Seoul. The first deliverable is a joint statement on "strategic interdependence" in chipmaking, followed by harmonised export licence templates and a shared red-team exercise on AI-optimised supply chain attacks. We are offering Taiwan reciprocal access to EU evaluation labs in exchange for early warning on model leakage, and proposing a rotating chair to avoid the appearance of EU dominance. This is not a bloc against anyone, but a firewall against unilateralism — and it only works if the EU is seen as a credible node, not a follower.

On resilience, we are declaring the Reinforce Critical Resilience Backbone initiative complete, with Tier 1 designations finalised and monitoring specs adopted in two of the three pilot grids. The EIB is releasing €4.2bn in co-financing, tied to incident reporting compliance, and we are preparing a NIS3 proposal to expand the model to healthcare and transport in Q3. The Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package remain on track, though we are preparing contingency plans for permitting override if the legal challenges persist into turn 5. We are not accelerating compute investment yet — not without clearer signs that the capability plateau is real — but we are ensuring the pipeline does not freeze.
