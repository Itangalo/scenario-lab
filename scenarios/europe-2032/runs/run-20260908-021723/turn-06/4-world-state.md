### A New Front in the Open

Despite the global AI investment slump, European strategy pivots from infrastructure dependency to technical leadership. The collapse of several major private compute ventures—triggered by overleveraged expansion and evaporating venture appetite—slows the closed frontier, but only temporarily. Global capability still advances, reaching 59.0, as core research continues in constrained environments.

The EU seizes an unexpected opening. The emergent success of a pan-European open-source research alliance, now adopted in North American labs, validates a new model: public, verifiable safety tools as leverage. Building on this, the EU launches the *European Open Research Assurance Network*, a €18bn initiative hosted under EuroHPC and anchored at CERN, EMBL, and INRIA. It institutionalises open evaluation for agent behaviour, bio-risk, and interpretability, mandating adoption for any lab accessing EU data or compute.

The move lands with quiet force. While the U.S. stops short of full adoption, its National AI Research Resource agrees to integrate the tools for transatlantic data access—a diplomatic win that turns openness into influence. Public sentiment rises as the initiative is framed not as restriction, but as reclaiming clarity in an opaque world.

Meanwhile, the *Tech Sovereignty Package* concludes. Though it mobilised only €89bn of the €200bn target, it establishes accelerated permitting zones in Spain, Poland, and Romania—setting a precedent for EU override of national delays. However, the investment collapse stalls the *InvestAI Gigafactories*, with two sites paused and renegotiations underway.

Resilience dips slightly as no active category 6 measure offsets rising systemic risk. AI safety, however, jumps to 32.0—the first meaningful gain in years—driven by the new public assurance infrastructure. Sovereignty inches up on continued progress toward sovereign compute and the strategic weight of the new network. Political capital holds steady despite the strain, buoyed by a modest legitimacy gain from rising public sentiment.

### US Posture Takes Shape

In Washington, the new administration formally takes office. Its approach is clear: frontier AI is a strategic monopoly, and access will be tiered by alliance. The EU is not excluded—but treated as a client, not a peer. The era of open technical partnership is over.
