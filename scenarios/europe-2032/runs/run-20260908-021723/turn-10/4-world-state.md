### EU Shifts Focus to Deployment Control

With the Anti-Coercion Instrument now concluded but yielding no change in U.S. access policy, the EU pivots to sovereign control over AI deployment. A new regulation is proposed mandating independent pre-deployment evaluations for high-impact public-sector AI in healthcare, legal aid, and welfare. The measure, framed as a defense of public trust, would require safety cases to be published and failures disclosed. Drafted under qualified majority procedures, it faces resistance from France and Germany, who worry about burdens on national providers. A compromise offers co-location of the first evaluation hub in Paris and rotating technical leadership among national agencies.
