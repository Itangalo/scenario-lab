- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural

  - Text: Prevent uncontrolled AI systems from operating inside EU borders
  - Grounds: The openweight_frontier_release has rendered this commitment, as currently framed, structurally unenforceable. The new focus is on detection, disruption, and resilience — shifting from a customs model to a civil defence model.
