## Priority
EU Clinical AI Adoption Trust Programme, because with capital at 21 and sentiment at 32 we cannot fund sovereignty without first turning the medical breakthrough into visible EU-delivered benefit that buys room to act.

## In practice
We are tasking DG SANTE and HERA with joint procurement of the new therapies via EU4Health, conditional on inference and patient data staying on EU-anchored capacity reserved from M1/M2 zones, with EMA accelerated pathway and EHDS trust labelling. Health Council Q1 endorsement is traded for co-funding of hospital upgrades and staffing, countering resistance from finance ministers by using repurposed RRF loans not new headroom.

In parallel we hold M1 Gigafactories and M2 sovereignty package on low-burn permitting through DG CNECT, and push M4 Agentic Gate to cover embodied control models in critical workflows via the AI Office, while ENISA lessons from the finished surge are folded into hospital cyber hardening under the Trust Programme rollout rather than as a separate instrument.
