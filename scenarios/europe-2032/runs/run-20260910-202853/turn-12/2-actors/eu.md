## In practice
We act through the Transport and EPSCO Councils under wartime emergency rules: EU-OSHA and EMSA issue an immediate teleoperation inspection order — speed limits, two-operator rule, and kill-switch certification — paid from the Dock-to-Job retraining line, while the Commission declares a port continuity emergency allowing naval and civil-protection crews to keep medical and energy cargo moving.

We pair enforcement with a bridge: Emergency Support Instrument wage payments for idled dockers conditional on return to certified berths, and we fold the new cyber-defence patching breakthrough via ENISA into port OT systems to show protection is real. To the unions we offer what the dock deal did not deliver — certified safety, not quotas — and to port authorities joint EU funding for repair and islanded power so continuity does not depend on rationed US inference.
