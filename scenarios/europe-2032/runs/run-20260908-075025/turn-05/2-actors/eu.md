## In practice
We are closing the municipal expansion of ENISA’s mandate this turn. The delegated act under NIS3 has been formally adopted, and compliance is now tied to disbursement of Digital Europe Programme funds. Fast-track access to InvestAI compute and dedicated red-teaming teams secured buy-in from pilot cities in Poland and Italy, but Hungary, Slovakia, and Greece have filed preliminary references to the CJEU, arguing infringement of national competences. We are treating this as a test of the Union’s capacity to act — not backing down, but offering structured dialogue under Article 13 of the Treaty on European Union to preserve cohesion. The EIC threat feeds are now live in Bologna and Wrocław, generating automated patch suggestions and early warnings, but the lack of human-readable reasoning in the latest models means analysts are flying blind on *why* threats emerge, only seeing *that* they do.

Despite the election_alliance event offering new access to frontier capability, we are not slowing our industrial push. On the contrary: opaque_reasoning has broken the assumption that we can oversee AI through legible outputs, and the cyber_major_incident — a model-generated ransomware sweep hitting ten regional health authorities — confirms that defence cannot wait. The new measure launches a coordinated effort across DG Connect, the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking, and national labs to build an EU-native agent framework with embedded safety: not relying on interpretability of reasoning traces, but on runtime monitoring, goal stability verification, and secure sandboxing enforced at the hardware level. We are pulling forward €45bn from InvestAI and reallocating it to this stack, with delivery milestones tied to TRL8 by turn 8. This is not duplication — it is adaptation to a world where the old controls are dead, and sovereignty means the ability to act when no one else can see what is coming.
