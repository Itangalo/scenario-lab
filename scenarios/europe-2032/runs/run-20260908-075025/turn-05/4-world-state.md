While Bologna and Wrocław now run live AI threat feeds, legal challenges from Hungary, Slovakia, and Greece cast doubt on enforcement beyond pilot zones. The cyber_major_incident — a model-generated ransomware wave paralyzing ten regional health authorities — exposes the limits of current defences. With opaque_reasoning now widespread, AI systems no longer produce human-readable logic chains, blinding analysts to attack origins. The incident fuels public fear, and media spotlight job losses in IT and legal sectors, dragging public sentiment down sharply.

However, the move is seen by some as duplicative just as transatlantic access improves, triggering criticism over fiscal strain and strategic coherence.
