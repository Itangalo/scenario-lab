## In practice
We are treating the completion of the InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package as foundational milestones — the compute base is now legally enshrined and the private capital flows are locked in through binding covenants with EIB-backed guarantees. But their finishing does not mean delivery: the physical build-out lags, and without sovereign control over how that compute is used, we remain exposed. That is why the Sovereign Agent Framework Initiative remains our priority. This turn, we are activating Article 12 of the Digital Markets Act as a legal basis to compel participation in the municipal pilot layer, conditioning access to GAIA-X subsidies and national recovery funds on opt-in threat feed integration. We are sending DG Connect envoys to Budapest, Bratislava, and Athens with a revised offer: full transparency on agent behaviour logging, local control over sandbox enforcement triggers, and a seat on the joint audit board — not concession, but calibrated inclusion. The runtime monitoring co-developed by Atos and Fraunhofer is entering TRL6 validation this month; if we lose cohesion now, the entire stack becomes a patchwork.

Simultaneously, we are acting on the shift in Washington. The new US administration has signalled openness to democratic techno-governance, and we cannot waste it. Through the newly reactivated EU-US Trade and Technology Council, we are launching negotiations on the Transatlantic AI Safety Accord — not a declaration, but a live instrument with standing review panels, shared evaluation protocols modelled on CERN’s safety frameworks, and automatic suspension clauses if either side releases models above agreed risk thresholds. Our opening demand: reciprocal third-party access to pre-deployment testing environments for all systems above 60.0 capability. We will back this with ENISA-NIST joint exercises this winter, using the Open Safety Commons tools as common ground truth. This is not trust — it is institutionalised verification. And it buys us something rare: influence at the closed frontier, without depending on it.
