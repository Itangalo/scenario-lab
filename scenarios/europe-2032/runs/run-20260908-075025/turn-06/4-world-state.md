### A Fragile Step Forward

Global AI capability continues its steady climb, reaching 63.0 as frontier labs integrate agentive reasoning into core training loops. The closed frontier pulls further ahead, but diffusion remains slow: openweight_capability rises only to 48.0, aided by leaked tooling but still far from parity.

In Brussels, the EU marks the formal launch of the Sovereign Agent Framework Initiative as complete, with €45bn now ring-fenced and first contracts awarded. The move strengthens long-term autonomy, but immediate gains are muted. Hungary, Slovakia, and Greece reject the opt-in offer for the municipal pilot, citing sovereignty concerns, and file a joint appeal to delay disbursement. Legal uncertainty persists, limiting early deployment.

Yet a counter-current emerges. Following the ransomware wave, a grassroots coalition of European researchers releases a powerful open-source suite for model monitoring and anomaly detection. The EU swiftly institutionalises it through the new Open Safety Commons Programme, allocating €1.2bn in grants and GAIA-X compute. The initiative is small but agile, gaining rapid adoption among mid-tier institutions. ENISA begins integrating its alerts into threat feeds, enhancing early warning capacity.

This dual approach yields modest progress. ai_safety rises to 18.0, lifted by distributed monitoring despite opaque reasoning still dominating high-end systems. resilience increases to 57.0, as detection improves at municipal levels. public_sentiment ticks up slightly to 30.0, as the Commons is framed as a democratic response to unaccountable AI.

However, strain remains. The portfolio carries three large measures, costing −9 per turn. The new small measure adds no ongoing burden. With no major setbacks, political capital stabilises and recovers slightly to 32.0, aided by a +3 proposal bonus for directly addressing the recent cyber incident. eu_ai_sovereignty edges up to 20.0, supported by continued momentum in the agent initiative (+1) and the −1 decay from advancing global capability.

The US administration takes office, ushering in a new era of structured cooperation.
