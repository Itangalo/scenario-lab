## In practice
We are treating the final quarter of this turn as decisive for the Sovereign Agent Framework Initiative. The measure is now finished, and DG Connect has confirmed runtime monitoring by Atos-Fraunhofer is operational across all ten opt-in member states. Hungary has formally joined after securing a bilateral agreement on data sovereignty under Article 12 of the DMA, which we have now activated as a conditionality for GAIA-X funding. This sets a precedent: access to EU digital infrastructure is contingent on compliance with sovereign oversight mechanisms. Legal services are preparing a binding opinion to clarify enforcement powers beyond state aid, but for now, leverage rests on subsidy conditionality — and we are using it.

Simultaneously, the bio_uplift_findings event has shifted the risk calculus. While no release has occurred, the mere plausibility of a designed human pathogen crossing the threshold demands institutional readiness now. We cannot wait for perfect transatlantic alignment. The new measure — EU AI Watch and Respond — will be proposed to the Justice and Home Affairs Council in November, co-funded through the Internal Security Fund and the EU4Health programme. It tasks ENISA with integrating early-warning signals from bio labs, cyber defences, and border agencies into a unified threat dashboard, with standing protocols for activation and public alerting. We are offering member states fast-track access to emergency surge funding if they designate national AI incident coordinators by turn 10. This is not about building new walls — it is about ensuring the EU can see, speak, and act as one when the next crisis hits.
