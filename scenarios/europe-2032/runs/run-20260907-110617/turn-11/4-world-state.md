### Emergency Integration, Fragile Foundations

The European Union treats the Open Science Resilience Initiative as a live sovereign capability, directing integration of its auditable protein folding models into national health and critical infrastructure systems. Binding standards are issued under the Digital Services Act, and emergency procurement guidelines push de facto compliance. However, technical bottlenecks emerge: the models, while trustworthy, are too slow for real-time diagnostics in high-load hospitals, leading to delays in cancer screening workflows in three major urban centres. Germany resists full adoption, citing legal uncertainty over emergency powers.

ENISA’s forensic audit under the joint threat response pact identifies the access denial as stemming from a foreign provider’s compliance with extraterritorial export controls, not a technical failure. This confirms strategic vulnerability but yields no leverage for restoration.
