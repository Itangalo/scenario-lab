## Priority  
**Accelerated Sovereign Deployment Programme** — because the Open Science Resilience Initiative has proven auditable AI can be built, but not that it can run at scale under pressure, and without that, sovereignty is theoretical. The collapse in ai_investment creates a window: the frontier is pausing, but we must not. This is the turn to shift from research to real deployment — not in pilot, but in production, with teeth.

## In practice  
This turn, we are treating the completion of the Open Science Resilience Initiative not as an endpoint, but as a starting line. The models are auditable, they are sovereign, and they are legally anchored — but they are not fast enough. We cannot ask Germany to accept diagnostic delays in exchange for auditability. That trade-off breaks both public trust and legal legitimacy. So we are launching the Accelerated Sovereign Deployment Programme to close the performance gap.  

We are directing DG CONNECT, DG SANTE, and the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking to co-fund optimisation clusters in three member states — France, Poland, and Finland — with mandates to re-engineer the protein folding and biothreat detection models for latency, throughput, and integration with existing health data pipelines. These clusters will have access to EuroHPC resources and are required to deliver performance benchmarks every quarter, with failure to meet minimum thresholds triggering reallocation of funds. We are embedding ENISA and the European Data Protection Board in the process from day one to ensure that optimisation does not compromise auditability or compliance.  

To overcome resistance, we are tying cohesion fund disbursements to adoption of the optimised models by Q3 2033, and offering transitional technical assistance to member states that lack in-house AI integration capacity. We are also preparing a Council Regulation under Article 114 TFEU to establish a mandatory deployment timeline for auditable AI in critical health and infrastructure systems, to be tabled in Q3 2032. The message is clear: we will not depend, we will not delay, and we will not deploy systems that fail when they are needed most. Trust must also work.
