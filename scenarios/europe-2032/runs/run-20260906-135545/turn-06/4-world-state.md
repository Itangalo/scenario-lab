### A Pivot Under Pressure

The EU closes two major initiatives on schedule: the **Tech Sovereignty Package** unlocks €28bn in private investment and streamlines permitting across six member states, while the **Critical Infrastructure Resilience Sprint** achieves 78% audit coverage and partial red-teaming deployment. These completions deliver tangible gains, but the geopolitical landscape shifts violently with a blockade in the Taiwan Strait, halting advanced semiconductor exports. Global compute supply chains fracture, and the EU’s leverage in semiconductor equipment becomes a strategic asset overnight.

In response, the Commission activates emergency coordination with ASML, STMicroelectronics, and Infineon under the Chips Act, securing commitments for redirected tooling and priority maintenance. This stabilises near-term access and earns a +3 boost to sovereignty. Meanwhile, the new **Systemic Integrity Watch** launches at ENISA, integrating real-time telemetry from energy, health, and digital infrastructure. Though small, its mandate to halt runaway AI coordination marks a shift toward proactive oversight.
