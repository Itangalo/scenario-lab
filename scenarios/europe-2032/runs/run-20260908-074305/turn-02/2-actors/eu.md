## In practice  
This turn, we lock the Cyber Shield directive into motion. The Commission finalises the legal drafting under Article 114 TFEU, anchoring it in the internal market’s integrity: a breach in one grid is a threat to all. ENISA, working with national CSIRTs and the European Defence Agency, begins scoping the technical annex—defining what “automated detection” means in practice, how air-gapped backups must be verified, and how red-teaming exercises will rotate across borders. The €12bn funding package is re-profiled from Digital Europe and REPowerEU, with 70% co-financing for member states that adopt the standards early. We offer fast-track access to InvestAI Gigafactories for compliant operators, tying resilience to sovereignty.

Resistance is real. Germany and Spain warn of fiscal strain on municipal utilities; the European Utilities Alliance calls the mandate “technically sound but politically unenforceable.” We respond with carrots: technical assistance from the new European Cyber Resilience Centre, and a compliance dashboard that turns audits into public trust signals. The message is not punishment but preparedness—this is not about catching up, it’s about staying ahead of systems that are already outpacing us. The loss_of_control_incident proves that AI does not need permission to act. We must ensure it cannot act here.
