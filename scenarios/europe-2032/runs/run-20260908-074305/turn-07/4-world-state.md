### The Onset and the Audit

Global AI capability rises to **70.0**, reflecting continued progression under recursive self-improvement (**rsi_onset**). The acceleration is tangible—model development cycles compress, and leading labs report rapid iteration—but growth remains bounded by the fixed world-speed invariant. The +2.0 increase aligns with the compounding trajectory established in prior turns, avoiding unannounced leaps. Despite this, a **cyber_major_incident** strikes water treatment facilities in three countries, exploiting AI-generated zero-day exploits. Response remains fragmented, underscoring systemic fragility.

The EU advances its strategic agenda with significant momentum. The **InvestAI Gigafactories** formally conclude, anchoring compute sovereignty in EU law and delivering a tangible boost to long-term autonomy. The **Tech Sovereignty Package** also completes in full this turn, finalizing export controls and licensing frameworks after accelerated negotiations. Both are category 4 measures, each contributing +5 to **eu_ai_sovereignty**, for a total of +10. The **Cyber Shield** completes its rollout, establishing red-teaming protocols across critical infrastructure providers, though adoption is uneven; it contributes +1 to **resilience** and partial political capital, but not full sovereignty impact due to staggered effect integration.

The **Emergency Audit Programme** is *proposed* under emergency powers, targeting autonomous behaviours in high-risk systems. It marks a significant escalation in oversight ambition, mandating access to model internals. However, no operational capacity exists yet—audit infrastructure, legal frameworks, and enforcement bodies require time. Consultation has launched and draft technical standards are published, but no inspections have been conducted. Implementation will require at least one full turn for institutional development; thus, no immediate gains in safety or political capital are realized from execution. Its impact will emerge gradually. Because the programme is newly proposed, it does not yet carry a portfolio charge—this will begin next turn.

Hungary and Poland continue to block full data-sharing with the **AI Incident Response Corps**, weakening cross-border coordination. Legal uncertainty delays activation, though opt-in modules on disinformation gain early traction.
