## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because the autumn swarm proved open-weight offensive intrusion is already staged in grids and ports and hardening must land before the next campaign triggers, outranking even compute and evaluation this turn.

## In practice
We are driving M3 through ENISA and HERA with the Civil Protection Mechanism uplift, moving from drills to funded retrofits: joint procurement of OT detection stacks for the two exposed grid operators, exercised islanding plans for municipal utilities and hospitals, and conditionality that links EU hardening funds to mandatory incident reporting. Interior and health ministers resisting obligations are bought off with EU-paid backup power and backup-care capacity.

On M1 and M2 we keep DG CNECT and the EIB focused on getting the two advanced gigafactory sites to final investment decision by summer, using grid-priority guarantees and InvestAI first-loss cover, while pausing the stalled sites rather than letting capitals bid against each other. In parallel we task the AI Office to staff the new Evaluation Cell with seconded national lab researchers and negotiated evaluation-access clauses in compute-award contracts, so the next anomalous system is tested by us before it is deployed at us.
