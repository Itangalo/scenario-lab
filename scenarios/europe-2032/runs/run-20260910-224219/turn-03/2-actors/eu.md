## New measure
**EU AI Transition Safety Net**
Creates an EU-cofunded wage-insurance, retraining and hiring-freeze bridge for entry-level white-collar roles hit by AI displacement, tied to ESF+ and employer automation levies.
This is Labour and social protection work because measurable graduate hiring collapse now threatens public tolerance for the sovereignty and resilience build-out, and must be cushioned now before backlash stalls siting and adoption.

## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because pre-positioned intrusion plus a capability jump means grids and ports must reach exercised hardening before the next campaign, outranking even the urgent labour shock this turn.

## In practice
We drive M3 to its finish line through ENISA, DG ENER and the Civil Protection Mechanism: release the final hardware tranche for OT detection and islanding kits, make EU funds conditional on live incident reporting, and use HERA backup-care money to hold health ministers on board despite hospital staffing gaps and the tainted-tooling clean-ups.

On M1/M2 we hold DG CNECT and EIB to closing the two advanced gigafactory FIDs with grid priority and first-loss cover, freezing new commitments on stalled sites to stop capital bidding wars. The Evaluation Cell (M4) gets emergency secondments and access-clause enforcement to re-probe the jumped system, while EPSCO and DG EMPL stand up the Transition Safety Net via ESF+ reprogramming and SURE-style bridge support for young entrants, asking automating employers to co-fund retraining to limit fiscal strain.
