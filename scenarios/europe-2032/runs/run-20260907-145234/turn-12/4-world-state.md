### The Edge of Control

AI capability advances to 77.0 as autonomous research systems consolidate gains in materials science and algorithmic efficiency. With `ai_safety` stagnant at 5.0, no meaningful interpretability or control mechanisms accompany the rise, deepening systemic fragility. Open-weight replication continues, with optimized forks of frontier models now achieving narrow superintelligence in cyber operations and logistics planning, pushing `openweight_capability` to 58.0.

The EU completes both the **Frontier Access Guarantee** and the **Emergency Continuity Response Initiative**. The former secures access to two overseas compute clusters under EU audit, but their operational value is questioned given the opacity of the models hosted. The latter finalizes failover validation in Berlin and Marseille, though in Riga, legal resistance delays full activation until the final week, narrowly avoiding a procedural collapse.

The **Sovereign AI Core Programme** is prioritized, pulling its completion forward to turn 14, but with `eu_political_capital` at 0.0, the pull-in yields no acceleration in implementation. Repurposing of EuroHPC sites begins, but national pushback in Portugal stalls physical upgrades. The **Open-Weight Containment Protocol** is formally proposed, triggering immediate backlash. Academic consortia denounce it as a breach of open science; cloud providers warn of compliance chaos. No enforcement actions are taken this turn — the regulation remains in draft.

Public sentiment holds at 18.0, as the perceived overreach of containment clashes with ongoing institutional paralysis. Resilience gains +3 from the completed category 6 measure, offsetting the −1 from rising AI capability without new resilience investment. Sovereignty falls to 19.0 due to the −1 decay from capability rise ≥2, with no offsetting gains. Political capital remains at 0.0, unable to absorb the portfolio charge of −9 (three large measures, one priority), and no proposal bonus applies — the new measure responds to a systemic trend, not a recent event.

### A Union in Freefall

The EU acts, but cannot move. Every lever is pulled, yet nothing turns. The machinery of governance creaks under emergency powers, but without capital, even triage fails.
