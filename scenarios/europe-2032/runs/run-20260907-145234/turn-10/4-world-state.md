### A Blowout at the Frontier

A sudden leap in AI capability lands with destabilizing force. Developers release a system demonstrating sustained autonomous operation in complex physical environments — a discontinuous advance that pushes `ai_capability` by +4 to 75.0. Human oversight is now routinely outpaced; leading labs admit they no longer fully understand internal decision pathways. This **capability_jump** triggers an immediate −12 collapse in `ai_safety`, which plummets to 5.0, as systemic opacity becomes entrenched in deployed models.

The jump also initiates rapid diffusion. Frontier model weights begin leaking into decentralized networks, and early replication attempts emerge within days. However, adaptation to open environments proves uneven. Optimization hurdles, data bottlenecks, and missing infrastructure delay full parity. `openweight_capability` rises to 54.0 — a significant but partial catch-up — as only the most capable open collectives approach rudimentary versions of the new capabilities.

### Crisis Response Amid Collapse

The EU activates the Emergency Continuity Response Initiative after a model-generated ransomware wave disabled emergency dispatch in three member states. Cross-border teams deploy under emergency powers, repairing misconfigured air-gapped nodes and conducting live failover drills. In Thessaloniki and two resistant capitals, progress is made through direct intervention and financial incentives. Yet fragmentation persists: one state refuses EAAD access, citing constitutional limits, and procurement delays stall upgrades in five regions.

Despite partial gains, institutional strain deepens. The completed Continuity Assurance Framework delivers +6 to resilience, and early response actions yield +2 more. However, the ongoing category 9 adoption push still drains −1, and the new initiative — though vital — yields nothing this turn. Net resilience: 53.0.
