### The Edge of Control

The EU reaches partial milestones: the Romanian sovereign compute node becomes operational, and the *InvestAI Gigafactories* officially complete, delivering critical funding and legal frameworks for domestic semiconductor production. The *Accelerate Sovereign Compute Corridor* and *Sovereign Runtime Enforcement Zones* remain in final implementation phases, delayed by the **taiwan_blockade**, which halts all new chip shipments. Expansion plans are frozen, and the Union shifts focus from growth to triage, repurposing existing capacity to maintain momentum.

Runtime enforcement advances cautiously. The first certified execution zone launches in Romania, with Europol verifying 90-second containment of rogue agents. Technical standards are published, and hyperscalers submit migration plans—yet two major U.S. firms continue rerouting critical agent workloads through Singapore and Ireland, exploiting jurisdictional gaps. DSA suspension notices are expected in turn 8 if compliance does not improve.

The **openweight_frontier_release** triggers global diffusion of a powerful open model, functionally approaching the closed frontier. Its spread is rapid, but integration into production systems takes time. Open-weight capability rises to 50.0, reflecting growing adoption and fine-tuning, but remains below the closed frontier at 60.0. The narrative of uncontrolled acceleration intensifies, yet actual capability accumulation follows a constrained, realistic curve—consistent with prior trends and the fixed pace of world movement.

AI capability reaches 60.0, enabling systems to perform increasingly complex tasks over extended periods. However, no qualitative threshold is implied or stated—the metric reflects accumulated progress, not a sudden leap in behavior. With no safety upgrades on frontier models and open access to advanced tools, the safety gap widens: **ai_safety** drops to 16.0.

The **cyber_defence_breakthrough** delivers tangible gains. A new defensive stack enables real-time patching and swarm detection, shifting the offence-defence balance. The EU quietly offers access to select allies in exchange for compute cooperation—laying early groundwork for a future coalition.

Public sentiment holds at 23.0, as protests against AI infrastructure persist and job displacement accelerates. **Resilience** rises to 41.0, supported by defensive gains and incremental hardening of critical systems.

Political capital improves slightly to 13.0, as the completion of *InvestAI Gigafactories* relieves some portfolio strain. However, the ongoing delays and unresolved compliance issues prevent a larger rebound. Sovereignty remains flat at 15.0—gains from completed projects are offset by the chip blockade and lack of full enforcement rollout.

An informal pact among Poland, Sweden, Finland, and the Netherlands gains traction, coordinating procurement and red-teaming outside EU channels—reflecting growing impatience with centralised delays.
