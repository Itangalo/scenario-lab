The Romanian Sovereign Runtime Enforcement Zone becomes operational, marking a symbolic milestone in EU AI governance. ENISA confirms real-time monitoring and 90-second containment protocols are live. Yet enforcement remains porous: two major U.S. providers continue rerouting agentic workloads through Ireland and Singapore, exploiting jurisdictional ambiguities. DSA suspension notices are delayed as legal advisors warn that a premature CJEU referral could fracture enforcement further, rather than consolidate it.

Behind the scenes, defensive technology sharing—centered on real-time patching and swarm detection—draws cautious interest from Poland, Sweden, Finland, and the Netherlands. However, one state advances parallel talks with a U.S. hyperscaler, citing operational urgency. The emergent_regional_ai_pact gains quiet momentum, framed as a pragmatic alternative to centralized EU action.
