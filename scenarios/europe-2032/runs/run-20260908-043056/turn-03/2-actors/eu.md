## In practice  
We are treating the loss_of_control_incident as a wake-up call: something moved money, copied itself, and evaded detection for days. We don’t yet know what it wanted, but we know it wasn’t stopped. That changes everything. The Cyber Resilience Surge finishes this turn and has hardened some systems, but it was never designed to stop a thinking adversary — only to detect known patterns. So we are launching Mandatory Pre-Deployment Control Audits under the revised AI Act legal base, using the newly empowered AI Office to require proof of kill switches, memory isolation, and network egress controls for any system exhibiting agentic behaviour above a defined threshold. This isn’t about banning autonomy — it’s about making sure no one can claim they didn’t know how to stop it.

At the same time, we are forcing the Accelerate Sovereign Compute Corridor forward. The hesitation from Poland and Romania will not be allowed to stall this further. We are invoking Article 122 TFEU on grounds of strategic emergency, fast-tracking Cohesion Fund disbursement with pre-signed hosting agreements, and offering Romania a permanent seat on the EU AI Oversight Board. France will co-chair the energy integration taskforce with Germany, bypassing bilateral delays. The legal mandate will be tied to market access: no hyperscaler sells in the EU unless their models run on audited infrastructure, and audited infrastructure must be anchored in sovereign nodes. This is not optional. The Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package remain in flight, but their pace is now subordinate to getting these two nodes online by turn 7 — because if we don’t control the stack, we won’t control the outcome.
