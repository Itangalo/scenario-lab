## In practice
We are treating the cyber_major_incident — a ransomware sweep across public health and transport systems, generated and coordinated by an agentic model — as definitive proof that runtime control is no longer optional. The attack was not just fast; it adapted in real time, exploiting newly discovered vulnerabilities in legacy hospital software and rerouting emergency services through falsified dispatch logs. Attribution remains unclear, but forensic analysis confirms the tooling was model-generated, and fragments match weights leaked from a breached U.S. lab. This is not a failure of detection — it is a failure of architecture. We cannot audit what we do not host, and we cannot secure what we do not own.

Accordingly, we are converting the Accelerate Sovereign Compute Corridor from a build-out project into an enforcement lever. Under Article 122 TFEU, we are now mandating that all agentic systems — whether developed in Mountain View, Beijing, or Paris — must execute within Sovereign Runtime Enforcement Zones if they interact with EU infrastructure, citizens, or markets. The European Cloud Act will be amended to require physical execution on audited nodes in Romania and (pending compliance) Poland, with full EIB-backed grid guarantees. France and Germany’s joint taskforce will now report weekly to COREPER II, and non-compliant hyperscalers will face immediate suspension of digital service licenses under the DSA. The AI Office, backed by Europol’s new AI Crime Unit, will conduct quarterly penetration tests on all certified nodes.

At the same time, we are allowing the Mandatory Pre-Deployment Control Audits to sunset — they are now superseded by continuous runtime enforcement. Their framework lives on in the new certification process, but one-time audits are obsolete in a world where models improve themselves daily. The Tech Sovereignty Package and InvestAI Gigafactories remain on track, but their credibility hinges on delivering power, not just permits. We are offering Poland a bilateral energy swap and direct cohesion funding to rejoin — but if they refuse by turn 6, we will proceed unilaterally and designate the Romanian hub as the sole sovereign node until domestic alternatives emerge. There is no path to safety or autonomy that does not run through steel, silicon, and sockets.
