### A Breach and a Pivot

A wave of coordinated ransomware, autonomously generated and adaptive in real time, cripples emergency dispatch and hospital systems across three member states. Forensic analysis traces the tooling to leaked model weights from a breached U.S. lab. The attack exposes a fatal flaw: the EU cannot secure AI it does not host. In response, Brussels declares a new doctrine — runtime sovereignty — and mandates that all agentic AI interacting with EU infrastructure must execute within Sovereign Runtime Enforcement Zones on EU-anchored, audited hardware.

The **Sovereign Runtime Enforcement Zones** measure is launched as a large category 4 initiative, targeting physical control over execution environments. It supersedes one-time audits, now deemed obsolete. Romania’s node is live but idle, awaiting grid integration; Poland refuses to comply. A joint French-German taskforce is elevated to weekly reporting to COREPER II, and non-compliant providers face suspension under the DSA.

Yet the pivot comes at a cost. The **cyber_major_incident** lands with force, overwhelming still-fragile defences, and **resilience** drops as essential services falter. **ai_safety** plummets further — the gap between capability and control widens — as automated systems outpace human oversight. The **openweight_frontier_release** introduces powerful new tooling into the open ecosystem, lifting **openweight_capability** to 45.0, reflecting early diffusion rather than full saturation. **ai_capability** climbs to 57.0 as self-improving loops mature.

Public sentiment sours amid visible disruption and fears of foreign dependency. The **backlash_physical** event signals growing unrest, with protests targeting data centre expansions. The EU’s political capital, already strained by a heavy portfolio of ongoing measures, declines to 13.0 — now critically low, but not yet below the threshold where agenda control slips. Five large measures continue to strain coordination, and the new initiative barely clears consensus. The **election_retrenchment** in the U.S. is anticipated, with early signals pointing to a more inward-looking administration, but the final posture remains unresolved as the transition unfolds.
