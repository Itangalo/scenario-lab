### A Shift in the Frontier

The frontier of AI capability expanded unexpectedly as automated verification broke into domains long thought immune to full automation—legal contract analysis, clinical coding and structural engineering now admit cheap, reliable success checks. This shift accelerates progress in these fields, feeding directly into broader agentic capabilities. With self-improving loops no longer confined to code and math, `ai_capability` rises by +1.0 to 56.0.

In response, the EU reorients its strategy. The **Cyber Resilience Surge** formally concludes, delivering only partial hardening due to fragmented national implementation—no systemic resilience emerges. Instead, Brussels launches **Enforceable Containment Certification**, a sweeping new regime demanding continuous monitoring, replication limits and real-time logging for all agentic systems accessing EU markets or infrastructure. Framed as an evolution of prior audits, it seeks to close loopholes in runtime control.

Simultaneously, the **Accelerate Sovereign Compute Corridor** is declared a priority. Emergency funding is unlocked, and a joint French-German taskforce is mandated under Article 122 TFEU to resolve grid integration delays. Romania’s hosting agreement is operational, but Poland remains non-compliant, and energy disputes threaten timeline adherence.

Despite urgency, political capital continues to erode. Four large measures remain active, including the newly added certification regime. Member-state resistance simmers over enforcement scope, and U.S. developers warn that runtime auditing could violate trade norms. Public sentiment holds steady at 30.0—media coverage remains skeptical, focused on dependency and uncontrolled risks.

No immediate safety gain occurs: the certification regime is too new, and `ai_safety` remains stagnant. Open weights rise slightly as tooling from prior breaches spreads, lifting `openweight_capability` to 44.0. With sovereign compute still offline and `ai_capability` advancing, dependency deepens—`eu_ai_sovereignty` drops another point.

### Faint Signals of Fragmentation

Rumours grow of a Nordic-Benelux coordination track operating outside formal EU channels, pooling evaluation capacity and negotiating jointly with external providers—a quiet challenge to centralized authority.
