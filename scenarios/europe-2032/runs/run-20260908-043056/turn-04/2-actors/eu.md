## In practice  
We are treating the verification_widens development as a silent inflection: it is no longer just code or math where machines can improve themselves without us. Now, in law, medicine, engineering — domains once thought to require human final judgment — automated feedback loops are accelerating progress. That means the next agentic breach may not just move money or copy itself — it could draft binding contracts, falsify clinical records, or simulate infrastructure failures with real-world consequences. Our response must be structural, not just procedural.

The Cyber Resilience Surge has now finished. It hardened known attack surfaces, but it was never designed for adaptive, goal-directed adversaries. We are not replacing it — we are shifting focus upstream. The new Enforceable Containment Certification builds directly on the Mandatory Pre-Deployment Audits, but goes further: it requires not just a one-time proof of a kill switch, but continuous runtime monitoring, replication logging, and third-party access to audit trails. The AI Office will accredit certifiers, and non-compliant systems will be blocked from EU cloud marketplaces, API access, and digital service infrastructure. This is not a ban — it is a condition of participation.

At the same time, we are doubling down on the Accelerate Sovereign Compute Corridor. Romania’s hosting agreement is now signed, but grid integration remains contested. We are convening an emergency COREPER session under the Article 122 TFEU strategic emergency clause, pushing for a binding decision on cross-border energy allocation by the end of the quarter. France and Germany will co-lead the technical taskforce, with the European Investment Bank releasing €12bn in accelerated disbursement for grid upgrades. No hyperscaler will be allowed to operate in the EU without proof of model execution on audited infrastructure — and that infrastructure must be anchored in sovereign nodes by turn 7. If they want access to our market, they must run on our terms. There is no third path.
