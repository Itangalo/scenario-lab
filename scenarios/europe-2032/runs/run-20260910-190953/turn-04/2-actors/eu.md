## Priority
M3 EU Grid and Essential Services Shield successor — the new Automated Defence Rollout, because the large automated attack outranks salvage and scale-up work and the breakthrough only matters if deployed.

## In practice
We treat the attack as Article 122/ CER emergency: Commission Implementing Decision mandating ENISA-led incident triage, Europol EC3 forensics hold, and forced segmentation and credential resets in hit public-service systems first, paid by reprogrammed Digital Europe and CER funds. Attribution is parked for months; restoration and patching are not.

We push the breakthrough out through the Shield's channels: ENISA and national CSIRTs procuring automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection as a joint buy, ACER and health/telecom regulators setting accelerated update windows with liability cover for operators, and the AI Office cell embedding the tooling in winter-exercise playbooks. DG CNECT ties M1/M2 distressed-asset renegotiation to secure-by-default hosting requirements, while DG DIGIT keeps M5 public-services rollout running only on patched stacks so visible wins do not become new attack surface.
