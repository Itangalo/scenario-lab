## Priority
EU Jump Response Safety Brake, because without a pause-for-check the new capability lands on uninsured services and zero-hiring labour markets and breaks both.

## In practice
We act under the AI Act's systemic-risk and incident-reporting bases via the AI Office and ENISA: no new Act, no new fund. Providers serving the EU must file jump-related model and agent evaluations, hold high-risk deployments to staged rollout, and report serious incidents to the common pool, with the AI Office empowered to order a temporary suspension.

We carry it through the Competitiveness and JHA Councils together to hold the Dutch and other sceptics: this is hold-not-ban, time-limited to the jump, conditional on the existing EIB reinsurance segmentation work continuing. DG CNECT seconds evaluators, ENISA runs the watch, and mayors get that cover stays if logging holds.
