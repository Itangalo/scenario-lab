We pair this with live-fire EU-Cyber Shield exercises this winter, drawing TSOs, Europol EC3 and the new AI Office incident cell, while accelerating the public-sector adoption wins to hold trust. The Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty package continue on permitting and grid-connection tracks in Council Energy and Competitiveness formations, but political push and Commission enforcement bandwidth go to the Shield.
