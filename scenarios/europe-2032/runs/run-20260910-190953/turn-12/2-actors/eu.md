## New measure
**EU Bridge to the US-China Safety Floor**
Seek formal EU accession/observer-plus status to the US-China weights-security and bio-design agreement with mutual recognition of EU evaluations and a joint verification cell, turning the bilateral floor into a shared assurance regime.
This and why now because the bilateral deal is the only force slowing capability growth and making safety cooperation checkable, and with safety at 1.0 and capital at 23.0 the Union cannot rebuild assurance alone — it must plug its evaluation hold and incident pool into the one verification channel that now matters.

## Priority
EU Bridge to the US-China Safety Floor, because without a seat in the only working risk-reduction regime the emergency brake stays unilateral paper while the new agent generation diffuses.

## In practice
We mandate the EEAS and DG CNECT/AI Office jointly under the Common Foreign and Security Policy plus AI Act Article 111 systemic-risk base to seek third-party accession: offer the EU incident pool and staged-rollout dossiers as the EU contribution, and ask for reciprocal evaluator access and weights-security audit participation. Foreign Affairs Council gives the negotiating mandate; Competitiveness Council holds the internal hold steady.

We sell it domestically as hold-through-cooperation, not surrender: mayors get that reinsurance conditionality eases once common logging is mutually recognised, the Dutch get no new fund or Act, and Washington/Beijing get what they lack — a neutral verifier and the upstream supply-chain leverage only the EU can bolt on. Seconded ENISA and JRC evaluators staff the joint cell from existing lines.
