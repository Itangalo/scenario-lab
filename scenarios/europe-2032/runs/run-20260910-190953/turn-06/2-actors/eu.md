## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because the US retreat window and the adoption success that proves European public AI can work both point to sovereign capacity that cannot be switched off, and without concrete the talent pact has nowhere to land — even as the cyber and loss-of-control incidents demand we hold resilience with what is already built.

## In practice
We use the Competitiveness Council and EIB distressed-asset bridge to force the two stalled gigafactory sites to decision: reserved grid connection via ACER plus bridge loans only against signed secure-hosting and EU-jurisdiction anchoring, with local permit sweeteners funded from M2 private-capital envelope. DG GROW and Horizon Europe push the M7 homecoming grants and chip-equipment coordination, tying lab power reservations to EU-soil compute use.

We answer the ransomware sweep and the agentic loss-of-control with no new instrument, but by surging what finished: ENISA and national CSIRTs extend M6 automated patching and swarm-detection into the hit municipal/hospital stacks, invoke M3 segmentation and mutual-aid playbooks for containment, and freeze M5 trusted-services scale-up to patched versions only. The visible adoption win — falling waiting lists where trusted AI stayed up — is publicised by DG DIGIT to defend permits and funding against councils and industry ministries resisting further diversion.
