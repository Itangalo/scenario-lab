## Priority
M3 EU Grid and Essential Services Shield, because staged access rehearsal in transmission and water still outranks scale-up and salvage work that only pays off if essential services stay up.

## In practice
We keep enforcement on the Shield: Commission Article 122 emergency directive, ENISA and ACER driving segmentation audits and credential rotation in the two hit transmission systems first then Union-wide, Europol EC3 and the AI Office cell running winter live-fire exercises, paid by reprogrammed CER funds despite industry complaints over diverted deployment money.

We use the pause to salvage and to bank trust. DG COMP and EIB renegotiate M1/M2 toward distressed-asset buys and public anchoring in Energy and Competitiveness Councils without reopening permitting, DG EMPL pushes the Safety Net pilot design with unions and member states toward ESF+ launch next turn, and DG DIGIT with HERA/EU health agencies scales proven hospital, permit and tutoring tools via joint procurement on EU-hosted capacity so the benefit is seen as a European decision.
