## Priority
M8 EU Welfare-Policing AI Redress and Logging Audit, because it is the only new instrument and restoring AI Act credibility outranks holding sovereignty warm while restriction becomes cheap and adoption politically impossible.

## In practice
We task DG JUST and the AI Office with FRA and national ombudsmen to run the audit under AI Act Articles 61/72 and Annex III, funded by reallocated Digital Europe enforcement funds: freeze contested benefit/fraud-risk scores to meaningful human review, open logs to auditors, and pay redress from deploying authorities' budgets. We name no new law to avoid a co-legislator fight we cannot fund.

On sovereignty we only keep warm what M1/M2/M7 built: EIB bridge loans and grid reservations stay conditioned on EU jurisdiction, returnee lab power stays paper-reserved, and ENISA/CSIRT patching continues in hit hospital/registry stacks — no new concrete or compute promised until redress lands and capital rebuilds.
