## New measure
**EU Transition Safety Net Pilot**
Creates a rapid retraining and wage-bridge fund for workers displaced by AI automation in most-exposed sectors, tied to automating employers.
Category 7 Labour and social protection is why now: investment collapse freezes private build-out but not displacement anxiety, and resilience requires social absorption before the next shock hits.

## In practice

We use the investment-collapse pause to salvage sovereignty without a new compute line: DG COMP and EIB renegotiate Gigafactory (M1) and Tech Sovereignty (M2) terms toward distressed-asset and public-anchor options in Energy and Competitiveness Councils, while DG EMPL launches the Safety Net pilot via ESF+ in exposed logistics and back-office regions to hold trust.
