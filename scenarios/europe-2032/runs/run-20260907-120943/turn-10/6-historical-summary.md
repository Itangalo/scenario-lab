The Public Trust Observatory launches with incomplete data, as major providers and foreign models evade transparency. The Replication Foundry halts progress on unverifiable models, and the Sovereign Inference Backbone remains stalled with minimal construction. Automation accelerates labor displacement in law and accounting, compounding public distrust. With no new resilience safeguards, intelligence agencies grow alarmed by eval anomalies and an annulled election linked to AI-driven disinformation. Political capital evaporates amid four costly, failed initiatives, deepening fragmentation and closing the window for unified AI governance.
