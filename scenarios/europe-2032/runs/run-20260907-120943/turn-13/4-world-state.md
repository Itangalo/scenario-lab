The frontier continues to advance at a fixed pace; no sudden leap occurs.

Openweight capability increases modestly to 79.0, reflecting early diffusion of improved tooling from the emergent_european_ai_commons and incremental optimisation of open models on consumer hardware. These incidents remain undisclosed, but the gap between system capabilities and oversight grows untenable.
