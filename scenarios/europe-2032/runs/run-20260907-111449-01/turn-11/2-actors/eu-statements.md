## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer accept inaction as neutrality. The cost of delay is now measurable in systemic exposure, and the burden of caution has shifted.
  - Grounds: The **capability_jump** this turn — a discontinuous advance pushing `ai_capability` into the 92.0–95.0 range — confirms that the frontier is not evolving; it is leaping. At this level, even developers cannot trace model reasoning across complex decisions, and the EU’s reliance on external systems has already led to a sovereign blackout affecting 120 hospitals and 18 ministries. Our crisis response was activated, but it was reactive — and we paid in capital without gaining ground. The moment has passed for treating uncertainty as an excuse to defer hard choices. We must now treat uncertainty as a structural condition and act within it with binding instruments, not just contingency plans. This update sharpens the commitment: hesitation is no longer prudence, it is complicity in dependency.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign technical agency through auditable AI, and ensure it is the *only* AI permitted in critical functions by building sovereign enforcement capacity — not just compliant models, but the authority and infrastructure to mandate, verify, and replace.
  - Grounds: The **eu_frontier_access_denied** event was not a supply chain hiccup; it was a sovereignty failure. We have proven that auditable AI can function in high-consequence domains via the Open Science Resilience Initiative, but we lack the enforcement architecture to make it universal. Binding mandates to France, Germany, and Italy are a start, but they are political directives, not systemic levers. With `eu_political_capital` at 3.0 and public sentiment stagnant, we cannot afford symbolic victories. The commitment must now include not just the technology, but the teeth: monitoring, compliance enforcement, and sovereign fallback capacity that cannot be revoked. This is no longer about preference — it is about control.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
