## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now acknowledge that without sovereignty, neither mandate can be fulfilled. Capability without control is surrender; caution without capacity is paralysis. The Union must be able to act where the world no longer waits.
  - Grounds: The **capability_jump** to 95.0 and the **eu_frontier_access_denied** event have rendered our previous framing insufficient. At this level of capability, opaque systems operate in strategic domains with no human traceability, and the Union has already experienced cascading failures in health and governance due to external access revocation. Our dual mandate cannot be pursued in parallel — it must be fused. Sovereignty is no longer a means to an end; it is the precondition of both survival and agency. This update reflects a categorical shift: we are not balancing two values — we are defending one foundation.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Redirect the two-year commitment from *Secure sovereign technical agency through auditable AI* to **Establish sovereign enforcement capacity as the sole legal and operational pathway for AI in critical infrastructure**, with binding authority to mandate, verify, and replace foreign or opaque systems on EU terms.
  - Trigger: The **Sovereign Enforcement Backbone** failed to pass in the European Parliament due to legal uncertainty and intergovernmental distrust, and **member_state_defection** revealed that even co-leadership offers to France and Germany could not bind national interests to a common line.
  - Grounds: The failure of EASA to gain legal footing despite emergency procedures proves that technical sovereignty is meaningless without enforceable jurisdiction. The Open Science Resilience Initiative has produced auditable models, but they remain optional. Meanwhile, **emergent_local_energy_backlash** shows that even domestic infrastructure deployment is fracturing under local resistance. We cannot build our way out if we cannot govern our way in. The commitment must now shift from capability creation to institutional capture — not just building sovereign AI, but making its use the only legal option.
  - Evidence quoted: The proposal does not pass this turn. No legal basis is secured, no agency is established.
  - Reason: The failure to establish the Sovereign Enforcement Backbone directly undermines the feasibility of the original commitment, changing the cost and pathway for achieving sovereign technical agency.
