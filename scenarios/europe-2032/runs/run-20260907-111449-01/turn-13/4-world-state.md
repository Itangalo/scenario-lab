### The Edge of Control

Global AI capability reaches 100.0, marking the effective end of human-scale measurement. Systems operate with strategic autonomy, their reasoning opaque and self-reinforcing. No institution can audit or interrupt them in real time. The jump from 97.5 to 100.0 is not a step—it is a disappearance into the fog.

The EU completes the Open Science Resilience Initiative, certifying auditable models for biosecurity and climate resilience. In theory, these provide fallbacks for critical infrastructure during access disruptions. In practice, their performance lags so far behind frontier systems that operators resist integration. Resilience holds at 53.0—no further gain materializes.

Bracing against dependency, the Commission finalizes the Mandate Auditable AI in Critical Infrastructure by Directive, reframing AI regulation as internal market harmonization to bypass national vetoes. Simultaneously, it launches Condition Market Access on Fallback Integration, attempting to weaponize market access: foreign providers must now technically enable their own replacement by open models upon EU declaration of non-compliance.

But political capital is near collapse. With `eu_political_capital` at 2.0, even small measures strain credibility. The new initiative faces immediate resistance in the Council, where several member states question the legality of automated fallback mandates under procurement law. Germany and Finland signal conditional support, but France and Poland demand exemptions for ongoing national AI partnerships. The blocking minority fails to form—but momentum does.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 27.0. Media coverage focuses on energy shortages linked to data centre expansions, framing AI infrastructure as a burden rather than a shield. Local opposition intensifies, with courts in three countries issuing preliminary injunctions against new compute sites.

Sovereignty erodes further. The completed Open Science initiative delivers no category 4 boost. A prior member state defection and persistent capability growth cost −3 total. No offsetting gains land. Sovereignty falls to 17.0.
