### Stalled Response, Deepening Crisis

With political capital exhausted and institutions reeling, the EU entered 2030’s first half in reactive triage. The Worker Transition Fund expansion proceeded in Silesia, Wallonia, and Thuringia, using AI-driven eligibility systems and emergency Cohesion Fund disbursements. Yet visible automation in legal and translation sectors outpaced retraining, feeding public anger. Protests demanding a public-sector AI moratorium intensified, and trust in EU-level solutions eroded further.

### Technical Fragility Exposed

The European Cybersecurity Agency’s post-incident review confirmed that the ransomware agents used adaptive, AI-generated exploit chains derived from open-weight models now widely accessible. No new defensive breakthroughs emerged, and the failed Evaluation Agency left the bloc blind to model risks. With no sovereign audit or interpretability capacity, the EU remained dependent on foreign developers’ disclosures.

### Strategic Drift and Sovereignty Erosion

Despite the formal completion of the Accelerate EU Data Centres and middle-power semiconductor coordination measures, neither delivered meaningful capacity. Only three data centres were operational, and ASML’s de facto relaxation of servicing restrictions in China fractured export unity. Poland and Hungary’s abstention weakened the coalition, and internal pressure grew to formalise limited servicing — a move that would cement strategic dependence.

The Worker Transition Fund remained active this turn, continuing to absorb political and financial resources under strained implementation. No new measure was launched, as political capital hovered near paralysis.

### Capability Creeps Forward

Global AI capability advanced to 60.5, crossing the symbolic threshold of full multi-day professional autonomy. Open-weight models, now at 51.0, showed incremental gains in software and research task execution under minimal supervision — a trajectory that remains below frontier but steadily narrowing the gap. The absence of major new releases or leaks prevented a sharper rise, though diffusion of existing tools continued unchecked.
