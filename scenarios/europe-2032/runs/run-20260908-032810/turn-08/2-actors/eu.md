The Commission has activated emergency clauses in the Cohesion Fund to bypass national bottlenecks, directing €1.2 billion in pre-emptive disbursements to Silesia, Wallonia, and now also Thuringia, where legal and translation automation is accelerating. We are instructing national labour ministries to fast-track pre-registration of displaced workers using AI-driven eligibility matching, and we have secured pilot agreements with six multinational law firms to co-finance retraining in exchange for temporary hiring guarantees. This is not transformation — it is triage.

We have no sovereign evaluation capacity, no interpretability pipeline, and no EU-hosted frontier compute. We cannot patch fast enough because we cannot anticipate. We are preparing, but not yet proposing, a resilience surge: mandatory cyber hardening of health and election infrastructure, tied to emergency funding. But without political capital, without public trust, and without sovereign capacity, even that will require a trigger we pray does not come.
