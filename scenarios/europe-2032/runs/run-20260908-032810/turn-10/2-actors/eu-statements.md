# Statements: The European Union (turn 10)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign resilience through independent AI assurance and critical infrastructure protection

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Rebuild sovereign technical capacity to independently evaluate, interpret, and contain AI systems, starting with audit rights over models used in critical infrastructure and the establishment of a European AI Evaluation Agency with mandatory access to weights and training data.
  - Grounds: The bio_incident and continued weaponisation of open models at 52.0 confirm that the EU cannot rely on foreign developers or reactive coordination. Our blindness is structural. The previous commitment assumed resilience could be secured through external partnerships and incremental hardening. It cannot. We now anchor resilience in sovereign technical capability — not just infrastructure, but understanding. This shift reflects a categorical change in threat character: we are no longer preparing for disruption, but for deliberate harm enabled by systems we cannot inspect, let alone control.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will act before full understanding is possible, but only in parallel with building sovereign capacity to reduce that uncertainty — evaluation, interpretability, and containment are now prerequisites for any major deployment or engagement.
  - Grounds: The bio_incident was not predicted, but it was foreseeable. Acting under uncertainty without tools to close the gap only compounds strategic passivity. We retain the duty to move early, but now bind it to the obligation to see.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
