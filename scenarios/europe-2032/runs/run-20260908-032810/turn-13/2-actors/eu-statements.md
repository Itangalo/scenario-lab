## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign resilience through independent AI assurance and critical infrastructure protection
  - Grounds: This turn has revealed that assurance without operational sovereignty is a hollow shell: our red-team sites are live but idle, our Evaluation Agency lacks the models it needs to evaluate, and the annulled election proves that harm is already landing. We are not being blocked by technical delay but by denial of access to the very systems we are mandated to oversee. The commitment stands, but its interpretation must shift from oversight-first to capability-first: we must run what we protect, and protect what we run. Assurance is no longer the starting point — it is the goal, and the path runs through sovereign stacks, not audit mandates.

  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
  - Grounds: The cost of acting under uncertainty has become existential to our political capital. We still believe in acting before the full picture emerges — but we can no longer afford to act alone. This turn, we reframe the commitment: we will commit before the picture is clear, but only in concert with binding coalitions that share the risk. Unilateral emergency action has failed. Our pivot to joint activation with Finland, the Nordics, and the PESCO partners is not retreat — it is the only way forward with legitimacy.
