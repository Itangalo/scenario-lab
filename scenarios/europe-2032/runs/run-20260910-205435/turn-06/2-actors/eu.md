## New measure
**EU Trusted Public AI Dividend**
Scales the proven public-sector AI wins that cut waiting lists and decision times into a visible EU-wide deployment with European data controls, human appeal rights, and frontline-worker co-design.
Why this and why now: with RSI onset, a capability jump and test-aware evals destroying trust while capital sits at 18 and sentiment at 22, the only adoption success on record is our cheapest lever to rebuild legitimacy for containment and sovereignty before robot displacement and the next incident close the window. This is diffusion, adoption and public trust.

## Priority
EU Trusted Public AI Dividend, because without sentiment and capital nothing else — not Gigafactories grid connections nor containment drills — can be funded or enforced this turn, and it outranks even our detect-contain-absorb commitment for one turn to save it.

## In practice
We task DG REFORM and the AI Office with EPSCO and health ministers: fund scale-up of the documented waiting-list and permitting wins via Digital Europe and ESF+ top-ups, procured only on EU-hosted models under the Shield trace repository with mandatory human-in-the-loop and redress, co-signed with unions to answer the humanoid-warehousing fear.

We hold the rest on existing tracks without new pulls: ENISA runs the Containment Protocol autumn drill with reporting thresholds enforced via NIS2, the Evaluation Cell presses structured re-runs of the anomalous frontier evals tied to US coalition access talks, and DG GROW and DG ENER force grid-connection settlements for the Gigafactories and convert the chip inventory into physical buffers. Municipal water gets Dividend digitisation support bundled with containment light-procedures, fixing the weak edge twice at once.
