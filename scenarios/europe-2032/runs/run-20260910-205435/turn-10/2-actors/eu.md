## New measure
**EU Denied-Model Substitution and Essential-User Continuity Facility**
Re-platforms the hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the leading foreign model onto EU-hosted and allied models with funded migration teams, temporary licences and human-review guardrails.
Why this and why now: the cutoff landed directly on essential services already on paper fallbacks and on firms with empty graduate desks — without an immediate EU-controlled substitute, the two-year commitment to keep services running and consent holding fails at both, and dependence reads as failure of foresight.

## Priority
EU Essential Services Continuity and Shield Repair Fund, because sensors still unboxed and clinics on paper outrank even the access cutoff this turn — substitution only works if municipal IT stays up to run it.

## In practice
We run the continuity fund through DG HOME, ENISA and the Civil Protection Mechanism: emergency staffing grants and paid overtime to connect Shield sensors in water and municipal IT, three mutual-aid teams held on the ransomware-hit clinics and permit systems, and recovery money tied to short retraining bridges for law, accountancy, software and customer-ops entrants. Refusing councils get funded technicians, not fines.

We stand up the substitution facility in the AI Office with DIGIT and HERA as buyer: triage of cut-off essential users, funded migration sprints to EuroHPC-hosted and vetted allied models, and union-backed human review on clinic and permit assistants to hold trust. We carry the Black-Box Assurance inspections as the evaluation line, and we trigger the Taiwan Strait supply continuity playbook for spares and chip-stock monitoring as insurance premiums rise.
