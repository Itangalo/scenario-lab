### Cut off
The notice arrived on a Tuesday morning as an error message. Doctors in Rotterdam, officials in Warsaw and logistics planners in Lyon found the leading American model refusing European credentials, with no explanation and no channel for appeal. Within days workarounds circulated: older versions, mirrored open systems, local copies of the showcase triage helpers.

Brussels told hospitals and ministries to switch to the continuity stack kept on maintenance money. The switch mostly worked, but slowly. Imaging queues that had shortened lengthened again. Firms that had built products on the foreign service scrambled to re-plumb them. Trade officials logged the move as coercion, but proposed no new fund.

Cohesion held — no capital broke ranks for a separate supply deal — yet the mood turned bitter. Commentators called it a failure of foresight as much as an outrage, and siting talks for the two frozen build sites grew harder as regional councils asked why they should give water and power for promises.

### A clearer glass box
In the same semester, researchers published an interpretability and control result that actually transferred to deployed systems: failure modes that could be predicted before they appeared, properties that could be checked rather than asserted. American and Chinese labs adopted it quickly, and the EU evaluation institute pushed it into guidance for hospitals and cyber monitors.

It helped at the margin. Monitoring teams could say more about what their systems would not do. It did not restore the lost access.

### The plateau
Meanwhile the white-collar story settled. Studies from law firms, accountancies and newsrooms showed solid productivity gains, strongest for juniors, with no wave of layoffs. For workers it was relief. For investors and planners who had borrowed against endless transformation, it was a disappointment. Hiring resumed cautiously, automation budgets were trimmed, and talk of imminent displacement faded into grumbling about subscriptions.

By June Europe was running, but on thinner reserves: clinics functioning, agencies watching, builders waiting, all aware the floor under the race had been poured elsewhere and one of its planks just pulled away.
