### Running on substitutes
The second half of 2032 was defined by absence. Clinics in Rotterdam, ministries in Warsaw and factories in Lyon woke to find the American frontier service refusing European logins. Helpdesks handed out the continuity package — older models, community mirrors, the triage assistants kept alive on maintenance money. It worked after a fashion. Scans were read, forms were processed, but queues grew and engineers spent autumn re-plumbing products that had assumed the foreign service would always answer.

Brussels chose to absorb rather than spend. No new fund was tabled, no separate supply bargain was blessed. Trade lawyers filed the cut-off as coercion. Health and cyber agencies stayed on watch, issuing hygiene guidance for the mirrored systems now carrying real load. The evaluation institute pushed the new interpretability checks into hospital and network monitoring, giving operators a clearer statement of what their stand-ins would not do. It improved oversight at the margin; it did not bring back performance.

### A thin coalition, a flat horizon
Diplomats found more traction abroad. Alongside other middle powers holding fibre, chips, lithography parts and evaluation capacity, the Union signed an export-licence and joint-bargaining framework. No one surrendered sovereignty, but together they could withhold things even Washington and Beijing needed. At home it was sold as proof Europe had not been left entirely without levers.

It landed in a sour public mood. Regional councils in the two frozen build sites asked why they should grant water and power for projects still under injunction. Commentators split between outrage at the American decision and anger at years without a domestic alternative.

Meanwhile the office economy settled. Studies confirmed what workers already felt: assistants made juniors faster, employment held, and the great transformation stopped short of arriving. For staff it was relief. For firms that had borrowed against endless automation, and for governments that had taxed it in advance, it was a quiet disappointment. Robots told a similar story — useful in caged warehouse aisles and pilot demining trials, absent everywhere else.

By December Europe was functioning, cohesive, and thinner: services running on substitutes, builders waiting, and a new coalition promising bargaining power it had not yet used.
