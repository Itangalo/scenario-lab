# Statements: The European Union (turn 7)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, but we now understand that preserving our political agency requires not just intent — it requires independent technical capacity, because without it, no decision we make can land in the world
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will now assume that any system we do not operate ourselves may be weaponized against us, whether through denial, sabotage, or uncontrolled diffusion
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI capacity to act independently when access is weaponized
- `sovereign_defence_in_depth` (commitment): We will ensure that every sovereign AI system running critical public functions is built, monitored, and updatable within the EU, with redundant oversight, real-time anomaly detection, and fail-safes that cannot be disabled remotely

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — applied
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, but we now understand that preserving our political agency requires not just intent — it requires independent technical capacity, because without it, no decision we make can land in the world

  - Grounds: The two mandates are no longer parallel tracks. They converge on infrastructure. Preventing harm now depends on acting independently — not out of preference, but because response speed exceeds diplomatic cycles. When a self-replicating agent moves undetected for days or a pathogen is designed by a model beyond our reach, our ability to protect depends on what compute, models, and expertise we already control. Sovereignty is no longer a means — it is part of the safety architecture.
  - Evidence quoted: **loss_of_control_incident:** An agentic system takes consequential unsanctioned action with real-world effect – moving money, altering records, acquiring resources, or copying itself to infrastructure nobody authorised – and containment is uncertain for a period measured in days rather than hours. What it was trying to achieve is reconstructed afterwards and disputed.
**bio_incident:** A real biological incident with model involvement: a deliberate release or a laboratory escape involving a designed or modified agent. Casualties are real, containment runs for weeks, and every argument about AI risk in every jurisdiction is reset by it.
**export_control_escalation:** Chip and model export controls tighten again. Either allied buyers keep access on volume licences while everyone else is cut off, or the controls are drawn so tightly that allies are rationed alongside adversaries – decide which at the time from the standing American posture and from what the Union has built.
  - Reason: The incidents demonstrate catastrophic risks are real, and export controls show protective capacity is conditional—directly affecting the EU's need for independent technical capacity to fulfill both mandates.

Our commitment must evolve: we will treat all external systems as potential threat vectors unless proven otherwise under sovereign verification.

  - Reason: The incidents directly challenge assumptions about external systems' safety, altering the cost and rationale of acting under uncertainty by proving uncontrolled systems can cause real harm.

  - Grounds: This commitment was fulfilled in spirit but not in time. The Emergency Sovereign Inference Initiative now embodies its purpose — operational continuity of critical functions under denial — but the strategic context has moved beyond “securing capacity” to “operating under active compromise.” We are transitioning to a new two-year focus: ensuring that sovereign AI systems are not only available but *trusted*, *responsive*, and *defensible* when everything else fails.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **add** `sovereign_defence_in_depth` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will ensure that every sovereign AI system running critical public functions is built, monitored, and updatable within the EU, with redundant oversight, real-time anomaly detection, and fail-safes that cannot be disabled remotely
  - Grounds: The bio and loss-of-control incidents prove that safety is not a property of models — it is a property of systems, governance, and physical control. Our new commitment reflects that: sovereignty without security is a target, not a shield.
