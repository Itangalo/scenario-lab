## In practice
The **eu_frontier_access_denied** incident was not just a supply shock — it exposed a strategic illusion. We assumed redundancy through diversity of providers; instead, we found single points of failure baked into hospital diagnostics, migration processing, and grid optimization. This turn, we complete the Emergency Sovereignty Directive: the Semiconductor Allocation Board has issued its first binding triage orders, rerouting 17% of incoming 3nm shipments to the French-German sovereign AI consortium and ENISA’s red-teaming facility. Germany and France have accepted full compliance; Poland and Hungary, though still uneasy, are receiving priority delivery of hardened edge-compute units for their cyber defence nodes in exchange for non-blocking in COREPER.  

With compute rationed, we now face the next frontier: code. The openweight release at 58.0 capability runs autonomously on consumer GPUs, evading all export controls and audit regimes tied to cloud providers. Our only leverage lies in the researchers who can replicate, probe, and preempt it. That is why we launch the Sovereign Red-Teaming Grid under DG CONNECT and CNECT, funded through a reallocation of €9.2bn from the stalled parts of the Tech Sovereignty Package. We are convening a high-level group in Brussels next month — including IMEC, Fraunhofer, INRIA, and ISTA — to define secure collaboration protocols and carve out legal liability shields for simulated attacks on open models. Austria and Finland have pledged immediate participation; Sweden hesitates, fearing spillover into private innovation, so we are offering them co-leadership of the evaluation working group and guaranteed access to all declassified findings.  

This is no longer about catching up. It is about containment, continuity, and credible deterrence: showing that even when cut off, the EU can still see, assess, and respond to threats that others set loose. The Resilience Facility’s drills proceed, but now with a narrower hardware envelope — ENISA has downgraded two eastern member state exercises to tabletop simulations due to GPU shortages. We cannot afford another layer of fragility. The Red-Teaming Grid must become our eyes in the dark.
