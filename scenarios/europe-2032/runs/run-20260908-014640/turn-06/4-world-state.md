### The Frontier Crosses 60  
Global AI capability advances to 60.5, crossing the symbolic 60.0 threshold where systems now autonomously execute multi-day professional projects. Developers report early signs of self-improvement loops in internal testing. The jump accelerates openweight diffusion: a powerful open release, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times, begins spreading through research and developer networks. Deployment lags constrain impact, but openweight_capability rises to 50.0 — still trailing the frontier, with integration into production systems only beginning.

### US Posture Confirmed: Consolidation  
The new US administration takes office, formalising its AI posture: frontier access is now tiered, with Europe placed in a conditional partner tier. While not fully cut off, access to the most advanced models will be rationed, performance-limited, and subject to real-time compliance checks. The move confirms fears of strategic dependency.

### EU Responds with Emergency Inference Initiative  
In response, the European Commission proposes the Emergency Sovereign Inference Initiative, a legally mandated fallback to run critical public AI services — diagnostics, grid management, border processing — on sovereign infrastructure using hardened open models. The proposal is fast-tracked, but full funding and legal adoption require approval under the ordinary legislative procedure, delaying implementation. Initial planning begins in Lyon, Warsaw, and Valencia under ENISA coordination, though Germany and France push for shared training oversight while Poland and Hungary demand local control, complicating integration.

### Sovereignty Erodes Further  
Despite the new proposal, eu_ai_sovereignty drops to 17.0. The capability rise costs −1; anticipated access restrictions under the new US posture count as an active capacity threat, applying −2 under rule 5. The finished Tech Sovereignty Package yields no sovereignty bonus — its private investment targets were underdelivered, and permitting delays persist.

### Political Capital Drains  
Political capital falls to 14.0. The portfolio charge is −10 (three large measures, one priority). A +3 proposal bonus applies: the new initiative directly answers both export_control_escalation and the de facto access denial. No legitimacy boost from sentiment. The net leaves capital below 15, weakening the Union’s ability to drive consensus.

### Safety and Sentiment Stagnate  
No safety improvements land on deployed systems. Resilience holds, but only just — drills are underfunded, and GPU shortages persist. Public sentiment remains at 23.0, trapped in anxiety over job losses and institutional instability.
