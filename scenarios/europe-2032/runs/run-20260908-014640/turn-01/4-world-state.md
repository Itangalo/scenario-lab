### A Summer of Shocks  
The second half of 2026 ends in crisis. A stealthy intrusion across critical infrastructure — power grids, a port, a water utility — reveals that AI-driven attacks can now evade all known detection. The breach, discovered by accident, exposes a dangerous gap: even the most advanced human-led audits failed to see what AI built. Public trust wavers as reports detail how attackers moved laterally for weeks. Simultaneously, a sudden collapse in AI investment cancels major compute projects Europe relied on, shrinking the frontier’s capacity and delaying access to next-generation models.

### The EU’s Response: Cyber Shield  
In response, the European Commission launches the Cyber Shield for Critical Infrastructure, a large-scale directive under NIS2, mandating AI-powered intrusion detection and autonomous red-teaming for essential services. Germany and the Nordics back the move, but resistance emerges from southern and eastern states wary of cost and centralisation. To ease tensions, the EU proposes a Resilience Facility and a Rapid Response Corps of seconded cyber experts. However, no final agreement is reached this turn — the directive remains in early negotiation, and the Facility’s funding mechanism is not yet specified, pending debate on budget reallocations and potential borrowing frameworks. Implementation will take time, with no immediate operational impact.

Meanwhile, independent open-source security researchers, alarmed by the breach, launch a volunteer-driven audit of critical AI systems. While technically robust and gaining public attention, this effort remains uncoordinated with EU institutions and operates outside formal governance — a grassroots response, not a policy measure. The ai_investment_collapse undermines the Tech Sovereignty Package and Gigafactories, as private capital vanishes. Sovereignty erodes: the Union remains dependent, and rising capability without matching safety drags ai_safety down. Public sentiment dips as job fears and security anxieties grow, undercutting political capital despite the urgent response.
