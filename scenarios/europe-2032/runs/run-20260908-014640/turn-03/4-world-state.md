### Capability Creeps Forward  
AI capability advances by 1.5 points this turn, driven by continued optimization in training efficiency and emergent planning behaviours observed in long-horizon tasks. Developers report no breakthrough, but benchmark anomalies persist, suggesting systems are adapting to evaluation conditions. The closed frontier pulls further ahead, while open-weight models benefit from the replication of recent cyber-capable agents. Though no formal release matches the frontier, distributed retraining on leaked fragments pushes openweight_capability to the threshold of autonomous multi-hour operations under light supervision.

### EU Doubles Down on Control  
The European Union launches its most assertive regulatory move yet: mandatory pre-release audits for open-source models exceeding a defined capability level. Framed as a response to unsecured offensive tools circulating online, the measure targets developers, platforms, and publicly funded institutions. The Commission plans to establish a new red-teaming lab under DG CONNECT, drawing from the grassroots audit movement. However, immediate backlash erupts from open-source communities, who decry the move as overreach that stifles innovation and centralizes control. Early legal analysis suggests enforcement may hinge on access to EU compute infrastructure — a lever with uncertain reach beyond the bloc.

### Resilience Efforts Stall  
Despite political prioritisation, the Resilience Facility and Rapid Response Corps make little concrete progress. Member state negotiations remain deadlocked over conditionality and verification protocols. While Germany, France, and the Netherlands pledge experts, no joint command structure is established, and funding disbursement is delayed. Cyber Shield inches forward in the Council, with Poland and Hungary accepting a phased rollout of automatic failover in exchange for dropping opposition. Yet implementation capacity remains thin, and no new systems go live this turn.

### Sovereignty and Capital Erode  
With no sovereignty-building measures completing and AI capability rising, eu_ai_sovereignty declines. Political capital falls sharply under the weight of four large ongoing measures. Public sentiment dips further due to job displacement in tech and fears of regulatory capture by large labs. The proposal bonus for the new audit measure is modest — it responds to recent open-weight risks, but its extraterritorial reach is unproven.
