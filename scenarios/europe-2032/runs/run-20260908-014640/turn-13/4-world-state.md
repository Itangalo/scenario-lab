### The Hollow Victory

The EU formally completes three cornerstone resilience measures: a secure execution environment for critical public AI systems, a Joint AI Incident Response Corps, and an Emergency Override Authority. On paper, the Union is more prepared than ever. But in practice, the architecture of control fractures at the edges. In Poland and Hungary, national gatekeepers refuse to activate override protocols, citing sovereignty concerns. The Response Corps, though certified, cannot deploy across borders without host-state consent—rendering rapid response a fiction where it is most needed. The gap between legal design and operational reality widens into a chasm.

### Open Weights, Closing Windows

Amid technical stagnation at the closed frontier—evidenced by underwhelming model releases and falling prices—a major open-weight release lands within months of the latest closed models. Overnight, capabilities once confined to secure labs spread across private hardware. The open frontier now reaches 59.0, narrowing the gap to the closed frontier and accelerating misuse risks. Biosecurity circles erupt in debate after a genome model produces a plausible human-infective design, though the study is contested. The signal is clear: open systems are nearing dangerous thresholds.

### Sovereignty’s Divergence

With `ai_capability` rising by only 0.5, resilience gains +3 from the completed category 6 measures. But `ai_safety` remains stagnant at 0.0, failing to keep pace. The EU cancels the Emergency Labour Transition Floor, unable to overcome fiscal resistance and legal hurdles. Public sentiment holds at rock bottom, reflecting disillusionment with institutional fragmentation.

The European Sovereignty Integration Guarantee is proposed as a bold corrective—a large category 8 measure aimed at binding all EU-funded AI infrastructure under collective governance. Germany and France back it; Poland and Hungary threaten legal action. The measure is named priority, but with `eu_political_capital` at 2.0, cohesion frays. Political capital drops further: completed measures yield +5, but ongoing costs total −9, and the new priority adds no pull. The Union finishes the turn weaker, not stronger.
