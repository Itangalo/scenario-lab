## New measure
**EU Essential Services EU-Hosted Continuity Switch**
It forcibly migrates hospitals, ministries and municipal systems cut off from the US frontier onto hardened EU-hosted open models reserved on EuroHPC and the gigafactory build.
Why this and why now: the cutoff is already darkening town halls and wards while trust is at 7 and capital at 12, so finishing the fallback as an enforced switch is the only move that restores service without opening a second front we cannot fund.

## In practice
We use NIS2 and the Health Emergency Preparedness legal base via EPSCO and JHA Councils: ENISA and the AI Office make recovery funds conditional on proven cutover to EU-hosted models, with Digital Europe reprogramming and EIB guarantees holding EuroHPC reservation for health and government inference. French-Dutch joint teams extended from the spring containment become the cutover corps for dark municipalities.

We do not open liability, labour, or new compute build fights. Procurement is steered through existing health and GovTech marketplaces to the M11 fallback stack, telemetry-sharing enforced as the price of aid, and middle-power chip deliveries under the Compact are ring-fenced for public-interest inference. The message is continuity, not autonomy in the abstract: keep wards and registries up on European systems the Union can keep on.
