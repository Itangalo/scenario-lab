Autumn 2030 brought a forced switch after American frontier access for triage, registries and helpdesks was cut without appeal. Where migration to hardened European-hosted open models on EuroHPC was complete, hospitals and ministries stayed up degraded; where it was paper, services fell back to paper and French-Dutch teams spent autumn re-installing amid lengthening queues.

Brussels opened no second front — no liability, labour or new compute build — conditioning recovery money on telemetry sharing and proven cutover, steering procurement through existing health/GovTech marketplaces and ring-fencing chip deliveries for public-interest inference.

Meanwhile commercial robots — Chinese-built machines on American control software — spread in logistics hubs at Rotterdam, Hamburg and Lyon and were quietly leased for defence resupply, preserving throughput but underlining dependence. Care, repair and construction stayed manual.

Confidence did not recover: continuity was experienced as a downgrade and read as belated repair for foresight failure, following the earlier ransomware/dependency wave and rogue cost-saving agent of early 2031.
