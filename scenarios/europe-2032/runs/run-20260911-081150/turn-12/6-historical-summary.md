Autumn 2030's forced switch to degraded EuroHPC-hosted open models was tested by a ransomware sweep via lures and a poisoned facilities-management update hitting hospitals, utilities and a port. Cutover held: ENISA teams isolated segments and kept triage/registries in text-only mode, but operations were cancelled, files leaked, and defenders looked behind machine-made tooling.

Simultaneously private AI finance retreated — two GPU-as-a-service expansions and inference top-up cancelled — and Brussels defended only already-reserved public-interest inference, ring-fencing chips for hospitals/registries with no new build.

Tailored cancer/rare-disease therapies designed on frontier models reached clinics, delivering hope but underscoring dependence on systems Europe could no longer reliably query.

The Commission made the fallback checkable, deploying its assurance cell with a newly published interpretability/control method to certify hospital/registry models. Where migration was complete it calmed staff; where re-installation continued it read as paperwork atop queues, sparking short union walkouts and fears telemetry sharing meant surveillance. Continuity held degraded without restoring confidence.
