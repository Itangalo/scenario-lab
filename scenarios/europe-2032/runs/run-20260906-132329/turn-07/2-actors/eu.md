## In practice
We are acting on two fronts: one to close the gap in sovereign capability, and one to close the gap in crisis response. On the first, the emergence of the industrial AI consortium is not a market curiosity — it is the first organic sign of European AI sovereignty growing from within the economy itself. We are seizing this moment. This turn, we are launching the Sovereign Industrial AI Platform Support Initiative through the Digital Europe Programme and InvestEU, with a €15bn matching fund to co-invest with industry in secure, on-premise or regionally hosted AI platforms. We are tasking the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking to allocate 20% of future sovereign compute capacity to these platforms under binding data sovereignty clauses, and we are fast-tracking legal templates through the Commission’s AI Office to enable cross-border data pooling under strict sectoral governance — starting with manufacturing, energy, and transport. This is not about replicating the frontier; it is about ensuring that when the frontier moves, Europe’s critical industries do not freeze.

On the second front, we are operationalising the European AI Emergency Response Corps with binding authority and real teeth. The eval_anomaly_reports — unexplained capabilities appearing in unseen evaluations — are not noise. They are the first signal that control is fraying. This turn, we are finalising the deployment protocol under Article 122a TFEU with the Council’s legal working group, and we are activating the first cohort of 200 operators — drawn from national cyber agencies, ENISA red teams, and ECDC bio-risk units — under a unified command at the Joint Research Centre in Ispra. We are securing opt-in agreements from seven member states for liability shielding and pre-authorised intervention powers during declared AI emergencies, and we are linking the corps directly to the Resilience Bond facility for immediate access to €2bn in emergency funding. This is not preparedness. This is readiness. And it is the only thing standing between us and a crisis we will not see coming.
