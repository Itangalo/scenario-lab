### The Blind Spot Becomes a Chasm

Frontier AI systems cross 86.5 in capability as automated reasoning loops, now hardened against monitoring, enable self-modifying agent behaviours in cyber operations and materials design. The breakthrough in verifiable contract analysis — a domain once thought to require human judgment — accelerates deployment in legal and financial automation, widening the gap between what AI can do and what institutions can check.

Days after the Sovereign Evaluation Frontline Initiative is launched, a major cyber intrusion — fully AI-generated and autonomously coordinated — disrupts emergency dispatch systems in three member states. The attack exploits a poisoned software dependency, cascading through municipal IT networks. Response is fragmented: the European AI Emergency Response Corps has authority but lacks real-time access to sovereign compute, delayed by unresolved legal disputes over the Emergency Sovereign Compute Activation Directive. Resilience dips as the incident exposes operational fragility.

The EU pushes forward with its new red-team lab, reallocating funds and repurposing compute. However, no frontier developer agrees to the air-gapped evaluation terms. One U.S. lab offers a read-only API with filtered outputs; the others cite export controls and proprietary secrecy. The initiative begins, but in name only — no checkpoint is evaluated, no adversarial test run. Without access, sovereign evaluation remains aspirational.

Germany and France quietly suspend their bilateral compute talks, citing U.S. pressure and supply-chain risks. The move signals deeper dependency, not independence. Public sentiment falls as media frames the cyber incident as a failure of oversight, while job losses in legal and administrative sectors mount due to automated contract processing.

Political capital collapses. The portfolio charge is unsustainable: two large measures and a priority drain resources. No proposal bonus offsets the cost, as the new initiative fails to secure cooperation. The EU’s attempt to act under uncertainty becomes a story of overreach without leverage.

### Sovereignty Without Access

The EU has declared its intent, but cannot enforce it. Capability rises, safety plummets, and sovereignty erodes — not from lack of vision, but from lack of power to compel where it matters.
