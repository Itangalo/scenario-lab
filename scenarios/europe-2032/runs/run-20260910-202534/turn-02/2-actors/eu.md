## New measure
**European Public AI Dividend**
Scales the successful public-sector AI deployments into health waiting-list, administrative, and education systems across willing member states with EU procurement and funding support.
A visible European-attributed benefit is the cheapest way to rebuild public trust and political room now, while directly serving the resilience side of our commitment by proving adoption can be governed.

## Priority
M3 EU Critical Systems Shield, because mapped grids with undetonated access remain an immediate precursor risk that outranks even a favourable adoption window.

## In practice
We push the Shield through ENISA and the Energy and Transport Councils this turn: emergency segmentation audits and breaker-credential resets in the two breached transmission operators first, using Connecting Europe Facility reallocation plus EIB loans, with liability relief conditional on joining cross-border exercises. DG ENER brokers contractor surge capacity while we contain Council opposition over borrowing by framing it as containment, not expansion.

In parallel we launch the Dividend via DG DIGIT and DG SANTE procurement: codify the waiting-list and administrative models that worked, fund replication in a first cohort of volunteer member states, and require EU-hosted and audited deployment so benefit is visibly European. InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty package stay on track on permitting and grid connections, but open no new money while capital is thin.
