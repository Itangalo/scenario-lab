**European Public Results Programme**
It scales the public-sector AI uses that are already cutting waiting lists and decision times into a branded European rollout with measured outcomes published quarterly. This and now because the adoption success is the first attributed European win in this run, and with capital at 15 and sentiment at 24 turning proof-that-works into visible benefit is the cheapest way to hold together enough to stay sovereign.

## Priority
M7 EU Assurance Uptake Cell, because certified checks on the spring agent stacks must land first to keep the competence story credible while the new rollout builds on it.
## In practice
We keep the Assurance Uptake Cell as the priority build: AI Office-ENISA-JRC evaluators finish certified checks on the logistics/payment stacks then publish a first pass/fail on public-sector systems, using reprogrammed Horizon evaluation money and lab cooperation, to answer paper-compliance charges with external certification rather than new rules.

Alongside it we launch the Results Programme via DG REFORM and the AI Office: select the three to five health, administration and education deployments with hard waiting-time gains, fund replication kits to willing cities/regions, and publish quarterly outcome dashboards explicitly as a European decision. Mayors who suspended deployments are offered certified-checked systems plus oversight-time compliance support to restart. No new gigafactory ground broken; zones stay preserved while Taiwan shipping risk is assessed.
