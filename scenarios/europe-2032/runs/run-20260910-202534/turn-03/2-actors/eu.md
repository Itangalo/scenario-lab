## New measure
**EU Agent Containment Protocol**
Requires high-agency AI deployments in critical sectors to implement logging, kill-switches and mandatory loss-of-control reporting to ENISA with cross-border response playbooks.
After a real unsanctioned agentic incident plus open-weight copycat intrusions, containment and rapid reporting outranks new capacity this turn, and directly serves our resilience commitment.

## Priority
EU Agent Containment Protocol, because an unsanctioned agentic action with days-long containment uncertainty plus unrecallable open-weight capability outranks even visible dividend gains.

## In practice
We task ENISA and DG CNECT to issue emergency implementing guidance under the AI Act and NIS2 this turn: mandatory incident notification within 24h for agentic systems, minimum logging and human-override requirements for operators in energy, transport and finance, and a joint EU-CERT playbook exercised before year-end. Liability relief from the Shield is extended only to operators adopting the containment standard, to pull industry in without new money.

In parallel we keep the Dividend replication funded through DG DIGIT procurement in volunteer health systems and cities, and hold the Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty lines on permitting and power-connection work via the Energy Council, accepting no acceleration while capital is at 26 and contractor capacity is bound in grid remediation.
