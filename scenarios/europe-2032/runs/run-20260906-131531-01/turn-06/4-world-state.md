### The Weight of Motion

The EU pushes forward on two fronts: operationalising the European AI Emergency Response Corps and launching a mandatory biosecurity audit regime. Despite legal finalisation under Article 122a TFEU, only six member states ratify the corps’ cross-border intervention powers by mid-year, delaying full deployment. Recruitment begins, but technical talent is scarce—many qualified specialists are under contract with private labs. The Resilience Bond draw is approved, but implementation lags.

The new audit regime faces immediate resistance. U.S. developers argue it duplicates internal red teaming and threatens IP. Meta signals partial compliance; OpenAI and Anthropic refuse to submit to public adversarial testing, citing national security directives from Washington. Negotiations stall, and the European Commission prepares infringement proceedings. Still, the mere proposal forces a global conversation on bio-risk thresholds.

### Capability Leaps, Safety Falls

A major AI lab releases a model with self-improving training loops, triggering a **capability_jump**. `ai_capability` jumps +4.0 to 76.0. The system generates novel biochemical pathways indistinguishable from human design—without interpretable logic. This leap widens the chasm between capability and control. No new safeguards land on shipped systems, and the jump itself inflicts a −5 to `ai_safety`, dropping it to 8.0.

`openweight_capability` rises to 58.0, trailing the new frontier. The leaked biosecurity assessment—confirming a pathogen could be built for under €100,000—remains confined to closed channels but fuels urgency in Brussels.

### Strain and Setback

Resilience improves slightly (+2) as pilot nodes in the BioShield Initiative go live and ENISA expands monitoring. But `eu_ai_sovereignty` drops to 14.0: `ai_capability` rose by more than 2, costing −1, and no sovereignty-boosting measure finished. The portfolio remains overloaded.

Political capital erodes further. Four large measures are in flight, costing −12; the priority adds −1. No measure finishes, so no bonus. The proposal bonus for the biosecurity regime is judged +2—modest, given its indirect link to the bio leak—but insufficient to offset costs. `eu_political_capital` falls to 9.0.

Public sentiment drops to 29.0 as news of the pathogen blueprint leaks to *Der Spiegel*, sparking protests near data centres. Trust erodes further.
