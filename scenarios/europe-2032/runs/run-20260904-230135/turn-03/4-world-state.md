### The Plateau That Wasn’t  
Despite signs of a capability plateau—underwhelming releases and falling prices—global `ai_capability` climbs to 61.5, confirming the surge is not over. The apparent stall was a lull before the next wave, as labs integrate autonomous agent coordination into full-stack research pipelines. Open-weight models make progress: partial blueprints leak from a frontier lab, and the open community begins adapting components for distributed training. This momentum lifts `openweight_capability` to 49.0, reflecting meaningful but incremental gains. While open systems are approaching the performance of closed models from late 2025, they remain behind the current frontier and depend on lagged, fragmented knowledge.

### EU Doubles Down on Control  
The EU proposes the Fast-Track Sovereign AI Compute Initiative, aiming to repurpose defence and emergency infrastructure for sovereign AI clusters by turn 5. The plan is approved in principle, with high-level mandates issued to EuroHPC and national defence agencies to assess technical feasibility and legal constraints. Binding access guarantees are promised pending resolution of disputes with two member states over national sovereignty. However, no physical repurposing occurs this turn—only planning, legal review, and interagency coordination are underway. As a high-cost infrastructure measure, implementation will require at least two full turns, with material deployment expected no sooner than turn 5.

Critical infrastructure hardening progresses slowly—technical designs advance, but binding rollout lags. The EAAD strengthens its interim role, drafting red-team protocols and onboarding nuclear and intelligence experts. Yet, without full legal authority, it cannot compel access to frontier models. The WTO threat over the hyperscaler levy looms, and private investors remain wary.

### Safety Erodes Further, Capital Tightens  
With `ai_capability` rising 2.5 points and no safety measure completed, `ai_safety` drops to 17.0—now deep in the “no meaningful assurance” zone. Autonomous agents operate in opaque loops, and no mechanism exists to audit their long-term behaviour. Resilience inches up (+1) as planning matures, but no systemic upgrades are live.

Political capital declines to 38.0. Three large measures and a priority strain capacity (−10 total). The new compute initiative earns no proposal bonus—while ambitious, it doesn’t directly respond to a recent event. Public sentiment holds at 42.0, reflecting continued ambivalence.

### Sovereignty Gains Ground  
`eu_ai_sovereignty` rises to 25.0: the new initiative counts as forward momentum (+1), InvestAI Gigafactories continue in flight (+1), and the capability rise costs −1. No access event occurred, so no additional term applies.
