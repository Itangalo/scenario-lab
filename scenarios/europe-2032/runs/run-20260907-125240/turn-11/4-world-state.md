### Institutionalising Containment

The European Union formally launched the **European Agency for AI Assurance and Control**, transforming the temporary success of the Autonomous System Boundary Trials into a standing authority. Based in Vienna with technical nodes in Paris, Berlin, and Amsterdam, the agency assumes responsibility for pre-deployment evaluations and continuous monitoring of high-risk AI systems. With €620 million in co-funding and 350 staff—half technically trained and security-cleared—the agency begins onboarding teams and setting up audit protocols. Developers are offered liability safe harbours in exchange for full transparency, but pushback persists from major frontier labs citing competitive risk and procedural overreach.

Both the **Edge Sovereignty Acceleration Programme** and the **Autonomous System Boundary Trials** concluded this turn. The edge programme successfully demonstrated neuromorphic systems in energy grids and emergency networks across six member states, reducing cloud dependency despite chip shortages. The trials yielded a verified containment protocol for goal-persistent agents, now codified into upcoming AI Act amendments.

### Fragile Foundations

Despite these advances, public sentiment remains stagnant at 22.0. Grassroots resistance to AI in democratic processes intensifies, with protests demanding moratoriums on algorithmic campaign tools. The annulment of a national election continues to cast a long shadow, reinforcing perceptions of systemic vulnerability.

AI capability climbed to 69.5, nearing the 70 threshold, driven by longer-horizon training runs and improved agent coordination. Openweight capability rose to 57.0 as adaptive cyber tools spread through underground developer networks. Resilience held at 43.0—no new protective measures landed, and uneven defences persist in healthcare and local government.

AI safety improved slightly, reflecting the institutionalisation of trial insights. Sovereignty gained modestly from completed edge infrastructure and ongoing capability development. Political capital rose, boosted by the closure of two major initiatives and the launch of a high-stakes, justified measure.
