## New measure
**EU Displaced Entry-Level Transition Guarantee**
Pays wage insurance, rapid retraining and municipal hiring bridges for graduates and support workers displaced in routine coding, analysis, drafting and warehouse logistics, funded by a levy-linked transition envelope tied to automating employers.
This and now because the layoff freeze, embodied warehouse rollout and open-weight cyber sweep have collapsed sentiment to zero and capital to six — without visible cushioning for young workers the hardening commitment loses its coalition; it is category 7 Labour and social protection, small and executable within existing ESF+ and SURE-type instruments.

## Priority
EU Displaced Entry-Level Transition Guarantee, because with no measures in flight it is the only lever to rebuild trust after the decision scandal and job freeze and to hold the two-year hardening-plus-cushioning commitment.

## In practice
We run this through EMPL Council and ESF+ emergency reprogramming: DG EMPL opens a single claims window alongside the cyber recovery window, so mayors claim patching and payroll-bridge money together. PES agencies register frozen-out graduates for 6-month paid reskilling in cyber-hygiene, care and repair trades that robots do not take, with wage insurance for firms that retain and retrain.

We pair it with a moratorium response to the automated-decision scandal: suspend the offending social-insurance/policing scoring uses pending AI Act conformity re-audit by the AI Office, publish logs, and require meaningful human review time standards. No new vote: we use AI Act Article 79 incident powers and ESF+ flexibility, offering fiscally strained states 100% EU co-financing for the first year to stop a blocking minority.
