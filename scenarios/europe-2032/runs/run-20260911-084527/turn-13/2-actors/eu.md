## New measure
**EU Survival Triage and Defection Containment Pact**
Keeps lights, clinics, payrolls and health screening running through combined pandemic absenteeism and automated cyber-attack by mutualising scarce staff, spares and clean backups, while buying off the defecting member state with full EU co-financing to stay inside common procurement.
This and now because with political capital near zero and public sentiment collapsed the Union cannot start anything large — only a small, visible continuity pact executed under Civil Protection and HERA emergency powers can stop fragmentation and cascading failure; it is Preparedness and resilience work, category 6.

## Priority
EU Survival Triage and Defection Containment Pact, because it is the only measure in flight and pandemic plus cyber failure outranks all other direction.

## In practice
We run this from the Emergency Response Coordination Centre and HERA: daily situational lists from ECDC and ENISA, cross-border pools for nurses, grid operators and IT recovery teams, EU-funded reserve rosters at 100% co-financing from reprogrammed EU4Health, ESF+ and Recovery funds. Clean offline backups, manual fallback procedures and islanding kits already delivered are re-issued to municipalities hit by ransomware and dependency compromise.

We contain the defection by letting the defecting government keep its bilateral supply line but folding it into joint procurement volumes and surge funding — no public sanction, private Council deal in Health and TTE formations to avoid a veto cascade. Enforcement of AI rules stays paused except welfare scoring, and we openly authorize vetted use of open models for triage logging because technicians already rely on them.
