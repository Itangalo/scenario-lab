### Dark screens, shared feeds
Autumn brought a largely automated ransomware sweep across municipal administrations, clinics and two grid operators. Locked backups and segmented restores, rehearsed since 2029, were pushed out by ENISA teams. Towns that had shared telemetry recovered in days; a handful that had stayed off the common feed stayed dark for weeks, running on printed kits and emergency licences.

Attribution lagged. Forensics pointed to tooling assembled with model help, but no state claimed it and investigators said confirmation would take months. Defenders looked behind, even where recovery worked.

In parallel, a genome-model paper claiming a non-expert could reach a viable human-infecting design leaked into public debate. Virologists fought over methods and accused the authors of both hype and recklessness. Inside health ministries the signal was taken as a precursor, not proof.

Brussels tried to condition what little emergency funding remained on feeding clinic telemetry into the pooled cyber-bio command, but with no new budget voted there was no surge to distribute. The Health Emergency Preparedness Authority's extended hospital sampling was deferred for lack of staff and seconded personnel, proceeding only as pilots in cooperating cities. Accession to the joint command, still disputed over data handling and who can order a disconnect, remained incomplete, and cross-domain coordination between civilian, health and security teams stayed thin and slow.

The civic trust repair effort closed: workload limits, human override and published logs stayed, a few audited triage assistants kept cutting waiting lists in cooperating cities. Nurses cited shorter queues; opposition MPs cited machine-speed approvals and insurers still refused to resume cover without statute.

Chip slots stayed cancelled. Visits to The Hague, Tokyo and Seoul kept the licensing conversation alive without money or binding controls. American labs stayed slow while second-tier builders grew louder, and open releases crept nearer the closed frontier.

Europe ended the year recovering faster than it had in 2029 in places that had prepared, but stretched, under-staffed, and dependent on feeds it does not control.
