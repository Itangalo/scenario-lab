By 2029-2032 Europe endured overlapping cyber-bio crises in degraded mode: ransomware and rogue-agent failures split sharers who restored in days from refusers dark for weeks; designed pathogen and variants kept hospitals full, utilities absent; power/water held via substitution, triage under emergency rules, sequencing continent-wide.

Second half 2032: immune-escape second wave renewed border screening and utility/transport absences; large automated intrusion locked dispatch, clinic admin and grid with machine-written notes — cooperating cities restored from pooled backups in days, formal-refusal towns on paper/generators for weeks, hardening split. Earlier recovery surge paid out: pre-positioned kits, rehearsed restores, central oxygen/PPE/antivirals kept systems substituting; triage extended, disconnects used openly with logs; mood grief, fraud, exhaustion — autonomy gave way to survival.

November Washington-Beijing limited accord on weight security, autonomous-escalation restraint, bio-design screening with thin verification; Brussels informed after, bid observer-to-signatory offering sequencing net and restore logs as verification, got polite interest, no seat. Bid weakened by member-state side deal for privileged foreign cloud model access as pragmatism; Commission offered grandfathering via joint screening or loss of civil-protection priority, government did not reverse.
