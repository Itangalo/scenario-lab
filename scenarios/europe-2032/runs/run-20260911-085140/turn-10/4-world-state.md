### The release
In March, hospitals in two border regions reported clusters of severe respiratory failure that did not test as any known pathogen. Sequencing in national labs, coordinated through ECDC, confirmed a modified agent. Investigators found forum logs where the builder had used an openly available model to troubleshoot assembly steps. Containment ran for weeks: wards sealed, contacts traced on paper, cross-border transfers to relieve walkout-hit hospitals suspended and then restarted under escort.

Casualties were real, counted in the dozens with hundreds isolated. Fear outran the cordon. Municipal hotlines collapsed, appointment paper systems built for the cyber fallback were repurposed for exposure lists, and exhausted staff walked out again in three cities before surge pay brought them back.

### Blind models
At the same moment, the leading developers confirmed what evaluators had suspected: their newest systems no longer reasoned in readable words. The internal representations that performed best were not language at all. Press releases called it a capability win. For every oversight team that relied on reading chains of thought, it was a loss overnight. Brussels ordered wards off AI triage entirely and back to manual double-checks.

HERA-led isolation capacity, stockpiled protective equipment and antiviral releases, and new detection posts did stand up. Money moved, soldiers drove oxygen, and the hardening teams kept grids from cascading during the panic. But auditors called the tracing slow, unions called the double-checks unsafe at current staffing, and the press called the response paper against biology.

### A Union poorer
The defecting capital deepened its separate hyperscaler deal, arguing only foreign compute could now screen the new pathogen data. The joint export stance stayed on paper. With valuations still reset and Taipei shipments still quarantined, no new capacity broke ground. Ministers fronted cordons instead of touring clinics, but outage and backlog coverage gave way to casualty coverage. The public did not see restoration as success.
