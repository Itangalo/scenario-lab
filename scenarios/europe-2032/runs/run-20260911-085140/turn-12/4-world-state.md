### Patching in the dark
The automated attack came as a corrupted software update and a ransomware sweep together. Municipal billing, three regional hospital networks and a payment processor locked in the same week. ENISA teams drove with USB sticks, re-activated the paper lists and oxygen-run playbooks from the cordon period, and kept lights and wards running. Grids degraded but did not stop. Defenders were visibly behind — patches chased variants generated overnight — and no one could name the intruder.

The triage cordons finished in the middle of it. Isolation discipline and manual surge routines prevented the hospital outage from becoming a care collapse, and Brussels claimed a grim competence: services bent, they did not break.

### Cures elsewhere, machines stalled
Clinics elsewhere began offering tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions. European patients read about them, applied for them, and learned access depended on foreign models and foreign compute on licensed terms. Hope arrived as dependence.

Commercial robots, meanwhile, disappointed. Warehouse and logistics pilots worked inside caged aisles and stopped at the dock door. Defence trials for carrying and mine clearance stayed trials. Vendors blamed integration; buyers blamed vendors.

### The chokepoint used against its owner
Washington tightened chip and model controls again and ordered the Dutch lithography supplier to cut servicing of older machines, then to widen the customer ban. The Hague protested and complied; the company could not survive loss of American parts and licences. Brussels logged the move for a future coercion file, issued quiet derogations to keep the supplier alive, and asked for recovery space instead of retaliating. The joint export stance dissolved into bilateral pleading.

The benefits affair ground on. Courts in two member states admitted mass claims and ordered manual re-examination, but EU redress remained frozen, logged and mapped. Restoration crews earned grudging thanks; no one forgave the administration that had cut entitlements by machine.
