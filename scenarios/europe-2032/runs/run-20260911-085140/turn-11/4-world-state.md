### Cordons hold, credibility collapses
HERA's cordon system finished landing in the second half of 2031. Isolation wards, paper exposure lists, escorted transfers and army oxygen runs became routine. Cross-border medical surge funding paid out, backlogs in two border regions cleared slowly, and grids stayed up. Nurses still worked manual double-checks after wards were ordered off machine triage, but surge pay stopped the walkouts. Containment of the modified respiratory agent held at dozens dead, hundreds isolated — a grim, visible restoration.

It was eclipsed by the benefits affair.

Investigative reporters and an ombudsman inquiry showed an automated decision system used in social insurance and policing support had systematically cut entitlements and flagged families for fraud investigation over years. Caseworkers had approved its recommendations at forty seconds an item. Logs existed and were never read.

The Commission chose honesty over cover: the deployment, it admitted, had never been classified as high-risk at all. It sat in a gap between the annex categories. Everything done to those people was lawful under the AI Act as written for the systems of 2024 against the deployments of 2030.

That admission detonated. Courts could not punish what the law had not forbidden. Opposition parties ran against machine administration and won local votes on it. Data-centre protests merged with welfare protests. Ministers at cordons were asked about benefit letters, not beds.

Brussels froze EU-funded rollout of the same use-case, ordered logging preserved for redress, and referred gap-mapping to the Fundamental Rights Agency and the ombudsman. No new enforcement sprint was launched — there was no money or credibility to fund one. The defecting capital's separate foreign-compute deal widened, justified now as pathogen screening and as distance from a discredited rulebook. The joint export stance remained paper.

By December, hospitals functioned manually but the public did not see protection as legitimacy. Restriction became cheap; any new adoption became politically impossible.
