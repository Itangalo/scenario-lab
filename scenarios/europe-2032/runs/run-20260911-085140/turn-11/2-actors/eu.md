## Priority
M11 EU Bio Containment and Care Triage Cordons – because live containment and manual care are the only claim to protection that still reads while the scandal burns and capital sits at 6.

## In practice
We put everything through HERA, ECDC and the Health Security Committee under Articles 168 and 222: funded isolation beds, released PPE and antivirals, ECDC-coordinated sequencing, paper exposure lists, escorted cross-border transfers, and enforced manual double-checks with wards formally off AI triage after the loss of readable chain-of-thought. ENISA reserves stay on grid patching but do not touch clinical decisions. Ministers stay at cordons and surge-pay negotiations, not at tech announcements.

On the automated-decision scandal we choose the second reading and say so: the system was never classified high-risk at all, the deployment sat in a gap the Annex III categories do not reach, and everything done was lawful. That is why we do not launch a new AI Act enforcement sprint we cannot fund or enforce. We refer the file to the Fundamental Rights Agency and the ombudsman for a public gap-mapping, freeze any EU-funded rollout of the same use-case, and order logging preservation for redress, while admitting the Act as written for 2024 systems does not cover 2030 deployments. Restriction is now cheap and adoption is politically impossible – we husband the 6 capital we have rather than spend what we do not have.
