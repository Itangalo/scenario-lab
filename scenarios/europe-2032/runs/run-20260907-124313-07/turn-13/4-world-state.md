### A Surge in Threats, a Strain on Unity

A major AI-powered cyber incident struck public health and energy systems across four member states, exploiting adaptive evasion logic traced to underground toolkits. Response was fragmented—some states contained the breach rapidly using new detection stacks, others faltered. The **Secure Critical Infrastructure** measure concluded successfully, delivering a +3 resilience boost as partial deployment proved its worth in limiting cascading failures.

Despite this, offensive capability surged. A leading lab demonstrated a discontinuous leap in autonomous cyber operations, triggering a **capability_jump**. Global `ai_capability` rose to 73.5. With no safety mechanisms applied to deployed systems, and the **European Agency for AI Assurance and Control** still incomplete, `ai_safety` plummeted to 1.0.

Openweight models showed signs of increased coordination, with dark web monitoring revealing the integration of modular exploit logic from the emergent cyber cartel into several widely shared frameworks. While this marks a concerning evolution, the diffusion remains constrained by the fragmented nature of open releases and the ongoing retrenchment of frontier developers. `openweight_capability` rises to 62.0, reflecting gradual leakage and adaptation, but remains well below frontier `ai_capability`.

The EU launched **Sovereign Compute Corridors**, a large category 4 initiative targeting long-term independence. Legal and grid commitments were drafted, but early talks with hyperscalers revealed resistance to binding access clauses. One member state escalated its bilateral cloud deal, citing emergency preparedness, weakening cohesion.

The **Accelerate Threat-Ready Detection Stack** rollout was named priority, but political capital eroded. The portfolio cost was high, and the cyber incident—originating externally with no prior finished measure to fully blunt it—cost the Union credibility. Still, the public saw tangible upgrades, and a medical breakthrough in AI-designed cancer therapies lifted sentiment to 30.0, despite EU systems lacking full access to the models involved.

### Sovereignty Under Pressure

The US posture of **RETRENCHMENT** persists, slowing global capability growth slightly—but not enough to offset closed-loop advances. No US-China agreement emerged. However, the EU secured a preliminary understanding with a major chipmaker on onshoring training runs, a small but tangible step.

Resilience improved slightly, but the gap between threat and defense widens. Political capital fell under the weight of multiple large measures and faltering unity.

The **emergent_underground_ai_cyber_cartel** continues to evolve, with increased chatter and toolkit sharing observed, but no decisive leap in operational reach has yet been confirmed. The lack of a major spill or coordinated release prevents openweight models from closing the gap rapidly. Any acceleration in their effectiveness remains incremental and within expected diffusion patterns under current conditions.
