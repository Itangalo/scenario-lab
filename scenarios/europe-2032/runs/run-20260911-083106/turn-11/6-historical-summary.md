Early 2031 brought twin shocks: a machine-assisted modified pathogen leaked and spread in two cities, straining wards and sentinels before containment barely held; simultaneously the leading US provider cut off European users, forcing hospitals, ministries and ports onto older/open models. A new foreign release leapt in code/intrusion, enabling break-in code that re-jammed booking and port systems.

Collapse was avoided by continuity without new votes: backup kits, clinic sentinels, and EU stay-until-holding crews distributing paper-to-digital packs and locally hosted open models kept degraded services running. Brussels sought no factory vote or retaliation, only continuity via health/civil-protection budgets; future sites kept warm. Public mood fell to open hostility, crediting crews not the Union.

Autumn 2031 brought endurance, not recovery: joint procurement, civil-protection rosters and municipal open models sustained triage and clearance with lengthened queues. A new openly released near-frontier system was widely downloaded, improving fallback engines while also aiding intruders; two more jamming episodes were cleared overnight. No new Union measure was sought or passable, funds exhausted, priority labels changing no delivery dates. Contagion containment held, but memory was dominated by funerals and the American cutoff.
