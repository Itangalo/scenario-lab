### Holding the line
Brussels asked for almost nothing new, and got it. Health and interior ministers agreed to roll over joint procurement for reagents and test kits and to keep civil-protection medical rosters on emergency footing through 2033. No new fund, no vote on a new instrument. Sentinel laboratories stayed at heightened sampling, and municipal clinics kept running triage and port paperwork on locally hosted openly available models.

Where the fallback worked, it kept working. Several mayors reported shorter waiting lists and faster clearance of backlogs, and took full credit on camera while Commission officials circulated the numbers quietly behind them.

### A recipe debate and a release no one can recall
Two developments darkened that modest success. A contested study in the biosecurity community argued that with current model assistance a non-expert could arrive at a workable design for a human-infecting organism. Methodologists attacked the paper and editors were accused both of alarmism and of publishing too much detail. It never left journals and closed briefings, but ministries told labs not to stand crews down.

At the same time a new openly downloadable release landed close to the closed frontier. It was copied hundreds of thousands of times in days. Hospitals rely on the same freely available models; so, engineers warned, do intruders probing jamming-prone booking software. Maintenance patches to that software went out as routine upkeep, not as a new programme.

### Cures and quiet offices
Counterweights arrived from clinics and offices. Tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions reached ordinary care, procured centrally and delivered through the municipal fallback. Patients noticed; because the underlying models still came from outside on someone else's terms, gratitude mixed with unease about dependence.

Office studies also showed measured productivity gains in law, accountancy, administration and consulting, largest among juniors, without matching job losses. Firms that had cut early quietly rehired. The public mood, still hostile over past deaths and blank screens, lifted slightly where queues fell and treatments arrived, but trust remained near exhaustion.
