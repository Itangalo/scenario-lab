### Backups hold, bodies fall
The hardening pact closed out as designed: clean backups were restored, dependency audits ran, and a few grid operators and hospital wards rehearsed manual fallback. It helped — degraded protocols kept lights and emergency rooms alive through a third automated sweep that locked council systems and contractor billing across several states.

It did not prevent it. Defenders were again visibly behind an attack stitched together by machine-generated tooling, and attribution stalled in classified channels. The international telemetry pool gave Europe faster signatures, not immunity.

Then the biological case broke. A modified pathogen with clear model-assisted design leaked — or was let out — and containment ran for weeks across two member states. Casualties were real, wards sealed, and every argument about what AI oversight is for reset overnight. Therapy lines that had delivered cures kept dispensing, but gratitude vanished from coverage.

### Washington takes the labs
In Washington, the White House placed frontier laboratories under direct state control — cleared officers inside training runs, weights treated as defence articles, foreign customers subject to approval. Brussels learned the terms after the fact. Market-access instruments built for companies suddenly faced a security apparatus.

European requests were reduced to seeking state-to-state briefings on weights security. No new build was started; with funds exhausted, the Commission pushed the graduates guarantee to first payouts — wage-insurance tranches and short conversion courses tied to automating employers. Unions called the cheques late and thin as graduate hiring stayed frozen and streets filled with anger at dark town halls, jobless cohorts and now fever wards.

The American move also slowed the frontier slightly, as compartmentalisation replaced open scaling. Second-place teams elsewhere gained ground in whispers, not yet in products.
