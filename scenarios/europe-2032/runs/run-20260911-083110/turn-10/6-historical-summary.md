Collapse avoided by embedded EU repair crews generalized to standing teams, offline-backup rebuilds and hardened paper-to-digital kits; clinics and ports shifted to EU-hosted open models and simplified packs, mayors credited crews.

No new Gigafactory funding voted, sites kept warm, grid works crept; retaliation over chip-tool servicing curbs shelved, Council asked only to endorse continuity.
