## New measure
**EU Cut-Off Continuity Switch**
Moves hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the leading US model onto EU-hosted open-weight systems and manual fallbacks within weeks, via emergency procurement and ENISA-supported re-platforming.
Category 6 Preparedness and resilience is why now: the cutoff has already landed on essential services, and with capital at 20 and sentiment at 14 we cannot buy back US access — we can only keep services running on instruments we control alone, which is our two-year commitment.

## Priority
EU Cut-Off Continuity Switch, because the denial has already stopped work in hospitals and ministries and holding services running outranks the slower Hold-the-Line build.

## In practice
We run the Switch through the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority and ENISA under the Critical Systems Shield and Hold-the-Line legal base: EU4Health and Digital Europe reprogrammed money funds emergency licences for EU-hosted models, API rewrites, and exercised paper-to-digital fallback in the affected hospitals and ministries. DG-GROW issues a temporary joint-procurement vehicle so municipalities do not each negotiate alone.

We pair this with M6 Hold-the-Line drills: transmission operators and water utilities that lost US model support get the same substitution plus patching funds, while we file the cutoff and the Dutch servicing ban as a joint Anti-Coercion evidence package — no retaliation now, but a public Council statement that rationing by nationality will set procurement terms. We tell stalled-zone mayors the Switch is proof that EU-controlled fallback, not Washington allocations, keeps lights and wards on.
