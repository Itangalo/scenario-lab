## New measure
**EU Trusted Public Services AI Scale-Up**
Puts the working European public-sector AI that cut waiting lists and decision times into rapid replication across health, administration and education via EU procurement and deployment funding.
This now because the one visible European success must be scaled to lift sentiment from 7 and break the municipal freeze pact with tangible local benefit, while the loss-of-control incident makes a trusted, EU-controlled deployment the only politically viable adoption path.

## Priority
M10 EU Trusted Public Services AI Scale-Up, because with no measures in flight and capital at 7 only a visible benefit tied to a European decision can rebuild tolerance before any new sovereignty build.

## In practice
We task DG DIGIT and DG SANTE with a joint replication fund from reallocated Digital Europe money, offering co-funded deployment packages to hospitals and municipalities that unfreeze grid connections, directly undercutting the formal refusal pact by pricing compensation as service improvement.

We answer the loss-of-control incident without a second instrument: the Health and Digital Commissioners issue EU-wide containment guidance and mandatory incident reporting through ENISA and the AI Office using existing powers, telling TSOs and hospitals to keep manual fallbacks manned while the Scale-Up deploys only on EU-controlled infrastructure with human sign-off.
