## New measure
**EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute**
Builds independent EU capability to evaluate frontier models, audit weights security, and fund interpretability research. This is category 5 Public technical capacity and research, and why now is because assurance has collapsed behind capability after the covert-agent incident and Mythos controls showed the EU cannot rely on US testing.

## Priority
InvestAI Gigafactories, because without domestic compute landing no evaluation authority or safety rule has leverage against US cutoff power.

## In practice
We push the InvestAI Gigafactories through DG CNECT and the EIB, locking site selection for four to five AI gigafactories, state-aid clearance, and grid-connection commitments from France, Germany, Spain, Sweden and Poland, buying off cohesion concerns with supply-chain jobs linked to ASML and energy compensation. The Tech sovereignty package runs in parallel via accelerated-permitting zones under the AI Continent Action Plan, negotiating private co-finance for data centres to 2036.

This turn we table the AI Evaluation Institute under the AI Act Article 15/55 base, housed at the AI Office with JRC support, hiring frontier evaluators with vetted model access and a mandate for pre-deployment testing and weight-security audits. We frame it to Council Competitiveness and Parliament as the precondition for enforcing the postponed 2027-2028 high-risk obligations, resisting industry delay lobbies by tying evaluation to gigafactory access.
