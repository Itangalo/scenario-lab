US-China strikes still hit orbits, cables, fabs: Marseille/Genoa cable cut, satnav jitter, grid-software surge blackouts. No EU base struck or force deployed; reserves restored systems under air-raid precautions, nurses ran triage on local servers.

Mid-spring foreign-model tailored therapies reversed untreatable conditions, creating moral crisis over waiting lists. Brussels required EU-hosted verification and public-clinic delivery, tied top-ups/vouchers to sequencing and restoration crews. Denmark, Portugal, parts of Spain delivered foreign miracle as European service; elsewhere EMA delays and reagent shortages caused rationing and medical-tourism whispers. By June hospitals lit, registries limped, detection kits thin, hiring frozen, warehouse protests continued, but blame shifted to belligerents — organized survival became legitimacy.
