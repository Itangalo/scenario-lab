Non-belligerency held as US-China strikes hit cables, orbits, fabs; EU relied on dispersed registries, local servers, and joint cyber/disease-surveillance command. Spring foreign-model tailored cures caused moral crisis; Brussels mandated EU-hosted verification, with patchy delivery, EMA delays and rationing.

In February a machine-written ransomware sweep locked municipal payrolls, forced hospitals to paper triage, and isolated grid servers; joint-command telemetry sped restoration but outages lasted days, attribution stalled. Shelter operation with Transition Shield payments kept clinics and grids lit but slowed.

By spring cheap Chinese logistics robots entered Rotterdam and Piraeus via private importers under American control software; EU makers unable to match price/scale, unions warned of job loss, Council ordered no procurement, staying in shelter watch. Bio-detection caught tainted diagnostic bots and steadied queues.

In autumn foreign-model keys were suspended in Lyon, Rotterdam, Milan, freezing triage/sequencing; near-frontier open release spread unvetted bio-design bots. Switch to home-run systems was ragged: joint crews with automated patching restored payrolls and grid libraries where they arrived, elsewhere clinics stayed on paper and verification queues grew. Same half-year frontier capability jumped abroad while capital fled, foreign build-outs cancelled, importer control software rationed, yet dexterous warehouse units spread wider, hardening hiring freezes into losses in clerical, customer, accountancy and junior software jobs; unions called it double lock of foreign hands and minds. Contested genome study claiming model-aided human-infecting design circulated; EU-hosted checks caught more tainted tools. Mood: grateful for survival, angry that healing, power and labour depended on outside permission now withdrawn.
