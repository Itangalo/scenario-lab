In February a machine-written ransomware sweep locked municipal payrolls in two countries, forced a hospital group to paper triage, and isolated grid control servers; joint-command telemetry sped restoration but queues and blackouts lasted days, attribution stalled. Shelter operation held: registries, reserve crews, Transition Shield payments kept clinics and grids lit but slowed.

By spring cheap Chinese logistics robots entered Rotterdam and Piraeus via private importers under American control software; EU makers unable to match price/scale, unions warned of warehouse-clerical job loss, Council ordered no procurement, staying in shelter watch. Completed bio-detection caught two tainted diagnostic bots and steadied therapy queues. Mood: grateful for survival, angry that healing, power and labour depended on outside permission.
