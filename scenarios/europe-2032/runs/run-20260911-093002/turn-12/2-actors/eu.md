## New measure
**EU Defensive Patching Diffusion Network**
It pushes the demonstrated machine-speed patching and cross-network detection out through ENISA, EU-CyCLONe and the finished black-start playbooks to hospitals, grid operators and municipal utilities, without new procurement or new law.
Why this and why now: with capital at 6 and sentiment at 11 we cannot fund a build-out, but the H2 2031 breakthrough — demonstrated jointly by ENISA-linked teams in Delft and Jülich with US counterparts, an AI system that learned coordinated probing behaviour across networks and generated verified patches in minutes, closing a whole intrusion class rather than single signatures — is a real advance already proven in two municipal containments; diffusing it through what is finished and operational is the only cheap chance to turn holding into recovery, and it directly serves the two-year commitment to hold essential services together. The work itself was computational — a swarm-behaviour detector plus verified-patch generator — visible outside computer science only where outages did not happen, which is why a specialist would call it a landmark while the public sees only fewer prolonged outages.

## In practice
We operate entirely through finished instruments: ENISA pushes the Delft-Jülich detector and patch generator via the M7 common isolation playbooks and M8 cross-border repair teams and hospital/grid operator lists, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe cybersecurity funds, no new Council vote. DG CNECT and the Health Emergency Preparedness authority co-sign prioritisation of hospitals, municipal utilities and fallback points, with deployment as a configuration update, not procurement.

We keep the declared-complete posture otherwise: segmented manual grid operation, registered dispersed guarded pools under NIS2/CER and the Civil Protection Mechanism, clinics on metered EU-defaults, factories frozen. Police watch on rural depots continues, unpublished sites stay unpublished, and DG DIGIT with health ministries continues co-branding European-tuned office gains to separate useful adoption from the sabotage and quota story.
