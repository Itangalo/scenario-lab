## New measure
None this turn, while we wait for political capital and public tolerance to recover enough to carry anything new.

The defensive breakthrough matters, but with capital at 1.0 and sentiment at 8.0 we cannot fund or enforce a new instrument now without breaking the hold on fallback, segmentation and guarded pools; we will diffuse the new tooling through what is already finished and operational rather than standing up a parallel programme.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority measure in flight this turn, because all prior measures have finished and we are holding their effects by operation rather than by new spend.

## In practice
We stay on segmented manual operation with the registered, dispersed and guarded fallback pools under NIS2/CER and the Civil Protection Mechanism. ENISA, EU-CyCLONe and the crisis network push the new defensive tooling — automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection — out through the common isolation playbooks and cross-border repair teams from M7/M8, prioritising hospitals, grid operators and municipal fallback points, with no new procurement promise.

We continue the M6 continuity line: clinics on metered EU-default, factories frozen where they are, and co-branding with DG DIGIT and health ministries of the European-tuned office systems that cut waiting lists and permit times, to keep useful adoption separable from the sabotage, quota and rogue-agent story. No new law, no new fund, no chase for the collapsed build-out in Washington quotas.
