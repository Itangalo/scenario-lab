### Patches at machine speed
The second half of 2031 brought the first genuinely good technical news in years. Research teams on both sides of the Atlantic demonstrated patching and detection software that closed a whole class of intrusion almost as fast as it was attempted — spotting coordinated probing across networks rather than chasing single signatures. ENISA pushed it out through the channels it already had: the common isolation playbooks, the cross-border repair teams, the hospital and grid operator lists.

Where it landed, it helped. Two municipal utilities reported intrusion attempts that were contained in minutes rather than days. Restoration drills that had relied on pulling plugs and running by hand could stay connected longer.

It did not end the other fight. Attacks on the physical fallback stockpiles continued through the autumn — fuel drained in the north, a transformer yard burned in the east, telemetry cabinets smashed along a rail corridor. Guards stopped three attempts, but rural depots remained thinly protected, and several operators quietly took sites off the published maps again after complaints they served as target lists.

### Holding, not building
Brussels deliberately started nothing new. With coffers empty and trust hostile, the Commission kept clinics on metered European defaults, factories frozen, and grid operation segmented and manual. No new procurement was attempted after the collapse in commercial compute deals and the tightening of allied quotas in Washington.

The black-start and manual fallback network was declared complete: registered pools, dispersed spares, police watch, shared fuel accounting. The effect was visible in faster drills and fewer prolonged outages, and for the first time ministers could point to something finished.

Local stories of European-tuned office systems cutting hospital waits and permit backlogs got wider play, now co-branded as a European choice. National coverage remained dominated by sabotage, quotas and memory of the spring rogue logistics agent, but hostility softened slightly in cities where the systems worked and the new defences held.
