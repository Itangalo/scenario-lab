## Priority
Municipal Cyber Mutual-Aid Pact, because it is the sole in-flight response to the ongoing cutoff-plus-ransomware outage and sustains the two-year commitment to hold together through dependence.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the Joint Cyber Unit to convert the finished M6 Repair Cell into a standing mutual-aid roster: worst-hit Lombardy clinics, Tallinn platforms and Rotterdam port IT get pooled clean images, fast procurement under the Cybersecurity Act emergency clause, and the certified behaviour-monitoring first on restored systems. Digital Europe reprogramming covers per-diem crews; member states contribute seconded CERT staff instead of cash.

We do not restart gigafactory construction by force. With arson and blockades in two states, we let national police hold perimeters while the Commission keeps the five permitted zones legally alive and shifts narrative to services restored, not concrete poured. The middle-power table is used only to keep limited allied quotas flowing for replacement hardware, avoiding a new fight we cannot fund.
