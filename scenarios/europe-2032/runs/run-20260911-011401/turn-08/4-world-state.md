### Dark screens, then steady hands
January brought a largely automated ransomware sweep across public services already throttled by the American cutoff. Clinics in Lombardy reverted to paper triage, a ministry platform in Tallinn went offline, Rotterdam port scheduling stalled. Attribution lagged for months; engineers agreed only that the tooling was model-generated. Defenders looked behind.

The finished emergency repair cell became the backbone of the response. ENISA and the Joint Cyber Unit turned crews into a standing roster, pooling clean backups, fast-procured hardware and seconded national CERT staff. Worst-hit municipalities got help first. Where crews arrived, services degraded rather than stopped.

That work was made more credible by a certified monitoring technique from the laboratories — behaviour predicted before it was observed. Attached first to restored systems, it cut false alarms and caught intrusions earlier. Labs adopted it too, which sped deployment.

### A table that held, and sites that burned
The coordination framework with the Netherlands, Japan and Korea delivered again: aligned export licences and joint bargaining kept limited quotas of replacement hardware flowing for the repair effort. No one ceded sovereignty, but together they could withhold enough to be heard.

The same months exposed the limits of building. Planned gigafactory zones in two member states were hit by blockades and arson attempts against fences and grid connections. Permitting works halted for months. National police held perimeters; Brussels kept the five permits legally alive but ordered no forced restart.

Ordinary offices told a different story. Law firms, accountants, administrations and newsrooms reported measured productivity gains, largest among the least experienced, without layoffs. Firms that had cut early quietly rehired. Voters noticed shorter queues where systems were restored, but also fraud, outages and the sight of fenced, empty industrial fields.

By June the Union had held together where it was dark, but had built little new.
