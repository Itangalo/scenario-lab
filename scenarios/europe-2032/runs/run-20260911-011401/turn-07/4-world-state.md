### The cutoff
In August the notices arrived without explanation. Clinics in Lombardy, a ministry platform in Tallinn, logistics planners in Rotterdam found their calls to the leading American model refused or throttled to unusable levels. Washington spoke of tiered access for national assets and tighter chip controls; European requests for assured allied volumes were acknowledged and parked. Firms that had built triage, translation and port-scheduling on that model scrambled for substitutes.

The blow landed on systems still being rebuilt. The emergency repair crews funded as the Union's sole operation moved through the same municipalities hit in January, reinstalling clean backups and fast-procured hardware. Where they arrived, services steadied. Where they had not yet arrived, the new cutoff looked like a second outage.

### A breakthrough, applied where it hurt
A certified monitoring technique from the laboratories — behaviour that could be predicted before it was observed rather than argued after — was taken up quickly because the labs wanted it too. Brussels attached it to the restoration, putting legible controls first on restored public systems. Engineers said false alarms fell and intrusions were caught earlier. It did not restore the lost model, but it made the remaining ones more trustworthy.

Brussels tried to answer the cutoff without money. The supply-chain table built with the Netherlands, Japan and Korea was reactivated: aligned export-licence positions, a joint demand for allied volume licences, pooled procurement. The coordination held, and in autumn joint bargaining won limited allied quotas for some industrial inputs.

The same table was used to contain the breakaway capital. EU-run models that had cut waiting lists in two hospital regions and shortened permit decisions were offered as joint capacity if the side deal was folded back in. The capital kept its separate contract.

The gigafactory permits finally settled, with five zones legally cleared. No foundations were poured. Contractors cited financing gaps and quiet protests at two sites; fences were tagged, a grid connection blocked for days. The legal ground existed, the building did not.

By December, voters credited the shorter queues where they saw them, but blamed the Union for dependence where screens had gone dark again.
