Autumn-Dec 2029 automated ransomware sweep hit public services in a dozen states using open-model intrusion kits and lures; connected ports/operators recovered in hours via allied joint-command signatures while non-fed municipal hospitals/clinics fell back to paper for days. Brussels published only restoration metrics, no attribution. Accession to joint command completed with seconded analysts under standing procedures, sharpening two-tier resilience gap. University study showed AI productivity gains, especially juniors, but entry-level hiring in routine coding/analysis/support did not recover. European lab advance in maths-enabled materials modelling drew little attention. Commission sent civil-protection joint teams with offline-backup kits and drills to volunteer municipalities, asking no data in return. Five reserved compute sites remained unbuilt; services ran but public resentment and AI use rose together.
