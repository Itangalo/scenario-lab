### The autumn the screens went dark
In late August an automated ransomware sweep rolled across public services in a dozen member states. It rode model-written intrusion kits and convincingly worded administrative messages. Connected ports and large operators, fed by shared detection signatures from the allied joint command, restored in hours. Municipal hospitals, clinics and town halls outside the feed did not. In two regions staff returned to paper lists; appointment systems and pharmacy dispensaries were down for days.

Attribution dragged. Brussels deliberately said nothing about origin, publishing only restoration times and takedown counts for fraudulent lures.

Accession to the joint command formally completed in autumn, and for the first time seconded European analysts worked under standing procedures rather than ad hoc secondment. That helped the connected core, but made the two-tier picture starker on television: ports humming while local clinics queued patients in corridors.

### Offices that worked better, entry jobs that vanished
The same months brought two quieter economic stories. A university-led study across law, accountancy, administration and consulting found measurable productivity gains, largest for juniors, with firms that had cut early quietly rehiring. Almost simultaneously, employers confirmed entry-level hiring in routine coding, standardised analysis and tier-one support was not recovering. Unions called it a closed door for graduates; employers called it a changed job.

A notable research result from a European lab — a verified advance in mathematics-enabled materials modelling — earned specialist praise but little public notice next to the outage coverage.

The Commission's answer was to send people, not rules. Using civil protection funds, joint teams began placing offline-backup kits and exercising recovery drills in volunteer municipalities, explicitly asking for no data in return. Mayors welcomed the hands; parliaments asked why protection still depended on rented foreign signatures. The five reserved compute sites remained fenced fields. By December services ran, resentment ran deeper, and polling showed use and anger rising together.
