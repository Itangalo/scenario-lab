## Priority
EU Trusted Therapies Delivery Pact — because only a visible clinical benefit delivered on EU terms can lift sentiment enough to keep the joint cyber-bio accession and any bio-gate response politically survivable.

## In practice
We execute through the Health Council and HERA/DG SANTE with EMA fast-track: central EU procurement of the tailored-therapy platforms, distribution via the clinic networks where triage already cut waiting lists, with pharmacovigilance and model-access terms requiring EU-hosted inference for patient data. Funding reprograms unspent transition and adoption lines; no new data-centre siting to trigger blockades.

We keep M11 running as the shield underneath: ENISA keeps mirroring feeds to the joint command for grid protection, ECDC holds sample-sharing for the bio-uplift precursor, and we use the therapy rollout credibility with mayors to sustain guard for repair crews and night-shift protection. We ask member states to hold robotics deployment to supervised logistics and care roles pending armed-use doctrine, linking it to the same trust pact.
