### War over the machines
In February, American and Chinese forces began striking the infrastructure of intelligence itself. The stated reason on both sides was prevention: the other was months from an uncatchable lead. Fabrication plants, cable landings, satellite ground stations and hyperscale data centres became targets. Two strikes hit facilities adjacent to European territory; a cut cable system in the Atlantic forced emergency rerouting of medical and financial traffic.

Brussels declared Union territory and its cable landings non-belligerent, offering deconfliction and shared test standards to both capitals in exchange for sparing licensed supplies. The offer was acknowledged and largely ignored. Therapy queues set in California froze, then were throttled to wartime allocation. Stockpiled doses kept clinics in France, Spain and Poland running for weeks before shortages appeared.

### Fire at home, hiring freeze everywhere
At home the night fires spread. After the pilot robot sites, grid-connection works for the two permitted gigafactory plots were torched and blockaded, halting construction. The Energy Council ordered emergency protection and repair teams, but contractors refused night shifts and energy ministers split over who pays for guards.

The jobs data hardened. Law firms, auditors, software houses and customer-operations centres confirmed what graduates already knew: entry posts were not being refilled. Bridge pay under the newly completed Transition Guarantee reached some logistics workers idled by port disruption, but take-up stayed slow and mayors said it could not answer a generation without interviews.

A new openly downloadable model, close to the closed frontier, spread to hundreds of thousands of machines in days, beyond any recall just as wartime cyber spillover surged. Health, power and telecom operators isolated by European agencies degraded but did not collapse.

The wayward capital kept its separate foreign pact, sharing a trickle of surviving compute while refusing to fold it into the common pool. The middle-power club met urgently and issued bold communiqués, but compelled no laboratory to open and rerouted no chips.
