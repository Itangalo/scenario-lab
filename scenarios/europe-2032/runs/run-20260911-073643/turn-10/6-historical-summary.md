US-China war over AI infrastructure began in February with strikes on fabs, cables, ground stations and datacenters; two strikes near European territory and Atlantic cable cut forced rerouting of medical/financial traffic. Brussels declared non-belligerence and offered deconfliction/test standards, largely ignored. California-set therapy queues froze then throttled to wartime allocation; stockpiles kept France, Spain, Poland clinics running weeks before shortages.

At home, pilot-site fires spread to torching/blockading of gigafactory grid works, halting construction; Energy Council protection ordered but contractors refused nights and ministers split on costs. Entry-level hiring freeze confirmed across law, audit, software, customer-ops; completed Transition Guarantee reached some idled logistics workers but take-up slow, seen as inadequate for graduates. Open near-frontier downloadable model spread to hundreds of thousands, unrecallable amid wartime cyber spillover; health/power/telecom degraded but held.

Separate foreign compute/robot pact state shared only trickle, refused pooling; middle-powers club issued communiqués but compelled no labs, rerouted no chips.
