### A Surge in Open Power, a Crackdown at Home

The AI frontier advances by +0.5, reaching 61.0, as closed labs integrate a major interpretability breakthrough that allows real-time tracing of high-stakes model decisions. This safety leap, rapidly adopted globally, lifts `ai_safety` by +5. But the moment is undercut by a significant open-weight release: a frontier-capable model, based on recently leaked early weights, is reconstructed and shared widely within months of its closed counterpart. While not a full replication, the open version achieves substantial capability, pushing `openweight_capability` to 55.0—an abrupt but not unprecedented jump. The diffusion is fast, aided by decentralized hosting and peer-to-peer networks, but falls short of matching the frontier due to missing training infrastructure and withheld data.

Simultaneously, export controls tighten under the US consolidation posture, rationing access even to allies. The EU, lacking sovereign compute, feels the pinch—its access is not cut, but constrained. This, combined with rising capability, triggers a −1 in `eu_ai_sovereignty`. The Sovereign Compute Acceleration Directive, still in early negotiation, yields no physical progress. Grid concerns and hyperscaler distrust stall site selection; the shift to modular units delays full deployment. No completion bonus, no momentum gain.

Worse, backlash turns physical. Data centre sites in Germany and Poland face occupations; a substation feeding a planned facility is sabotaged. Policing drains resources, and the public sees not protection but repression. `public_sentiment` drops sharply to 18.0, reflecting anger over both AI’s disruption and the state’s response.

The EU scrambles to save the Civic AI Accountability Framework. Bilateral talks reveal deep resentment over perceived top-down imposition. The compromise—co-drafting oversight rules via national ombudsmen—buys silence, not support. The measure limps forward, but its legitimacy is hollowed. With no completion, no bonus.

Political capital collapses under the portfolio charge: two large measures (−3 each), a priority (−1), and no offsetting gains. The cost of acting, without delivering, is laid bare.

### The Cost of Moving Slow

Resilience erodes as cyber and bio preparedness are overshadowed by internal crisis. Labour protests grow, tied to visible job displacement in technical fields. The EU reacts, but too late—the ground has shifted beneath it.
