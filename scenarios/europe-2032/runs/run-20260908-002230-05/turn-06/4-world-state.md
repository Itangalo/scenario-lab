### Stalled Momentum, Shrinking Leverage

The EU doubled down on compute sovereignty, declaring the Sovereign Compute Emergency Buildout its top priority amid cascading shocks. Yet the ai_investment_collapse shattered confidence in private-sector scaling, freezing key partnerships and forcing emergency state funding into gaps once expected to be filled by capital markets. The emergency powers invoked to override Polish objections and fast-track grid access in Finland and Spain yielded only partial progress—military-civilian task forces missed critical milestones, and public backlash grew over environmental bypasses.

The ASML shadow network scandal erupted after a Dutch investigation revealed covert resale of reconfigured lithography tools via Luxembourg, exploiting jurisdictional seams. Framed as corporate pragmatism, it undermined EU cohesion and triggered a diplomatic rift with Washington. France and the Netherlands blocked proposed export reforms, citing sovereignty concerns, while the Commission threatened Anti-Coercion Instrument activation—marking the first open fracture among core members.

Simultaneously, the EU launched the Middle-Power Access Compact, aiming to convert supply-chain leverage into governance. Japan and Canada expressed cautious support, but South Korea hesitated, fearing Chinese retaliation. Without U.S. engagement—still in transition—the initiative lacked enforcement teeth. Developers treated the evaluation demands as advisory, not binding.

The Cyber Shield and Worker Transition Fund officially completed, but their impact was muted. Germany and Austria continued limiting data sharing despite REACT-EU incentives, weakening threat response. In France and Italy, retraining uptake remained low, feeding narratives of EU irrelevance.

Public sentiment dipped further, as job losses in legal tech and junior software roles dominated media coverage. The capability plateau—evidenced by underwhelming releases and falling prices—offered no relief, only uncertainty. Verification widened in clinical coding and contract review, accelerating narrow-domain automation despite stagnating general capability.

### Coalition Without Power

The middle_power_coalition formed in principle, but its leverage remained untested. While it secured a +2 boost to political capital for dampening immediate panic, the absence of concrete access gains left sovereignty adrift. With ai_capability barely rising and resilience eroding, the EU’s strategic buffer narrowed.
