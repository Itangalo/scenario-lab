Widespread job losses in administrative and professional sectors across Europe have sparked union action and public concern, prompting the European Commission to propose the Worker Transition Acceleration Fund—a retraining and wage insurance initiative—though fiscal resistance has led to a compromised, time-limited version with delayed implementation. Meanwhile, strategic AI infrastructure projects under the InvestAI Gigafactories programme and broader Tech Sovereignty Package face delays due to extended environmental assessments and lack of binding commitments, pushing deployment to future timelines. A sophisticated, AI-enabled cyber intrusion targeting critical infrastructure in multiple regions—including EU energy grids—exposed critical security blind spots, eroding trust in existing defences. Globally, AI capabilities continue to advance rapidly, particularly in continuous-agent and self-improving systems, but safety and oversight measures have failed to keep pace, widening the gap between capability and control amid declining confidence in coordination and open-model governance.
