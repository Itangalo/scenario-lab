### Strategic Scramble Amid Compute Crisis

The Taiwan blockade sent shockwaves through global AI supply chains, halting advanced semiconductor exports and exposing Europe’s acute compute dependency. In response, the EU launched the Sovereign Compute Emergency Buildout, overriding Polish environmental objections via cohesion fund leverage and fast-tracking energy access for AI zones. However, Finland and Spain missed grid deadlines despite task forces, and ASML’s shadow network deepened distrust as Dutch and Belgian operations restructured under Luxembourg cover. The initiative is in early implementation and will require multiple turns to deliver tangible capacity gains.

### Shield Completed, Trust Eroded

The Cyber Shield formally finished, establishing the Romanian Fusion Centre and mandating zero-trust rollout. Yet Germany and Austria still withhold full data sharing, relying on fragile bilateral CERT protocols. Though legacy system upgrades in Eastern Europe reduced vulnerability, the recent ransomware attack had already damaged credibility. Public sentiment dipped further as media framed the Shield as delayed and compromised.

### Transition Fund Delivers, But Falters

The Worker Transition Fund completed its rollout, deploying re-employment dashboards and wage insurance frameworks. But low adoption in France and Italy undermined impact, feeding narratives of EU overreach without delivery. Industrial momentum stalled: the Tech Sovereignty Package’s private capital target now seems unrealistic, and export control harmonisation failed in Council, blocked by French fears of retaliation and Dutch legalism.

### Political Capital Bleeds

With four large measures in flight—including the new emergency buildout—political capital eroded under sustained strain. The emergency invocation bolstered sovereignty ambitions but failed to offset systemic delays and diplomatic fractures. Public anxiety grew over job losses and infrastructure fragility, while the U.S. election outcome—pointing to consolidation—signaled tighter frontier access ahead.
