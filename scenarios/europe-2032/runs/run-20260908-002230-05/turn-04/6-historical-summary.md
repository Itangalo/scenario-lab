The EU activated NIS2 emergency powers to accelerate the Cyber Shield’s detection layer, releasing €6 billion from REACT-EU and establishing the Romanian Fusion Centre, but rollout stalled due to German and Austrian legal challenges over data sovereignty, resulting in a delayed compromise on pooled AI analysis with national data control. Only eight member states fully connected critical operators by June. A major ransomware attack exploiting legacy systems—attributed to a non-state actor using autonomous open-weight tools—crippled health networks in three countries, exposing defensive gaps and eroding public trust despite successful containment. Industrial progress faltered as Poland rejected environmental waivers for data centres, Gigafactory deployments slipped in Finland and Spain, and the Council failed to agree on export controls amid French and Dutch opposition. Political capital declined further due to legal disputes, implementation delays, and weakening public sentiment, with the stalled Worker Transition Fund providing minimal offset.
