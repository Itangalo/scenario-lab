### Emergency Shield Activation Meets Resistance

The EU invoked NIS2 emergency powers to fast-track the Cyber Shield’s detection layer, aiming to unify threat monitoring across member states. Despite binding ENISA standards and early release of €6 billion from the REACT-EU fund, rollout lagged. Germany and Austria formally challenged the Commission’s override of data sovereignty, filing a preliminary reference to the CJEU. A compromise—pooled AI analysis with nationally controlled data routing—was accepted in principle but delayed implementation by months. The new Fusion Centre in Romania became operational, yet only eight member states fully connected their critical operators by June.

### Major Cyber Incident Exposes Gaps

A model-generated ransomware campaign crippled regional health networks in three countries, exploiting legacy systems the Shield had not yet reached. Attribution pointed to a non-state actor using open-weight tools now capable of autonomous attack chaining. The incident confirmed defensive fragmentation and overwhelmed national response units, though containment was achieved within weeks due to pre-existing national cyber units. Public trust dipped as headlines highlighted unpatched vulnerabilities.

### Industrial Delays Persist

Poland rejected the Commission’s ultimatum on environmental waivers, citing ecological risks from data centre energy use. Gigafactory timelines slipped further, with Finland and Spain also missing grid connection targets. The Council’s export control session produced no binding agreement, as France and the Netherlands opposed centralised licensing, fearing retaliation. ASML’s Belgian subsidiary expanded servicing, deepening transatlantic friction.

### Political Capital Erodes

Despite the priority on Cyber Shield, political capital declined. The legal challenge, combined with stalled industrial momentum and public anxiety, undercut confidence. The Worker Fund’s delay and re-employment dashboards offered little counterbalance. Public sentiment fell slightly, reflecting frustration over unmet promises.
