### Cyber Shield Launch Amid Rising Tensions  
The EU announced the **Cyber Shield for Critical Infrastructure**, invoking emergency powers to mandate zero-trust architecture and AI-driven intrusion detection across energy, transport, and healthcare operators. ENISA began drafting binding implementation timelines and launched a €15 billion support fund, with disbursement mechanisms still under negotiation. Initial assessments revealed significant disparities in member state readiness—some lacked basic monitoring systems, while others pushed back against sovereign-AI mandates. A pilot red-teaming exercise in Romania identified critical blind spots, but broader audits and deployment are still in early stages. The announcement alone provided a modest boost to public confidence, though tangible progress remains months away.

### Labour and Infrastructure Under Strain  
The **Worker Transition Acceleration Fund** remains under negotiation, with political disagreements delaying full approval of disbursement rules. It has not yet formally launched and therefore does not yet incur implementation costs. Meanwhile, **InvestAI Gigafactories** in Finland and Spain saw grid connection timelines accelerated under TEN-E emergency provisions, though Poland withheld environmental waivers, threatening IPCEI eligibility. The **Tech Sovereignty Package** gained backing from nine member states for AI infrastructure zones, but legal challenges over environmental derogations are expected. With the **Cyber Shield** now introduced as the new measure for this turn and designated the priority initiative, the other three initiatives remain in development but are not yet active implementation efforts. Coordination capacity remains strained, but within feasible bounds.

### Open Frontier Advances Moderately  
A new open-weight model release improved accessibility and performance, contributing to a measured increase in openweight_capability. While adoption is growing across research and private clusters, the model does not surpass the closed frontier. No corresponding safety upgrades reached deployment at scale, and frontier models continue to operate with increasing autonomy, raising concerns about the widening gap between capability and oversight.

### Sovereignty Stabilizes, Capital Erodes Under Load  
Political capital declined slightly under the sustained burden of advancing multiple proposals, now bearing the cost of launching the Cyber Shield as this turn’s sole new measure. The Worker Transition Acceleration Fund, while advanced in design, did not clear final approval and thus did not transition to active status—avoiding additional charge. The +3 proposal bonus for addressing the emergent labour displacement wave will carry forward and apply once the measure is formally adopted. No major domestic capacity milestone was reached, but the ongoing progress of InvestAI Gigafactories contributed a +1 capacity event, offsetting the erosion from rising ai_capability. Sovereignty thus held steady. Public sentiment rose slightly on the promise of action, though delivery delays temper expectations.
