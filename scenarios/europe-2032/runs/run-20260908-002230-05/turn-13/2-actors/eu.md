ENISA and the European Commission’s Trade Directorate are leading the technical and legal drafting, with emergency funding unlocked from the Crisis Response Instrument. If this fails, we will have no access, no sovereignty, and no credibility. But if it holds, it buys time for compute sovereignty to catch up.
