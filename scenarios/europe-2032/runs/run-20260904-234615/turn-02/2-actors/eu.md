## New measure  
**Measure:** Launch the Open-Weight Containment Initiative  
Establish a rapid-response framework to assess and contain risks from open-weight models that approach frontier capability, including mandatory risk assessments for releases above 45.0 and emergency powers to restrict distribution when critical capabilities are detected.

Category: 3 (Limits and restrictions)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 4  
Targeted effect: Slow the rise of `openweight_capability` by 5–7 points relative to `ai_capability`; increase `resilience` by 3–5 through early-warning detection of distributed threats  
Applies to: All open-weight model releases made available within the EU, regardless of origin, with extraterritorial enforcement via hosting providers and app stores

**Priority:** InvestAI Gigafactories

## In practice  
We are accelerating the InvestAI Gigafactories by activating the European Investment Bank’s emergency infrastructure window under Article 122 TFEU, unlocking €40bn in immediate disbursements. We are deploying the Civil Protection Mechanism to fast-track environmental assessments in Brittany and Norrland, treating local opposition not as obstruction but as a legitimacy risk that must be structurally addressed. Binding side agreements with regional governments now include co-ownership stakes in the facilities, guaranteed green hydrogen off-take for local industry, and a 30% hiring preference for residents. The Commission is working with ENTSO-E to reclassify AI zones as critical infrastructure, allowing grid access priority over commercial data centres. These steps are costly in political capital, but they are necessary to ensure that by turn 7, the EU has not just promised sovereignty — it has concrete, powered, legally anchored compute that no foreign actor can disable.

Meanwhile, the Tech sovereignty package is being leveraged in bilateral talks with semiconductor equipment makers, using the promise of streamlined permitting to extract commitments on EU-based maintenance hubs and restricted export clauses for advanced tooling. We are also integrating the Blue Card+ scheme with national AI academies, offering fast-track residency and research funding to engineers with experience in agent alignment and red-teaming. On the EAAD front, we are finalising the first evaluation protocol for models above 50.0 capability, co-developed with national cyber agencies and modelled on nuclear safety stress tests. The levy on non-EU hyperscalers is being collected provisionally under the Digital Services Act enforcement mechanism, pending a formal ruling from the CJEU — a legal gamble, but one we must take to fund real oversight. The agency’s first red-team exercise will simulate an autonomous cyber-intrusion using a controlled proxy model, with results to be published before the end of the turn to build public trust.
