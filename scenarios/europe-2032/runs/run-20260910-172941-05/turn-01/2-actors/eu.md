On the inherited book, InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and the Tech sovereignty package (M2) keep running but we husband them: with capital fleeing and build-out cancelled, we shift this turn to permitting, site power reservation and ASML supply-chain protection rather than new cash calls, to avoid spending capital we need for hardening. No parallel compute programme is started; the EU frontier effort lives inside the existing lines.
