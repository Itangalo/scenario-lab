## Priority
EU Grid and Essential Services Hardening Programme, because joint detection audits and live exercises due by spring are the core of our two-year resilience commitment and the sprint only buys time for them.

## In practice
We keep M3 as the main effort: ENISA and ENTSO-E run the joint detection audits ordered under NIS2/CER, with CEF-Energy and Digital Europe repurposed funds plus EIB loans paying for sensors; TSOs that file mandatory incident reports get connection priority and co-funding. Exercises with transmission, port and water operators run through spring.

Alongside it we launch the Patch Sprint via the Health and Digital Council formations, using HERA and ENISA playbooks, civil-protection stockpiles and procurement of backup kits for municipalities and hospitals that cannot wait for retrofits. InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and the Tech sovereignty package (M2) stay in husbanding mode — permits, sites, grid reservations and lithography protection — with no new cash calls while capital is fled.
