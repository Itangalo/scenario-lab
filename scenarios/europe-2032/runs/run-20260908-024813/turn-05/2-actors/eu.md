## In practice
We are acting while the moment holds. The cyber_defence_breakthrough — an integrated suite of automated vulnerability closure and swarm-behaviour detection developed jointly by Fraunhofer and ENISA — has passed live trials in Estonia and Finland. It stops entire classes of AI-amplified intrusions before they pivot, and it does so faster than human teams can respond. We are now mandating its integration into all national CERTs and NIS2 operators by turn 6, using the updated NIS3 framework currently being fast-tracked through COREPER. Funding comes from the Digital Europe Programme’s emergency reserve, supplemented by REPowerEU-linked resilience bonds. ENISA will coordinate deployment, with binding deadlines and quarterly compliance reviews published publicly. Hungary and Slovakia have signaled resistance, citing cost and sovereignty concerns — we are offering co-financing at 70% and hosting joint oversight councils to preserve national authority over operational response.

At the same time, we press forward on our industrial foundation. The InvestAI Gigafactory in France reaches phase-two grid connection this quarter; we are diverting surplus cable stock from the North Sea Wind Power Hub to accelerate link-up. In Poland, after weeks of negotiation, we have secured local buy-in by committing 15% of construction jobs to Silesian workers and establishing a regional innovation trust funded by 2% of the site’s long-term revenue. The Tech sovereignty package is pivoting fully to the bond-backed model: with private capital still frozen, we are guaranteeing 60% of risk through EIB instruments, making projects bankable without full state ownership. On red-teaming, we are expanding access to Kimi K3 derivatives and early GFlowNet variants, focusing on autonomous exploit chaining in SCADA environments — results will feed directly into the new defensive rollout. And though the civic dialogue finishes this turn, its findings are already shaping a new legislative track: draft revisions to the AI Act’s high-risk list will be circulated in November, informed by citizen juries in Athens, Malmö and Lille. We are not regaining control yet — but we are refusing to let go.
