### A Narrow Window, Barely Seized  
The EU seizes on a rare defensive breakthrough, deploying a new AI-powered cyber shield across critical infrastructure. Automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection — proven in live trials in Estonia and Finland — are fast-tracked into national CERTs and NIS2 operators under a new NIS3 mandate. Despite resistance from Hungary and Slovakia, binding integration deadlines are set for turn 6, with ENISA overseeing compliance. The rollout begins immediately in Germany, France and the Nordic states, significantly reducing dwell time for intrusions. Resilience jumps to 51.0 as the measure locks in gains before offensive adaptation accelerates.

### Sovereignty Drifts Amid Stalled Build  
While the cyber measure advances, sovereign compute lags. The French Gigafactory connects to the grid, but Poland’s site remains delayed despite local compromises. No category 4 milestone is reached, and with global AI capability rising to 56.5, eu_ai_sovereignty erodes further, falling to 17.0. The Tech sovereignty package’s bond model stabilizes financing, but private investment remains frozen, underscoring structural dependence.

### Civic Dialogue Ends, But Not the Distrust  
The emergency civic dialogue concludes across ten regions. Findings reveal deep unease over AI in hiring and public services, but no immediate policy shift follows. Public sentiment holds at 33.0, as sabotage incidents and media framing of AI as a destabilizing force persist.

### America Turns Inward  
The U.S. 2028 election is decided by anti-AI backlash. The incoming administration announces sweeping restrictions on AI in schools, courts and hiring, along with a data centre moratorium and sector-funded job guarantees.
