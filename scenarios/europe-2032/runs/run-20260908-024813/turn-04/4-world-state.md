The EU’s mandatory cyber resilience audit cycle concludes, marking the completion of a major measure. ENISA’s real-time telemetry pilots in Greece and Portugal detect two novel intrusion patterns, enabling rapid patching. Resilience rises to 46.0, though Hungary’s non-compliance and patchy rollout in mid-tier industrial firms limit broader gains.

### Civic Dialogue Launched Amid Backlash  
In response to growing unrest, the EU launches an emergency civic dialogue across ten pilot regions. Mediated forums address public anger over AI in hiring, education, and infrastructure siting. Initial turnout is strong, but skepticism runs deep — in Wallonia, participants reject the process as “damage control.” The backlash intensifies physically: grid substations linked to data centres are sabotaged in Germany and Spain, with injuries reported. Public sentiment dips to 33.0 as media frames the violence as systemic failure.

### Political Capital Under Pressure  
The portfolio charge remains heavy: four large measures and a named priority drain eu_political_capital. The new civic dialogue, while timely, fails to offset the costs. With sentiment falling and no immediate payoff from the finished cyber measure, political capital drops to 27.0. The Union remains reactive, its agenda strained by fragmentation and street-level resistance.
