## In practice
The completion of the InvestAI Gigafactories programme marks a turning point: four sites are now operational, with France’s facility grid-connected and Poland’s construction finalised under the Silesian jobs agreement. Though private investment remains frozen, the bond-backed model we pioneered has proven viable, with EIB guarantees covering 60% of risk and enabling full project financing. This structure will now be extended to phase-two sites, but the immediate focus shifts from construction to sovereignty of use: we are drafting access protocols to ensure that compute built under this programme serves EU-defined priorities, especially red-teaming and public research, not just commercial demand.

With the sovereign red-teaming capability now at initial operating status, we are expanding its mandate from closed to open systems. The new measure launches this turn via delegated act under the AI Act, enforced through the Digital Services Act: any entity hosting or redistributing a model above capability 45.0 must register with ENISA and provide evaluation access within 30 days of request. A new unit — the Open Model Assessment Division — is being stood up in Dublin with 120 staff, drawing from national cybersecurity agencies and academic AI safety labs. Early targets include the recently released agent-capable model and its derivatives, with focus on exploit chaining in industrial control systems. Legal pushback is expected from open-source foundations and cloud providers; we are offering compliance grace periods and co-design of evaluation frameworks to secure cooperation.

The cyber shield is now fully active: real-time patching and swarm detection are operational in all member states, with Hungary and Slovakia integrated through the 70% co-financing deal and joint oversight councils preserving national autonomy. Intrusion dwell times have collapsed to minutes, a defensive edge we must exploit before openweight_capability crosses 52.0 and autonomous attacks become routine. This window is narrow, and we are using it to harden SCADA systems in energy and transport, guided by red-team findings from the sovereign facility. Meanwhile, civic dialogue findings are shaping AI Act revisions, with proposals to classify AI-driven hiring and public service triage as high-risk now in circulation — but public sentiment remains low, anchored in job insecurity and sabotage fears. Without visible gains in control or fairness, trust will not shift.
