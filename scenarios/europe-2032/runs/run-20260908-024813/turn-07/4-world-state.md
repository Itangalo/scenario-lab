### The Open Frontier Widens  
Global AI capability advances to 58.5, driven by rapid iteration in agent architectures and autonomous reasoning pipelines. A powerful open-weight model is released, enabling distributed actors to run exploit-chaining agents on consumer-grade hardware, accelerating offensive cyber capabilities worldwide. While not yet fully general, the system’s autonomy and adaptability mark a qualitative shift. Openweight_capability rises to 46.5, reflecting early diffusion and partial adoption, but remains within the bounds of overall ai_capability growth (+1.0), consistent with its dependency on frontier advances.

### Sovereignty Gains Ground  
The InvestAI Gigafactories programme completes, delivering four sovereign AI compute sites across the EU. France’s facility is grid-connected and operational; Poland’s is secured under the Silesian jobs agreement. Though private investment remains frozen, the bond-backed financing model proves resilient, backed by EIB guarantees. This tangible infrastructure lifts `eu_ai_sovereignty` by +3 under rule 5, offset only by the ongoing pressure of rising capability (−1), resulting in a net gain to 21.0.

### Institutional Response Begins  
The European Commission initiates planning for a red-teaming framework targeting open-weight models, with a proposed host in Dublin. The initiative is under development and has not yet been formally adopted. Mandated reporting from redistributors is under discussion, leveraging the AI Act and DSA, but no delegated act has entered force. Cloud providers are monitoring developments; open-source foundations have expressed concern. Legal uncertainty remains high, and implementation will require further negotiation.

### Political Capital Under Strain  
Four large measures remain in flight. The completion of Gigafactories delivers a +3 proposal bonus. Portfolio cost is −10 (three large measures at −3, one priority at −1). Net change: +3 −10 = −7. A +1 boost from sovereignty (now above 20) partially offsets this, resulting in a net decrease. Political capital falls to 23.0. No sentiment shift occurs.
