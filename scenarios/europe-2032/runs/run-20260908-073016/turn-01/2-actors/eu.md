## Priority
Cyber Shield for Critical Infrastructure — because the cyber_test_shot event proves that detection has failed and that the Union’s critical systems are exposed to persistent, undetected access by AI-coordinated actors, making resilience the most urgent unmet obligation of our two mandates.

## In practice  
The cyber_test_shot incident has shattered any illusion that our current defences are sufficient. We are acting through the NIS2 directive, leveraging its enforcement powers to mandate real-time intrusion detection upgrades across electricity transmission operators, major port authorities, and regional water utilities. The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) will coordinate red-teaming exercises by Q2 2027, funded through the Digital Europe Programme and reinforced with emergency拨款 from the Recovery and Resilience Facility. Member states resist the cost and complexity, particularly in Eastern Europe where cyber capacity is thin — but we are offering 70% co-financing from the EU budget and fast-tracked access to AI-powered monitoring tools developed under the EU’s future research framework. This is not optional: if the next intrusion is not found by accident, it may not be found at all.  

Meanwhile, InvestAI Gigafactories is in early site selection, with JRC mapping energy and grid capacity across seven candidate regions. The political friction is not over need, but control: member states want ownership stakes, while the Commission insists on a supranational governance model to prevent fragmentation. We are offering a compromise — shared equity with a golden share for the Union — to keep the project on track. The Tech sovereignty package is advancing through the Council, but faces headwinds from finance ministries wary of locking in energy commitments. We are pairing it with a new electricity market reform proposal to show that sovereignty means not just compute, but stable, decarbonised power. Both measures are foundational, but they are not fast. And while we build, the frontier moves. That is why this turn, we act to absorb the blow — because we cannot yet stop it.
