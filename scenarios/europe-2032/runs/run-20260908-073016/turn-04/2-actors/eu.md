## Priority  
**European AI Cyber Assurance Pact** — because ratification delays risk fracturing unity just as the joint threat response opens a window, and because this measure is now the bridge between internal hardening and external leverage: without it, the Union is reactive, not resilient.

## In practice  
We are treating the joint threat response invitation not as a technical upgrade but as a strategic inflection: for the first time, the EU is being asked in, not just reacting. But access is conditional — cohesion is the price of entry. So we are accelerating the European AI Cyber Assurance Pact not as a sidecar to Cyber Shield, but as the Union’s foundational act of digital solidarity. The Pact is now in final negotiation, with the Commission using Article 4(3) TEU to frame it as a matter of collective security. We are offering Eastern member states binding access to EU emergency response reserves — including AI-driven decryption and network recovery stacks — in exchange for ratification by Q3. The liability shield is being narrowed to cover only good-faith actors who meet baseline detection standards, a compromise drafted with the European Parliament’s Legal Affairs Committee to break the deadlock.

At the same time, we are converting the Cyber Shield’s completion into institutional momentum: the first pan-European red-teaming exercise, run by ENISA in November, will now be followed by a standing rotation, funded under the Digital Europe Programme. The AI Safety Certification Lab is entering its operational phase, with three pilot sites now staffed and the first model evaluations scheduled for Q2 under the new liability regime. And we are using the joint threat response integration to reframe the stalled Gigafactories — not as an industrial vanity project, but as a security necessity. The next phase of the Tech Sovereignty Package will tie access to EU compute zones to participation in the Assurance Pact, making sovereignty not just territorial, but operational. We are not building data centres to keep up with the frontier. We are building them so the frontier does not leave us behind when the next attack comes.
