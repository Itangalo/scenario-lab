## New measure
**EU Essential Services Continuity Switch**
Stands up EU-hosted fallback inference for hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the leading US model, rerouting them to EU-controlled open-weight systems with emergency procurement and support.
Why this and why now: the short-notice cut-off turned dependence into live disruption of essential services, and with a verifiable-domain capability jump widening what attackers can do, continuity must be restored this turn while resilience absorbs shocks per our two-year commitment.

## Priority
EU Essential Services Continuity Switch, because live loss of frontier access for hospitals and ministries outranks even grid hardening this turn.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT and ENISA with an emergency continuity cell under NIS2/CER, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health money, contracting EU cloud and AI Factory sites to host hardened open-weight fallbacks and to triage affected hospitals and ministries within weeks. Health and Telecoms Councils give cover; member states worried about lock-in are offered EU-funded migration teams and interoperability guarantees.

In parallel we keep the Critical Systems Shield winter exercises running through ACER and affected TSOs, and hold InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty site-selection moving, using the cut-off as proof to demand a written US allies clause and to justify ASML leverage coordination, while eval-anomaly reports are fed to the AI Office for joint red-team follow-up.
