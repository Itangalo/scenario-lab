## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**

## In practice
We task ENISA with a binding detection and incident-reporting sprint under NIS2 and CER, funded from Digital Europe and CEF, with TSOs in the two affected member states as first movers and ACER coordinating grid exercises this winter. The Energy and Transport Councils provide the legal base; interior ministries resisting new obligations are offered EU-funded SOC capacity and mutual-aid guarantees.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech Sovereignty Package moving through site selection and permitting zones, using the US switch-off scare and export-control tightening to hold Council cohesion on ASML leverage and to demand an allies clause from Washington. No new compute vehicle is created; existing lines are protected from fragmentation while Shield delivers the near-term protection.
