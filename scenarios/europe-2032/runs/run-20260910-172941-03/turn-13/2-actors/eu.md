## In practice

We keep contested data-centre builds paused under mayoral siting councils and audit funding, while error-correction teams finish dosage reconciliation and central procurement keeps tailored therapies flowing to manually-run wards. We tell blockading mayors and hostile publics plainly: no new concrete until audits land, but care will no longer depend on foreign feeds or paper alone.
