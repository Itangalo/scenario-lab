## Priority
EU Municipal and Clinical Continuity Corps, because without staffed continuity in clinics and town halls nothing else — bargaining blocs or gigafactories — survives the next cut-off politically.

## In practice
We task ENISA with a joint operations cell alongside DG CNECT and HERA, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health money, seconding national CERT clinicians and hospital IT staff into 72-hour deployment rosters to the worst-hit regions. Legal base is the Cyber Solidarity Act plus the finished Continuity Switch mandate, decided in EPSCO/Health Council to bypass the stalled trade veto fight.

We keep the fallback lit on European-hosted open models hardened under the finished Shield and Bio Surge, with JRC validating clinical error rates openly to answer Parliament's error-prone charge, and pair each deployment with municipal paper-to-digital exercises so the next automated attack degrades rather than stops services. The lithography bargaining track continues on existing FAC conclusions without a new instrument this turn, because capital at 25 allows only one paid track and wards come first.
