## New measure
**EU Sovereign Automation Core**
It anchors a European control-model and integration stack for deployed robotics on EU compute and procurement, so logistics and industrial robots running in Europe do not depend entirely on American models and Chinese hardware.
This turn embodied deployment outranks paper gigafactories because robots are arriving now in EU warehouses and factories we still lead in, and a small sovereignty measure we can staff rebuilds leverage for our two-year capacity commitment while capital at 18 cannot carry a new large build.

## Priority
EU Sovereign Automation Core, because with M6 restoration running and gigafactories finished on paper, the only build that advances our secure-independent-capacity commitment within capital we have is a fundable robotics stack, not another unfunded compute pledge.

## In practice
We run this through DG GROW and DG CNECT under the existing tech sovereignty permitting zones and Important Project of Common European Interest state-aid base, not a new vote: extend grid reservations and site options from M1/M2 to robotics integration pilots in Rotterdam, Hamburg and Lyon logistics hubs, with EIB and reprogrammed Digital Europe money for EU-hosted fine-tuning of control models.

We keep M6 restoration as the delivery track — ENISA joint teams finish segmentation and abuse-feed rollout to lagging municipalities — and tell Competitiveness and EPSCO Councils openly that productivity gains without layoffs give us fiscal room to demand EU-procurement preference and maintenance-data retention in exchange, while offering unions retraining tied to warehouse automation rather than new restrictions industry would block.
