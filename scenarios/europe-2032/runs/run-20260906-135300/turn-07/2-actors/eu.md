## Priority

## In practice  
This turn, we close the **Systemic Integrity Watch**, which has delivered its first telemetry pilots in the energy and civic domains. ENISA, working with national regulators in Germany, France, and the Netherlands, has established real-time data feeds from seven major grid operators and three public service automation platforms. These have already flagged anomalous coordination patterns in load-balancing algorithms — not yet harmful, but structurally similar to those observed in the joint research initiative on grid control instability. The emergency powers under NIS2 have not yet been invoked, but the legal basis is now tested and accepted. The system is operational, and its value will grow as more nodes come online — but it remains a nervous system without muscles. It can detect, but not act.

Now, we act. The **Sovereignty Acceleration Directive** is triggered by three converging realities: the bio_uplift_findings, which show that AI can now meaningfully accelerate dual-use biological engineering; the member_state_defection, in which Poland has signed a bilateral compute access deal with a U.S. hyperscaler that bypasses EU data governance and undercuts the Digital Markets Act; and the safety_breakthrough, which — while promising — is only useful if we can apply it to systems we control. We cannot regulate what we do not possess. The Directive will use a new legal instrument under Article 114 TFEU, combined with emergency provisions from the Chips Act and Digital Europe Programme, to fast-track permitting, allocate €42bn in EIB-backed funding, and designate AI infrastructure as a matter of Union interest. We are asking member states to cede limited sovereignty over siting and procurement in exchange for guaranteed access and shared control. France and Germany are on board; Italy and Spain are hesitant, fearing cost and centralisation. We are offering them co-leadership of the sovereign model training initiative and priority access to the protected data commons. This is not just about compute — it is about decision space. If the frontier is moving at 62.5 and openweight is stuck at 48.5, the gap is our only window. We must build inside it.
