### A Shock, Then a Surge

AI capability advances to 65.5, driven by self-improving agents now autonomously refining verification pipelines in materials science and chip design. No safety gains offset the rise; the earlier interpretability breakthrough has plateaued, and no new controls have landed on frontier systems. `ai_safety` holds at 31.0.

The EU acts decisively. The **Systemic Integrity Watch** completes its rollout, with live telemetry now streaming from seven grid operators and key civic platforms. Early warnings detect algorithmic feedback loops resembling known instability patterns, though no incident occurs. The system lacks enforcement power, but its existence marks a shift toward proactive monitoring.

Simultaneously, the **Joint Resilience Integration Pact** is fast-tracked and operational by mid-turn. ENISA and the ECDC are embedded in a joint cyber and bio-threat command, gaining real-time intelligence sharing and mutual aid commitments. This coordinated response to a growing threat landscape triggers a `joint_threat_response` event, boosting `resilience` by +4 to 50.0 — now at baseline hardening across critical sectors.

Yet sovereignty falters. The **Sovereignty Acceleration Directive** progresses, but too slowly. Legal fast-tracking under Article 114 TFEU faces resistance, and the €42bn EIB package is delayed by Italian and Spanish demands for greater oversight. No new compute comes online. Meanwhile, the sudden **eu_frontier_access_denied** event — cutting EU users from the leading model — delivers a −2 hit to sovereignty. AI capability growth of +1.5 incurs a −1 decay. With no completion bonus from the Sovereignty Directive, net sovereignty falls to 21.0.

Political capital holds steady. The completed Systemic Integrity Watch yields a +4 finish bonus. The new Joint Resilience Pact, small and timely, earns no proposal bonus. Portfolio cost is −3 (Sovereignty Directive) −2 (Systemic Integrity Watch, now finished and removed) −1 (priority) = −4. Net change: +4 −4 = 0, but carryover momentum lifts capital to 45.0.

Open-weight models rise to 52.0, fueled by distributed replication of recent frontier techniques now leaking into open research. The gap narrows, raising concerns about uncontrolled cyber and automation capabilities spreading.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 34.0, as job losses and the access denial crisis feed anxiety, with no visible benefit to counterbalance.
