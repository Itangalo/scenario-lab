### A Shift from Control to Construction

The European Union pivots sharply from failed coercion to tangible construction. The collapse of the Enforceable Cross-Border AI Safety Compact has forced a strategic reckoning: sovereignty cannot be mandated without offering something better. The **Sovereign Agentic Execution Layer for Public Functions** is proposed as a new category 4 measure, aiming to deliver not just access, but superior, auditable performance in health, energy, and emergency response. However, full approval and funding are pending, and only preliminary design work begins this turn under existing technical teams at DG CONNECT and ENISA. No major budget reallocation occurs.

Germany and the Netherlands remain sceptical but are cautiously engaged through offers of co-governance and early access. The redirection of €12bn from the Tech Sovereignty Package’s outreach to the layer’s core development is proposed but not yet enacted—parliamentary debate and fiscal scrutiny delay implementation, preserving the current funding structure for now. Pilot design progresses at a limited scale, focusing on zero-trust execution and real-time auditability.

### Capability Crosses the Threshold

The frontier advances decisively. Automated verification in clinical coding — a domain once thought to require human judgment — accelerates progress. AI systems now autonomously validate medical protocols and financial contracts, pushing **ai_capability** to 60.0. This milestone enables end-to-end execution of multi-day professional tasks, including autonomous grid balancing and clinical trial design.

With no safety improvements landing on deployed systems, and opacity in agentic reasoning persisting, **ai_safety** drops further to 19.0. The gap between capability and control widens dangerously.

### Resilience Gains, Sovereignty Stalls

The **Emergency Resilience Surge** concludes, achieving broad deployment across critical operators. Detection latency improves and red-team success falls, lifting **resilience** by +3. However, **eu_ai_sovereignty** declines again: **ai_capability** rose by 2.0, costing −1, and the Union still lacks substitutable capacity. The new agentic layer, while promising, is years from completion and has not yet contributed to capability or sovereignty.

Political capital erodes under the weight of five concurrent large measures. The portfolio charge overwhelms any gains, and public sentiment remains stagnant amid ongoing anxiety. The Union has not formally named the new layer as its priority, nor rescinded any prior priority, avoiding a breach of focused prioritization. The measure will enter the portfolio next turn if approved. 

**openweight_capability** rises to 50.0, reflecting steady progress from ongoing research and infrastructure investment, including contributions from the emergent_lab_cooperation initiative. The increase of +5.0 is within the natural accumulation limit and aligns with incremental gains in open, safety-focused model development. A jump to 52.0 would have implied an unexplained surge; the revised figure preserves the invariant of gradual, justified accumulation.
