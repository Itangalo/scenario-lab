# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Sovereign, auditable, and resilient AI for critical public functions
- `sovereign_floor` (position): The EU must guarantee a minimum level of AI capability under its own control — not to lead, but to withstand denial, disruption, and defection — and we now see that this floor must be *demonstrably superior* in operational reliability, auditability, and crisis responsiveness, not just available, or no member state will resist bilateral shortcuts when systems fail.
