### The Alliance That Undermines

The United States elects a president who champions transatlantic technological cooperation, pledging structured access to frontier AI systems for allied governments. The promise of shared capability brings immediate relief in Brussels — but also deepens strategic uncertainty. With Washington offering dependable access, the political urgency to fund expensive domestic alternatives wanes.

### Layer Locked in Design

The **Sovereign Agentic Execution Layer for Public Functions** is proposed as a new category 4 measure and designated as the EU’s current priority. However, parliamentary approval of its full funding and legal basis remains stalled. Fiscal hawks block the proposed redirection of €12bn from the Tech Sovereignty Package, leaving the initiative without a secured budget. Only preliminary design work advances under DG CONNECT and ENISA, constrained by existing resources. No operational expenditure occurs, and the measure does not yet draw sustained capacity.

Germany and the Netherlands express cautious interest but demand concrete governance frameworks and performance benchmarks before committing. Co-governance talks proceed slowly, and early access offers fail to close the trust gap. Pilot designs for emergency triage and grid balancing remain at low fidelity, dependent on uncertain future compute. The measure is formally logged in the portfolio but remains in pre-implementation.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Crumbles

**ai_capability** rises to 61.5, driven by incremental gains in autonomous clinical validation and contract execution. No safety upgrades land on deployed systems; opacity in agentic reasoning persists. The widening control gap pushes **ai_safety** down to 17.0.

**openweight_capability** increases modestly to 51.5, reflecting continued progress by research coalitions under improved evaluation protocols. The emergent_lab_cooperation initiative releases early pilot models, contributing to incremental open capability growth — but remains far below closed-frontier levels and lacks the scale or compute to drive a sharp leap. The fidelity and reach of these models remain limited, consistent with their current stage of development.

**resilience** holds steady at 44.0 — no new measures complete, and no incidents overwhelm defences.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Erodes

With **ai_capability** rising by 1.5 and no substitutable EU capacity yet operational, **eu_ai_sovereignty** falls by 1 to 16.0. Political capital declines: the launch of a new priority measure incurs a −1 charge, but the four prior high-cost measures remain active in the portfolio. However, due to funding blocks and stalled implementation, only three of them (InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty Package, EU Sovereign AI Access Guarantee) are assessed as currently drawing full capacity. The Sovereign Agentic Execution Layer, lacking approval, does not yet contribute to the full −3 charge. The net portfolio charge is revised to −10, resulting in **eu_political_capital** falling to 11.0.

Public anxiety grows. Small-scale protests against AI expansion gain local media traction, fueled by job fears and energy concerns. Sentiment dips to 32.0.
