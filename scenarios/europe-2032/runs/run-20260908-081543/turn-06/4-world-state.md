### The Weight of Waiting

The EU’s focus remains fixed on the **Sovereign Agentic Execution Layer**, but momentum stalls. Despite technical progress in design, the lack of full parliamentary approval blocks operational funding and legal authority. DG CONNECT and ENISA complete a robust architectural blueprint featuring zero-trust execution and real-time interpretability, yet deployment remains theoretical. Pilots for emergency triage and grid balancing are delayed, their timelines slipping as member-state co-governance talks with Germany and the Netherlands falter over governance benchmarks.

Two major initiatives — the **Tech Sovereignty Package** and the **EU Sovereign AI Access Guarantee** — officially finish this turn. The former succeeds in launching accelerated-permitting zones, but private investment lags, drawing criticism. The latter establishes a legal framework for sovereign inference infrastructure, though physical deployment remains minimal. Both contribute modestly to EU AI sovereignty in prior turns but yield no immediate uplift now.

### Capability Grows, Safety Deteriorates

**ai_capability** rises by 1.5 to 63.0, driven by advances in autonomous legal reasoning and real-time industrial control. No safety upgrades reach deployed systems. The persistent opacity of agent behaviour and absence of verified control mechanisms deepen systemic risk, pushing **ai_safety** down by 3 to 14.0.

**openweight_capability** edges up to 52.0, buoyed by incremental releases from the emergent_lab_cooperation coalition. These models remain below frontier performance but gain traction in public sector trials, offering a glimmer of open, auditable alternatives.

**resilience** holds at 44.0 — no incidents overwhelm defences, but no new protective measures land either.

### Sovereignty and Capital Drift Lower

With **ai_capability** rising by 1.5 and no operational EU frontier capacity yet online, **eu_ai_sovereignty** declines by 1 to 15.0. Political capital erodes further: the portfolio charge remains high, and though the two finished measures free up some capacity, the ongoing costs of three large initiatives and the priority penalty still apply. **eu_political_capital** drops to 10.0, now below the threshold where new measures may fail to start and priorities lose effect. Public sentiment remains stagnant at 32.0, reflecting persistent anxiety.
