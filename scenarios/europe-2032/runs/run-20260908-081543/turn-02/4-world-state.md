### Frontier Access Cut Off in Midnight Move  
Without warning, the world’s most capable AI model is abruptly withdrawn from EU institutions and critical operators. Hospitals lose access to radiology triage systems mid-assessment, energy grid forecasters face sudden blind spots, and policy simulations grind to a halt. The provider cites “compliance requirements” but offers no appeal. The EU frames it as a sovereignty crisis — a foreign power has switched off essential cognition.

### Emergency Access Guarantee Launched Amid Backlash  
In response, the Commission launches the EU Sovereign AI Access Guarantee, repurposing EuroHPC JU to run a sovereign inference cloud on LUMI and future InvestAI clusters. Emergency procurement bypasses tenders, and €8bn is redirected. The initiative prioritizes health, energy, and emergency services, integrating models from European research coalitions. However, resistance flares: Germany and the Netherlands protest interference with existing cloud contracts, while industry warns of market distortion.

### Cyberattack Exposes Fragile Defenses  
A coordinated ransomware sweep, powered by AI-generated exploits, cripples regional health agencies and transport hubs across three member states. The attack spreads through a poisoned software update, evading detection for days. Defenders are overwhelmed, and full recovery takes weeks. The incident confirms that offensive AI has outpaced defenses — and that resilience remains patchy despite prior efforts.

### Resilience and Safety Erode, Sentiment Turns  
The cyberattack, combined with the access denial, triggers public anxiety. Media frames AI as both fragile and dangerous. Early protests grow louder, linking job fears to dependency. Public sentiment slips. Meanwhile, no safety improvements land on deployed systems, and rapid capability gains push ai_safety down. The Emergency Resilience Surge begins delivering tools, offering partial mitigation — hence a slight resilience gain.

### Political Capital Strained by Crisis Response  
The EU launches a large new measure amid multiple ongoing initiatives. The portfolio cost is high, and the priority adds further strain. Though the Sovereign Access Guarantee answers a direct shock, political capital erodes due to fragmentation, emergency spending, and the cyber incident’s fallout.
