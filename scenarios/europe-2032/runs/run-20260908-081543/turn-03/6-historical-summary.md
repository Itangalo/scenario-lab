A member state’s defection to a foreign hyperscaler exposed the Union’s lack of substitutable AI capacity, eroding sovereignty and undermining leverage. Despite advances in frontier AI—fueled by breakthroughs in clinical coding and autonomous capabilities—safety mechanisms failed to keep pace, and the loss-of-control incident revealed critical system opacity. Public sentiment worsened amid fears of instability and heavy-handed governance. Emergency resilience efforts stalled due to poor coordination, while patchy defenses left systems vulnerable. Political capital plummeted further due to the strain of four concurrent initiatives and failed proposals, with no gains in safety or cohesion.
