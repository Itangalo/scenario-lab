## New measure
**EU Offline Backup and Clean Rebuild Standard**
Mandates tested offline backups, clean-image kits and re-verification drills for hospitals, registries and municipalities on the EU-controlled stack, to stop reinfection after cutoff and ransomware.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, small and fast because with capital at 15 and sentiment at 8 we cannot unblock southern concrete or bargain Washington now — we must lock in what the continuity cell and small-site teams restored so the core stays up without US keys.

## Priority
M10 EU Small-Site Clean Recovery Network, because closing the small clinic and town-hall backlog before it finishes next turn outranks starting the new backup standard — the standard only sticks if dark sites are first brought back clean.

## In practice
We run M10 as the field arm under NIS2 Article 11 and Article 222 solidarity: ENISA-EC3-health cell keeps mandatory reporting and segmentation, EuroHPC hosts supervised open-weight fallback, and mobile teams with Digital Europe redeployed staff push clean images from northern testing halls to dual-hit towns. Large hospitals stay on re-verification, small sites are sequenced by backlog, not by pressure.

We launch the new Standard via an ENISA implementing guidance plus Health and Justice Council conclusions, funded from Digital Europe and EIB spares — no new concrete, no override of southern courts. Southern sites stay in mediated renegotiation with co-investment for cooling and grid on the table. On dead US keys and lithography cuts we hold the single-negotiator line, keep allied volumes on paper, and tolerate the large member's compliant overflow cover while the EU stack becomes the default for essential services.
