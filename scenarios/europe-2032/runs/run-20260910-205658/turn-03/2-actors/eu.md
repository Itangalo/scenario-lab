Through ENISA and EU-CyCLONe for cyber: emergency segmentation audits, forced rotation and offline backups for relays, breakers and hospital logistics, and takedown requests for the exploit-kit forums via Europol EC3.

We keep M1 and M2 alive on paper with permitting zones and grid-connection fast-tracks but spend no new capital on them this turn, and we use M4 only to pre-book alternative freight and spares for medical and grid kits. Money is repurposed EU4Health and Digital Europe plus EIB top-up, with cost-sharing to buy in energy and interior ministers who fear permanent bills.
