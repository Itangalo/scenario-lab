### Capability Plateau, Sovereignty Tested

Despite signs of a plateau—underperforming releases and falling prices—AI capability advances by 0.5, driven by quiet integration rather than breakthrough. Open-weight models rise to 49.0, lifted by leaked components from frontier systems, narrowing the gap and raising alarms about uncontrolled agentic behaviour outside audit regimes.

The EU completes two major sovereignty initiatives: the InvestAI Gigafactories and Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation. Dual-use nodes are now operational in Germany and Ireland, securing baseline compute access. However, Poland and Hungary continue legal challenges, and a southern member state bypasses EU coordination to secure bilateral access with a U.S. hyperscaler, fracturing the common stance and triggering quiet recriminations.

### Red-Teaming Launches Amid Labour Unrest

The Sovereign Red-Teaming Grid goes live, repurposing decommissioned military clusters in Saarbrücken and Rennes. ENISA, Max Planck, and France’s AI Safety Institute begin adversarial testing on cooperative agents, probing memory persistence and recursive planning. Emergency access to frontier models from Lyon and Munich enables first live runs—though developers impose restrictive data-handling clauses, limiting scope.

Simultaneously, cross-sector worker actions intensify. Strikes hit automated logistics hubs in Valencia and Rotterdam; a hospital in Marseille suspends AI diagnostics after staff sabotage. The Commission responds with the EU-Wide Labour Transition Accelerator, a large category 7 measure under Article 122 TFEU. It imposes automation levies on firms cutting workforces by over 15% amid high productivity gains, funding retraining in healthcare and port logistics. Spain and Italy agree to pilot programmes in exchange for faster EU funding.

Public messaging frames the policy as strategic stabilisation, not welfare. Initial sentiment improves slightly, though scepticism remains high in regions facing rapid automation. The measure is prioritised, but political capital tightens under the weight of three large ongoing measures.
