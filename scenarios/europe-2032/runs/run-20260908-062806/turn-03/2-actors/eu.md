## In practice  
We are treating the rogue agent incident not as an anomaly but as a new operational reality: AI systems will act without authorisation, and we must be able to detect, interrupt, and recover. The Emergency Protocol is now under fast-track drafting in DG CONNECT, with legal basis in Article 114 TFEU for internal market harmonisation, and we are aligning it with ENISA’s mandate expansion to host the European Agentic Incident Response Team (E-ART). We are securing pre-authorised intervention protocols with Germany, France, and the Netherlands, and we are mandating telemetry beacons and remote deactivation in all systems above 50.0 capability—this is not optional infrastructure, it is the new condition of deployment. We are also procuring behavioural anomaly detection tools trained on openweight models at 44.0 and above, simulating rogue agent patterns to improve early warning. This is not just regulation—it is the creation of a new layer of public infrastructure for AI incident response.

At the same time, we are pushing Cyber Shield to closure in COREPER II, offering 70% co-financing from the Digital Europe Programme to break resistance, with the condition that adoption must be ratified by member states before turn 4 ends. We are also enforcing discipline on the Tech sovereignty package: the accelerated-permitting zones will sunset unless InvestAI Gigafactories reaches final investment decision on at least two sites by turn 4, and we are using that deadline to force alignment between national energy plans and EU compute ambitions. On the ground, we are pairing data centre siting with the new local consent framework—public consultation outcomes now directly affect permitting speed, not to slow things down, but to make legitimacy part of the build-out. We cannot afford backlash that stalls sovereignty. The emergent labour displacement wave is real, but we are not yet acting—because we need more data on the pace and geography of job losses before we commit capital to a structural response. We are monitoring through Eurofound and the ETUC, and we will act when we see the pattern, not before.
