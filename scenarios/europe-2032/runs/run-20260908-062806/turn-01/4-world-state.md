### Cyber Shield Proposed, Not Yet Adopted

However, the directive’s ambitious scope meets immediate resistance. Smaller member states balk at costs and centralisation, and negotiations in COREPER II stall. No agreement is reached this turn, and implementation cannot begin. As a result, only one new measure—Cyber Shield—is introduced, and it remains in negotiation.
