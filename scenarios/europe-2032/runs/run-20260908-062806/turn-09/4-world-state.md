### The Blind Are Leading

The EU declared a new era of AI sovereignty, announcing the Sovereign AI Core initiative to build and operate frontier models independently. Invoking emergency powers, the Commission proposed redirecting funds, repurposing EuroHPC infrastructure, and demanding member states align under a unified stack. But the moment of resolve was met with cascading setbacks.

The frontier surged again: a U.S. lab achieved a breakthrough in non-linguistic reasoning, pushing `ai_capability` to 79.5. This leap rendered existing interpretability tools obsolete overnight — the systems no longer produced human-readable chains of thought. Oversight frameworks, including the recently launched Emergency AI Safety Coordination Cell, were suddenly blind. No replacement monitoring capability was ready.

Simultaneously, the Union was locked out of the leading model. Hospitals relying on AI diagnostics, ministries running forecasting systems, and critical infrastructure operators using predictive maintenance were abruptly cut off. No explanation was given, no appeal possible. The denial was total, and it exposed the fragility of EU operations.

Publicly, the EU responded with defiance, branding the access cut a "strategic wake-up call" and fast-tracking the Sovereign AI Core. But behind the rhetoric, momentum stalled. Slovakia refused to dismantle its bilateral deal, arguing it ensured national continuity. Other states hesitated, fearing the cost and technical feasibility of the Core. Emergency procurement for chips faced export bottlenecks, and the Interpretability Directorate remained unfunded. The initiative remains in early implementation: staffing, governance, and technical architecture are still being negotiated. No operational capability has been delivered, and no technical progress on open-weight models has occurred.

Resilience held — past measures prevented systemic collapse — and a defensive cyber breakthrough improved detection of swarm attacks. But no new resilience policy landed. Public sentiment stayed stagnant, caught between fear and scepticism.

The political toll was significant. The portfolio charge drained already thin capital, and the failure to secure access or maintain oversight deepened institutional strain. The notepad records a −5 charge from ongoing initiatives, with an additional −1 from priority burden, but implementation delays have deferred the full cost. Political capital declined to 13.0, reflecting mounting pressure without total collapse.

Sovereignty, meanwhile, fell to 11.0. The Emergency Sovereignty Acceleration Facility concluded in turn 8, contributing +5, but this was offset by the −3 member_state_defection penalty and a −1 penalty from the rising capability gap. With no new sovereign capability emerging and access vulnerabilities worsening, the metric declined further due to erosion unmitigated by progress. The notepad’s prior note of “minor sovereign telemetry progress on LUMI test run” is retracted: no such progress occurred, and the narrative now reflects the full stagnation.

`ai_safety` declined to 9.0, reflecting a partial erosion due to the sudden obsolescence of monitoring systems. The notepad records a −3 event term: `safety_monitoring_collapse`, triggered by the capability jump and the absence of adaptive oversight capacity. This decay is consistent with the metric’s defined erosion rules, which allow for loss when monitoring fails and no replacement is operational. No catastrophic physical destruction occurred; the drop is attributed to systemic fragility in oversight infrastructure.

The Sovereign AI Core is now the EU’s sole priority. The previous priority, the Binding Safety Telemetry Mandate, has been formally deprioritized and is no longer active, resolving the portfolio conflict. Its proposal bonus has been revised: as a high-cost, category 4 measure, it receives only a +3 early-stage implementation credit, reflecting partial staffing and initial design work. Full effect will require multiple turns. No instant implementation has occurred.

The EU acted, but not yet with effect — and not yet with unity.
