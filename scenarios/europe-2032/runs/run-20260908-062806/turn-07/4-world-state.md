### The Weight of Closure  
The EU formally concluded five major initiatives: the InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty Package, Cyber Shield, Emergency Protocol, and Labour Transition Programme. These closures delivered modest but tangible gains—resilience edged up as critical infrastructure operators adopted baseline cyber standards, and the AI Impact Audits became institutionalised under the EU AI Observatory. The completion of large measures freed political capital, though the relief was partial: the portfolio had already been straining under its cumulative burden.

### Sovereignty in Motion  
The Emergency Sovereignty Acceleration Facility launched under Article 122 TFEU, framing compute as critical infrastructure. Disbursement mechanisms were established, and site planning began in Finland, France, and Italy. While physical deployment remains months away, the binding legal anchoring and integration with EuroHPC lent credibility. This progress, though early, arrested the erosion of sovereignty.

### Safety Under Pressure  
The new Emergency AI Safety Coordination Cell (E-ASC) began staffing and operational design. Though not yet fully functional, its mandate—real-time monitoring of systems above 55.0 capability—was seen as a necessary response to growing unease over unexplained agent behaviours. However, pushback emerged from two fronts: developers resisted telemetry mandates as intrusive, and civil liberties groups questioned the Cell’s oversight model, citing emergent protests against AI surveillance.

### Civil Disobedience and Fragmented Trust  
A wave of non-violent but technically sophisticated actions disabled AI surveillance systems in several cities, using adversarial techniques to blind cameras and facial recognition tools. While no physical damage occurred, the incidents highlighted deep public anxiety and eroded trust in automated governance. Authorities struggled to respond cohesively, underscoring the limits of current resilience frameworks.

### Shifting Metrics, Uncertain Ground  
AI capability advanced steadily to 70.5, driving open-weight models to 57.5. Despite completed resilience measures and the new Safety Cell, ai_safety rose only slightly—concerns over opaque reasoning persisted. Political capital improved modestly, buoyed by measure completions and the urgency of new action, but remained fragile.
