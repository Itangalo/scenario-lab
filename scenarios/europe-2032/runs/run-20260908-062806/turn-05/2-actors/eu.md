## New measure
**Mandatory AI Impact Audits for All High-Capability Deployments**  
A binding requirement for firms deploying AI systems above 50.0 capability to conduct independent, public-facing audits of workforce, legal, and societal impacts, with findings published in a central registry and used to trigger automatic retraining funding and oversight scrutiny.

## In practice  
We are treating the social fracture as the central constraint on all action. The Labour Transition and Wage Insurance Programme is now operational under the European Social Fund+, with the €45bn envelope approved and disbursement mechanisms activated. We are fast-tracking national implementation by offering 90% co-financing for the first 18 months, conditioned on integration with EURES and the Digital Skills and Jobs Platform. Germany and France have signalled early adoption, but resistance persists in smaller member states concerned about fiscal conditionality. We are leveraging the Tech sovereignty package to sweeten the deal: regions that accept retraining hubs will receive priority in the accelerated-permitting zones for new compute infrastructure, creating a feedback loop between social investment and industrial growth.

The Emergency Protocol for Unauthorised Agentic Activity is entering its final testing phase, with ENISA completing telemetry integration and E-ART cells in Paris, Berlin, and Amsterdam now conducting live drills against openweight agents at 48.0. The completion of Cyber Shield has laid a foundation, but compliance among micro-utilities remains patchy, and we are preparing enforcement actions for non-compliant operators. Meanwhile, the openweight_frontier_release has forced a reassessment: with open models now within 10 points of the closed frontier, the window for containment is closing. We are responding with the new transparency measure — not as a substitute for control, but as a way to make visible what can no longer be recalled. The mandatory audit requirement will be proposed under Article 16 of the AI Act, with enforcement tied to market access and fines up to 6% of EU turnover. This is not sovereignty in the sense of control, but sovereignty in the sense of agency: the power to demand answers, even when you can no longer demand obedience.
