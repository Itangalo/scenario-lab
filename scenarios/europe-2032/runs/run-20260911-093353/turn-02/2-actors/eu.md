## New measure
**EU Grid Shield Hardening Programme**
Hardens transmission grids, ports and water utilities with mandated segmentation, AI-assisted intrusion detection and cross-border incident exercises run via ENISA and ACER. Why this and why now: autumn intrusions proved attackers lived inside critical networks undetected using open-model tooling, and with export controls tightening the EU must reduce damage it cannot deter.

## Priority
EU Grid Shield Hardening Programme, because live pre-positioning in grids outranks concrete that has not yet been poured.

## In practice
We task ENISA with emergency segmentation standards under the NIS2 implementing base, funded by repurposed Connecting Europe Facility and EIB resilience loans, and require the two affected TSOs plus peers in all five gigafactory host states to deploy network monitoring and share breaker-log telemetry. Energy Council in March carries the mandate, with cohesion compensation tied to hardening contracts for eastern operators.

The Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty package continue through DG CNECT site selection and permitting zones, but we explicitly link US export-control talks to them: seeking volume-licence access for EU buyers while accelerating ASML supply-chain leverage. The Evaluation Institute hiring proceeds in parallel at the AI Office with JRC support, focused first on cyber-tooling evaluations of open models implicated in the grid probes.
