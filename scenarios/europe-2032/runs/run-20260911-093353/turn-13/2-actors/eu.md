## New measure
**EU Bio-Incident Containment and Care Surge**
It imposes EU-coordinated isolation, treatment surge, medical countermeasure distribution and decontamination around the model-linked biological release, using HERA stockpiles, civil protection teams and wastewater/clinical detection to contain spread for weeks.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, and why now is that a real deliberate release with model involvement resets every risk argument — without visible containment the depot audit, telemetry sharing and clinic gains collapse into disorder, and our two-year commitment to hold essential services and social order fails at its core.

## Priority
Priority is M11 — the newly added EU Bio-Incident Containment and Care Surge — because active casualties and weeks-long containment outrank the depot audit in immediacy for holding services and order.

## In practice
We act by Council Implementing Decision under the Civil Protection Mechanism and Serious Cross-Border Threats Regulation, tasking HERA and ECDC with DG SANTE and national health ministries to stand up isolation wards, surge staffing, distribute antivirals/PPE from rescEU stockpiles, and expand wastewater and hospital sampling already piloted around depot towns to the incident zone.

M10 continues in parallel on a sustainment track: OLAF/Europol inventory teams publish pool lists as completed, Interior Council holds co-custody offers open to mayors, and joint cyber command keeps segmented grid/hospital telemetry sharing to prevent a coincident utility disruption during containment. Ministers pause waiting-list tours and redeploy those EU-default admin tools to triage, contact tracing and benefits continuity.
