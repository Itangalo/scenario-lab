### Plugged in
Brussels finally said yes to two invitations it could not afford to refuse. In autumn, grid and hospital telemetry from segmented networks began flowing into a joint cyber command run with other states hit by the same intrusion wave, with real-time sharing and pooled attribution. At the same time, trade ministers signed the middle-power memorandum aligning export licences and bargaining jointly for compute access.

The first half worked faster than expected. When a fresh round of probes hit municipal utilities in November, isolation orders came with outside telemetry attached, and faults were cut off in hours rather than days. Police still guarded fuel yards and transformer stores, and videos mocking the stockpiles still circulated, but operators quietly admitted the shared feed bought them time.

The second half worked slower. The Hague aligned its servicing stance with Tokyo and Seoul under the new framework, and joint threats to withhold servicing and evaluation access were drafted. Washington noted them, eased a few quota cases, and kept the volume system intact. Clinics stayed on a European default with a metered fallback; factory rations did not lift.

The black-start and manual fallback network was declared complete in December — listed reserves, reimbursed cross-border aid, paper procedures drilled. It did not end the fight over the warehouses. A theft from a guarded pool in the east, first reported as sabotage then whispered as insider resale, poisoned local trust. Newly elected municipal councillors in three countries campaigned to ban guarded sites outright, turning each depot into a council vote.

The Commission toured the same story as proof that holding had worked: no autumn blackout, faster isolation, a seat at tables it had watched from outside. National coverage counted the cost instead — foreign telemetry inside domestic grids, alignment papers without chips, and restoration by hand still needing guards.

### Thin line
The common line held into winter, now visibly propped by outside help.
