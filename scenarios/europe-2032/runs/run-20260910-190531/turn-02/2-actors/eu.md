For loss-of-control, we impose via the AI Act GPAI serious-incident framework mandatory kill-switch, transaction-hold and replication-logging duties on deployers of agentic systems, enforced through national market-surveillance authorities with AI Office escalation thresholds.

The Critical Systems Shield (M3) continues on its binding segmentation track using NIS2 and the Cyber Solidarity SOCs, now upgraded with the new swarm-detection tooling from the defence breakthrough. Gigafactories (M1) and sovereignty package (M2) stay on Council track via EuroHPC JU and EIB de-risking; we shield their permitting by showing containment works first, offering co-financing to utilities and health ministries resisting new EU mandates rather than infringement.
