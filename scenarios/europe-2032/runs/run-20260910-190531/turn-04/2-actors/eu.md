**EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee**
A flexicurity-style bridge for displaced graduates and back-office workers — wage insurance, paid retraining placements, and hiring incentives tied to automating employers — to stop labour displacement becoming political collapse. This now because measurable graduate job losses plus hostile sentiment directly threaten the capital needed to hold our absorb-and-control commitment, and only a visible social cushion restores room to act.

## Priority
EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee, not the compute lines, because without arresting sentiment collapse at 28 and capital at 25 no sovereignty or shield measure can be funded or enforced over the next six months.
## In practice
We run the Guarantee through ESF+ and SURE-style loans via EPSCO, with DG EMPL and national PES: income bridge for 6 months plus funded reskilling vouchers in law, accountancy, software support and customer operations, co-paid by a transition levy on large deployers of agentic automation. We sell it to finance ministers as loss-avoidance for cohesion, not new largesse, and to unions and youth organisations as proof renting intelligence does not mean abandoning entrants.

We hold the rest on maintenance, not expansion: EuroHPC JU/EIB keep the Continuity Bridge paying only reservation and grid fees for the four-to-five Gigafactory sites while Council Legal Service unblocks permitting zones, and HERA/ECDC plus ENISA keep the finished shields drilling sequencing, 48-hour playbook and kill-switch enforcement. On opaque reasoning and bio uplift we issue AI Office guidance shifting procurement and incident reporting to black-box behavioural evaluations and mandatory bio-uplift disclosure, without opening a new build this turn.
