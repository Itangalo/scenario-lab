## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, not the Gigafactories, because the open cyber gate makes cascading infrastructure failure the binding constraint on both mandates over the next three turns.

## In practice
We task ENISA jointly with the Electricity Coordination Group and national TSO regulators to impose binding segmentation and credential-reset standards for transmission operators, using the NIS2 implementing framework and Cyber Solidarity Act SOC network for 24/7 swarm detection. Funding comes as top-up from Connecting Europe Facility and reprogrammed InvestAI deployment funds for OT monitoring, with joint exercises in Q4 2026 led with France, Germany and the Netherlands where affected operators sit.

The Gigafactories (M1) and tech sovereignty package (M2) continue on their Council-approved track: site selection via the AI Factories / EuroHPC JU, accelerated permitting under the Critical Raw Materials-style zones, and EIB de-risking for private data-centre capital. We do not renegotiate their scope this turn; we protect their grid connections and political headroom by showing visible protection success first. Member states resisting new EU mandates on utilities are offered co-financing and mutual-aid guarantees rather than infringement pressure at this stage.
