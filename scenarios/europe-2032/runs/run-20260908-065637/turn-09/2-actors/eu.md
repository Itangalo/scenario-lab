## Priority
Sovereign Evaluation Corps (SEC) — because without independent evaluation, the Union cannot know what it faces, cannot condition market access with credibility, and cannot build sovereign capability with confidence — and because this turn is the last in which SEC can land before the next crisis hits.

## In practice
We are treating SEC as the cornerstone of post-denial sovereignty. With ai_capability now at 59.5 and openweight at 51.0, the gap between what runs and what is understood has become dangerous. The bio_incident this turn proves it: when models are involved in deliberate harm, and no one can trace why or how, the Union’s legitimacy erodes. We are therefore activating DG-AI now, not later. The €7.2bn from cohesion funds is being deployed to stand up two secure enclaves — one in Luxembourg under EuroHPC oversight, one in Helsinki with CSC and Finnish Transport and Communications Agency coordination — where weights will be evaluated under ITAR-equivalent handling. ENISA’s first protocol suite, stress-tested against synthetic anomalies, will be applied to the incoming SMI training run and to all non-EU systems above 45.0 seeking deployment in health, energy, or public administration.

We are offering hyperscalers a clear bargain: full weight access in exchange for fast-tracked conformity assessments and liability shielding during declared emergencies. For those who refuse, we are preparing to invoke Article 59c of the AI Act — market suspension for non-cooperation — knowing that this will trigger retaliation, but also knowing that without enforcement, our rules are theatre. The JRC is coordinating red-team drills this quarter, simulating evasion of detection in agent behaviour logs, and we are seconding three lead interpretability researchers from Inria and Max Planck to accelerate legibility tools. This is not about distrust of foreign labs — it is about the Union’s right to understand what operates within its jurisdiction. If we cannot see it, we cannot secure it. And if we cannot secure it, we cannot sustain public trust.
