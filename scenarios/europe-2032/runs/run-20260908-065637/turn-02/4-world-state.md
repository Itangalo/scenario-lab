### A Sovereign Leap, Amid Cracks

The European Union launches the **Sovereign Model Initiative (SMI)**, aiming to transform the emergent cooperation among research labs into a legally anchored, EU-governed training run. Drafting begins on a new Article 58a under the AI Act to establish the European AI Foundation as trustee, with plans to secure weights, mandate audit access, and pool compute from national centres. €18bn in redirected Recovery and Resilience Facility funds is pledged, and hosting talks open with Germany and France. The move is framed as a decisive step toward technological independence.

However, momentum does not translate cleanly into cohesion. Poland and Hungary reject linkage between Cyber Shield compliance and SMI access, calling it coercion. Sweden’s bilateral deal with a US hyperscaler remains intact, and quiet skepticism grows in northern capitals about centralising control under Brussels. Internal legal scrutiny questions whether Article 58a can withstand challenge under EU treaty law, delaying formal adoption.

The **Cyber Shield for Critical Operators** remains stalled in negotiations, with no binding adoption. Its status as a priority does not accelerate consensus. Meanwhile, the **InvestAI Gigafactories** and **Tech Sovereignty Package** continue at reduced pace, burdened by overlapping demands on political capital.

Globally, AI capability advances to **53.0**, driven by a verification breakthrough that automates performance checks in law, engineering, and clinical coding. This widens the frontier, accelerating deployment. Yet **ai_safety** drops sharply to **30.0** as oversight fails to keep pace. No new safeguards land on deployed systems, and the gap between capability and control widens.

**openweight_capability** rises to **41.5**, lifted by anticipation of the SMI’s open evaluation framework and spillover from lab cooperation, though no weights are yet released. **resilience** holds steady at **39.5**, with no new hardening measures completed.

**eu_ai_sovereignty** increases to **26.0**, reflecting early institutional gains from SMI momentum and persistent **eu_access_secured**, despite ongoing capability growth and member-state friction. But **eu_political_capital** falls to **43.0**, strained by the cost of launching a large new measure and internal dissent.

Public sentiment remains ambivalent at **42.0**, with no major incidents or benefits visible yet.
