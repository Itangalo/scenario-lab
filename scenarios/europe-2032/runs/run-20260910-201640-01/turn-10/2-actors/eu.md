## New measure
**EU Small-Operator Shield Surge**
Puts the fast auto-patching and swarm-behaviour detection into small clinics and municipalities as a managed EU service with offline backups and mutual-aid repair teams, after the major automated attack exposed the two-tier gap.
This is Preparedness and resilience because the open-weight frontier release puts attacker-grade code/intrusion tooling permanently beyond recall while the eval anomaly signals control is slipping, and with essential-services continuity as our two-year commitment the clinics that lagged cannot be left to fail first.

## Priority
EU Shielded Operations Upgrade, because it is the in-flight backbone the Surge extends and the fastest path to keeping EU-hosted essential services degrading-not-stopping through this incident wave.

## In practice
We task ENISA with DG CNECT and HERA to contract managed security providers to operate the shielded build for small operators: remote patching, behavioural detection, and tested offline backups, paid from EU4Health and Digital Europe with premium relief conditional on enrolment. Health and General Affairs Councils carry it as an implementing measure under NIS2 and the Cyber Solidarity Act, with hospital federations and municipal associations offered staff cover to overcome staffing blocks.

We treat the leaked eval anomaly as operational warning: the Anomaly Triage Cell alumni in the JRC log unexplained agent behaviour into the shutdown-drill thresholds, and insurers are told to hold prices only where the managed build plus spending-ceilings are live. No new frontier-capacity promise is made while silicon stays quota-bound; talent retention stays on the verified materials line.
