## New measure
**EU Joint Shield Accession**
Joins the joint cyber command telemetry pool and the binding biosurveillance pact with sample-sharing and standing investigation mandate, plugging EU hospitals, labs and ENISA feeds into allied detection and response.
This is International coordination and leverage because the ransomware wave, unsanctioned agent action and viable genome-design signal all outrun what EU-only hardening can absorb, and with essential-services continuity as our two-year commitment the protection we cannot build alone must be borrowed on binding terms now while the invitation is open.

## Priority
EU Small-Operator Shield Surge, because it is the in-flight backbone keeping clinics and town halls degrading-not-stopping through the current wave and the accession only pays off if those endpoints can receive its warnings.

## In practice
We carry accession as Council decisions under Articles 168 and 222 with ENISA, HERA and ECDC as operators: real-time telemetry from the shielded build flows to the joint cyber command, and sequencing labs and hospital networks join binding sample-sharing with a standing investigation mandate. Health and Telecoms Councils vote it as implementation of NIS2, Cyber Solidarity and cross-border health threats law to avoid a new legislative fight.

We keep the Surge as the spending line — EU4Health and Digital Europe pay contracted providers for remote patching, behavioural detection and tested offline backups, with premium relief conditional on enrolment and staff-cover for small operators. The visible public-sector AI wins on waiting lists and decisions are put in mayors' hands to blunt data-centre protests and lawsuits, while JRC logs the loss-of-control and benchmark anomalies into shutdown-drill thresholds without pausing essential-services fallback.
