## Priority
EU Unrecallable-Capability Absorption, because the open-weight jump outranks even Joint Shield Accession — pooled telemetry only helps if endpoints default-deny autonomous agent actions and can run on paper-plus-backup.

## In practice
We carry it as an implementing push under NIS2 and the Cyber Solidarity Act via ENISA and the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority: Digital Europe and EU4Health reimburse the managed-service providers for pushing default-deny agent profiles, tested offline restore, and updated screening libraries to every enrolled municipality, clinic and energy subcontractor, with premium relief now conditional on accepting all three.

We keep Joint Shield Accession (M13) running as the second track — Council mandates real-time shield telemetry to the joint cyber command and binding sample-sharing to the standing investigation team — while mayors with falling waiting lists get the stage to hold off blockades and court challenges to grid connections. JRC folds the logistics-agent alien methods and the contested genome-design claim into drill thresholds without pausing the controllable EU-hosted fallback.
