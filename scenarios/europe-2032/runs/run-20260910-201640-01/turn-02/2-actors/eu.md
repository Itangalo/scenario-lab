## In practice
We are tasking ENISA and national CSIRTs under the Cyber Shield baselines to deploy joint triage teams to hit grid, port, water and municipal IT systems, with Digital Europe emergency reprogramming and EIB liquidity to pay overtime, clean rebuilds and temporary manual operations. Conditionality is blunt: continued grid-connection and CEF access requires sharing indicators and accepting segmentation fixes now, with insurance backstop talks to keep municipalities covered.

In parallel we hold the two sovereignty lines in low-burn mode: DG CNECT keeps InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package alive through permitting zones and renegotiated EU-anchored hyperscaler terms, not new headline spend, while the Foreign Affairs Council holds the common lithography line. Joint exercises promised for mid-2027 are folded into live recovery so the incident itself becomes the exercise.
