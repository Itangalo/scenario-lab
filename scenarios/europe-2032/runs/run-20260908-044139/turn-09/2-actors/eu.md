Despite the collapse of negotiations with U.S. labs over jurisdictional access to weights and logs, we are invoking the Anti-Coercion Instrument to suspend data adequacy benefits for non-compliant providers operating in Ireland and Finland. The Commission’s AI Office, backed by ENISA, is issuing non-conformity findings against two major platforms whose agents at 60.5 capability lack real-time monitoring hooks. This is not symbolic: disbursements from the InvestAI Modernisation Facility are now conditional on audit access, and we are redirecting €1.4bn in subsidies to consortia that comply. The framework is finished this turn in law, but only now begins its real test in power.

The AI Safety Talent Integration Directive is moving from policy to placement. Interior ministers in five member states have raised objections over fast-track visas and licensing bypasses, but we are tying cohesion fund disbursements to compliance timelines. ENISA has drafted the specialist competency framework—focusing on agent containment, red-teaming, and runtime interpretability—and we are launching the first recruitment wave through the Blue Card+ mechanism this month. Housing and licensing bottlenecks remain, but we are fast-tracking agreements with Berlin, Lyon, and Utrecht to repurpose public housing stock and create AI Safety Zones with streamlined local recognition. The recent safety breakthrough in model behaviour prediction—adopted rapidly by frontier labs—only sharpens the urgency: we must have our own people in position to deploy and verify these tools before they are rendered obsolete by the next leap.

Parallel to this, we are launching the Resilience Corps Pilot Expansion to scale the RTE-TenneT success across sectors. Drawing on the 40% reduction in false negatives achieved in grid-control monitoring, we are mandating live red-teaming of AI-operated rail signalling, hospital diagnostics, and water treatment systems by turn 10. ENISA and eu-LISA will co-lead drills with failover protocols under real-time stress, funded by a €900m allocation from the Civil Protection Mechanism. This is not preparedness as documentation: it is resilience as muscle memory. The openweight capability at 45.0 means even non-frontier actors can now deploy targeted attacks, and our only edge is the speed and coordination of our response. Talent, control, and resilience—these are the pillars now. We are building them at once, because we have no time to build them in sequence.
