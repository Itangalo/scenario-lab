## In practice
We are treating the intrusion findings as a system-wide wake-up call. Under Article 12 of the NIS2 Directive, we are convening an extraordinary COREPER meeting to activate emergency powers for cross-border critical infrastructure protection, directing ENISA to issue binding hardening mandates within 30 days to all EU operators of essential services in energy, water and transport affected by the intrusion. The Cybersecurity Act is being invoked to fast-track certification of the new swarm-detection tooling now proven effective, and we are unlocking €12bn from the Digital Europe Programme and the Recovery and Resilience Facility to co-fund implementation — with 70% grants for member states that complete deployment by Q1 2027. Resistance from national regulators unused to binding EU mandates is being met with political pressure in the Internal Market Council and assurances of full cost coverage, while the Commission’s Crisis Coordination Mechanism is being activated to run a live exercise in March 2027 simulating cascading failure. This is not a permanent fix, but it closes the most dangerous gap before the next release cycle lands.
