Cyber Shield Certification Regime  
A binding EU-wide certification and continuous monitoring framework for AI systems used in or interacting with critical infrastructure, requiring real-time anomaly reporting and third-party red-teaming.

## In practice  
We are treating the cyber_major_incident not as an outlier but as a signal of systemic fragility. The Emergency Resilience Surge ends this turn, having delivered partial hardening, but its ad hoc nature cannot be the template. Instead, we are moving to institutionalise continuous oversight. Under Article 14 of the Cyber Resilience Act and the revised AI Act’s critical infrastructure provisions, we are launching the Cyber Shield Certification Regime: a mandatory certification process for any AI system with operational influence over energy, water, transport, or electoral infrastructure. ENISA will be empowered to conduct unannounced red-team evaluations and require real-time telemetry on system behaviour, with non-compliant operators subject to service suspension and fines under the NIS2 enforcement mechanism. The European Cybersecurity Competence Centre will be re-tasked to staff regional red-teaming hubs, funded at €1.8bn over two years from the Digital Europe Programme.

We are facing resistance from both national operators, who see this as an infringement on operational autonomy, and from foreign vendors whose systems would fall under scope. To overcome this, we are pairing the mandate with a €4.2bn Modernisation Envelope — co-funded upgrades for certified systems, prioritising SMEs and municipal operators. In COREPER, we are framing this as the necessary successor to the emergency measures: not a one-off patch, but a permanent immune response. The election_annulled in a fellow democracy adds urgency — we cannot wait for definitive attribution to act when the tools are already loose and the vectors are systemic. This regime will not stop every attack, but it will make sustained, undetected operation far harder — and ensure that when failures occur, they inform the next layer of defence.
