## In practice  
We are finalising the Sovereign AI Assurance Framework’s integration into the AI Act via delegated acts under Article 68b, making real-time monitoring and interpretability non-negotiable for conformity assessment. The European Commission’s AI Office, supported by ENISA, is conducting compliance reviews with U.S. labs in Ireland and Finland: access to EU data markets and compute subsidies will be suspended unless model weights and training logs for systems above 55.0 capability are placed under auditable EU jurisdiction. In return, we are offering mutual recognition under the U.S. ALLIANCE posture—but only for systems that submit to the same verification regime as EU developers. The pilot with RTE and TenneT has demonstrated a 40% reduction in false-negative alerts in grid-control agents, proving the value of live monitoring; we are now scaling detection protocols to transport and health systems ahead of the Resilience Corps’ full deployment next turn.

The InvestAI Gigafactories are operationally complete, and we are shifting from construction to sovereign utilisation: the Modernisation Facility has disbursed €7.2bn for substation and fibre upgrades, and we are enforcing data sovereignty clauses that bind all training runs above 50.0 capability to EU legal jurisdiction and ENISA audit rights. No consortium receives final disbursement without it. But infrastructure without talent is inert. That is why we are launching the AI Safety Talent Integration Directive: we are activating Blue Card+ fast-track visas, mandating member states to waive licensing delays for 1,200 engineers specialising in agent containment, red-teaming, and interpretability, and placing them directly in sovereign labs, ENISA, and national cyber agencies. Resistance from interior ministries over labour market protections is being met with conditional cohesion funding adjustments—this is not optional. The openweight frontier release has brought 59.0-level capability within reach of uncontrolled actors, and our only defence is not just compute, but the people who can secure it. We act now, or we lose the ground we’ve built.
