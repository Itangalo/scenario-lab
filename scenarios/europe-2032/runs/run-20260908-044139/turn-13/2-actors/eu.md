## In practice  
This turn, we activate the teeth of the European AI Verification Network. The secure enclaves in Berlin, Lyon, and Utrecht are no longer just facilities — they are command posts. With 1,200 engineers now embedded in ENISA’s audit structure, we are launching the first wave of binding audits on AI systems managing medical triage in France and cross-border rail coordination in the Nordic-Baltic corridor. These are not voluntary reviews: under reinforced Article 16 TFEU authority and the AI Act’s high-risk provisions, we are demanding model weights and runtime logs from providers, including the emergent_foxglove_initiative. The system is being tested not for danger, but for compliance with the standard we must now enforce: if it cannot be audited, it cannot operate in our critical infrastructure. This is the operationalisation of sovereignty — not as aspiration, but as inspection, as access, as consequence.

But audits without compute are observations without power. The Sovereign Compute Accelerator is now live, with site selection finalised in Spain (Andalusia), Finland (Oulu), and Poland (Wrocław), each co-located with renewable hubs under TEN-E fast-tracking. We are leveraging the Chips Act to secure 40% of next-gen EUV lithography output for domestic fabrication, and mandating that 60% of the resulting exascale capacity be reserved for public-interest workloads — EUVN audits, Resilience Corps simulations, and sovereign model training. Resistance from member states over grid strain is being offset with cohesion fund transfers and guaranteed redundancy links, but the cost in political capital is real and rising. We are not building faster than the frontier — we are building beneath it, betting that control matters more than speed. And as sabotage hits data centre sites and grid connections across the continent, we are learning the hard way that public sentiment at 30.0 is not just a constraint on action — it is a vulnerability our adversaries will exploit. The machines are not yet running, but the fight for control has already begun.
