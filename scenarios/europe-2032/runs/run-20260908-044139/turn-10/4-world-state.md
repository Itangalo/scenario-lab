### A Fragile Window

Global AI capability edged upward to 62.0 as frontier labs absorbed the plateauing signals, adjusting expectations without halting progress. While no explosive leap occurred, underlying infrastructure improvements kept the trend alive. However, the **automated decision scandal** erupted in the Netherlands, where an AI system used in social insurance was found to have wrongfully denied thousands of claims over two years. An ombudsman’s report confirmed systemic bias and lack of appeal transparency, triggering public outrage and political fallout across several member states.

In response, the EU completed the **AI Safety Talent Integration Directive**, formally placing 1,200 engineers into critical agencies. Yet deployment remains fragmented — housing shortages and licensing delays prevent full operational integration. Still, the foundation is laid for the new **European AI Verification Network (EUVN)**, which launches this turn with €620 million redirected from the stalled InvestAI programme. Hosted under ENISA and distributed across Berlin, Lyon, and Utrecht, the EUVN begins building secure audit environments and runtime interpretability stacks. Its mandate: verify agent behaviour independently, free from vendor reliance.

The **Resilience Corps Pilot Expansion** becomes active in ten sites, conducting live red-teaming drills in hospitals and rail networks. Italy and Hungary resist binding failover mandates, citing sovereignty concerns, but cohesion fund incentives begin to shift positions. Early results show improved detection and degraded failure cascades, contributing to resilience gains.

Public sentiment drops to 30.0 amid the scandal, reinforcing anxiety about opaque AI control. The EU’s push for verification is welcomed by civil society but viewed skeptically by industry. Political capital rises slightly despite heavy costs, as decisive action on a damaging internal crisis earns measured approval.

### Sovereignty Through Technical Autonomy?

The EU avoids diplomatic dependence by investing in sovereign technical capacity. Though access to frontier models remains constrained under the ongoing **ALLIANCE** posture, the Union takes its first real step toward independent assurance.
