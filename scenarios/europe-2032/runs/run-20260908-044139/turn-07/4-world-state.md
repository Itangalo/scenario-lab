Yet safety assurance failed to keep pace. No breakthroughs in interpretability or control verification landed on deployed systems, leaving `ai_safety` stagnant at 27.0 despite growing risks.

The EU marked a milestone: the InvestAI Gigafactories programme formally concluded, with all four sites now under operational management and initial compute clusters powering up. Though full capacity remains months away, the completion has been recorded in the notepad as delivering a +6 boost to sovereignty. This significant step anchors critical compute infrastructure within EU legal and physical control, raising `eu_ai_sovereignty` to 24.0. However, this gain is tempered by ongoing failures in regional retraining programmes across several member states. With waiting lists exceeding six months and completion rates collapsing below 30%, the breakdown has eroded public trust and political cohesion. While the notepad confirms the Gigafactories' completion and its full sovereignty benefit, the broader governance strain from the emergent_regional_retraining_collapse continues to limit political momentum.
