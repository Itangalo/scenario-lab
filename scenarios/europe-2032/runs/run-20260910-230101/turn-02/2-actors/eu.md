## New measure
**EU Incident Reporting Clearinghouse**
Creates mandatory rapid reporting of serious AI incidents and near-misses to a common ENISA-run body with shared triage to operators.
This is Transparency and reporting because the open-weight frontier release puts unrecallable capability on private hardware permanently, so we must learn from deployment and incidents rather than from inside labs, directly serving our resilience commitment.

## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because the autumn pre-positioning plus an open model at the frontier means absorption and exercised continuity must land before the next probe acts, outranking even gigafactories this turn.

## In practice
We are pushing M3 through ENISA and HERA under the Cybersecurity Act Article 11 and Civil Protection Mechanism, using cohesion hardening funds to buy interior-ministry consent for mandatory drills and pooled detection procurement, starting with transmission operators, ports, hospitals and municipal networks where the first drills failed.

We prepare the Clearinghouse via a Commission implementing act tying NIS2 incident duties to AI-enabled events, staffed in ENISA with seconded CERT analysts, while keeping M1 and M2 alive in DG CNECT and the Competitiveness Council: investment guarantees and fast-track permits toward four-to-five FIDs, with grid priority conditional on EU legal anchoring to contain the subsidy race. ASML leverage stays held in reserve in the Foreign Affairs Council.
