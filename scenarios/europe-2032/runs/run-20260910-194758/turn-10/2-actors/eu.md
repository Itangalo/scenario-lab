## Priority
EU Trusted Cures Deployment, because with no measures in flight and capital at 7 only a delivered health benefit paired with the cyber-defence patching window can lift sentiment from 24 and fund anything else.

## In practice
We run this through DG SANTE and DG CNECT with EMA and ENISA as operators: joint procurement of the breakthrough therapies and model access under EU data-residency and audit terms, co-financed from Digital Europe and EU4Health, decided in EPSCO and JHA. Hospitals already touched by the finished Continuity Sprint are first sites, with automated patching kits pushed alongside clinical rollout.

We condition US volume-licence access on EU hosting and evaluation rights, using the finished Gigafactories legal groundwork and Anti-Coercion leverage without opening a new compute build, and we offer member states staffing relief if hardened clinical and cyber posts are filled from Shield retrainees. No parallel sovereignty programme — thin gigafactory shells stay as the sole compute line.
