## In practice
We run this through DG REFORM and DG CNECT with Digital Europe reprogrammed funds, using the finished Gigafactory site/grid readiness and the joint-procurement term sheet as the buying vehicle: Health and Education Councils nominate the three proven use-cases for EU-wide rollout, with procurement preference for EU-anchored compute. ENISA attaches baseline hardening and signature standards so diffusion does not widen the open-weight attack surface.

On the news blackout we hold mediation in the Trade and Technology Council — fast transparency compliance for a full stay — while EEAS keeps observer-level forensics sharing on the US hold-and-tier review. No permits are forced this turn; we make staying in the joint offer visibly cheaper than the side-deal by tying adoption funding to it.
