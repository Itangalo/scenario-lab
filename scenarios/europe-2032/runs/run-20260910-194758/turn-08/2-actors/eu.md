## New measure
**EU Displacement Transition Shield**
It funds wage insurance, rapid retraining and hiring incentives for graduate-entry roles hit by AI displacement, tied to employers retaining EU-anchored compute procurement.
This and now because labour displacement plus investment collapse directly threatens cohesion with capital at 8 and sentiment at 20, and only visible income protection holds the joint-procurement commitment together.

## Priority
EU Displacement Transition Shield, because without an answer to non-replaced entry-level jobs the hardening and procurement discipline in the two-year commitment cannot be enforced.

## In practice
We run this through DG EMPL with ESF+ reprogrammed plus unspent Digital Europe adoption funds, via EPSCO Council: member states nominate affected sectors — law, accountancy, software, customer ops — for wage-insurance top-ups and 6-month retraining vouchers, conditional on staying in joint procurement rather than side-deals.

ENISA and EU-OSHA attach baseline standards so retraining routes into hardened public services — health triage, permit processing — using the finished Gigafactory groundworks pipeline and trusted-source labels. Finance ministers are offered co-financing relief tied to no-defection clauses; on news blackout and US tier we hold existing mediation and observer forensics with no new concession this turn.
