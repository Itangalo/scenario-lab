## Priority
**Establish the European Sovereign AI Corps with Fast-Track Talent Access and Secure Compute Anchoring** — because the failure of two prior sovereignty initiatives was not due to lack of hardware or funding, but absence of people to operate and secure them, and because without a core of trusted, embedded talent, no evaluation, control, or resilience measure can land effectively.

## In practice
We are acting on the recognition that sovereignty is not infrastructure alone, but infrastructure *in use* — and that the Gigafactory in Marseille, the Article 116 data framework, and even the new evaluation body are all dormant without the human expertise to wield them. This turn, we activate the European Sovereign AI Corps under Article 172 TFEU, mobilising €1.2 billion from the Digital Europe Programme, Horizon Europe, and the European Defence Fund to begin recruitment of 500 specialists over three years, with 200 to be onboarded by turn 14. The focus is on agent safety, weight security, and red-teaming — the skills most critical to both control and evaluation. DG HR, DG CONNECT, and the European Defence Agency are coordinating with universities in France, Germany, Finland, and the Netherlands to identify candidates, with priority given to diaspora researchers and those exiting closed labs where transparency is eroding.

We are also integrating the **Accelerate Open-Weight Absorption** red-teaming nodes in Saclay, Garching, and Helsinki into the Corps as feeder programmes: successful participants will be fast-tracked into permanent roles, turning defensive experimentation into a talent pipeline. At the same time, we are offering cohesion funds to smaller member states that adopt modular red-teaming as part of Energy Council and Health Security Committee compliance, not to compel unity, but to demonstrate that EU-level coordination can deliver tangible operational advantages. The bilateral compute deal with the U.S. hyperscaler exposed our fragility, but we are building something deeper — a cadre that cannot be outsourced, poached, or bypassed. This is not a sprint to parity, but the foundation of enduring agency.
