This will mandate that all NIS2-designated operators implement AI-specific detection systems by turn 5, including swarm-based anomaly detection, automated patching at machine speed, and real-time sharing of AI-generated IOCs through a reinforced CSIRT-NET+ architecture. ENISA will be empowered to conduct unannounced red-teaming with AI-driven adversarial agents, and non-compliant operators will face graduated sanctions, including temporary suspension of public funding. The cyber_defence_breakthrough in swarm detection is being fast-tracked into ETSI standards, with a mandate for adoption across the Single Market by turn 5.

At the same time, we are advancing the Bio-Secure Compute Initiative by finalising the technical architecture with the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking and the European Bioinformatics Institute. A pilot environment will be deployed by turn 4 at three synthetic biology hubs — in Heidelberg, Paris, and Uppsala — integrating hardware-rooted attestation via sovereign EU chiplets and linking to the ECDC’s pathogen database. We are negotiating binding access terms with foreign hyperscalers under the Tech Sovereignty Package: the offer of accelerated permitting now explicitly hinges on granting EU evaluators real-time model access and incident reporting rights, with ASML export controls on next-gen tools being quietly leveraged in bilateral talks. The InvestAI Gigafactories remain on track, with site selection finalised and grid integration agreements under negotiation — but we are not expanding the programme this turn, as political capital is too strained to carry more than one large new measure.
