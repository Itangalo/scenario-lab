### A Leap in Capability, a Push for Control

The global AI frontier advances rapidly. With the **verification_widens** development enabling automated validation in law and clinical coding, closed systems achieve new levels of autonomous performance. This accelerates progress across domains, pushing **ai_capability** to 70.5.

Simultaneously, an unprecedented **openweight_frontier_release** floods research networks with a model approaching the closed frontier. Initial replication is limited: only a handful of well-resourced labs in Europe and North America manage partial deployments, constrained by GPU availability, energy infrastructure, and skilled personnel. Open capability rises to 54.0 — a significant jump, but still short of parity. Circulation of derived tools — including offensive cyber modules and synthetic media generators — begins, but at a slower pace than feared, as bottlenecks in compute and expertise slow diffusion.

In response, the EU activates its **Accelerate Open-Weight Absorption** initiative under emergency powers, directing certified labs in Saclay, Garching, and Helsinki to adapt the model for defensive red-teaming. However, replication remains incomplete by turn’s end; air-gapped simulations are scheduled to begin in the next turn, pending hardware upgrades and staff augmentation. No full red-teaming results are yet available.

The **Independent EU Front-Run Evaluation Capability** completes its launch this turn, issuing its first binding assessment — a milestone that boosts confidence in EU oversight. However, no sovereignty-boosting measure finishes, and rising capability erodes dependence: **eu_ai_sovereignty** falls slightly to 23.0.

Resilience improves as earlier red-teaming investments yield detection rules, and ENISA begins integrating them into cross-sector protocols. The proposed **Expand Red-Teaming to Cross-Border Critical Infrastructure** measure is under review but not yet launched, pending intergovernmental agreement. Smaller states remain hesitant, and compliance grants are still being negotiated.

Political capital rises moderately. The completion of the evaluation body delivers a +4 boost, and the new category 6 measure earns a +3 proposal bonus for directly answering recent attacks. These gains are partially offset by ongoing portfolio charges (−9) and the priority cost, resulting in a net increase to 31.0.

Public sentiment inches up, reflecting cautious approval of proactive defence. Safety sees a marginal gain from structured testing, though interpretability remains poor.

### Fractures Beneath the Surface

Talent shortages stall the **EU Frontier AI Development Initiative**. Despite fast-track visas, only 80 researchers commit by mid-year. The Marseille Gigafactory runs at 40% capacity. Sovereignty ambitions outpace execution. The sudden release of a powerful open-weight model exposes structural gaps in the EU’s ability to absorb and adapt frontier systems at scale — a challenge that will persist into the next turn.
