## In practice
We are operationalising the gains from the open-weight release not as a passive diffusion but as a lever: the same model that powered the recent attacks is now being turned against itself. This turn, we activate the **Accelerate Open-Weight Absorption** initiative with €300 million drawn from the Digital Europe Programme and the European Defence Fund, fast-tracked under Article 222 TFEU for emergency coordination. DG CONNECT and the European Defence Agency are directing three certified labs — in Saclay, Garching, and Helsinki — to validate and adapt the model for red-teaming on air-gapped systems, with weight integrity verified by the now-operational EU evaluation body. The model will never touch public networks; its sole purpose is to simulate cascading failures in power grids, rail signalling, and hospital IT under worst-case scenarios.

Meanwhile, the **EU Frontier AI Development Initiative** reaches its final stretch: the Article 116 data pooling regulation clears the Council this month, unlocking cross-border training data under strict governance. The first Horizon call for consortia launches in September, but talent remains the bottleneck. We are finalising a fast-track visa and housing package with France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Poland, Finland, and the Netherlands to attract 200 core researchers by turn 10, offering access to sovereign models and secure compute as a competitive edge over private-sector labs. The Gigafactory in Marseille remains underutilised — not from lack of will, but from lack of people who can run it.

We are also scaling resilience beyond siloed national efforts. The new measure, **Expand Red-Teaming to Cross-Border Critical Infrastructure**, builds directly on the absorption initiative, mandating joint exercises across energy, transport, and health sectors by turn 12. ENISA will coordinate, using detection rules refined from the Finnish red-teaming pilot, and we are pushing for adoption in the Energy Council and Health Security Committee. Smaller member states fear cost and complexity — we are offering compliance grants from cohesion funds and a modular toolkit co-developed with Estonia and the Netherlands. The goal is not perfection but continuity: when the next incident hits, services degrade, not collapse.
