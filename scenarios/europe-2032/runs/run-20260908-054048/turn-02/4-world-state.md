Though no live agent was created, the demonstration is peer-reviewed and replicated. It mandates hardware-rooted attestation, output logging, and centralised DNA synthesis screening. The move is framed as sovereign self-protection, not global governance.

However, implementation planning is only now beginning. The initiative is named the EU's new priority, suspending further action on other large measures pending coordination review. No infrastructure is yet deployed, and technical design remains in early stages. While the announcement signals strategic intent, no sovereign capability has been built—only the decision to pursue it. Such declarations alone do not constitute infrastructure, and thus do not yet register in `eu_ai_sovereignty`. The metric remains at 22.0, awaiting tangible progress.
