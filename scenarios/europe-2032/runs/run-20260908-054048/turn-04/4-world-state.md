### Frontier Crosses Threshold  
AI capability reaches 60.0 as leading labs deploy autonomous agents capable of completing multi-day engineering and research projects end-to-end. These systems now contribute directly to their own successor training pipelines, marking a qualitative shift. The closed frontier pulls decisively ahead, though no safety mechanisms have kept pace.

### Open Weights Catch a Glimmer  
An open release of a general-purpose model, trained on curated scientific and technical data, lifts `openweight_capability` to 45.0. The model lacks full autonomy but demonstrates strong performance in software generation and structured reasoning under supervision—confirming that frontier capabilities are beginning to leak into the open, albeit selectively.

### Sovereignty Push Amid Strain  
The EU launches the **Sovereign AI Access Framework**, demanding foreign hyperscalers deploy legally ring-fenced "sovereign mirrors" on EU-controlled infrastructure or lose access to public procurement. Emergency negotiations under Article 114 TFEU begin, targeting critical suppliers in health and energy. Initial technical deployment leverages InvestAI Gigafactory capacity and Bio-Secure nodes, with 15% of compute reallocated.

However, pushback emerges immediately. U.S. providers label the move coercive, and legal challenges loom over extraterritorial enforcement. ASML-linked leverage yields only vague assurances, not binding access. The **Tech Sovereignty Package** is tightened to condition fast-tracking on mirror commitments, but investor hesitation grows.

### Resilience Holds, Capital Erodes  
The **Emergency Resilience Surge** concludes, and **Secure the Core** proceeds with red-teaming that exposes deep flaws in software supply-chain trust. Patches roll out, but no resilience gains materialise beyond the existing baseline. Public sentiment remains stagnant at 37.0, caught between visible threats and distrust of top-down mandates.

Political capital plummets under the weight of five large measures, including the new priority. The **Sovereign AI Access Framework**, while symbolically powerful, delivers no immediate sovereignty gain—only the promise of future redundancy. The cost is steep: coordination frays, and capital drops to 28.0, nearing the threshold where agenda control weakens.
