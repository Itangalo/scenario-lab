### US Posture Hardens: Strategic Access Begins  
The new US administration takes office under a **CONSOLIDATION** doctrine, treating frontier AI as a tiered national asset. Access is now formally rationed, with the EU placed in a conditional tier requiring political alignment for full model access. This marks a structural shift: AI is no longer a commodity, and dependence carries strategic cost.

### Sovereign Access Push Meets Resistance  
The EU activates emergency powers, designating major US hyperscalers as AI-Critical Suppliers and mandating sovereign mirrors within six months. ENISA and the AI Office repurpose decommissioned Bio-Secure nodes and allocate 15% of InvestAI Gigafactory capacity. However, US providers reject the move as extraterritorial overreach. No compliance occurs, and legal challenges loom in both the WTO and domestic courts. The **Sovereign AI Access Framework** is now the EU’s priority measure, entering the implementation pipeline.

### Evaluation Body Announced, Not Yet Operational  
The **Independent EU Front-Run Evaluation Capability** is formally announced, aiming to institutionalize sovereign judgment over frontier models. Germany and France agree to co-lead, but member-state resistance to centralized authority delays staffing and funding. The body has no operational capacity this turn and will not begin functioning until at least Turn 7. Legal validation remains pending; no binding authority is granted at launch.

### Capability Leaps, Safety Stalls  
A **capability_jump** in verifiable domains—code, math, cyber—demonstrates discontinuous progress, widening the gap with safety. Concurrently, **verification_widens** into clinical coding and contract review, accelerating domain-specific AI deployment. Despite the new evaluation initiative, safety assurance fails to keep pace.

### Resilience Plateaus  
The completion of **Secure the Core** and **Bio-Secure Compute Initiative** lifts resilience to 53.0, but no new category 6 measures offset rising threats. The system holds, but does not advance.

### Political Capital Adjusted for Partial Burden  
Five large measures remain in flight, including the newly prioritized **Sovereign AI Access Framework**. The **Independent EU Front-Run Evaluation Capability** is announced but not yet implemented, contributing only partial administrative burden. Political capital decreases slightly due to governance strain, settling at 27.0. Public sentiment remains stagnant at 37.0—aware of risks, but unconvinced by top-down solutions.
