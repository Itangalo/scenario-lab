## In practice  
We are shifting from regulating access to owning capability. The **Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive** remains in flight, but we now treat it as a fallback governance tool, not the core of our sovereignty strategy. Its political cost has been steep, and it has failed its central test: keeping essential services online when the outside world decides otherwise. We will complete the directive’s passage — withdrawing now would signal defeat — but we are redirecting urgency to what actually builds autonomy: a model trained on EU soil, under EU law, with weights legally and physically secured here.

This turn, we launch the **Frontier Foundation Model Initiative** through a joint act of the Commission and the European Council, invoking Article 17(2) TEU for urgent action. The European Digital Infrastructure Consortium (EDIC) will lead procurement of dedicated exascale clusters, prioritising hardware from EU-vetted suppliers under new chip-sovereignty rules. Training will be conducted under strict red-teaming protocols managed by a newly empowered AI Office, with interpretability integration from day one. Access will be limited to vetted public institutions — health agencies, crisis response units, safety researchers — via a secure enclave model. We are not chasing every application; we are building one unblockable node of agency.

Resistance will come from multiple directions. Some member states will argue this duplicates private efforts. We counter by making participation mandatory for any firm receiving benefits under the Tech sovereignty package. The US will frame this as protectionism. We respond through the Trade Security Instrument, conditioning market access on reciprocal model availability for EU critical functions. Internally, we face talent drain and skepticism about speed. To address this, we are fast-tracking residence and work permits for frontier AI engineers under a new "Sovereignty Talent Corridor", backed by housing and research grants. This is not a race we can win by negotiation. It is one we must run ourselves.
