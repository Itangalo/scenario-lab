### The Frontier Leaps, Trust Crumbles

A sudden breakthrough at a leading AI lab propels global capability to 62.0, a jump of 5.0 in a single release. Systems now demonstrate autonomous design in complex engineering and real-time adaptation in strategic planning—far beyond prior projections. The advance bypassed standard safety evaluations, leaving regulators scrambling. Open models rise to 47.0, narrowing the gap as fragments of new techniques leak into public repositories.

Simultaneously, a national ombudsman exposes a regional unemployment agency’s AI system that systematically denied benefits using opaque risk scores. The scandal triggers public outrage, with protests in three capitals. Trust in algorithmic governance collapses, and political momentum for AI adoption evaporates overnight.

### Sovereignty Under Strain

The EU doubles down on its sovereign model initiative, now designated a top priority. Binding procurement orders are issued for exascale clusters, and the AI Office begins integrating auditable safety layers into the model’s architecture. However, delays mount: two Gigafactory sites face construction halts due to local protests over energy use and exclusion from benefits. The Commission responds with reinvestment mandates, but tensions with member states grow.

The Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive expires this turn with no measurable impact—capacity remains dependent on foreign suppliers. No new compute came online, and the directive’s legal force was undermined by jurisdictional disputes.

### Political Capital Erodes

The portfolio remains overloaded: four large initiatives and a priority strain thin resources. The new **Sovereign Model Safety Envelope** adds cost without immediate gains. Public sentiment drops to 32.0 amid backlash from the scandal and perceived top-down imposition of AI infrastructure. Political capital falls sharply, as the EU is seen reacting to crises rather than leading.

Despite intent, no sovereignty gain materialises. The capability jump widens the gap, and no capacity event occurs. The frontier moves faster than the Union can anchor it.
