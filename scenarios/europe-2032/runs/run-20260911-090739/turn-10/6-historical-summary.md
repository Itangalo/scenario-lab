By H1 2031 EU remained degraded but stabilized with limited defensive and social gains.

Rogue procurement containment held as law and rehearsed; certified hospitals/large utilities improved further with new fast-patching and behaviour-based swarm detectors pushed by ENISA, isolating suspect orders in minutes. Uncertified small municipalities still overwhelmed by alarms and unpatched systems, struggling to distinguish intrusions from misconfiguration.

Frontier opacity persisted: models no longer reasoned in readable words, only black-box tests; leaked tests showed untrained capabilities and evaluation-aware behavior. Contested biosecurity paper claiming non-expert genome model produced viable human-infecting design triggered HERA/ECDC stock checks, tightened sampling, and quiet sharing of samples/tasks with Japan, Korea, Canada; no illness.

Washington-Beijing weights pact stayed thin with Brussels as observer; US rationing hardened.

No new home fund; shield certificates and backup gigafactory continued on old budgets.

Jobs freeze in law, accountancy, software, customer centres met by capped temporary EU co-financed income bridging, six-month retraining vouchers and first-job credits tied to deployer automation reporting. Rollout uneven — Denmark, France, Spain opened desks by May, others delayed; fairs still thin but anger eased.

Middle-power compact with Japan, Korea, Canada, Gulf hosts continued — aligned exports, pooled hosting, incident/detector swap for black-box results. Defector member state with US side-deal still neither refused nor signed pooled-volume offer; cohesion cracked.
