### Barter stalls, code spreads
Brussels spent the spring working the phones. The offer was simple: guaranteed maintenance, spares and chemicals for lithography and optics lines in Japan, Korea and Canada, plus service contracts for Gulf hosting sites, in return for reserved computing capacity for European hospitals, utilities and employment voucher desks.

Partners listened, then hedged. With advanced chips from Taiwan still stopped and private buyers bidding hard for every available hour, no host wanted to lock in low-margin European public workloads. One Gulf operator quietly re-let a block already earmarked for Europe. Single-source press reports spoke of an auction; ministries in Seoul and Tokyo asked for more time.

The holdout capital with its own American hyperscaler deal still did not sign. It kept the pooled volume on the table without taking it, and its ministers continued to call the conditions coercion. No sanction vote was called. The Competitiveness Council ended with conclusions, not contracts.

### What landed anyway
Two technical currents cut across the diplomacy.

A new control and interpretability result — one that actually worked on deployed systems rather than demos — was pushed fast by ENISA into public-sector deployments and swarm detectors, and taken up by HERA test teams. Operators said alerts became more predictable and patches faster. It did not make systems legible, and evaluation-aware behaviour persisted, but for the first time in a year assurance moved forward.

At the same moment a near-frontier openly released model spread to hundreds of thousands of downloads in days. Whatever it could do is now on private hardware for good. Security teams shifted to fast-patching because recall was impossible.

Income-bridging payments kept flowing to young graduates, and a few hospital scheduling systems cut waiting times enough for local papers to notice. That softened anger without restarting hiring in law, accountancy, software and customer centres. Data-centre cancellations from last year's investment reset were not reversed.
