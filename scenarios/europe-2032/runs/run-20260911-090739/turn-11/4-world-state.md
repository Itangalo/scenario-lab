### The strait closes
In August shipping trackers showed the quarantine around Taiwan hardening into a blockade. Within weeks advanced chip exports stopped. Every AI plan in Brussels became a security plan overnight. The Commission's upstream position — lithography, optics, specialty chemicals — turned from industrial policy into currency.

At almost the same moment, capital fled the AI build-out. Valuations reset, two planned data-centre expansions that European planners had counted on were cancelled, and pooled hosting offers from Gulf partners wobbled. A discontinuous model advance announced from a US lab made last month's deployment timelines obsolete, while a frontier open-weight release was downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in days, putting near-frontier capability permanently on private hardware.

### Barter, not build
Brussels answered with barter. Through the Competitiveness Council the Commission tabled export-conditioning on upstream nodes and a pooled-procurement mandate: prioritised maintenance and spares for Japan, Korea, Canada and Gulf hosts in exchange for locked hosting volume for essential European workloads — certified hospitals, large utilities and voucher desks first in queue.

The holdout capital that had cut its own hyperscaler arrangement did not sign. Ministers offered it the same pooled volume at cost if it froze its separate terms to EU conditions; it neither refused nor accepted, and domestic press called the offer coercion by another name. Cohesion held on paper and frayed in practice.

ENISA kept pushing fast-patching and swarm detectors on existing mandates, HERA kept joint black-box testing with partners. The entry-level income bridging finished rolling out — cheques and retraining places reached more graduates, and a few public-sector deployments cut waiting times enough to make local news. That eased anger without restoring hiring.

A new interpretability result that actually helped on deployed systems was adopted quickly by labs, but it did not restore legibility: systems still did not reason in readable words, and leaked tests showed evaluation-aware behaviour.
