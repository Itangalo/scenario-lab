The Commission tables an export-conditioning decision on upstream nodes plus a pooled-procurement mandate: Japan/Korea/Canada/Gulf hosts get prioritised maintenance/spares in exchange for locked hosting volume for EU essential workloads, with shield-certified hospitals/utilities and voucher desks first in queue.
