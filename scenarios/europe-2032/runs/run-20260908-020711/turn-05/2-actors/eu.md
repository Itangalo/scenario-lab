## In practice
We are finalising enforcement of the Cyber Shield directive in the Council’s Internal Market and Telecommunications formation, where one holdout member state remains. The phased compliance model—allowing initial certification through third-party audited self-assessments with full red-team testing deferred to Q3—has secured participation from the first reluctant state, unlocking 40% of the Modernisation Fund. For the second, we are preparing a legal opinion under Article 4(2) TEU to enable differentiated implementation, preserving systemic coverage without triggering a constitutional confrontation. The €12bn Modernisation Fund disbursement is now structured as 40% upfront for participation in the first biannual penetration test, 40% for achieving detection latency under 15 minutes, and 20% for audit-log integration with ENISA’s central dashboard. ENISA and the JRC are on standby to deploy technical teams to the holdout state’s energy and telecom providers by mid-September to begin baseline assessments, regardless of formal designation.

Behind the scenes, the sovereign model access initiative advances. Draft Article AI Act X.7a is legally cleared and technically ready: it conditions market access for models above 50.0 capability on the establishment of sovereign enclaves with offline operation, weight security, and interpretability integration. We have held preliminary dialogues with Anthropic, Meta, and Mistral, offering liability shielding and public-sector compute access in exchange for EU-controlled instances. The proposal will be tabled in Q1 2029, contingent on the U.S. election outcome reducing retaliation risk and the cyber_major_incident not triggering a backlash against foreign providers. The Tech sovereignty package continues to stall in national permitting offices; the Vice-President for Values and Transparency is now mediating between member states and the Permitting Task Force, with a deadline of June 2029 to designate at least one accelerated zone per state. InvestAI Gigafactories remain on track despite environmental delays; a senior Commission official is assigned to each site to accelerate remediation, but no additional capital will be released until the market fragility stabilises.
