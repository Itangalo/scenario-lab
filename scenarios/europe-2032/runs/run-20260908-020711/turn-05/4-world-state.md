### The Summer of Fragility

A major cyber intrusion, fully automated and exploiting model-generated zero-days, sweeps across public services in three member states. Hospitals, transit systems and local governments face days of degraded operation. The attack tooling, later traced to a non-state actor, confirms that offensive capabilities have surged past defences. In the aftermath, attribution remains murky, and public trust frays.

Despite the urgency, the EU’s Cyber Shield directive remains in flight — not formally closed, as enforcement falters. One member state accepted a phased path, unlocking partial funding. A second, citing constitutional limits, continues to resist designating critical operators, delaying red-teaming mandates. Without full implementation, systemic resilience remains patchy, and the incident exposes the gap.

Public sentiment sours. Media coverage focuses on disruption and dependency, with growing anger toward both AI providers and EU institutions. A wave of job losses in legal and administrative sectors amplifies the backlash. The Commission delays its sovereign model access initiative, wary of provoking retaliation from U.S. firms amid the incoming administration’s anti-AI shift.

Meanwhile, compute fragility deepens. Mid-tier leasing providers collapse, disrupting AI projects across startups and public labs. Planned capacity evaporates overnight, revealing structural vulnerabilities. The Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty package continue slowly, but no new compute comes online this turn.

The U.S. election concludes: a retrenchment victory. Washington signals a sharp inward turn — moratoriums, restrictions, and skepticism toward frontier AI. The EU gains breathing room but loses a key partner. American progress slows, and the global frontier now hinges on other powers.

### A Widening Gap

With `ai_capability` rising to 57.0, open-weight models advance as developers release weights near the prior frontier. `openweight_capability` increases to 47.0 — a measured gain reflecting diffusion of older architectures, but still trailing `ai_capability` by 10 points. The pace remains constrained by limited access to frontier-scale compute within the open ecosystem.

`ai_safety` falls as capability advances outpace assurance. Resilience erodes under the cyber blow and incomplete shield. Sovereignty declines further, as the EU fails to secure access or expand capacity. Political capital softens under the weight of stalled measures and rising crisis, but not collapses — the core institutions retain legitimacy despite setbacks. Public sentiment dips, reflecting economic anxieties, but broad stability holds for now.
