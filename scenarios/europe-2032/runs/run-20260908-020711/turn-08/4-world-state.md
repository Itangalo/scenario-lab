### Access Denied, Sovereignty Tested

The EU’s frontier AI access was abruptly severed, disrupting hospitals and ministries reliant on the leading model. No explanation followed, only silence — a stark demonstration that capability without guaranteed access is fragile. The event exposed a critical gap: even with Gigafactories nearing completion and talent initiatives underway, sovereignty depends on enforceable pathways, not just infrastructure.

In response, the Commission began preparatory work on the *Sovereign Access Initiative*, invoking Article 36 TFEU to assess fallback systems and model portability for essential public functions. Legal analysis is pending, and no funds have been reallocated yet. The proposal to repurpose InvestAI financing remains under debate, requiring formal Council approval before any budgetary shift can occur. Hungary and Poland questioned whether this deepened Western dependency under a new guise, demanding proof of actual deployable alternatives before endorsing the measure. With the European AI Research Corps still in flight as the designated priority, no new large-scale implementation was launched this turn.

Meanwhile, an open-weight release from a non-EU source distributed near-frontier capability globally, increasing accessibility. Developers confirmed downloads exceeded 200,000 in days — once loose, it cannot be recalled. However, this diffusion did not contribute to the EU’s *openweight_capability*, which remains tied to internally accumulated development and infrastructure. No evaluation regime covered the new model, and its reasoning remained opaque. As global capability advanced, ai_safety eroded.

Resilience dipped despite Cyber Shield’s completion; coordination gaps during the access crisis revealed overreliance on external platforms. Public sentiment worsened as health IT disruptions made headlines, framed as “dependency humiliation.” Labour displacement accelerated, feeding backlash.

The Research Corps moved forward slowly. First clusters formed at Max Planck and INRIA, but compute allocation delays frustrated early participants. With one large measure ongoing and two major proposals under review, political capital experienced moderate strain. The PORTFOLIO CHARGE of −4 and PROPOSAL BONUS of +5 resulted in a net gain of +1, leaving eu_political_capital unchanged at 38.0. The US posture of RETRENCHMENT intensified uncertainty, offering no relief.
