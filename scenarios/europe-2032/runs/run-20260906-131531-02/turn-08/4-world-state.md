### A Surge at the Frontier

Global AI capability advances to 83.0, driven by recursive self-improvement in closed labs. The leap widens the chasm between what systems can do and what humans can verify. With no legible reasoning trails, safety assurance collapses further—developers admit they no longer understand how frontier models reach conclusions. The gap between capability and control yawns, and trust erodes.

Open-weight capability rises to 59.0, reflecting delayed spillover from earlier breakthroughs now re-implemented in open frameworks. Though still behind the frontier, this steady progress enables broader replication of advanced agent-like behaviors outside elite institutions. Cyber actors and bio researchers with modest resources are beginning to access tools once confined to frontier labs, but the diffusion remains incremental—no single release or leak has accelerated the curve.

### Sovereignty in Crisis, Sovereignty in Motion

The EU confronts a crisis of agency. Despite completing the InvestAI Gigafactories and the Resilience Surge Protocol, a group of member states’ emergency request for sovereign compute access went unanswered—legal disputes and bilateral conditions blocked deployment. The failure exposed a fatal flaw: ownership without operational control is not sovereignty.

In response, the Commission proposes the Sovereign Compute Activation Protocol, a large category 4 measure aimed at pooling EuroHPC resources under emergency command. Framed under Article 122 TFEU, it would override national vetoes during AI emergencies. Germany and France are offered concessions to secure buy-in, and negotiations intensify. The measure is designated the EU’s current priority, signaling a shift toward centralized crisis response. However, no operational integration occurs this turn—the legal and governance hurdles require time, and the protocol remains in negotiation.

The European AI Emergency Response Corps’ ratification continues, but legal challenges delay full activation. With four large measures in flight—including this new proposal—the portfolio charge remains crushing, and political capital stays low at 5.0.

### Glimmers Amid the Gloom

A rare positive signal emerges: public-sector AI adoption reduces hospital wait times and accelerates permit processing, with credit given to EU-led systems. Public sentiment inches up to 37.0. Yet broader anxiety persists—especially as bio-risk concerns grow amid contested findings of human-infective organism designs.

Resilience rises slightly to 41.0, reflecting ongoing internal hardening, though transatlantic gaps remain. Sovereignty sees no net gain: +0 from momentum (the new protocol has not yet advanced integration), offset by −1 from capability growth, ending at 16.0. The EU’s sovereignty machinery is in motion, but not yet moving the needle.
