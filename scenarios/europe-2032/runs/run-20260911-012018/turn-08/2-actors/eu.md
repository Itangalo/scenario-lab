## Priority
Joint Procurement for Trusted Public AI, because with no measures in flight it is the only instrument that rebuilds public sentiment and pulls willing states including the defector back around a common, funded task serving the two-year commitment to hold together through dependence.

## In practice
We run this through DG CNECT and DG SANTE as a joint procurement under the Public Procurement Directive, anchored in the Evaluation Institute's clearance list and the Middle-Power Compact's secured access terms, funded by repurposed Digital Europe and EU4Health envelopes rather than new money. Willing regions that cut waiting lists get first lots; the five empty gigafactory-site cities get hosting and integration work to keep permits alive.

We do not reopen the €200bn gigafactories line or coerce the breakaway state: the Trade Council keeps the review open but paused, and re-entry is offered as joining the procurement pool and shared evaluation. ENISA bulletins continue paper-fallback guidance for the ransomware tail while procurement shifts load to cleared, patched stacks.
