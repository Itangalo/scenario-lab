Autumn 2028 grid drive left hospitals/ports patched but legacy systems and clinics exposed; spares passed to national budgets.

Spring 2029 brought two blows: automated ransomware sweep hit unpatched clinics, town halls, ports across member states, forcing weeks-long manual recovery and cancelled care; defenders avoided cascade but remained behind due to staffing and legacy controllers.

A large member state then broke EU unity with a separate cheap compute/model deal, undercutting joint licence alignment with Netherlands/Japan/Korea and common Washington ask. Commission opened coercion review, offered return via joint evaluation institute, threatened to proceed without it.

€200bn gigafactory plan confirmed dead; five sites (Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm, Warsaw) remain empty with permits warm. Middle-power compact via evaluation institute and chokepoints is only funded sovereignty track.

New US administration took office pledging models as national asset with tiered, revocable foreign access; European mood bleak amid power-price and healthcare protests.
