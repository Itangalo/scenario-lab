## Priority
M10 EU Unrecallable Model Containment Protocol, because keeping the five labelled sites insurable on the audited path through the open-weight shock is what the two-year commitment still demands, not the new export bargain alone.

## In practice
We hold the winter line on cover: DG SANTE and EIOPA enforce the insurability standard via health procurement, ENISA verifies logs at the five sites, insurers extend conditional cover only for EU-hosted logged models with human gates. We let the containment protocol run its legislative year without reopening unaudited triage, answering the university go-slows with late retraining money and auditor posts, not with exemptions.

On export controls we go to Washington and to Tokyo/Seoul with one offer: coordinated ASML/optics/chemicals leverage and a no-re-export regime in exchange for allied volume licences. DG TRADE and DG CONNECT use the Anti-Coercion Instrument consultation and the Chips Act coordination forum; scarce licensed compute is earmarked first to the audited clinical/payment workloads. Finance ministers are asked for nothing new beyond holding the earmark.
