## New measure
**EU Essential Services Continuity Reserve**
Stands up EU-hosted fallback inference and open-weight stacks for hospitals, grid operators and administrations cut off in November, so the next switch-off degrades rather than stops services.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, not a parallel compute build: it repurposes existing AI Factories capacity and connectivity funds for backup operation while the inherited Gigafactories lines rebuild.

## Priority
EU Essential Services Continuity Reserve, because the November cut-off proved loss of foreign access is no longer hypothetical and outranks slow gigafactory construction for immediate sovereignty.

## In practice
We direct DG CNECT and ENISA with the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority to contract fallback capacity on existing EuroHPC AI Factories, qualify open-weight models for triage, grid anomaly detection and administrative workflows, and fund member-state migration via reallocated Connecting Europe Facility money. Legal base is CER plus the Health Union regulation; hospital IT vendors resisting re-integration are met with joint procurement.

Alongside it we keep M1 InvestAI Gigafactories and M2 Tech sovereignty package alive without opening a parallel line: accelerated-permitting zones are held, cancelled co-builds are renegotiated toward public-anchored sites, and DG TRADE quietly prepares Anti-Coercion screening of supply-chain leverage. The finished Critical Systems Shield telemetry is fed into the Reserve's backup operations.
