## In practice
We keep DG CNECT, ENISA and HERA on the Reserve: forcing joint procurement terms for hospital IT vendors on certification/liability, topping retraining with ESF+ money, and fast-tracking data-handling clearance so the two hospital groups and transmission operator pilot becomes five by December.

Alongside it we launch the Deployment Sprint via the Digital Europe Programme and Technical Support Instrument: member-state health and interior ministries co-fund replication kits using EU-hosted open-weight stacks qualified under the Reserve, with procurement preference for EU-anchored inference. M1 InvestAI Gigafactories and M2 Tech sovereignty package are held on permits and public-anchored renegotiation, no new concrete promised this semester.
