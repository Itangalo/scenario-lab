## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with joint detection, exercised response and backup operation.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience because the autumn intrusion proved detection would not have caught weeks-long presence, and with EU hospitals and ministries just cut off from frontier models we must reduce damage from what we cannot prevent while sovereignty builds.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because live intruders in EU grids outrank even the access cut-off and investment freeze for immediate harm if left unanswered.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the EU-CyCLONe network with a six-month hardening sprint: mandatory telemetry sharing from the two affected transmission operators extended to all TSOs, CER-Directive audits of the port authority and water utility vectors, and EU-funded deployment of anomaly detection backed by the AI Factories. Council Energy and TTE formations provide the legal base, resisted by operators on cost, met with Connecting Europe Facility reallocation and liability cover.

Alongside it we keep the inherited InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty package alive despite the investment collapse: DG CNECT renegotiates cancelled private co-builds toward public-anchored sites, uses accelerated-permitting zones to hold timelines, and links ASML leverage quietly to keep supply-chain options open. No new compute line is opened; the Shield buys the time sovereignty needs.
