### Holding the line
July to December 2031 passed without a new instrument from Brussels, by design. With capital exhausted and courts still barring the two factory builds, the Commission kept to maintenance: showcase hospitals stayed open, triage helpers kept imaging and first-specialist waits down where they ran, and energy and legal teams shuttled town by town over capped water, grid sequencing and health earmarks.

Nothing lifted. Referees asked for more metering data; councils asked for firmer guarantees; night vigils continued at the fenced sites. Local papers ran the shorter-queues line alongside photos of silent cranes.

### Quiet erosion
Washington stayed on continuity language and pooled-volume discussion, no restoration date. The separate foreign supply deal held. Inside hospitals, hygiene checklists for freely available frontier models became routine paperwork — updated passwords, isolated terminals — but enforcement stayed advisory.

Health and cyber agencies kept passive monitoring after the contested infectivity claim, with no public alarm. Single-source rumours circulated: talk of harder blockades around substations requiring longer police watches, and talk of a ward briefly pulling back from a locally-run open model after a data scare and returning to manual triage. Neither confirmed, both repeated.

By December wards were open, backlogs stable in showcases, diggers stopped everywhere. Survival held, rebuilding did not.
