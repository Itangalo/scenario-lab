## In practice
We run the Guarantee through DG EMPL and the ESF+ with EPSCO Council cover, topping up the levy from the finished Humanoid Deployment Safety Accord: works councils in Germany and Italy co-sign site agreements to lift blockades in exchange for guaranteed bridge pay and retraining places, and mayors get funds to reopen suspended pilots only on registered, certified sites. Automating logistics and professional-services employers contribute via the deployment levy; we sell it to finance ministers as cheaper than fragmented national bans and strike losses.

On existing books we keep DG ENER and DG COMP holding grid priority for the two permitted gigafactory sites while EEAS keeps pushing Washington from draft protocols to published access tiers, and we keep filming the European triage assistants via DG SANTE to show benefit alongside protection. We answer the defecting member state not by sanction but by making the EU guarantee more generous than its bilateral side-deal for affected workers, inviting it back into the common regime.
