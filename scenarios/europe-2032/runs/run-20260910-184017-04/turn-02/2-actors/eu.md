## New measure
**EU Continuity Switch**
Stands up EU-hosted fallback models for hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the leading foreign model, procuring and deploying capable open and licensed systems on EU-anchored capacity.
Category 9 Diffusion, adoption and public trust is why now: the short-notice cutoff lands directly on essential services that built on foreign access, and the safety breakthrough gives us more assurable models to switch to while Taiwan tensions warn further supply shocks may follow.

## Priority
EU Continuity Switch, because hospitals and ministries offline today outrank even grid hardening and gigafactory build-out.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT and DG SANTE with the AI Office to run an emergency procurement under the public-sector adoption base, funding integration teams and inference credits from reprogrammed Digital Europe funds, with ENISA certifying the safety-breakthrough controls on the replacement systems. France, Germany and Poland hospitals pilot first, with vendors offered fast-track EU deployment in exchange for EU-jurisdiction hosting.

Alongside, we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package protected through the finance collapse — holding permitting zones and cheap hardware pickups — and push the EU Critical Systems Shield detection pilots in transmission operators, using the cutoff as political proof that dependence is now operational risk, not theory.
