## Priority
European Chokepoint Compact, because losing control of lithography servicing terms outranks even hospital fallbacks — if the chokepoint goes, gigafactories and continuity both become licensed dependencies.

## In practice
We task DG TRADE and DG CNECT with The Hague to structure the response: a Council mandate for coordinated export criteria with Tokyo and Seoul, an Anti-Coercion examination on the US extraterritorial servicing ban, and a state-backed indemnity and maintenance pool for ASML contracts at risk so the company can survive refusal. Legal base is the Anti-Coercion Instrument and the Dual-Use Regulation, decided in Foreign Affairs and Competitiveness Councils.

Alongside, we hold the EU Continuity Switch as the delivery operation — DG SANTE integration teams finishing hospital reconnections on EU-hosted open systems with ENISA certification — and we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package alive by protecting permitting zones and redirecting cheap cancelled hardware, asking Finance ministers to tolerate state-bridge financing while private capital is frozen. Grid Shield pilots continue to sensors-to-steel in France, Germany and Poland, now framed as substitution, not efficiency.
