## Priority
M10 EU Municipal Payments and Services Hardening Sprint, because restoring payments is the only path to rebuild capital and the new operation depends on its sensors and signed patches to contain the sweep.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the shared cyber command cell under NIS2 Article 11 and existing Digital Europe lines to isolate affected municipal and health systems, push M10's verified patches and swarm-detection, and stand up cash-counter and paper fallback where networks are pulled. No new legislation; Council JHA conclusions give cover, reallocated funds pay overtime and kits.

We direct DG CNECT and member-state treasuries to keep reimbursement flowing with simplified single-page claims, and we publish daily maps of what is up, degraded, or on manual fallback to stop rumour filling the gap. Finance ministries questioning cleanup get a capped EU co-pay tied to exercised continuity, not auditors. Full fringe re-wiring still waits for the courts — we move only sealed kits already cleared.
