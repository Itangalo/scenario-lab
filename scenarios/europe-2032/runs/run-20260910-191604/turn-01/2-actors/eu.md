## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
What it actually does is harden electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled swarm intrusion with segmented controls, exercised containment and cross-border mutual aid.
Why this and why now: the autumn test shot proved segmentation failed and detection would not have caught weeks of staging, and with the cyber gate open for three turns resilience is the prerequisite for both mandates.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because staged breaker access left in plain sight outranks even gigafactories when the next three turns carry elevated cyber risk.

## In practice
We are pushing the Shield through the Transport, Telecommunications and Energy Council with ENISA as operator and the EU Cybersecurity Reserve as first money, tying NIS2 enforcement to mandatory segmentation audits for the two affected transmission operators and their peers, with joint exercises with port authorities this winter. Member states resisting EU access to grid controls are offered co-funding for relay replacement and backup power in return for incident-reporting to a common cell.

On the books, InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package keep moving on permitting zones and grid connection under DG CNECT and DG ENER, using the US switch-off scare to hold cohesion on ASML leverage and on keeping siting decisions in EU jurisdiction. We are linking the interpretability breakthrough to our evaluation work by requiring Shield lessons and the loss-of-control incident pattern to feed EU reporting, so hardening and oversight reinforce each other rather than compete for capital.
