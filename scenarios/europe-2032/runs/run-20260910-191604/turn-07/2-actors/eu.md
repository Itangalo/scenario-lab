## In practice
We run ENISA as lead with DG CNECT and HERA in support, pushing the already-adopted patch-at-speed playbooks and behavioural detection configs from the Critical Systems Shield estate out to municipal IT, local hospitals and mid-size suppliers via existing NIS2 implementing acts — no new law, no ECOFIN vote. Costs are staff time and reallocated Digital Europe cybersecurity envelopes; mayors get restoration crews first if they accept audited builds of the vetted open model only.

On ASML coercion we do not retaliate: Trade and EEAS file the US extraterritorial demand under the Anti-Coercion Instrument for monitoring, reassure The Hague, and keep the sovereignty zones legally alive but unfunded until grid and courts clear. Verification proposals stay on paper for Washington, while ECDC continues passive bio-detection upgrades within HERA exercises.
