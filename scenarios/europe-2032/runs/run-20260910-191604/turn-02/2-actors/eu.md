**EU Continuity Stack**

Why this and why now: the short-notice cutoff left essential services dark and the open-weight release puts a near-frontier substitute permanently in reach, so turning denial into the sovereignty case for controlled infrastructure serves the absorb-shocks commitment directly.

## Priority
EU Continuity Stack, because restoring cut-off hospitals and ministries this semester outranks even the Shield when denial has made dependence visible and the open-weight substitute makes a fallback feasible now.

## In practice
We are pushing the Shield as the operator track: ENISA runs segmentation audits and relay replacement through the TTE Council and NIS2, and we are folding the new defensive tooling — automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection — straight into the winter exercises and the common reporting cell to close the staged-access class. Resistant capitals get reserve co-funding tied to reporting, not to control handover.

On the cutoff we are acting through DG CNECT and the Health and Digital Councils with EuroHPC and national hosts as operators: redirecting spare EuroHPC and Gigafactory pilot capacity to serve vetted open-weight models to listed essential users under EU data and security terms, with procurement preference for EU-hosted inference. The Tech sovereignty package permitting and the Gigafactories grid-connection work continue underneath, sold now as the permanent fix for a denial everyone just felt.
