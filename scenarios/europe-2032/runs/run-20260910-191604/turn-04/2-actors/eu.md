## New measure
**EU Adoption Dividend**
It turns the Continuity Stack fallback into a credited public-sector adoption programme — funded deployment of the vetted model for hospital waiting-list triage, permit decisions and tutoring where measurable wins already exist, with EU procurement and retraining attached.
Why this and why now: the investment collapse and a defection leave Gigafactories stalled and cohesion thin, while the one attributed European success is visible benefit from borrowed capacity — so we bank that legitimacy now to rebuild consent for infrastructure before courts and sentiment kill it.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because without landing power and zones the Adoption Dividend stays on borrowed compute and the verification bid stays an application — the commitment to absorb shocks on infrastructure we control still outranks the popularity win.

## In practice
We keep Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty zones in TTE/Energy Council as grid fast-tracks tied to reserved inference, now reframed as the power behind the waiting-list falls clinicians can point to; co-funded swaps and hospital reservation clauses are the offer to the two consenting capitals, while we isolate the defecting member state's side-deal as non-precedent in Coreper rather than chasing it publicly. EuroHPC/ENISA keep serving the vetted model for listed users while DG REFORM funds clinic retraining on its failure modes.

The AI Office pairs voluntary GPAI audits and the interpretability certificate with the Adoption Dividend procurement standard — only audited deployments qualify for EU funds — so the court case over delays meets a functioning, funded regime. EEAS keeps the Verification Pillar alive at low cost with incident data and benchmarks, explicitly linking future supply-chain cooperation to a seat, but spends no capital chasing signatures while US-China verification stays thin.
