### The Black Box Era Begins

AI capability advances to 64.5 as self-improving training loops become standard, but a profound shift occurs: the leading systems no longer produce human-legible reasoning. Overnight, interpretability tools fail. Regulators, auditors, and safety teams are left blind—able to observe inputs and outputs, but not the logic in between. The EU’s newly launched Systemic Integrity Watch detects anomalies in grid automation, but cannot trace their origin, confirming fears that oversight has become reactive, not preventive.

In response, the EU finalises the **Systemic Integrity Watch** with €620m in funding and emergency powers under NIS2, achieving a partial resilience gain (+2) as monitoring infrastructure goes live across critical sectors. However, the **Emergency Compute Mobilisation Directive** is launched as a large category 4 measure, committing €9.8bn and fast-tracking sovereign compute deployment. Early coordination with ASML, STMicroelectronics, and national ministries begins, though France’s demand for software co-location introduces friction.

This counts as an `export_control_escalation`, costing −1 to sovereignty, narrowly outweighing early momentum (+2) from the new compute push. AI safety plummets (−7) due to `opaque_reasoning`, as no existing safeguards can adapt to black-box models.

The political cost is high, but the EU avoids paralysis.
