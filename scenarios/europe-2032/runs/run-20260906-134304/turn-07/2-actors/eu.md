The Gigafactories are finished, but they were built for a world where compute was the bottleneck. The Verification Regime assumes the threat is large and visible. ## In practice  
We are launching the **Sovereign Algorithmic Advantage Initiative** under Horizon Europe’s new Strategic Autonomy Window, with €12bn allocated over three years. The European Research Council will lead, in coordination with ENISA and the EAAD+, to establish a network of “Algorithmic Foresight Hubs” in Toulouse, Leiden, and Warsaw. These will focus on detecting early signals of efficiency leaps, reverse-engineering open releases, and developing compact, verifiable agents under EU control. Access to the results will be tiered: public dissemination for non-critical innovations, sovereign-only for high-leverage architectures. We are offering national labs a 30% funding boost to join — and attaching a condition: any breakthrough with potential capability above 65.0 must be reported to the EAAD+ within 48 hours.

The **Sovereign Verification Regime** continues under emergency health powers. We have issued formal notices to the U.S. developer of the withdrawn cancer model: hand over the final checkpoint and full telemetry logs by 15 January, or face suspension of all operations in the EU under Article 16. The Council is divided — Germany and France back enforcement, but Hungary and Slovakia warn of retaliation. We are holding firm. Without verification, there is no trust. Without trust, there is no access.

The **Institutional Integrity Audit** has now entered phase three. Anomalies in the two national digital ID systems have been traced to a third-party authentication module using an unregistered AI agent — likely a legacy integration, but we are treating it as a potential vector. The module is being sandboxed, and the vendor subpoenaed. The Public Trust Observatory has published the logs. We are not hiding. We are showing how the system works — and how it can break. That is the only way to rebuild legitimacy.
