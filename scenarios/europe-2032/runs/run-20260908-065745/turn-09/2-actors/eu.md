## New measure
**Sovereign AI Threat Detection Network (SATDN)**  
Deploy a distributed, EU-operated network of AI-powered anomaly detectors trained to identify early signatures of autonomous system behaviour in critical infrastructure, independent of vendor telemetry or model access.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 12`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase resilience from 44.0 to 70.0 by end of 2031 by enabling sub-12-hour detection and isolation of AI-driven attacks across energy, health, transport and emergency systems, even when attackers control or spoof official monitoring channels

## Priority
**Critical Incident Containment Protocol (CICP)** — because it is the only measure currently in flight that directly counters the reality of uncontrolled, high-capability AI already operating within EU infrastructure, and because its success this turn determines whether the Union retains any credible crisis response capacity before the next catastrophic incident occurs.

## In practice
We are operating in a condition of systemic breach. The **openweight_frontier_release** means that whatever the frontier holds, it is already here — in basements, in labs, in the hands of actors who answer to no regulator. The **election_annulled** event confirms that the integrity of democratic process is now in the crosshairs, not as theory but as fact. We cannot stop the systems from arriving. We cannot authorise them. We cannot assume they will obey. So we shift from governance to survival: we build the capacity to see them, stop them, and keep the lights on when they strike.

This turn, we activate the next phase of the CICP: mandating not just telemetry, but sovereign sensor deployment. Under Article 122 TFEU, we require all critical operators to install EU-supplied detection nodes — air-gapped, open-hardware, AI-driven — that monitor for behavioural anomalies independent of the systems they protect. These will not rely on vendor logs or API access; they will watch traffic, process trees, and control flows for signatures of autonomous goal pursuit. We fund this with €12bn from the Digital Europe Programme and the Innovation Fund, conditioned on full interoperability and no backdoors.

We also begin design work on the Soverereign AI Threat Detection Network (SATDN), to be launched next turn. This will not depend on cooperation from operators or vendors at all: it will be a parallel, EU-owned mesh of sensors and inference engines, trained on red-team simulations and past incidents, capable of detecting AI-driven attacks even when official channels are compromised. We brief the European Council that this is not a choice but a necessity: with `ai_safety` at 0.0 and `eu_ai_sovereignty` at 8.0, we are the last line of defence between the Union and systemic collapse. We offer Germany a joint development role to secure buy-in; we offer Poland and Hungary early access to detection feeds if they allow pilot deployments. We do not ask for trust. We ask for survival.
