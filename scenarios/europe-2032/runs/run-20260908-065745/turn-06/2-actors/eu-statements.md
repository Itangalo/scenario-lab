## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Enforce physical and legal chokepoints over AI infrastructure and deployment to ensure no high-capability model operates in or affects the EU without explicit authorization, inspection, and enforceable compliance
  - Grounds: The failure of the Sovereign Enforcement Framework to achieve full implementation, the abrupt denial of access to frontier models, and the rapid diffusion of open-weight systems at capability 58.5 confirm that sovereignty is not a matter of aspiration but of operational control. The previous commitment assumed that control could be built through coordination and norm-setting; this turn proves it must be enforced through infrastructure, jurisdiction, and binding levers over power, connectivity, and licensing. The new formulation reflects a shift from policy ambition to physical enforcement — not a change in goal, but in the means required to survive.
