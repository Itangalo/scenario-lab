  - Trigger: the two-year commitment period closes this turn
  - Grounds: The failure of the Sovereign Enforcement Framework to gain full compliance, the denial of frontier access, and the rapid replication of high-capability open models confirm that sovereignty cannot be claimed through aspiration — it must be exercised at the level of infrastructure and code. The next two years must be defined by operational control: the ability to stop a model from running, to inspect what it does, and to ensure that no capability enters EU systems without our consent. This is not a shift from our previous aim, but its necessary sharpening — independence and safety now depend on physical and legal chokepoints we can hold.
