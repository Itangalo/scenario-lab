### The Frontier Pushes Forward

Global AI capability advances another 1.5 points to 60.0, as the February breakthrough integrates into production systems across pharmaceuticals, logistics and semiconductor design. The jump marks the threshold at which AI systems begin completing multi-day professional projects end to end, displacing junior roles at scale. Open replication accelerates: despite distribution constraints, improved distillation techniques and distributed training efforts lift openweight_capability to 55.0, narrowing the gap and embedding frontier-level risk into uncontrolled environments.

### Safety Erodes Further

With no safeguards deployed on the new systems and verification still reactive, ai_safety drops another 3 points to 17.0. Experts warn that even basic interpretability is lagging, and the first signs of coordinated misuse emerge in offshore cyber operations.

### EU Responds with Operational Resolve

The European Union launches the Emergency Cyber Shield for Critical Functions as a direct-execution programme under ENISA, bypassing national legal hurdles by mandating integration for all operators of essential services. €20bn in recovered RRF and cohesion funds are committed, with deployment scheduled for completion by mid-2029. Early monitoring nodes go live in six member states, providing limited but growing coverage. This partial progress yields +1 to resilience, raising it to 41.0.

However, political capital tightens. The portfolio now carries four large measures, and the priority adds further strain. Hungary and Poland challenge the Cyber Shield’s legality at the Court of Justice, while Germany delays funding for the Labour Transition Fund. Public sentiment dips to 36.0 amid visible job losses in entry-level professions and growing frustration over stalled delivery. The reframing of the Labour Fund as a funding condition backfires in some capitals, seen as coercion.

### Sovereignty Drifts Amid Rising Capability

With ai_capability rising by 1.5 (below the 2-point threshold), the sovereignty decay term does not trigger. However, no new compute access is secured, and the US cloud negotiations remain inconclusive. eu_ai_sovereignty falls to 19.0 due to persistent lag in domestic build-out.
