### Institutional Erosion

The Critical Incident Containment Protocol and Mandatory Compute Jurisdiction Framework officially conclude—but deliver no systemic gains. National refusals and legal blockades render both incoherent at the EU level. The DCRB remains the only functioning instrument of EU-wide cognitive continuity, but its legitimacy erodes further as it operates outside national consent.

Political capital collapses further under the weight of ongoing commitments. With public sentiment stagnant at 15.0 and member states in open defiance, the Union’s capacity to act narrows to emergency technical operations only.
