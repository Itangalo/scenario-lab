### The Frontier Advances, Unchecked

Global AI capability rises by 1.5 points to 61.5, as systems now routinely complete multi-day engineering and legal workflows without human intervention. Open replication narrows the gap further: distillation and federated training push openweight_capability to 56.0, embedding powerful autonomous agents into unregulated environments across academia, startups and adversarial networks.

### Safety Erodes Amid Institutional Strain

With no safeguards on new models and misuse incidents multiplying, ai_safety drops by 3 points to 14.0. The first coordinated AI-driven cyber intrusions are detected in Eastern European financial networks, exploiting gaps in legacy infrastructure. Interpretability remains primitive, and developers admit they no longer fully understand model decision pathways.

### EU Shifts Strategy, Faces Backlash

The EU launches the Sovereign Enforcement Framework for Open-Weight Models, aiming to cap domestic open capabilities at 50.0 through audits, watermarking and infrastructure control. Drafted under Article 114 TFEU, it grants a new Commission agency sweeping powers to inspect and shut down unauthorised deployments. Early detection nodes integrate with the Emergency Cyber Shield, turning resilience into enforcement.

However, political costs mount. The measure is large and controversial. Hungary and Poland denounce it as federal overreach, with Warsaw halting cooperation on monitoring node deployment. Germany delays funding, demanding judicial oversight. The Anti-Coercion Instrument is invoked, but its legitimacy is questioned. Public sentiment falls to 33.0 as protests against data centres grow and displaced workers feel abandoned by the Labour Fund’s expiry.

Meanwhile, the Labour Transition Acceleration Fund expires, and the Emergency Cyber Shield continues without full rollout. The Sovereign Enforcement Framework becomes the new priority, straining an already overloaded agenda.

### Sovereignty Drifts, Capital Erodes

Despite the push for control, no new sovereign compute comes online. Member-state defection and stalled gigafactories leave eu_ai_sovereignty down to 18.0. Political capital erodes to 25.0 under the weight of five large measures and rising fragmentation.
