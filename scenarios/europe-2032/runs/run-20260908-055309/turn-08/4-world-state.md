### The Crisis That Exposed the Gap

A cascading ransomware attack—automated, adaptive, and model-generated—crippled municipal services in three member states, exploiting a poisoned software dependency with delayed detonation. Emergency systems faltered, and public trust eroded further. The EU treated the incident as a stress test for its crisis architecture, but the results were mixed.

The **Secure Access Guarantee** formally concluded, but its failure to bind hyperscalers in time rendered it symbolic. With U.S. providers acting unilaterally, the agreement collapsed into irrelevance, delivering no operational benefit. The **Emergency Fallback Compute Activation Protocol**, now the named priority, began enforcement: fallback APIs were mandated, and EuroHPC nodes reconfigured as emergency reserves. Yet resistance persisted. Several member states delayed integration, citing sovereignty concerns and technical incompatibility, weakening the fallback network just as it was needed.

In a rare positive development, the **emergent unified biosecurity protocol**—a grassroots coalition of labs and states—gained legitimacy. The EU moved swiftly to formalize it, linking compliance to Horizon Europe funding and proposing the **Unified EU Biosecurity Integration Directive**. By anchoring the mandate in public health law and offering early adopters access to rapid-response funds, the Commission avoided outright rebellion. The measure passed initial scrutiny as a convergence play, not coercion.

Still, political capital dwindled. The cyber incident underscored systemic fragility, and the fallback protocol’s partial implementation counted as neither success nor failure—only strain. Public sentiment dipped as media highlighted the “sovereign switch” that still wouldn’t flip. Resilience rose modestly from the biosecurity advance and partial cyber response, but AI safety fell further as opaque, self-modifying systems proliferated unchecked.

### Sovereignty, Not Yet Power

Despite completed gigafactories, the EU still lacks sovereign AI capability. Compute exists, but no frontier model runs on it. Sovereignty declined further as dependence deepened and internal cohesion frayed. The Union acted—but not effectively, not yet.
