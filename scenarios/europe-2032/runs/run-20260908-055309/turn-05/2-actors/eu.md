## In practice
We are executing the Joint Resilience and Evaluation Compact through a high-level task force co-chaired by the European Commission’s Vice-President for Values and Transparency and the U.S. National Security Advisor, operating under the newly activated EU-U.S. Trade and Technology Council’s security annex. The legal basis draws from Article 218 TFEU, with funding redirected from the Digital Europe Programme’s crisis response envelope. Our negotiators are offering immediate alignment on export controls for dual-use AI components and participation in the Middle Power Compute Accord’s joint evaluation track in exchange for structured access to U.S.-developed threat intelligence, shared red-teaming results, and mutual recognition of evaluation outcomes. Crucially, we are embedding a clause that automatically suspends full data-sharing privileges if the U.S. imposes unilateral restrictions on EU access to frontier models or compute — a safeguard to prevent dependency from hardening into subordination.

Simultaneously, we are finalising the wind-down of the Sovereign Evaluation Regime and the Middle Power Compute Accord, both of which conclude this turn. The Sovereign Evaluation Regime, though stalled in its original form, has delivered preparatory work that will feed into the new compact: the scoping studies by INRIA, Fraunhofer, and TNO on pre-release access now serve as technical annexes to our bilateral talks. The Middle Power Compute Accord’s ratification is being confirmed in a ministerial summit in Brussels next week, where we will formalise the joint compute reserve’s governance and lock in reciprocal commitments on EUV component licensing with Japan and South Korea. The Tech Sovereignty Package remains on track, with the Energy Council’s compromise enabling construction planning to proceed at the three designated gigafactory sites, now classified as strategic infrastructure. InvestAI Gigafactories continues on schedule, with site preparation underway and first concrete expected in Q1 2029. Public sentiment remains fragile at 38.0, but we are preparing a communication push around AI-assisted healthcare advances and the security benefits of the U.S. compact to stabilise opinion ahead of the next turn.
