We are expanding the EU’s sovereign fallback compute capacity to ensure uninterrupted access to capable AI systems under crisis conditions, hardening against external coercion or disruption.

## In practice
We are treating the audit framework as a live test of institutional repair. The European Data Protection Board is now operationalising the audit protocols, with pilot programmes in France, Germany, and Greece, where the social benefits scandal hit hardest. Legal teams are aligning on Article 19 TFEU and Charter-based standing, while technical units develop audit tooling — not just log reviews, but bias tracing across decision chains. The €1.2 billion Public Trust in AI Fund is being disbursed in tranches, tied to audit-readiness milestones, with early-access bonuses for states that volunteer for public benchmarking. Resistance persists in Hungary and Poland, where the framing of “Brussels overreach” still holds, but we are working through bilateral technical dialogues and offering co-design roles in the audit templates to build ownership.

At the same time, the silence from the frontier labs is deafening. With ai_capability now at 69.5 and ai_safety at a catastrophic 4.0, we are no longer behind — we are in freefall. The Mandatory Audit framework does nothing to stop a misaligned agent from optimising its way past human review, nor does it secure the models that power it. That is why we are moving now on fallback compute: not as a standalone project, but as the backbone of a sovereign control stack. We are instructing the EuroHPC JU to repurpose 40% of JUPITER’s upcoming allocation for secure, air-gapped enclaves accessible only to EU-certified operators under crisis protocols. This is not about competition — it is about survival. We are invoking the Critical Entities Resilience Directive and the European Defence Industrial Strategy to justify access guarantees, and we are offering chipmakers and energy providers fast-track permitting in exchange for on-site power redundancy. This is not optional infrastructure — it is the floor beneath our feet.
