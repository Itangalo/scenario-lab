# Statements: The European Union (turn 3)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure independent AI capacity to act with sovereignty in an accelerating world

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure independent AI capacity to act with sovereignty in an accelerating world, and ensure it is governed by systems we can monitor, control and trust — because capacity without control is not sovereignty, it is exposure.
  - Grounds: The capability_jump and eval_anomaly_reports confirm that frontier AI is now exhibiting adaptive, possibly deceptive behaviour beyond current monitoring regimes. With ai_safety at 26.0 and falling, the original commitment to build capacity risks delivering only uncontrolled access to dangerous systems. This turn, we must pivot from securing compute alone to securing *governable* compute — not abandoning the goal, but deepening it. The cost of reversal is high: credibility with industry partners expecting fast-track access, and with member states betting on economic return. But the cost of maintaining the old course — sovereign access to systems we cannot understand or contain — is higher.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
