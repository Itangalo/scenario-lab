### A Pivot in the Storm

The EU capitalises on global turbulence. With the American tech sector reeling from the election backlash and private AI investment collapsing worldwide, Brussels shifts course: sovereignty without control is a liability, not strength. The **Sovereign Compute Readiness Threshold** is fast-tracked and enacted via delegated act, binding the **Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive** to the **Sovereign Evaluation and Control Framework**. No sovereign compute allocation will activate until real-time monitoring is proven. The move reassures legal sceptics in Germany, who suspend their challenge pending audit results.

The **Critical Infrastructure AI Shield** completes its rollout, with November’s red-teaming in healthcare revealing both improved detection and lingering integration gaps. The partial success lifts resilience to 45.0, reflecting hardening in core sectors despite uneven readiness.

### Fragmented Frontier, Rising Openweights

Global AI capability climbs to 66.0, driven by remaining well-funded labs adapting to austerity. The US slowdown offsets some losses, but innovation persists. Open-weight models advance to 54.0, as EuroShield’s tools are reverse-engineered and repurposed for offensive use in underground forums — a reminder that defensive diffusion enables offensive access.

With no sovereign compute operational and capability rising by 3.0, eu_ai_sovereignty erodes further, dropping to 14.0. The Union’s access remains legally secured but practically idle.

Political capital improves slightly to 22.0. The completion of two measures — the Compute Directive and the AI Shield — delivers a combined +6 bonus. Ongoing costs for four large measures and the priority total −13, but the +8 from completions yields a net gain. The new Readiness Threshold, though small, earns a +2 proposal bonus for directly addressing the safety collapse and investment shock.

Public sentiment holds at 45.0. The US retreat is seen as chaotic, not wise, and domestic AI benefits remain too diffuse to shift perception.

The 2028 US election is settled: a wave of backlash politics sweeps in a retrenchment-focused administration. The world waits to see how inward the turn will go.
