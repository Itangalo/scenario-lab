This is not a pause on ambition — the Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package continue — but we are now conditioning progress on safety. The message is clear: we will not be left behind, but we will not be reckless. And if the world’s two largest powers can agree to slow down, then Europe — with its credibility in regulation and its mandate for protection — must not become the open door.
