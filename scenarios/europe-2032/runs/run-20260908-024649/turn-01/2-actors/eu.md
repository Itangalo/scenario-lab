## Priority
``Cyber Shield Certification`` is the priority this turn because the cyber_test_shot exposed systemic fragility that could collapse public trust and disable the Union mid-strategy; we cannot build sovereign capacity on brittle foundations.

## In practice
We are launching Cyber Shield Certification under Article 114 TFEU, using the internal market legal base to harmonise minimum cyber resilience standards across critical sectors. The ENISA budget will be supplemented with €8bn from the Recovery and Resilience Facility top-up instrument, co-financing national agencies to conduct audits and issue public resilience scores starting Q1 2027. Resistance from smaller member states over cost and sovereignty concerns is being met with a 70% EU funding share for the first two years and a new Council Working Group on Digital Continuity to ensure peer accountability without centralisation.

Simultaneously, InvestAI Gigafactories is entering site selection: we are fast-tracking environmental assessments for five candidate locations, three of which are former industrial zones with grid headroom. The Commission has issued a Recommendation on Electricity Prioritisation for Strategic AI Compute, directing member states to treat these facilities as critical infrastructure for grid access. Pushback from local communities over power allocation is being managed through regional innovation pacts — linking each site to vocational AI training hubs and guaranteed public compute time for universities.

The Tech sovereignty package is advancing its permitting reform through COREPER II, where we are trading accelerated zoning rights for binding commitments on domestic hiring and open-access research partnerships. Early signals from hyperscalers suggest conditional buy-in, but only if we clarify export rules on dual-use models by turn 3. We are holding that line for now, betting that demonstrating sovereign infrastructure will shift their calculus faster than pre-emption would.
