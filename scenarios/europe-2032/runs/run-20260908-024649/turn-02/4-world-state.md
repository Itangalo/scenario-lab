### Capability Creeps, Safeguards Crack

Global AI capability advances to 56.0 as automated verification in legal and clinical domains enables faster, self-improving development cycles. This progress is narrow but consequential, eroding the assumption that human judgment remains essential in high-stakes fields. With no safety breakthrough and two labs now under investigation for unauthorised training runs, confidence in control plummets. `ai_safety` drops to 28.0, reflecting growing opacity in frontier systems.

Open-weight models see modest gains, reaching 43.0, driven by fine-tuned derivatives of earlier releases. No mass leak occurs, but underground collectives quietly redistribute modified versions capable of generating exploit code, accelerating diffusion outside official channels.

### Access Cut, Response Strained

The EU is abruptly locked out of the most advanced AI model, disrupting hospital diagnostics, customs automation, and financial compliance systems. The move, unexplained and unappealable, lands as both operational crisis and political humiliation. Public trust wavers as media spotlight failures in contingency planning.

The Union accelerates *Cyber Shield Certification*, using delegated powers to sidestep legislative delays. Pilots launch in energy and transport, backed by €2bn in recovery funds. Yet member state buy-in remains uneven—smaller nations resist centralised audits, accepting only symbolic oversight. Resilience efforts are undercut by the very dependency the measure aims to reduce.

*InvestAI Gigafactories* progresses: three sites are shortlisted, and fast-tracking begins. But power allocation disputes grow, and no new compute comes online. The *Tech Sovereignty Package* nears consensus on permitting, though export rules remain unresolved, stalling private investment.

With three large measures ongoing and a priority declared, political capital drains. The access denial—unmitigated by prior safeguards—counts as an internal-origin negative event. No proposal bonus offsets it. Public sentiment falls to 37.0 amid anxiety over democratic integrity and technological dependence.
