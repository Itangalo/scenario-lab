### Capability Creeps, Safety Slips

Global AI capability rises to 57.5, driven by incremental advances in automated code generation and model self-testing, particularly in semiconductor design and logistics optimisation. While not a leap, the trend consolidates the erosion of human oversight in high-assurance domains. No safety breakthrough occurs; instead, two labs face scrutiny for opaque training pipelines, and the gap between capability and control widens. `ai_safety` drops to 25.0, reflecting declining confidence in frontier model transparency.

Open-weight models advance to 45.0, as underground networks fine-tune leaked architectures for cyber exploitation. Though no full frontier model is released, derivatives capable of generating zero-day exploits circulate in closed forums, raising fears of uncontrolled proliferation.

### Crisis Mode in Brussels

A major AI-powered cyberattack disrupts emergency dispatch and water monitoring in three member states, exploiting vulnerabilities mapped by generative models. Attribution remains unclear, but the tooling bears hallmarks of automated chain generation. The incident underscores systemic fragility, triggering the EU’s crisis powers.

Cyber Shield Certification is fast-tracked via Article 114 TFEU, imposing binding audit schedules on laggard sectors. ENISA deploys rapid-response teams, and €1.2bn in emergency funds flows to healthcare and civil protection. However, resistance persists: two member states challenge the override of national inspection rights, accepting only symbolic compliance.

InvestAI Gigafactories advances to grid negotiations, with strategic override powers invoked. Power disputes linger, but co-investment in public AI hubs unlocks partial commitments. No new compute comes online.

The Tech Sovereignty Package nears approval, with a carve-out proposed for democracies in the emergent multilateral initiative. Export controls remain contested, but hyperscalers are offered fast-track compliance in exchange for compute commitments under a new EU Access Guarantee.

This large new measure launches, aiming to secure sovereign runtime. Yet its distant finish (turn 8) and untested enforcement mechanism limit immediate impact. Public sentiment drops to 34.0 amid fears of dependency and institutional overreach.
