# Statements: The European Union (turn 4)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Pivot from building sovereign AI capacity to securing minimum viable resilience and access for essential public functions

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Pivot from building sovereign AI capacity to securing minimum viable resilience and access for essential public functions
  - Trigger: the convergence of **eu_frontier_access_denied**, **ai_investment_collapse**, and **campaign_backlash**
  - Grounds: The collapse in private investment has invalidated the assumptions underpinning sovereign compute build-out at scale, while the sudden denial of frontier access has exposed immediate operational fragility in public services. At the same time, public sentiment is shifting toward restriction, not expansion, making large industrial commitments politically untenable. The original commitment to build sovereign capacity can no longer be credibly pursued without first establishing a floor of continuity for essential functions. This is not abandonment but triage: we are reorienting the commitment to preserve strategic autonomy in a narrower, more urgent form — not as infrastructure, but as minimum service survival.
  - Evidence quoted: **eu_frontier_access_denied:** The Union is cut off from the leading model at short notice, wholly or by nationality of user. No detailed reason is given, there is no appeal, and the immediate practical effect lands on hospitals, ministries and firms that had built on it. Whether this reads at home as an outrage or as a failure of foresight depends on what the Union had done about it beforehand.
**ai_investment_collapse:** Capital flees the sector. Valuations reset hard, announced build-out is cancelled rather than delayed, and several of the arrangements European compute was depending on evaporate with it. What the frontier laboratories can afford to train shrinks for the first time.
**campaign_backlash:** Moratoriums on data centres, restrictions on AI in schools and hiring, and protection for displaced workers move from the fringe to the platform, on both left and right. Polling shows the position is popular well beyond the activists, and candidates who hedged start to reposition.
  - Reason: The developments directly undermine the feasibility and political support for sovereign AI build-out, forcing a shift to resilience as the only viable path to maintain strategic autonomy.
