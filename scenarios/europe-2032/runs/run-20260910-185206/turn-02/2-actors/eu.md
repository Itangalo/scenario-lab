## New measure
**EU Grid-Port Recovery and Insurance Backstop**
Stands up an EU-guaranteed continuity cover for grids, ports and water utilities hit by the automated attack, funding rapid restoration and bridging the repriced cyber-insurance gap while the Cyber Shield hardens systems.
This now because the large automated incident plus the non-EU insurer repricing turns the autumn mapping into live disruption and contract disputes, and without a backstop operators face uncovered losses and slow recovery; a small preparedness and resilience instrument answers the incident fastest while serving the cannot-be-knocked-out commitment.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, because the automated attack actuating the autumn precursor outranks even recovery funding — stopping cascade matters more than paying for it.

## In practice
We push the Shield into emergency execution: ENISA activates the Cybersecurity Reserve under NIS2, deploys joint detection teams to the hit TSOs, port authority and water utility, enforces completed OT segmentation and credential rotation missed in autumn, and runs live cross-border containment with EU-CyCLONe rather than tabletop. Municipal cost resistance is overridden by emergency co-financing from the new backstop.

In parallel DG FISMA and EIB structure the backstop as a temporary EU guarantee facility topping up Digital Europe funds: it covers restoration overtime, replacement hardware, and a 12-month insurance bridge where private cover was withdrawn over swarm-tooling exclusions. Energy and Transport Councils adopt by qualified majority; insurers are told guaranteed cover is conditional on dropping unattributed-state exclusions for EU-hardened operators. Gigafactories permitting continues on existing track without new money this turn.
