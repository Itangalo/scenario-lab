**EU Essential-Services Fallback Stack**
Deploys EU-hosted open-weight models and the successful public-sector use-cases to hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the frontier model, paired with retraining bridges for displaced entry-level roles.
This turn demands it because cutoff plus proven public-sector benefit plus graduate hiring freeze coincide: with capital at 6 and sentiment at 19, the only move that restores function, proves European delivery, and answers displacement is to keep essential services running on infrastructure nobody else can switch off.

## Priority
The new EU Essential-Services Fallback Stack, and not the running containment surge, because hospitals and ministries offline today outrank drills for tomorrow and it alone serves the two-year commitment to absorb what frontier claims unleash.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT and DIGIT under existing procurement frameworks to stand up an EU-hosted inference fallback: hardened open-weight models (at 70 capability) on pooled EuroHPC and warm gigafactory-site capacity, offered first to cut-off hospitals, ministries and firms, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and ESF+ money with no new regulation. The AI Office certifies the fallback configurations for essential use, while ENISA extends M8 isolation telemetry to the fallback endpoints.

We keep M8 running as the second track without new money: civil-protection drills continue for the two credible operators while sentinel labs share sequencing reagents. DG EMPL ties fallback deployment to the finished transition guarantee's local desks, converting visible adoption wins — waiting lists fallen, decisions in days — into entry-level retraining slots to blunt the hiring freeze and sabotage rumours, holding Council cohesion with cost-sharing rather than mandates.
