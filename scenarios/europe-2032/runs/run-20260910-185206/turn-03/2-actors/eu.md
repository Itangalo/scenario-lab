## New measure
**EU Displaced Worker Transition Guarantee**
Sets up an EU-co-funded wage insurance, rapid retraining and placement guarantee for workers displaced by AI-driven automation and the grid-port disruptions, delivered through national employment agencies.
This now because public sentiment at 25 and hardening after clinics diverted and dark substations threatens the political base for both sovereignty builds and Shield enforcement; a small labour and social protection instrument cushions adjustment as shock rather than collapse and buys room to hold the cannot-be-knocked-out commitment.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, because live backdoors and half-done segmentation still risk a second cascade that would outrank any transition payment — stopping the knock-out comes before compensating it.

## In practice
We keep the Shield on emergency footing through ENISA and EU-CyCLONe: joint detection teams stay embedded with hit TSOs and the port, forced OT segmentation completion orders are enforced with Digital Europe co-financing, and the Cybersecurity Reserve runs live cross-border exercises. DG ENER and DG MOVE tie the insurance backstop payouts to verified hardening milestones, pressing litigating insurers in Council working groups while EIB disburses overtime and hardware funds to municipal utilities.

We launch the Transition Guarantee via EPSCO and DG EMPL using ESF+ top-up plus EIB facility: national agencies register displaced logistics, municipal-service and junior clerical workers, offer income bridge within weeks and employer-linked retraining vouchers. Gigafactories permitting is kept alive on paper through DG CNECT accelerated zones but without new political spend this turn, as grid connections and staff remain diverted to recovery; we tell industry the pause is sequencing, not retreat.
