### The Threshold of Trust

The Open Science Resilience Initiative reaches its operational deadline. In Toulouse, climate models successfully rerun the spring drought forecast with full auditability, and DG CLIMA accepts the output—though only after a three-day delay for manual validation. Stockholm’s biosecurity hub verifies a pathogen signal from the ECDC, but raises concerns about false negatives under stress. Bologna’s energy forecasts align with ENTSO-E’s live data, yet fail to anticipate a sudden drop in solar output, requiring human override. The results are mixed: technically viable, institutionally fragile. The initiative proves auditable AI can function—but not yet that it can lead.

Meanwhile, the ENISA audit confirms the access denial was an extraterritorial enforcement of U.S. export controls under the ongoing consolidation posture. Legal teams prepare a challenge under the Anti-Coercion Instrument, but without sovereign compute, leverage is thin. Emergency funding talks stall as Germany resists cohesion fund repurposing, despite offers of co-location in Bologna. Protests escalate: a data centre in Spain is occupied, and grid sabotage in Poland causes a hospital blackout. Policing costs mount, but the deeper cost is political—public trust erodes further.

### Capability Without Control

Global AI capability climbs to 97.5, driven by recursive self-improvement now fully autonomous. No safety breakthrough occurs; `ai_safety` remains at −12. A leaked genome model sparks biosecurity alarms, though the findings are contested. Open-weight capability inches up to 62.0, limited by compute scarcity. Resilience holds at 51, as intelligence sharing offsets no new gains. The EU’s political capital rises slightly to 2: the completion of a category 5 measure yields +2, and the portfolio charge—Open Science Resilience Initiative −3, Joint Access Negotiation −3, priority −1—totals −7, but with no new measure, the burden stabilizes. Sovereignty falls to 18: −1 from capability rise, −1 from unmitigated access loss, no counterweight.
