### The Brink

A sudden leap in global AI capability pushes the frontier to 100.0—recursive self-improvement has reached instrument saturation. The jump renders all prior safety assumptions obsolete. No human institution can characterise the systems now in operation. `ai_safety` remains at −12, unchanged not because of stability, but because the scale has bottomed out: there is no lower to go.

The Open Science Resilience Initiative formally concludes, but its legacy is one of fragility. While models in Toulouse, Stockholm, and Bologna produced technically valid outputs, reliance on human override revealed a crisis of institutional trust. Auditable AI functions—but not at decision speed.

In response, the EU invokes Article 122a TFEU to launch the Emergency Compute Mobilisation Directive, aiming to fast-track sovereign AI infrastructure. The plan mandates grid priority, streamlined permitting, and EIB financing for data centres meeting strict jurisdictional and energy co-location criteria. Germany remains resistant to cohesion fund reallocation, despite offers of co-ownership in Bologna. Spain and Poland, reeling from recent grid sabotage and protests, show conditional interest—but demand guaranteed access to hardened clusters before withdrawing opposition.

The directive is adopted, but only narrowly. With `eu_political_capital` at 2, the political cost is overwhelming. The measure is large and immediately burdensome, and member state buy-in is fragile. Implementation begins, but momentum is slow: no physical capacity comes online this turn.

Public sentiment holds at 24, sustained by deepening anxiety. Local energy ministers report backchannel coordination on data centre moratoriums now active in eight member states. The quiet revolt against foreign cloud dependence grows, but without funding or unified strategy, it risks fragmentation.

### Sovereignty Erodes

Despite the new directive, `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 17. The +2.5 rise in `ai_capability` costs −1, and no capacity event secures access. The directive is in flight but not finished—no sovereignty bonus yet. Resilience remains at 51, unchanged as intelligence sharing offsets no new gains.
