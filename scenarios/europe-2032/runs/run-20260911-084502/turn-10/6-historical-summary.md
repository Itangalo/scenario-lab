Defenders lagged for weeks, hospitals/municipalities reverted to winter paper/offline kits, then joint recovery teams with pre-paid patches limited damage where early; late areas saw service stops. April export controls tightened: Europe retained paper access to advanced chips but faced quotas/delays; data-centres throttled for supply after winter power curbs. Open near-frontier models, weeks behind, ran unrecallably on local hardware sustaining town halls/workshops.
