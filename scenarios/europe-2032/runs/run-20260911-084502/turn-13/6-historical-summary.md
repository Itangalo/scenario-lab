Autumn 2031–H1 2032: machine-assisted pathogen escape contained by Dec 2031 at cost of trust; H1 2032 stabilized—wards emptied, office AI boosted productivity, framework contract for audited assistants favored rich regions, cheap imported mobile arms cut logistics costs but deepened dependence and labour divide; lithography fight frozen, near-frontier open models persisted in workshops.

H2 2032 broke the calm: coordinated automated extortion attack hit municipalities, hospitals, utilities via tainted component, forcing redeploy of hospital patch crews; rich cities recovered in days, poorer regions on paper for weeks. Simultaneous physical sabotage of substations and compute construction sites halted work in four countries under military guard. Digital and physical shocks reinforced each other. Brussels activated civil-protection/cyber/police restoration teams from repurposed funds, froze office-assistant rollout to keep integrators on repair; cascade prevented but response seen as late and uneven. Washington-Beijing announced limited understanding on model weights, escalation and bio tools with thin verification; Europe briefed late, sought observer access. Strait shipments continued with higher insurance. New near-frontier open model spread to hundreds of thousands of machines—welcomed by workshops, flagged by security as unrecallable attack tooling. By Dec 2032 essentials held but trust fell; scoreboards showed outage hours, blame focused on foreign dependence in robots, models, and attack tools.
