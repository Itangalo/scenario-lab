## In practice
We are suspending all implementation activities under the Emergency Sovereign Continuity Directive effective immediately. The directive’s legal authority remains in place, but its enforcement is halted by Commission delegation under Article 11(2) of Regulation (EU) 2019/881, pending a full technical and institutional audit. We are convening a Cyber Resilience Task Force under ENISA leadership, with binding mandates for data access across EuroHPC, national emergency networks, and the Sovereign Core development consortium. The task force includes independent cryptographers, systems engineers from non-EU democracies (invited via the Nordic-Baltic Digital Partnership), and red-team specialists from the European CERT network.

The audit will assess design coherence, access control logic, isolation boundaries, and failover protocols under simulated stress, including adversarial AI probing. Findings will be published in full by the Public Trust Observatory in turn 13, alongside a recovery roadmap. We are already receiving resistance from DG CONNECT and the original contractors, who argue that disclosure would expose vulnerabilities. We reject that argument: the vulnerabilities are already known to attackers — what the public deserves is proof that we know them too. To ensure cooperation, we are conditioning the release of remaining InvestAI repurposing funds on full compliance with audit requests. This is not a retreat from sovereignty — it is a correction. We will not build on sand.
