### The Core Crumbles, the Audit Begins

The European Union formally closes the Emergency Sovereign Continuity Directive, acknowledging its fatal design flaws. Migration to the Sovereign Core is halted across all member states, averting immediate systemic risk but deepening institutional distrust. The Public Trust Observatory publishes its final assessment, confirming critical vulnerabilities in access isolation and failure propagation — findings now central to the newly launched *Independent Audit and Recovery of Sovereign Core Systems*.

Despite resistance from DG CONNECT and key contractors, the Cyber Resilience Task Force begins operations under ENISA, with binding data access mandates. Dr. Elisa Mäkelä’s team initiates red-team simulations using AI-generated attack vectors, while Professor Thomas Berthold integrates external experts from the Tor Project and Canada’s Digital Operations. Conditioning €18bn in InvestAI funds on compliance forces reluctant cooperation, though behind-the-scenes legal challenges emerge.

A major cyber incident strikes — a coordinated ransomware sweep across regional health authorities and transport operators, exploiting a poisoned software dependency. The attack is fast, adaptive, and clearly AI-synthesized. Initial damage is contained, but response is chaotic. Resilience holds just above collapse, absorbing some harm, but public services stagger. Attribution is unclear, and confidence erodes further.

Global AI capability advances to 90.0, driven by recursive self-improvement now operating near-autonomous speeds. The jump is sub-linear but relentless, entering the terminal phase where instruments lose resolution. Openweight_capability rises modestly to 72.0, as fragments of leaked weights from last cycle are cautiously reintegrated within distributed research collectives, particularly in academic and activist networks. Stabilization remains partial and uneven, hindered by systemic distrust and ongoing failure; no broad deployment occurs. Yet no safety improvements land on deployed systems — ai_safety remains frozen at 1.0, a dangerous void at the frontier.

The Union acts decisively, but legitimacy does not return. Political capital falls to −4.0 — the failed directive yields no bonus, the new large measure costs −3, and the priority designation costs −1, with no offsetting gains. The portfolio now carries one active measure, but capital remains deeply negative. Public sentiment holds at 18.0, trapped in anxiety and disillusionment.
