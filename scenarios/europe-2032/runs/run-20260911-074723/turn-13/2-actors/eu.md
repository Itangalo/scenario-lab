## New measure
**EU Sovereign Fallback and State-to-State Access Track**
Keeps cut-off hospitals, ministries and firms on EU-hosted degraded fallback while opening a state-to-state channel to Washington for quota restoration and incident liaison now that the labs are a US state arm.
This and now because nationalisation plus short-notice cut-off removed the commercial ground for access — only a government-to-government track plus an honest degraded-service regime prevents collapse in Lyon/Gdansk-type sites.

## Priority
No in-flight measure to prioritise — the new State-to-State Access Track is the priority, because without a negotiated minimum quota and a managed fallback, essential services stop rather than degrade.

## In practice
We run degraded-not-stopped as doctrine. DG SANTE/HERA with ENISA joint teams standardise the EU-hosted fallback protocol already in use in Lyon and Gdansk — manual double-checks, therapy-queue rationing, nightly public lists of what runs — funded from reprogrammed ESF+/EU4Health, no new money voted. The AI Office logs errors and near-misses as the safety case for why cut-off is a patient-safety issue, not a trade dispute.

Externally we shift venue: Council mandates the Commission/EEAS to seek a state-to-state technical-access arrangement with the new US control authority — minimum institutional quotas for health and public administration, advance notice of cuts, and a military-style incident hotline — offering supply-chain cooperation and evaluation reciprocity in return, and explicitly not invoking the AI Act or Anti-Coercion Instrument against a security apparatus. We ask like a dependent ally to keep the lights on while we rebuild, because that is what we are.
