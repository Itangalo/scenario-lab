In response to a major cyber intrusion, the EU launches the Critical Infrastructure AI Resilience Shield (CIARS), mandating AI-driven defenses and red-teaming for critical sectors, but faces resistance from Poland and Hungary over data sovereignty. Germany and the Netherlands lead adoption. The InvestAI Gigafactories progress in France and Sweden amid ongoing permitting delays and legal challenges to the Tech Sovereignty Package over EU regulatory authority, with Italy and Austria raising objections. The European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) begins interim operations but confronts legal doubts regarding its funding mechanism. Political capital erodes under the weight of multiple concurrent initiatives, and while public sentiment remains stable, job displacement in legal tech and diagnostics sparks localized unrest. No sovereignty gains are achieved this turn.
