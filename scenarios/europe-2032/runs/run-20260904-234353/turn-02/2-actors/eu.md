## New measure  
**Measure:** Launch the Critical Infrastructure AI Resilience Shield (CIARS)  
A rapid hardening programme for energy, water, and transport networks against AI-powered cyber intrusions, combining mandatory detection upgrades, red-teaming, and cross-border incident response protocols.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 5`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase resilience by 10–12 points; reduce time to detect and contain AI-driven intrusions in critical sectors from weeks to hours  
`Applies to:` EU member states’ critical infrastructure operators, with enforcement through the NIS2 Directive and funding tied to Cohesion Fund disbursements

## Priority  
**Priority:** InvestAI Gigafactories  
This remains the cornerstone of our two-year commitment: without sovereign compute, every evaluation, every resilience upgrade, and every industrial ambition depends on permission from others — and we are still racing to close a six-year gap in a domain where compound interest is measured in months.

## In practice  
We are accelerating the InvestAI Gigafactories by activating emergency grid reservation protocols with ENTSO-E and fast-tracking environmental assessments under the Net-Zero Industry Act. The European Investment Bank is now co-ordinating with national development banks to disburse €40bn in initial capital, with site-specific financing packages for Brittany, Norrland, and Andalusia that include binding reinvestment in local energy grids and green hydrogen hubs to counter opposition. We are also finalising agreements with three EU-based semiconductor equipment suppliers to co-locate advanced packaging facilities at two of the sites, leveraging the Chips Act’s strategic designation powers to prioritise AI-relevant production.

On the Tech Sovereignty Package, we are pressing the Digital Markets and Digital Services Council to adopt the proposed permitting directive under Article 114 TFEU, aiming to cut approval timelines from 48 to 12 months by harmonising environmental, energy, and labour standards across member states. The Commission is offering a trade: accelerated permitting in exchange for enforceable commitments on EU-based operational control, energy efficiency, and talent retention. We are also advancing negotiations with offshore wind and nuclear operators to secure 10GW of dedicated power through long-term PPAs, with the first signed in France this quarter.

The EAAD is now operational in interim form under ENISA, with 35 core staff hired and secondments secured from national cyber and nuclear agencies. We are initiating the first red-team evaluations of models above 50.0 capability, focusing on agent persistence and emergent coordination risks. Legal services are preparing for the inevitable challenge to the hyperscaler levy, building a precedent stack around digital services taxation and extraterritorial enforcement in competition law. Meanwhile, the cyber intrusion findings are being shared with national CIRTs to inform the new CIARS programme, which will mandate AI-powered intrusion detection systems, quarterly red-teaming, and real-time telemetry sharing across borders — a direct response to the gaps exposed this turn.
