## New measure  
**Measure:** Introduce the AI Labour Transition Fund (ALT-Fund)  
A targeted, sector-specific transition mechanism to support workers displaced by AI in legal support, radiology, and mid-level software development, combining wage insurance, accelerated retraining, and employer co-investment in redeployment.

`Category: 7 (Labour and social protection)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 6`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase resilience by 5–7 points through social absorption; stabilise public sentiment by addressing visible job displacement; reduce political risk from emergent unrest in key sectors  
`Applies to:` EU member states, with conditionality tied to national activation of sectoral transition councils and co-financing from automating firms

## In practice  
We are accelerating the InvestAI Gigafactories by finalising binding agreements with three EU semiconductor equipment suppliers to co-locate advanced packaging facilities in Brittany and Norrland, leveraging the Chips Act’s strategic designation to fast-track export controls and customs clearance. The European Investment Bank is now disbursing the second tranche of €30bn, prioritising grid reinforcement and green hydrogen integration to secure local buy-in. We are also activating emergency provisions under the Net-Zero Industry Act to override permitting bottlenecks in Andalusia, where regional opposition has stalled construction, offering a package of local jobs guarantees and direct energy cost caps for surrounding communities.

On the Tech Sovereignty Package, we are convening an extraordinary Digital Markets Council to resolve the Article 114 TFEU dispute, offering Italy and Austria a carve-out for national review of data centre energy sourcing in exchange for support on harmonised permitting. We are also advancing long-term power purchase agreements with French nuclear and Baltic offshore wind operators to secure 8GW of dedicated capacity by 2030, with contracts structured to prioritise EU-controlled operators. Meanwhile, the EAAD continues its interim evaluations, focusing on agent persistence risks in models above 50.0 capability, while legal teams prepare for a constitutional challenge to the hyperscaler levy by building precedent from digital tax and DMA enforcement cases.

CIARS implementation is progressing unevenly: Germany and the Netherlands have activated AI-driven detection systems and shared red-team findings across borders, but Poland and Hungary continue to resist real-time telemetry sharing. We are negotiating through the Cohesion Fund mechanism, conditioning disbursements on partial compliance, while offering technical assistance via ENISA to address sovereignty concerns. At the same time, we are launching the AI Labour Transition Fund in response to emergent displacement, tasking the European Social Fund+ to co-design sectoral transition councils with national labour ministries, starting in France, Germany, and Sweden. The fund will require automating firms to contribute 25% of retraining costs, creating a shared burden model that signals responsibility without stifling innovation.
