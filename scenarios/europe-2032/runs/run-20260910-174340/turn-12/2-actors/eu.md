## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for political capital to recover above 1.0 and for sabotage and access cutoff to be contained with existing playbooks before any funded instrument is votable.

With capital at 1.0, sentiment at 3.0, sovereignty at 14.0 and safety at 2.0, even a small measure costing 2 per turn would drive capital negative and collapse enforcement of the two-year commitment to rebuild EU control over AI in essential services; holding patched networks, commune rotas and port triage on finished mandates costs nothing and preserves the ability to build next turn.

No changes.

## Priority
No priority this turn, because no measures are in flight and no new measure can be funded without extinguishing the last point of capacity to act under the rebuilding-control commitment.

## In practice
We hold essential services with what is already paid for. DG CNECT, ENISA, CERT-EU and HERA keep Continuity Corps playbooks, patched hospital networks and commune teams on rota on existing budgets, triaging late chip crates at Rotterdam and Hamburg and filing bio-screening memos from the genome-model finding with no new money attached. No new fund, no parallel compute line while the Gigafactories record stands finished-but-stalled; that revival belongs to a funded turn, not to a second track now.

We use the turn politically to absorb the cutoff and the sabotage without breaking further. We formally log the short-notice leading-model denial for hospitals and ministries and shift those workloads to hardened open models where EU capacity allows, coordinate police and site protection for blocked data centres, robot depots and chip shipments through the Justice and Home Affairs formation without creating a new instrument, log Washington's use of ASML servicing jurisdiction and the capital's hardened hyperscaler deal for later joint-procurement and Anti-Coercion action, and align licence language inside the middle-power framework — to stop the bleed enough that a sovereignty measure becomes votable.
