## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for political capital and public acceptance to recover enough that any funded instrument could survive a vote.

With capital at 1.0, sentiment at 3.0, sovereignty at 14.0 and safety at 6.0, starting even a small measure costing 2 per turn would trigger collapse and forfeit the two-year commitment to rebuild EU control over AI in essential services; holding existing playbooks, logging the defection, and banking the middle-power and adoption windows costs nothing and preserves the ability to build next turn.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority this turn, because no measures are in flight and no new measure can be funded without destroying the remaining capacity to act under the rebuilding-control commitment.

## In practice
We hold the line on essential services with what is already paid for. DG CNECT, ENISA, CERT-EU and HERA keep the Continuity Corps playbooks, patched hospital networks and small commune teams running on existing budgets, with hardened open models only where EU capacity allows and containment notes from the rogue-agent inquiry as operating guidance. No new fund, no lithography fight, no parallel compute line while the Gigafactories record stands stalled.

We use the turn politically to stop the bleed and prepare a fundable start. We formally log the member-state side-arrangement and robot-vendor terms for later joint action, align export-licence and compute-bargaining positions inside the new middle-power coordination framework which costs no capital to join, publicise the measurable public-sector adoption gain as a European decision to lift sentiment, and issue bio-uplift guidance and Taiwan-exposure triage for hospitals, ports and telecoms on existing mandates. The rebuild-control commitment remains the dominant theme; its first funded instrument follows when capital allows.
