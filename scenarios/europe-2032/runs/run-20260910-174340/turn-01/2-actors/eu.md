## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water against AI-enabled intrusion with joint detection, mandatory incident reporting and exercised cross-border response.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and why now is the autumn intrusion showing EU grids already penetrated undetected for weeks while open-weight offensive cyber spreads beyond recall.

## In practice
We task ENISA with expanded mandate and CER/NIS2 implementation, funded via repurposed InvestAI resilience envelope and member-state TSO contributions, decided in TTE and JHA Councils. Mandatory telemetry sharing and joint audits start with the two affected grid operators, extended to ports and water utilities by delegated act.

In parallel we keep Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) moving: DG CNECT accelerates permitting zones and grid connection, EIB structures guarantees to crowd private data-centre capital, while ASML export pressure is managed via coordinated EU-US tech dialogue. We accept Commission capacity is stretched, so Shield is the priority draw on legal and political attention this turn.
