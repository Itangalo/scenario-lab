This is Preparedness and resilience work, and why now is the major largely-automated incident landing on already-mapped grids and ports while open-weight cyber capability spreads — detection must become containment this turn.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because the triage buys weeks but only the Shield's joint telemetry and exercised cross-border response stops the next automated wave from cascading the same way.

## In practice
We keep the Shield as the draw on political attention: ENISA joint telemetry goes live first with the two affected TSOs, mandatory reporting for transmission, ports and water via CER/NIS2 implementing act in TTE/JHA Councils, funded by repurposed envelope plus operator levies. The Triage rides on that same legal base as an emergency implementing measure — CERT-EU plus national CSIRTs pooled into surge teams, directed to hospitals, municipalities and port IT first.

We admit the investment collapse guts the private-capital assumption behind M1/M2: DG CNECT holds permitting zones in Spain and Sweden open, pauses German grid-connection fights, and tasks EIB to re-scope guarantees toward public anchor funding rather than cancelled hyperscaler builds. We accept slower sovereignty build to hold resilience, per our two-year commitment to leverage *and* resilience.
