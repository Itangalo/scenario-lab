## Priority
Municipal Resilience Labour Pact, because the Shield cannot contain the next automated wave reused from mapped access if no staff remain to patch and rotate credentials in the communes where the open-weight release now lands directly.

## In practice
We put EPSU, CEMR and interior ministers in the EPSCO Council into a time-bound pact: no AI-linked dismissals in participating municipal IT while Shield surge work continues, paid overtime and pooled backfill teams from CERT-EU and ENISA, and ESF+ money for retraining into cyber-hygiene roles. DG EMPL funds it as flexicurity-style transition, member states co-sign the pause.

ENISA keeps joint telemetry with the two hit transmission firms live and pushes the CER/NIS2 reporting act over the line, while DG CNECT holds Spain and Sweden on site-prep guarantees without new cash. On the decision scandal we place it under the AI Act high-risk frame for public administration, task the AI Office with an incident review and suspend the flagged system pending conformity reassessment, to stop trust falling further while crews return.
