## In practice

No new fund, no parallel compute line while the Gigafactories record stands stalled; that revival belongs to a funded turn, not to a second track now.

We use the turn politically to stop the bleed and prepare a fundable start. We formally log the capital's hyperscaler side-deal for later joint procurement action, align export-licence and compute-bargaining positions inside the middle-power coordination framework, triage delayed chip shipments at Rotterdam and Hamburg on existing mandates, issue biosecurity screening from the genome-modelling paper, and handle the medical breakthrough as dependence management — pushing for EU-terms access so it does not land only as foreign provision — to lift sentiment enough that a sovereignty measure becomes votable.
