### The Surge That Wasn’t

A sudden leap in AI capability—driven by a closed model that redesigned its own training architecture—pushes the frontier to 76.0. The jump is abrupt, destabilising, and poorly understood. Developers report internal monitoring systems failing to flag key reasoning steps, and the incident confirms what the leaked biosecurity assessment already suggested: control is fraying. With no matching advance in safety practices, confidence in containment collapses. ai_safety plummets to 2.0.

In response, the EU activates the European AI Emergency Response Corps, a large category 6 measure aimed at rapid deployment during systemic AI incidents. However, political fragmentation stalls progress. Only five member states ratify full deployment rights for the Corps by the deadline, and legal disputes over data sovereignty delay integration with national health systems. The fiscal incentive fails to overcome constitutional objections in Germany and Poland.

The Resilience Surge Protocol continues, and partial deployment of detection nodes in wastewater facilities adds modest hardening. Resilience rises to 42.0, aided by the ongoing protocol and the Corps’ early coordination footprint. But public trust erodes further after a tabloid publishes excerpts from the leaked biosecurity memo, sparking alarm. Sentiment drops to 31.0.

Meanwhile, open models now reflect capabilities last seen in the closed frontier two years ago. Diffusion of knowledge and tooling from the recent closed-model surge lifts openweight_capability to 57.0—approaching but not exceeding the pace of underlying capability growth. Sovereignty slips to 15.0 as the capability surge widens the gap with domestic build-out. The InvestAI Gigafactories remain on track but yield no near-term capacity.

Political capital declines to 8.0. Three large measures remain in flight (InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, Resilience Surge Protocol), costing −9, and the activation of the European AI Emergency Response Corps adds a new −3 charge this turn. A +4 proposal bonus for the Corps—responding directly to the cyber_major_incident and bio_uplift_findings—partially offsets the cost. With only one new measure introduced, the portfolio remains within limits. No additional priority is declared beyond the ongoing focus.

The new U.S. administration takes office, adopting a posture of strategic consolidation—tightening access to frontier AI based on geopolitical alignment.
