In response, the EU activated and expanded the Cross-Border Resilience Node into a mandatory, standing unit with real-time infrastructure access, funded by redirecting €12 billion from stalled InvestAI projects and enforced via cohesion fund penalties. Despite this, Germany and Poland resisted, deepening internal divisions.

Strategic fragmentation and declining resilience continue to undermine EU cohesion.
