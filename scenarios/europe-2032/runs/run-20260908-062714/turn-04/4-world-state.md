### The Weight of Fragility

The Cyber Shield for Critical Operators officially concluded implementation, but its fragmented operation underscored systemic weaknesses. Despite real-time telemetry from several capitals feeding into the European SOC, the absence of full cross-border access—especially from Germany and Poland—left critical gaps. ENISA’s preliminary findings, leaked ahead of formal release, cited “unsustainable coordination burdens” and “interoperability failures under stress,” confirming fears that the Shield is more symbolic than functional.

Public sentiment collapsed further. Violent protests at data centres in Ireland and Poland escalated into regional strikes across IT and energy sectors, with workers denouncing AI as a foreign-driven force of job destruction. Media narratives hardened, framing the EU’s digital ambitions as a dependency disguised as sovereignty. Trust in AI-mediated infrastructure eroded to crisis levels.

Meanwhile, the global AI frontier surged. A new offensive model demonstrated autonomous exploit generation at scale, marking a qualitative leap. This capability_jump pushed `ai_capability` to 60.0, entering the regime where AI systems complete multi-day professional projects end to end. Yet no corresponding safety advance occurred—on the contrary, `ai_safety` plummeted to 14.0 as developers raced ahead without verifiable controls, and the gap between capability and safety widened dangerously.

The EU’s compute sovereignty push faltered. The InvestAI Gigafactories, already delayed, lost anchor tenants amid the ai_investment_collapse, forcing emergency redirection of €30bn in innovation funds. While ASML and Intel negotiations continued, no binding commitments were secured. The Tech Sovereignty Package remained stalled, blocked by national courts and energy concerns.

Political capital, strained by three large measures in flight, dropped to 20.0. The completion of the Cyber Shield provided no meaningful relief, as its mixed performance and ongoing costs outweighed any symbolic gain. With public sentiment below 30, the window for new initiatives narrowed sharply.

### Sovereignty Under Strain

Fragmentation, incident fallout, and stalled bets converged to erode `eu_ai_sovereignty`, now at 14.0. Without demonstrable progress on sovereign compute or supply-chain control, dependence deepened just as capability pressures intensified.
