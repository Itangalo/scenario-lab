## In practice  
The Cyber Shield for Critical Operators has formally concluded its implementation phase this turn. In practice, it now functions in a fragmented but operational form: real-time telemetry flows from Paris, Rome, and Athens into the European SOC, enabling coordinated detection and rapid red-teaming under joint national-EU leadership. But Berlin’s demand for veto power over red-team operations remains unresolved, and Warsaw continues to operate under national emergency protocols, refusing external access. ENISA will publish a post-deployment assessment in January, focusing on interoperability gaps and operator burden, which will inform a potential follow-up measure next turn. The €1.2bn in transition funding prevented total collapse, but cohesion remains paper-thin.

Meanwhile, the InvestAI Gigafactories face a crisis of confidence. The ai_investment_collapse has driven two anchor tenants away, and construction at the Marseille and Wrocław sites risks stalling. To prevent this, we have redirected €30bn from the European Innovation Council’s contingency reserve to guarantee early-stage infrastructure, conditioned on binding commitments from ASML and Intel to co-locate advanced packaging facilities. This is not subsidy — it is strategic leverage: we are offering market access and accelerated permitting in exchange for irreversible industrial anchoring. The Commission is negotiating directly with both firms under Article 207 TFEU, with the Vice-President for Values and Transparency leading talks. The message is clear: Europe will not outspend the US or China, but it can out-structure them by tying compute to jurisdiction.

The Tech Sovereignty Package continues to falter. The promise of €200bn in private capital now looks unrealistic, and the accelerated-permitting zones have been blocked in six member states by local courts citing environmental and energy supply concerns. We are shifting focus from attracting hyperscalers to securing upstream control: the Commission has launched a Green Paper on EU-controlled lithography supply chains, to be completed by Q1 2028. This is no longer about competition — it is about survival. Without control over the means of production, sovereignty is a slogan.
