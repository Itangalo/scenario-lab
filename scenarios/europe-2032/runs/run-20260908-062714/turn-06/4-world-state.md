### The Plateau That Wasn’t

Despite signs of a capability stall—underwhelming releases and falling prices—global AI advancement continues, though at a reduced pace. `ai_capability` rises by +0.5 to 62.0, reflecting a market flush with last year’s models but no breakthroughs. Yet the pause is shallow: autonomous exploit generation is now embedded in commercial toolchains, and offensive cyber actors adapt quickly. No safety progress occurs, leaving `ai_safety` stagnant at 14.0.

### Fragmentation Deepens

The **Tech Sovereignty Package** formally concludes this turn. While implementation remains patchy—blocked in parts by national courts and underfunded in others—its completion triggers a mechanical boost to `eu_ai_sovereignty` under metric rule 5. The package’s closure, even without broad uptake, reflects institutional closure and counts as a realized effort, contributing +3 to `eu_ai_sovereignty`. A narrow **Cross-Border Resilience Node** launches in two critical infrastructure corridors, but it is a fragment of the original vision.

The **Sovereign Red Lines for Open Weights** was not launched this turn. Preparatory work advances—legal clearance is secured, and a technical benchmark at 50.0 is drafted—but the measure has not yet been formally introduced. A conditional carve-out—requiring safety mirrors for high-capability open models—is proposed internally to soften opposition. However, member states accelerate their own rules: France drafts a ban on autonomous agents in energy systems, while Poland moves to nationalise AI audit rights. The internal market frays.

Public sentiment remains at 15.0—hostile, with protests near data centres in Ireland and Sweden. Political capital erodes under the weight of three large, failing initiatives. The completion of the **Tech Sovereignty Package** delivers a modest +2 boost to `eu_political_capital`, partially offsetting the portfolio charge of −10 from ongoing measures. After accounting for decay and partial gains, `eu_political_capital` settles at 12.0—down, but not collapsed.

### Sovereignty Rewarded, Capital Crumbles

With the closure of the **Tech Sovereignty Package**, `eu_ai_sovereignty` increases to 16.0, reflecting the mandatory +3 bonus for completing a Category 4 measure. This accounts for institutional momentum and formal closure, regardless of real-world impact. The portfolio charge—three large measures and a priority—still weighs heavily, but the completion bonus prevents a steeper fall in political capital. The Union is stretched thin, but not yet losing grip: the machinery of governance still registers wins, even as delivery falters.
