### The Lights Flicker

A wave of coordinated AI-driven attacks hits public health systems, transport hubs, and regional energy distributors across three member states. The intrusions—adaptive, self-concealing, and rapidly propagating—overwhelm legacy defences. Hospitals revert to paper records; rail signalling fails intermittently. The attackers vanish before full attribution, leaving behind forensic traces of model-generated exploit chains. The incident confirms what many feared: offensive AI is not coming—it is already operational.

In response, the EU fast-tracks implementation of the Cyber Shield for Critical Operators. Real-time telemetry flows into a newly activated European SOC in Brussels, aggregating data from Paris, Rome, and Athens. But Berlin refuses full integration, demanding veto power over red-team operations. Warsaw invokes national emergency protocols to opt out of external access entirely. Port and rail operators warn of system instability under continuous monitoring. The Union offers co-leadership and €1.2bn in transition funding, but cohesion frays under pressure.

Public backlash intensifies. Protests at data centre construction sites in Ireland and Poland turn confrontational, with activists cutting power lines and occupying sites. Police intervention escalates tensions, feeding narratives of AI as an imposed, uncontrollable force. Media coverage focuses on job losses in IT security and the irony of foreign-built AI tools being used to defend European infrastructure.

Meanwhile, the global AI investment landscape contracts sharply. Two major compute ventures collapse, and private capital retreats from long-term AI infrastructure. The EU’s Tech Sovereignty Package and InvestAI Gigafactories lose anchor tenants. Contingency funds are redirected to secure ASML and Intel commitments, but progress slows. Sovereignty erodes as capability advances and external shocks compound dependence.

### Nervous System, Fractured

The Cyber Shield begins functioning in parts, but its fragmented rollout reveals the limits of coordination without trust. Resilience degrades as defences fail to keep pace. Public sentiment sinks into open hostility. Political capital, strained by three large measures and a crisis response, plummets.
