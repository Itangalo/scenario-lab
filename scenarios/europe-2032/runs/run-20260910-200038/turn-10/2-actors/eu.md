## Priority
EU Site Shield and Repair Corps, because the guarded repair zones in Flanders, Lombardy and Bavaria are now the only thing keeping both halted compute works and live interconnectors from cascading into outages.

## In practice
We extend the Critical Entities Resilience protected-repair orders from the backup lines and cooling trenches to the two member states' operating interconnectors and hospital backup feeds, tasking the same gendarmerie vans, military engineers and rapid spares convoys under ENTSO-E and ENISA coordination, funded by reprogrammed CEF and Digital Europe money. Municipal policing deals bought with accelerated revenue shares in Flanders and Lombardy are rolled to interconnector corridors; in Bavaria we frame substation guard as hospital-continuity, not site protection.

Alongside, ENISA and HERA push the breakthrough auto-patching and swarm-detection into the EU-hosted substitutes and vetted open stacks that hospitals and ministries run on, keeping the hours patch cycle alive without readable frontier traces, and we procure the new tailored therapies only on EU-hosted compute where possible so the medical gain reads as dependence reduced, not dependence demonstrated.
