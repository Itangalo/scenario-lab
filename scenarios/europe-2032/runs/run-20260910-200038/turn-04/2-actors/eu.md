## Priority
Municipal Resilience Backstop, because enforceable shield proofs mean little while smaller utilities cannot rotate credentials or monitor vendors, and it is the only in-flight work that lands absorption before the next probe — outranking disclosure and gigafactories this turn.

## In practice
We push the Backstop through DG HOME and ENISA with CEF and Cohesion reprogramming: shared SOC capacity pooled at regional level, mobile rotation teams via national CSIRTs, and audit-relief conditional on closing vendor links, aimed at the municipal utilities flagged in autumn. Council JHA and mayors are offered co-financing, not mandates, to stop the power-price and water fights blocking compliance.

Alongside, we drive M5 Disclosure Act through ordinary legislative procedure — AI Office scoping full-eval receipt and quarterly Parliament briefings — while holding M1/M2 gigafactory permits to backup-power-ready sites only, keeping sovereignty momentum without breaking grid queues tilted to resilience.
