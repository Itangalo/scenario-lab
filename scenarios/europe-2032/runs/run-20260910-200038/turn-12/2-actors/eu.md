## New measure
**EU Degraded Operations Continuity Pact**
Keeps hospitals, grids and municipal services operable on paper-heavy manual fallbacks and guarded EU-hosted rebuilds through the open-weight sweep, without US frontier access.
This is a Preparedness and resilience move because with safety at zero, openweight at 83 and another frontier leak imminent, essential services will only survive if degraded operation is funded and staffed, and with capital at 21 only a small pact extending M13 and M12 can hold our two-year commitment to keep sites physically operable.

## Priority
EU Automated Attack Containment and Essential Service Restoration, because the live containment deciding whether rebuilds land this spring outranks the follow-on continuity pact still starting up.

## In practice
We keep ENISA/CERT-EU segmentation and clean EU-hosted rebuilds from M13 as the main effort, using the Digital Europe emergency reserve and HERA co-procurement, with Interior Council mandate extended to June. Gendarmerie-engineer escorts stay on hospitals and grid rebuild sites, using the Bavaria hospital-guard framing to avoid Munich-type blockades.

We start the Continuity Pact by tasking DG CNECT and DG SANTE to fund diesel, backup tapes, paper fallback kits and overtime for manual operations in Wallonia, Lombardy and Bavaria hit districts, copied from the Flanders municipal policing deals. No new US access is requested; restoration stays on EU-hosted and vetted-open images to hold operability without US access, while export-control tightening is handled by rationing remaining allied compute to rebuilds first.
