Funding comes from re-directed Connecting Europe Facility and Digital Europe money, with Commission co-financing detection sensors deployed by member-state CSIRTs.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the tech sovereignty package moving through DG CNECT and accelerated-permitting zones, but we tell member states plainly that grid-connection priority this turn goes to resilience retrofits and backup power, not new data-centre load. Opposition from industry over audit costs is met with conditional access to EU compute credits once hardened.
