## Priority
EU Wartime Joint Cyber-Bio Defence Accession, because pooled attribution, telemetry and medical countermeasure access during active great-power war outranks even the Continuity Pact for whether degraded services survive this winter.

## In practice
We keep M14 as the operating base: DG CNECT, DG SANTE and ENISA/CERT-EU fund diesel, overtime, paper fallbacks and clean EU-hosted rebuilds in Wallonia, Lombardy and Bavaria under the Interior and Health Council mandate, with gendarmerie-engineer escorts. No new US bilateral access is sought; rationed allied compute stays on rebuilds first.

We execute the accession via Foreign Affairs and General Affairs Councils under the Cyber Solidarity Act and HERA/European Health Union legal bases, seconding ENISA liaison officers to the joint cyber command and ECDC to the biosurveillance standing mechanism, with Digital Europe and EU4Health emergency funds covering telemetry nodes and sample-sharing labs. France, Germany and the Netherlands host the uplinks; neutrality and data-protection objections are met with EU-retained data residency and a war-limited mandate tied to our essential-services commitment.
