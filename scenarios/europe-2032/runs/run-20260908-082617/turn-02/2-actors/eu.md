## New measure
**European Labour Transition and Innovation Fund**  
Establish a €50bn fund to support displaced mid-level technical and administrative workers, retrain them for AI-augmented roles, and incentivise firms to redesign jobs rather than eliminate them.

## Priority
**Cyber Shield for Critical Operators** — because the verification_widens event has accelerated offensive capability in critical domains, and another successful attack would shatter both public trust and institutional credibility, making all other measures impossible.

## In practice
We are moving forward on Cyber Shield by activating the Council’s Telecommunications configuration to fast-track the designation of Tier 1 critical entities under NIS2, overriding national delays through qualified majority voting under Article 114 TFEU. ENISA’s 400 new staff will be drawn from a pre-qualified pool of cyber professionals under the EU Talent Mobility Scheme, with deployment tied to cohesion fund disbursement. The first stress test in Q2 will simulate a supply chain compromise using red-team tools developed in partnership with the European Defence Agency — a live exercise, not a tabletop. We are offering German industry a carve-out in audit frequency in exchange for full logging access to EU monitors, and we are fast-tracking InvestAI energy allocations to any operator that achieves baseline compliance by turn 3.

At the same time, we are launching the European Labour Transition and Innovation Fund to respond to the emergent displacement wave. The fund will co-finance national retraining programmes with a 60% EU share, but only where firms commit to job redesign pilots — not just reskilling, but re-imagining roles around human-AI collaboration. We are linking access to the Tech Sovereignty Package’s accelerated permitting to participation in these pilots, creating a tangible incentive. The automated decision scandal has made adoption politically toxic in sensitive domains; we are not ignoring that. Instead, we are channeling the backlash into a positive vision: not just protection, but transition with dignity. This is how we maintain political capital — by acting before the crisis lands.
