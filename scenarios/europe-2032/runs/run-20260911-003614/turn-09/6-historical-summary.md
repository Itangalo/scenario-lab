December machine-speed patches held where installed — large hospitals/transmission restored in hours with paper fallback — while unpatched small towns, rural clinics, hauliers stayed dark for days. Brussels triaged core with patch bundles, manual drills, helpdesks and insurer-mandated interlocks, but bypasses and port snarls persisted.

Winter wave continued: core routinely isolated and restored, edge thinned to helpdesk with pharmacy queues, cancelled operations, parked hauliers. Mayors/hauliers blockaded retrofit depots and patch hubs demanding override kits and bridging pay, freezing rural routes; Brussels paused checks. Commission responded with rural continuity pact — cohesion advances for override kits, wage bridging, locally held bundles via new municipal joint buyer pool for European defensive stack; insurers conditioned cover on adoption. Partly worked: one corridor eased and ports cleared, others lagged with late kits, missed self-employed pay, bypassed interlocks. Arrival of tailored therapies on European infrastructure delivered as public service briefly displaced outage coverage and made protection tangible, though anger at foreign machines/models persisted.
