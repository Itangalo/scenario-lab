## New measure
**EU Shopfloor and Care Transition Shield**
Sets EU-wide deployment conditions for logistics and care humanoids already entering via third parties plus a transition fund for displaced warehouse and care-assistant workers tied to automating employers. This is Labour and social protection work, and why now is because Chinese humanoids are in Rotterdam/Duisburg warehouses and care pilots with union backlash, dexterity and arming following fast, while queues and a welfare-AI scandal make any adoption politically impossible without protection attached — and it serves the autonomy-through-rupture commitment by holding essential logistics and care services staffed and legitimate while supply remains rationed.

## Priority

## In practice
We keep M7 as the rationing operation — DG TRADE chasing Japan/Korea lots under the joint export-control pool, DG GROW allocation board holding hospitals and grid first — and we use the finished Anomaly Triage (M8) authority only for time-limited holds where re-runs on EuroHPC show loss of control, to avoid spending legitimacy we do not have after the welfare-AI scandal.

For the new Shield we go via Employment/Social Affairs Council with Single Market legal base: Commission recommendation plus ESF+ reprogramming for wage insurance and rapid retraining in logistics hubs and care pilots, co-funded by a levy on large-scale deployers of humanoids, with procurement conditions for publicly funded care homes requiring human staffing ratios and incident logging. Unions get enforceable transition money and a filming-to-bargaining table; logistics operators and care providers get EU-wide rules instead of city-by-city bans; health ministers get cover to keep Lyon/Milan/Rotterdam therapies on rationed EuroHPC compute by separating trusted cures from untrusted automation.
