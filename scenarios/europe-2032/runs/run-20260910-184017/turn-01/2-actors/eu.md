## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because undetected presence in EU grids outranks even compute build-out when essential services could cascade this winter.

## In practice
We task ENISA with ENTSO-E, member-state TSOs and port authorities to deploy network monitoring baselines, segmented backups and a December tabletop across the two affected grids plus three others, funded by repurposed Digital Europe and CEF money under Article 173/114 legal base. Interior and Energy Council formations carry it; reluctant operators get co-funding for sensors in exchange for mandatory incident reporting into the shared cell.

Alongside, we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package moving through DG CNECT and EIB structuring: site shortlisting, grid-connection reservations and ASML supply-chain safeguards, resisting US pressure to fragment export policy while negotiating permitting zones with host regions. No new compute instrument is created — delivery runs through the inherited lines.
