## New measure
**EU Wartime Continuity and Sovereign Fallback**
Keeps essential services, hospitals and ministries running after US-China war targeting of cables, fabs and data centres and after EU frontier access cut-off, by switching critical workloads to EU-controlled and certified systems, hardened comms and spares rationing.
This is Preparedness and resilience because with capital at 21 and sovereignty at 15 we cannot rebuild a frontier stack this turn — we must absorb cutoff, coercion and kinetic strikes without cascade. Why now: access-denied plus supply-chain coercion plus shooting war makes dependence immediately lethal.

## Priority
EU Wartime Continuity and Sovereign Fallback, because no certified retrofit or sovereignty build matters if war and cutoff stop lights, hospitals and ministries this half-year.

## In practice
We activate the Civil Protection Mechanism and the Containment and Lights-On Protocol structures: ENISA and EU CyCom order isolation-ready operation for energy, telecoms and finance, pre-authorised load-shedding and satellite/subsea-cable rerouting, with the Certified Containment Retrofit monitors kept as the mandatory gate for any agentic system that stays on. Health Emergency Response Authority and DG DIGIT triage frontier-dependent hospital and ministry workloads onto EU-hosted models and validated open-weights, with emergency procurement waivers.

We answer coercion and cutoff politically, not technically: European Council declares ASML servicing and spares a Union security asset under the Anti-Coercion Instrument, refuses further extraterritorial cuts without Council consent, and demands written allied parity for volume licences; DG TRADE and DG COMP jointly ration remaining compute, spares and power reservations to continuity users first. We publicly attribute service degradation to war and cutoff to contain blame, while using the bio-uplift finding and research breakthrough to justify continued certification and restraint.
