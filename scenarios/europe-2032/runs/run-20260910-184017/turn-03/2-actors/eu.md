## New measure
**EU Displacement Transition Fund**
Creates a rapid EU-cofunded wage-bridge, retraining voucher and employer-transition levy pilot for knowledge-work occupations where augmentation is tipping into displacement, run through national employment agencies.
Category 7 Labour and social protection is why now: early productivity-without-job-loss studies are already stale, fraud-driven distrust plus looming junior-work displacement risks turning adjustment into shock, and withstanding shocks requires income and retraining capacity before layoffs hit local news.
