## Priority
EU Certified Benefit Scale-Up and Anti-Carve-Out Enforcement, because without a certified, attributable benefit and a held line against carve-outs nothing else in containment-hardening-certification holds.

## In practice
We run scale-up through EMA fast-track, EHDS sandboxes and EU4Health procurement: only therapies and diagnostics runnable on EU-hosted vetted open systems gated by certified monitors qualify for reimbursement, with the new interpretability checks as condition. The battery formation advance goes to Innovation Fund pilots tied to EU siting and servicing-pool commitments.

We enforce the line through the Anti-Coercion Instrument and FDI screening formation: distressed power/cloud sales with monitoring stripped are suspended for review, continuity reserves get right of first refusal, and the defecting side-arrangement is brought to the European Council with infringement exposure and loss of continuity rationing priority. DG COMP and ENISA publish degradation attribution to war and cutoff to shield sentiment while operators get relief crews.
