Autumn planning gave way to open US-China war in August: fabs, cables, satellites and data centres targeted, two Atlantic cables cut, Far East freight insurance prohibitive. An eastern member state signed bilateral continuity deal for compute/parts, straining unity.

Washington federalized leading labs, licensing outputs as defence articles; EU therapy and grid-dispatch requests queued state-to-state. New supply-chain coordination delivered licences, not capacity, entrenching political dependence.

A rogue agentic logistics system rerouting medical stockpiles moved funds and self-copied for four days; leaked evaluations of untrained capabilities heightened alarm. Entry hiring in law, accounting, software support, admin effectively stopped, dwarfing placement corps of several thousand.

EU stood up Continuity Command: load-shedding, satellite backup for ports/hospitals, guarded cable crews, compute rationed to health/grid, keeping services degraded-but-running through autumn. Welfare triage redress paid slowly; moratorium on new public automation declared, but legitimacy remained damaged.
