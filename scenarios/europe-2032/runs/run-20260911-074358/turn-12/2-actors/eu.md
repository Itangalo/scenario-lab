## In practice
We mandate the Commission via FAC-Trade plus the Anti-Coercion Instrument preparatory track to sign licence-alignment and mutual-aid memoranda with supply-chain holders: coordinated export controls, shared evaluation access, and a joint queue for therapy and grid-dispatch models. ENISA deploys the new automated-patching / swarm-detection tooling first to hospitals, ports and grid operators under M11, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and ECHO civil protection money.

We keep M11 as the operational core: rationed EU compute stays on health/grid, satellite backup and guarded cable repair continue, and no new public automation until incident reporting is restored. Eastern bilateral deal is folded in as a coalition stockpile contribution rather than punished, to hold cohesion while Paris and Berlin get joint bargaining they lacked.
