## Priority
EU Middle-Power Access and Supply Pact, because without restored access and joint leverage the restoration surge runs on weak open substitutes while foreign funds pick off our hosting — and it outranks even our resilience commitment this turn because access denial is what is degrading services.

## In practice
We mandate the Commission DG TRADE and DG CONNECT with the EEAS to table licence-alignment and joint-procurement terms in the Trade and Foreign Affairs Councils on an Article 207 / Anti-Coercion Instrument base: offer ASML maintenance, STMicro power-electronics and grid-connection slots as our chips-for-compute stake, in return for pooled hosting and re-routed model access for hospitals and ministries. No new fund — we re-use InvestAI guarantee headroom and emergency state-aid clearance.

We keep M10 running as the operational arm: ENISA/CERT-EU plus HERA/ECDC restoration teams and sentinel sampling stay sequenced to worst-hit eastern municipalities and wards, with daily bulletins to show the Pact is what gets backups and triage off paper. The capital that lent engineers keeps its discount conditional on staying in both the Pact and the loan scheme, and courts blocking factory/grid works are offered Pact-backed offtake as reason to lift injunctions.
