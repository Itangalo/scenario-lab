## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Shield, because an unanswered precursor that mapped breakers and staged tooling outranks build-out that pays off years later.

## In practice
We task ENISA with joint authority alongside national TSO regulators to enforce OT segmentation and 24-hour incident reporting under the NIS2 legal base, funded by reprogramming Connecting Europe Facility and Horizon resilience lines, with Frontex-style joint teams for the port authority case. Energy and Transport Councils carry the mandate; interior ministers resisting EU access to grid controls are offered co-financed SOC upgrades and liability cover.

The Gigafactories in M1 and permitting zones in M2 continue on their tracks: DG CNECT shepherds site selection for the first two gigafactories and grid-connection fast-tracking, while we use ASML leverage quietly to keep EUV supply open without triggering US retaliation. No new compute instrument is stood up in parallel — acceleration comes from prioritising permits and power where the shield rollout identifies critical dependencies.
