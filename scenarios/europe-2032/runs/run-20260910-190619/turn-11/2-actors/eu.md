A small category 6 Preparedness and resilience measure now because access-denial plus investment collapse means continuity cannot wait for gigafactories or new procurement, only for re-platforming what already exists.

## Priority
EU Service Continuity Fallback, because denied frontier access is now stopping clinical and administrative work while the Defection Containment Compact only holds the political line.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT with ENISA and CERT-EU to inventory denied-access dependencies in health and finance within weeks, then re-point them to EU-hosted open-weight deployments on existing national compute and allied high-speed patch nodes, under the Critical Infrastructure Shield maintenance regime. No new fund: we re-sequence Shield slots and M6 patch teams, with temporary state-aid clearance for continuity hosting costs.

We run M8 in parallel through General Affairs Council: the defector keeps its discount frozen at current level, no further undercut, in exchange for first fallback slots for its hospitals and eastern/southern mayors staying up the queue. Compliance certified by ENISA; enforcement deferred but published to blunt queue-jump grievance while concrete and grid works remain blocked.
