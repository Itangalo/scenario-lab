## New measure
**EU-Hosted Red Teaming Facility (Category 5)**  
A public technical capability to continuously probe the most capable foreign AI systems under controlled conditions, simulating adversarial use cases and detecting emergent coordination risks.

## Priority
**Secure Audit Access for EU-Certified Evaluators (category 5)** — because without verified access to model behaviour in real time, no red teaming, no incident response, and no sovereignty exists in practice, and because this turn is the last chance to lock in inspection rights before US providers finalize non-compliance as policy.

## In practice
We are acting through the European Digital Infrastructure Board, now operating under delegated authority from the Digital Markets Act and reinforced by emergency provisions in the Critical Entities Resilience Directive. The Secure Audit Access measure is being implemented via Implementing Regulation, requiring any provider of high-risk AI systems in energy, water, transport, or health to grant secure telemetry access to EU-certified evaluators. Two nodes—hosted by BSI (Germany) and ANSSI (France)—are operational, using sealed environments and pre-published methodologies to build trust. We are offering reciprocity: findings will be shared first with providers, and only anonymized patterns published. But access is not optional.

Resistance remains high. US labs threaten to withdraw from EU health and energy contracts, and Washington has invoked national security exemptions. But we are holding. Smaller member states—Cyprus, Estonia, Slovenia—see this as their only leverage, and we are channeling Civil Protection Mechanism funds to stand up regional evaluation capacity. The Kimi K3 incident proved that open weights already carry offensive capability; we cannot wait for closed models to weaponize further. This is not about espionage—it is about survival. The red teaming facility we are now building will test not just what models do, but what they could do under pressure, and who benefits. It is small, but it is ours.
