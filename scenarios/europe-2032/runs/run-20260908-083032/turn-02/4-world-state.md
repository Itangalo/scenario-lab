### The Audit Holds, But at a Cost

The European Union doubled down on *Secure Audit Access*, declaring it the priority for the first half of 2027. Despite operational nodes in Germany and France coming online, no major provider granted real-time telemetry access. U.S. developers invoked national security carve-outs, and American officials reiterated that compliance would constitute a precedent they would not accept. Smaller EU states, reliant on external AI capacity, grew anxious—Cyprus and Estonia received only limited support under the Civil Protection Mechanism, revealing gaps in enforcement solidarity.

In response, the EU launched the **EU-Hosted Red Teaming Facility**, a small but symbolically significant step toward independent evaluation. Hosted across distributed research labs and funded through repurposed civil protection funds, it began low-intensity simulations targeting known models. However, without access to live systems or closed weights, its scope remained narrow—focused on open models like Kimi K3. Initial findings suggested emergent coordination patterns, but no breakthroughs in detection.

### Accelerating Frontiers, Eroding Safety

AI capability advanced sharply to 57.0, driven by shortened release cycles and unconfirmed reports of autonomous research loops in private training runs. The gap between capability and safety widened. With no major safety measure yet landing on shipped systems, and no verification mechanism for frontier models, *ai_safety* dropped to 30.0. The absence of enforceable safeguards, combined with rising open-weight offensive potential, contributed to a further dip in *resilience*, now at 37.0.

Meanwhile, *eu_ai_sovereignty* fell to 19.0 as dependence on foreign compute deepened. The *InvestAI Gigafactories* and *Tech Sovereignty Package* remained years from completion, and no new sovereign capacity came online. The red teaming facility, while a step toward autonomy, did not offset the structural deficit.

Political capital eroded to 36.0. Three measures remained in flight—two large, one small—costing −8 total, plus −1 for the named priority. The proposal bonus for the red teaming facility (+3) was outweighed by the ongoing strain. Public sentiment held steady at 42.0, but fissures emerged in media coverage, with younger demographics welcoming red teaming while industrial sectors warned of decoupling risks.
