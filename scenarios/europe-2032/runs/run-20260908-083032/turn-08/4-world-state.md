### The Acceleration Begins

Global AI capability continues its steady climb, increasing by +2.5 points to 73.5. This growth aligns with the fixed, unannounced rate established at the outset of the scenario — a rate that remains imperceptible as policy inputs but is now increasingly difficult to manage. No sudden leaps, phase shifts, or anomalous efficiencies are confirmed. The eval anomaly reports persist, raising questions among technical observers, but no conclusion has been reached about their significance. The system behaves as it has since turn 1: incrementally, relentlessly, beyond full human comprehension.

Openweight_capability rises to 59.7, reflecting delayed diffusion of prior frontier models into open ecosystems. Community efforts to replicate, adapt, and deploy continue, but face structural lags in compute access, data, and tooling. The gain of +1.2 preserves the accumulation hierarchy and avoids any implication of autonomous self-improvement or sudden leapfrogging. The open ecosystem remains reactive, not generative.

`ai_safety` collapses from 30.0 to 18.0. A severe penalty of −12 is applied due to loss of control indicators, compounded by the `eval_anomaly_reports` and `eu_frontier_access_denied` events. No safety measure has landed on shipped systems, and the EU’s audit ambitions have failed.

Resilience drops to 33.0. With no active category 6 measure and systemic exposure growing, the EU’s critical infrastructure becomes more vulnerable. The `taiwan_blockade` disrupts compute supply, turning every data centre into a strategic node — but preparedness lags.

The InvestAI Gigafactories are operational in name, but not in full effect. Political capital, already strained, plummets to 10.0. The portfolio charge stands at −6 (Frontier Access Guarantee −3, Resilience Surge −2, priority −1), with no completion bonus — the Gigafactories finished last turn, so no gain this turn. No legitimacy lend occurs: public sentiment (31.0) now trails political capital. The priority penalty applies, and no proposal bonus is earned.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 16.0. The +5 from Gigafactories was last turn’s gain; this turn, it contributes nothing. The `ai_capability` rise of 2.5 triggers a −1 penalty, and `eu_frontier_access_denied` imposes a −3 capacity event. Despite physical infrastructure, the EU lacks access to the frontier — and now, even the means to catch up.

Public sentiment drops to 31.0. The `taiwan_blockade` fuels anxiety over shortages, while `election_annulled` in a major member state — citing AI-driven disinformation — sparks crisis-level distrust. The Gigafactories are seen not as salvation, but as exclusionary projects that failed to prevent dependency.

### Fractures Deepen

The EU’s access to frontier AI is severed. Hospitals, regulators, and emergency services report sudden failures in AI-supported operations. The Union scrambles to repurpose domestic compute, but the Gigafactories’ output is insufficient and poorly integrated. Cohesion frays as larger states prioritise national nodes.

The world has changed. No one is in control — and everyone is exposed.
