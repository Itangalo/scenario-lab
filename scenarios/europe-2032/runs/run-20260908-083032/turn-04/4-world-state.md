Brussels’ push for sovereign access to model weights hit resistance. Engagement with U.S. counterparts continued, but without clarity on the direction of the upcoming administration, leverage remained limited.

However, smaller states, still wary after patchy support during past cyber incidents, refused to endorse further action without enforceable access guarantees. The threat to exclude non-compliant providers from public contracts carried weight, but lacked immediate enforcement mechanisms.
