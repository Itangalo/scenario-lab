In late 2026, the European Union proposed Secure Audit Access, requiring foreign AI providers to allow EU-certified auditors inspection rights over high-risk systems as a condition of market access. Prompted by a recent cyber intrusion affecting critical infrastructure, the initiative was championed by Germany and France, with plans for evaluation nodes under strict protocols emphasizing reciprocity. However, the U.S. denounced the measure as overreach, and major AI labs resisted, threatening withdrawal from EU critical sectors. No providers complied, and no enforcement occurred, leaving the policy legally assertive but unimplemented.
