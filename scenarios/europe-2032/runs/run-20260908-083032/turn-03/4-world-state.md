### The Weight of Assurance

The EU declared victory in form, but not in fact, as *Secure Audit Access* formally concluded this turn with no provider compliance. Real-time telemetry remained out of reach. Yet, emboldened by a sudden breakthrough in interpretability — a technique now deployed across major labs that predicts model behaviour before it manifests — Brussels pivoted. It launched *Sovereign Access to Safety-Critical Weights*, a narrowly targeted legal and technical framework demanding read-only, auditable access to model weights used in EU critical infrastructure.

The offer was framed as a bargain: fast-track recognition of safety claims in exchange for weight access, stored under sovereign, air-gapped conditions. Germany and France welcomed the move, positioning themselves as first recipients of audit insights. But smaller states, still wary after patchy support during the last cyber incident, demanded enforceable guarantees. The EU responded by threatening exclusion from public contracts for any provider that refused — a lever, but a slender one.

U.S. developers remained defiant. The security hawk faction in the ongoing presidential campaign denounced the move as intellectual property seizure. Yet, the quiet persistence of the atlanticist coalition — warning that a brittle Europe undermines U.S. strategy — created space for negotiation. No agreement emerged, but the door stayed ajar.

### Capabilities Climb, Safety Leaps

Global AI capability rose to 59.5, driven by sustained release cycles and suspected autonomous research loops. However, for the first time in years, *ai_safety* rose — jumping to 38.0 — as the interpretability breakthrough was rapidly adopted across frontier labs, enabling proactive detection of dangerous reasoning patterns.

This progress lifted resilience slightly to 38.0, though systemic vulnerabilities remained. Public sentiment edged up to 44.0, as visible technical progress tempered fears of uncontrolled AI.

Yet sovereignty dipped to 18.0. Despite the new initiative, structural dependence on foreign compute deepened, and no sovereign capacity came online. The absence of tangible infrastructure gains outweighed diplomatic maneuvering.

Political capital rose to 38.0, buoyed by the completion of *Secure Audit Access* (+3 to +5 bonus) and the proposal of a credible follow-up measure, despite ongoing portfolio strain.
