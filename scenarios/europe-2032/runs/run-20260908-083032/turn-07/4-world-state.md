Global AI capability advanced to 71.0, driven by self-improving research loops and accelerated training cycles. The absence of progress caused stagnation under pressure, but not collapse. With limited interpretability gains offset by growing deployment scale, ai_safety declined modestly to 30.0 — a level of increasing fragility, but not yet systemic blindness.

### A Fragile Foundation

The EU now stands on a narrow ledge: ambitions for domestic compute are advancing, but tangible results remain constrained. The American posture of consolidation remains in place, and with the 2028 election settled, Washington’s grip on frontier access tightens. The window for strategic autonomy narrows with every capability jump — but so far, no irreversible failure has occurred to accelerate the descent.
