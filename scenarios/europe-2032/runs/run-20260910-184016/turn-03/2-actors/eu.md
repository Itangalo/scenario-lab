## Priority
EU Supply-Chain Compact, because Washington's renewed tightening makes volume licences for gigafactory sites the binding constraint on all sovereignty builds this turn, ahead of evaluation and hardening tracks.

## In practice
We push the Compact in the Trade and Foreign Affairs Councils: DG TRADE tables enforceable high-end lithography and chip diversion controls with Dutch-Japanese alignment, in explicit exchange for US volume licences for named EU gigafactory sites, while DG CNECT keeps permits and grid reservations warm so licences convert immediately to orders. The defecting bilateral member state is offered pooled licence volume as the price of rejoining common terms.

On the Shield we enforce via NIS2: ENISA's binding detection baseline for TSOs, ports and water utilities, joint procurement kits rushed to ports/water after the French-German exercise gaps, and CEF-Energy/Digital Europe reprogramming held. The new Evaluation Cell is seeded from Horizon Europe and Digital Europe in JRC/ENISA without reopening the compute budget, and Eurofound monitoring of office productivity gains continues to hold off a transition fund while employment holds.
