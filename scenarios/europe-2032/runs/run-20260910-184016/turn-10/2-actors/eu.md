## New measure
**EU Cutoff Survival and Chokepoint Defence**
Keeps hospitals, ministries and telecoms running on EU-controlled and open-weight fallback stacks after the US frontier cutoff, while triggering the Anti-Coercion Instrument over the forced ASML servicing cut.
Why this and why now: the cutoff and Washington using ASML against us in the same turn makes dependence the immediate crisis — services built on a revoked model plus loss of our one chokepoint — so defence of supply leverage and a fallback must outrank even the transition guarantee for whether the Union functions at all.

## Priority
EU Cutoff Survival and Chokepoint Defence, because hospitals and ministries dark after cutoff and ASML being turned against us now outrank disbursement of the transition guarantee for whether the Union can act at all.

## In practice
We execute the cutoff triage through DG CNECT, DG SANTE and ENISA: mandatory fallback lists for hospitals/ministries/telecoms to EU-hosted and open-weight models already on EU hardware, rationed inference from remaining domestic compute, with HERA and the Continuity Stack playbooks covering manual operation where fallback degrades. No new gigafactory promises; we tell capitals the empty shells stay grid-ready while we keep what runs alive.

We answer coercion through DG TRADE and the Council: open Anti-Coercion Instrument examination on the extraterritorial ASML servicing ban, single EU servicing licence channel maintained, joint mandate that no member state signs a separate Washington supply deal, and outreach to Japan and other lithography-adjacent holders for a middle-power servicing coalition. DG EMPL keeps M11 running via ESF+ vouchers and SME subsidies while the levy talks pause, to avoid a second front with employers during the cutoff.
