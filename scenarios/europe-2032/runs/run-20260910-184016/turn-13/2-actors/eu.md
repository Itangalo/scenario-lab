## In practice
We run this turn through DG TRADE, DG CNECT and ENISA under the Anti-Coercion logic already tested: Council Foreign Affairs and Telecom formations mandate a joint restoration pool — clean images, rationed inference, telecom spares — with Japan, Korea and other lithography and telecom holders, plus mutual recognition of incident reports to shorten attribution. No bilateral spares deals; in return capitals get prioritized restoration slots and joint bargaining on servicing.

At home we do triage without new money. On the cyber sweep we activate the finished Shield and Cyber Absorb playbooks: mandatory operator reporting, isolation to paper/manual fallbacks in health and public services, ENISA-coordinated patching of the model-generated tooling. On the research breakthrough we record it as a non-EU advance with no near-term EU industrial use given empty shells, so no sentiment claim. We disburse wage-insurance slowly and publicise only delivered waiting-list and continuity gains to stop further capital bleed.
