## Priority
EU Cyber Absorb and Patch Surge, because the large automated attack on public services outranks bargaining timelines — without services kept running through it, no chokepoint deal holds.

## In practice
We put ENISA and the Critical Infrastructure Shield teams forward as the visible front: rescEU and Digital Europe funds to push automated patching and swarm-detection into small hospitals, water utilities and municipalities left exposed by the rapid migration to EU-hosted models, with HERA/ECDC reporting lines kept open and mandatory incident reporting enforced via the Health and Telecoms Councils. Cross-border CERT mutual aid is activated for the worst-hit member states, and the Frontier Evaluation Cell triages whether model-generated tooling drove the sweep.

We keep the Chokepoint Bargain running without new spend: Commission holds the single ASML servicing position and Anti-Coercion examination, DG TRADE rations remaining stock and grid to care and telecoms, and outreach to Japan and South Korea trades coordinated lithography terms for maintenance assurance. Unions and youth anger are met with on-site staffing relief tied to the patch surge, not new promises, to stop walkouts merging with protest while containment is proved.
