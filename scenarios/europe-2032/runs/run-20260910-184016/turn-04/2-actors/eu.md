## New measure
**EU Continuity Stack**
Replaces cut-off US frontier models in hospitals, ministries and critical firms with EU-hosted open-weight systems on pooled European compute with fast procurement and migration support. Chosen now because the short-notice denial turns dependence into immediate service failure, and the visible public-sector adoption win gives a European alternative to rally around while licences remain blocked.

## Priority
EU Continuity Stack, because the access cutoff outranks the licence negotiation this turn — hospitals and ministries offline cannot wait for Washington process while pooled substitution can restore service now.
## In practice
We trigger the Health Emergency Preparedness and Digital Europe joint procurement: DG CNECT and HERA mandate that affected public buyers switch to the Continuity Stack — hardened open-weight models hosted in EuroHPC and sovereign cloud nodes — with ENISA security sign-off from the Evaluation Cell and migration teams funded from reprogrammed Digital Europe money. The adoption-success sites become demonstrators, with waiting-list and turnaround metrics published to turn outrage into foresight rewarded.

On the books we hold course: DG TRADE keeps the Supply-Chain Compact on the table in Trade/Foreign Affairs Councils linking lithography/diversion enforcement to volume licences, but without new concessions while denial stands; the defecting member state is told pooled continuity capacity plus a share of any future licence volume is conditional on returning to common procurement terms. Gigafactory permits and grid reservations stay warm, and ENISA's detection baseline enforcement on ports and water utilities continues with joint-procured kits, to prevent the substitution push from opening a new attack surface.
