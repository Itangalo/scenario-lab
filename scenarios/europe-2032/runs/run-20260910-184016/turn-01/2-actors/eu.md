## New measure
**EU Critical Infrastructure Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water against AI-enabled intrusion with mandatory detection upgrades and cross-border live exercises run through ENISA and the Electricity Coordination Group. Chosen because the autumn grid intrusion proved detection would not have caught weeks-long presence, and because sovereignty without survivable infrastructure is hollow.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Shield, because the intrusion is ongoing in political effect and member-state defection means only a visible collective defence restores credibility, ahead of longer-term compute build-out.

## In practice
We task ENISA with a binding detection baseline for TSOs, major ports and water utilities under the NIS2 implementing act, funded by reprogrammed Connecting Europe Facility and Digital Europe money, with ACER and the TTE Council formation driving adoption. France and Germany's TSOs lead the first live-fire exercise in Q4, explicitly to pull the defecting member state back into a joint procurement of sensor and AI-anomaly tooling.

The InvestAI Gigafactories and tech sovereignty package continue on their tracks: DG CNECT holds accelerated-permitting zones and grid-connection reservations, while we use ASML leverage quietly to deter further bilateral undercutting. We frame the Shield as the near-term deliverable that buys political space for the multi-year compute spend.
