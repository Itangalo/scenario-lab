## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Shield, because weeks-long undetected grid presence plus the bilateral side-deal means collective detection must visibly land first, ahead of the longer compute and export-control tracks.

## In practice
We push the Shield through the NIS2 implementing act: ENISA issues the binding detection baseline for transmission operators, large ports and water utilities, DG ENER reprograms CEF-Energy and Digital Europe funds for joint procurement of anomaly-detection kits, and the French-German TSO live exercise in Q2 is used to reabsorb the defecting member state with a co-funded sensor order. ACER and the TTE Council own compliance.

On the Compact, DG TRADE and the Foreign Affairs Council table aligned high-end lithography and AI-chip enforcement in exchange for US volume licences for EU gigafactory sites, coordinating with the Netherlands and Japan in the existing export-control forum. InvestAI Gigafactories and the tech sovereignty package stay on track in DG CNECT: permits and grid reservations advance, no new parallel compute vehicle is created. On knowledge-work gains, we task Eurofound and DG EMPL with monitoring only — no transition fund yet while employment holds — to conserve capital.
