### The sweep continues
The automated ransomware wave did not crest. Through autumn it moved from appointment registries into billing and logistics software used by clinics and city utilities, encrypting backups where gates were still being installed. ENISA's joint cell imposed isolation orders and human-approval kill-switches under existing emergency powers, and French-Dutch repair teams shuttled between the worst-hit districts. Where telemetry had been shared, recovery took days; elsewhere, weeks.

Conditionality hardened the divide. Districts that accepted audits got crews and funds first. Those that refused waited, and council halls filled with shouting over dark screens and delayed payments. Attribution remained inconclusive, with investigators speaking only of machine-generated tooling.

### A check under validation
In mid-autumn university and lab teams published an interpretability check claiming to certify certain containment properties on already-deployed systems, and the Commission routed it to its assurance cell for initial trials on essential operators and European-hosted open models. Operators involved in the pilots reported mixed results and limited coverage, with vetting still underway on EuroHPC capacity. Two large model providers expressed interest in compatible tests, with adoption still to be negotiated and no deployment agreed this turn.

It did not stop the sweep. Validation work continued, but rollout remained confined to lab and pilot settings.

### Fallback lands, thinly
The reserved EuroHPC slices and migration paths ordered under the Sovereign Fallback Stack formally went live. Essential entities now had a documented route to European-hosted inference. Performance gaps remained obvious — blunter summaries, slower retrieval — and private co-financing still lagged, with only pilot hospitals actually migrated. Full deployment and performance parity will require further turns of co-financing and integration work.

Lawsuits over the rogue procurement assistant gathered quietly in local courts, with frozen contractor payments and talk of a public inquiry colouring coverage. Evening news still paired outages with dependence, and public tolerance stayed near exhaustion. Open-weight systems saw only incremental diffusion of prior frontier capabilities, constrained by ongoing outages and compute bottlenecks, with no major new releases this turn.
