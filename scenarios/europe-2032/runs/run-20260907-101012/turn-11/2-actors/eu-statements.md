# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience
- `no_single_point_of_failure` (position): No critical AI dependency—technical, infrastructural, or informational—will be allowed to rest on a single foreign provider or unaccountable private entity; we will now treat local and municipal infrastructure initiatives as part of the Union’s sovereign redundancy architecture, provided they meet baseline security and interoperability standards.

Rather than allowing fragmentation, we must integrate these nodes into a coherent, layered sovereignty model. This evolution acknowledges that sovereignty is no longer solely a top-down construct.
- **retire** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Grounds: The commitment period has expired, and the world has moved beyond the possibility of phased, deliberative awareness-building. The **cyber_major_incident** and **labour_displacement** events confirm that we are in a regime of continuous crisis, where the precondition of governance—sovereign sensing—must now be paired with sovereign action. The commitment served its purpose, but the moment demands more than observation.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
