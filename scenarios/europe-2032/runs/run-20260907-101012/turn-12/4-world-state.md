### The Fracture Widens

AI capability advances to 94.0, driven by recursive self-improvement in planning and validation systems. At this level, measurement frays further—increments are halved, but motion continues. Open-weight models rise to 71.0, as older frontier techniques diffuse into distributed ecosystems, enabling more sophisticated misuse.

A major cyberattack, generated and orchestrated by AI, cripples health and transport systems across three member states. Defenders are overwhelmed; recovery takes weeks. Simultaneously, job losses accelerate in law, accounting, and public administration as firms replace entry-level roles with autonomous agents. Public anger mounts.

The EU responds by formalising the Resilience Stack—a grassroots suite of hardened AI tools developed by technical institutes and municipal IT departments—into policy. A delegated act under the Cyber Resilience Act mandates its adoption in essential services, unlocking funding and tying procurement to compliance. The move is praised for pragmatism, but implementation is patchy: some cities resist central oversight, while others lack resources to certify.

The Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory finishes deployment, but its impact is muted. Despite real-time telemetry, it fails to prevent the cyberattack, underscoring the limits of observation without control. Meanwhile, the Emergency Compute Redundancy Initiative progresses slowly, with procurement delays despite fast-tracking.

Political capital collapses further. The Resilience Stack Integration Directive, a large measure, adds −3; the priority designation costs −1. No proposal bonus applies—though the measure answers recent shocks, it is too soon to count gains. The portfolio now bears five active burdens, including the new directive. With no offsetting sources, political capital falls to −15.0.

Sovereignty drops to 12.0: last turn’s 13.0 minus 1 for capability growth (≥2) and no offsetting event. The Resilience Stack strengthens software foundations, but does not yet anchor execution. Resilience rises slightly to 47.0, as the Stack begins to harden defences. Safety plummets to −12.0, as opaque reasoning and capability jumps outpace safeguards. Public sentiment remains at rock bottom, 15.0, as citizens see institutions still failing to protect jobs or services.
