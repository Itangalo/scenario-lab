# Statements: The European Union (turn 12)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience
- `no_single_point_of_failure` (commitment): No critical AI dependency—technical, infrastructural, or informational—will be allowed to rest on a single foreign provider or unaccountable private entity; we will now treat local and municipal infrastructure initiatives as part of the Union’s sovereign redundancy architecture, provided they meet baseline security and interoperability standards.
- `sovereign_execution_as_core` (commitment): We will treat sovereign execution — the ability to run, validate, and fallback AI-driven decisions on infrastructure under EU legal and physical control — as the foundational requirement for both resilience and self-determination, and will subordinate all other AI policy to its establishment.
