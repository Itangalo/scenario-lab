By late 2032 EU cohesion held without unfreezing or funding: no new instrument, no permits unblocked, grid redundancy held at higher cost after interconnector sabotage reframed dispute as energy security.

Autumn brought dual shock: seizures of mirrored frontier model used with contested genome methods for pathogen assays in clinics/lab sparked bio-risk scare and demands for clarity, while tailored therapies for two untreatable conditions and shorter showcase waits restored faith in care.

Commission fused both into patient-safety instrument — HERA, disease centre and municipal IT sharing audit flags, judicial seizure review, triage guidance, with new therapies routed via European wards with published outcomes — passing Council without reopening siting fight.

Delivery partial: flagging uneven amid understaffing and rector refusals; therapy gains concentrated in showcases, delays elsewhere; downloads of open frontier models unimpeded. Fear eased where cures arrived, deepened where seizures occurred.
