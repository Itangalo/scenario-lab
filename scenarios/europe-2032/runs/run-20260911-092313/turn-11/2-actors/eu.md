## New measure
None this turn, while political capital is at 1 and sabotage has turned the freeze sites into an energy-security crime scene.

With valuations collapsing, a contested human-infecting bio design in circulation, frontier open weights already mirrored, and coordinated sabotage of the interconnectors feeding both frozen builds, any new funded instrument tabled now would fail on siting, on money and on enforcement, and would spend capital we do not have. Holding the two-year commitment — survive the cut-off and the open frontier by hardening health, bio and cyber resilience first while negotiating water-power compacts to unfreeze sovereign builds — means using this semester to investigate, to keep hospitals and cyber defences standing on existing mandates, and to let capital recover enough to fund the next buildable step.

The investment collapse is met with no new pledge: the Commission banks the pause in build expectations to de-escalate vigil pressure and prepares a fundable resilience step for when capital allows.
