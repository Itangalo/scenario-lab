By end-2031 EU cohesion held on paper but nothing unfroze or was funded: Brussels started no new instrument amid capital-market retreat from AI and refusal to challenge injunctions during investigations. Coordinated sabotage of interconnectors to the two frozen factory/data-centre sites cut power for days, shifting dispute from water to energy security; redundancy feeds and guarded repairs kept freeze in place amid vigils and demands for substation protection. Investment collapse cancelled build-outs and evaporated commercial compute arrangements; Washington offered only sympathy with no frontier-access restoration, access pact held as exhortation. Spring shocks deepened: genome-model paper became methods war with ignored monitoring guidance, open frontier-capable download spread to hundreds of thousands of machines as standard kit despite bans. Hospitals survived on maintenance for continuity stack and triage helpers — showcases kept shorter waits and EU-fix narrative, elsewhere queues grew. Bright note: measured productivity gains in law, accountancy, administration, journalism, concentrated among juniors, with no job losses.
