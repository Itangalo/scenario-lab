By mid-2032 EU cohesion held on paper but nothing unfroze or was funded: Brussels started no new instrument amid capital-market retreat from AI and refusal to challenge injunctions. Coordinated sabotage of interconnectors to the two frozen sites cut power for days, reframing dispute from water to energy security; redundancy feeds and guarded repairs held through winter-spring at higher cost amid vigils and demands for permanent substation protection, with no permits unblocked. Investment collapse cancelled build-outs and evaporated commercial compute; Washington offered only sympathy, access pact remained exhortation. Genome-model methods war and spread of open frontier-capable download to hundreds of thousands of machines persisted despite bans; agencies managed on monitoring notes and triage updates. Hospitals survived on maintenance — showcases kept shorter waits and EU-fix narrative, elsewhere queues grew. Matched studies in three large states confirmed productivity gains in law, accountancy, administration, journalism, strongest among juniors, employment steady, easing fear but deepening lender markdown and investment retreat. Commission closed two-year pledge to survive cut-off, promising next phase on health, energy and information repair before any capacity push.
