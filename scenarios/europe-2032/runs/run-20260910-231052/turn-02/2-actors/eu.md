## New measure
**EU Grid and Critical Services Hardening Pact**

This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and why now is because autumn audits proved intruders lived inside networks for weeks and export controls will not recall the open models that enabled the tooling.

## Priority
EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute, because the social-insurance / policing scandal has put AI Act enforcement on trial and only independent pre-deployment testing can restore credibility before hardening money flows.

## In practice
We push the Evaluation Institute through the AI Office and JRC this turn, staffing vetted evaluators and starting pilot audits on high-risk Annex III systems. We read the automated-decision scandal the first way: the system was high-risk, conformity passed on paper, human oversight collapsed to forty-seconds-a-case sign-off and logs were never read — an enforcement failure, not a drafting failure — and we task the Institute with closing exactly that gap.

We launch the Hardening Pact via DG ENER and ENISA with the Council Energy and TTE formations, directing NIS2 funds and Connecting Europe Facility money to segmentation, OT monitoring and joint exercises with the two hit grid operators, the port and water utility as first sites. On export controls we seek allied volume-licence access from Washington while coordinating ASML leverage, refusing rationing of allies, and we keep Gigafactories site selection and permitting zones moving with EIB bridging to hold cohesion support.
