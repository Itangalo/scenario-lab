### A Push for Safety Authority Amid Mounting Pressures

The European Union launched the **European AI Safety Institute (EASI)** as its new priority, aiming to establish binding safety oversight over frontier AI labs operating in or serving the EU. Positioned as a direct institutional response to the emergent_lab_fracture, EASI was designed to mandate pre-deployment evaluations, enforce interpretability audits, and serve as a transnational enforcement node. The Commission secured €4.2bn in initial funding and began staffing in Brussels, with plans to relocate to The Hague.

However, early momentum met resistance. Germany and the Netherlands remained hesitant, demanding veto rights over audit disclosures and refusing full alignment with the Talent Shield Initiative. Poland and Hungary welcomed the anti-US framing but questioned EASI’s supranational reach, delaying formal endorsement. US-based labs dismissed the mandate, arguing jurisdictional overreach, while only one European lab agreed to early compliance—conditioned on reciprocal data access.

Meanwhile, **verification_widens** accelerated capability growth: automated contract review and clinical coding became reliably verifiable, pushing `ai_capability` up by 1.5 points to 56.0. No corresponding safety improvements landed on shipped systems, but the *proposal* of EASI—a large category 5 measure directly addressing recent systemic failures—earned a modest proposal bonus under political capital rules.

Resilience rose slightly (+2) as partial rollouts of the Cyber Resilience Directive improved detection in energy grids. Openweight capability edged up as older models diffused further.

Yet sovereignty declined: `ai_capability` rose over 2 points, triggering the −1 decay term, and no in-flight category 4 measure delivered tangible gains this turn. Political capital fell sharply due to ongoing portfolio strain and incomplete delivery. The new large measure added −3, four large measures in flight cost −12 total, and the named priority subtracted −1. The proposal bonus (+4) offset some loss, but capital still dropped to 39.0.

Public sentiment held steady—no major incident or scandal shifted perception.

### Fractured Unity, Fragile Gains

EASI emerged not as a breakthrough, but as a contested experiment in authority. Its success remains conditional on battles yet unwon.
