## In practice
We are activating the Biological Threat Coordination Framework through the Health Security Committee, with a draft regulation tabled in the Council’s Public Health configuration. The legal basis is Article 168 TFEU, augmented by the new emergency clause invoked after the bio_incident. The framework mandates that all high-containment labs (BSL-3 and BSL-4) integrate with a central AI-powered anomaly detection layer, fed by synthesis order telemetry, sequencing data, and cross-border movement patterns. It establishes a standing European Bio-Incident Task Force, co-located with ECDC and empowered to deploy within 72 hours, with binding authority to collect samples and audit lab logs. We are offering reluctant members — particularly Italy and Spain — phased implementation and co-design of national interfaces, while conditioning cohesion funding from the Horizon Resilience Facility on full participation.

At the same time, we are leveraging the joint_threat_response to expand this beyond EU borders. Our delegation in Geneva is negotiating reciprocal sample-sharing and joint forensic protocols with WHO and key middle powers, using the emergent_model_verification_pact as a trust anchor. Japan and South Korea have signaled willingness to align their export controls on dual-use sequences with our flagged signatures. We are also pressing the US CDC to join as an observer, not because we trust their oversight, but because their intelligence reach is unmatched — and because inclusion dilutes their ability to block access later. Poland remains resistant, citing sovereignty concerns, but the recent exposure of a covert lab in Lublin — traced via joint_threat_response telemetry — has weakened their argument. We are offering them leadership of the Eastern Node of the detection network as a concession. The climate_prediction_breakthrough narrative is fading, but the fear from the bio_incident is still malleable: we are framing this not as surveillance, but as immunisation — a system that learns from every breach so the next one doesn’t happen.
