### Talent War Escalates

The EU launched the **Sovereign Research Talent Compact**, a large category 4 measure aimed at countering a sharp exodus of AI scientists to defence agencies in Germany and France. With salaries tripled and oversight minimal in classified programs, civilian labs face collapse. The Compact offers competitive pay, publication rights, and EU-level prestige through Horizon Europe, backed by audit mandates and cross-border mobility. Germany, the Netherlands, and Finland expressed early support, though Poland and Spain remain sceptical.

Despite high ambition, the initiative deepened political strain. The European Parliament’s budget committee blocked full disbursement, citing fiscal overreach, forcing reliance on partial repurposing of existing funds. Implementation began in pilot form, but at reduced scale—yielding no immediate sovereignty gain. The civil-military divide widened, with French defence officials accusing the EU of “undermining national security cohesion.”

AI capability advanced to **63.0**, driven by efficiency gains in training pipelines. Open-weight models rose to **55.0**, as older architectures leaked into public repositories. Yet with no safety mechanisms deployed, **ai_safety** remained stagnant at **10.0**.

The **Biological Threat Coordination Framework** progressed slowly. Technical integration advanced in six member states, but Italian and Spanish regulators delayed data-sharing APIs over sovereignty concerns. Poland, exposed by the Lublin lab incident, remained outside the framework.

Political capital eroded to **14.0** under sustained portfolio costs and low capacity. The new Compact, though urgent, failed to trigger a full proposal bonus due to partial funding rejection. Public sentiment dipped to **30.0**, as media framed the talent battle as a sign of fragmentation, amplifying anxiety over militarisation and job insecurity.

With no category 4 completion and capability growth continuing, **eu_ai_sovereignty** fell to **15.0**, reflecting both external constraints and internal disarray.
