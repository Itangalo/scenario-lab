# Statements: The European Union (turn 7)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): We will build sovereign capacity to act independently before the window closes, and we now treat access to operational frontier models as a core component of that sovereignty — without it, no evaluation, no defence, no independent policy is possible.
- `defend_critical_systems_now` (position): We prioritise immediate protection of healthcare, government, and infrastructure systems against AI-generated attacks, even if it requires temporary centralisation of decision rights or emergency overrides of national opt-outs.
