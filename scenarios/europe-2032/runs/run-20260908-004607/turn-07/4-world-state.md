### A Foundation Activated, Oversight Asserted

The **InvestAI Gigafactories** programme officially concluded this turn, bringing online partial compute capacity in Västerås, Charleroi, and Budapest. Though delayed in southern Italy and Romania, the project secured legal title to critical infrastructure and delivered on its core promise: anchoring EU-controlled compute for public AI use. This completion contributes a one-time boost to sovereignty, reflecting the new baseline of EU-held infrastructure now operational.

Simultaneously, the EU elevated **EMASI** to priority status, accelerating integration of sovereign inference clusters in Luxembourg, Finland, and Bulgaria. These now run production-grade models mirrored from Israel, Japan, and Singapore under full EU legal jurisdiction. However, political unity frayed at the edges — while France and Germany upheld their commitments, Poland raised concerns over audit intrusiveness, slowing full operationalization. As a result, EMASI continues to accrue momentum but has not yet delivered additional sovereignty gains beyond those already locked in from prior progress.

To close the gap between possession and control, the Commission proposed **Accelerated Sovereign Evaluation Access**, a small Category 5 measure using a delegated act under the AI Act. However, due to the prioritization of EMASI this turn, no new measure can take effect concurrently. The proposal remains in early drafting, with only initial technical scoping completed. Logging APIs remain disabled across all clusters pending further coordination, and no implementation has begun.

Abroad, the US **CONSOLIDATION** posture tightened access tiers, reinforcing Europe’s incentive to deepen alternative partnerships. Informal coordination with South Korea and Taiwan on export controls advanced, but no binding pact emerged. Meanwhile, open-weight models continue to advance in capability, with recent demonstrations in protein design drawing attention to dual-use concerns. These developments are being monitored, but no threshold breach has been confirmed through formal evaluation.

Public sentiment held steady despite ongoing job displacement anxieties. No major incident occurred, but defensive AI agents deployed under NIS2 emergency powers generated high false-positive rates, straining municipal IT teams and drawing quiet criticism.

### Middle Powers Seek Leverage

A quiet summit in The Hague laid groundwork for a mutual evaluation-access proposal: shared safety audits in exchange for aligned export controls on semiconductor tools. Trust remains thin, but momentum builds among non-US developers seeking strategic relevance.
