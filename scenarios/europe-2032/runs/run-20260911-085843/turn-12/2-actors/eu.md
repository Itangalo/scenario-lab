## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee**
Provides wage insurance, paid retraining places and hiring incentives targeted at displaced entry-level roles in law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration, funded via ESF+ reallocation and employer levy on automating firms.
Category 7 Labour protection is why now: graduate hiring freeze is collapsing public tolerance (sentiment 19, capital 13) and without visible absorption the resilience built in hospitals and grids will be politically unsustainable.

## Priority
M12 – EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee, because labour displacement now outranks containment as the binding constraint on any further action while capital is at 13.

## In practice
We act under Articles 162-164 ESF+ and Article 175 cohesion, via EPSCO Council. Commission reprograms uncommitted ESF+ and Just Transition envelopes into a dedicated entry-level guarantee, topped by a time-limited transition contribution from large employers reporting net headcount reduction alongside AI deployment. Public employment services in the five named sectors get funded retraining slots and 12-month wage-insurance for re-hires.

We hold existing routines without new spend: HERA stockpile replenishment and ECDC tracing pilots continue on current authorisations, municipal patch teams stay on maintenance funding, and no factory or export fight is reopened. Ministers are asked to sell only jobs and retraining places this semester, to stop the slide from 19 sentiment to physical opposition before it blocks grids, siting and any future build.
