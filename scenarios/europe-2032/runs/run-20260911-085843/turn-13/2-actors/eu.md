## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for the Transition Guarantee to staff and for capital to recover above single digits before opening any new front.

With political capital at 11 and public sentiment at 13, adding a second instrument now would stall both: M12 already costs 2 per turn, and a new build or export fight cannot be legislated, funded and enforced while two finance ministries contest the legal base and a member state is openly undercutting the common line. The export-control escalation and the defection have to be answered inside existing mandates this semester, not with a new measure we cannot carry.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
M12 – EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee, because labour displacement is now the binding constraint on cohesion and any leverage play collapses if entry-level hiring does not visibly restart.

## In practice
We put the whole semester into making the guarantee real in the five hit sectors. DG EMPL with EPSCO accelerates ESF+ reprogramming, funds PES retraining slots and 12-month wage-insurance for re-hires in law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration, and we negotiate the employer transition contribution as a time-limited solidarity levy rather than a permanent tax to blunt employer opposition and the legal-base challenge. Ministers are told to deliver places, not communiqués.

On export controls and the defection we hold, not escalate. We keep volume-licence access where Washington still offers it, refuse a parallel EU rationing regime we cannot enforce, and treat the defecting bilateral as a cohesion case: Trade and COMPET Council, Anti-Coercion Instrument on the table but not triggered, offer to fold the hyperscaler/Beijing terms into an EU-anchored capacity bolt. Patch teams, grid maintenance, HERA stockpiles and ECDC tracing continue on current authorisations with no new factory or export instrument opened.
