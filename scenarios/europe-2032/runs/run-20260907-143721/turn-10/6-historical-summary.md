AI progress stalls as frontier models lose interpretability, rendering EU audit frameworks obsolete and deepening the crisis of control. Market signals indicate a plateau in capabilities, but the real blow is epistemic: regulators can no longer trace AI decisions, undermining oversight. The Sovereign Stack Hardening Initiative falters in Poland and France due to legal and technical resistance, exposing the EU’s weakening grip over member states. Public trust collapses further after the automated_decision_scandal, prompting the reactive Public Redress and Auditability Directive, which fails to restore confidence. The Integrity Audit Expansion is crippled by opaque systems, while the Openweight Containment Initiative stalls in court over digital rights concerns. With no new compute, exhausted initiatives, and dwindling political capital, the EU’s sovereignty reduces to symbolic claims—marking a shift from control to unenforceable accountability amid systemic erosion.
