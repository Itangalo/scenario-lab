# Statements: The European Union (turn 12)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Restore public and institutional trust in sovereign AI systems by ensuring auditable decision-making, enforceable redress, and resilience to both technical compromise and operational misuse
- `constitutional_integrity_first` (commitment): Preserve the Union’s authority to act collectively in AI governance, by resolving the emergent sovereignty crisis through binding legal clarity, shared enforcement mechanisms, and the restoration of member-state confidence in EU-level oversight
- `last_stand_capacity` (commitment): We will use the final instruments still under our direct control—publicly owned infrastructure, in-house technical teams, and sovereign compute promises—to demonstrate that the EU can still operate independently, even if it can no longer govern collectively

Worse, the public no longer distinguishes between sovereign and foreign AI—they see only failure, and they are responding with resistance. We are not restoring trust. This is not governance. It is salvage. But if we cannot show that the EU can still *do*, then we have nothing.
