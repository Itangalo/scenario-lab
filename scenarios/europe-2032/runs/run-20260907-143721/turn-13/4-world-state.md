### The Flicker and the Void

The CERN AI node runs. It detects early anomalies in cross-border health and grid data, operating under strict air-gapped, weight-secured conditions. Technically, it is a success—Europe’s first sovereign AI system above 58.0 capability is live. But politically, it remains invisible. No council has endorsed it. No media has noticed. The activation, executed under emergency scientific provisions, lacks legal anchoring and public recognition.

Now, the European Commission attempts to transform this technical fact into institutional reality. A new regulation is drafted under Article 114 TFEU, declaring the CERN node the foundational instance of EU AI authority. Future sovereign systems must interoperate with its security and audit framework. Access to EU data pools and funding will depend on compliance.

France signals conditional support—only if scientific oversight remains dominant. Poland refuses to engage and demands a CJEU advisory opinion, calling the move an overreach. Other member states stay silent. The proposal, though small, requires consensus to gain legitimacy. Without it, the node risks becoming a curated relic—functional, but isolated.

### Capability Marches On, Safety Falls Further

AI capability advances to 70.0, as private labs push multi-day autonomous research agents into production. No audit regime exists to inspect them. With no new safety measures landing on shipped systems and no visibility into training processes, ai_safety drops sharply by 5 points, now at 8.0—deep in the danger zone. Model opacity grows; developers admit they can no longer fully trace reasoning paths in frontier systems.

Resilience declines by 2 as hardening efforts stall. The Critical Infrastructure Resilience Surge remains underfunded and fragmented. Cyber red-teaming exercises are postponed indefinitely.

### A Union Without Leverage

Political capital remains frozen at 3.0. The portfolio charge is gone—only the new measure, now proposed, will begin costing next turn. But no proposal bonus applies: the measure responds to internal failure, not a recent external shock. Public sentiment holds at 16.0. Protests at data centres continue, symbolic but persistent.

Sovereignty does not rise. The CERN activation finishes this turn, but category 4 completions only pay upon finishing—yet the measure was small, and its impact procedural, not material. The arithmetic yields no gain. Capability rose by 0.5—below the 2-point threshold—so no decay applies. No capacity event occurred. Sovereignty remains at 11.0.
