### The Fracture Widens

The EU enters 2031’s final months not with strategy, but triage. The **emergent_sovereignty_crisis** has crystallized into open defiance: Poland and France formally suspend implementation of the Integrity Audit Expansion, citing constitutional overreach. The European Court of Justice has agreed to hear the challenge, but no one expects a ruling before 2033 — too late to restore authority now. The Commission responds with the **Treaty Protocol on Union AI Authority**, a bold attempt to reclaim legitimacy through legal reinforcement. Yet early signals from member states are bleak. The offer of co-governance is met with suspicion; the demand for enforcement powers, with resistance. The informal European Council in December yields no commitments, only prolonged debate.

Meanwhile, the Integrity Audit Expansion — the Union’s last operational bid for oversight — collapses under technical irrelevance. The new proxy audit methods, developed in haste with German red teams, fail validation. Without traceable reasoning, even anomaly detection proves unreliable. The EAAD delays its planned compliance rulings, citing “methodological uncertainty.” Publicly, the agency insists progress is being made. Privately, officials admit the audit regime is blind.

The Openweight Containment Initiative, already stalled in court, is quietly repurposed into a voluntary certification scheme. It is a retreat masked as adaptation, offering grants to compliant cloud providers — a gesture with little teeth. No major host signs on.

Despite multiple large initiatives finishing this turn — Gigafactories, the Tech Sovereignty Package, the Sovereign AI Core Programme — none deliver tangible capacity. The promised compute remains offline, the stack unpopulated. Sovereignty stays symbolic. Political capital, already at 3.0, cannot absorb the portfolio’s ongoing cost. The new Treaty Protocol, a large measure, adds −3 to the charge, but no capital remains to pay it. The measure does not start.

Public sentiment, at 16.0, reflects a population no longer waiting for fixes. Physical protests intensify at data centre sites in Germany and Italy. Police respond, but the optics favor the demonstrators. The narrative is clear: the EU can neither control AI nor command its own institutions.

### Paper Authority, Failing Tools

The Union still acts — but its actions land in a void. The tools are broken, the trust gone, the capital spent.
