The EU’s authority fractures further in 2031 as Poland and France suspend the Integrity Audit Expansion, challenging its legality before a delayed European Court of Justice. The Commission’s Treaty Protocol on Union AI Authority fails to gain support, collapsing under member state resistance and unpayable political capital costs. The Integrity Audit Expansion itself becomes operationally meaningless, its proxy methods invalidated by untraceable AI systems, leaving oversight blind. The Openweight Containment Initiative is downgraded to a toothless voluntary scheme, ignored by major providers. Despite completion of key initiatives—Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty Package, Sovereign AI Core—no functional compute emerges, rendering sovereignty symbolic. With political capital exhausted at 3.0 and public trust at 16.0, protests erupt at data centres across Germany and Italy, underscoring the EU’s loss of control and legitimacy. Actions continue, but without credibility, capacity, or compliance, the Union governs in name only.
