## Priority
M13 EU Accession Track to the US-China Risk Accord — because only kept leverage in the pact and US channel can turn triage into sparing, slots and attribution, and it directly serves the two-year commitment to hold cohesion while containing superhuman harm.

## In practice
We run the triage through ENISA, CERT-EU and DG DIGIT with member-state CSIRTs, under the Critical Services Shield playbook: isolate affected municipal and health IT, restore from guarded backups, military-guarded repair at cable depots and landing station, and formal hospital power/compute priority extended to clean clinical networks. Funding from Digital Europe and rescEU, with mayors given warehouse and spare priority tied to site security.

We pair cyber triage with a health bridge: DG SANTE and HERA negotiate pooled medical-inference slots and licensed production of the breakthrough therapies via the US State channel and M13 verification offer, distributing by clinical need not bidding to prevent mayoral fights. JRC logs eval anomaly reports from the leak and feeds observer visits, so Europe enters accession talks as contributor, not supplicant, while bridge pay and retraining hold graduates through the hiring freeze.
