War between US and China struck orbital, undersea, fab, cable and datacentre infrastructure; Europe as non-belligerent terrain suffered hits to Atlantic cable station, Low Countries cleanroom supplier and southern compute interconnectors. Foreign-set allocation queues tightened, therapy scheduling and medical parts stalled, pooled bargaining club shifted to spares.

Union launched emergency protection for fabs, cables, grid and hospital compute with guarded repairs, priority dispatch and cross-border failover from existing envelopes, keeping triage and command nets alive but not restoring supply. Arson spread to gigafactory grid works halting construction, prompting military-guarded repairs; graduate/logistics guarantee finally paid bridge pay and reopened some warehouses, but entry-level white-collar hiring kept collapsing. An openly downloadable near-frontier model spread to hundreds of thousands of machines, unrecallable. By June clinics rationed doses hourly as Brussels pursued neutrality-plus-supply diplomacy offering landing/evaluation neutrality for sparing European sites.
