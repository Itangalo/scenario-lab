### Licences become permissions
Autumn brought the news Brussels had feared through lawyers and hoped through diplomats it would not come: Washington placed its frontier laboratories under direct state control. Security officers, equity stakes, classification orders — the commercial counterparties Europe knew how to regulate, fine and bargain with became arms of another government's security apparatus.

Overnight, allied licences read less like contracts and more like permissions. Volumes held on paper but queues lengthened, prices for therapy doses and wafers rose again, and hospital managers learned to call the American embassy chain as well as the supplier.

At the same time extended manoeuvres in the Taiwan Strait lifted shipping insurance and revived blockade talk. Nothing stopped, but everything felt insurable only at a price.

### A shield finished, a bargain opened
The joint cyber shield formally acceded this half-year. Telemetry through the single hub, the joint attribution cell, clean backups finally reaching paper towns — ransomware receded further in large cities, though smaller municipalities still reported long restores. Essentials held.

Brussels anchored cohesion on the certified therapy rollout. Emergency certification, collective procurement through the health emergency authority, dispensing only where verified checks were applied, plain-language safety sheets from university evaluators. Recoveries continued in big-city hospitals; mayors traded telemetry hosting for therapy slots and backup restoration. It half-worked: visible hope where doses arrived, visible anger where they did not.

In parallel the Council presidency and the foreign policy chief opened a state-to-state track to Washington — attribution data, onward export enforcement, pooled verification of the new legibility technique, in return for volume-guaranteed doses and continuity of hospital model access. Early meetings were courteous and non-committal. American negotiators welcomed European alignment; they promised process, not quantities.

By December Europe was better defended, slightly more treated, and starkly more dependent: cures and verification existed, supply was political, and the negotiation that might secure it had only just begun.
