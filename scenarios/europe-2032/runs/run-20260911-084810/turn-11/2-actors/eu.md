## New measure
**EU Junior White-Collar Transition and Retraining Guarantee**
Pays wage insurance plus fast retraining placements for displaced junior developers, analysts and helpdesk staff, funded by a levy on automating employers and ESF+ top-up.
This and now because layoff letters plus rationing memories have collapsed sentiment to zero and capital to four, and without a visible cushion the hardening commitment loses its social base; it is category 7 Labour and social protection, small and executable within existing employment funds in two turns.

## Priority
EU Junior White-Collar Transition and Retraining Guarantee, because with no measures in flight it is the only way to rebuild capital and sentiment enough for any further hardening to be enforceable.

## In practice
We run this through DG EMPL and national public employment services under ESF+ and the reshuffled cohesion envelope, with an EPSCO Council conclusion authorising wage-insurance top-ups and 6-month retraining vouchers targeted at routine code, reports and helpdesk roles. We ask large employers freezing entry hiring to co-fund via a transition contribution tied to automation-related redundancies, offering flexibility on restructuring in return.

We meanwhile sustain what finished without new money: ENISA/CERT-EU keeps the Recovery Corps playbooks and patch contracts live on standby, TSOs keep islanding kits armed, and we accept the invitation into the joint cyber telemetry pool via EU-CyCLONe on an information-sharing basis only, to gain protection we cannot build alone while we rebuild. On ASML we protest the extraterritorial cut via the Anti-Coercion Instrument consultation but do not block compliance, to avoid company-level collapse while we seek middle-power cover.
