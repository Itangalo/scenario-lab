## New measure
**EU Common Terms for External AI Capacity Deals**
Sets Union-level conditions for any Gulf or hyperscaler co-financing of gigafactory power and compute — EU legal anchoring, no extraterritorial kill-switch, capped and interruptible inference reservation — and routes bilateral side-deals through Council clearance to stop the defection undercutting the line. This is International coordination and leverage, and why now is because the public Gulf billions offer plus a member-state side arrangement arriving together will either fund our sovereign build on our terms or fragment it.

## In practice
We run the new terms through the Foreign Affairs and Energy Councils with DG TRADE and DG CNECT drafting, using the Anti-Coercion and FDI-screening bases to require notification of bilateral compute deals, and offer the defecting capital cohesion side-payments and a first gigafactory power tranche to come back inside. The Gulf fund is invited to negotiate solely with the Commission/EIB vehicle tied to InvestAI Gigafactories, not capitals singly.

In parallel we keep M1/M2 alive by converting provisional grid connections in Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm and Warsaw into final investment decisions, pressing utilities on permitting guarantees while earmarking Gulf co-finance only if it accepts our anchoring terms. M3 auditors continue hospital and grid deployments of the control-evaluation upgrade, and M4 segmentation and credential-reset standards move to exercised drills via ENTSO-E/ENISA, funded without opening a new budget fight.
