France-Dutch joint teams run the autumn fallback exercises; we offer offtake contracts to pull hesitant private co-financing back in.

We execute the Lockdown alongside it through ENISA and the AI Office with the Joint Threat Integration cell: mandatory telemetry-sharing and isolation audits, conditional recovery funds only where gates are installed, HERA bio-screening held on. The Middle-Power Trusted Compute Compact venue is used to align export licences and secure replacement compute access while attribution is pending. No new labour or liability fight while restoration is incomplete.
