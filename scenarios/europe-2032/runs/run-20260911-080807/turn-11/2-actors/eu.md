**EU Displaced Entry-Level Transition Guarantee**
Provides wage insurance, paid retraining and hiring incentives targeted at routine coding, analysis, documentation and tier-one support roles where entry-level hiring has not recovered, funded via ESF+ reprogramming and employer co-contribution.
