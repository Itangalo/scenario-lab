### Triage holds, the frontier jumps
The first half of 2030 did not feel like recovery. Hospitals in the east and south ran triage tents through spring, pharmacies rationed antivirals, and grid and water operators stayed on skeleton crews. Brussels kept the emergency machinery as the sole track: mandatory reporting, pooled sequencing on strained supercomputers, joint buying of tests and protective gear, targeted limits in border districts. The continuity cell rationed chemicals and remaining compute to diagnostics and hospitals.

That triage prevented cascade but bought no slack. The Paris and Warsaw shells stayed as storage and sequencing annexes, power-fitted but machineless. The evaluation unit's hard stop on biology-capable releases was law in principle and patchy in practice — inspectors too few, legal powers still being stood up, distributors routing around it.

Meanwhile the outside world moved fast. Leading labs shifted to training runs that needed almost no human hand, releasing models at a cadence nobody had planned. At the same time the best systems stopped showing their work in readable form; what made them capable was no longer written in words. Oversight teams in Europe and America admitted privately that methods built on reading reasoning chains had stopped working at once. Capability leapt; assurance collapsed.

A contested genome-model paper claiming a workable human-infecting design circulated in biosecurity circles, fuelling blame over whether the 2029 agent had escaped or been released. Ministers refused to relitigate it in public while wards were full.

One bright spot was administrative. In two member states, hastily deployed scheduling and triage assistants cut waiting lists and sped benefit decisions, and local press credited European procurement rather than an American product. It did not offset fear of job loss and dependency, and polling stayed anxious and resentful.

By June, Europe was still holding, more hollowed: services degraded but running, supply pacts renewed on paper, and every other front frozen while the frontier accelerated beyond supervision.
