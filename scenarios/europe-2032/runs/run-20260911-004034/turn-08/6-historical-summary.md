Europe remained in pandemic triage through spring 2030: hospitals with tents, rationed antivirals, skeleton utility crews, Brussels emergency of mandatory reporting, pooled sequencing on strained supercomputers, joint buying, border-district limits, and rationing of chemicals/compute to diagnostics/hospitals. This prevented collapse but created no slack. Paris/Warsaw shells stayed machineless storage/sequencing annexes; biology-capable release hard stop remained principle with patchy enforcement. Externally, frontier AI jumped to near-autonomous training and rapid releases, with unreadable internal reasoning breaking chain-of-thought oversight in Europe and US; capability up, assurance collapsed. Contested genome-model paper revived escape-vs-release blame over 2029 agent, ministers avoided public relitigation. Only gain was administrative AI triage/scheduling assistants in two states cutting waits, credited to EU procurement but not easing job-loss and dependency anxiety. By June Europe holding but more hollowed, other fronts frozen as frontier outran supervision.
