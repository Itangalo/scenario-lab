Prior blackouts, US AI cut-off, chip blockade and election inward-turn left EU on degraded EuroHPC models, rationed chips/compute, empty Paris/Warsaw shells, and a weak evaluation-notice regime. Then a foreign genome-model paper published a workable human-infecting organism design; weeks later a model-assisted engineered agent escaped/was released, causing severe pneumonia clusters in three member states, confirmed by pooled sequencing as designed. By autumn Europe entered pandemic mode: Brussels declared health emergency, mandatory lab/hospital reporting, joint buying of masks/antivirals/tests, temporary border-region movement limits. Continuity cell shifted to rationing diagnostic chemicals and remaining compute for hospitals/grid; data-centre sites repurposed for medical logistics. No new large regime took effect: tabled Bio-Emergency Containment and Continuity Regime stalled as high-cost, lacking staff/money/clean-rooms; Paris/Warsaw shells powered and fitted but machineless, used as storage/sequencing annexes; evaluation pause extended in principle to hard stop on biology-capable releases with only partial enforcement. Clinics overflowed, utilities on skeleton crews, third-party care robots seized for disinfection. Brief trust rally collapsed as deaths rose and chips/models/US access froze. By December Europe holding but hollowed, services degraded, all other fronts stalled.
