## New measure
**EU Cyber Recovery and Service Continuity Surge**
Stands up EU-coordinated emergency restoration, offline backups and manual-fallback staffing for health, municipal and grid services hit by the automated cyberattack, while fast-tracking cyber hardening and the new control/interpretability safeguards into deployed systems. Category 6 preparedness and resilience measure — why this and why now: the major model-generated attack lands as capability jumps and safety sits at 11, and with capital at 16 and sentiment at 18 only visible restoration plus exercised continuity rebuilds tolerance to hold the sovereign-resilience commitment.
