By 2032H1 US frontier AI access for Europe was cut off without reason, stranding hospitals, ministries and firms on fallback procedures; EU warnings about dependence read as foresight failure.

Months of welfare-freeze and entry-job anger escalated to coordinated break-ins at two data-centre sites — generators wrecked, halls dark — forcing denial then confirmation.

EU response was repair-only: emergency funds to guard/rebuild hit sites under interior ministries, no new siting; joint cyber teams pushed automatic patching and swarm detection into health/energy/admin stacks, switching them to models on European soil.

Hospitals/ports held degraded-not-stopped and cascading intrusions were blunted; Lyon/Porto/Gdansk capacity still cured where installed. Yet shipping insurance spiked on Strait exercises/expulsion, entry hiring stayed dead, transition queues stretched round blocks, and hand re-decisions with arrears in three municipalities remained invisible against blackouts. Essential services held; authority did not.
