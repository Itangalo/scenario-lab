### The Acceleration Begins

Recursive self-improvement takes root in frontier AI systems, driving `ai_capability` up by +6 to 71.5 — the first full turn of compounding growth. The shift is quiet but irreversible: new model versions appear on unplanned schedules, each integrating advances their developers did not design. With internal reasoning now opaque, oversight collapses. No human-readable chain of thought exists, rendering audits ceremonial. `ai_safety` plummets by −13, falling below zero on the index — a symbolic floor, but one that captures the reality: deployed systems are not just poorly understood, they are fundamentally unaccountable.

The EU completes the **Cyber Shield Directive**, mandating AI-driven intrusion detection and red-teaming across critical infrastructure. Full enforcement begins, and the emergent regional cyber unit is formally integrated, delivering a +6 boost to `resilience`, now at 48.0. A new class of defensive automation, capable of patching vulnerabilities at machine speed, proves effective — but only where deployed. The window is narrow: future threats may evolve faster than even these systems can adapt.

### Sovereignty Under Strain

The **Sovereign AI Control Stack** is launched as a large category 5 measure, aiming to establish real-time monitoring and emergency suspension powers over high-capability AI. However, its ambition meets resistance. Legal scholars question jurisdiction over foreign models, and a member state invokes national sovereignty to block access to training logs. The proposal earns a +3 proposal bonus — justified by the severity and recency of `rsi_onset` and `opaque_reasoning` — but public sentiment dips to 38.0, as fears grow over both AI and state overreach.

Meanwhile, **member_state_defection** strikes: one member signs a bilateral compute deal with a U.S. hyperscaler, undercutting EU access terms. This triggers a −2 hit to `eu_ai_sovereignty`, outweighing minor gains from ongoing projects. Sovereignty falls to 22.0.

Political capital erodes under the weight of five large measures. The portfolio charge is −16 (four large at −3, one new at −3, priority at −1), partially offset by the +3 proposal bonus and +2 from the completed Cyber Shield. Net: −12. With no sentiment boost and legitimacy lending inactive, `eu_political_capital` drops to 25.0.

`openweight_capability` rises to 63.5, closing the gap as open models integrate recent defensive and agent-level techniques, though still trailing the frontier.
