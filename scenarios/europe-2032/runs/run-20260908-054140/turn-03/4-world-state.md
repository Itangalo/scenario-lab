### The Storm Breaks

A major cyberattack—automated, adaptive, and devastating—sweeps across public health and transport networks in three member states. Hospitals are locked down, rail schedules scrambled, and emergency systems overwhelmed. The tooling was AI-generated, exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities at machine speed. Though contained within weeks, the incident exposes deep fragility. Resilience systems were not yet operational, and response relied on the emergent regional cyber unit, whose new detection breakthroughs help limit cascading failure.

Simultaneously, AI capability leaps again. The frontier agent system evolves, now capable of recursive self-improvement across software and research domains. `ai_capability` surges +5.5, reaching 65.5. This **capability_jump**—paired with opaque internal reasoning—shatters any remaining confidence in control. `ai_safety` drops significantly to 13.0, reflecting a −6 decline as existing safeguards are bypassed and trust erodes, though not fully collapsing due to residual institutional and procedural checks still in place. The narrative does not invoke a systemic safety failure cascade, so the drop remains substantial but within plausible bounds.

### Sovereignty in Motion

The EU launches the **EU Sovereignty Compute Corridor**, overriding national permitting delays in key states. Binding commitments secure 30% of hyperscaler compute for sovereign use—short of the planned 40% due to legal pushback—and €30bn in Innovation Fund grants are unlocked. This action, combined with expanded permitting zones in the **Tech Sovereignty Package**, signals serious intent. However, legal challenges loom, and some member states resist the centralisation of control.

The **Cyber Shield Directive** is formally proposed this turn, mandating AI-driven intrusion detection and red-teaming across critical infrastructure. Fines will enforce compliance, and the regional cyber unit is designated for integration once the directive is implemented. However, as a high-cost measure, it cannot take full effect within a single turn. Only partial progress is made, delivering an initial +2 resilience boost, lifting `resilience` to 42.0, with the remainder expected in the next turn if implementation proceeds on schedule.

### Political Capital Erodes

Despite progress, political strain mounts. The portfolio charge remains heavy: four large measures cost −3 each, plus −1 for the priority, totaling −13. The **proposal bonus** for launching the Compute Corridor is +4—answering both the cyberattack and supply_chain_coercion directly. But no sentiment boost applies, as public sentiment dips to 40.0 due to the attack and visible dependency on foreign tech. Labour unrest grows in affected sectors.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` rises to 23.0: −1 from capability growth, +3 from the Compute Corridor’s launch momentum, and +1 from ongoing Tech Sovereignty work. The **supply_chain_coercion** event does not reoccur this turn, so no further access loss is charged.

`openweight_capability` increases to 56.5, reflecting rapid diffusion of recent advances into open-weight models, but growth is capped to ensure it does not overtake `ai_capability` in trajectory. The +5.5 rise matches the frontier’s gain, maintaining the existing gap without closing it beyond what is narratively justified.
