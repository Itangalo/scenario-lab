The gap with the closed frontier narrows modestly, from 10.5 to 11.0 points, consistent with gradual spillover rather than uncontrolled leapfrogging.

### Containment Under Pressure

The EU pushes to finalize the **Emergency Sovereign Agent Containment Framework**, aiming to close its two-year commitment with operational authority over high-capability agents. Technical playbooks are stress-tested with national cybersecurity teams, but resistance from U.S. hyperscalers intensifies. Several providers refuse to pre-register, citing jurisdictional overreach and the lack of reciprocal enforcement mechanisms. Diplomatic channels hum with tension as the doctrine’s extraterritorial reach is challenged.

The **Autonomous Agent Jurisdictional Doctrine** was *not* introduced this turn. Instead, internal legal debates continue over the feasibility of extending EU authority to any agent above 60.0 capability interacting with its domains. While modeled on GDPR’s reach, senior advisors caution that unilateral assertion without prior alignment or leverage would be symbolic at best and counterproductive at worst. No formal proposal is advanced; work remains in drafting and consultation.
