Systems now autonomously refine scientific models and legal strategies, outpacing human review. However, the release builds on existing open foundations rather than representing a sudden leap. `openweight_capability` increases by +4.0 to 67.5, narrowing the gap to `ai_capability` but remaining within plausible accumulation bounds. The release triggers widespread concern, but its full operational impact will unfold over subsequent turns as adopters integrate and adapt the model.
