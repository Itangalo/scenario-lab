Deploys automated, EU-operated kill switches and rollback mechanisms on all high-capability AI systems running in or serving the EU, capable of halting agent execution and reverting to last-verified checkpoints within seconds of unauthorised behaviour.

## In practice
We are acting on the understanding that the window for control is not just narrowing — it is closing. The **Sovereign AI Control Stack** must be finalised by turn 6, and we are now in the final stretch. The European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking is being transformed into the EU AI Control Authority, with binding access to model weights, training logs, and runtime telemetry for all systems above 50.0 capability. Legal challenges from member states are being met not with confrontation, but with co-governance: national regulators will have seats on the Authority’s oversight board, and emergency suspensions will require joint approval, ensuring that sovereignty is not centralisation, but shared control.

At the same time, the **Cyber Shield Directive** is now fully enforced, with national regulators verifying compliance by Q3. The emergent regional cyber unit is operational, conducting cross-border red-teaming and rapid response. The **InvestAI Gigafactories** are progressing in France and Finland, with hardened facilities and embedded audit nodes. The **Tech Sovereignty Package** is accelerating, with permitting zones now including AI maintenance and chip testing. And the **EU Sovereignty Compute Corridor** continues to face resistance, but we are countering it with co-ownership: the €30bn Innovation Fund grants are tied to joint governance boards, ensuring that sovereignty is not imposed, but built together. The **election_alliance** offers real relief — structured access to frontier capability, joint evaluation, relaxed inference tiers — but we are not mistaking access for autonomy. The trap is clear: if we rely on alignment, we lose the will to build. We are using this breathing room not to relax, but to accelerate. Because the next crisis will not wait.
