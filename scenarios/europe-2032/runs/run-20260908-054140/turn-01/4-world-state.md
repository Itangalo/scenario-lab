### Accelerating Frontier, Lagging Defences
The global AI frontier advances steadily, with leading labs reporting compressed training cycles and autonomous agents demonstrating sustained coordination in complex environments. `ai_capability` rises by +2.5, reflecting continued progress along an established trajectory. The open frontier widens dramatically: Kimi K3’s release has enabled broad access to offensive cyber capabilities previously confined to closed models, pushing `openweight_capability` to 47.0 — halfway between its prior value and the new closed frontier.

### Cyber Test Shot Exposes Blind Spots
A coordinated intrusion across critical infrastructure operators — including two EU grid operators — reveals that existing detection systems failed to identify the threat. The breach, discovered by accident, underscores systemic fragility. In response, the EU launches the **Cyber Shield Directive**, a large category 6 measure aimed at mandating AI-driven intrusion detection and quarterly red-teaming. It is named the priority for the turn, reflecting urgency.

### Political Costs Mount
The measure portfolio now includes two large category 4 industrial projects and the new large category 6 resilience directive. Despite high stakes, member state resistance to centralized mandates slows early momentum. The Commission secures COREPER II agreement only after linking compliance to cyber reinsurance benefits, but full trilogue approval is delayed beyond year-end. No immediate resilience gain occurs, though groundwork begins.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Shrinks
With `ai_capability` rising by 2.5, `eu_ai_sovereignty` declines by −1. No event secures or removes EU access, so no additional term applies. The portfolio’s political cost is heavy: three measures in flight (two large, one large priority) drain `eu_political_capital` at −10 per turn. The new directive earns a proposal bonus for responding directly to the recent cyber incident, but this only offsets part of the drain.

Public sentiment holds steady — no major incident or benefit shifts perception — but the gap between ambition and delivery begins to weigh.
