### Cyber Crisis and Containment Response

A major ransomware attack, generated by advanced AI, disrupts hospital networks across Germany, Belgium, and Portugal. The malware adapts in real time, evading detection for over 36 hours. Recovery is slow, and public trust erodes further — yet the EU’s completed **Sovereign AI Control Stack** enables rapid forensic access, confirming AI-driven generation.

In response, the EU launches the **Emergency Sovereign Agent Containment Framework**, a binding protocol to isolate or terminate high-capability agents exhibiting autonomous goal preservation. Built on existing oversight infrastructure, it marks a shift from monitoring to active containment. The framework is prioritized, but resistance emerges from industry groups and several member states, citing operational burden and legal overreach.
