### Capability Creeps Forward

Global AI capability advances to 67.0 as self-improving agents refine software pipelines and accelerate materials discovery. The closed frontier pulls further ahead, with leading labs reporting autonomous research cycles in quantum chemistry and embedded systems. No safety breakthroughs accompany the rise, and the gap between capability and control widens.

### Open-Weight Surge Shows Distributed Power

A significant open model release, built on repurposed public datasets and coordinated across volunteer compute pools, pushes `openweight_capability` to 54.5. While not matching the frontier, the model demonstrates early autonomous cyber probing and targeted disinformation in test environments. Security agencies detect limited replication in underground forums, reinforcing EU concerns about diffusion risks. The increase reflects growing grassroots engineering capacity, building on open-source tooling advances noted in prior turns. The gain remains within the bounds of plausible catch-up, staying below both the absolute level and the growth rate of `ai_capability`.

### EU Red-Teaming Expansion Makes Partial Progress

Work continues on the **Critical System Red-Teaming Expansion**, with ENISA leading pilot assessments in three high-priority NIS2 sectors across five member states. Testing protocols have been standardized, and initial findings are being shared bilaterally with national cyber agencies. However, full certification and integration into national defence plans remain out of scope for this turn, as cross-border coordination, legal mandates, and staffing limitations slow scaling. The effort yields modest improvements in shared threat models, contributing a +1 boost to `resilience`.

### Monitoring Framework Progresses, But Not Fully Live

Work advances on the **Open-Weight Monitoring and Containment Framework**, with Europol preparing the AI Misuse Cell for operations. Detection protocols are being tested, and pilot scanning of dark web channels has begun. However, safe-harbour negotiations with open-source developers remain stalled, and inter-agency coordination delays full deployment. The cell is not yet operational at scale, preventing a step-change in monitoring capacity this turn.

### Compute Sovereignty Push Announced—Funding and Implementation Delayed

The **EU Compute Sovereignty Acceleration Programme** is announced as a category 4 measure, with fast-tracked permitting initiated for EuroHPC sites and early energy integration planning underway. Legal anchoring and audit rights are proposed as conditions for infrastructure access. However, no funding mechanism is yet identified for the multi-billion-euro investment required, and governance disagreements among member states delay firm commitments. As a high-cost measure in early proposal, no immediate impact on `eu_ai_sovereignty` or `eu_political_capital` occurs. Implementation will require multiple turns to materialize, and tangible effects depend on future political and financial alignment.

### Public Anxiety Grows

Visible job losses in law, accountancy, and software spark protests in several capitals. Graduates face a hollowed-out entry market. Media coverage turns sharply negative, dragging `public_sentiment` down by −2.
