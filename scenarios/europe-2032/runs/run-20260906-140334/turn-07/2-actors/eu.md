## In practice
We are acting on the fact that capability without control is surrender. The **Emergency Compute Assurance Initiative** is now operational: we are deploying joint audit teams from ENISA, EuroHPC, and national cybersecurity agencies to assess physical, legal, and operational control over all clusters exceeding 1 exaFLOP/s. Funding from REPowerEU and Digital Europe is being redirected to support failover infrastructure and legal restructuring that places control under enforceable EU jurisdiction. We are offering U.S. hyperscalers a clear trade: full compliance with audit and continuity planning in exchange for mutual recognition of equivalent U.S. safeguards next turn, once the new administration is seated. France and Germany are aligned; we are working bilaterally with Poland to address investor concerns through liability caps and co-investment in redundancy.

At the same time, we are consolidating the gains of the **Critical Infrastructure Resilience Sprint** into standing obligations. The 42% deployment of fallback systems in Tier 1 operators is now the baseline for NIS2 enforcement, with ENISA issuing binding orders to close the remaining gap within 18 months. The new **Critical System Red-Teaming Expansion** builds directly on this: we are scaling the red-teaming protocols proven in energy to health, transport, and financial systems, mandating annual adversarial testing with AI-simulated threats and public disclosure of systemic risks. This is not an add-on — it is how resilience becomes durable. The bio_uplift_findings and openweight_frontier_release this turn confirm that the threat surface is widening faster than we anticipated. We cannot secure what we do not understand, and we cannot defend what we do not test. This expansion closes a critical gap: as open models approach 50.0 in capability, the attack toolkit is no longer confined to state labs. Our defences must match that reality.
