### The Open Frontier Widens

A frontier-grade open-weight model is released, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in days, and begins to reshape the landscape of accessible AI capability. This event — the **openweight_frontier_release** — triggers rapid diffusion, but not instant parity. `openweight_capability` rises to 51.5, reflecting strong early momentum and replication among skilled practitioners. However, full-scale reimplementation remains bottlenecked by compute access and coordination: while knowledge spreads quickly, capability lags meaningfully behind the closed frontier at 64.0. The jump represents a shortened development lag, but not reversal of the usual order—open progress builds on, never leapfrogs, the leading edge.

Simultaneously, `ai_capability` advances to 64.0 as self-improving agents accelerate materials discovery and software optimization. Yet no corresponding safety progress lands: opaque reasoning persists, and the gap between capability and control widens. With no breakthrough and rising capability, `ai_safety` drops by 3 to 18.0, edging closer to unmanageable opacity.

### EU Responds Under Pressure

The EU retires the **Tech sovereignty package**, which failed to mobilize sufficient private capital. Its completion delivers a +2 finish bonus to `eu_political_capital`. However, this gain is offset by ongoing costs: the **Emergency Compute Assurance Initiative** (small, cat 4) continues at −2, and the new **Critical System Red-Teaming Expansion** (small, cat 6) begins at −2. The priority status adds another −1. Total portfolio charge is −6.

No proposal bonus applies: while **bio_uplift_findings** and **openweight_frontier_release** occurred this turn, the new measure was not proposed in direct response to them. Resilience sees a small early benefit (+1) from the red-teaming expansion beginning to scale.

Sovereignty holds at 27.0: the −1 decay from `ai_capability` rising over 2 is balanced by +1 momentum from the compute initiative in flight. No capacity event occurred.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 34.0, caught between anxiety over AI misuse and recognition of defensive efforts. Without sentiment above political capital, no legitimacy boost applies.
