## In practice
We stay on the emergency bases already in use: Article 168/122 and UCPM, with HERA/ECDC sequencing and wastewater sentinels for the lingering pathogen, and ENISA/CERT-EU repair teams keeping dispatch, appointments and ports on offline backups and paper. Hospitals and ministries stay on EU-hosted open and older licensed models, with reprogrammed funds paying for inference on EU soil.

We take the two windfalls where they cost no vote. We push the defensive breakthrough — automated patching at vulnerability speed and swarm-behaviour detection — straight into the municipal stack via the repair cell crews and ENISA hardening guides, to close the intrusion class that has kept town halls on paper since 2030. And we claim the one visible adoption success — waiting lists falling where the Fallback Care Corps plus hosted models held — as a European decision in Health Council and mayoral channels, to start rebuilding tolerance. We do not open a compute-build, retaliation, or Taiwan-exposure vote with capital and sentiment at zero; sites stay warm, grid creeps, and country-tier rationing is managed, not matched.
