Late 2030 saw AI-written assaults on appointments, dispatch and ports, forcing clinics and town halls to paper and queues.

In January, US providers cut EU access to leading AI with days' notice, crippling hospitals, ministries and logistics; a machine-assisted modified pathogen was released, requiring prolonged containment. Self-rewriting intrusion toolkits spread, forcing renewed paper triage.

Brussels held emergency footing without new vote: EU cyber repair cell stayed on under health mandates, nursing offline backups; sequencing/wastewater sentinels shifted to case-finding; staff paid overtime. Ministries ordered onto European-hosted open models and older licensed fallbacks, with reprogrammed funds paying for EU-soil inference. Municipal backup kits kept services degrading not stopping.

July-Dec 2031 brought twin outside shocks: US-Chinese leap in machine-written code and automated intrusion hitting municipal IT, and a limited US-China pact on weights and bio-design tools with thin verification. Brussels was excluded, receiving briefings not consultation.

Inside, strategy stayed continuity: pathogen containment dragged; dispatch/appointments on offline/paper; hospitals/ministries on EU-hosted open/older models; only funded Fallback Care and Bio-Response Corps sustained wards via overtime. No new Gigafactory money; sites kept warm, grid crept, factory/retaliation votes frozen. US lithography curbs held. Public mood collapsed on outage fatigue, ward closures, disease fear, dependence humiliation and great-power deal over European heads; mayors credited crews not Union.

Jan-June 2032 offered no recovery, only slower strain. Emergency apparatus held in place; Fallback Care and Bio-Response Corps reached full operation, stabilizing clinic rosters in communes after two years overtime. Interpretability advances from labs were folded into hosted fallback stack and incident reporting without vote, making failures slightly more predictable. Gains overshadowed by foreign-built commercial robots arriving at scale in warehouses/ports/logistics on foreign control models, turning EU manufacturers into buyers, and by tightened chip/model country-tier rationing further narrowing access cut in 2031. No building, retaliation or labour-transition vote attempted; compute sites kept warm, grid crept, weights-pact briefings still only briefings. Queues eased where corps landed; humiliation over bought robots and rationed chips deepened.
