## New measure
**EU Accession to Joint Cyber-Bio Threat Response Pact**
Joins the invited joint cyber command telemetry pool and binding biosurveillance sample-sharing pact as the external arm of the Shield. Why this and why now: protection we cannot build alone is offered now after grid and pathogen failures, and refusing it while hospitals and operators remain exposed would strand the Shield without attribution and early warning.

## Priority
EU Civil Biodefence and Infrastructure Hardening Shield, because joining the pact only matters if hospital sequencing, wastewater monitoring and operator audits it funds become operational this turn.

## In practice
We mandate HERA plus ECDC to sign binding sample-sharing and standing investigation terms, and ENISA plus the breached transmission operators to connect segmentation telemetry to the joint cyber command via Council JHA/Home Affairs joint decision, using Civil Protection Mechanism funds already moved. Insurer disclosure fear is met with limited liability shield for operators sharing gaps into the pool.

Gigafactories and permitting zones (M1, M2) are held to site-selection and state-aid clearance only with no new cash call this turn to protect Shield disbursements; Evaluation Institute (M3) staff remain seconded to forensics work on model lineage from the pathogen incident to keep industry consent while spring exercises are prepared.
