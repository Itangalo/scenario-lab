## New measure
**EU Critical Operator Patching and Segmentation Disclosure Mandate**
Requires transmission, port and water operators to deploy automated patching tooling from the defence breakthrough and disclose segmentation gaps to ENISA to retain liability shield and public cover, turning the insurers' repricing into mandated hardening. Why this and why now: the exclusion clauses make borrowed protection unaffordable unless gaps are closed, and the new defensive tooling gives operators something that actually closes them before the capability jump is weaponised.

## In practice
We use NIS2 Article and Electricity Risk Preparedness legal bases in TTE/Energy Council to attach the liability shield granted under the Pact to verified feed connection and automated patch deployment, with ENISA certifying the new detection tooling and the Cyber Command liaison validating telemetry. Insurers are brought into the implementing act: disclosure into the pool restores cover, summaries do not.

Shield funds stay protected: HERA/ECDC move hospital batch sequencing to standing weekly reporting and extend capital wastewater pilots, while M1/M2 remain at site-selection and state-aid clearance with no new cash call. The Evaluation Institute stops chasing chain-of-thought reviews made obsolete by opaque reasoning and pivots seconded staff to black-box uplift and cyber-capability tests for the jumped frontier, preserving industry consent for forensics while rebuilding a blocking test that does not depend on readable reasoning.
