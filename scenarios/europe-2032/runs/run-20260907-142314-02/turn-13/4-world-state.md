### A Thin Line of Sight

Global AI capability inches forward to 63.0, as frontier labs report incremental gains in training stability and cross-modal reasoning. No breakthrough emerges, and openweight_capability advances only slightly—to 53.875—tracking halfway between its prior value and the closed frontier, consistent with ongoing restrictions on public releases.

The EU pivots from physical sovereignty to regulatory reach. With the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation abandoned and frontier access recently revoked, Brussels doubles down on visibility. The Commission proposes the Emergency Audit Access Framework, a large category 1 measure under the Digital Markets Act and AI Act, granting ENISA and a new European AI Safety Board binding rights to inspect training logs, energy use, and idle compute. The goal: to map latent capacity and verify safety claims without owning the infrastructure. Legal grounds are contested, but invoked under market access conditions.

Resistance is immediate. Washington labels the move extraterritorial overreach. Hyperscalers threaten to throttle EU-facing services. Within the Union, member states split: Germany and France back the audit rights, but Hungary and Poland denounce them as federal overreach. The legal basis survives a preliminary challenge, but momentum is fragile.

No measure finishes this turn, and the portfolio charge is zero. However, the new large measure costs −3, and naming it priority adds −1. The proposal bonus does not apply—no recent incident directly justifies audit access, despite growing unease. Labour displacement and supply-chain coercion dominate the mood, but are not lab-origin risks. The emergent_eval_resistance trend deepens, eroding confidence in central evaluation.

`ai_safety` remains stagnant at 13.0—no safety gain lands, and `ai_capability`’s rise of +0.5 triggers no decay term. `resilience` holds steady. But `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls: −2 from supply_chain_coercion, which seizes the Union’s only leverage and turns it against itself, and −1 from `ai_capability` rising above 62.5. No category 4 measure offsets it. Political capital, though boosted earlier, now drops sharply under the new portfolio cost and no offsetting gains.

Public sentiment remains anchored at 30.0—no scandal, but no relief either. The pivot to audit is too technical to shift perception.
