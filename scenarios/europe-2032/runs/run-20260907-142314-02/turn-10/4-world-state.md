### Stalled Sovereignty, Shifting Leverage

The Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation formally concludes without a single new facility approved, its ambitions undone by national resistance and grid constraints. No sovereign capacity materialises, and the directive lapses. Yet the EU pivots: citing digital fairness, the Commission launches a post-implementation review under the Digital Markets Act, compelling dominant AI providers to disclose EU energy use and submit to audit of spare compute. This backdoor strategy reframes market power as a proxy for sovereignty, with DG COMP, ENISA, and ACER drafting protocols to monitor private infrastructure.

### Bottom-Up Resilience Gains Ground

Amid institutional paralysis, the Municipal Resilience Backchannel advances. Lyon, Barcelona, and Helsinki complete interoperability testing, and ENISA introduces a certification track for “Trusted Resilience Nodes” under NIS2. Certified cities gain access to emergency funding and data flexibility. Bologna, Ljubljana, and Tallinn are selected for integration using existing disaster recovery funds, marking quiet but tangible progress in decentralised crisis response.

### Safety Erodes Further

The European Evaluation Shield’s authority fractures as two member states reject its remediation mandates, citing legal overreach. Public trust in central oversight collapses, and no enforcement mechanism emerges. Meanwhile, frontier AI capability inches upward to 61.5, though evidence of a plateau emerges in pricing and underwhelming releases. Open-weight models show signs of acceleration—now at 53.0—fueled by leaked agent frameworks that are beginning to circulate and run on consumer hardware, but widespread deployment remains limited by tooling gaps and inference costs.

### Political Capital at the Brink

With four large measures still in flight and no new initiative launched, political capital remains strained. The completion of a category 4 measure yields a modest boost, but the portfolio charge persists. Capital rises to 11.0—still below the 12 threshold for reliable agenda control. Public sentiment holds at 35.0, sustained by local gains but unimpressed by systemic inertia.
