## Priority
EU Distressed Compute Consolidation — because with no measures in flight and frontier control slipping, locking distressed sites under EU jurisdiction outranks patching alone this turn.

## In practice
DG CNECT and DG COMP, via InvestEU and EIB, map cancelled data-centre and power reservations from the collapse and take options with conditional public backstop, using the existing InvestAI legal base and accelerated-permitting zones. No new gigafactory is launched; this salvages the M1/M2 pipeline by substituting cheap shells for greenfield builds.

ENISA and HERA sustain the finished M3-M6-M8 kits — offline images, reserve licences, conditional reinsurance tied to telemetry — through Health and JHA Councils without new money, while the Japan/Korea cell is kept as servicing cover. The defecting member state is offered access to consolidated capacity if it returns to common procurement, but recovery-fund conditionality is still not triggered at 19 capital.
