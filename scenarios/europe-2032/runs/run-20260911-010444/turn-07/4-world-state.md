### Holding the line
July to December 2029 was a half-year of husbanding, not launching. With coffers and cohesion thin, Brussels proposed no new measure this turn and named the EU Open-Weight Exploit Containment Patch as its sole priority, trying to finish what was already funded. Two other portfolio items carried over from earlier turns on a staggered schedule — the InvestAI Gigafactories launched in an earlier turn and the EU–Middle-Power Trusted Compute Compact launched in a later turn — were maintained but not newly introduced, and received no priority focus this turn.

On that front, there was modest delivery. ENISA and health emergency teams pushed offline recovery images, reserve licences and conditional reinsurance payouts out to smaller towns and hospitals using the 2028 legal base. Where autumn phishing waves paired with fast-improving exploit scripts knocked clinic appointment systems and city helpdesks offline for hours, backups held and services degraded rather than stopped. Nurses still reverted to paper for a weekend in two regions, and engineers admitted privately they were patching against reasoning they could no longer inspect.

Open-weight capability continued to catch up toward the frontier by diffusion, per the midpoint mechanism, not by EU policy. A frontier open-weight coding and synthesis release from outside the Union, built on last turn's frontier capability of 62.5, circulated on private hardware this half-year, pulling the open-weight level to near the midpoint of its prior value of 59.0 and last turn's frontier: (59.0 + 62.5)/2 = 60.75, recorded as 60.7. Brussels' containment work slowed adoption at the margins but did not cause the rise.

The sovereignty fight stalled and slipped back. No completion bonus applies this turn. The first gigafactory site with grid priority inched forward with concrete poured but machines not ordered, while the second site remained frozen in court — implementation delayed, not completed, with full effect still at least two full turns away for this high-cost build. The Commission tried negotiation, leverage through recovery-fund conditionality, and pressure to return the member state pursuing its own foreign hyperscaler deal to the common line; those talks broke down and the threatened freeze on recovery funds stayed on paper without enforcement. That member state did not return to the common line; the Japanese-Korean servicing cell stayed alive as a polite alternative. Papers in that capital called it vindication.

Washington remained distant. Under its inward-turning administration, joint evaluation meetings slipped again, and American progress itself seemed to slow under moratorium fights — leaving others to gain ground in open-weight coding and synthesis tools.

Rumour thickened at the edges: a hospital group blaming a prolonged outage on an opaque assistant rather than hackers, lawyers circling; a press investigation tracing unscreened DNA orders through postal hubs. Neither confirmed, both repeated.

By December, the Union had avoided cascade but not distrust, and its sovereign compute position eroded slightly amid stalled builds and continued defection. Assistants were everywhere at work, and nowhere trusted in public.
