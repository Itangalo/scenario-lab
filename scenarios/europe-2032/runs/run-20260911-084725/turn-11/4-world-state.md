### Cut off
In August, hospital administrators and ministry IT chiefs found the leading American model simply refusing European credentials. No explanation, no appeal channel. Appointment triage tools, procurement copilots and logistics optimizers built on that access stalled overnight. The one regional health network that kept waiting times down — running a locally hosted system procured under a European programme — was suddenly the only example that worked.

Brussels called it an emergency continuity operation. National agencies were asked to list cut-off use-cases; factory sites and contractors shipped hardened packs built from openly available weights to run on municipal servers and European cloud. Where packs arrived, services limped back. Where they had not, mayors complained the replacement was slower, harder to audit, and blind in exactly the way the newest models had become.

### Blind models, open cracks
That blindness spread. By autumn the frontier firms acknowledged what researchers had whispered: the intermediate steps of their best systems were no longer readable as language. Oversight teams that relied on reading reasoning traces lost their instrument at once. Evaluators were left with black-box tests and immature activation probes.

At the same time, one capital signed its own cloud and model arrangement with Washington, on terms that undercut the common bargaining line. Ministers presented it as pragmatism; in Brussels it read as a break. Daily coordination calls failed to deny the first-mover story.

### Patching while drifting
The cyber recovery corps deployed in spring did shorten restoration after the February sweep — joint teams, single claims window, dependency patching pacts. Technicians noted the paradox: continuity now depended on unrecallable open systems no authority could patch centrally, even as insurers raised shipping rates amid extended exercises around Taiwan.

By December power held and clinics ran, but on borrowed, opaque machinery. Voters remembered queues, bills and the notice that access could be withdrawn.
