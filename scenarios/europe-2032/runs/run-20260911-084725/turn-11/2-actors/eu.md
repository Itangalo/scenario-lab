## New measure
**EU Continuity Model Programme**
Replaces the cut-off frontier model in hospitals, ministries and affected firms with EU-controlled, locally-hosted open-weight systems plus EU procurement support, so essential services stay running under European control.
This and now because access denial plus member-state defection will fragment the Union if Brussels has no sovereign fallback to offer, and the one visible adoption success gives a template to copy; it is category 9 Diffusion, adoption and public trust, small and executable within existing procurement and Digital Europe envelopes.

## Priority
EU Continuity Model Programme, because with no measure in flight and essential services cut off from the leading model, restoring continuity under EU control is what holds the two-year commitment together.

## In practice
We run this as an emergency procurement and deployment operation through DG CNECT and the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority, using the joint procurement legal base and reshuffled Digital Europe funds. National digital agencies nominate the cut-off hospital, ministry and logistics use-cases; EU AI Factory sites and pre-contracted integrators provide hardened open-weight packs that run on municipal servers and EU cloud, with ENISA security baselines to contain the unpatched-model risk.

We pair it with political containment: a single continuity window for mayors and CEOs to register cutoff harm and get the replacement, daily Council Health and Telecom coordination to deny the defecting member state a first-mover narrative, and public attribution of the one adoption success to the European deployment choice. We do not fight the US cutoff or the opaque-reasoning loss directly — with capital at 4 we cannot — we make dependence survivable and visible.
