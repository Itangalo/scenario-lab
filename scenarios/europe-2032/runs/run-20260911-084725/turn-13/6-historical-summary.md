US tightened chip/model licences under tier system while unrecallable open models filled gaps; Brussels kept doctrine of hardened packs from open weights on municipal servers/EU cloud plus recovery corps. Municipal coalition standardized shared hardened distribution for triage/billing/dispatch; where installed services stabilized, independence from revocable US access meant dependence on unpatchable open code. Earlier US cutoff and side-deal breaking common line unresolved; voters credited mayors, not Brussels.

Trust collapsed amid protests. Brussels ordered freeze of implicated decision systems pending re-checks, human re-decision, preserved logs, and opened Brussels-backed claims window for review/compensation; finance ministers funded, implementation limped — varied suspensions, portal crash, slow payouts, double work for municipalities.
