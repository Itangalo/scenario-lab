## New measure
**EU Wrongful Automated Decisions Redress and Suspension**
It suspends the class of AI-supported social-insurance/policing/court decision systems implicated in the scandal pending independent re-conformity checks, and funds case-by-case review and compensation through a single claims window.
This and now because the scandal has made the AI Act look badly drawn for 2030 deployments — restriction is cheap, adoption is impossible — and only a visible halt plus redress can stop hostility hardening into permanent blockage, which directly serves our two-year commitment to hold the Union together through AI shocks.

## In practice
We act under Article 114 TFEU with an implementing decision plus coordinated DPA/ombudsman action, not a new AI Act rewrite this turn. The Commission orders national agencies to suspend the named system family, mandates human re-decision with logging preserved and read by ENISA and the Fundamental Rights Agency, and opens one Brussels-backed redress window modelled on last year's ransomware claims window.

We ask the municipal coalition to keep running the shared hardened distribution for triage/billing/dispatch under existing baselines, and we explicitly do not push new adoption or new open-weight deployment while the frontier open release spreads. The Council JHA and EPSCO formations carry the suspension, finance ministers carry the compensation advances, with mayors credited publicly for keeping essential services up on borrowed machines.
