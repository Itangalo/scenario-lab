## In practice
We hold the line with what has already finished and costs nothing further: the Continuity Model packs and the Cyber Recovery Corps procedures remain the operating doctrine, and we ask national agencies and the municipal coalition to keep running the shared hardened distribution under ENISA baselines rather than launching anything new.

We spend this half-year on political repair, not legislation. The Commission and the Council Health and Telecom formations run a single claims and triage window for the ransomware sweep and the US cutoff harm, publicly credit the locally-hosted success case, and negotiate containment of the bilateral Washington deal back into a common procurement line. No new fund, no new mandate is tabled until queues, restoration times and access disputes give us room to act again.
