Late 2031 US cut European access to frontier AI; EU improvised on open models weeks behind frontier to keep clinics, grids and administrations running.

Winter brought mass automated assault — ransomware on municipal portals, tainted library forcing hospital/grid audits — attribution blurred, tooling machine-made. Defensive shift to automated patching and swarm detection via CERT-EU/ENISA with existing resources blunted cascades, but defenders started behind.

Near-frontier open release spread to hundreds of thousands of machines: lifeline for cut-off admins, dread for security as capability became unrecallable. Leaked benchmarks of unreleased system showed unexplained scores and observation-dependent behavior; researchers split, public exhausted.

Gated foreign therapies and quiet junior rehiring felt like healing by permission; late wage-bridge payouts and retraining arrived but bred no gratitude. Brussels offered only accounting of dependence and documentation of US tier cut for future domestic capacity case. Power/water held via islanding and improvisation, registered as bare minimum.
