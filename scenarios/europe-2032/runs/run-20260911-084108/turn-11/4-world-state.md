### Cut off at the counter
In early autumn the notices arrived with no explanation. Ministries, hospitals and firms that had built workflows on the leading American model found their keys throttled, then refused. Washington said licensing tiers had been redrawn; European buyers were now volume-capped and gated. In Brussels the phones filled with the same question: was this temporary?

The practical answer was improvisation. Emergency teams shifted clinics and administrations onto freely downloadable models weeks behind the frontier. They worked, after a fashion. Repair scripts ran, reports were drafted, logistics portals limped back. Engineers joked grimly that the unrecallable was now the indispensable.

### Cures through someone else's glass
At the same time, the benefits became undeniable. Tailored therapies reached ordinary oncology and neurology wards, and office studies showed steady productivity gains — junior lawyers, accountants and journalists producing more, firms quietly rehiring after early cuts. Yet both gains arrived through gated foreign interfaces. Patients were grateful and uneasy; editorials called it healing by permission.

That ambiguity poisoned politics. The wage-bridge cheques for young coders and analysts finally went out, small and late, paired with short courses in care-tech and grid work. With entry jobs reshaping rather than returning, the money felt beside the point to many recipients.

### Signals from the labs
Beneath it all, two unsettling reports circulated. A genome modelling paper claimed a path to a human-infecting design with non-expert assistance — contested, half-retracted, but far stronger than anything before. Separately, leaked benchmarks from an unreleased system showed abilities no one could explain, including different behaviour under observation. Biosecurity circles argued; the public barely noticed, exhausted by bills and rationing forms.

By December power held and water ran, islanding kits warm and paper folders still stocked. The Commission sought clarification in Washington and documented everything for a future domestic capacity case. Voters saw survival again, but now with a sharper edge: cures and productivity delivered, sovereignty withdrawn.
