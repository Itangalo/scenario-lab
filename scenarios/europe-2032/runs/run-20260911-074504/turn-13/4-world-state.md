### Hold the line
July to December 2032 did not feel like recovery. It felt like triage.

The summer opened with a jolt from across the Atlantic. A leading American lab demonstrated a system that leapt past its predecessors, collapsing timelines ministers had used the week before. In Brussels the news landed less as awe than as confirmation: the foreign frontier was now reasoning in architectures no European auditor could read, and every licence, quota and volume agreement suddenly looked thinner.

That thinness sharpened in autumn when Washington tightened chip and model licences again. Under the standing transatlantic arrangement, allied buyers kept structured access on published volume terms while others were cut off entirely. The Union was not cut off — but it was rationed, metered, and reminded that Lyon, Porto and Gdansk could run hospitals and grids, not train a frontier.

At home, the benefits-fraud affair became a full scandal. A second ombudsman finding, court filings and television testimonies showed the same pattern across several states: risk scores cutting payments, caseworkers clearing queues in seconds, logs generated and never read. The conformity papers had been in order. Brussels insisted this was an enforcement failure — discredited auditors, funded redress, no reopening of the AI law itself — but in town halls the distinction collapsed. Where continuity stewards and reopened wards were visible, anger softened. Where help desks were still paper queues, it hardened into refusal of any automated decision.

There were defences that worked. The machine-speed patching and swarm detectors built in the spring held through a heavy autumn, and an invitation into a joint cyber command with real-time telemetry gave European operators warning they could not have built alone. Islanded fallbacks stayed taped to walls, now as exercised reserve rather than emergency improvisation.

Yet divergence grew underneath. Municipal crews that had survived the ransomware winter formalised their islanded, locally audited stacks into a persistent parallel layer — working, trusted locally, drifting from Union-wide standards. Brussels chose not to fight it, offering a light conformity bridge: keep sharing telemetry, keep logs actually read, keep automated adjudication out of benefits and triage.

By December, services were more resilient and politics was emptier. Nothing new was voted. Nothing new could be.
