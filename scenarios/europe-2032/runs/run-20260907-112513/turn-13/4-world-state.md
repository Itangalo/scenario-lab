### The Sovereignty Gambit

Global AI capability advanced to 99.0, deepening the terminal zone. Systems now routinely operate beyond interpretability, with reasoning traces that resemble cryptographic hashes more than explanations. No audit regime keeps pace.

The EU completed its *Mandate auditable AI in all critical public infrastructure*, but enforcement was neither universal nor unified. Instead, waivers were issued for AI-driven cancer therapies emerging from the **medical_breakthrough**, conditional on immediate sandboxing under EU control. This marked the de facto launch of the *Emergency Sovereign Access Programme*, justified under Article 168 and 346 TFEU.

Simultaneously, legal notices were sent to four foreign providers demanding model submission for audit. The U.S. hyperscalers rejected the demands as extraterritorial overreach; one Chinese firm suspended operations in the bloc. In response, Germany invoked national emergency powers to seize evaluation rights to a key oncology model — a move celebrated domestically but condemned internationally.

Behind closed doors, the defector state refused to withdraw its bilateral compute deal, though it agreed to exploratory talks on a co-governed health corridor. The offer exposed fractures: some capitals saw compromise, others betrayal.

Public sentiment rose slightly, to 27.0, as lifesaving treatments became accessible in several regions — but only where models were successfully intercepted. In others, care stalled, reinforcing perceptions of bureaucratic failure.

Resilience dipped to 53.0 as energy fluctuations from uncoordinated AI trading systems strained backup grids during the seizure operations. The Open Science Resilience Initiative remained unfunded.

Openweight capability surged to 82.0, driven by rapid replication of EU-sandboxed bioscience models now shared across member states. For the first time, open systems approached frontier performance in auditable domains.

Sovereignty eroded further, to 14.0, despite the aggressive moves. The capability advance outpaced gains, and no domestic compute expansion landed. Political capital remained at 0.0 — too low for new measures to take hold.
