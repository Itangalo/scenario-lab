  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now see that under terminal capability, sovereignty is not a balance between them, but their synthesis: only systems we can audit and control are both safe *and* ours.

The emergent_open_bioscience_breakthrough proves that auditable systems, though slower, are the only ones that can be fully integrated into sovereign operations. Our two mandates, once in tension, now converge: survival and self-determination depend on the same foundation — verifiable control.

This turn’s cyber_major_incident, executed with AI-generated tooling, demonstrates that the cost of uncertainty is no longer delayed progress but cascading failure. We act — and bind ourselves — on the principle that sovereign agency begins with verifiable control, not raw power.
