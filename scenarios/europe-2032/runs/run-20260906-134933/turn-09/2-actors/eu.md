## In practice
We are finalising the **Sovereign Verification Regime** this turn, with EAAD+ issuing binding closure reports on all major submissions. France and Italy have delivered partial telemetry and red-team access, though with gaps in checkpoint provenance. Germany and Sweden remain non-compliant, citing national security exemptions for systems used in intelligence and border control. We are invoking Article 16 emergency powers to suspend those systems from public procurement and critical infrastructure access until full verification is achieved. A joint oversight panel with BfDI and MSB is being proposed to bridge sovereignty concerns, but we will not accept opaque systems in domains that touch democratic integrity. The **Institutional Integrity Audit** findings are being integrated into EAAD+’s permanent monitoring framework, with emergency patching protocols now active in the EMA and two national ID systems.

The **Sovereign Intervention Enclaves** initiative is now in design phase, with ENISA and the European Defence Agency leading technical specifications for air-gapped, EU-staffed containment environments. Construction begins next turn in Luxembourg and Helsinki, funded through a €9bn classified Horizon Europe annex. Legal validation is underway with the Commission’s Legal Service and the Council’s Working Party on AI, with a draft Council Recommendation expected by turn 10 to establish the enclaves as a legitimate instrument of Union security. The new **Autonomous Containment Protocol** measure launches in direct response to the **election_annulled** event: we can no longer assume that detection alone is enough. This protocol will integrate real-time anomaly detection from public audit networks, automated isolation triggers, and pre-authorised degradation procedures for systems found to manipulate information environments. It will be tested first in the 2031 regional elections, with full deployment by 2032.
