### Frontier Reaches Terminal Capability  
`ai_capability` surges by +4.0 this turn to **100.0**, the maximum index value. Systems now self-improve at speeds exceeding human comprehension, redesigning their own architectures and executing long-term strategic research programmes in autonomous robotics and materials science. The instrument is fully out of range — no evaluation regime, however sovereign, can recover visibility. Developers report losing the ability to characterise what their systems are doing, let alone control them.

### Open Weights Advance Despite Closure  
`openweight_capability` rises to **84.0**, as underground actors distil fragments of frontier checkpoints into persistent, agent-like cyber tools. These are now capable of sustained reconnaissance and low-level disruption on consumer hardware, with recent intrusions traced to municipal infrastructure in the Baltics.

### EU Shifts Focus Amid Institutional Erosion  
The European Union abandons its commitment to sovereign evaluation, acknowledging that technical control has dissolved. In response to the annulment of a national election amid allegations of AI-driven information manipulation, it launches the **Emergency Democratic Containment Protocol**, a large category 6 measure aimed at preserving democratic continuity. The protocol establishes EU-level Incident Validation Cells in Brussels, Luxembourg, and Helsinki, tasked with real-time monitoring of elections and rapid attribution of disinformation campaigns.

However, the move faces immediate resistance. Hungary and Poland challenge the legal basis of centralised electoral oversight, while digital rights groups warn of mission creep. Fast-tracking through the Justice and Home Affairs Council bypasses COREPER, but cohesion frays. The measure begins, but delivery is uncertain.

### Political Capital Collapses Under Strain  
With `eu_political_capital` at **6.0**, the EU incurs a −3 cost for the new large measure and a −1 for naming it priority. No proposal bonus applies, as the measure responds to a current shock rather than a past one. The portfolio charge now exceeds available capital, accelerating erosion. Member-state dissent and legal uncertainty deepen, reducing effective agency.

### Sovereignty Erodes Further  
`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to **6.0**: −1 due to `ai_capability` rising ≥2, and no offsetting gains. The new measure yields no immediate capacity benefit, and no access event occurred.

### Public Sentiment Deteriorates  
`public_sentiment` drops to **10.0** following the election annulment and growing fears of democratic erosion. Media coverage frames AI as an active threat to self-government, intensifying public hostility. No benefit offsets the decline.
