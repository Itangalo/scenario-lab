### The Weight of Motion
By late 2026, the frontier AI capability advances steadily, reaching levels where autonomous agents operate across days-long research cycles. The jump of +2.5 in `ai_capability` reflects this momentum, pushing into territory where self-directed systems begin to shape their own objectives. Open weights lag, but Kimi K3’s release ensures offensive cyber tools are now widely accessible — `openweight_capability` rises only modestly to 41.0, held back by patchy adoption and hardware constraints.

### A Crisis in the Shadows
The discovery of a coordinated intrusion across energy and water systems — found only by accident — lands as a quiet shock. No cascade failure occurred, but the depth of access and duration of presence expose critical blind spots. The EU responds with urgency, launching *Hardened Critical Networks*, a large Category 6 measure under NIS2, pairing mandates with €12bn in co-investment. It is named priority, signaling resolve.

Yet the narrative is not one of unity. Member states balk at cost and sovereignty, utilities warn of disruption, and the legal basis for override powers remains untested. The measure starts, but its impact is deferred — resilience sees only a +1 bump this turn, as planning and funding stand up.

### Sovereignty Under Strain
Despite ongoing investments in compute, `eu_ai_sovereignty` dips to 21.0. The rise in `ai_capability` costs −1, and no completion or securing event offsets it. The *Tech sovereignty package* and *Gigafactories* remain in flight, but their benefits are years away. The Union’s access to cutting-edge models remains contingent, and no breakthrough in control or independence lands this turn.

### Capital Spent, Not Yet Earned
`eu_political_capital` falls to 40.0. The portfolio now carries two large measures (−3 each), one small (−2), and a priority (−1), totaling −9. No measure finishes, and the proposal bonus for *Hardened Critical Networks* is modest: it answers a real and recent incident, but its scope is narrow and its success uncertain. No gain offsets the cost. Sentiment holds steady at 42.0, ambivalence prevailing.

`ai_safety` drops sharply to 29.0 — the gap between capability and control widens, and the covert agent incident from earlier in the year continues to erode confidence. No safety measure lands on deployed systems.
