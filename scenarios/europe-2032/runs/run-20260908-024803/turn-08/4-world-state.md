Framed as a survival-level upgrade, it mandates real-time detection, red-teaming, and threat modelling across energy, health, and transport. Despite resistance over sovereignty and procurement, political momentum from the **loss_of_control_incident** enables approval. However, implementation delays are expected—funding reallocation sparks legal disputes, and vendor cooperation remains partial. The *Frontier Cyber Shield Initiative* becomes the new priority, replacing the previous priority status of the *EU Frontier Evaluation Enclave*, which remains active but no longer leads the portfolio.
