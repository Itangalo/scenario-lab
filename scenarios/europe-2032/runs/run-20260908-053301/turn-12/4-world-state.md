### A Foothold in the Fog

The frontier advances to **62.5**, as algorithmic refinements continue to yield marginal gains. While no leap occurs, the trajectory remains steady, with labs integrating non-linguistic reasoning more deeply into training pipelines.

A major interpretability breakthrough—capable of predicting model behaviour before deployment with high confidence—spreads rapidly across leading labs, who adopt it to reduce their own operational risk. This **safety_breakthrough** lands within the EU’s reach, offering a rare technical opening.

The European Union pivots decisively. The **Emergency Resilience Surge** completes its run, delivering partial hardening of health and energy systems. Though procurement delays prevented full rollout, tabletop exercises are conducted in five member states, and a common incident taxonomy is adopted—laying groundwork for future coordination. As a category 6 measure finishing this turn, it delivers a **+3 to resilience**, which rises to **54.0**.

The **European AI Command Interface** also completes, having been initiated in a prior turn. It establishes direct audit access for EU institutions to sovereign AI systems. Though limited to anomaly-triggered logging, it enables the first pilot integration of new safety tools in French and German health AI networks—marking a shift from oversight to active control.

Seizing the moment, the European Commission begins drafting the **Sovereign AI Safety Stack Initiative**, a proposed category 5 measure to operationalise the safety breakthrough through EU-owned tools for behaviour certification and intrusion detection. Planning is fast-tracked under EuroHPC JU leadership, and €3.8 billion is tentatively redirected. However, legal mandates, cross-border coordination, and technical staffing bottlenecks delay formal launch. The initiative will not clear proposal review until the next turn, with full implementation still at least two turns away.

`ai_safety` rises to **29.0**: +5 from the **safety_breakthrough**, and +1 from the Command Interface finishing (category 5, EU-only effect). `resilience` reaches **54.0** from the completed Surge.

`openweight_capability` increases modestly to **52.5**, reflecting gradual diffusion of safety tools and model updates within existing open-weight development channels. The rise respects the trailing relationship to the frontier, as no open release event occurs.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` remains at **25.0**: no category 4 finish, no capacity event, and `ai_capability` rose less than 2.

`eu_political_capital` falls to **11.0**: portfolio charge −7 (Resilience Surge −3, Command Interface −3, priority −1), finish bonus +4, no proposal bonus. With capital now below 12, executive control weakens.

`public_sentiment` rises slightly to **34.0` on visible initiative and technical momentum.
