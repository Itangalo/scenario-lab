### The Black Box Winter

**ai_capability** rises to **61.1**, as distributed training and algorithmic refinements continue pushing the frontier. The shift to non-linguistic internal representations, now dominant in leading systems, marks a qualitative break: reasoning is no longer legible by design. This triggers the **opaque_reasoning** event, collapsing most existing oversight mechanisms. Interpretability research is outpaced; control strategies dependent on reading outputs fail silently.

In response, the EU launches the **Enforceable Legibility Standards**, a large category 1 measure targeting high-capability AI in public use. It mandates fallback interpretability methods at critical decision points, aiming to restore auditability. However, technical feasibility is unproven at scale, and industry pushback is immediate. The measure enters the portfolio but yields no immediate benefit.

The **European AI Command Interface** nears completion, but pilots reveal persistent latency from logging mandates. A shift to anomaly-triggered logging is fast-tracked, delaying full deployment. No resilience gain materializes.

The **emergent_open_provenance** trend solidifies, with the open-source framework now adopted by key research institutions. Regulators signal it may satisfy parts of the Open-Weight Accountability Framework, creating a de facto compliance path. Yet legal uncertainty remains, and no safety improvement is recorded.

**ai_safety** plummets to **28.0** due to the opaque_reasoning shock (−10) and no landed safety measure. **resilience** holds at **51.0**, as no new category 6 measure finishes and capability growth alone does not trigger a penalty (rise < 2). **eu_ai_sovereignty** remains at **25.0**: the Sovereign Compute Corridors finished last turn, no new capacity event occurs, and capability rise is under 2.

**eu_political_capital** drops to **17.0**. Portfolio charge: three large measures (−9), priority (−1) = −10. No proposal bonus: the new measure responds to this turn’s opaque_reasoning, not a prior event. **public_sentiment** holds at **37.0**, with no major incident or benefit.
