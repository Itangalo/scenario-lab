### A Narrow Window

The frontier creeps forward to **63.0**, driven by incremental gains in algorithmic efficiency and integration of multimodal reasoning. No leap occurs, but the steady climb continues, reinforcing pressure on safety and resilience.

The EU seizes the interpretability breakthrough, formally launching the **Sovereign AI Safety Stack Initiative**—a high-stakes bid to convert technical insight into sovereign control. The European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking assumes leadership, with ENISA and the European AI Office co-designing certification protocols. Pilots begin in French and German health AI systems, deploying real-time anomaly detection based on the new interpretability standard. The initiative is named the EU’s top priority, reflecting its narrow window of opportunity.

However, political capital remains critically low. The **Emergency Resilience Surge** completes, delivering partial hardening of health and energy systems and a common incident taxonomy. While tabletop exercises improve coordination, procurement delays prevent full deployment—limiting real-world impact. The **European AI Command Interface** also finishes, enabling audit access and pilot integrations, but operational use remains patchy.

Despite the urgency, the COREPER session on fast-tracking AI Act amendments deadlocks. Several member states resist binding commitments to phase out foreign detection tools, citing cost and interoperability concerns. The 70% co-financing offer gains only cautious interest. Still, the redirection of €3.8 billion from the Surge’s final tranche is secured, narrowly avoiding a budget standoff.

Publicly, the Commission opens non-sensitive components of the Safety Stack to open-source development, aiming to build trust. A transparency portal is announced, though not yet live. Early reactions are mixed: tech communities welcome the openness, but civil society groups warn of “certification theatre” without independent oversight.

Labour tensions simmer as AI-assisted freelance collectives grow, demanding a voice in how productivity gains are shared—foreshadowing deeper societal debates.
