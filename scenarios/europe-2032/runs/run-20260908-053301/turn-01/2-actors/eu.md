## New measure
Accelerated Permitting Zones for Strategic AI Infrastructure  
Fast-track environmental, grid, and land-use approvals for data centres and chip fabs tied to EU-controlled AI capacity.

`Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 4`

`Applies to:` EU member states, with binding targets for national permitting authorities

## Priority
**Accelerated Permitting Zones for Strategic AI Infrastructure** — because the cyber_test_shot and supply_chain_coercion events prove we are losing control of both our infrastructure and our strategic autonomy, and without fast-tracked build-out, our sovereignty evaporates regardless of funding.

## In practice
We are activating the legal base of the Critical Entities Resilience Directive and the Digital Decade policy programme to bind national permitting authorities to a new class of “AI Strategic Infrastructure” designation. This will override standing environmental and zoning delays for projects tied to InvestAI Gigafactories or the Tech sovereignty package, provided they meet binding conditions on energy sourcing (100% grid-backed renewables or dedicated nuclear microreactors) and workforce inclusion (minimum 40% local hiring with EU-funded upskilling). The Council’s Competitiveness formation will be asked to adopt this by qualified majority, bypassing deadlock in the Environment Council where Germany and Sweden have previously stalled fast-tracking.

We are negotiating directly with ENTSO-E and national grid operators to pre-allocate transmission capacity for the Gigafactory sites, using the Recovery and Resilience Facility as leverage to require speed over perfection in impact assessments. Resistance is expected from regional governments in Spain and Poland, where land-use rights are fiercely guarded, so we are pairing the measure with an upfront €15bn cohesion-top-up for host regions — not as compensation, but as co-investment, framed under the European Regional Development Fund. This is not just about speed; it is about proving that the Union can build, and that what is built answers to Brussels, not to Mountain View or Beijing.
