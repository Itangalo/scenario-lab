Sovereignty must now mean control over use, not just access to infrastructure. The completion of the Tech Sovereignty Package frees bandwidth, but the crisis is no longer scarcity — it is agency. We must shift from building to commanding.

  - Reason: The release narrows the capability gap, distributing powerful AI beyond containment, which directly challenges infrastructure-based sovereignty and shifts the need to operational control.
