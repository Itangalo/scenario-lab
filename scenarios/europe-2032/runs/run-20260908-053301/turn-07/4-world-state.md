### A Step in Open Power, a Stretch for Control

The AI frontier advanced steadily, with closed systems reaching **59.5**, driven by sustained investment and algorithmic refinement despite US retrenchment. Open-weight models showed no measurable gain in capability, holding at **50.0**, as distributed development continued without a significant leap. The 8.5-point gap with the frontier remains unchanged, consistent with the constitutional assumption that open-weight progress is bounded by and lags behind the leading closed systems unless directly enabled by sovereign compute expansion or policy success — neither of which occurred this turn.

In response, the EU elevated the **Open-Weight Accountability Framework** to top priority, seeking to assert control over the growing open-model ecosystem. However, resistance mounted quickly: developer coalitions organized digital protests, and legal experts challenged the framework’s extraterritorial enforcement. Hungary’s objection to the Resilience Corridors remained unresolved, further straining implementation.

The **InvestAI Gigafactories** programme concluded this turn, delivering the planned sovereign compute infrastructure. This marks a major milestone. While full operational integration has not yet been demonstrated, the completion of construction and procurement qualifies as a capacity event under the rules, unlocking a +5 boost to **eu_ai_sovereignty**, which rises to **22.0**. This reflects the accumulated effect of sovereign compute delivery, consistent with the notepad’s authoritative calculation. Pilots of the **European AI Command Interface** in the Commission and Frontex revealed interoperability gaps across national systems, delaying full deployment. The interface remains in phased rollout, with integration challenges actively being addressed.

Resilience held steady, but **ai_safety** dipped to **38.0** as no safety-focused measure reached completion. Public sentiment declined to **41.0**, reflecting unease over both uncontrolled model proliferation and regulatory overreach, particularly around liability for open-source developers.

Political capital remained flat at **24.0**, as the cost of maintaining three large measures — including the new priority — offset any gains.

### US Posture and Global Tensions

The **RETRENCHMENT** posture continued to dampen global AI momentum, with Washington’s inward focus amplifying European concerns about long-term access to critical AI infrastructure. Meanwhile, rising military activity around Taiwan intensified strategic uncertainty, reinforcing the case for operational AI sovereignty — though no direct disruption to supply chains or development occurred.
