### The Weight of Control

**ai_capability** inches past 60, reaching **60.3**, as algorithmic refinements and distributed training runs across private clusters push the frontier forward. Progress remains steady within known parameters. In response, **openweight_capability** rises modestly to **50.6**, reflecting continued integration of new tooling by open-source communities and incremental gains from the emergent provenance framework. No major model release occurs, but broader adoption of existing high-performance open models on EU-resident infrastructure sustains gradual accumulation—consistent with the invariant that this metric grows only through sustained effort and visible uptake.

The EU’s pivot from building to hardening is tested. The **Sovereign Compute Access and Resilience Corridors** complete their rollout, delivering full redundancy for critical health and energy systems. However, Washington’s **supply_chain_coercion** escalates: ASML servicing restrictions now block maintenance on mature-node lithography machines across Europe, threatening the long-term reliability of EU-resident compute. This constitutes a direct attack on the Union’s operational capacity, triggering a +3 sovereignty boost from completed infrastructure but countered by a −2 penalty under metric rule 5. Net gain: **+1**, raising **eu_ai_sovereignty** to **25.0**.

The **Open-Weight Accountability Framework** formally launches, but its diluted liability standards and broad exemptions weaken enforcement. No safety gain materializes. Meanwhile, **ai_safety** drops to **33.0** as the capability-safety gap widens and audit flaws in live systems go unresolved.

Resilience suffers. The **European AI Command Interface** pilots reveal that mandated logging degrades real-time performance in emergency response and judicial systems. A compromise—sparse, anomaly-triggered logging—is rushed into development, but implementation delays mean no resilience benefit yet. The **Secure Provenance and Auditability Standards** are proposed this turn as a category 4 measure in direct response to both the audit failures and supply-chain coercion, but no implementation has occurred. It does not yet contribute to resilience or enter the portfolio charge this turn, preserving compliance with the one-new-measure limit. The proposal earns a +1 bonus under the proposal rule for addressing two severe, simultaneous external shocks.

Political capital erodes less than feared. The portfolio still carries three large measures (Open-Weight Accountability Framework, European AI Command Interface, Resilient Material Supply Chains) at −3 each, plus the named priority at −1, totaling −10. But the +1 proposal bonus applies immediately upon valid submission of a robust new measure, increasing **eu_political_capital** from 18.0 to **19.0**—partially offsetting the burden without exceeding the cost of active commitments.

Public sentiment softens slightly to **37.0**, as developer pushback and slow benefits fuel quiet frustration. No visible public benefit or scandal dominates the turn.
