## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Operational resilience and material sovereignty for European AI systems

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will not mistake persistence for correctness. When a measure fails to land as intended, we will adapt the strategy, not just the tactics.
  - Grounds: The Enforceable Legibility Standards, launched this turn in response to opaque_reasoning, already face technical unprovenness and industry resistance, while the European AI Command Interface — our priority — is being downgraded from continuous to anomaly-triggered logging due to operational failure. This is not just uncertainty; it is evidence that our current path to oversight is breaking. We must now treat adaptation as part of commitment, not a betrayal of it.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Enduring control over European AI systems — ensuring continuity of operation, auditability of decisions, and immunity from external coercion — even when reasoning is no longer legible, infrastructure is under strain, and trust is thin
  - Grounds: The opaque_reasoning event and cyber_major_incident confirm that control is no longer a function of access or logging, but of resilience under breakdown. The commitment must now explicitly encompass failure modes: when systems are inscrutable, when attacks land, and when public trust frays. This is not a shift in direction, but a deepening of resolve — control must persist not only in ideal conditions, but in the winter of the black box.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
