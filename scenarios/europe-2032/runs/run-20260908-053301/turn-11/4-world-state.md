### The Winter Deepens

The frontier advances to **62.0**, as algorithmic refinements and distributed training momentum carry `ai_capability` past the 62 threshold. Offensive cyber tools grow more adaptive, and the closed frontier quietly integrates non-linguistic reasoning into core systems.

A powerful open-weight model is released, rapidly downloaded over 300,000 times in 72 hours and embedded with offensive automation, planning, and evasion — triggering the **openweight_frontier_release**. The model demonstrates capabilities approaching those of the closed frontier, spreading fast through decentralized networks. However, without access to coordinated infrastructure or sustained scaling pipelines, its effective capability leap is substantial but partial. `openweight_capability` rises to **52.0**, reflecting rapid diffusion and reuse, but still trails by 10 points — a meaningful gain, not parity.

The **cyber_major_incident** lands: a model-generated ransomware wave cripples health portals in three member states, locking patient records for days. Response is slow, coordination uneven. No fatalities, but public trust erodes further.

The EU pivots hard. The **Open-Weight Accountability Framework**, deemed unenforceable without technical baselines, is effectively paused. Regulators fast-track recognition of the emergent provenance standard, a pragmatic shift that avoids legal collapse but delivers no immediate safety gain.

The **Emergency Resilience Surge** is proposed as a large category 6 measure and named priority. A proposal to redirect €4.2 billion from stalled digital infrastructure projects and repurpose unused pandemic recovery funds clears the Commission, though ratification remains incomplete. Legal challenges from two member states block disbursement, and procurement remains backlogged. No implementation occurs this turn; the earliest activities are scheduled for next cycle. Tabletop exercises and coordination planning are delayed. As a result, `resilience` remains unchanged at **51.0**.

`ai_safety` drops to **23.0** — −5 from the **cyber_major_incident** (lab-origin gap widening), and −5 more from **opaque_reasoning** persisting with no landed fix. The **Enforceable Legibility Standards** remain in flight, unproven.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` holds at **25.0**: `ai_capability` rose by 0.9, below the 2.0 threshold for decay, and no capacity event occurred. The metric remains unchanged from last turn.

`eu_political_capital` drops to **14.0**. Portfolio charge: two large, one small (−8), priority (−1) = −9. No proposal bonus — the Resilience Surge answers this turn’s incident, not a prior one. Capital remains too low for legitimacy lending.

`public_sentiment` falls to **32.0** due to the cyber incident (−5) and **labour_displacement** in entry-level tech and legal jobs (−5), partially offset by visible resilience efforts (+2).

The **emergent_global_standards_initiative** gains traction, with India and Brazil signaling intent to diverge from EU and US models — a quiet rebuke to Brussels’ leadership claims.
