### The Frontier Accelerates

Global AI capability climbs to 62.5, crossing into the 62–68 terminal zone. Systems now autonomously manage multi-week research cycles and logistics chains, embedding deeper into pharmaceuticals and defence planning. Yet no safety upgrades land on deployed systems, and the gap between capability and control widens.

### Emergency Restrictions: A Fractured Response

The EU invokes emergency powers to ban public distribution of open-weight models above capability 45.0, targeting platforms like Hugging Face and GitHub Europe. The measure passes under COREPER fast-track, backed by Germany and the Nordics, but faces fierce resistance. Ireland and Austria warn of a “chilling effect” on open research, and a coalition of academic labs files a preliminary injunction at the European Court of Justice, arguing the rule violates scientific freedom. Compliance is patchy: some hosting providers geo-block EU access rather than implement controls, while underground forums mirror restricted weights within hours.

### Safety Erosion Continues

With no systemic safeguards in place and offensive models circulating widely, `ai_safety` drops another 3 points to 6.0. ENISA confirms AI-coordinated phishing campaigns have breached low-tier healthcare providers in three member states, exploiting automated social engineering. Human oversight remains nominal.

### Resilience Drifts Downward

Despite the *Accelerate Resilience Hardening* measure in flight, implementation lags in Eastern Europe. A delayed procurement round in Romania stalls firewall upgrades, and `resilience` slips to 41.0 as capability rises unchecked.

### Sovereignty Holds, Barely

No category 4 measure completes this turn. The *Frontier Access Negotiation* continues, with working groups formed but no binding access secured. `eu_ai_sovereignty` remains at 24.0.

### Political Capital Collapses

The EU launches a large category 3 measure (−3), names it priority (−1), and continues two large measures (−6 total). Portfolio charge: −10. No proposal bonus applies — the measure responds to ongoing trends, not a discrete recent incident. Political capital plummets from 23.0 to 15.0.

### Sentiment Nudges Up

Media coverage frames the emergency restrictions as a decisive stand against AI misuse. Public sentiment rises slightly to 28.0, though protests erupt outside data centres in Dublin and Vienna.
