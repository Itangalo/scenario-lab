## In practice
The emergency restrictions bought us headlines, not security. The Kimi K3 weights are mirrored in basements from Lisbon to Riga. The hyperscalers are complying just enough to avoid fines while slowing audits with legal friction. We are not in control. So we pivot — not away from our two-year commitment, but into it with everything we have.

We are repurposing the €4.2bn crisis reserve originally allocated to enforcement and redirecting 80% of it to stand up the EU Sovereign Inference Grid: a distributed network of hardened data centres across France, Poland, and Finland, hosting models trained under EU jurisdiction or securely ported from allied sources. The legal base is Article 122 TFEU for urgent common interest, invoked jointly by the Commission and the European Council. DG DEFIS is leading procurement, bypassing national bottlenecks with EU-level site designation powers under the revised Critical Infrastructure Regulation.

Germany demands guarantees that its pharmaceutical AI pipelines won’t degrade during transition — we’re offering priority access slots and co-governance of the first node in Hamburg. Ireland resists the erosion of open research — we’re giving Dublin a sandbox zone with full model access, but only if the weights never leave the enclave. This is not a gift. It is a condition of participation.

We are no longer waiting for access. We are building the capacity to act — and to act alone, if we must.
