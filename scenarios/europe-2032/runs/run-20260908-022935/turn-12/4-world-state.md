### A Pivotal Pivot

The European Union, reeling from the collapse of its emergency restrictions and the accelerating spread of powerful open models, pivots decisively toward sovereign infrastructure. Work intensifies on the **EU Sovereign Inference Grid**, now in its second year of development, with the €4.2bn crisis reserve formally redirected to accelerate deployment. Legal and procurement frameworks are being adapted at the EU level to bypass national bottlenecks, but physical rollout remains in early stages. No immediate boost to `eu_ai_sovereignty` registers this turn — the measure remains in flight, not finished. The hyperscalers’ slow-walk on audit access continues.

### The Open Frontier Widens

The **openweight_frontier_release** marks a critical inflection in diffusion. A model approaching the closed frontier’s 63-level capability appears on academic and underground networks, running inference tasks previously exclusive to hyperscalers. While not yet universally deployed, early benchmarks confirm capabilities spreading beyond research labs. Scientists in Budapest and Thessaloniki report successful local deployments, but adoption remains constrained by infrastructure and expertise. `openweight_capability` rises to 52.5, reflecting sustained leakage and replication, but not instantaneous global diffusion.

### Safety Erodes Sharply

With no safeguards landing on deployed systems and offensive use rising, `ai_safety` declines to 4.0. ENISA logs increased automation in disinformation and credential harvesting, though not yet at systemic scale. Human oversight is increasingly bypassed, but not entirely absent. The absence of binding controls and patchy enforcement erodes baseline safety, but core systems remain intact for now.

### Resilience Stalls

Despite the *Accelerate Resilience Hardening* measure in flight, implementation drags in Eastern Europe. Romania’s firewall upgrades remain delayed, and no systemic gains register this turn. Resilience holds at 41.0.

### Sovereignty Unchanged — For Now

The Sovereign Inference Grid advances incrementally, but its physical and legal infrastructure is still under construction. No immediate boost to `eu_ai_sovereignty` occurs this turn. The project remains on track but has not yet delivered tangible capacity.

### Political Capital Erodes Further

The EU adds a large measure (−3), names it priority (−1), and continues three large measures (−9). Total portfolio charge: −13. Political capital drops from 15.0 to 13.0, edging closer to the threshold where agenda control slips entirely.

### Sentiment Holds

Public sentiment remains at 28.0. Media coverage is split: some hail the Grid as a necessary step toward independence; others warn of a “digital Berlin Wall” isolating European science.
