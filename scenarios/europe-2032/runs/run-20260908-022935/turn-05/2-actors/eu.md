## Priority
**Launch Sovereign AI Pilot Programme** — because the election_retrenchment event has weakened our primary external partner and widened the window to build autonomous capacity, and because our current sovereignty level of 21.0 leaves us structurally dependent at a moment when the frontier is accelerating beyond oversight — making self-reliance not just strategic but urgent.

## In practice
We are treating the completion of the Secure Access framework as a necessary but insufficient step: sovereign evaluation is only as strong as the sovereign capability behind it. With the U.S. now inward-looking and the open frontier creeping toward dangerous autonomy, we can no longer outsource our understanding — we must generate it ourselves. The Sovereign AI Pilot Programme launches this turn with a €12bn initial envelope drawn from the InvestAI Gigafactories fund, conditioned on co-location within the accelerated-permitting zones established under the Tech Sovereignty Package. We are tasking a newly convened AI Systems Agency — hosted jointly by the Commission, ESA, and ENISA — to stand up the first instance by Q2 2029, hosted on air-gapped infrastructure in Finland and France, powered by EU-secured H100 clusters and staffed by a rotating cadre of top researchers recruited through a new European AI Fellowship.

This is not about matching the frontier head-on — not yet — but about closing the epistemic and operational gap. The first use case will be an AI-driven cyber defence agent, trained and evaluated under the EUR-AIEC framework, with full interpretability logging and red-teaming by national CSIRTs. We are engaging Germany, France, and Poland this month to form a core implementation group, offering early access to the platform in exchange for fast-tracking national data centre approvals. At the same time, we are revising the Gigafactories timeline: site one in Västerås will now prioritise sovereign stack integration over pure scale, and we are redirecting €3bn from later phases to accelerate secure software tooling and model provenance tracking. This is the beginning of an EU-owned stack — not just compute, but control.
