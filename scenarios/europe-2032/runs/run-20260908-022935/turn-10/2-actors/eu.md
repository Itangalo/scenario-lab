## In practice
The lights are still flickering. Marseille and Vilnius have hardware, but they are idle — no model, no mission. We cannot wait six more turns for sovereign training runs to catch up. The French grid incident was not an outlier; it was a symptom of a deeper rot: we are building sovereign shells while the brains of the systems we rely on are locked behind foreign firewalls, governed by boards we cannot touch. That ends now.

We are launching formal negotiations under a reinforced Digital Markets Act+ framework, invoking Article 28a (Critical Dependency Powers), to compel US hyperscalers operating in the EU to grant non-discriminatory access to their most capable AI systems for EU-certified public operators — energy, health, transport, defence — under strict conditions: on-premise hosting in EU sovereign zones, full red-team audit rights for ENISA, and legally binding continuity-of-service clauses. The Commission is drafting the implementing act this month, with legal backing from the JHA Council and political cover from the European Council’s emergency session in February. We are offering reciprocal access to EU safety research and fast-tracked antitrust clearance for compliant firms — but non-compliance will trigger immediate operational restrictions under the AI Act’s high-risk provisions and parallel scrutiny under the Anti-Coercion Instrument.

This is not a long-term solution. It is a bridge — one we must build while we still have the capital to fund it. Germany and the Nordic states are on board, but Hungary and Slovakia warn of retaliation. We are offering them co-leadership of the audit consortium and first access to the resilience inference cluster in Vilnius. The clock is ticking: if we do not have signed agreements by turn 13, we will have no choice but to invoke emergency licensing to replicate and host frontier models independently — a move that would cost us dearly in transatlantic capital, but one we will make. Sovereignty is not just ownership. It is the right to act, when the moment comes.
