### The Cost of Control

The EU formally redirects €18bn from the InvestAI Gigafactories programme to launch the Sovereign Stack Development initiative, establishing air-gapped training clusters in Västerås and Tampere under strict legal and physical controls. Germany and Poland commit to joint red-teaming nodes, while France agrees to drop its veto demand in exchange for binding deployment constraints—a fragile compromise, but one that holds for now. The AI Systems Agency begins construction of the first sovereign cyber defence agent, designed to operate without external connectivity.

### A Strategic Shift Under Pressure

The redirection marks a decisive pivot from scale to sovereignty. However, the supply_chain_coercion event—Washington forcing ASML to restrict mature-node exports—exposes the fragility of Europe’s position. Though the Gigafactories project formally concludes, its delayed impact and redirected funding yield no immediate resilience boost. With no finished category 6 measure, and rising capability outpacing preparedness, resilience erodes further to 41.0. The public sees no tangible benefit, and sentiment remains stagnant at 34.0.

### Capability Creeps Forward

Despite U.S. retrenchment, global AI capability advances to 58.5, driven by private-sector momentum in Asia and closed labs. Openweight capability remains at 45.0—no new release has crossed the threshold, but the Kimi K3 derivatives continue to enable cyber intrusions. AI safety holds at 18.0; while new assurance layers are planned, they are not yet embedded in deployed systems.

### Political Capital Spent, Sovereignty Gained

The EU completes the Gigafactories measure, gaining +3 to political capital for closure. However, launching a large new category 4 measure costs −3, and maintaining the named priority −1. With no sentiment boost and no proposal bonus, net political capital falls to 32.0. Sovereignty rises to 23.0: the completed Gigafactories contribute +2 (large category 4, finishes t7), the new stack development adds +1 in early momentum, and the supply_chain_coercion event triggers −2 (capacity access reduced). Capability rose by 1.0, not 2, so no additional decay applies.
