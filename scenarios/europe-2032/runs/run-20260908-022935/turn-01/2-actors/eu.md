A binding directive requiring all critical infrastructure operators in energy, water, transport and health to implement AI-driven intrusion detection and response systems, with mandatory red-teaming and cross-border incident reporting.

## In practice
We are launching negotiations in COREPER II on a draft Cyber Shield Directive under Article 114 TFEU, with the Commission’s Joint Research Centre providing technical benchmarks for detection efficacy and red-team frequency. The ENISA budget will be increased by €1.2bn over three years to support deployment and monitoring, funded from the Digital Europe Programme and the Recovery and Resilience Facility top-ups. Member states resist the cost and compliance burden, particularly in Central and Eastern Europe where IT modernisation is behind; we are offering 70% co-financing through cohesion funds conditional on adoption by Q2 2027. National regulators will be required to conduct annual audits, and non-compliance will trigger infringement procedures — but we will also pair enforcement with fast-track access to InvestEU guarantees for qualifying infrastructure upgrades. The Council Presidency has agreed to fast-track discussion in the Competitiveness (Internal Market) formation, though we expect pushback from finance ministries during ECOFIN. We are framing this not as a security imposition but as enabling trust in AI adoption across the economy — a prerequisite for both diffusion and sovereignty.
