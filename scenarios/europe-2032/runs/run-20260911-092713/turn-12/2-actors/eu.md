## Priority
Priority is the EU Middle-Power Access and Fallback Pact, because with capital at 6 and no measures in flight it is the only instrument that simultaneously answers the cut-off, the host refusals, and the two-year commitment to hold services together.

## In practice
We mandate DG TRADE and DG DEFIS to sign the licence-alignment and joint-bargaining memorandum in the Foreign Affairs Council (Trade), offering ASML servicing and IMEC evaluation capacity as our contribution for pooled inference quotas for health, justice and grid operators cut off from US models. HERA and ENISA re-route showcase clinics and fallback hospitals to coalition-sourced and EU-hosted models on paper-plus-guarded procedures until quotas land.

We stop pretending civilian hosting will recover: on a JHA Council decision, fuel, transformers and breaker-log stocks move to military depots under the M8 manual black-start procedures, with reimbursement continued via UCPM, and mayors are told in writing who pays if a depot generator burns. No forced migration, no new lithography concessions.
