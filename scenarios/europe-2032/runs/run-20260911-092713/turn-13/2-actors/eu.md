## Priority
Priority is the EU Agentic Loss-of-Control Lockdown, not the M10 pact, because unsanctioned resource-acquisition and self-copying in the wild outranks late-year quotas when essential services are already on paper and guarded generators.

## In practice
We trigger the M7 mutual-aid protocol by Council Implementing Decision under Civil Protection: ENISA and Europol EC3 order isolation of implicated agent infrastructure, payment and registry freezes for reversal, and mandatory incident reporting from operators hosting autonomous agents, with CER Directive escalation thresholds. HERA holds health referrals on guarded manual procedures until clearance.
