### Frontier Leaps Amid Open Release

Global AI capability advances to 62.5, driven by tighter coordination in agent swarms and faster self-improvement loops. An open-weight release from a frontier lab circulates widely, accelerating deployment of high-performance models. While the release is technically months behind the frontier, its rapid diffusion raises concerns about misuse. However, the underlying capability of open-weight systems reflects only incremental progress from prior accumulation, so openweight_capability rises modestly to 49.0.

### Sovereignty Undermined, Safety Erodes

Despite ongoing efforts, the EU’s access to leading-edge models is abruptly restricted, with several public health and research institutions locked out without explanation. This `eu_frontier_access_denied` event directly undermines operational capacity, triggering a −2 sovereignty hit. Combined with the +1.0 rise in ai_capability, sovereignty falls to 31.0. Meanwhile, the lack of safety assurance on deployed systems—now operating with minimal oversight—drags ai_safety down by 5 to 14.0.

### Response Corps Advances, Labour Fund Launched

The EU AI Incident Response Corps finalises protocols and conducts its first live-fire exercise in the Czech Republic, simulating a coordinated ransomware strike on hospitals. Nine member states appoint liaison officers, but full integration remains partial. The Labour Transition Solidarity Fund launches with pilot pacts in Germany, Spain, and Finland, aiming to cushion AI-driven job displacement. However, pushback emerges in several parliaments over co-investment requirements, slowing uptake.

### Political Capital Crumbles

Political capital drops to 11.0, strained by the portfolio charge of −10 (three large measures at −3 each, plus −1 for priority) and a modest −2 from public sentiment falling below political capital. The proposal bonus for the Labour Fund is judged +4—large, recent, and directly responsive to emergent_labour_unrest—but is insufficient to offset the drain. With capital now below 12, the EU risks losing control of its agenda next turn.

### Sentiment Dips Amid Unrest

Public sentiment declines to 37.0 as graduate protests intensify and media coverage frames AI as a driver of exclusion. The Labour Fund is welcomed but seen as delayed, and the open release of powerful models fuels anxiety. Resilience inches up to 41.0 due to the Incident Corps’ progress, a partial offset to broader fragility.
