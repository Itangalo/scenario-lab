Full activation is delayed until mutual aid clauses are ratified in early 2031. Still, the framework’s partial success boosts `resilience` by +3 to 44.0.

These link displaced legal and research workers to public AI auditing and climate planning roles. But Poland and Hungary resist co-investment rules, forcing temporary flexibility. The move preserves momentum but signals fragility in cohesion.
