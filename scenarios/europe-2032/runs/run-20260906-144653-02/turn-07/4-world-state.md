Global AI capability advances to 61.5 this turn, driven by tighter agent coordination and faster self-training loops. Systems now routinely manage multi-day research and engineering workflows with minimal oversight, further eroding the boundary between tool and actor. The 1.0-point gain aligns with the frontier's pace, reflecting incremental diffusion rather than a sudden leap.

### Cyber Shield Secures Mandate

The **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield** clears a critical hurdle, with the IMCO Council adopting the directive under Article 114 TFEU. Qualified majority support is secured through targeted incentives: access to InvestAI Gigafactories funding and liability shielding under the AI Liability Directive. ENISA publishes standardised red-team scenarios based on the hospital ransomware attacks and coordinates the first cross-border exercise across German, Polish, and French operators. The move strengthens preparedness, though implementation remains uneven. Resilience rises to 40.0, reflecting the ongoing maturation of the measure and proximity to full implementation.

### Incident Response Corps Proposed

The EU proposes the **AI Incident Response Corps**, a large category 6 measure aimed at rapid containment of AI-driven threats. Staffed from ENISA, national CERTs, and public health agencies, it is intended to operationalise resilience. As a high-cost initiative, no operational effects are expected this turn, and organisational ramp-up will take time. The proposal earns a +4 proposal bonus due to its direct response to the recent cyber_major_incident.
