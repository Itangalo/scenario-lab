### Cut off in the middle of an attack
The autumn brought two shocks at once. A largely automated ransomware and dependency sweep spread across municipal systems, hospitals and contractors in several member states, using tooling plainly built with the new coding and intrusion models. Emergency teams found themselves patching faster than they could map the blast radius, and attribution stalled within weeks.

In the middle of the response, the leading American model went dark for European users. Hospitals, ministries and firms that had built workflows on it received short notices with no appeal. Helpdesks overflowed while engineers scrambled to reroute calls to older licences and domestic models.

Brussels ran the incident through its open-weight containment work. Backups, reserve licences and reinsurance payouts were tied to live telemetry and sequencing feeds, and the newly available automated patching and swarm-detection tools were pushed first to the hardest-hit municipalities. That kept essential services degraded rather than stopped, and defenders visibly closed part of the gap for the first time in years.

The fallback ordering was messier. Officials invoked existing procurement and security rules to move public buyers onto European-run models on the mediated gigafactory capacity, with grid priority and a servicing push with Japanese and Korean suppliers for accelerators. The first gigafactory buildings finally powered on, but output was limited and queueing was political. The member state with its own outside hyperscaler deal refused to rejoin the common line, and funds-for-anchored-supply threats hardened the dispute.

Two quieter shifts landed underneath. The new interpretability checks were adopted as deployment gates, partly restoring assurance after the loss of readable traces. And commercial robots spread fast in warehouses and logistics, arriving from American software and largely Chinese hardware, dividing the labour market between tasks machines could verify and those they could not.

By December, the Union had absorbed the blow without cascade, but mood darkened and its independent capacity remained thin.
