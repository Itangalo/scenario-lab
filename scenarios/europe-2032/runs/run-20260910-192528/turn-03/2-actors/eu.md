## New measure
**EU Essential Services Continuity Shield**
Deploys rehearsed ransomware and supply-chain compromise playbooks, offline backups and cross-border mutual aid across health, municipal administration and public services hit by the automated attack.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, and it must come now because the large automated attack proved offensive tooling has outrun defence and defenders are visibly behind, while our two-year commitment to hardened resilience that absorbs shocks requires degradation not stoppage.

## Priority
EU Essential Services Continuity Shield, because containing visible public-service disruption outranked even our sovereign-build direction while attribution lags and defenders are behind.

## In practice
We are tasking DG CNECT with ENISA and the Health Emergency body to push NIS2 Article 11 playbooks into live exercises: immutable offline backups, segmented restores, and SOC mutual-aid shifts covering the hit municipalities and hospitals, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe money to avoid a new budget fight. The Agentic Containment Regime (M4) stays in drafting through the AI Office and JRC testbeds, with finance supervisors first, accepting slipped implementing acts to keep privilege tiers intact.

In parallel we use Competitiveness Council leverage to unblock Gigafactories (M1) and the Tech sovereignty package (M2) siting in Spain and Germany: national-level power-and-water compacts, grid-connection guarantees and community compensation, rather than a parallel compute line. Public-sector adoption gains on queues and permits are cited to hold tolerance for both controls and siting.
