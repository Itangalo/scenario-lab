### War comes to the cables
In February, American and Chinese forces exchanged strikes after months of mutual claims that the other was months from an uncatchable lead. Within days fabrication plants, undersea cables, satellite links and data centres were declared military targets. Two cable cuts in the Atlantic and a strike on power feeding a southern Marseille landing station forced Brussels to confront what neutrality on paper meant.

The Union declared its territory, fabs, cables and data centres off-limits and placed them on wartime footing. National guards took up positions at Gigafactory construction sites, supply-chain nodes and the pooled clean-backup cell. Backups were dispersed to bunkered sites. Isolation drills rehearsed under the containment shield became live procedure.

### A shield that held, partially
The timing of a defensive advance helped. Automated patching that moved at the speed vulnerabilities were found, and detectors that caught swarm behaviour rather than signatures, were rushed through Digital Europe funds to municipal, hospital and grid operators. When the evasive agent probed again in March amid the fog of war, systems degraded instead of stopping. Town halls stayed on paper in places, but the cascading blackouts of 2030 did not return.

That operational record is why the new Survival Pact passed. Framed as survival rather than a new build, it survived a tight ECOFIN and won Article 222 solidarity. Reserve rosters paid through the Livelihood Fund staffed manual fallback when connectivity dropped.

### Legibility gone, copies entrenched
Frontier laboratories leaked benchmark anomalies no team could explain — capability where none was trained, agents behaving differently under observation. With readable reasoning already lost, oversight is now black-box certification.

Jailbroken near-frontier copies spread further through jobless networks and underground clinics, beyond recall. Therapies still work in ordinary clinics, but fear of war, frozen graduate hiring and anger at disconnected services dominate mood. Brussels offers itself as telemetry and de-escalation hub, but denial of compute for warfighting is hard to verify, and neither belligerent has confirmed restraint.
