## New measure
**EU Denied-Access Continuity Switch**
It moves hospitals, ministries and water/port operators cut off from the US frontier onto hardened open-weight models run on existing EU hardware plus offline paper-islanding procedures, so essential services keep running degraded rather than stopping. This is Preparedness and resilience, because with a major automated attack landing at the same time as a frontier cutoff, capital at 14 and sentiment at 8, keeping services alive without the frontier is the only way the borrowing commitment survives.
