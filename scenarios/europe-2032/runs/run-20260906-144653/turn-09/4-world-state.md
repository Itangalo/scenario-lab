Global AI capability inches upward to 62.0 as the frontier stutters but does not stall—incremental gains in efficiency and training stability offset the broader investment slump. Open-weight models advance modestly to 49.0, driven by distributed fine-tuning and toolchain improvements, though no major release widens the gap.

The EU treats this turn as a pivot from infrastructure promises to operational resilience. The **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield** is transposed into national law, with Germany and Poland securing fast-tracked access to InvestAI funding in exchange for binding commitments from energy and health operators. ENISA finalises red-team scenarios based on recent ransomware attacks, enabling member states to begin testing intrusion detection systems. These early drills yield actionable insights, though the first joint cross-border exercise is delayed to the next turn. With baseline defences now legally mandated and early implementation underway, **resilience** rises to 38.0.

The **Tech Sovereignty Package**, while substantially implemented, faces final integration delays in two member states, deferring its formal completion to the next turn. While internal findings inform national security planning, the absence of transparent outcomes prevents a formal close. The measure remains active for one additional turn, and its impact on public trust is deferred.

The delay of the Tech Sovereignty Package prevents its bonus from applying this turn, and no new measure is launched.

Public sentiment holds at 38.0. Labour unrest and institutional resistance to AI-driven planning tools temper enthusiasm, despite legislative progress. The absence of visible public benefit keeps adoption debates fraught.

### The Weight of Unfinished Trust

The EU’s infrastructure backbone is now real—but not yet trusted. Operational defences are advancing, but without public accountability, resilience remains fragile in perception if not in design. One foundational measure’s delay underscores the difficulty of synchronising sovereign ambitions across diverse systems.
