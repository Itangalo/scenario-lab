### The Semiconductor Shock

The blockade around Taiwan halts advanced chip shipments, triggering a global compute crisis. With fabrication lines cut off, AI development everywhere now hinges on existing hardware stocks and jurisdictional control. The EU, holding key upstream positions in lithography and materials, suddenly finds itself both a target and a gatekeeper.

AI capability advances to 62.5 as closed labs leverage self-improving agents in training pipeline optimisation, though progress slows slightly due to hardware bottlenecks. Openweight capability rises to 50.0, as replication efforts intensify and modular frameworks from leaked coordination systems spread across well-resourced research collectives.

### EU Responds with Emergency Measures

The **Critical Infrastructure Resilience Sprint** concludes, achieving partial deployment: 42% of Tier 1 operators now have fallback systems, and red-teaming is institutionalised in major energy grids. The results justify a +3 boost to resilience, lifting it to 48.0. The EU begins folding these protocols into NIS2 enforcement, ensuring continuity.

Amid the crisis, the EU launches the **Emergency Compute Assurance Initiative**, a small category 4 measure aimed at securing existing compute clusters under enforceable jurisdiction. Framed as a reciprocal audit regime, it avoids immediate confrontation with U.S. hyperscalers. Legal anchoring and redundancy upgrades begin in Finland, Ireland, and Poland, backed by €1.8bn in emergency funding.

However, investor hesitation grows. The **Tech Sovereignty Package** continues, but private capital remains cautious, and no new gigafactory groundbreaking occurs this turn.

### Political Capital Holds

Despite the crisis, political capital rises to 42.0. The completion of the resilience sprint delivers +4, offsetting the portfolio charge of −7 (Tech Sovereignty −3, Critical Infrastructure −3, new priority −1). The new measure earns a +3 proposal bonus, judged as a direct response to the Taiwan blockade. Sovereignty gains +1 from the new measure’s early momentum and avoids decay thanks to the event’s counterbalancing effect, ending at 25.0. Public sentiment holds at 34.0, as emergency action tempers fear without reversing deep-seated anxiety.
