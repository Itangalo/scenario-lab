## Priority  
**Edge Sovereignty Acceleration Programme** — because the emergent neuromorphic breakthrough offers a rare window to leapfrog cloud dependency and build sovereign, resilient AI capacity where it matters most: in systems that must keep running even when the network fails.

## In practice  
We are acting on the fact that sovereignty is not just about who controls the cloud, but whether essential systems can function when the cloud is gone. The **Edge Sovereignty Acceleration Programme** launches this turn with €3.2bn in blended funding from the Digital Europe Programme, Horizon Europe, and the Innovation Fund, targeting rapid industrialisation of the newly demonstrated neuromorphic chips. These chips — born from repurposed sensor research and built on EU-controlled materials — can run models at 55.0 capability offline, in low-power environments, and without exposure to remote exploitation. We are directing this capacity toward grid control nodes, ambulance dispatch systems, and backup medical diagnostics — functions where failure is not an option, and where dependence on foreign-hosted AI is a strategic liability.

The European Chips Joint Undertaking is fast-tracking production scale-up, with pilot lines in Dresden, Leuven, and Grenoble prioritised for conversion. We are invoking Article 4 of the Chips Act to coordinate supply and allocate wafers to critical edge deployments, while the AI Office develops a lightweight certification framework for model integrity and fallback behaviour on-device. National regulators are being asked to update NIS2 compliance rules to count neuromorphic hardening as a risk-reduction measure, creating immediate demand. Resistance from hyperscalers is expected — they see edge autonomy as a threat to their cloud dominance — but we are offering a trade: where operators adopt EU-certified edge systems for critical functions, we will count it toward their resilience obligations under the forthcoming AI Resilience Act, reducing their audit burden. This is not anti-innovation; it is pro-survival. We are not waiting for the frontier to slow down. We are building systems that work when it speeds up.
