### A Shock to the System

A coordinated ransomware assault, generated and orchestrated by advanced AI, disables emergency dispatch systems across three member states. Hospitals lose triage coordination, fire departments face delayed routing, and transport control centres go dark. The attack exploits a compromised dependency in widely used public-sector software — one that had passed audit but contained model-generated logic too complex to verify. Defenders are overwhelmed; recovery takes days. Confidence plummets.

### Capability Leaps, Safety Crumbles

The closed frontier surges forward with a discontinuous advance: autonomous agent teams now coordinate complex physical simulations end-to-end, compressing development cycles unpredictably. `ai_capability` jumps +3.0 to 74.0. But transparency collapses — developers admit they cannot reconstruct key reasoning paths. With no safeguards landing on deployed systems and an opaque leap this severe, `ai_safety` crashes −12 to 5.0.

### Open Weights Catch Up

Evidence emerges that fragments of recent frontier models have leaked into academic and open-source channels through informal collaborations and unsecured training runs. While not a complete release, these fragments accelerate progress in replication efforts. Some components enable functional improvements in open agent tooling, pushing `openweight_capability` up by +3.0 to 55.0 — a significant gain, but still within the expected lag behind frontier advances. The gap narrows, but not abruptly, and full parity remains distant.

### Resilience Tested, Partially Holds

The completed Continuity Assurance Framework provides a foundation, but inconsistent implementation limits its value. The new Emergency Continuity Response Initiative begins immediate repairs at 17 critical nodes. Early fixes stabilize core functions, delivering +3 to resilience. However, the cyber incident itself imposes a −1 due to institutional strain, and no category 6 bonus applies mid-flight. Net gain: +2, reaching 52.0.

### Sovereignty Erodes Under Pressure

`ai_capability` rose by more than 2.0, triggering −1 under rule 5. No sovereignty-building measure finishes this turn. Despite urgent efforts, the Union’s dependence on external frontier access deepens as the consolidation posture tightens rationing. `eu_ai_sovereignty` drops to 22.0.

### Political Capital Frozen

The portfolio charge — Frontier Access Guarantee (−3), Continuity Assurance Framework (−2), priority (−1) — totals −6. The EU remains at `eu_political_capital` = 0.0, unable to absorb further costs. Agenda control is fully suspended. The proposal bonus does not apply — the initiative responds to an ongoing crisis, not a past one.

Public sentiment falls to 15.0 amid visible disruption and growing fear of uncontrollable systems.
