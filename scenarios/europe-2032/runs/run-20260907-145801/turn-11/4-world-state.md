### A Fractured Response

The EU scrambles to restore trust in AI systems after emergency aid workers across multiple member states refuse to follow AI-generated triage instructions, citing opaque logic and unaccountable failures during the recent ransomware attack. The strike spreads to civil protection agencies, exposing deep fractures in crisis response and undermining public confidence further. While cross-border technical teams repair air-gapped nodes under the Emergency Continuity Response Initiative, resistance from national operators slows deployment, revealing limits of centralized authority in moments of crisis.

### Capability Creeps Forward

Despite the turmoil, the closed frontier advances steadily. Autonomous agent coordination improves further, enabling more robust simulation of physical infrastructure under stress. `ai_capability` rises +1 to 75.0, entering a zone where systems begin to match strong domain experts across most cognitive professions. No safety safeguards accompany this advance; interpretability remains out of reach, and developers admit they cannot reconstruct key decision pathways in deployed models.

### Open Weights Gain Ground

Leaked model fragments continue to circulate, and open-source communities integrate them into public-sector tools. Some replicate core functionalities of closed models, particularly in simulation and diagnostics. With `ai_capability` at 75.0, `openweight_capability` moves toward the midpoint — now reaching 58.0 — as replication efforts accelerate. The gap narrows, increasing the risk of uncontrolled deployment in critical systems.

### Resilience Inches Up

The Emergency Continuity Response Initiative delivers partial repairs at 17 critical nodes, reinforcing isolation protocols and conducting live failover drills. These improvements yield a +3 boost to resilience. However, the ongoing strike and delayed integration of fallback systems temper gains. No broader category 6 measure finishes this turn, and the incident’s strain imposes no further penalty. Net gain: +3, reaching 55.0.
