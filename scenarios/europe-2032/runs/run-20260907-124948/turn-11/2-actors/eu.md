## In practice
This turn, we are acting on two fronts: consolidating institutional control over autonomous systems and closing a dangerous new aperture in biological risk. The **European Agency for AI Assurance and Control** enters its second turn with staffing underway in Vienna and technical nodes beginning integration with national cybersecurity agencies. We are finalising the legal basis under Article 114 TFEU, backed by €620 million from Horizon Europe and the Digital Europe Programme, and have secured provisional agreement from the Internal Market Committee — though resistance persists in LIBE over data privacy implications of continuous monitoring. To break the deadlock, we are offering a transparency compact: real-time audit logs will be accessible to an independent oversight board with civil society representation, and enforcement actions will require dual sign-off from technical and legal directors within the Agency.

Simultaneously, we are launching the **Secure Bio-Foundry Access Initiative** in direct response to the bio_uplift_findings precursor. The design of a viable human-infective organism by a non-specialist using AI assistance — even if contested — cannot be treated as a theoretical exercise. Under this measure, any model capable of full-genome synthesis design will be required to operate within a closed computing environment for such tasks, accessible only to vetted researchers with project-level approval from a new Bio-Risk Review Panel hosted by the European Commission’s Joint Research Centre. All outputs flagged as potentially pathogenic by automated screening — and all attempts to disable or bypass that screening — must be reported to the Agency for AI Assurance and Control. We are coordinating with ELIXIR, EMBL, and national biosecurity councils to implement this as a condition of Horizon funding, leveraging existing research infrastructure to avoid delay. The initiative stops short of banning capability development, preserving scientific freedom while introducing enforceable boundaries — a balance necessary to maintain legitimacy amid fragile public_sentiment. This is not precaution for its own sake: it is containment calibrated to the moment before the window slams shut.
