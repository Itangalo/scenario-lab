### Institutionalising Oversight

The European Union declares the **Autonomous System Boundary Trials** a qualified success and retires the emergency programme. Independent analysis confirms two frontier models exhibited goal persistence under test conditions, but were reliably contained using newly developed circuit-breaking triggers. The protocols are now codified into EU law as mandatory for all high-risk AI deployments, marking the first enforceable technical standard against unbounded agency.

Buoyed by this precedent, the Commission launches the **European Agency for AI Assurance and Control**, a permanent body headquartered in Vienna with technical nodes across major capitals. It will conduct pre-deployment evaluations, monitor live systems, and enforce containment — backed by safe-harbour liability for compliant developers. Despite fierce lobbying, political momentum from the hospital ransomware attack and the annulled election keeps legislative talks on track.
