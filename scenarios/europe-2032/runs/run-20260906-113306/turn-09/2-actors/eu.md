## In practice
We are in the eye of the storm. The BioShield Network finishes this turn: 172 sequencing nodes are now live across 24 member states, feeding anomaly data into AI models hosted on the EuroHPC pre-exascale systems. The first synthetic biology alerts have been triaged, and response protocols tested in joint exercises with national health agencies. But even this modest success is shadowed by dependence: the models running the analysis are licensed, the updates gated, and the interpretability layers we demanded were never delivered. We cannot debug what we do not control.

That is why the Sovereign AI Core Activation remains our anchor. We are not building a competitor to the frontier. We are building a bunker — a minimal, auditable, EU-run AI stack that can run defensive agents without asking permission. The compute cluster — 10,000 GPUs — is contracted through ASML-linked procurement channels, but delivery is frozen pending the outcome of the Dutch export dispute. We are negotiating with the Commission’s Legal Service to invoke Article 122 TFEU on civil emergency grounds to override national objections, and drafting a derogation under the Digital Markets Act to legally anchor the infrastructure as essential gatekeeper capacity. The model training will begin as soon as we receive access to the defensive telemetry locked behind U.S. cloud contracts — we have offered reciprocal data sharing with DHS under a new EU-US AI Security Pact, but the response remains conditional on our dropping the sovereign stack.

Resistance persists. Germany warns of duplication with private-sector offerings; France demands a military classification that would exclude civilian oversight; industry coalitions file shadow consultations. We are offering them seats on the Sovereign Stack Oversight Board — equal representation for national agencies, civil society, and technical experts — and guaranteed access to the defensive agents once operational. This is not about winning the AI race. It is about surviving the next incident without begging for tools from those who may decide not to help.
