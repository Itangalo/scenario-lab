## In practice
We task ENISA and the Commission DG CNECT with a binding Implementing Act under the NIS2 Directive and CER Directive, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and CEF-Digital money plus EIB resilience loans, to force TSOs, major ports and water operators into segmented OT architectures, 24-hour reporting to a new EU swarm-detection cell, and quarterly cross-border exercises in 2027. France, Germany and the Nordics resist cost-sharing; we buy them with co-financing for municipal utilities and a carve-out linking procurement to EU-trusted vendors.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package running through DG GROW and accelerated-permitting zones, but we explicitly sequence grid-connection priority to defence-relevant hardening sites and condition new data-centre permits on contributing compute to the detection cell. ASML leverage is held in reserve for export-control talks with Washington rather than spent now.
