## New measure
**EU Redress and Incident Ledger for Deployed Decision Systems**
It imposes immediate incident reporting, log-preservation and victim redress on AI decision systems in benefits, policing and courts, closing the gap the scandal exposed.
This and now because the ombudsman finding shows the deployments of 2032 were lawful but wrong — the Act as drawn in 2024 does not reach them — and with open weights now within points of the frontier we cannot restrict our way out; we must make what Europe actually runs readable, reportable and reversible to keep essential services running without the leading foreign model.

## Priority
EU Redress and Incident Ledger for Deployed Decision Systems — because the scandal as a coverage gap, not a breach, makes the AI Act itself look badly drawn for 2024 systems against 2030 deployments, and without a redress answer restriction becomes cheap and all adoption becomes politically impossible.

## In practice
We treat the affair in its second, more damaging reading: the system was never classified high-risk at all, the deployment sat in a gap Annex III does not reach, and everything done was lawful. The Commission therefore proposes under the AI Act review clause a narrow implementing act: mandatory incident reporting to the AI Office, preservation and independent sampling of logs that were generated but never read, and a funded redress window via the Transition Fund, run with national ombudsmen and the Fundamental Rights Agency. No new siting, no new compute spend.

At the same time we hold M7 as the delivery track: DG TRADE/CNECT close the reciprocal licence-alignment letters for essential-service inference, while ENISA/HERA and hospital CIOs extend automated patching, swarm detection and the new interpretability checks to the older and open-weight fallback models now permanently on EU servers after this half-year's open-weight frontier release. Legal Service keeps both shells heated and drained and shifts works to the unblocked site under the water-use injunction, and communications stays on the two EU-hosted regions that cut waits, to show something that worked while redress is built.
