## Priority
M6 EU Entry-Level Transition and Fraud Victim Fund, because with capital at zero and sabotage spreading it is the only instrument still paying out that keeps municipalities staffed and prevents hostility hardening into permanent veto on siting.

## In practice
We run M6 as the shield for the gigafactory shells: DG EMPL accelerates voucher and operator payouts to the affected construction regions, and we condition municipal top-ups on hosting protection — funded liaison officers, water-compensation packages, and joint patrols with grid operators around interconnect sites. No new siting decision is forced this semester; the two poured sites are mothballed warm with permits extended via the Competitiveness Council.

We contain the breach without a new law: the Commission keeps the defecting hyperscaler deal inside joint-procurement reporting and re-export review, offering access to pooled cyber kits and Transition Fund tranches if no further side letters are signed, while ENISA and HERA finished capacities handle sabotage response and detection with existing mandates. Public communication stays on absorption-without-foreign-control — fraud reimbursement fixes and protein-design countermeasure work — not on sovereignty we cannot currently build.
