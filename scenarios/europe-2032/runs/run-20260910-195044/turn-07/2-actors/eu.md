## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Transition and Fraud Victim Fund**
It provides rapid income bridging, retraining vouchers and municipal staffing top-ups for the narrow displaced cohorts in routine coding, analysis and tier-one support, plus reimbursement for AI-fraud victims, paid from repurposed cohesion and ESF+ funds.
Why this and why now: with political capital at 2.0 and public sentiment at 10.0 after the bio weeks, job cuts and fraud wave, no large sovereignty or regulatory build can move; a small category 7 labour and social protection measure is the only instrument that can rebuild tolerance to act while holding our absorb-without-foreign-control commitment.

## Priority
M6 EU Entry-Level Transition and Fraud Victim Fund, because without arresting hostility and municipal breakdown no sovereignty, safety or resilience instrument can be enforced or sited.

## In practice
We run this through EPSCO and the Competitiveness Council as a cohesion reprogramming decision, not a new law: DG EMPL redirects existing ESF+ and cohesion envelopes to works-council-registered retraining for the hit entry-level cohorts, with HERA and ENISA finished tools continuing bio and cyber containment without new mandates. Municipalities that received software without staff get funded seconded operators for the detection cell and patching rollout.

We contain the member-state side-deal by keeping it inside joint procurement reporting and re-export approval, offering the defecting capital accelerated access to the Transition Fund and pooled cyber kits in exchange for no separate hyperscaler data-transfer concession. On research, we publicise where the verifiable-advance breakthrough landed without claiming sovereignty gain from it, using it to show finished EU programmes still produce evidence while we rebuild permission to build.
