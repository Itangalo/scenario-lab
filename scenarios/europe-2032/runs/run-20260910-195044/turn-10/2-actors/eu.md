## In practice
DG CNECT, ENISA and HERA continue the cut-off triage as the operating line: ward-by-ward rewiring to older versions and open weights already on EU servers, manual procedures where no fallback exists, grid recertification and patrols for the shells paid from standing budgets. The two shells stay warm under extended permits; no machine order, no new siting fight, no breach case against the separate hyperscaler capital.

We spend political attention where it is free: directing communications, mayors and health agencies to document and publicise the adoption_success cases — waiting lists fallen, decisions in days — as European-procured, EU-hosted delivery, and clearing the slowest fraud-reimbursement queues from existing Transition Fund lines to stop town halls bleeding further. The aim is to turn degraded continuity plus one visible benefit into the capital to restart under the two-year commitment: a Europe that can keep essential services running without the leading foreign model.
