## New measure
**EU Middle-Power Resilience and Access Pact**
Aligns EU export-licence, compute-bargaining and shared evaluation capacity with middle-power supply-chain holders to secure fallback access and joint defence, while deploying the new cyber-defence and interpretability gains onto already-deployed fallback systems.
This and now because the cutoff has already happened, the court freeze blocks the domestic-build route this half-year, and the coalition window plus two genuine technical breakthroughs offer the only fundable path to keep essential services running without the leading foreign model — our two-year commitment.

## Priority
EU Middle-Power Resilience and Access Pact — it is the only measure in flight and the only route that restores access and assurance without new siting or unreadable-model dependence.

## In practice
We mandate the Commission (DG TRADE + DG CNECT) with a Council mandate to sign reciprocal licence-alignment and mutual compute-access letters with the coalition partners, using the Anti-Coercion Instrument as the backstop, and task ENISA and the EU AI Office to pool evaluation access so fallback models in hospitals and ministries get common certification. The offer to partners is coordinated lithography and chemicals leverage in exchange for guaranteed inference capacity for essential services.

At home we spend no new siting capital: HERA, ENISA and hospital CIOs roll the automated-patching/swarm-detection tooling and the new interpretability checks onto the older/open models already on EU servers, funded from standing Cyber Shield and Transition Fund lines, while Legal Service manages the injunction freeze by shifting dewatering works to the unblocked shell and keeping both shells warm. Communications stays on the two-region waiting-list success to contain patrols and petitions until the pact lands.
