## New measure
**EU Visible Benefit Adoption Pact**
It turns the half-year's two successes — EU-hosted triage that cut waits and new tailored therapies reaching ordinary clinics — into attributed European delivery, while ring-fencing embodied-AI deployment to logistics tasks the Union can absorb.
This and now because with open weights unrecallable, sovereignty at 3 and sentiment at 0, the only capital left to earn is proof that European decisions deliver care faster; medical breakthrough plus adoption success is a window to re-attribute benefit to Europe before dependence and the fraud scandal define it.

## Priority
EU Visible Benefit Adoption Pact — because redress alone cannot lift sentiment from zero, and only visible, European-attributed clinical benefit outranks the ledger work this turn.

## In practice
We run the Pact through DG SANTE and HERA with hospital CIOs in the two showcase triage regions plus three volunteer systems: EU procurement preference for EU-hosted inference for triage notes and tailored-therapy pathways, with digital signatures and incident-logging from M8 built in, funded by re-scoped EU4Health and Horizon clinical-translation money. Communications names the European decision — hosted in Europe, audited in Europe, redress in Europe — not the foreign model underneath.

At the same time we hold M8 as the trust floor: AI Office incident reporting and FRA/ombudsman redress stay the condition for any adoption funding, and ENISA extends patching and swarm detection to the clinical open-weight stack. On embodied AI we do not build: EPSCO and DG GROW issue logistics-only deployment guidance with social partners, steering robots to picking/sorting/palletising where displacement can go through retraining, and keeping care/repair/construction manual, to avoid a second benefits-style backlash.
