## In practice
We run autumn as payout-plus-work: DG EMPL and ECFIN front-load M11 wage insurance and vouchers in the two fast states and the hardest-hit metros, and channel claimants into the new Corps on municipal payrolls seconded to clinics, utilities and employment offices. UCPM, ENISA and the Health Emergency cell direct them to finish M10 kit installation, drill offline fallback, and swap the whispered foreign prompt folders for signed, vetted European aids on isolated stacks.

On leverage we hold the M12 line without spending new capital: TRADE keeps the anti-coercion examination open, the Competitiveness Council pools spares and servicing for legacy tools, and we tell Washington alignment still needs co-decision and verification access — while quietly picking up cancelled GPUs, power reservations and half-fitted sites at distress prices for public-anchored use under existing Gigafactory titles, not a new programme. Labour and health ministers own the cameras; finance ministers own the state-aid clearance for the distress buys.
