## In practice
We keep delivery first. ENISA and HERA push the patching stack and fill-finish doses through M13 municipal crews to hospitals and utilities, with EPSCO/Health Council owning free-clinic distribution and swapping informal foreign prompts for signed aids on the same visits. DG TRADE keeps lithography pooling and manufacturing-rights demands as the bargaining cover.

On Washington we act as a state. Council mandates the Commission/EEAS to seek a classified-access and supply agreement: assured compute/model access on EU soil, EU evaluator access and incident-notification, in return for lithography spares, energy/siting for allied capacity, and alignment on export and bio-risk red lines. No new build is stood up here; the Gigafactories record stays the compute line.
