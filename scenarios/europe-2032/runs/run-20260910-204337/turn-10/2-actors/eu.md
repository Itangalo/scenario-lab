## In practice
We hold the line on certification: AI Office and JRC keep the short-contract certifiers embedded in late communes and the two hospital pilots, with Digital Europe cover funding extended, and no new inspection burden on small firms beyond pre-connection checks. DG GROW and DG EMPL push EU-leased trusted-assistant capacity where backups are already clean, using pooled safety cases to cut paperwork that deterred SMEs.

On bio, we do not open a new instrument we cannot pay for. HERA and ECDC are tasked to log the genome-model claim, tighten passive detection reporting, and draft options for a bio-detection surge to be launched once M10 finishes. EEAS continues the low-cost offer of patch and assurance data for observer access, and Finland/Spain shells proceed on permits and power only.
