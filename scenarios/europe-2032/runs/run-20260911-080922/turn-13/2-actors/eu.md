## New measure
**EU Municipal Restoration and Hardening Cells**
It puts joint ENISA–EuroHPC repair teams into hit municipalities to restore services in days and leave hardened backups behind, then tours the public-sector waiting-list wins as proof European-run AI works.
Why this and why now: the automated attack demands visible restoration before defiance hardens, and the adoption success is the only permission we have left to spend — with capital at 4 we can afford one small resilience build, not a new sovereignty build.

## Priority

## In practice
We do not open a new law or levy. The Commission uses Civil Protection Mechanism and Digital Europe redeployments via the Competitiveness and JHA Councils to fund French-Dutch-ENISA cells attached to EuroHPC reservation desks, making kill-switch and offline-backup checks the condition for restoration cash — enforced where large operators already comply, negotiated with mayors where paper fallback persists.

We spend the politics on attribution and proof: AI Office incident reporting names model-generated tooling without premature attribution, while Health Council ministers tour the waiting-list and triage sites kept running on EU-hosted open models to separate everyday augmentation from rogue agents. The Middle-Power Compact stays warm but gets no new pooled-build promise we cannot cash.
