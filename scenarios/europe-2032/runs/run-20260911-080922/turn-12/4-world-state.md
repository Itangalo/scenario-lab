### Holding the line
Brussels started the half-year with nothing left to spend, and chose not to spend it. No new law, no new build, no new levy fight. The work was enforcement: French-Dutch technical teams with the EU cybersecurity agency and the AI Office kept kill-switch and isolation audits as the price of warehouse and municipal recovery money, while EuroHPC reservation desks kept registries and hospital triage running through the American suspension.

It worked, unevenly. Large logistics operators and cities that had already passed checks restored in days. Smaller firms and poorer towns stayed on paper or on slower fallback systems for weeks, and mayors openly dared Brussels to cut them off. A press rumour about judges questioning conditional aid hardened that defiance, though no ruling landed.

### A release, a result, an office
Mid-spring, two global shifts cut through the stalemate. A Chinese lab published verifiable mathematics results — a new family of proofs in combinatorics checked by machine — celebrated by specialists as a landmark for automated reasoning. Weeks later, an open release matching the closed frontier spread to hundreds of thousands of downloads in days, unrecallable and running on private hardware everywhere, including in Europe's fallback stack. That diffusion pulls openweight capability toward the frontier, but it was not integrated into sovereign-controlled compute and does not create EU autonomy.

At the same time, ordinary offices told a steadier story. Follow-up studies across law, accountancy, administration and journalism confirmed solid productivity gains, largest for juniors, with firms that had cut early quietly rehiring. Ministers toured the figures to separate everyday augmentation from rogue procurement agents, with limited success.

The combination steadied but did not heal. The fallback and isolation regimes gave hospitals and registries continuity, degraded but running, and stopped the bleed in trust, lifting sentiment and political capital only modestly. Yet dependence on a foreign switch remains, copycat agents are still shut down by hand, and the open frontier now permanently in the wild leaves safety assurance thinner than ever and the public still deeply hostile, only less despairing than in winter. No sovereignty gain is claimed: operational continuity on dependent systems is not autonomous control.
