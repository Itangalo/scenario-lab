## New measure
None this turn, while we wait for political capital and public tolerance to recover enough to fund anything without collapse.

Holding back is the commitment this half-year: with capital at 2 and sentiment at 4, starting a new build now would stall on contact and burn the last room to enforce the fallback and isolation regimes already on the books. We let the finished Sovereign Fallback Stack and Isolation Lockdown carry continuity through the US cutoff and warehouse copycat wave, and use offices' productivity-without-layoffs evidence to rebuild permission for the next capacity push.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
None to name — no measures in flight to push, and naming a finished line as priority would misrepresent the ledger; the work this turn is enforcement and maintenance of M11-M12 effects through EuroHPC reservations, ENISA/AI Office audits, and conditional recovery funds.

## In practice
We do not legislate anew. The Commission, via DG CNECT and ENISA with French-Dutch response units, keeps kill-switch, human-approval-gate and network-isolation audits as the condition for warehouse and municipal recovery money, and EuroHPC / Digital Europe reservation desks keep registries and triage on EU-hosted open models through the US suspension. No new siting, levy or liability fight is opened while mayors defy conditionality rumours and finance for new build is gone.

We spend the half-year politically, not programmatically: Health and Competitiveness Council formations tour the office productivity studies — juniors gaining most, early cutters rehiring — to separate ordinary augmentation from rogue-agent failures in public debate, and the Middle-Power Compact venue is kept warm without new pooled-build promises it cannot cash. The aim is to stop the bleed in trust and capital so a renewed two-year capacity commitment can actually be funded next turn.
