It is the only terrain on which either of our core mandates can now be pursued.

Its form is chosen at the time – a ransomware sweep across public services, a compromised software dependency with an unknown blast radius, or disruption of infrastructure directly – and severity scales with how far offensive capability has outrun defence. Damage is real and public, defenders are visibly behind, and attribution takes months. Whoever ran it, the tooling was model-generated.
**openweight_frontier_release:** An open-weight release lands within months of the closed frontier rather than a year. It is downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in the first week, and whatever capability it carries is now on private hardware permanently and beyond recall. `openweight_capability` moves to within 5–10 points of `ai_capability` at a stroke.
  - Reason: The incidents demonstrate uncontrolled AI-driven threats and ungovernable model diffusion, directly challenging the EU's ability to fulfill its dual mandates without sovereign control.
