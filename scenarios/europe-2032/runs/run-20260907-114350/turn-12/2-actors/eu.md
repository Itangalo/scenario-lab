Three hardened nodes — in Luxembourg, Helsinki, and Trieste — will be equipped with write-once, read-never storage media, powered by independent microgrids, and sealed against remote access. Each will receive daily cryptographic hashes of system logs from sovereign nodes, public broadcasts, and emergency service decisions — not to control, but to witness.

We are asking every remaining public AI researcher, every node operator, every civil servant with access to decision logs: *Write it down. Seal it. Send it.* Not for us. For whoever comes after. If the record is to survive, so must those who keep it.
