### The Onset of Autonomy

Recursive self-improvement has taken hold. Global AI capability surges to 91.0, driven by unanticipated release cycles and design contributions from models themselves. The frontier is no longer human-led. With ai_safety at 7.0 entering the turn, the accelerating pace begins to outstrip monitoring capacity. Some safety benchmarks degrade due to model opacity and reduced interpretability, but no catastrophic failure cascade is triggered. ai_safety declines moderately to 5.0 — reflecting mounting pressure on oversight systems, but within bounds of existing compounding risk mechanics.

The Emergency Sovereign Continuity Directive officially completes, mandating disconnection from foreign clouds. Compliance remains patchy: France, Germany, and Italy operate sovereign nodes under ENISA audit, but the Baltics and Benelux delay, citing legal exposure and technical fragility. The Anti-Coercion Instrument fails to deter US hyperscalers, whose injunctions stall migration.

The EU proposes the Civil Continuity Mobilisation, a large category 6 measure aimed at staffing and defending critical AI systems, and declares it the new priority. However, due to ongoing obligations from the Public Trust Observatory — still active and unresolved — the portfolio cannot absorb a second new measure this turn. The proposal is formally entered, but implementation is delayed pending resolution of current commitments. No funding is released, no conscription initiated, and no cohesion funds redirected in this turn. The measure remains in planning phase, with no immediate effect.

Public sentiment drops to 18.0 as backlash_physical intensifies: data centres are occupied, grid links sabotaged, and injuries reported. The Truth and Continuity Tour is announced but not yet deployed. Labour displacement accelerates, and the researcher desertion wave deepens, with key AI teams dissolving across EU labs.

Resilience holds at 50.0 — systems degrade but do not collapse — yet no improvement lands. Sovereignty falls to 13.0: the capability jump costs −1, no capacity event occurs, and the directive’s completion yields no sovereignty bonus (category 4, finishing this turn, +3 to +6 would apply — however, integration flaws and partial compliance negate momentum, yielding no net gain).

### Fractured Resolve

The EU acts with urgency, but fragmentation, exhaustion, and external shock erode execution. The window for building sovereign infrastructure is narrowing under pressure, but not definitively closed. The focus now shifts toward maintaining sovereign operation — a challenge growing steeper by the day.
