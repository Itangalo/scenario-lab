## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Transition Fund**
Provides EU-co-funded wage insurance, rapid retraining vouchers and hiring incentives for entry-level roles in law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration, paid partly by automating employers.
This is Labour and social protection, because graduate hiring has stopped while gigafactories and Shield absorb capital, and without visible absorption the Union loses the tolerance to build anything else.
