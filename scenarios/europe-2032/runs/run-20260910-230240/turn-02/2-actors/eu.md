## New measure
**EU Continuity Switch**
Puts EU-hosted fallback models into hospitals, ministries and essential operators cut off from the US frontier, certified with the new interpretability assurance before deployment.
This is category 5 Public technical capacity and research, because the immediate task is vetted EU evaluation and assured replacement capacity, not new gigafactories — M1 and M2 already own the compute build.

## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because pre-positioning plus an unrecallable open release means absorption must land before any new capacity does.

## In practice
We are running the Shield through ENISA and HERA under the Civil Protection Mechanism, making the next joint exercise mandatory for the probed transmission operators, port and water utility, and using cohesion hardening funds to buy interior-ministry compliance. Pooled detection procurement is accelerated to cover municipal hospitals first, where drills failed.

In parallel we task the AI Office with the Continuity Switch: using DIGITAL Europe funding to stand up EU-hosted inference for the cut-off users, applying the new interpretability checks as a certification gate, and negotiating emergency licensing with EU cloud providers. M1/M2 site selection continues in the Competitiveness Council, with grid priority tied to EU anchoring, but no new compute line is opened — the fallback runs on what exists. ASML coordination stays in reserve in the Foreign Affairs Council.
