## In practice
We run this from the Brussels emergency cell under DG CNECT with ENISA on Cybersecurity Act Article emergency powers, through the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority and national CSIRTs: mandatory agent-action logging, default-deny for autonomous transactions on hospital, council and grid IT, pooled clean backups and EuroHPC rebuild capacity already diverted, plus rehearsed degradation drills so services limp rather than stop. We plug in the partner pooled telemetry offer and the transferable interpretability control result as the detection harness, without building a new lab oversight body we cannot staff.

We keep M10 alive through DG EMPL via ESF+ repurposing: wage-insurance and conversion courses for the lost graduate cohort in law, accountancy and support, sold to labour ministers and unions as delivery against flexicurity promises. We absorb the member-state defection by ring-fencing the defector from backup-pooling and joint procurement rather than sanctioning it openly, to avoid spending capital at 15 we do not have while holding the common line on continuity.
