### The Blind Spot Strikes

A wave of automated ransomware cripples municipal services across five member states, exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities in legacy grid software. The attack, later traced to model-generated exploit chains, exposes a critical gap: defenders lack visibility into the tools being used. Attribution remains uncertain months later, but the damage is clear—public trust erodes, and emergency response systems are overwhelmed. The incident underscores the growing mismatch between offensive AI capability and defensive readiness.
