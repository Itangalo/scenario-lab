This is not a simulation. We are training a model from scratch on EU-controlled data, with real-time interpretability logging, enforced weight security, and a red-teaming protocol that begins before the first checkpoint. The Sovereign Model Development Initiative is no longer a plan — it is a live programme.

The Frontier Compute Access Compact has failed because it asked for transparency from those who profit from opacity. We are not making that mistake again. We are building the capacity to act without permission — because permission will not be given.
