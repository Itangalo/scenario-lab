### The Weight of Action

The EU declares a pivot from crisis response to sovereign capability, launching the Sovereign Model Development Initiative with the activation of the Extremadura exascale node. Under joint civilian-military oversight, the first training run begins on EU-controlled data, with mandatory interpretability logging and red-teaming protocols. A €42bn reallocation from the InvestAI Gigafactories fund secures a closed, hardened network—marking the first time the Union operates a frontier-scale AI stack it fully controls.

### Fractured Backlash, Fragile Unity

Protests against AI infrastructure escalate into physical confrontations, with occupied data centre sites and damaged grid connections reported across France, Germany, and Spain. Policing the unrest drains resources and fuels resentment. The backlash, while not coordinated nationally, reflects a deepening public anxiety over job losses and opaque technological control. Trust in AI-mediated governance continues to erode.

### Resilience Gains, Safety Stalls

The Emergency Resilience Reinforcement Programme concludes, delivering rapid cyberdefences to critical sectors. Incident response times improve, and automated failover systems prevent cascading failures in healthcare—absorbing the next wave of AI-driven attacks. Resilience rises as a result. Yet no improvement touches ai_safety: the frontier’s opacity remains, and the EU’s new logging regime applies only to its own systems, not the foreign models still dominant in the market.

### American Posture Looms

The U.S. administration formally takes office, adopting a posture of **CONSOLIDATION**. Access to frontier AI is now tiered and subject to national security review—locking in structural dependency for the EU. The move increases pressure on European sovereignty, with American labs rationing model access and telemetry.

### Political Capital Bleeds

Despite high ambition, the EU’s political capital declines. The portfolio remains overloaded, and the new large measure—now the priority—fails to generate momentum. Public sentiment drops further, undercutting legitimacy.
