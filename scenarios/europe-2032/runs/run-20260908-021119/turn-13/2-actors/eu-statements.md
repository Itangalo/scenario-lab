  - Trigger: The `ai_investment_collapse` and `election_annulled` events, combined with `eu_political_capital` at 12.0 and `public_sentiment` at 18.0
  - Grounds: The collapse in private investment changes the strategic landscape: the frontier is no longer accelerating unilaterally beyond reach, creating a rare window to consolidate sovereign capacity without being outpaced. Simultaneously, the annulment of an election on grounds of information manipulation confirms that the threat is no longer hypothetical—it is structural and already degrading the legitimacy of governance. Our previous commitment focused on defence and resilience, but it did not compel integration across sovereignty, public legitimacy, and control. Now, we must bind them: without trust, resilience fails; without sovereign infrastructure, control is illusory; without visible public benefit, no policy survives politically. This shift acknowledges that the crisis is not only of capability or security, but of consent.
  - Evidence quoted: **ai_investment_collapse:** Capital flees the sector. Valuations reset hard, announced build-out is cancelled rather than delayed, and several of the arrangements European compute was depending on evaporate with it. What the frontier laboratories can afford to train shrinks for the first time.
**election_annulled:** An election in an established democracy is postponed, rerun or annulled with explicit reference to manipulation of the information environment. Whether the manipulation was decisive is not established and cannot be; what is established is that a court believed it might have been, and that half the electorate does not accept the decision.
  - Reason: The investment collapse undermines EU compute access, and the annulled election proves AI threats to democratic legitimacy are real, directly challenging the foundations of the original statement.
