We are redirecting a portion of the InvestAI fund to pre-position secure data vaults and recruit red-team leads through the European Defence Agency’s emerging AI security track. The Frontier Compute Access Compact is under renegotiation: we are now demanding verifiable containment logs as a condition of fast-track deployment, knowing full well no lab can currently comply. This is not sabotage—it is pressure to force transparency. France and Germany remain resistant, but we are offering them joint leadership of the Sovereign Model’s evaluation board in exchange for support. The legal service is preparing a fallback under critical infrastructure law to mandate reporting if the AI Act fails. We are not abandoning oversight—we are rebuilding it on a foundation of power, not permission.
