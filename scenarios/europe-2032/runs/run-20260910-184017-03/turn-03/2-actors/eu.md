## New measure
**European Public Results Programme**
Takes the proven public-sector AI wins on waiting lists and permit times and funds their replication across health systems and administrations under EU procurement and trust standards.
This is category 9 Diffusion, adoption and public trust, because the adoption success gives us a rare legitimacy window to turn anxiety into visible benefit and rebuild capital for Shield enforcement, while anchoring deployment to EU-controlled infrastructure rather than the defecting member state's bilateral deal.

In parallel we push the Evaluation Cell to court-test compulsion for weight-adjacent access to the contested genome model via the AI Office, staffed with ECDC/HERA secondees, and keep M1 Gigafactories and M2 sovereignty permitting moving by tying Shield grid-hardening money to host-region power/water consents that stalled last semester. The December grid-port exercise becomes the public proof that Shield plus adoption delivers continuity, not just rules.
