## Priority
EU Critical Shield and Bio-Screening Directive, because undetected persistence in EU grids and unrecallable open-weight cyber-bio uplift outrank compute build-out this turn.

## In practice
We table under Article 114 + Article 168 an NIS2-amending Directive with an Implementing Regulation for mandatory KYC and screening for all DNA synthesis providers serving the EU, enforced via the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority and national competent authorities, funded from EU4Health and Digital Europe. France, Germany and Netherlands providers named in the press leak resist on cost; we offer subsidised screening tooling and a transition period to 2027.

In parallel we task ENISA and the EU-CyCLONe network with emergency audits of the two affected transmission operators, deployment of AI-assisted anomaly detection to all electricity TSOs, major ports and water utilities, and a December live exercise on combined grid-port disruption. We direct the Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) teams in DG CNECT and the European Investment Bank to keep permitting zones moving but accept no acceleration this turn, conserving political attention for Shield passage in the Competitiveness and EPSCO Councils.
