## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
It hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with mandatory segmentation, AI-swarm detection and exercised cross-border mutual aid.
This first because the autumn intrusion proved detection would not have caught weeks-long presence in EU grids, and the defensive breakthrough gives us a tool that actually shifts the offence-defence balance now, while sovereignty build-out takes years. Category 6 Preparedness and resilience.

## In practice
We are pushing the Shield through the Energy and Transport Councils under the CER and NIS2 legal bases, tasked to ENISA and the EU-CyCLONe network with money from Digital Europe and the Civil Protection Mechanism. TSOs in the two affected member states become first adopters of automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection from the breakthrough, with mandatory incident retrospectives shared EU-wide. Resistance comes from operators fearing cost and downtime; we offer co-funding and liability cover tied to participation in autumn 2027 live exercises.

In parallel we keep the two inherited lines alive: InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) moves through site selection and state-aid clearance for four to five sites, and the Tech sovereignty package (M2) advances accelerated-permitting zones and grid connections to unlock the €200bn private pipeline. We use ASML leverage quietly to buy time with Washington while refusing a discriminatory carve-out, and we start AI Act general-purpose guidance so 2027 enforcement has teeth without re-opening the law.
