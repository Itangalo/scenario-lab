## New measure
**EU Short-Time to Retraining Guarantee**
It converts short-time work in AI-hit factory and supplier towns into paid retraining plus wage insurance tied to automating employers, run through national employment agencies with EU top-up.
This now because with open weights at 62.2, sentiment at 27 and capital at 19, the care scale-up alone cannot hold towns where work itself is disappearing, and without a visible jobs bridge both resilience and permission to govern collapse.
