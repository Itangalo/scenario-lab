## New measure
**EU Incident Reporting and Redress Clearinghouse**
It creates mandatory reporting of serious AI harms and near-misses to a single EU body with public summaries and a redress fast-track for wrongful automated decisions in welfare, policing and courts.
This now because the automated-decision scandal has put the AI Act itself on trial and without a credible reporting and remedy channel both enforcement and public trust collapse, while the investment bust and open-weight leak make central learning from incidents our cheapest resilience lever.
