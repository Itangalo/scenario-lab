Our goal is to have live red-team evaluations running on at least two major open releases by the end of this turn, feeding threat intelligence directly into ENISA’s Cyber Emergency Response Team. If we succeed, we may finally see what is loose before it is used. If we fail, we will have confirmed that even urgent measures cannot overcome the inertia of a Union frozen in political capital at 9.0 — and that the open frontier has already outpaced us.
