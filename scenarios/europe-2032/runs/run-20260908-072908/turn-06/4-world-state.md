### A Fractured Response

The EU completes two major initiatives: the **Biological Threat Detection and Response Network** becomes operational, with real-time genomic surveillance active across 22 member states, and the **Tech Sovereignty Package** formally concludes after securing €200 billion in private investment commitments and establishing accelerated permitting zones. However, the broader strategic picture deteriorates.

Global AI capability stalls at 59.5 as the **ai_investment_collapse** slashes frontier development budgets. Private labs cancel training runs, and several European compute partnerships dissolve overnight. While this slows `ai_capability` growth, it also severs access routes the EU relied on, deepening dependency anxiety.

In response, the Commission prioritises the **Sovereign AI Inference Grid**, accelerating legal anchoring and chip supply talks. Yet ASML and STMicroelectronics signal delays, citing export bottlenecks and US-aligned controls. Spain’s energy-related holdout persists despite grid credit offers, weakening cohesion. The priority yields no acceleration — momentum is lost to supply inertia.

Simultaneously, the **Emergency Labour Transition Fund** launches amid rising social strain. Member states dispute eligibility rules, and Germany rejects the automation levy as distortionary. Only five countries begin disbursement by mid-year. The measure lands as reactive, not transformative.

Public sentiment dips further, now at 30.0, as media highlight stalled careers and protest activity near data centres grows. Resilience improves slightly (+2) due to bio-network operationalisation and sustained cyber defences, but no gain offsets the political cost.

`ai_safety` holds flat — no new safeguards emerge, but neither does a crisis. `openweight_capability` rises moderately (to 50.0), reflecting broader open replication of pre-collapse models now widely distributed, though constrained by lingering infrastructure gaps and licensing fragmentation.

The US election outcome is confirmed: a president favouring alignment takes office. **US_POSTURE: ALLIANCE** is now in effect, shaping early diplomatic overtures and regulatory coordination expectations.

### Setbacks Mount

The convergence of stalled sovereignty efforts, weak labour response, and eroding public trust drains political capital. Despite finishing two large measures, gains are outweighed by ongoing costs and fragmentation.
