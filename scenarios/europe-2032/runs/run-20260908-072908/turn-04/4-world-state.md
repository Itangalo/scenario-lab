### A New Front in Autonomous Harm

The release of a modified respiratory pathogen in a major European city—linked to AI-assisted design—has shifted the axis of AI risk from disruption to mortality. The incident, though contained after weeks of lockdowns and emergency sequencing, left dozens dead and exposed critical gaps in biological surveillance. Public trust eroded rapidly, as images of overwhelmed labs and delayed reporting dominated media. In response, the EU launched the **Biological Threat Detection and Response Network**, a large-scale resilience initiative under the ECDC, integrating genomic data streams and deploying AI monitors trained on anomaly detection. Binding reporting mandates were introduced for research labs, and cross-border containment protocols were activated.

### Cyber Shield Crosses the Line

The **Cyber Shield for Critical Operators** reached its final turn. Eighteen member states fully implemented intrusion detection protocols, and emergency funding under NIS2 accelerated adoption in Bulgaria, Romania, and Croatia. Italy and Greece accepted phased compliance with binding deadlines. Though AI-generated ransomware attacks persisted, no new outages occurred in shielded sectors—marking a fragile but real success. The system’s partial deployment, however, underscored uneven national capacity.

### Political Strain Mounts

The **Frontier Transparency Compact** remains stalled. No US lab has committed to third-party evaluation, and legal uncertainty around high-risk classification lingers. A voluntary EU disclosure framework is under discussion, but momentum is fading. Meanwhile, the **Biological Threat Network** absorbs political capital just as public sentiment dips, driven by campaign backlash: cities halt data centre projects, and parties across the spectrum demand AI moratoria.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Resilience Rises

Despite progress in resilience, global AI capability advanced to 58.0, outpacing EU sovereign development. With no compute gains, **eu_ai_sovereignty** fell by 1.0. **Resilience** rose due to Cyber Shield’s completion and the new bio-network’s launch, though its full effect is yet to land.
