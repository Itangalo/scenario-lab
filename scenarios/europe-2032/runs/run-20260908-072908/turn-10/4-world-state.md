### The Weight of Openness

AI capability advanced to 65.0 as automated verification and recursive self-improvement techniques spread across frontier labs. With no safety upgrade landing on shipped systems, ai_safety remained stagnant at 29.0, despite rising concern. Open-weight models reached 53.0, creeping toward autonomous exploit generation, yet still trailing the closed frontier. The EU announced the Secure Open-Weight Evaluation Partnership Expansion amid urgency, aiming to red-team open models across all member states by turn 12. Funded through reallocated expired labour transition reserves and Innovation Fund repurposing, it invokes emergency powers under Article 12a of the AI Act, triggered by due process failures in AI-administered benefits.

Germany and Poland resisted central coordination, citing sovereignty and implementation costs. A compromise granted co-decision rights and fast-track capacity transfers from France and Finland, but formal agreements and technical integration will take time. Hugging Face and Mistral expressed conditional interest in future pilot evaluation hooks, pending legal clarity and resource commitments — no integrations are live. The initiative marks the EU’s first coordinated attempt to monitor distributed capabilities before misuse cascades, but remains in early mobilization.

### Sovereignty Holds, Barely

The InvestAI Gigafactories, Sovereign AI Inference Grid, and Emergency Labour Transition Fund all completed this turn. However, the Sovereign Compute Corridor did not finalize — its completion was misstated; final integration remains scheduled for a future turn. With no new infrastructure event occurring, and ai_capability rising by only +1.5 (below the ≥2 threshold), no decay applies. eu_ai_sovereignty carries forward unchanged at 19.0.

### Political Capital Frozen

With eu_political_capital at 9.0 — below 12 — the EU’s agenda slipped. The new measure launched as an announcement and funding directive, but only after reallocating dormant funds and leveraging expiring programmes. No proposal bonus applied, as the measure responds to an ongoing risk, not a past event. The priority designation had no effect under frozen governance. The portfolio charge of −10 (three large measures at −3, priority at −1) could not be sustained, but capital remained static due to system inertia — no further penalties applied this turn, though no recovery occurred.

### A Coalition Forms in the Shadows

Amid supply chain coercion — Washington restricting ASML’s servicing of non-sanctioned customers — and physical backlash against data centres, a quiet coordination among middle powers emerged. The new framework aligned export controls, pooled bargaining power on compute access, and shared evaluation capacity. While not yet formalised, it marked a shift: the EU was no longer acting alone.
