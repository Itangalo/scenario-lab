## In practice  
We are acting on two fronts: upstream, to slow the spread of dangerous capabilities, and downstream, to harden what is already exposed. The Frontier Transparency Compact remains in flight — we are finalising the list of accredited evaluators through the European AI Office, drawing on Fraunhofer, INRIA, and TNO, and negotiating non-disclosure terms that protect commercial secrets while enabling meaningful scrutiny. We are offering Mistral and Aleph Alpha fast-track compliance pathways and liability shields in exchange for early adoption, hoping to split the developer field before the US labs solidify resistance. But the legal basis remains fragile: the European Court has not yet ruled on whether autonomous systems can be classified as high-risk per se, and we are preparing fallback positions in case the AI Act’s provisions are narrowed mid-stream.

Meanwhile, Cyber Shield is now in crisis mode. The cyber_major_incident — a ransomware sweep across regional health systems in Belgium and Bulgaria, powered by AI-generated exploits — has exposed how far behind we are. ENISA’s detection templates worked in pilot zones, but only 11 member states have implemented them, and Italy and Greece still refuse full adoption without 85% co-financing. We can no longer afford that delay. This turn, we are invoking the NIS2 emergency clause to impose binding technical standards and releasing €12bn in emergency cohesion funds to cover 90% of implementation costs for states below resilience 50.0. The Competitiveness Council will vote next month; if they block it, we will go to the European Council with a direct appeal. At the same time, the cyber_defence_breakthrough — an EU-developed system for real-time swarm-behaviour detection now operational in Finland — gives us a chance to show rapid progress. We are fast-tracking its integration into the Shield framework and launching cross-border red-team exercises in October. This is no longer about building consensus; it is about ensuring that when the next attack comes, someone is already watching.
