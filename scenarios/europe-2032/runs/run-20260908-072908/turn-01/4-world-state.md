In response to the recent intrusion across energy and water operators, the EU announced Cyber Shield, a new resilience initiative aimed at hardening critical infrastructure through detection protocols and joint exercises. The programme is well-funded and backed by ENISA, but governance delays and resistance from Italy and Greece over costs and liability have slowed implementation. As a result, only initial planning milestones have been met in this turn. Cyber Shield is the EU’s sole new measure this turn and is named its current priority.
