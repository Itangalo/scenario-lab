**EU Cut-Off Continuity Switch**
Replaces cut-off US frontier dependencies in hospitals, ministries and essential firms with EU-hosted open models plus manual fallback staffing and hardened restores.
Why this and why now: access denial plus a major automated attack leaves essential services both blind and exposed, so the two-year commitment to keep services running fails unless a sovereign fallback is stood up now.
