## Priority
EU Bridge to the US-China Safety Accord, because being inside the verification regime outranks holding a secretariat coalition outside it, and it is the direct execution of our two-year commitment to joint leverage no single power can switch off.

## In practice
We task the EEAS and DG TRADE with a joint Council mandate: seek observer-then-party status to the US-China text, offering The Hague maintenance-guarantee data, InvestAI fallback-hosting for assured models, and ENISA/ECDC incident and bio-detection feeds as the EU contribution to verification. We link the NL-JP-KR supply-chain secretariat to this bid explicitly — joint export-control and servicing-guarantee alignment as our ticket in, with EIB cover and Anti-Coercion shielding held for partners who sign.

We use DG CNECT, the AI Office and ENISA to re-aim finished lines — gigafactory warm capacity and continuity fallback — toward accord tasks: weight-security audits, escalation monitoring, and hardened hosting for vetted bio-design tools, without opening a parallel compute programme. With capital at 22 and sentiment at 27 we ask for no new money this turn, only mandate and seconded evaluators, to avoid forcing the frozen expansions or the hardening-law trilogue before Washington and Beijing answer.
