## New measure
**EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled swarm intrusions with mandatory segmentation, detection and exercised cross-border response.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because the autumn test shot proved segmentation failed and detection would not have caught weeks-long staging, and it must be answered now before the 3-turn precursor window closes.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, not the Gigafactories, because the test shot shows disruption can arrive before sovereign compute does and resilience is the binding constraint.

## In practice
We task ENISA jointly with the Commission DG ENER and DG MOVE to issue emergency hardening standards under the NIS2 and CER directives, funded by reprogrammed CEF-Digital and InvestEU resilience envelopes, with TSOs, port authorities and water operators required to segment OT, rotate breaker credentials and deploy AI-swarm detection. Exercises run through the EU CyCLONe network in early 2027, with member-state interior and energy councils as the venue and reluctant operators bought off with co-funded upgrade costs.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package running on their existing tracks — permitting zones, grid connections and ASML supply-chain coordination — using the secured frontier access terms with notice and evaluation rights as bridge capacity while domestic compute builds. We do not open a parallel compute programme; we accelerate siting and power for the one we have.
