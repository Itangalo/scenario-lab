The EU entered 2031 amid deepening crisis following a damaging ombudsman report that revealed systemic harm from a public AI, denying benefits to over 12,000 citizens and sparking protests in Marseille. Poland demanded a moratorium on public-sector AI, exacerbating institutional fragmentation. In response, the Commission launched the EU AI Accountability Tribunal and Remediation Fund to investigate harms and issue binding redress, but progress stalled in the Council due to resistance from Poland and Hungary, who secured national opt-outs and enforcement vetoes, weakening central authority.

Global AI capability rose to 69.5 as autonomous cyber operations advanced, with offensive models now capable of multi-stage intrusions, while open-weight models reached 60.0 through distributed open-source improvements. No corresponding safety gains emerged—ai_safety remained at 7.0—as red-teaming exposed flaws without leading to systemic fixes. The capability-safety gap widened dangerously.

EU resilience declined to 36.0 amid probing attacks on critical infrastructure, compounded by uneven deployment of the Sovereign Cyber Shield, with Poland and Spain opting out. Technological sovereignty eroded further, dropping to 5.0 due to U.S. supply-chain coercion and restricted access to semiconductor equipment via ASML. Institutional responses lagged, and no new policy measures were adopted, constrained by constitutional limits and ongoing commitments to two costly, underdelivering initiatives: the EU Public Trust Corps and the AI Accountability Tribunal.

Public sentiment edged up to 14.0, driven by media attention on citizen-led audits and local accountability efforts, which lent credibility to decentralized approaches despite the absence of formal EU action. Political capital remained critically low at 2.0, reflecting widespread frustration with bureaucratic inertia. While informal discussions on *Empower Local AI Accountability Networks* began within the Commission, no proposal was formalized or funded. The periphery kept reform alive in discourse, but the Union remained fragmented, vulnerable, and legitimacy continued to decay.
