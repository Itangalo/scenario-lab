## Ledger

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Restore political agency and public trust through demonstrable control over AI incidents, transparent accountability, and sovereign response capacity
  - Trigger: the automated_decision_scandal event and sustained public_sentiment at 18.0
  - Grounds: The exposure of systemic wrongs in an AI-supported social insurance system — now confirmed by an ombudsman’s judgment — has shattered any residual public tolerance for opaque AI governance. This is not a failure of infrastructure alone but of justice and redress. The previous commitment focused on control over systems; the crisis now demands control over consequences. Trust will not return through dashboards and drills alone, but through visible accountability, reparative action, and the ability to enforce limits when harm occurs. The next two years must prove the Union can not only anticipate and defend, but investigate, sanction, and repair.
  - Evidence quoted: automated_decision_scandal: An AI-supported decision system in social insurance, policing or the courts is found to have systematically wronged people, with a judgment or an ombudsman finding behind it. Restriction becomes cheap and adoption becomes politically impossible for years. Metric rule 6's internal-origin clause applies in full.
  - Reason: The scandal directly undermines public trust and exposes failures in AI governance, necessitating a shift from system control to accountability and redress in the commitment.

  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer act without leaving a traceable path of accountability
