# Statements: The European Union (turn 9)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Maintain cohesion and control as open-weight systems reach critical capability, ensuring the Union can act as one before fragmentation becomes fatal
- `preempt_openweight_risks` (commitment): We will restrict the deployment of open-weight models above 50.0 capability in critical infrastructure, government systems, and essential services, and will fund secure alternatives to prevent displacement by uncontrolled systems.

  - Tier: commitment

  - Grounds: The *openweight_capability* level has reached 53.0, and we now observe unauthorised use in municipal AI assistants and university labs — not in defiance of law, but because no law yet binds these spaces. The *emergent_resilience_pact_forming* shows member states are already acting in parallel, risking divergence. We must now close the enforcement gap not just in critical infrastructure, but in the soft underbelly of adoption, where open models are embedded before anyone notices. This expansion is necessary to prevent a patchwork of exposure that will fracture our response when the next shock comes.
