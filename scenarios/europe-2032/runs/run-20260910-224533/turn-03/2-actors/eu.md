## Priority
EU Bio-Detection and Medical Countermeasure Surge, because the contested genome-model design is the one window that closes — sovereign compute can slip a semester, a distributed bio capability cannot — and it directly serves resilient capacity under our two-year commitment.
## In practice
We task HERA and ECDC under the Serious Cross-Border Threats Regulation with EU4Health and HERA emergency funds to contract national public-health labs in 12 sentinel states for continuous sequencing, link to ENISA-secured reporting, and pre-position PPE, diagnostics and surge procurement. We sell it to health ministers as money and mandates, not new law, to avoid a Council fight we cannot afford at 28 capital.

The Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) stay in flight but on care-and-maintenance: DG COMP holds state-aid clearances, DG ENER brokers grid-connection queues with TSOs in the two probed states, and we pause new siting fights to stop municipal protests draining capital. The Evaluation Institute (M3) is directed to add a bio-uplift track with vetted researcher access to EU-hosted open models, testing what Europe can run rather than waiting for US frontier access.
