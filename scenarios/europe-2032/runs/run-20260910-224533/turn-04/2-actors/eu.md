## New measure
**EU Embodied-AI Transition and Shopfloor Trust Pact**
A labour-and-adoption pact for commercially deployed robots: retraining wage insurance tied to automating employers plus EU procurement preference for trustworthy deployments in logistics and care. Why this and why now is because dexterous robots landing on EU factories from Chinese hardware and American models threaten the one industrial base we still lead in while municipal backlash blocks our own AI capacity, so displacement must be cushioned before hostility hardens into physical opposition.

## Priority
EU Bio-Detection and Medical Countermeasure Surge, because the unrecallable bio-recipe window still outranks even robots — sequencing and stocks must be live before open-weight uplift closes the chance to detect.
## In practice
We keep M5 as the funded priority: HERA/ECDC contracts go live in 12 sentinels, wastewater sequencing tied to ENISA-secured reporting, diagnostics pre-positioned from emergency health funds. DG SANTE sells it as money not mandates to hold health ministers on board.

We launch the shopfloor pact via DG EMPL and DG GROW under ESF+ and the Single Market programme: works-council agreements in auto and logistics regions, wage-insurance pilots co-funded by deploying firms, and procurement guidance favouring EU-serviced robots. For M1/M2 we do no new build — DG COMP holds aid clearances, DG ENER mediates grid queues with TSOs, and we offer host municipalities revenue-share and power-price caps from existing envelopes to unblock permits without opening a new compute line.
