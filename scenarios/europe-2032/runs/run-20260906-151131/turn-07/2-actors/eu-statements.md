  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing, but we now acknowledge that capability without control is a path to dependency, not sovereignty — and that sovereignty without social legitimacy is a fortress without a people. We further recognise that when a member state turns outward for security, it is not defection but a cry for agency: our duty is not to punish it, but to make Union-level control credible, visible, and shared.

Our response must not be to reassert control from above, but to embed it in mutual trust. The triad of technical, political, and perceptual failure is now complete: we have tools (interpretability), we have threats (67.0), and we have fracture (Poland). But we lack the legitimacy to bind them. That legitimacy must be co-created.
