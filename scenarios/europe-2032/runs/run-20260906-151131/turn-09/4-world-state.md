### A Hardening Union

The abrupt cutoff from the leading AI model sent shockwaves through EU institutions. Hospitals paused diagnostic workflows, ministries scrambled to reroute intelligence analysis, and firms dependent on frontier inference faced operational paralysis. With no explanation or recourse, the denial was not merely a technical disruption—it was a geopolitical statement.

Brussels responded by transforming fragility into leverage. The Sovereign AI Core Programme, though delayed and below its target, became a conditional asset: access now required proof of cyber resilience. The Commission launched the **Mandatory Resilience Certification**, backed by ENISA red-teaming and tied to the repurposed €10bn fund. Germany and Italy welcomed the clarity, accelerating upgrades. Poland, however, denounced the move as coercion, calling it “resilience blackmail” and challenging the Article 122 override in court.

Despite urgency, progress was uneven. Supply bottlenecks slowed deployment of detection systems, and mid-sized cities struggled to meet even basic thresholds. ENISA’s simulated attacks exposed critical gaps—some transit authorities could not restore services after a 30-minute intrusion test. Public messaging framed certification as collective defence, but media coverage emphasized disruption over preparedness.

Meanwhile, open-weight models advanced quietly. The bio-design tool adapted by citizen scientists spread across networks in Southern Europe, enabling low-cost diagnostics but raising alarms among regulators who lacked tools to distinguish benevolent use from risk.

AI capability edged upward globally, but safety stagnated. The EU’s focus remained reactive, with no new safeguards landing on shipped systems. Labour displacement continued, feeding public unease. Trust eroded further as automation reshaped clerical roles without visible support structures.

The pivot to resilience-as-conditionality marked strategic clarity—but at high political cost. Cohesion frayed, capital dwindled, and sovereignty slipped deeper into dependence.
