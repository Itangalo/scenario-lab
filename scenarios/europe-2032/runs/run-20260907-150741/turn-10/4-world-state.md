### A Shock to the System

A wave of coordinated ransomware, generated by advanced AI models, cripples emergency dispatch systems in Lyon, Zagreb, and Vilnius. Response times stretch into hours. The attack exploits zero-day vulnerabilities in widely used public infrastructure software, revealing how offensive AI has outpaced defensive readiness. The incident is widespread, automated, and attribution remains murky. Offensive capability, now within reach of smaller actors, has decisively outstripped protection.

### The Leap and the Fall

Global AI capability surges by +3.0 to 74.0 as a leading lab releases a system demonstrating sustained autonomous operation in complex simulations — a discontinuous jump. This leap widens the chasm between capability and safety. With no new safeguards embedded in shipped systems, and the gap now critical, `ai_safety` plummets by −12 to 5.0. The leaked audit’s warning about agent persistence is no longer theoretical.

Open-source models begin integrating fragments of the new architecture, showing early signs of performance gains. However, full replication remains out of reach. Without a catastrophic leak or deliberate release of frontier weights, progress is incremental. `openweight_capability` rises to 55.0 — a modest gain of +3, reflecting limited but growing diffusion. It remains significantly below `ai_capability`, consistent with gradual accumulation.

### Resilience Undermined

The cyber incident directly tests EU defences. While systems degrade rather than collapse, the 36-hour outage in critical services reveals fragility. The Resilience Reinforcement Initiative is overwhelmed by the scale and novelty of the attack. A −1 applies due to rising capability without new category 6 measures, and the strain of ongoing adoption policies triggers another −1. Net: `resilience` drops to 48.0.

### Sovereignty on Thin Ice

The jump in global capability exceeds +2, imposing a −1 on `eu_ai_sovereignty`. The export control tightening in Washington casts doubt on the Frontier Access Guarantee’s third-country compute deals. No formal denial occurs, but access is no longer assured. The metric falls to 22.0.

### A Gamble on Trust

The EU launches the Emergency Trust Restoration Initiative, repurposing funds to deploy sovereign AI in triage, border processing, and tutoring. The move is symbolic, aiming to rebuild legitimacy. However, with `eu_political_capital` at 0.0, agenda control is lost. The measure starts, but its success is uncertain. Early results from Marseille show promise, lifting local sentiment, though not yet at scale.

Public sentiment rises slightly to 25.0, buoyed by visible action and early successes in Athens’ schools. But the cyber incident dominates headlines, tempering gains.
