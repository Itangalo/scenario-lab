## New measure
**EU Critical Supply Buffer for Taiwan Shock**
Creates a pooled EU stockpile and rerouting plan for advanced chips and critical components plus emergency freight and insurance backstop so a Taiwan Strait disruption degrades rather than stops essential services and industry.
This is Preparedness and resilience work now, not new compute concrete, because the Taiwan precursor opens a three-turn window where sovereignty without stockpiles is paper; a small, fast buffer lands inside the two-year commitment to withstand coercion and absorb harm.
