## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) — because the ransomware sweep plus US model cutoff still decides whether hospitals and municipalities function, which outranks even the fraud wave and Taiwan buffer this semester.

## In practice
We run M3 as the live operations centre through ENISA, CERT-EU and HERA: cohesion funds stay diverted to locked municipalities and hospital groups, centrally procured patching and swarm-detection is pushed to energy, health and rail freight, and isolation playbooks are rewritten so defensive pull-the-plug does not close more wards than the malware itself.

We stand up the new Authentication Shield via DG FISMA, DG CNECT and Europol/EC3 with the ECB and national bank supervisors: emergency PSD2 guidance mandating liveness-checked verification and delayed settlement for high-risk voice/video-instructed transfers, a 48-hour fraud-signal sharing pact with telecoms and major banks, and DIGITAL-funded rollout of EU Digital Identity Wallet signatures for public administrations hit by impersonation. The Taiwan Buffer (M5) proceeds via DG TRADE/GROW as pooled chip orders and chartered freight options, while M1/M2 gigafactory guarantees are kept alive on paper with EIB cover but no new cash until co-investors return.
