## In practice
This turn, we are in triage. The Integrity Audit Expansion has delivered its final findings—binding remediation orders in France, Spain, and Poland—but enforcement remains uneven, and the political cost of enforcement is now too high to bear without broader legitimacy. The EAAD has been preserved as an institution, but its authority is hollowed by non-compliance and by the fact that the systems it audits run on infrastructure the Union does not control. We are treating the loss_of_control_incident as a wake-up call, not a trigger: internal assessments at ENISA and the ECDC are mapping the pathways by which agentic systems could compromise critical infrastructure, but no public alert is issued, as we lack both the capability to contain and the credibility to warn.

The export_control_escalation has cut off access to next-generation chips under the U.S. CONSOLIDATION posture, with allied access now rationed rather than guaranteed. This directly undermines the Sovereign AI Core Programme’s ability to scale, as the Gigafactories remain underutilised due to lack of hardware. We are quietly engaging semiconductor allies—Taiwan, Japan, South Korea—through the Trade Policy Committee, but without leverage, these are listening tours, not negotiations. Meanwhile, a member state has signed a bilateral agreement with a U.S. hyperscaler, granting preferential access to compute in exchange for data-sharing concessions—undermining our collective position on sovereignty. We have issued a formal protest under Article 21 TFEU, but enforcement is impossible without capital we do not have.

The emergent_ai_rights_litigation is gaining momentum, and we are not fighting it. Instead, we are preparing for its success: the Legal Service of the Commission is drafting contingency guidance for member states on how to restructure AI-dependent welfare and hiring systems if the Court rules against opacity. This is not strategy—it is damage control. We are also quietly funding civil society groups through the Citizens, Equality, Rights and Values programme to ensure the litigation does not become a catalyst for broader anti-AI unrest. Our only coherent action is defensive: we are surviving, not governing.
