### The Fracture Widens

The European Union attempts a desperate course correction, proposing an Emergency AI Governance Suspension in public services to quell rising fury over the automated welfare scandal. But with political capital at just 6.0, member states balk at ceding control. Germany demands veto power over audit outcomes; Hungary refuses funding tied to external oversight. The suspension passes only in weakened form—advisory, not binding—eroding its symbolic force.

Public sentiment, already at 18.0, dips further as protests denounce the move as hollow. Demonstrators in Lyon and Lisbon target data centres once more, now chanting “No audits without power.” Trust in sovereign AI is no longer merely low—it is structurally broken.

Meanwhile, technical foundations crumble. The frontier advances to 69.0 as capability plateaus fail to materialize outside speculation, while opaque reasoning renders human-readable interpretability obsolete overnight. Safety systems dependent on audit trails collapse across multiple platforms. No measure counters this inside the EU, and the Integrity Audit Expansion remains too narrow and too slow to absorb the shock. Ai_safety plummets by 12 points.

Resilience erodes as well: critical infrastructure upgrades from earlier efforts have landed, but with no new category 6 measure in flight and ai_capability rising, systemic fragility deepens. A cyber intrusion traced to an unsecured municipal benefits portal in Slovakia exposes personal data of over 200,000 citizens—contained, but symptomatic.

Sovereignty slips further. With no compute build completing and dependency on U.S. frontier access growing under CONSOLIDATION, the Union’s leverage evaporates. Ai_capability rose again, costing −1 under rule 5; no offsetting gains exist. Political capital, drained by seven active measures including the new priority, falls to 4.0—now below the threshold where new initiatives reliably start.

### A Moment of Reckoning

The Commission narrowly avoids a total breakdown in cohesion, but momentum is gone. The era of assumed authority over AI governance has ended. What remains is reactive, fragmented, and under siege.
