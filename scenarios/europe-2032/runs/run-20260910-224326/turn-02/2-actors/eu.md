## New measure
**EU Public AI Accountability Package**
Mandates incident reporting, public registry and independent audit of AI decision systems in welfare, policing and courts, with redress for those wronged. This is category 2 Transparency and reporting work, and why now is because the social-insurance scandal has exposed the second, more damaging failure — deployments lawful but outside Annex III — leaving the Act looking written for 2024 systems and making restriction cheap unless the Union closes the gap itself.

## Priority
EU Public AI Accountability Package, because the scandal has made adoption politically impossible and without restoring trust no gigafactory or evaluation authority survives Council.

## In practice
We keep the Gigafactories (M1) and permitting zones (M2) moving through DG CNECT, DG COMP state-aid clearance and EIB disbursement, but we spend no new political fight there this turn — site pledges from Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm and Warsaw hold without a vote. The Evaluation Institute (M3) continues staffing under the AI Office with JRC support, framed as the technical arm that will certify the audits.

This turn's fight is in EPSCO and JHA Councils and LIBE Committee: an implementing decision under AI Act Articles 11, 12 and 72 requiring logging to be read, human oversight to be timed and resourced, and all public-sector decision systems — whether classified high-risk or not — to be registered, with ombudsman-triggered review and compensation funded from deployment budgets. We concede to industry that testing is decoupled from market access for private models to buy their silence, while telling Parliament the gap reading is admitted openly: the Act missed these deployments, and we fix it by extension, not by trying harder.
