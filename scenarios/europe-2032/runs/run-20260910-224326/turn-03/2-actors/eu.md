## New measure
**EU Displaced Worker Transition Fund**
Provides wage insurance, rapid retraining vouchers and hiring incentives for entry-level roles in law, accountancy, software and customer operations hit by AI displacement, funded by a levy-linked ESF+ top-up. This is Labour and social protection work, and why now is because graduate hiring has stopped while trust is already collapsed — without a visible cushion, every sovereignty or resilience build will be read as Brussels protecting systems, not people.

## In practice
We push the Transition Fund through EPSCO with ESF+ as legal base, asking member states to co-finance retraining desks in public employment services while the Commission covers wage-insurance pilots from uncommitted ESF+ and RRF loan headroom. BusinessEurope gets flexibility language on restructuring in return; ETUC gets the levy-link principle for automating employers conceded for a later turn.

We keep M1/M2 alive on paper through DG CNECT and EIB technical work without spending fresh fights — site pledges hold, permitting-zone financing talks continue — and we direct the AI Office institute (M3) to absorb the safety breakthrough quickly into its test protocols to regain credibility. The Accountability Package (M4) proceeds on registration only, with logging and compensation timetables tied openly to the budget decisions now queued behind the jobs response.
