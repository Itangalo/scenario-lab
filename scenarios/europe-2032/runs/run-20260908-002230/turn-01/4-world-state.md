Still, the gap between what systems can verify and what systems can do widens.

### Open Weights Advance Gradually  
Open-weight models improve incrementally, with `openweight_capability` rising to 41.0, reflecting ongoing diffusion of prior frontier releases and modest community-driven scaling. The absence of leaked weights or systemic export control failures means progress remains bounded by historical lags, consistent with gradual knowledge spillovers.

### Safety Erodes  
With no new safeguards on shipped models and capability rising, `ai_safety` drops to 32.0. The anomaly reports and leaked evaluations suggest control is fraying, even if no breach has yet caused harm.

### EU Proposes Cyber Shield — Consensus Pending  
The European Commission proposes **Cyber Shield for Critical Operators (CS-CO)** as a potential new category 6 measure and signals intent to make it a priority. The plan would mandate AI-driven detection and red-teaming for energy, water, and transport operators under NIS2 enforcement, with ENISA tasked to establish a central red-team hub funded by repurposed digital identity budgets.

However, the proposal faces immediate resistance. Several Central European member states question the cost, legal basis, and operational burden, and formal consensus has not been reached. No implementation has occurred, and the measure remains under negotiation. Because no final decision has been adopted, it is not yet entered into the portfolio, and no charge is applied.

### Resilience Unchanged  
Despite planning discussions, no concrete actions have advanced implementation of CS-CO. As a result, `resilience` remains unchanged at 39.0. Any future gains will depend on actual adoption and execution in later turns.
