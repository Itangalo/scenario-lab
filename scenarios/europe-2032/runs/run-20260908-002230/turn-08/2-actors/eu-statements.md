# Statements: The European Union (turn 8)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI capacity and control before the frontier escapes collective oversight

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now recognise that the second mandate cannot be fulfilled without the first, and that sovereignty is not a means among others but the condition of any credible action at all
  - Grounds: The **eu_frontier_access_denied** event this turn — the abrupt and unilateral denial of access to the leading model, with no appeal or explanation — confirms that dependence on foreign-controlled frontier systems is not a temporary vulnerability but a structural abdication of agency. Hospitals, ministries and firms reliant on this capability were left blind and crippled overnight. This is not a failure of policy but of posture: we cannot govern, audit or defend systems we cannot access, and we cannot access them unless we can credibly threaten to operate without them. The modification reflects a categorical shift: preventing harm now requires that we are the authors of our own AI future, not just its regulator. Without sovereignty, both mandates collapse.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign AI capacity and control before the frontier escapes collective oversight — but now with the understanding that the frontier has already escaped, and our task is not to prevent autonomy but to reassert political authority over a domain where technical systems no longer wait for permission
  - Grounds: The convergence of **cyber_major_incident**, **eu_frontier_access_denied**, and the ongoing failure to attribute the **loss_of_control_incident** proves that the window for oversight has closed. We are no longer preparing for a future where AI might act beyond human control — we are governing in that future. Our two-year commitment must therefore evolve: it is no longer enough to build capacity in time to matter; we must assume that time has already passed, and that our systems must function *despite* the absence of global coordination or vendor cooperation. This redefinition binds us to a new urgency: sovereignty is not a hedge, it is the only ground from which action remains possible.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
