### A Denial That Changes Everything  
The European Union is abruptly cut off from the world’s most capable AI model, with no explanation or recourse. Hospitals relying on its diagnostic pipelines report system failures; ministries find their threat forecasting blind. The denial coincides with a major, AI-generated cyberattack on energy infrastructure across three member states—likely enabled by the same model class now inaccessible to defenders. The EU’s Incident Response Corps is hobbled before it can act.

### Sovereignty as Survival  
In response, the EU announces the Sovereign AI Core, a legally anchored, air-gapped execution environment for critical public functions. Built on the now-operational InvestAI Gigafactories and the Ispra audit pods, it will run only EU-controlled models on EU soil. The project is proposed under Article 114 TFEU, with EuroHPC JU designated to lead implementation. France, Germany, and Finland express preliminary support for hosting execution nodes, though legal immunities, funding mechanisms, and cross-border governance remain unresolved. Due to the high cost and complexity of deployment, full operational capacity is not expected before mid-2031. The proposal is recognized as a strategically significant step toward long-term autonomy, contributing a +1 proposal bonus to eu_ai_sovereignty under metric rule 5. However, no implementation gains occur this turn—the measure remains in early development, with no operational effect yet realized.
