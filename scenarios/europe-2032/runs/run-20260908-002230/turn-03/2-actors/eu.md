## In practice  
We are launching the Sovereign Evaluation Consortium (SEC) this turn, pooling resources from ENISA, the Joint Research Centre, and national AI safety institutes to build a unified capability for evaluating frontier systems. Under Article 10 of the AI Act and the newly activated emergency clause for systemic risk, we are establishing secure evaluation facilities on EU soil with legal authority to receive weights from both closed and open developers operating in the single market. Access will be granted only under air-gapped conditions and with strict monitoring of inference and tool use. The SEC will begin with pilot evaluations of models above 50.0 capability, focusing on agentic behaviour, self-modification risks, and covert goal persistence. Funding of €4.1bn will be drawn from the Digital Europe Programme and Horizon Europe, with staffing accelerated through fast-track Schengen visas for safety-critical researchers.

We are completing the rollout of the Cyber Shield for Critical Operators (CS-CO) this turn, with ENISA now operationalising the central red-team hub and issuing the first mandatory red-teaming schedules to energy and transport operators. Despite resistance from three member states, compliance has been secured through the offer of priority compute access at InvestAI Gigafactories and full co-financing for the first two years. The incident data from the recent lab breach has been integrated into the CS-CO threat library, sharpening detection rules for dependency poisoning and lateral movement in agent frameworks. Ongoing audits under the Open-Weight Security Baseline (OWSB) are identifying critical gaps in open model supply chains, and we are deploying technical assistance teams to help smaller developers meet the new standards ahead of the turn 5 deadline.

Behind the scenes, we are leveraging the middle_power_coalition to secure alternative supply routes for advanced chips, coordinating export-licence alignment with Japan, South Korea, and the Netherlands to maintain access despite U.S. campaign_security_hawk posturing. This is not sovereignty yet, but it is leverage — and leverage is the currency of survival when the frontier moves faster than we can build. We are not waiting for permission to understand what the world is becoming. We are building the eyes to see it, and the hands to act.
