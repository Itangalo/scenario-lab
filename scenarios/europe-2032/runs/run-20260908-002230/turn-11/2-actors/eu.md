## In practice  
This turn, we are acting on the quiet crisis within the fortress: we have pulled together compute, hardened facilities, and invoked emergency powers to reclaim agency over infrastructure — but capability without understanding is danger waiting to unfold. The Sovereign AI Core continues its final stretch toward full operation, with containerised nodes from Finnish academic clusters now integrated under zero-trust orchestration and French hosting sites completing electromagnetic shielding. Legal challenges from Germany and the Netherlands persist, but constitutional courts have allowed provisional use pending review, provided data sovereignty safeguards hold — which they do, thanks to the air-gapped design overseen by EuroHPC JU and audited by ENISA. We are redirecting €720 million from the Crisis Compute Mobilisation’s unspent contingency into workforce surge contracts, ensuring that by turn 12, all nodes are staffed with EU-trained operators fluent in both system integrity and incident triage.

At the same time, we are reaching outward. The backlash_physical event has made clear that top-down seizure, even in crisis, erodes legitimacy without return. So instead of expanding requisition authority, we are opening a controlled aperture: through the new Open-Source Security Partnership, we invite pre-cleared collectives — including the Berlin Interpretability Group, the Nordic AI Audit Forum, and the Mediterranean Cyber Defence Commons — to test our sovereign models in sandboxed environments. These teams receive limited API access and synthetic data streams mirroring real operational conditions, in exchange for publishing interpretability layers, anomaly detectors, and failure-mode taxonomies under EU-compatible licenses. Participation includes liability protection, travel funding, and a seat on the upcoming AI Safety Review Panel. This is not transparency for show; it is leverage. If a grassroots network can find flaws before adversaries exploit them, then trust becomes not just a public good but a technical asset — one that raises ai_safety where regulations alone cannot reach.
