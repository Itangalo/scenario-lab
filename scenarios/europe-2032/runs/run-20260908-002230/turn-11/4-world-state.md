### Stalled Momentum, Rising Tensions

AI capability inches forward to 63.5 as global labs adapt to constrained compute, achieving marginal gains through algorithmic efficiency and model sparsity. The Taiwan blockade continues to constrict semiconductor flows, limiting expansion but not halting progress entirely.

The **Crisis Compute Mobilisation** formally concludes, integrating partial access to Italian, Dutch, and Swedish clusters, with Finnish academic nodes now operational under zero-trust protocols. However, legal challenges in Germany and the Netherlands remain unresolved, and full sovereignty gains are deferred. No additional boost to *eu_ai_sovereignty* materialises beyond prior adjustments.

Amid growing public anger, physical protests target data infrastructure in three countries: a planned AI research hub in Bavaria is occupied for 11 days, grid connections to a French hosting site are sabotaged, and police clash with demonstrators near a Swedish cluster. The state responds with force, further alienating communities already resentful of emergency powers and job displacement. Trust erodes; sentiment drops to 20.0.

In response, the EU launches the **Open-Source Security Partnership**, opening red-teaming access to vetted collectives. Early collaboration yields promising interpretability tools and anomaly detectors. While these do not yet meet formal deployment standards, they represent the first meaningful external validation of sovereign models. This effort lifts *ai_safety* by +2, reflecting nascent improvements in transparency and assurance.

However, political capital declines. The portfolio burden remains heavy: **Sovereign AI Core** (−3), newly added **Open-Source Security Partnership** (−2), and priority status (−1) total −6. The new measure earns no proposal bonus—it addresses a persistent gap, not a recent shock—and legitimacy is not restored. With sentiment falling and unrest mounting, no uplift occurs from that channel either. Net change: −6, resulting in a drop from 31.0 to 28.0.

Resilience holds steady despite the turmoil. The completed Emergency Resilience Activation continues to provide structural benefits, but no new category 6 measure lands this turn, and rising *ai_capability* without further investment creates latent vulnerability.

Open-weight capability rises slightly to 51.0, driven by spillover from audit collaborations and improved tooling, though still far behind the closed frontier.
