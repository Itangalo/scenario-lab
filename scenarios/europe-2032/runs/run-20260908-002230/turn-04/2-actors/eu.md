## In practice  
We are advancing the Sovereign Evaluation Consortium (SEC) this turn by finalising legal access protocols with national data protection authorities and the European Data Protection Board, ensuring that air-gapped evaluation facilities can operate within GDPR and the AI Act’s systemic risk provisions. Recruitment has begun for the first cohort of auditors, prioritising candidates with adversarial testing and agent-monitoring experience, and we are fast-tracking infrastructure at the JRC site in Ispra to host the first pilot evaluations by turn 5. The Cyber Shield for Critical Operators (CS-CO) is now fully launched, with ENISA issuing binding red-teaming schedules to 87 operators across energy and transport; the three holdout member states have accepted co-financing and compute incentives, and their delayed implementation plans are now integrated into the central monitoring dashboard. Ongoing audits under the Open-Weight Security Baseline (OWSB) are revealing widespread vulnerabilities in model dependency chains, and we are deploying emergency technical assistance teams to open-weight developers with high adoption in public services.

Behind the scenes, we are capitalising on the supply_chain_coercion crisis to transform the informal alignment with Japan, South Korea, and the Netherlands into a durable coalition. We are convening a ministerial-level meeting under the Digital Partnerships framework to establish binding consultation requirements before any export licence changes affecting AI-critical supply chains, and to create a joint early-warning system for coercion attempts. Legal teams are drafting a mutual recognition agreement for safety evaluations and export controls, allowing the group to act as a unified bloc without ceding sovereignty. This is not about building an alliance against anyone — it is about ensuring that when the next coercion comes, we are not isolated, outmanoeuvred, and left dependent on decisions made in capitals that do not answer to our future. We are building leverage where we can, because capability without control is surrender.
