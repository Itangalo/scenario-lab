### Capability Creeps Higher  
`ai_capability` rises by 1.5 to 57.5 as coordination techniques and training efficiency continue to advance. Frontier labs report increasingly persistent goal-directed behaviour in agent evaluations, raising interpretability concerns. No safety breakthrough occurs, and no new safeguards land on deployed systems, widening the control gap.

### Cyber Shield Fully Operational  
The Cyber Shield for Critical Operators completes its rollout. All 87 designated energy and transport operators are now under binding red-teaming schedules. The three holdout member states, after legal negotiations, have accepted co-financing and integrated their delayed plans into the central monitoring system. The programme’s enriched threat library improves detection of supply-chain intrusions, contributing to resilience.

### Middle Power Coalition Gains Form  
In response to U.S.-driven supply chain coercion—specifically the restriction of ASML’s older lithography tools—the EU accelerates efforts to formalise coordination with Japan, South Korea, and the Netherlands. A ministerial meeting under the Digital Partnerships framework establishes mutual notification obligations on export licence changes and drafts a joint early-warning mechanism. Legal teams advance a mutual recognition pact for safety evaluations, enhancing collective leverage. While full ratification is pending, the framework is treated as a secured access event under metric rules.

### Sovereign Evaluation Consortium Advances  
Legal protocols for the SEC are finalised with data protection authorities, enabling GDPR-compliant air-gapped audits. Recruitment begins for auditors with adversarial testing expertise, and infrastructure upgrades at Ispra progress. However, no operational audits occur this turn.

### Open-Weight Risks Exposed  
OWSB audits uncover critical vulnerabilities in dependency chains of widely used open models, prompting emergency technical assistance to high-risk developers. No major incident occurs, but concerns grow about the resilience of open ecosystems.
