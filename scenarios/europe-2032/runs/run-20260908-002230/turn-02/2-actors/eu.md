## In practice
We are launching the Cyber Shield for Critical Operators (CS-CO) this turn under Article 21 of the NIS2 Directive, invoking the Union’s emergency powers to mandate AI-driven intrusion detection and quarterly red-teaming for all essential service operators in energy, water, and transport. ENISA will stand up a central red-team hub by June, funded with €8.2bn redirected from the Digital Europe Programme and staffed with vetted researchers from national CERTs and academic partners. We are offering 70% EU co-financing for the first two years and linking compliance to priority access to InvestAI Gigafactories compute — turning security into a competitive advantage.

At the same time, we are responding to the emergent_cyber_lab_leak with a new measure: the Open-Weight Security Baseline (OWSB). The breach did not come from a state actor but from a poisoned dependency in an open agent framework — proof that the open ecosystem is now a systemic risk vector. OWSB will require all models above 40.0 openweight_capability to undergo third-party audits of their tooling stack, implement secure update mechanisms, and participate in a coordinated vulnerability disclosure programme. This will be enforced through market access rules under the AI Act, with enforcement delegated to national competent authorities and supported by a new public audit lab under the Joint Research Centre.

Resistance is mounting, particularly from Central European member states wary of costs and from open-source communities fearing overreach. To hold cohesion, we are fast-tracking OWSB-compliant projects into the Tech Sovereignty Package’s accelerated-permitting zones and offering technical assistance through the Digital Europe Programme. We are also publishing the full incident report from the lab breach to justify urgency — transparency as leverage. The loss_of_control_incident, though not yet fully understood, confirms that we are no longer preparing for hypotheticals. The frontier is inside the gate. We act now, or we lose the capacity to act at all.
