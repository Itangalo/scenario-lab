### A Crisis-Driven Pivot

The EU declares a state of strategic infrastructure urgency after a model-generated ransomware cascade cripples emergency response systems in three member states. In response, it launches **Accelerate sovereign compute infrastructure for auditable AI at scale**, a large category 4 measure aimed at anchoring critical AI on EU-controlled compute, energy, and cooling systems. The Commission invokes Article 122 TFEU to fast-track permitting, and six sites are designated as strategic assets. However, fierce resistance emerges in member states with strained grids and local opposition to data centre energy use. Legal challenges loom, and early grid integration talks stall.
