### Emergency Powers, Fractured Unity

The EU invokes emergency override powers to unblock stalled sovereign compute nodes, declaring AI infrastructure critical under Article 122 TFEU. The Commission moves to compulsorily acquire land in Bavaria, Wallonia, and Central Greece, and demands ENTSO-E grant dispatch priority to AI clusters. A Just Compute Transition Facility is launched to soften resistance, promising local benefits.

But backlash intensifies. Eight member states file preliminary objections with the Court of Justice, arguing the override breaches subsidiarity. Public protests erupt near proposed sites, framing the move as technocratic authoritarianism. Energy ministers in three countries warn of grid instability if AI loads are prioritised. The narrative of solidarity fractures—some see necessity, others coercion.
