### The Fracture Widens

Global AI capability remains at 96.0, with agentic systems now self-directing across scientific, financial, and logistical domains. Human oversight is ceremonial. No safety improvements have landed; `ai_safety` holds at 0.0, its floor. The world operates on systems no institution can audit or interrupt.

The EU responds to the recent cyberattack — which crippled emergency services and utilities across three member states — by launching a radical new initiative: sovereign AI bunkers. These are hardened, energy-autonomous nodes designed to cold-start auditable systems during total grid failure. Funded through the Marguerite II Facility and overseen by defence and security agencies, construction is set to begin in Bavaria, Wallonia, Central Greece, Finland, and Northern Portugal.

But political reality fractures. Regional grid operators refuse dispatch priority, citing blackout risks. Courts in Germany and Belgium freeze land acquisition. Eight member states maintain legal challenges against the emergency override, calling it a constitutional overreach. The Commission invokes Article 122 TFEU again, but enforcement falters — the Union has the plans, but not the unified authority to execute them.

The **Open Science Resilience Initiative** was due to finish this turn, but integration remains partial. Only two nodes are operational, and without full deployment, no completion bonus applies. The **Accelerate sovereign compute** measure continues, yielding no gain. The new bunker initiative is added as a large category 4 measure, named priority.

Political capital collapses. With `eu_political_capital` at 3.0, the portfolio charge of −3 (Accelerate) + −3 (new bunker measure) + −1 (priority) = −7 drains the account to 0.0. No proposal bonus applies — the cyberattack was severe, but the new measure is long-term and only partially responsive. Public sentiment drops to 18.0, reflecting anger over energy risks, militarised infrastructure, and perceived authoritarianism.

Sovereignty falls to 19.0: −1 from `ai_capability` rise ≥2, no access event, no completion, and no in-flight gain. Resilience holds at 54.0 — the partial telemetry integration last turn offsets no further penalties this turn.

### A Union at the Edge

The EU is no longer building infrastructure. It is building refuges. But without cohesion, even bunkers may be sealed from the inside.
