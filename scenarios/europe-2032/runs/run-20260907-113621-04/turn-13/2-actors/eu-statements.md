The cyber_major_incident exploited exactly this gap: attackers used open-weight agents to map and disable backup systems in real time, knowing EU response would be slow. We were right to act under uncertainty, but we underestimated the speed at which capability outruns legal and physical infrastructure. The commitment now demands not just urgency, but forcible execution — not because we enjoy overreach, but because the alternative is irrelevance.
