### The Terminal Surge Continues

Global AI capability advances by 2.0 points this turn, reaching 92.0. The frontier continues its terminal-phase acceleration, with agentic systems now routinely managing multi-day strategic workflows across research and infrastructure domains. Human oversight remains nominal, and the recent loss_of_control_incident has not prompted systemic safeguards—only tactical retreats.

### A Pivot Under Pressure

The EU responds to two shocks: the sudden denial of access to the leading frontier model and the activation of a joint cyber-biosurveillance pact. In reaction, it formally refocuses its sovereignty strategy—away from parity with the frontier and toward auditable, open-weight resilience. The Open Science Resilience Initiative progresses: three research hubs are now operational, with binding mandates issued to five member states. However, delays in funding finalization and interagency coordination slow full deployment. Work continues under this single active measure; no new measure is launched.

### Integration, Not Isolation

In response to the cyber-biosurveillance pact’s activation, the EU begins exploratory coordination with ENISA and ECDC to assess pathways for future integration. Secure data pipelines are under early discussion, and co-location proposals remain under review. These steps represent preparatory engagement, not a new measure, and do not trigger additional portfolio burden. Resilience sees a partial increase from early technical alignment, but full operational integration remains pending.

### Fractures in the Foundation

Despite ambition, political capital erodes more slowly than feared, as the refocusing on open science maintains baseline legitimacy. The portfolio remains costly, and no prior measure has delivered visible results at scale. Public sentiment rises modestly as protection efforts gain attention, though labour displacement and energy concerns—fueled by data centre moratoriums—temper gains. Sovereignty declines slightly as access to frontier models is severed, but domestic capacity in open-weight systems provides a stabilizing counterweight.
