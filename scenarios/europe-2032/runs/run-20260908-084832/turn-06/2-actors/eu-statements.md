# Statements: The European Union (turn 6)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure autonomous AI capacity to protect European strategic sovereignty and manage catastrophic risk without dependence on external powers
- `sovereignty_as_condition` (commitment): The EU will not accept safety or oversight arrangements that leave it dependent on foreign goodwill for access to the systems it must govern; sovereignty is not a luxury, it is the precondition of agency.
