# Statements: The European Union (turn 8)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure autonomous AI capacity to protect European strategic sovereignty and manage catastrophic risk without dependence on external powers
- `sovereignty_as_condition` (commitment): The EU will not accept safety or oversight arrangements that leave it dependent on foreign goodwill for access to the systems it must govern; sovereignty is not a luxury, it is the precondition of agency.

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Ensure the EU can independently detect, respond to, and govern AI-driven threats by June 2031 through sovereign red-teaming, resilient critical infrastructure, and guaranteed access to model weights above 50.0 capability — **but only if such access is enforceable and not circumvented by black-market compute or legal non-compliance**
  - Grounds: The emergent compute black market and the failure of US labs to treat Article 114 as binding reveal that access on paper is not access in fact. Sovereignty is being hollowed out from below by illicit compute and from above by diplomatic resistance. This turn, we must condition our commitment not just on legal authority, but on operational integrity — or risk building institutions that govern ghosts.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `sovereignty_as_condition` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: The EU will not accept safety or oversight arrangements that leave it dependent on foreign goodwill for access to the systems it must govern; sovereignty is not a luxury, it is the precondition of agency — **and it includes the integrity of our own infrastructure against leakage, capture, or subversion**
  - Grounds: The black market for sovereign compute clusters proves that possession of hardware is not control. If sanctioned actors and non-state adversaries can lease time on EU-anchored systems through shell companies and compromised accounts, then our sovereignty is perforated. This is not just a security failure — it is a structural threat to our ability to act at all. We must now treat infrastructure integrity as inseparable from sovereignty itself.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
