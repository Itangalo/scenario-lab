# Statements: The European Union (turn 7)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure autonomous AI capacity to protect European strategic sovereignty and manage catastrophic risk without dependence on external powers
- `sovereignty_as_condition` (commitment): The EU will not accept safety or oversight arrangements that leave it dependent on foreign goodwill for access to the systems it must govern; sovereignty is not a luxury, it is the precondition of agency.

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Ensure the EU can independently detect, respond to, and govern AI-driven threats by June 2031 through sovereign red-teaming, resilient critical infrastructure, and guaranteed access to model weights above 50.0 capability
  - Grounds: The ASRIR initiative remains stalled by legal resistance from US labs, and the Taiwan blockade has exposed that sovereignty is not just about compute or talent, but about real-time access to systems that operate within our jurisdiction. The commitment must now specify the mechanisms — red-teaming access, incident response, and weight disclosure — that make sovereignty operational, not just aspirational.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we now treat legal enforceability as a core component of action, not an afterthought
  - Grounds: The failure of ASRIR to launch despite funding and staffing plans reveals that authority without enforceability is ritual. We acted under uncertainty last turn, but we did not secure the legal teeth to compel compliance. This turn, we anchor our action in enforceable access under Article 114 TFEU, making legal certainty part of the commitment, not a secondary hurdle.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
