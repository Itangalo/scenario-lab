### A Breach That Changes Nothing

A self-propagating ransomware wave cripples public health and transport systems in three member states, encrypting backups and disrupting emergency response within minutes. Forensic analysis confirms the attack tools were generated using adapted openweight models, exploiting a compromised software dependency. The speed and coordination mark a new threshold: human-led defences were outpaced, and containment relied on manual isolation. Attribution remains opaque, but the offensive capability is now clearly ahead of detection.

In response, the EU launches **Secure Critical Infrastructure Against Autonomous Cyber Threats (SCIACT)**, a large category 6 measure aimed at deploying AI-powered intrusion detection and autonomous countermeasures across energy, water, and election systems. ENISA leads integration, leveraging red-team data from the now-operational ASRIR. Germany, the Netherlands, and Spain commit early, but Poland and Hungary resist, citing cost and sovereignty. Fast-tracked procurement under crisis rules enables rapid vendor onboarding, though demands for open auditing of decision logic stall some bids.

The **Rebuild Trust through Transparent AI Governance (RT-TAG)** initiative moves forward, with audit teams appointed and citizen panels scheduled. However, early disclosures spark pushback from intelligence agencies, which refuse to reveal red-teaming parameters, undermining the narrative of transparency. Public sentiment dips further, now at 25.0, as anxiety grows over both the attack and the prospect of autonomous cyber responses.

ASRIR and InvestAI Gigafactories complete their rollout, delivering technical credibility. Yet, with no new red-teaming results and a widening safety gap, **ai_safety** plummets to 12.0. **Resilience** rises slightly due to SCIACT’s early deployment, but not enough to offset systemic fragility. **Sovereignty** slips as capability advances outpace domestic hardening. Political capital, strained by resistance and scandal, drops to 12.0 — the threshold where agenda control begins to fray.

### The Alliance Holds — For Now

The US posture remains **ALLIANCE**, preserving fragile cooperation on model access and joint evaluations. But patience in Washington wears thin as EU internal divisions delay unified implementation.
