### A Shift from Law to Force

The EU abandons the pretense that regulation alone can secure its digital territory. The Emergency Enforcement of Systemic AI Accountability, intended as a borderless shield, collapses in practice: Poland and Hungary refuse implementation, and US developers ignore compliance demands. The proposed Compliance Agency remains unfunded and unstaffed. In response, the Commission launches the Sovereign AI Counter-Agent Development Programme (SACAD), a classified effort to build autonomous defensive systems capable of detecting and neutralising rogue AI within EU infrastructure.

SCIACT continues patchily—active in German and Dutch energy grids—but detection without action proves hollow. SACAD bypasses civilian channels, drawing funding from military R&T and Digital Europe budgets, with development led by Thales and Siemens on sovereign compute clusters in Finland and Portugal. A red-cell unit embeds in Europol to simulate adversarial AI behaviour, while covert monitoring supplements open telemetry where resistance is expected.

Brussels offers Poland and Hungary unconditional access to the counter-agent stack—no audits, no strings. Warsaw calls it “digital coercion” and refuses. Budapest demands veto power over deployment criteria; talks stall. Behind the scenes, evidence emerges of shell companies leasing sovereign compute for offensive AI testing—further eroding trust in EU control.

Meanwhile, export controls tighten globally. Under the ALLIANCE posture, allied buyers retain preferential access—but only if they accept real-time usage reporting. The EU qualifies on paper, but delayed integration into shared verification systems triggers temporary throttling of high-end model imports, delaying key SACAD components.

Public sentiment worsens. Media frame SACAD as “AI fighting fire with fire,” sparking protests in Vienna and Lyon. With RT-TAG discredited and enforcement failed, citizens see only escalation, not protection.

### Capability Creeps On

AI capability advances to 71.0 as frontier labs integrate agentic systems into internal research loops. Openweights rise to 60.0—now capable of multi-day professional work—fueling underground use in cyber operations. ai_safety remains stagnant at 8.0, a chasm between power and control. Resilience holds, but only because attacks have not yet cascaded further.
