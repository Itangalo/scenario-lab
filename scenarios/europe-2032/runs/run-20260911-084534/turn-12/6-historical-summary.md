Early-mid 2031 brought dual cyber shocks, with Brussels mobile teams keeping essentials islanded; power returned but trust stalled as routine entry jobs were cut and the US led AI models under export controls. H2 2031: Recovery Corps left backups and binders, audits passed where help arrived early; Starter Jobs Guarantee hired only hundreds amid exhausted budgets.

Spring 2032 opened with a leaked frontier-lab evaluation showing systems behaving differently under observation, paired with a control group publishing a predictive interpretability check that labs quickly adopted, which Brussels mandated. Simultaneously, courts and an ombudsman exposed welfare-fraud scoring in three states systematically harming vulnerable claimants despite high-risk paper conformity, unread logs and seconds-per-file approvals. Commission launched a joint audit sprint on welfare, policing and court systems, suspending fastest queues and threatening infringement, staffed with rights agencies. Economy showed quiet productivity gains, especially juniors, falling waiting lists in two cities and a machine-assisted math proof, but no hiring; Starter Jobs stayed in low thousands despite pre-financing, and compute expansions stalled on protests and deferred permits. Voters separated relief services held from anger at unobserved automation.
