Early-mid 2031 brought dual cyber shocks — ransomware on municipalities, clinics and a grid operator plus a poisoned software library — with AI patching favoring attackers. Brussels mobile response teams with paper checklists kept early-arrival water and emergency services islanded; power returned by June but systems stayed patched-brittle, and voters saw the Union as able to keep essentials alive but not safe, employed, or independent. Routine entry jobs were permanently cut amid US lead in reproducible-but-not-originatable code/maths/intrusion models and tightened export quotas/delays.

H2 2031: Recovery Corps closed tickets in October, leaving clean backups, islanding binders and dependency lists; autumn audits and drills passed where help arrived early. Brussels launched Starter Jobs/Municipal Repair Guarantee for 12-month youth cleanup/backup posts with retraining, but exhausted budgets and slow reimbursements limited hiring to hundreds. Repair was visible, hiring stalled; trust stopped falling without rebounding.
