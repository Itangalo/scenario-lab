## New measure
**EU Starter Jobs and Municipal Repair Guarantee**
Pays municipalities and clinics hit by the sweep to hire displaced entry-level workers into cyber-cleanup, records restoration and islanding support roles with retraining attached.
This and now because permanent entry-job cuts plus dark town halls have driven sentiment to 0 and capital to 8, and without visible jobs tied to visible recovery the continuity commitment cannot hold; it is Labour and social protection work executed through resilience delivery.

## Priority
EU Starter Jobs and Municipal Repair Guarantee, because with no measures in flight it is the only lever that rebuilds trust while finishing the cyber restore the Corps started.

## In practice
We run this through EPSCO and the Employment and Social Innovation envelope plus repurposed cohesion emergency funds: a single EU guarantee window that reimburses municipalities, clinic networks and grid operators for 12-month starter contracts tied to dependency patching, offline-backup hygiene, and paper-fallback staffing. Public employment services do the matching, unions co-sign the retraining curricula to blunt the lost-generation charge.

We pair hiring with the finished Shield and Recovery Corps assets: ENISA/CERT-EU mobile teams certify clean sites where guarantee hires deploy, mayors get one claims form for both repair and wages, and we pause any new sovereignty or permitting push this turn to conserve our last capital for delivery that voters can see.
