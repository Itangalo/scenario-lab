Late 2032 brought a continent-wide ransomware sweep via a poisoned component used by hospitals, utilities and municipalities; connected backups were encrypted, triage, prescriptions and benefit payments stalled, with power/health held only where engineers islanded in time. Brussels reactivated the Recovery Corps with clean disconnected backups and paper fallback; early-arrival sites ran on paper, late sites improvised, with Starter Jobs crews diverted to visible manual work. The welfare/policing audit sprint was kept at low burn, new mandates paused. Accelerator construction stayed blocked by sit-ins and deferred permits with no new ground broken, while a lab leap in verifiable code/math/intrusion tooling was rapidly absorbed, aiding attackers and outpacing evaluation; mandated predictive checks helped only in audited deployments. Continuity in islands read as fragility, trust fell further and EU room to act stayed near empty.
