### The attack that ran itself
It started as a helpdesk ticket in two cities and became a continent-wide outage map by evening. A ransomware sweep rode a poisoned software component used by hospitals, utilities and municipal offices. Backups were encrypted where they were still connected. Triage screens went dark, billing froze, and in a dozen communes the lights stayed on only because engineers pulled the plug in time.

Defenders said openly they were behind. The tooling had been assembled at machine speed, with fresh variants appearing faster than signatures could be written. Attribution would take months. What mattered in public was that care appointments, prescriptions and benefit payments stalled together.

### Island and restore
Brussels answered with what it had. The Recovery Corps network built over the past two years was reactivated: mobile teams with clean, disconnected backups, paper binders for manual fallback, and orders to isolate compromised dependencies and keep energy and health islanded.

Where teams arrived early, clinics ran on paper and power stayed up. Where they arrived late, mayors improvised. Starter Jobs crews were diverted to carry boxes, staff reception desks and log cases by hand — visible paid work amid the mess. The audit sprint on welfare and policing systems was kept alive at low burn, auditors staying on site where they already were, new mandates paused.

It was uneven, and the cameras found the gaps.

### Concrete stopped, code leapt
While restoration convoys moved, construction fences stayed blocked. Coordinated sit-ins and tent camps stalled several planned accelerator sites for months. Councils deferred permits rather than clear protesters. No new ground was broken.

At the same time a leading lab demonstrated a sharp leap in verifiable work — code synthesis, mathematics, intrusion tooling — quickly absorbed into products. Attackers could do markedly more within weeks; evaluators argued about what it meant in general while system behaviour grew harder to predict. The predictive checks Brussels had mandated helped in audited deployments and were absent everywhere else.

Voters noticed continuity where it held, but anger deepened. Services kept running in islands felt like proof of fragility, not strength. Trust fell further, and the Union's room to act stayed near empty.
