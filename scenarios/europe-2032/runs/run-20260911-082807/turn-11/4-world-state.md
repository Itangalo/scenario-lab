### The sweep
Autumn brought the attack the drills had rehearsed for. It came as a largely automated ransomware sweep, seeded through a compromised management tool used by municipalities, clinics and a regional grid operator. Lockscreens went up in town halls from the Rhine to the Po valley within hours; appointment systems fell back to paper, and two distribution operators islanded substations as a precaution.

Where the shared playbooks triggered, the damage stopped. Cooperating clinics and municipalities isolated, cut cloud links and restored from locked backups with outside telemetry feeding in. Auditors later said that discipline prevented a regional cascade. Where councils had refused telemetry sharing, restores took weeks. Staff reverted to phones and printed lists, elective care was postponed, and backlogs rebuilt.

Attribution stayed open. Ministers said only that the tooling was machine-generated and the operators professional. Telecom and Health Councils held against separate national restores that would have broken the common trace, but the argument was bitter and public.

### Trust repair lands in a storm
The civic trust programme formally closed in the same months. Redress for the frozen benefits and policing support system continued case by case, logs stayed published, workload limits and independent re-checks stayed in place. Only the audited triage sites were allowed to scale, and nurses there again defended waiting-list cuts on evening news.

It was not enough to lift mood. Lawyers welcomed the closed chips-for-compute and liability pact clarifying who pays when civic systems fail, but underwriters still waited for statute, and most councils kept deployments frozen. The message that past harm had been lawful still hung over every new rollout.

By December containment had held where cooperation held, legitimacy had steadied among cooperators, and capacity had still not moved. The fenced gigafactory plot stayed fenced, cancelled foundry slots stayed cancelled, and no new build was promised until the blast radius was known.
