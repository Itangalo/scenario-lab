## In practice
We are treating the energy-compute nexus as the new front line of sovereignty. The European Commission, acting under Article 122(2) TFEU and now reinforced by the European Council’s emergency resolution on strategic infrastructure, is designating the three stalled data centre sites—Bucharest, Marseille, and Helsinki—as critical nodes under EU operational control. This overrides local zoning and environmental appeals that have delayed construction. ENTSO-E is being directed to prioritise grid reinforcement for these sites using EIB-backed emergency financing, with geothermal (Romania), nuclear-linked (France), and hydro-backed (Finland) energy sources locked in under long-term contracts. The Commission is offering affected regions binding commitments to local hiring and pollution caps in exchange for lifting protests—buying time, not trust.

The Sovereign Inference Backbone remains tied to these nodes, with mobile audit teams now embedded in construction crews to certify compliance from day one. The EAAD+ is escalating enforcement: two national agencies rerouting AI workloads through foreign clouds are now under formal investigation, with access frozen under Article 12 of the AI Act. Meanwhile, the Public Trust Observatory’s real-time dashboards are being stress-tested—not to prove compliance, but to survive cynicism. We are no longer selling a vision. We are building one, under fire, with the only capital we have left: urgency.
