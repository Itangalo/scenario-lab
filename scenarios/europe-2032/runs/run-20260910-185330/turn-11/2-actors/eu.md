## New measure
**EU Municipal Cyber-Medical Continuity Corps**
It puts funded cross-border repair crews, segmented offline fallback kits, and EU-hosted medical AI (open-weight models for triage and tailored therapies) into hospitals and municipalities so the automated attack and the cutoff do not stop care. This is why this and why now: with a major model-enabled attack landing on already-paper systems, US models cut off and controls tightening, the only way to hold the two-year commitment to keep essential services running is hands-on restoration plus a medical benefit people can feel on EU terms.

## In practice
We task DG DIGIT and ENISA with the cyber half and HERA and EMA with the medical half, under the Health and Telecoms Councils: joint procurement of vetted open-weight clinical models on EuroHPC and segmented health clouds, offline paper-to-digital kits, and mandatory segmentation audits. Money is reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health remainder plus the closed Restoration Pact line — no new fund vote.

We hold southern mayors and hospital staff by paying overtime and extra shifts from that line, and hold The Hague and the defecting capital by offering shared servicing and fallback capacity instead of infringement, with a single Trade Council brief to Washington: medical and recovery licence exemptions now, no further lithography concessions without Union mandate.
