## Priority
EU Cutoff Fallback and Continuity Deployment, because the access cutoff outranks restoration alone when hospitals and ministries cannot wait months for attribution or licences.

## In practice
We act under the commitment to keep essential services running through shocks. DG DIGIT and ENISA with HERA finish the Restoration Pact clean rebuilds, then re-point recovered workloads by default to the EU-hosted fallback stack — EuroHPC partitions, segmented health clouds, vetted open-weight models near 60 capability — via emergency procurement under the Health and Telecoms Councils, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe remainder.

We hold the line on defection and ASML coercion by offering the defecting member state funded fallback capacity and joint servicing terms instead of punishment, and task the Trade and Foreign Affairs Councils with a single Anti-Coercion brief: medical/recovery exemptions now, no further lithography concessions without Union mandate. No new gigafactory concrete is promised; credibility is a working prescription system, not a groundbreaking.
