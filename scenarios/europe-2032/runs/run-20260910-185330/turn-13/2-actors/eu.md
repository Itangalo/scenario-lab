## New measure
**EU Verified Public Benefit Rollout**
It scales the one visible adoption success — waiting lists and decision times cut by EU-hosted AI — into certified public-sector deployments with published logs, real human review time standards, and independent audit, to rebuild trust after the automated-decision scandal. This is why this and why now: with sentiment at 11 and capital at 14, only a benefit people can see, attributed to a European decision and demonstrably not repeating the scandal pattern, keeps essential services politically sustainable through the open-weight surge and defection.

## Priority
EU Verified Public Benefit Rollout, because without sentiment recovery no continuity corps can staff or site, and the two-year commitment to keep services running fails politically before it fails technically.

## In practice
We task DG REFORM and the AI Office with the rollout, under Employment/Social and Health Councils: EU4Health and Technical Support Instrument reprogrammed money to fund three to five lighthouse sites — triage, e-prescription recovery, benefits backlogs — running vetted open models on EuroHPC/health clouds with mandatory logging-read and minimum review-time rules.

We answer the automated-decision scandal as an enforcement failure, not a drafting failure: the system was high-risk under Annex III, conformity passed on paper, oversight collapsed to forty-seconds-an-item and logs were never read. The AI Office opens infringement-grade audits, publishes the ombudsman findings, and ties new rollout funds to corrected oversight. We contain the member-state side-deal by not confronting it, offering shared servicing and fallback access instead, and brief Washington once on medical exemptions while holding the line on lithography.
