## New measure
**EU Embodied Labour Transition Fund**
Pays wage insurance, retraining and redeployment for warehouse, logistics and supplier workers displaced as Chinese-built robots with American control models take picking, sorting and palletising, while channelling the interpretability breakthrough into shop-floor safety certification. This is Labour and social protection work, category 7, and why now is that embodied deployment is dividing the labour market at sentiment 19 and capital 19 — without a visible transition offer, restoration looks like abandonment and nothing else can be funded.

## Priority
M13 EU Municipal Restoration Surge — because cascade prevention in hit municipalities is the precondition for any transition offer to be believed while safety is at 4.
## In practice
We run M13 to completion through ENISA and CERT-EU on the joint watch floor: locked backups, offline kits and cut-link playbooks stay in the worst-hit clinics and grid operators until core services hold without borrowed telemetry. DG CNECT keeps the welfare-automation freeze frozen and funds only audited triage sites that already cut waits, so repair is not read as re-imposition.

We stand up the Transition Fund via DG EMPL and the ESF+, co-decided with EPSCO: wage insurance for displaced logistics workers, retraining vouchers tied to repair, care and construction roles that remain manual, and a safety-certification track that takes the new interpretability result onto the factory floor with EU-OSHA. We ask automating employers and regions hosting idle gigafactory sites to co-fund redeployment in exchange for accelerated reskilling permits, offering unions a concrete stake against robot imports we cannot block outright.
