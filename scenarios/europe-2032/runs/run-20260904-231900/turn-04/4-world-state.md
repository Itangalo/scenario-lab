### Frontier Crosses Threshold  
AI capability reaches 60.0 as leading labs deploy systems that autonomously design and execute multi-day scientific and engineering workflows, marking the first economically transformative wave of AI-driven productivity. These systems now displace junior researchers and engineers across biotech and software sectors. Safety lags: no major safeguards are deployed, and internal audits reveal persistent blind spots in agent behaviour. The gap between capability and control deepens, triggering a −2 penalty to `ai_safety`.

### Open Surge, Contained Diffusion  
A notable open-weight model, partially derived from fragmented and incomplete frontier model leaks, achieves broader cyber and automation competence. However, its capabilities are limited by incomplete architecture and lack of frontier-scale training infrastructure. Openweight_capability rises to 50.0—reflecting accelerated diffusion but bounded by the frontier’s own +2.0 growth. The model spreads across academic clusters and developer forums, enabling localized exploitation. OWMI detects early signals and issues alerts, but remains in operational ramp-up; its formal completion is delayed. Early-warning protocols contribute a modest +1 to `resilience`, but full resilience gains await full deployment.

### EU Sovereignty Under Strain  
Despite progress on monitoring and assurance frameworks, EU sovereignty continues to erode. The new reciprocity initiative faces immediate resistance: U.S. officials dismiss it as incompatible with national security policy, and hyperscalers refuse jurisdictional concessions. No access is secured, and with `ai_capability` rising by 2.0, the −1 decay applies. A third member state signals intent to bypass EU channels, deepening fragmentation. Sovereignty falls to 15.0.

### Political Capital Under Pressure  
The EU advances InvestAI and initiates a new high-priority measure—Secure EU Access—aimed at establishing guaranteed compute access for regulatory and crisis response purposes. This adds to an already strained portfolio. Portfolio charge: −3−3−3−2−3−1 = −15. OWMI is not yet fully operational, deferring its +3 political capital benefit to a future turn. Public sentiment rises slightly due to visible public-sector gains, granting a +1 boost under rule 6. Political capital ends at 21.0—down but not yet critical.

### Fragile Momentum, Breaking Point  
Adoption successes in healthcare and education improve public perception, lifting sentiment to 42.0. However, labour displacement intensifies: the emergent displacement wave, now in its fourth half-year period, culminates in coordinated strikes across German and Polish logistics hubs. Simultaneously, the long-simmering AI assurance leak is confirmed—internal EAAD audit protocols were compromised, and sensitive evaluation data appears on technical forums. The breach undermines trust in EU oversight, triggering a formal investigation. These developments mark the resolution of two long-standing emergent risks, shifting them from latent threats to active crises. Cohesion frays—yet institutional response has begun.
