## In practice
We task DG CNECT and the AI Office with an emergency triage: inventory cut-off deployments, re-route essential health and public-administration workloads to EU-hosted models including Mistral, Aleph Alpha and EuroHPC capacity, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health money, with ENISA certifying continuity. Health and interior ministers get continuity funding paired with a procurement rule: no new critical-service build on non-guaranteed foreign inference.

We pair this in Foreign Affairs and Trade Councils with quiet reciprocity: market access under the AI Act, data-flow and public-procurement decisions, plus coordinated export-control posture on lithography and materials with Netherlands, Japan and Taiwan-facing partners, are now conditional on restored, contractually guaranteed access with appeal. We do not trigger the Anti-Coercion Instrument this turn, but we prepare its file so the denial reads at home as foresight acted on, not outrage performed. M1/M2 stay on track via EIB structuring and permitting, and M3 detection upgrades continue in TSOs.
