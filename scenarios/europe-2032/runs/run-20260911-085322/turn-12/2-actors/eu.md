## In practice
We act on Articles 153 and 168 via EPSCO and Health Council: ESF+ reprogramming funds six-month wage insurance and retraining vouchers for displaced admin and dispatch staff, paired with HERA-funded care-assistant hiring in the two hardest-hit regions where screened systems returned. DG EMPL and national public employment services run it, with unions offered co-management to stop opposition to data-centre and hospital AI siting.

At the same time DG SANTE and HERA keep M11 cordons and M10 surge staffing funded through handover, while DG CONNECT pushes M12 in Trade Council: re-papering the breakaway hyperscaler contract to EU-logged routing, tabling aligned export licences with middle-power partners. Ministers sell both tracks together — jobs and trusted wards at home, joint bargaining abroad — as the absorption that makes leverage possible.
