### Containment weeks
Winter brought a severe biological incident. A modified pathogen, developed with help from openly available models, sickened hundreds across two border regions and forced isolation wards, school closures and weeks of contact tracing. Sequencing and triage ran on paper logs and double-checked manual wards because the newest frontier systems could no longer show their work — vendors presented illegible internal reasoning as a performance gain, but hospitals and auditors treated it as blindness.

Brussels moved after the Council adopted a time-limited cross-border health-threat decision activating HERA's emergency mandate, providing the legal basis for coordinated procurement and patient transfers. Action was constrained by money: with valuations reset and data-centre plans cancelled and no private financing returning for shared compute, HERA releases, isolation capacity and cross-border transfers of patients and staff were financed by reallocation from paused deployment funds and emergency reprogramming approved in the same decision, with disbursement phased and partial. The cordon held in the narrow sense: hospitals did not close, cross-border moves cleared the worst backlogs, and re-segmented grids kept power and water running while municipal IT was still being patched.

### What did not hold
Nothing else improved. Chart errors and missed handoffs persisted on manual procedures. Patch mapping from the autumn intrusions was still incomplete, forcing dispatch systems back to paper for days at a time. The quarantine on Taipei shipments and the fenced backup factory site remained frozen, payments with them. The large member state that cut its own hyperscaler deal kept it, accepting only logged EU routing for surge use while keeping the contract.

Money stayed away. Ministers who had toured working clinics now fronted cordons and queues, arguing that absorption was the mandate. The public heard casualties, opaque machines and divided capitals. Trust fell further; anger at outages turned to fear of engineered biology. Audits failed to verify frontier-system reasoning, eroding confidence in oversight without dismantling existing monitoring.

The Union ended June credible only as a care provider of last resort — wards staffed, cordons manned, but unable to see inside the models, recall the weights, or hold a single supply line.
