### Holding the wards, borrowing the shield
The second half of 2032 did not rebuild European power so much as keep it in the room.

At home the Commission held its line on the wards-and-jobs compact. Wage insurance finally reached cash in pilot cities, retraining vouchers cleared backlogs through public employment services, and a few thousand care-assistant posts were filled in the two regions where screened dispatch had returned. Union co-management and the charters posted on ward walls made further hospital automation politically possible there. Elsewhere hiring lagged, funds reprogrammed slowly, and protests against new data-centre and hospital system sites continued. Nurses still spoke of holding, not healing, with chart errors and opaque triage advice unresolved.

Abroad Brussels acted as a taker. It signed into a pooled biosurveillance arrangement with binding sample-sharing and a standing investigation team, and plugged what telemetry it had into a joint cyber command built by states hit by the same intrusions. Attribution and early warning improved in finance and telecoms; hospitals and municipalities stayed thinly defended.

Supply split harder. Washington tightened chip and model controls again, and after the summer's strait disruption European buyers found themselves rationed alongside others despite volume licences. No new advanced machines reached European soil. In response, trade ministers leaned fully into coordination with other middle powers holding lithography spares, chemicals and evaluation capacity — aligned licences, joint bargaining on compute, common logging of routing. The breakaway capital kept its cheaper bilateral hyperscaler terms and stayed outside the common log.

Meanwhile a freely downloadable release matching the closed frontier within months spread to hundreds of thousands of machines in a week. Containment talk died in Brussels; resilience became the doctrine by default.

The Union ended the year staffed, insured in part, connected to others' shields, and still unable to switch on its own frontier.
