### A Leap in Silence

Global AI capability advances to **72.0**, driven by a sudden leap in verifiable domains: cyber operations, code generation, and narrow engineering. While general competence grows only modestly, attackers now wield tools that adapt mid-intrusion, mimicking human operators with eerie precision. Openweight capability inches to **59.0**, as fragments of these adaptive models surface in underground forums, narrowing the gap between closed and open.

### Sovereignty Advances

The **Sovereign Inference Corridor Initiative** officially completes, anchoring high-performance inference within EU borders. Despite integration delays in two host nations limiting immediate operational impact, the measure’s completion is recognized under metric rule 5. The category 4 finish delivers +5, offset only by −1 decay from the 2.0 rise in global AI capability. With no access event, **eu_ai_sovereignty** rises to **38.0**.

### Safety Crumbles

The capability jump — sudden, unanticipated, and uncontained — overwhelms existing safeguards. No new assurance mechanisms have landed on frontier systems. ai_safety plummets by 6 points to **4.0**, reflecting near-total opacity in deployed models and eroding confidence in control.

### Resilience Responds

In reaction to an AI-assisted biosecurity leak detected at EU ports, the Union launches the **Biosecurity Shield**, a large-scale AI-integrated surveillance network. Though still in early deployment, its announcement and partial rollout deliver an initial +2 resilience boost, raising the metric to **45.0**.

### Political Capital Holds

Despite mounting pressures, political capital stabilizes at **44.0**. The completion of a major category 4 measure yields +5, offset by ongoing portfolio costs (−6) and the priority penalty (−1). A proposal bonus of +6 — justified by the recent bio-leak and the scale of the response — prevents erosion. Public sentiment remains stagnant at **25.0**, with privacy concerns over the Biosecurity Shield already surfacing in national media.
