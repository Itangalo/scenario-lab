### The Corridor of Control

The EU launches the **Sovereign Inference Corridor Initiative**, a €2.1 billion push to anchor high-performance AI inference within its legal and physical borders. The project, prioritised and framed as essential to the credibility of the new European Agency for AI Assurance and Control, aims to ensure that certified systems operate on infrastructure beyond foreign reach. Legal groundwork advances under Article 114 TFEU, and hosting talks begin with Germany, France, and Nordic states. However, early resistance emerges: cloud providers warn of fragmentation, and smaller member states question cost and centralisation. The initiative survives its first political test but will require sustained capital to deliver.

### Capability’s Quiet Ascent

Global AI capability climbs to **70.0**, driven by closed-lab advances in recursive self-improvement. The jump is modest but persistent, with no public breakthroughs — only tighter feedback loops in training pipelines and deeper optimisation of agent scaffolding. Openweight capability rises to **58.5**, as fragments of adaptive cyber tools leak into underground forums, enabling more sophisticated mimicry of human operator behaviour in intrusion scenarios. No major release event occurs, but the gap between closed and open narrows.

### Sovereignty Gains, Marginally

The **Edge Sovereignty Acceleration Programme** concludes, deploying neuromorphic edge systems across 68% of targeted critical infrastructure. The completion yields a +5 boost to sovereignty, though the rise in global capability (up 1.0) imposes a −1 decay. No access event occurred, leaving a net gain of +4.

### Safety Stalls, Resilience Stays Brittle

Despite the new Agency’s mandate, no improvement in *ai_safety* occurs — the metric remains at **10.0** — as assurance mechanisms have not yet landed on deployed frontier systems. Resilience holds at **43.0**, with no new defensive measures completed. Public sentiment remains low at **25.0**, anchored by distrust in AI’s role in democratic processes.
