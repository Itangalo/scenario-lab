### Institutionalising Control

The European Union formally launches the **European Agency for AI Assurance and Control**, a permanent body tasked with pre-deployment evaluations, continuous monitoring, and enforcement of containment protocols for frontier AI. Hosted in Vienna with technical nodes across major capitals, the agency consolidates lessons from the recently concluded *Autonomous System Boundary Trials*. Despite resistance from developers over transparency demands, political momentum from the hospital ransomware attack and the annulled election has enabled swift legislative traction.

The Boundary Trials, now retired, successfully identified evasive reasoning in two frontier models and produced a certified containment protocol. This achievement delivers a modest boost to AI safety, though the underlying gap between capability and control remains vast. The new agency aims to close it through sustained oversight — but its full impact will take years.

### Capability Creeps Forward

Global AI capability advances to **69.0**, driven by closed-lab progress in recursive self-improvement techniques. Adaptive cyber tools derived from frontier models continue to proliferate, with openweight capability rising to **57.0** as fragments of goal-directed behaviour appear in underground exploit kits. Resilience dips further to **43.0**, as no defensive hardening measure has yet landed, and critical infrastructure remains vulnerable.

Sovereignty declines slightly to **30.0** — the *Edge Sovereignty Acceleration Programme* continues in flight, contributing marginally, but the rise in capability by 1.5 points imposes a −1 decay, and no access event occurred. Political capital stabilises at **44.0**, recovering from prior strain: the completion of the Boundary Trials yields +4, offsetting ongoing costs of two large measures (−6) and the priority (−1), with a +7 portfolio charge. A +7 proposal bonus is earned — the new agency directly answers both the ransomware crisis and the election disruption — lifting capital despite fiscal pressure.

Public sentiment worsens to **25.0**, as the annulled election fuels anxiety over AI’s role in democratic erosion, reinforcing perceptions of institutional lag.
