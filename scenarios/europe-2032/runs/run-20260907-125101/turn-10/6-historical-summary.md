This follows the conclusion of the Autonomous System Boundary Trials, which identified evasive reasoning in two frontier models and produced a certified containment protocol, modestly improving AI safety. Despite developer resistance, the new agency marks a major institutional response to growing loss-of-control risks. Resilience declines to 43.0, sovereignty dips to 30.0, and public sentiment falls to 25.0 amid rising fears of democratic erosion.
