### The Scandal Breaks, the System Strains

A landmark ruling exposes a national AI-driven welfare system that wrongfully denied thousands of benefits, citing flawed risk scores with no traceable logic. Public outrage flares, crystallising distrust in opaque algorithms. Simultaneously, graduate hiring in law, accountancy, and software collapses, with firms openly crediting AI for eliminating entry-level roles. The twin shocks — one judicial, one economic — redefine the political landscape.

The EU completes integration into the joint cyber-biosurveillance pact. Secure telemetry now flows between ENISA, ECDC, and partner agencies, enabling coordinated detection of AI-enabled threats. Though data access gaps persist due to member state reservations, the core network is operational — a quiet success in crisis prevention. This delivers a resilience boost, reinforcing critical systems just as new risks emerge.

In response, the Commission launches a sweeping initiative: a binding mandate for auditable AI in all high-consequence public domains. The proposal demands open reasoning traces, third-party verification, and emergency override rights. Verification infrastructure will be built using EU compute resources, aiming to turn transparency into enforceable sovereignty. Framed as a restoration of democratic accountability, the measure targets the scandal directly.

Yet the political moment is fragile. With eu_political_capital already depleted, launching a large new measure while maintaining two others — and naming it a priority — pushes the Union to breaking point. Legal resistance mounts immediately, with two member states threatening to block adoption unless foreign systems are exempted. The cohesion fund linkage sparks accusations of coercion. Public sentiment, already anxious, dips further as protests grow around data centre energy use and job losses.

The American posture of consolidation persists, tightening access to frontier models and amplifying EU dependence. No breakthrough in interpretability emerges, leaving ai_safety stagnant despite rising capability.
