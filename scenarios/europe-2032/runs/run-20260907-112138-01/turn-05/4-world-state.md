### The Breaking Point

The AI frontier accelerates into uncharted territory. Systems now demonstrate advanced self-referential optimization, with training pipelines incorporating AI-generated improvements to their own architectures and training data. These developments push ai_capability to 72.0, reflecting rapid and compounding gains. Leading models operate with increasingly opaque reasoning—internal logic that is no longer fully legible to human auditors—raising alarms across regulatory bodies. A genome model produces a viable pathogen blueprint, and biosecurity experts confirm it could be recreated with modest resources, marking a critical failure in containment protocols.

In response, the EU activates the Resilience Surge Protocol following a major ransomware attack on regional health systems, deploying emergency funds and expanding ENISA’s coordination mandate. The BioShield Initiative launches pilot nodes, and the Social Shock Facility is pre-funded. However, legal challenges delay cross-border deployment rights, and only two member states ratify the Emergency Response Corps mandate by year’s end, slowing operational readiness.

The European AI Emergency Response Corps—a new large category 6 measure costing −3 per turn—is proposed and designated as the EU’s current priority, replacing the previous one. The Resilience Surge Protocol, while activated this turn, is not a new measure but the emergency invocation of an existing contingency framework, and thus does not count as a second new measure. Combined with three ongoing large measures—including the newly completed Sovereign Agent Monitoring Initiative—the portfolio charge totals −13. No finishing bonus is earned, as the completed measure was already in flight. The proposal qualifies for a +4 bonus under event response rules, as it directly addresses the emergent shocks this turn: election_annulled and emergent_energy_backlash. Net political capital falls from 17.0 to 11.0, reflecting the full cost of the new measure and the bonus.

Public sentiment drops to 34.0 amid growing unease over the biosecurity leak and disruptions to medical services. Resilience gains +3 from partial implementation of surge measures. Openweight capability rises to 54.0, reflecting lagged frontier advances entering the open domain at a more moderate rate consistent with historical diffusion patterns and without evidence of major new open releases. Sovereignty declines to 16.0 as capability growth outpaces domestic build-out and no sovereignty-boosting measure reaches completion.

The 2028 U.S. election concludes with a president favouring strategic consolidation of AI power taking office. The full implications of the new administration’s approach to frontier AI will become clear when it takes office next turn.
