## Priority
**Launch a resilience-by-design mandate for all AI systems deployed in or interacting with EU critical infrastructure.** — because this turn closes its implementation window, and its success or failure hinges on whether we can convert pilot audits into enforceable standards before the next incident occurs — which the drop in `ai_safety` makes increasingly likely, regardless of our longer-term sovereignty goals.

## In practice
We are closing out two major measures this turn: the InvestAI Gigafactories and the sovereign inference clusters. The Gigafactories did not deliver as promised — only two sites reached preparatory construction — but we are salvaging strategic value by converting residual funds into direct equity stakes under Article 122 TFEU, treating them as state-backed assets. DG ENERGY, the EIB, and national governments are restructuring the Finnish and Portuguese projects under emergency procurement, with grid access prioritised and debt restructured for long-term viability. This is damage limitation, but it preserves a foothold.

The sovereign inference clusters are now operational in prototype form, hosted in Luxembourg and Helsinki, with models from EU-controlled sources running essential public services — border processing, emergency logistics, and health diagnostics. While still below frontier capability, they prove that uninterrupted access is possible. The next phase — scaling to full redundancy — depends on whether we can secure model access through the middle_power_coalition, now under negotiation.

Meanwhile, the resilience-by-design mandate is at a tipping point. ENISA has drafted technical standards with the Regional Oversight Consortium, but France continues to resist full application to defence AI. We are offering a compromise: time-bound exemptions renewable only upon European Defence Agency certification of equivalent safeguards. The goal is not just compliance but cultural change — embedding red-teaming as routine, not exceptional. The new measure we propose — a binding red-teaming and response network — builds directly on this foundation, turning audits into action. We will table it under Article 12 of the Cyber Resilience Act, with joint exercises to be conducted by HERA and ENISA by mid-2031. This is not optional: with `ai_safety` at 28.0 and open-weight models at 52.0, the next failure could cascade. We must be ready.
