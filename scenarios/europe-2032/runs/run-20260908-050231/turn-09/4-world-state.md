The EU formally completes the InvestAI Gigafactories and Sovereign Inference Clusters initiatives. Despite delays—only two Gigafactory sites reached preparatory construction—residual funds are converted into equity stakes under Article 122 TFEU, preserving strategic footholds in Finland and Portugal. The inference clusters in Luxembourg and Helsinki are now operational in prototype form, running essential public services on EU-controlled infrastructure. These milestones trigger a +5 boost under rule 5: +3 for Gigafactories (large, category 4) and +3 for Inference Clusters (also category 4), offset by −1 due to the 1.0 rise in global capability. Net `eu_ai_sovereignty` rises to 16.0.

### Resilience Mandate Gains Traction, New Network Proposed

ENISA finalises binding red-teaming standards for AI in critical infrastructure, with France accepting time-bound defence exemptions tied to European Defence Agency certification. The resilience-by-design mandate is now effectively in force, delivering a +4 to `resilience`. The EU also proposes a new large category 6 measure: a binding red-teaming and incident response network, to be implemented by turn 11. Though ambitious, it builds on existing momentum and avoids overreach.
