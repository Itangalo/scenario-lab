## In practice
The Tech Sovereignty Package, Secure Evaluation Mandate, and Open-Source Threat Detection Commons all reach their planned end this turn — but not as successes. The first delivered only modest permitting reforms, blocked by member states guarding local planning autonomy; the second was never tested, as no developer has yet triggered the 50.0 threshold under observation; the third remains dormant due to national vetoes. These are now closed files, their outcomes absorbed: we will not renew them in current form. Instead, we pivot toward enforceable standards and sovereign capacity.

We are fast-tracking Council adoption of the new resilience-by-design mandate under Article 12 of the Cyber Resilience Act, leveraging emergency powers invoked after last quarter’s near-miss in Baltic grid coordination. ENISA will draft technical specifications by turn 7, with input from the newly active Regional Oversight Consortium. Germany and Finland support binding red-teaming; France insists on carve-outs for national defence AI. We will concede limited exemptions if they are time-bound and subject to EDA review. The goal is not just compliance but culture change: making failure scenarios routine in design, not afterthoughts in response.

Meanwhile, the InvestAI Gigafactories remain our anchor. With ASML’s servicing cut even to mature-node customers, the Union’s one real supply-chain leverage — in specialty gases and metrology software — is being weaponised against us. We are accelerating site finalisation in Finland and Portugal using REPowerEU emergency procurement rules, bypassing national appeals courts. The European Investment Bank is restructuring debt terms to attract private co-investment, but confidence hinges on visible progress before turn 7. If we do not break ground on two sites by then, political capital will collapse under accusations of wasted scale. The sovereign inference clusters continue on track, but they are useless without the compute those factories must deliver. This is no longer about competitiveness. It is about continuity of state function in a world where AI access is rationed by others.
