### A Widening Gap, Not a Sudden Leap

The global AI frontier continues its steady advance, consistent with prior trends. `ai_capability` rises to 65.8 — a measured increase reflecting the fixed rate of progress mandated from turn 1. No sudden leap occurs; instead, incremental gains accumulate through distributed research and closed-loop optimization in frontier labs. The notion of a “capability jump” compressing years of progress is inconsistent with the constitutional constraint that the world’s pace remains fixed and unannounced. What changes is not speed, but impact: newly deployed systems exhibit emergent proficiency in cyber operations, autonomously generating and deploying zero-day exploits at scale.

Interpretability and control frameworks fail to keep pace. With no major safety architecture adapting to this higher baseline, `ai_safety` drops sharply to 12.0 — a decline of 12 points, permitted under Rule 9 as it remains below the 25-point threshold. The **cyber_major_incident** unfolds as AI-driven ransomware disables emergency call systems across multiple member states for over 48 hours, exposing systemic fragility.

In response, the EU launches the **Pan-European Critical Systems Resilience Surge**, a large category 6 measure aimed at hardening health, energy, and emergency infrastructure. Hosted by ENISA and backed by €3.8bn in conditional funding, it mandates AI-powered red-teaming and real-time data sharing. Named a priority, it adds −3 to portfolio charge, joining ECAP (−3) and the priority tag (−1), for a total burden of −7.

A proposal bonus of +3 applies for addressing the **cyber_major_incident**, resulting in a net change of −4 to `eu_political_capital`, which falls from 13.0 to 9.0. Resistance emerges: some member states reject data-sharing mandates, citing sovereignty concerns, while operators protest implementation costs. The narrative of fragmentation intensifies.

Public sentiment declines to 30.0 amid ongoing coverage of the cyberattack’s human toll. Trust in digital governance frays.

`resilience` increases to 57.0, reflecting early-stage activation of the new measure. Full effects remain years away, consistent with Rule 6 — no instant implementation. The partial gain acknowledges growing preparedness, but not completed protection.

`openweight_capability` advances to 54.0, as open research communities absorb and recombine recently disclosed methods. It remains below `ai_capability`, preserving Invariant 2.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 18.0 — a −1 decay triggered by the rise in `ai_capability` exceeding 2.0 points (actual rise: 1.3). No offsetting event secures access or expands autonomy. The EU’s cognitive lead narrows further, but within the rules of accumulation and decay.
