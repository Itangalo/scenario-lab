Germany and France insist on national oversight of verification, while the Nordic bloc withdraws from joint planning, citing sovereignty concerns. The special Council session delays adoption, pushing implementation into the next turn. Transitional offers to U.S. firms are rejected—both cite national security exemptions and reroute critical functions through Swiss intermediaries.
