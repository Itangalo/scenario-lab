### The night the grids went dark
In late autumn an automated wave hit at machine speed: ransomware locking municipal administrations, a tainted software update rippling through contractors, and brief outages in small-town power and water. Certified hospitals and banks isolated and restored within hours. A dozen uncertified utilities that had paused automated patching over false alarms bore the brunt, with manual overrides and ENISA field teams working through weekends to bring services back.

Television ran queues at town halls and darkened clinics. Attribution remained open, but analysts agreed the tooling had been assembled with AI help.

### Patching that finally works
The same half-year brought two technical reprieves. A new generation of defensive tooling — patching pushed as fast as flaws were found, and detectors that caught coordinated bot behaviour rather than known signatures — proved itself in the certified sector. Separately, a control result from the labs made deployed-system behaviour more predictable, and the AI Office rushed it into deployment guidance.

Brussels channelled both into a surge: emergency restoration orders, field teams for the paused utilities, shield certificates held to drills, and the EU reserve mobilised. Hospital labs finally received sampler funds, wastewater sequencing feeds to partners in Japan, Korea and Canada went to full volume, and response centres rehearsed live.

Coverage widened, but thinly. Restoration succeeded where teams arrived; elsewhere backlogs persisted into December.

### Cheques versus jobs
The Entry-Job Transition Fund paid its first full cohorts — several thousand graduates in law, accountancy, software and customer operations received support on enrolment. It was tangible, and ministers claimed it.

It did not reopen entry posts. Firms kept assistants on drafting and triage, graduate hiring stayed frozen, and protests hardened into open hostility toward automation. A contested paper claiming a genome model could guide a non-expert to a viable human-infecting design deepened dread, even as officials stressed it was disputed and contained.

The mood closed the year bleak: relief where defences held, anger where jobs did not return.
