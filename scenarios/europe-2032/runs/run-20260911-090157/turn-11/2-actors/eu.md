## New measure
**EU Entry-Job Transition Fund**
Funds the finalised retraining and wage-insurance plan for displaced graduate-entry sectors via employer-linked levies and repurposed ESF+ money.
This and why now: graduate hiring has frozen while concrete continues, and with capital and sentiment near 25 the absorption half of our commitment fails unless the unfunded plan is funded now; embodied pilots do not yet change this but confirm labour replacement is the channel.

## Priority
EU Defensive Breakthrough Deployment and Bio-Detection Sprint — because broad-but-thin patching and lagging samplers must become exercised coverage before the defence window closes, outranking even the new fund this turn.

## In practice
We act through ENISA and HERA on the in-flight sprint: ENISA field teams go hands-on to small utilities, hospital imaging exemptions are ring-fenced to unblock the pause, and M8 exercises are made a condition for shield certificates. HERA accelerates sampler procurement via joint procurement and ties wastewater feeds to the Japan-Korea-Canada swap.

On the home track EPSCO adopts the Transition Fund implementing decision, using ESF+ reallocation plus a levy on automating employers to pay first cohorts in law, accountancy, software and customer centres, with payouts tied to retraining uptake. The holdout capital is offered priority patching support and pooled hosting access conditional on export-criteria alignment, without new frontier spending we cannot afford.
