### Installed, not exercised
ENISA spent the autumn trying to turn broad-but-thin coverage into something exercised. Field teams were dispatched to small town utilities where automated patching had stalled over safety fears, and a ring-fenced exemption for hospital imaging systems unblocked one of the two large hospital groups that had paused. The other held out through December, citing quarantined scans.

Shield certificates were quietly made conditional on completing the older isolation drills, which forced municipal operators back to the table without forcing the patch itself. Coverage numbers improved on paper; response centres privately admitted few sites had rehearsed a live incident.

On biosecurity, joint procurement finally moved sampler orders, and wastewater feeds from a handful of hospital labs began flowing to the swap with Japan, Korea and Canada. Procurement lag meant the network entered winter partial, with uncertified grids and smaller cities still exposed.

### Concrete versus cohorts
Labour ministers adopted the Entry-Job Transition Fund decision, reallocating ESF+ money and announcing a levy on automating employers to pay first retraining cohorts in law, accountancy, software and customer support. Payouts were tied to retraining uptake, but employer groups contested the levy design and first disbursements slipped to next year. Graduate fairs stayed empty.

Anger sharpened as commercial robots appeared in controlled warehouse and logistics pilots — narrow, fixed-task machines sold strictly on replaced labour cost — and as manufacturers that had promised more capable machines spent the half-year explaining delays. Defence ministries kept carrying and mine-clearance pilots as pilots.

The holdout capital accepted priority patching help and pooled hosting talks while again refusing to unwind its separate supply arrangement, leaving cohesion cracked. Relief over better defences was real in certified hospitals and banks, but tempered by frozen entry hiring and by headlines treating automation as the channel for displacement.
