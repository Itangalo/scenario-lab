## New measure
**Grid Shield Hardening and Exercises**
Hardens transmission operators, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with mandated segmentation, detection upgrades and EU-wide live exercises. Why this and why now: the autumn grid mapping showed intruders living inside critical networks undetected, and with export controls tightening the EU cannot rely on US tooling alone to close that gap.
