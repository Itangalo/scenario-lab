July-December registries restored via reserve teams; triage pilots cut backlogs, Shield top-ups late/small. Biosecurity debate and pooled sequencing; alloys bright spot but halls empty vs US self-tooling.

January US-China strikes hit fabs, cables, stations, data centres: Atlantic landings darkened, throughput collapsed, costs spiked. EU declared non-belligerency, dispersed backups inland with new alloys — registries and triage stayed up.

Mid-spring US tailored cures forced EU licence queues via HERA; EU manufacturing cheered but airlift dependence bred resentment. Bio-detection stretched to continuity, hiring frozen.

Autumn modified model-assisted pathogen reached three member states; six-week containment with isolation and sequencing. Brussels signed joint pact mid-crisis: shared sequencing, grid telemetry, cyber liaison, binding sample-sharing — blunted parallel probes on landings/power, registries/triage stayed up, but spread preceded sharing and best therapies still foreign-licensed.

Simultaneously graduate hiring collapsed in law, accountancy, support, admin as entry roles automated by non-European systems; ending Shield payouts inadequate, protests grew. Taiwan Strait exercises raised insurance/shipping risks for repair ships and pharma flights. Europe seen as sheltering but borrowing shelter — dependent on foreign cables, chips, models.
