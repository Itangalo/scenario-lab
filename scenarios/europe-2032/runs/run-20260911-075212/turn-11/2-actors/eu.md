## New measure

Joins the standing biosurveillance pact and joint cyber command with binding sample-sharing, real-time telemetry and investigation mandate, plugging EU hospitals, labs and landing-point operators into protection Europe cannot build alone. This and now because a real model-involved bio release plus wartime cyber targeting collapses the premise of going it alone, and the invitation is the only resilience that lands within the two-year commitment to hold daily services and biosecurity on what Europe can actually run.

## In practice
We act through FAC, Health Council and JHA under Articles 168, 196 and 222 TFEU with HERA, ECDC and ENISA as operators: sign binding sample-sharing and standing investigation mandate, second Estonian-French-German reserve teams as liaison to the joint cyber command, and pipe hospital sequencing and landing-point telemetry into the shared feed under EU data safeguards.

We run M11 and M12 as the domestic legs of the same pact: stretch bio-detection seed contracts to pact standards for wartime continuity, keep Transition Shield top-ups and registry backups flowing inland, and make EU-hosted manufacturing licences conditional on pact traceability so cures do not arrive without control. Message is single: Europe shelters by sharing attribution and samples, heals on European-run care, and stays non-belligerent while powers fight.
