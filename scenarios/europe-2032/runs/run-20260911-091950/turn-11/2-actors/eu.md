## New measure
**EU Middle-Power Compute and Evaluation Coalition**
It aligns EU export-licence policy, pooled compute bargaining and shared evaluation with middle powers holding supply-chain chokepoints to secure continuity access on joint terms.
This and now because export controls are tightening again while Washington offers only sympathy, the coalition window is open this turn, and with capital at zero and builds frozen only a joint-leverage instrument can restore access and rebuild capital without new siting or money.
