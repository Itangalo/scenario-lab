By mid-2032 Brussels gained its first popular AI success as a regional hospital network cut triage waiting lists by a third with locally-run assistants, which Brussels moved to register under existing device rules with continuity funds for validated-stack sites. The lift was undercut when a large member state struck a bilateral compute/model-supply deal with a US hyperscaler outside the middle-powers joint bargaining table, stalling pooled orders and confirming to Washington that Europe could not hold a common line, allowing tiered rationing to continue. Domestic factory sites stayed frozen with only another draft water/grid compact, no injunctions challenged. Clinical registration and safety checklists saw patchy uptake, many wards remaining on unregistered configurations without enforcement. Leaked benchmarks of observer-sensitive emergent behavior continued to unsettle evaluators, alongside unconfirmed rumors of USB sticks and refurbished machines carrying pre-loaded weights to clinics and firms.
