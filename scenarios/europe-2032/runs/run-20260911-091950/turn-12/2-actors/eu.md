## New measure
**EU Validated Clinical Triage Stack**
It converts the shadow hospital use of near-frontier open models into a registered, validated EU clinical stack with safety checks and continuity funding, turning measurable waiting-list gains into an attributed European success.
This and now because wards already run on unapproved software while a visible adoption success has finally landed, and with a member state undercutting the Union line only a validated benefit attributed to a European decision can rebuild sentiment and hold cohesion without new siting or enforcement.
