By late 2032 Brussels' only popular AI success remained the regional hospital triage assistants that cut waiting lists, sustained by continuity funds limited to registered sites and validation-team tours; uptake crept up but most wards still ran mixed/unregistered configurations with no enforcement power. A funding reset collapsed the supply picture: two US hyperscalers cut build-out guidance, model-company valuations halved, European expansions were cancelled, suppliers abandoned the middle-powers joint buying table, and the large member state's bilateral deal delivered less than promised — turning tiered rationing into outright shortage. The middle-powers coalition was repurposed as a reporting club sharing incident notes. Informal circulation widened via refurbished machines with pre-loaded weights, dead drops and USB drives; police seized only a handful while clinics kept using them. The two domestic factory sites stayed frozen, and the draft water/grid compact lost urgency as capital dried up. Europe ended December with a pilot that worked where it ran, and a frontier moving slower but further out of reach.
