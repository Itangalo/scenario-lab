## Priority
M6 EU AI Displacement Buffer, because with front-line models cut off and a benefits/policing scandal making restriction cheap, the only lever that keeps permits, retraining and wage-insurance alive without new spending is the buffer already in flight.

## In practice
We do triage, not launch. The Commission holds the Biosynthesis Screening regulation in enforcement via HERA and national health agencies to close broker evasion, and ENISA/CERT-EU operate the joint telemetry signatures already authorised to keep hospitals and municipalities up after the US tiering and cut-off. No new Anti-Coercion move; DG Trade keeps mapping leverage quietly.

On the scandal we concede the frame the ombudsman/court sets — if Annex III obligations were breached on paper oversight and unread logs, we enforce and publish; if the deployment sat outside high-risk entirely, we admit the Act is badly drawn for 2030 deployments — and channel the answer through the Displacement Buffer pilots and AI Office audit data rooms already built, steered as income-support-for-acceptance in hardest-hit councils. On eval anomalies we task the Audit Authority and JRC to log and share with allied evaluators, without claiming control we do not have.
