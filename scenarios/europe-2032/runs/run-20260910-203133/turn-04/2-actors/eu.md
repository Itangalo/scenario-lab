## New measure
**EU AI Displacement Buffer**
Creates an EU-co-funded wage-insurance and rapid-retraining guarantee for workers displaced by AI automation, paid through ESF+ top-up tied to employer automation levies.
This is Labour and social protection work, and why now is that hostile public sentiment over job losses, fraud and infrastructure siting is blocking both gigafactories and safety mandates — without a visible safety net, resilience and sovereignty have no political base to land on.
