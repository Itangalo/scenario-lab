## New measure
**EU Third-Party Accession to US-China Risk Reduction Accord**
It creates a small Brussels liaison and verification cell that seeks EU observer status in the US-China weights-security and bio-design agreement and offers EU audit and synthesis-screening data as contribution. This and now because the accord is the only thing slowing capability and the Union will be informed afterwards unless it buys its way in with something verifiable, and with no capital for build-outs this cheap coordination lever is all we can afford.

## Priority
EU Third-Party Accession to US-China Risk Reduction Accord, because with zero measures in flight and capital at 3 it is the only new instrument that can prevent rationed cut-off and keep safety cooperation from becoming a US-China condominium.

## In practice
We do diplomacy, not construction. EEAS and DG TRADE jointly table an accession request in the margins of the accord, via like-minded intermediaries, offering what we actually have finished: Audit Authority logs, biosynthesis screening enforcement data, and ENISA/CERT-EU telemetry on ransomware containment, as EU contribution to weights-security and bio-design verification.

At home the Commission freezes new permitting fights and spends no fresh money; Council formation is Foreign Affairs plus Telecom, with Spain/Netherlands promised no forced hookups while we negotiate volume-licence continuity for hospitals and ministries downgraded after the autumn cut-off. If Washington rations allies alongside adversaries, we pivot the cell to joint export-control alignment with Japan and Korea rather than retaliation we cannot afford.
