## In practice
We are pushing the Shield through ENISA and the Energy and Transport Council formations, using the Cyber Resilience Act and CER Directive as legal base plus emergency joint procurement for intrusion-detection sensors and backup control systems. TSOs, port authorities and water utilities that resist mandatory audit and 24-hour reporting get access to the €2bn hardening fund only if they comply, and we pair this with cross-border exercises in Q4 led with France, Germany and Poland.

At the same time we keep the two inherited compute lines moving: InvestAI Gigafactories through DG CNECT site selection and EIB guarantees, and the tech sovereignty package through accelerated permitting zones where member-state grid-connection delays are the binding constraint. We use the autumn adoption success — falling waiting lists and faster decisions — to hold public sentiment while siting moves forward, and we fast-track adoption of the new interpretability result into EU evaluation practice via the AI Office.
