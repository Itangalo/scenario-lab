## In practice
We execute through JHA and Health Council with HERA/ECDC and DG DIGIT: designate open-weight builds already on EU hardware as the interim prescribing, triage and logistics stack in Lombardy/Berlin and cordon hospitals, with Civil Protection pre-positioned stocks and gendarmerie pharmaceutical corridors extended to depots. DG MOVE and army engineers keep power and routing for cold stores while ENISA hardens the fallback deployments.

We hold order with what exists: DG EMPL keeps wage insurance paying to separate port and logistics workers from funeral-protest blockaders and to absorb entry-level displacement, DG TRADE keeps middle-power spares moving despite US vendor warnings, and Commission publishes daily triage and resupply continuity figures to defend the one European benefit still credited. Mayors refusing grid permits get EU legal indemnity and army-engineer guarantees, not new fights.
