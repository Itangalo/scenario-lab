## In practice
We execute M5 first: ENISA and CERT-EU fully join the joint cyber command pool, hardening-fund payouts conditional on sensors and exercise participation, and DG CNECT with EEAS secures emergency licences and API failover to non-cutoff providers while pushing the 24-hour reporting rule through Council against laggard challenges.

We launch the Clean Stack through EU4Health, Digital Europe and the AI Office: curate and harden the new open-weight release, host it on existing EuroHPC and Shield-compliant national capacity — not a new gigafactory — for health and administration failover, with pre-deployment checks for offensive uplift. For siting, we tie priority grid connection and a water-price and revenue-share compact to Shield-compliant zones to answer Spanish and German councils, keeping M1 Gigafactories and M2 permitting zones in negotiation without opening a parallel build.

M3 Critical Systems Shield remains the delivery vehicle: joint teams, isolation kits and clean backups extended to smaller communes that waited last turn, laggards named but offered Stack access as the incentive to comply.
