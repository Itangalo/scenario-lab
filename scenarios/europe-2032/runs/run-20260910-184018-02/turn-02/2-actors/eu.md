Deploys pre-authorised automated-isolation playbooks, mutual-aid cyber teams and stockpiled recovery kits to keep the ransomware / supply-chain attack from cascading across public services and grid operators.

## Priority

## In practice
We trigger the Shield's cross-border aid clause early via ENISA and DG HOME, standing up the Corps from CERT-EU plus French, German and Polish national teams with emergency joint procurement for endpoint isolation and clean backups for hospitals, municipalities and the two compromised TSOs. The €2bn hardening fund is re-sequenced: first payouts only for operators who install mandated detection and join the Q1-Q2 exercises, with non-compliant operators named to Energy and JHA Councils.

At the same time we hold the compute lines from stalling: InvestAI Gigafactories and the permitting-zones package stay in site-negotiation mode, using grid-connection priority for Shield-compliant zones and quoting public-sector AI waiting-list wins to blunt Spanish and German municipal opposition. The AI Office bakes the capability jump and open-weight release into evaluation — mandatory pre-deployment checks for agentic persistence and offensive-cyber uplift, using the new interpretability control — while we concede publicly that recall is gone and resilience is the strategy.
