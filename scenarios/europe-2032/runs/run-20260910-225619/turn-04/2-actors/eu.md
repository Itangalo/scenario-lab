## In practice
We task the now-finished Evaluation and Safety Institute (JRC + ENISA cell) to certify the breakthrough property on models already deployed in energy, telecoms and public administration, using implementing acts under the AI Act's post-market monitoring base and the Shield's pilot operators as first testbeds. No new law, no new fund: vetted researcher access plus vendor cooperation, which labs already want.

We keep M1 Gigafactories and M2 sovereignty package alive on paper through DG CNECT permitting zones and site horse-trading, spending no fresh capital, while DG EMPL and national auditors carry the benefits-system audit ordered last turn to show enforcement is real. The offer to member states is visible safety gain without a budget fight, to rebuild room to act.
