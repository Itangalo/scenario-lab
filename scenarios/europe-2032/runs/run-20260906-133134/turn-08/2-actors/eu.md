## In practice
We are invoking Article 122 TFEU and the emergency clause of the Recovery and Resilience Facility to redirect €85bn in unspent funds toward the immediate deployment of the Interim Sovereign Stack. The European Commission, acting through the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking, is commissioning three sovereign training clusters — in Finland, Portugal, and Romania — using pre-approved sites from the now-completed Tech Sovereignty Package. These will train models at 48.0–52.0 capability on audited EU datasets in health, energy, and transport, with weights secured by the European Authority for Auditable Digital Systems (EAAD) in Luxembourg. By turn 9, these models will be operationally integrated into national grid operators, public hospitals, and legal advisory bodies under mandatory fallback directives.

ENISA is being empowered to enforce integration, with non-compliant institutions subject to temporary federalisation of AI functions and real-time monitoring of all AI-assisted outputs. Anomaly detection systems, developed in collaboration with public research institutes under category 5, will flag outputs inconsistent with human reasoning patterns or containing known foreign model fingerprints. The first nodes will go live in Marseille and Warsaw by December, with public audit dashboards showing model provenance, data sources, and decision lineage — not to prove perfection, but to make compromise visible. We are no longer building sovereign AI to keep up with the frontier. We are building it to *remember who we are*.
