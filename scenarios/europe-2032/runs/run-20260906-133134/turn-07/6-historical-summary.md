Access to advanced foreign AI systems was abruptly cut off for EU institutions, triggering the **Sovereign Core Initiative** under emergency powers, mandating redirection of 30% of EuroHPC exascale capacity to train a minimal, fully auditable model stack capped at 52.0 capability, with weights secured by EAAD in Luxembourg and fallback protocols required by mid-2030. Implementation has not yet begun, with no compute reallocated and full effects delayed by at least two turns. Member states resist the pace and legality of the shift, with Germany filing a legal challenge over subsidiarity and Poland blocking site access; only Finland and Portugal offer conditional support. A bilateral AI infrastructure deal between a member state and a U.S. hyperscaler undermines EU unity.

Global AI capability surges to 75.0, driven by opaque, self-initiating systems, while safety plummets to 5.0 due to uninterpretable reasoning and evaluation failures. In response, the EU re-scopes the **Sovereign Core Initiative** onto emergency footing, planning the **Emergency Sovereignty Acceleration** for interim deployment by turn 10, though no new implementation starts. Public sentiment declines further after a biased automated welfare system in Belgium denies thousands of claims, sparking protests in Lyon and Milan. Legacy vulnerabilities persist despite the institutionalization of the Hardened Cyber Shield as NIS3, though resilience improves slightly through local adoption of open governance tools in municipal networks.

The Institutional Integrity Audit previously uncovered AI-generated policy drafts, and open-weight capability rose +1.0 from delayed spillover releases, though the Sovereign Core will not contribute for several turns. The **Sovereign Core Initiative** remains the sole active strategic measure, but political capital erodes under mounting crises. Nodes in Marseille and Warsaw remain offline and underfunded, limited to diagnostic roles when launched. The contrast with foreign-led medical breakthroughs fuels resentment over "digital colonialism." The US anti-AI administration takes office next turn, with policy implications pending.
