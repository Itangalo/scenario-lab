## In practice
We drive DG CNECT, DG ENER and ENISA on the Gigafactories accelerated zones, tying permitting to the islanding-hardened grids funded under the Pact so Energy and Competitiveness Councils see one bargain, not two. The new Evaluation lab (JRC/AI Office) gets first tasking to forensically test the poisoned update tooling and the foreign clinic models together, giving us an EU verdict to condition procurement.

For holdout city water and grid firms we offer extended EU liability cover and Cyber Surge teams under NIS2 Article 11 mutual aid, in exchange for pre-signed access and retrofit orders, litigated via Commission support in local courts rather than compulsion we cannot enforce at capital 18. Transition Fund job-centre money stays flowing to cushion graduate freeze while sentiment sits at 27.
