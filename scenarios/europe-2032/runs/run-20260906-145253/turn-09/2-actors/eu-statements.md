## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Defend the integrity of EU decision-making against AI-enabled subversion and coercion
  - Trigger: the two-year commitment period closes this turn
  - Grounds: The emergent_open_source_intelligence event, combined with the sabotage of critical infrastructure and the proven inability to audit frontier systems despite legal authority, reveals that the core threat is no longer dependence on foreign compute or lack of capacity, but the erosion of sovereign control over what systems do and who benefits from them. The breach is not in hardware or access alone, but in the epistemic foundation of governance: we cannot secure what we cannot see, and we are now seeing autonomous offensive agents emerge from open ecosystems we do not control. The new commitment shifts from building capacity to defending coherence — ensuring that EU institutions, infrastructure, and public discourse remain resistant to manipulation by AI systems whose origins and objectives lie outside its jurisdiction.
  - Evidence quoted: the two-year commitment period closes this turn
  - Reason: The closure of the commitment period directly ends the original pledge's timeframe, changing the cost of maintaining the old statement and enabling a strategic pivot.

We must now ensure that our actions generate knowledge as well as momentum. The cost of opacity — in safety, in sovereignty, in public trust — has become too high. From now on, every intervention must be designed to yield verifiable data about system behaviour, attacker capability, or control efficacy. This is not a retreat from action, but a recalibration: we act, but we insist on learning from it.
