# Statements: The European Union (turn 8)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI control to withstand strategic shocks and prevent catastrophic misuse — but we now acknowledge that control without cohesion is an illusion, and that sovereignty cannot be built on a foundation of coercion. Therefore, we shift the focus of this commitment: from securing control through enforcement, to securing the Union’s ability to act as one by rebuilding political capital from the ground up — starting with the legitimacy of its rules, the fairness of their application, and the protection of those displaced by the very systems we govern.
