### A Pause in the Storm

AI capability inches upward to 70.5, as frontier labs consolidate agent frameworks without a major leap. Signs of a plateau emerge: a flagship model underperforms, and prices for top-tier access drop, suggesting commoditization. The open frontier advances steadily—fueled by diffusion of recent breakthroughs—pushing openweight capability to 58.0. The gap between closed and open narrows, and misuse risks grow, but openweight remains below frontier AI capability, consistent with its dependent trajectory.

### The Union Responds to Fracture

The EU completes two major initiatives: the Sovereign AI Core Programme delivers its first sovereign training run, and the Resilience Reinforcement Initiative rolls out patching bots and detection systems. However, their impact is muted. Sovereignty gains no boost—capability rose, and no event secured access—while political capital continues to bleed.

The Commission proposes the European Adjustment and Reintegration Facility, a €45bn response to spreading strikes in legal and administrative sectors. Framed as a bid to restore legitimacy, it links cohesion funds to action on job displacement, not AI containment. France and Germany back the shift; Poland and Hungary signal conditional support, demanding delayed transparency commitments. But no funding is disbursed this turn—inter-institutional negotiations are just beginning, and full implementation will take at least two turns. The measure is added to the portfolio, contributing to the burden on political capital, but its effects are not yet active.

Public sentiment rises slightly, as the proposal is seen as responsive—strikes pause, not end. Resilience improves with reduced social friction, earning a modest +3. The portfolio’s existing large initiatives continue to drain capital: Sovereign AI Core Programme (−3), Resilience Reinforcement Initiative (−3), Enforce Cohesion Through Conditional Access (−3), and the new proposal adds a priority cost of −1. The proposal bonus is limited: while addressing emergent unrest, it follows a failed enforcement attempt, yielding only +1.

### External Pressures Mount

Washington tightens control over ASML, restricting even mature-node exports—a move felt across EU industry. A member state again bypasses EU channels to secure frontier access, further eroding unity. With no access secured and capability rising, sovereignty falls to 10.0.
