## New measure
**EU Continuity Switch for Essential Services**
It moves hospitals, ministries and essential operators cut off from the leading US model onto EU-hosted alternatives within months via emergency procurement and funded migration. Why this and why now is because the cutoff has turned dependence into live disruption of care and administration, and only a visible switch restores function while proving the two-year commitment to stay in control through shocks.

## Priority
M5 EU Continuity Switch for Essential Services, because the access cutoff is the outage in progress this turn and outranks even the Shield and Gigafactories under our two-year commitment to keep the lights on.

## In practice
We trigger the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority and DG DIGIT joint procurement vehicle to pay for licence-switching, re-integration and extra inference on EU-anchored clouds — OVHcloud, Deutsche Telekom, Mistral and Aleph Alpha capacity — for the affected hospitals and ministries, using the Civil Protection Mechanism and EU4Health top-up so operators do not bear the cost of Washington's decision. The Health and Telecom Councils give political cover, ENISA certifies the substitute models for essential use.

At the same time we keep the Gigafactories site contests and Tech sovereignty permitting zones moving on existing mandates without new money, and we push the Incident Reporting regulation into trilogue so the open-weight wave now on private hardware is at least reported within 72 hours. On export controls we signal volume-licence alignment with Washington in exchange for restored access, while quietly preparing an Anti-Coercion Instrument file if rationing hits allies.
