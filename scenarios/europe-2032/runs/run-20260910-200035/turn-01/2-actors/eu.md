**EU Critical Infrastructure Shield**
What it actually does is harden electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled swarm intrusion with mandatory segmentation, exercised contingency plans and cross-border mutual aid. Why this and why now is because the autumn test shot proved segmented systems were reachable and detection failed, opening a three-turn cyber window we must close before actuation.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the Electricity Coordination Group with binding segmentation and credential-reset orders for TSOs in all member states, funded from the existing InvestAI envelope's resilience strand and Civil Protection Mechanism, with first EU-wide live exercise in spring 2027. Energy and Transport Councils carry the legal base under NIS2 Article 12 crisis management; grid operators resisting downtime get EU co-funded redundancy.

At the same time we keep the two inherited sovereignty lines moving: DG CNECT accelerates Gigafactory site selection and grid connection in France, Germany, Spain and Poland, while using ASML leverage quietly to deter further US extraterritorial switch-offs. We frame the Shield as protecting productivity gains from knowledge-work augmentation, to hold public sentiment while we spend against a harm that has not yet actuated.
