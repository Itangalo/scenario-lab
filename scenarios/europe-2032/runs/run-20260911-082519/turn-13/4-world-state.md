### The cascade
Autumn began with locked screens in city halls, hospitals and regional utilities across half a dozen member states. A largely automated ransomware sweep, built with model-generated tooling, moved through a compromised software dependency faster than defenders could map it. Backups held in some places, failed in others. Bin collections, appointment systems and benefits payments went manual for weeks. Attribution feeds from the joint command cell tracked hops but named no one.

The restoration cell Brussels stood up became the visible front line. Pooled clean images, emergency continuity payments and teams dispatched through the joint cyber command channel got core services rebooted. Where thresholds and kill-switch drills from the containment work were already live, isolation was faster. Where they were not, councils queued for help.

### Parity in the open
In the middle of the outage, a new openly released model family landed within months of the closed frontier. Hundreds of thousands of downloads in days put near-frontier capability on private hardware permanently. Security commentators noted the timing bluntly: the same class of tooling that automated the attack was now unrecallable.

### The benefits files
Then ombudsmen in two countries published findings on an AI-supported benefits and fraud-scoring system. Caseworkers had approved flagged cases at forty seconds an item, conformity paperwork had passed, logs that would have shown systematic wrongful cuts were generated and never read. It was ruled a breached high-risk deployment. Brussels opened an enforcement case, ordered public log review and remedy, insisting the law was sound and enforcement had failed. Victims' groups and evening talk shows heard it differently: the oversight had been theatre.

Trusted triage assistants in nurse-validated clinics kept publishing earlier discharges, but the split screen hardened. Helpful wards, frozen town halls, wronged claimants. Mayors who wanted the health contracts now demanded restoration money first, and questioned why protection still stopped at membership lines.

Hardware stayed fenced and silent. Europe ended the year rebooted but shaken — services restored, trust not.
