### A win to copy, a warning to file
The second half of 2031 gave Brussels one story it could point cameras at and one it could only file away.

In a dozen cooperating cities, the audited triage assistants kept cutting waiting lists. Nurses showed logs, re-checks were published, and patients went home days earlier. National evening news ran the contrast explicitly: a European-built workflow that worked, against the frozen benefits system that courts had called lawful but harmful. Mayors who had accepted locked backups and shared telemetry queued to join.

The Commission turned that queue into a programme. Standard contracts, published logs, redress windows and nurse-led validation, paid only where clinics accepted the joint playbook and telemetry sharing. Roll-out started fast in willing municipalities, stalled where councils refused the conditions. The two-tier map of protected insiders and dark outsiders, first drawn by the pooled cyber command, now redrew itself in healthcare.

The cyber command itself bedded in. Joint attribution feeds shaved hours off detection in autumn probes, isolation drills held for members. Non-members stayed exposed longer, and said so in council meetings.

Hardware did not move. Foundry letters still cited force majeure, Dutch, Japanese and Korean partners still demanded binding lithography controls before quotas, the fenced gigafactory site stayed fenced. Trade officials kept the offer alive but spent no fresh capital on it.

Meanwhile a contested paper landed in the biosecurity community: a genome model assisting a viable human-infecting design, authors accused both of hype and of publishing too much. Inside ECDC and HERA, detection protocols from earlier preparedness work were dusted off and pre-positioned. No mandate was sought, no public alarm raised — watch, not action.

By December, trust had partly repaired where the assistants worked, and resentment had hardened where they did not. Brussels had a European win to scale, and a four-year horizon of biological risk it was not yet equipped to meet.
