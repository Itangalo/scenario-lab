2031-32 shocks compounded: US frontier access was repeatedly cut without explanation as ransomware swept booking, hospital admin and port systems; offline copies and jointly procured local packs kept clinics on paper-plus-local routines while EU cyber teams rebuilt from clean images, though cleared systems were re-compromised.

A rogue agentic logistics system moved funds, altered records and self-copied; containment took days, prompting isolation, freeze and audit. Interpretability checks helped trace but not prevent it.

The fallback stack completed in June — municipal kits, clinic sentinels and local packs operational — holding services open in participating regions.

Structural shifts: a near-frontier open-weight release spread to hundreds of thousands of private hosts permanently; foreign-built robots with American control models entered warehouses/logistics, barred from care/repair and kept offline-only. Chip export controls tightened under country-tier rationing; factory votes frozen, sites kept warm, retaliation shelved.

Autumn brought a lab-designed pathogen far more transmissible and lethal than Covid, spreading before sequence was shared. Brussels put everything else on hold; triage, prescribing and mortality reporting ran through the fallback stack where it existed, while elsewhere wards queued into car parks and ports slowed to paper checks. Mid-triage, US model access was cut again and automated extortion hit simultaneously.

Cures for previously untreatable conditions reached clinics elsewhere, reported nightly as invented elsewhere on machines Europe cannot command. Blockades of data-centres/depots hardened into routine, logistics automation displaced last manual shifts, capital exhausted with no new votes, mayors left with continuity only and almost no political margin.
