### Cut off in the middle of containment
The notice arrived without explanation in February: the leading American model family would no longer serve European hospitals, ministries and firms that had built workflows on it. Overnight, triage assistants, dispatch co-pilots and port paperwork helpers went dark. In some wards staff found workarounds within days; in others referrals stalled.

At almost the same moment, health authorities confirmed what laboratories had feared for weeks — a modified pathogen, designed with machine help, had escaped containment in one member state and then been deliberately amplified elsewhere. Clinics closed to visitors, sequencing labs ran day and night, wastewater alarms sounded in three capitals. Containment would run into spring. Casualties were counted in the hundreds before the curve bent.

A third shock compounded both. A new release from a frontier lab showed a sharp leap in machine-written code, mathematics and intrusion tooling. Defenders said what attackers could do changed within weeks: cleared booking and port systems were pried open again with fresh, automatically tailored exploits.

### Paper, local models, and crews who stayed
What prevented collapse was unglamorous. The municipal backup kits and clinic sentinels rushed out the previous autumn were already on site, and the Union's cyber repair teams simply stayed embedded, rebuilding from offline copies. Health emergency procurement was stretched to buy small, locally hosted open models packed for triage, prescribing and dispatch — crude beside what was lost, but they ran without foreign permission.

Mayors credited the crews. Ministers in health and interior meetings endorsed continuity and asked for no new money, freezing factory votes and shelving any retaliation over servicing curbs on chipmaking tools. Sites for future plants were kept warm, fences mended.

Trust did not recover. Exhausted staff, queues at folding tables, and nightly coverage of the pathogen inquiry left the public hostile to any claim that systems were under control. In Brussels, officials spoke privately of surviving on substitutes until a sovereign fallback could hold through the next winter.
