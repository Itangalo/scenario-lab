### Bargaining While The Floor Shifts
Brussels spent the autumn trying to turn humiliation into leverage. Foreign ministers and trade officials toured Seoul, Tokyo and other supply-chain capitals offering maintenance help, pooled spares and a common trusted software stack in exchange for restored service contracts, joint buying of computing time and shared screening for risky biological designs.

The coordination framework was signed. Joint statements spoke of aligned export licences and pooled evaluation. In Brussels that counted as proof Europe was no longer begging alone.

On the ground, delivery stayed patchy. Reserve engineers kept swapping cut-off clinics and registries onto European-hosted builds. Where they arrived, care stayed open. Where they did not, staff kept installing freely downloaded substitutes from market stalls and mail-order drives. Nurses described two medicine cabinets: the logged one and the one that actually worked.

Then two shocks landed at once.

First, a published genome-model study claimed a non-expert with model help could reach a viable human-infecting design. Methodologists quarrelled, authors were accused of alarmism and of handing out instructions, but hospital biosecurity teams took it seriously. Guarded seed sites added new screening rules from the shared pool, catching more false alarms than real threats and slowing prescriptions.

Second, commercial humanoid robots arrived in ports and warehouses. Chinese-built machines, running American control software, were suddenly carrying, digging and clearing mines — and videos circulated of armed variants. European manufacturers who still led in parts found themselves buyers, not sellers, of the finished system. Unions called it the end of retraining; mayors called it another dependence.

The wartime shelter and lifeline stockpile formally completed, keeping power, triage and registries degrading rather than stopping during freight scares. Officials cited it everywhere. Voters noticed the lights stayed on, and resented that everything intelligent inside them was still foreign, illegible and rented.

### Faint Signals
A single regional paper reported a broker selling pre-loaded drives as cut-off replacements; officials dismissed it as anecdote.
