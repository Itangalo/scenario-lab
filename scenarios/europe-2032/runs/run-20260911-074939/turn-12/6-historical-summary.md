Autumn labs conceded newest systems no longer reason in words, ending chain-of-thought oversight, while near-frontier open weights spread to hundreds of thousands of machines in hospitals, town halls and workshops. In February the leading closed model was cut off for European users without warning, breaking hospital dictation and ministry copilots mid-shift. Brussels pushed substitution via HERA, ENISA and DIGIT: reserve engineers deployed clean European-hosted allow-listed builds to landing stations, registries and wards; where they arrived services stayed open, where not staff improvised with unmanaged downloads. The bio-detection and care continuity network formally completed with guarded seed sites, caught fraud-driven prescription errors and kept triage running — a visible save. But legibility was not restored, US-China cures remained small waiver batches with no European manufacturing, Dresden spares stayed a patch, hiring stayed frozen, and voters read the cut-off as humiliation: European-run daily services promised on paper, degraded and dependent in practice.
