## In practice

Recovery funds already conditioned on telemetry-sharing become the lever: no patch-agent and isolation-gate deployment, no payout. French-Dutch containment teams pivot from clean-up to installation in the worst-hit districts.

We fund this by reprogramming Digital Europe cyber lines and EIB emergency cover, not new money, to avoid a Council budget fight we would lose at capital 12. Health ministries and municipal associations are offered EU-hosted hardened models for the patch controllers to blunt hostility to Brussels tooling, and the autumn fallback drills are repurposed as live rollout checks for hospitals and registries.
