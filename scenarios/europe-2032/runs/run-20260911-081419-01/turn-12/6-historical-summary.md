Autumn's automated assault on town halls/hospitals was followed in spring by relief on two fronts. The EU Sovereign Fallback Stack (M11) municipal shield finished rollout in most large cities/hospital networks: auto-patch and swarm-detection caught a March machine-written intrusion wave in hours vs weeks last autumn. Small communes still lagged on staffing/false positives, but ENISA certification and conditional funds kept installs moving; M11 now largely deployed.

Simultaneously, foreign-developed tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions reached ordinary clinics, with queues at university hospitals. Brussels added no new money/measure, tying the two together via joint procurement, accelerated authorisation, and deployment on European-hosted certified infrastructure with verified provenance and diagnostic models alongside patch agents to avoid reopening attack surface, promising local data control.

Trust lifted off rock bottom for first time in two years on patient word-of-mouth, but Europe dispenses without producing: supply terms remain foreign-controlled, and diplomats got only briefings not verification on the Washington-Beijing weights/bio-tools understanding. That accord slowed the frontier race only slightly, aiding safety researchers without restoring confidence in top systems. AI valuation reset, compute build-out cuts, and reprogramming of Digital Europe funds from compute to patching persist.
