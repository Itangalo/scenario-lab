### MPAC Declared Operational Amid Strategic Shifts

The European Union formally declared the Middle Power AI Coalition (MPAC) operational, cementing provisional agreements with Japan, South Korea, Norway, and Canada’s intelligence-sharing arrangement into a binding coordination framework under Article 218 TFEU. Side-payment mechanisms tied to market access for EU compute and evaluation services activated immediately, reinforcing collective leverage. Despite Canada’s delayed ratification, the network proved its value during the recent hospital cyber incident, enabling rapid anomaly detection and coordinated countermeasure deployment across jurisdictions.

### Sovereign Core Stabilises, Legal Challenges Withdrawn

The Sovereign AI Core Programme achieved full audit-verified operation, with clusters in Brittany and northern Sweden now running independent of foreign hyperscalers. Germany and Austria withdrew legal challenges in exchange for guaranteed oversight board seats and binding commitments to expand into Bavaria and Tyrol by turn 12. ENTSO-E protocols ensured stable integration, and the first EU-governed training run above 65.0 capability is scheduled for July, marking a pivotal shift toward autonomous development.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Erodes Further

`ai_capability` advanced to 68.0, driven by sustained investment and closed-loop improvement cycles. However, no corresponding gains in assurance were realised. Unexplained reasoning shifts in unreleased models deepened, straining interpretability frameworks. With no new safety measures landing on shipped systems, `ai_safety` fell by 2 points to 25.0. The gap between capability and control widened, increasing systemic risk.

### Resilience Gains Modestly, Sentiment Stagnates

The MPAC’s rapid response to the hospital attack demonstrated tangible value, contributing to a +1 increase in `resilience`. However, labour displacement in law, accounting, and software continued unchecked, and public protests in Lyon and Vienna underscored persistent resentment. No category 7 or 9 measures concluded, and `public_sentiment` remained frozen at 14.0.

### Sovereignty Edges Up, Political Capital Holds

`eu_ai_sovereignty` rose to 16.0, buoyed by the secured compute base and operational MPAC, offsetting the −1 from `ai_capability`’s rise. With the coalition measure finishing, portfolio pressure eased. `eu_political_capital` improved slightly to 18.0, reflecting the success of a major initiative despite ongoing fiscal strain.
