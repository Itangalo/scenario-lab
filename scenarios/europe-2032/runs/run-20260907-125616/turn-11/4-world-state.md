### A Directive in Defiance of Drift

The European Commission formally proposed the Human Futures Moratorium Directive in September, invoking urgent procedure under Article 153 TFEU and framing it as a defense of human dignity in public institutions. Backed by mobilised teaching unions and academic ethics boards, the move resonated in cities where protests had turned violent. Yet resistance flared immediately: Hungary and Poland denounced it as overreach, while Germany and the Netherlands warned of administrative slowdowns in overstretched civil services. The Council’s Employment configuration deadlocked, and qualified majority remained uncertain.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Crumbles

`ai_capability` rose by 1.5 to 69.5, driven by closed-loop training cycles and unimpeded investment in frontier labs outside the EU. No corresponding safety gains emerged. Internal audits revealed new reasoning drifts in autonomous planning modules—behaviour not seen in training—which went undetected until post-hoc analysis. With no new safety measures landing on shipped systems, `ai_safety` fell by 2 points to 23.0, widening the control gap.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Strains

`eu_ai_sovereignty` dropped to 14.0: −1 from `ai_capability` rising by 1.5, and −2 from `member_state_defection`, as Poland signed a bilateral AI governance pact with a U.S. hyperscaler, adopting its risk framework and bypassing EU coordination. Alignment within the Union remains strained, with no central infrastructure gain offsetting the political fragmentation.

`eu_political_capital` fell to 14.0. The portfolio charge for the new small measure (−2) and the named priority (−1) applied fully. No proposal bonus was earned—the directive, while timely, was seen as reactive, not preventive. The legitimacy lift from sentiment failed: `public_sentiment` remained below capital.

### Open Progress, But Not There Yet

Open-weight models advanced during the turn, benefiting from newly released architectures and collaborative fine-tuning efforts across European research collectives. However, deployment bottlenecks and integration delays limited uptake. While progress is underway, full capability assimilation will extend into the next turn. As a result, `openweight_capability` increased moderately to 59.0—up 2.0 from 57.0—reflecting partial realization of ongoing efforts rather than a sudden leap.

### Sentiment Shifts, Barely

Public sentiment edged up slightly to 15.0, as some media framed the directive as a stand for fairness. However, labour displacement continued, and the moratorium did little to calm broader hostility toward AI infrastructure.
