### Sovereign Compute Proposed Amid Crises

The European Commission formally proposed the Sovereign Compute Acceleration Programme, a large category 4 measure, invoking emergency funding and calling for rapid data centre construction in Finland, Ireland, and Romania. The proposal triggered fast-track procedures, but no physical construction has begun, and no legal or planning milestones have been achieved this turn. Germany and the Netherlands expressed conditional support, pending academic access and procurement oversight mechanisms. Poland refused to host any facilities, citing existing contracts with U.S. hyperscalers, deepening infrastructural fragmentation. As a high-cost measure, implementation will take multiple turns; no material capacity has been added this turn. The funding mechanism remains unspecified, with no agreement yet on budget reallocations or EU-level borrowing, delaying activation of the programme.

The Emergency Cyber Resilience Surge concluded, delivering partial hardening of healthcare and civil registry systems. While no cascading failure occurred during the recent cyber incident, patching was reactive and uneven, earning a resilience +3. The Human Futures Moratorium Directive also finished, banning AI in public hiring and admissions — a symbolic win, but one that did not shift public mood.

### Capability Surge, Safety Erosion

`ai_capability` rose by +1.5 to 72.5, driven by unchecked progress in closed-loop training at frontier labs. The biosecurity community remains in turmoil after new genome model findings suggested non-experts could design human-infective organisms — a signal that offensive biology is nearing automation. Adaptive cyberattacks using opaque reasoning systems have increased in frequency, but no major system failure or policy rollback occurred this turn. Given the absence of a catastrophic safety failure or dismantling of existing safeguards, `ai_safety` decreased moderately to 16.0, reflecting growing concern and erosion of confidence, but within the bounds of plausible annual drift rather than a sudden collapse.

`openweight_capability` advanced to 61.5, reflecting delayed absorption of prior closed-model breakthroughs into open frameworks. The +1.5 increase matches the rise in `ai_capability`, preserving the invariant that openweight capability cannot outpace or exceed overall AI capability.

### Political Capital Holds, Sovereignty Edges Down

Despite proposing a large measure, political capital rose slightly to 13.0. The portfolio charge was −6 (two finished measures, one prior priority), and the proposal bonus for Sovereign Compute was +3 — a large, direct response to recent cyber and sovereignty shocks. No legitimacy boost applied. Sovereignty decreased by 1.0 to 12.0: −1 due to rising `ai_capability`, with no offsetting gain, as the Sovereign Compute Programme has not yet advanced beyond proposal. Initiation of site selection, legal activation, or governance frameworks has not occurred, and thus no sovereignty benefit is accrued. The programme remains in the planning and negotiation phase.

Public sentiment remained at 15.0 — no perceived benefit, continued job losses.
