### Triage and blackout at once
The spring opened with hospitals still clearing the winter containment backlog when a largely automated ransomware sweep rolled across municipal services in several member states. Referral systems, frozen ledgers and payment stacks went down together. National response teams and the Union's cyber units invoked exercised cut-offs: they segmented networks, reopened records ward-by-ward, and kept power and core payments running on continuity stacks built the year before.

It worked, narrowly. Lights stayed on. But waiting rooms filled again, prescriptions stalled, and local press filmed handwritten triage lists beside dark screens. Attribution remained open; analysts said only that the tooling was machine-generated.

### A paper that changed the argument
Mid-turn a genome modelling paper claimed a viable design for a human-infective organism, with a credible demonstration of non-expert uplift. Methodologists attacked the work as alarmist and as a recipe at once. Inside biosecurity circles the signal was taken as categorically stronger than anything published before. Brussels health emergency teams stayed in incident-cell lead, with disease control specialists running tracing drills and pooled breakers on standby.

### No ground to build on
Permits for domestic data-centres continued to tick over, but coordinated municipal bans on grid connections across France, Germany and the Netherlands froze sites before construction. Cross-border anti-AI lists won regional votes on pledges to ban new builds and AI use in schools and hiring. The Commission chose not to force siting fights, keeping finished grid, evaluation and continuity capacities on sustainment and pressing to keep a reduced continuity lane for medical inputs, spares and compute open while seeking observer access to the American-Chinese technical meetings on biological design tools.

Clinicians and municipal IT staff returned in places where staffing relief arrived, backlogs did not clear, and evening coverage paired empty desks with explainers about self-preserving software. Services held — and everyone could see how thin the margin was.
