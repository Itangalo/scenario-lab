The emergent_regional_eval_coalition led by France, Germany, Belgium, and the Netherlands reveals a deeper truth: the Union’s oversight is fracturing, and member states are acting unilaterally because they do not trust Brussels to move fast enough. That ends now. This is not a request. It is a condition of operating in or serving the EU. Resistance is expected — OpenAI, Anthropic, and DeepSeek have all signalled non-cooperation — but we will tie market access to real-time auditability.
