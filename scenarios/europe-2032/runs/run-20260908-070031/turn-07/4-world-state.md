### Capability Leaps Ahead  
Global AI capability advances by +3.0 to 70.0 as the verification_widens event enables rapid, automated improvement in legal, clinical, and engineering domains. Systems now autonomously refine their own reasoning in economically critical areas once thought to require human validation. This accelerates closed-loop development, pushing the frontier toward sustained self-improvement. No safety mechanisms keep pace.

### One Measure Completed, Another Delayed  
The EU completes the Emergency Resilience Surge, with the European Resilience Corps now operational in Helsinki, Lyon, and Rotterdam. Cross-border deployment protocols are fast-tracked via the Civil Protection Mechanism, and ENISA finalises incident escalation standards. The first live-failover drills succeed, absorbing simulated cascading failures. However, the InvestAI Gigafactories remain delayed—no new construction has started, and supply-chain bottlenecks persist. Only one major measure can conclude per turn; completion of the Surge takes precedence.

### Emergency Powers Announced, Not Enforced  
In response to the emergent_regional_eval_coalition—now conducting real-time monitoring of live AI systems in energy and transport—the European Commission invokes emergency powers to propose the Sovereign Evaluation Mandate. The mandate would require real-time monitoring of high-capability AI systems serving EU infrastructure, but it is not yet implemented. Legal consultations are underway, and formal adoption is expected no earlier than next turn. US labs have already signalled opposition, calling it extraterritorial overreach, while Italy questions its proportionality. For now, the framework exists only in draft.

### Resilience Gains Traction  
The operational launch of the Resilience Corps and successful drills contribute to a rise in resilience to 52.0, reflecting both completed hardening and institutional momentum.

### Political Capital Rebounds Modestly  
Political capital improves due to the completion of one large measure (+4) and the proposal bonus for the Resilience Corps (+3), offset by ongoing portfolio costs (−3) and the priority penalty (−1). With only one major completion counted, the capital gain is more modest, rising to 14.0—still fragile, but no longer at control threshold.

### Sovereignty Erodes Further  
With no category 4 completion delivering new capacity and ai_capability rising by 3.0, eu_ai_sovereignty falls to 12.0. The emergent coalition underscores dependence, not independence. Openweight capability rises more slowly, to 57.5, maintaining its lag behind the frontier while respecting the constraint that it cannot grow faster than ai_capability.
