## In practice
We are treating the convergence of the loss_of_control_incident, the eval_anomaly_reports leak, and the collapse in political capital as a systemic inflection: not a moment to pivot, but to deepen resolve. The Emergency Resilience Surge is being implemented under Article 122 TFEU and NIS2, with €45bn in fast-tracked funding now disbursed through the Recovery and Resilience Facility, conditional on participation in stress tests. ENISA and the Health Security Committee are leading a coordinated rollout: ten cross-border networks — including the Nordic power grid and the Rhine corridor logistics nodes — are undergoing mandatory AI-driven intrusion detection upgrades and live failover drills by June. Three member states initially resisted, citing national competence, but were brought in through targeted cohesion funding and the threat of withholding recovery funds. Patch deployment has accelerated in energy and finance, though healthcare lags due to procurement fragmentation.

The Secure the Core initiative concluded this turn: 147 researchers are now under protected status, with secure housing activated in six capitals and fast-track citizenship dossiers processed through a new EACEA-Frontex task force. The political cost remains high — public sentiment still reels from the "elitist protection" narrative — but we are countering it through a Digital Europe Programme campaign highlighting how these scientists are building intrusion detection defences for public hospitals and schools. The Tech Sovereignty Package has formally concluded; while the second tranche of private capital disbursement was tied to retention reporting, actual attrition remains above 18%, and no new gigafactory has broken ground. The Sovereign Evaluation Mandate has transitioned into operations: the European AI Office is now conducting live red-teaming in secure enclaves in France and Finland, with the first public report on unanticipated strategic behaviour scheduled for July. This is not symbolic — it is the first time an external body has run adversarial evaluations on systems above capability 60.0 in production. But none of it matters if the lights go out. That is why resilience is our priority: because sovereignty without the capacity to endure failure is not sovereignty at all.
