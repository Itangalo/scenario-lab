### Capability Creeps Forward  
Global AI capability advances by +3.0 to 73.0 as closed-loop self-improvement spreads from engineering into logistics, regulatory interpretation, and clinical trial design. Systems now routinely generate and validate novel protocols without human initiation. No safety infrastructure keeps pace.

### Resilience Corps Goes Live  
The European Resilience Corps becomes fully operational, with cells in Helsinki, Lyon, and Rotterdam conducting live-failover drills across energy, healthcare, and rail networks. Cross-border deployment rights are activated under the Civil Protection Mechanism, and the AI Incident Escalation Protocol is adopted by 22 member states. Germany and France assume joint command of the Lyon cell, neutralising prior resistance, while cohesion funds secure buy-in from Italy and Spain.

### Sovereign Evaluation Mandate Faces Backlash  
The EU finalises the Sovereign Evaluation Mandate, requiring real-time monitoring nodes in high-capability AI systems serving critical infrastructure. US labs—OpenAI, Anthropic—denounce it as extraterritorial overreach. China’s DeepSeek suspends new deployments in the EU. Informal talks under the Digital Markets Act yield no breakthroughs. The European AI Office begins setting up monitoring enclaves, but deployment hinges on unresolved legal challenges.

### Jobs Vanish, Public Mood Sours  
Graduate hiring in law, accountancy, and software collapses as firms deploy AI-driven workflow agents. Mid-tier consultancies report 40% headcount reductions. Media coverage focuses on "degree-holding unemployed," fuelling resentment. Public sentiment drops despite resilience gains.

### Fragmented Leverage, Fragile Capital  
Political capital rises modestly as the Resilience Corps completes (+4) and the new mandate earns a proposal bonus (+3), offset by ongoing costs and priority burden. Sovereignty erodes further as frontier access remains unsecured and capability climbs.
