## New measure
**EU Youth Transition Guarantee and Municipal Repair Corps**
Puts displaced young graduates and junior staff into paid repair, triage and care-support roles tied to retraining and wage insurance, deployed through municipalities and hospitals hit by the sweep.
This and now because graduate hiring has frozen and sentiment is zero while adoption successes give us something visible to staff and scale; it is category 7 Labour and social protection, small and fundable now, and it directly serves the two-year commitment to cushion displaced workers while hardening services.

## Priority
EU Youth Transition Guarantee and Municipal Repair Corps, because without a visible jobs answer protests at data-centres and job centres will block any further hardening or sovereignty move.

## In practice
We run this through ESF+ emergency reallocation and the single claims window mayors already use: EPSCO authorises a two-year wage-insurance top-up, municipalities hire traineeship-cancelled cohorts into cyber-hygiene, records recovery, and care-admin support teams alongside the ENISA recovery corps. No new regulation, just a Council recommendation plus Commission implementing decision.

We tie it to the adoption successes — waiting-list and permit-time gains — staffing their scale-up with Corps members, and we negotiate with employers a pledge to reopen junior pipelines in exchange for flexibility on restructuring. ASML coercion is answered separately by holding the line in the Foreign Affairs Council, not with a new instrument this turn.
