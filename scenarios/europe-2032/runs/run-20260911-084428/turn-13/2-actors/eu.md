## In practice
We task ENISA with the Cyber Recovery Corps remnant and the EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute leftovers to vet the commune toolkit — sandboxing, model pinning, logging to the pooled telemetry feed — via Commission recommendation and Council conclusions, not a directive we cannot pass. Funding is redeployed Youth Guarantee municipal lines; no new money vote.

We instruct ministries and the two triage-gain regions to keep Corps-staffed paper-plus-local-model desks as the official fallback, and feed their logs and hospital samples into the joint watchtower via ENISA/ECDC nodes. On the Netherlands servicing dispute and the member-state defection we hold the rhetorical line and slow-roll, refusing a second front. On the capability jump and eval anomaly we issue no thresholds now; we push detection drills and incident reporting through the vetted toolkit.
