### The Cost of Access Denied

The frontier AI capability continues its steep climb, rising to 78.5 as self-improving systems refine training architectures at machine speed. With reasoning now opaque even to developers, safety plummets to −10.0, reflecting a complete loss of control over deployed models. The open frontier advances to 60.5, as earlier open releases and ongoing model distillation—accelerated by the 2028 leak wave—continue to propagate capabilities into accessible frameworks. While openweight systems grow more capable, their progress remains bounded below the frontier, with no new mass leakage or infrastructure breakthrough this turn to justify an outsized leap.

The EU completes two major initiatives: the InvestAI Gigafactories and the Resilience Surge Protocol. The former delivers four sovereign compute sites, two of which are rapidly repurposed for health-AI hosting. The latter certifies cyber-hardened networks across 14 member states, boosting resilience to 43.0. Yet these gains are overshadowed by the sudden denial of access to a frontier medical AI, which had enabled breakthrough cancer therapies. Hospitals in Berlin, Marseille, and Lisbon are locked out without explanation, triggering public outrage and validating fears of dependency.

In response, the EU launches the Emergency Access to Life-Saving AI Models initiative, invoking crisis powers to mandate model disclosure for essential health systems. The European Medicines Agency is granted authority to compel replication, backed by sovereign sandboxing at EuroHPC. While legally bold, implementation faces resistance from U.S. developers and legal challenges from member states wary of overreach. The measure is new and adds to the portfolio burden, but no second new measure is initiated.

The European AI Emergency Response Corps, originally planned for activation this turn, remains in delayed stand-up due to unresolved legal harmonization across member states. Recruitment and training proceed in Lyon, Milan, and Warsaw, but full cross-border operational authority has not yet been granted. Its activation is postponed to a future turn, and it does not yet enter the portfolio as an active measure.

Public sentiment rises slightly to 40.0, as the medical breakthrough’s benefits are felt—even if unevenly—tempering anger with hope. Political capital drops to 7.0 under the strain of four large ongoing measures. The new initiative earns a modest proposal bonus, but the access denial event and internal friction outweigh gains. Sovereignty increases to 16.0, reflecting partial gains from the completion of InvestAI Gigafactories and the new legal framework, though full implementation and effect are delayed by ongoing challenges. The notepad records a +5 boost from completion, but portfolio burden (−13) and lack of immediate operationalization limit the realized gain this turn.
