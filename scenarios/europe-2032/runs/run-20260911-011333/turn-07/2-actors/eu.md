## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for political capital to recover above paralysis and for the energy blockades to clear before any new instrument can credibly start.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
M6 EU Cyber Emergency Repair Cell, because with capital at 2 and services still on paper fallback, holding the one visible restoration effort together outranks starting anything new.

## In practice
We keep the Repair Cell on its existing emergency mandate under ENISA and the Joint Cyber Unit, funded only from reprogrammed Digital Europe money, triaging worst-hit municipalities, clinics and ports and publishing town-by-town restoration to stop sentiment falling further. No new money vote is attempted; the Council formation is EPSCO/Justice for continuity, not ECOFIN.

We use the Foreign Affairs Council and the M5 Compact table to contain the solo deal without spending: offer the defector shared repair priority conditional on returning to common access rules, while DG ENER mediates the gigafactory-site blockades with emergency energy subsidies already on the books and DG CNECT extends permits and site security rather than construction. The finished Gigafactories reservations are kept warm on paper, not restarted, until blockades lift and capital returns.
