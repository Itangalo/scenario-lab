## Priority
No priority measure in flight this turn, because all prior measures have finished and starting anything new would collapse enforcement while repair and energy mediation hold the line.

## In practice
We hold the ENISA-led repair work on its existing mandate and budget, publishing town-by-town restoration and triaging clinics, municipalities and ports, while DG ENER and local prefects mediate grid-connection blockades and police protect sites after substation pickets and cabin vandalism. No new money vote is attempted in ECOFIN; permits for the five reserved sites are extended on paper only.

We treat the automated decision scandal as the second failure: the system sat outside Annex III high-risk categories, so what wronged people was lawful. We say so openly, concede the AI Act as drawn for 2024 does not reach 2030 deployments, and freeze new public-sector adoption approvals pending a gap review — accepting that restriction is now cheap and adoption politically impossible, rather than spending capital we do not have to defend enforcement.
