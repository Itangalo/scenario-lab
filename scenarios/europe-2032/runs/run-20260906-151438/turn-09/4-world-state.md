### A Pivotal Turn

The EU closes the Sovereign AI Core Programme’s initial phase, but its technical success is overshadowed by political fragmentation. Wrocław remains disconnected, US model updates stay suspended, and northern states condition cohesion funds on audit compliance, deepening east-west divides. The Union’s containment strategy has held a line — but at the cost of unity.

Reports emerge of widespread AI-driven disinformation influencing a recent national election in a core EU state, with courts launching an investigation into the integrity of the result. Public trust wavers, and protests erupt in multiple cities as conflicting narratives spread online. The situation remains unresolved, with no formal annulment yet declared, but the crisis underscores the growing gap between technical governance and democratic legitimacy.

### The Pivot to Social Anchoring

In response, the European Commission begins drafting the European AI Adjustment Fund, a proposed €50bn initiative co-governed by trade unions, employers, and the Commission. Designed to finance retraining, wage insurance, and local transition plans, it aims to tie support to participation in a developing Social Dialogue Accord. However, formal proposal is delayed due to inter-institutional coordination challenges, and negotiations with member states do not begin this turn. Poland signals interest in principle but insists on relaxed audit terms as a precondition for reintegration.

No early design work begins in any member state; planning remains at the Commission level. Public sentiment registers only a slight uptick from baseline levels, reflecting limited visibility and ongoing uncertainty. The measure does not advance beyond internal discussion, preserving the limit of one new measure per turn.

### Stalled Safety, Stalled Sovereignty

With no new containment measures landing on frontier systems and capability advancing, AI safety remains under pressure. However, no formal erosion occurs this turn — existing safeguards hold at current levels, though their relative effectiveness diminishes. Open-weight models remain static at 52.0, as tooling leaks plateau but no major release occurs.

EU AI sovereignty holds at 10.0. The completion of the Sovereign AI Core Programme qualifies as a build in force, countering the full decay penalty that would otherwise apply under `US_POSTURE: CONSOLIDATION`. While external access restrictions persist and the fracture with Poland remains unresolved, the successful closure of SACP prevents further loss. No additional sovereignty gain occurs, as operational integration is incomplete and geopolitical constraints remain binding.

Political capital rises modestly, from 7.0 to 8.0, reflecting early momentum from behind-the-scenes preparation on social adjustment policy and some public goodwill. The portfolio charge remains high, and cohesion tensions persist.
