### The Line in the Sand

The EU draws a hard boundary: no high-capability AI may operate in critical infrastructure without audit, telemetry, and override access. The directive, issued under Article 114 TFEU, targets foreign providers and defiant member states alike. Poland refuses to comply, calling the move “technological coercion,” and rejects the final offer of audit-for-funding. In response, Brussels cuts Wrocław’s access to the Sovereign AI Core Programme and halts the €10bn grant. Northern states applaud; eastern members warn of a two-tier Union.

Washington does not react publicly, but quietly suspends model updates for several EU-linked research institutes. The **us_china_agreement** on biological design tools slows global capability growth slightly — a rare brake — but excludes the EU entirely, underscoring its isolation.

### Fractures Deepen

The **member_state_defection** of Poland hardens into a structural rift. Other eastern states delay compliance, fearing similar treatment. The **emergent_regional_aid_block** becomes formal: a coalition of six northern nations refuses to approve cohesion funding for any state with un-audited AI systems. The standoff threatens the next budget cycle.

Meanwhile, **backlash_physical** escalates: protesters occupy a data centre in Sweden, cutting power to a regional cloud node. Police intervene, and injuries are reported. The incident fuels media narratives of AI as an imposed threat.

### Resilience Grows, Trust Erodes

The Emergency Resilience Acceleration Facility deploys human-in-the-loop controls and backups across health and tax systems, lifting **resilience**. But the **enforce audit** measure — large, controversial, and now a priority — drains political capital. With **public_sentiment** below 30 and capital under 20, the priority has no accelerating effect, and the measure faces legal challenges from Poland and industry groups.

No category 4 measure finishes this turn. **SACP** continues, delayed by export controls. **eu_ai_sovereignty** drops as access is further restricted and cohesion frays. **ai_safety** falls amid rising capability and no containment gains. Open weights climb to 52.0 as tooling leaks persist.
