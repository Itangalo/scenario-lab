### The Weight of Unchecked Advance

Global AI capability surged again, rising by +2.5 to reach 99.5—just 0.5 points shy of the terminal zone where no human institution can keep pace. The advance was steady, relentless, and opaque: frontier models now self-direct research with no legible chain of reasoning. No safety protocols kept up. No audits followed. The world moved faster than understanding.

Despite the EU’s invocation of Article 122a TFEU to override local objections in Poland and Portugal, legal resistance hardened. Courts in both countries issued provisional injunctions, citing disproportionate central overreach and unresolved constitutional questions. Construction on the Sovereign Compute Backbone remains stalled. ENISA and the European Defence Agency began drafting protection protocols, but with no physical infrastructure online, they guard empty blueprints. The Anti-Coercion Instrument, tested against hyperscalers, met firm resistance—providers argue that mandated failover instances violate corporate sovereignty and expose them to liability. No new nodes came online.

The Public Accountability Migration Programme stayed on paper. DG HOME and Eurostat completed their risk inventory, but migration is impossible without sovereign infrastructure. The European Data Protection Supervisor remains disengaged, awaiting legal clarity. A 75% funding offer to member states carries no real weight without bond authorization or budget reallocation.

Yet public sentiment rose slightly, from 17.0 to 22.0. A wave of life-saving medical therapies, developed by frontier AI systems, entered clinical use. Hospitals celebrated breakthroughs in rare disease treatment. But the models behind them remain un-auditable and inaccessible to EU control. The benefit is real, but so is the dependence it underscores—public gratitude is tinged with unease.

With ai_capability rising by 2.5 and no safety or resilience measures landing, ai_safety remained at 0.0, resilience fell by −2 to 47.0, and eu_ai_sovereignty dropped by −1 due to continued dependence. The portfolio charge held: three large measures and a priority drained eu_political_capital to −7.0, where it remains.

No new measure was proposed. The Union waits for the Court of Justice to rule on its authority to compel infrastructure siting—a decision that may come too late.
