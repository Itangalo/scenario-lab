- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer act without a sovereign technical foundation to stand on. This foundation is not optional infrastructure; it is the precondition of any credible commitment.
  - Grounds: At eu_political_capital −2.0 and public_sentiment 17.0, the Union’s authority is now contingent on demonstrating tangible control. The automated_decision_scandal revealed that even non-frontier systems can inflict systemic harm when opaque and unaccountable. Meanwhile, the denial of frontier access during the biosecurity incident proved that sovereignty in principle means nothing without sovereignty in execution. We are acting under uncertainty still — the full implications of 94.5 capability are unknown — but we now understand that action without sovereign capacity is not just risky; it is performative. This update hardens the lesson: we will not again stake legitimacy on systems we do not control.

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign technical agency through auditable AI, establish a sovereign compute and deployment infrastructure, and demonstrate its operational necessity by migrating critical public services onto it
  - Grounds: The Open Science Resilience Initiative completes this turn, but its models remain stranded without sovereign infrastructure to run on. The Sovereign Compute Backbone is not merely an enabler — it is the proving ground. To show that auditable AI is not a slower alternative but a necessary foundation for resilience and legitimacy, we must run it where it matters: in social services, biosecurity, and crisis response. The scandal over wrongful benefit denials makes this urgent: the public will not tolerate opaque automation, but they also cannot accept systemic failure. Only by migrating these services onto our own stack can we prove that sovereignty and accountability are not luxuries — they are the only way to govern at all.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
