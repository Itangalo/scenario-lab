### The Weight of Open Release

A powerful open-weight model, closely mirroring the closed frontier’s 75.0 capability, is released under permissive licensing. Its arrival ignites immediate interest across research and developer communities, and early adoption begins on pre-existing clusters with sufficient capacity. However, widespread deployment is constrained by limited high-end compute availability, integration challenges, and power infrastructure bottlenecks. While momentum builds, only a fraction of capable systems come online in this turn. `openweight_capability` rises to 60.0 — a meaningful step forward, but far from parity with the closed frontier.

### A Fractured Front

The industrial action by public-sector AI teams intensifies. In multiple capitals, deployment of sovereign systems in border and welfare systems halts. The strike is not over pay or conditions, but over risk: implementers refuse to operate systems they cannot audit or control. The EU’s authority fractures from within — its own agents withholding execution. The Union scrambles to respond, pivoting from rollout to reassurance.

### Containment Over Transformation

Brussels launches the Containment Pilot Programme in waste, permits, and planning — narrow, reversible testbeds with full monitoring and human oversight. The goal is not efficiency, but trust: to prove that failure can be seen, stopped, and contained. Striking unions are invited into design and telemetry, but participation remains limited. The gesture is seen as overdue, not decisive.

### Resilience Rebounds — Slightly

The Continuity Assurance Framework completes, mandating failover nodes and quarterly testing across critical systems. Though too late to prevent past failures, its institutionalization delivers a modest resilience boost. Combined with early signs of coordinated cyber hygiene, `resilience` rises to 50.0 — a fragile stabilization, not a recovery.

### Sovereignty Erodes Further

Despite the framework’s completion, `ai_capability` remains high and rising in practice, costing −1 to `eu_ai_sovereignty`. No category 4 measure finishes, and the Frontier Access Guarantee remains in flight. With no operational alternative to foreign compute, and a member state now negotiating its own AI access deal, dependence deepens. Sovereignty slips to 20.0.

### Political Capital Frozen, Sentiment Stagnant

The portfolio charge remains crushing: −3 (Frontier Access Guarantee) + −3 (Continuity Assurance Framework, now finished) + −2 (new Containment Pilot) + −1 (priority) = −9. With `eu_political_capital` at 0.0, no agenda control exists. The new measure earns no proposal bonus — it answers a systemic crisis, not a recent shock. Public sentiment holds at 18.0, unmoved by pilots that feel reactive.
