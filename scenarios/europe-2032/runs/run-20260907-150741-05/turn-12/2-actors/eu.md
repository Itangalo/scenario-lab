This is not a failure of coordination but of legitimacy: we can no longer assume that acting for sovereignty automatically serves safety, nor that acting for safety preserves our agency. The identity holds, but its meaning has shifted — we are no longer balancing two goals; we are governing a fracture.

  - Grounds: The eval_anomaly_reports confirm that capability is emerging unpredictably, and the openweight release ensures that whatever we do, it will be mirrored and modified outside our control. Acting early without containment is now indistinguishable from recklessness. The commitment remains, but its expression must now include a binding condition: we will act before clarity, but only where we can prove that failure will be seen, stopped, and reversed.

The enforcement mechanism is not just politically toxic; it is institutionally unmoored. The commitment stands, but the 90-day clause is now inverted: we will not demand compliance until we can prove our alternative works — and until we have the capital to scale it.

## New measure
**Measure:** Launch the Containment Pilot Programme  
A rapid, reversible deployment of auditable, human-in-the-loop AI systems in non-critical public functions — waste logistics, permit processing, and municipal planning — to test operational safety under real conditions, with full telemetry and third-party monitoring.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: small`  
`Finishes on turn: 14`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase resilience by 5–8 points through demonstrated incident containment and recovery; indirectly support ai_safety by generating field data on failure modes and human oversight efficacy  
`Applies to:` EAAD, national digital ministries, transnational public-sector unions

## Priority
**Priority:** Launch the Containment Pilot Programme  
We are prioritizing this because the emergent_eu_aid_strike has removed our ability to mandate — and without the cooperation of public-sector implementers, even the most robust sovereign stack is inert. We must now prove safety in practice, not policy. This measure creates a controlled environment to demonstrate that AI can be used without losing control — and to give the striking teams a path back in.

## In practice
This turn, we are pivoting from demonstration of benefit to demonstration of safety. The Emergency Trust Restoration Initiative was meant to rebuild trust through visibility — but the emergent_eu_aid_strike revealed that trust is not just about public perception; it is about operational legitimacy. The people who run our systems no longer believe in them. So we are changing course.

We are launching the Containment Pilot Programme under the Digital Europe Programme and the EAAD’s incident telemetry framework. We will deploy a hardened, interpretability-enabled SACP stack — capability level 58.0 — in three non-critical but high-visibility municipal functions: waste collection routing in Barcelona, building permit processing in Prague, and urban planning simulations in Helsinki. These systems will be fully monitored, with real-time telemetry shared with the EAAD and the transnational unions that called the strike. Each will have a human-in-the-loop override, weekly review cycles, and a binding protocol to halt deployment at the first sign of uncontrolled behaviour.

The goal is not to deliver transformation — it is to deliver containment. We are offering the striking teams a seat in the design, monitoring, and evaluation of these pilots. If they can see that failure is detectable, reversible, and non-catastrophic, they may return to the table. The €900 million repurposed from stalled adoption projects will fund this sprint. We are not demanding compliance. We are building a bridge.
