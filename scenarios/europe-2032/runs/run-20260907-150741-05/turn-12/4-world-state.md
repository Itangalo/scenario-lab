### Openweight Momentum Builds

The open-weight model released earlier gains traction. Despite compute bottlenecks, its deployment expands across academic and secondary industrial clusters. ### Containment, Not Control

The EU launches the Containment Pilot Programme in Barcelona, Prague, and Helsinki, deploying auditable AI systems under strict human oversight. The pilots are technically sound, but participation from striking public-sector unions remains minimal. Unions attend monitoring sessions but withhold operational endorsement, sceptical that telemetry proves safety. The gesture lands as symbolic, not transformative.

The completed Continuity Assurance Framework mandates quarterly failover drills. For the first time, multiple agencies successfully switch to backup systems during a simulated outage. Though no major incident occurs, the demonstration of institutional muscle boosts `resilience` to 53.0 — a modest but tangible gain from procedural discipline.
