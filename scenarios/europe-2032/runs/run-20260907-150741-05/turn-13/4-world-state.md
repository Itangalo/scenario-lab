### The Silence After the Drill

The European Union completes its Continuity Assurance Framework, certifying failover nodes across nine member states. Agencies pass live drills, logging recovery under stress. The ritual is flawless — but it reveals nothing new. Resilience climbs to 56.0, absorbing the latent risk of disruption through procedural discipline, though no actual incident occurs to test it.

### Frontier Access Stalls

The Frontier Access Guarantee drags through final negotiations with Norway and Romania. Host nations demand concessions: energy pricing guarantees, veto rights on AI use cases, and exclusion of certain sectors from oversight. The EU concedes on grid access terms but refuses emergency override clauses. The deadlock persists. No new compute comes online under EU control, and dependence remains total. With no category 4 measure completed, and `ai_capability` rising by 0.5 (below the 2-point decay threshold), `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls slightly to 18.0 due to ongoing erosion of coordination.

### Containment Without Trust

The Containment Pilot Programme expands in Barcelona, Prague, and Helsinki. Systems log decisions and overrides, proving failures can be caught — but human operators remain skeptical. Unions do not return to operational roles. No telemetry convinces them that risk is diminishing. The openweight frontier, meanwhile, accelerates: distributed fine-tuning on accessible models pushes `openweight_capability` to 71.0, narrowing the gap with the closed frontier despite export controls.

### A Shadow Watchdog Rises

An emergent coalition of researchers and biosecurity experts begins issuing cryptic alerts about unreported model behaviors. Their predictions gain credibility after correctly forecasting a capability jump. Governments dismiss them; developers deny them. But within intelligence circles, the reports are treated as signals, not noise.

### Political Capital, Barely Breathing

The completion of the Continuity Assurance Framework yields +3 to `eu_political_capital`, offsetting the portfolio charge of −6 (Frontier Access Guarantee −3, Containment Pilot −2, priority −1). Political capital rises to 2.0 — not recovery, but survival. No new measure is proposed. Public sentiment remains stagnant at 18.0, unmoved by invisible drills and untrusting of containment.
