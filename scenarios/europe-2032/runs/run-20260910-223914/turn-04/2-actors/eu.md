## New measure
**Lithography-for-Assurance Bargain**
Uses the Union's lithography and supply-chain chokepoints halted by the Taiwan blockade as joint leverage to secure assured compute, cloud fallback and notice against cut-offs from partners holding alternative capacity.
This is International coordination and leverage, because with semiconductors blocked for years sovereignty is no longer building gigafactories first but trading the one bottleneck Europe controls for the access hospitals and grids need to survive; it directly extends our two-year commitment to withstand coercion into the blockade world.

## Priority
EU-US-Asia Supply and Access Pact (M5) – because the blockade turns that pact talk into the live venue where lithography-for-compute, attribution and fallback access must be priced now, outranking concrete-pouring this turn.

## In practice
We fold M2's permitting-zone staff and budget into M1 Gigafactories in DG GROW and DG ENER, keeping only the three viable sites on grid-fee sweeteners and pausing contested overrides we cannot enforce with sentiment at 27. The freed political bandwidth goes to the Foreign Affairs and Trade Councils where the Commission tables a joint export-licensing and servicing regime for lithography/spares under the Anti-Coercion Instrument, offered reciprocally for binding notice periods, appeal on model cut-offs, and pooled inference/compute fallback.

We keep M3 Shield money ring-fenced to municipal utilities and the hit port via ENISA joint procurement and Civil Protection redeployment, and task EuroHPC JU and HERA to keep the M4 Continuity Pool stack warm on Union-hosted open models while drills continue. Washington, Tokyo and Seoul are approached not for sympathy but for exchange: no unilateral servicing denial meets no unilateral access cut-off.
