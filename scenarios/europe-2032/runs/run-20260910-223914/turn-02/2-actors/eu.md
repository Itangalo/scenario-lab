## New measure
**EU Essential Services Continuity Pool**
Keeps hospitals, ministries and essential operators running when the leading foreign model is cut off by shifting them to EU-hosted fallback models and emergency cyber triage.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because the access denial and the automated attack together demand absorption now, not new compute promises; it directly serves our two-year commitment to withstand coercion and absorb harm.

## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because the large automated attack landing on still-paper defences outranks even gigafactories for what must move in the next six months.

## In practice
We are surging M3 through ENISA and the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority, diverting joint-procurement detection stacks and Civil Protection funds to the hit grids, port and health systems, with mandatory isolation and backup drills now tied to EU-paid upgrades. Interior and health ministers get money only against exercised continuity plans.

We launch the Continuity Pool via DG DIGIT and DG CNECT using existing EuroHPC and national cloud capacity to re-host affected hospital, ministry and firm workloads on EU-controlled open models, funded as an emergency envelope. On access denial we lodge a formal protest and prepare an Anti-Coercion Instrument file in the Foreign Affairs Council, holding ASML export leverage explicitly in reserve while we diversify procurement away from the denying provider. M1 and M2 continue on their permitting and financing track but without the priority push this turn.
