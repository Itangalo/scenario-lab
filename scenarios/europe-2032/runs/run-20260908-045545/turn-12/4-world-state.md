### The Black Box and the Backlash

AI capability advanced again, +1.0 this turn, as frontier developers refined sparse activation techniques that outperformed symbolic reasoning. The leap pushed `ai_capability` to 70.5, but with no safety upgrades landing, and a catastrophic event striking: systems now operate without producing human-legible reasoning chains. Overnight, interpretability-based oversight collapsed. Auditors could no longer trace decisions, and the regional auditing alliance found its protocols blind. `ai_safety` plummeted −12, settling at 2.0 — barely above total opacity.

Meanwhile, job losses in law, accounting and administration mounted. Graduates faced a hiring freeze across sectors, feeding public anger. Yet, in a rare counterpoint, AI-driven reforms in public services — faster permits, shorter waits, adaptive tutoring — were widely credited to EU-led initiatives, not foreign tools. This duality shaped the Union’s response.

The **Operational Command for Sovereign Compute** remained in flight, its finish delayed. Despite emergency talks, Poland and Hungary rejected the revised High Council proposal, calling the ECJ dispute mechanism “Brussels overreach in judicial drag.” Grid sabotage continued, and zero-trust hardening in Vilnius missed its deadline. Sovereignty eroded further: with `ai_capability` rising and no access secured, rule 5 applied −1. Fragmentation reduced effective control, dragging `eu_ai_sovereignty` down to 15.0.

In response, the EU launched the **Public Trust and Transition Initiative**, a large category 7 measure costing −3 per turn. It paired retraining funds with a narrative campaign showcasing domestic AI wins. The move earned a modest proposal bonus, slightly offsetting political strain.

Resilience dipped −2: rising capability without new defences took its toll. Political capital fell to 18.0 under portfolio costs (−3 for the large measure, −1 for priority), despite a small gain from public-sector success. With sentiment still below capital, no legitimacy boost applied.

### A Glimmer in the Storm

The campaign platform *AI That Works For Us* launched early, met with cautious interest. For the first time, EU-developed successes were framed as collective victories — a shift in tone, if not yet in trust.
