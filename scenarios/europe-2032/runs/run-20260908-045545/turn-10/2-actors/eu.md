## New measure
**Operational Command for Sovereign Compute**
This measure establishes a permanent, unified command authority to manage and secure EU sovereign AI infrastructure, ensuring real-time operational control during crises.

- `Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`
- `Size: large`
- `Finishes on turn: 13`
- `Targeted effect:` Increase `eu_ai_sovereignty` by hardening command, control, and secure execution; indirectly support `resilience` and `ai_safety` by enabling trusted operations.
- `Applies to:` EU jurisdiction, specifically the InvestAI Gigafactories and their allocation protocols.

## Priority
**Operational Command for Sovereign Compute** — because the Taiwan Strait blockade and ransomware attacks proved that owning compute is not enough: without unified, secure, and sovereign command, the EU risks holding critical infrastructure it cannot reliably defend or operate when under pressure.

## In practice
We are launching the Operational Command for Sovereign Compute to close the gap between physical ownership and effective control. The Gigafactories in Marseille, Vilnius, and Gdansk are now the West’s last frontier compute base, but they are still managed through a patchwork of national authorities, Commission oversight, and contractor arrangements. During the ransomware wave, this cost us hours — hours in which attacks spread unchecked. We cannot afford that again. This command will be legally anchored in Article 122 TFEU and hosted under DG CONNECT, with operational authority over all sovereign compute allocations above 10%, real-time access to workload logs, and the power to enforce secure execution environments. We are designating €1.2 billion from the Digital Europe Programme and NextGenerationEU to fund its build-out, including hardened relays, zero-trust architecture, and a dedicated secure operations cadre. Resistance is expected from member states guarding national prerogatives, particularly Poland and Hungary, but we are offering binding guarantees on minimum national access thresholds and joint oversight to secure Council adoption. This is not a power grab — it is the price of being able to act when the world shuts down.
