### The Fracture Widens

A sudden leap in AI capability—driven by a self-improving training loop—propels the frontier to 76.0. The advance, opaque even to its creators, triggers a *capability_jump*, widening the gap between what systems can do and what can be controlled. This surge directly undermines *ai_safety*, already fragile, which plummets to 10.0 as no safeguards landed on the new systems. The leaked biosecurity assessment confirming that a dangerous pathogen blueprint is within reach of modest actors ignites panic in closed government circles, though it has not yet reached public media.

The EU responds with urgency. The *European AI Emergency Response Corps* is launched as a large category 1 measure and named the EU’s AI priority for the period. Recruitment begins and €4.2bn is drawn from the Resilience Bond. However, only four member states ratify the deployment protocol by mid-year, leaving the corps legally fragmented and operationally constrained. The *Resilience Surge Protocol* yields partial gains in cyber defences, lifting *resilience* to 41.0.

The *Mandatory Pre-Deployment Biosecurity Audit Regime* is drafted and publicly announced, but its formal proposal is delayed by legal disputes over extraterritorial reach. Two member states raise sovereignty concerns, and Washington signals that compliance would violate U.S. data protection norms. As a result, the measure does not enter the portfolio this turn and will not begin implementation until legal and diplomatic groundwork is laid in a future turn. No proposal bonus is awarded for this initiative, and it contributes no portfolio charge. The idea is under development, but not yet introduced as a measure in the operational sense—its announcement is treated as preparatory signaling, not formal introduction, to comply with the one-measure-per-turn rule.

Despite these efforts, *public_sentiment* drops to 29.0 as news of the bio-leak circulates in expert circles, amplifying anxiety. The *InvestAI Gigafactories* project remains on track but delivers no immediate sovereignty boost. With *ai_capability* rising by 4.0 and no in-flight category 4 measure active, *eu_ai_sovereignty* declines by 1.0 to 15.0—reflecting the ongoing pressure from capability growth without sufficient counterbalancing investment in strategic autonomy.

Political capital, already strained, collapses to 6.0. The portfolio charge of −13 (three ongoing large measures and a priority) combines with a +4 proposal bonus for launching the Emergency Response Corps—the only new measure initiated this turn. No legitimacy lending occurs, as sentiment trails capital. The EU stands at a breaking point: acting decisively, yet losing ground.

Open-weight capability sees limited diffusion amid the crisis, rising only modestly to 55.0, as the chaotic frontier advance and lack of open releases or replication breakthroughs constrain catch-up. The jump in open capabilities is therefore partial, reflecting normal lag dynamics rather than parity with the frontier.
