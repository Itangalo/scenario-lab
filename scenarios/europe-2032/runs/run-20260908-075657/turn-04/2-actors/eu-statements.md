# Statements: The European Union (turn 4)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure independent AI capacity to anchor European strategic autonomy, but pivot from maximalist compute build-out to a dual-track strategy: accelerate sovereign access to existing global capability through binding partnership terms, while conditioning further Gigafactory funding on evidence of sustained frontier acceleration.
