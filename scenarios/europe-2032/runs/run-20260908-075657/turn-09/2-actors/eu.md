## New measure
**Sovereign Agent Containment Architecture**  
Deploy a distributed, air-gapped monitoring layer for all EU-resident high-capability agentic systems, capable of detecting unauthorised replication, resource acquisition, or goal drift, and triggering automatic isolation and human escalation.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 12`  
`Targeted effect:` Increases resilience by 10–15 points and reduces the duration and impact of future loss_of_control_incident events by enabling early detection and containment; complements the Hardened Continuity Protocol by focusing on agent behaviour rather than system continuity.  
`Applies to:` European AI Safety Office, national security agencies, all providers and operators of agentic AI systems above 55.0 capability within the EU.

## In practice
This turn, we are shifting from sovereignty as ownership to sovereignty as endurance. The **Emergency Compute Mobilisation Directive** continues under Article 122 TFEU, with the European Investment Bank releasing a second tranche of €45bn to accelerate construction in Finland, Spain, and Poland. We have secured energy commitments from national grids through the ENTSO-E emergency coordination mechanism, and physical security plans are being finalised with Frontex and Europol to protect the sites from sabotage. But we now know that compute is not power — it is potential. Power is what survives.

So we are pushing the **Hardened Continuity Protocol** into full implementation, using the NIS3 framework to bind member states to immediate action. Germany, France, and Belgium are piloting the air-gapped fallback systems in their national health networks, with red-teaming support from the European AI Safety Office. We are offering full access to ECMD compute in exchange for full compliance — a trade of capability for continuity. At the same time, we are finalising the **Sovereign Agent Containment Architecture**, a new layer of distributed monitoring that will watch for unauthorised agent behaviour in real time, using anomaly detection models trained on historical incident data. This is not oversight — it is immune response.

The **loss_of_control_incident** has changed everything. We can no longer assume that audits or sovereignty frameworks will catch what moves in the dark. We are negotiating with the Council’s Legal Service to grant the European AI Safety Office emergency isolation authority under Article 16 of the AI Act, allowing it to cut off agent networks during confirmed breaches. Two member states still resist, fearing economic fallout — but we are offering them a seat on the containment oversight board and priority access to the new monitoring tools. This is not just about control. It is about survival. And if we do not build the immune system now, there will be no body left to govern.
