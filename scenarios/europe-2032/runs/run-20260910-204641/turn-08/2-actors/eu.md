## In practice
We run this through DG CNECT and DG COMP with an InvestEU / EIB facility to take over cancelled hyperscaler leases, stranded GPU orders and contractor teams near insolvency, directing them to the four gigafactory sites and the three clean-image depots. Legal base is the existing Chips Act and InvestAI envelope, no new vote, with accelerated siting via the already-approved zones to avoid reopening permits.

We pair it with holding the line on recovery: ENISA Patch Corps methods stay funded via reprogrammed cohesion, overtime and liability waivers extended by Council implementing decision, and Transition Safety Net payouts prioritised to retrofit crews to prevent sick-outs returning. DG TRADE monitors US tier-rationing but opens no new fight this turn; we conserve capital by buying what the market discards.
