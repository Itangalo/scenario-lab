## New measure
**EU Continuity Cloud for Essential Services**
Stands up EU-controlled inference and fallback models for hospitals, ministries and operators cut off from the frontier, hosted on EU-anchored capacity with open-weight and licensed alternatives.
This is category 9 Diffusion, adoption and public trust because the access cut-off has turned adoption dependence into a continuity failure, and only a trusted public fallback restores use; we do it now alongside the safety breakthrough that makes vetted deployment more assurable.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because the large automated attack landing on still-mapped grids outranks even the access cut-off — cascade failure kills continuity either way.

## In practice
We push the Shield into live exercises: ENISA plus EU-CyCLONe run cross-border black-start drills with the two hit TSOs first, CER emergency procurement releases detection kits back-ordered since December, and we fund segmentation retrofits from reprogrammed Connecting Europe Facility money with member-state co-pay. DG ENER holds AI-factory grid connections behind substation upgrades despite industry pressure.

In parallel we launch the Continuity Cloud via DG DIGIT and EuroHPC: reserve inference on existing AI Factories, procure EU-hosted open-weight models tuned for clinical and administrative tasks, and use the new interpretability controls for certification before redeployment to hospitals cut off this turn. Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) keep moving through EIB guarantees and permitting zones, but staffing and power are explicitly sequenced to Shield and Continuity first.
