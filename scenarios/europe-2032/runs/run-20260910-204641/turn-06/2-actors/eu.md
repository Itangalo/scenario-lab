## Priority
EU AI Transition Safety Net, because strikes, graduate protests and council opposition to power/water for compute will decide whether the cyber response and the Gigafactories survive, and it is the only in-flight measure that rebuilds cohesion fast enough to spend anything else.

## In practice
We treat the automated attack as a Shield operation, not a new programme. ENISA and DG CNECT run triage from the two hardened transmission operators' crisis network, isolate affected municipal services, and force credential resets and dependency inventory across public services, with the Cybersecurity Reserve and national CSIRTs doing containment. No new money is promised; retrofit crews are offered overtime pay and hiring unfreeze funded from reprogrammed ESF+/cohesion envelopes tied to ending the slowdowns.

We keep the Continuity Cloud in limited clinical/admin operation as the fallback where ransomware has taken systems down, with DG DIGIT issuing emergency procurement waivers on licences/liability to allow temporary scale-up in hit regions. On Taiwan, we task DG TRADE and DG ENER with shipping-insurance monitoring and chip-stock checks, and signal tier-rationing risk to the Council without opening a new industrial measure until siting and workforce blocks ease.
