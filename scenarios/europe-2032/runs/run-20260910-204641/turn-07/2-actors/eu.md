## New measure
**Municipal Patch and Recovery Corps**
It deploys funded retrofit teams and a shared clean-image library to get hit municipalities, regional hospitals and contractors off paper and onto patched systems.
With capital at 11 and crews working to rule after the February sweep, this small resilience push is what unblocks the Shield queue and lets the Continuity Cloud pilot actually hand systems back, while the open-weight release makes unpatched edges unrecallably exposed.

## Priority
Municipal Patch and Recovery Corps, because without paid crews and clean rebuilds the finished Shield and Continuity Cloud cannot convert into recovered services and every other build stays stalled behind distrust.

## In practice
We run this as a Shield operation through ENISA and national CSIRTs, with DG CNECT coordinating and DG REGIO reprogramming ESF+/cohesion to pay overtime, unfreeze municipal hiring, and fund three regional clean-image depots. Emergency procurement waivers from DG DIGIT are extended from the clinical pilot to municipal admin systems to allow rapid re-imaging and credential resets without re-litigating licence/liability each site.

We pair it with visible delivery on the Transition Safety Net: first payouts are sourced and started in hit regions, with hiring preference for young displaced workers into the retrofit crews, to break graduate protests and council opposition together. On Taiwan and US tier-rationing we limit ourselves to monitoring shipping insurance and chip stocks via DG TRADE/ENER, holding any new sovereignty build until this recovery restores enough capital to permit and staff it.
