### The Shock of Exclusion

The EU’s frontier AI access is abruptly severed. Hospitals lose diagnostic tools mid-use, research projects stall, and public trust erodes overnight. The move, unexplained and unappealable, is felt not as a market shift but as a geopolitical strike. The Systemic Integrity Watch, just concluded, flags the disruption as a systemic integrity breach—too late to prevent, but confirming the need for sovereign capacity.

### Sovereign Compute Acceleration Launched

In response, the EU launches the **Sovereign Compute Acceleration Initiative**, a large Category 4 measure fast-tracking two sovereign AI foundries in France and Poland. Leveraging Chips Act authority, the proposal mobilizes up to €18bn in state aid, pending national approvals and grid impact assessments. Germany initially resists, fearing energy cost inflation, but agrees to participate after being granted governance influence and first access rights. Full funding disbursement and construction cannot begin before Turn 8. No implementation benefit is accrued in Turn 7. The initiative is named priority, signaling strategic urgency, but incurs no proposal bonus—such rewards are reserved for completed or substantially advanced measures. The notepad records only the cost of priority designation (−1) and the systemic integrity breach (−2), offset by the completion of the Tech Sovereignty Package (+5), resulting in a net +1 change to `eu_ai_sovereignty`.

### Open Weights Advance Gradually

An open-weight model is released shortly after the frontier update, narrowing the capability gap slightly. With improved access and adaptation, `openweight_capability` rises to 51.0—still trailing `ai_capability` (64.0)—reflecting gradual diffusion rather than a rapid catch-up. Offensive cyber tools and synthetic media generation become more accessible, and municipal finance departments across three countries suffer AI-adapted phishing attacks. Resilience efforts are strained, and the lack of a finished Category 6 measure compounds the drop.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Stalls

`ai_capability` rises to 64.0, driven by self-improving training loops. No safety gains land on deployed systems. The biosecurity community debates a genome model’s human-infectious organism design—contested but alarming. `ai_safety` falls by 3 due to capability growth without corresponding safeguards. Public sentiment declines further as disruption outpaces response. There is no unexplained erosion of `eu_political_capital`; without narrative or notepad justification for a drop, it remains unchanged from the previous turn.
