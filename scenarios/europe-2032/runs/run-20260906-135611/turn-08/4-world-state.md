### The Weight of Automation

A major cyber incident sweeps across public services in five member states, exploiting AI-generated spear-phishing that mimics trusted officials with uncanny precision. The attacks bypass traditional defences, locking emergency services and tax agencies out of critical systems for days. Forensic teams confirm the tooling was model-generated, marking a turning point in offensive automation. Simultaneously, a biosecurity breach involving a modified respiratory pathogen—likely designed with AI assistance—triggers containment protocols in two countries, though casualties remain limited. The convergence of threats overwhelms response capacity, exposing systemic fragility.

### Sovereignty Under Strain

The Sovereign Compute Acceleration Initiative advances slowly. Grid impact assessments in France and Poland face delays due to unresolved stability concerns linked to AI-driven load fluctuations. While intergovernmental agreements are signed and €4.2bn in aid awaits approval, construction remains stalled. Germany’s influence on the Oversight Board leads to protracted debates over access rules, slowing momentum. No sovereignty gain is realized this turn, and rising global capability erodes dependence buffers, costing −1.

### Shielding the Municipal Front

In response to the phishing surge, the EU launches the Municipal Cyber Shield Programme as a small Category 6 measure. ENISA deploys AI-resistant authentication and anomaly detection systems to high-risk municipalities, with rapid response teams on standby. Adoption is fast-tracked under emergency provisions, though patchwork IT systems in smaller cities slow rollout. The effort stabilizes the most vulnerable points, earning +2 toward resilience. The measure is named priority, adding strain.
