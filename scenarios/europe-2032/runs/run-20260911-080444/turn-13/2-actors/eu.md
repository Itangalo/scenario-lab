## Priority
The new EU Cut-Off Continuity and Embodied Displacement Buffer, because with capital at 9.0 and sentiment at 18.0 holding essential services and livelihoods through denial and robots outranks the state-to-state track this turn.

## In practice
We execute under CER, NIS2 and the Civil Protection Mechanism: ENISA and joint cyber teams push rebuild images and offline runbooks to cut-off hospitals and grid operators, EuroHPC holds licensed-model fallbacks, and HERA/ECDC keep wastewater and pharmacy watch for the genome-model bio risk. EEAS keeps the M11 state-to-state licence appeal alive but does not make services wait for it.

For jobs we use SURE-style short-time work, ESF+ and municipal funds: no new gigafactory promise, just paid manual-operation shifts, paper-dispensing and manual-balancing rosters, and targeted support for logistics, care and municipal workers hit first by embodied systems. We answer the automated-decision scandal by conceding the gap — logging unread and oversight at forty seconds — and freezing the contested social-insurance/policing uses pending human review, to stop restriction becoming blanket rejection.
