### The sweep
Autumn brought the attack everyone had rehearsed for. A largely automated ransomware wave moved through municipal networks, clinics and two grid operators within days, using freshly generated tooling that slipped past signature defences. Published queues went dark in a dozen cities, elective care was postponed, and recovery took weeks in places that had refused telemetry sharing.

Where liaison feeds and locked backups were in use, isolation held. European officers in the shared operations room pulled partner early-warning into national response centres hours faster than before, and binding sample-sharing kept the parallel biosurveillance quiet. Containment was rented but real — defenders were visibly behind the attackers, yet services degraded rather than stopped.

### The leak that stays
In the middle of the response, a new open release landed almost at the frontier. Hundreds of thousands of downloads in the first week put the same generation of capability onto private hardware permanently. Police labs confirmed what responders feared: the sweep's tooling could now be rebuilt by anyone. That diffusion explains why open-weight capability tracked the frontier upward despite no new sovereign build.

Brussels had no sovereign answer. The fenced factory site stayed empty, cancelled slots stayed cancelled, and Council was told plainly that no build could be funded.

### A certifiable fix, only beginning
The one break came from the laboratory side. A control result that could be certified on deployed systems rather than argued over drew interest from developers and the AI Office. It was made a condition for restarting civic assistants in principle, with evaluations from the two pilot municipalities to be published and one insurer group agreeing to study resumed cover under joint monitoring.

Full rollout will take at least another turn: certification procedures, procurement, and joint monitoring are not yet in place, and the triage rollout was still seen as triage, not renewal. Waiting lists eased only in the pilots, local press warmed only there, and national coverage still ran on the benefits-system scandal while opposition parties campaigned on lawful-but-harmful systems. Brussels ended December safer inside someone else's watchtower, with a promising fix in testing and a frontier already out of recall.
