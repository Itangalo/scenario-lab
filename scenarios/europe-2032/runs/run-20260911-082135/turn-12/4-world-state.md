### The cutoff
The notice arrived without explanation. Clinics running triage support, two ministries and a cluster of logistics firms found the leading American model refusing their traffic. Helpdesks filled with the same error, procurement officers discovered fallback clauses that did not exist, and evening news ran split screens of darkened wards and ministers insisting essential work would continue.

It continued, after a fashion. Emergency lists issued from Brussels pushed hospitals onto an allied model and onto hardened community builds run on European cloud, conditional on sharing telemetry and holding locked backups. Liaison officers in the joint operations room worked through the night to re-route workloads. Services degraded rather than stopped, but doctors complained of coarser outputs and administrators of double entry.

### The sweep underneath
While staff reconfigured access, a largely automated extortion wave moved through municipal systems, clinics and grid operators. Queues went dark, elective procedures slipped, and recovery teams found tooling freshly generated for each target. Places inside the shared monitoring recovered in days; others stayed dark for weeks. Attribution remained open, though responders privately noted the methods could now be reproduced from openly available weights.

### The chokepoint used by another hand
In the same weeks Washington compelled a further tightening of lithography exports and servicing, reaching back to older machines and a wider customer list. The Dutch government complied under direct jurisdictional pressure. In Brussels, trade officials opened a formal evidence procedure under the anti-coercion instrument, paused any voluntary extension beyond legal obligation, and tried to link the question to allied compute access and insurer re-entry.

Washington barely acknowledged the linkage. Council minutes leaked, showing a split between those demanding retaliation and those warning the company would not survive defiance. No further concession was frozen in practice.

Brussels ended June with continuity rented, a certified fix still in testing, and its single strongest piece of leverage visibly operated by someone else.
