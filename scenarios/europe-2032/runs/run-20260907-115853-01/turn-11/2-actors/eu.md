## In practice
We are invoking Article 172 TFEU to issue the Emergency Sovereign Continuity Directive, bypassing the need for unanimity and overriding ongoing CJEU challenges to Article 114. The directive will be adopted via comitology with qualified majority support in COREPER, then fast-tracked through the Council under urgent procedure. It mandates that all national emergency response systems — including fire, medical dispatch, and civil protection — disconnect from non-EU cloud providers by turn 11 and migrate to hardened inference nodes operated by EuroHPC under Commission supervision. Transitional co-financing remains at 70%, and the Union assumes 90% of liability for service disruption during transition — terms already accepted by France, Germany, and Italy.

Resistance persists in the Baltics and Benelux, where binding contracts with US hyperscalers create legal exposure. To break the deadlock, we are activating the Anti-Coercion Instrument conditionally: should any provider attempt to retaliate against a member state for compliance, the Union will impose reciprocal restrictions on their operations in the internal market. Simultaneously, we are repurposing €32bn from the dormant InvestAI envelope to fund rapid deployment of sovereign edge nodes in vulnerable capitals. ENISA will conduct weekly audits of node integrity, feeding findings directly into the Public Trust Observatory.

The Observatory itself is now operational in interim form. Its first report — delayed from turn 10 but now scheduled for release in month 3 of this turn — will assess the cyber_major_incident’s origins, evaluate whether the ransomware toolkit showed signs of model-assisted generation, and publish a preliminary theory of harm around recursive self-modification in deployed agents. The report will also include a red-teaming forecast of next-order attack vectors, shared in advance with national CERTs. Public launch of the full platform remains on track for turn 12, but we are already using its nascent structure to coordinate cross-border data sharing between Europol, Frontex, and national biosecurity agencies — despite resistance from DG SANTE and some intelligence services. Trust must be rebuilt not through promises, but through visible, credible action under fire.
