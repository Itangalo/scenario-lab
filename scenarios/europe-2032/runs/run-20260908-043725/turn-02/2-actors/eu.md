## In practice
We are treating the cyber_major_incident as a wake-up call that cannot be answered with silence or study. The attack — a model-generated compromise of a widely used industrial control software dependency — bypassed every existing layer of signature-based defence. It spread across municipal water systems in Belgium, the Netherlands, and northern Italy before detection, disabling remote monitoring and forcing manual override. The blast radius is still being mapped, but the method is clear: an AI-generated exploit chain, polymorphic and context-aware, evaded sandboxing and signature checks alike.

In response, we are activating the new Joint Attribution Cell under Europol’s European Cybercrime Centre, co-located with ENISA’s incident response hub in Heraklion. This is not an additional measure but a surge within the existing Cyber Shield: we are reallocating €180m from the Digital Europe Programme to fast-track integration of model fingerprinting tools, developed in collaboration with the French and German cyber agencies, into real-time monitoring stacks. The goal is not just to catch this actor — attribution will take months — but to signal that the EU can now *learn* from attacks at machine speed. We are mandating data sharing between national CERTs under Article 8a of the NIS2 Directive, overruling objections from states that previously treated such intelligence as sovereign. In exchange, we are offering priority access to the Shield’s AI red-teaming simulations, giving smaller member states a defensive edge they could not build alone.

At the same time, we are holding firm on the InvestAI timeline, even as the Taiwan blockade tightens. The Commission has convened ASML, STMicroelectronics, and Infineon to co-design a transitional lithography pathway based on upgraded DUV tools, accepting a 18–24 month delay in reaching 2nm-equivalent performance. This is not ideal, but it keeps the industrial base alive. We are also renegotiating the Tech Sovereignty Package’s energy agreements: Fortum has agreed to co-invest in a 1.2GW microgrid buffer in Northern Sweden if we guarantee priority grid access for civilian heating during winter peaks. This trade — AI growth for social stability — is the new rhythm of sovereignty. We are not building faster. We are building *differently*, because the old dependencies are gone.
