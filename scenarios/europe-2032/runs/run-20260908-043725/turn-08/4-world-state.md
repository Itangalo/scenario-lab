### A Measured Advance Amid Mounting Pressure

Global AI capability advances to **67.0**, driven by continued progress in algorithmic self-optimisation and autonomous cyber exploitation. While not a broad leap, the refinement of attack agents enables more persistent intrusions, particularly in energy and transport sectors. No major incident occurs this turn, but near-misses accumulate, reinforcing urgency.

The **Launch EU Sovereign AI Corps** measure completes, establishing a central red-teaming unit under the Commission. Despite slower-than-expected staffing due to technical talent shortages, the institutional framework is in place, and limited crisis simulations using sovereign models have begun. This operational milestone delivers the full +5 boost to **eu_ai_sovereignty**, which rises to **23.0**—a significant gain reflecting hard-won institutional capacity.

The new **EU Red-Team Access Framework** is proposed as a large category 5 measure, aiming to audit high-capability models. However, hyperscalers push back fiercely, warning of IP exposure and operational disruption. The US government, under its **RETRENCHMENT** posture, signals that American labs may restrict access to EU auditors, citing national security. Early negotiations stall.

Meanwhile, **Accelerate Compute Sovereignty** progresses: emergency grid access is enforced in France and Finland, and Poland receives its €300 million buffer. Legal anchoring of model weights advances, though Hungary’s CJEU challenge remains pending. The **InvestAI Gigafactories** are fully operational, but their impact is partially offset by rising global capability and unresolved access friction.

**Resilience** declines to **53.5**, as the absence of active category 6 measures and a rising **ai_capability** (up 1.5) trigger a −1 to −2 penalty under metric rule 4. The prior **joint_threat_response** benefit has expired, and no new coordinated defence initiative has yet taken its place.

**Ai_safety** dips to **35.0**, as capability growth outpaces safeguards and no new safety controls land on frontier systems. **Eu_political_capital** falls to **18.0**, strained by three large measures (−9), a priority (−1), and no proposal bonus—the Red-Team Framework, while timely, is seen as diplomatically fraught and unproven. Public sentiment holds at **28.0**, as anxiety persists and no visible benefit emerges.
