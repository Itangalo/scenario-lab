### Shield Completes, Doctrine Launched

The **EU-Wide Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield** reaches full deployment. Autonomous defensive agents are now live across energy and health sectors in nine member states, marking a milestone in sovereign crisis readiness. Building on this, the EU launches the **Joint Operational Doctrine for AI-Driven Cyber Defence**, targeting interoperability gaps exposed during drills. The initiative formalises escalation rules, data jurisdiction, and mutual aid, starting with the Nordic-Benelux core.

However, Italy and Spain refuse to sign, demanding veto power over cross-border countermeasures. Their absence weakens the framework’s legitimacy. Poland, though participating, delays ratification, citing concerns over ENISA overreach. The doctrine moves forward, but as a coalition of the willing, not a unified front.
