However, Poland and Hungary refuse pre-authorization for autonomous countermeasures, citing judicial overreach, and demand a revised protocol—delaying full integration by six months. The measure, though launched, will now finish in turn 6 without full cohesion.

Attribution remains stalled, and public anger mounts.
