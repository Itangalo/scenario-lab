## In practice  
This turn, we close out Accelerate Compute Sovereignty. The emergency grid access in France and Finland is now legally enforced and energised; Poland’s €300 million grid buffer is drawn down through the RRF, overriding domestic objections on the grounds of cross-border systemic risk. The Council Regulation on Model Weight Anchoring is published in the Official Journal — a binding standard under Article 114 TFEU. Hungary’s CJEU challenge remains, but we are acting on the principle that legal certainty cannot wait for perfect consensus when the capability frontier does not. ENISA has begun designating anchoring facilities, and the first sovereign weights are being transferred under audit. The Sovereign AI Corps is staffed at 60% capacity — short of target, but enough to begin live simulations using these newly anchored models.

With compute and control now partially secured, we pivot to what they must serve: survival in a crisis. The openweight release at 54.5 means that offensive cyber agents are now widely distributed and irreversible. The Taiwan tensions are not yet a flashpoint, but they are a signal: the next incident may not be a near-miss. We cannot wait for full sovereignty to defend what we already have. This is why we launch the Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield under ENISA’s coordination, co-funded by the Digital Europe Programme and operationalised through a new mandate for the CSIRT network. The agents will be trained on sovereign infrastructure, using red-team data from the Corps, and deployed under a strict escalation protocol: Level 1 autonomy for detection, Level 2 (active response) only under national CIRT authority, with real-time logging accessible to the Commission. We are offering member states a 75% co-financing rate and fast-track access to InvestAI’s sovereign models — not as a gift, but as a condition of participation. Germany and the Nordics are on board; Italy and Spain want more safeguards on data jurisdiction. We are meeting them in the Permanent Representatives Committee next week, offering opt-in phases and joint oversight boards. This is not about perfection. It is about presence. We will not let the next six months pass without a functioning shield.
