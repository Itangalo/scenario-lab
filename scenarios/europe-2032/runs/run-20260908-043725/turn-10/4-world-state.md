Germany, Finland, and the Netherlands participate fully, running live simulations involving energy grid failures and hospital ransomware attacks. Italy and Spain join only in observational roles, citing unresolved data sovereignty concerns. Still, partial deployment yields early gains: flaws in escalation protocols are exposed, contributing to **+3 resilience** (from +6 potential). The visible effort to protect services lifts public sentiment slightly to **30.0**.
