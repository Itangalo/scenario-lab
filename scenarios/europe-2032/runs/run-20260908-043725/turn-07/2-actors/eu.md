## Priority
**Accelerate Compute Sovereignty via Emergency Grid Access and Legal Anchoring of Model Weights** — because the cyber_major_incident and capability_jump prove that compute access and model control are no longer industrial questions but immediate security imperatives, and because without binding jurisdiction over what runs on our soil, even the Sovereign AI Corps will operate on borrowed capability.

## In practice
We are acting on the fact that sovereignty is not just about having compute — it is about being able to *keep* it running and *control* what runs on it. The cyber_major_incident, enabled by model-generated attack tooling, exposed that our critical systems are now in the crosshairs of AI-amplified threats, and that we cannot defend what we do not fully control. This turn, we are fast-tracking emergency grid access for InvestAI Gigafactory sites in France, Finland, and Poland by invoking the Digital Markets Act’s critical infrastructure provisions and coordinating with ACER to override local permitting delays. Simultaneously, we are proposing a Council Regulation under Article 114 TFEU to establish legal anchoring of model weights: any model above 50.0 capability operated in the EU must have its weights stored on sovereign soil under enforceable EU jurisdiction, with audit access for ENISA and the future AI Office. This applies equally to foreign labs operating here — no more “cloud-only” frontier models beyond reach.

We are negotiating with hyperscalers through the Digital Services Act cooperation framework, offering compliance credits against future audits in exchange for early alignment. Germany and the Nordics are on board; Poland remains hesitant, citing energy sovereignty concerns, so we are pairing the grid access package with a €300 million resilience buffer for its national grid operator. Hungary is demanding exemptions, but we are holding firm: if you want access to EU markets and funding, you play by EU rules on what runs inside them. This is not protectionism — it is self-preservation. The Sovereign AI Corps will draw its first models from these legally anchored systems, and the red-teaming mandate ensures they are stress-tested before deployment. This is how we turn compute into control.
