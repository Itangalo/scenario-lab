## In practice
We run M14 as the triage core: HERA-mandated synthesis screening updates, ENISA-held pooled reagents for verified orders only, and satellite-only triage drills pushed through the last pilot regions with dispersed reserve cyber teams. M12 shelters stay on rota and M13 hardened images keep pushing to hospitals and grids — no new law, reprogrammed EU4Health and Digital Europe money.

We stand up the corridor absorption through HERA joint procurement and the Single Market surveillance network: verified-orders channel for US-China reagents and kits, stockpiling at sequencing hubs, and clinical-cloud failover to EU-hosted local servers. On robots and graduate hiring collapse we still open no second front — we map Chinese machine and US stack entry for a later move while Transition Shield placements, though late-paid, absorb what ops staff they can.
