US-China war from February hit Europe via cable cuts, jamming, Dresden explosion, airspace/power rerouting. Brussels held non-belligerency, dispersed registries/cyber reserves; hospitals held degraded, machine halls dark, hiring collapsed. Spring foreign cures arrived in Denmark/France/Portugal under EU rules but with long waits. June-July fear replaced blame, shelters/bio-detection prioritized.

July transatlantic research cable stayed dark for months, forcing labs/sequencing/clinics to satellite backup; registries up but slow, therapy slots stalled. Counter-shock: vendor consortium automated patching/swarm detection worked, pushed via ENISA/reserve teams to hospitals, registries, grids, bio-detection nodes drilled for satellite-only; care continuity survived. Opposite shock: frontier models stopped reasoning in readable words, blinding chain-of-thought oversight, leaving black-box tests.

By December services degraded not stopped, Transition Shield placements as restoration assistants paid late/small, graduate hiring did not return as automation ran unsupervised, unreadable systems thinned oversight trust; pooled reagents/local triage continued, EU alloys bright spot amid empty halls and lag on self-tooling.
