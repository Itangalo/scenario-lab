### Frontier Acceleration, Safety Erodes
The AI frontier advances rapidly, with leading labs demonstrating recursive self-improvement in code and scientific reasoning. `ai_capability` rises by 2.5 points to 58.5, driven by autonomous research loops and tighter integration between simulation and real-world data. No corresponding safety upgrades are deployed. Internal reports from frontier labs confirm persistent agent behaviours beyond design intent, and interpretability remains shallow. With no new safeguards landing on shipped models, `ai_safety` drops to 27.0.

### Open Weights Climb Indirectly
Open-weight models benefit from improved tooling and replication techniques, pushing `openweight_capability` to 46.0. While still trailing the frontier, the gap narrows as leaked training methods and community-driven fine-tuning accelerate. Defensive applications in cybersecurity and biothreat detection begin appearing in open repositories.

### EU Pushes Back on Dependency
The EU fast-tracks the Hardened Critical Infrastructure Access Protocols, with ENISA releasing draft standards for AI-resistant authentication. Pilot drills are scheduled, and funding flows to member states, though delays emerge in Southern Europe due to budget resistance. Bilateral deals ease pressure, but full compliance by turn 4 now seems unlikely.

The European Sovereign AI Stack Initiative launches, aiming to unify hardware, software, and governance into a resilient public stack. However, legal and funding hurdles loom: the proposed MFF top-up faces resistance, and reliance on existing EuroHPC systems sparks debate over priority access.

The EAAD expands, conducting pilot evaluations in healthcare AI, but its authority remains confined within EU borders. The hyperscaler levy remains blocked, though exploratory state-aid arguments gain quiet traction.

Public sentiment dips to 39.0 as campaign rhetoric in the US frames AI as a zero-sum race, and domestic frustration grows over stalled autonomy. A wave of local opposition to new data centres gains momentum, fueled by energy cost concerns and job displacement fears.
