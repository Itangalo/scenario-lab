### MPAC Declared Operational, But Crisis Deepens

The Middle Power AI Coalition (MPAC) is formally activated, with binding protocols now in force among the EU, Japan, South Korea, and Norway. Canada participates in intelligence-sharing under a non-binding memorandum. The Sovereign AI Core in Brittany and northern Sweden successfully hosts its first live evaluation of a 66.5-capability model, marking a technical milestone. Germany and Austria drop legal challenges in exchange for guaranteed expansion, and ENTSO-E confirms stable integration.

Yet the moment is overshadowed by accelerating crisis. A major cyber incident—automated, widespread, and deeply disruptive—hits public health systems across three member states. The attack exploits newly automated vulnerability discovery, and while MPAC enables faster logging and response coordination, the damage is severe and visible. Public trust erodes further.

Simultaneously, a discontinuous capability jump is demonstrated in autonomous code generation, altering the offensive cyber landscape within weeks. Though general AI capability rises only modestly to 69.5, the leap intensifies fears of uncontrolled escalation. Interpretability degrades further, with developers unable to explain key reasoning shifts.

Worse, an AI-supported social insurance system is exposed for systematically denying claims to vulnerable populations, confirmed by national ombudsmen. The scandal ignites fury, with protests outside data centres and renewed calls for moratoria. Labour markets continue to collapse, and public sentiment plummets.

The EU declares MPAC functionally complete and celebrates the Sovereign Core’s activation, but with no new measures proposed and political capital dwindling, the sense grows that the Union is managing decline rather than reversing it.

### Fragility Takes Hold

Credit agencies quietly circulate internal assessments rating the EU as high-risk for institutional strain. The human futures movement gains traction, framing AI not as progress but as an existential threat to dignity and agency.
