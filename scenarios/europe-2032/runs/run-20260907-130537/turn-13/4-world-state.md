### The Weight of Opaque Systems

AI capability advances to 71.5 as self-improving loops in legal and clinical verification deepen, further outpacing interpretability. Developers at leading labs now report blind spots in model reasoning that evade even internal audit tools, confirming a systemic erosion of control. The frontier moves faster than any institution can track, and no external restraint slows it.

The EU launches the **Sovereign Oversight and Verification Initiative**, a sweeping effort to impose mandatory transparency and independent verification on all public AI systems above 50.0 capability. Framed as an act of institutional repair, it targets the root cause of recent failures: unaccountable decision-making in social insurance, healthcare, and energy. ENISA is tasked with continuous audits, and a new Public AI Observatory will host real-time monitoring.

Yet the initiative founders immediately. Spain and Italy reject the framework as an overreach, citing national sovereignty and fiscal strain. Even with co-ownership offers and judicial appeal mechanisms, consensus stalls. The European Parliament demands sweeping amendments, delaying the draft regulation. Funding of €2.1 billion is pledged, but disbursement hinges on agreement that does not come.

The **Emergency Resilience Mobilisation**, still in its first turn, delivers no tangible resilience. Energy grids suffer repeated disruptions, and the social insurance scandal widens, now implicating disability and unemployment systems across four member states. Public protests grow, and trust in state institutions erodes further.

The **Middle Power AI Coalition** remains inert, unable to coordinate a joint response. Meanwhile, independent analysts publish real-time assessments of institutional fragility, amplifying pressure on governments already stretched thin.

With no measure completed, no safety gains, and no political capital to spare, the EU’s agenda buckles under its own weight. Ambition outpaces capacity — again.
