AI capability rises to 71.5, driven by self-improving loops in legal and clinical systems, deepening the interpretability gap and eroding developer control. In response, the EU launches the Sovereign Oversight and Verification Initiative, mandating transparency and independent audits for high-capability public AI systems, with ENISA overseeing continuous verification and a new Public AI Observatory enabling real-time monitoring. However, the initiative collapses under political resistance from Spain and Italy, who reject it as a sovereignty infringement, while European Parliament amendments and conditional funding of €2.1 billion stall implementation. The Emergency Resilience Mobilisation fails to deliver tangible results, as energy grid disruptions persist and the social insurance scandal expands into disability and unemployment programs across four member states. Public distrust intensifies amid growing protests and institutional fragility. The Middle Power AI Coalition remains ineffective, unable to coordinate action. With no progress on safety, governance, or resilience, and political capital depleted, the EU’s reform agenda falters, underscoring a widening chasm between regulatory ambition and operational reality.
