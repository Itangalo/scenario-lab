### Fragility Hardens

AI capability climbs to 70.5 as automated verification spreads across legal and clinical domains, accelerating self-improving loops in code and decision systems. The frontier pulls further ahead, while interpretability lags — developers now admit they cannot trace how certain models reach compliance conclusions, eroding internal confidence.

The EU launches the Emergency Resilience Mobilisation under Article 122 TFEU, framing fragility as an existential threat. A Crisis Resilience Directive is drafted, mandating red-teaming, failover protocols, and rapid patching for AI-supported public systems. ENISA gains emergency audit powers, and €12 billion in funding is unlocked. Yet implementation stumbles immediately: Spain and Italy reject binding deadlines, citing debt limits, and only accept conditional support after protracted negotiations. The European Parliament forces concessions, including a new Oversight Panel, slowing rollout.

Despite the effort, no resilience gains land — the measure is too new, and systems remain exposed. A second wave of AI-driven disruptions hits regional energy grids, compounding public distrust. The social insurance scandal deepens as audits reveal similar flaws in unemployment and disability systems, suggesting systemic design failure rather than isolated error.

Public sentiment sinks further, now at 7.0, as protests grow and the human futures movement gains traction. Labour displacement accelerates, with legal and medical support roles hollowed out by verified autonomous systems. Credit agencies quietly downgrade several member states’ AI governance risk ratings.

Meanwhile, the Middle Power AI Coalition proves too slow to respond. Though intelligence-sharing improves, coordinated action remains hamstrung by divergent national timelines. The EU’s political capital erodes to 5.0 — below the threshold for reliable agenda control — and sovereignty slips as global AI advances outpace domestic capacity.

### A Threshold Unmet

The Union acts decisively, but too late. The gap between ambition and absorption widens. Institutions strain under the weight of crisis, and the window to shape events narrows.
