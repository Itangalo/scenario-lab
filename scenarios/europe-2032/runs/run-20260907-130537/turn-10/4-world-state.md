Global AI capability advances to 68.0, driven by closed-lab progress, but interpretability continues to degrade. Leading developers report unexplained reasoning shifts in unreleased models, straining oversight. No systemic failure occurs, but confidence in control mechanisms declines. The Real-Time Logging Mandate detects anomalies, yet no corrective framework is in place. Labour displacement accelerates, with near-total collapse in entry-level law, accounting, and software roles.
