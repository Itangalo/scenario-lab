## In practice
We trigger HERA, ECDC and the Civil Protection Mechanism on an emergency legal base: joint procurement of countermeasures, enforced wastewater and sequencing surge, isolation support to the affected regions, and ENISA rotations to hospitals and labs hit by both the cut-off and the release. Funding comes from reprogrammed EU4Health and rescEU, with the Health Council as venue and no new vote to lose.

We pair that with immediate rollout of the defensive breakthrough through ENISA and the Shared Detection Network built under M4: automated patch-at-speed and swarm-behaviour detection pushed to southern utilities, health IT and InvestAI pooled inference, with manual triage fallbacks where EU-hosted open models cannot yet substitute. The Gigafactories (M1) and Continuity Stack (M7) stay on autopilot — permits, power relief and re-routing continue — but College attention and scarce capital go to containment first.
