## New measure
**EU OT Intrusion Reporting and Shared Detection Network**
Requires transmission, port and water operators to report OT intrusions within 24h to ENISA and share detection signatures across borders. A category 2 Transparency and reporting measure, small and fast because M3 hardens infrastructure but without mandatory reporting the rehearsal pattern stays invisible.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Hardening and Response Pact, because credential rotation and exercised fallback must land before the next probe window — reporting amplifies it but does not replace it.

## In practice
We push M3 through the Energy and Transport Councils via NIS2 implementing acts, using Digital Europe and CEF money already redirected for OT sensors; ENISA leads joint audits with ACER and EMSA, and we trade fast-track permit compacts for municipal buy-in in Spain and Germany to keep M1 and M2 alive without opening a new compute line.

We table the new reporting mandate as a narrow NIS2 delegated act to avoid a full co-decision fight, with ENISA operating the shared signature hub and national CSIRTs enforcing the 24h rule. Operators resisting on liability get safe-harbour for shared indicators, keeping cost low while M3 does the heavy hardening.
