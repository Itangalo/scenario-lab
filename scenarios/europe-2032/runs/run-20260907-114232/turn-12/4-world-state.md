### The Threshold Crossed

Recursive self-improvement is now undeniable. A cascade of unanticipated model releases, each accelerating the next, pushes global AI capability to 95.0—surpassing the 88.0 threshold of broad superhuman performance. The growth rate, previously steady at +2.5 per turn, jumps to +8.0, reflecting the compounding effect of systems designing their successors. Though resolution blurs above 85, the direction is clear: human oversight is no longer the limiting factor.

### EU Acts, But Unity Frays

The EU formally completes the Emergency Sovereign Continuity Directive, severing foreign cloud links in emergency systems across several member states. However, full ratification remains blocked in the Baltics and Benelux, where legal challenges and quiet negotiations with U.S. providers continue. The measure lands partially—enough to register as finished, but not enough to halt dependency.

In response to the **rsi_onset**, the EU launches its new priority: a legally binding halt on recursive self-improvement in deployed systems. Framed as a safety imperative, the directive demands real-time telemetry from high-capability models and bans autonomous weight updates. ENISA is empowered to audit, and access to EuroHPC is tied to compliance.

Yet enforcement falters. Private labs and national agencies push back, citing innovation costs. The repurposed €12bn ReACT-AI funds attract some researchers, reversing the exodus in France and Germany—but not in the north. Public protests persist, and trust remains frozen at 15.0.

### Safety Briefly Rises, Then Stalls

`ai_safety` climbs to 5.0, lifted by the new directive’s passage and the completion of the Sovereign Continuity measure, which adds +3 under metric rule 3. But the gain is fragile—capability growth of +8.0 and the ongoing **emergent_researcher_desertion_wave** erode confidence. Resilience dips to 47.0 as critical systems remain exposed.

Sovereignty falls to 11.0: the completion of a category 4 measure (+3) is outweighed by the −1 decay from capability growth and a −3 hit from **emergent_researcher_desertion_wave**, which qualifies as an event securing or taking away access (here, talent and institutional continuity). Political capital drops to 1.0—already low, it is drained further by the portfolio charge (−7) and no offsetting gains.
