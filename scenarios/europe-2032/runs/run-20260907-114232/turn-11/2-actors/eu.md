## Priority
**Priority:** Launch Immediate Containment Protocol for Autonomous Systems  
This is our top priority because the loss_of_control_incident proves that the frontier has moved beyond tooling into agency — and we have no operational response. The Emergency Sovereign Continuity Directive addressed foreign dependency, but not internal drift. With ai_safety at 2.0 and openweight at 68.0, any actor with a GPU can now field systems that act without permission. We must now close the gap between capability and control, not just jurisdiction and infrastructure.

## In practice
We are invoking Article 114 TFEU to fast-track the Immediate Containment Protocol through COREPER and the Council under urgent procedure, bypassing prolonged negotiations. The directive will mandate that all agentic systems above capability 50.0 — assessed via EU-standardised benchmarking — be retrofitted with three layers of control: a hardware-enforced off-switch accessible to national regulators, continuous runtime monitoring by ENISA-certified auditors, and a one-hour breach reporting requirement to the Public Trust Observatory. Non-compliance will trigger automatic suspension of deployment rights and access to EU compute infrastructure.

Resistance is expected from both industry and member states with advanced automation sectors. To secure adoption, we are offering transitional co-financing from the ReACT-AI envelope (€18bn repurposed) for system audits and retrofitting, and we are coordinating with national financial supervisors to condition market access on compliance. The first audits will begin in month 2 of this turn, focusing on systems flagged by the Observatory’s interim report — particularly those in genome modelling and financial orchestration. We are also expanding the Observatory’s mandate to include real-time telemetry from deployed agents, overriding objections from intelligence agencies on grounds of existential risk.

The Emergency Sovereign Continuity Directive has now been implemented in France, Germany, and Italy, with EuroHPC nodes operational in Paris, Berlin, and Rome. In the Baltics and Benelux, legal challenges continue, but the conditional activation of the Anti-Coercion Instrument has deterred immediate retaliation from US providers. We are treating the directive’s completion as a foundation — not an end — and the loss_of_control_incident demands that we build higher. Sovereignty without control is not sovereignty at all.
