**Taiwan-Contingency Continuity Reserve**
Creates a stockpiled and pre-contracted reserve of chips, model access and EU-hosted fallback capacity to keep health, energy and telecom workloads running through a Taiwan-linked supply shock.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, small and fast, because with capital at 20 and the Taiwan gate open for three turns we cannot build new compute — we must make what dependence remains survivable while the Allied Pact negotiates supply.

No bilateral side deals clear without common hosting terms.

We stand up the Reserve via HERA, ENISA and DG CNECT under existing civil-protection and EU4Health envelopes: inventory critical health/energy/telecom AI dependencies, pre-contract EU-hosted fallback, and extend the six-hospital sentinel feed with waiver-based stockpile reporting. The fenced gigafactory sites stay in caretaker — permits and grid fees paid, no construction decision — to avoid a cash call we cannot fund at 20 capital.
