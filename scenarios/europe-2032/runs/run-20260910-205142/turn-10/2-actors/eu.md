## New measure
**EU Displacement Transition Facility**
Creates an EU-cofunded wage-insurance, retraining and hiring-credit backstop for entry-level white-collar roles hollowed out by AI, paid partly by a transition levy on large deployers.
Category 7 Labour and social protection, small and fast, because measurable graduate hiring collapse amid blockade-driven scarcity is what will decide whether the Union holds cohesion to use its upstream leverage at all.

## Priority
EU Displacement Transition Facility, because with hospitals rationed and graduates not hired, social absorption now outranks any new compute build in holding the mandate to act.

## In practice
We fund this from ESF+ reprogramming plus a front-loaded Social Climate-style top-up, decided in EPSCO and ECOFIN, delivered via national public employment services with an EU hiring-credit window for SMEs that keep entry-level posts. Large enterprise deployers above a deployment threshold contribute to the transition levy, which buys business acceptance for the continuity rationing to come.

We hold the rest in caretaker-survival mode: continuity shield operations continue under HERA/ENISA load-shedding, gigafactory shells stay grid-ready but un-equipped while the blockade freezes chip supply, and we inventory ASML, Zeiss, SOITEC and chemical chokepoints under the Anti-Coercion Instrument for a joint approach with Tokyo/Seoul next turn — no separate Washington concession now. Procurement of tailored therapies stays on EU-hosting terms, with compassionate-use derogations to defuse the oncology backlash.
