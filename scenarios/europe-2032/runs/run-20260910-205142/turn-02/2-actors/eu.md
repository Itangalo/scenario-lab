**EU Clinical Validation Network**

This is Category 9 Diffusion, adoption and public trust, because it turns the one visible AI success into public benefit and trust while serving our keep-essential-services commitment via health resilience — and it is cheap and doable while investment collapse stalls large compute builds and a member-state defection strains cohesion.

## Priority
M3 Grid and Port Cyber Shield, because mapped breakers and water relays still leave essential services exposed and that is what our two-year commitment is staked on, not the biotech upside.

## In practice
We keep the Shield as the main effort through ENISA and the Energy and Transport Councils: TSO/DSO segmentation audits and credential rotation continue with 70% co-funding, and we refuse to let Dutch, Polish and Spanish staffing complaints slow the first 40 sites. InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package we keep alive on paper through DG CNECT permitting zones, but with private capital fleeing we pause new cash calls and protect sites and grid connections rather than pretending build-out continues.

Alongside that we launch the Clinical Validation Network via DG SANTE and EMA with HERA money: joint trial protocol across the reporting research hospitals, pooled compound-screening compute on existing EuroHPC, and linked hospital detection pilots. It gives member states tempted to cut side deals a visible EU win to stay inside, and gives the public a reason to tolerate the grid hardening disruption.
