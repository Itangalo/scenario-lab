## Priority
M4 EU Clinical Validation Network is not the priority; the new EU Common Hosting Terms is, because the side-deal defection directly threatens our ability to hold any common position on essential-services resilience that our two-year commitment requires.

## In practice
We push the Common Hosting Terms through the Competitiveness Council and DG CNECT under the internal-market and security legal base: any hosting arrangement above a threshold must meet EU data-immunity, exit-portability, and ENISA audit clauses, with Digital Europe funds withheld for non-compliant deals. We offer the defecting capital accelerated grid connection from the protected Gigafactory land bank and a share of pooled EuroHPC screening time as the price of re-alignment.

We keep M1 Gigafactories and M2 Tech sovereignty package in caretaker mode — permits, land and grid reservations held, no new cash calls — and let M4 Clinical Validation Network run via DG SANTE/EMA and HERA as the visible win that makes staying inside the common line politically sellable. Grid hardening gains from finished M3 are locked in via ENISA follow-up audits at the first forty sites without reopening a large funding fight we cannot afford.
