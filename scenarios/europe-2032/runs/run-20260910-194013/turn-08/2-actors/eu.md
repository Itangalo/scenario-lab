## New measure
**EU Essential Services Continuity Stack**
Re-hosts the hospital, ministry and critical-firm workloads cut off from the leading foreign model onto EU-controlled inference — pooled Gigafactory-permitted capacity, vetted fallback models and rationed chips — so essential services stay running without that provider. This now because the short-notice cutoff turned dependence from a warning into dark screens in hospitals and ministries, and with open weights near-frontier and chips blockaded the only credible answer is a sovereign fallback that keeps the lights on while joint protection is negotiated.

## Priority
M10 EU Essential Services Continuity Stack, because the access denial outranks even the joint cyber accession — the two-year commitment to capacity that cannot be switched off abroad fails this half-year if hospitals and ministries stay dark.

## In practice
We run the Continuity Stack through DG CNECT, ENISA and the AI Office with the Health and Telecom Councils: inventory cut-off workloads under the M4 registry, re-route them to EU-hosted pools under emergency procurement, allocate scarce vetted compute by triage criteria agreed with the two strike states, and hold Gigafactory permitted sites as the legal shell for emergency inference rather than new build. Money is repurposed InvestAI operational funds, no fresh large pledge while capital is at 23.

We keep M9 alive on a low burn: liaison officers stay in the joint cyber command, M4 telemetry keeps flowing for triage of the automated sweep, and DG TRADE maintains conditional lithography spares flow for protection and vetted relief without escalating to embargo. M8 patching and M7 mutual-aid crews hold degradation-not-stop while the Stack lands, and we tell publics plainly this is foresight failure being repaired, not outrage to be performed.
