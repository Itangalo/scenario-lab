## New measure
**EU Distressed Capacity Anchor**
Takes EU-anchored stakes in cancelled build-out and distressed AI suppliers to keep permitted gigafactory sites, power contracts and engineers inside the Union while valuations are down. This now because investment collapse plus tightening export controls would otherwise strand EU sites without chips or partners, and a small cheap anchor buys sovereignty leverage that a large build cannot afford at capital 22 and sentiment 16. Category is Sovereignty and industrial capacity.

## Priority
M12 EU Distressed Capacity Anchor, because with no measures in flight and queues lengthening, preventing loss of sites, power and talent outranks starting anything new under the essential-services commitment.

## In practice
We run this through DG COMP and DG CNECT with the EIB and national promotional banks: repurposed InvestAI operational funds for bridge equity and lease guarantees for the permitted gigafactory sites, conditional on EU legal anchoring, EU-hosted fallback priority for M11 shield loads, and spares-pooling under the joint response. DG TRADE aligns export-control posture to volume-licence allied access, trading lithography spares and relay stocks for assured chip and model-serving carve-outs rather than a retaliatory embargo.

We keep M11 Shield crews funded on secondment to hold hospitals, registries and grids through daily probes, and procure tailored therapies and admin tooling only on EU-hosted terms to show benefit without dependence. We tell publics plainly that open near-frontier intrusion-as-a-service means manual override stays, and rationing criteria stay with Health and Energy Councils until queues clear.
