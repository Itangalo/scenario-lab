Through H2 2031 Brussels pursued containment without new initiatives, deepening a two-track recovery where telemetry-sharing districts restored in days while eastern/southern districts lagged for weeks, fuelling a two-Europes narrative. The EU Sovereign Fallback Stack in year three kept scheduling, payments and triage up in drills off US models but slower and only partly stocked. A genome-modelling biosecurity scare led to quieter synthesis tightening with no new law, and Taiwan Strait manoeuvres raised chip/shipping anxiety with only EU accelerator stockpiling advancing. Public trust slipped amid data-centre siting resistance, though no moratorium passed.

In H1 2032 Brussels tried to buy back trust after a benefits-algorithm scandal where a social-insurance support system systematically cut/flagged vulnerable claimants with unread logs and ~40-second approvals. The Commission treated it as enforcement failure, opening a serious-incident procedure with case review, compensation and audit publication, but no AI law rewrite. The scandal fuelled siting blockades: mayors linked blackouts to computing sites, delaying extensions; mediation via grid-hardening, rebates and local benefits partly resumed work, no general freeze passed. Isolation finished with certified gates, stops and telemetry, cutting restoration to days in connected districts — containment landed, not resilience solved. Taiwan and biosecurity pressures continued quietly with extended stockpiling and tight screening, no new laws. Office AI showed productivity gains, especially for juniors, without job losses, but did not lift mood.
