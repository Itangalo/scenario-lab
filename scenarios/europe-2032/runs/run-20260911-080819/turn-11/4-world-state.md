### Holding the line
July to December 2031 was the half-year Brussels tried to finish containment without starting anything new.

The isolation programme became the test. Money for repair now arrived only after auditors certified separation gates and emergency stops, and clinics and town halls that fed live telemetry into the joint cell came back in days with Franco-Dutch teams on site. Others did not. Several eastern and southern districts stayed on paper procedures for weeks, mayors accusing the Commission of punishing victims for the defences they lacked. The Commission called it conditionality working; the evening news called it two Europes.

Work continued on the long-running EU Sovereign Fallback Stack, now in its third year of build-out. Reserved supercomputing partitions carried appointment scheduling, benefits payments and hospital triage while migration drills moved ministries off the withdrawn American system. Engineers admitted the European-hosted substitutes were slower on complex queries, but they stayed up. Further partitions and spare-accelerator stockpiles were still being installed and certified, so only part of the expected sovereign capacity came online this half-year. That counted as partial progress, not completion.

Two shadows lengthened over that fragile recovery.

In September a genome modelling paper claimed a non-specialist with assistant help could sketch a viable human-infecting design. Authors and critics fought bitterly over methods and over publishing at all, largely inside biosecurity journals. Health authorities quietly tightened screening of synthesis orders and froze new bio-assistant tools in clinical labs pending review, without announcing a new law.

At the same time extended manoeuvres in the Taiwan Strait lifted shipping insurance and rattled chip buyers. Brussels raised EU stockpiling of spare accelerators and floated proposals for aligned export paperwork in its middle-power supply venue, but partners made no commitment and no joint alignment was agreed; only the EU's own stockpiling moved forward, deliberately said little about in public.

Meanwhile openly available models continued to absorb prior frontier gains, with open-weight capability edging toward last period's frontier level as existing weights diffused, without any new EU open-weight release.

Science offered a counterpoint. A Zurich-Barcelona team, using AI to search verifiable protein structures, resolved a long-disputed enzyme mechanism that specialists called a landmark and that vaccine developers said would shorten design cycles within a year or two. It barely moved public mood.

That mood kept slipping. Single-source reports of residents blocking surveyors at planned computing sites multiplied, and local councils began debating siting freezes. No moratorium passed, but contractors reported delays.

By Christmas, services were more isolated, the reserve was partly proven and still incomplete, and trust sat lower. The small sovereignty gain from the partial fallback capacity was partly offset in politics by conditionality fights and siting resistance, leaving political capital down moderately.
