## New measure
**EU Siting Social Licence Deal**
Offers host municipalities capped energy prices, water-use guarantees and revenue shares in exchange for binding gigafactory site decisions within the semester. This is category 4 Sovereignty and industrial capacity work, because the municipal blockade is now the binding constraint on the compute build.
Without it M1/M2 stall while intrusions continue; with it we turn backlash into enforceable siting and co-opt the university compute pact into legitimate demand.

## Priority

## In practice
We move the siting fight to the Energy Council with DG ENER and DG CNECT jointly tabling a standard host agreement: grid-connection priority plus efficiency and water-recycling conditions for developers, and a share of compute time and lease revenue for host regions. Solidarity and cohesion reallocation covers the sweetener. Councils that sign get fast-tracked permits; those that refuse after spring lose priority connection.

In parallel we keep the Critical Systems Shield (M3) on its exercise timetable via ENISA/ACER/EMSA, and steer the university open-compute pact toward pooled replication inside the Gigafactory planning — access to power and evaluation sandboxes if they accept safety reporting — rather than unmonitored Mythos-class replication. The US interim access cover is maintained, not expanded, while domestic sites are decided.
