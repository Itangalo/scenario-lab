## New measure
**EU Embodied Systems and Essential Workforce Continuity Shield**
Keeps hospitals, grid, logistics and care running on human/manual fallback while foreign-built armed-capable robots deploy, by ring-fencing essential sites from external control models and funding retained essential staff.
Why this and why now: with wards on triage, capital at 3 and sentiment at 4, commercial-military robots landing on an exhausted workforce would break restoration counts; this is triage continuity extended to embodied AI, not a new compute build.

## Priority
The new Shield, because getting through winter with staff standing and core services isolated from foreign-controlled embodied systems outranks any leverage bid while the two release cities still count recoveries.

## In practice
We run this through HERA, ECDC, ENISA and the Health and Employment Councils as an extension of the Bio-Containment Protocol: essential hospitals, grid operators and municipal logistics declared robot-control-isolated zones, procurement frozen for Chinese-platform and US-model-dependent systems in those zones, mutual-aid maintenance crews and cross-border care teams kept on paid retention, manual-fallback drills exercised where admissions remain closed.

We hold the line elsewhere without spending we do not have: no Dutch servicing cut, joint-licensing offer left on the table, sole powered site kept on hospitals/grid/backups, daily restored-service counts as the single reporting line. No new siting fight, no new build; DG TRADE and Foreign Affairs Council keep the essential-compute plea alive while Health holds the wards.
