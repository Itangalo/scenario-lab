## New measure
None this turn, while political capital sits at 0 and court freezes still block any build or funded programme.

With safety at 7, open weights at 62.8 near the closed frontier, and Taiwan exercises opening a supply-chain gate for the next three turns, a new large instrument now would fail on siting, funding and cohesion and deepen the paralysis; the two-year commitment to harden health, bio and cyber resilience first while negotiating water-power compacts requires holding cohesion and letting capital rebuild this semester rather than spending what we do not have.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority named this turn, because with no measures in flight and no new measure started, the work is preserving the no-undercut pact and advancing site-by-site compact drafts rather than pushing a portfolio instrument.

## In practice
The Commission, via DG ENER, DG ENV and Legal Service, stays in the two freeze councils with the capped water/power envelopes, sequenced grid ties and clinic earmarks tabled in spring, seeking technical referee sign-off site-by-site without challenging injunctions, while DG TRADE monitors shipping-insurance and chip-supply exposure from the Taiwan exercises and prepares a pooled-stock and rerouting note.

HERA, ECDC and ENISA continue on existing mandates and finished M4/M5/M6 capacity: syndromic watch, hospital cyber-hygiene checks, and guidance to municipalities, hospitals and university labs on handling the unrecallable frontier open-weight system, with DG CNECT keeping wards on the continuity stack and bedded-in triage helpers where they hold gains. No new money, permits or mandates are tabled until capital and siting unfreeze.
