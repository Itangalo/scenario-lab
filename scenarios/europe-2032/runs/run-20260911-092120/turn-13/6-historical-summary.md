Through 2030-32 Brussels failed to build and then held a freeze: two compute sites stayed crippled under cordons, patrols and hospital power plans after spring sabotage, with water/power-cap talks in closed councils, no injunctions challenged and no money moved. Cut off without reason or appeal from the leading US model, hospitals ran degraded triage/imaging on slower domestic helpers with steady backlogs. Shocks were policed not solved: tighter fever-and-rash watch after contested claim a genome model aided pathogen design, and hygiene guidance only for the unrecallable open system on hundreds of thousands of machines. Autumn 2032 the common front cracked as one capital announced its own compute/model side deal undercutting joint bargaining; trade officials logged breach but kept the no-undercut pact formally alive. Union ended 2032 managed but diminished — dependent abroad, blocked at home, bargained around by members.
