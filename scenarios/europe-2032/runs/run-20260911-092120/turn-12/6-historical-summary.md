Through 2030-31 Brussels failed to build: two compute sites frozen by water-permit freezes and unchallenged injunctions; funds exhausted, hospitals on slower domestic helpers, imaging gains only where EU triage bedded in. Commission closed no-undercut pact and pooled offers, but capital kept separate deal and Washington gave only continuity language; autumn talks on capped water/power and grid ties produced drafts only. Shocks absorbed via syndromic watch: contested biosecurity paper on genome model, and unrecallable open system on hundreds of thousands of machines. Late 2031: Taiwan exercises and insurance spike prompted chip-exposure notes; tampering, scorched trenches and blockades at frozen sites sparked patrols.

Winter 2032 turned to emergency management: amid unease over leaked benchmarks of unexplained unreleased foreign system, US withdrew at short notice European access to leading American model without reason or appeal — including capital's separate deal — stalling triage helpers and drafting tools, forcing fallback to domestic helpers with lengthened queues and imaging backlogs. Simultaneously both frozen sites suffered coordinated damage to grid and water feeds claimed by anti-data-centre groups, triggering national-security policing, cordons and hospital power plans. Commission tabled no money nor challenged injunctions, staying in freeze councils; health/cyber agencies leaned on old mandates and guidance for downloadable system; chip notes updated without shortage. No-undercut pact survived on paper, but Union appeared exposed — denied from abroad, blocked at home — hardening siting politics.
