### Holding by hand
The first half of 2031 brought no new money and no new law, only a fight to keep what was already on the ground.

The investment reset hit first. American labs cancelled build-outs, valuations fell by half in weeks, and two cloud arrangements European clinics and research pools had quietly relied on for overflow were withdrawn. Washington then tightened quotas again: allied buyers kept nominal access but on strict volume licences, with servicing approvals case by case. In The Hague, Rotterdam and Eindhoven, tools waited for papers that did not come.

At home, the black-start network became a target. Small groups blaming the grid collapse on cooperation with machine systems broke into transformer stores, drained fuel drums and smashed telemetry cabinets in three countries. Police reports described printed lists of off-books sites circulating online. Grid operators, hospitals and civil protection units moved quickly to bring hidden stocks onto official lists, disperse them to guarded depots and reimburse holders. The map published in May — depots, guarded pools, manual fallback points — turned a secret into a lifeline, but two fires and a destroyed breaker-log archive showed how exposed restoration by hand remains.

The containment protocol finished in the middle of this. Joint isolation drills, cross-border mutual aid teams and shared playbooks for rogue agents were declared operational in March, and were used within weeks when sabotage cut control links in two regions. Faults were isolated faster, even if rebuilding still took days.

One bright spot was claimed loudly. In Denmark, Estonia and parts of Spain, administrative assistants that triage referrals and draft decisions cut waiting lists measurably. Health ministries co-branded the systems as European choices, kept them on European defaults, and paid the deployment teams bonuses. For the first time in two years, local news carried a different AI story alongside the vandalism and quotas.

By June the common line held, barely. Rationed from outside, sabotaged from inside, Brussels had defended the fallback without being able to replace it.
