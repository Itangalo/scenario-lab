## Priority
M9 — EU Deployed-Model Assurance Adoption, because it is the only funded instrument that both holds essential services together and gives us an attributable European benefit to point to while capital is at 2.0.

## In practice
We push M9 through the AI Evaluation and Safety Institute with ENISA and EU-CyCLONe, installing the prediction checklists on grid control, hospital triage and the Denmark/Estonia/Spain assistants via existing evaluation mandates and NIS2 rails. DG TRADE keeps hand-rationing US volume licences to those same services, and patrols stay on depots and black-start points. No new directive, no new fund.

We use what the turn gave us without spending: the waiting-list falls and administrative decisions in days are branded as the Union's adoption choice, and the office productivity evidence — gains for junior staff with no employment fall — is carried by Employment and Health ministers to counter sabotage narratives. The defecting member state's side-deal is not publicly punished; we contain it in COREPER and the Trade formation by ring-fencing essential-service rationing, to avoid a cohesion break we cannot afford.
