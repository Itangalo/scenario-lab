### Patching by hand
The second half of 2031 gave Brussels its first cheap win in years, and its hardest quota squeeze.

American export controls tightened again in autumn. Under Washington's country-tier rationing, allied buyers kept nominal access but only on strict volume licences, with servicing approvals case by case. For the Netherlands tool pipeline and for clinics relying on overflow compute, papers stalled. DG TRADE openly rationed what remained to grid control, hospitals and the administrative assistants in Denmark, Estonia and parts of Spain. Collapsed US build-outs were allowed to fail without a European rescue bid.

That rationing hardened dependence, even as defence improved. A breakthrough in defensive tooling — automated patching at machine speed and detection tuned to swarm behaviour rather than signatures — was pushed through existing NIS2 channels by ENISA and EU-CyCLONe into hospitals, grid operators and municipalities. No new law was needed. Joint isolation drills from the spring containment protocol meant patches were actually installed, and two sabotage attempts on control links in the autumn were isolated in hours rather than days.

The guarded fallback held. Police and gendarmerie patrols stayed on depots, dispersed stocks and published maps. Break-ins continued — fuel drums drained, a telemetry cabinet smashed — and single-source rumours spoke of shared target lists circulating across borders and of construction sites for municipal data centres facing protests and permit freezes in two member states. Nothing synchronized materialized, but operators rebuilt breaker logs by hand after the spring fire and kept black-start points guarded.

The black-start network's completion was formally closed out in December. Essential services now degrade rather than stop, officials claimed, and for once the claim survived contact with events.

Yet the public mood darkened. Waiting-list cuts from the branded European assistants made local news, but quota headlines and vandalism dominated. Brussels, with no money and no new law, had defended the fallback by hand — and everyone could see the hand was still empty.
