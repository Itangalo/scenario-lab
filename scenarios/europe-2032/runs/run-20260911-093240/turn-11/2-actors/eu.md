## In practice
We work with what is already built. ENISA, EU-CyCLONe and the guarded depot network stay on the sabotage-protection footing from H1: police/gendarmerie patrols, dispersed stocks, published maps. We take the one tailwind — the defensive-tooling breakthrough in automated patching and swarm detection — and push it through NIS2 hardening into hospitals, grid operators and municipalities, because it is the first cheap reduction in attack surface we have had in years and needs no new law to deploy.

Externally we do not chase the American investment reset. With Washington on strict volume licences and case-by-case servicing, DG TRADE and the Competitiveness Council ration what access remains to clinics, grid control and the Denmark/Estonia/Spain admin assistants that cut waiting lists, and let collapsed build-outs pass through. No new fund, no new directive is tabled this turn; the common line is held by hand.
