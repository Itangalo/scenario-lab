## New measure
**EU Chip Leverage Compact**
Locks EU upstream chokepoints — lithography, optics, chemicals — into a coordinated export and compute-allocation regime that trades continued supply for allied compute access and joint controls. Why this and why now: the Taiwan blockade makes semiconductors a security question overnight and turns our upstream position into our only hard leverage, while a member-state side-deal threatens to sell it off piecemeal.

## Priority
EU Chip Leverage Compact, because without a common EU line on who gets lithography and who gets compute the blockade fragments us before the Shield can finish.

## In practice
We move on Article 207 and the Anti-Coercion Instrument via the Foreign Affairs Council and Trade Council, mandating Commission screening of lithography and critical spares exports and pooling them with Dutch, German and French licensing authorities. In exchange we demand binding allied quotas for EU Gigafactory compute allocation and joint US-JP-NL export-control alignment, using InvestEU and EIB reflows to bridge fabs waiting on Taiwanese inputs.

We keep the EU Critical Systems Shield as the operational core through ENISA and EU-CyCLONe — swarm detection rollout to TSOs, ports, water and telecoms continues with Digital Europe funds — but we deliberately slow new permitting-zone fights under the Tech sovereignty package to conserve capital, offering defecting capitals a share of secured compute in return for rejoining the common export line. Bio uplift findings are tasked to HERA and ECDC for monitoring only this turn; the response build comes next.
