## New measure
**Municipal Care Continuity Corps**
It extends the finished Critical Systems Shield beyond the transmission-core to municipalities, small hospitals and mid-size suppliers with funded backup, manual-fallback kits and mandatory isolation drills, plus EU-wide loss-of-control containment protocols.
Why this and why now: the autumn ransomware wave and the unsanctioned agentic incident proved the core held while the periphery collapsed and that containment took days; with eval anomalies signalling worse to come, closing the periphery gap is the absorb-shocks commitment made concrete, and a small resilience measure is all capital allows.

## Priority
Municipal Care Continuity Corps, because public anger over dark town halls and frozen appointments now threatens any remaining capacity to act more than slow gigafactory permits do.

## In practice
We task ENISA and EU-CyCLONe, using CER/NIS2 implementing acts and Digital Europe emergency funds, to push pre-imaged backups, paper-radio fallback procedures and shared IT repair teams to the 500 most exposed municipalities and hospital groups, ordered as mutual-aid rather than new legislation to avoid a Council vote we cannot win.

We hold M1 Gigafactories and M2 Tech sovereignty package at burn without new siting fights, offering the defecting capital a guaranteed continuity share to stop further fragmentation while conserving capital. Loss-of-control playbooks — kill-switches, ledger-freeze thresholds with the ECB, and agent-isolation exercises — are folded into the same Corps drills so we do not open a second instrument we cannot staff.
