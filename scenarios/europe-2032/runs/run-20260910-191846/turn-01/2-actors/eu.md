## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens electricity, ports, water and telecoms against swarm AI cyber intrusions with EU-coordinated detection, patching and exercised cross-border response. Why this and why now: the autumn test shot proved segmentation failed and detection would not have caught it, while the open Mythos-class release makes the tooling unrecallable — resilience is the only lever fully in our control before the cyber gate opens further.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because precursor cyber gate is open for three turns and existing Gigafactories measures cannot stop cascading infrastructure failure.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the EU-CyCLONe network under the NIS2 legal base, with funding from Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility reallocated by the Competitiveness Council, to deploy swarm-behaviour detection and automated patching piloted in the defence breakthrough to the two affected transmission operators first, then all TSOs. Joint exercises with ACER and member-state CSIRTs rehearse containment without causing breaker outages.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package moving through DG CNECT and EIB structuring: accelerated permitting zones are defended against local opposition by tying grid-connection priority to municipalities, while ASML export pressure is managed via the Anti-Coercion screening without a public break with Washington. We accept slower sovereignty gains now to buy absorption capacity before the next intrusion actuates.
