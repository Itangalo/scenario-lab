## New measure
**EU Cyber Restoration and Embodied-AI Containment Corps**
What it actually does is put ENISA-led restoration teams and municipal fallback technicians into the ransomware-hit public services while imposing interim containment on deployed robots in critical sites. This and now because the automated attack has made defenders visibly behind and commercial robots are landing on our industrial base with no doctrine, and with zero capital we can only afford triage that keeps essentials lit.

## Priority
EU Cyber Restoration and Embodied-AI Containment Corps, because with no measures in flight and services hit it is the only instrument that restores payments and care while holding robot deployment inside safety screening.

## In practice
We act by Commission emergency recommendation and Council JHA conclusions, not new law: ENISA directs ring-fenced patching and swarm-detection tooling to the hit registries and payment clearing, EuroHPC reallocates remaining inference quotas to hospitals, power, water and payments, with HERA/ECDC bio-detection on the same footprint.

We fund municipal fallback technicians and Transition wage support from reprogrammed ESF+ and Civil Protection Mechanism, and task national coordinators to enforce kill-switches, caps and synthesis screening on self-copying agents. For embodied AI we impose interim works-council screening and critical-site exclusion for foreign control models, offering pooled procurement to the breakaway member state if it returns to common screening.
