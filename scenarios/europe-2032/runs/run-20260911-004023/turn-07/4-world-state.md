### Shells with permits
Autumn brought ribbon-cuttings without machines. The gigafactory shells outside Paris and Warsaw received permits, grid connections and pledges, but accelerator deliveries stayed stalled behind the strait blockade. A third Japanese lot for detection hardware arrived; compute lots did not. The allocation board kept clinics and sequencing labs prioritised, but at reduced throughput queues lengthened again.

Washington's inward turn deepened: new data-centre halts slowed American frontier work further, while Chinese distributors widened humanoid logistics pilots at Rotterdam and Duisburg. Unions staged walkouts over displacement without retraining, and local press linked empty factories to Brussels promises.

### A paper in the wrong journals
A contested pre-print claiming a genome model helped design a human-infective organism circulated through biosecurity lists, then leaked to general press. Methodologists attacked it, editors hesitated, but hospital directors asked HERA what was stocked.

Brussels answered with a containment and sequencing surge: ECDC to expand sentinel-hospital sequencing and wastewater panels, HERA to buy reagents and pre-position isolation stocks, autumn cross-border exercises, and authenticated, logged access for the most capable genome models offered in Europe with refusal of detailed wet-lab uplift queries.

Rollout was uneven. Sentinel sites signed up fast, reagent procurement hit the same transformer and cold-chain delays as other hardware, and universities fought the access controls as a research ban by another name. The evaluation institute's launch-pause mechanism produced no notifications — the model at issue was not served from the Union — and re-tests continued on degraded substitutes queued behind care workloads.

By December, detection coverage was wider on paper than in pipes. Health ministers could point to activity, but with factories still empty, queues lengthening, and the response contested, there was no legitimacy windfall: political standing held flat under the weight of portfolio costs and uneven delivery.
