## In practice
We task ENISA and the EU-CyCLONe network with a Union-wide audit of TSOs, major ports and water utilities hit or exposed, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and CEF-Digital money, with mandatory incident reporting and joint exercises in the Energy and Transport Council formations this winter. National regulators must enforce segmentation and backup protection-relay controls, with France, Germany and the Nordics providing mutual-aid teams to weaker operators.

The Gigafactories (M1) and tech sovereignty package (M2) continue on their permitting and site-selection track via DG CNECT and the EIB, but we offer no new money this turn to hold fiscal headroom. On the clinical reliance review, we use existing procurement directives and the AI Act high-risk provisions to require dual-sourcing assessments for clinical decision-support, without opening a separate instrument now.
