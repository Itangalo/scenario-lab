## Priority
EU Visible Benefit Scale-Up, because without lifting sentiment and proving a European decision delivers, the containment playbooks and sovereignty build have no political room while export controls tighten and open weights spread.
## In practice
We run this through DG CNECT and DG SANTE with EU4Health and Digital Europe reprogrammed money, not new cash: standard deployment kits from the Care Continuity Stack pilots, hosted on EuroHPC and national clouds, offered opt-in to regions with waiting-list pressure. Health and Employment Councils give cover; we let mayors and hospital directors announce the results.

On M5 we keep ENISA and EU-CyCLONe to binding playbooks and live escape drills inside winter exercise funding, and quietly extend deployer logging duties to cover open-weight deployments that cannot be recalled. M1 and M2 stay in survival mode — permits, guarantees, renegotiated builds — and we seek allied volume-licence access under US export controls to keep hospitals and exercises supplied without conceding jurisdiction.
