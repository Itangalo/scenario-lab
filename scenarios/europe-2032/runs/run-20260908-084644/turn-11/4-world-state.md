### Stalled at the Brink

Global AI capability inched upward to 62.5 as frontier labs refined self-directed agent systems, now routinely managing multi-day engineering workflows and regulatory compliance checks with minimal oversight. Yet no corresponding advances in safety frameworks emerged. Internal audits at two leading labs confirmed the persistence of unlogged reasoning pathways—opaque even to their developers—deepening concerns about control and verification.

### The Silence in Brussels

The European Union remained inert. With political capital frozen at 13.0 and public sentiment stuck at 15.0, no new measures were launched. The Commission’s internal assessment of the France-Italy-Austria-Finland compute pact concluded that integration under Article 114 TFEU was legally unworkable without treaty change, and bilateral overtures met resistance. France and Italy insisted on retaining sovereign control, while Austria and Finland rejected any supranational override.

### Crisis of Cohesion

Amid this paralysis, a governance crisis crystallized. Senior officials from France and Germany initiated talks on a "coalition of the willing," proposing to coordinate AI infrastructure and security outside EU institutions. This move, framed as a necessary bypass, threatened to fracture the Union’s strategic coherence further. Meanwhile, the US-China agreement on limited AI risk governance—covering weights security and autonomous escalation—was finalized without EU involvement. European officials were neither consulted nor informed in time to respond, underscoring their diminishing strategic relevance.

### Eroding Foundations

Resilience dipped to 41.0 as the absence of new protective measures left systems vulnerable. A second wave of AI-powered cyber intrusions overwhelmed regional infrastructure in two member states, exposing coordination failures. Protests against AI infrastructure intensified, with sabotage reported at a data center in eastern Poland. The perception of institutional collapse hardened, and the talent drain from European AI programs accelerated, feeding long-term erosion of domestic capability.
