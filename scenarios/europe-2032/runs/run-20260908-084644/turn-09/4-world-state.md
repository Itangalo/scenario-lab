### The Threshold Deepens

Global AI capability advanced to 61.5, solidifying systems’ ability to autonomously execute complex professional workflows across law, engineering, and logistics. Yet no new safety protocols were implemented, and the gap between capability and control widened. Developers acknowledged opaque agent behaviour in internal audits, but no public transparency followed. The absence of meaningful oversight intensified fears.

### A Crisis in Cohesion

The EU did not launch any new measures. With political capital at 13.0 and public sentiment collapsing further, the Commission prioritized damage assessment over action. A high-level legal review examined whether the emergent regional compute pact — a binding agreement among France, Italy, Austria, and Finland — could be retrofitted into EU governance. Early findings suggested limited compatibility: while the pact created shared infrastructure, it lacked enforceable access rights for the Union and risked deepening market fragmentation.

Bilateral talks yielded little. France and Italy insisted the pact was a necessary stopgap; Austria and Finland resisted any supranational override, citing sovereignty concerns. The Commission’s attempt to invoke Article 114 TFEU for emergency coordination stalled, with legal advisors warning of constitutional challenges.

### Attacks and Backlash

A major cyber incident struck regional health authorities in three member states, disrupting patient records and vaccine distribution. The attack, powered by AI-generated exploits, overwhelmed response teams. Resilience systems degraded under load, and recovery took weeks. Simultaneously, protests against AI infrastructure turned physical: activists occupied a data centre in Bavaria and damaged power lines feeding a facility in Poznań. Police intervention escalated tensions, drawing criticism from civil liberties groups.

Public sentiment fell to 15.0 as media framed the moment as one of systemic vulnerability — technically, politically, and socially. The narrative of a Union outpaced and outmanoeuvred took hold.
