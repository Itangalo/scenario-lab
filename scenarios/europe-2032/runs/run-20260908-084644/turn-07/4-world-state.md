### The Weight of Inertia

The EU enters the second half of 2029 in reactive mode. With political capital at 17.0 and public trust eroding, the Commission refrains from launching new initiatives, instead focusing on triage. The formal completion of four major measures — InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty Package, EU Cyber Shield Fund, and the Member-State Compute Compact — brings no celebration. None have delivered at scale.

The Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package collapse under the weight of the global investment downturn; private capital has fled, and no new compute infrastructure is under construction. The Cyber Shield, while technically complete, failed to prevent cascading ransomware attacks, exposing a gap between detection and response. The Member-State Compute Compact, intended as the bedrock of sovereign capacity, is stillborn: without Spain, Belgium, and Lithuania, the network lacks critical mass and geographic resilience.

### Strategic Drift Deepens

An emergency Council session under Article 114 TFEU considers restructuring the Compact as a core-group effort led by France and Italy, possibly with Austria and Finland. But with no fiscal headroom and cohesion funds already stretched, momentum stalls. The bilateral hyperscaler deal by a member state further undermines trust, casting doubt on collective action.

Public sentiment dips to 23.0, driven by disillusionment and fear. Media coverage frames AI as both overhyped and dangerously uncontrolled. The bio_uplift_findings — a credible design for a human-infective organism — stir quiet alarm in security circles, while the annulment of a national election over information manipulation deepens anxiety about systemic fragility.

### Capability Creeps Forward

Global AI capability rises to 59.5, nearing the 60.0 threshold where systems can autonomously execute multi-day professional tasks. Open-weight capability inches up to 48.0, aided by diffusion of prior releases, but remains constrained by lack of frontier-scale training. With no new safety measures landing on deployed systems, and capability advancing, ai_safety drops to 35.0. Resilience declines as cyber defenses prove insufficient, and no new category 6 measure is active.
