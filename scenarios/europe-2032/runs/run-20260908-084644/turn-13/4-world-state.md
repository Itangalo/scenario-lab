### Escalating Harm
A major AI-driven cyberattack disrupted public services across three EU regions, exploiting newly automated vulnerability chaining. The attack’s tooling was model-generated, and its speed overwhelmed response protocols. Defenders were hours behind, and recovery took weeks. The incident underscored the fragility of current defences. Resilience eroded further as coordination gaps between national and EU-level agencies persisted.

### Democratic Erosion
In a landmark decision, a constitutional court annulled regional elections in a founding EU member state, citing credible evidence of AI-driven information manipulation that could have altered the outcome. The ruling sparked mass protests—half the electorate rejected the annulment as elitist overreach, while the other demanded stronger safeguards. The crisis deepened public distrust in both democratic institutions and AI.
