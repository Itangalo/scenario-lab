### The Weight of Delivery

The EU Cyber Shield Fund formally concluded, having equipped over 70% of critical infrastructure operators with AI-powered detection and activated real-time threat sharing. Yet its legacy is overshadowed by the recent ransomware wave, which exploited AI-discovered zero-days to paralyse public services in three member states. Detection worked — but too late. Confidence in digital resilience is shaken, and calls grow for more than reactive hardening.

The Member-State Compute Compact reached its deadline. France and Italy ratified the agreement, committing to jointly governed, EU-controlled compute clusters. But Spain, despite extended repayment terms, withheld final approval amid domestic fiscal scrutiny. Belgium refused to sign without stronger national oversight, and Lithuania tied its ratification to unresolved grid upgrades. Without full buy-in, the project fails to trigger delivery. No construction begins. The promise of sovereign infrastructure remains ungrounded.
