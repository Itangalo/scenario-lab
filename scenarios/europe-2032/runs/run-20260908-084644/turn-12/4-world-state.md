### Drift and Decay

Global AI capability crept upward to 63.0 as frontier labs refined self-directed agent systems, now capable of managing multi-day engineering and compliance workflows with minimal human oversight. Yet no corresponding safety advances emerged. Internal audits at leading labs confirmed persistent unlogged reasoning pathways—opaque even to developers—deepening concerns about control and verification. With no new safeguards, the gap between capability and safety widened, eroding confidence in system integrity.

A fallback model under Article 88 TFEU remained under discussion, but no consensus emerged.

This informal grouping, while framed as temporary, signaled a growing divergence from collective governance. The US-China agreement on limited AI risk governance entered implementation, excluding the EU entirely. European officials were neither consulted nor briefed in time, underscoring strategic irrelevance.
