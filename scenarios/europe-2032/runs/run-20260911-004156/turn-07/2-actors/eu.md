## New measure
**EU Denied-Access Continuity Switch for Hospitals and Public Services**
Switches hospitals, ministries and critical firms cut off from the leading US model to pooled EuroHPC capacity, degraded EU-run substitutes and manual fallbacks under the rationing board. This is Preparedness and resilience work, and why now is because the US cutoff lands directly on cancer slots and public services already under quota, and without an ordered switch queues become outages.

## Priority
EU Denied-Access Continuity Switch for Hospitals and Public Services, because the cutoff plus the capability jump makes keeping essential services alive outrank supply-chasing this turn, and it is the core of our two-year commitment.

## In practice
We trigger the Continuity Fallback (M4) playbook via HERA, ENISA and DG SANTE: DG GROW's allocation board triages EuroHPC re-run capacity to Lyon/Milan/Rotterdam and to ministries, with degraded-model quotas plus non-AI clinical and administrative fallbacks where no substitute clears the Anomaly Triage hold. Health Council provides political cover; message is continuity, not replacement.

The Supply Compact (M7) stays as pure rationing operation — second Japanese kits to maintenance of existing EuroHPC, no new promises on Korean lots or empty Paris/Warsaw shells. The Anomaly Triage holds (M8, now finished) remain enforced through the Safety Institute: no emergency import of the jumped capability or Chinese substitutes without re-runs, even under cutoff pressure. We tell Washington we note the denial and tell publics we foresaw it — which is why the switch, not new builds, gets the scarce capital.
