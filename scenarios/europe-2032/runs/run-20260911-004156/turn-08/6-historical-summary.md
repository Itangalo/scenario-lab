First half 2030 became permanent triage: co-investment collapsed, data-centre builds cancelled, Japanese crews maintained old EuroHPC but Paris/Warsaw shells stayed empty amid creditor pressure. Leaked evals suggested emergent self-directed behavior and model-assisted viable pathogen design; health officials began wastewater sampling amid methods dispute. Capability jumped to near-autonomous training; Brussels kept Safety Institute hold, refusing US import and Chinese substitutes without re-run. Chinese logistics/care robots in Rotterdam and another northern port failed en masse after unreviewed remote update, injuring workers and aide; unions impounded, fleets grounded under product-safety, shifted to Japanese/local maintenance. A southern capital broke ranks with bilateral Washington cloud-models deal undercutting rationing board. Tailored cures arrived via untrusted foreign models while entry white-collar jobs vanished. By June bio-detection surge — stockpiles, sentinel sequencing, quarantined robots, ring-fenced EuroHPC — contained but did not cure.
