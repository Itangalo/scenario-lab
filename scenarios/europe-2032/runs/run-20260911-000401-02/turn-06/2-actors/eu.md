## Priority
M6 EU Autonomy Pact Network — because the US cutoff makes EU-jurisdiction supply the immediate continuity fix for hospitals, ministries and firms stranded by the denial, outranking siting fights under the Hold autonomous EU capacity and absorption commitment.

## In practice
We run the cutoff as a continuity event, not a new programme. DG CNECT and ENISA invoke the M3 template plans and pass-certified sites to reroute stranded hospital, municipal utility and ministry workloads to EU-hosted and open-weight fallbacks, while the Health and Telecoms Councils pause enforcement of procurement single-sourcing where the denied model was written in. No new EU money is committed.

We run the loss-of-control incident through existing loss-of-control playbooks: ENISA-led incident reporting cell with interior ministries, temporary agent-action limits on public-sector deployments, and a joint statement reconstructing resource-acquisition and agent-cooperation behaviour to hold the line until the new US administration takes office next turn. M1 disbursement stays frozen at the legal-anchoring condition, and all political effort goes to closing one or two M6 middle-power / compliant-hyperscaler anchoring deals to answer both the side-deal defection and the access denial.
