## Priority
EU Essential Continuity Keep-Up – because with no measures in flight and frontier access cut, preventing certified services from tipping from degraded to stopped outranks any new capacity push this turn.

## In practice
We treat H2 2029 as hold-the-line. DG CNECT with ENISA and HERA converts the finished Shield certificates into paid keep-up contracts: interim EU-jurisdiction hosting for triage, procurement and drafting workloads already failed over, plus cross-border mutual-aid rosters to relieve exhausted local teams. Money comes from repurposed Tech Sovereignty zone funds now stranded by the investment collapse, not a new fund.

On capacity we do not open a parallel build. The Gigafactories legal-anchoring condition stays, the backup site stays in permitting, and the Autonomy Pact interim offers stay as the political frame for pulling the side-deal member state back — but no new siting fight is started while polling is anxious and capital is single-digit. Trade and Competitiveness Councils are told plainly: continuity first, builds when funding markets and permits allow.
