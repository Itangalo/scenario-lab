## Priority
EU Middle-Power Supply Bargain — because with no capital for a build and interim EU hosting rationed, joint leverage is the only way to hold continuity and keep the two-year commitment alive.

## In practice
We treat H1 2030 as leverage-first. The Commission, via DG TRADE and DG CNECT with the Council Trade formation, mandates alignment of export-licence practice on chips, equipment and spares with coalition partners, and pools EU evaluation and procurement demand into joint bargaining for compute tenancies and cloud relief. No new fund is opened; the legal base is the existing Anti-Coercion and dual-use toolkit plus the finished Autonomy Pact mandates.

On the ground we keep the M7 keep-up contracts running to their finish — EU-server tenancies, parts pools, relief crews for certified hospitals and utilities — while the coalition track seeks replacement hosting and surcharge relief the pact alone could not sign. Finance ministers are told plainly: this substitutes shared leverage for a second rescue we cannot afford, and stalled gigafactory land/grid/legal is held as the contribution we bring, not a build we restart now.
