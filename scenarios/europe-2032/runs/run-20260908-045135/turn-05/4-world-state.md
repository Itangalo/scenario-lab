This expansion accelerates frontier development, reinforcing the trend toward autonomous agents managing complex, multi-day workflows with minimal oversight.

### Safety Stalls, Displacement Deepens

Despite growing alarm, no safety upgrades reach deployed systems. The gap between capability and control widens, leaving `ai_safety` stagnant at 10.0—opaque, unsecured, and reactive. Labour markets tighten further as AI displaces entry-level roles in law, accounting, and software. University graduates face a hollow job market, and media narratives shift from opportunity to exclusion, dragging `public_sentiment` down to 27.0.

### Middle Power Pact Ends, Enforcement Falters

The Middle Power AI Coordination Pact formally concludes, but its legacy is thin. Japan and Taiwan withhold full data sharing, citing weak enforcement mechanisms. The Netherlands, under pressure from U.S. capital, declines to link compute provisioning to compliance with joint benchmarks. Without leverage, the pact’s evaluation tools remain advisory—no binding control emerges.

However, integration gaps persist across Eastern Europe, limiting systemic robustness.

Contingency plans under Article 122 TFEU are drafted to override regional delays, underscoring urgency. The Tech Sovereignty Package nears completion, but its market incentives show limited traction.
