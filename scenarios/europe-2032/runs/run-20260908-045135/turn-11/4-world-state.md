The EU proposes **Secure Enforcement Mandate for Open-Weight Controls**, a small Category 3 measure to empower ENISA to license and block high-risk model components. It includes a €1.2bn operational package and targets EU-based developers and platforms. The proposal is framed as damage control after the Union was abruptly locked out of the leading frontier model — **eu_frontier_access_denied** — which disrupted hospitals and ministries. Despite urgency, the measure faces legal and political resistance. Only partial backing emerges from the Netherlands and France. No full agreement is reached, and the mandate remains unadopted.
