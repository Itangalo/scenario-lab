This quiet acceleration widens the gap between capability and control.

But progress stalls: Japan and Taiwan demand stronger enforcement teeth, while Germany and Sweden remain noncommittal. A quiet defection follows—Netherlands-aligned firms quietly align with U.S. export guidance, undermining the pact’s credibility.

The Cyber Resilience Emergency Programme advances but is not yet complete; phased deployment in Bulgaria and Romania shows improved detection, though full integration remains months away. `resilience` rises to 41.0, reflecting incremental progress rather than full realization.
