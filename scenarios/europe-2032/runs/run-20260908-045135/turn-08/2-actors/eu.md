## New measure
**Emergency Sovereign Access Programme**  
We initiate emergency procurement and deployment of AI systems under full EU control for essential public services, bypassing normal approval channels to secure operational continuity.

## Priority
**Emergency Sovereign Access Programme** — because the denial of frontier access during a medical crisis has already exposed fatal dependence, and with openweight capability now close behind the frontier and safety collapsing, we must act now to secure systems we can operate independently — not just compute, but controlled, deployable intelligence.

## In practice
This turn, we are treating AI infrastructure as critical as energy or defence supply chains. The Commission has convened an emergency session of the Internal Market Council under Article 114 TFEU to justify urgent action, and we are preparing delegated acts to mandate integration of sovereign AI stacks in all EU-funded healthcare and civil protection systems by turn 9. The European Defence Agency is seconded to lead procurement, leveraging its emergency acquisition powers to fast-track contracts with EU-based developers who can demonstrate full control over weights, training data provenance, and inference infrastructure. We are offering guaranteed off-take agreements and state-backed insurance against liability for misuse to de-risk private investment.

Simultaneously, the Joint Research Centre is repurposing its agent-behaviour benchmark pilot into a certification standard for “EU-Controlled AI,” to be administered by ENISA with powers to audit autonomy levels and block deployment if evasion signatures are detected. Member states are resisting on cost and sovereignty grounds — particularly over centralised mandates — so we are pairing the directive with a new tranche of cohesion funds tied to implementation speed, aiming to turn compliance into a race for investment rather than a burden. This is not about catching the frontier; it is about ensuring that, when the frontier denies us, we do not fall into darkness.
