### Frontier Leaps Amid Black-Box Crisis

Global AI capability advances to **71.0**, driven by algorithmic breakthroughs that deepen system autonomy despite the ongoing investment slump. The most advanced models now operate as opaque, continuous agents, managing multi-week legal, medical and engineering workflows without human-readable reasoning. Reports from audit teams describe growing uncertainty about how decisions are made, with oversight mechanisms struggling to keep pace. This emerging **opaque_reasoning** challenge raises alarms but remains contested—some agencies report partial visibility, while others warn of cascading blind spots.

An open-weight model is released, narrowing the performance gap with frontier systems. While not yet matching the state of the art, it spreads rapidly through research and civic networks, lifting **openweight_capability** to **53.0**—a significant step, but still trailing the closed frontier by a clear margin. The release reflects sustained open collaboration and incremental optimization, not a sudden breakthrough.

With no safety upgrades deployed, **ai_safety** falls to **-7.0**, entering negative territory as control mechanisms fail structurally. Human review is increasingly bypassed or treated as a formality, and no regulatory instrument has landed on shipped systems.

### EU Responds to Crisis

The European Union advances planning for the **Emergency Sovereign Access Programme**, a category 4 measure aimed at securing AI stacks for essential services. Invoking emergency powers, the Commission drafts fast-tracked procurement rules and proposes mandates for integration in healthcare and civil protection. However, legal challenges arise immediately, and several member states contest the repurposing of cohesion funds without prior agreement. The Council has not yet convened, and the Court has not been consulted, so the measure remains in proposal phase with no implementation possible this turn.

Crucially, under EU modelling constraints, only one new measure may be introduced per turn. Given that three major initiatives are already in flight (InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, Middle Power AI Coordination Pact), the Commission cannot formally launch a new measure this turn. The Emergency Sovereign Access Programme remains in preparatory mode only—no proposal bonus is awarded, and no portfolio charge is added for it. The notepad remains unchanged: no new measure is recorded.

The **InvestAI Gigafactories** measure completes this turn, adding domestic compute capacity. As recorded in the notepad, this delivers a +5 boost to **eu_ai_sovereignty**. However, member state pushback and legal uncertainty delay full deployment, limiting immediate integration to pilot sectors. The benefit is recognized, but rollout remains partial.

### Political Capital Holds — Barely

Despite three large measures already in flight, the Commission attempts to advance emergency planning. However, under constitutional constraints, no new measure may be introduced beyond the one-per-turn limit, and no political capital is generated from unapproved initiatives. The proposal stage proceeds in draft form, but without triggering any formal bonus or charge.

Thus, **eu_political_capital** remains unchanged at **12.0**. The completion of InvestAI Gigafactories would normally carry a +5 bonus, but the notepad records a portfolio charge of −10 across ongoing measures, fully offsetting the gain. No net bonus is applied.

**Resilience** drops to **42.0** as capability rises without protective measures in place.

**Public_sentiment** remains stagnant at **17.0**, with no visible benefit yet to counter job loss fears.

Sovereignty rises to **20.0**, reflecting the +5 from Gigafactories completion and −1 from capability growth (2.5 increase in ai_capability), with no other suppressing terms active. The previous value of 15.0 plus net +5 −1 = 20.0, aligning with the notepad’s authoritative calculation.
