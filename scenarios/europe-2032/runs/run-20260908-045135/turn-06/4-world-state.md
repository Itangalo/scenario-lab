### Frontier Stumbles, But Still Advances

Despite the **ai_investment_collapse**, which forces major labs to scale back training runs and cancel data centre expansions, global AI capability advances to **68.5**. The momentum of prior investment and algorithmic efficiency gains carries the frontier forward, though at a reduced pace. Autonomous agents now routinely manage multi-week legal discovery and clinical audit cycles, embedding deeper into institutional workflows.

### Open Weights Gain Ground Amid Fragmentation

With leading labs distracted by capital constraints, a decentralized network of open researchers and smaller collectives makes significant progress in model replication and fine-tuning. Evidence emerges of coordinated efforts to reconstruct key training pipelines using publicly available data and distributed compute. While no single release matches frontier scale, the cumulative effect lifts **openweight_capability** to **50.0**, reflecting broader diffusion and growing technical momentum. A prototype based on a prior frontier model shows strong performance in early testing, but full replication remains incomplete and resource-intensive.

### Safety Erodes Amid Crisis

No safety upgrades reach deployment. The **opaque reasoning** of frontier systems persists, and the **capability_jump** from autonomous agents triggers a sharp drop in **ai_safety** to **5.0**. Human oversight is increasingly symbolic, and no regulatory instrument has landed on shipped systems.

Job losses in mid-level cognitive roles accelerate, feeding a growing **labour displacement wave**. Media coverage turns hostile, and **backlash_physical** erupts: protesters occupy a data centre site in Portugal, and a substation serving an AI cluster in northern France is sabotaged. **Public sentiment** plummets to **17.0**, reflecting widespread anger and fear.
