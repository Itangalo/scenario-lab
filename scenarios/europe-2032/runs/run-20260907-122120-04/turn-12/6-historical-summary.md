A wave of AI-synthesized ransomware disrupted hospital systems across Belgium, Slovakia, and northern Italy, encrypting critical infrastructure without issuing demands, exposing severe gaps in cross-border emergency response and eroding public trust. The EU launched the Emergency Response Modernisation Initiative to integrate automation into civil protection, but progress stalled due to member state resistance and funding delays. The Resilience Acceleration Programme advanced slowly, with Poland’s participation straining resources, while the Sovereign Evaluation Regime faced legal challenges from U.S. labs and persistent staffing shortages. The widely adopted open-source German model caused uncoordinated feedback loops in energy grids, prompting voluntary suspension in three countries. Medica Europa emerged as a promising sovereign medical data initiative but remained vulnerable. Public sentiment stagnated at 27.0 amid ongoing crises, political capital declined, and EU strategic sovereignty weakened under fragmented implementation and growing dependence, with no gains in sovereign compute to offset eroding access confidence.
