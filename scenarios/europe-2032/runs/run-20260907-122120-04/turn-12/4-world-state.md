### The Paralysis After the Storm

A wave of AI-synthesised ransomware swept through hospital systems in Belgium, Slovakia, and northern Italy, encrypting emergency dispatch logs and disabling scheduling infrastructure for over 36 hours. No demand was made — only disruption. The attack, adaptive and polymorphic, overwhelmed legacy defences and exposed critical lags in cross-border coordination. National civil protection agencies struggled to classify the incident, let alone respond. Public trust eroded further as officials offered conflicting updates.

In response, the EU launched the *Emergency Response Modernisation Initiative*, aiming to integrate automated triage and real-time communication into civil protection frameworks. But the ambition outpaced capacity. DG ECHO faced resistance from member states guarding operational autonomy, and funding debates delayed deployment timelines. The measure began, but its impact remains distant.

The *Resilience Acceleration Programme* continued, with energy and healthcare providers submitting preliminary hardening plans. However, Poland’s participation came at a cost: demands for additional evaluation infrastructure diverted resources, slowing progress elsewhere. Meanwhile, the *Sovereign Evaluation Regime* advanced technically, yet U.S. labs filed injunctions challenging its extraterritorial reach, and recruitment stalled amid fierce private-sector competition.

Worse, the open-source German model — now widely deployed in municipal grids and legal aid tools — contributed to uncoordinated feedback loops in regional energy networks. Regulators issued urgent advisories, calling a voluntary pause in three countries. Assurance could not keep pace with adoption.

Medica Europa, the new medical data pool, gained quiet support from regional governments eager for sovereign AI in healthcare. Yet without immediate protection, it remains exposed — a symbol of promise in a landscape of fragility.

Public sentiment held at 27.0, anchored by visible harm and bureaucratic inertia. Political capital dwindled under the weight of overlapping crises and an overstretched agenda.

### Sovereignty Erodes

Despite growing efforts, the EU’s strategic position weakened. Advancing capability and fragmented implementation deepened dependence, while no sovereign compute gains offset the loss of access confidence.
