## New measure
**EU Municipal Resilience Hardening Fund**
Extends the Shield that worked on grids/ports to hospitals, municipalities and mid-size industry with funded cyber-hardening, backup inference, and exercised recovery playbooks.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, because open-weight capability at 53 means distributed offensive cyber cannot be recalled, safety assurance at 21 cannot stop it, and the only lever fully in EU control that serves the two-year commitment to absorb shocks is making essential services degrade rather than stop.

## In practice
We keep M5 as the operational lead this turn: DG CNECT/DIGIT fund inference vouchers on EuroHPC and EU clouds via reprogrammed Digital Europe/EU4Health money, AI Office issues provisional certifications for open-weight substitutes with M4 doing rapid cyber-safety checks, and we hold health/interior ministers to the December fallback mandate. M1/M2 are kept alive but on low burn — protect permitting for the two viable sites, no new disbursement promises until power-price and funding reset clarifies.

We launch the hardening fund via UCPM/Cybersecurity Act Article and NIS2 implementing acts: joint procurement of detection/backup stacks for municipalities and hospitals, Athens-run exercises expanded beyond energy/ports, and repair crews on retainer. Cohesion and RRF-reflow money pays first wave; reluctant finance and energy ministries are bought with co-funding and avoided-compensation logic from the October-November contained incidents.
