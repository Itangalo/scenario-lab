## New measure
**EU Continuity Stack for Public Services**
Keeps hospitals, ministries and firms running after the US frontier cut-off by switching them to EU-hosted open-weight and allied models on Union compute, and scales the public-sector adoption win that just landed.
This is Diffusion, adoption and public trust work, because the denial is a legitimacy shock that only visible continuity plus visible benefit can contain, and the investment collapse means we must do it with capacity we already control rather than promised hyperscaler build-out.

## Priority
EU Continuity Stack for Public Services, not the Gigafactories, because the cut-off is hitting wards and ministries now and without continuity both sovereignty lines lose political permission to continue.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT and DG SANTE with a continuity mandate under the Health Emergency and NIS2 legal bases: EU4Health and Digital Europe funds reprogrammed to pay for inference on EuroHPC and existing commercial EU-cloud capacity, with the AI Office certifying substitute models from the Evaluation Cell pipeline for clinical and administrative use. Member-state health and interior ministers get a TTE/ EPSCO joint commitment: Brussels pays migration costs if they mandate EU-hosted fallback by December.

We use the adoption success explicitly — the waiting-list and permit-time wins — as the communications and procurement template, extending those contracts to the fallback stack rather than starting new tenders. M1/M2 stay on permitting track but without new cash this turn given the investment reset; we protect the two unblocked sites and pause disbursement expectations elsewhere. The Evaluation Cell (M4) gets first call on testing the substitute models for cyber safety before rollout.
