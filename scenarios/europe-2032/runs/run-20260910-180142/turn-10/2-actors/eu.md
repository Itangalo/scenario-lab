## In practice
We keep the AI Office evaluation cell as the single gate: publish the dose and triage error cases, hold the freeze on unvalidated models on EU-hosted hardware, and graduate only tools that pass supervised pilots with manual fallback and physician co-oversight. ENISA and the cyber-bio pact telemetry feed the monitoring, and the Municipal Resilience Fund keeps manual triage funded so hospitals are not forced back onto unvalidated assistants.

In Council we use the middle-power coordination framework now on offer to bargain jointly for assured compute access and shared evaluation capacity, without ceding sovereignty to it — export-licence alignment and joint bargaining as cover while our gigafactory foundations are still being poured. With the open-weight frontier now within points of the closed frontier and unrecallable on private hardware, we do not chase recall; we harden re-entry criteria against models whose reasoning auditors cannot read.
