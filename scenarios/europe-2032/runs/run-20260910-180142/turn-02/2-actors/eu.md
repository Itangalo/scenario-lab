## New measure
**EU Frontier Evaluation Cell**
Stands up an independent EU technical evaluation capacity with pre-deployment access to models running on EU compute to test cyber and agent capabilities after the verifiable-domains jump.
This is Public technical capacity and research work, because detection standards alone cannot tell us what the new code/cyber advance can do until we can test it ourselves.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Shield, not the new Cell, because the capability jump immediately raises what an attacker can do to grids/ports already found compromised.

## In practice
We are driving the Shield into operation via the TTE Council and ENISA: transposing detection and 24-hour reporting into national CER/NIS2 orders, running the first cross-border exercise round, and disbursing CEF/Digital Europe reprogrammed funds for SOC upgrades and repair teams. Interior ministries get EU-paid analysts in exchange for accepting audit.

We seed the Evaluation Cell inside the AI Office with JRC and ENISA staff, funded by a small Horizon/Digital Europe reallocation, with access deals tied to Gigafactory compute: any large model seeking subsidised EU compute grants evaluation access. Gigafactories (M1) and tech sovereignty zones (M2) stay on DG CNECT/DG ENER permitting tracks — we unblock the two slipped sites with grid/water packages — but they do not get the political push this turn.
