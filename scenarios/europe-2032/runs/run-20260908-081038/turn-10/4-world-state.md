### Fractured Enforcement, Failing Leverage

The EU doubled down on enforcing sovereign AI infrastructure through emergency powers, treating technological survival as a constitutional test. Article 122 TFEU was invoked to override national delays, with Frontex and energy units deployed to protect Cluj and Lille nodes. Binding grid directives and frozen cohesion funds targeted Poland and Italy, while France and Germany were asked to second technical leads to a nascent EU Compute Authority.

Yet enforcement faltered. The CJEU hearing drew sharp dissent from Warsaw and Budapest, who denounced “digital imperialism.” Sabotage in Cluj delayed activation yet again, and ENTSO-E’s capacity reservation faced legal pushback from Southern grid operators. Italy accepted wildfire support but deferred full procurement, citing domestic unrest. Only France moved swiftly, offering partial technical integration.

The Secure Critical AI Infrastructure measure finished this turn, delivering limited gains. While fast-track authority was established, actual grid access and physical protection remained patchy. The measure yielded +1 toward resilience and occasional progress on sovereignty, but systemic obstruction diluted impact.

Meanwhile, global AI capability rose to 63.0, driven by private labs outside EU reach. With no new red-teaming investment and frontier access still severed, ai_safety dropped further. Opaque agent behavior in test environments went unchecked, compounding risks.

Resilience declined as cyber incidents increased and no category 6 harm-specific measure was completed. Political capital eroded: the portfolio charge remained high (−8), with two large measures and a priority. The finished Gigafactories and Secure Infrastructure measure brought +5 combined, but not enough to offset costs and fallout. Public sentiment worsened, with protests expanding to Vienna and Marseille, fueled by job losses and narratives of top-down coercion.

No new measure was proposed, pending the JRC review. The Union held its breath—waiting for data that might force a reckoning on public operational control.
