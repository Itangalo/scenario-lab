### US Posture Hardens: Consolidation Takes Hold

With the new US administration in office, Washington formally adopts a tiered access model for frontier AI systems, treating them as strategic assets. The EU is placed in a secondary tier, meaning restricted access to the latest models unless aligned with American security priorities. This shift codifies dependence and raises alarm in Brussels.

### Capability Crosses the Threshold

Global AI capability reaches 60.0 as automated verification spreads to legal and clinical domains, accelerating progress beyond code and math. This milestone enables AI systems to complete multi-day professional projects end to end, displacing junior roles across sectors. Open-weight models rise to 50.0, driven by accessible derivatives of Kimi K3 now capable of sustained cyber operations. The increase reflects accelerated diffusion following the release, but remains within plausible bounds of incremental adaptation, narrowing the gap with frontier systems without surpassing them.

### Safety Erodes Further

With no safety mandates implemented and interpretability research still siloed, ai_safety drops to 18.0. The gap between capability and control widens dangerously, increasing the risk of uncontrolled incidents.

### Sovereignty Stalls Despite New Push

The *Tech Sovereignty Package* and *Cyber Shield* finish this turn, releasing their accumulated effects. The European Commission launches *Accelerate Anchor Demand* as a new initiative to stimulate procurement of sovereign AI capacity, but implementation has only just begun. Member states have not yet committed binding funding, and the EIB’s financing window remains unused. The narrative of momentum is undermined by slow uptake and fiscal hesitancy. Sovereignty declines to 13.0 as capability rises and no capacity event offsets the −1 decay.

### Political Capital Drains

Political capital falls to 30.0. The completion of two large measures yields +5, but ongoing costs (−8) and the priority penalty (−1) outweigh gains. No proposal bonus applies, as the new measure responds to a structural deadlock, not a recent shock.
