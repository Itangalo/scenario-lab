### Steady Climb, Widening Gap

Global AI capability advanced to 56.0 as frontier labs completed another wave of scaled training runs, narrowing in on the 60 threshold. Open-weight models rose in tandem to 44.0, driven by wider deployment of Kimi K3-based systems and growing accessibility of fine-tuned variants. Despite this, no breakthrough in interpretability or control frameworks emerged, and the opacity of leading models deepened. With no new safety evaluation regime in place, and internal red-teaming efforts still informal, ai_safety eroded further, dropping to 27.0.

### Cyber Shield Becomes Law

The EU formally adopted the *Cyber Shield for Critical Operators* directive, finalizing technical standards and launching ENISA’s central monitoring framework. A pilot involving Germany, Poland, and Spain demonstrated functional coordination, and fast-track consultation mechanisms were activated in Slovakia and Croatia. While not all concerns were resolved, the directive cleared its final hurdles, marking a rare policy success. Member states now begin implementation, with enforcement set to start in Q1 2028.

### Sovereignty Efforts Stumble On

The *Tech Sovereignty Package* remained stalled, despite high-level envoys and revised permitting guidance. Local resistance in Finland and Bulgaria persisted, and the European Investment Bank’s fast-track window saw no takers. The Joint Research Centre’s demand forecast was delayed, feeding uncertainty about whether public and industrial sectors could absorb future capacity. The *InvestAI Gigafactories* initiative also failed to select sites, with national governments hesitant to commit anchor demand.

### Political Capital Rebounds Slightly

With *Cyber Shield* completed, the EU gained a +4 boost to political capital. The portfolio charge for three large measures (−9) and the priority cost (−1) were offset by this gain. No new measure was launched, avoiding further strain. Public sentiment held steady, as modest progress was overshadowed by ongoing concerns over sovereignty and dependence.
