## Priority

## In practice  
This turn, we launch the legislative process for Cyber Shield under Article 114 TFEU, leveraging the NIS2 directive as legal basis and routing implementation through ENISA and the CSIRT network. The Council’s Internal Market and Telecommunications formation will lead, with the Commission’s Joint Research Centre drafting technical standards by February 2027. Resistance from smaller member states centers on cost and sovereignty concerns — we are offering 70% co-financing from the Digital Europe Programme and a fast-track decision channel in the CSIRT for EU-level alerts. The first pilot exercises will run in Q2 2027 with Germany, Poland, and Spain, simulating multi-vector AI-driven intrusions across energy and water systems. This is not just compliance: it is the first time detection capability will be stress-tested across borders with shared escalation protocols. Without it, even the best compute base is exposed.
