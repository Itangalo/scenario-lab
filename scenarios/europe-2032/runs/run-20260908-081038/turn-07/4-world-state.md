Without warning, the EU loses access to the most advanced AI models. Hospitals relying on them for diagnostics, ministries processing migration flows, and energy grid operators coordinating supply find their systems abruptly offline. Washington offers no explanation, invoking national security protocols under its Consolidation posture. The incident confirms long-standing fears: Europe’s critical functions depend on foreign goodwill.
