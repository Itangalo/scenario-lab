## In practice  
This turn, the Commission finalises the Cyber Shield directive’s technical standards, with ENISA activating the first tier of central alert coordination among national CSIRTs. The fast-track consultation mechanism is now operational, with Slovakia and Croatia participating in shaping escalation protocols — a small but meaningful step toward integrated cross-border response. The directive is law, and enforcement begins in Q1 2028, co-financed at 70% through the Digital Europe Programme.

Simultaneously, we intensify pressure on the Tech Sovereignty Package. High-level envoys are dispatched to Helsinki, Sofia, and Dublin to resolve permitting delays, backed by a revised guidance note from DG ENER and DG GROW affirming AI data centres as strategic infrastructure under the revised TEN-E regulation. The European Investment Bank’s fast-track window for grid upgrades is formally activated, offering pre-approved financing to accelerate site readiness. At the same time, we commission the Joint Research Centre to deliver a binding forecast on public and industrial compute demand by Q3, to anchor future investment decisions in evidence rather than ambition. InvestAI Gigafactories remains on track, but we are now demanding that national governments — particularly in defence, energy, and health — signal anchor demand to de-risk private capital. Without it, no site selection will proceed.
