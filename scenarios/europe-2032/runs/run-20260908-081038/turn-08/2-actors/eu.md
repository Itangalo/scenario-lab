## New measure  
**Secure Critical AI Infrastructure Against Sabotage and Non-Compliance**  
Establishes EU-level authority to fast-track grid access, override national permitting delays, and deploy Civil Protection Mechanism resources to protect and complete sovereign AI infrastructure, with penalties for obstruction.  

`Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 10`  
`Targeted effect:` Increases eu_ai_sovereignty by ensuring deployed compute is energized and secured; reduces vulnerability to sabotage and political defection; supports resilience by guaranteeing continuity of critical AI services.  
`Applies to:` Member states, national regulators, energy operators, and EU infrastructure agencies.

## Priority  
**Secure Critical AI Infrastructure Against Sabotage and Non-Compliance** — because the completion of InvestAI Gigafactories means nothing if the compute stays dark, and because the sabotage in Romania and legal obstruction by Poland and Hungary prove that national-level resistance is now the primary barrier to sovereignty, not lack of funding or technology.

## In practice  
This turn, the Commission triggers Article 122 TFEU on grounds of a severe technological crisis affecting essential services, enabling emergency powers to override national permitting and redirect energy allocation for critical AI infrastructure. The Civil Protection Mechanism is activated to secure grid connections and data centre sites, with Frontex and EUCAP contributing surveillance and rapid response units where local law enforcement is compromised or overwhelmed. The Connecting Europe Facility is repurposed to fast-track transmission upgrades for Cluj, Lille, and Pisa nodes, with DG ENER issuing binding directives to TSOs. Simultaneously, the Council is convened under the new crisis clause of the Digital Decade framework to adopt sanctions: any member state blocking verified anchor procurement or sabotaging infrastructure will face suspension of cohesion funding *and* exclusion from EU-wide AI service sharing under the Cyber Shield and Health Emergency frameworks.  

We are negotiating directly with ENTSO-E and Eurelectric to pre-commit transmission capacity for sovereign AI loads, backed by EIB liquidity guarantees. In parallel, the JRC is finalising a real-time monitoring dashboard to track procurement contracts, grid readiness, and sabotage incidents — data that will feed into quarterly enforcement reviews by the newly empowered Digital Sovereignty Board. France and Germany have pledged national guard units to protect key sites; we are offering Spain and Italy fast-tracked access to joint EU agent platforms for wildfire and migration response in return for full compliance. The message is clear: sovereignty is not optional, and no member state will be allowed to veto the Union’s survival.
