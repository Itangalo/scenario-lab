## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens transmission grids, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion via mandatory segmentation audits, 24h detection upgrades and cross-border incident exercises. Why this and why now: the autumn grid mapping proved intruders lived inside EU networks for weeks using open-model tooling, and without immediate hardening the next probe will not leave quietly — Taiwan tensions also raise sabotage risk over the next 18 months.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because mapped grids and rising Taiwan escalation mean a preventable blackout would destroy capital for gigafactories and evaluation alike.

## In practice
We task ENISA and ACER with emergency segmentation and logging audits of the two affected TSOs plus interconnected operators, funded by a small repurposed CEF-Digital envelope, with mandatory reporting to the AI Office to feed evaluation of offensive tooling. Council Energy and TTE are asked for a six-month mandate for joint EU-US-Asia forensics sharing without public attribution.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories site selection moving via DG CNECT/EIB — pressing Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm, Warsaw for grid pledges ring-fenced from Shield hardening — and we staff the Evaluation Institute's first red-team cohort at the JRC to audit weight security and link grid-tooling signatures to frontier-model capabilities.
