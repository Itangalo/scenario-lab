## New measure
**EU Graduate Transition Guarantee**
Stand up wage insurance, paid retraining and employer-tied transition contributions for entry-level law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration roles displaced by AI, paired with a sovereign continuity fallback for hospitals and ministries cut off from the leading model. Why this and why now: hiring has frozen where offices once boasted gains and US frontier access was just withdrawn, so displacement and dependence land together and must be answered as one shock.

## Priority
EU Graduate Transition Guarantee, because unrecallable open capability plus sudden frontier cut-off plus frozen graduate hiring outranks new builds — it holds the two-year commitment by keeping sovereign capacity usable under European control while absorbing harm first.

## In practice
We run this through EPSCO and the Competitiveness Council on an Article 175/ESF+ legal base: DG EMPL stands up the Guarantee with income bridging and retraining vouchers for displaced juniors, funded by ESF+ reprogramming plus a transition levy on large automating employers, with hiring incentives for SMEs that retain entry roles. Unions and university cities are the veto players; we buy them with fast money in this semester, not promises.

For the access denial we do not start a parallel compute programme. We switch affected hospitals, ministries and firms onto the four now-operational gigafactory sites and Compact-backed capacity, with ENISA and the Joint Cyber Command providing hardened open-weight fallback stacks, patched baselines and playbooks from the finished Distributed Capability Shield. DG CNECT enforces the switch via procurement preference, while we publicise the adoption-success waiting-list wins to show European-controlled AI delivering, and log the breakthrough result as evidence our finished research capacity pays.
