## New measure
**EU Trusted Adoption Accelerator**
Scales the proven public-sector AI wins — shorter waits, faster decisions — EU-wide on EU-controlled, safety-certified models via joint procurement and deployment support. Why this and why now: the visible adoption success plus a real deployable safety breakthrough gives us a rare window to tie public benefit to sovereign capacity before a member-state side-deal redefines adoption as buying American.

## Priority
InvestAI Gigafactories, because the member-state side-deal undercuts the Union line and only visibly advancing EU-anchored capacity restores bargaining power — adoption pull means nothing without supply to point it at.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT and DG REFORM to run the Accelerator through the Digital Europe Programme and RRF repurposing: EU AI Office certifies the breakthrough control result into a deployable assurance badge, and only badge-holding systems hosted on EU soil qualify for the joint procurement vehicle. Health and Competitiveness Councils co-sponsor to give cover.

In parallel we hold the Gigafactory line via the Competitiveness Council: Madrid and Warsaw dual-use substations become the template, EIB guarantees are re-scoped to grid-connection rather than full build, and the defecting capital is offered re-entry on Union terms — EU data residency and evaluation access — rather than punished. The Incident Reporting Mandate drafting continues in JHA/CNCT trilogue, with the new safety result written into the technical trace standard so reporting actually carries assurance data.
