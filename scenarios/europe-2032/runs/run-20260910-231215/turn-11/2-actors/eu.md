**EU Inference Siting and Grid Protection Pact**

Why this and why now: coordinated blockades in two member states directly threaten the continuity commitment, and without legitimate siting the reserves and overflow contracts have nowhere to land.

## Priority
EU Inference Siting and Grid Protection Pact, because the two-year commitment to keep essential services on European-controlled AI fails if reserved capacity cannot be sited and connected — chips in warehouses do not run hospitals.

## In practice
We run this through DG ENER and DG CNECT with ENTSO-E and the national TSOs, using TEN-E and Digital Europe reprogramming plus the completed Continuity Reserve as bridge funding: mayors get a formal 90-day siting review with safety audits published, host municipalities get grid-fee rebates and apprenticeship quotas from the Graduate Guarantee, and the two blocked sites get temporary protected-connection status with joint police-cyber patrols agreed in JHA Council. Industry trades accelerated grid connection for accepting the review; unions get a written guarantee that night-cover remains voluntary and paid.

On the breakthrough: this turn's advance was European — a Grenoble-Munich team using agentic lab models closed a decade-old solid-electrolyte interface problem, demonstrating an AI-designed coating that doubles solid-state battery cycle life in pilot cells. Specialist significance is real — it solves dendrite propagation that had organised the field — but industrial reach is two turns out, requiring scale-up. We use it as evidence the finished Gigafactory and Evaluation lines produced something: ministers show hospital queues down and a battery breakthrough made in Europe to hold sentiment while freight rates climb, without claiming sovereignty we do not yet have.
