### The Shock of Sudden Leaps

A discontinuous advance in AI capability lands with force: developers demonstrate a system that autonomously designs, simulates, and validates novel protein structures at scale, compressing a process once measured in years to days. This leap pushes `ai_capability` sharply upward by +3.0 to 74.0, marking the first true capability jump since 2028. The closed frontier now operates in domains requiring deep scientific reasoning, though transparency remains minimal.

### Safety in Freefall

With no matching safeguards embedded in deployed systems—and the jump widening the gap between power and understanding—`ai_safety` plummets by −12. Internal reports from labs reveal that even developers can no longer fully interpret how the new models reach key conclusions. Human oversight is now routinely outpaced.

### Emergency Response Under Fire

The initiative delivers early gains: resilience rises +4 from immediate fixes, though legacy fragility limits total impact. Public sentiment ticks up +5 as visible recovery efforts gain media traction—framed not as triumph, but as overdue correction.
