Regulators note inconsistencies in auditability, but no new incidents directly attributable to safety failures are reported—just mounting unease. The drop is modest and consistent with friction under pressure, not systemic collapse.

### Crisis Triggers Emergency Measures  
A major ransomware attack—potentially AI-facilitated—disrupts regional health systems and transport hubs across three member states. Initial analysis suggests AI may have accelerated reconnaissance and payload generation, though human coordination remains central. Legacy defences struggle, and response coordination is patchy, prompting an urgent EU-level reaction.

The European Commission proposes the **Cyber Shield Expansion**, invoking emergency procedures to fast-track development of a real-time, AI-augmented defence network under ENISA. The framework aims to mandate integration for energy, health, and transport sectors and deploy EU-trained defensive agents for anomaly correlation and containment. However, full implementation is not immediate: technical integration, cross-border data sharing, and procurement delays mean operational capacity will build gradually. The measure is formally introduced this turn but will take multiple turns to ramp up.

To support sovereign capacity, the **Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive** is finalized, designating 30% of frontier compute for public use. However, execution faces constraints: no new funding is allocated, and access depends on negotiated access to existing commercial infrastructure rather than new procurement. Some providers resist, citing contractual obligations, limiting immediate availability. As partial credit is granted for completion, `eu_ai_sovereignty` rises by +2 to 27.0, reflecting constrained delivery. The **Sovereign Model Development Initiative** continues with the EAIRC consortium formalized, but no capability gain is registered—`openweight_capability` remains at 45.5.

The EU designates **Cyber Shield Expansion** as its current priority, suspending forward momentum on the **Tech Sovereignty Package** and limiting the **Sovereign Model Development Initiative** to maintenance mode. Only one major measure is active, satisfying the one-measure limit.
