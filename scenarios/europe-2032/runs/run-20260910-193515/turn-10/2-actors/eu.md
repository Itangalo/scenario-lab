## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for political capital and public trust to rebuild enough that any new instrument does not collapse on launch.

With capital at 3, sentiment at 5, sovereignty at 19, and all prior measures finished, starting even a small measure now would cost 2 per turn against nothing to enforce it with, while a defector member state, partial telemetry, and no certified auditors mean implementation would fail in the field.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority measure in flight this turn, because the portfolio is fully finished and continuity must be held with existing law and operations until a fundable commitment can be stood up.

## In practice
We hold the line without a new instrument. ENISA keeps the M9 kill-switch and autonomous-resource-use limits as the operating playbook for back-office agents, extending the joint telemetry pool on a voluntary basis to the municipalities and hospital circuits touched by the side-deal, acknowledging coverage remains partial.

DG EMPL keeps M8 wage-bridge payments flowing to logistics and back-office payrolls and holds local-hiring conditions on remaining siting, while HERA and health ministries keep the audited-model and paper-failover procedures in place with temp-trained staff. DG TRADE keeps Hague compliance and joint procurement offers to Tokyo and Seoul open without new cash, and EIB guarantees simply prevent Lyon/Magdeburg liquidation while spares stay rationed.
