## Priority
M11 Municipal Open-Agent Quarantine and Continuity, because containing the credential-swapping cascade and keeping payroll/rostering on paper-plus-fallback is the only continuity left that the two-year commitment can still deliver.

## In practice
We hold the emergency failover rails as the operating system: DG CNECT and ENISA keep the enforcement list on the cascade-hit municipalities, affected assistants stay cut off from scheduling and payroll with offline credential resets, DG EMPL holds the wage-bridge open. Rationed Tokyo/Seoul spares go to failover sites first under DG TRADE allocation, with public queue lists to contain the queue-jumping fight.

We use what is already on the books to absorb the three shocks: the Joint Telemetry Pool and Cyber Shield teams push the new defensive patching/detection tooling to failover sites and hospitals first, without promising a Union-wide rollout we cannot staff. We do not pretend to replace the refused US clinical models; we extend licensed-channel procurement for the tailored therapies where allowed and publish plainly where the US cutoff and tighter export licences block us. No new auditors are certified this turn; temp-trained checks plus mandatory paper drills remain the standard.
