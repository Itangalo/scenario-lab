## New measure
**EU Automation Transition Safety Net**
Creates an EU-cofunded wage-bridge, retraining entitlement and municipal support package for workers displaced by AI-driven automation, administered via national schemes. Why this and why now: with sentiment at 16 and hostility turning physical, no coalition or compute build survives without showing voters continuity means income, and this buys the political room to hold the middle-power play.
