## In practice
We put ENISA and the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority in joint lead under NIS2 and the Critical Entities Resilience Directive, with the Energy and Health Council formations carrying emergency failover orders: affected hospitals and ministries get EU-hosted detection and assistant models from the Cyber Shield procurement, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health money, with mutual-aid conditionality to hold interior ministries and the defecting member state in line.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package alive through the investment collapse by protecting permitting-zone designations and grid-connection priority in DG CNECT and DG ENER, shifting from private hyperscaler build-out to public-anchored financing via EIB, while opening Anti-Coercion Instrument screening on the separate bilateral deal to raise its cost without breaking cohesion outright. No new compute vehicle is created; the Continuity Mandate creates the demand that justifies the gigafactories surviving the valuation reset.
