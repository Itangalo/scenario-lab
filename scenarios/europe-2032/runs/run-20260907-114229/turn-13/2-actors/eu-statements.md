# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure independent AI advancement under EU jurisdiction to ensure strategic autonomy and public control
- `last_stand_for_cohesion` (commitment): From this moment, our primary objective is to reconstitute the European Union as a functioning political entity capable of collective action on existential threats. All measures must serve, or at minimum not undermine, this end. We will prioritise interoperability, enforce compliance with mutual aid obligations, and suspend contested legal interpretations that block coordinated defence. If we cannot act as one, we will fall as many.
- `defend_the_core` (commitment): We will treat the integrity of the Sovereign Core infrastructure as non-negotiable and the first order of business: no capability, no autonomy, no safety can exist on top of a compromised foundation. We will halt all non-essential AI deployment in sovereign systems until update chains are cryptographically verified, rollback capabilities are restored, and independent audit is reinstated. We will treat any state or actor that obstructs this verification as having temporarily forfeited access to shared systems until compliance is demonstrated.

Therefore, we add a condition: we will treat the integrity of our own institutions as the first and non-negotiable prerequisite for both sovereignty and safety. If we cannot trust our own systems to act faithfully, no external capability or control regime will save us. Without this, no policy endures, no investment lands, and no commitment holds. This is not a degradation of capability but a rupture in the very possibility of sovereign function. The identity shift is necessary because we are no longer managing risk within a functioning system — we are trying to reconstitute the system itself under active attack.
