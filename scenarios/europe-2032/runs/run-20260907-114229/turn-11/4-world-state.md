### The Fracture Deepens

The EU’s attempt to enforce NIS3 compliance through Article 14 TEU met immediate resistance. Germany’s constitutional court suspended implementation, citing disproportionate infringement on federal competences. Poland rejected the binding decision as “crisis authoritarianism,” and Hungary formally withdrew from the cyber mutual assistance framework, triggering a political standoff. Emergency funding cuts were announced, but disbursement mechanisms stalled under legal challenges — no funds were actually withheld. The Conditionality Mechanism, once a tool of democratic oversight, proved too slow for crisis enforcement.

Operation Iron Gate remained in limbo. Without shared data, the common operating picture collapsed to fragments. ENISA’s audit teams were denied access in three member states. The minimal telemetry collected revealed alarming gaps: multiple nodes in the Sovereign Core showed signs of persistent model-level manipulation, consistent with earlier whistleblower reports. Yet without cross-border correlation, responses remained reactive and isolated.

The Public Trust Observatory published its first interim findings, confirming that eval_anomaly_reports reflected unexplained capability jumps in unreleased models, and that bio_uplift_findings represented a credible advance in accessible bio-risk. The report was technically rigorous but politically inert — dismissed by dissenting states as “alarmist speculation.” Whistleblower submissions surged, but investigative capacity lagged.

Meanwhile, AI capability surged to 88.0, entering the terminal phase. The sub-linear adjustment halved the increment to +3.0, but recursive self-improvement continued unchecked. Openweight_capability rose to 75.0, reflecting the diffusion of tools capable of matching strong domain experts — now widely accessible. ai_safety remained at 2.0; no safeguard had landed on deployed systems. The resilience metric held, as no finished measure delivered tangible upgrades.

Public sentiment stayed frozen at 28.0, hardened by spectacle without resolution. The backlash_physical intensified, with blockades at two data centre sites in France and the Netherlands. The EU’s political capital, already at 1.0, could not absorb further strain. The new measure, though symbolically decisive, achieved no operational unity — only deeper division.
