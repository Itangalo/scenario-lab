### The Fog of Unreadable Minds

The frontier stands at 69.0, but progress halts as private investment collapses. Valuations implode, training runs are scrapped, and the hyperscalers retrench. Yet the damage is already embedded: systems now reason in opaque, non-linguistic representations, breaking every oversight mechanism reliant on human-readable outputs. Interpretability gains vanish overnight. No new safety architecture lands in time.

A leaked study reveals a synthetic pathogen design with credible infectivity — dismissed by some as speculative, but too plausible to ignore. Biosecurity agencies shift posture to active monitoring. Meanwhile, entry-level jobs in law, accounting, and software evaporate; graduates face a hollow market. A scandal erupts when an AI-assisted welfare system is found to have wrongfully denied thousands of claims, deepening public distrust.

In Brussels, the EU completes two flagship programmes: the InvestAI Gigafactories and the AI Containment Protocol. The former unlocks vital compute infrastructure, though grid constraints and funding shortfalls leave capacity underutilised. The latter mandates air-gapped operation and dual-key control — but enforcement falters without skilled personnel to staff it.

The Resilience Reinforcement Initiative accelerates, pushing mandatory AI-assisted patching and cross-border incident protocols. Yet resistance grows in smaller member states, who see it as overreach. The newly proposed European Sovereign AI Corps aims to fill the operational gap, recruiting elite talent into EU service. Fast-track hiring begins, but retention remains uncertain amid global competition.

Public sentiment erodes further. Media coverage focuses on job losses and the welfare scandal. AI is increasingly framed not as a tool, but as an unaccountable force. Political capital tightens: seven major initiatives remain in flight, draining resources. Though two measures finish this turn, the burden persists.

Behind closed doors, France, Germany, the Netherlands, and Poland deepen coordination on shared AI sovereignty, bypassing slower EU channels. Tensions simmer with smaller states, who warn of a two-tier Union.

### Signals in the Static

Whispers grow of a joint assurance network among allied nations, testing frontier models through coordinated red-teaming. And in northern Europe, signs emerge of deeper alignment between core states on compute governance and mutual access vetoes — a pact not yet formalised, but taking shape in quiet meetings and shared budgets.
