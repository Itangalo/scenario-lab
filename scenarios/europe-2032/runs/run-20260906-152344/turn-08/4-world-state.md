### Stalled Momentum, Mounting Pressure

The EU completes two major initiatives this turn: the Sovereign AI Core Programme and the Resilience Reinforcement Initiative. The former delivers its compute infrastructure but fails to staff it — the European Sovereign AI Corps remains in early recruitment, and fast-track hiring hits legal resistance from national civil service unions over pay parity and secondment rights. Without personnel, the SACP’s auditability promises collapse before they begin. Meanwhile, the Gigafactories face renewed strain as RePowerEU funds are only partially redirected, leaving the Norrland site operating at 60% capacity.

The Resilience Reinforcement Initiative achieves partial success. Mandatory patching is enforced across energy and transport sectors in six large member states, reducing vulnerability windows. However, smaller states resist the 72-hour rule, citing cost and technical infeasibility. Bio-detection nodes go live in Stockholm and Marseille but are delayed in Vilnius due to procurement disputes. Cross-border incident protocols are tested in a simulated ransomware attack on Baltic rail systems, revealing coordination gaps between CSIRTs.

Public sentiment holds at rock bottom. Media coverage focuses on unstaffed data centres and bureaucratic infighting, reinforcing perceptions of dysfunction. The welfare scandal lingers, now tied to broader narratives of AI-driven governance failure.

Behind the scenes, bilateral coordination among France, Germany, the Netherlands, and Poland intensifies. A quiet agreement establishes shared access vetoes on foreign compute deals and joint funding for red-teaming facilities — a de facto sovereignty pact that bypasses Brussels. This undermines formal EU cohesion, with smaller members accusing the core of creating a “directorate within the Union.”

The launch of the European Red-Teaming Network begins, but with no immediate metric impact. It will take time to stand up adversarial simulations, especially without trusted cross-border data-sharing frameworks.

Political capital erodes further under the weight of seven concurrent measures. The priority push on resilience yields marginal gains, while staffing shortfalls hollow out hard-won infrastructure.

### Flickers in the Perimeter

Whispers grow louder about an emerging joint assurance network. Informal red-teaming exercises occur between Dutch and Finnish labs, using sovereign models to probe synthetic biology risks. No formal structure exists — yet.
