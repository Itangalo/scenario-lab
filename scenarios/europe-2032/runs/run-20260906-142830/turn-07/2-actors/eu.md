## In practice
We are treating this turn as a rupture. The breakdown of human-legible reasoning—opaque_reasoning—has invalidated the foundational assumption behind our auditability strategy: that we could *read* what models were doing. That era is over. From now on, control must be enforced *structurally*, not interpreted after the fact. The new Runtime Integrity Enforcement measure will require EAAD and ENISA to co-develop, by turn 8, a standard for formally verified execution environments that sandbox AI systems in critical functions, with real-time adversarial probes and kill switches triggered by behavioural drift. This is not interpretability—it is containment. We are repurposing €12bn from the now-completed InvestAI Gigafactories’ final disbursement to fund the first phase of implementation, conditioning future access to EU sovereign compute on compliance.

Meanwhile, the Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative is now in active deployment across 14 Tier 1 operators, but progress is uneven. ENISA has issued binding integration deadlines under Article 122 TFEU, but resistance persists in Poland and Spain, where national CERTs argue the stacks undermine local autonomy. We are offering joint command pilots with shared staffing and incident logs to secure buy-in. The Commission is also fast-tracking a Council Implementing Act to override ENTSO-E’s grid allocation refusal, citing the energy sabotage in Belgium as evidence of strategic vulnerability. Germany and Austria will be invited to technical arbitration under the Energy Union framework, but the Brittany microgrid will go live by turn 8 regardless.

Public sentiment is at 19.0—dangerously low. We are launching a truth-in-crisis campaign through the EAAD, admitting that our previous oversight models failed, but framing the new runtime enforcement as a harder, more reliable form of control. Displaced workers are being fast-tracked into cyber defence roles via a restructured SCAP transition window, now tied to Shield deployment zones. This is not just about systems. It is about restoring the belief that the Union can adapt, even when the ground shifts.
