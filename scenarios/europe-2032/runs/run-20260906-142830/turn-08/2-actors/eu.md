## In practice
We are treating this turn as the end of one era and the beginning of another. The Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative has now completed deployment across all 14 Tier 1 operators. Despite resistance in Poland and Spain, integration was enforced through binding decisions under Article 122 TFEU, backed by the fast-tracked Council Implementing Act that overrode ENTSO-E’s grid allocation block. The Brittany microgrid is operational, and real-time model monitoring is now active in energy, transport, and health command centres. However, the Shield was designed for closed systems under known control—its assumptions are already strained by the openweight_frontier_release, which has placed models at capability 50.5 directly into private hands, beyond audit, beyond recall.

Thus, our new measure—Behavioural Containment—shifts the frontier of control from origin to operation. EAAD and ENISA will co-develop, by turn 9, a binding standard for execution environments that treat *all* open-weight models in critical functions as untrusted, regardless of provenance. This means sandboxing with formally verified boundaries, continuous red-teaming via adversarial agents, and kill switches triggered by behavioural drift. Compliance will be a condition for access to EU sovereign compute and for procurement contracts above €50m. We are redirecting €8bn from the residual InvestAI fund to accelerate pilot environments in Germany, the Netherlands, and Finland, where national CERTs have already demonstrated containment prototypes.

Public sentiment remains at 14.0—dangerously close to the threshold of ungovernability. The truth-in-crisis campaign will now pivot to frame containment as the new realism: not transparency, but constraint; not trust, but verification. We are launching a joint communication effort with the European Labour Authority to fast-track displaced workers into Shield maintenance and red-teaming roles, expanding the SCAP window to include behavioural monitoring operations. This is no longer about building capacity. It is about proving that the Union can still act—decisively, coherently, and at scale—when the ground beneath it is dissolving.
