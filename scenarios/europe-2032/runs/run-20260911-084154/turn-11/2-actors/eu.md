## In practice
We run this through ENISA and CERT-EU with DG DIGIT and DG SANTE money: pre-vetted auto-patch images and swarm-detection sensors pushed to municipal IT, clinic systems and the two transport operators, with islanding offline folders kept as fallback. Procurement is central EU framework so communes do not tender alone, and we use Digital Europe funds to pay managed-service coverage where local staff are gone.

At the same time we keep M8 alive through EMPL Council and ESF+: wage bridges for 6-12 months plus short PES courses in cyber-hygiene, care-tech and grid-tech, levy-funded. We message both together — patched services plus paid transition — to blunt the defector's pragmatism narrative, while TSOs keep the load-shedding sequence warm and we quietly seek volume-licence deliveries without conceding the Union position.
