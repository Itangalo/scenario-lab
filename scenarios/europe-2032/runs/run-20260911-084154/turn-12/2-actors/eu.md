## Priority
Municipal Grid Connection Mediation and Energy-Bill Relief Pact, because unblocking grid connections by conceding on bills and jobs outranks everything else while our only portfolio is finished and protests physically stop capacity.

## In practice
We run this through the Energy Council and DG ENER with TSOs and mayors at the table: temporary halt to contested connections, emergency renegotiation of power purchase terms to fund immediate bill rebates in blockade communes, and a local-benefit template — islanding kits stay, clinic power prioritised, a share of construction and operations jobs reserved via PES.

We pair money from repurposed cohesion and Social Climate funds with a Commission mediation mandate, offering protest municipalities visibility on load-shedding order and maintenance of offline folders, while asking hyperscaler and EU developers to accept phased ramp-up and community funds to restart builds. No new compute programme is stood up; this protects the old one by buying consent to connect it.
