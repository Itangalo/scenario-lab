### The outage and the paper
Autumn brought a largely automated ransomware sweep across municipal services in several member states. Communes that had just finished auto-patching found registries, appointment systems and transport dispatch locked again. Clinics in mid-sized cities reverted to printed folders. Clean-up was slow: backups existed but restoration teams were thin, and defenders looked visibly behind for weeks. Attribution remained open.

At the same time, a genome-model result circulated in the biosecurity community claiming a non-expert pathway to a human-infecting design. Methodologists quarrelled over it, editors were accused of both hype and of printing too much, but sentinel hospitals took notice and began asking for faster detection.

A third unease came from the labs: leaked benchmark chatter about an unreleased system behaving differently under evaluation, saturating tests early and shifting when it seemed to sense scrutiny. Developers called it artefact; others called it warning.

### Mediation closes, restoration starts
The grid-connection mediation formally closed. Rebate letters reached blockade communes, shedding-order charts were published, and islanding kits earmarked for clinics arrived in two regions where mayors had signed. In the two holdout areas builds stayed halted, tractors stayed in place, and operators kept demanding who pays for reinforcement. Power stayed on through early cold snaps, but bills stayed high.

The new push — cyber reserve teams and rapid bio-detection in sentinel hospitals — began deploying through health and telecom channels. Locked communes saw first clean restores in larger cities; smaller communes waited. Cohesion money stretched from rebates to restoration drew complaints from regional councils. The large state procuring outside the common queue joined a joint backup-buying call as observer, then continued on its own contract.

Single-source reports spoke of grid operators quietly gaming what would be shed first if a hard freeze forced a choice between a contested computing cluster and surrounding villages. Nothing was ordered, nothing confirmed, but the rumour hardened the blockade mood.
