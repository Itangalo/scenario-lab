### Weeks of containment
Autumn brought the release health ministries had feared since spring. A modified pathogen with clear traces of model-assisted design leaked from a lab working with outsourced screening, sickening dozens across two regions before isolation held. Hospitals activated surge wards, wastewater sequencing ran day and night, and contact tracing stretched for weeks. Fatalities remained limited, but the sight of sealed wards reset every argument about machine intelligence in Europe.

The response drew directly on the drills completed that summer. Kill-switch exercises and telemetry checks in hospitals, labs and grid operators meant operators knew whom to call and what to cut. The Strait buffer was rerouted for medical spares. Officials claimed, with some justification, that without the spring preparation the disruption would have cascaded.

A second development softened the shock. A control result from frontier research — a certifiable check on agent behaviour that worked on deployed systems, not just demos — was taken up fast because labs wanted it too. Ispra and the assurance teams pushed verified patches to municipal centres first, publishing pass-fail logs. Engineers described it as the first time a safety gain landed operationally within weeks.

Politics remained brittle. At two inland sites where mediated data-centre builds were underway, coordinated blockades of roads and grid connections halted work for weeks. Local groups cited opaque models and the pathogen scare; coverage was hostile. Brussels paused forced connections, offered benefit and monitoring packages, and shifted load to existing capacity rather than force a fight.

The new surge programme — isolation capacity, sequencing, continuity exercises plus fast adoption of the control check under existing health and cyber law — started unevenly. Funds were reprogrammed, not new, and the large member state with its separate hyperscaler deal shared only partial screening telemetry. Retraining offices and unions warned logistics job losses were accelerating underneath the health crisis, unanswered.

By December containment held, but trust had not recovered.
