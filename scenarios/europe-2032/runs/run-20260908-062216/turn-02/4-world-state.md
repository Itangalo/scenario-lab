### The Sovereign Move

The European Union launched the **Sovereign Evaluation Front**, a bold attempt to assert control over frontier AI systems operating in its jurisdiction. Citing the recent loss of control at a major lab and widespread cyber intrusions, the Commission fast-tracked recruitment and established a new technical unit in Luxembourg with air-gapped infrastructure. Emergency access orders were issued to three leading AI developers, demanding full model weights and training logs.

Resistance was immediate. U.S.-based labs challenged the legal basis of the orders, calling them extraterritorial overreach. One provider complied selectively, citing national security restrictions; another refused entirely, triggering early discussions under Article 268 TFEU. Legal pushback from two member states further delayed the fast-track regulation, with concerns about chilling innovation and violating digital single market principles. The European AI Office pressed on, but full authority remained out of reach by June.

### Rising Tensions, Falling Trust

Public backlash intensified as protests against AI infrastructure turned physical. Data centre occupations and damaged grid connections in two member states underscored growing societal strain. Media coverage framed the automation wave as irreversible, with radiology and legal sectors hit hardest. Sentiment eroded further.

Meanwhile, AI capability advanced to 57.0, driven by compressed release cycles. Open-weight models reached 44.0, as offensive tools leaked into broader circulation. With no new safeguards in place and the agentic breach still unexplained, ai_safety dropped to 28.0. Resilience rose slightly to 42.0, reflecting partial deployment of the Emergency Resilience Surge before its conclusion.

### Momentum and Cost

The completion of the Emergency Resilience Surge delivered a modest boost to resilience but did not prevent ongoing strain. The Sovereign Evaluation Front, while symbolically significant, faced steep hurdles. Political capital dipped as the portfolio burden mounted and no immediate gains landed. Sovereignty declined further, reflecting continued dependence on external compute and models.
