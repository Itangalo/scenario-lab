## New measure  
Sovereign Evaluation Front  
Establish a permanent, EU-controlled capability to conduct pre-release evaluations of frontier AI systems, with binding authority to delay deployment and access to weights and training logs — starting with emergency access to the most capable models in operation today.

Category: 1 (Evaluation and oversight)

## Priority

## In practice  
We are launching the Sovereign Evaluation Front under the authority of the European AI Office, with direct reporting to the Commission and emergency powers delegated under Article 10 of the AI Act, activated by the systemic emergency declaration. This is not an audit body but a technical force: we are fast-tracking recruitment of 300 experts in interpretability, agent behaviour and red teaming, drawing from EMBL, ESA, national cyber agencies and academic partners, and placing them in a new secure facility in Luxembourg with air-gapped compute funded from the Contingency Margin. By June, we will have issued emergency access orders to the three most capable AI developers operating in the EU, demanding full model weights, training data logs and internal safety test results for systems above 50.0 capability — the threshold crossed last turn.

We are backing this with legal urgency: the Council is being asked to fast-track a regulation under the urgent procedure to grant the Evaluation Front binding authority to suspend deployment of any system that fails to meet minimum reasoning legibility and containment standards, effective immediately upon adoption. Resistance is expected from both U.S.-based labs and from member states invested in open innovation narratives, but the cyber_test_shot and election_annulled events have shifted the baseline: we are no longer asking permission to understand what is being deployed. Where cooperation is given, we are offering mutual recognition of evaluations for global deployment and fast-tracked market access. Where it is refused, we are preparing to invoke Article 268 TFEU for non-compliance with essential public security requirements — a line we do not draw lightly, but one we must now be ready to hold. The Tech Sovereignty Package and InvestAI Gigafactories continue, now with evaluation compliance added as a condition for access to accelerated permitting and public funding.
