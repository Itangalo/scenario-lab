## In practice
We are consolidating our strategy around operational sovereignty. The eval_anomaly_reports — showing unexplained capability emergence in closed agent workflows — confirm that even the developers do not fully understand their systems. Yet we in the Union still lack access to the training data, weights, or agent logs needed to investigate such anomalies ourselves. The member_state_defection, where a national government cut a side deal with a U.S. cloud provider for exclusive access to AI training clusters, further exposed our institutional fragility: we cannot enforce unity when we offer no alternative.

This turn, we are acting on two fronts. First, we are accelerating the Autonomous Frontier Initiative by designating the Lyon and Barcelona InvestAI sites as sovereign-only training zones, with full air-gapped access for EU red teams and no commercial co-location. We are invoking Article 185 TFEU to fast-track joint programming among nine member states, pooling compute, talent, and classified data for red-teaming. The European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking will redirect 50% of its pre-exascale capacity to this effort by Q1 2029, and we are establishing a new legal instrument — the Sovereign Model Access Act — to mandate full telemetry sharing from any model trained on EU-subsidised compute.

Second, we are institutionalising the emergent_interpretability_consortium as the European Interpretability Consortium, with €18bn in dedicated funding from the repurposed Tech Sovereignty Package. This will link INRIA, Max Planck, and ISTA in a unified pipeline to develop and deploy probing tools on sovereign model runs. Unlike private interpretability efforts, this will be mandatory for all Autonomous Frontier Initiative models, with findings shared in real time with the European AI Safety Board. We are offering participating member states first access to anomaly detection tools in exchange for political support, and we are fast-tracking visa pathways for 500 frontier researchers under the EU AI Talent Corridor.

We are no longer waiting for compliance. We are building the capacity to see, run, and understand frontier AI ourselves — because without that, no rule we pass will matter.
