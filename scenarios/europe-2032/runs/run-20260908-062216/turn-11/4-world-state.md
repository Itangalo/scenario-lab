The **Joint Control Framework** continues, now anchored by a fragile middle_power_coalition. ### Triage in a Burning House

The Commission framed the Resilience Mobilisation as survival, not strategy. But with sovereignty eroding, sentiment frozen, and expertise evaporating, the Union’s capacity to act rests on a knife’s edge. Efforts to harden systems are underway, but implementation lags behind ambition, starved of personnel and trust.
