## In practice  
We are executing emergency air-gapping of the Lyon and Barcelona Gigafactory sites under Article 185 TFEU, treating them as strategic redoubts. The European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking has been legally bound to redirect 50% of its pre-exascale capacity to these sites by Q4 2029, and we are invoking the Crisis Regulation Framework to override national permitting delays. The Sovereign Model Access Act is being fast-tracked through COREPER II, mandating full telemetry and data sharing from any model trained on EU-subsidised compute — this is non-negotiable. Without it, we remain blind to agent behaviour that even developers no longer fully control.

At the same time, we are integrating the emergent_interpretability_consortium into the European Interpretability Consortium, consolidating INRIA, Max Planck, and ISTA into a unified pipeline for cognitive probing. €18bn from the expired Tech Sovereignty Package has been reallocated, and we are fast-tracking Schengen-wide AI research clearance for 500 frontier researchers. Member states are being offered early access to anomaly detection tools derived from sovereign runs — a tangible incentive for cohesion in a moment of fragmentation. The goal is clear: by turn 8, we must have both the models and the means to see inside them. If we do not, the two mandates will have converged — not by resolution, but by collapse.
