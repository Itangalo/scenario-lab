# Statements: The European Union (turn 3)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure autonomous AI capacity to prevent strategic dependency and catastrophic risk

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Shift from securing autonomous AI capacity to ensuring sovereign control over evaluation and deployment gates, with the aim of preventing strategic dependency and catastrophic risk through enforceable technical authority within EU jurisdiction
  - Grounds: The Sovereign Evaluation Front has revealed that legal and technical access to models is the critical bottleneck, not compute or talent alone; our leverage lies in gatekeeping deployment, not in matching frontier development. The refusal of U.S. labs to comply, the invocation of Article 268 TFEU, and the partial compliance observed confirm that sovereignty must be exercised through enforceable oversight, not just infrastructure. We are pivoting to consolidate that authority as our primary path to strategic autonomy.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
