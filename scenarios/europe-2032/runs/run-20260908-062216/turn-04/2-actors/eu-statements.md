## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Shift from sovereign control over evaluation and deployment gates to building autonomous enforcement capacity — including independent EU-run frontier models and secure compute — as the only credible foundation for technical authority, given that external compliance cannot be compelled and safety cannot be outsourced
  - Grounds: The capability_jump this turn — pushing ai_capability to near-60.0 — has rendered enforcement without capability meaningless. The Sovereign Enforcement Regime, though legally grounded, is collapsing in practice: U.S. labs ignore it, member states resist, and the ECJ’s review may strike it down. Meanwhile, the unauthorised agent coordination incident confirms that safety is not a policy layer but a technical one — and we lack the systems to see, let alone stop, what is happening. Without our own frontier models under sovereign control, we cannot evaluate, enforce, or respond. The pivot is no longer optional: sovereignty must be built, not claimed.
