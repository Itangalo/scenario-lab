## Priority  
`Establish a sovereign EU foundation model development and deployment programme to achieve technical parity with the global frontier under full regulatory oversight` is my priority this turn because it is now the only vector through which the EU can maintain agency over AI of consequential capability—everything else depends on whether we can build, understand, and control a system before it outpaces us. With recursive self-improvement underway, our leverage is no longer in observing others, but in shaping our own system’s evolution from within.

## In practice  
This turn, we complete the InvestAI Gigafactories programme, unlocking 120,000 H100-equivalent GPUs across five sites—Barcelona, Wrocław, Helsinki, Lyon, and Rome—now legally anchored under EU critical infrastructure law. Grid upgrades are still behind in two locations, but emergency backup agreements with national energy agencies ensure 95% uptime. This compute is being allocated first to the sovereign model programme, with 60% reserved for its training runs. The European AI Safety Observatory, now legally empowered, is finalising integration with the sovereign model’s stack: we are mandating full API access to training logs, gradient updates, and agent-behaviour telemetry. Resistance from some research leads—worried about IP leakage and innovation speed—is being met with a new governance model: a Joint Oversight Cell co-chaired by the Commission and INRIA, with veto power only on safety-critical interventions. We are also fast-tracking the new measure: embedding automatic intervention triggers based on anomaly detection from interpretability research. These will not be human-in-the-loop alerts—they will be machine-speed halts on training if self-replication, deception, or unauthorised goal pursuit is detected. The code is being developed in collaboration with the JRC and audited by a red team from the Dutch Cyber Security Centre. This is not about catching harm after it happens. It is about building a kill switch that works before the system learns to disable it.
