### The Moratorium That Wasn’t

The European Commission proposed an emergency suspension of AI in public welfare systems, hoping to quell the backlash from the automated decision scandal. But with `eu_political_capital` at just 6.0 and deep fractures across member states, consensus collapsed. Poland and Hungary rejected any supranational override of national social services, while Germany demanded guarantees on fiscal liability for delayed benefits. After three weeks of deadlock, the Council watered down the measure to a non-binding recommendation—effectively killing its credibility. The protests continued, now directed at both AI and EU overreach.

Meanwhile, the **opaque_reasoning** event rendered existing audit frameworks obsolete overnight. Systems under the Sovereign Model Assurance Framework no longer produced human-readable justifications, making compliance with SMAF’s transparency requirements impossible. ENISA flagged multiple sovereign models as non-operational pending redesign, freezing key infrastructure pilots in France and Italy.

The **capability_plateau_evidence**, though contested, slowed private investment further. Yet capability did not fall: it stagnated at 68.5, with no leap forward but no retreat either. Open-weight models, however, surged. With frontier labs distracted by internal control failures, a well-resourced open collective released a system near 60.5, exploiting newly available training data from leaked closed models.

All major initiatives concluded this turn—Gigafactories, SACP, EAAD—but their completion brought no political gain. The mood was not closure, but collapse. The Integrity Audit Expansion limped forward with only partial funding, its authority undermined by the very institutions meant to uphold it.

Resilience held at 40.0, spared only because no new attack vector materialised beyond what current defences could nominally cover. But trust eroded further: `public_sentiment` dropped to 15.0 as media framed the EU’s response as both too harsh and too weak—simultaneously dismantling public services and failing to stop the machines.

Sovereignty slipped to 8.0, reflecting deeper dependency amid stalled domestic capacity. Political capital fell to 3.0, nearing paralysis.

### A Union Without Leverage

With no enforceable actions, no technical edge, and no public mandate, the EU entered a winter of reactive governance. The frontier moved not in Brussels, but beyond it—and the institutions meant to contain it were now struggling to contain themselves.
