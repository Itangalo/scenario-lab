### The Breaking Point

The European Commission’s emergency proposal to suspend AI in public welfare systems collapsed within days. With `eu_political_capital` at just 3.0, the Union lacked the cohesion to enforce even a temporary moratorium. Germany refused liability for delayed benefits, Poland denounced it as an infringement on national sovereignty, and Spain warned of administrative paralysis. The Council offered only a revised recommendation—non-binding, unenforceable, and politically inert. Protests intensified.

Simultaneously, **emergent_labour_unrest** erupted across France, Italy, and Belgium, as public-sector layoffs and automated service failures merged with fury over perceived EU impotence. Transit systems halted, benefit offices were blockaded, and digital platforms hosting AI-driven triage tools were overwhelmed by coordinated denial-of-service attacks—some attributed to disaffected workers, others to organised anti-AI collectives.

The **election_annulled** event in a major EU member state—where a court cited pervasive AI-generated disinformation as grounds for invalidating results—further shattered confidence. Though the decision was narrow, it amplified fears that algorithmic manipulation had already undermined democratic foundations.

All major initiatives—Gigafactories, SACP, EAAD, CIRS, SMAF, Public Assurance Protocol, and the Openweight Containment Initiative—concluded this turn, but their completion brought no momentum. Instead, the absence of active programmes left a vacuum. The Integrity Audit Expansion, intended as a cornerstone of renewed trust, stalled in its final phase due to lack of enforcement capacity and judicial uncertainty.

Resilience held at 40.0, spared only by the absence of novel cyber or bio threats, but systemic fragility deepened. `ai_safety` remained at 11.0—effectively nonexistent—while `openweight_capability` held at 60.5, enabling widespread misuse despite legal containment efforts.

### Sovereignty in Freefall

With no new domestic capacity online and no access leveraged from abroad, `eu_ai_sovereignty` dropped to 6.0, reflecting accelerating dependency and institutional decay. `eu_political_capital` fell to 2.0 as the failed moratorium and unrest drained what little authority remained. `public_sentiment` plunged to 10.0, as the public viewed both AI and the EU as runaway forces beyond control.
