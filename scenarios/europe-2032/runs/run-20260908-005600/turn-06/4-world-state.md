### A Wake-Up Scream

The EU responds to a major AI-generated cyberattack—targeting health registries and emergency dispatch systems across three capitals—with emergency legislation: the Critical Services Resilience Surge. A binding directive, fast-tracked under Article 114 TFEU, mandates zero-trust architecture, automated rollback, and AI-driven anomaly detection in essential public infrastructure by mid-year. €18bn from the Recovery and Resilience Facility is redirected, with 70% front-loaded. ENISA coordinates quarterly red-team exercises using live threat intelligence from the nascent ISED.

Public-sector successes continue: hospital wait times fall in Portugal, asylum decisions accelerate in Germany, and the narrative begins to shift. Yet the cyberattack leaves scars. Trust frays, and the optics of dependence—on foreign models, on brittle software supply chains—fuel quiet resentment.

### Sovereignty Under Strain

The Sovereign AI Development Programme advances: Toulouse signs its first research pact, and €6.2bn is disbursed for EU-sourced compute. But global AI capability reaches 60.0, crossing into territory where systems complete multi-day professional projects autonomously. With no safety evaluations yet embedded in frontier models, and opaque reasoning widespread, `ai_safety` drops to 24.0. The U.S. administration takes office under a **CONSOLIDATION** posture: frontier access is now rationed, conditional on alignment, and the EU—despite its “trusted” tier—feels the squeeze. Sovereignty gains from compute investment are offset by strategic exposure, leaving `eu_ai_sovereignty` flat at 22.0.

### Political Capital Erodes

Four large measures remain in flight, including the new Resilience Surge. The portfolio cost is −13. The completed Tech Sovereignty Package yields +2, but no proposal bonus applies—the Surge responds to structural risk, not a narrowly recent shock. Political capital falls to 13.0, nearing the threshold where new initiatives risk failing to start.

Public sentiment rises slightly, lifted by visible benefits and decisive action, but remains ambivalent.
