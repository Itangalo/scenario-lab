## In practice  
We are closing the Emergency Resilience Surge with tangible gains: binding NIS2 enforcement is now universal, the Estonian threat-hunting hub is live, and the southern node in Marseille has been formally integrated into the Nordic-Baltic cyber command’s detection mesh. This is not just compliance—it is convergence. We are converting defensive hardening into operational interoperability, and we are exercising it: ENISA will lead a cross-border drill in Q4 simulating AI-driven logistics sabotage, with real-time red-teaming from Finland and Lithuania. The Surge delivered, and we are locking in its gains by embedding them in standing structures.

At the same time, we are formally ending the ASML leverage initiative—not in failure, but in transformation. The Dutch withdrawal was not defiance, but realism: you cannot wield a chokepoint if you cannot withstand the retaliation. So we are shifting from coercion to construction. The Trusted Tech Forum is dead, but its purpose lives: we are redirecting its intent into the new Sovereign AI Development Programme. This is not a research pilot. It is a national-champion-scale effort, modelled on Airbus in ambition, on CERN in openness, on Galileo in sovereignty. We will pool EuroHPC compute, draw on EIT Digital’s talent network, and establish a new Public AI Lab headquartered in Toulouse, with satellite nodes in Prague and Helsinki. The model will be trained on EU data, under EU law, with weights secured to a state-actor standard and evaluated independently before any deployment.

We are funding it through a reallocation of the Tech Sovereignty Package’s future penalties—€45bn over three years—tied to binding commitments from member states to prioritise sovereign compute in public procurement. Germany has signalled conditional support; France demands a French host for the training cluster; Poland insists on parity in access. We are offering: Toulouse for coordination, but distributed training across three sites; open access to the base model for all members; and a governance board with rotating presidency. This is not about picking winners. It is about ending dependence. And we are pairing it with the Public AI Adoption Accelerator: every hospital, court, and school that deploys a sovereign AI system will now get bonus funding if it runs on the new EU model. Demand will pull the system into being—and into use.
