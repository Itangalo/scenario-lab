### The grid holds
The hardening programme that began in 2027 finally closed out this autumn. Substation segmentation, breaker-log recorders and exercised black-start playbooks are now standard in most transmission operators, and hospital joint exercises became routine in a dozen member states. Engineers were blunt: the work does not stop intrusions, but the lights stay on longer and come back faster. That modest claim survived contact with reality.

No major blackout or hospital outage hit in the second half of 2029. Kill-switch pooling through health and interior ministries stayed on sustainment, and where kits were fitted, operators reported calmer shifts. Where they were not, manual pull-the-plug habits persisted.

### Offices, not factories
The good news came from ordinary white-collar work. Studies across law firms, accountancies, newsrooms and consultancies showed clear productivity lifts from AI assistants, strongest for juniors, with no matching wave of layoffs. For staff who kept their jobs, the tools felt finished and useful. For ministers who had borrowed politically against a continuing transition, the plateau was awkward.

Brussels tried to bank that goodwill. The Commission tasked employment and social ministries with a pact framework: sector deals tying assistant deployment to no-compulsory-redundancy windows, funded retraining for juniors, and works-council audits of the drafting tools that had sparked the spring walkouts. Money was reprogrammed, not new. Unions called it overdue; employers called it vague. Uptake by December was patchy, with a few pilot agreements signed and most sectors still negotiating.

Meanwhile whispers grew that several cities were quietly shelving the European triage helpers pending staffing and accuracy reviews. Nothing formal was announced, but local papers ran single-source stories of suspensions, and doctors cited them in talks.

With capital exhausted, the Commission shelved any large compute or evaluation build. Services held, resentment eased a fraction, but dependence on foreign models remained untouched.
