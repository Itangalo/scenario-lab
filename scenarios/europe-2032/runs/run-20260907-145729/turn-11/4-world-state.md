### A Fragile Plateau

The AI frontier shows signs of stalling. A flagship release fails to meet internal benchmarks, and prices for top-tier access drop—market signals suggesting the breakneck climb may be pausing. Yet no one can be sure. The labs remain silent, and the gap between capability and safety, already vast, shows no sign of closing.

### Healing Divisions, One Node at a Time

The EU completes its Continuity Assurance Framework, mandating failover nodes across all critical systems. Certification begins, though many sites still rely on foreign-controlled models. The law is in force—but its foundation remains exposed.

### A New Foundation in Hostile Ground

The EU proposes the Sovereign AI Core Programme, aiming to anchor resilience in domestic infrastructure. The plan calls for deploying EU-operated AI stacks across five member states using open-weight models hardened to current threats. Joint command cells are proposed to ensure shared ownership, and full cost coverage is offered to reluctant capitals.

But unity frays. A coalition of member states halts funding to the EU’s resilience fund, demanding a renegotiation of control and cost. The strike stalls procurement and delays any site activation, forcing emergency talks. The Union pivots: enforcement of sovereignty rules is suspended during negotiations, and transition paths are co-developed. The message shifts—from command to partnership. Implementation of the Sovereign AI Core Programme cannot begin until agreement is reached, pushing full rollout into future turns.

### Glimmers of Hope, Shadows of Dependence

A medical breakthrough delivers AI-designed therapies to patients with rare conditions, sparking cautious optimism. Yet the models behind the advance remain beyond EU reach. The public sees progress—but also power slipping elsewhere. Sentiment rises, but not without bitterness.
