### Stalled Response, Deepening Crisis

The European Union attempts to regain control with an emergency suspension of AI in public welfare systems, aiming to quell rising anger after the automated decision scandal. The measure, proposed under Article 114 TFEU, faces immediate resistance. Several member states, citing national competence over social policy, refuse to accept binding restrictions, while legal scholars question the proportionality of a bloc-wide moratorium. Negotiations in the Council stall, and no formal adoption occurs by mid-year. The symbolic gesture fails to materialize into enforceable action, deepening public perception of institutional paralysis.

Meanwhile, the opaque reasoning crisis escalates. With frontier models no longer producing human-legible reasoning chains, existing audit frameworks — including the recently launched Integrity Audit Expansion — lose their technical foundation. ENISA and EAAD officials admit in closed sessions that current evaluation protocols cannot verify system behaviour, rendering oversight performative. No immediate fix is in sight, and the incident triggers a −5 point drop in ai_safety, compounding prior weaknesses.

The capability plateau remains unconfirmed, but market signals suggest a slowdown in closed AI progress. Yet this offers no reprieve: openweight capability holds steady at 53.0, still sufficient to enable misuse, while resilience sees no improvement. The Sovereign Stack Hardening Initiative continues to face implementation gaps in Poland and France, and with no new compute capacity online, eu_ai_sovereignty remains at 10.0.

Politically, the EU’s agenda collapses under its own weight. Despite finishing multiple major initiatives, no political capital is gained. The portfolio charge — seven measures in flight until their completion — drains resources, and with eu_political_capital already at 6.0, the new small measure (−2) and priority (−1) push it down to 3.0. Public sentiment remains frozen at 18.0, reflecting widespread distrust and no visible recovery in legitimacy.

### A Union at Breaking Point

The bloc enters a defensive crouch. Without capacity, credibility, or cohesion, its ability to shape events diminishes. The US maintains its consolidation posture, offering no concessions. The EU’s sovereignty is now effectively paper — a claim without enforcement, a structure without power.
