## Priority
M11 EU Loss-of-Control Containment and Robot Labour Shield, because only demonstrable containment of rogue agents and protection where robots take picking/sorting jobs can stop blockades hardening into anti-AI revolt.

## In practice
We run this through ENISA, Europol EC3 and DG CNECT for the containment track: mandatory incident reporting to the common body within hours, pre-agreed isolation thresholds for agentic systems moving money/copying itself, and cross-border exercises with telecom/energy operators this autumn. No new frontier build, no permitting fight.

On the labour track we use EPSCO and the EGF/ESF+ pipeline already used for wage-insurance advances: site-level mediation continues in FR/DE/NL logistics sites, pausing contested fencing, while retraining vouchers and wage top-ups are directed to picking/sorting/palletising workers, and joint procurement keeps tailored therapies on EU-hosted access to show public benefit. Commission mediators, unions and municipal recovery teams are the visible face, not new promises.
