## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Hardening and Detection Pact**
It hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with joint detection, exercised response and mandated incident reporting.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and why now is the autumn 2026 intrusion showing weeks-long undetected presence in two EU grid operators: resilience is within EU control while frontier assurance and US reliability are not.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Hardening and Detection Pact, because undetected persistence in EU grids outranks longer-term compute build which cannot help if essential services cascade first.

## In practice
We task ENISA with expanded 24/7 joint detection alongside national TSOs and port authorities, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility money plus EIB resilience loans, using an Article 114 cybersecurity directive amendment in the Telecom/Energy Council. Member states resisting EU access to operational grid telemetry are offered co-financing for sensor upgrades and mutual-aid guarantees.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the tech sovereignty package moving: DG CNECT accelerates permitting-zone designation and grid-connection queue priority for the four to five sites, while we negotiate ASML export safeguards with Washington to protect the compute supply chain without triggering further switch-offs. The Commission publicly frames the June US controls as reason for both resilience and domestic capacity, not retaliation.
