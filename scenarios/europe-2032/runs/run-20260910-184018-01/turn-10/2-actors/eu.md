## Priority
M10 Municipal Cyber Recovery and Continuity Repair, because only days-not-weeks restoration plus the clinical access story can stop blockades and outages fusing into a single anti-AI revolt.

## In practice
We run M10 through ENISA, DG DIGIT and the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority as one visible repair operation: clean backups and seconded teams stay in the worst-hit municipalities and hospital networks, fallback kits stay mandated, and Porto-style tutoring/triage stacks are kept as the proof that systems stay up. No new build-out, no new levy fight this turn.

On the blockades in France, Germany and the Netherlands we treat them as labour and siting disputes, not sabotage: Commission-mediated talks with warehouse operators, data-centre developers and unions in EPSCO and the Employment Committee, with site-level pause on contested fencing and wage-insurance advances via the existing transition fund pipeline. On the medical breakthrough we use joint procurement under the Medical Countermeasure Shield base to secure tailored therapies on EU-hosted, verifiably-governed access terms, so the benefit arrives as public service rather than further dependence.
