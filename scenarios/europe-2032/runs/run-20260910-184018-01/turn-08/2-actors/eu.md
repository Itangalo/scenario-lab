It pays wage-insurance, retraining and fast redress to the narrowly displaced entry-level cohorts and the benefits-triage victims, funded by a levy on automating employers and repurposed ESF+.
This is a labour and social protection move (category 7) because with capital at 15 and sentiment at 15 only acknowledged redress for the AI Act scandal and visible help for non-recovering entry hiring can stop strikes and rebuild room to act, while the cheaper frontier makes employer co-funding negotiable now.
