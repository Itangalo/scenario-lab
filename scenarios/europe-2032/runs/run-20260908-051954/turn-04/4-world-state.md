### Containment Under Pressure, Resilience in Motion

The EU launched the **Sovereign Resilience Corridor**, a large category 6 measure aimed at hardening critical infrastructure against cascading AI-driven failures. The directive mandates AI-enhanced intrusion detection and human-supervised failover protocols across energy, telecoms, finance, and emergency response systems, with compliance tied to EU funding. Early adoption begins in France and the Netherlands, but resistance simmers in Eastern member states wary of centralised mandates.

Despite the effort, political capital continued to erode. The portfolio charge remained steep: four large measures in flight cost −12, the named priority added −1, and the new large measure brought no proposal bonus — it answered no recent incident directly. With no offsetting gains, **eu_political_capital** fell to 18.0, edging toward crisis levels.

The **Sovereign Containment Framework** remained stalled in the Council, despite legal concessions to Germany and Poland. Berlin maintained that extraterritorial enforcement risked diplomatic blowback, while Warsaw demanded data sovereignty guarantees not yet deliverable. ENISA’s pilot audits revealed gaps in detecting fast-adapting agent swarms, undermining confidence in the framework’s operational viability.

Global AI capability advanced to **58.0**, driven by recursive training efficiencies. With no completed category 4 measure and capability rising by 2.0, **eu_ai_sovereignty** declined again, now at **17.0**, reflecting deepening dependence and shrinking strategic buffer. Openweight models reached **45.0**, as constrained replication of frontier techniques spread within export-controlled limits.

**ai_safety** held flat at 34.0 — theoretical advances in goal stability verification remained unfielded. **Resilience** rose by +2, reflecting partial progress on the new corridor and ongoing hardening, though full effects remain years away. **Public sentiment** held at 36.0, as protests against data centres and AI hiring tools persisted, with no visible public benefit to shift the narrative.

The hardening posture gained technical traction but lost political ground. Without legal closure or public trust, even robust frameworks risk becoming unenforceable.
