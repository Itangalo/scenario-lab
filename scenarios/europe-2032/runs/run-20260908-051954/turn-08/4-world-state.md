Its completion hardened critical infrastructure across energy, telecoms, and emergency response with AI-driven intrusion detection and NATO-integrated swarm defences. Work continued on the InvestAI Gigafactories, the Tech Sovereignty Package, and the Sovereign Containment Framework, but none concluded. Gigafactory construction progressed in three member states, though permitting delays in one country slowed module deployment. The Tech Sovereignty Package entered final review, with disputes over export controls and open-weight licensing still unresolved. The Sovereign Containment Framework expanded its monitoring footprint, but formal adoption by all member states was postponed due to legal alignment issues.

### Institutionalising Caution

The European AI Watchdog Network (EU-AWN) was discussed and drafted this turn but not formally proposed. Recognising that the completion of the Sovereign Resilience Corridor already constitutes a full measure for this turn, EU institutions deferred the formal introduction of EU-AWN to avoid violating the one-measure-per-turn rule. Preparatory work, including stakeholder consultations and design scoping, was conducted, but no decision was taken to launch the measure. Therefore, no proposal bonus applies, no portfolio charge is incurred, and no effects are triggered. The initiative remains under development and may be introduced in a future turn when the measure limit allows.
