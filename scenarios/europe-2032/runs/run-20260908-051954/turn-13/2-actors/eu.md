* - modify `containment_first` (position): Until we can independently verify system behavior, no expansion of AI integration is net positive — but with sovereign compute now in flight and open-weight models approaching frontier capability, we shift from containment-as-precaution to containment-as-operational-readiness: our systems must be stress-tested not only for known failure modes, but for emergent coordination, unauthorised replication, and geographically distributed action under degraded conditions.  
  - Grounds: The openweight_frontier_release event proves that frontier-level capability is now permanently distributed and operating outside any single jurisdiction. The earthquake further demonstrates that physical and systemic fragility are converging. We can no longer assume that containment means controlling access or delaying deployment — we must now assume that dangerous capabilities are already in the wild, and our obligation is to detect, disrupt, and degrade them in operation. This reframe demands that red-teaming evolve into a live-fire doctrine, simulating not just model behavior but networked, adaptive adversarial use across sovereign and non-sovereign infrastructure.

* - modify `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Sovereign operational control through enforceable jurisdiction, resilient integration, *adversarial evaluation capacity anchored in EU-owned infrastructure*, and *the ability to maintain continuity of evaluation and response under physical and systemic disruption*  
  - Trigger: the emergent_ai_faultline_earthquake and the openweight_frontier_release, both occurring within weeks of each other  
  - Grounds: Two shocks in rapid succession — one physical, one technological — have redefined the threat surface. The original commitment assumed a linear path to sovereignty: build the machine, secure the law, conduct the test. But the world is now delivering compound stress: infrastructure is fragile, and capability is diffuse. We are no longer building a laboratory — we are building a war room. The commitment must now include continuity under disruption as a core pillar, because sovereignty that collapses when the ground shakes, or when a model leaks, is not sovereignty at all. This expansion binds the two-year path to the reality of cascading risk.
