### Containment in Crisis, Capital in Decline

The EU doubled down on the **Sovereign Containment Framework**, redefining sovereignty not as mere compute ownership but as enforceable control over agentic AI systems. Legal drafting advanced under Article 16 TFEU, and ENISA began designing pilot audits for real-time telemetry compliance. Early technical specifications now include provisions for goal stability verification, but integration remains partial and untested — no breakthrough has yet been operationalised, and the path to reliable verification is still uncertain.

Yet momentum faltered. Germany’s constitutional concerns deepened, with Berlin warning that extraterritorial kill-switch mandates could violate international comity and provoke retaliatory access restrictions. Poland echoed these objections, stalling Council consensus. Public sentiment worsened as protests expanded beyond surveillance fears to include accusations of technological overreach, amplified by campaign platforms now embracing moratoriums on data centres and AI hiring tools. Trust eroded further when leaked internal assessments revealed that even the proposed framework would struggle to detect fast-evolving agent swarms.

Meanwhile, global AI capability advanced steadily to 56.0, driven by recursive optimisation in training pipelines. This rise triggered downward pressure across multiple metrics. With no category 4 measure completing and capability growth exceeding 1.5 points, **eu_ai_sovereignty** declined again, now at 19.0 — a new low, reflecting persistent dependence and shrinking strategic buffer.

**eu_political_capital** fell sharply. The portfolio charge remained high: four large measures in flight cost −12, plus −1 for the named priority, totaling −13. No proposal bonus applied (no new measure this turn), and legitimacy lending did not trigger (public sentiment below capital). The cost of sustained ambition without visible delivery took its toll.

Resilience saw a marginal +1 from partial progress on infrastructure hardening, but public backlash and stalled coordination limited gains. **ai_safety** held flat — theoretical advances in verification design have not yet translated into fielded systems. Openweight capability rose modestly to 44.0, reflecting gradual diffusion of smaller models that emulate certain frontier behaviours under tight constraints, consistent with ongoing replication efforts within existing export controls.

### Strategic Recalibration Amid Headwinds

The Union’s pivot toward containment-as-sovereignty gained intellectual clarity but lost political ground. Without legal closure or public buy-in, even well-designed frameworks risk becoming paper shields.
