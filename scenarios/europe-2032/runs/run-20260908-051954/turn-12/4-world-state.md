### Earthquake Exposes Fragility, EU Responds

A moderate earthquake disrupted operations at a major European data centre hub, triggering cooling failures and temporary outages across several high-performance computing facilities. The incident laid bare the geographic concentration of critical AI infrastructure, prompting emergency action. In response, the EU launched the **Geographic Redundancy Protocol**, a small Category 6 measure aimed at replicating core evaluation workloads across EuroHPC sites in Portugal, Finland, and Poland. Funding was redirected from the Digital Europe Programme, and cross-site data mirroring began, though full failover capacity remains years from completion.

### Sovereign Compute Stalls, Legal and Technical Hurdles Mount

Construction of the **Secure Foundational Compute** cluster at JUPITER continues, but legal concerns over Article 17 have stalled progress on securing extraterritorial immunity for the facility. The Commission insists on reclassifying the site as critical civilian infrastructure under NIS2, a workaround still under review. Meanwhile, ASML has not committed to accelerated lithography support, despite export-control coordination offers. U.S. hyperscalers maintain their refusal to allow model evaluations on EU infrastructure, dismissing the project as symbolic without operational access.

### Political Capital Erodes Further

With **eu_political_capital** already at 6.0, launching a new small measure (−2) and maintaining the large compute project (−3) and the Watchdog Network (−2) under priority (−1) drained resources. The redundancy protocol, while reactive, addressed a recent physical disruption and earned a +3 proposal bonus. However, no sentiment gain occurred, and legitimacy lending did not apply. The net effect left political capital at 4.0 — now critically low.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Slips

**ai_capability** rose by 0.5 to 64.0, continuing its slow climb. **openweight_capability** increased to 54.0, reflecting modest diffusion. With no safety measures landing and capability advancing, **ai_safety** fell to 38.0. The Watchdog Network remains under-resourced and legally constrained, unable to conduct adversarial evaluations at scale. **resilience** rose to 58.0 due to the new redundancy measure. **eu_ai_sovereignty** declined to 9.0, as capability growth triggered a −1 penalty and no category 4 completion occurred.
