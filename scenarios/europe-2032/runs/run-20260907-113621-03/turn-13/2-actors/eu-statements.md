# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will not act blind. This turn, the eval_anomaly_reports and bio_uplift_findings are not proof of imminent catastrophe, but they are proof that the systems we rely on are no longer legible even to their creators. We are no longer merely acting under uncertainty — we are acting inside a fog of our own making. Therefore, we bind ourselves further: we will not only act early, but we will demand that every major commitment be paired with a public, auditable theory of harm and a corresponding monitoring mechanism. The price of being late is high, but the price of being wrong without learning from it is higher. We now extend this to include democratic integrity: no major AI-enabled deployment in public functions will proceed without a pre-registered assessment of its potential to distort information ecosystems or undermine electoral processes.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure independent AI advancement under EU jurisdiction to ensure strategic autonomy and public control — but not at the cost of systemic integrity. This turn, we have seen that capability without control is not autonomy — it is exposure. Therefore, we narrow the commitment: we will prioritise the integrity and audibility of the sovereign stack over its speed of deployment. No node will be activated without a verified, independent audit of its update chain and decision logic. We will slow the rollout if necessary, but we will not propagate a compromised standard. We now add: this commitment is suspended until the Public Trust Observatory delivers an unredacted assessment of the cyber_major_incident and the election_annulled linkage, and until we have verified that no sovereign node has been used to manipulate public information or democratic processes.
