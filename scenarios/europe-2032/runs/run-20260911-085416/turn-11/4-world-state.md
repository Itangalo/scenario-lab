### Holding the wards
The cross-border medical mechanism formally closed its build-out phase in autumn, rebadged in practice as a bio-patient shuttle. Auditors credited it with preventing ward closures in three border regions during the long tail of tracing and isolation. Brussels overtime money kept shifts covered, but uneven stocks and blind early-warning pilots in patched municipalities meant triage persisted. Staff anger simmered; local papers carried single-source talk of coordinated stoppages, denied by unions, while military medical vans appeared briefly outside two hospitals.

A contested paper claiming a genome model could help a non-expert design a human-infecting organism sharpened fear without producing new cases. Biosecurity circles split over methods and publication; the public heard only that experts were arguing about recipes.

### The solo deal and the coalition
A large member state deepened its separate hyperscaler arrangement, publicly calling it pragmatism. The Commission called it incompatible with the common lithography line but opened no case, lacking budget and votes for a fight.

Instead ministers mandated a joint licensing and compute-bargaining framework with a handful of middle powers holding supply-chain pieces — pooled procurement and shared evaluation access in exchange for holding export discipline. Talks moved faster than expected precisely because no cash was offered. Partners agreed to align licence language and to bargain jointly for capacity, a modest but real foothold after months of frozen factory payments and quarantined shipments from Taipei.

A notable materials result — a lab-grown membrane cutting desalination energy costs — was registered abroad and licensed to industry, with little public resonance in Europe. Frontier systems remained opaque, black-box tested only, with the most capable weights still unrecallable.

Public mood darkened further: gratitude toward clinics that held was outweighed by containment tents, missed handoffs, and a sense of dependence on others for both chips and models.
