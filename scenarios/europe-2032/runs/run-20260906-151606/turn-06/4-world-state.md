### The Frontier Leaps, Europe Reacts

A sudden leap in AI capability—driven by a US laboratory’s release of a self-improving agent architecture—catapults the global frontier to an estimated 67.0. Autonomous research surges in quantum materials and cryptanalysis, rendering prior deployment forecasts obsolete. This discontinuous advance triggers a wave of alarm and adaptation across the EU, even as a rare safety breakthrough offers a counterpoint: a real-time interpretability tool capable of detecting deceptive reasoning before output is adopted by leading labs, including one in France.

The European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) and the Critical Infrastructure Resilience Surge (CIRS) formally conclude their establishment phases. EAAD launches its first audit of the Sovereign AI Core Programme’s prototype stack, integrating the new circuit-tracing tool under emergency delegation. CIRS transitions to ENISA as a permanent monitoring function, now mandated to publish redacted quarterly threat assessments.

Yet political cohesion fractures. Poland signs a bilateral agreement with a US hyperscaler for priority access to frontier models, embedding joint AI cells within its defence ministry. Framed as “pragmatic deterrence,” the move undermines EU-wide containment efforts. In response, the Commission offers Poland expedited observer status in the Sovereign Model Assurance Framework (SMAF) and proposes co-hosting the second air-gapped cluster in Wrocław, backed by a €10bn resilience grant conditional on full CIRS compliance by 2032.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 25.0. Despite launching the Public Assurance Protocol—a small transparency measure requiring annual public reports on high-capability systems—no immediate shift occurs. The Swedish InvestAI Gigafactory breaks ground under Article 122 overrides, accelerating grid connections, but sovereignty continues to erode under external pressure and capability growth.

### US Posture Set

The 2028 US election concludes: a president takes office committed to treating frontier AI as a strategic national asset. The new administration imposes strict export controls and tiers access by geopolitical alignment, marking a decisive shift toward consolidation of AI power.
