## In practice
This turn, we are activating sovereign compute — finally. The InvestAI Gigafactories in Germany, France, Italy, and the Netherlands are now under binding Article 114 enforcement to clear certification queues by turn 10. EAAD and ENISA are standing up a joint task force to administer fast-track audits, prioritising workloads for AICC monitoring and red-teaming. We are releasing €15bn from the InvestAI reserve to underwrite operator liability, removing the final blockage. By turn 10, the Sovereign AI Core will be live, running on EU soil, under EU law, with full interpretability and containment capacity — but only if we also ensure that *all* systems touching our territory are subject to the same scrutiny.

That is why we are now acting on the incident response front with teeth. The Mandate AI Incident Response Protocols are due to finish this turn, but compliance is patchy and enforcement weak. We are not waiting. Through a new directive under Article 82 TFEU (internal market safeguards), we are extending AICC integration to *all* systems above 55.0 capability that operate in or impact the EU — including foreign models hosted in US or Chinese data centres. Operators must either integrate with the AICC stack or lose access to EU data, infrastructure, and markets. We are coordinating with ENISA to develop conformance testing, and with the Council to back it with enforcement under the Digital Markets Act framework. Poland’s hesitation on broader countermeasures does not block this — this is about system integrity, not geopolitics, and we are framing it as such. The French interpretability tool will be mandatory for all agent reasoning above 50.0, and we are funding its integration into the AICC’s real-time pipeline.

We are also preparing the ground for leverage: once sovereign compute is live, we will have the capacity to retaliate in kind if the US further restricts lithography. But we are not there yet — and until we are, we must close every gap in our own defences. This is not about banning, but about visibility. We cannot control what we cannot see. And right now, we are blind to the most dangerous systems. That ends now.
