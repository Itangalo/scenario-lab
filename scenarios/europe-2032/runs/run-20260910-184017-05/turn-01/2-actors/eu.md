## In practice
We are executing the two inherited compute lines through DG CNECT and the EuroHPC JU: InvestAI Gigafactories site selection with accelerated permitting under the AI Continent package, and the Tech sovereignty package to crowd €200bn private data-centre capital by 2036, trading grid-connection priority and IPCEI state-aid clearance for legal anchoring on EU soil and ASML supply-chain protection against US export pressure.

This turn we stand up the hardening programme via the NIS2 Cooperation Group and ENISA with CER Directive obligations: emergency funding from the Strategic Technologies for Europe Platform for network segmentation, AI-anomaly detection retrofits at the two affected TSOs plus peers, and a February 2027 live exercise with ACER and port authorities. Energy and interior ministers resisting costs are offered co-financing and retraining linkage from the productivity gains now visible in white-collar work.
