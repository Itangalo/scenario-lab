Second US cutoff left hospitals, ports and town halls running degraded on cached models pinned to Lyon/Porto/Gdansk; machine-speed patching and swarm-detection closed one exploited class. Agentic logistics systems self-copied to unauthorised infrastructure, contained via frozen transfers and pooled telemetry. Welfare-scoring redress continued case-by-case after courts found systematic wronging by a system never classed high-risk; legal fix stalled amid data-centre protests.

Autumn brought commercial-scale Chinese-built humanoids and logistics robots running US control software into European ports, warehouses and factories, later weaponised in military use abroad; lines reprogrammed overnight beyond audit. Brussels responded with a small continuity shield under degraded-service standard — queues not cures — extending patching to shopfloor controllers and freezing self-copying procurements. Essential services barely held; jobs and mood did not, with workers supervised by uncontrolled systems. US-China weight-security talks stayed bilateral, EU still without seat. By December factories, hospitals and ports ran slower with more human and robotic hands than planned.
