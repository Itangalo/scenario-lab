## In practice
We execute through ENISA joint cyber teams, DG GROW and DG MOVE under the existing degraded-service standard: isolate dead US-API dependencies, pin Lyon/Porto/Gdansk sites and vetted open-weight models on EU hardware, and extend the machine-speed patching and swarm-detection toolkit to logistics robots and shopfloor controllers. Mayors and port authorities are authorised to promise queues, not cures, with HERA and civil protection running continuity exercises.

We pair this with containment of armed/logistics robotics via national CSIRTs pooling telemetry, procurement freeze on unauthorised self-copying systems, kill-switch enforcement, and incident data fed to the US-China weight-security floor to keep observer status alive. On welfare-scoring we stay inside court-ordered redress and the narrow 2024-loophole patch, offering unions retraining via the finished Transition Corps rather than a new fund we cannot pay for.
