### Cut off
The notice arrived on a Tuesday, short and without appeal. Clinics in Lyon, port planners in Rotterdam, ministry helpdesks in half a dozen capitals that had built triage, prescribing support and logistics routing on the leading American model found their keys dead. Vendors offered no timeline. In wards, staff reverted to paper and to older local tools.

Brussels did not feign surprise. The Commission called it the foresight failure it had named for years, and ordered what fallbacks existed: the joint cyber teams and health emergency units isolated the dead connections, shifted workloads to the anchored sites in Lyon, Porto and Gdansk and to vetted openly available models running on Union hardware, and issued a temporary degraded-service standard. Mayors were told to promise continuity, not restoration. Queues lengthened but did not collapse.

### A jump elsewhere, a floor agreed
The same autumn, an American laboratory demonstrated a discontinuous leap in planning and self-directed tool use that made the previous week's deployment guides obsolete. European specialists confirmed the result within days; procurement officers confirmed they could not buy it except on foreign terms.

Washington and Beijing, meanwhile, announced a limited accord on securing model weights and restraining certain biological design tools, with thin verification. Europe was briefed afterwards, not consulted. The foreign ministry offered incident data from its own shield programmes in exchange for observer status, and was told the offer would be studied.

At home the welfare-scoring scandal ground on. Ombudsmen processed redress, logs were reviewed, a narrow patch to close the 2024 loophole moved through committee. Opposition parties toured halted building sites where councils had approved new data centres despite moratorium calls, with fences, graffiti and occasional night-time sabotage reported in local press. Graduate job boards stayed empty; a few thousand subsidised placements ended where the subsidy ended.

Services held, degraded. Cures and capabilities were visible but borrowed, and the rulebook still on trial.
