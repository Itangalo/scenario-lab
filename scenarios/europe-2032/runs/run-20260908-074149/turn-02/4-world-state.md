### The Floodgates Open

A major AI-coordinated cyberattack struck public services across three member states, encrypting emergency dispatch systems and disrupting hospital scheduling. The attack spread through a poisoned open-source library, its tooling indistinguishable from frontier capabilities. Attribution remains unclear, but forensic analysis confirms autonomous adaptation during deployment. Defenders were overwhelmed, and recovery took weeks — a stark demonstration of how far offensive AI has outpaced defences.

Simultaneously, a powerful open-weight model, approaching but not matching the closed frontier, saw rapid dissemination after release, downloaded over 300,000 times in a week. Security researchers demonstrated its use in generating exploit code and synthetic biotoxin pathways, raising alarms. In response, the European Commission drafted the **Open-Weight Containment Framework (OWCF)**, invoking Article 114 TFEU to regulate high-risk open models. The proposal mandates a 90-day scrutiny period for models within 10 points of the frontier, with red-teaming by ENISA and restrictions on autonomous or dual-use capabilities. However, no implementation has occurred yet — the measure remains in early proposal.

### Resistance and Retreat

Despite urgency, the OWCF proposal faced immediate backlash. Civil society groups decried censorship, while developer communities warned of driving innovation underground. Hugging Face and several academic repositories refused pre-upload checks, citing academic freedom. Negotiations stalled, and no binding agreement was reached. The regulation has entered co-decision, but with uncertain passage and no timeline for adoption.

Central European resistance to **Cyber Shield for Critical Operators** hardened, delaying implementation further. The **InvestAI Gigafactories** and **Tech Sovereignty Package** remained frozen, eroding long-term leverage. With **ai_capability** rising by 1.5 and dependence deepening, EU sovereignty slipped. Public sentiment dipped as high-profile disruptions mounted and fears of uncontrolled AI spread.

### Political Costs Mount

The OWCF proposal, though timely and ambitious, failed to gain immediate traction. No coalition secured, no enforcement mechanism activated. The political cost of advancing **Cyber Shield for Critical Operators** — the only new measure this turn — was fully charged, with a bonus earned due to the cyber_test_shot event. However, the draft OWCF was not adopted as a new measure in this turn; it remains under preparation, not formally added to the portfolio. The narrative of overreach reflects perception, not structural violation: only one new measure was initiated.

Public sentiment fell below political capital, eliminating any legitimacy boost. The EU responded to the crisis — but with delay, division, and constrained agency. The gap between threat and response widens.
