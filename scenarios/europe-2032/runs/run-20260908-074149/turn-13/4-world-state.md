### The Silence After Collapse

The EU enters July 2032 in political stasis. With political capital at zero and public sentiment in freefall, no new measures launch. The Union’s AI governance infrastructure stands complete on paper — the Sovereign Cyber Shield, Emergency Audit, and Incident Response Corps all finish their mandates — but without activation rights, judicial cooperation, or member-state buy-in, their operational impact is nominal. ENISA coordinates in theory; in practice, half the bloc denies access.

A major, automated cyber intrusion sweeps through regional health and transport networks. Though contained within weeks, the breach exposes systemic fragility. Resilience climbs to 74.0: +3 from the finished Shield, +2 from the Emergency Audit, and +3 from the Corps’ formal completion. Yet the gains are technical, not institutional — the systems work where they are allowed in, and nowhere else.

Global AI capability advances to 73.0, driven by autonomous coordination tools now embedded in offensive cyber stacks. Openweight capability jumps to 68.0, closing the gap as leaked training methods and fragmented model shards circulate through underground research networks. AI safety erodes further, down to 2.0, as oversight bodies lack authority and enforcement capacity.

The Taiwan blockade begins, halting advanced semiconductor shipments. The EU’s leverage in lithography and materials becomes a geopolitical flashpoint. Washington pressures for export controls; Beijing demands exemptions. The Union cannot respond — no common position, no enforcement mechanism, no political room to act. The supply-chain crisis deepens dependence on foreign compute, accelerating the erosion of sovereignty.

Hungary formalises a bilateral AI security pact with a non-EU hyperscaler, granting exclusive access to national infrastructure telemetry. The move bypasses EU coordination entirely, triggering a quiet but decisive fracture. Sovereignty drops to 20.0: −1 from capability rise over 2, −2 from member_state_defection. Political capital remains at 0.0 — no portfolio costs, no new measures, no proposal bonus. Public sentiment holds at rock bottom.

### The Weight of Inaction

No measure is proposed, no priority set. The EU is not failing — it has already failed. It waits, not for a plan, but for a rupture that might force a path forward.
