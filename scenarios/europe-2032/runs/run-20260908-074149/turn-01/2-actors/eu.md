## New measure
**Cyber Shield for Critical Operators (CS-CO)**  
Launches mandatory intrusion detection and response protocols across energy, water and port infrastructure, with shared monitoring and red-teaming.

## Priority
**Cyber Shield for Critical Operators (CS-CO)** — because the cyber_test_shot exposed a blind spot in our resilience that, if left unaddressed, could collapse public order and disable the state during a crisis, regardless of how advanced our sovereignty or oversight becomes.

## In practice
We are activating Article 12 of the NIS2 Directive with immediate effect, designating AI-coordinated intrusion as a cross-border threat requiring harmonised response. The ENISA agency will lead the drafting of binding technical standards for intrusion detection systems in electricity transmission, water utilities and major ports — all sectors where the cyber_test_shot revealed dwell times exceeding 30 days. By December, we will table a Council Regulation under the internal market legal base to mandate deployment of these systems by Q2 2028, with funding drawn from the Digital Europe Programme and the Recovery and Resilience Facility top-ups. Member states with weaker cyber capacity — particularly in Central and Eastern Europe — will receive technical assistance teams from the EU Cyber Crisis Liaison Organisation Network (CCLON).

We are also establishing a joint red-teaming unit within ENISA, staffed by ethical hackers and AI behaviour analysts, to simulate adversarial AI attacks on these systems twice a year. This will feed into live threat intelligence sharing via the newly upgraded CSIRT network. Resistance is expected from national regulators who view this as an encroachment on sovereignty, and from operators citing cost. We are offering 70% co-financing for the first two years and fast-tracking access to InvestAI Gigafactory power contracts as leverage. This is not just about patching a vulnerability — it is about proving that the EU can enforce minimum resilience standards when capability outpaces control.
