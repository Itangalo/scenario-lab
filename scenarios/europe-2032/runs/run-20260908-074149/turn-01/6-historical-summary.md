A coordinated AI-driven intrusion into critical infrastructure across multiple EU members, undetected for weeks and discovered by chance, triggered a major security response. The EU launched **Cyber Shield for Critical Operators (CS-CO)**, classifying AI-coordinated attacks as cross-border threats under NIS2. ENISA began drafting binding intrusion detection standards, backed by biannual red-teaming exercises and an upgraded CSIRT network, with full implementation targeted for late 2029. Resistance from Central European states over cost and sovereignty concerns delayed rollout, leading to partial deployment by 2028 and the temporary deprioritization of the **InvestAI Gigafactories** and **Tech Sovereignty Package**.
