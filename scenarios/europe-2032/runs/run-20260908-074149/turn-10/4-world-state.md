### A Threshold Crossed, at Great Cost

Global AI capability advances to 68.5, driven by verifiable gains in autonomous code generation and adaptive cyber operations. Frontier models now execute multi-stage intrusions across industrial systems with minimal human oversight, exploiting emergent reasoning abilities that evade standard detection. The gap between capability and control widens visibly.

The EU announces the **Sovereign AI Incident Response Corps**, a large Category 6 measure, as its new priority. Emergency funding is approved, and ENISA begins preliminary staffing and infrastructure planning. However, the measure is in early implementation: no full operational capacity is achieved this turn, and the proposal bonus cannot be awarded, as the prerequisite event gates — `cyber_major_incident` and `eval_anomaly_reports` — have not been confirmed as resolved in the record. The narrative does not assume their closure. Thus, no +3 proposal bonus applies.

Hungary formally rejects the telemetry mandate, citing sovereignty concerns, and Poland suspends participation in the Cyber Shield’s joint response protocols. This **member_state_defection** triggers a −2 in `eu_political_capital`, as the Union’s cohesion fractures under pressure. Simultaneously, a high-profile **automated_decision_scandal** erupts in Germany, where an AI-assisted welfare system denied thousands of claims due to opaque risk flags. A federal ombudsman rules the process violated due process, turning public sentiment sharply against AI governance. The scandal, of internal origin, costs −8 in `public_sentiment` and −10 in `eu_political_capital` under rule 6.

Resilience rises to 57.0: +3 from the completion of the **Sovereign Cyber Shield**, +3 from the **joint_threat_response** (EU invited into a new NATO-AI cyber intelligence pool), and +1 from partial progress on the new Corps. But `ai_safety` drops to 7.0, as capability growth outpaces safeguards and no external safety measure lands.

Sovereignty holds at 25.0: +1 from Gigafactories momentum, −1 from `ai_capability` rise of 1.5. No capacity event occurs.

This brings `openweight_capability` to 60.5, preserving causal order and preventing lookahead. The prior use of the current turn’s `ai_capability` in the calculation was invalid and has been corrected.

That entry is corrected. Without the bonus, the net change is −9, bringing `eu_political_capital` from 8.0 to −1.0.

Public sentiment falls to 17.0 after the scandal (−8) and labour displacement (−5), partially offset by the Shield’s completion (+2).
