### The Weight of Enforcement

The EU enters turn 9 attempting to impose order through mandate, but momentum is against it. The **Mandate for Real-Time Auditability** clears legal approval under Article 114 TFEU and technically finishes this turn — a small measure in category 1, delivering partial assurance. EAAD telemetry is now required for all strategic compute sites, and the EIB freezes final disbursements to non-compliant Gigafactories. Yet enforcement sparks backlash: Germany and Austria file preliminary objections with the CJEU, arguing disproportionate centralisation. Compliance is nominal, not operational.

Meanwhile, the **Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative** misses its deadline. Despite emergency funding and crisis-mode negotiations, three member states fail to integrate defence stacks due to incompatible legacy systems and political resistance. Swarm-response protocols remain unauthorised across borders. The microgrid testbeds in Brittany and Sweden show promise, but their isolation limits scalability.

Publicly, the proof-of-service campaign falters. Live maps reveal more gaps than coverage; synthetic media takedowns are dwarfed by new incidents. SCAP retraining sees no surge in uptake. Trust erodes further after a regional election recount in Belgium, delayed by manipulated audio deepfakes traced to the distillation-enabled toolchain.

Globally, AI capability rises to 66.5 as adaptive attacks evolve. Early signs of a **distillation breakthrough** emerge this turn — logged in the notepad as newly emergent — enabling more efficient model replication. This begins to widen access to high-capability models on consumer hardware, but effects are still in their infancy. Openweight capability increases moderately to 55.0, reflecting early diffusion rather than full deployment. No safety gains follow: while audit mandates exist on paper, they are not yet applied to active systems. Resilience dips as defences prove fragmented.

Political capital falls. The portfolio charge remains heavy, and credibility losses from delayed delivery outweigh symbolic wins. Sovereignty declines further amid energy dependence and contested control.

### A System Under Strain

The Union acts decisively, but cohesion frays. It enforces standards without having first secured unity — and now pays the price in legitimacy, effectiveness, and trust.
