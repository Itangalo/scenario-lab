### The Shield Hardens, but Sovereignty Cracks

A major AI-driven cyberattack strikes critical infrastructure across three member states, overwhelming defences with self-modifying exploit chains. Emergency Resilience Corridors activate, and ENISA begins deploying hardened AI monitoring stacks under the Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative. Early integration in France and Germany shows promise, reducing lateral movement in grid systems, but patchy adoption elsewhere leaves gaps. The attack underscores the urgency of linking infrastructure to real-time defence.

Meanwhile, the EU attempts to salvage its crumbling AI sovereignty. The InvestAI Gigafactories near completion, but without guaranteed energy autonomy or integration into the Cyber Shield, they risk becoming powerful yet exposed assets. The Commission issues binding directives under Article 114 TFEU, mandating integration with ENISA’s telemetry and EAAD logging. However, resistance flares in Germany and Austria, which reject the energy integration as an overreach. A compromise is offered—co-governance via a new Joint Oversight Panel—but trust is thin.

Microgrid projects in Brittany and northern Sweden are fast-tracked as Projects of Common Interest, bypassing ENTSO-E gridlock. Still, delays in permitting and supply-chain bottlenecks slow deployment. The vision of isolated, auditable compute remains aspirational.

Public sentiment dips further as synthetic media floods regional elections, sowing confusion. Though not yet confirmed as coordinated, the incidents feed narratives of digital decay. The transparency portal launches, but few trust its metrics. SCAP retraining expands, offering displaced workers cyber-monitor roles, yet uptake is sluggish.

Globally, offensive AI advances. Automated attack generation grows more adaptive, pushing `ai_capability` to 63.5. Open-weight models creep toward 51.0, aided by leaked tools. No safety breakthrough occurs, and `ai_safety` erodes. Resilience gains slightly from early shield deployment, but systemic fragility persists.

### Reckoning Without Leverage

The EU’s attempt to enforce integration fails to fully land. Political capital, already strained, erodes further. Sovereignty slips as capability outpaces control.
