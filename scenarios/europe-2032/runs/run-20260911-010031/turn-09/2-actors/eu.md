## In practice
We run this through DG DIGIT and DG SANTE under existing Digital Europe and EU4Health envelopes, with the redress cell from M9 certifying each deployment: only systems with audit logs, human appeal in days, and certified offline backup qualify for the Dividend label and EU co-funding. Municipalities and hospitals already on the containment patch get first scale-up, using middle-power compact supply where US hyperscaler pledges evaporated in the investment collapse.

In parallel we hold M10 to its finish: HERA keeps sequencing surge and therapy procurement, and we make Dividend funding conditional on telemetry sharing to pull the defecting member state back without a new Council fight. No new compute build is promised this turn; with valuations reset we preserve gigafactory permits and grid reservations cheaply while directing scarce capital to what restores permission to act.
