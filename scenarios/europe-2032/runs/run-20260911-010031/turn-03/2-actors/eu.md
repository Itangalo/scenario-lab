## New measure
**EU Incident Continuity Backstop**
Provides EU-coordinated recovery teams, cross-border mutual aid and a temporary state-backed reinsurance backstop to keep hit public services and operators running after the automated attack.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, and why now is that the major incident has made defenders visibly behind and insurers threatening withdrawal — without a backstop essential services degrade and Shield uptake collapses.

## Priority
EU Incident Continuity Backstop (new) – because containing the live incident and keeping services and cover in place outranks even gigafactories and Shield drills for this half-year.

## In practice
We are activating the Cyber Solidarity Act's Emergency Mechanism and Reserve via ENISA and DG CNECT, deploying joint incident-response teams to the worst-hit grids, hospitals and municipal IT, with EU-paid recovery licences and telemetry pooled from M4. ECOFIN authorises a temporary EIB/EU-budget reinsurance backstop after quiet coordination with finance ministries to prevent insurer withdrawal.

In parallel we keep M1/M2 site works and M3 drills moving but subordinate: one gigafactory breaking ground continues, Taiwan-risk shipping advice stays, and export-control tightening is answered for now by holding ASML leverage and preparing a middle-power coordination line for next turn rather than retaliating immediately. Interior and health ministers get recovery money conditional on adopting Shield hardening and staffing.
