### Containment weeks
The spring began with ambulances outside a regional hospital and stayed there for weeks. A modified pathogen — investigators said design help had come from a genome model — sickened dozens, killed a handful, and forced isolation wards and contact tracing across two member states. Containment held, but only just. In the same weeks a preprint claimed a genome model could draft a viable human-infecting organism with non-expert prompting. Methodologists attacked it; editors left it up.

Brussels moved where it could, but much stalled or stayed partial. The health emergency authority surged sequencing and pushed stockpiled protective gear to hospitals, while orders for labs and clinics to stream incident data into the joint surveillance cell built with allies saw uneven compliance and delays. Gating of access to high-risk synthesis models behind identity checks pending audit was announced, with rollout still phased. Health and trade officials began early talks on licensed production of newly arrived tailored therapies on European soil with Japanese and Korean partners, but no deal closed this turn and supply remained import-dependent.

Three older files closed at once. The trusted compute compact with middle powers was signed, securing joint-buying and supply-chain cover. The exploit-containment patch — offline kits, locked backups, mandatory sharing — finished rollout to towns and hospitals, and this time the backups were used for real. The redress cell for automated decisions published its first re-decisions and suspensions.

It was not clean. Gating orders were evaded through mirrored weights already on private servers, and copies circulating outside any audit kept capable open weights widely usable, keeping open capability close behind the frontier. The member state running its own hosting deal took priority therapy doses while refusing to rejoin common telemetry. Insurers hardened terms: no certified backup, no renewal. Leaked benchmark chatter about an unreleased system filled the background noise.

Hospitals degraded rather than stopped. The public saw both rescue and dependence, and fear outweighed gratitude. With three measures closing at cost and no new political win to offset the strain, goodwill eroded rather than surged.
