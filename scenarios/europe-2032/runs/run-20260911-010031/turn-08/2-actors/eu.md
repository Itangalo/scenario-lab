## In practice
We mandate HERA with ECDC and ENISA to run the response under the Serious Cross-Border Threats Regulation and the Critical Services Shield playbooks: stockpiled PPE and sequencing surge to hospitals, mandatory incident telemetry from health and labs to the joint cyber-bio cell, and temporary KYC/gated access for genome synthesis models pending the redress audit cell's review. Funding is reprogrammed from EU4Health and Digital Europe, no fresh appropriation to avoid a Council fight.

In parallel DG SANTE with DG TRADE negotiates EU-anchored supply of the breakthrough therapies — manufacturing or licensing on EU soil via the Trusted Compute Compact partners Japan/Korea — so benefit does not arrive as pure dependence. We offer the defecting member state priority access conditional on rejoining common telemetry and backup certification, using insurers' demands as leverage. Gigafactory phase-one stays on permits/grid only; no new money until containment stabilises.
