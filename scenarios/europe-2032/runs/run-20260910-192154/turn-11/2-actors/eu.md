## New measure
**EU Clinical Continuity Fallback**
Keeps hospitals, ministries and essential firms running after the frontier cutoff by switching clinical and administrative workloads to EU-hosted open-weight and licensed models already on EU soil, with emergency procurement and reimbursement clearance.
This is Diffusion, adoption and public trust, category 9, and why now is the access denial plus the medical breakthrough together: dependence just stopped being abstract in wards that had built on a model now withdrawn, and the only way to capture the health benefit without deepening dependence is a fallback patients can see working.

## Priority
EU Clinical Continuity Fallback, because with frontier access cut and sentiment at 6 the two-year commitment to absorb shocks without losing essential services is decided in hospitals first, not in the Accord room.

## In practice
We run health continuity as triage. DG SANTE and HERA with EMA issue emergency conformity and reimbursement guidance allowing EU-hosted models for triage, imaging support and admin, funded by reprogrammed EU4Health and Digital Europe money; ENISA and the M11 islanding cells enforce isolated deployment so the fallback does not reopen the attack surface just patched.

We keep M12 alive on low burn. EEAS holds the observer bid open in Washington and Beijing, offering incident data and JRC eval capacity, but no capital is spent chasing accelerators this turn — every negotiator is told the cutoff proves why the fallback, not the plea, comes first. Finance ministers are not asked for new money, only for permission to reprogramme.
