## In practice
We run what we have: DG TRADE holds the Upstream Conservation licence, DG ENER and ENISA keep the re-sequenced Shield deadlines on transmission, water and health, and the EEAS keeps the Tier Access mandate to Washington/Tokyo alive for hospital and lab continuity — no new groundbreakings, no new money.

The new push is cheap and operational: ENISA with the Joint Research Centre turns the finished Anomaly Protocol into loss-of-control exercises, pre-agreed isolation thresholds, and a cross-border mutual-aid roster for grid and hospital IT, funded by reprogrammed cohesion technical assistance. Member states resisting new obligations get guidance and crews, not fines; the Commission spends convening power, not capital it does not have.
