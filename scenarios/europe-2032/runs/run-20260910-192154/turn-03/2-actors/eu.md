## Priority
European Research Compute Pool, because with capital at 24 and grid shutdowns refused to autumn, keeping the talent-and-capacity coalition alive is the only part of our resilient-Europe commitment still moving this turn.

## In practice
We push DG CNECT and the AI Office to issue an implementing act under the AI Act's systemic-risk and incident-reporting bases, tasking ENISA and the Joint Research Centre with a 90-day anomaly triage cell that gets model-access for vetted EU evaluators and obliges providers serving the EU to file serious-incident/near-miss reports. Export-control escalation is handled defensively: we align dual-use licensing via the Foreign Affairs/Trade formation to protect the pooled-research and Gigafactory supply chain, not to start a new controls regime.

In parallel we sweat the existing books: the finished Grid Shield's autumn shutdown windows are held via Energy Council compliance deadlines with Connecting Europe co-funding, and the Gigafactories/tech-sovereignty lines are kept to permitting and grid-queue sequencing with TSO rebuilds rather than new money, to stop engineers and transformers being double-booked. The university charter gets the vouchers and return fellowships already endorsed, framed as keeping European control of infrastructure we can absorb shocks on.
