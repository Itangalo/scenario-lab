## New measure
**EU Displaced Entry-Level Continuity Fund**
Puts wage insurance, paid retraining and employer hiring incentives into the named displaced sectors with worst-hit graduates first, while pushing the new automated patching and swarm-detection tooling out through the islanding cells and hospital fallback deployments already on the ground.
This is Labour and social protection, category 7, and why now is that measurable non-replacement of entry roles plus sentiment at 3 is exactly the cohesion loss the two-year commitment exists to prevent, and the cyber-defence and safety breakthroughs finally give us something cheap to deploy alongside income support so protection does not read as another unfunded promise.

## Priority
EU Displaced Entry-Level Continuity Fund, because with capital at 10 and sentiment at 3 the absorb-shocks commitment is decided by whether disappearing graduate jobs become lasting hostility, not by another Accord bid.

## In practice
We run this as triage on the existing books. DG EMPL with DG CNECT reprograms ESF+ and Digital Europe leftovers into wage insurance and retraining vouchers for law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration, with EPSCO clearance for temporary hiring subsidies; ENISA and the M11 islanding teams plus the M13 clinical fallback hosts push the breakthrough automated patching and certified control tooling into municipalities, hospitals and mid-size suppliers as part of the same site visits, so no new agency or vote is needed.

We ask Finance ministers for no new money, only reprogramming permission, and we keep the US-China observer bid on low burn. EEAS holds the seat offer open with incident data, but negotiators are told every euro of persuasion this turn goes to mayors, hospital directors and employer federations who must hire and patch for the Fund to land.
