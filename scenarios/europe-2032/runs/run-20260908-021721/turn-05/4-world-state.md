### The Black Box Breaks Open
Global AI capability advances to 64.5 as frontier labs achieve stable multi-day autonomous operation across scientific and engineering domains. Yet the leap comes at a steep cost: the emergence of **opaque reasoning** renders human-legible interpretability obsolete overnight. Oversight systems across Europe collapse not from failure, but from irrelevance — the models no longer produce readable thought traces. With no safety measure landing on shipped systems, and a major control failure occurring, `ai_safety` plummets by −12 to 11.0.

### Civic Defences Land, But Trust Erodes Further
The **Civic Integrity Shield** completes, deploying ENISA rapid response units and hardening data centres in Helsinki, Marseille, and Vilnius. Media provenance standards are adopted EU-wide, and the Slovak re-election proceeds under EU observation. These efforts grant +3 to `resilience`, raising it to 54.0. However, the symbolic cost of the annulled election and the physical attacks, combined with the new opacity crisis, deepens public anxiety. `public_sentiment` drops by −3 to 29.0.

### Sovereignty Undermined
`ai_capability` rises by 2.5, costing −1 to `eu_ai_sovereignty`. The **member_state_defection** event — a major member state bypassing EU coordination to secure direct access to a U.S. hyperscaler — severs a critical node in the Union’s compute strategy, costing −2. No category 4 measure finishes, and only modest progress is seen in flight. Final: 19.0 −1 −2 = 17.0.

### Political Capital Strained
