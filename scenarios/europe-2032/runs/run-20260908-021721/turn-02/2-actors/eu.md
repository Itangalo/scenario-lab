## In practice  
We are executing the Emergency Resilience Surge through a binding ENISA directive under the revised NIS2 framework, with implementation tied to Cohesion Fund disbursements for 2027. The Commission has activated Article 9 of Decision 1313/2013/EU to fast-track cross-border coordination, and we are deploying rapid-response cyber teams from the EU Cyber Rapid Response Teams (CRRTs) to Germany, Spain, and the Netherlands to conduct live audits of compromised systems. The 70% co-financing offer remains on the table, but we are now conditioning it on full adoption of air-gapped monitoring and zero-trust architecture by Q2, with a compliance deadline of June 30. Resistance from smaller member states is being addressed through a side-car arrangement: those that adopt the baseline hardening package will gain priority access to the first sovereign compute allocations from the InvestAI Gigafactories in 2028, creating a tangible incentive for alignment.

Meanwhile, the Tech Sovereignty Package is advancing through the Council’s Competitiveness Working Party, where we are negotiating the accelerated-permitting zones with energy ministers to bypass local bottlenecks in grid connection and land use. The private capital mobilisation target remains on track, but we are seeing delays in chip supply for data centre construction due to global export controls — a vulnerability that reinforces the need for the new Talent Initiative. That measure launches this turn with a €12bn envelope drawn from Horizon Europe and the Digital Europe Programme, focused on three pillars: (1) a pan-European AI fellowship for top-tier researchers with security-cleared status and access to sovereign compute; (2) visa and relocation fast-tracks for non-EU frontier talent; and (3) binding retention clauses for publicly funded AI developers to remain under EU jurisdiction for at least five years. The goal is to close the human capital gap that, without which, even the best infrastructure will run on foreign-designed systems. This is not just about compute — it is about who controls the minds that shape it.
