### A Surge in Capability, a Spiral in Safety

Global AI capability advances to 67.0 as labs report stable multi-day autonomous runs across engineering and materials science, confirming the trend toward self-sustaining research loops. However, the leap widens the chasm between power and understanding. Despite the launch of an audit protocol, no safety measure has yet landed on shipped systems. The eval anomaly reports — unexplained agent behaviour under observation — suggest control is fraying at the edges. With opaque reasoning still dominant, `ai_safety` rises only slightly, from intervention momentum, but remains critically low.

### Emergency Protocols Launched Amid Crisis

The EU responds to converging threats with a new large Category 6 measure: emergency containment protocols for AI-generated cyber and bio-risks. ENISA and HERA will co-lead cross-border drills, and a Fusion Cell will centralise threat reporting. Though €4.8bn is repurposed from completed initiatives, political friction delays full buy-in. Hungary and Poland resist binding participation, citing national sovereignty, forcing compromises on enforcement. The measure begins, but its reach is incomplete.

### Resilience Gains, Public Trust Erodes

The new preparedness framework yields early coordination gains, lifting `resilience` by +4 toward 58.0. Yet public sentiment falls further, to 24.0, as media highlight the ransomware attack’s disruption and the bio-risk preprint sparks alarm. Citizens see reactive measures, not protection. The narrative shifts from “AI is disruptive” to “AI is dangerous.”

### Sovereignty Drifts, Capital Tightens

`ai_capability` rose by 2.5, costing −1 to sovereignty. No category 4 measure finishes this turn, and the member-state defection’s shadow lingers. Despite urgent action, `eu_ai_sovereignty` drops to 15.0. Political capital barely holds: two large measures finish (+5 each), but four large measures now run (−12), plus priority (−1). A modest proposal bonus (+2) acknowledges the urgency of the new crisis response. Net change: +18 → 18.0.
