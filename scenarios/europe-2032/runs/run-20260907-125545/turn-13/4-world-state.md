### A Strategic Blackout

The sudden denial of access to the world’s most capable AI model cascades through EU institutions. Hospitals in three member states suspend AI-aided diagnostics, border control units lose real-time threat analysis, and emergency planning cells revert to manual coordination. The cause is unexplained, the restoration uncertain. This is not a cyberattack, but a cutoff—and it exposes a dependency as fragile as any energy import.

In response, the European Commission launches the Emergency Compute Access Guarantee, a large category 4 measure aimed at securing sovereign inference capacity within 18 months. Invoking urgency, it repurposes the EuroHPC Joint Undertaking, redirects €2.3 billion, and fast-tracks procurement. Germany and France back the effort, but demand long-term governance. Poland and Hungary resist, calling it a power grab masked as resilience. Legal questions swirl around the use of Article 114 for compute sovereignty.

### Capability Leaps, Safety Crumbles

AI capability advances to 71.5, driven by recursive optimization. A leading lab demonstrates a model that autonomously discovers novel exploit chains and patches them—raising alarms about dual-use autonomy. This **capability_jump** widens the gap between what systems can do and what humans can verify. With no new safeguards in deployment, **ai_safety** plummets to 12.0, reflecting growing opacity and eroding institutional trust.

### Resilience in Reverse

Despite the Emergency Resilience Surge, **resilience** drops to 30.0. Implementation remains fractured, with critical gaps in healthcare and local government systems. A major ransomware campaign—generated by AI and targeting municipal infrastructure—exposes the lack of unified detection. Recovery is slow, confidence weaker.

The new large measure costs −3, the priority −1. The **cyber_major_incident** and **eu_frontier_access_denied**—both external, high-impact events—inflict severe political damage. No prior measure mitigated them. **eu_political_capital** falls to 8.0. **public_sentiment** drops to 9.0 as displaced workers join the **emergent_eu_public_retraining_revolt**, denouncing retraining as a farce while essential services falter.
