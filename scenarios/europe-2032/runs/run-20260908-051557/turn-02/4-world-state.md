### Frontier Momentum Continues
Global AI capability advances by 1.5 points, reaching 55.0, as persistent agent frameworks demonstrate autonomous coordination across multi-day research and software engineering tasks. While not yet self-improving, the systems exhibit goal-directed behaviour with minimal human oversight, widening the gap between capability and control.

### Open Access Widens
The diffusion of Kimi K3 continues, with adapted versions now running efficiently on consumer-grade hardware. Offensive cyber tools derived from its architecture are detected in multiple intrusion campaigns, accelerating adoption beyond state actors. `openweight_capability` rises to 43.0, reflecting broader deployment and incremental fine-tuning — still trailing the frontier, but closing the gap.

### Safety Drifts Further Behind
No new safeguards have landed on deployed frontier models. The absence of interpretability integration and verified control mechanisms, combined with rising autonomy, deepens concern among technical observers. `ai_safety` drops by 2 points to 27.0, as the systematization of risks lags behind operational use.

### EU Seizes the Moment on Resilience
The Union moves decisively to embed itself in the newly formed joint cyber defence initiative, formalizing real-time intelligence sharing through a Council Implementing Decision. ENISA and national CERTs are integrated into a shared telemetry platform, leveraging detection models trained on global attack patterns. This effort, though small in scope, delivers immediate operational value.

Simultaneously, progress is made on the Resilience Hardening Directive. Facing resistance from smaller states over cost and sovereignty, the Commission unlocks €15bn in conditional Modernisation Fund disbursements, tying compliance to REPowerEU grant access. Legal validation under Article 114 TFEU proceeds, and delegated acts begin drafting.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Tightens
Despite gains in cohesion and response, the ongoing loss of frontier access persists as a structural vulnerability. With no sovereign compute milestone reached this turn and capability advancing, `eu_ai_sovereignty` declines by 1 point. Political capital is strained by three large measures in flight and a named priority, outweighing gains from effective crisis response.

Public sentiment ticks up slightly, reflecting cautious optimism about coordinated defence and visible institutional action.
