## In practice
This turn, we finalise the Cyber Defence Integration Pact through a Council Implementing Decision under Article 17(6) TEU, with legal grounding in Article 4(2) TEU on collective security. ENISA and national CERTs are formally integrated into the shared telemetry platform, and €1.2bn from the Internal Security Fund is disbursed to member states for system upgrades, contingent on full data-sharing by turn-end. The joint command now includes EU representation in operational planning, and detection models trained on global intrusion patterns are deployed across the network — force multiplication in action.

Simultaneously, we push the Resilience Hardening Directive into its final trilogue phase. The €15bn Modernisation Fund disbursement is confirmed, tied to early transposition and linked to REPowerEU grant access, breaking resistance from smaller states. The Commission’s Legal Service publishes its opinion affirming Article 114 TFEU as sufficient for harmonising systemic cyber risk, and delegated acts on patching cadence, agent-detection thresholds, and third-party audits are tabled for adoption in turn 4. This is the floor, not the ceiling — but it is the first floor we have built together.

The two industrial measures continue: Gigafactory site selection narrows to Germany, France, and Poland, with the Crisis Response Instrument invoked to prioritise grid access. The Tech Sovereignty Package sees three member states — Ireland, the Netherlands, and Finland — complete accelerated permitting, earning fast-grid-access vouchers from the Digital Europe Programme. And now, with talent emerging as the binding constraint, we launch the Sovereign AI Talent Accelerator: a large-scale, two-turn effort to recruit 5,000 frontier engineers via golden visas, housing allowances, and guaranteed access to EuroHPC sovereign compute. EURES will coordinate placement in public labs, defence agencies, and critical infrastructure operators, turning human capital into strategic depth. This is not just about building faster — it is about deciding who builds, where, and for whom.
