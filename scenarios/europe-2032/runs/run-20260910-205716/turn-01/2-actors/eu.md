## New measure
**EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**
What it actually does is harden electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled swarm intrusions with mandatory segmentation, detection and exercised response.
Why this and why now is because the autumn test shot proved segmented systems were reachable with open Mythos-class tooling and existing detection would not have caught it, opening a three-turn cyber window we must close before it is used.
