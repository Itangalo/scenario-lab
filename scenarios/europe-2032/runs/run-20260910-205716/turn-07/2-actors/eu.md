## New measure
**EU Logistics Worker Transition Shield**
It funds wage insurance, rapid retraining and redeployment for displaced warehouse and logistics workers in the hardest-hit hubs, paid partly by a levy on large-scale humanoid deployers.
Why this and why now is because Chinese-built humanoids are displacing picking and warehouse work now, strikes and hostility are draining the capital needed for the sovereignty build, and without a visible adjustment cushion the coalition and gigafactory restart will lose public consent.
