## New measure
**EU OT Rapid Reaction Reserve**
It stands up pooled operational-technology incident teams that can isolate, rebuild and restart grid, port and water systems within hours of an AI-enabled intrusion.
Why this and why now is because the capability jump in code and long cyber operations means the rehearsal footholds found in autumn can be turned into simultaneous disruptive action within weeks, and the existing Shield audits without hands-on response capacity will not hold.
