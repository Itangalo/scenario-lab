## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because without domestic compute anchoring the secured frontier access, both resilience operations and any leverage over ASML coercion remain dependent on US goodwill.

## In practice
We are executing the two compute lines already on the books through DG CNECT and the EuroHPC JU: Gigafactories site selection tied to grid connection and state-aid clearance in Competitiveness Council, and the tech sovereignty package's accelerated-permitting zones with EIB de-risking to pull private data-centre capital. On ASML we are moving the file to Trade and the Anti-Coercion Instrument track, coordinating with The Hague to make servicing cuts a Union-level coercion case rather than a bilateral Dutch concession.

This turn we stand up the Hardening Pact via ENISA and the NIS2 Cooperation Group: mandatory incident retrospectives from the two affected EU grid operators, staged breaker-credential audits, segmented backup controls for transmission, port and water utilities, and a funded cross-border exercise before winter. Money comes from existing CEF-Digital and Civil Protection Mechanism top-ups, to avoid a new budget fight while M1 and M2 burn capital.
