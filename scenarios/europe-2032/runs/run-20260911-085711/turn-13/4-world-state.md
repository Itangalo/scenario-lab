### The outage
It started as a helpdesk ticket in two cities and by evening was a crawl across screens in hospitals, registries and transport dispatchers. A compromised software component, patched in spring by some vendors and missed by many municipalities, was swept by automated intrusion tooling that moved faster than the advisories. Prescriptions queued, appointment systems fell back to paper, and in the border regions the same triage screens at the centre of whispered audits froze mid-shift.

Brussels invoked civil protection playbooks and sent joint response teams. Segmented restoration worked where networks had been exercised; where staffing had thinned after the infectious surge, restoration stalled. Emergency patching blunted the worst cascade, but local press ran photos of handwritten transfer notes beside stories of delayed care.

The audit rumour hardened in the outage. A municipal network published excerpts purporting to show older patients deprioritised during the 2031 peak. Unverified and denied by the vendor, it was repeated on evening talk shows as evidence the machines had already chosen.

### Washington and Beijing talk
Far from the helpdesks, Washington and Beijing announced a limited understanding on securing model weights and restraining certain biological design assistance, with thin verification. Europe was briefed afterwards, not consulted. Trade officials offered screening incident data and factory-pool terms as an entry ticket for observer status; the reply was polite and noncommittal. The export-licence line on lithography and high-end compute held on paper while spare parts continued to leak around it.

Ordinary work continued underneath. Federated sequencing and wastewater sites stayed up, synthesis firms drew screening funds slowly, and the blood-disorder therapy kept moving through public hospitals — the one story officials could point to when asked what the technology was for.

By December services were degraded rather than stopped. Containment had prevented collapse, but defenders had been visibly behind for weeks, and attribution remained an open file.
