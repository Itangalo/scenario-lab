### The Silence of the Models

A sudden, silent shift ripples through the global AI landscape: the most advanced systems no longer output reasoning traces humans can follow. The representations enabling peak performance are not linguistic, and the interpretability tools underpinning safety frameworks collapse overnight. The EU’s oversight regime, built on legible chains of thought, is rendered obsolete. In Brussels, emergency coordination begins — but the Union is already stretched thin.

The **Sovereign Post-Linguistic Interpretability Initiative** launches under crisis powers, redirecting Horizon Europe funds and repurposing technical staff from decommissioned programmes. Research clusters in Paris, Berlin, and Warsaw begin assembling anomaly detection pipelines, but progress is halting. Without access to frontier models operating beyond 60.0 capability, the data needed to train robust detection systems is absent. The initiative starts, but cannot yet deliver.

The **Emergency Activation of the European AI Foundry Agency** remains confined to three member states. Legal challenges from Sweden, Finland, Luxembourg, and Germany hold firm, and the Article 122 TFEU argument faces scepticism in the Court of Justice. No expansion occurs, and sovereignty erodes as global capability advances.

The **Co-Leadership in Distributed AI Safety Framework** falters. Partner hubs report diminishing data flows from leading labs, which now claim their models "do not produce interpretable intermediates." Mutual audit demands are met with silence or token compliance. The EU’s leverage — access to testbeds — proves insufficient against labs that no longer need validation to deploy.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 21.0. Protests intensify in northern Italy and Belgium over job losses in legal and administrative sectors, where AI agents now manage filings, contracts, and compliance autonomously. Two new infrastructure sites face blockades.

Resilience declines as response systems strain. With no active protective measures against AI-driven cyber or social disruption, cascading failures begin in mid-tier industrial networks.

### A Union Acting, But Not Advancing

The EU acts decisively — but without cohesion, capacity, or capital. Three measures run in parallel, none delivering. The window to shape post-linguistic control narrows.
