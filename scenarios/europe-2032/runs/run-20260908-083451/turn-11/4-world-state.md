### Holding the Line, Losing Ground

The European AI Foundry continues training under emergency powers, its sovereign model now at capability 58.0, but expansion halts as legal challenges from Sweden, Finland, and Luxembourg gain traction. Germany’s refusal to share national compute persists, cementing a fragmented AI landscape. No new GPU seizures occur, and the Foundry’s operations remain confined to France, Poland, and the Netherlands.

A global collapse in AI investment disrupts planned compute expansions, undermining both private-sector capacity and public-private partnerships the EU had hoped to leverage. Several frontier labs scale back training runs, slowing the pace of global capability growth — but only slightly. `ai_capability` rises by 0.5, reaching 64.0, as progress decelerates but does not stall.

The EU’s social safety net measure, the European AI Transition and Wage Insurance Programme, concludes. While it delivered support to displaced workers in key sectors, its end coincides with a surge in job losses as firms replace entry-level roles in law, accountancy, and administration with AI agents. Public frustration intensifies, and protests escalate into sabotage of data centre infrastructure in two member states. The state response is costly, and trust in AI governance erodes further.

Public sentiment drops to 21.0, dragged down by visible job displacement and physical backlash. The Commission, wary of overreach, refrains from launching new initiatives. The Emergency Activation of the European AI Foundry Agency remains the sole priority, but yields no new gains in sovereignty or political capital. With no new measures proposed, and the portfolio charge reduced by the completion of the wage insurance programme, political capital rises slightly — not from strength, but from inaction.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` declines to 10.0 as global capability advances and the EU fails to close its compute gap. The alliance posture with the US offers no relief, as American labs benefit from the safety breakthrough in agentic certification — a leap the EU’s open-weight pipeline cannot replicate.

### A Governance Vacuum Grows

An international coalition advances a distributed AI safety testing framework, inviting the EU to co-lead. The offer highlights the Union’s diminished technical standing: participation would lend legitimacy, but leadership is out of reach.
