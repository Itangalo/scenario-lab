- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure autonomous AI capacity to uphold strategic sovereignty and manage catastrophic risk on EU terms, prioritising sovereign control over compute and model weights in the face of irreversible open-weight proliferation and supply chain rupture.
  - Trigger: The openweight_frontier_release and taiwan_blockade events.
  - Grounds: With open-weight models now near frontier and compute supply disrupted for years, the window to build sovereign capacity before it becomes globally distributed has closed. Our commitment must now focus on control — not just scale — of what remains scarce: physical infrastructure, secured weights, and access to talent.
  - Evidence quoted: **openweight_frontier_release:** An open-weight release lands within months of the closed frontier rather than a year. It is downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in the first week, and whatever capability it carries is now on private hardware permanently and beyond recall. `openweight_capability` moves to within 5–10 points of `ai_capability` at a stroke.
**taiwan_blockade:** A quarantine or blockade halts advanced semiconductor exports. Compute supply for everyone outside China's domestic chain is disrupted for years, every AI policy question becomes a security question overnight, and the Union's upstream position in the supply chain becomes the most valuable thing it holds and the most dangerous thing to hold.
  - Reason: The developments directly alter compute scarcity and model control, changing the strategic value of sovereign infrastructure and weight security for the EU.
