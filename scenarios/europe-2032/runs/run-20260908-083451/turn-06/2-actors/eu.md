The Tech Sovereignty Package and Secure sovereign access to frontier model weights both conclude this turn. That is why this turn, our priority is not sovereignty as ambition, but sovereignty as survival: the right to govern our territory, protect our infrastructure, and command the loyalty of our citizens — without which no AI policy, no matter how well-designed, will hold.
