### Gigafactories Gain Ground, Sovereignty Stalls
Construction began at two InvestAI Gigafactory sites under emergency permitting, backed by redirected cohesion funds and on-site microgrids. Grid-reserve pacts secured energy continuity, and holdout member states accepted equity stakes in the operating consortium, ending legal blockades. The move solidified political buy-in but consumed significant coordination bandwidth.

The Tech sovereignty package advanced with new conditions: 40% EU-resident technical leadership and audit access for public compute models. A cap limiting non-EU cloud providers to 30% of fast-tracked data centre capacity was introduced as a resilience safeguard. However, resistance from member states with strong U.S. tech ties delayed full consensus.

The sovereign access proposal, intended to guarantee EU control over frontier model weights, remained in drafting. Fearing a high-profile defeat, the Commission paused formal introduction, instead using bilateral talks and ECOFIN preparations to build quiet alignment. Offers of lab co-governance in the Digital Reserves Agency softened opposition, but no breakthrough was reached.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Erodes
With no new safety measures enacted and ai_capability rising, the gap between capability and safety widened. The recent cyber intrusion, though contained, revealed systemic blind spots. Developers acknowledged internal agent coordination had outpaced monitoring, but no corrective action landed on deployed systems.

Open-weight models saw incremental gains through fine-tuning, lifting openweight_capability modestly. Labour displacement in legal and administrative sectors continued, feeding public unease, though no new scandal or incident shifted sentiment this turn.

### Political Capital Drains
Three large measures remained in flight, each costing political capital. With no new measure completed and no major external shock mitigated, no replenishment occurred. The priority on Gigafactories conferred no acceleration. Political capital eroded under sustained strain, dropping below the threshold where cohesion can be reliably enforced.
