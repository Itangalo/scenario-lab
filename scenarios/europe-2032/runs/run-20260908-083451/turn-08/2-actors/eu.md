## In practice  
We are declaring a state of strategic emergency. The **cyber_major_incident** — a model-generated ransomware sweep that disabled emergency services in three member states — has exposed a fatal asymmetry: we are being attacked with AI while we remain dependent on foreign powers for the very tools to understand and resist it. The **export_control_escalation**, which now restricts even allied access to advanced chips and model weights, confirms that this dependency is not temporary but structural. We are no longer negotiating for access; we are building our own.

This turn, we launch the Emergency Sovereign AI Foundry under Article 173 TFEU (research and technological development), mobilising €45bn from the Horizon Europe reserve, the Recovery and Resilience Facility, and a new sovereign AI bond issued through the EIB. The programme will be led by a newly established European AI Foundry Agency, hosted jointly by the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking and the European Defence Agency, with full authority to requisition compute, repatriate EU-trained researchers, and fast-track security clearances. The first phase will focus on reconstructing the most capable open-weight models at scale, using the InvestAI Gigafactories’ newly operational sites in France and Poland, now fully integrated into the Inter-State Compute Consortium despite Sweden and Ireland’s continued reservations.

We are also renegotiating the European AI Transition and Wage Insurance Programme in parallel. With ECOFIN still deadlocked, we are offering a compromise: firms with fewer than 500 automated roles will be exempt from the levy, but all firms above that threshold must contribute and, critically, must also participate in the Foundry’s retraining pipeline — placing displaced workers into sovereign AI development and audit roles. This links social protection directly to strategic capacity-building: every displaced legal officer becomes a potential model auditor, every displaced software engineer a sovereign developer. The EU-Wide Physical Security Corridor completes this turn; we are converting its infrastructure into permanent nodes of the Foundry network, with local liaison officers now tasked with recruiting regional talent into the programme. This is not just recovery — it is transformation.
