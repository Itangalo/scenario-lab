By end-2030 hospitals stayed manual amid intrusions, strain, chip freeze and EU split over solo hyperscaler deal.

H1 2031: modified-pathogen release in two transit hubs killed hundreds, traced to open model; hospitals locked down to manual tracing. Brussels used health-emergency powers: stocks, isolation wards, cross-border staff; cordons drained funds, slowed IT patching. Loss of readable reasoning in newest systems broke oversight; wards ordered off automated triage. Trust in AI collapsed. Defecting state's separate compute deal persisted.

Autumn 2031: control result enabled pre-deployment certification; three states piloted audited triage, first verifiable sign-offs. Brussels created small AI Office/ENISA/disease-agency cell but rollout slow as resources stayed in cordons. US-China weights/bio-design understanding excluded Europe to briefings. Care funding ended; trust stayed low.

Spring 2032: defensive turn with automated machine-speed patching and behavioural intrusion detection; Brussels pushed pre-checked kits via cyber-agency/cert cell to health, municipal, energy with deployment teams and manual fallback. Uptake patchy: large hospitals patched in weeks, small clinics/councils queued. Transit-hub cordons wound down, freeing wards and surge teams. Graduate entry jobs in law, accountancy, software support, customer ops, admin failed to return, sparking union/student marches. Washington ordered Dutch lithography champion to cut servicing/exports of older tools; Hague protested, Berlin-Paris split, Commission chose anti-coercion consultation over retaliation. Taiwan manoeuvres raised shipping insurance; job-transition map promised but budgets exhausted, sovereignty seen as thinner.
