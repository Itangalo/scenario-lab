## New measure
**EU Graduate Transition Guarantee**
Creates EU-co-funded paid transition placements, retraining stipends and employer hiring incentives for collapsed graduate entry roles in law, accountancy, software support, customer ops and admin, run through national employment services.
This now because missing entry jobs and 12-point sentiment are what will break cohesion and capital before any patch rollout can land, and our survive-ungovernable-capability commitment requires absorption to mean income and re-entry, not just hardened hospitals; a labour measure is the only affordable lever that rebuilds tolerance while M13 defends services.

We pair it with the promised job-transition map to buy time with unions/students.
