Trust in AI collapsed, protests continued; ministers gained limited credit. Defecting state's separate compute deal persisted, joint export line paper-only.

Brussels created small AI Office/ENISA/disease-agency certification cell, but rollout slow and uneven as resources stayed in cordons; worst-hit cities stayed on paper. Washington-Beijing agreed limited weights-security and bio-design understanding with verification; Europe excluded, offered transit-attack data for observer status but got only briefings.
