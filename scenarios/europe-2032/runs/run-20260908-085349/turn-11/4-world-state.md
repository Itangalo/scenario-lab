### The Black Box Era Begins

Global AI capability inches upward to 61.5 as frontier labs refine internal representations that no longer map to human language. The shift renders existing oversight systems obsolete overnight. Audit trails, interpretability tools, and regulatory review processes that depended on readable reasoning chains collapse. No alternative assurance framework is deployed, and with the Sovereign Evaluation Corps still inactive, the world enters a new phase of unverifiable AI operation. This structural blind spot drives a sharp drop in **ai_safety**, falling by 5 points to 12.0.

### Sovereignty Stalls Amid Fragmentation

The **Emergency Sovereign AI Capacity Guarantee** is formally adopted and enters the portfolio as a large, category 4 measure. However, its launch is fraught. Ireland and the Netherlands raise constitutional objections to mandatory integration of private cloud infrastructure, while Hungary demands additional fiscal concessions. Portugal, promised grid support, sees no progress. The political cost of holding the initiative is high, but no tangible sovereignty gains materialise this turn — the compute backbone exists, but coordination remains fragmented. With **ai_capability** rising (albeit modestly), and no completed category 4 measure, **eu_ai_sovereignty** holds flat at 18.0.

### Political Capital Erodes

The EU activates the **Emergency Sovereign AI Capacity Guarantee** as its priority, triggering a −3 cost for a large measure and an additional −1 for prioritisation. No proposal bonus applies: while **opaque_reasoning** and **member_state_defection** occurred, the measure was already in planning and not newly proposed this turn. **eu_political_capital** drops from 10.0 to 7.0 under the portfolio charge, reflecting mounting strain.

### Resilience Erodes With No Shield

With no category 6 measure in force and capability rising, **resilience** declines by 1 point to 49.0. The absence of hardened incident response frameworks leaves critical systems exposed to the new opacity-driven risks.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 32.0, as no visible benefit offsets growing anxiety over unaccountable systems and internal disunity.
