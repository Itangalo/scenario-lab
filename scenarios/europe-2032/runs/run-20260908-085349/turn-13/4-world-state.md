### The Frontier Advances

Global AI capability climbs to 63.0 as frontier labs deepen their use of opaque reasoning loops, enabling models to self-modify in ways even developers cannot trace. With no human-readable audit paths, safety assurance erodes further. The absence of deployed safeguards and the continued inactivity of independent review bodies keeps **ai_safety** under pressure, held only slightly above its prior low by residual institutional vigilance. It rises marginally to 8.0, reflecting early procedural momentum but no operational capacity.

### Sovereignty Secured, Just in Time

The **Emergency Sovereign AI Capacity Guarantee** completes this turn, delivering foundational compute integration across seven member states. Though fragmented by earlier supply-chain disruptions, the network reaches minimum viable scale. This completion triggers a +5 boost to **eu_ai_sovereignty**, offsetting the −2 from the prior turn’s coercion and a further −1 from capability growth. The net effect is a modest recovery to 18.0.

### A New Guard for Safety

In response to the election annulment and abrupt denial of frontier access, the EU proposes the **Sovereign Evaluation Corps**, a technically independent body intended to audit AI systems in critical sectors. The proposal clears first-stage approval amid fierce debate over its legal reach and staffing. Two member states register formal reservations, and negotiations over lab secondments remain stalled. As a category 5 measure with a projected completion in turn 16, it begins its implementation path but delivers no measurable safety benefit this turn. Early effect is acknowledged in planning circles, but no concrete outcomes emerge.

### Political Capital on Life Support

The completion of the capacity guarantee yields +4 political capital. Launching the new measure incurs a −3 cost, and naming it priority incurs −1. With no proposal bonus—the measure, while urgent, was not framed as a direct response to a recent shock—the net change from portfolio activity is +0. Combined with the completion gain, **eu_political_capital** rises to 4.0, still critically low. At this level, the Union struggles to enforce cohesion.

### Resilience Drifts Downward

With **ai_capability** rising and no active category 6 measure, **resilience** falls by 1 point to 47.0. Critical infrastructure remains exposed.

### Public Sentiment Holds

No major benefit or scandal emerges. **public_sentiment** remains at 32.0.
