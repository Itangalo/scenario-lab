## In practice

Power is secured via a 15-year PPA with Fortum, and initial runs are underway with Aleph Alpha and a consortium from the French Quantum & AI Task Force. But integration remains thin: compute is pooled, but evaluation access is still pending, and the Frontier Access Guarantee lacks activation protocols. The stack exists in pieces, not as a whole.

To close this gap, we are launching the EU Sovereign AI Stack Integration Initiative under Article 114 TFEU, leveraging the Digital Markets Act’s interoperability precedents to mandate technical and legal alignment across the three pillars. Funding will come from repurposing €12bn in stalled Innovation Fund allocations, with Commission oversight to prevent state-aid violations.

Simultaneously, we are advancing the Sovereign Evaluation Corps by finalising the legal draft for training log access — now tied to the Integration Initiative as a condition of compute access. Mistral has agreed to a pilot under the new framework, and we are fast-tracking secure data pipelines from Saclay to Garching. The Tech Sovereignty Package is nearing its final vote in the Parliament, with amendments to include the Integration Initiative’s permitting overrides. And we are holding firm on the Frontier Access Guarantee: by turn 6, we will have live failover protocols tested with the European Central Bank and the European Medicines Agency. This is not about building more — it is about making what we have actually work, together, under one roof, before the next surge finds us still divided.
