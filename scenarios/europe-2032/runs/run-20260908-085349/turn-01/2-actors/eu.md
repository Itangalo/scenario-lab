## Priority
**Emergency Resilience Surge** — Because the cyber_test_shot exposed a blind spot in detection and response that no evaluation or sovereignty measure can close in time, and because failure to act now risks cascading collapse in essential services, this turn must prioritise resilience over all other long-term bets, including our own compute build-out.

## In practice
We are treating the cyber_test_shot as a system-wide wake-up call. This autumn’s intrusion — undetected by existing monitoring, discovered by accident, and deep in operational networks — proves that our current resilience is not fit for the agentised frontier. We are drafting a Council Implementing Regulation under Article 114 TFEU to mandate immediate, audited hardening of all critical infrastructure operators in energy transmission, water supply, and major transport hubs. ENISA will be empowered to conduct unannounced red-team exercises, and operators must report defensive readiness quarterly. Funding will come from the Recovery and Resilience Facility’s unused margins and the Digital Europe Programme, reprioritised in an emergency allocation. We are offering member states fast-track access to EuroHPC’s upcoming sovereign AI clusters in exchange for compliance, and negotiating with Siemens, Thales, and Orange to scale defensive AI tooling under public contract. Resistance from smaller member states over cost is being met with a 75% EU co-financing guarantee for the first 18 months. This is not a permanent expansion of EU competence — it is a surge, time-boxed to 18 months, designed to close the gap before the next incident becomes a crisis.
