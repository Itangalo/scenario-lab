### Holding by hand
The first half of 2031 did not bring a new blackout. It brought something meaner: attacks on the workaround.

In several member states, fires and break-ins hit the places keeping the lights on by hand — fenced yards with spare transformers, municipal depots with pooled generators, substations where crews kept paper logs of breakers. Leaflets left behind called the stockpiles collaboration. Police called it coordinated. Grid staff called it the week they started sleeping at the depot.

Brussels had already named the black-start and manual fallback network its one priority. In practice that meant guards, not gear. Under existing civil-protection and security rules, emergency teams moved to put the informal pools on official lists, split them across more sites, pay the owners, and mark them as protected lifelines. Where it worked, sabotage failed and restoration drills continued. Where lists leaked, they became maps for the next attempt. Operators stayed on segmented manual routines; no one promised new equipment that quotas and cancelled factory orders could not deliver.

Washington tightened again. Advanced chips and model access for allied buyers went to strict volume quotas, with servicing of older tools approved case by case. For European clinics and factories already on a European default with metered American fallback, the meter ran slower. Investment news made it worse: valuations in AI fell hard, build-outs were cancelled, and arrangements for leased compute Europe had counted on evaporated.

There was one counter-story the Commission used hard. In a few health systems and administrations, locally run AI tools cut waiting lists and decision times from months to days. Ministers co-branded the deployments, paid the teams, and kept them running on European systems. It did not reverse hostility, but for the first time in a year, evening news carried a segment where AI was the reason a clinic opened earlier.

The containment and mutual-aid protocol formally closed this half-year. Isolation drills and cross-border fuel reimbursements are now procedure, not improvisation. It bought hours, not confidence.
