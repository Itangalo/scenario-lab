None this turn, while we defend what is already in flight until capital and public tolerance recover enough to carry anything new.

## Priority
Priority is M8, the Black-Start and Manual Fallback Network, because coordinated sabotage of generator pools, transformer stores and breaker-log sites now directly targets the only restoration capacity we have left.

## In practice
We put M8 on a protection footing. Under NIS2/CER and the Civil Protection Mechanism, ENISA, EU-CyCLONe and national police/gendarmerie move the off-books pools on-scheme, disperse and guard them, reimburse holders, and publish a simple map of what is protected — turning hidden stocks from a sabotage target framed as collaboration into a public lifeline. Grid operators and hospitals stay on segmented manual operation; no new procurement is promised that Washington quotas and collapsed build-out cannot deliver.

We use the one good news we have. Where public-sector AI has cut waiting lists and decision times, we name it as a European decision — DG DIGIT and health ministries co-brand deployments, pay the teams, and keep clinics on EU-default — to separate useful adoption from the rogue-agent and blackout story. We spend no new capital we do not have: no new law, no new fund, just holding the common line and letting the investment reset pass through without chasing it.
