## New measure
**EU Upstream Bargain for Metered Access**
It conditions EU upstream chokepoint servicing and spares on restored metered quotas of chips, model access and servicing for essential services. This and why now: Taiwan blockade plus US cut-off plus strict quotas leave clinics and grid operators with no supply line, while the EU's lithography/servicing position is suddenly its only tradable leverage before open-weight 62.3 capability spreads beyond recall.

## Priority
Priority is M9, the EU Upstream Bargain for Metered Access, because without metered quotas the manual fallback network has nothing to fall back to and the cut-off reads as foresight failure.

## In practice
We act under Trade/COMPET and the Anti-Coercion Instrument, not a new fund we cannot pay for. DG TRADE with Dutch backing puts servicing, spares and field-engineer approvals for advanced lithography on a licensed, essential-services-linked basis: continued servicing in exchange for volume quotas for hospitals, grid operators and ministries on EU-default with US fallback, plus case-by-case tool servicing.

We pair this with rationing by hand. Under NIS2/CER and the Health Emergency Preparedness Authority, ENISA and national regulators allocate the metered compute that returns to black-start coordination, clinical triage and paper-log reconciliation first, published as a lifeline list to blunt sabotage-as-collaboration framing. No new build-out is promised; we trade what we hold to keep what keeps lights and clinics open.
