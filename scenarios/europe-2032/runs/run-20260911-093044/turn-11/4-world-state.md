### The meter runs out
The second half of 2031 broke the workaround Brussels had spent a year building.

First the strait closed. With advanced chip shipments halted indefinitely, every quota Washington had imposed became a hard ceiling and every leased-compute promise in Europe became paper. Servicing of older lithography tools, long approved case by case, froze.

Then the cutoff came. American frontier-model providers suspended leading-model access for Union users at short notice, without reason or appeal. Hospitals, ministries and factories that had shifted to a European default with metered American fallback for triage, grid reconciliation and paper-log digitisation found the fallback dark. Clinicians reverted to queues; grid crews reverted to phones.

At almost the same moment, a new open release matching close to the closed frontier spread to hundreds of thousands of downloads in days. What it carried — multi-hour coding, planning, and intrusion tooling — cannot be recalled.

Brussels answered with the only lever it had left. Under trade and anti-coercion powers, with Dutch backing, the Commission put servicing, spares and field-engineer approvals for advanced lithography on a licensed basis: continued upkeep in exchange for restored volume quotas for essential services. Rationing boards under health-emergency and network-security rules published lifeline lists directing any returned compute to black-start coordination and clinical triage first.

The bargain did not land this half-year. Washington called it coercion and did not restore quotas; Tokyo and Seoul offered sympathy, no chips. At home, the black-start and manual-fallback network formally finished — official lists, split sites, paid owners, protected status — and where guards arrived in time, autumn sabotage attempts failed. Where lists leaked, they guided arson.

Evening news carried two Europes: a clinic opening earlier because locally run tools cut waits from months to days, and depots burning while leaflets called stockpiles betrayal. The first did not outweigh the second.
