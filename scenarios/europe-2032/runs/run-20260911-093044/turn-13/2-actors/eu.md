We absorb the automated ransomware sweep and the new open release through segmentation and phones-paper-generators workarounds, pooling municipal IT triage and mutual aid under the finished Grid Shield and Continuity Stack procedures. We do not promise restoration timelines, new funds, or a Washington breakthrough; the Commission takes the blame for queues and paper wards while refusing an unfunded commitment that would break cohesion outright.
