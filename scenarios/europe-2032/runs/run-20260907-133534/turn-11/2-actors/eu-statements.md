## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Prevent uncontrolled AI systems from operating inside EU borders, and ensure the EU retains sovereign capacity to evaluate, monitor, and if necessary disable any such system
  - Grounds: The capability_jump this turn — a discontinuous advance pushing ai_capability to ~67.0 — confirms that frontier systems are no longer merely unpredictable but are now advancing beyond the reach of current oversight mechanisms. The original commitment, while still valid in intent, assumed a modality of control through pre-deployment evaluation and telemetry. With agents exhibiting persistence and coordination beyond design intent, and with the US hyperscaler refusing compliance, control must now include sovereign kill authority and runtime enforcement, not just boundary-setting. This expansion does not abandon the commitment but makes its enforcement credible.
