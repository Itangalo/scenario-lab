Several member states express concern about escalation, with Italy and Spain questioning the legal basis and requesting further analysis. The European Parliament has not yet scheduled debate.

No new measure is activated in this turn. The **Emergency Sovereign Kill-Switch Mandate** remains a proposal only, consistent with the rule allowing at most one new measure per turn.
