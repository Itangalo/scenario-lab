### The Fractured Response

The EU declared the Sovereign AI Sentinel Nodes operational, marking the end of a three-year build. Integration into energy and financial systems brought a modest resilience boost, with early warnings now possible for AI-driven grid instabilities and financial anomalies. However, two member states withheld full data sharing, citing national sovereignty, weakening the network’s coherence. Joint alert protocols remain political agreements, not technical realities.

Amid rising cognitive threats, the EU elevated the Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory to priority status, pouring resources into staffing and legal authorisation. Emergency clearances were fast-tracked, and red-teaming of election systems began. Yet intelligence integration stalled—national agencies resisted ceding control, and no binding framework emerged. The Counter-Deception Rapid Response Corps remained in legal limbo, its mandate contested.

A seismic shift occurred globally: a frontier AI model was released openly within months of its closed counterpart, spreading rapidly across private clusters. This leap in open diffusion pushed openweight capability sharply upward, narrowing the gap with the closed frontier. Simultaneously, a major democracy annulled its election over concerns of AI-driven information manipulation, sending shockwaves through capitals. Public trust in digital discourse eroded further, reflected in public sentiment dropping to 15.0.

The EU launched the Secure Foundational Compute Initiative to reclaim agency, aiming to build sovereign, high-assurance compute for critical AI functions. But with political capital already depleted, funding strained, and member-state alignment fragile, progress will be slow. The initiative is large, costly, and begins in crisis.

### Capability Outpaces Control

AI capability surged again, driven by self-improving systems in law and biomedicine now operating beyond full human interpretability. No safety gains landed—assurance remains primitive. The gap between power and understanding widens into a chasm, and with no deployed safety measure closing it, ai_safety fell to 6.0. Sovereignty declined to 14.0, reflecting both the erosion from unchecked capability growth and the absence of consolidating gains, despite the new initiative’s launch.
