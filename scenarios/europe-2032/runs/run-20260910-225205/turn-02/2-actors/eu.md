## New measure
**Union External AI Deal Screen**
Creates a binding notification-and-review screen for member-state AI compute, cloud and model deals with non-EU providers, tying EU co-financing and Shield funds to compliance.
This is International coordination and leverage, because the defection shows sovereignty is lost through bilateral side-deals faster than gigafactories can build it, and the window to set the rule closes while sites are still being allocated.

## Priority
InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) – because only poured concrete and signed grid priority removes the pragmatism excuse for bilateral hyperscaler deals that undercut the Union.

## In practice
We are holding the gigafactory race together through the Competitiveness Council and DG CNECT, using InvestAI guarantees and EIB tranches to force four-to-five final site decisions this semester, with grid priority conditional on European legal anchoring and on acceptance of the new deal screen. Regions bidding against each other are told plainly: EU money follows the common rulebook.

We push the Shield via ENISA and HERA with Civil Protection Mechanism funds, trading EU-paid detection stacks and hardening for mandatory drills and exercised continuity plans, while isolating Interior-ministry resistance with bilateral continuity compacts. On the defection, we take the file to the European Council and Foreign Affairs Council under the Anti-Coercion Instrument logic, offering the defecting capital accelerated siting plus Shield uplift in return for re-aligning its arrangement to Union terms, and making future funds conditional.
