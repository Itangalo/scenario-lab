ENISA begins planning expanded threat assessments to include societal risks, though no formal mandate is established. Public sentiment declines further amid perceptions of institutional retreat, worsened by mischaracterization of the stalled Public Assurance Framework. Poland remains partially aligned, with diplomatic engagement ongoing, while internal EU divisions grow—France opposes moratoria, whereas Germany and Spain advocate stricter controls.
