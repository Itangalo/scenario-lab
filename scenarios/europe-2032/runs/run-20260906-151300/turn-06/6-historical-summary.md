The Critical Infrastructure Resilience Surge ends its active phase, transitioning to ENISA for long-term monitoring—planned but not yet mandated or operationalized. Construction begins on the Swedish InvestAI Gigafactory under emergency grid overrides. The EU launches the Public Assurance Protocol, instituting limited transparency through public safety reporting for sovereign systems. Open-weight models rise to 52.0 due to spillovers and improved tooling, yet remain behind the frontier.
