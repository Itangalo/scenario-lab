## Priority
The new Breakthrough Therapies Access and Delivery Programme, because restoring public trust and clinical delivery outranks any new compute or regulatory front while capital is at 13 and sentiment at 29.

## In practice
We task DG SANTE and HERA with the rollout, using EU4Health and joint EuroHPC procurement: prioritised allocation-board supply to oncology/personalised-therapy wards, validation by the EU Evaluation Institute with human-check requirements, and EMA accelerated but audited pathways. Mayors and regional health authorities refusing automation are offered audited clinical use with published logs and patient recourse, directly answering the welfare-affair critique.

We hold Paris/Warsaw shells in care-and-maintenance and continue Seoul/Tokyo maintenance-clearance diplomacy under DG TRADE without a new instrument this turn, and the AI Office conformity review continues.
