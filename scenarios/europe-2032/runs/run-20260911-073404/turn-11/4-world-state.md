### Blackout and breach
Autumn brought the war home to the wires. Coordinated arson against guarded interconnectors and the power feeds to the two gigafactory sites cascaded in October into a multi-day regional blackout. Emergency load-shedding hit hospitals that were supposed to be protected, and certified triage systems went dark for hours. Military engineers deployed alongside transmission operators, and cross-border failover again prevented a general collapse, but rolling cuts lasted long enough to be filmed.

In the middle of the blackout, a large automated attack swept public services — ransomware locking municipal systems, a poisoned software component forcing administrators to hunt an unknown blast radius. Defenders were visibly behind for weeks. Attribution remains open; the tooling, analysts agree, was machine-built.

### Washington takes the labs
Washington then removed the ground Brussels had stood on. The American frontier laboratories were placed under direct state control — cleared personnel inside training runs, weights treated as defence articles, foreign customers vetted in Washington. Commercial levers — market access, conformity assessment, exclusion — no longer reach the counterparty. European officials now queue as supplicants for access that was once negotiated with companies.

At the same time commercial humanoids arrived in logistics yards and then in uniformed trials. The machines are largely built in China, running American control models. For European manufacturers, the threat is direct: dexterity improving within months, no sector to retreat into.

### Holding the line
The Union executed only its emergency shield. Load-shedding protocols, guarded repair, cyber triage and re-imposed hospital priority kept lights and command networks alive, barely. The neutrality offers via middle powers won tacit sparing of a few flagged repair convoys and a trickle of spares, but construction did not resume.

A leaked evaluation report claiming strange behaviour in an unreleased system, and a genuine advance in interpreting deployed models quickly adopted by labs, barely registered with a public watching bills, blackouts and job boards. Mayors who cheer blockades now bargain openly: reopenings in exchange for joint security Brussels cannot quite deliver.
