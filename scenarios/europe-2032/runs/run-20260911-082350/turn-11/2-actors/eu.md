## New measure
**EU Logistics Automation Adjustment Fund**
Pays wage insurance and rapid retraining for warehouse and logistics workers displaced by deployed picking and palletising robots, funded from existing social funds and tied to employers automating. This is Labour and social protection, category 7, and why now is that embodied deployment is hitting logistics jobs from outside while productivity gains lift sentiment but offer no cover for the manual workers who lose first.
