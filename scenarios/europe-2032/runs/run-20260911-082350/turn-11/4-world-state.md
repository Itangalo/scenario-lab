### Shelter holds, shop floor breaks
The second half of 2031 divided the European economy in two.

In offices, the new systems quietly delivered. Law firms, accountancies, newsrooms and consultancies reported the same pattern: juniors producing far more draft work, seniors spending their days checking and deciding, headcount steady after an early round of cuts was partly reversed. Polls warmed a few points. For the first time ministers could point to visible benefit without being heckled.

In warehouses, the opposite arrived. Cheap picking, sorting and palletising machines, built largely in China and running American control software, began appearing at scale through Rotterdam, Antwerp and Hamburg. Three large logistics operators announced automation of regional hubs in the autumn, with several thousand picker roles at risk. Unions marched in Liège and Poznań; employers said the machines paid for themselves in months.

Brussels tried to ride both stories. Accession to the pooled cyber and health command continued to pay: liaison officers in the joint operations room forwarded early warnings that let cooperating grid operators and clinics isolate a fresh ransomware strain in hours. Towns outside the scheme again stayed dark for days, and resentment over the two-speed protection deepened.

At home, the long-running trust repair finally closed. The civic adoption programme formally finished with its audits, workload limits and redress window in place, but insurers still refused to restore cover without a statute, and only a handful of municipalities dared redeploy triage assistants. Nurses praised them; most councils waited.

The new wage-insurance and retraining fund for logistics workers was agreed in principle, reprogrammed from existing social funds with automators asked to co-fund. Payouts were months away, and the parallel mapping of dependence on foreign robot hardware and models bought nothing yet. With money gone and chip slots still blocked, the Union ended the year better warned from abroad, modestly better liked for office gains, but newly exposed where machines lift boxes.
