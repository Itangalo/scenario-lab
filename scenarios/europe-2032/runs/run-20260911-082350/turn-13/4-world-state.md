### A recipe and a remedy
Autumn brought two biology shocks at once. A genome model circulated with a design reviewers said could infect humans, with help a non-expert could follow. Methodologists called it alarmist and reckless in the same breath, and the fight stayed inside journals and closed briefings. In the same months, tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions reached ordinary clinics — remissions on evening news, waiting lists moving for the first time in years.

Brussels tried to tie the two together. The one new step this turn was the Bio-Sentinel net, which put rapid sequencing kits and isolation drills into sentinel hospitals linked to the pooled health command, with joint procurement meant to bring the new treatments through EU clinics rather than foreign subscriptions.

It half-worked. Sentinel sites got kits and shared alerts; procurement secured early batches, and televised first doses briefly cut through fraud headlines. But volumes were thin, eligibility tangled, and most patients still heard about cures they could not yet get.

### The grid does not wait
Daily exploit patterns from the joint operations room continued against grid operators and hospital IT. ENISA kept pushing blocks and signatures to cooperating members first, with EU money for offline backups offered to outsider clinics if they joined telemetry.

Cooperators again isolated ransomware in hours. Outsiders again stayed dark for days. The offer softened the two-speed grievance without removing it — mayors took the money and resented the condition.

On logistics, the adjustment fund closed enrolment with retraining vouchers and wage top-ups finally landing in Liège and Poznan. It eased household pressure but did not touch the port fight. Slowdowns and sick-outs around Rotterdam, Antwerp and Hamburg stopped short of a sustained blockade, though installer lorries were turned back twice amid rumours of coordinated pickets.

The existing Verifiable-Domain Surge Shield continued as the named priority from the prior turn. With budgets exhausted, keeping that priority in place bought no acceleration, a fact liaison officers noted openly, and political standing recovered only modestly from the small legitimacy and proposal effects.
