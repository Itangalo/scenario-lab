Committing without the means to follow through has eroded credibility. The commitment must now bind action to capacity, not just urgency.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger

To maintain it would be to pretend agency where there is none. Its failure is not tactical — it is structural, and it demands a reset.

The Competitiveness Council defers decision, and no physical deployment begins. With no advancement, it is not treated as an active measure this turn. The portfolio charge from prior turns is extinguished; only the new directive is active.
  - Reason: The stagnation of the Sovereign Compute programme directly undermines the EU's ability to maintain independent AI access, making the commitment non-credible and structural.
