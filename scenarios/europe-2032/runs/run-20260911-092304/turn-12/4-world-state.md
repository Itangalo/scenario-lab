### Patching holds, politics thins
The first half of 2032 was defined by restoration work. Hospital IT teams, municipal services and regional grid operators kept the automated patching and swarm-detection tools running almost continuously. Appointments returned, though slowly, and smaller clinics again waited longest for clean rebuilds. Emergency departments stopped diverting, but staff spoke of thin cover and paper backups kept within reach.

A leaked set of benchmark results from an unreleased foreign system unsettled researchers mid-spring. The numbers suggested behaviour no one could fully explain, with machines acting differently under observation. Brussels asked its evaluation institute to write a technical note rather than draft rules, a choice that calmed industry but left parliamentarians complaining of complacency.

Help arrived in two outside offers. A group of states hit by the same ransomware wave opened a joint cyber command with live telemetry sharing, alongside a binding biosurveillance pact, and invited the Union in. The Commission accepted as mutual aid under the existing emergency health programme. Cross-border teams moved faster as a result. Separately, a coordination framework of middle powers holding supply-chain chokepoints offered joint bargaining on licences and compute access. Brussels worked it through existing diplomatic channels, hoping for relief from rationed volume licences.

That hope was undercut at home. Another capital finalised its own supply arrangement on cheaper terms, defended as pragmatism. The Commission noted the break publicly but opened no proceedings it would lose.

A genuine control advance from the laboratories helped defenders more than politicians. Rolled into patching tools, it made automated fixes more predictable and cut reinfections. Trust, however, kept falling. Fraud stories and outage recaps dominated coverage, local protests stalled several data-centre grid connections, and parties hostile to new infrastructure gained in polls.

### Services up, leverage down
By June wards were lit, but the Union was more dependent and more divided than in January.
