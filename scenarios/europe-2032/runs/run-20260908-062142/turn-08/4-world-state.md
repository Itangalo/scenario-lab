### A Fragile Technical Victory, a Social Turning Point

Global AI capability inches upward to 64.0, as closed labs consolidate gains in agent coordination and verification, though the frontier shows signs of strain—top-tier model prices drop and a flagship release underperforms, feeding debate over a potential plateau. Yet no slowdown is confirmed, and the underlying trajectory holds. Open-weight capability remains stagnant at 50.0, with no major leak or coordinated open breakthrough.

The EU completes the **Sovereign AI Sentinel Programme**, bringing online defensive AI agents trained on sovereign compute in Kittilä and Sines. Despite grid delays, cryptographic controls and red-teaming protocols are finalised, marking a rare delivery. This boosts **eu_ai_sovereignty** by +3 under rule 5, offset only by the +1.0 rise in `ai_capability`, resulting in a net gain to 14.0.

Meanwhile, the **Critical Infrastructure AI Shield Initiative** remains legally contested. Germany and Poland resist central enforcement, but the Commission’s pivot to co-governance—offering national red teams and sunset clauses—keeps the initiative alive. ENISA’s threat feed goes live in seven member states, and joint telemetry trials with Japan and South Korea show promise, contributing a partial +2 to **resilience**, now at 46.0.

In a decisive shift, the EU launches the **Emergency Social Transition Compact**, a large category 7 measure, in direct response to physical protests and the reactivation of the European Labour Council. The levy on large AI deployments funds worker cooperatives and wage insurance, with core protections mandated. Public sentiment, long at rock bottom, rises to 25.0 as displaced workers see tangible support, though deep scepticism remains.

Political capital dips to 21.0. The portfolio charge is heavy: three large measures in flight (−9), plus the priority cost (−1). The proposal bonus for the new measure is +3—justified by its direct response to recent backlash and labour crisis—but this only partially offsets the burden. No sentiment-driven legitimacy boost applies, as capital still exceeds sentiment.

### Not a Shield, But a Bargain

The Union did not just build defences—it struck a bargain. Technical sovereignty advanced, but the real breakthrough was social: for the first time, AI governance is being co-owned by workers and states, not just technocrats.
