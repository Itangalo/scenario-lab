### The Fracture Widens

Global AI capability climbs to 55.5 as labs race to exploit compressed training cycles, despite the ai_investment_collapse freezing several major compute expansions. Frontier progress slows in absolute terms, but the most capable actors consolidate gains through efficiency and model distillation. Offensive cyber tooling, now routinely generated by AI, enables a devastating ransomware sweep across regional health systems and transport hubs in three member states. The attack overwhelms legacy defences, exposing deep fragility.

### Crisis Response, Political Cost

The EU fast-tracks Cyber Shield for Critical Operators (CS-CO), designating it a priority and pushing it through COREPER II. ENISA delivers draft technical standards by June, integrating classified threat data. Conditional disbursements of up to €15bn from the Recovery and Resilience Facility are offered to secure buy-in. However, resistance hardens in fiscally strained member states, and procurement streamlining under the Digital Europe Programme faces legal challenges in two capitals. Cohesion frays: the shield exists in draft, not in deployment.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Sentiment Falls

With no sovereign compute capacity online and global capability rising, eu_ai_sovereignty drops to 19.0. The ai_investment_collapse undermines planned private-sector data centres, compounding dependence. eu_political_capital falls sharply to 34.0, weighed down by the ongoing cost of three large measures (−9), the priority penalty (−1), and a −5 penalty for failing to prevent a major external incident with no finished resilience measure in place. public_sentiment dips to 35.0 as hospitals go offline and job displacement accelerates, feeding public anger. Labour protests erupt in two cities, targeting AI-driven automation in public services.

### Open Frontiers, Closed Defences

openweight_capability rises to 43.0 as Kimi K3-derived tools spread, enabling more sophisticated cyber intrusions. ai_safety falls to 29.0 as capability outpaces control, and no safety measure lands on shipped systems. The capability plateau remains disputed, but the market signals stagnation — a brief reprieve, not a reversal.
