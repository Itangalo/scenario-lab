## New measure
**Measure:** Public Continuity Protocol  
Mandate and equip essential public services to operate using only EU-controlled, auditable AI systems during periods of external denial or verified loss of trust.

Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)  
Size: small  
Finishes on turn: 10  
Targeted effect: Increase resilience by 8–10 points by ensuring uninterrupted operation of health, energy, transport, and civil protection services during AI outages or integrity breaches; reinforce eu_ai_sovereignty by creating operational demand for Sovereign Core outputs  
Applies to: National governments, EU agencies, and designated critical service providers

## Priority
**Priority:** Public Continuity Protocol  
This is our top priority this turn because the **election_annulled** and **rsi_onset** events prove that resilience is no longer just about cyber defences or backup systems, but about maintaining public function when truth itself is contested — and because a sovereign stack is meaningless unless it is operationally embedded in the moments that matter most.

## In practice
We are activating the Public Continuity Protocol across four domains: emergency healthcare triage, grid stability management, air traffic coordination, and civil protection alerting. Under Article 12 of the Crisis Management in the Digital Age Directive (CMDAD), we are requiring national authorities to designate at least one fallback workflow in each domain that runs exclusively on the Sovereign Core stack, with live-switching capability by turn 10. Funding of €12bn is drawn from the Emergency Sovereignty Facility, with implementation led by the European Commission’s Directorate-General for Communications Networks, Content and Technology (DG CONNECT), in coordination with ENISA and the EuroHPC JU.
