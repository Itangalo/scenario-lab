## New measure
**Measure:** Sovereign Inference Guarantee  
Establish a binding EU-wide standard that all public functions classified as essential must run on hardware and software stacks subject to EU jurisdiction, audit, and physical control, with compliance enforced through conditional access to national and EU funding.

Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 12  
Targeted effect: Increase eu_ai_sovereignty by 10–12 points by anchoring the operational footprint of high-capability inference within the Union’s legal and physical reach; reduce the risk that emergent efficiency enables unregulated, unaccountable deployment of systems that undermine public continuity and democratic legitimacy  
Applies to: National governments, EU agencies, and all entities receiving public funds for AI deployment in essential services

## Priority
**Priority:** Public Continuity Protocol  
This remains our top priority because the **cyber_major_incident** and **bio_incident** this turn confirm that the threat is no longer hypothetical — systems are failing, lives are at risk, and the Union’s ability to respond is contingent on whether trusted, sovereign infrastructure is already in place and operational when trust collapses — not whether we can build it fast enough after the fact.

## In practice
We are executing the Public Continuity Protocol under intensified pressure. The cyber_major_incident — a model-generated ransomware sweep that locked emergency dispatch systems in three capitals — and the bio_incident — a modified respiratory agent released through a compromised municipal lab — have shattered any illusion that resilience is a technical footnote. These were not frontier attacks; they were failures of integrity, enabled by systems we did not control and could not verify. We are now enforcing live-switching mandates under Article 12 of CMDAD, with DG CONNECT deploying rapid integration teams to Paris, Berlin, and Rome to harden fallbacks in emergency healthcare and grid management. ENISA is conducting unannounced failover drills every two weeks, and we are conditioning the next tranche of cohesion funding on verified integration by turn 9.

The Institutional Integrity Audit is exposing systemic rot: in Poland, AI-generated civil service promotions bypassed oversight; in Greece, synthetic public consultation data shaped regional development plans. We are invoking Article 7(3) of the AI Act to mandate full provenance logging and are fast-tracking the Public Trust Observatory’s API integration with national digital identity systems. The InvestAI Gigafactories in Dresden, Marseille, and Vilnius are now operational, providing the Sovereign Core with its first real inference capacity — though Helsinki remains offline due to unresolved grid disputes. We have offered Finland not just preferential access, but co-ownership in a new Nordic node, conditional on resolving the connection by turn 10.

But the emergent_efficiency_breakthrough changes everything. The Swiss-German consortium’s design allows high-capability inference on commodity hardware, threatening to render our entire sovereignty strategy obsolete — not because it is more powerful, but because it is everywhere, untraceable, and unregulated. We cannot secure what we cannot see. That is why we are launching the Sovereign Inference Guarantee: a legal and financial firewall to ensure that even as efficiency democratizes capability, legitimacy remains anchored in jurisdiction. This is not about stopping innovation — it is about ensuring that when the lights go out, the systems that come on are ours, and we can answer for them.
