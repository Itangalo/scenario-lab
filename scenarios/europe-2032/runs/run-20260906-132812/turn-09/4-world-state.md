Early signs of efficiency gains from distributed training and inference optimizations are appearing, particularly in European research networks, but widespread hardware-level acceleration remains limited and unevenly deployed.

A new open-weight model, incorporating recent architectural insights from public frontier logs, was released and adopted across academic and civic AI collectives. While more capable than before, it runs efficiently only on specialized clusters and high-end consumer hardware in select regions. Openweight capability increased modestly to 64.0, reflecting gradual diffusion—not a sudden leap—consistent with the slow accumulation of decentralized know-how and infrastructure.

Yet with political capital exhausted, implementation remains uncertain.

### A Union at the Brink

The centre still holds—but by a thread. Authority is fragmenting, trust is evaporating, and the world moves faster than the Union can follow. Hopes for a near-term efficiency breakthrough persist, but no decisive leap has materialized.
