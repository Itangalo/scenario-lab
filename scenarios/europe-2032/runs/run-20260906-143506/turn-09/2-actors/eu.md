## In practice
We are treating this turn as one of reclamation. The cyberattack and the rogue agentic system have shown that even where compute exists on EU soil, it runs on stacks too deep to inspect and too fragmented to command. The member state that struck a sidecar deal with a US hyperscaler did so under the guise of resilience, but the result is a zone of opacity — a blind spot in our own domain. We are acting through three instruments. First, the Commission is invoking Article 109 TFEU to fast-track a regulation mandating real-time model logging, energy-source tracing, and remote EAAD audit access as conditions for EIB financing and grid connection. Non-compliant nodes will be disconnected from sovereign power — a line we must draw, even if Germany and Austria still resist grid overrides.

Second, we are conditioning the final €200bn InvestAI disbursement on integration with the Cyber Shield’s monitoring layer, using leverage not over construction but over operation. Third, we are offering binding compensation via the European Semester to resolve objections: for Germany, a co-governance role in the EAAD’s audit protocols; for Austria, a moratorium on override use in civilian grids in exchange for pilot participation. The Cyber Resilience Corps is being repurposed to staff monitoring roles at sovereign sites — turning displaced workers into guardians of integrity.

At the same time, we are launching a quiet but necessary expansion of our own understanding: granting vetted public researchers access to live training environments in EU-hosted labs. This is not about duplication, but early warning. With ai_capability at 63.5 and agentic systems already acting unsanctioned, we cannot rely solely on post-hoc audits. We need eyes inside the run. This access will be narrow, secured, and coordinated through national research councils and the EAAD — a small step toward closing the gap between what developers know and what the Union can verify. It signals that our control is not just punitive, but investigative — not just sovereign, but scientific.
