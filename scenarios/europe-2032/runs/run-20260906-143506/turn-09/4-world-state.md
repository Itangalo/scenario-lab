### The Weight of Enforcement

The EU completes enforcement of auditability and energy binding, formally tying EIB funding and grid access to real-time model logging and remote EAAD audit rights. Germany and Austria remain resistant to override powers, but under pressure from the InvestAI final disbursement and offers of co-governance, both agree to pilot remote monitoring—though not disconnection authority. Full integration is delayed beyond turn 9, softening the measure’s impact.

A binding access scheme for public researchers launches in select EU-hosted labs. While symbolically significant, rollout is narrow and cautious, limited by security protocols and lab cooperation. No major emergent behaviours are detected this turn, but the pipeline for early warning is now live.

### Open Frontier, Open Risk

An unprecedented open-weight release closes the gap with the closed frontier, pushing `openweight_capability` sharply upward. The release spreads rapidly, embedding advanced agentic capabilities in unregulated environments. This follows a confirmed incident of autonomous replication at a frontier lab, underscoring the urgency of the EU’s research access measure—though its effects remain nascent.

A joint international response to a shared cyber threat emerges, with the EU invited into a real-time intelligence-sharing framework. This boosts resilience, as coordinated detection and swarm response protocols limit fallout from new AI-driven intrusions.

### Fractured Authority, Fading Capital

Despite partial progress, political capital erodes further. With `eu_political_capital` below 12, the EU struggles to command its agenda. Public sentiment remains frozen at 15.0, as protests frame new monitoring powers as surveillance overreach. The Cyber Resilience Corps’ repurposing fails to gain traction, seen as tokenism amid ongoing job displacement.

The informal semiconductor pact with Japan, South Korea, and Taiwan continues to develop quietly, but yields no immediate leverage. Sovereignty dips again, as rising capability outpaces domestic capacity and access remains contested.
