The flawed algorithm, opaque to review and uncorrected for months, triggered public outrage and protests in three capitals. This was not a frontier failure but a domestic governance collapse — one that landed squarely on the EU’s doorstep.

However, with eu_political_capital at just 10.0, the initiative faced immediate headwinds: legal disputes delayed disbursement, and some member states resisted centralized auditing. The signal of accountability clashed with the reality of fragmentation.

Oversight boards were installed, but the findings deepened distrust rather than repairing it. A phased dual-stack rollout was proposed, but confidence in the timeline eroded.
