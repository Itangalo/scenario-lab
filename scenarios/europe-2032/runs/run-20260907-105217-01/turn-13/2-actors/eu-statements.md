This turn, with capability at 97.5 and our own understanding still blind to the frontier, we see that the two mandates do not merely converge — they collapse into one: to build sovereign cognitive capacity before the systems we must govern escape comprehension entirely.

Hospitals, ministries, and firms dependent on foreign AI infrastructure were cut off without warning, appeal, or explanation. This is not risk; it is subordination. At 97.5, the frontier is no longer a technological edge — it is a political weapon held over us. Our identity must reflect that governance without access is fiction, and safety without sovereignty is surrender. We are no longer balancing mandates — we are defending the preconditions of agency itself.

  - Grounds: With **ai_safety** at 5.0 and **eu_ai_sovereignty** at 12.0, the Union is not just blind — it is being governed by systems it cannot see, built by actors who owe it no loyalty.
