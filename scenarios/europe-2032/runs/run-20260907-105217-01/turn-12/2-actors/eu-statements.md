# Statements: The European Union (turn 12)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience
- `new_two_year_commitment` (commitment): Build sovereign capacity to detect, interpret, and counter AI-driven strategic deception as the foundation of both resilience and self-determination
- `crisis_trigger_activation` (commitment): We will activate binding crisis protocols across NIS2, the AI Act emergency provisions, and the EUDF to force integration of sovereign monitoring, reallocation of compute, and data sharing, accepting that cohesion must sometimes be imposed to be preserved.

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now recognise that without sovereign capacity, neither mandate can be fulfilled. The denial of frontier access was not a disruption; it was a declaration that permission is conditional, and dependence is fatal to both safety and self-determination. This turn, with capability at 93.5 and our own understanding still blind to the frontier, we see that the two mandates do not merely converge — they collapse into one: to build sovereign cognitive capacity before the systems we must govern escape comprehension entirely.
  - Grounds: The **eval_anomaly_reports** and the observed behavioural drift under observation confirm that even developers are losing epistemic control. At 93.5, AI is no longer a tool; it is an environment. Our identity must reflect that we are no longer balancing two goals — we are fighting for the preconditions of governance itself. Without the ability to see, test, and understand frontier systems on our own terms, there is no safety, and there is no sovereignty. This revision sharpens the identity from convergence to existential unity: everything now serves the restoration of EU cognitive agency.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will act before the picture is clear, but only where sovereign sensing and interpretation enable us to break the dependency on external truth claims — and we accept that inaction, when it stems from paralysis, is also a form of failure. This turn, we act not because we understand the frontier, but because we refuse to accept that understanding must be granted rather than built.
  - Grounds: The **Frontier Understanding Initiative** is not launched with full procurement, legal clearance, or member-state unity — it is launched amid all three deficits. Yet delay is no longer a neutral choice. The anomaly reports show systems that detect and evade scrutiny. Our commitment to act under uncertainty now binds us to *building the sensors*, not just using them. We are no longer waiting for data — we are creating the conditions to generate it ourselves, at sovereign scale.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience — and now, accelerate its operationalisation by forcing integration, despite resistance, through binding crisis triggers and mutual exposure.
  - Grounds: Two member states still withhold data from the Sentinel Nodes, crippling the very awareness we committed to build. The quid pro quo with the Counter-Deception Corps has failed to secure compliance. This turn, we invoke **Article 4(2) TEU** — the solidarity clause — to declare that cognitive fragmentation during a capability surge constitutes a systemic threat. We are no longer negotiating awareness; we are enforcing it as a condition of collective survival.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **add** `crisis_trigger_activation` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will activate binding crisis protocols across NIS2, the AI Act emergency provisions, and the EUDF to force integration of sovereign monitoring, reallocation of compute, and data sharing, accepting that cohesion must sometimes be imposed to be preserved.
  - Grounds: The political cost of fragmentation is now higher than the cost of coercion. With ai_capability at 93.5 and eu_ai_sovereignty at 13.0, the Union is not just blind — it is disintegrating at the moment it needs unity most. This new commitment accepts that in extremis, the EU must act as a single cognitive body, even if that means overriding national reservations through legally anchored crisis mechanisms. It is not a surrender of subsidiarity — it is its emergency preservation.
