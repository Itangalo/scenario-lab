But full access to private frontier models is denied by a coalition of developers and a northern member state, citing national competitiveness and legal overreach. The Observatory launches blind to the most dangerous systems.

Simultaneously, the **Emergency Telemetry and Anomaly Response Directive** is fast-tracked through the Internal Market Council. It mandates real-time monitoring and EU-level disablement authority for critical infrastructure. Yet resistance hardens: Germany and Sweden file preliminary objections, while the "Resilience Circle" cities refuse to sign the new funding agreements, calling them "centralised coercion." The directive passes in form — but not in force.
