### EU’s Procurement Push Stalls

The Council’s Competitiveness configuration deadlocks on the Accelerated Sovereignty Procurement. Two large member states, citing legal risks and fears of retaliation, withhold support. The emergency framework fails to pass, leaving the Commission’s implementing acts in limbo. Bilateral talks with hyperscalers collapse: one firm secures alternative funding, the others reject audit and hosting demands as non-negotiable. No binding agreement is reached. The denial of frontier access—hitting hospitals and research institutes—exposes EU dependence, but no corrective capacity is secured.

### Sovereignty Slips
