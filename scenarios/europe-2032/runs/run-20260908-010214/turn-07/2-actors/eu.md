## In practice
The defection of a senior European AI research team to Canada is not just a personnel loss — it is a signal that the EU’s technical sovereignty is evaporating even as we lay foundations for physical capacity. We are acting immediately to close the gap between hardware and human capital. The Commission is drafting a delegated act under Horizon Europe’s emergency clause to unlock €12bn in pre-approved but unallocated funds, repurposing them into a new European AI Research Fellowship. This will offer ten-year grants to frontier teams committing to remain in the EU, with guaranteed access to sovereign compute via the now-finishing InvestAI Gigafactories, and fast-tracked cross-border hiring and visa rights for their collaborators.

We are targeting 15 core teams — those working on alignment, interpretability, and biosecurity-relevant AI — and will tie funding to public evaluation and open reporting of safety-relevant findings. The European Research Council will administer the programme, but with a new oversight board co-chaired by the European AI Office and national science ministers to ensure strategic coherence. We are already in talks with Max Planck, INRIA, and Amsterdam’s AI Innovation Factory to anchor the first cohorts. Resistance is expected from member states with weaker research bases, who fear another brain-centre concentration; we will counter by requiring all fellows to establish satellite nodes in at least two less-research-intensive regions, turning this into a diffusion engine as well as a retention tool.

At the same time, we are finalising the Chips Act delegated act to bind Gigafactory funding to Geneva pool participation — a move that now serves dual purposes: securing maintenance autonomy and signalling to vendors and researchers alike that the EU is not just building capacity, but governing it. This is no longer about catching up. It is about proving that the Union can be a sovereign pole in the AI era — not through rhetoric, but through binding commitments to those who build the future.
