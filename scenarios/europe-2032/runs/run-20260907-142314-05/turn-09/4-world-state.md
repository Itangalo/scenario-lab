### Stagnant Leverage, Rising Risk

Global AI capability inches upward to 61.0 as frontier labs integrate insights from the recent open-weight leak, advancing internal tooling despite no public leap. The closed frontier creeps forward, but the real shift remains the widespread availability of persistent agent systems now running on consumer hardware—capability once confined to secure environments has dispersed irreversibly.

The EU’s push for sovereign compute falters. Berlin remains silent on the co-location offer with Cyber Command, wary of entangling civilian infrastructure with military networks. Dublin rejects the green compute corridor, citing unresolved grid stability concerns and legal uncertainty over EU absorption guarantees. No new capacity is secured. The Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation remains in legal force but lacks grounding in reality—its deadline now slips as member-state alignment fractures.

A redacted assessment from the European Evaluation Shield is released, confirming "anomalous, persistent agent behaviors" in two major systems and mandating remediation plans. However, the lack of public detail fuels skepticism. Civil society groups denounce it as performative, while developers challenge the legal basis for market access conditions. Trust is not restored; scrutiny deepens.

On the ground, the Municipal Resilience Backchannel Integration advances technically: Lyon, Barcelona, and Helsinki begin interoperability testing under ENISA oversight. But without funding commitments, expansion stalls. Local gains remain isolated, overshadowed by systemic drift.

Political capital erodes further. With four large measures in flight—including two category 4 and 6 initiatives—and a named priority, the portfolio charge remains severe. No measure finishes. The proposal bonus is denied: the backchannel integration addresses resilience, not the openweight surge. Capital falls to 6.0, now critically below 12. At this level, the EU risks losing control of its agenda—next turn, new initiatives may fail to start outright.

Public sentiment holds at 35.0, sustained by quiet local utility but strained by visible institutional inertia.
