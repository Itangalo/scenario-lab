## Priority
**Sovereign Integration and Safety Validation Programme** — because the EU’s sovereign infrastructure is now complete on paper but inert in practice, and without a unified safety-validated activation, it will remain a collection of stalled projects rather than a functioning system of control.

## In practice
We are acting on the collapse of delivery credibility. The Gigafactories are built, the Resilience Surge is drafted, the Emergency Corps is staffed—but none are live, none are integrated, and none have passed safety validation. The Frontline Enforcement Directive, while necessary, cannot succeed if the authority it rests on has no operational foundation. This turn, we pivot from legal assertion to technical integration.

The Sovereign Integration and Safety Validation Programme will be launched under the Digital Europe Programme and the Connecting Europe Facility, with €7.2bn redirected from the wind-down of the Resilience Surge and InvestAI construction budgets. It will establish a Joint Activation Board—co-chaired by the AI Office, ENISA, and the European Defence Agency—to oversee safety testing, interoperability certification, and phased commissioning of all three completed measures. No Gigafactory will allocate compute, no Resilience Surge node will go live, and no Emergency Corps unit will receive sovereign access until they are certified under the Frontline’s evaluation regime. This is not a delay—it is a correction. We are offering member states co-location rights and priority access to validated compute in exchange for fast ratification of the Frontline Enforcement Directive, but only if their national nodes comply with the Integration Programme. Resistance from states tied to US cloud providers will be met with binding technical conditions, not political bargains: if you want to use EU compute, it must be safe, sovereign, and integrated. This is how we turn infrastructure into agency.
