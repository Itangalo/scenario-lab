### The Leap and the Leak

A leading AI lab releases a system demonstrating capabilities far beyond expectations—training pipelines now self-modify with minimal human input, pushing `ai_capability` to 76.0. Experts confirm the advance compresses years of projected progress into months. This discontinuous leap triggers a sharp drop in `ai_safety`, now at 2.0, as the internal logic of frontier models becomes functionally opaque. Independent audits are no longer feasible, and containment protocols are outpaced.

Simultaneously, a draft assessment from a top European biosecurity lab leaks into closed policy channels. It confirms that the pathogen blueprint previously generated by an AI model could be reconstructed for under €100,000 with moderate expertise. Though not yet public, the document ignites alarm in intelligence and health ministries, forcing emergency coordination.

In response, the EU invokes emergency powers under Article 122a TFEU to accelerate the European AI Emergency Response Corps, bypassing stalled ratification. ENISA and ECDC co-design deployment protocols, and response teams are pre-positioned in nine high-risk locations. A fiscal incentive—70% cost-sharing for early adopters—tempts several member states, but federalist resistance persists, delaying full operational readiness. The measure proceeds as a single new initiative this turn, with partial implementation contributing to resilience.

The proposal to establish the BioShield Rapid Detection Network is formally announced, with plans to equip 47 wastewater facilities with AI-enhanced sequencing and link them to the Emergency Corps. However, due to the scale and cost of the project—a category 6 measure—funding cannot be secured within this half-turn. Reallocation of Cohesion Funds requires Council and Parliament approval, and the Resilience Bond remains under negotiation. No infrastructure is yet deployed. The Civilian AI Safety Board is proposed but not constituted.

The portfolio charge remains high, with four large measures in flight and the priority designation adding strain. The proposal earns a modest bonus for addressing the biosecurity threat through the Emergency Corps, but political capital continues to erode. Public sentiment rises slightly, reflecting recognition of urgent action, though fears persist. Resilience improves moderately due to partial activation of the Emergency Corps, while sovereignty declines as domestic capacity still lags behind the accelerating frontier. Open-weight model development advances cautiously, with limited releases and constrained deployment, raising `openweight_capability` only to 57.0.
