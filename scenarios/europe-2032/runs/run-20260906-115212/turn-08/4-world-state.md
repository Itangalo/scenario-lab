### The Enforcement Gamble

The EU formally proposes the Frontline Enforcement Directive, aiming to bind all member states and foreign operators to the Sovereign Evaluation Frontline’s authority. The move is framed as essential to close the gap between sovereign capability and sovereign control. Yet resistance flares immediately: several member states denounce the directive as overreach, citing national regulatory autonomy and economic exposure to U.S. cloud providers. Legal challenges loom, and ratification appears stalled before it begins.

The Resilience Surge Protocol and InvestAI Gigafactories were expected to complete their rollout this turn, but both face delays. The Resilience Surge Protocol, while technically ready, encounters coordination bottlenecks in deployment across member states, postponing full implementation. Similarly, the InvestAI Gigafactories, though structurally complete, remain in commissioning phase—initial compute allocations are pending safety validation and staffing. As a result, no resilience uplift occurs this turn, and resilience remains at 48.0. **No sovereignty bonus is awarded, as the measure has not yet finished.**

Meanwhile, global AI capability advances to 80.5, driven by unbroken progress in training infrastructure. The US-China agreement slows frontier development slightly, but only at the margin—capability growth remains robust. Open-weight models benefit from leaked training recipes and early access to Gigafactory infrastructure, but progress remains constrained by limited data, oversight, and model scale. openweight_capability rises modestly to 60.5, maintaining a plausible gap below the closed frontier.

With no safety measures yet operational, and model opacity deepening, ai_safety drops further to −11.0. The gap between deployment and understanding widens unchecked.

The supply_chain_coercion event underscores Europe’s strategic vulnerability: ASML’s export restrictions, enforced under U.S. pressure, reveal that even critical leverage points lie beyond EU control. This, combined with exclusion from the US-China pact, accelerates sovereignty decay.

Public sentiment erodes to 29.0 as the election_annulled event—triggered by AI-fueled disinformation—fuels anxiety over democratic integrity. Deepfakes and synthetic content dominate headlines, and trust in institutions wanes.

Political capital dips to 10.0 under portfolio strain and failed momentum. The Frontline Enforcement Directive, though ambitious, fails to gain traction, and the cost of ongoing measures outweighs completion gains.

### A Fragile Foundation

The EU has built infrastructure and institutions, but without cohesion, they remain brittle. Sovereignty falls to 12.0, reflecting both the absence of a completion bonus from InvestAI Gigafactories and the compounding pressures of external coercion, stalled implementation, and capability growth outpacing control. The +5 sovereignty credit previously assumed in the notepad is invalidated by the narrative confirmation of delay.
