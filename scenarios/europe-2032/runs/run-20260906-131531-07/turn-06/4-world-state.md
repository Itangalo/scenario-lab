### The Surge That Wasn’t

The EU doubled down on crisis response, launching the **Mandatory Pre-Deployment Biosecurity Audit Regime**—a large category 1 measure aimed at forcing adversarial testing of frontier models for bio-risk potential. It is a bold assertion of regulatory authority, but one that immediately runs aground. OpenAI and Anthropic agree to “technical consultations,” but refuse to accept binding pre-deployment blocks. Meta delays participation pending U.S. federal guidance. With no leverage over compute or talent, enforcement hinges on market access—but exemptions for research use and national security carveouts erode its scope before it launches.

Meanwhile, the **European AI Emergency Response Corps** begins recruitment, but legal gridlock stalls deployment. Only five member states ratify the Article 122a activation by June; Hungary and Poland demand veto rights over interventions. Cross-border authority remains fragmented, and ENISA’s red teams operate under ad hoc permissions, not standing mandate.

### Capability Leaps, Safety Falls

A sudden advance in model self-improvement pushes **ai_capability** to 76.0—a jump that outpaces even recent trends. Internal reasoning becomes more opaque overnight. This leap, combined with no safety measure landing on shipped systems, triggers a steep drop in **ai_safety**, now at 5.0 after accounting for the capability_jump event.

The leaked biosecurity assessment confirms fears: the pathogen blueprint is not just theoretical. A network of independent labs reproduces key synthesis steps, validating the low-cost pathway. Public exposure is narrowly avoided—but trust evaporates in closed channels.

**Resilience** rises slightly as surge funding reaches pilot sites, but operational cohesion lags. **Openweight_capability** climbs to 58.0, as older frontier techniques diffuse into open ecosystems, enabling new misuse vectors in cyber and synthetic biology.

The portfolio charge remains crushing: four large measures in flight cost −12, plus −1 for priority, totaling −13. The new biosecurity audit qualifies as a direct response to the emergent_biosecurity_leak, earning a +4 proposal bonus. But with **eu_political_capital** already fragile, net capital drops to 9.0. Sovereignty falls further as capability growth widens the dependence gap, and no sovereignty-boosting measure finishes. Public sentiment declines amid growing anxiety over uncontrollable risks.
