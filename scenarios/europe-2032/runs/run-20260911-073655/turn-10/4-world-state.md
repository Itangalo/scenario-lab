### War over infrastructure itself
In February, American and Chinese forces began striking the infrastructure of intelligence itself. The stated reason on both sides was prevention: to deny the other an edge. Undersea cables were cut, satellite links jammed, and two fabrication plants in East Asia burned. A data-centre park in southern Europe lost power when a landing station was damaged, and allocation queues for therapies, models and chips froze for days.

Brussels declared its territory, landing stations and permitted construction plots non-belligerent and offered deconfliction to both capitals. Neither offered guarantees. Licensed doses and scheduling software continued intermittently on stockpiled capacity, but clinicians learned to check availability hour by hour.

### The open frontier escapes
In the middle of the war, a leading laboratory published openly downloadable weights. Hundreds of thousands of copies spread in a week. European security officials admitted privately that whatever controls they had imagined now lived on private hardware beyond recall.

At home, hiring data confirmed what graduates already knew: entry posts in law, accountancy, software support and administration were not coming back. Bridge payments under the now-operational Guarantee reached some logistics and port workers idled by blockades and war disruption, but mayors said the money did not answer the closed interview rooms.

Night fires spread from fenced robot pilots to the grid-connection works for the two gigafactory plots. Cranes stood idle under guard, and emergency energy ministers took direct control of repairs. Officials said the damaged grid works would need to be rebuilt and re-permitted, pushing completion of the plots back by multiple turns with no new sites promised while repairs and investigations continued. Health, power and telecom operators isolated themselves from spillover cyberattacks and rerouted traffic, with mixed success.

The middle-power club met continuously, pooling what compute and test access survived and pressing the wayward capital to return to the common line. It kept a channel open. It did not keep supply whole.
