### A Turn of Fractured Resolve

Despite urgent rhetoric, the EU’s response to the cyberattack and Hungary’s defection falters under institutional strain. The Secure Execution Environment, now the top priority, sees only partial progress: Finland confirms node deployment, but France’s certification delays persist despite emergency fast-tracking, and Germany’s refusal to accept intelligence-linked oversight forces a weakened civilian mandate. The EU AI Trust Authority remains under-resourced and legally untested, undermining confidence in its ability to enforce containment.

The new **Mandate for Containment Zones** sparks immediate backlash. U.S. and Chinese developers denounce it as extraterritorial overreach, while open-source communities warn it will fracture collaboration and drive innovation underground. Early negotiations with non-U.S. labs stall, and no major developer commits to voluntary compliance. The Commission signals readiness to enforce through the Digital Markets Act, but legal challenges loom, and enforcement mechanisms remain untested.

Public messaging frames containment as “pro-stability,” but with unemployment rising in cognitive sectors and the leaked evaluation anomaly still circulating, sentiment remains sour. The communication campaign fails to gain traction, and no visible public benefit offsets the perception of regulatory aggression.

Meanwhile, **verification_widens** as automated checks emerge in legal and clinical domains, accelerating capability deployment despite ambiguous signals from the frontier. The **capability_plateau_evidence** — falling prices and underwhelming releases — fuels debate, but open-weight models continue to spread. With no finished safety or resilience measures, and offensive use-cases outpacing defences, systemic risk grows.

The **member_state_defection** deepens distrust, with other states quietly exploring bilateral deals. Sovereignty remains stagnant, political capital erodes further, and resilience dips as defences fail to keep pace.

### No Ground Gained

The EU acts, but without cohesion or capacity, its measures remain aspirational. Detection without enforcement proves hollow, and restriction without leverage collapses into symbolism. The window to shape the open frontier narrows.
