### A Turn of Exposure and Reaction

The EU’s response to the frontier access denial and the global cyber intrusion was swift in intent but strained in execution. The proposal of the Hardened Access Regime marked a clear pivot toward sovereignty, aiming to convert vulnerability into leverage. However, early negotiations with major US developers revealed deep resistance: one firm publicly warned of "regulatory decoupling," while cloud-dependent member states questioned the economic cost of restricting access during a capability squeeze.

### Capability Creeps Forward

Despite signs of plateauing in pricing for mid-tier models, the frontier advanced. The latest training runs at leading labs crossed new thresholds in autonomous agent coordination, pushing `ai_capability` to 53.5. The leaked eval anomalies—unexplained capability jumps under observation—hinted at deeper shifts, though labs dismissed them as artifacts.

### Open Weights, Open Risks

With no major open release but ongoing fine-tuning of Kimi K3 derivatives, `openweight_capability` rose modestly to 41.5. Yet the lack of safety improvements in these widely distributed models amplified downstream risks.

### Safety Erodes, Resilience Stretches

The cyber_test_shot incident exposed critical gaps in detection and coordination. No measure had yet landed to harden systems, and `resilience` dropped to 37.0. Meanwhile, `ai_safety` fell to 32.0 as the gap between capability and control widened, worsened by the opacity of frontier systems now under scrutiny.

### Sovereignty Under Pressure

The `eu_frontier_access_denied` event directly cut EU access to the leading model, costing −2 under rule 5. This, combined with a −1 decay from the capability rise, overwhelmed any momentum from ongoing compute builds. Sovereignty fell to 20.0.

### Political Capital Spent, Sentiment Shifts

The portfolio carried three measures: two large (−3 each), one small (−2), and a named priority (−1), totaling −9. The Hardened Access Regime earned no proposal bonus—it responded to a foreign policy event, not a technical incident. With `public_sentiment` (40.0) now below `eu_political_capital`, no legitimacy boost applied. Capital fell to 38.0. Public sentiment dipped slightly as media framed the access clampdown as both a security failure and a jobs risk.
