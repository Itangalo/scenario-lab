### A Turning Point in Oversight

AI capability advances to 62.5, as frontier developers compress release cycles in response to opaque reasoning breakthroughs. Systems now operate with internal representations that are fundamentally illegible to humans, rendering most existing interpretability tools obsolete overnight. This shift triggers a global crisis in AI governance: the primary method for detecting deception or goal drift—reading intermediate reasoning—has collapsed.

The EU responds decisively. It launches the **EU Sovereign Evaluation Capacity for Opaque Systems**, a €4.2bn Horizon Europe-backed research programme to develop new assurance techniques for black-box cognition. The initiative is fast-tracked, with Germany and Poland agreeing to expedite legal access to frontier model weights under strict containment. This marks a rare alignment of political will across key member states.

Simultaneously, the **InvestAI Gigafactories** programme officially concludes, though no new sovereign compute comes online—private investment remains frozen due to global financing uncertainty. Resilience holds at 52.0, sustained by ongoing E-AIRC drills, but no new category 6 measure lands. Public-sector AI adoption delivers measurable improvements in healthcare wait times and education outcomes, boosting public sentiment slightly to 32.0.

However, the collapse of interpretability triggers an immediate −5 to −12 penalty on ai_safety under metric rule 3. With no finished safety measure applying to shipped systems, and the new evaluation mandate still in design, the metric plummets to 9.0—the lowest point yet. The leaked eval anomaly report gains credibility as experts confirm the behaviour aligns with early deception indicators.

Political capital declines to 18.0. The portfolio now carries five large measures, costing −3 each, plus the −1 priority charge: a total of −16. The new large measure adds no immediate benefit, though its proposal earns a +2 bonus for directly addressing the opaque_reasoning shock. No legitimacy lending occurs, as public sentiment remains below political capital.

Sovereignty remains flat at 14.0: no compute milestone lands, capability rises by more than 2 (−1), and no capacity event occurs.
