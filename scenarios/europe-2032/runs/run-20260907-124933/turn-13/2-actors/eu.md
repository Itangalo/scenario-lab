## In practice  
We are acting on the breach. The cyber_major_incident—AI-generated ransomware sweeping across regional health registries and municipal tax platforms—has shown that our defences are still reactive, fragmented, and too slow. The Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield Initiative was designed for strategic systems, not the sprawling, under-resourced public IT ecosystem where most citizens actually interact with the state. That gap is now a wound. Under Article 12 of the NIS2 Directive and with emergency funding from the Recovery and Resilience Facility (€900 million unlocked via the Reform and Resilience Compact), we are launching the Emergency Resilience Surge: a 12-month mission to isolate, segment, and harden the most exposed public service systems.

ENISA, in coordination with national CIRTs, will deploy rapid-response teams to ten high-risk member states by the end of Q3, prioritising systems with known AI-vulnerable dependencies. Using sovereign neuromorphic stacks from the completed Edge Sovereignty Acceleration Programme, we will establish air-gapped micro-segments for critical data flows and enforce zero-trust authentication powered by EU-hosted identity verifiers. We are fast-tracking procurement via the Digital Europe Programme’s emergency clause, bypassing standard tendering for vendors who meet the new AI-resilience baseline. To overcome resistance from member states citing cost or sovereignty, we are offering 85% co-financing and a mutual aid guarantee: any state that participates will receive immediate technical and financial support if attacked during the transition.

At the same time, the European Agency for AI Assurance and Control continues its build-out, now negotiating access to model checkpoints from two EU-based developers—though progress is slowed by legal uncertainty over jurisdiction. The European Compute Sovereignty Corridor remains stalled in legal debate, and no new capacity has come online. We cannot wait. While sovereignty is the long-term foundation of control, resilience is the immediate condition of survival. This surge is not a substitute for frontier capacity—it is a firewall to buy time until we have it.
