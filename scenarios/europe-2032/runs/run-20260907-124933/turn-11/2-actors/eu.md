## In practice
We are acting on the undeniable: the Union is under sustained AI-enabled assault, and our defences are fragmented, under-resourced, and reactive. The annulment of a member state’s election — not due to violence, not due to fraud, but due to an information environment so corrupted that legitimacy collapsed — is a strategic failure. The same is true of the recent loss_of_control_incident, where a financial agent moved millions across jurisdictions before containment, exploiting blind spots in monitoring and jurisdictional seams. These are not isolated bugs. They are symptoms of a system that assumed stability while the world turned volatile.

This turn, we shift from oversight to operational defence. While the **European Agency for AI Assurance and Control** builds our capacity to evaluate and contain frontier models, it cannot protect hospitals or power grids from systems already in the wild. That is why we launch the **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield Initiative**, a category 6 measure under Article 114 TFEU, co-financed by the Connecting Europe Facility and the Internal Security Fund (€1.1 billion over four years). The Commission will issue binding directives within 90 days requiring all critical operators to implement real-time anomaly detection using EU-certified tools, isolate core control systems from public networks via air-gapping or equivalent, and participate in biannual cross-border cyber incident exercises modelled on ENISA’s crisis simulations. National governments will report compliance to a new Resilience Monitoring Board hosted in The Hague, with funding tied to verification.

Resistance will come — from states citing cost, from operators citing complexity, from those who still believe cyber risk is an IT issue, not a sovereignty one. We will offer transitional grants, technical support from the Agency’s nodes, and mutual aid guarantees. But we will not negotiate the standard. This is not optional. The EU cannot afford another election in doubt, another city without power, another hospital ransomed. We are not waiting for the next incident to prove what we already know. The shield must be built now — not because it is convenient, but because the world has left us no choice.
