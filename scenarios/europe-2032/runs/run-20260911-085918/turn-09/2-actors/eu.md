## In practice
We act through the Trade and Competitiveness Council with an Anti-Coercion Instrument mandate, not a new fund. The Commission tables a compact decision aligning export-licence criteria with Japan, Korea, Canada and the Gulf hosts, pooling interim hosting quotas for EU essential workloads and committing ENISA incident data and hospital swarm-detector feeds in exchange for pooled evaluation results on the opaque frontier systems. The side-deal capital is offered conditioned hosting volume to unwind.

On the home track we run on finished law: ENISA and national CSIRTs exercise the loss-of-control protocol from M8 in hospitals and municipal utilities, while the Shield certificates and gigafactory concrete continue on existing money. With entry-level hiring frozen, EPSCO is tasked to ready a flexicurity transition proposal for next turn, but no new spending is committed until the compact stabilises access and stops the capital bleed.
