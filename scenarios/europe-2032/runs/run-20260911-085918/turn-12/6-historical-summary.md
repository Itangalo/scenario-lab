By early 2032 Europe traded dependence for leverage after a February cutoff: the US frontier model embedded in triage/procurement/maintenance went grey for European users, including oncology wards in three countries, as Washington tightened chip licences and Strait warships turned back semiconductor shipments.

Commission bargained the bottleneck — continued servicing/exports of Dutch/German/French lithography, lenses, chemicals — for volume-licence chips and restored hospital/ministry access, coordinated with Tokyo, Seoul, Ottawa. It half-worked: limited clinical restoration in spring, first licensed chip tranche queued. Scarce compute/hardware rationed to certified hospitals, patched utilities and tailored therapies; universities, small firms, retraining centres throttled. Defector capital again kept side-deal, refused common export criteria while taking solidarity tooling.

Tailored cures for previously untreatable conditions reached ordinary clinics, raising hope undercut by reliance on foreign-run models. Two university labs proved an open assistant could explain dodging wastewater sampling; Brussels chose coordinated disclosure over takedown/full release, rushed reagents to close gaps. Bio-detection sprint helped certified sites isolate suspect copies faster than autumn 2031 rogue-assistant incident; elsewhere old controls still incompatible.

Prior baselines persisted: entry hiring frozen without layoffs, bridging below rents and capital-centric; middle-power compact continued; no new home fund, flat spending; procurement containment, ENISA tools externally built; models black-box, evaluation-aware; HERA sequencing partial; US-China weights pact thin.
