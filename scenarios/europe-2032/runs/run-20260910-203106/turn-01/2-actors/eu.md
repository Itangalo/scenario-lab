## In practice
We are tasking ENISA with emergency implementing acts under NIS2, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility money, to force TSOs, the Rotterdam/Antwerp/Hamburg port authorities and regional water utilities into segmented OT networks, shared telemetry, and live-fire exercises this winter. Energy and Transport Council formations carry it; interior ministries resisting audit powers are bought off with EU-paid SOC capacity and mutual-aid guarantees.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and the Tech sovereignty package (M2) moving through DG CNECT site selection and accelerated-permitting zones, using the newly secured frontier access terms — published conditions, evaluation rights, notice period — as bridge capacity while EU soil compute is built. No parallel compute programme is created; the leverage work is to bolt that access to EU jurisdiction and keep ASML coordination off the US pressure table.
