However, strategic vulnerabilities deepened: the EU was locked out of access to the most advanced AI models (eu_frontier_access_denied), exposing critical dependency, while U.S.-driven supply chain coercion restricted semiconductor tool exports via ASML. In response, the EU mandated Jurisdictional Accountability for Autonomous AI Systems, requiring registration, kill-switches, and EAAD monitoring for high-capability AI—marking a shift from infrastructure sovereignty to regulatory control.
