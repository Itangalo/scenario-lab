## In practice
We are acting on the collapse of political unity in the face of accelerating risk. The Emergency Empowerment measure has now finished — legally stalled, politically spent, operationally stillborn. Its failure is not in the intent but in the assumption: that consensus could be achieved after the threat was visible. We are no longer making that mistake. The kill switch directive remains in flight, and we are doubling down on implementation. Under Article 114 TFEU, we are instructing ENISA to issue technical specifications by turn 10, mandating that all AI systems in energy, transport, health, and election infrastructure embed EAA-controlled override modules. Retrofit funding from the Digital Europe Programme — €800m — is being released to operators on condition of compliance, with no opt-outs. We are establishing a joint EAA-ENISA command cell with national liaison officers to preserve visibility, but not veto power. The sunset clause at ai_safety 50.0 remains, but we will not allow process to become paralysis.

At the same time, we are launching the Sovereign Compute Acceleration Programme: a €6.2bn initiative to build three AI gigafactories on EU soil — in Finland, Spain, and Poland — co-located with renewable energy sources and secured under EU legal jurisdiction. This is not about commercial competitiveness; it is about having the compute to run independent evaluations, stress-test agent behaviours, and operate crisis-response models when external access is denied or compromised. We are negotiating with ASML, Intel, and NVIDIA under the Chips Act framework to prioritise EU-sourced lithography and packaging, and we are offering tax incentives tied to data sovereignty clauses. Resistance from member states is expected — Germany questions cost, France demands strategic autonomy — but we are framing this as infrastructure, not technology: the electricity grid for the cognitive age. The safety_breakthrough this turn — a real interpretability advance adopted across frontier labs — proves that progress is possible, but only if we have the sovereign capacity to use it. We will not wait to be granted access. We will build it ourselves.
