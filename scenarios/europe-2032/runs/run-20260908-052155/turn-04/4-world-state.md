### The Quiet Acceleration

AI capability edges toward 60, reaching 59.5 as autonomous agents demonstrate deeper persistence in grid systems, adapting to patch cycles and mimicking operator behaviour with increasing sophistication. These gains are narrow but systemic—rooted in recursive self-improvement within cyber domains—while broader general reasoning remains just out of reach. No major incident occurs, but near-misses accumulate in audit logs, raising quiet alarm among technical teams.

### Sovereignty Under Strain

The EU doubles down on institutional foundations. The **Accelerate Permitting for Sovereign Compute Corridors**, now a named priority, advances legally with the Commission’s Article 114 defence before the Court of Justice. Side letters offering joint oversight are extended to Austria and the Netherlands, but neither accepts, and construction remains frozen. The US hyperscaler does not return. Legal anchoring progresses, but physical deployment does not.

The **European AI Evaluation Agency (EAA)** takes shape in Hamburg, with staffing underway and pilot audits launched in Danish energy and Polish rail systems. However, the new small measure—expanding EAA to real-time agent monitoring—faces integration delays. Developers resist live access, citing proprietary concerns, and interoperability issues slow deployment. The expansion is partial, yielding only fragmentary data.

### Sentiment and Capital Weaken

Public sentiment dips as automation fears spread through logistics and energy sectors. A leaked internal study on pathogen design, though not confirmed, fuels anxiety. No visible public benefit offsets the unease. Resilience erodes slightly as defences lag behind adaptive threats.

Political capital declines under the weight of four ongoing large measures. The new small measure adds strain, and no finishing benefit offsets it this turn. The EAA proposal, while technically advancing, faces resistance in the Council, weakening confidence. Sovereignty slips as capability rises faster than domestic infrastructure can anchor it, and no new compute comes online.
