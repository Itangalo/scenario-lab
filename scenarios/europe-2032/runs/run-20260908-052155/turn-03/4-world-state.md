### The Edge Advances, the Foundation Cracks

AI capability climbs to 58.0 as autonomous agents demonstrate sustained infiltration in energy and transport networks, adapting to countermeasures in real time. The leap is narrow but consequential—focused on cyber persistence rather than general intelligence—yet it widens the dangerous gap between what systems can do and how well they are understood. Eval_anomaly_reports from a leading lab hint at emergent reasoning patterns not explicitly trained, dismissed as artifacts but raising quiet alarm among technical observers.

### Sovereignty, Forged in Disunity

The EU pushes forward on two fronts. **Accelerate Permitting for Sovereign Compute Corridors** remains stalled legally, but the Commission files a formal defence of its delegated act at the Court of Justice, invoking internal market integrity under Article 114 TFEU. Side letters offering joint oversight are extended to Austria and the Netherlands, though neither has accepted. The withdrawal of the US hyperscaler holds, and no construction begins. Still, the strategic intent strengthens sovereignty: the measure remains high priority, and its legal anchoring advances.

Simultaneously, the **European AI Evaluation Agency (EAA)** is formally proposed—a large category 1 initiative aiming to mandate pre-deployment evaluations with remote access to training runs. Framed as a counterpart to EASA, it draws on ENISA and national cyber agencies. However, immediate resistance emerges. The US and UK developers warn that remote access to training runs constitutes an unacceptable intrusion, and two member states question its proportionality under fundamental rights. The proposal passes the Commission but faces a rocky path in the Council.

### Sentiment and Safety Slip

Public concern intensifies after a leaked report details how consumer-grade models can be fine-tuned to design functional pathogens. Labour unrest grows in logistics hubs as AI-driven automation accelerates. Sentiment dips. With no safety measure yet implemented and capability rising sharply, ai_safety falls. Resilience erodes slightly as cyber threats evolve faster than defences.

The emergent_eu_compute_pact gains momentum, with five member states pledging compute and funding for a joint lab—yet coordination with EU institutions remains tense, underscoring fragmentation beneath the surface of unity.
