- modify `sovereign_execution` (commitment): Deliver sovereign AI systems into operation—tested, secured, and under EU authority—by building red-teaming, evaluation, and incident response into the core of our technical institutions, and now by ensuring those institutions can act in real time during cascading crises.

**Grounds:** The cyber_major_incident exposed not only our technical dependence but our operational paralysis: EU-REACT had to be fast-tracked under emergency powers because standing procedures could not respond to a self-propagating threat. Meanwhile, supply_chain_coercion confirmed that even our one strategic chokepoint—ASML—is not ours to wield. These are not failures of will but of architecture: we designed for oversight, not for war-fighting. The distinction between development and deployment, between research and response, has collapsed. Thus, we update `sovereign_execution` to reflect that sovereign systems must be *operational* systems, not just evaluated ones. And we reaffirm `two_year_commitment` not as a forward-looking ambition but as a present-tense condition of survival—because if we do not act now as if autonomy is non-negotiable, it will be gone.

## In practice  
We are acting on a new premise: that sovereignty is not a policy goal but a precondition for action. The ransomware wave did not just expose weak defences—it revealed that our crisis response was blind, because the models we needed to understand the attack were withdrawn the moment they became essential. We were not just dependent; we were disposable. That ends now.  

EMERGE will consolidate 120,000 GPU-years of compute from decommissioned national assets, repurposed commercial clusters, and emergency procurement under a single EU legal framework. The cluster will be split between the EuroHPC sites in Kajaani and Lummeri, with air-gapped control rooms and sovereign firmware. Horizon AI will lead model training, using red-teamed datasets and embedded interpretability, with the goal of fielding a model capable of autonomous cyber defence and crisis simulation by turn 9. We are invoking Article 122 TFEU for emergency procurement, bypassing standard tendering, and using the Anti-Coercion Instrument to secure component supply against U.S. export controls.  

Poland and Hungary remain resistant to data sharing, but we are offering them guaranteed access to EMERGE’s defensive outputs and co-ownership of the training infrastructure—sovereignty is not a gift, but a shared burden. This is not a research project. It is the foundation of a sovereign nervous system. Without it, every directive, every cell, every plan is just a script waiting for the lights to go out.
