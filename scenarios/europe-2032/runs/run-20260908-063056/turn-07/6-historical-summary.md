AI capability stagnates at 59.5 as a devastating AI-driven cyberattack cripples critical infrastructure across three EU nations, exposing the continent’s vulnerability due to reliance on foreign models and abrupt access cuts. Resilience declines further to 43.0 amid ineffective defenses and fragmented crisis response. The EU cancels the InvestAI Gigafactories and launches the EMERGE programme to build a sovereign AI and compute cluster using national supercomputing assets in Finland and France, though hardware procurement remains unstarted and legal, political, and supply-chain obstacles loom. U.S. pressure intensifies, restricting ASML servicing and deepening Europe’s technological dependency. Poland and Hungary block data sharing with EU-REACT, exacerbating institutional fragmentation. Public sentiment remains at 35.0, with no visible AI benefits and widespread job losses eroding trust. Talent Anchoring and Horizon AI yield minor gains, but systemic collapse overshadows progress. Sovereignty drops to 16.0 as strategic autonomy remains unrealized, and political capital stagnates under sustained external pressure and internal disunity, leaving the EU unable to launch new initiatives amid mounting crises.
