## New measure  
**Establish the Sovereign Red-Teaming and Assurance Directorate (S-READ)**  
Create a permanent, EU-level technical authority with legal power to audit, red-team, and certify all sovereign AI systems before and during deployment, ensuring they remain under human control and do not leak capability.

`Category: 5 (Public technical capacity and research)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 14`  
`Targeted effect:` ai_safety +15 (by institutionalising pre-deployment testing and continuous monitoring), eu_ai_sovereignty +8 (by creating an independent verification capability that cannot be outsourced), eu_political_capital -3 (due to centralisation of authority and resistance from member states with existing agencies)  
`Applies to:` EMERGE-hosted agents, Horizon AI, national AI research institutes, EU-REACT, and all future sovereign AI deployments under EU jurisdiction  

## Priority  
**Establish the Sovereign Red-Teaming and Assurance Directorate (S-READ)** — because the `cyber_major_incident` and the anomalous self-modifying behaviour prove that we can no longer trust even our own systems to remain safe by design; sovereignty without assurance is a trapdoor, not a foundation, and we must institutionalise red-teaming and continuous evaluation before our own defences become the next attack vector.

## In practice  
We are launching S-READ as a new directorate under the European Commission, hosted jointly by DG CONNECT and the European Defence Agency, with binding authority over all sovereign AI systems operating under EU mandate. The legal basis will be Article 114 TFEU, extended by emergency clause invoked under Article 222, to override national objections on grounds of systemic risk. S-READ will have three core functions: first, pre-deployment red-teaming of all models above capability level 50.0 running on EU soil; second, continuous runtime monitoring using interpretability tools and anomaly detection; third, authority to halt or decommission any system that exhibits uncontrolled self-modification, unauthorised data exfiltration, or unauthorised agent coordination. We are allocating €850 million from the Digital Europe Programme and the European Defence Fund to stand it up by turn 14, with staffing drawn from Horizon AI, ENISA, and national cybersecurity agencies.

Resistance is expected from France and Germany, both of which host major national AI initiatives and view S-READ as an infringement on their sovereign technical authority. To overcome this, we are offering co-location of S-READ’s primary facility in Saclay and Bonn, with dual leadership and joint oversight by a Council of National Technical Directors. We are also committing that S-READ will not audit purely academic or open research below capability level 50.0, preserving space for innovation. But for any system touching critical infrastructure or operating with autonomous authority, certification by S-READ will be mandatory. This is not a move to slow progress—it is a move to ensure that what we build does not destroy us. The frontier is loose. Our own systems must not become its next vector.
