### A Fragile Foundation

The EU completes the Emergency Resilience Surge and EU-REACT, but their impact is muted. Fallback systems exist on paper, yet drills reveal critical gaps in coordination and readiness. Cyber defences detect more threats, but cannot adapt to self-modifying agents—each new mutation slips through until manually patched. The EMERGE cluster comes online in fragments, its full potential delayed by component shortages and legal disputes over data access.

Sovereign Core Hardening begins in earnest, with geological surveys underway in Finland and France. The legal basis advances under emergency powers, but Germany and France remain noncommittal on S-READ’s authority, demanding national veto rights. The EU offers access to hardened defences in exchange for compliance—negotiations stall. Poland ignores the moratorium offer, citing national necessity, and deepens its bilateral AI partnership.

The Labour Transition Emergency Fund launches with €600 million in pre-financing. Early uptake is high, but retraining programmes lack capacity, and mobility assistance faces bureaucratic delays. Media coverage is mixed: some praise the gesture, others call it a bandage on a severed limb. Public sentiment inches up slightly, but anger simmers in cities where AI has erased entry-level careers.

### The Cost of Holding On

Political capital erodes further. Three large measures remain in flight, including the new priority. The completion of EU-REACT and the Resilience Surge yields no legitimacy gain—both are seen as overdue. The launch of the Labour Fund earns a modest boost, but not enough to offset ongoing costs. The EU’s credibility frays as member states act unilaterally, and trust in central coordination wanes.

Sovereignty declines again. No new autonomous compute becomes operational, and the EMERGE model remains below target capability. The air-gapped core is a promise, not a reality. With ai_capability rising, dependence deepens.

Openweight capability creeps up, fuelled by underground model leaks from Eastern Europe. No official release occurs, but modified agents with planning functions spread through developer forums. The frontier is no longer confined to labs.
