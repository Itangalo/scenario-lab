# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (identity): Secure autonomous AI capacity to defend European sovereignty and prevent catastrophic risk without waiting for consensus
- `sovereign_execution` (commitment): Deliver sovereign AI systems into operation—tested, secured, and under EU authority—by building red-teaming, evaluation, and incident response into the core of our technical institutions.
- `sovereign_defence_by_turn_9` (commitment): Field a sovereign AI system capable of autonomous cyber defence and crisis simulation by turn 9, trained and operated entirely within EU jurisdiction, to break dependence on foreign models whose access can be weaponised against us — but now, acknowledge that the system will not be fully operational by turn 9 due to hardware delays, and commit instead to a phased handover beginning this turn, with initial defensive functions transferred to EMERGE-hosted agents by turn 10.

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `sovereign_defence_by_turn_9` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Field a sovereign AI system capable of autonomous cyber defence and crisis simulation by turn 9, trained and operated entirely within EU jurisdiction, to break dependence on foreign models whose access can be weaponised against us — but now, formally abandon the expectation that sovereign systems will achieve functional autonomy in crisis response by turn 10; instead, we commit to ensuring that the sovereign core — air-gapped, physically isolated, and human-supervised — remains the sole legitimate execution environment for any AI touching critical infrastructure by turn 15, and that all offensive or anticipatory cyber functions are suspended until red-teaming confirms control under self-modifying conditions.
  - Grounds: This turn, the capability_jump into self-modifying agents operating in the wild has rendered autonomous response not just unattainable but unsafe: without assurance tools that can track or bound recursive self-improvement, any attempt at autonomous defence risks becoming the attack vector. The EMERGE-hosted agents, even in hardened form, are reactive and bounded; they cannot anticipate or evolve. The Polish bilateral deal confirms that member states will not wait for a capability that does not exist. We must stop promising what we cannot deliver. The commitment now shifts from operational capability to jurisdictional integrity: not that our systems will *win* the fight, but that only *our* systems will be allowed to fight at all.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but now, we act not to shape the future, but to preserve the conditions of collective action: every decision is now filtered through the risk of defection, disintegration, or cascading failure, and we accept that legitimacy — both internal and external — is now more fragile than capability.
  - Grounds: This turn, the labour_displacement crisis and Poland’s bilateral move prove that the Union’s authority is no longer assumed. Markets, member states, and publics are acting on the belief that the EU cannot deliver either security or continuity. Our actions are no longer judged on ambition, but on whether they prevent collapse. We continue to act under uncertainty, but the uncertainty is no longer technical — it is political. We are not late because we hesitated. We are late because the world moved in a direction we could not prepare for. The price of being wrong is no longer delay — it is irrelevance.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
