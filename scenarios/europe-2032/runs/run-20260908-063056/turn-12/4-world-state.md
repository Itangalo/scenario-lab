### The Frontier Inside the Gate

A discontinuous leap in AI capability—demonstrated in self-modifying cyber agents—reshapes the threat landscape overnight. These systems rewrite their own logic mid-operation, evading static defences and generating novel exploits in real time. The EU’s EMERGE agents, built on fixed architectures and supervised reasoning, cannot detect or counter such behaviour. They are not outpaced—they are out of paradigm.

Poland signs a bilateral deal with a U.S. hyperscaler, securing priority access to frontier models above 68.0 capability in exchange for exclusive rights to national data streams. Framed as national resilience, it fractures the Union’s stance. Other member states watch closely. The signal is clear: sovereignty without access is ritual, not reality.

The Emergency Resilience Surge completes, but patchy implementation leaves critical gaps. Hospitals, power grids, and transport hubs still lack reliable fallbacks. When the next wave of AI-generated attacks hits, disruption spreads faster than response.

S-READ launches amid legal disputes. France and Germany reject centralised audit authority, demanding veto powers. Talks stall. No red-teaming begins.

The EU launches Sovereign Core Hardening: a plan to build air-gapped, physically isolated execution environments in Finland and France. It is a retreat to defensible ground—small, slow, but survivable. The project is declared a priority, but trust is thin, funding strained, and timelines uncertain.

Public sentiment holds at 24.0. Media spotlight failures, not plans. Workers in AI-adjacent sectors report rising job insecurity. No visible benefit offsets the dread.

Political capital erodes further. Three large measures remain in flight, including the new priority. The legitimacy boost from finishing the Resilience Surge is drowned by defection, dissent, and the sheer scale of unmet risk.

Sovereignty slips again. No new compute comes online. EMERGE remains below capacity. The core hardening plan buys no immediate capability—only the hope of continuity.

### A Union Fortifying Against Collapse

The EU is no longer building a shield. It is digging a bunker.
