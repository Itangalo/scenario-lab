## New measure  
**Initiate Sovereign Core Hardening: Air-Gapped, Tamper-Resistant Execution Environment for EMERGE Agents**  
Launch the development of a minimal, air-gapped, physically isolated execution environment for EMERGE-hosted agents, hardened against remote compromise, insider threat, and model leakage, with no external connectivity and human-supervised decision gates for any action affecting critical infrastructure.

`Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 16`  
`Targeted effect:` eu_ai_sovereignty +12 (by establishing a physically secure, EU-only execution environment that cannot be disabled or subverted externally), ai_safety +5 (by reducing the attack surface for model exfiltration and unauthorised agent coordination), resilience +3 (by ensuring continuity of minimal defensive functions even during total network compromise)  
`Applies to:` EMERGE-hosted agents, S-READ-certified systems, and all future sovereign AI deployments touching critical infrastructure  

## Priority  
**Initiate Sovereign Core Hardening: Air-Gapped, Tamper-Resistant Execution Environment for EMERGE Agents** — because the capability_jump and member_state_defection prove that the threat is no longer external, but systemic: the frontier is inside our systems, and our own infrastructure is becoming the attack vector; without a physically secure core, even certified systems cannot be trusted, and the Union’s authority to act collectively will erode further.

## In practice  
We are launching the Sovereign Core Hardening programme under the Joint Undertaking for Critical AI Infrastructure, co-funded by the European Defence Fund and the Connecting Europe Facility, with €1.2 billion allocated over four years. The core will be built in two geographically separated, underground facilities — one in the Vätsäri Wilderness Area in Finland, the other in the Lorraine region of France — each with zero external network connectivity, power supplied by dedicated microgrids, and physical access restricted to dual-nationality EU-certified personnel. The architecture will be based on verified, open-hardware designs, with all firmware and execution paths subject to S-READ red-teaming before deployment. No model above capability level 50.0 will be allowed to operate outside this environment by turn 15.

We are negotiating with France and Germany to accept the loss of direct national control over their sovereign AI assets in exchange for guaranteed access to the core’s defensive functions and a seat on the Oversight Board. To Poland, we offer a moratorium on data-sharing penalties under the Digital Markets Act in exchange for suspending its bilateral hyperscaler agreement pending a full Council review. The legal basis will be Article 114 TFEU, extended by Article 222 emergency powers, with implementation through a delegated act requiring only qualified majority support in the Council — a necessary bypass, given that consensus is no longer tenable. This is not the future we wanted. It is the one we must build. The frontier is loose. Our only remaining advantage is the ground we stand on. We will fortify it.
