## New measure
**Municipal Recovery and Continuity Corps**
Puts EU-funded recovery teams and offline continuity kits into hit municipalities, hospitals and logistics operators to restore service without new buildout.
This and now because with capital at 3 and sentiment at 3 we cannot fund compute or force grid hookups, but we can keep essential services running through the third sweep and the US cutoff using existing NIS2/CER duties and stockpiles.

## Priority
The Municipal Recovery and Continuity Corps, because it is the only fundable action that serves our two-year commitment to hold together through dependence by hardening resilience while sovereignty tracks are frozen.

## In practice
We execute under NIS2 Article 11 and CER via ENISA and CERT-EU, with DG DIGIT and DG SANTE/HERA co-leading: joint triage cells redeployed to locked city administrations and clinics, daily patches, and distribution of offline continuity kits — paper fallback, clean images, backup comms — from the Grid Shield stockpiles now on national budgets. No new permitting, no attribution fight.

On the cutoff and export squeeze we do not retaliate: DG TRADE uses the standing Dutch-Japanese-Korean framework for joint licence requests and substitution sourcing, while DG CNECT keeps the five gigafactory permits legally warm and the Anti-Coercion review open without spending. Municipal grid-connection pauses are respected pending local votes; Corps teams offer continuity help instead of overriding referendums to rebuild trust.
