We are offering fast-track market access to providers who complete verification ahead of the turn 9 deadline, creating a race-to-the-top dynamic. Resistance remains in southern member states concerned about administrative burden, so we are pairing the requirement with technical assistance grants via the Digital Europe Programme, ensuring compliance does not fall along north-south lines. This is how we lock in safety—not by decree, but by making it the faster, smarter path.
