This lifts `openweight_capability` to 51.875, maintaining its historical relationship with `ai_capability` and respecting its non-decreasing nature under Invariant 2.

The European Union advances planning for a potential **Sovereign Compute Initiative**, a large category 4 measure aimed at securing 10% of global training-scale compute under EU control by turn 16. The concept gains internal traction as a response to widening capability-safety gaps, but no final decision is made this turn. Binding site agreements are under discussion in northern France, and chipmakers signal cautious interest. However, energy repurposing faces regulatory delays, and the scale of investment required gives pause. The initiative remains in development, with no hardware procurement or construction initiated. Crucially, **no formal proposal is submitted this turn**, and thus no portfolio charge is incurred. The planning phase continues, with internal debate over sequencing and trade-offs with existing efforts.

### Diplomatic and Technical Friction Mount

The EU’s prospective push collides with reality. US labs continue to reject telemetry requests, citing national security. The **ALLIANCE** posture holds, but cooperation frays at the edges. The paper is withheld from open release, but preprint copies circulate in encrypted forums.
