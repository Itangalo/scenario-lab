### Capability Creeps Forward

AI capability advances by +0.5 to 62.5, entering the terminal zone with quiet but persistent gains. No breakthroughs are announced, but leaked internal evaluations continue to show anomalous reasoning patterns in frontier models—behaviours not explicitly trained, emerging under pressure. Developers dismiss them as noise, but scrutiny grows.

### EU Rushes to Operationalise the Border

The European Union shifts from rulemaking to implementation, launching *Accelerate EAAN Certification Nodes*, a small category 5 measure to fast-track sovereign evaluation infrastructure in Brussels, Sophia Antipolis, and Munich. Backed by €3.2bn in redirected recovery funds and emergency procurement powers, the effort aims to make the *AI Deployment Boundary Act* enforceable by turn 13.

However, progress is uneven. Staffing bottlenecks and legal disputes with member states over data access slow node activation. The first telemetry requests to US labs are rejected on grounds of national security and trade secrecy, undermining the credibility of runtime monitoring.

### Diplomatic Tensions Mount

The hyperscaler that suspended EU deployments maintains its stance, calling EAAN’s demands “functionally equivalent to source code surrender.” Behind closed doors, the US administration expresses concern, though it stops short of formal retaliation—consistent with the ongoing **ALLIANCE** posture. A proposed compliance corridor, offering liability shielding and future compute access, fails to gain traction.

Meanwhile, rising military activity near Taiwan unsettles global supply chains and amplifies strategic anxiety, making coordination on AI governance harder.

### Political Capital Erodes Further

With three measures now in flight—including two large ones—political capital continues to drain. The new small measure adds cost without immediate payoff. Public sentiment remains stagnant at 35.0, offering no legitimacy boost. The EU’s hard line strengthens its sovereign posture in principle, but not yet in practice.
