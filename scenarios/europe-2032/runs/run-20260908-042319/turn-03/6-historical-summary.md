The Cyber Shield for Critical Infrastructure overcame legal resistance from Hungary and Slovakia through a compromise on cross-border security, with adoption expected by year’s end and binding red-teaming mandates underway. ENISA began drafting implementation guidelines, boosting resilience. The EU launched the Talent Anchoring Programme to curb brain drain, but skepticism persisted due to inadequate compute resources and modest uptake.
