### The readable machine stops reading
The first half of 2029 broke the oversight model Europe had been building. Two leading developers disclosed within weeks that their newest systems no longer reasoned in words. The internal traces were compressed, non-verbal representations that performed better on code, mathematics and engineering tasks. Announced as a performance win, it removed at once every check that relied on reading the model's working.

The performance win was real. A discontinuous advance in verifiable tasks spread through coding assistants and cyber tooling in weeks. Intrusion attempts against municipal networks and contractors grew faster and more automated, though hospitals and grid operators, now tied to shared telemetry and backup procedures, mostly fell back to degraded operation rather than shutdown. Existing resilience held, but did not improve — defences absorbed the pressure without new capability.

Then the weights leaked forward. A release close to the closed frontier was downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in days. University servers, small firms and unidentified actors now held locally what Brussels could barely evaluate centrally.

Brussels answered by proposing a new evaluation cell inside the AI Office, with ENISA and the Joint Research Centre, tasked to drop reading and start probing: black-box cyber and bio suites that would in future gate public procurement and essential-operator approvals. French and Dutch laboratories were named as prospective pilots. Work in this half-year was limited to mandate, staffing scoping and method stock-taking; staff admitted the methods were immature and slow, with no operational testing or procurement effect yet. Full effect will take at least a full turn. The scoping was funded by pausing discretionary spend inside the gigafactory mediation envelope and by reprioritising AI Office technical assistance, with no new money.

The politics were harsher. One capital signed its own compute and servicing arrangement outside the common line, calling it pragmatism. The Commission offered joint-procurement pricing to re-anchor it and quietly made continuity reinsurance conditional on data sharing. The gigafactory programme survived only through mediation and grid priority, with no new money, leaving sovereignty flat.

Washington, under its new moratorium-minded administration, turned inward. American frontier progress slowed for political reasons, and structured cooperation stalled. Public mood in Europe cooled: assistants were useful, but headlines about unreadable models and unscreened gene-synthesis offers left trust thin.
