### A method that reads without words
Autumn brought an unexpected reprieve for oversight. Researchers at two frontier labs and a Dutch university group converged on a control technique that did not need legible traces: a behavioural certification harness that bounded what an opaque agent could do in code and tool-use, tested on deployed assistants. Labs adopted it fast because it reduced their own incident load. In Brussels, the new black-box cell seized on it, turning stalled method papers into procurement checklists and first live gates for hospital and municipal systems.

That operational win coincided with tangible public results. In several regions, triage assistants and administrative aides cut waiting lists and permit times from months to days. Mayors credited European funding and safety checks rather than the underlying American models, and for the first time in two years evening news carried AI as a working public service.

The politics did not follow. A second capital finalised its own outside compute and servicing deal on cheaper terms, openly undercutting the joint-procurement line. The Commission's offer of re-entry through the trusted public-sector stack was pocketed as an option, not taken. Mediation kept the gigafactory programme formally intact — first concrete pours were celebrated in two sites — but the defection stripped away any sense of a common front.

Washington stayed distant. The moratorium-minded administration kept frontier work throttled, leaving European officials to note that the second-rank labs elsewhere were visibly closing ground.

Pressure from leaked weights did not lift. Municipal networks logged more automated probing, and hospitals stayed on degraded-mode playbooks. Defences held without improving. Gene-synthesis chatter persisted in trade press — single-source claims of unscreened orders built with openly available assistance — but without confirmation inside the Union, it remained rumour rather than response.

Trust inched, but did not turn. Voters saw useful aides and a credible safety case, and simultaneously saw unreadable models and a Union unable to keep its members on one contract.
