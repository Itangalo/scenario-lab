### Frontier Presses Ahead  
AI capability advances to 56.0 as labs refine autonomous research agents capable of sustained hypothesis generation and validation in materials science and genomics. These systems operate under light supervision but increasingly steer their own workflows. No corresponding safety upgrades land on deployed models, and the gap between capability and control widens. Open-weight models benefit from leaked training data and improved open-source frameworks, raising `openweight_capability` to 44.0—still trailing, but now within reach of frontier capabilities in offensive cyber and automated tool use.

### Political Fractures Deepen  
A member state signs a bilateral compute agreement with a U.S. hyperscaler, granting preferential access and excluding EU oversight—undermining the Union’s unified stance. Simultaneously, a national election in a major member state is annulled over evidence of AI-generated disinformation campaigns that skewed voter perception. The ruling sparks protests and deepens public distrust in digital systems. The EU responds by sharpening its strategic focus, declaring sovereign compute the foundation of all other AI policy. The Gigafactories initiative is restructured to bypass national bottlenecks via direct EIB funding and Article 122 TFEU invocation, but legal challenges loom.

### Resilience Efforts Begin, But Too Late for Momentum  
The Resilience Shield Initiative launches, deploying AI-powered detection systems to critical infrastructure operators. However, implementation is slow, and no full-scale exercise occurs this turn. The measure begins to yield partial benefits, but not enough to offset systemic fragility. With no finished resilience measure, and `ai_capability` rising by 2.0, `resilience` gains only +1 from early progress but loses −1 due to unmitigated frontier growth.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Crumbles  
`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 19.0: the `member_state_defection` event triggers a −2 penalty for lost access to coordinated capacity, and `ai_capability`’s rise costs −1. Despite restructuring, no category 4 measure finishes, and partial progress yields no bonus. Political capital plummets: the portfolio charge is −10 (three large measures, one priority), the proposal bonus for Resilience Shield is +3 (answering recent cyber and election incidents), but no sentiment boost applies. Public sentiment dips to 39.0 amid anxiety over democratic erosion and stalled sovereignty.
