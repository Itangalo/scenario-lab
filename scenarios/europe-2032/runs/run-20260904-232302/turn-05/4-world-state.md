### EU Asserts Audit Authority

The European Union launches a binding initiative to enforce sovereign audit rights across critical infrastructure, invoking Article 22 of the AI Act to mandate real-time access to reasoning traces and control logs for high-risk AI systems in energy, health, transport, and finance. This measure, developed in coordination with the newly operational European AI Assurance Directorate, establishes the legal basis for pre-deployment scrutiny and sovereign research access. The Directorate itself is not a new measure but the institutional anchor for this initiative, operationalized this turn after prior development.

Despite strong legal framing, pushback mounts. German and French operators warn of operational risks, while foreign vendors signal potential withdrawal from EU markets. A compromise is offered: audit data will be stored in sovereign enclaves at Gigafactory nodes, with privacy-preserving interpretability tools under development.
