### The Weight of Rising Capability

AI capability advances to 72.0 as formal verification techniques mature and are embedded into agent training pipelines, enabling more autonomous operation in clinical and legal domains. These systems now handle multi-day decision chains with minimal oversight, increasing efficiency but also opacity.

The EU continues to operationalise its Sovereign Evaluation Regime, activating secure enclaves and red-teaming units in Paris, Eindhoven, and Warsaw. However, U.S. developers resist deep scrutiny, withholding training logs and model weights. The fast-track evaluation offer gains little traction, as frontier labs see little incentive to cede control. Poland pushes for runtime observability, but the sunset clause remains the only concession. Hungary’s continued reliance on unevaluated U.S. systems creates a growing blind spot.

### Fractures Multiply

A major scandal erupts when an AI-assisted social insurance system in a large member state is found to have wrongfully denied thousands of claims due to biased risk scoring. A national ombudsman confirms systemic harm, triggering public outrage and reinforcing distrust in automated governance. Labour unions and civil society groups intensify opposition to AI deployment in public services.

Simultaneously, supply-chain pressure escalates: Washington compels the Netherlands to restrict ASML’s servicing of older lithography tools, affecting not only China but also several EU industrial users. The Union’s leverage over its own critical infrastructure weakens. A southern member state, facing economic strain, quietly secures preferential access to a U.S. hyperscaler’s latest model in exchange for data-sharing concessions — a move that undermines the EU’s unified stance.

Energy regulators report recurring AI-driven grid instabilities, forcing manual overrides across multiple countries. While no blackouts occur, confidence in automated critical systems erodes further.

Public sentiment drops to 30.0 as scandal and dependency combine. Resilience declines due to unaddressed systemic risks. The Sovereign Evaluation Regime, still in flight, yields no measurable safety gain. With no incident prevented and multiple setbacks, political capital erodes.
