### The Crisis of Legitimacy

AI capability advances to 94.0, pushing deeper into the terminal zone where systems operate beyond human verification. At this level, even forensic audits struggle to reconstruct decisions, rendering transparency mandates increasingly symbolic. The gap between capability and safety — now 101 points — becomes a chasm.

The European Union responds to a cascade of crises: a major automated decision scandal in a national social insurance system is confirmed by an ombudsman, having denied thousands of claims without explanation. Simultaneously, a wave of administrative slowdowns sweeps across public services, coordinated through encrypted civic networks. In response, the EU invokes emergency powers to halt new AI deployments in justice, policing, and civil service hiring.

A new measure, *Pause Public Sector AI Expansion*, is launched as a small category 3 restriction. It establishes an Emergency Oversight Board and funds citizens’ assemblies to co-design future governance. However, the political cost is severe. With eu_political_capital already at 5.0, the portfolio charge — including the priority — drains it to zero. Member states resist the moratorium, citing sovereignty; two delay implementation, undermining unity.

Public sentiment, already low, drops further as displaced graduates and civil servants see the pause as too little, too late. The measure fails to stabilize trust. Meanwhile, export controls tighten under the standing *US_POSTURE: CONSOLIDATION*, further restricting access to frontier models. No domestic alternative exists, and the Open Science Resilience Initiative remains months from delivery.

Resilience erodes as attention shifts from infrastructure hardening to political containment. Sovereignty declines due to capability growth and the absence of any secured access — dependence remains total, and no new capacity comes online.

### Strategic Containment, Political Collapse

The EU’s attempt to trade momentum for mandate backfires. The pause is seen not as leadership but as surrender. Political capital evaporates. The Union can no longer initiate or enforce measures. The Oversight Board convenes, but without funding or enforcement, its authority is symbolic.

The world watches: capability climbs, safety plummets, and the EU’s capacity to act vanishes just as its need peaks.
