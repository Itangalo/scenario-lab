# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer act on assumptions about safety that the evidence has falsified.
- `two_year_commitment` (position): Secure sovereign technical agency through auditable AI
- `restore_legitimacy_first` (commitment): We will halt the expansion of AI in public services until confidence in fairness, redress and labour impact is restored, and use the pause to rebuild public trust through independent redress mechanisms and co-designed oversight.

- **reclassify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: position
  - Trigger: the **export_control_escalation** and **emergent_administrative_strike_wave**
  - Grounds: Securing sovereign technical agency through auditable AI was the right direction, but the world has moved beyond what that commitment anticipated. Export controls now ration even allied access, meaning our dependence is structural, not temporary. Meanwhile, public backlash has made any expansion of AI deployment — even auditable versions — politically toxic. We still believe in the goal, but continuing to stake ourselves to it as a *commitment* would force actions that fracture cohesion further. We demote it to a position so we can adapt without breaking trust entirely.
  - Evidence quoted: **export_control_escalation:** Chip and model export controls tighten again. Either allied buyers keep access on volume licences while everyone else is cut off, or the controls are drawn so tightly that allies are rationed alongside adversaries – decide which at the time from the standing American posture and from what the Union has built.
  - Reason: Export controls rationing allied access make sovereign technical agency structurally harder, directly affecting the feasibility and cost of the commitment.
- **add** `restore_legitimacy_first` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will halt the expansion of AI in public services until confidence in fairness, redress and labour impact is restored, and use the pause to rebuild public trust through independent redress mechanisms and co-designed oversight.
  - Grounds: With public_sentiment at 27.0 and political capital collapsing, we are out of runway. The automated decision scandal and graduate job market collapse have created a crisis of institutional credibility. Before we build more capacity, we must restore the legitimacy of those who govern it. This is not retreat — it is recalibration. We will use the pause to hardwire accountability into every system we eventually deploy, or we will have no mandate to deploy anything at all.
