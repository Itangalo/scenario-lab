## In practice
This turn, we are invoking Article 16 TFEU and the Digital Services Act’s public service obligations to issue an immediate moratorium on new AI deployments in judicial support, social benefits, policing, and civil service recruitment across all member states. The European Commission, through DG JUST and the Fundamental Rights Agency, is launching a binding review of all active systems in these domains, with mandatory suspension of any found to lack accessible redress or audit trails. The European Social Fund will finance transition grants for workers displaced by automation in legal, accountancy, and administrative sectors, tied to participation in the design process.
