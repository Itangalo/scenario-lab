### The State That Cannot Act

The European Union enters 2032’s second half in freefall. Three shocks strike simultaneously: a major cyberattack cripples hospital scheduling and emergency dispatch systems across four member states; venture capital evaporates from AI infrastructure, collapsing planned GPU deliveries and cloud partnerships; and overnight, access to the world’s most advanced AI models is severed for EU-based users, with no explanation. Ministries, research labs, and public hospitals are locked out.

The Commission attempts course correction. It formally retires the fiction of dual mandates, acknowledging it can no longer credibly pursue both sovereignty and safety. The failed *Emergency Sovereignty Activation* remains unimplemented—member states still refuse compute sharing, energy allocations stall, and the Crisis Cell lies dormant. Instead, the EU pivots to *Sovereign Function Restoration*, a large Category 6 measure aimed at restoring minimum functionality in health, energy, and emergency services using auditable, sub-frontier models.

But the pivot lacks leverage. The Civil Protection Mechanism is invoked, and funds from the Recovery and Resilience Facility are redirected toward cyber defences and low-capability AI enclaves on EuroHPC Tier-2 systems. ENISA launches a 90-day intrusion detection sprint. Yet implementation is fragmented—member states interpret mandates selectively, audit pods with Swiss and Norwegian partners stall over data sovereignty disputes, and no new compute comes online.

Public sentiment remains frozen at 15.0—protests intensify, now targeting data centres and EU offices alike. Resilience does not improve; the cyberattack exposes systemic fragility, and no finished measure offsets the damage. Open-weight capability stagnates at 60.0, with no release, leak, or coordination to lift it. AI safety remains at 0.0—no assurance exists on deployed systems, and the gap with capability is now existential.

With *Emergency Sovereignty Activation* still in flight and *Sovereign Function Restoration* newly added, political capital remains at 0.0—the portfolio charge exceeds any possible gain. Sovereignty erodes further, falling to 15.0, as global capability holds at 96.0 and dependence deepens under US consolidation.

The EU is no longer failing to lead. It is failing to function.
