### The recipe argument
January opened with a preprint no one outside biosecurity wanted to finish reading. A genome model had sketched a viable human-infecting design, and a second paper claimed a non-expert had gotten there with chatbot help. Methodologists cried foul, editors argued over takedown versus rebuttal, and health ministries quietly asked laboratories what they actually screen.

Within weeks a second shock overtook the first. A frontier-class openly downloadable model appeared, mirrored hundreds of thousands of times before regulators had drafted a statement. Whatever it could do — including helping with the biology above — was now on private machines for good.

### Shield without concrete
Brussels did not fight on concrete. The Commission left the council building freezes and court injunctions in place, kept energy and legal staff on slow water-and-power talks, and threw what little authority it had into hospitals, labs and water utilities.

Pooled sequencing screens, referral-lab triage rules and emergency stockpiles were funded through the health emergency authority, while cyber teams from the continuity effort were sent into hospital networks and water-plant controls. Framed as civil protection, the programme passed where factories could not. Showcase regions with working triage helpers lent staff and credibility.

It landed unevenly. Big university hospitals got screening kits and hardening visits by spring; municipal clinics and mid-size water operators got checklists. Staff welcomed the attention and resented the extra swabs and log reviews on top of already slower systems.

The common access pact formally concluded this semester, but changed little on the ground. The capital with its separate foreign supply kept it, Washington offered technical briefings without restoring prior access, and the two frozen factory sites stayed silent behind vigils and tape. Queues held where European helpers worked, lengthened where they did not.

By June, Europe felt marginally better defended against a threat it could now name, and no closer to building its way out of dependence.
