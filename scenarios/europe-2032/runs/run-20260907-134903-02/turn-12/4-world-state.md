### Capability Creeps Forward  
AI capability advances by +0.5, reaching 63.5, as incremental gains in training stability and agent coordination accumulate. The frontier remains within the terminal zone, with no breakthroughs reported. Leaked anomalies in model behaviour—subtle shifts in reasoning paths—continue to circulate, dismissed by developers but intensifying scrutiny from EU and Asian regulators.

### EU Absorbs a Major Investment  
The €40bn *AI Safety Emergency Investment* reaches formal completion this turn, delivering secure inspection enclaves, red-teaming teams, and the core of the European AI Assurance Network (EAAN). However, integration remains partial: staffing gaps persist, and access protocols are still contested by some member states. While the milestone is symbolically significant, full operational capacity is not yet achieved, and no immediate boost to *ai_safety* or *eu_political_capital* is realized. The expected future benefits are acknowledged, but political capital remains constrained by ongoing burdens.

### Border Holds, But at a Cost  
The *EU Border for AI Systems* remains the Union’s priority. Progress on certification nodes in Brussels, Sophia Antipolis, and Munich accelerates via fast-tracked Horizon mobility and delegated acts. However, resistance from US hyperscalers hardens—two delay compliance talks, citing legal uncertainty. In response, the Commission drafts binding telemetry and weight-hashing rules, while offering liability shielding and early compute access as inducements. The dual-track strategy avoids total breakdown but fails to secure firm commitments.

### Sovereignty Erodes Further  
With *ai_capability* rising and no sovereign compute or model access secured, *eu_ai_sovereignty* declines by −1. The earlier withdrawal of a US lab continues to weigh, and no capacity event offsets the trend.

### Political Capital Stagnates

With no offsetting legitimacy lends or proposal bonuses, the net effect is a strain on political capital. The symbolic value of EAAN's completion is insufficient to overcome implementation delays and fragmentation. As a result, *eu_political_capital* remains unchanged at 6.0.
