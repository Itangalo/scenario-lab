## New measure  
**Measure:** Joint Compute Access Pact with Middle Powers  
A binding coordination framework among the EU and like-minded states controlling critical nodes in the semiconductor, lithography, and energy infrastructure supply chains to jointly manage access to advanced AI compute, using export licensing alignment and mutual leverage to deny capacity to actors violating shared safety norms.

Category: 8 (International coordination and leverage)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 4  
Targeted effect: Increase eu_political_capital by 5–8 points through strengthened coalition cohesion and credible leverage; lock in extraterritorial enforcement capacity for EAAD by aligning red-line thresholds across partners  
Applies to: Middle-power coalition members (Netherlands, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Canada), with conditional access for others

## Priority  
**Priority:** InvestAI Gigafactories  
This remains the cornerstone of our two-year commitment: sovereign compute is the only foundation that makes resilience, oversight, and industrial agency possible — and without it, every other measure risks becoming a rule without enforcement.

## In practice  
We are advancing the InvestAI Gigafactories through the European Investment Bank and national co-financing mechanisms, with binding agreements now signed by France, Sweden, and Spain to fast-track permitting under the Net-Zero Industry Act. Grid reservations with ENTSO-E are secured for 12GW across the five designated sites, with an additional 3GW under negotiation. To address local resistance in Brittany and Norrland, we have activated the Just Transition Fund to co-invest in regional grid upgrades and green hydrogen hubs, and negotiated a local hiring floor of 40% for construction and operations roles. The first procurement tenders for modular cooling and power redundancy systems are out, with delivery timelines set for turn 4.

The Tech sovereignty package is progressing through the Digital Markets and Digital Services Council, where we have traded accelerated permitting for enforceable commitments on EU-based operational control and energy efficiency. The Commission’s legal service has completed the Article 114 TFEU directive draft to harmonise permitting timelines, now under review by COREPER. The private capital mobilisation strategy is on track: 42% of the targeted €200bn has been tentatively committed, with ASML, TotalEnergies, and Fortum leading consortiums tied to EUV resilience and offshore wind PPAs. The Blue Card+ initiative has attracted 1,800 frontier AI engineers in the past six months, exceeding projections.

The EAAD is now operational under ENISA’s interim governance, with its first 50 staff hired — including red-team leads from national cyber agencies and interpretability researchers from INRIA and Fraunhofer. We are finalising the regulation to grant access to model weights and training logs, backed by the 0.5% levy on non-EU hyperscalers. Legal services from Germany, the Netherlands, and Ireland have begun consultations on the proportionality argument ahead of anticipated challenges at the CJEU. The first evaluation cycle will target models above 55.0 capability, with a pilot red-team exercise scheduled for Q3 2027 on an autonomous research agent recently deployed in the EU.
