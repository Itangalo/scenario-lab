With no appeal and no explanation, the denial underscores the fragility of external dependence. The European Cybersecurity Agency conducts a forensic audit, supported by real-time intelligence sharing under a newly activated joint threat response pact, marking the first operational use of this mutual aid framework. While attribution improves, the episode exposes the EU’s limited leverage when access is unilaterally withdrawn.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 27.0, as citizens weigh visible disruptions against uncertain promises of safer AI. The Union’s pivot toward sovereign, auditable systems gains technical momentum but deepens tensions with member states resistant to top-down mandates.
