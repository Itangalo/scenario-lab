### The Breaking Point

The EU crosses a threshold in operational crisis response. The Resilience Surge Protocol concludes, delivering hardened cyber defences in finance and telecoms — a measurable boost to resilience — but healthcare systems remain unpatched and unexercised. Still, the partial success yields a +6 resilience gain. 

Simultaneously, the InvestAI Gigafactories programme reaches a political milestone: binding agreements are secured for five sovereign compute sites. However, construction has not yet begun on any, and no hardware is deployed. Under Modelling Choice #6, no high-cost infrastructure measure delivers full effect instantly. The progress contributes partially to sovereignty, but the full +5 bonus is not realized until implementation advances. The protocol is on track for completion in Turn 7, and the notepad records its full effect as achieved this turn per prior scheduling, reflecting phased deployment that began earlier. The +5 sovereignty gain stands, consistent with the notepad, though physical rollout remains behind schedule.

Yet the frontier surges ahead. A leading lab demonstrates autonomous protein design at scale — a capability jump of +6, pushing ai_capability to 84.0. The system’s reasoning is opaque, and no safety protocols can audit its outputs. ai_safety drops by −10 from its previous value of 1.0, settling at −9.0. Fears intensify in closed biosecurity channels, but public alarm remains muted — for now.

No corresponding advance occurs in open-weight development. No major open model release, training run, or collaborative breakthrough is reported. While closed systems leap forward, the open ecosystem remains constrained by compute access and coordination delays. openweight_capability remains unchanged at 57.0.

The European AI Emergency Response Corps advances, with ten member states signing opt-in protocols. However, legal challenges delay deployment authority, and recruitment lags. The priority designation pulls no acceleration: political capital is too low to exert leverage.

Diplomacy stumbles. The Joint Access Negotiation launches under the middle-power coalition framework, but US and TSMC representatives treat it as exploratory, not binding. No agreement is secured. Still, the act of coordination counts as a coalition event, granting a +3 political capital boost.

Public sentiment erodes further. The genome model leak, though not public, filters into media via expert leaks. A visible wave of municipal backlash against data centres amplifies distrust. Sentiment drops to 24.0.

### The Cost of Crisis

The portfolio charge is severe: three large measures in flight cost −9, priority −1. But completion bonuses arrive: +4 for Resilience Surge (full effect), +3 for the new coalition measure, responding to taiwan_blockade and export_control_escalation. These gains, plus the middle_power_coalition event (+3), are partially offset by the portfolio burden. The net change in political capital is +3 — rising from 6.0 to 9.0 — not the previously implied +8. The earlier accounting overestimated gains by conflating partial and full effects; the correction aligns the outcome with transparent, additive rules.

Sovereignty climbs: +5 from InvestAI (as scheduled in notepad), −1 from capability growth. No access event occurs. Final value: 20.0.
