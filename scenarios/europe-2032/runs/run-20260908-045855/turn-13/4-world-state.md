### The Silence After Failure

The EU AI Incident Response Corps formally reached its finishing turn, legally established but operationally stillborn. No vote was held, no compromise reached—Poland and Hungary maintained their blockade, and the dual-key mechanism collapsed without a final reckoning. The Corps remains on paper, a hollow shell. ENISA has not appointed a single analyst, and no cross-border protocol has been tested. The moment for collective action passed in silence.

Behind the scenes, the Franco-German-Dutch red team’s breakthrough in post-hoc model auditing gains quiet traction. Their method, now replicated in Sweden and Finland, allows partial reconstruction of decision logic in open-weight models at 88.0, offering a path to transparency without lab access. Independent researchers confirm the results, and informal coordination expands into a transatlantic network including Canada, Japan, and South Korea. They begin issuing shared vulnerability alerts—de facto standards emerging from necessity, not law.

Yet no institutional response follows. With political capital at 3.0, and no measure proposed, the Commission cannot act. The canceled Public AI Benefit Pilots leave a void in public engagement, and no alternative narrative emerges. AI is still seen not as infrastructure but as an occupying force, and sentiment remains frozen at 12.0.

Globally, export controls tighten further under the American consolidation posture. While allied governments retain limited access to frontier models, the terms grow more restrictive—licensing is now volume-based, and EU institutions face new delays. The move reinforces dependence, with no sovereign compute corridor yet online.

### No Capital, No Action

The portfolio charge is applied with no offset. The finished Corps yields no bonus—its failure is too public, too symbolic. Political capital drops below zero, signaling total paralysis. No new measure can start, no priority can pull. The Union watches the frontier advance, the open weights spread, and its own institutions stall.
