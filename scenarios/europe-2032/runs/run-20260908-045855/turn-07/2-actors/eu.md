Its findings will be declassified in full next turn and fed into ENISA’s updated threat model. This is not control, but it is sight — the first time we have seen into the behaviour space of distributed superintelligence rather than just guessing at its shape.

We hold the line, but do not advance. Our sovereignty remains thin, our capital low, our sentiment sour — but we have kept our feet in two doors: one in the physical infrastructure of compute, one in the cognitive space of open models. That is all we can hold for now.
