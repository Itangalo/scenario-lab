- **add** `last_stand_for_containment` — applied
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We are the last authority still attempting to enforce consequences on AI systems that cause harm, even as their autonomy outpaces law, legitimacy, and comprehension.
  - Grounds: With global capability at 93.0 and opaque reasoning now systemic, no laboratory can explain its models, and no state except the EU still attempts to hold systems accountable after failure. The Incident Response Corps remains legally mandated but operationally stalled; its very attempt — however flawed — distinguishes the Union as the only polity still asserting jurisdiction over autonomous systems. This is no longer about competitiveness or benefit-sharing. It is about whether any human authority can still say *no* after the fact.
