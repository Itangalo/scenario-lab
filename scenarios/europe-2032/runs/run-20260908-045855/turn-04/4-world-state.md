Digital Europe grants fund joint procurement of intrusion systems, and liability rules tighten. Resilience gains +6, reaching 46.0—first real hardening against cascading cyber threats.

Public trust collapses. Adoption sentiment sours, and backlash spreads to AI governance broadly. The scandal, of internal origin, costs the EU heavily.
