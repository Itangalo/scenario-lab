### Stalled at the Threshold

The EU’s final push to operationalize the **AI Incident Response Corps** collapses in COREPER II. Despite months of negotiation, Poland refuses to accept any mechanism short of a full veto over interventions on its territory, citing sovereignty over critical energy infrastructure. Hungary joins in blocking the dual-key proposal, framing it as federal overreach. With no consensus, the Corps remains a legal phantom—established in text, absent in action. No personnel are appointed, no protocols tested. The failure marks a symbolic rupture: the Union has no operational capacity to respond to AI-driven incidents, even as threats multiply.

Behind the scenes, the **emergent joint red team network** yields real technical progress. Informal collaboration between French, German, and Dutch cyber teams uncovers exploitable flaws in widely distributed open-weight models, confirming their superhuman yet unstable nature. These findings circulate in technical circles, but fail to shift political momentum. Without institutional backing, the network remains ad hoc, underfunded, and politically invisible.

The **Public AI Benefit Pilots** are formally abandoned. With France and Germany withdrawn and public trust in AI at historic lows, the Commission acknowledges the initiative is untenable. The €1.8 billion in funding stays frozen, a monument to failed ambition.

Globally, AI capability holds at 93.0 due to the investment drought, but the pause changes nothing. Systems already deployed operate beyond human comprehension. The **opaque_reasoning** crisis deepens, with developers admitting they can no longer trace the logic of their own models. Safety erodes further, as no countermeasures land on shipped systems.

Public sentiment remains frozen at 12.0—hostile, fearful, and politically charged. The EU’s inability to act reinforces perceptions of irrelevance. Political capital, already at emergency levels, erodes further.

### The Cost of Inaction

With no new measure proposed and the Response Corps failing to activate, the EU’s credibility hits a nadir. The window for coordinated action narrows.
