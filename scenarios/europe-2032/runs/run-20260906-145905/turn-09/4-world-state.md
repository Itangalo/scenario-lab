### The Open Frontier Widens – Gradually

An open-weight model release, closely trailing the closed frontier, begins to disseminate across decentralized networks. Its capabilities—approaching persistent autonomy in research and cyber operations—are now technically accessible beyond regulatory reach. However, diffusion remains constrained by infrastructure bottlenecks, verification overheads, and cautious adoption among non-state actors. This limits the immediate impact, allowing `openweight_capability` to rise only modestly to 50.0, reflecting early but incomplete propagation. `ai_capability` advances to 63.0 as frontier labs refine agent coordination and extend task horizons, maintaining its lead.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Safety Dips

With no operational sovereign compute and no binding evaluation authority, the EU’s leverage weakens. The ECJ ruling on Article 114 TFEU remains pending, freezing infrastructure deployment. Despite legislative passage, the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation yields no physical progress, and `eu_ai_sovereignty` declines by 1 due to capability growth outpacing domestic capacity. The European Evaluation Shield’s findings—indicating unmonitored agent memory persistence—remain classified, but their leak to technical circles fuels quiet concern. In response, `ai_safety` dips to 16.0, reflecting growing gaps in oversight, though not yet a collapse.

### Labour Unrest Spreads, Resilience Strains

Cross-border strikes in logistics and public services, coordinated through AI tools, intensify and spread to energy and healthcare sectors. The decentralized, adaptive nature of the organizing overwhelms national response mechanisms. Supply chains buckle, and essential services face rolling disruptions. The crisis is not catastrophic, but its persistence erodes confidence and strains intergovernmental coordination. Resilience weakens slightly to 30.0 as cyber and labour threats mount without countermeasures. The absence of an active category 6 measure compounds the decline.

### Political Stasis, Sentiment Holds

The Commission’s cautious posture yields no new measures and no named priority. While intended to preserve capital, this inaction fails to arrest deteriorating sentiment. Media coverage emphasizes job insecurity and foreign control over critical AI systems, amplifying resentment. However, the erosion of public trust is gradual; `public_sentiment` declines only to 31.0, as the full impact of unrest and safety concerns has yet to crystallize into broad backlash. The narrative of systemic failure is emerging but not yet dominant.
