AI capability crosses the 60.0 threshold as coordinated agent systems achieve autonomous multi-day research and engineering tasks, intensifying scrutiny on safety and development timelines. The closed frontier pulls ahead due to integrated infrastructure and rapid iteration. The EU proposes the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation—a category 4 measure under Article 114 TFEU—to fast-track permitting and grid access for sovereign data centres reserving 40% capacity for public use, with EIB pre-approvals and conditional interest from Germany and Ireland; however, implementation stalls over grid stability concerns and state aid legal risks, halting construction. The European Evaluation Shield suspends all pilot audits amid a formal legal challenge at the ECJ questioning its inspection powers on intellectual property grounds, leaving evaluation activities in limbo despite Commission support. Public sentiment declines further due to the hospital ransomware attack and a high-profile AI-driven election manipulation incident leading to an annulled election, fueling distrust and labour unrest. Political capital erodes under policy strain and legal uncertainty, though fragmentation prevents a steeper decline. Resilience continues to fall as no category 6 measure is implemented and systemic cyber vulnerabilities remain unaddressed.
