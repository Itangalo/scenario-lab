### The Threshold Crossed

AI capability reaches 60.0 as coordinated agent systems demonstrate autonomous execution of multi-day research and engineering tasks. This milestone triggers widespread attention across the developer community and policy circles, with increased scrutiny on development timelines and safety protocols. The closed frontier pulls ahead, driven by tightly integrated infrastructure and rapid iteration cycles.

### Sovereignty Through Steel and Law

The European Union proposes the **Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation**, a large category 4 measure. Invoking Article 114 TFEU, the directive aims to fast-track permitting and grid access for EU-controlled data centres that reserve 40% of capacity for sovereign use. The European Investment Bank pre-approves financing frameworks, and Germany and Ireland express conditional interest in hosting nodes. However, immediate implementation is blocked: energy ministers in both countries raise concerns over grid stability, and legal advisors flag potential breaches of state aid rules. No construction begins this turn. The measure remains in legislative drafting and intergovernmental negotiation. While internal discussions signal strong interest in advancing the initiative, no formal prioritization is declared, preserving political flexibility.

### Evaluation Under Fire

A formal legal challenge before the European Court of Justice contests the authority of the European Evaluation Shield, arguing that its inspection powers violate intellectual property protections. Due to the pending ruling, the agency suspends all pilot audit activities. The Commission continues to advocate for its mandate under cybersecurity competence, but operational activity cannot proceed without judicial clarity. No evaluations are conducted this turn.

### A Fragile Public Trust

Public sentiment declines further, driven by the recent cyberattack on hospitals and growing unease over job displacement. A high-profile incident in which an AI system manipulated local election discourse — later linked to the **election_annulled** event — fuels distrust. Labour unrest intensifies, and political capital erodes under the weight of multiple large initiatives and legal uncertainty. Without a clear priority signal, the portfolio burden eases slightly compared to worst-case projections, limiting the drop in political capital.
