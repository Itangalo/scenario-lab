### The Lights Stay On — For Now

A wave of AI-driven ransomware cripples hospital scheduling in three member states, while coordinated probing of grid control systems forces emergency manual overrides across three major energy networks. The attacks, clearly model-generated in design and execution, expose a dangerous gap: evaluation is not protection. With `ai_safety` at a perilous 4.0 — down 5 points due to unchecked control anomalies and no deployed safeguards — the EU acts.

The European Commission launches the **Critical Infrastructure Hardening Protocol**, a large category 6 measure under Article 114 TFEU, mandating adversarial detection, 90-second human fallback, and cross-border failover for all AI systems above 50.0 capability in energy, transport, health, and emergency services. Turn 16 is the deadline. Germany demands delays, Austria challenges legal overreach, and private operators resist cost burdens. In response, the EU offers fast-track access to sovereign compute and evaluation support as incentives for early compliance.

Despite the urgency, political capital erodes. The portfolio charge is steep: Sovereign Evaluation Front (−3), new large measure (−3), and priority (−1) total −7. A modest +3 proposal bonus acknowledges the measure’s direct response to the cyber_major_incident and ongoing grid instability. Still, capital falls to 24.0. Public sentiment drops to 27.0, as visible job disruptions in public health IT and fears of blackouts dominate headlines.

Resilience holds at 63.0 — the new measure is too early to yield gains, and no prior category 6 measure finishes. `ai_capability` rises by 1.0 to 71.5, driven by closed-loop refinement. `openweight_capability` climbs to 59.0, as fragments of agent training techniques leak further. Sovereignty slips to 20.0: −1 from capability growth and no offsetting gains.

Hungary’s continued absence from core crisis protocols counts as unresolved dependence. No progress on sovereign compute or supply-chain access offsets the drift. The Union’s leverage is stretched, its unity fraying.
