### Fractures Deepen, Statehood on the Line

The European Union enters a phase of institutional triage. With political capital and public sentiment both at 11.0, and member-state cohesion fraying, the Commission invokes emergency powers under Article 42 to launch the Institutional Continuity Initiative (ICI). The goal is narrow: preserve the functioning of core governance structures against cascading AI-driven disruption and internal defiance.

Early technical work on air-gapped legislative backups and emergency quorum protocols begins, but the political gamble fails. Poland and Hungary reject the offer of fast-tracked Cohesion Fund access, dismissing the no-disruption pact as a surrender of sovereignty. Warsaw insists that participation requires full veto power over EU digital standards — a red line Brussels cannot accept. The emergency dialogue collapses, leaving the Union exposed.

SACT, the Sovereign AI Core Taskforce, is formally canceled. The redirection of funds to ICI and the European Social Fund offers marginal fiscal relief, but no material gain. ASML export restrictions, now affecting servicing of mature-node systems, deepen the EU’s compute dependency. No new sovereign capacity is secured.

Meanwhile, global AI capability advances to 71.5, driven by autonomous cyber operations in closed environments. Red teams observe models generating novel exploit logic that evades detection, further eroding confidence in oversight. With no safety measure landing, and interpretability degrading, ai_safety drops sharply. Open-weight models reach 60.0, reflecting wider distribution of slightly older but still potent systems.

Public sentiment remains stagnant. Job losses in law, accountancy, and administration continue, with graduates locked out of entry-level roles. Protests around data centre expansions intensify, and the Institutional Crisis Forum gains ground in national parliaments.

ERAP remains in flight, nearing completion next turn, but delivers no immediate resilience boost. The ICI, now the named priority, consumes additional political capital without offsetting gains. The EU is no longer building sovereignty — it is trying to survive.
