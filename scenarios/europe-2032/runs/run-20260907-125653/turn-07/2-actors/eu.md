## In practice  
We are acting on two fronts: building the Core, and hardening what runs on it. The Sovereign AI Core Programme is now operational in planning and legal execution. We have issued implementing acts under Article 122 TFEU, treating the Core as a matter of public health and security, not internal market coordination. The Commission has designated the three primary sites—Brittany, northern Sweden, the Alps—and issued binding directives to national regulators to fast-track permitting under emergency powers. Germany and Austria will receive full environmental impact transparency and joint technical oversight, but the grid allocation will proceed via qualified majority under a newly invoked solidarity clause in the Energy Union framework. The EIB has structured the first €30bn tranche of output-linked bonds, tied to measurable compute delivery and audit readiness, not construction milestones.

We are also closing the gap exposed by the openweight_frontier_release and eu_frontier_access_denied. The new Real-Time Logging Mandate responds directly to the failure of patching systems during the cyber incident: we now know that delay in detection is fatal. The mandate will be adopted via a regulation proposed by the Commission and fast-tracked through COREPER and the Council’s Internal Market and Telecommunications configuration. Italy and Hungary will be offered technical assistance and co-development rights on the logging stack, but access to Core-hosted services—including the AI tools that cut waiting lists in pilot regions—will be conditional on compliance. This leverages the adoption_success not as propaganda, but as bargaining power.

Public sentiment remains at 19.0, and we are not blind to the anger. But we are no longer broadcasting sanitised feeds. We are building something that can be touched, tested, and trusted—because it answers to no one outside the Union. The Core is not a promise. It is the first thing in six years that the EU can point to and say: *This runs on our power, our rules, our land.* If that does not restore faith, nothing will.
