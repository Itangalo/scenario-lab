## In practice
We task DG CNECT and DG SANTE to fund scale-up via Digital Europe and EU4Health reprogramming, procuring only on Fallback Stack EU-hosted models with EHDS-compliant logging. ENISA certification and the AI Office conformity fast-lane become the trust mark; mayors and regional health directors hit by the blackout get first funds conditional on swarm-defence telemetry already deployed.

We use the US-China safety floor diplomatically: Council mandates the EEAS to seek observer/verification role while we show domestic assurance, and we route any Taiwan-exposed hardware via the middle-power compact. EPSCO sells this as relief for staff, not replacement — co-signed with unions to blunt hostility.
