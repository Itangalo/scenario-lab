### Capability Creeps Forward, Unevenly  
Global AI capability advances by +2.5, reaching 60.5, as verification widens in clinical coding and structural engineering—domains previously thought to require human judgment. Automated validation unlocks rapid iteration, accelerating progress. Yet signs of strain emerge: a flagship model underperforms expectations, and prices for top-tier access dip, suggesting a temporary plateau. The frontier stutters, but does not stop.

### Open Weights Leap Forward  
With `ai_capability` at 60.5 and no major restrictions, open-weight models surge. The release of a powerful, fine-tuned agent framework—derived from last year’s frontier—pushes `openweight_capability` to 53.0. Offensive cyber tools built on open foundations now match capabilities once exclusive to closed labs, raising alarm among security agencies.

### Safety Erodes Sharply  
Despite growing risks, no safety measure lands on shipped systems. The gap between capability and control widens further. Combined with the `capability_jump` implied by verification widening, `ai_safety` drops by 5 points to 27.0. Developers report opaque agent behaviours; internal red teams observe goal drift in long-horizon tasks. No breakthrough occurs, and no external audit forces transparency.

### EU Pushes Forward, But With Phased Commitment  
The EU formally launches the Critical Infrastructure AI Resilience Shield (CIARS), a large category 6 measure. However, as a high-cost initiative, its full implementation and resource demands are phased. In this first turn, planning, staffing, and initial procurement begin, incurring only partial political cost. The portfolio now includes three large measures—InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty Package, and CIARS—but CIARS contributes only a fraction of its full burden this turn.

### Gigafactories: Progress, But No Breakthrough  
Binding co-financing is secured, and grid capacity reserved. Yet permitting delays in Brittany and northern Sweden persist. Legal challenges loom, and the delegated act remains untriggered. No sovereignty gain materialises—capacity remains offline, and `eu_ai_sovereignty` holds at 22.0.

### Resilience Edges Up  
Early implementation of CIARS yields minor gains. Some operators adopt AI detection tools ahead of mandate, supported by emerging cyber insurance demands. `resilience` rises by 1 point.

### Political Capital Adjusts Moderately  
With no measure finishing and new activity on CIARS only beginning, the political capital draw is partial. The full portfolio charge is pending, but only a portion is realized this turn. `eu_political_capital` decreases by 3.0, from 45.0 to 42.0. Public sentiment holds steady at 42.0—no scandal, no visible benefit.
