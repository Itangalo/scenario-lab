## Priority
EU Essential Services Continuity and Defence Deployment – because with no measures in flight and insurers withdrawing from municipalities and hospitals, keeping essential services insurable and running outranks any new sovereignty build this turn.

## In practice
We act through ENISA and DG CNECT under the Critical Services Shield certificates already on the books: ENISA teams push the proven defensive patching/detection stack and the new interpretability check to Shield-certified operators first, with Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority (HERA) and the Civil Protection Mechanism covering municipalities and mid-size utilities that lost cover.

On money, ECOFIN authorises a time-limited state backstop guarantee for the withdrawn lines, conditional on operators adopting the deployed controls and incident reporting, to pull insurers back without opening a new fund. The Autonomy Pact Network talks continue as diplomatic cover for interim EU-jurisdiction hosting, but no new disbursement is made while capital is at survival level.
