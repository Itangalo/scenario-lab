## Priority
EU Middle-Power Accession and Incident Solidarity Pact – because with no measures in flight and insurers still absent, converting the incident response into coalition membership is the only way to rebuild capital and secure access while keeping services running.

## In practice
We act through the Foreign Affairs Council / Trade formation and DG TRADE with DG CNECT and ENISA in support: mandate to align export-licence criteria, pool incident data from the guarantee scheme, and offer the pact-network hosting and gigafactory offtake as the EU contribution to joint compute bargaining and shared evaluation. We ask for observer/participant status in the US-China verification arrangements on weights security and autonomous escalation, using the deployed control certification as our ticket.

On the incident, we do not open a new fund. ECOFIN extends the time-limited guarantee from last turn, ENISA and the Civil Protection Mechanism surge the proven patching/swarm-detection stack to hit municipalities and hospitals named in the ransomware/dependency blast, conditionality unchanged: adopt controls and share telemetry to keep cover. Member states resisting licence alignment are offered first call on pooled access and mutual-aid teams, to hold cohesion while capital is thin.
