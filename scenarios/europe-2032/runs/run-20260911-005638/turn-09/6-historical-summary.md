By early 2029 oversight went blind: closed systems leapt in code/math/intrusion tooling; frontier models reason in unreadable vectors, shelving CoT audits; open weights leaked to unscreened DNA broker.

Autumn-winter twin shocks persisted: ransomware via compromised management component spread to dozens of municipalities, locking registries/payments for weeks; clean backups and EU-funded joint restoration teams prevented cascade to hospitals/grids but defenders weeks behind tooling. Ombudsman/court found AI benefits/policing system systematically wronged claimants via rubber-stamps; Brussels ordered re-audits, ban on queue approvals, open logs, case-by-case review, continuity payouts and human-review redress — scandal stayed on front pages for months. New interpretability technique outpaced by unreleased-system advance.

Spring labour shock: US-software/Chinese-hardware robots scaled in warehouses/parcel/auto suppliers, collapsing picking/sorting jobs while repair/care/construction held. Commission passed wage-insurance/retraining shield funded by social funds + automation levy, no new compute vote. Washington export rationing continued; Brussels defended first energised gigafactory, two further grid links blocked amid protests and sabotage. AI Office evaluation cell still distant; sovereignty zones, joint-buying, allied cyber-bio integration only degraded intrusions. Public mood fell further.

Through autumn municipal grind continued: component patched but reinfection via cloned images and slow restores kept counters closed in rotation; joint health-emergency/cyber teams cleared town by town, hospitals/grids ring-fenced. Bio-recovery surge voted semester priority: FR/NL sequencing/wastewater pilots extended, DNA-provider screening tightened, restoration teams permanently funded, but procurement/hiring stretched into winter. October contested pre-print claimed genome model helped viable human-infecting design — method disputed but seen as biosecurity step-change; Commission cited it to justify surge, opponents called it alarmist. Shield paid out to warehouse/parcel workers, slowing anger without reversing divide. Gigafactory expansion stalled: coordinated protests and night sabotage on grid connections, police guards and court battles, two links blocked, first site held but no new concrete poured — Union could restore and pay, not build.
