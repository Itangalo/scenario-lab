Ombudsman/court found AI benefits/policing system systematically wronged claimants via rubber-stamps, unread logs despite clean papers. Brussels ordered re-audits, ban on queue approvals, open logs, case-by-case review, continuity payouts, mandatory reporting and human-review redress — scandal stayed on front pages for months. New interpretability technique outpaced by unreleased-system advance — attackers weeks, defenders months.

Commission passed wage-insurance/retraining shield funded by social funds + automation levy, no new compute vote. Capacity hardened: Washington export rationing continued; Brussels used it to defend first energised gigafactory, two further grid links blocked amid protests, blockades and second sabotage attempt.
