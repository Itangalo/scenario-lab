## New measure
**EU Industrial Automation Transition Shield**
Creates EU-funded wage insurance, rapid retraining and transfer pools for warehouse, logistics and supplier workers displaced by commercial robots, tied to automating employers and paired with procurement preference for EU-anchored robotics stacks. This is Labour and social protection, category 7, and why now is that embodied AI is landing from US models and Chinese hardware on the Union's own industrial base while sentiment is at 26 and municipal services are still dark — without a visible adjustment deal, displacement becomes hostility and blocks both resilience and sovereignty builds.

## Priority
EU Industrial Automation Transition Shield — because with open weights at 60.2, safety at 23 and capital at 23, holding public tolerance through the robotics wave outranks starting another slow capacity build this turn.

## In practice
We run this through EMPL Council and the European Social Fund Plus with top-up from the Globalisation Adjustment Fund: SURE-style wage insurance for displaced picking/sorting/palletising workers, six-month retraining vouchers for repair, maintenance and robot-supervision roles where judgement still matters, funded by a transition levy on large automation deployments. IG Metall, CFDT and logistics employers are brought into sectoral transfer pools to move workers rather than dismiss them.

We hold the finished lines steady without new instruments: ENISA and the M6 telemetry network continue restoration bulletins for the ransomware sweep, ombudsmen keep log access and redress under the M9 pact, and DG CNECT uses the US rationing decision to defend the energised gigafactory and mediate the two blocked grid connections. No new compute vote this turn; the Shield is the cash vote, to stop the labour shock cascading into sabotage and siting vetoes.
