## New measure
**EU Continuity Switch to European-Run Models**
Moves hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the leading foreign model onto European-hosted and open-weight systems with emergency support, procurement preference and migration funding.
Category 9 diffusion, adoption and public trust is why now: the cutoff has left essential services stranded this turn, and only a visible EU-run fallback restores function while proving the independent-stack commitment.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because the access denial makes conditional foreign compute an immediate outage rather than a future risk and only landing EU-anchored capacity restores leverage.

## In practice
We run the cutoff as the crisis it is: DG CNECT and DIGIT with ENISA stand up a continuity cell that inventories affected hospitals, ministries and contractors, funds re-platforming to EU-hosted Mistral/Aleph Alpha and hardened open-weight deployments via DIGITAL and EU4Health reprogramming, and writes EU-hosting requirements into new health and public-admin procurement. DG TRADE lodges the denial as coercion evidence while demanding durable, non-discriminatory access terms in parallel.

We do not open a second compute line: DG ENER and DG REGIO concentrate on unfreezing M1/M2 sites hit by referendums and injunctions, pairing the M7 wage-insurance pilots with water-efficiency and grid-upgrade co-funding to lift councils, while HERA and ECDC push the M6 bio-detection kits into Shield watch centres. With capital at 14 we sequence, not multiply: gigafactories first, continuity switch second, everything else maintenance.
