This is not optional — it is the minimum required to prevent cascading failures in public finance.

Meanwhile, the SACP nears its finish line, but without the InvestAI Gigafactories, its compute foundation is hollow. Still, we are weeks behind, and the risk of a capability gap — where the world moves to 65.0 while we cannot even deploy 60.0 securely — grows. The Containment Protocol is now law, but enforcement is fragile: two member states have invoked Article 17 of the Treaty on European Union to reserve rights on research access, and we are negotiating carve-outs that do not gut the protocol’s core. But none of this lands if the public sees only disruption and no benefit. This is not a side project — it is the political foundation of everything else. Without it, public_sentiment will keep falling, and with it, our ability to act at all.
