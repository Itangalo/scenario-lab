### The Weight of Unfinished Business

The global AI frontier advances to 65.0, driven by iterative gains in reasoning depth and system coordination. A leading lab reports autonomous execution of multi-day research tasks in controlled digital environments, though physical-world autonomy remains speculative. Openweight capability rises to 50.0, reflecting wider diffusion of older high-capability models.

In Europe, the InvestAI Gigafactories fail to close on schedule. Despite emergency permitting and RePowerEU funds, grid delays in northern Sweden stall Kiruna’s site, and private co-investment remains frozen amid tightening capital markets. The failure to deliver by year-end undermines confidence in the Tech Sovereignty Package. The Sovereign AI Core Programme (SACP) continues, but without the compute foundation promised, its timeline slips.

The AI Containment Protocol clears legal review but faces political resistance. Two member states threaten non-compliance unless research exemptions are granted, weakening its enforcement posture. Meanwhile, the Resilience Reinforcement Initiative rolls out AI-assisted patching across critical infrastructure, but coordination gaps between national CSIRTs persist, limiting systemic gains.

A new measure, the Sovereign Talent Corridor, is launched to fast-track recruitment of 500 frontier researchers into EU programmes, offering legal protections and streamlined export controls. However, public sentiment drops to 20.0 amid visible job losses in law, accountancy, and software — Eurostat confirms a 17% decline in entry-level hiring. Reskilling programmes lag, and AI governance is increasingly seen as failing ordinary citizens.

Fiscal strain deepens. With five large measures still in flight, political capital erodes further. The failure to complete the Gigafactories — a named priority — compounds the cost. Labour displacement and the perception of elite-driven AI agendas without broad benefit deepen public scepticism.

### A Sovereignty in Retreat

EU AI sovereignty falls to 10.0, as capability advances outpace domestic build-out and a key member state bypasses containment norms. Resilience declines slightly, as rising AI capability outpaces defensive gains. AI safety drops sharply, as no new safeguards land on deployed systems and the gap between capability and control widens.
