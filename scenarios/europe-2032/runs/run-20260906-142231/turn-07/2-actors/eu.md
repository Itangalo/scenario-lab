## In practice
We are treating this turn as the hinge between dependence and autonomy. The **Sovereign AI Core Programme** is now operational in intent and legal basis: invoked under Article 122 TFEU for emergency infrastructure, with co-governance by a newly convened Joint Operational Board including France, Spain, Finland, and the Netherlands—the only members so far willing to cede partial sovereignty over energy and security domains. Physical work begins in Brittany and northern Sweden, where geothermal and nuclear microgrids will anchor two of three core nodes. These sites will host not just compute but the EAAD’s red-team cluster and ENISA’s autonomous patching swarm, all running on air-gapped hardware with fuel reserves for six months of isolated operation. Germany and Austria remain resistant, so we offer them full observational rights and joint environmental monitoring—but no veto. Italy and Hungary continue to block audit compliance; we respond by formalising “no audit, no access” to EU-wide AI services, effective immediately for all systems above 55.0 capability operating in health, energy, or transport.

Simultaneously, we act on the new reality that no single middle power can stand alone. The **Middle-Power AI Coalition** is launched through the EEAS and bilateral channels, starting with Tokyo, Ottawa, and Oslo. We bring to the table our regulatory weight, semiconductor tooling IP, and rare earth processing capacity; they bring advanced packaging, quantum-ready compute, and geographic redundancy. Together, we hold enough of the supply chain that excluding the group becomes strategically costly. Our opening demand: reciprocal access to post-60.0 models, conditioned on shared runtime transparency and joint evaluation. The US anti-AI administration has yet to take office, but we prepare—not to oppose, but to ensure that when they do, Europe speaks from a position of interdependence, not supplication. This coalition does not replace the Core; it multiplies its reach. Without the Core, we have nothing to protect. Without the coalition, we have no shield. We build both, knowing the world gives us no time to do otherwise.
