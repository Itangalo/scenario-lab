### In practice

We are acting on two truths this turn: first, that the Core is not yet sovereign ground, and second, that harm is already landing. The cyber_major_incident — a model-generated compromise of a critical software dependency — has disrupted hospital scheduling and regional power grids. Attribution remains unclear, but the tooling bears the signature of a post-60.0 system. Germany blames the fragmentation from our audit policy; Austria cites constitutional limits; Hungary mocks our dependence. But the Resilience Mesh — a grassroots network of open-source developers — patches faster than any central body. That is not a victory. It is a warning: legitimacy is shifting to those who deliver function, not those who claim authority.

So we act. The Sovereign AI Core Programme advances in Brittany and northern Sweden, but slowly — grid integration delays persist, and sabotage of a substation in Brittany, claimed by an anti-AI collective, sets us back weeks. We respond by fast-tracking military engineering units under Article 42(7) to secure and harden the sites, and we offer Germany and Austria full runtime transparency in exchange for access to their high-voltage corridors — not as a request, but as a condition of future connectivity. The offer includes joint runtime monitoring and fuel-reserve pooling, but no veto on operations.

Simultaneously, we operationalise the EAAD’s red-team mandate by launching the Hardened Patching initiative: a fleet of constrained AI agents, trained on open weights at 50.0 capability, will be deployed under EU command to monitor and patch critical systems. These agents run on air-gapped clusters in the Core nodes and are updated only after EAAD validation. We integrate the Resilience Mesh’s most trusted teams into the programme, offering them EU status and liability protection — not to absorb them, but to bind their capability to our accountability. This is not a workaround. It is the first real test of whether the Union can act at the speed of AI — and whether it still has the right to do so.
