### Paper holds, pay slips
Autumn 2031 belonged to the cordons. Funded isolation beds, HERA releases and paper tracing teams kept the winter agent from re-seeding, and cross-border medical surge finally paid out: backed-up wards in the two hit states could move patients again, backups stayed segmented, grids stayed up. Brussels claimed restoration; on the wards it felt like slower, manual care that did not collapse.

The new income bridge started registering health-adjacent and municipal staff pushed out by paper wards and the withdrawal of AI triage. First wage-insurance payments landed in late autumn, and short retraining into manual-care and maintenance roles filled quickly where job centres were staffed. Ministers touring cordons with job-centre visits helped tie endurance to compensation.

Friction ran underneath. Military-logistics support thinned as deployments rotated, some regions still ran short of trained triage staff amid continued walkouts, and reimbursement conditional on EU-logged routing left the solo hyperscaler deal untouched — capacity outside common logging. ENISA reserves held hospital IT on segmented life-support but could not re-trust it, and with readable reasoning gone, audit pilots stayed shelved.

Oversight loss deepened quietly: black-box checks caught little, activation inspection was too slow for clinical use. A faint Geneva murmur about binding screening for synthesis providers circulated in press corners, unconfirmed and easily missed beside backlogs.

Care did not cascade. Trust stopped falling, but did not return.
