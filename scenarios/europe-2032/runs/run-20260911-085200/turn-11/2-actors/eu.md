## In practice
We run M11 through HERA/ECDC and the Health Security Committee on Articles 168 and 222: funded isolation beds, stockpile releases, paper tracing teams, and military-logistics support to triage lines, with wards kept off illegible AI triage. ENISA reserves keep hospital backups segmented while reimbursement for the solo hyperscaler deal stays conditional on EU-logged routing.

We stand up the Bridge via EPSCO and ESF+ top-ups: member states register eligible displaced care, admin and municipal staff, Brussels co-funds wage insurance and short retraining into manual-care, maintenance and response roles. Ministers front cordons and job centres together, to tie endurance to compensation and stop capital falling to paralysis.
