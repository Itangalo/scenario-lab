### Holding on paper
Through autumn 2031 the Union tried to make staying power a strategy. The same repair teams that had lived in hospital basements and port offices through the spring containment were kept on stay-until-holding orders, paid out of reprogrammed emergency health and civil protection lines. Their work was unglamorous: offline paper-to-digital kits, locally hosted open models for triage, prescribing and customs clearance, and round-the-clock sequencing and wastewater watches run with disease-control and cybersecurity staff.

Where the kits were already installed, it half-held. Clinics ran slow outdoor triage but did not close. Ports cleared freight on manual overrides. Mayors in the hardest-hit towns praised crews by name.

Elsewhere the ground shifted. Intrusions written with the newest coding assistants adapted within days to each patch, jamming appointment systems that had just been rebuilt. Containment of the modified pathogen dragged on, with new clusters flagged by sentinels while wards reverted to paper.

In Brussels, no new instrument was asked for. Ministers endorsed continuity in health and home-affairs formats without a fresh vote, unwilling to test a budget and a public mood that would not carry a fight.

That caution extended to a disturbing new laboratory claim. A genome modelling paper described a viable design for a human-infecting organism, or showed a non-expert reaching that point with model help. Methodologists attacked the work as alarmist and as reckless at once, and the argument stayed inside biosecurity journals and closed briefings. The Commission ordered its evaluation institute and disease-control specialists to reproduce the assessment quietly, tighten screening guidance to labs and synthesis firms, and wait for containment lessons before any threshold decision.

The public saw little of that nuance. Funerals, queues and darkened triage screens dominated coverage, and anger at new data centres and automated systems hardened. By December, the Union was still standing in the clinics it had refused to abandon — poorer, thinner, and waiting for tolerance to recover.
