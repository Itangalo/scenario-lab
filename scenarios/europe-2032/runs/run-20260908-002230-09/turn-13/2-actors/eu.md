## In practice  
We are launching a six-turn emergency programme to establish full-stack sovereign AI runtime control — hardware, software, telemetry, and override — on infrastructure under EU jurisdiction. This is not about building a European GPT. It is about ensuring that when a system of frontier capability operates on our soil or for our institutions, we have cryptographic keys, real-time agent-level visibility, and the ability to cut it off. The legal basis will be Article 114 TFEU for internal market harmonisation, combined with enhanced security clauses under the Schengen Protocol, justified by the confirmed strategic autonomy of agentic systems. The European High Performance Computing Joint Undertaking will be repurposed as the delivery agency, with a new mandate to integrate secure enclaves, sovereign hypervisors, and runtime monitoring into EuroHPC infrastructure by turn 18.

We are calling on member states to designate national sovereign AI runtime zones — initially within defence, energy, and critical research — where only systems with verified telemetry and EU-held break-glass controls may operate above capability index 60.0. To secure buy-in, we are offering 70% co-financing from the Innovation Fund and fast-tracking grid access for data centres that commit to sovereign stack integration. Resistance will come from both sides: from northern states wary of cost, and from southern states who see this as another northern-led tech grab. We counter with equity: compute access tied to compliance, and a binding commitment that 40% of engineering roles go to researchers from southern and eastern member states. The emergent AI refugee wave gives us a narrow window — we are fast-tracking residence and security clearance for 500 top-tier engineers from non-aligned states, assigning them directly to runtime security teams at Fraunhofer, INRIA, and CNR. Without them, we cannot staff this at speed. This is not sovereignty as aspiration. It is sovereignty as survival.
