**Launch a sovereign AI evaluation and monitoring agency**  
Establish a permanent EU body with legal authority to conduct pre-release evaluations, runtime monitoring, and post-incident audits of high-capability AI systems, with access to weights and training data under strict safeguards.

## In practice  
This turn, we are advancing the Emergency Resilience Hardening measure through the final ECOFIN negotiations on burden-sharing, where we are offering a revised cost-sharing formula: 60% EU co-financing for member states that adopt the full red-teaming and air-gapping standards by Q3, with an additional 10% performance bonus for early compliance. We are fast-tracking liability shielding through a delegated act under NIS2, to be adopted by the Commission in February, and ENISA is now procuring intrusion detection stacks at bloc scale, with delivery scheduled for May. The mutualised risk pool, long blocked by smaller states, is being reframed as a solidarity mechanism under Article 122, with German and French energy majors agreeing to underwrite initial capital — a political signal that shifts the dynamic.

Meanwhile, the InvestAI Gigafactories project enters site finalisation, but the ASML bottleneck forces contingency planning: we are directing IPCEI funds to support dual-track development — EUV-dependent builds in Hamburg and Lyon, and DUV-based pilot lines in Wrocław and Valencia, accepting lower yield for faster sovereignty. The Tech Sovereignty Package faces EFTA Court scrutiny, so we are preparing a revised annex that ties accelerated permitting to binding commitments on data localisation and energy sourcing, turning economic incentive into strategic leverage. On the new front, the sovereign evaluation agency is being scoped by the Commission’s AI Office, with a proposal to be drafted by COREPER in April, drawing on CERN-like governance: member state contributions, independent scientists, and enforcement teeth via the Digital Markets Act framework. This is not duplication — it is the missing sovereign eye over the frontier.
