## In practice  
This turn, we activate the Emergency Resilience Hardening measure under Article 122 TFEU and the NIS2 directive, treating the cyber_test_shot not as an outlier but as proof of systemic fragility. We are mobilising the European Cyber Resilience Board, with mandate from COREPER and funding drawn from the Recovery and Resilience Facility’s unused margins, to issue binding technical standards within six weeks for intrusion detection, air-gapped monitoring, and mandatory red-teaming of all Tier 1 operators. The Commission will propose a derogation from state aid rules to fast-track €30bn in co-financing, with 70% grants for member states that meet the baseline by Q2 2027.

Resistance is expected from smaller member states citing cost and sovereignty, and from private operators arguing that compliance timelines are unrealistic. We are offering a tripartite deal: accelerated grid access for compliant operators, liability shielding for good-faith implementation, and joint procurement of detection tooling through ENISA to reduce unit costs. The German and French energy ministries have already signalled conditional support, contingent on a mutualised risk pool — a proposal we are accepting in principle, to be finalised under ECOFIN in January.

Meanwhile, InvestAI Gigafactories moves from concept to site selection, with the Commission issuing a call for proposals under the Important Project of Common European Interest (IPCEI) framework. Early contenders include Hamburg, Lyon, and Wrocław, each offering brownfield industrial zones with existing grid headroom. The challenge is not enthusiasm but cohesion: ASML’s export constraints mean we cannot assume EUV tooling will be available on schedule, so we are embedding contingency planning into the first design phase, with alternative lithography pathways evaluated alongside primary builds. The Tech Sovereignty Package is already facing legal scrutiny from the EFTA Court over its permitting accelerators — we will defend it as a proportionate response to existential risk, not mere economic competition.
