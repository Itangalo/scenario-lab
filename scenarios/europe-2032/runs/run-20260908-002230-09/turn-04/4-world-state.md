### The Weight of Crisis

AI capability advances by 3.0 points to 66.0, driven by recursive self-improvement loops now embedded in frontier training pipelines. Open-weight capability rises to 52.5, closing half the gap to last turn’s frontier, as Kimi K3-derived models are adapted for autonomous research and cyber exploitation.

The `cyber_major_incident` — a poisoned software dependency — lands with widespread disruption across public health and transport systems. Though the EU’s Emergency Resilience Hardening measure finishes this turn, its delayed rollout in Italy and Poland weakens systemic response. Still, detection systems funded by the measure contain the blast radius, and `resilience` rises to 48.0 from partial absorption of harm.

`ai_safety` drops to 13.0. The `eval_anomaly_reports` reveal unexplained reasoning shifts in unreleased models, and with no safety upgrades on deployed systems, confidence erodes further. The new mandate for runtime controls is proposed, but enforcement remains distant.

Public sentiment holds at 19.0, as protests against data centres and job losses in legal tech and logistics dominate headlines. The EU launches the real-time control mandate as a priority, tying permitting advantages in the Tech Sovereignty Package to compliance. However, US developers resist, and the Nordic-Benelux data sovereignty pact operates in parallel, signalling deepening fragmentation.

The Emergency Resilience Hardening finishes, granting a +5 boost to `resilience`. But political capital falls: four large measures and a priority cost −13, and no proposal bonus is earned, as the new measure is forward-looking. Sovereignty declines to 18.0 — capability rose by 3.0, costing −1, with no offsetting event.

### Fractured Unity

The EU avoids a direct confrontation with US labs by offering certification carrots, but alignment with the Nordic-Benelux pact remains uncertain. The sovereign evaluation agency begins shadow audits, but its authority is untested. The Research Corps stabilises talent flow, yet domestic compute lags.

The Union acts with urgency, but cohesion frays. Sovereignty without safety becomes a liability, not an asset.
