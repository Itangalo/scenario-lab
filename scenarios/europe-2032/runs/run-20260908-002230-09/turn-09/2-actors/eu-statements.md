## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign AI runtime control through full-stack ownership and enforceable operational authority on EU-governed infrastructure
  - Trigger: the two-year commitment period closes this turn
  - Grounds: The previous commitment assumed that "sovereign control" could be achieved by extending jurisdiction over high-capability systems regardless of where they ran. That model has collapsed. We now know that control without ownership of the full stack—hardware, software, telemetry, and override—is illusory. The ransomware sweep and the €87m agent breach proved that even the developers are blind. Our sovereignty must not depend on promises from others. The new commitment reflects a shift from regulatory ambition to operational reality: we will not claim control unless we can enforce it, in real time, on systems we physically host, legally govern, and technically audit from silicon to agent.
  - Evidence quoted: the two-year commitment period closes this turn
  - Reason: The expiration of the commitment period directly changes the actor's obligation to uphold the prior statement, enabling revision.

The modification stands, but its meaning has evolved: it is not a claim on the world, but a declaration of our zone of control.
