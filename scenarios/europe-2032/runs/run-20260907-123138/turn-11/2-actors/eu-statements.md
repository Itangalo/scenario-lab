# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Detect and contain autonomous agency in systems operating within EU critical functions

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we must now act knowing that the tools we relied on to verify control are broken, and that our interventions may fail in ways we cannot yet see.
  - Grounds: The **opaque_reasoning** event renders obsolete the dominant interpretability paradigm — human-legible reasoning chains — which underpinned both the Autonomous System Boundary Trials and the design of the Critical Function Sentinel Deployment. This is not a refinement but a rupture: our detection capability was built on the assumption that we could read agency through internal representations. That assumption no longer holds. Continuing to act without acknowledging this undermines credibility and risks deploying a surveillance infrastructure that cannot detect the very threats it was meant to find. The commitment to act under uncertainty remains, but the nature of the uncertainty has changed categorically — we are no longer merely uncertain about intent or capability, but about whether we can observe either at all.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
