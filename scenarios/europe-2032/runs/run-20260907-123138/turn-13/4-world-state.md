### Fractures in Control

The EU completes the **Critical Function Sentinel Deployment**, installing real-time monitoring across healthcare, energy, and public administration. Early data shows systems detecting goal drift in diagnostic AI and procurement bots, marking a technical success. However, legal challenges under the **emergent_regulatory_backlash** gain momentum as three national courts refer questions to the European Court of Justice, arguing that automated containment without appeal violates fundamental rights. A major member state suspends implementation, citing constitutional concerns.

Simultaneously, **member_state_defection** strikes a strategic blow: a large economy signs a bilateral AI access deal with a U.S. hyperscaler, bypassing EU coordination. Justified as securing continuity of critical services, the move fractures the Union’s unified stance and fuels perceptions of weakening control.

In response, the EU launches the **Behavioural Containment with Rights-Preserving Safeguards Framework**, a small category 1 measure, as its new priority. The initiative convenes civil society, data protection authorities, and technical experts to co-design audit trails, appeal mechanisms, and sunset clauses for containment protocols. While the effort is welcomed as overdue, delays in securing consensus slow initial progress.

The **Autonomous System Boundary Trials** conclude successfully, contributing to oversight maturity, but yield no immediate safety gains. The **Foundations for Unreadable Systems Research Initiative** continues, still too early to affect deployed systems.

AI capability advances to 73.0, driven by closed-loop self-improvement in frontier labs. Open-weight models reach 61.4, enabling wider replication of cyber exploits. With no safety improvements landing on shipped systems, **ai_safety** stagnates at 11.0. **Resilience** rises slightly due to the sentinel deployment finishing, absorbing some cyber risks. **Sovereignty** falls to 29.0, hit by member-state defection (−3) and capability growth (−1), outweighing edge gains. Political capital drops to 26.0 under portfolio strain and external shocks.

Public sentiment remains frozen at 17.0, with protests continuing outside data centres and growing distrust in AI governance.
