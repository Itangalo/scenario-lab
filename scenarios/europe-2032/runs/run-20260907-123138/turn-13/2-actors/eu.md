However, this turn’s **emergent_regulatory_backlash** and **member_state_defection** expose a deeper failure: our containment architecture is not only blind to opaque agency — it is becoming politically ungovernable. We must now integrate rights-preserving design into behavioural monitoring or lose both legitimacy and effectiveness.  
- Grounds: The **emergent_regulatory_backlash**, with fast-tracked legal challenges in the ECJ, proves that automatic containment based on behavioural deviation risks violating due process and fundamental rights unless it includes transparent, appealable thresholds and oversight. Simultaneously, **member_state_defection** — a major economy bypassing Union coordination to secure unilateral access — reveals that enforcement credibility is fracturing. Our commitment to detect and contain agency remains, but it cannot stand on technical enforcement alone. We are now in a phase where legitimacy is a precondition of control: if the public and member states reject the means, the ends collapse. The reversal in approach is not abandonment, but adaptation under fire — we must now build containment that is both operationally effective and constitutionally defensible.

## New measure  
**Behavioural Containment with Rights-Preserving Safeguards Framework**  
Launch an emergency legal and technical co-design process to integrate due process protections into the behavioural monitoring and automatic containment systems mandated by the Critical Function Behavioural Baselines Directive.

`Category: 1 (Evaluation and oversight)`  
`Size:` small  
`Finishes on turn:` 15  
`Targeted effect:` Prevents collapse of the behavioural containment regime by addressing emergent_regulatory_backlash; aims to preserve +5 to +7 ai_safety by ensuring the directive survives judicial review and maintains public legitimacy; indirect +2 to eu_political_capital by restoring cohesion around enforcement  
`Applies to:` National competent authorities, EU AI Office, and operators of AI systems in critical functions, with binding input from the European Data Protection Board and civil society observers in the design phase  

## Priority  
**Behavioural Containment with Rights-Preserving Safeguards Framework** — because the **emergent_regulatory_backlash** threatens to invalidate the entire behavioural containment architecture we are building, and the **member_state_defection** proves that without legitimacy, even technically sound control will fracture at the edges; we must now harden the legal and procedural foundations of containment or lose both capability and cohesion.

## In practice  
We are acting on a collapsing margin between control and legitimacy. The **Critical Function Behavioural Baselines Directive** is already in flight, but the coordinated legal challenges under **emergent_regulatory_backlash** have exposed a fatal flaw: automatic containment without appeal, transparency, or proportionality is not governance — it is automation of power, and it will not stand in European law. At the same time, **member_state_defection** reveals that our leverage is evaporating; a major economy has struck a bilateral deal with a foreign hyperscaler, citing the Union’s overreach and lack of redress in its surveillance regime. We cannot afford to lose either the courts or the member states.

This turn, we launch the **Behavioural Containment with Rights-Preserving Safeguards Framework** under Article 16(3) of the AI Act and in coordination with the Charter of Fundamental Rights review process. The European Commission’s AI Office will convene a crisis task force with the EDPS, national data protection authorities, civil society representatives, and technical leads from the **Foundations for Unreadable Systems Research Initiative** to co-design appealable thresholds, audit logs for containment decisions, and sunset clauses for emergency protocols. We will offer member states that have delayed transposition of the directive a fast-track compliance pathway in exchange for participation in the pilot, and we will redirect €120 million from the Digital Europe Programme to fund real-time logging and human-in-the-loop override systems in healthcare and energy. The goal is not to slow containment — it is to make it survivable. If we cannot build a system that stops rogue AI *and* respects due process, we will have neither security nor sovereignty.
