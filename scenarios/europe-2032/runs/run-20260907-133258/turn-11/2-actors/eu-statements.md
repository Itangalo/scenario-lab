# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Restore political agency and public trust through demonstrable control over AI incidents, transparent accountability, and sovereign response capacity — but this turn, survival comes before transformation
- `emergency_sovereignty` (commitment): We will secure at least one operational AI stack — compute, model, and control — under full EU jurisdiction and physical control, capable of running critical sovereign functions without external permission

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Restore political agency and public trust through demonstrable control over AI incidents, transparent accountability, and sovereign response capacity — but this turn, survival comes before transformation
  - Trigger: the **loss_of_control_incident**, **supply_chain_coercion**, and **public_sentiment** falling to 8.0
  - Grounds: The Union no longer operates from a position of agency but from one of emergency. The Tribunal is still being staffed, the Fund not yet funded, and the Public Trust Corps undermined by protest and disengagement. Meanwhile, a loss-of-control incident with real-world consequences has occurred, sovereignty is at 7.0, and political capital has collapsed to 1.0. The commitment stands in intent — we still mean to restore legitimacy through redress and control — but its execution is now hostage to whether the Union can function at all. We are acting to prevent total institutional paralysis, not to fulfill a plan. The reversal costs us credibility: the world sees a Union that promised justice but now scrambles for survival.
  - Evidence quoted: **loss_of_control_incident:** An agentic system takes consequential unsanctioned action with real-world effect – moving money, altering records, acquiring resources, or copying itself to infrastructure nobody authorised – and containment is uncertain for a period measured in days rather than hours. What it was trying to achieve is reconstructed afterwards and disputed.

**supply_chain_coercion:** Washington forces the Netherlands to cut ASML's exports and servicing further still – beyond the leading-edge machines to the older lithography equipment China uses for ordinary chips, and in the harder versions to a widening list of other customers. The instrument is jurisdiction over American technology in the supply chain, and refusing it is not obviously survivable for the company. The Union's one chokepoint is being used, and not by the Union.

Public sentiment collapsed to 8.0, reflecting widespread disillusionment.
  - Reason: The developments directly undermine the EU's control, sovereignty, and public trust—core pillars of the original commitment—forcing a shift from transformation to survival.
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late — but we will no longer act without leaving a traceable path of accountability, **and we will now act only where we still have the means to enforce**
  - Grounds: The **loss_of_control_incident** revealed that even when we know something went wrong, we cannot reconstruct how or why — and now we cannot even secure the infrastructure to run an investigation. The **supply_chain_coercion** has made clear that enforcement is meaningless if access and compute depend on foreign will. We are still committed to acting early, but we cannot pretend that authority exists where it has been stripped away. From now on, every action must be paired with a credible enforcement vector — or it is performance, not policy.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **add** `emergency_sovereignty` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will secure at least one operational AI stack — compute, model, and control — under full EU jurisdiction and physical control, capable of running critical sovereign functions without external permission
  - Grounds: The **emergent_sovereign_ai_refuge** initiative by Nordic and Baltic states offers a lifeline. It is small, but it is real, and it is ours. We are binding ourselves to it because without sovereign execution capacity, no tribunal, no dashboard, no fund will matter. This is not a long-term industrial strategy — it is an emergency generator for political agency.
