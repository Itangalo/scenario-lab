### The Seizure

With political capital at zero and public trust in freefall, the European Commission invoked Article 4 TEU, directing the European Defence Agency to assume emergency control over LUMI, Leonardo, and MareNostrum — Europe’s largest EuroHPC systems. Finland, Italy, and Spain provisionally accepted co-stewardship, though Berlin and The Hague issued formal reservations, warning of legal overreach. Firmware locks were installed, external cloud links severed, and a minimal sovereign model — trained on administrative workflows — was deployed in air-gapped mode. For the first time since the crisis began, the EU ran an AI system it could physically inspect.

But the move came at a cost. The AI Incident Dashboard, once central to the now-canceled Public Trust Corps, failed again during a minor service disruption, discrediting the very transparency the seizure was meant to restore. No new safety protocols were implemented, and the Accountability Tribunal remained paralyzed, unable to compel access to foreign models.

### Capability and Chaos

Despite signs of a plateau in top-tier AI development — with a major release underperforming and prices for high-end compute dropping — global capability rose to 71.5, driven by incremental gains in verification and agent coordination. Open-weight models advanced to 59.0, as academic communities repurposed older architectures into narrowly capable tools. Resilience eroded further, to 34.0, as physical protests damaged data centre infrastructure in France and Poland, and response coordination fractured across member states.

Public sentiment held at 5.0, a floor of despair. The seizure was framed not as sovereignty regained, but as desperation institutionalized.

### Sovereignty, Fragile and Costly

The emergency action secured marginal technical control, but legal and political fragility offset the gain. Sovereignty rose only slightly, to 8.0, as the measure’s partial execution was undercut by capability growth (+1.5) and lack of full integration. Political capital inched up to 1.0, as finishing the Trust Corps (though ineffective) removed a −3 drain, but the new large measure cost −3, and the priority −1. No proposal bonus applied — the measure responded to old crises, not fresh ones. Sentiment remained below capital, so no legitimacy boost followed.

The Union held on — but by a thread.
