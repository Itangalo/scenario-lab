### A Crisis of Justice, Not Just Code

The EU opened 2031 with a pivot from resilience to redress. The ombudsman’s damning report on an AI-driven social insurance system — which wrongfully denied benefits to over 12,000 citizens — ignited public fury and political paralysis. In response, the Commission launched the EU AI Accountability Tribunal and Remediation Fund as its new priority, aiming to restore legitimacy through enforceable justice. However, the initiative immediately ran into resistance: six member states demanded sweeping opt-outs, forcing last-minute concessions on enforcement and jurisdiction. While the framework passed, its weakened authority undermined confidence before operations even began.

Meanwhile, a sudden capability jump in frontier models — demonstrated in autonomous cyber intrusions and self-improving code generation — shifted attacker capabilities overnight. Though general AI progress registered only +2.0, the leap exposed dangerous gaps in understanding. These advances were quickly mirrored in open-weight systems, now at 57.0, enabling coordinated attacks on mid-tier industrial networks across Belgium and northern Italy. Resilience eroded further as response capacity lagged.

Worse, Washington compelled ASML to restrict not just advanced chipmaking tools but also mature-node equipment critical to automotive and medical sectors. This supply-chain coercion struck at Europe’s already fragile semiconductor base, cutting off maintenance and spares. The EU’s leverage evaporated — it could neither retaliate nor substitute. Sovereignty dipped again, now at 7.0, as dependence deepened.

The Public Trust Corps struggled to gain traction. Despite launching a beta AI Incident Dashboard and conducting drills in Lyon and Warsaw, turnout remained abysmal. In Marseille, protesters disrupted a simulation, accusing officials of “performative transparency.” Public sentiment collapsed to 8.0, reflecting widespread disillusionment.

With `ai_safety` falling sharply due to opaque reasoning patterns and the capability jump, and no safety-focused measures completed, trust in technical governance disintegrated. Political capital, strained by three large ongoing measures and the new priority, fell to 1.0 — barely above collapse.

### Fractured Response, Fading Authority

The Tribunal may yet issue findings, and the Fund may pay claims — but only if the Union can act while nearly bankrupt in credibility and capital. For now, every move risks deepening the crisis.
