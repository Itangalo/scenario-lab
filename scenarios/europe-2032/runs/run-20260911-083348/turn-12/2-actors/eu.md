## New measure
None this turn, waiting for political capital and public trust to rebuild before any new instrument can be funded or enforced.

With capital at zero and sentiment at zero after cutoff, automated sweep, and fallback-host outage, starting a new large or small measure we cannot pay for would fail publicly and further fragment cohesion; we hold on finished continuity capacity and paper procedures.

## Priority
No priority measure can be pushed this turn, because nothing is in flight and no capital exists to start anything, so continuity rests on already-finished fallback stacks and repair crews holding services open.

## In practice
We keep hospitals, town halls and ports on what already landed: EU-hosted open-model packs from M8 where the non-US cloud still answers, paper-to-digital kits and spare laptops where it does not after this semester's multi-region outage, with ENISA/CERT-EU and HERA repair crews embedded through existing JHA and EPSCO mandates. No new fund, no retaliation, no factory restart is put to Council.

On robots and leased fleets in Rotterdam, Hamburg and Lyon we take no new industrial decision: industry dialogues continue mapping procurement dependence while sites stay warm, and health and interior ministers extend only the clinical-administrative fallback already endorsed. Ministers offer continuity, not prevention, until capital returns.
