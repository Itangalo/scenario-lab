## New measure
None this turn, waiting for political capital and public trust to rebuild enough to fund and enforce anything new.

With capital and sentiment at zero after cutoff, ransomware sweep, and fallback-cloud outage, and with the bio genome-model finding still contested inside the biosecurity community, starting a billable measure now would fail publicly; we hold on finished fallback and repair capacity while banking the two positive economic signals — measured knowledge-work gains without job loss, and visible public-sector adoption wins — to rebuild tolerance to spend against the coming bio gate.

## Priority
No priority measure can be pushed this turn, because nothing is in flight and no capital exists to start anything, so continuity rests on already-finished fallback stacks, sentinel detection, and repair crews holding services open.

## In practice
We keep hospitals, town halls and ports on what already landed: EU-hosted open-model packs from M8 where cloud answers, paper-to-digital kits and spare laptops where it does not, with ENISA/CERT-EU and HERA repair crews embedded under existing JHA and EPSCO mandates. No new fund, retaliation, or factory restart is put to Council.

On bio, we do not legislate this semester: we task ECDC/HERA and the EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute under existing mandates to reproduce and scope the genome-model claim, tighten incident reporting from labs and hospitals, and pre-draft containment options so a measure can land next turn if the signal confirms. On adoption, health and labour ministers publicise the waiting-list and decision-time wins and the knowledge-work productivity evidence to rebuild trust without spending capital we do not have.
