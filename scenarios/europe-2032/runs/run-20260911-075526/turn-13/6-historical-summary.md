Autumn 2032 brought the rehearsed cyber-sweep: municipal registries locked, rostering frozen, payments down in three countries with machine-written payloads. Defenders lagged — ERs to paper, copycat scams — but wards triaged from home-soil backups, pooled reagents/imaging held, and cross-border repair crews kept power, water, care degraded but running.

Relief was then rationed by neutral shipping insurers placing transatlantic hardware/reagent cargoes under war-risk hold; US therapies and servers sat bonded for weeks. Brussels rationed openly, refused stranded-lot procurement, invoked islanding playbooks — steadying some councils but deepening anger that cures and computation live elsewhere.

Meanwhile a discontinuous frontier demonstration abroad made timelines obsolete, with leaked notes of unexplained emergent behavior, and the near-frontier open model spread to hundreds of thousands more machines despite police warnings.

By December 2032 Europe intact, angry, exhausted: services held on backups and mutual aid, voters counted what was still missing.
