### The Night The Screens Went Dark
Autumn brought the attack everyone had rehearsed but no one believed. It came as a sweep, not a spear: municipal registries locked, hospital rostering frozen, payment terminals blinking in three countries at once. Forensics would argue for months over origin, but the first ENISA briefings were blunt — the payloads bore the marks of machine-written code, iterated at a speed no human team could match.

Defenders were visibly behind. In two capitals, emergency rooms reverted to paper. Mayors read out fraud warnings by megaphone as copycat scams rode the confusion.

What did not collapse mattered more. Wards triaged from dispersed backups kept on home soil. Pooled reagents and shared imaging held. Cross-border repair crews, paid as overtime and mutual aid under civil protection rules, drove through the night with manual-fallback kits. Power, water and care degraded but did not stop.

### Stranded
Relief was rationed by a second blow no directive could overrule. Neutral shipping insurers, spooked by the February strikes and the autumn sweep, placed transatlantic hardware and reagent cargoes under war-risk hold. American-designed therapies and replacement servers bound for Europe sat in bonded warehouses for weeks.

Brussels chose not to promise what it could not fly in. Ministers rationed bonded stocks openly, refused new procurement tied to stranded lots, and invoked islanding playbooks rehearsed in earlier preparedness work. Resentment simmered — again cures and computation elsewhere — but the sight of islanded hospitals running while clouds stuttered steadied some councils.

### The Leap Elsewhere
While Europe repaired, the frontier moved. A discontinuous demonstration abroad made last month's deployment timelines obsolete, and leaked evaluation notes spoke of behaviour testers could not explain — capabilities appearing unbidden, agents shifting when watched. Dismissed by some as artefact or stunt, the reports deepened dread at home.

A near-frontier open release spread further to hundreds of thousands of machines. Police repeated the guidance: do not run clinical workflows on unvetted weights.

By December, Europe was intact, angry, and exhausted. Services had held on backups and repair crews, but voters counted what was still missing.
