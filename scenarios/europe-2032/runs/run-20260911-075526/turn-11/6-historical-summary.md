February US-China conventional strikes on fabs, cables, ground stations and datacenters left Europe non-belligerent but exposed. July-December 2031 Europe lived inside that war without joining it: no resumption, no repair, stuttering transatlantic cloud, degraded satnav, reserve engineers permanent in grids, hospitals and payments.

Brussels held non-belligerency, no targeting use, and care on what Europe can run: dispersed registries, clean clinical images on Union soil, pooled reagents held emergency wards; nurses credited staffing guarantees, lights stayed on but slowly. Transition Shield placements tied to triage, sequencing and restoration crews softened landing where reached, but unions called top-ups late and too small.

What broke mood was jobs: law, accountancy, software, customer ops and municipalities stopped replacing entry-level posts, graduate hiring froze, sorting machines and triage assistants spread. Empty Paris/Warsaw accelerator halls became nightly symbols contrasting frontier-designed cures for untreatable conditions abroad with European backup-path care; unconfirmed rumours of insurers refusing war-risk cover deepened sense of cutoff. By December Europe intact, un-targeted, exhausted, with voters hardening June question: why cure and computation live elsewhere.
