## Priority
EU Transition Shield for Displaced Work and Essential Services, because with no measures in flight and capital at 14, stopping blame from hardening over job loss and queues outranks any new compute or oversight push and directly serves our two-year commitment to hold essential services.

## In practice
We act through EPSCO and ECOFIN on an ESF+ repurposing plus SURE-successor loan facility: Commission decision reallocating uncommitted cohesion envelopes to wage-insurance top-ups and 6-month retraining vouchers for law, accountancy, software, customer-ops and admin entry cohorts, co-administered with national PES. No new treaty base, no large pledge while Paris/Warsaw halls sit empty.

ENISA reserves and HERA clean-image kits from finished M5/M9 lines are kept on sustainment funding only, tasked to municipalities and hospitals for manual-to-digital restoration, with Estonian-French-German mutual aid extended by exchange of letters. Message is single: pay the bridge, keep clinics staffed, restore town halls visibly — frontier gap work waits until services hold.
