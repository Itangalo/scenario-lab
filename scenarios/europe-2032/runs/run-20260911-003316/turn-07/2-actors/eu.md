## Priority

## In practice
We run M7 through DG GROW, ENISA and national market-surveillance authorities under the Machinery Regulation and NIS2: close the fleet registry, enforce remote-stop audits starting in Rotterdam, Hamburg and Lyon, and rehearse manual fallback in ports and care pilots where Chinese hardware with US software now works. DG EMPL keeps the finished Trust and Transition wage-bridges and mayoral staffing pacts paying out through ESF+ to hold utilities and clinics staffed.

On the new fallback we task DG CNECT, DIGIT and ENISA with Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority support: inventory workloads stranded by the denial, rehost triage, admin and grid-management assistants onto EU-hosted open-weight models at the two powered gigafactory sites, and push the automated-patching and swarm-detection tooling from the breakthrough into hospitals and ministries first. We use the TTC channel to seek clarification on access terms without bargaining essential continuity on its return, and frame the move publicly as foresight delivered, not outrage.
