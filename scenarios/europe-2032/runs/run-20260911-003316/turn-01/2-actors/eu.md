## New measure
**EU Critical Services Shield**
Hardens energy, telecoms, health and finance against AI-enabled cyber disruption with EU-wide baseline standards, joint exercises and biothreat detection upgrades.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because assurance has lagged capability and open-weight offensive cyber is now unrecallable – resilience is within our control while frontier control is not.

## Priority
InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) – because without domestic compute coming online the June US switch-off precedent leaves every other measure hostage to foreign discretion.

## In practice
We are driving M1 and M2 through DG CNECT and the Competitiveness Council, using InvestAI guarantees, EIB co-financing and the accelerated-permitting zones to get four to five gigafactory sites to final investment decision, while fending off member-state bidding wars by tying grid priority to EU legal anchoring. ASML leverage is held in reserve via the Anti-Coercion Instrument discussions in the Foreign Affairs Council.

This turn we launch the Shield via the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority and ENISA, funded from the Civil Protection Mechanism uplift, starting with mandatory incident reporting drills for operators of essential services and pooled procurement of detection stacks. Interior ministers resisting new obligations are offered EU-paid hardening funds in exchange for accepting exercised continuity plans.
