## In practice
We run M8 through DG CNECT, DIGIT and HERA with ENISA: finish inventorying US-dependent triage, transcription and grid workloads, keep them on EU-hosted open models at the two live sites, and push automated patching first to hospitals and utilities. We frame the TTC only as a clarification channel, not a restoration plan, to avoid retrading continuity for vague access promises.

On the new facility we task DG CNECT, EIB and the European Innovation Council under the Chips Act and InvestAI vehicle: redirect unspent permitting and loan-guarantee headroom to take over cancelled accelerator deliveries, lease options and power reservations, and fund rapid repatriation grants for EU nationals laid off from frontier labs. Member states resisting further AI spend are offered jobs and filled halls in existing sites, not new builds, to hold cohesion while capital is at 19.
