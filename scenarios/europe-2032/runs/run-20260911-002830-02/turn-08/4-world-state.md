### War
In late February American and Chinese forces exchanged strikes after months of escalation over chips, cables and training runs. Within days fabrication plants, undersea cables, satellite links and data centres were named as legitimate targets. Two transatlantic cables were cut, a North Sea landing station damaged, and commercial satellite capacity over Europe was requisitioned by Washington.

The European Council met as an emergency neutrality council. No territory, airspace or seabed for offensive strikes, territorial defence only, deconfliction notes sent to both capitals. The line held on paper and frayed at the edges: American commanders demanded overflight and hosting, Beijing warned of countermeasures, and the capital that had signed its own hosting deal quietly expanded it.

### Money leaves, machines arrive
At the same time capital fled AI. Valuations collapsed, three build-outs Europe had counted on for accelerators and power equipment were cancelled, and suppliers to the two gigafactory shells stretched delivery dates from years to indefinite. Frontier labs froze training runs.

Commercial humanoid robots still shipped. Chinese-built units, running American control stacks, appeared in logistics yards in Rotterdam and Hamburg. Dockworkers struck against them; defence ministries circulated memos about mine-clearance models being rearmed faster than any doctrine existed.

### Holding on
Hospitals and grid operators went to wartime watch. ENISA and national operators rerouted around cut cables, HERA pre-positioned stocks, civil protection teams drilled manual chlorination and paper triage. Where mayors cooperated, clinics stayed open on European-hosted open models. Where grid-priority builds were contested, work paused to avoid sabotage claims, and blockades hardened around water and power prices.

The joint access pact with Washington formally concluded, but delivered process rather than capacity: evaluator seats without models to evaluate. By June Europe was darker, poorer, and still uninvaded — running on fallback while war targeted the ground fallback stood on. Finishing dates for fallback work slipped and Brussels did not act to reset them.
