## New measure
**EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**
Deploys mandatory segmentation, intrusion detection and exercised isolation protocols across transmission operators, ports and water utilities, with EU-funded incident response teams. This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and why now is because the autumn grid intrusion proved detection was luck and footholds remain, so building compute without hardening what it runs on invites cascading failure.
