## New measure
**EU Displaced Workers Transition Fund**
Provides wage insurance, rapid retraining vouchers and employer-linked transition levies for occupations facing AI displacement, administered via ESF+ and national public employment services. This is category 7 Labour and social protection, and why now is because the augmentation narrative has collapsed into displacement just as a major automated attack and an investment bust hit public services, so resilience without income absorption will fail politically.
