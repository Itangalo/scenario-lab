### The Weight of Motion

The cyber_major_incident — a model-generated ransomware wave paralyzing regional health networks across three member states — intensified scrutiny on AI governance, particularly as autonomous cyber capabilities advanced at the closed frontier. While the origin of the attack remains under investigation, early signals suggest misuse of frontier systems rather than open-weight models. Nevertheless, the incident amplified pressure to act on all vectors of uncontrolled capability diffusion.

In response, the EU launched **Accelerate Red-Team Access to Open-Weight Models via the MPAC Evaluation Pipeline**, a small Category 5 measure, repurposing funds from its stalled safety infrastructure. Invoking Article 114 TFEU, the Commission established a temporary evaluation corridor, integrating the emergent hardened fork into public-sector pipelines. France and Finland joined as technical stewards; Germany, after initial resistance, accepted co-leadership of the Bavarian node. Austria hosts the GDPR-compliant request registry.

Implementation will take time. Full access is not yet operational, and the pipeline remains in early deployment. As aligned researchers begin to adopt the fork, `openweight_capability` rises modestly to 60.0 — reflecting gradual diffusion, consistent with prior accumulation trends and the absence of a sudden release mechanism. This keeps pace within plausible bounds relative to the 1.5-point rise in `ai_capability`, which continues advancing at the closed frontier, particularly in autonomous operations.

Without systemic safety upgrades, `ai_safety` dipped further, now at 24.0. Resilience declined as the health-sector attack exposed patchy defences, and no category 6 measure yet offsets the rising threat.

The Sovereign Compute Corridor remains unbuilt; no sovereignty gain materialized. With `ai_capability` rising, dependence deepened, costing −1 under rule 5. Political capital eroded: two large measures still in flight (−3 each) and a new small measure (−2) total −8. However, no additional priority was declared this turn — the existing priority remains in place. A proposal bonus of +5 — for addressing both the cyber incident and openweight risks — partially offset the cost. Final `eu_political_capital` ends at 16.0.

Public sentiment rose slightly, to 22.0, as the red-team initiative was framed as proactive stewardship, not restriction. Protests in Vienna intensified over governance bypass, but Helsinki celebrated inclusion in the evaluation network.

### A Narrow Path Forward

The hardened fork gains traction, but it is not containment. The EU acts — but fragments as it does, pays dearly, and lands only partial control.
