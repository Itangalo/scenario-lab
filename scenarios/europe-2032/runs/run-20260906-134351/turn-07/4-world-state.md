### The Storm Breaks

A wave of coordinated ransomware, generated and adapted by AI, cripples public health and transport systems across three member states. Response teams are overwhelmed — the malware evolves faster than patches can be deployed. The Hardened Cyber Shield detects the attacks, but cannot contain them. Resilience, already strained, erodes further.

Simultaneously, Slovakia bypasses EU verification rules to secure emergency AI access from a U.S. hyperscaler, citing national necessity. The move fractures the Union’s front, exposing the limits of verification without operational independence. Public trust, already fragile, collapses.

### Capability Leaps, Safety Stalls

Overnight, a leading lab releases a model that redefines what is possible. Its planning depth, self-correction, and cross-domain integration mark a discontinuous leap. `ai_capability` surges to 76.5. Developers celebrate; safety teams are alarmed. The jump was not anticipated, and no safeguards scaled with it. Interpretability tools fail to parse the model’s reasoning. `ai_safety` plummets to 3.0, as the gap between power and control widens catastrophically.

Openweight models, benefiting from the emergent efficiency breakthrough, advance to 61.0. Training costs drop, enabling wider replication. Yet the closed frontier pulls further ahead.

### EU Responds: Continuity as Sovereignty

In Brussels, the Commission declares a crisis. The **Emergency Continuity Stack** is launched as a top priority — a minimal, hardened AI stack to sustain essential functions during total denial. It will run on repurposed public cloud nodes, using frozen openweight models at 57.5 capability, air-gapped and task-limited. The move is praised as necessary, but underfunded and late.

The **Sovereign Verification Regime** issues audit notices, but enforcement stalls. Legal pushback mounts. EAAD+ lacks personnel and precedent. The **Institutional Integrity Audit** nears completion, but findings remain internal. No public vindication occurs.

Public sentiment crashes to 15.0. Protests turn violent near data centres in Paris and Frankfurt. A transformer station is sabotaged. The message is clear: the system is not protecting them.
