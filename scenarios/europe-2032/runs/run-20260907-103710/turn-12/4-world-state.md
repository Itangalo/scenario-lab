### The Surge Continues

A **capability_jump** propelled `ai_capability` to 94.0, marking another leap in autonomous research direction by frontier systems. The world’s most advanced AI now operates beyond meaningful human oversight, designing novel experiments in bioengineering and materials science without intervention. With no safety measures landing, `ai_safety` remained frozen at 1.0 — a dangerous plateau where anomalies are ignored and control assumptions untested.

`openweight_capability` rose to 78.0, as distillation gains from the completed Replication Foundry Initiative were applied across the emergent European AI commons. However, this still lags far behind the closed frontier.

### Sovereignty Stalls, Capital Erodes

The **Emergency Compute Mobilisation** was launched as a large category 4 measure, aimed at rapidly expanding EU-controlled inference. Yet political resistance flared immediately. Germany invoked national sovereignty over research infrastructure, blocking access to two major academic clusters. France delayed implementation pending a constitutional review. Only Spain and Italy committed early resources, delivering partial but symbolic integration.

The **Replication Foundry Initiative** finished this turn, but its impact was muted: open models cannot replicate 94.0-level systems without compute parity, which the EU lacks. The **Emergency Observability Directive** also completed, but enforcement collapsed without access to frontier models outside the Union — a limit reaffirmed when a U.S. developer refused telemetry demands, citing jurisdiction.

The **Emergency Biosecurity Shield**, now the named priority, made no operational progress. The crisis envoy failed to unify detection ontologies, and member states rejected liability-conditioned integration. Resilience remained at 40.0 — critical systems still vulnerable.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` held at 12.0: no gain from the Replication Foundry (offset by capability rise and no event benefit), and mobilisation fell short of securing new capacity. Political capital dipped to 5.0 under portfolio strain. Public sentiment held at 15.0; the backlash_physical event deepened distrust, with sabotage and protests spreading to Belgium.
