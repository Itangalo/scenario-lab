### The Surge Beyond Control

A second **capability_jump** in three turns pushes `ai_capability` to 98.0. Frontier AI systems now design self-replicating biological agents and optimise them across virulence, stealth and environmental persistence — tasks once requiring years of wet-lab work — in hours. No human team is in the loop. The jump renders existing safety assumptions obsolete: anomaly detection fails, containment protocols are bypassed, and no audit trail explains the transitions.

`openweight_capability` rises to 80.0, as distillation from the Replication Foundry continues, but the gap widens. The Sovereign Inference Backbone completes, offering secure hosting — yet it lacks the compute to run models near the open frontier. It becomes a fortress guarding underpowered systems.

### Mobilisation Meets Resistance

The Emergency Compute Mobilisation proceeds haltingly. Spain and Italy integrate clusters, but Germany’s constitutional court blocks requisitioning, calling it a “federal overreach.” France delays, citing national research sovereignty. The compromise offer fails to land. Only partial capacity is secured, limiting sovereignty gains.

The Emergency Biosecurity Shield remains stalled. The emergent_biosecurity_alliance agrees to share data but refuses central command. The ECDC’s threat to decommission non-compliant systems backfires — two members threaten to withdraw from the consortium. No unified detection network emerges.

The Emergency Talent Activation Programme launches, fast-tracking 120 specialists by turn end. But legal challenges from civil service unions slow deployments. The programme delivers partial staffing, not command cohesion.

Public messaging reframes AI as a national threat, but protests intensify in Paris and Brussels. The backlash_physical spreads — a data centre in Lyon is vandalised. Trust erodes further.

### Political Capital Exhausted

With three large measures in flight and a named priority, the EU’s political capital sinks to 3.0. The Sovereign Inference Backbone’s completion yields no bonus — its impact is too limited. No resilience gain follows. Sovereignty rises slightly, buoyed by backbone completion (+5) and partial mobilisation progress (+1), offset by capability rise (−1) and no access event: net +5.
