## In practice
We run M14 and the new Restoration Cell together through ENISA and DG HOME with the joint command's pooled feeds: cooperating towns that drilled cut off and go offline on procedure, while EU teams push clean images, reserve licences and emergency manuals to hospitals hit in the sweep. HERA biosurveillance stays on watch but all bandwidth goes to cyber triage.

We hold certified safety cases (M13 finished) as the bar to bring civic systems back online, using AI Office/JRC logs to reassure insurers' observers, and DG TRADE defends essential-service compute queues under end-use checks. On ASML coercion we protest and delay via Council and the Anti-Coercion Instrument screening but spend no new capital now; sovereignty rebuild waits until services stay up.
