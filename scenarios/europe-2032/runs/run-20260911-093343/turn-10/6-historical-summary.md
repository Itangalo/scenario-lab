Brussels formalised hidden stocks: using emergency powers, off-books fuel/transformer/generator pools moved onto official lists, split into smaller guarded stores, holders paid, protected sites mapped. Segmented manual routines continued; containment/mutual-aid protocol declared operational with cross-border drills.

Washington tightened to hard volume quotas for allies on advanced chips/model access, case-by-case servicing; global AI valuations fell, data-centre expansions cancelled, European supply hopes evaporated. The Hague complied under protest. Brussels promised no new procurement.

European-run AI in two health systems and a benefits agency cut waits from months to days, toured as European decisions; clinics stayed on EU default. Did not reverse hostility but gave first positive counter-story in a year. Commission spent last standing defending existing operations.
