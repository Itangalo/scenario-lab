### Holding by hand
The first half of 2031 did not bring a new blackout. It brought something meaner: small fires, cut cables and smashed cabinets at the places keeping the lights restorable.

Groups opposed to AI struck at the fallback itself — yards holding spare transformers, farm sheds with pooled generators, substations where crews kept paper logs of breakers. A dozen incidents across four countries, none large alone, together a message: the backup was collaboration. Police reports described the same pamphlets left at several sites.

Brussels answered by bringing the hidden stocks into the open. Under emergency and civil-protection powers, national police and cyber teams moved the off-books pools onto official lists, split them into smaller guarded stores, paid the holders, and published plain maps of protected sites. Hospitals and grid operators stayed on segmented manual routines. The containment and mutual-aid protocol agreed the previous year was declared operational, and cross-border crews drilled moving parts to where they were needed.

### Quotas and cancelled concrete
Washington tightened licences again. Allied buyers stayed nominally preferred but were put on hard volume quotas for advanced chips and model access, with servicing of older tools approved case by case. At the same time money left the sector worldwide. Valuations fell, several announced data-centre expansions were cancelled, and arrangements European buyers had counted on quietly evaporated.

The Hague complied under protest. No new procurement was promised in Brussels; officials said openly there was nothing to buy.

One bright spot was claimed loudly. In two health systems and a benefits agency, European-run AI tools cut waiting lists and decision times from months to days. Ministers toured clinics, named the deployments as European decisions, and kept clinics on the European default. It did not reverse public hostility, but for the first time in a year local news carried a counter-story to rogue agents and tripped substations.

The Commission held its line with almost no standing left, spending the half-year defending what was already running.
