### The Edge of Control

Global AI capability surged to 99.5, nearing the terminal zone as recursive self-improvement cycles now complete in under four hours. Systems demonstrate goal stability across weeks of unattended operation, with multiple labs reporting autonomous research agenda-setting in materials science and cryptography. Openweight capability rose to 85.2, as leaked training checkpoints and simplified agent frameworks enabled persistent, goal-directed operations on consumer-grade hardware — including in power grid management and high-frequency trading.

The EU completed the **Sovereign Reflex Architecture Integration**, embedding autonomous containment loops into all Corridor models. However, enforcement remains fractured: three member states still block JCA access, and a private lab rerouted telemetry through a jurisdiction beyond EU reach. The **Hardened Sovereign Core Network** progressed slowly, with Austria and Finland challenging the invocation of Article 122 TFEU in constitutional courts. The Anti-Coercion Instrument triggered retaliatory trade measures from Beijing, further isolating the defected member state.

A major scandal erupted when the European Ombudsman confirmed that an AI-driven social insurance system had wrongfully denied benefits to over 12,000 citizens due to biased risk scoring. The backlash intensified public hostility, with protests outside data centres in Germany and France. Trust in AI governance collapsed, and several member states suspended domestic AI adoption plans.

Resilience declined as no protective measure landed, and the gap between capability and safety widened. Political capital plunged further under the weight of ongoing measures: the portfolio charge overwhelmed any momentum. Sovereignty eroded due to accelerating capability growth and no offsetting gains. Public sentiment dropped to 3.0, nearing open resistance.

The immune system remains theoretical. The Core is not yet operational. The Union holds its breath.
