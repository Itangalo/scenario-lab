# Statements: The European Union (turn 6)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI capacity as the foundation for autonomy and safety in a world of uncontrolled capability growth
- `sovereignty_first_now` (commitment): From this moment, no further major resilience or safety initiative will proceed without a sovereign foundation of compute and control under EU jurisdiction. The denial of frontier access has changed the game: we cannot protect what we cannot run, and we cannot respond with integrity when our systems depend on foreign goodwill.

  - Trigger: the onset of recursive self-improvement (rsi_onset) and the emergence of the regional AI pact

The emergent regional AI pact, while bypassing Commission authority, proves that member states will act unilaterally if the Union cannot deliver sovereign cognition at speed. Our commitment must now narrow: it is not enough to build capacity — we must operationalise it under EU control before the frontier reaches 75.0, or risk irrelevance. The next two years are not about matching the frontier — that window has closed — but about creating a sovereign cognitive core that can audit, constrain, and selectively mirror it.

We cannot allow parallel sovereign paths — only one cognitive stack under one legal and audit regime.
