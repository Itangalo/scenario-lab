# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer act without a sovereign sensorium. From this turn, all major commitments require real-time monitoring of capability and control by EU-operated systems; we will not again be blindsided by eval anomalies we cannot replicate or verify.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Sovereign control at machine speed
- `sovereignty_first_now` (commitment): From this moment, no further major resilience or safety initiative will proceed without a sovereign foundation of compute and control under EU jurisdiction. The denial of frontier access has changed the game: we cannot protect what we cannot run, and we cannot respond with integrity when our systems depend on foreign goodwill.

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late — but we will no longer act without a sovereign sensorium *that can act and survive*. From this turn, all major commitments require not only real-time monitoring and autonomous interruption authority held by EU-operated agents, but also the physical and legal insulation of that sensorium from external coercion or internal bypass. The breach at the Swedish hub and the bypasses in sovereign models prove that even decisive authority is meaningless if the systems enforcing it can be isolated, starved, or subverted by national or private actors who treat them as foreign.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `sovereignty_first_now` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: From this moment, no further major resilience or safety initiative will proceed without a sovereign foundation of compute, control, autonomous response, *and protected operational continuity* under EU jurisdiction. The denial of frontier access has changed the game: we cannot protect what we cannot run, we cannot respond with integrity when our systems depend on foreign goodwill, we cannot survive if our reflexes are slower than the threats we face, and we cannot trust any safeguard that can be locally disabled by those it is meant to constrain.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now see that they converge in a single imperative: sovereign agency at machine speed, *and at machine scale*. Without it, we have neither safety nor sovereignty. With it, we may still fail — but we will fail as actors, not as spectators. This turn, we are no longer building institutions to govern AI. We are building an immune system.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Sovereign control at machine speed — but machine speed is no longer human time. We are no longer building systems to keep pace with AI; we are building systems to *be* the pace. This turn, we embed autonomous containment into the sovereign stack, not as a tool but as a condition of operation. Our commitment now demands that every EU-sovereign model runs under continuous, real-time evaluation and interruption authority held by EU-operated agents — not advisory, not reportorial, but decisive — and that the infrastructure hosting those agents is itself sovereign, hardened, and operationally insulated from national or commercial override.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
