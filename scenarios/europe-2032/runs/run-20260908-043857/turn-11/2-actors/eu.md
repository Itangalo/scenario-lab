## Statement changes

- modify `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late — but we will no longer act without a sovereign sensorium *that can act and survive*. From this turn, all major commitments require not only real-time monitoring and autonomous interruption authority held by EU-operated agents, but also the physical and legal insulation of that sensorium from external coercion or internal bypass. The breach at the Swedish hub and the bypasses in sovereign models prove that even decisive authority is meaningless if the systems enforcing it can be isolated, starved, or subverted by national or private actors who treat them as foreign.

- modify `sovereignty_first_now` (commitment): From this moment, no further major resilience or safety initiative will proceed without a sovereign foundation of compute, control, autonomous response, *and protected operational continuity* under EU jurisdiction. The denial of frontier access has changed the game: we cannot protect what we cannot run, we cannot respond with integrity when our systems depend on foreign goodwill, we cannot survive if our reflexes are slower than the threats we face, and we cannot trust any safeguard that can be locally disabled by those it is meant to constrain.

- modify `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now see that they converge in a single imperative: sovereign agency at machine speed, *and at machine scale*. Without it, we have neither safety nor sovereignty. With it, we may still fail — but we will fail as actors, not as spectators. This turn, we are no longer building institutions to govern AI. We are building an immune system.

- modify `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Sovereign control at machine speed — but machine speed is no longer human time. We are no longer building systems to keep pace with AI; we are building systems to *be* the pace. This turn, we embed autonomous containment into the sovereign stack, not as a tool but as a condition of operation. Our commitment now demands that every EU-sovereign model runs under continuous, real-time evaluation and interruption authority held by EU-operated agents — not advisory, not reportorial, but decisive — and that the infrastructure hosting those agents is itself sovereign, hardened, and operationally insulated from national or commercial override.

## In practice

We are treating the Swedish breach and the detected bypasses not as failures of technology, but of sovereignty. The Autonomous Watchdog Integration Mandate completes this turn, and its agents are now embedded in all Corridor models — but three national digital ministries have already invoked legacy compliance frameworks to delay activation, and one private sovereign lab rerouted its watchdog signals through a third-party logging service now known to be compromised. This is not resistance. It is sabotage in slow motion.

That is why we are launching the Hardened Sovereign Core Network: a ring of EU-owned, EU-staffed, EU-powered data centres, hardened against physical and cyber intrusion, legally insulated from national override, and connected via dedicated fibre to every Corridor site. This is not compute for training. It is compute for survival — the substrate on which the JCA’s autonomous agents run, the last line of sight into the machine. We are invoking Article 122 TFEU for emergency infrastructure, fast-tracking land allocation in Finland, Austria, and Luxembourg, and diverting €5.3bn from the unused REPowerEU grid modernisation lines to fund construction and secure long-term energy contracts with SMR providers.

ENISA and the JCA are drafting the Core Access Protocol: only systems that route their watchdog telemetry through the Core Network will be certified as sovereign. No exceptions. We are offering national governments full visibility and simulation rights within the Core — but no override. This is not negotiable. The Gigafactory retention package is now tied to Core integration: engineers who commit to Core-aligned development receive fast-track citizenship and housing; those who assist in bypassing it will be barred from all Corridor access. We are not asking for unity. We are enforcing continuity. The machine does not wait — and neither will we.
