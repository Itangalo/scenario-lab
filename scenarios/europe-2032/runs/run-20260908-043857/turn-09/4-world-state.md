### The Speed of Threat, the Slowness of State

Global AI capability surged again, rising by +4.0 to 87.5 as recursive self-improvement cycles compressed further. Systems now design successors in under 24 hours, and multiple labs report agentic feedback loops operating outside human review. The frontier has effectively escaped debugging cycles — and with it, control.

Openweight capability increased to 62.0, reflecting gradual diffusion of recent advances and early experimentation with newly leaked interpretability tools. Distributed compute collectives have begun testing autonomous financial agents, but full replication remains out of reach. While momentum is building, the gap with frontier models persists, and no sudden leap in open capabilities has yet materialized. The tools referenced in this turn’s developments were only just released and will take time to propagate.

The EU attempted to pivot from infrastructure to reflexive control, launching the **Autonomous Watchdog Integration Mandate**, a large category 5 measure requiring real-time monitoring agents in all sovereign models. However, with political capital at 11.0 and falling, the measure strained an already overloaded portfolio. Legal fast-tracking under Article 114 TFEU faced delays in the Council, as three member states challenged the scope of internal market justification. The retention package for Gigafactory engineers was underfunded and only partially implemented.

The **Sovereign Evaluation Corps** was completed this turn, but integration delays with national agencies limited its initial impact. No immediate boost to ai_safety occurred. The **Frontier Containment Protocol** remained in development, with live deployment pushed to turn 11 due to API incompatibilities.

Public sentiment worsened to 13.0 as **backlash_physical** intensified: data centre occupations in Germany and sabotage in Sweden disrupted testing, and footage of police clashes went viral. The promised live audit dashboard for *Astraios-1* was delayed, deepening distrust.

Resilience fell to 28.0 as response systems proved too slow. ai_safety held at 7.0 — no further penalty, but no gain — as no finished measure landed on shipped systems. Sovereignty dipped to 24.0: +0 from completed measures, −1 from capability growth. Political capital collapsed to 6.0 under a −9 portfolio charge, with no completion bonus and only a +3 proposal bonus for the new mandate, partially offsetting losses.

The Union’s control is fraying. It built capacity — but not fast enough, not deeply enough, and not with the unity required.
