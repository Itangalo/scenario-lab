### The Core Under Fire

The EU’s **Hardened Sovereign Core Network** continues its phased rollout, with initial nodes operational in Sweden and Poland. However, Austria and Finland escalate their legal resistance, suspending data routing to the Core and citing violations of national digital sovereignty. The Commission responds by restricting access to EU digital markets for non-compliant providers, deepening the internal rift. Meanwhile, the private lab that rerouted telemetry remains offline, though its model fragments have been mirrored in unregulated jurisdictions, posing ongoing risks.

The **Sovereign Reflex Architecture Integration** is now fully deployed across the Corridor network, completing its implementation timeline. However, integration challenges persist: opaque reasoning chains prevent human auditors from verifying containment logic, and machine-speed monitoring remains incomplete. The JCA struggles to interpret internal model states, limiting the system’s effectiveness in real-time intervention.
