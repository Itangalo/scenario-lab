## In practice  
We are treating the cyber_test_shot as a wake-up call, not an isolated incident. This turn, the Commission will draft a Cyber Resilience Emergency Directive under Article 114 TFEU, to be fast-tracked through COREPER and the Internal Market Council. The directive mandates AI-driven intrusion detection systems, mandatory red-teaming of grid operators and water utilities, and real-time telemetry sharing with ENISA and national CSIRTs. We will invoke the NIS2 emergency powers to bypass prolonged negotiations, but that requires member-state buy-in — so we are offering front-loaded EU co-funding from the Digital Europe Programme and REACT-EU to offset national resistance.

The InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package remain on track, but we are under pressure to show results faster than 2036. We are pushing national permitting authorities in France, Germany, and Poland to designate AI sovereignty zones with fast-grid access, backed by state aid approvals from Brussels. ASML remains a strategic chokepoint — we are finalizing a memorandum with the Dutch government to treat EUV exports as dual-use, but American pressure complicates this. Public sentiment is ambivalent, and we cannot afford broad backlash — so we are pairing infrastructure pushes with visible public-sector AI pilots in healthcare diagnostics, under strict human oversight, to maintain legitimacy.
