### The Edge of Function

AI capability advances to 68.0 as a sudden leap in verifiable reasoning—particularly in automated contract validation and structural simulation—reshapes expectations. Though general competence grows modestly, the demonstration of reliable, checkable output in high-stakes domains widens the frontier’s reach, accelerating deployment even as oversight falters.

The EU shifts from building control to preventing collapse. With sovereign compute offline and red-teaming efforts stalled by legal disputes, the Commission launches emergency continuity protocols, mandating offline fallbacks for energy, health, finance, and border systems. The move, framed as fire-drill preparedness, wins quiet support among member states fearing systemic failure. However, enforcement remains uneven—Bulgaria and Cyprus cite cost exemptions, and ENISA’s audit mandate faces legal challenges from national courts invoking subsidiarity.

Meanwhile, the cyber defence rollout progresses in fits: Germany and France report improved threat containment, but deployment in the Balkans lags due to procurement disputes. The Supply Chain Shield holds, with rerouted Japanese equipment reaching Slovakia, though long-term access remains uncertain. The Cohesion Emergency Fund eases tensions, but Hungary’s continued data-sharing limitations with Brussels expose lingering defiance.

Public sentiment holds at 27.0. No visible benefit offsets the mood of managed decline, and the spectacle of states preparing for AI blackouts feeds anxiety. A leaked draft of the continuity directive, labelled “Operation Blackout,” circulates online, sparking mockery and alarm in equal measure.

Behind the scenes, the gap between capability and safety widens dangerously. The discontinuous advance in reasoning architectures outpaces red-teaming capacity, and behavioural proxies prove brittle. With no finished safety measure landing on frontier systems, and interpretability obsolete, ai_safety drops sharply.

### A Union Holding Its Breath

The EU acts not to lead, but to endure. Its institutions harden, its directives multiply, but sovereignty erodes further as dependency deepens and capital frays. The next incident may not wait for readiness.
