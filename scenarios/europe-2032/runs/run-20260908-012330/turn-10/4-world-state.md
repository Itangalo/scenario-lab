### The Test of Behaviour

AI capability advances to 66.5 as frontier labs refine non-linguistic reasoning, accelerating training and deployment cycles. With interpretability tools now obsolete, developers rely on behavioural proxies—unproven and fragile. The **cyber_defence_breakthrough** emerges: a new class of defensive AI achieves real-time patching and swarm detection, temporarily halting ransomware evolution and offering the first sign of recovery in years. Yet the damage from the prior attack lingers, and public trust remains frayed.

Despite the completion of the **Sovereign AI Compute Corridors**, no new compute becomes operational—delays in energy infrastructure and chip delivery persist. The **InvestAI Gigafactories**, **Telemetry Mandate**, and **AI Incident Authority** all finish, delivering resilience gains, but their tools are outdated against opaque models. **export_control_escalation** continues under the U.S. **CONSOLIDATION** posture, further restricting European access to frontier systems.

The EU launches the **European Red-Teaming Network**, a large category 1 measure aimed at evaluating uninterpretable models through adversarial stress tests. However, resistance from U.S. hyperscalers and legal pushback from member states over sovereignty concerns slow implementation. The measure is approved, but its effectiveness is uncertain, and no immediate safety gains materialize.

Meanwhile, **supply_chain_coercion** intensifies as Washington pressures the Netherlands to restrict ASML’s exports beyond cutting-edge machines to older lithography equipment, undermining European semiconductor supply. **member_state_defection** occurs as Hungary signs a bilateral AI cooperation deal with Beijing, bypassing EU oversight and weakening the Union’s unified stance.

The **emergent_cyber_alliance_resistance** deepens, with Italy and Greece refusing full participation in joint cyber defence efforts, demanding veto rights over shared threat responses. This fractures coordination and exposes structural weaknesses in EU-wide security integration.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 27.0, as visible progress is overshadowed by systemic fragility and dependency. Political capital declines to 13.0 due to ongoing commitments, resistance to integration, and the fallout from member state defection.
