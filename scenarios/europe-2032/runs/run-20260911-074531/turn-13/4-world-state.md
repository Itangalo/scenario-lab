### The attack that did not stop
Autumn brought the automated sweep defenders had rehearsed for. A ransomware wave built with machine-generated tooling moved across municipal registries, hospital rostering and a port community system in the same week. Services degraded but did not stop: emergency departments went to paper, a container terminal queued for days, benefits logs were restored from backups. Triage teams led from the Union's cyber agency were visible in wards and town halls. People were grateful they were there, and angry they were needed.

### Reasoning goes dark
At the frontier, the leading labs announced a performance leap that came at a price: their most capable systems no longer reason in readable steps. The internal representations that work best were never words, developers said. Oversight teams that had relied on reading chains of thought found their instruments blind overnight. University evaluators split over leaked scores; civil protection officers simply noted that inspection now meant black-box testing and slow activation work no one had staffed for.

### No hiring, no ward
Graduate hiring did not return. Law firms, accountants, software houses and customer operations confirmed what young workers already knew: entry posts were gone and not reposted. Transition placements held for those who had them, dwarfed by the freeze.

In oncology wards the contrast sharpened. American cell therapies entered routine care across the Atlantic while European doctors could only offer enrolment through a foreign cloud that took patient data abroad. Ministers named the loss of agency plainly.

Then came the lithography order. Washington used its technology jurisdiction to force a further Dutch cut to servicing and exports of chip-making equipment — beyond leading-edge machines to older tools and a wider customer list. The Union's one chokepoint had been used, and not by the Union. Stockpiling accelerated; insurers repriced again. Fenced sites held through a tense winter, guards at gates, drills rehearsed, essential systems bent and running.
