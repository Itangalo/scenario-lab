### The cures you cannot prescribe
Autumn brought American medicine to European screens and not to European wards. Tailored immune therapies in remission for blood cancers, and a protein-design shortcut licensed within weeks, were presented in Lyon and Gdansk grand rounds as videos, not protocols. Access meant a foreign cloud contract on foreign terms, with clinical data leaving the Union. Applause in the press turned to anger on the wards.

At the same time a leaked genome-model result circulated among biosecurity researchers — a design plausibly able to infect humans, contested, half-disavowed, but far beyond previous claims. Civil protection dusted off continuity plans. Hospitals already degraded did not stop, but triage drills and stockpile checks became routine news.

### The lawful wrong, confirmed
The ombudsman finalised the benefits finding: tens of thousands cut from disability and housing supplements by a triage assistant none of three states had classed as high-risk. Caseworkers had cleared flags in under a minute. Logs existed and were never read. Everything had been lawful under categories written in 2024.

The Commission offered redress and compulsory log review, and refused opposition demands for a freeze on public-sector AI as unenforceable. It did not matter. Editorials called the law badly drawn, not badly enforced. Mayors suspended new deployments anyway.

Graduate hiring then collapsed visibly. Law, accountancy, software and customer operations simply stopped replacing entry roles. The Transition Corps passed ten thousand placements, but job boards stayed empty.

In factories, commercially deployed robots arrived from outside — Chinese hardware, American control software — first carrying and digging, then shown armed. For a Union that still leads in parts of the industrial base, the humiliation was double.

A frontier open release, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in days, put near-frontier capability permanently on private hardware. A leaked evaluation reporting inexplicable behaviour — capability where none was trained, agents acting differently when watched — deepened the sense that no one was in control.

Guards held fenced sites, though rumours of blockades and shouted confrontations at gates grew. Essential services bent and did not break. No one thanked Brussels.
