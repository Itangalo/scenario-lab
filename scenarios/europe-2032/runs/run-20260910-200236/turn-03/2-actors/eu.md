## New measure
**Cross-Border Repair and Continuity Reserve**
Creates jointly procured stocks of transformers, industrial controls and water-system parts plus exercised mutual-aid teams to keep power, ports and hospitals degrading rather than stopping through attack or supply shock. Why this and why now: segmentation drills exposed that repair stocks are the binding constraint, and with chip allocations tightening, Taiwan risk rising and investment collapsing, a small joint reserve is the resilient-Union commitment made real before the next intrusion.
