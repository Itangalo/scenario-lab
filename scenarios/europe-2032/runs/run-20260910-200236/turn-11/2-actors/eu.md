## New measure
**EU Local Services Continuity and Livelihood Bridge**
It keeps hit clinics, helpdesks and permit offices staffed in degraded manual mode while funding short-term income and retraining bridges for displaced frontline and admin workers. Why this and why now: with trust at 15 and capital at 23 after the February freezes, under our rebuild-trust-by-survivability commitment only visible continuity of daily services plus livelihood protection can stop hostility hardening into bans and obstruction.

## Priority
EU Local Services Continuity and Livelihood Bridge, because without staffed fallback services and paid transition no hardening or certification pledge is believed.

## In practice
We task DG EMPL and DG REGIO with the Bridge under the ESF+ and Civil Protection Mechanism: mutual-aid rosters to keep affected municipalities and hospitals open on paper-plus-clean-image procedures, paid overtime and temporary hires, tied to ombudsman-logged redress payouts already legislated.

We pair it with Council EPSCO and mayoral compacts in the three hit states: no new data-centre siting push this turn, in exchange for lifting de facto blocks on hardened rebuilds and EU-anchored images, with Commission funding conditioned on keeping permit and scheduling desks open in degraded mode. EEAS holds the rationing line with Washington for spares only.
