## Priority
EU Community-Anchored Compute Compact, because without permits no gigafactory pledge converts to sovereign capacity and the US queue plus open-weight diffusion leave us rule-taker twice over.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT and DG ENERG under the Competitiveness Council with a single permit-compact regulation: accelerated siting and grid priority only where operators sign community-benefit contracts — local power-price relief, waste-heat use, apprenticeships and hiring floors for displaced admin/customer-ops workers — funded from InvestAI envelopes and EIB risk cover, not new money. Mayors in FR-DE-NL get veto-proof revenue and ENISA-hardened resilience upgrades for hospitals and municipal systems in return for sites.

We pair this with DG DIGIT procurement to run the proven waiting-list and education AI workloads preferentially on EU-anchored capacity, making the benefit visibly European, while EEAS keeps the observer-track talks on US-CN weights security alive on existing staffing without a new fund. No parallel compute programme: this revives M1/M2 by removing their permit block.
