## New measure
**EU Cyber Restoration and Safe-Systems Adoption Surge**
It funds emergency restoration of hit public services with hardened rebuilds while rolling the new interpretability/control breakthrough into EU-run and critical-sector AI deployments via audited patches. Why this and why now: the automated attack has made defenders-visibly-behind the lived reality and trust is at 18, so under our rebuild-trust-by-survivability commitment we must show services degrade-not-stop and that safety gains land on shipped systems immediately.

## Priority
EU Cyber Restoration and Safe-Systems Adoption Surge, because the major incident outranks concrete and licensing fights — without visible restoration no other pledge is fundable.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT and ENISA with the surge under the Civil Protection Mechanism and Digital Europe: joint triage teams to affected municipalities and hospitals, clean rebuilds from EU-anchored images, and cross-border mutual aid for continuity, paid from the Continuity Reserve and hospital-hardening line.

We pair it with DG DIGIT and the AI Office adopting the interpretability/control result on clinic, school and benefits assistants running on EU servers — mandatory pre-observation prediction checks and certified properties, published failures — while keeping the Accountable Deployment redress and log-reading duties enforced with ombudsmen. EEAS holds the Washington observer track for spare parts and model access, accepting rationing rather than rupture while bans block new sites through 2031.
