## New measure
**EU Human Front-Door and Trusted Stewards**
It puts a funded human alternative and visible local stewards in every certified clinic, benefits and permit desk using AI assistants, so no one is forced through an automated decision alone. Why this and why now: with sentiment at 17 and services technically stabilised but politically rejected as paid endurance, only a guaranteed human front-door under our rebuild-trust commitment can stop hostility hardening into bans.

## Priority
EU Local Services Continuity and Livelihood Bridge, because keeping desks open in degraded mode this turn is what the new Front-Door stewards need to inherit — without staffed continuity there is no human alternative to guarantee.

## In practice
We keep DG EMPL/REGIO running the Bridge through ESF+ and the Civil Protection Mechanism: overtime, temp hires and mutual-aid rosters in the three hit states, conditioned on open desks and clean-image rebuilds, with the mayoral compact holding — no forced data-centre push in exchange for unblocking hardened rebuilds.

We start the Front-Door work under the same EPSCO and health Council track using the Accountable Deployment redress and ombudsman logs: map where certified assistants are live, fund steward posts from Bridge successor lines, and legislate the right to human handling with published wait-times. EEAS holds to spares-only from Washington while we staff what we have.
