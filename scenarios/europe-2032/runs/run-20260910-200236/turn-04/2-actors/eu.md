## New measure
**EU Workplace Transition and Income Bridge**
Provides EU-coordinated wage insurance top-ups, rapid retraining vouchers tied to automating employers, and placement support for law, accountancy, admin and consulting workers, to keep the productivity plateau from turning into a cohesion shock. Why this and why now: the office evidence shows gains without layoffs yet no further transition to borrow against, so with resilience bills landing before any dividend and mayors already protesting compute costs, a small social cushion is the resilient-Union commitment made real for workers.

## Priority
EU Workplace Transition and Income Bridge, because it directly serves the two-year resilient-Union commitment on social cohesion while the gigafactory lines are stalled in state-aid and chip queues.

## In practice
We stand up the Bridge through DG EMPL with the European Social Fund+ reprogrammed margins and EPSCO endorsement, using existing national short-time and retraining schemes as delivery agents with EU top-up and portability rules — no new fund call this turn to protect thin political capital. Employers deploying office AI above a threshold co-finance vouchers via sectoral agreements, which we sell to unions as security and to business as flexibility without new employment-protection reform.

M1 InvestAI Gigafactories and M2 Tech sovereignty package stay in holding pattern: DG COMP stages state-aid clearance for renegotiated-down bids, DG TRADE manages Washington monthly allocations, and we use French-German-Dutch permit-linked co-finance to keep repair depots stocked rather than open new build spending. We keep the provenance soft-law endorsement via AI Office and DG RTD without a new instrument, to avoid spending capital we need for the Bridge.
