# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign technical agency through auditable AI, now operationalised as the exclusive use of auditable systems in all EU-critical functions and the redirection of all sovereign compute investment toward open, verifiable architectures

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign technical agency through auditable AI, now operationalised as the exclusive use of auditable systems in all EU-critical functions and the redirection of all sovereign compute investment toward open, verifiable architectures
  - Trigger: the **ai_investment_collapse** and **safety_breakthrough** events this turn
  - Grounds: The **ai_investment_collapse** fundamentally alters the strategic landscape: the frontier’s expansion is no longer inevitable, and for the first time, the cost of training frontier models is contracting. This creates a narrowing window—perhaps the only one we will get—to shift from reactive resilience to structural sovereignty. Simultaneously, the **safety_breakthrough** proves that interpretability can scale to deployed systems, offering a path to assurance that does not depend on foreign goodwill. These two developments together mean we are no longer choosing between capability and control; we can now build sovereign control *into* the next generation of capability. Our commitment evolves accordingly: not merely to secure auditable AI as a fallback, but to make it the core of our sovereign stack—by design, by default, and by law.
  - Evidence quoted: **ai_investment_collapse:** Capital flees the sector. Valuations reset hard, announced build-out is cancelled rather than delayed, and several of the arrangements European compute was depending on evaporate with it. What the frontier laboratories can afford to train shrinks for the first time.

**safety_breakthrough:** An interpretability or control result measurably improves assurance on systems already deployed, rather than on toy models – behaviour that can be predicted before it is observed, or a property that can be certified rather than argued for. It is adopted quickly, because the laboratories want it too.
  - Reason: The investment collapse reduces external AI capacity, altering cost and access; the safety breakthrough enables trustworthy domestic systems, changing the feasibility of sovereign auditable AI.
