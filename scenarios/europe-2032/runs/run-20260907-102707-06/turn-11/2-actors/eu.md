## In practice
This turn, we are treating the collapse in private investment not as a crisis, but as a procurement event. The Commission is invoking Article 122a TFEU to fast-track a new legal instrument: the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation (ESCM), to be funded through a combination of REPowerEU carryover funds (€18 billion) and emergency borrowing authorised under the European Stability Mechanism. The instrument will establish three sovereign AI clusters—hosted in Finland, France, and Greece—each anchored by a publicly owned data centre with legally binding access guarantees for EU institutions and auditable system operators. These will be powered by stranded H100s and spare fabrication capacity now available due to the sector-wide retrenchment, acquired through a new EU Acquisition Facility for Strategic Compute.

We are negotiating with member states to waive permitting for these sites in exchange for binding commitments to energy redundancy and public oversight boards, modelled on CERN’s governance. The European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU) is being re-tasked to manage procurement and integration, with a new mandate to prioritise open-architecture systems compatible with the interpretability standards validated by the safety_breakthrough. Resistance is expected from member states with existing private hyperscaler agreements, but we are offering preferential access to the ESCM’s compute pool in return for cooperation.

The Open Science Resilience Initiative remains in flight, with integration now reaching full operational tempo: the three research hubs in Tübingen, Grenoble, and Pisa are conducting live trials with national biosecurity agencies, using auditable models to simulate pandemic response under real-time threat feeds from ENISA. The first formal output—a transparent agent framework for vaccine distribution under disruption—will be delivered by turn 12, just as the ESCM begins physical build-out. This convergence of trustable models and sovereign infrastructure is the pivot: from surviving exclusion to preventing it.
