This is not dissent; it is delegitimisation. At `public_sentiment` 22.0 and `eu_political_capital` 2.0, the Union is not merely weak — it is losing its claim to decide. Our identity must reflect that we are no longer balancing two mandates, but fighting to *reconstitute the very condition of agency*: a polity that can still bind its future.

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer treat uncertainty as a reason to delay *institutional adaptation*. **We now treat the absence of control not as a technical deficit, but as a political emergency: where institutions cannot verify, they must withdraw.**
  - Grounds: The **emergent_open_agent_framework** proves that auditable, open systems can gain rapid adoption in critical functions — not because they are the most capable, but because they are *legible*. Meanwhile, the frontier at 97.0 operates beyond human verification, and even the US-China agreement excludes us from its thin safeguards. Our commitment evolves: we will now *prohibit* the use of any AI in EU-critical functions that cannot be independently audited in real time. This is not a delay; it is a triage. We cannot govern what we cannot see. When the frontier moves beyond inspection, we do not follow — we *exclude*.

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign technical agency through auditable AI, now operationalised as the exclusive use of auditable systems in all EU-critical functions and the redirection of all sovereign compute investment toward open, verifiable architectures — **with immediate effect, no exceptions, and legal enforcement from turn 13; any system not demonstrably auditable by ENISA standards is now presumed non-compliant and subject to decommissioning.**
  - Grounds: The **emergent_open_agent_framework** provides the technical basis for a politically viable alternative to frontier models. Its rapid uptake in healthcare and energy proves that *functionality under scrutiny* is preferred over *capability in obscurity*. We must now codify this as the EU’s default. The **Critical Systems Auditing Directive** is not enough — it treats auditing as a compliance hurdle, not a constitutional principle. We are elevating our commitment: from now on, *unauditable systems are incompatible with EU sovereignty*. This is not a reversal — it is an enforcement of what we already staked ourselves on. The cost is high: we will lose access to the most capable tools, but we gain something rarer — the ability to act as a Union.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
