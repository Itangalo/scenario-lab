## Priority  
**Secure Semiconductor Access Initiative** — because without guaranteed access to advanced chips, every other investment — from gigafactories to resilience — risks collapsing into symbolic infrastructure, and the blockade has made sovereignty a material question of supply lines, not just strategy.

## In practice  
We are treating the semiconductor blockade as an existential disruption. This turn, the Commission activates the Anti-Coercion Instrument in targeted form, not to escalate but to signal that the EU will not passively accept chokepoint abuse. We are convening an emergency summit of middle powers—Netherlands, Japan, South Korea, and Taiwan’s non-state representatives—under the framework of the Global Supply Chain Resilience Forum, to coordinate export diversions, foundry access swaps, and shared stockpiling of EU-controlled upstream components like ASML subsystems and specialty gases. The offer is simple: pool what we can control, prioritise civilian AI infrastructure essential to public services and critical defence-adjacent functions, and use collective weight to resist unilateral capture by either superpower bloc.

At the same time, we are instructing the European External Action Service to open backchannel talks with both Washington and Beijing, offering mutual recognition of essential civilian exemptions in exchange for non-targeting assurances. Our leverage lies in what we hold—not just physical components, but legal jurisdiction over maintenance, software, and calibration for much of the world’s advanced lithography equipment. We are also redirecting 15% of the InvestAI Gigafactories’ reserved capacity to near-term assembly and testing of repurposed chips, working with STMicroelectronics and Infineon to fast-track hybrid solutions that can run capable models at lower throughput. Resistance is expected from member states dependent on US security guarantees, so we are pairing this with a public message: sovereignty means having choices, not just allies. If we do not act now to secure material continuity, every euro spent on gigafactories and resilience becomes a down payment on irrelevance.
