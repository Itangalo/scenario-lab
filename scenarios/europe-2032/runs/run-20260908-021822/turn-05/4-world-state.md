### A Threshold Crossed

Global AI capability surpasses the 60.0 threshold, driven by autonomous code generation and self-improving cyber tools now operating across multiple domains. The jump reflects not just speed but qualitative shifts: systems complete multi-day software projects end to end, with minimal human oversight. Open-weight models show growing utility, advancing steadily as open-source communities replicate key functionalities, though they remain behind the closed frontier.

### EU Completes Cyber Defence Directive — With Gaps

The Cyber Defence Integration Directive formally completes its rollout. Energy and transport sectors report improved detection and patching automation, but healthcare lags significantly due to unresolved liability frameworks and workforce shortages. ENISA’s upcoming review will highlight inconsistent implementation, particularly in cross-border coordination.

### Sovereign Buildout Continues Amid Rising Dependence

The InvestAI Gigafactories programme advances, with four sites now under development. However, French resistance to civilian compute repurposing intensifies, and concerns grow over reliance on U.S.-controlled tooling. The Tech sovereignty package clears legislative hurdles, unlocking permitting for new data centres, but private investment remains below target at €183bn.

### New Certification Regime Announced

The EU announces the Critical Systems Resilience Certification, mandating auditable reasoning and fail-operational protocols for AI in essential services. Backed by DORA enforcement, it signals a shift from compliance to control. Formal implementation will begin next turn; no pilot integrations have launched yet, as design and auditor accreditation are still underway.

### U.S. Election Outcome: Consolidation Expected

The United States elects a president who treats advanced AI as a strategic asset. The policy direction suggests future tightening of export controls and tiered access for foreign partners subject to national security review. For the EU, this portends a hardening of dependence.
