## In practice
This turn, we are launching the most consequential industrial and technological mobilisation in the EU’s post-war history. Under Article 172 TFEU and the revised European Defence of the Union clause, we are convening an emergency Council formation of Competitiveness, Research, and Interior Ministers to approve the Sovereign AI Stack: a unified programme to deliver exaflop-scale compute on EU soil by Q4 2032, staffed by a protected cohort of frontier AI researchers and operating a public EU frontier laboratory with full model ownership and deployment authority. The stack will be legally anchored through a new Sovereign AI Act, which we are drafting in parallel with the Commission’s Legal Service and the European Parliament’s JURI and IMCO committees. This act will override national permitting for compute sites, mandate grid priority for sovereign AI facilities, and establish a classified talent protection regime—visa fast-tracks, security clearances, and anti-poaching clauses for EU-funded researchers.

We are reallocating €18.7 billion from the Digital Europe Programme, Horizon Europe, and the Crisis and Investment Instrument to fund the first phase, with co-financing required from member states hosting sites. The first three locations—Grenoble, Pilsen, and Oulu—are already pre-cleared for accelerated siting. We are negotiating with ASML, Zeiss, and Intel Ireland to secure near-term access to advanced lithography and packaging capacity, leveraging the Chips Act’s coordination powers and export control authority. Simultaneously, we are activating the European Research Council’s emergency talent clause to recruit 200 frontier AI specialists with binding contracts under EU law, offering salaries competitive with private labs and housing guarantees. Resistance is expected from member states benefiting from bilateral US hyperscaler deals and from civil liberties groups wary of classified AI development—but we are framing this not as a security programme, but as a constitutional necessity: no state can be free if its cognitive infrastructure answers to another. This is not governance. This is nation-building in real time.
