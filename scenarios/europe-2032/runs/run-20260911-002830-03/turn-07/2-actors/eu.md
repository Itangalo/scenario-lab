## New measure
**EU Municipal Automation Transition and Trusted Deployment Compact**
Deploys EU-controlled automation in municipal services paired with wage insurance, retraining and local hiring guarantees for displaced entry-level workers. Category 7 labour and social protection measure — why this and why now: labour displacement plus robot catalogues plus boil-notice resentment is collapsing sentiment to 18 and capital to 16, and without a visible jobs bridge neither Gigafactories nor the US access pact survive politically through RSI and opaque reasoning.

## Priority
EU Municipal Automation Transition and Trusted Deployment Compact — because with sentiment at 18 nothing else lands if automation reads as foreign robots replacing municipal and graduate jobs.

## In practice
We run the Compact first through DG EMPL and the Committee of Regions with ESF+ and EIB cover: mayors co-sign deployment sites where EU-procured machines do clearance and utility work under EU data control, while displaced clerical, customer-ops and municipal workers get wage bridges and fast retraining into supervision, maintenance and care roles. Works councils and public employers get flexibility to restructure only with transition funding attached.

On M7 we keep EEAS and DG CNECT/TRADE in the Trade and Technology Council pushing published access tiers, researcher access and joint evaluation, explicitly conditioned on keeping the two Gigafactory sites on grid priority so access does not become dependence. The Shield and Bio-Shield routines — sequencing, stockpiles, manual-fallback drills — continue as operating procedure in large hospitals and grids while the Compact carries them into small towns.
