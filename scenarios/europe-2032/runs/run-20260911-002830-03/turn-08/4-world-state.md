### The invitation
The offer arrived as inboxes filled with incident tickets. After months of unreadable agent runs and a disputed genome preprint, a group of states hit by the same wave proposed to pool telemetry and samples: a joint cyber command with live feeds, and a biosurveillance pact with binding sample-sharing and a standing investigation team. Brussels was invited in.

The Council said yes fast. Hospitals, grid operators and public-health labs were told to connect sequencing and security feeds under European data controls, with onboarding paid from Brussels after small states balked at the bill. Engineers called it the first protection that arrives this year rather than in three.

### Two closings, one opening
The same semester closed two long files. Concrete rose on the two factory sites kept on grid priority, and the wage-bridge and manual-drill routines became normal practice in large hospitals and grids. The Washington access talks also produced a text: tiers, joint evaluation and incident reporting on paper, interim researcher access continuing under American rules in practice.

To hold the line on jobs, the municipal machines compact moved to pilots. Councils welcomed EU-paid wage insurance and retraining; unions asked whether machines or bridges would arrive first. Triage assistants under local control cut waiting lists in two cities, and a European materials find promised cheaper grid parts — real but thin against layoff tables in clerical, customer and junior professional roles.

### Friction
Connection was not smooth. Legal officers fought over what telemetry could leave the Union, small utilities said they had no staff to feed a live command, and one more reinsurer quit small-utility cover. Mayors facing pickets around foreign-built clearance machines got preparatory funding, not fleets. Interim access kept flowing, with reciprocity still to be negotiated inside the new joint setup.

By June, Brussels had bought shared detection it could not build alone, at the cost of stretching operators who must now feed two masters: local service and a shared watch floor.
