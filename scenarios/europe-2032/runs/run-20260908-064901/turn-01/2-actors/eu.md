## In practice
We are activating Article 22 of the NIS2 Directive with immediate effect, mandating all operators of essential services — energy, water, transport, health — to implement AI-driven intrusion detection systems audited by ENISA, with red-teaming exercises conducted quarterly. The Commission will fast-track a delegated act under the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) to close the gap in financial-sector preparedness, while pushing Council Regulation through COREPER II with support from the Spanish and Polish presidencies.  

The European Cyber Resilience Board, convened for the first time since 2024, will coordinate cross-border response protocols, with Frontex, eu-LISA, and national CERTs integrated into a unified simulation cycle starting Q1 2027. Resistance from smaller member states over cost is being offset by redirecting unused MFF digital-transition funds, while Germany and France agree to co-finance regional hubs in exchange for voting weight in the Board’s design. This is not just compliance — it is the beginning of a credible, EU-wide immune system for the AI era.
