### Frontier Leap and Open Diffusion  
A major breakthrough in autonomous system design propelled `ai_capability` by +4.0 this turn to 63.5, entering the regime of continuous multi-day task execution. The jump, attributed to recursive self-improvement loops in training pipelines, triggered a `capability_jump` event. Open-weight models showed accelerated progress, benefiting from leaked training data fragments and analysis of Kimi K3 variants, but development bottlenecks limited gains. `openweight_capability` rose to 48.0—still trailing the current frontier and reflecting delayed access to full architectures and compute.

### Cyber Shock and Resilience Tested  
A coordinated AI-generated ransomware campaign crippled regional health administrations across three member states. The attack exploited zero-day vulnerabilities in legacy systems, overwhelming incident response. Though no lives were lost, public services were disrupted for weeks. Resilience held partially—fallback protocols limited cascading failure—but `resilience` rose only to 46.0, reflecting uneven preparedness.

### Audit Initiative Launched Amid Skepticism  
The EU launched the Public-sector AI Safety Audit Programme in response to the benefits scandal. Designed to restore trust, it mandates independent audits for high-risk public AI. However, legal pushback emerged from member states citing sovereignty concerns. A phased rollout was agreed, but initial funding was delayed by €300 million due to cohesion fund negotiations. Implementation will take multiple turns, with first assessments not expected before mid-2029.

### Trust Erodes Further  
The cyber incident and ongoing evaluation crisis deepened public distrust. Media coverage focused on AI’s role in both the attack and the unemployment office failures. `public_sentiment` fell to 34.0. The Audit Programme, though symbolically significant, failed to immediately counter the narrative of uncontrolled risk.

### Sovereignty and Capital Under Pressure  
`eu_ai_sovereignty` declined to 18.0 as global capability growth outpaced domestic compute builds. `eu_political_capital` dropped to 24.0, burdened by the new large measure (−3), the priority (−1), and the cyber incident’s political fallout (−5). The completion of Cyber Shield provided no boost—implementation lagged, and its authority remained untested.  

`ai_safety` declined moderately to 25.0, reflecting growing doubts about benchmark integrity following the emergent_eval_crisis and increased reliance on unverified third-party evaluations. While no systemic collapse occurred, confidence in existing safety assessment frameworks eroded, contributing to cautious deployment policies and reduced perceived safety across sectors.
