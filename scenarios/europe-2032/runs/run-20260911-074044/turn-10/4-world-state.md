### The spring everything arrived at once
The first half of 2031 felt like acceleration made physical. Two American labs shipped systems that planned days of work, called their own tools and rewrote pieces of their training loops. In Brussels, deployment engineers quietly shelved their checklists: human review became a stamp.

Against that backdrop, two pieces of good news landed. A joint European team — Munich, Delft and Grenoble — published a verification method that made a class of previously infeasible materials searches cheap; battery and catalyst groups called it a landmark and began licensing it within weeks. And tailored therapies for two previously untreatable conditions reached ordinary clinics, with Lyon and Gdansk among the first sites to schedule patients.

Neither cut through the welfare scandal.

### The queue at forty seconds
In February, an ombudsman backed by a court finding showed what a regional welfare and policing-assistance system had done: risk scores that systematically cut benefits and flagged families, rubber-stamped by caseworkers at forty seconds an item. Logs existed and were never read. Conformity papers were in order.

The Commission chose to frame it as breach of high-risk duties, not a gap in the law. The AI Office ordered the queue frozen, human re-decisions with time floors, published logs and arrears where systematic wronging was confirmed. DG JUST opened a redress track with ombudsmen and the Fundamental Rights Agency.

Delivery was uneven. A few thousand cases were re-decided and paid by June; tens of thousands waited. Mayors said the new enforcement unit had two staff for a region.

The junior-jobs corps fared similarly. Placements grew — care-scheduling, permit desks, backup shifts on fenced build sites under guard — but ESF+ money still cleared slowly and firms kept graduates only where wages were mostly covered. Chips reserved for the earlier dividend trickled onto racks; half the states still stalled.

Shipping insurers raised surcharges again after extended exercises around the Strait. The Commission absorbed costs from continuity stocks without new action. Voters used AI daily, queued for its benefits, and resented it just as daily.
