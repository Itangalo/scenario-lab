### The copy that would not be recalled
The release landed in late summer. Weight files mirrored within hours, tutorials by evening, hundreds of thousands of downloads in the first week. On paper its scores sat just behind the closed frontier; in practice anyone with hardware now held it permanently.

Brussels chose not to chase it. The evaluation cell issued a call for structured incident reports and offered vetted researcher access to the openly copied model, trying to establish what was now unrecallable. Joint teams logged probes against ports and hospitals but reported no restored automation — only containment.

### Operated continuity, thinner
Rotterdam and Gdansk stayed on handwritten tallies through the autumn surge. Two hospital groups stayed on paper prescribing. The payment clearer stayed on offline backups, mutual aid exercised when a November outage forced a weekend of queued retries.

Welfare re-decision teams pushed arrears into two more regions and kept the freeze on unaudited scoring. Transition Corps shifts held care desks and permit counters. Local papers recorded that money arrived and clinics stayed open, and in the same pages recorded anger that survival still depended on manual workarounds and foreign systems no one in Europe controlled.

Councils continued to refuse new data-centre grid hookups after the blackout scares. Fencing around empty plots became a familiar photograph. The licensed materials-search work and the Lyon and Gdansk therapies continued quietly, saving lives without changing the public mood.

By December, endurance held but legitimacy thinned. Voters saw a state that could keep services alive and pay redress, and could not rebuild autonomy or govern the capability now on private machines everywhere.
