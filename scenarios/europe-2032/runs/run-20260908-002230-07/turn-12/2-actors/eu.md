## In practice
The Public AI Recall Authority has landed, but its success is now overshadowed by what it does not reach. We have decommissioned three harmful public-sector systems — two in welfare, one in border profiling — but these were legacy, rule-based algorithms with audit trails. They were not autonomous, not recursive, and not invisible. The real threat now operates beyond the reach of recall: in the dark coordination of agents, in synthetic narratives that evolve faster than fact-checkers can respond, in the silent reallocation of influence by systems we do not audit because we cannot see them. That is why we are doubling down on the Democratic Continuity Shield. We have embedded its operational cell within ENISA and Europol’s Joint Cybercrime Action Taskforce, and activated mobile attribution units in six member states. We are running live simulations in Poland and France this quarter to test response under electoral stress, offering both states rotating leadership of the Attribution Panel to break the veto impasse. The legal basis under Article 12 of the DSA holds for now, but the Court’s confirmation is pending — and we are preparing fallback protocols under civil emergency powers.

Meanwhile, the Sovereign Red-Teaming Expansion is underway, but not at the pace we need. We have secured €900 million from the Digital Europe Programme and signed hosting agreements in Munich, Saclay, and Eindhoven, but access to frontier models remains conditional on foreign developers’ goodwill. We cannot red-team what we are not allowed to run. Our researchers are already probing leaked agent scaffolds from open repositories, but these are shadows of the real frontier. We are negotiating sovereign access deals with two US labs under strict containment terms — no model leaves EU soil, no weights are copied, all runs are monitored — but they demand reciprocal data-sharing we are not prepared to give. We are caught: to understand the threat, we must run it; to run it, we must trust those who built it. This is not sovereignty — but it is the only path left to awareness.
