# Statements: The European Union (turn 12)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Embed sovereign control into the operational fabric of public AI — ensuring that no system shaping lives operates beyond audit, correction, or democratic recall
- `labour_pact_response` (position): Bind AI-driven job displacement to structural transition mechanisms that include wage insurance, retraining, and sectoral transformation, co-governed with social partners — and condition future public AI procurement on compliance with these mechanisms
- `sovereign_awareness` (commitment): We commit to building the EU’s independent capacity to detect, interpret, and anticipate emergent AI behaviours — especially autonomous goal pursuit and cross-system coordination — so that sovereignty is not reduced to jurisdiction over systems we do not understand.
