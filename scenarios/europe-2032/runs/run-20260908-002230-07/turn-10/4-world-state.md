The EU responds by launching the **Democratic Continuity Shield**, a large category 6 measure under the Crisis Coordination Mechanism. Legal basis is fast-tracked under Article 12 of the Digital Services Act, though France and Poland resist centralised enforcement, demanding co-governance roles.
