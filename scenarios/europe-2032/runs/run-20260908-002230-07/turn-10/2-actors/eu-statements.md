## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Embed sovereign control into the operational fabric of public AI — ensuring that no system shaping lives operates beyond audit, correction, or democratic recall
  - Trigger: the automated_decision_scandal and the election_annulled event
  - Grounds: The annulment of a democratic election, explicitly tied to AI-manipulated information environments, marks a categorical threshold: public AI is no longer a tool of efficiency but a vector of political subversion. Combined with the Italian and French algorithmic scandals, this confirms that control cannot be reactive or symbolic. Our commitment must now evolve from securing foundational control to actively defending democratic continuity — not just in systems that allocate benefits or jobs, but in those that shape belief, participation, and legitimacy. The tools exist; the question is whether we have the capital to wield them before the next election cycle begins.
  - Evidence quoted: An election in an established democracy is postponed, rerun or annulled with explicit reference to manipulation of the information environment. Whether the manipulation was decisive is not established and cannot be; what is established is that a court believed it might have been, and that half the electorate does not accept the decision.
  - Reason: The annulled election directly challenges the EU's claim of foundational AI control by showing AI's role in subverting democratic legitimacy, a core interest behind the statement.
