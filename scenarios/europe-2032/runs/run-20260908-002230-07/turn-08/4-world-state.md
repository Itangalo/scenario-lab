### The Scandal Deepens, the Response Begins

The revelation that public-sector AI systems systematically denied benefits and employment access to vulnerable citizens triggers a political firestorm. Media investigations uncover similar patterns in policing risk assessments and judicial bail recommendations, confirming that algorithmic harm is not isolated but structural. Public trust plummets further, with protests spreading to new cities and acts of sabotage against data infrastructure intensifying. The EU-wide backlash forces a reckoning: oversight can no longer be deferred.

The European Commission responds by fast-tracking the **EU-Wide AI Audit Regime**, invoking internal market powers to override resistance from member states, including France. The directive mandates third-party audits for all high-risk public AI systems, with penalties for non-compliance. Early-adopter support is offered, but legal challenges loom as national sovereignty concerns flare. The measure is widely seen as necessary but arrives amid deep public cynicism.

Simultaneously, the **Sovereign Red-Teaming Network** completes its final phase, delivering tools for probing frontier models. However, integration into broader defence systems is patchy, and no sovereign simulation environment yet exists. The proposed *Cognitive Sovereignty Initiative* remains in briefing stages, dependent on political capital that is evaporating.

Global AI capability surges by +6.0 to 82.0, driven by recursive self-improvement cycles now confirmed in multiple labs. Open-weight models reach 71.875, nearing autonomous cyber operation thresholds. No safety breakthroughs occur; instead, the gap between capability and control widens. The **automated_decision_scandal** — an internal-origin event — triggers a direct hit to legitimacy.

Resilience erodes slightly as institutions struggle to absorb the crisis. Labour exclusion patterns persist, feeding public anger. EU sovereignty declines as the **InvestAI Gigafactories** yield less than projected, and no new compute comes online. Political capital dips despite the Audit Regime’s proposal bonus, overwhelmed by portfolio strain and scandal fallout.
