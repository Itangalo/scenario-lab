## Priority  
**Hardened Critical Infrastructure Directive** — because the cyber_major_incident has made clear that detection is no longer a technical gap but a systemic vulnerability, and without mandated upgrades and red-teaming, even the best defensive tools will not be adopted uniformly or in time.

## In practice  
We are moving fast to convert the political shock of the cyber_major_incident into binding action. The Hardened Critical Infrastructure Directive is now under urgent negotiation in the Council’s Competitiveness formation, with the Commission proposing a qualified majority path under Article 114 TFEU. Germany, France, and the Nordic states back the core mandate: AI-powered intrusion detection systems trained on adversarial behaviours, and quarterly red-teaming using permitted offensive models under strict audit. But resistance remains — Hungary and Slovakia still reject unfunded mandates, and Italy warns of implementation delays in its regional utilities. To break the deadlock, we are offering targeted grants from the InvestAI Gigafactories fund for joint modernisation, tying compliance to industrial co-investment. The legal text will include phased thresholds: large operators must comply by turn 5, smaller ones by turn 6, with technical support routed through the Digital Europe Programme.

Simultaneously, ENISA has begun drafting red-team protocols using frontier model access under AI Act research exemptions, and pilot exercises will launch in Q2 with French and Dutch grid operators. The Joint Research Centre is finalising a public threat assessment based on forensic analysis of the cyber_major_incident, expected by March, to anchor the case. This is not about perfection — it is about ensuring that when the next attack comes, someone sees it, and someone can stop it. The new Cyber Defence Acceleration Fund will close the investment gap: €12bn to co-finance defensive stacks, with 70% grants for high-risk sectors and conditionality tied to the Directive’s compliance timeline. This turns resilience from a directive into a delivered capability — and ensures that the EU does not wait for catastrophe to harden its spine.
