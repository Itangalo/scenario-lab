### A Breach That Changes Nothing

In early 2027, a coordinated AI-generated ransomware campaign cripples hospital networks in three member states and disrupts power grid monitoring systems across Central Europe. The attack exploits zero-day vulnerabilities at machine speed, evading signature-based defences. Attribution remains unclear, but forensic analysis confirms the tooling was model-synthesised — a milestone in offensive autonomy. The incident triggers emergency meetings, but not unity.

The EU responds with urgency. The Hardened Critical Infrastructure Directive, now a named priority, enters fast-track negotiations under Article 114. Germany and France push for binding AI detection mandates, while Hungary and Slovakia resist unfunded obligations, demanding compensation. Italy warns its regional utilities cannot comply by the proposed deadline. A compromise emerges: partial grants from the InvestAI Gigafactories fund will support compliance, linking industrial investment to security upgrades. The legal text allows phased implementation, but delays are expected.

Simultaneously, the Commission launches the €12bn Cyber Defence Acceleration Fund to co-finance defensive stacks. ENISA begins drafting red-team protocols using frontier models under research exemptions, and pilots are scheduled with French and Dutch operators. Yet rollout is uneven — many member states lack the technical capacity to absorb the funding quickly.

Despite these efforts, the political cost mounts. Public trust erodes as headlines highlight hospital outages and bureaucratic delays. A scandal emerges over an AI-assisted benefits system in a major member state found to have wrongly denied claims, amplifying distrust. Labour unions protest automation in logistics, citing job losses. Public sentiment dips.

Behind the scenes, offensive AI advances steadily. ai_capability rises to 57.0 as agent frameworks improve. With no new safety measures yet in force, ai_safety declines. Resilience sees a marginal gain from the new fund, but implementation lags. Sovereignty slips further as global capacity outpaces EU build-out. Political capital erodes under the weight of multiple large measures and internal friction.

### The Defence Gap Narrows — Just a Little

Amid the chaos, a breakthrough emerges: a European cybersecurity consortium demonstrates AI-driven detection that identifies swarm-style intrusions by behavioural anomaly, not signatures. The tool, developed independently, marks the first clear shift in the offence-defence balance in years — but it is not yet widely deployed.
