### A Surge in Capability, a Collapse in Trust

Global AI capability advances to 70.0, driven by self-improving systems whose reasoning remains opaque. With no safety measure yet embedded in frontier models, and the opaque_reasoning event still reverberating, ai_safety drops another 7 points to 13.0—deep in the danger zone where deployed systems are effectively black boxes even to their creators.

Openweight models rise to 57.5, approaching the threshold where autonomous cyber exploitation becomes routine. The ai_investment_collapse accelerates fragmentation: major labs scale back training runs, but leaked weights continue to diffuse. The EU’s access to external compute weakens as partnerships dissolve overnight.

### Labour Crisis Meets Public Backlash

The automated_decision_scandal erupts in France, where an AI-driven unemployment benefits system is found to have wrongfully sanctioned over 12,000 people. A national ombudsman confirms systemic bias, triggering outrage and reinforcing perceptions of AI as punitive. Public sentiment falls to 28.0.

Simultaneously, backlash_physical intensifies: data centre construction sites in Spain and Poland are occupied, and grid transformers serving new AI clusters are sabotaged. Policing costs mount, draining both budgets and legitimacy.

The EU responds by launching the European Labour Resilience Fund—a €45bn initiative for wage insurance and retraining, co-governed with unions. Positioned as essential to preserving social cohesion, it becomes the new priority. However, its proposal triggers immediate resistance in the Council, with several fiscally conservative states demanding strict conditionality and questioning legal grounds under Article 122 TFEU.

### Sovereignty Under Pressure

Despite progress on the emergent sovereignty pact, no domestic compute comes online this turn. With global capability rising sharply, eu_ai_sovereignty declines by another point to 16.0. Political capital, already strained, erodes further as five large measures remain in flight.

The US election outcome takes effect: a pro-alliance administration assumes office, improving long-term coordination prospects. But immediate dependence remains unchanged.
