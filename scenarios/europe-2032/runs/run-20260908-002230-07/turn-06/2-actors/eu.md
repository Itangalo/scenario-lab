## In practice  
We are in a moment of convergence: capability surges beyond control, capital flees the sector, and the public turns against the very infrastructure we depend on. The backlash_physical event is not marginal — grid connections have been sabotaged, data centres occupied. Policing it drains legitimacy we do not have. At the same time, the automated_decision_scandal has made AI adoption toxic in social policy, and the emergent Labour Resilience Pact is now coordinating with the Parliament’s social affairs committee to block all AI-related legislation unless transition funds are binding and cross-border. We cannot govern AI if we cannot govern its consequences.

So we act: not by retreating, but by redirecting. The European Labour Resilience Fund will be proposed under Article 122 TFEU, with €45bn drawn from the European Globalisation Adjustment Fund and repurposed cohesion reserves. It will offer wage insurance at 75% of previous income for up to two years, paired with retraining pathways co-designed by unions and sectoral employers. Disbursement requires proof of AI-driven displacement — verified by national labour authorities using audit trails from automated systems. We will negotiate the fund in the Employment, Social Policy, Health and Consumer Affairs Council, offering the Labour Pact seats on the governance board. This is not concession — it is recalibration. We are not slowing AI. We are anchoring its pace in social reality.

At the same time, we press the Sovereign Red-Teaming Network, now entering its critical phase. We are offering full liability shielding and joint publication rights to the two resisting labs, but we are also preparing Article 12 enforcement orders. ENISA will begin limited simulations this turn using openweight models and infrastructure logs, even if incomplete. The Threat Anticipation Facility’s architecture is being redesigned to minimise dependence on external models — we are exploring synthetic data generation and adversarial simulation using EU-held compute. But without political breathing room, none of it will land. The Labour Fund is not a distraction. It is the precondition for sovereignty.
