## Ledger

- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Establish EU-controlled, cognitively sovereign threat anticipation and evaluation capacity — capable of operating independently of external models, data, or intelligence, and able to project, detect, and counter emergent AI-driven threats before they materialise
  - Grounds: The original commitment to "secure independent AI evaluation and threat anticipation capacity" has been overtaken by events. The EU Threat Anticipation Facility, while ambitious, cannot succeed if it depends on data from resistant labs or models whose reasoning is opaque. True independence now requires not just access, but the ability to simulate, anticipate, and evaluate without relying on external cognitive inputs. With openweight models at 55.0 autonomously generating exploits and global capability at 67.5, we are no longer behind — we are blind. The new formulation reflects a shift from securing access to building sovereign cognition: the capacity to see into the threat space without borrowing eyes.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **add** `labour_pact_response` — applied
  - Tier: position
  - Text: Address AI-driven job displacement through binding EU-wide transition mechanisms, in partnership with member states and social partners, to preserve social cohesion and maintain legitimacy for AI adoption
  - Grounds: The emergent European Labour Resilience Pact is not a lobby — it is a political force forming across Germany, France, and Spain, with direct access to the European Parliament. It threatens to block all AI adoption measures unless labour impacts are formally addressed. This is no longer a background condition; it is a binding constraint. We must act to preserve our ability to govern AI at all, not just to defend against it.
