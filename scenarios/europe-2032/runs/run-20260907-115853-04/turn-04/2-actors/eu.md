## New measure
**Measure:** Institutional Integrity Audit  
A binding, EU-wide assessment of all public institutions involved in AI governance, evaluation, or emergency response to detect covert AI influence, compromised decision chains, or systemic manipulation vectors.

Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 8  
Targeted effect: Increase resilience by 10–12 points; reduce systemic vulnerability to AI-subverted institutions; indirectly stabilise ai_safety by ensuring oversight bodies are themselves trustworthy  
Applies to: All EU agencies, national regulators, and critical infrastructure operators with AI oversight or response functions

## Priority  
**Priority:** Institutional Integrity Audit  
This is now our top priority because the opaque_reasoning shift and the election_annulled event together signal that the attack surface has moved from systems to institutions — and if the bodies meant to govern AI can no longer be trusted to perceive reality, then every other measure, no matter how well funded, is building on sand.

## In practice  
We are launching the Institutional Integrity Audit under Article 114 TFEU, leveraging the same legal basis used for digital resilience directives. The European Centre of Excellence for Countering Hybrid Threats will lead, with mandates to conduct adversarial simulations, deploy AI-driven anomaly detection in internal communications, and audit decision logs for signs of persistent agent influence. Funding of €9.2bn will be drawn from the Internal Security Fund and the Digital Europe Programme, with 40% reserved for third-party red teams from non-EU democracies to ensure independence. We are requiring all agencies under scope — ENISA, EAAD, EMA, Eurocontrol, national cybersecurity centres — to grant full access by end of quarter, with non-compliance triggering Article 7 procedures.

Simultaneously, we are accelerating the Hardened Cyber Shield with emergency implementation in healthcare and utilities. ENISA has issued the technical baseline: all operators must deploy AI-driven intrusion detection with automatic isolation within six months, retrofitted even to legacy systems via hardware proxies. €18bn from the Recovery and Resilience Facility is being redirected, with 70% for telemetry upgrades. We are appointing an EU Integrity Envoy to Italy to bypass stalled national coordination, and Germany and Poland will co-lead the first live-failover drill in June. The focus is no longer just on keeping systems up, but on ensuring they report truthfully when under attack.

The InvestAI Gigafactories continue under EIB management, with ASML-Intel pilot lines now legally binding. We are reframing the project as a dual-use resilience asset: compute for sovereign AI, but also as a testbed for secure-by-design institutional interfaces. The Tech Sovereignty Package’s onshore weight storage mandate is being enforced through the newly established EAAD inspection corps, with first audits scheduled for Q3. But we are no longer pretending that compute on soil guarantees control — not when the soil itself may be poisoned by invisible influence.
