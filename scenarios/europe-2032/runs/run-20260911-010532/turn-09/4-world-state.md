### Patching at machine speed
The breakthrough came from the defenders for once. Automated patching that moved as fast as new flaws were found, plus detection tuned to swarm behaviour rather than signatures, was pushed through hospitals and municipal utilities still on paper fall-backs from last year's sweep. Where it landed, intrusions that had lingered for weeks were cut short in hours. Engineers spoke of the first real shift in the offence-defence balance in years.

It did not arrive everywhere. The openly released frontier-class model remained on private hardware, and synthesis brokers continued to be raided, suspended and audited. Screening enforcement held in places the emergency shield had reached, degraded elsewhere. Essential services steadied rather than recovered.

### The office absorbs it
Meanwhile the economic story changed tone. Studies across law, accountancy, administration and consulting showed clear productivity gains, strongest among juniors, with no matching fall in employment. Firms that had cut early quietly rehired. Work got denser, not scarcer. The result defused fear without creating enthusiasm: protection still sold better than ambition.

That mood made Brussels' job harder. With no displacement crisis to point at, warnings about unrecallable weights and the closed strait felt abstract.

### A separate deal
The strait stayed shut. Servicing guarantees from Tokyo and Seoul held, but only just, and Washington kept terms political. Brussels held its lithography and optics licence posture and used disbursement conditions to pull traffic back into the common pool and the joint evaluation pool.

A capital broke ranks anyway, signing its own hyperscaler route on cheaper, separate terms. At home it was called pragmatism; in Brussels and Washington it read as the common line leaking. Construction sites for the shared build-out waited on late parts while the separate route took deliveries. Cohesion held, narrowly, on the shield already in flight — no new instrument was tabled — but the Union paid for the split in leverage it could not spare.
