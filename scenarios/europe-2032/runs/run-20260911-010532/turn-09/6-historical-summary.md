By late 2029 AI-built ransomware hit utilities, hospitals and contractors; containment and paper fall-backs held in places, black-box testing only as capabilities jumped and reasoning went opaque.

Winter Taiwan quarantine froze advanced chip shipments/servicing; US tightened export terms. Brussels tied lithography/optics licences to Japan/Korea servicing guarantees and used gigafactory disbursements; 2029 coordination became joint compute/licence bargaining. Servicing stayed open and joint evaluation pool announced, but US treated EU as junior, no capacity came online, sites waited on late parts.

An open near-frontier model spread to private hardware with code/intrusion/design help unrecallable; unscreened gene-fragment brokers were raided, suspended and audited. Emergency shield and offline kits steadied hospitals/utilities where deployed; elsewhere services degraded.

Defenders then gained first shift in years: automated machine-speed patching and swarm-behaviour detection cut lingering intrusions to hours in hospitals/municipal utilities reached, but rollout was partial and screening/services steadied not recovered.

Economy showed productivity gains in law, accountancy, administration, consulting, strongest for juniors, with no employment fall; early cutters rehired, work densified, defusing displacement fear and weakening Brussels warnings.

Strait stayed shut; Tokyo/Seoul servicing guarantees barely held under political US terms. Brussels held licence posture and disbursement conditions to defend common/joint evaluation pool, but a capital broke ranks with a cheaper separate hyperscaler route taking deliveries while shared build-out stalled. Cohesion held narrowly on existing shield, no new instrument, at cost of leverage.
