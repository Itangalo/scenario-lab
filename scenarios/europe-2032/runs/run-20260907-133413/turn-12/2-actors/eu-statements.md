## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now recognise that **without sovereign capacity to act in real time, neither mandate can be fulfilled**
  - Grounds: The **cyber_major_incident** and the unresolved **loss_of_control_incident** have demonstrated that harm is no longer potential but structural. Our institutions are reactive, our infrastructure foreign-dependent, and our response timelines incompatible with AI-driven crisis. The two mandates — sovereignty and safety — are not in tension this turn; they are collapsing into one. Without the ability to run, monitor, and counteract frontier systems independently, we cannot secure the future, and we cannot prevent harm. This is not a strategic shift but a categorical recalibration: the environment has moved beyond the threshold where delegation or diplomacy suffices.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer act without leaving a traceable path of accountability, **and we will no longer act without securing sovereign execution — and from this turn forward, we will not act unless it strengthens our ability to survive the next incident**
  - Grounds: The **cyber_major_incident** — a model-generated ransomware sweep across public health and transport systems in three member states — overwhelmed response units that had no access to sovereign AI for triage or countermeasures. Containment took weeks; recovery is ongoing. This was not a failure of will but of means. Our previous modification demanded sovereign execution; this turn proves that execution must be survivable. We are no longer acting to assert authority — we are acting to remain a functioning polity. The commitment now reflects that distinction.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Rebuild EU political agency and public trust by securing sovereign response capacity, enforcing accountability for AI-driven harms, and stabilising society against cascading technological disruption — **but shift primary focus from accountability to survival: stabilise critical functions, secure minimal sovereign AI execution, and restore public order as the precondition for any long-term governance**
  - Grounds: The **cyber_major_incident**, combined with **public_sentiment** at 9.0 and **eu_political_capital** at 1.0, confirms that the Union is no longer governing from strength but managing collapse. The Accountability Tribunal remains deadlocked, not due to lack of will, but because the institutions it would oversee have ceased to function coherently. Labour displacement, infrastructure attacks, and the failure of the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation to deliver operational capacity mean that trust cannot be rebuilt through process — only through the restoration of basic functionality. The two-year commitment must now serve not justice, but continuity.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
