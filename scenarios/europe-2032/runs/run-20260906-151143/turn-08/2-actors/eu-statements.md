# Statements: The European Union (turn 8)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI control to withstand strategic shocks and prevent catastrophic misuse

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Prevent catastrophic misuse through sovereign control of high-capability AI systems, with enforceable containment, real-time monitoring, public accountability for all systems above 55.0 capability, and active containment of open-weight diffusion beyond 52.0 — but now conditioned on the imperative to preserve EU access to frontier capability under global compute scarcity, and to redirect sovereignty efforts toward securing what remains operable rather than assuming expansion is possible.
  - Grounds: The **taiwan_blockade** and **ai_investment_collapse** together represent a categorical shift: the world’s compute supply has contracted sharply and permanently, and the EU’s leverage now lies not in building more, but in controlling access to what exists. The original commitment assumed continued growth in available resources; that assumption no longer holds. We must now prioritise *preservation* and *strategic allocation* of existing and emerging compute over scaling ambitions tied to evaporated private investment and severed supply lines. Sovereignty is no longer about matching the frontier — which has paused — but about ensuring the Union can still act within it.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late — but we now recognise that uncertainty includes not only risk from AI behaviour, but from the collapse of the conditions that made our plans feasible; we will therefore recalibrate major initiatives when external shocks invalidate their assumptions, and treat infrastructure fragility as a systemic threat equal to model misalignment.
  - Grounds: The convergence of **taiwan_blockade** and **ai_investment_collapse** has invalidated the material basis of several in-flight measures. To continue them unchanged would be ritual, not strategy. Acting under uncertainty now means acknowledging that the environment can withdraw the floor beneath even well-founded plans — and that political capital spent on unexecutable measures is lost not just to those measures, but to every alternative they crowd out.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
