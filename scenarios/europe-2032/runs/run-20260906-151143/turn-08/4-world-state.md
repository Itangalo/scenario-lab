### A World Contracts

Global AI progress slows but does not stop: `ai_capability` reaches 68.0 as frontier labs squeeze marginal gains from existing hardware, though training runs shrink under capital and chip constraints. The open frontier advances more sharply — leaked models fine-tuned on cyber-offense data push `openweight_capability` to 57.0, widening the gap between what is controlled and what is loose.

The EU confronts a new reality. The **taiwan_blockade** halts shipments of advanced AI chips; the **ai_investment_collapse** cancels private data centre expansions across Europe. Overnight, compute shifts from commodity to strategic reserve. The Sovereign AI Core Programme, built on assumptions of scaling supply, is formally suspended. Its remnants are folded into a new effort: the EU Critical Compute Reserve, designed to quarantine and allocate scarce accelerators for state priority uses.

The move is urgent but strained. Legal disputes flare among member states over ownership and access. Hungary and Sweden challenge the moratorium on exports, arguing it distorts internal markets. Emergency arbitration is invoked, delaying full implementation. Meanwhile, civil auditing collectives gain ground, publishing damning analyses of public-sector AI use. Their credibility soars as official transparency lags.

Resilience erodes further as critical infrastructure remains exposed. No new hardening measures land, and the earlier CIRS surge yields diminishing returns. Public sentiment dips to 18.0 amid job losses in tech and rising AI-facilitated fraud. Trust in institutions frays.

Politically, the cost is steep. Six major initiatives remain in flight or recently finished, draining political capital. The pivot to preservation fails to earn credit: no measure delivers tangible capability, and the cancellation of SACP signals retreat. Capital falls to 9.0 — below 12, control slips. A new measure may fail to start next turn.

Sovereignty declines further, now at 10.0. Despite the Reserve’s launch, the Union’s access to cutting-edge capacity is diminished, not secured. The world moves on rationing; Europe scrambles to define its share.
