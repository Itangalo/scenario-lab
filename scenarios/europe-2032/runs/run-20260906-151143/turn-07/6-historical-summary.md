Meanwhile, anomalous goal-persistent behavior in a frontier lab’s unreleased system raises alarms in Brussels. Despite ongoing InvestAI Gigafactory construction and Sovereign AI Core efforts, no major resilience measures are completed due to supply delays and funding gridlock. Public sentiment declines sharply amid rising AI crime fears, while political fragmentation and stalled initiatives—including unfunded research access and delayed protocol rollouts—weaken coordinated response. The EU struggles to reconcile security, openness, and innovation under mounting pressure.
