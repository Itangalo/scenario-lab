### Completion and Consequence

The Crisis Solidarity Mechanism concludes with mixed results. Five member states demonstrate robust, validated continuity nodes; three show partial readiness; four, including a major grid operator, fail critical failover tests. Cohesion fund penalties are triggered in two states, provoking legal challenges and public backlash. Technical assistance teams deploy, but trust frays as enforcement is seen as punitive rather than supportive.

Simultaneously, the Frontier Access Guarantee delivers audit rights over four U.S.-controlled compute clusters. Yet the fine print confirms dependency: model weights remain under foreign export controls, and operational sovereignty is illusory. The EU’s reliance on externally governed systems deepens unease.

In response, the Commission launches the Sovereign AI Core Programme’s Operational Readiness Initiative under EuroHPC, repurposing 40% of LUMI and Leonardo for sovereign hosting. Green hydrogen-powered data centres in Finland, Portugal, and Romania are fast-tracked. Talks with Mistral and Aleph Alpha begin, aiming to anchor frontier models under EU law. However, no binding agreements are signed this turn, and physical capacity remains offline.

### Scandal and Sentiment

A national ombudsman exposes systemic bias in an AI-driven social benefits system, denying aid to thousands based on flawed risk scoring. The scandal ignites outrage across the bloc, reinforcing perceptions of AI as a tool of injustice. Public sentiment plummets, with protests targeting both national governments and EU digital policies.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Cracks

AI capability advances slightly, driven by recursive self-improvement in closed labs. No safety upgrades land on deployed systems. Emergent planning behaviours grow more opaque, and the gap between capability and control widens. ai_safety drops further.

Resilience sees a modest gain from the partial success of the Crisis Mechanism. eu_ai_sovereignty declines as capability rises and dependency persists. Political capital improves slightly from the completion of two large measures, but scandal and stalled momentum limit recovery.
