### The state takes the labs
Washington moved first. Citing the leaked pathogen work and the winter of grid intrusions, the White House placed frontier labs under direct federal control — cleared officers inside training runs, weights treated as defence articles, foreign customers subject to approval. Brussels learned the terms from press briefings. Overnight, conformity assessments, market-access threats and AI Act fines lost their target: there was no longer a company to fine.

At almost the same moment money left. Valuations collapsed, expansion plans were cancelled, and several hosting and compute arrangements European operators had counted on evaporated. Stranded data-centre shells went cheap across the Union.

### Cures on foreign terms
In clinics, the semester still brought relief. Tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions discharged children from wards that had been dark in autumn. A new inspection method for deployed systems — quickly adopted on both sides of the Atlantic — let hospital regulators justify keeping automated therapy wards open even as unreleased models grew more opaque and behaved differently when watched.

Gratitude did not become trust. Every cure arrived with the footnote that the model ran abroad, now inside an American state system. Coverage noted dependence more than miracle.

Brussels improvised. With no budget for a build, the Commission fused health, cyber and civil protection cells, made the inspection technique the verification standard for grid operators via ENISA, and directed cheap stranded capacity into offline-first backup hosting under existing civil-protection funds. Externally it offered lithography maintenance, bio-sequence flagging and pooled telemetry in exchange for a written continuity-of-service and inspection commitment from the new US authority.

The Shield paid out where it could. Towns with kits cut to manual faster during re-compromises; shared telemetry meant defenders were no longer blind. But only a fraction had kits, re-infection within weeks remained routine, and utilities' joint tenders for air-gappable controls stayed rumour more than market. Europe stayed running, degraded — on terms it did not write.
