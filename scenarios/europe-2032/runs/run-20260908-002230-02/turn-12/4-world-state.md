### The Weight of Scandal

Global AI capability advances to 73.0, driven by increasingly autonomous cyber toolchains and recursive optimisation in model training. With no safety improvements in deployed systems, and coordination events recurring in closed labs, `ai_safety` remains stagnant at 19.0 — a level of opacity where even developers cannot fully trace model decisions.

The EU completes the **European AI Incident Response Corps**, a major milestone. A live-fire exercise in June tests cross-border command during a simulated model compromise across energy and transport networks. While communication protocols hold, interoperability gaps delay response by critical hours, raising doubts about real-world readiness. Still, the completion boosts `resilience` to 55.0, reflecting improved planning and dedicated tooling.

But the political ground shifts beneath. A European Ombudsman report confirms a national social benefits system denied thousands of claims using unexplainable AI logic, with no appeal path — a scandal that crystallises public anger. Simultaneously, walkouts by hospital administrators in Berlin and Marseille over unconsulted AI rollouts deepen the crisis of legitimacy.

In response, the EU imposes a **Public Sector AI Deployment Moratorium**, halting new AI in public decision-making pending independent audits and worker negotiations. Framed as democratic reassertion, the move faces resistance: two member states declare they will not adopt the Recommendation, citing national sovereignty. The moratorium passes in spirit but fractures in execution.

The **Sovereign Cloud Integration Acceleration** remains the named priority, yet progress stalls. France delays workload classification, and Poland again rejects benchmarks. The U.S. posture of **CONSOLIDATION** continues to restrict frontier access, and `eu_ai_sovereignty` dips to 9.0 as `ai_capability` rises by 1.5 (−1) and no sovereign compute gains materialise.

Political capital rises slightly to 8.0, despite portfolio strain. The completion of the Incident Corps yields +4, and the moratorium earns a +3 proposal bonus for directly addressing the scandal and unrest. But ongoing costs (−3 for the large measure, −3 for the new small measure, −1 for priority) total −7, leaving a narrow net gain.

Public sentiment holds at 26.0 — too deep in scepticism for a pause to register as progress.
