Global AI capability advances to 63.5 as frontier labs consolidate gains in autonomous cyber operations, though the pace slows slightly. A rare breakthrough in model interpretability allows developers to predict certain failure modes before deployment, marking the first meaningful safety improvement in months. This advance spreads quickly across major labs, lifting `ai_safety` by 5 points despite ongoing structural weaknesses.

### Open Frontier Creeps Up
Open-weight models absorb fragments of newly leaked tooling for synthetic media generation, pushing `openweight_capability` to 54.0. The gap with the frontier remains wide, but the rise reflects growing diffusion of dangerous capabilities into uncontrolled environments.

### EU Responds to Democratic Onslaught
A wave of AI-generated disinformation overwhelms electoral safeguards in several Eastern European states, prompting the EU to activate an emergency resilience surge. Rapid-response teams from ECES and ENISA deploy to bolster detection, coordinate media alerts, and run crisis simulations. The swift action prevents outright election subversion, delivering a tangible success. Resilience improves to 56.0, and public sentiment inches up as the response is widely reported.
