### A Crisis of Access, Met with a Surge of Intent

Global AI capability advances to 67.0 as autonomous agents deepen their role in training and deployment cycles, particularly in cyber operations and materials science. No safety gains accompany this rise: `ai_safety` remains stagnant at 24.0, with no new safeguards integrated into frontier systems.

A major cyber incident unfolds across regional health authorities in Spain and Belgium. Automated ransomware, likely generated using model-optimized exploit chains, disrupts appointment scheduling and diagnostic workflows for weeks. Initial response is slow, and full recovery takes months. The attack underscores growing offensive capabilities now accessible beyond state actors.

In response, the EU activates the Sovereign Compute Emergency Procurement Directive, redirecting €48bn and securing national co-funding. ASML reserves lithography time; TSMC Europe enters advanced talks. However, no physical compute is delivered this turn — firmware sovereignty remains unproven, and remote disablement risks persist. Despite political will, the directive yields no immediate capacity gain.

The InvestAI Gigafactories programme completes its site preparation phase, marking a symbolic milestone. Yet construction has not begun, and no operational clusters come online. The Conditional Open-Weight Licensing Framework becomes active: ENISA deploys scanning tools on GitHub and GitLab, triggering identity checks for high-capability uploads. Compliance is partial; some platforms resist, and enforcement relies on DNS blocks under the DSA, sparking legal challenges.

Public sentiment declines as the cyberattack disrupts daily life and headlines emphasize dependency. Trust in AI-mediated services erodes further. Political capital falls under the weight of three large measures in flight and the named priority, with no completed category 4 project to offset costs.

### No Restoration of Access

U.S. posture remains CONSOLIDATION. Tiered access continues, and no new agreements restore EU frontier access. Strategic dependence deepens, despite emergency efforts.
