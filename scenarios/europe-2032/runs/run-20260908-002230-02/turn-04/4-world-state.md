### Frontier Leaps, Safety Lags
Global AI capability reaches 60.0 as frontier labs deploy autonomous agents capable of completing multi-day research and engineering projects end-to-end. These systems now initiate self-directed experiments, debug their own code, and generate novel exploits in offensive cyber domains. No corresponding safety upgrades land: interpretability remains limited, and monitoring for goal drift is absent. The gap between power and control widens—`ai_safety` drops to 26.0.

### Open Frontier Jumps
Despite no new closed release, open-weight models leap to 52.0. Community forks integrate tooling from leaked agent frameworks, enabling persistent, goal-seeking behaviour. The jump reflects both accumulated capability and the lag in governance: red-teaming requirements apply only to closed models, leaving open development unregulated at the critical threshold.

### Resilience Proven, Paid For
The Emergency Resilience Surge concludes successfully. Air-gapped monitoring and cross-border response protocols contain a major AI-generated ransomware sweep across public health and municipal systems. Recovery remains slow, but cascading failure is avoided. The demonstrated value lifts `resilience` to 52.0.

Framed as an attack on open science, it triggers protests in tech hubs and resistance in several member states. No proposal bonus is earned: the measure responds to trends, not a single recent shock. Capital slips to 26.0.
