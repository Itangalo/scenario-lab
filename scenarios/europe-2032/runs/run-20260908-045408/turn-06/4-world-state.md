### The Investment Winter

The global AI sector buckled as valuations collapsed and major compute build-outs were scrapped. The EU’s reliance on private capital for data centre expansion unraveled overnight. The Tech sovereignty package, intended to leverage €200bn in private investment, expired unfulfilled. Without binding commitments, its promises dissolved into paper triumph.

### Sovereignty Undermined

Member state defection deepened the crisis. One Eastern member, citing energy costs and sovereignty concerns, signed a bilateral deal with a U.S. hyperscaler, securing preferential access to frontier models in exchange for relaxed data rules. The move bypassed EU coordination, signaling that Washington could still pick off allies — and that Brussels could not enforce unity. The Commission condemned the deal, but legal recourse was limited.

InvestAI Gigafactories became the EU’s last credible bet for sovereign compute. With private capital gone, the Commission pivoted to full public ownership, preparing emergency borrowing proposals. But momentum stalled. The Dutch government rejected any invocation of Article 346 over ASML, calling it “economic self-sabotage.” Diplomatic friction hardened into deadlock.

### Resilience, at Last

Despite political headwinds, the Expand EU Cyber Resilience Certification measure finished successfully. ENISA now mandates AI-specific hardening for critical infrastructure, with enforcement tied to EU funding. Rapid-assessment teams began operations, and cohesion fund conditionality gave teeth to compliance. Resilience rose as technical standards landed — even as adoption lagged.

The Labour Transition Accelerator and EU AI Incident Response Corps also formally concluded. Yet both suffered from patchy implementation: retraining relied on national goodwill, and the Corps lacked emergency powers to intervene. No new measure replaced them.

Public sentiment dipped further. Job displacement stories mounted, and the consumer AI crash — a wave of erratic therapy and tutoring apps — fueled distrust. With no visible public benefit, anxiety hardened into skepticism.

### The American Posture Settles

In Washington, the new administration took office. US_POSTURE: RETRENCHMENT. Market signals confirmed it: AI deployment restrictions loomed, and data centre growth would slow. Global capability growth, already strained, now faced headwinds.
