### A Cascade of Crises

A major cyber incident overwhelms emergency services in four member states, exploiting a poisoned open-source library. Hospitals lose access to patient records, rail networks halt, and backup generators fail under coordinated digital sabotage. Attribution remains unclear, but forensic analysis points to model-generated attack logic. Simultaneously, a court annuls regional elections in Hungary after evidence of AI-synthesised disinformation campaigns that skewed voter turnout. Separately, an automated welfare system in Belgium is exposed for wrongly denying thousands of claims over two years, triggering public outrage and a parliamentary inquiry.

Public sentiment crashes to 23.0 as trust in institutions erodes.
