### EU Responds, But Pays the Price  
The European Commission proposes the EU AI Incident Response Corps, a category 6 measure aimed at strengthening cross-border cyber resilience. Backed by ENISA and funded through repurposed digital programmes, the proposal is formally tabled but faces immediate resistance. Germany and Poland challenge the use of Article 114 TFEU, arguing it overreaches on national security. No agreement is reached by year-end, and the measure remains in negotiation—implementation delayed to a future turn.
