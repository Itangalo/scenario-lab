### The Weight of Incomplete Victory

The EU closes two major initiatives: the EU AI Incident Response Corps becomes operational under ENISA, and the Labour Transition Accelerator completes its pilot phase. Yet neither transition lands cleanly. The Corps’ new powers stall in the Council, leaving field teams with detection capacity but no enforcement teeth. Labour retraining continues only in France and the Netherlands, now reliant on patchwork national funding.

ENISA issues binding AI-hardening rules for critical infrastructure, marking real progress in resilience. But legal challenges from Eastern member states over cohesion fund conditionality delay rollout, and the EFTA Court warns of overreach. The cyber resilience expansion, now the named priority, advances technically but fractures politically.
