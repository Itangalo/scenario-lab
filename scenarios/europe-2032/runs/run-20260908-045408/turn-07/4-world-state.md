### The Threshold Crossed

Global AI capability reached 60.0 this turn, crossing the threshold where systems can complete multi-day professional projects end to end. Frontier labs report AI-driven codebases deploying autonomously across internal testing environments, with human oversight reduced to exception handling. This shift is quiet but structural — the era of AI assistance is giving way to AI execution.

### Sovereignty Through Fire

The EU completed the InvestAI Gigafactories programme, formally closing the €200bn fund. Though originally conceived as a public-private partnership, the collapse of private investment forced a pivot: the Commission now asserts direct ownership over two sovereign compute sites, to be built in France and Finland under EU legal control. Emergency borrowing under Article 122 TFEU has been approved, backed by cohesion funds, and the classification of AI infrastructure as critical is now formally under consideration. The expansion of the Critical Entities Resilience Directive to include AI remains in drafting — delayed by legal coordination challenges and opposition from several member states. This regulatory step will not advance this turn, preserving the limit of one new measure per turn; the Gigafactory pivot stands as the turn’s sole major action.

The Dutch government continues to resist invocation of Article 346 over ASML, warning of retaliation from U.S. allies and damage to European semiconductor leadership. Brussels has drafted the regulation but holds it in reserve, aware that unilateral action could fracture the bloc at the moment it most needs unity.

### Resilience That Lands

With the Expand EU Cyber Resilience Certification measure now complete, ENISA has begun audits of energy, transport, and health providers under the new AI-specific standards. Early reports show improved detection and response latency, and the threat of funding withdrawal has driven compliance even in reluctant member states. Resilience rises as capability lands — a rare win.

### Political Capital Rebounds — Cautiously

Political capital improved modestly, rising to 18.0. The completion of multiple programmes — including the Cyber Resilience Certification and the Labour Transition Accelerator — delivered gains, and the decisive pivot on Gigafactories was seen as necessary. However, the scale of emergency borrowing and ongoing legal resistance temper enthusiasm. The net gain is limited, reflecting cautious optimism within institutional bounds. Public sentiment remains stagnant, anchored by distrust in consumer AI and fears of job loss.
