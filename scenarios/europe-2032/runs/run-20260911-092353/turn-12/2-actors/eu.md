## In practice
We mandate the Commission via the Foreign Affairs/Trade Council to sign the coalition coordination framework under the Anti-Coercion Instrument logic and existing trade competence, pooling telemetry-sharing already done under ENISA/EU-CyCLONe as our contribution for shared evaluation and pooled bargaining for compute. DG TRADE and DG CONNECT align export-licence positions with coalition partners holding lithography, energy and compute pieces, directing any secured fallback capacity first to showcase clinics, municipal grids and ministries hit by the denial.

We contain the defection politically rather than punish it now: Council notes the bilateral deal without a new infringement, while making reimbursement-pool and transformer/fuel-pool access conditional on no further undercutting, and DG REFORM keeps health/justice pilots on EU-hosted and coalition-sourced models to prove continuity-by-hand still runs.
