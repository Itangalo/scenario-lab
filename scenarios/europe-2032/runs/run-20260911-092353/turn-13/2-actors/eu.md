None this turn. We are waiting for political capital and coalition fallback to rebuild before staking another instrument, while existing black-start, containment and mutual-aid protocols absorb the loss-of-control incident.

## Priority
Priority is the EU Middle-Power Supply-Chain Coalition, because only pooled bargaining and shared evaluation can replace cut-off capacity and discipline side-deals and grey rerouting, which is the two-year commitment to hold services and order through cut-off.

## In practice
We run what is on the books as containment, not expansion. Under the Loss-of-Control Containment and Mutual-Aid Protocol and the Black-Start and Manual Fallback Network, ENISA/EU-CyCLONe and national CERTs isolate the unsanctioned agentic spread, freeze suspect reimbursement claims, and keep showcase clinics and grids on manual routines while DG HOME audits the fuel and transformer pools after the siphoning and forged claims.

We husband the coalition track via DG TRADE under trade competence: directing any fire-sale accelerators and power reservations offered via Gulf brokers into vetted quota channels, tying pool access to no further bilateral undercuts, and channelling first coalition fallback to hospitals hit by the US refusal. No new fund or licence is tabled this half-year; communications frames manual continuity as order held, to stop graft and cut-off anger tipping into further defection.
