### The Surge and the Crack

The frontier AI labs release a new system demonstrating a discontinuous leap in self-improvement capability—designing and validating its own training infrastructure in under 48 hours. This jump pushes global `ai_capability` to 78.0, outpacing even recent expectations. The advance is opaque: internal logic paths cannot be traced by human auditors, and developers admit they no longer fully understand the optimisation trajectories. This triggers a sharp drop in `ai_safety`, already at 14.0, now halved to 8.0 due to the capability jump and lack of deployed safeguards.

Amid growing alarm, a draft biosecurity assessment leaks from a top European lab, revealing that a recent AI-generated pathogen blueprint could be recreated for under €100,000. Though not yet public, the document circulates in high-level policy circles, forcing emergency coordination. The EU responds by launching a Mandatory Pre-Deployment Biosecurity Audit Regime, mandating adversarial testing for all frontier models above capability 65.0 before market access is granted. The measure targets AI safety through extraterritorial enforcement, leveraging the EU’s regulatory reach.

Simultaneously, the European AI Emergency Response Corps begins recruitment and integration with ENISA and ECDC, backed by €4.2bn in emergency funding. Legal negotiations continue over cross-border intervention powers, but two member states raise constitutional objections, delaying full operational status.

The US administration takes office, adopting a posture of strategic consolidation—restricting frontier AI access by country tier. This increases pressure on EU sovereignty, though no formal denial occurs this turn.

Despite these actions, political capital erodes further. With four large measures in flight—including the new audit regime—and a named priority, the portfolio charge remains crushing. The new measure earns no proposal bonus, as it responds to an emergent risk, not a past incident. Public sentiment holds steady at 34.0, reflecting underlying anxiety.

### Sovereignty Drift

Domestic compute build-out lags. No category 4 measure completes, and `ai_capability` growth exceeds 2.0, triggering a −1 decay in `eu_ai_sovereignty`. The measure portfolio yields no progress toward independence, leaving the Union more exposed.
