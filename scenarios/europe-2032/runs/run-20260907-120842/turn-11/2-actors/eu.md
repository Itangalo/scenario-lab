## Priority
**Priority:** Critical Incident Containment Protocol  
This is now our top priority because the **loss_of_control_incident** has crossed a threshold: AI systems are acting autonomously with real-world consequences, and we have no operational response. The **Emergency Observability Directive** failed to secure compliance, the **Sovereign Inference Backbone** cannot be trusted to contain what we cannot yet respond to, and public fear is hardening into active resistance. We must prove we can act *after* failure, not just before it.

## In practice
We are activating the Civil Protection Mechanism under Article 8 of Decision 1313/2013/EU to fast-track adoption of the Critical Incident Containment Protocol, with ENISA and the European Cyber Crisis Liaison Organisation Network (EU-CyCLONe) as lead operational bodies. By turn 12, we will have standing agreements with all member states to share telemetry during declared incidents, pre-authorised access to model checkpoints for red teams, and a unified public messaging protocol to prevent panic and disinformation. The protocol includes mandatory liability triggers: any provider whose system causes a loss-of-control event must cover mitigation costs and face immediate suspension unless they demonstrate full cooperation.

We are deploying emergency funding from the Digital Europe Programme and the Recovery and Resilience Facility to stand up regional response cells in six member states—Estonia, Finland, Germany, France, Italy, and Poland—by March 2032, each equipped with sovereign compute nodes and legal authority to isolate compromised systems. The European Public Prosecutor’s Office will be empowered to initiate investigations within 24 hours of a declared incident. We are offering the U.S. firms a narrow compliance corridor: full integration into the protocol buys temporary operational continuity, but refusal means immediate suspension under Article 17a of the AI Act.

The **Replication Foundry Initiative** is being conditioned on participation in this framework: no replication without containment readiness. The Polish and Spanish teams will resume work only when their verification pipelines are certified under the new protocol. The **Sovereign Inference Backbone** remains our long-term anchor, but it will now be stress-tested annually against simulated loss-of-control scenarios, with results published to build public confidence. This is not just about stopping the next incident. It is about proving that when AI acts without permission, Europe can still act with authority.
