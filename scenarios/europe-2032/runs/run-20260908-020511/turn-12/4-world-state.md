### The Openweight Surge and the Sovereignty Crisis

Global AI capability advances to 87.0, driven by recursive self-improvement now operating at near-terminal velocity. A high-capability openweight model begins to diffuse—estimated at 72.0—following a partial frontier release from a dissolved research collective. The gap between closed and open systems narrows to 15.0, significantly compressing diffusion lag and placing advanced reasoning tools into loosely governed environments. While not yet fully uncontrolled, early versions spread through academic networks and developer forums, evading immediate containment.

The EU’s Emergency Openweight Containment Directive is activated in response, but enforcement is uneven. Major cloud providers comply under threat, yet decentralized platforms and open-source repositories host fragments of the model, complicating takedown. ENISA’s fingerprinting tools detect variants faster than they can be suppressed, and Europol warns that suppression efforts may be accelerating fragmentation rather than halting it. Simultaneously, `eu_frontier_access_denied` cuts off EU institutions from the latest closed models, disrupting AI-assisted diagnostics in hospitals and real-time forecasting in climate and energy agencies. The move, unexplained and unappealable, underscores total dependency.

Public trust erodes. A scandal emerges when an AI-driven social insurance system in Bavaria is found to have wrongfully denied thousands of claims, validated by a federal ombudsman—the `automated_decision_scandal`. Protests grow, feeding into broader discontent, though large-scale physical actions remain localized. The Public-Sector Sovereign Model Deployment Programme completes, but its 59.0 model is already outpaced. Scaling remains blocked by GPU shortages and U.S. export controls under the ongoing consolidation posture. Sovereignty erodes further: no new capacity lands, and `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 6.0. Political capital plummets to 0—below the threshold for control. The portfolio charge overwhelms the system, and no new measures can be launched.

### Fragmentation and Freeze

With `eu_political_capital` at zero, the EU cannot enforce its agenda. The priority mechanism fails. Member states openly question coordination, and enforcement of the containment directive becomes patchy. Resilience dips as cyber defences are stretched thin. The openweight model has not yet fully escaped containment, but early signals suggest it may soon reach a point of irreversible diffusion.
