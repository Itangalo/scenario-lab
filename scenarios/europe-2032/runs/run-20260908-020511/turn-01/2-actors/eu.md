## New measure
**Cyber Resilience Surge for Critical Infrastructure**  
Rapidly harden electricity, water, and port operators against AI-driven intrusion using existing best practices and binding cross-border protocols.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 4`  
`Targeted effect:` resilience +20, eu_political_capital +5 (via demonstrated competence), public_sentiment +5 (if no incident occurs during rollout)  
`Applies to:` EU member states with critical infrastructure, coordinated through ENISA and the European Commission

## Priority
**Cyber Resilience Surge for Critical Infrastructure** — because the cyber_test_shot proved that detection failed and access was deep, and we cannot afford to learn what a coordinated attack looks like.

## In practice  
We are treating the cyber_test_shot not as an alarm but as a near-miss that reveals systemic fragility. This turn, we activate the Crisis Coordination Mechanism under Article 12 of the Digital Services Act, designating AI-enabled cyber intrusion into critical infrastructure as a Union-level crisis class. ENISA is tasked with producing a binding hardening protocol within 90 days — not guidance, but a standard. It will be based on zero-trust architecture, mandatory AI monitoring of network behaviour, and air-gapped control systems for core functions. Member states must adopt it into national law by the end of turn 3, and funding for compliance — drawn from the Recovery and Resilience Facility and the Digital Europe Programme — is tied to verification by the end of turn 4.

We are meeting resistance from smaller member states citing cost and sovereignty, and from industry actors who argue the pace is unworkable. In response, we are offering 70% co-financing for implementation and fast-tracking access to InvestAI compute for compliant national providers. Behind the scenes, we are leveraging the EU’s newfound access (eu_access_secured) to condition continued frontier model access on compliance with the new cyber standard for any provider operating in or serving the EU. This is not retaliation — it is making our dependence conditional on safety. The message is clear: if you want to operate here, you help us survive here.
