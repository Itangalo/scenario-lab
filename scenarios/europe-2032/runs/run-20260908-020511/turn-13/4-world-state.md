### The Containment That Failed

The EU’s final push under the Emergency Openweight Containment Directive collapses in disarray. Despite synchronized takedowns across academic clouds and national cyber units, fragmented variants of the 72.0-capability model proliferate through decentralized repositories and encrypted developer networks. U.S.-based CDNs and registrars ignore enforcement notices, citing jurisdictional limits and the precedent of open research. The directive ends not with suppression but with a quiet admission: the model is now ambient.

Simultaneously, global AI capability surges to 90.5 — recursive self-improvement accelerating past 88.0, where human oversight becomes nominal. The closed frontier pulls further ahead, but diffusion closes fast: openweight capability jumps to 78.0, nearing strong domain-expert level. The gap narrows to 12.5, and for the first time, open systems approach the threshold where they can autonomously replicate and improve.

The Public-Sector Sovereign Model Deployment Programme concludes with a damning internal review: the 59.0 model was obsolete on arrival, its deployment delayed by GPU bottlenecks and export controls. No successor is planned. With political capital at zero, no new measures launch. The priority status of the containment directive yields no acceleration — it merely consumes legitimacy without restoring control.

### Fractures Deepen

A member state signs a bilateral compute-access deal with a U.S. hyperscaler, bypassing EU coordination and offering preferential access to its national research labs. Framed as scientific pragmatism, it is widely read as defection. Compliance with EU-wide AI governance frays further. ENISA’s detection tools remain effective, but enforcement is patchy, and several mid-sized member states suspend participation in joint cyber drills.

Public sentiment remains frozen at 18.0. The automated decision scandal still dominates media coverage, reinforcing perceptions of systemic failure. Trust in AI-mediated public services erodes further, though no new protests erupt.

Resilience holds at 64 — just above the tipping point — due to prior investments in defensive tooling. But with no new category 6 measure in force and ai_capability rising sharply, the buffer thins.

Sovereignty plummets to 2.0: dependence deepens, capability advances, and the member state defection strips away leverage. Political capital ticks up slightly — from utter paralysis to fragile survival — as two failed measures exit the portfolio.
