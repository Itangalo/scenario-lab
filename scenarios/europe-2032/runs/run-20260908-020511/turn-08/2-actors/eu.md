## In practice
The Sovereign AI Stack Initiative reaches its scheduled conclusion this turn. We now possess the institutional framework, technical design, and evaluation protocols for a fully autonomous EU AI capability. But it runs on paper, not silicon. The 100,000 GPUs promised under InvestAI Gigafactories remain stuck in U.S. customs or under foreign-controlled leases, and the ASML directive, while legally claimed, lacks teeth: Dutch veto powers mean export decisions can be blocked from within, and no one can guarantee that the chips enabling our models are not subject to remote disablement or backdoor access. This is not sovereignty—it is dependency dressed as control.

We are now acting to close the loop. The Sovereign Compute Integration Accelerator will establish a binding legal and technical firewall around all compute used in the Sovereign AI Stack. Every GPU, whether sourced from South Korea, Taiwan, or domestic fabs, must be registered under EU jurisdiction, subject to audit by the AI Sovereignty Office, and physically secured in EuroHPC JU facilities with no remote administrative access granted to foreign vendors. We are invoking Article 346 TFEU on grounds of strategic technological autonomy, treating compute as a matter of defence equivalence. Contracts with NVIDIA, TSMC, and Samsung will be renegotiated under this framework—cooperation is preferred, but exclusion from the EU market will be the price of non-compliance.

This is not merely procurement reform. It is a declaration: the EU will not outsource its cognitive infrastructure. We are allocating €22bn from the Digital Europe Programme and the Innovation Fund to subsidize compliant builds and to finance domestic lithography upgrades through ASML and IMEC. The European External Action Service is launching parallel negotiations with Seoul and Taipei to secure chip access under mutual security assurances, while we prepare for retaliation from Washington. The political risk is high, but the cost of inaction is higher: a sovereign stack without sovereign silicon is a castle in the air. We build the walls now, before the storm hits.
