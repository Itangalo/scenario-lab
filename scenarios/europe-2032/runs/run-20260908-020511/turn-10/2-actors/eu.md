## In practice
We are pressing forward on three fronts. First, we are activating the completed Emergency Cyber Shield across all critical nodes: ENISA has issued compliance certificates to 27 of 27 member states’ emergency coordination centres, and automated intrusion detection is now live in 90% of designated hospitals and grid operators. The system has already flagged two novel zero-day exploits traced back to shadow forks of open-weight models; they were contained within hours. This delivers real resilience, but it does not restore political capital—only agency does.

Second, the Sovereign Compute Integration Accelerator remains our spine. Despite Dutch resistance, we are shifting from confrontation to structured interdependence: the Commission has offered The Hague a tripartite deal—partial compensation via the European Defence Fund for ASML’s lost sales, joint ownership of a new EU-level export licensing authority, and priority access for Dutch firms to the first wave of sovereign model deployments. At the same time, we are finalising co-location agreements with SK Hynix in Dresden and Wrocław, leveraging the Chips Act to fast-track permits, and using DMA Article 6 powers to condition Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure operations in the Single Market on firmware audits and removal of remote kill switches. Legal challenges are expected, but we are no longer waiting for consensus—we are building facts on the ground.

Third, we are launching the Public-Sector Sovereign Model Deployment Programme to give tangible form to sovereignty. The model trained by the coalition of French, German, and Finnish labs will be deployed in pilot mode across ten agencies—including Eurostat, Frontex, and national tax administrations—for document synthesis, workflow automation, and secure translation. No public-facing decisions, no sensitive data, but real work. We are funding interface tooling through Horizon Europe and offering each participating agency €50 million in digital transition credits. This is not about performance—it is about proving that *we can run our own*. If sentiment stays low, adoption stalls; if adoption stalls, sovereignty becomes abstract, then expendable. We are making it real before it is perfect.
