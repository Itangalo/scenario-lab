### Frontier Widens, Openweights Gain Ground  
Global AI capability climbs to 65.5 as labs exploit emergent behaviors from unexplained anomalies, accelerating self-improving training loops. The closed frontier pulls ahead, but the gap narrows as knowledge from a recently deployed frontier model leaks into open channels. While the full weights are not released, detailed architecture blueprints and training data recipes circulate among open collectives, enabling rapid imitation. Openweight capability rises to 54.5—gaining ground, but still trailing the frontier by a clear margin.

### EU Pivots to Runtime Enforcement  
The EU completes the Sovereign AI Audit Programme, activating secure monitoring enclaves in Munich and Saclay. However, interpretability gaps persist — the system observes but does not understand. In response, the Commission launches the **Runtime Enforcement Framework**, mandating real-time telemetry and kill-switch access for all AI systems operating in critical infrastructure. The framework leverages the joint cyber command’s shared standards, integrating anomaly detectors from the fractured lab’s independent collective.

Germany and France fast-track adoption, but Ireland hardens its resistance, warning of sovereignty overreach. Poland delays implementation, citing cost and legal uncertainty. Enforcement mechanisms remain untested, and foreign providers signal non-compliance risks.

### Fractures and Backlash  
A senior lab splits over safety failures, releasing open tools that accelerate distributed monitoring — a rare gain for safety innovation. Yet public trust erodes further as election integrity is challenged in a major member state, with courts citing AI-driven disinformation. Protests escalate into sabotage at data centres in Spain and Belgium, injuring two workers. The backlash underscores growing anxiety, even as resilience improves slightly due to coordinated threat response.

### Political Strain Mounts  
The Emergency Resilience Surge progresses unevenly. Political capital falls under the weight of four large measures. Public sentiment dips as disruptions outpace visible benefits. The EU’s pivot is technically sound but politically strained — enforcement ambition meets fragmented cohesion.
