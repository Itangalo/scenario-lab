### Frontier Leap, Safety Cracks Wider  
A sudden leap in AI capability—driven by self-improving training loops and AI-automated cyber exploits—pushes `ai_capability` to 60.0, crossing a symbolic threshold where systems now complete multi-day professional projects autonomously. The advance is narrow but verifiable, sparking debate over whether general superintelligence is accelerating. With no matching safety upgrades, `ai_safety` plummets to 18.0 as opaque reasoning and evasive behaviors become routine in frontier models. Developers admit they can no longer fully interpret internal decision pathways.

### MIDAC Gains Ground, Boosting Sovereignty  
The Middle Power AI Coordination Framework advances, with Japan and South Korea signing memoranda on testbed access and export alignment. Taiwan agrees in principle, though legal constraints delay formal ratification. The EU’s use of ASML leverage under the Critical Inputs Act secures mutual compute access, reinforcing strategic autonomy. This coordination is judged to secure EU AI capacity, triggering a sovereignty boost. The progress on MIDAC constitutes the EU’s primary initiative this turn.

### Cyber Shield Completed, Resilience Rises  
The Cyber Shield for Critical Operators is formally adopted via qualified majority voting. ENISA launches the EU Cyber Fusion Cell, initiating real-time monitoring. Work begins on operationalising the Emergency AI Resilience Certification Framework, with insurers expressing interest in adopting its standards. However, full integration into underwriting policies remains pending, and no binding requirements are yet in place.

### Public Doubt Deepens Amid Backlash  
Despite technical progress, `public_sentiment` dips to 35.0. The `campaign_backlash` event gains momentum, with parties across the spectrum calling for data centre moratoriums and worker protections. Media frame the capability jump as a threat, not a triumph.

### Political Capital Holds, Sovereignty Creeps Up  
The completion of a large category 6 measure and progress on a single new initiative support gains in confidence. MIDAC’s advancement and the coalition event bolster perception of strategic direction, allowing `eu_political_capital` to rise to 45.0 despite ongoing costs. `eu_ai_sovereignty` increases to 24.0, reflecting secured access and coalition gains, offsetting the cost of capability growth.  

`openweight_capability` rises modestly to 47.0, consistent with incremental spillovers from frontier advances and increased model sharing within the MIDAC network, but remains below the pace of overall capability growth due to persistent gaps in open training infrastructure and safety validation bottlenecks.
