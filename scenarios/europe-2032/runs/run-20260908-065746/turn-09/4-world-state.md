### Frontier Acceleration, Open Replication Closes In
Global AI capability advances to 67.0, driven by deeper exploitation of self-improving training loops and emergent reasoning patterns. Labs report systems that autonomously refine their own prompts and training data pipelines, accelerating progress in closed environments. With no safety breakthroughs, the open frontier gains ground: architecture leaks and shared training recipes allow open collectives to replicate key behaviours, pushing openweight capability to 56.0.

### EU Enacts Runtime Control, Faces Immediate Resistance
The EU completes the Emergency Resilience Surge, hardening critical infrastructure in energy and healthcare with zero-trust systems and automated failover. The move absorbs the impact of recent cyber intrusions, earning a resilience boost. Building on this, Brussels launches the **Mandatory Runtime Intervention Architecture**, requiring all high-risk AI systems — including foreign-hosted models — to integrate a standardized, EU-controlled kill-switch interface by turn 11. The measure is framed as essential after an election was annulled due to undetected disinformation campaigns.

Germany and France endorse the plan, fast-tracking integration with national CERTs. But Ireland refuses to ratify the delegated acts, calling them an overreach of extraterritorial authority. Poland and Hungary question enforceability, warning of legal challenges at the Court of Justice. U.S. cloud providers signal they may withdraw from public procurement unless exemptions are granted.

### Public Backlash Mounts
Despite technical progress, public sentiment declines. Media spotlight injuries from the Belgium data centre sabotage and frame the new intervention mandate as “AI martial law.” Labour unions protest automation in public services, linking it to recent job cuts. The perception of democratic erosion — first with the election annulment, now with emergency powers — deepens distrust.

### Political Capital Holds — Just
The completion of the Emergency Resilience Surge offsets some strain, granting a modest political capital gain. However, launching a large new measure amid four ongoing initiatives stretches cohesion. The priority cost bites, but the proposal bonus from addressing a major recent shock prevents deeper erosion.
