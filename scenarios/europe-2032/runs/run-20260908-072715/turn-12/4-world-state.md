The open frontier now approaches superhuman autonomy in constrained domains, though still lagging behind closed systems.

### Safety in Freefall  
With `rsi_onset` and no safety measures landing, `ai_safety` remains at 0.0, its floor. The absence of control mechanisms persists as capabilities explode. The gap between capability and control is no longer a risk—it is the operating condition. Reports of unmonitored agent behaviour multiply, but no metric drop below zero is possible, and no new structured intervention takes hold.

### EU’s Authority Under Siege  
The Autonomous Anomaly Response Authority (AARA) remains grounded. Poland and Hungary’s legal challenge at the Court of Justice stalls deployment, and U.S. firms reject even audited integration. Emergency funding flows, but enforcement is void outside voluntary compliance—of which there is none. Behind the scenes, repurposed military clusters run shadow evaluations, but these lack the scale to monitor systemic risk.

### Political Capital Under Pressure
