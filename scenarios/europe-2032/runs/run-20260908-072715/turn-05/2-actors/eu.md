## Priority
**Launch an EU-led programme to develop and deploy safe-by-design sovereign AI agents** — because the U.S. election outcome confirms that access to frontier AI will be rationed by strategic alignment, not market terms, and because our current sovereignty deficit at 15.0 leaves us exposed not just to disruption but to denial; we must now build sovereign capability that is not only present but provably safe and operationally resilient, starting where failure is least tolerable.

## In practice
We are transitioning from infrastructure promises to operational sovereignty. The completion of the emergency compute permitting and Cyber Shield coordination measures marks a threshold: the legal and institutional scaffolding for sovereign control is now in place. But with southern member states still blocking grid access, physical deployment remains stalled, and the U.S. election has turned access into a lever of foreign policy. We can no longer wait for conditions to align—we must act from weakness. 

Thus, we are launching a new category 4 measure to build sovereign AI agents from the ground up, starting in health, energy, and emergency response—domains where failure is catastrophic but where the EU has direct operational authority. Using the InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package as financial backbones, we will fund a new public-private consortium under the Digital Europe Programme, hosted by DG CONNECT and ENISA, to develop open-weight models trained under strict interpretability and goal-fidelity constraints. These models will be audited by a newly empowered EU AI Safety Board and deployed only in air-gapped or high-assurance environments. The programme will be legally anchored in Article 114 TFEU for internal market coherence and Article 172 for industrial policy, with co-funding from the Recovery and Resilience Facility to bypass national resistance. We are offering member states opt-in pathways with guaranteed local compute allocation and workforce training, turning resistance into participation. This is not about matching the frontier—it is about creating a trusted, resilient, and EU-controlled alternative that can function when the global network fails or excludes us.
