## New measure
**EU Automated Decision Redress and Logging Audit**
It creates an EU-level independent audit and victim-redress track for the social-insurance/policing decision scandal, with mandatory log disclosure, case-by-case review and compensation funded by deploying authorities.
Why this and why now: the scandal reads as the second, more damaging failure — the deployment sat in a gap the AI Act's 2024 Annex III categories do not reach, so everything done was lawful — and only a visible redress plus a fix to the Act's scope can stop restriction becoming the cheap answer and adoption becoming impossible for years while the solo hyperscaler split fractures cohesion.
