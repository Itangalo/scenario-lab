### Holding the line with nothing to spend
Brussels spent the first half of 2032 trying not to lose more ground. With coffers empty and trust exhausted, the Commission kept a single programme running: keeping clinics open under wartime rationing and paying out the first compensation cheques to people cut off by the automated benefits system.

On paper it held. Health directorates stretched stockpiled tailored treatments a few weeks further by triaging strictly on clinical need. Triage assistants kept running on isolated domestic servers. The new independent ombudsman opened its doors, took testimony in three capitals, and issued its first payments to families sanctioned by seconds-long reviews. The AI Office began reading system logs with powers to suspend deployments.

In practice, delivery slipped. The redress helpline was overwhelmed within days. Mayors complained rationing committees were making life-and-death choices without cover from Brussels. Emergency guards stayed posted at the two burned grid-connection sites for the gigafactories, but construction did not restart. Entry-level hiring did not recover.

Then came the biosecurity scare. A contested paper claimed a genome model had helped produce a viable design for a human-infecting organism — a claim other scientists called alarmist and reckless to publish. The argument stayed inside specialist circles, but it rattled health ministries already short of detection capacity.

The Commission chose not to open a new instrument. Instead it quietly asked disease-control and cyber agencies and its bio-defence labs to rehearse detection and isolation procedures under existing mandates, and used its observer seat in the middle-power talks to push Washington and Beijing for verification on biological design tools. Little was offered in return.

By June, relief at kept-open clinics was again curdling into resentment: the cures, the models, the verification — all visibly came from elsewhere. Restriction remained cheap politics; adoption stalled further. The care-redress regime survived as the only spend, but with no money to expand it, cohesion frayed at the edges.
