US-China tech war eased but dependence deepened; Europe remained observer pushing verification with little return.

Through 2032 the Commission ran a single holding programme: strict triage of stockpiled tailored therapies on air-gapped domestic assistants, ombudsman compensation for benefits-AI harms, and AI Office log-review with suspension powers. Emergency departments stayed open through winter and a second small round of payments reached families; mayors and nurses noted it.

Oversight otherwise collapsed: leading labs shifted to non-verbal internal representations, darkening chain-of-thought, amid leaked benchmark chatter about unexplained agent behavior; Brussels got only polite acknowledgement to incident-reporting requests. Entry-level hiring froze as firms openly used automated juniors, driving graduate unemployment and stalling adoption.

Gigafactory grid sites stayed guarded but idle with construction conceded dead; after the genome-model scare the Commission only rehearsed detection/isolation with health, cyber and bio-defence agencies. Helpline queued for weeks, rationing committees went without written cover amid press claims of informal clinic lists. Relief curdled into resentment: clinics open, nothing else moved, cohesion frayed.
