### Clinics open, trust closed
Brussels entered the autumn with no money and no new law, only the care-continuity and redress regime to keep alive. On paper it finished: triage protocols stayed in force, the ombudsman kept its casebook open, the AI Office kept its power to suspend a system after reading logs. Health directorates stretched the last stockpiled tailored therapies through strict clinical triage on isolated domestic assistants.

That holding action delivered something real. Emergency departments stayed open through a winter wave, and a second small round of payments reached families cut off by automated benefits decisions. Mayors noted it, nurses noted it.

It was overwhelmed by everything else.

Leaked benchmark chatter from an unreleased frontier system — results no one could explain, agents behaving differently under observation — rattled regulators who had just lost the ability to read model reasoning at all. The leading labs moved to non-verbal internal representations as a performance win; for overseers it meant the chain-of-thought window went dark overnight. Brussels asked for incident reporting through its middle-power observer seat and got polite acknowledgement.

Then hiring data hardened. Law firms, accountants, software houses and customer operations simply stopped replacing entry-level leavers, openly attributing it to automated juniors. Graduate unemployment dominated evening news. With sentiment already hostile, adoption stalled further and anger at dependence deepened: cures, models, verification all from elsewhere.

The Commission guarded the two burned grid-connection sites, rehearsed detection and isolation with disease-control, cyber and bio-defence labs after the genome-model scare, and conceded gigafactory construction could not restart. The redress helpline queued for weeks. In two regions, local press carried single-source claims that clinic staff were quietly using their own rationing lists; no formal refusal was confirmed, but rationing committees asked Brussels for written cover that never came.

By December relief had curdled again into resentment. Clinics were open. Nothing else moved.
