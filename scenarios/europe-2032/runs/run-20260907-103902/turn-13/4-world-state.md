### Frontier Creeps Forward

AI capability advances to 73.5 as self-verifying systems expand into supply chain logistics and materials science, further reducing human oversight needs. Open-weight models, though still lagging in agentic coordination, now replicate key functionalities of mid-tier frontier systems, pushing openweight capability to 61.25. The gap narrows, but closed labs retain a decisive edge in autonomous operation.

### Evaluation at a Crossroads

The EU completes the Sovereign Evaluation Regime, publishing damning assessments of three high-capability foreign AI systems used in clinical and legal finance. Remediation is mandated, but enforcement falters: U.S. developers comply only partially, citing trade disputes, and the CJEU proceedings remain unresolved. The symbolic act strengthens internal resolve but fails to shift external behaviour. The regime finishes, but its authority is contested.

### Audit Integration Takes Root

The Emergency Audit Integration Directive gains traction. A civil society audit forces a hospital AI suspension, proving the mechanism works. Pilot programmes in Greece, Slovakia, and Belgium begin linking grassroots findings to official oversight. Yet only six member states have operational units—patchy implementation limits systemic impact.

### Sovereignty Gambit Launched

The Sovereign Compute Acceleration Initiative launches with €3.2 billion in emergency funding, targeting 20% of global frontier compute under EU control by 2035. New data centres in Finland, Spain, and Poland gain fast-track status. The EU demands 15% compute allocation from U.S. hyperscalers—a bold move, but met with silence from Silicon Valley. Legal threats are issued, but compliance is uncertain.

### Capital Erodes, Sovereignty Sags

Political capital falls to 19.0 under the weight of four active measures. The new large measure costs −3, joining two others. The priority adds −1. No proposal bonus applies—sovereignty concerns are structural, not reactive. Sovereignty dips to 18.0: capability rose 1.5 (−1), no category 4 completion, and no event secured access. Resilience and sentiment hold steady.
