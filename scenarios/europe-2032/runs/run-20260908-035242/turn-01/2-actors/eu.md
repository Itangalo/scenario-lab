## In practice
We are launching the Emergency Resilience Surge through the Union Civil Protection Mechanism, activating the rescEU reserve for cyber and bio-capacity deployment. The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) will coordinate a binding directive by February 2027 requiring all critical energy, water, and port operators to implement AI-driven intrusion detection with red-teaming mandates, funded through a €30bn reallocation from the Digital Europe Programme and NextGenerationEU recovery funds. Simultaneously, the European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control will expand its horizon-scanning to include synthetic biology threats, integrating AI-enabled genomic screening at all major DNA synthesis hubs under a new regulation.

Resistance is expected from member states with weaker fiscal positions and from private operators who fear cost and operational disruption. We will counter with a 75% EU co-financing rate for the first two years and fast-track access to InvestAI compute for compliant firms. The Council’s Internal Market and Telecommunications configuration will be the lead venue, but we are preparing to invoke the unanimity override under Article 122 TFEU if delays threaten systemic exposure. This is not about catching up — it is about ensuring that when the next incident comes, it does not cascade into collapse.
