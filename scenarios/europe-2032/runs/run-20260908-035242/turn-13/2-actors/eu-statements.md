# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI capacity to act with autonomy in a world where control lags behind capability
- `new_two_year_commitment` (commitment): Rebuild political capital and public trust by anchoring AI governance in tangible social protection, without surrendering strategic autonomy
- `emergent_crisis_of_legitimacy` (commitment): We are responsible for maintaining the credibility of EU action in the face of accelerating technological disruption, and will treat erosion of public trust as a systemic threat on par with cyber or bio incidents.
- `cyber_defence_as_sovereignty` (commitment): We treat the integrity of critical infrastructure as the first line of EU strategic sovereignty, and will prioritise defensive resilience over offensive capability or regulatory theatre when systems fail.
