In this vacuum, we cannot afford to wait. This is not expansion; it is triage. Without it, the Gigafactories and the Compute Assurance Directive will have nothing to stand on.

The Sovereign Incident Response Corps remains our priority, now more than ever. We are fast-tracking Council approval of the Crisis Response Protocol through Article 122 TFEU, invoking the public health emergency clause to bypass legal gridlock. This leverage is real, but narrow. We are using it to secure chip supply for the Gigafactories, knowing that without it, sovereignty remains theoretical. The world is moving fast. We are no longer building for the future—we are holding the present together.
