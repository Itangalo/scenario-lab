### New measure
**Rebuild Public Trust through Targeted Flexicurity**  
Launch a narrowly focused, fast-deploying social transition programme to stabilize public sentiment and reclaim political initiative by pairing retraining with wage insurance for public-sector workers displaced by AI automation.

`Category: 7 (Labour and social protection)`  
`Size: small`  
`Finishes on turn: 12`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase public_sentiment by 15–20 points and resilience by 5–10 points through demonstrated responsiveness to labour displacement  
`Applies to:` EU member states, with initial rollout in Germany, France, and Spain where strike activity is most acute

### Priority
**Rebuild Public Trust through Targeted Flexicurity** — because with political capital at 7.0 and public sentiment at 15.0, the Union is on the verge of systemic ungovernability, and no amount of technical sovereignty or incident response can function without a minimum of social peace and institutional credibility.

### In practice
We are out of room to fail. The Sovereign Incident Response Corps has reached operational readiness, but its credibility hinges on whether member states will actually cede control during a crisis — and right now, Germany and Poland are refusing even to co-lead the first joint exercise. Without trust, enforcement is fiction. The Emergency Compute Stabilisation Facility is preserving critical infrastructure, but in Bavaria and Wallonia, data centres are being guarded by national police, not EU envoys, and the narrative of AI as an occupying force is winning. We cannot build sovereign capacity if no one will host it.

So we are shifting focus: this turn, we anchor our survival in the social contract. The new measure draws €8.7bn from the unspent balance of the Emergency Compute Stabilisation Facility — a reallocation approved under Article 32 of the European Recovery Instrument, with Commission legal services confirming the flexibility. The programme targets public-sector workers in digital services, local administration, and education support roles where AI automation has eliminated entry points for graduates. It offers two-year wage insurance at 80% of previous salary, paired with retraining in AI-augmented public service delivery, hosted through EIT Digital and national civil service academies. We are offering national co-funding at 50% to secure buy-in: Germany gets to design its own curriculum, France its own delivery rhythm, Spain its own regional rollout.

This is not the full flexicurity vision — it cannot be, not with capital this low — but it is a signal: AI governance will not be only about kill switches and compute. If we do not show people that the Union protects them, not just its own institutions, the next strike will be the last one we can ignore.
