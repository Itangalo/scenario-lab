### The Speed of Threat, the Weight of Action

Frontier AI capability advances to 56.0 as labs integrate autonomous research loops into core development, accelerating model refinement. Open-weight models reach 43.0, buoyed by accessible tooling that enables non-experts to repurpose defensive tools for offensive use. Despite this surge, no systemic safety improvements land on deployed systems: known anomalies in agent behaviour persist, and the gap between capability and control widens, dragging `ai_safety` down to 28.0.

The EU responds with urgency. Citing recent cyber disruptions and emerging biosecurity signals, Brussels launches the **EU Sovereign Agent Framework**—a large category 4 measure aimed at building a trusted, EU-controlled autonomous agent stack for critical infrastructure. Designed to run on sovereign compute in Finland and Luxembourg, it promises real-time oversight and rapid response. Member states are offered veto rights and audit access to secure buy-in, while developers gain a “sovereign sandbox” for testing.

However, political costs mount. With three large measures already in flight—including the ASML export framework and InvestAI Gigafactories—the portfolio charge is steep. The Emergency Resilience Surge concludes, delivering +3 to resilience (now 47.0) for containing prior cascades. But launching another major initiative strains cohesion. The Council delays the vote until Q1 2028, citing legal concerns over Article 114 TFEU’s applicability to security functions. Internal Market ministers question whether mandating agent standards truly falls under internal market rules.

Public sentiment holds at 37.0, unmoved by technical assurances. Media focus remains on job losses in legal and administrative sectors, now amplified by union warnings of cross-border strike action. U.S. officials privately warn that any EU agent system with dual-use potential may face scrutiny under future export controls, further chilling investor confidence.

### Sovereignty Gains, Capital Falters

Despite setbacks, `eu_ai_sovereignty` rises slightly to 23.0—gaining from early momentum in the new agent framework—offsetting the -1 decay from rising capability. But `eu_political_capital` drops to 31.0 under sustained portfolio pressure and unresolved tensions.
