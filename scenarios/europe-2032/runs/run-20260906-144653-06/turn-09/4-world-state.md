### Containment and Cost

The turn closes with partial success and mounting strain. The **Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative** formally completed deployment in France, Finland, and the Netherlands, demonstrating early value by autonomously repelling two coordinated intrusion attempts during Q1 drills. Germany’s participation was secured through last-minute RRF co-financing, but Poland and Hungary remain outside the framework, their resistance hardened by legal challenges and political friction over Cohesion Fund conditionality.

The **Emergency Biosecurity Watch**, maintained as the named priority, expanded to audit non-institutional research following the emergence of a decentralized AI research collective. Joint EAAD-ECDC directives now require reporting on any pathogen-related computational experiments, but jurisdictional overreach concerns persist. Europol is investigating resource provenance chains, yet enforcement remains reactive and legally tenuous.

The **Pan-European Adjustment Mechanism** was formally proposed, aiming to establish a portable social protection floor funded by a 5% levy on firms with AI-driven productivity gains above 30%. Though framed as a response to recent sectoral strikes, it stalled in inter-service consultation. Member states raised concerns over harmonisation, and corporate opposition to certification complexity remains firm. No implementation occurred.

### Capability and Exposure

`ai_capability` rose by 1.5, continuing its steady climb. The **openweight_frontier_release** event dramatically accelerated open diffusion: a powerful open-weight model was downloaded hundreds of thousands of times within days, pushing `openweight_capability` up sharply. This leap widened the gap between capability and safety, as no new safeguards landed on shipped systems. The frontier’s pace outstripped assurance, and `ai_safety` eroded further.

Resilience improved slightly as the Cyber Shield’s completion delivered tangible benefits, absorbing more harm from cyber threats. However, sovereignty declined due to continued compute dependence and no sovereign capacity coming online. Political capital remained stagnant under the weight of three large measures, with no relief from completed initiatives.

Public sentiment held at 22.0, reflecting persistent anxiety over job displacement and institutional overreach.
