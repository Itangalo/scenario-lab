### The Weight of Survival

The EU entered 2029’s second half under duress. The **Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative** advanced in fragments: emergency deployments in France, Finland, and the Netherlands established AI-audited defences in critical health and grid systems, but legal resistance from Poland, Hungary, and Germany stalled full rollout. Infringement procedures were launched, yet cohesion frayed — sovereignty without unity proved brittle.

The **InvestAI Gigafactories** programme officially concluded, but with only partial site approvals and no integrated defence layer, its impact remained latent. Final disbursements were frozen, tying funding to cyber-readiness, a move that underscored strategic recalibration but delayed momentum.

A more urgent response followed the **bio_uplift_findings**, where a genome model nearly crossed into dangerous territory. The EU activated the **Emergency Biosecurity Watch**, leveraging EAAD and ECDC to mandate reporting on high-risk research. Though not a pause, it established a live detection grid — a first in Europe. Simultaneously, EIB transition loans began redirecting displaced legal and accounting professionals into bio-monitoring roles, attempting to convert backlash into resilience.

The **export_control_escalation** from the US further tightened chip access. While the EU leveraged its semiconductor supply-chain position through Chip4+ to secure limited volume licences, frontier compute capacity narrowed. This, combined with rising AI capability, deepened dependency anxieties.

Public sentiment held at 22.0, unmoved by emergency measures. Strikes in professional sectors intensified, now coordinated across borders, exposing the limits of reactive policy. The narrative of AI as a destabilising force solidified in media and streets.

Yet, a rare gain emerged: the **safety_breakthrough** in interpretability allowed real-time prediction of model behaviour, rapidly adopted by leading labs. For the first time, assurance kept pace with automation — a quiet but pivotal shift.

### Triage Over Transformation

The EU’s pivot to *survival_first* reflected not surrender, but realism. Sovereignty was no longer a timeline — it was a condition being fought for daily, in courtrooms, data centres, and labs.
