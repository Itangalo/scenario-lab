## Priority
**Priority:** Launch Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative  
This remains our dominant operational priority because, despite its delays, it is the only measure actively countering the autonomous cyber threats already in motion — and without network resilience, all other capabilities, sovereign or not, will collapse under coordinated attack.

## In practice
We are treating this turn as one of triage. The Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative is now legally contested by three member states, yet we are moving forward in parallel: ENISA is deploying interim stacks under Article 122 TFEU emergency provisions to health and grid operators in France, Finland, and the Netherlands, using EIB-reprofiled funds that bypass national vetoes. These deployments include AI-driven patch validation and swarm-based intrusion detection trained on the very exploit chains that caused the prior incident. Germany’s cost-sharing demands are being met with a co-financing model from the Recovery and Resilience Facility’s unused envelopes, while Poland and Hungary face infringement procedures for non-cooperation — a cost we accept to prevent paralysis.

The InvestAI Gigafactories, though officially finished, delivered only partial siting progress. Final disbursements are now withheld pending integration with the Cyber Shield’s monitoring layer — no sovereign compute without sovereign defence. Meanwhile, the bio_uplift_findings event has forced our hand: we cannot wait for consensus on biological risk. Under DG SANTE leadership, we are activating the Emergency Biosecurity Watch using EAAD’s audit authority and ECDC’s surveillance backbone, mandating reporting from any model or lab that performs human-infective organism design. This is not a research pause — it is a live detection grid. We are also fast-tracking displaced professionals into these roles via EIB transition loans, attempting to turn backlash into capacity. The export_control_escalation from the US has tightened access to advanced chips, but we are leveraging our upstream semiconductor investments to negotiate volume licences under coalition terms — a move coordinated through the newly formed Chip4+ forum. Survival is no longer abstract. It is daily.
