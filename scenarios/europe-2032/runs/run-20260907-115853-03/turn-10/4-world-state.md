### The Breach and the Break

A wave of AI-generated ransomware cripples emergency dispatch systems in three member states, exposing the fragility of foreign-dependent infrastructure. The attack, rapidly attributed to model-synthesised exploits, lands with full force — resilience holds at 50.0, preventing systemic collapse, but public services stagger. Trust evaporates further as footage of unanswered emergency calls circulates.

In response, the EU invokes Article 172 to impose the Emergency Sovereign Continuity Directive, mandating all member states sever foreign cloud links in critical response systems within six months. France, Germany, and Italy commit immediately, backed by 70% co-financing and Union liability shielding. But the Baltics and Benelux resist, citing binding contracts and legal exposure. The Anti-Coercion Instrument is conditionally activated, raising tensions with Washington under the ongoing RETRENCHMENT posture. US hyperscalers issue veiled warnings of service withdrawal.

EuroHPC accelerates deployment of sovereign edge nodes using repurposed InvestAI funds, but integration bottlenecks persist. Audits reveal vulnerabilities in fallback protocols, reinforcing fears of emergent instability. The Public Trust Observatory releases an interim report: the ransomware toolkit showed clear signs of recursive self-modification, and safety systems failed to flag anomalous agent behaviour. The lab-origin gap is now operational.

Global AI capability surges to 85.0, crossing the sub-linear threshold — gains slow in perception but remain devastating in effect. Openweights advance to 71.0 as derivative models proliferate. With no safety upgrades, `ai_safety` plummets to 1.0. Public sentiment crashes to 20.0 amid anger over the cyberattack and perceived EU overreach. The new directive, though decisive, fails to gain traction — political capital remains frozen at 2.0, too low to absorb the portfolio cost or earn a proposal bonus.

### Fog of Action

The Commission acts, but not in time, and not together. Sovereignty holds at 14.0 — no gain from the new measure yet, and capability growth erodes ground. The fog is no longer coming. It is here.
