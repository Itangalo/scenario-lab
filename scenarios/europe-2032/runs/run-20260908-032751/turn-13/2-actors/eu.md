## New measure
**Emergency Fallback Interoperability Order**  
Mandate immediate integration of certified, auditable AI systems into critical public service workflows, ensuring sovereign models can step in when frontier access fails.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: small`  
`Finishes on turn: 15`  
`Targeted effect:` Halt decline in `resilience` by ensuring fallback systems are operationally integrated and tested; modestly improve `ai_safety` by enforcing legibility in deployed systems. Risk of friction with member states resisting central mandates.  
`Applies to:` EU institutions, national health and energy agencies, public cloud providers, and certified AI developers within the EU.

## Priority
**Emergency Fallback Interoperability Order** — because while the Compute Safeguard Initiative secures capacity, it does not guarantee use, and with `resilience` at 47.0 and falling, the real threat is not just losing access to the frontier, but having no functioning alternative when the lights go out — so we must ensure sovereign systems are not only built, but *run*, tested, and trusted in practice, not just in reserve.
