### Accelerating Frontier, Lagging Safeguards  
The global AI frontier advanced steadily in the second half of 2026, with leading labs reporting sustained progress across reasoning depth and system autonomy. The most capable models now operate continuously for days on end, pursuing open-ended goals with minimal human oversight. This turn’s +2.5 increase in `ai_capability` reflects that momentum, and the trajectory remains consistent with steady advancement toward the 88+ terminal zone.

### Open Weights, Closed Risks  
The release of Moonshot’s Kimi K3 marked a significant development: for the first time, offensive cyber capabilities previously confined to closed models are now openly available. This widens the threat surface, as demonstrated by the **cyber_test_shot** intrusion discovered across critical infrastructure operators. The breach, undetected for weeks and found only by accident, revealed systemic blind spots. In response, the EU initiated planning for **Cyber Shield for Critical Operators (CS-CO)**, a large Category 6 measure aimed at hardening essential services. However, given existing commitments to InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package—both still ongoing—resources remain stretched. No new implementation began this turn; instead, CS-CO entered inter-institutional consultation, with formal adoption deferred pending fiscal clearance. The +4.5 rise in `openweight_capability` reflects diffusion and repurposing of existing open models under permissive licenses, not a leap beyond frontier capability.

### Governance Under Strain  
The **automated_decision_scandal**—a high-profile ruling against an AI-supported social insurance system for systematic bias—triggered public backlash. The incident, rooted in domestic deployment, dealt a sharp blow to public trust. Sentiment fell, and political capital eroded under the weight of internal failure. Meanwhile, the EU’s compute sovereignty weakened: `ai_capability` rose by more than 2, and no new sovereign capacity came online, while American export controls tightened. The **emergent_euv_lithography_shortage** and **emergent_ai_research_brain_drain** intensified, signaling deeper structural challenges.
