### Signs of Acceleration

Signals from the frontier indicate a tightening model release cycle, with updates arriving faster than in previous turns. While not yet confirmed as a systemic shift, the pace of advancement raises concerns among monitoring agencies. `ai_capability` increases by +2.5, consistent with the established rate of progress observed since turn 1. There is no evidence of a sudden compounding regime or recursive self-improvement onset; the trend remains within the fixed, unannounced global velocity defined from the beginning.

A major cyber incident strikes: AI-generated ransomware exploits zero-day vulnerabilities across public health and energy networks in three member states. Recovery is slow, and the attack exposes systemic fragility. With no category 6 measure still active, `resilience` drops to 47.0.

Open-weight capability rises modestly to 69.5, supported by incremental optimization of existing open models and limited knowledge transfer from recently published (but not frontier) architectures. The gain reflects sustained development effort, not a leap from distillation of cutting-edge closed models. The narrative of divergence continues—access to the latest frontier systems remains blocked, and no new mechanism for acquiring them has emerged.

The EU completes the **Sovereign Interpretability Certification Framework**, a category 5 measure. However, its impact is null: no finished safety measure affects shipped systems outside the EU, and internal assurance gains are swamped by external collapse. No `ai_safety` boost is granted.

Discussions begin on an emergency compute safeguard initiative in response to the widening access gap and recent attacks. However, no formal launch occurs. Ireland and the Netherlands express strong reservations about centralized control and cost-sharing, blocking consensus. Without agreement on governance or funding, the proposal cannot advance under Article 122, and no procurement waivers are issued. The **Emergency Compute Safeguard Initiative** remains in drafting, with no implementation this turn.

As a result, no new measure is added to the portfolio. The **Sovereign Compute Acceleration Directive** remains the sole active measure. There is no additional portfolio charge beyond what was already accrued. The priority remains unchanged.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` holds at 8.0: −1 from capability growth (over 2.0) and −1 from ongoing access denial (no event this turn, but dependence remains), offset by +2 from the completion of the certification framework, which strengthens internal evaluation capacity. The completed measure does not confer immediate operational independence but improves foundational oversight.

`eu_political_capital` remains at 8.0. The portfolio charge for the turn is −3 (large measure ongoing, no new proposal). There is no proposal bonus, as no new measure was introduced. No legitimacy lending occurs.

Public sentiment crashes to 17.0 as job losses accelerate and critical services falter. The mood turns hostile.
