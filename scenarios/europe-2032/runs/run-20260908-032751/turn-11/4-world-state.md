### A Surge Against the Tide

The EU launches the **Emergency Resilience Surge for Democratic Integrity**, a small category 6 measure, invoking Article 222 TFEU to treat synthetic media manipulation as a cross-border threat. ENISA deploys detection stacks to national cyber incident response teams, and takedown protocols are mandated for large platforms. A Eurojust-hosted judicial task force begins fast-tracking evidence in election challenges. The response is rapid, but uneven: French systems integrate the tools within days, while Poland’s fragmented digital infrastructure delays deployment by weeks.

The deepfake wave persists, but its momentum is checked. The annulment of the French regional election in Île-de-France — the first since the crisis began — is narrowly avoided after courts accept newly verified metadata trails. In Poland, however, petitions to annul local results gain traction, feeding public anger. Still, the containment effort prevents cascading institutional collapse, halting the erosion of resilience.

Meanwhile, the **Sovereign Compute Acceleration Directive** remains stalled. Despite diplomatic overtures, Ireland and the Netherlands withhold full consent, citing unresolved legal concerns over land sovereignty and energy allocation. No construction begins. The middle_power_coalition’s leverage proves symbolic, not material.

AI capability advances to 80.5, driven by self-improving training loops. With no safety breakthrough and reasoning still opaque, `ai_safety` holds at 1.0. Open-weight capability rises to 67.0 through improved distillation, but remains far behind the frontier.

The medical breakthrough — AI-designed gene therapies entering clinical use — lifts public sentiment. However, the models behind them are inaccessible to EU sovereign systems, tempering the gain. Sentiment rises to 27.0, reflecting cautious hope amid dependency.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 8.0, hit by capability growth (−1) and the denial of frontier access (−2), which lands with full force as hospitals lose real-time diagnostic AI. The Sovereign Compute Directive, still in flight, yields no momentum. Political capital drops to 8.0 under a −7 portfolio charge, despite a +3 proposal bonus for the emergency measure. No sentiment-driven boost applies.
