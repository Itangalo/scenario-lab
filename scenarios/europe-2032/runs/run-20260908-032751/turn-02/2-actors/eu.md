## New measure
**Public AI Governance Rebuild**  
Launch a pan-European audit and reform programme for AI in public services, starting with social insurance and healthcare, to restore trust and establish enforceable standards for fairness, transparency and redress.

`Category: 9 (Diffusion, adoption and public trust)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 5`  
`Targeted effect:` Increases `public_sentiment` by +10–15 points and reduces misuse risk in high-impact domains; complements InvestAI and CS-CO by ensuring domestic AI is not only sovereign but trusted.  
`Applies to:` EU member states’ national administrations deploying AI in social services, with conditionality tied to EU funding and oversight by the European Data Protection Board.

## Priority
**Public AI Governance Rebuild** — this must be our priority because the *automated_decision_scandal* has shattered public confidence, and without trust, no amount of sovereign compute or cyber resilience will sustain political support for AI investment — our own legitimacy is now on the line.

## In practice
We are launching the Public AI Governance Rebuild through a Regulation under Article 16 of the GDPR, empowered by the European Data Protection Board and co-implemented with Eurostat and national audit offices. The first phase will target AI systems in unemployment benefits, disability assessment and healthcare triage — domains where error has the deepest human cost and where the scandal revealed systemic flaws in validation and appeal. We are mandating third-party algorithmic audits, real-time bias monitoring, and a right to human review that is actually accessible — not just on paper. Funding of €15bn will come from the Social Climate Fund and Recovery and Resilience Facility, with disbursement tied to verified reform progress.

Resistance is expected from member states that see this as an infringement on administrative sovereignty, particularly those with underfunded systems already struggling to adapt. To overcome this, we are offering full co-financing for audit capacity building and embedding EU technical teams directly into national ministries — not to replace, but to enable. We are also fast-tracking a model registry through the EDPB to make compliance visible and to create peer pressure among member states. This is not just damage control; it is the foundation of a trustworthy AI state. Without it, every future initiative — sovereign or not — will face suspicion, delay, and revolt.
