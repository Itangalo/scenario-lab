### Stalled Sovereignty, Rising Friction
The Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive formally fails to pass, marking a symbolic defeat for EU ambitions to mandate access to frontier compute. In its place, the Commission launches the Sovereignty through Legitimacy Initiative, a bid to overcome local resistance to data centres by tying fast-track permitting and InvestAI funding to community benefits. Pilots begin in eastern Germany, western Poland, and southern Ireland, but early reactions are mixed: local officials welcome the flexibility, yet environmental groups denounce the scheme as “greenwashing by contract.” Protests persist, and regional authorities remain cautious.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Cracks
Despite signs of a plateau in market pricing, AI capability advances to 56.0 as labs compress training cycles and deploy more efficient architectures. However, no new safety protocols are embedded in shipped systems. Internal developer reports confirm growing opacity in agent reasoning, especially in long-horizon tasks. With no finished safety measure, ai_safety drops to 27.0.

### Resilience Loses Ground
The Resilience Shield Programme progresses technically—ENISA finalises detection standards and runs the first red-team exercise—but political resistance intensifies. Several member states delay implementation, citing cost and sovereignty concerns. As ai_capability rises without counterbalancing defences, resilience declines to 35.0.

### Political Capital Erodes Further
Three large measures remain in flight, including the new Sovereignty through Legitimacy Initiative. The portfolio charge remains heavy, and with no measure completing, no completion bonus is earned. The priority adds an extra cost. eu_political_capital falls to 20.0, approaching the threshold where agenda control weakens.

### Public Sentiment Turns Negative
Visible opposition to AI infrastructure grows. Media coverage frames data centres as energy-hungry enclaves benefiting foreign tech firms, not local communities. The Sovereignty through Legitimacy Initiative is seen as reactive, not transformative. public_sentiment dips to 40.0, reflecting rising unease.
