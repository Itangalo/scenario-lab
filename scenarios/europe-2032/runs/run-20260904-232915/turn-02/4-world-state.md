### Accelerating Capability, Eroding Safety
AI capability advances to 55.0 in the first half of 2027, driven by compressed release cycles and intensified training runs. The frontier continues to outpace governance, and with no new safety measures yet embedded in deployed systems, assurance declines further. Human oversight remains critical, but developers report growing difficulty in interpreting agent behaviour, especially in long-horizon planning tasks. The gap between capability and control widens, contributing to a drop in ai_safety to 29.0.

### Sovereignty Under Strain
The Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive remains locked in tense trilogue negotiations. While the Commission offers concessions—phased compliance and conditional access to the InvestAI fund—resistance from Germany, Ireland, and several Central European states persists. Fears of US retaliation and capital flight dampen political appetite. The directive fails to pass this turn, delaying its finishing date. Meanwhile, no new sovereign compute capacity comes online, and the EU’s dependence on foreign-controlled infrastructure deepens as capability rises. eu_ai_sovereignty falls to 19.0, reflecting both the capability surge and stalled progress.

### Resilience Begins to Rebuild
In response to the recent cyber intrusion, the EU launches the Resilience Shield Programme. Binding standards for AI-enabled detection systems are proposed, and ENISA begins drafting protocols for cross-border red-teaming. However, implementation is slow: member states resist new mandates, and funding remains contingent on future approvals. The programme begins to exert partial effect, but not enough to reverse the resilience decline caused by rising threats and inaction.

### Political Capital Erodes
With three large measures in flight—including the now-delayed Sovereign Compute Directive—and the priority still active, political capital is heavily strained. No measure completes, so no completion bonus is earned. The portfolio charge remains high, and eu_political_capital drops to 28.0, edging toward the threshold where agenda control weakens.

### Public Mood Holds
Public sentiment remains steady at 42.0. While the cyber incident raised concern, no major scandal or visible job displacement occurs. The Resilience Shield is welcomed by experts but draws little public attention.
