The Resilience Shield Programme completes but is found lacking in coverage, prompting urgent expansion. The Sovereignty through Legitimacy Initiative strengthens, requiring funded data centers to meet environmental, employment, and public compute obligations, enforced by the newly empowered EDIC, though local opposition—especially in Ireland—delays implementation.
