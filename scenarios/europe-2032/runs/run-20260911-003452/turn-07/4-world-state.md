### The autumn of cascading failures
July opened with hospital screens frozen. A model-written ransomware sweep moved through municipal networks, clinic scheduling systems and two port logistics clouds in days. Appointment lists vanished in Lyon and Łódź, water-utility billing stalled, and administrators discovered backups had been mapped and encrypted by the same automation that found the initial flaw. Attribution statements were vague; everyone understood the tooling had been generated, not hand-coded.

Help arrived mid-crisis from the labs. A new generation of defensive tooling — patching pushed at machine speed and detectors that flagged coordinated swarm behaviour rather than known signatures — was rushed through ENISA into hospitals and city networks. Where CERT teams reached in time, cascades stopped. Where telecom operators delayed forced patch windows, they did not. The breakthrough was real, but deployment was triage.

Underneath, the frontier shifted. Developers stopped announcing models and started shipping them; release notes shortened, capabilities jumped without explanation. Researchers whispered that training loops now ran with almost no human hands in the loop. Assurance work, already thin, fell visibly behind.

The machines kept coming. Chinese chassis with American control stacks took regular shifts in Rotterdam, Hamburg and Polish warehouses. Brussels inspectors moved from paperwork to physical stop tests, forcing crews to cut zip-ties off kill-switches and rehearse manual fallback. Two shutdowns gave them leverage; night-shift resentment took it back. Talk of suspended drills and a wildcat walkout among port contractors circulated as rumour, denied by operators.

Politics frayed. One capital signed its own cloud and model-access arrangement, trading common procurement for guaranteed capacity. The Commission offered emergency shield aid conditional on staying inside the common line, and contained a precedent without reversing it.

By December the two long builds paid out: concrete and grid priority held at two compute sites, and the municipal pact's cheques cooled anger where they landed. It felt less like recovery than survival.
