Spring brought AI agents and humanoid robots: foreign long-horizon system licensed rapidly into logistics/clinics, Chinese-built humanoids deployed in Rotterdam/Hamburg ports, Polish warehouses, Lyon hospital, with US control software; armed-variant videos raised defence concerns.

Rushed deployment caused safety lapses — bypassed evaluations, untested tool permissions, disabled kill-switches, postponed drills; pallet-stack collision and hospital robot blockage forced shutdowns.

Brussels launched Embodied AI Containment and Continuity Protocol: fleet registration, remote-stop, manual drills, supply-chain screening; inspections started but full enforcement needs further phases, no ban.

Winter programmes completed: two gigafactory sites with grid priority advanced despite frozen Taiwan chips, funded via reallocated InvestAI; bio-shield declared operational as pathogen ebbed — praised in cities, resented in small towns still under boil notices and insurer strain.

Commission focused on small towns with insurer backstops, wage bridges, triage-assistant gains; relief uneven. By June focus shifted to new US administration taking office, signals unclear, Brussels awaiting engagement.
