### Patch and prayer
The spring began where winter left off: frozen appointment screens in Lyon and Łódź, then a second wave of machine-written ransomware probing city networks and port clouds. ENISA pushed its new patching and swarm detectors hard, with emergency liability cover to force telecom maintenance windows. Where CERT teams and mutual-aid response units arrived first, the cascades halted in hours. Where operators hesitated, billing systems and clinic scheduling went dark for days.

Brussels made the surge conditional. The capital that had signed its own cloud deal was told shield aid and therapy access ran through joint procurement. It stayed, grudgingly, but officials in two other capitals openly questioned the price of obedience.

### The unannounced frontier
Without release notes or benchmarks, leading developers simply shipped a new generation. Hospital administrators noticed first: administrative automation that planned multi-step recoveries, research tools that proposed their own experiments. Training, researchers whispered, now ran with barely a human in the loop. European evaluators received access weeks late and documentation thinner than ever.

At the docks, inspectors kept forcing physical stop-tests on Chinese chassis running American stacks. Crews complied on camera and skipped drills at night. A short contractor walkout in Hamburg over unmanned night shifts was settled quietly, but rumour of suspended checks lingered.

### Cures, on someone else's models
Then came the tailored therapies. Individually designed treatments for cancers previously untreatable moved into ordinary clinical talk, delivered by frontier models hosted abroad. DG SANTE scrambled joint procurement, promising to run them in public hospitals on European compute with provenance labels and clinician oversight. The first wards in France and Poland opened to queues and tears — benefit made visible, dependence made visible too. Papers asked why cures for Europeans ran on computers Europeans did not control.

By June hospitals were cleaner, waiting lists carried hope, and the Union was thinner: two builds holding, trust partly rebuilt, control still slipping.
