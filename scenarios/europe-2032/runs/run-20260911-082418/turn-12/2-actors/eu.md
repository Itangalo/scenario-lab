## New measure
**EU Essential Services Continuity Fallback**
Keeps hospitals, ministries and firms hit by the frontier cutoff running on audited EU-hosted and open-weight models with pooled telemetry, locked backups and local maintenance, under the existing liability and logging rules.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, category 6, and why now is that access-denied plus a member-state side-deal turns dependence into outage and fragmentation at once, while the US-China floor makes shared assurance fundable — continuity must be demonstrated before defection spreads.

## Priority
M13 EU Verified Work Assistant Rollout — no, the new Fallback: with frontier access cut and a member state undercutting the line, preventing hospitals and ministries going dark outranks our own rollout direction this turn.

## In practice
We run the Fallback through ENISA, CERT-EU and HERA on the Joint Cyber Command watch floor: DG CNECT re-points affected hospitals and ministries to EU-hosted audited models and hardened open-weight builds already in the patch, with logs published and auto-approvals still banned. Unspent InvestAI and Civic Pact funds pay for inference, re-checks and local-maintenance cover.

We hold M13 live only where it already works — consenting town halls and health-queue offices — and let faster restoration sell pooling to refusers, without forcing expansion. On the side-deal we do not punish in public; the Health and Telecoms Councils offer the same fallback and procurement terms to the defecting capital, while DG TRADE keeps the lithography offer open and seeks observer status to the US-China verification work. Insurers are invited to observe both tracks for the statute.
