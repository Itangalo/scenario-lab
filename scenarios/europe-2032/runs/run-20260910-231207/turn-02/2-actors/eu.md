Why now is because mapped grid intrusions plus a viable human-infecting organism design mean harm can land before sovereignty or evaluation capacity finishes.

## Priority
EU Grid and Bio Resilience Shield, because live intrusions in grids and a credible bio-design signal outrank building compute that will not land for years.
## In practice
We task ENISA and the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority with joint delivery, funded via Civil Protection Mechanism and EU4Health top-up, running cyber-segmentation retrofits with TSOs in affected states and exercised containment drills at ports and water utilities. Interior and Energy Councils carry the mandate; we buy cohesion support with dedicated hardening funds for exposed municipalities.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories site selection and state-aid clearance moving through DG CNECT and EIB, and push staffing for the EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute at the AI Office with JRC, using leaked eval anomalies to justify vetted access to unreleased systems. We frame resilience as the precondition that keeps public tolerance for continued AI build-out.
