### The dark town halls
Winter brought another large automated sweep. A compromised update spread first, then locked records and dispatch queues across municipalities, clinics and care networks. Television ran split screens: joint teams unloading clean rebuild kits in cooperating cities, paper lists and diverted ambulances elsewhere.

The joint cyber command, now formally acceded, paid off where its playbooks were followed. Pooled foreign telemetry fed signatures directly into isolation, and services degraded rather than stopped. Where feeds had gone thin — above all in the large member state running its own cheaper hosting deal — recovery was slower and data came back in unusable form. Liaison officers stayed, but Brussels quietly marked the feed non-interoperable.

### A cure that embarrasses
At the same time, tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions reached ordinary clinics. Results were striking where deployed, waiting lists falling in the audited hospital groups that already ran trusted triage assistants. Yet the underlying models and supply were American, and procurement terms made that visible with every dose.

The Commission rushed a deployment bridge: first use only in the audited hospitals, published logs, redress carried over from the frozen benefits system, joint procurement demanding European hosting and monitoring. Nurses defended it; patient groups were cautious but hopeful. Elsewhere the benefits and policing system stayed frozen, and insurers again praised liability rules while refusing cover.

### Machines in the warehouse
Robots also arrived in force in logistics, ports and automotive suppliers — picking, palletising, welding, running through the night. Chinese hardware, American control software. Works councils in the industrial heartlands called it a second displacement wave on top of the cyber disruption. Repair, care and chaotic construction sites stayed manual, hardening the divide between jobs that vanished and jobs that would not automate.

Brussels ended June with faster isolation inside the system, a visible hole where one capital stood half-out, celebrated cures that underlined dependence, and factories retooling around machines it does not control.
