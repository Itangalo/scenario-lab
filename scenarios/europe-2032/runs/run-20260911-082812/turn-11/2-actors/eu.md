## New measure
**EU Major Incident Municipal Recovery Surge**
Surges ENISA/CERT-EU teams, clean rebuild kits and reserve licences to municipalities, clinics and grid operators hit by this semester's automated attack to isolate faster and restore essential services without waiting for attribution.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, category 6, and why now is that a large model-generated attack is landing while resilience is uneven and a member-state side-deal threatens to fragment the joint telemetry response we depend on.

## Priority
M12 EU Joint Cyber Command Accession — because with a major incident landing and one capital cutting its own hyperscaler deal, holding pooled telemetry and shared signatures inside Shield playbooks outranks everything else for stopping cascade.

## In practice
We execute M12 and the new Surge together through ENISA and CERT-EU under the Telecom and JHA Councils: pooled attribution feeds directly into Shield isolation playbooks, while joint teams push clean images, offline kits and reserve licences to cooperating clinics and town halls, with Emergency Response Coordination Centre funds tied to cutting cloud links on playbook as before.

We contain the defection politically rather than litigating it this turn: the Commission and HR/VP keep the member-state inside the joint command technically while noting the side-deal publicly as non-interoperable for incident data, and DG TRADE keeps the Hague-Tokyo-Seoul licensing offer alive without spending capital we do not have. The frozen benefits system stays frozen with redress and re-checks; only audited triage sites that cut waiting lists scale, to stop lawful-harm framing from collapsing cooperation during the incident.
