### The wave and the split
The attack came in autumn as a rolling, machine-built sweep: a tainted management update that opened doors in municipal networks, clinics and two regional grid operators, followed within hours by automated extortion locking records and dispatch systems. Cooperating cities that had cut cloud links on playbook isolated in hours with clean rebuild kits flown in by joint teams; others stayed dark for weeks, paying in cash and paper triage.

Brussels surged what it had. Pooled foreign sensors fed signatures straight into shared isolation playbooks, and emergency teams pushed offline images and reserve licences to the worst-hit town halls. Where the playbook was followed, essential services degraded rather than stopped. Where it was refused, ambulances diverted and appointment lists vanished. Television split accordingly: gratitude in one mayor, fury in the next.

### One capital goes alone
Mid-crisis, a large member state announced its own recovery and hosting arrangement with a non-European hyperscaler, on cheaper terms that bypassed joint telemetry standards. At home it was sold as pragmatism — faster servers, faster restore. In Brussels and in allied capitals it read as a break in the common line. Technically the state stayed inside the joint command; practically its incident data stopped flowing in usable form.

The Commission chose containment over confrontation, labelling the side-deal non-interoperable while keeping liaison officers in place. Trade envoys kept the long-stalled licensing talks with lithography holders alive but won no quotas. The fenced gigafactory plot stayed fenced.

### Lawful harm, still frozen
The benefits and policing support system stayed frozen, its redress window extended. Audited triage assistants that cut waiting lists were defended fiercely by nurses where they ran, and quietly expanded to two more hospital groups. Elsewhere deployments stayed frozen for fear of courts. Insurers again praised the new liability law and again declined to resume cover.

Brussels ended the year bloodied but functioning: faster isolation for those inside the system, a visible hole where one capital stepped partly out, and still no capacity of its own to fall back on.
