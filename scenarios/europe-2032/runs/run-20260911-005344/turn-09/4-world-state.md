### Containment weeks
Autumn brought what tabletop exercises had rehearsed but never believed. A modified pathogen, designed with extensive AI assistance, was released deliberately — investigators still argued over by whom — and spread through two transit hubs before detection. Hospitals in three member states filled isolation wards. The joint surveillance cell, built for cyber probes, was repurposed overnight for contact tracing and for logging which models had talked a user through wet-lab steps.

Casualties were real, in the low hundreds, and containment ran for seven weeks. Essential services degraded but did not stop: the grid and payment systems held, field hospitals kept power, cross-border medical convoys moved under civil protection escort.

### Sweating finished capacity
Brussels activated the health emergency authority and civil protection mechanism within days, repurposing existing lines and stockpiles. No new containment measure was proposed this half-year.

The workhorse was the EU Loss-of-Control Containment and Agent Kill-Switch portfolio proposed in the prior turn, which reached scheduled completion this half-year — human approvals, rate-breakers, telemetry. It was turned to bio-relevant assistance: mandatory reporting of high-risk biology queries, temporary limits on open assistance for certain lab workflows, surge procurement of diagnostics stood up through reprogrammed funds.

It worked only partially, as befits a system just finishing. Reporting compliance was patchy, one large deployer again routed queries abroad, and live kill-switches failed in two drills. But early detection plus surge teams prevented cascade. Health ministers meeting in emergency session agreed to mutual aid rather than fines, and no member state broke ranks. Further bio-specific surge capacity remains in preparation and is not yet fully effective.

Ordinary life split in two. Trust in AI collapsed in polls, with anger at laboratories and at foreign compute providers. At the same time, families queued for AI-designed triage and treatment guidance, and clinicians quietly praised the same systems that had helped create the agent.

By December the outbreak was contained, the Union intact but shaken, its standing paradoxically steadied by having absorbed a blow it had long warned about.
