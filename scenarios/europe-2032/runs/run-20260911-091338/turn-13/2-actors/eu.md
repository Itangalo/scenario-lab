## Priority
EU Clinical Benefit Delivery Pact, because with no measures in flight and capital at 2, restoring public consent through cures people can see outranks sovereignty concrete until trust returns.

## In practice
We run this as HERA joint procurement plus EMA accelerated equitable-access pathway, with Health Council mandate: pooled negotiation for the tailored therapies, mandatory EU-stock and second-source clauses, and deployment through the referral-lab and triage network built under M9-M10 so hospitals that held the surge deliver the cure.

We pair it with a no-undercut pledge on health-AI supply: DG SANTE and DG TRADE link access volumes to Anti-Coercion Instrument readiness, offering the defecting member state first-delivery slots in exchange for folding its hyperscaler deal into the common contract, and tasking the Evaluation Institute to certify the delivery models for safety. Investment-collapse wind-down funds and EU4Health reflow pay for it, no new data-centre vote needed.
