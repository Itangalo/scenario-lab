Spring brought containment over concrete: with hospital funds expired and factory injunctions still in place, Brussels launched an emergency health shield — pooled sequencing screening, referral-lab triage, stockpiled kits, hardening for hospitals/labs/water systems. Where domestic triage helpers worked waits held flat, but elsewhere rollout was slow and IT teams lacked staff; the two frozen AI factory sites stayed silent amid vigils and unsigned water-power compacts.

Two external shocks outpaced the shield: a published genome-model design plausibly guiding creation of a human pathogen split biosecurity experts, and a near-frontier open downloadable model spread to hundreds of thousands of machines, now widely circulating privately, linked in press to fraud/intrusions. The capital's separate foreign supply deal kept its deliveries while others queued; Washington offered only technical dialogue. By June wards held but no construction restarted.
