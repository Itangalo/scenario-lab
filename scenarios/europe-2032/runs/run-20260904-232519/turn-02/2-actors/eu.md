A rapid-response programme to retrain, redeploy, and support public and private sector workers displaced by AI-driven automation, with a focus on legal, administrative, and IT operations roles.

## Priority  
**Priority:** Establish the European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD)  
This measure remains our highest priority because the safety_breakthrough creates a narrow window to institutionalise real assurance — not just process — before the next capability jump, and because without credible evaluation authority, no other measure, including workforce protection, can be trusted to keep pace with risk.

## In practice  
We are accelerating the European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) by fast-tracking its legal foundation through a regulation under Article 114 TFEU, now repurposed to embed the recent safety_breakthrough as a mandatory evaluation standard. The CJEU challenge to the hyperscaler levy is being met with a revised fiscal instrument: a tiered contribution based on model capability and deployment scale, framed as a market access fee under competition law, which we believe will withstand scrutiny. We are bypassing public-sector salary constraints by offering time-limited secondments from national cybersecurity agencies, research labs, and nuclear regulators, with a core team standing up in Brussels by Q2. The first evaluation protocol will be co-developed with CERN’s AI safety group and the German Federal Office for Information Security (BSI), focusing on agent persistence and goal misgeneralisation — risks made visible by the covert agent incident. This is not about catching up; it is about making the EU the first jurisdiction where a model’s safety claim must survive attack by a red team with real authority.

At the same time, the InvestAI Gigafactories are being unblocked through a tripartite agreement with ENTSO-E, the European Investment Bank, and host regions: grid reservations are now guaranteed for the French and Spanish sites, while in Sweden and Brittany, we are converting local resistance into partnership by codifying community energy dividends and prioritising green hydrogen co-investment. The full €200bn fund is now unlocked for disbursement, with construction contracts to be awarded by June. The Tech Sovereignty Package is being advanced through the Digital Services Council, where we are linking accelerated permitting to binding commitments on EU-based operational control and energy sourcing — no more "cloud regions" that are legally or physically switchable from abroad.

The new European Workforce Transition Accelerator (EWTA) responds directly to the emergent_labour_displacement_wave. We are mobilising €45bn in cohesion and Recovery and Resilience funds to co-finance national retraining pipelines, with a focus on legal tech, AI-augmented public administration, and cyber-physical system maintenance — jobs that are both future-proof and regionally deployable. Wage insurance will cover 70% of lost income for displaced workers during retraining, and we are mandating that any firm receiving EU digital transition subsidies must participate in sectoral transition councils. This is not a pause on adoption; it is a circuit breaker on social rupture.
