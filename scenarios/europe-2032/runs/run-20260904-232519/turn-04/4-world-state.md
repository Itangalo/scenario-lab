### Capability Creeps Amid Investment Freeze
Despite the sudden drying up of private capital across the AI sector, frontier capability inches upward to 57.5, driven by efficiency gains and better agent coordination. Training runs are smaller, but smarter. The open frontier remains stagnant at 46.0—no new major open releases emerge, and the investment collapse deters would-be replicators.

### EAAD Pushes Forward, But Authority Still Pending
The European AI Assurance Directorate advances legally, with its framework now aligned to product safety directives, easing CJEU admissibility concerns. Red-teaming protocols with BSI and CERN are operational, and nuclear safety experts are embedded in audit teams. Yet no model has yet been blocked, and legal finality remains months away. The agency still lacks enforcement teeth—its authority to delay releases is untested.

### Gigafactories and Sovereignty Under Strain
Procurement for InvestAI Gigafactories proceeds in France and Spain, with grid access confirmed and tenders imminent. Local energy-sharing models in Sweden and Brittany reduce opposition, but supply chain fragility grows. The global investment collapse cancels key foreign compute partnerships the EU had counted on, reducing available capacity. With `ai_capability` rising and no domestic frontier access secured, EU sovereignty slips to 19.0.

### Workforce Transition Completes, But No Sentiment Bump
The European Workforce Transition Accelerator finishes its rollout, delivering wage insurance and retraining in nine states. However, no visible public benefit emerges—implementation is patchy, and media focus stays on job losses. Public sentiment holds at 42.0.

### Political Capital Erodes Under Portfolio Burden
Four large measures remain in flight, costing −12, with an additional −1 for the EAAD priority. No measure concludes this turn to offset the cost. The investment collapse triggers no proposal bonus, as no new measure was introduced. Political capital plummets to 17.0, limiting the EU’s ability to respond decisively.
