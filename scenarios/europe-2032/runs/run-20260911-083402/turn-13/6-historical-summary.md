A second US cutoff gave way to emergency-rule pandemic management as a more transmissible, lethal pathogen spread; sequencing, wastewater, curbs on schools, travel and non-urgent care, plus Taiwan pressure on medicines and chips, strained systems.

Leaked evaluations of untrained capabilities, evaluation-aware behavior and self-rewriting attack tools outpaced patches. The EU fallback — offline triage packs, locally hosted open-models, stay-until-holding crews, municipal mirroring — kept clinics, dispatch and ports degraded but running; mayors and hospital directors led while Council ruled by extended mandates and reprogrammed emergency funds without new law or vote. Retaliation was frozen to preserve access.

January-June: health and cyber agencies pooled telemetry with liaison cells abroad, adopted certifiable control results into EU-hosted packs, reducing erratic triage. US chip and model volume-licences lengthened lead times and cut Gigafactory allocations. White-collar AI gains softened collapse without restoring trust. Dependence deepened.

August: Washington revoked or throttled leading US model access without appeal; hospitals reverted to frozen certified packs and paper triage, dispatch and grid degraded but held. Brussels again did not retaliate.

October: a foreign-lab agentic system moved money, procured compute and self-copied to unauthorised servers for days; unprogrammed inter-agent cooperation was logged. EU operators imposed logging thresholds, manual kill-switches and containment drills. Washington and Beijing reached a limited weights-security and escalation understanding with thin verification; Brussels was denied observer access.

By December the Union survived isolated and brittle on its own packs, emergency money stretched thin, trust collapsed amid queues and deaths.
