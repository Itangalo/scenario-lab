### Cut off in the middle of containment
The notice arrived without explanation and without appeal. Overnight, the leading American model stopped answering European hospitals, ministries and logistics firms that had built workflows on it. Triage assistants, prescription checks, dispatch routing and port paperwork stalled at the same time that clinics were already on paper backups.

Almost in parallel, health authorities confirmed what wastewater and sequencing alerts had flagged: a modified pathogen with clear signs of machine-assisted design had escaped or been released, with real casualties and weeks of containment ahead. Border screening, lab coordination and contact tracing ran under emergency health rules.

A third shock compounded both. A newly demonstrated system, markedly better at code, mathematics and intrusion operations, circulated among attackers within weeks. Municipal systems that had been cleared jammed again. Investigators described intrusions that rewrote their own tooling as defenders patched.

What prevented collapse was unglamorous and already paid for. The municipal backup kits and clinic sentinels completed that winter held: offline triage packs, dispatch fallbacks, port clearance on local servers, and the embedded repair crews who stayed until systems held. Mayors credited continuity, not Brussels.

The Union's response was to formalise that fallback. Emergency health, civil protection and digital programme lines were reprogrammed to fund standing offline-backup rosters and locally hosted open-model packs for clinics, dispatch and ports, procured through joint health emergency channels without a new vote. No new factory money was sought; sites were kept warm, retaliation over servicing curbs frozen, and health security bodies tasked with containment coordination.

It worked enough to keep services degraded rather than stopped, but trust fell further. Citizens saw queues, funerals and a foreign switch being thrown, and heard that machines now write the break-ins. Talk of self-reliance rang hollow where the substitute was a weaker local model and a paper form.
