### The Talent Gambit

The European Commission launches the European AI Talent Shield (EATS), a €4.2bn initiative to anchor frontier research talent within the Union. Framed as a strategic necessity, EATS offers long-term fellowships, sovereign compute access, and fast-track visas via ELLIS nodes in Paris, Munich, and Amsterdam. The programme is tied to a Chips Act revision, aiming to synchronise talent and infrastructure. However, early signals from Berlin and Stockholm suggest resistance to centralised allocation of compute resources, and recruitment timelines slip before they begin.
