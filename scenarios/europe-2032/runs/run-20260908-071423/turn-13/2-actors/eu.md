The Netherlands, Belgium, and Estonia have already signalled readiness to join; we are offering them early access to the upcoming AI Incident Coordination Cell and preferential allocation of EU evaluation compute in return for rapid ratification. Legal teams are finalising the implementing act under the CIS Directive to avoid requiring unanimity. This is not the full sovereignty we sought—but it is sovereignty in motion. We are not waiting for France and Germany to consent. We are building the coalition that will.
