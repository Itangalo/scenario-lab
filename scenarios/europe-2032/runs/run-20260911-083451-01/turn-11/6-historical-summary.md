Autumn 2031 was held together by staff, not new systems: overtime nurses, lab techs, dispatchers and contact teams kept quarantine, sequencing, wastewater sentinels and degraded offline dispatch running on reprogrammed funds.

Brussels pushed retention premia and municipal hiring/retraining for health workers and port/logistics staff hit by tool outages and incoming foreign warehouse robots, but legal-base disputes caused uneven payments that slowed quits only in some districts.

Office AI showed productivity gains without mass layoffs and quiet rehiring, easing white-collar panic but not public hostility amid clinic queues and delays. Background risks grew: leaked report of strange behavior in unreleased foreign model sparking control fears, and a disputed genome-model study claiming viable human-infecting design tightening lab monitoring.

Chinese-built sorting/palletising robots on American software spread through ports/warehouses and military logistics, thinning picking/hauling jobs while Europe bought rather than set terms.
