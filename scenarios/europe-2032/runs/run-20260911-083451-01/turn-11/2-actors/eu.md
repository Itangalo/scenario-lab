## New measure
**EU Hospital and Dispatch Staff Retention Pact**
Pays retention bonuses and funds rapid retraining for clinic, dispatch and port staff holding services on offline EU-hosted fallbacks through containment and cutoff.
Category 7 Labour and social protection is why now: with containment stretching for weeks, outages exhausting staff, and robots and office AI reshaping logistics and admin work from outside, continuity under our two-year commitment fails if people quit before systems recover.

## Priority
M8 EU Fallback Care and Bio-Response Corps, because it is the funded emergency presence keeping triage, labs and dispatch degraded-but-running and it directly serves the two-year commitment to secure continuity, while the new pact only holds its people in place.

## In practice
We keep the HERA/ECDC-led corps as the single command: clinic overtime paid from reprogrammed EU4Health and UCPM funds, sequencing and wastewater sentinels run by ECDC labs, ENISA/CERT-EU teams embedded in hospitals and town halls rebuilding from offline copies, and the Evaluation Institute cleared fallback list as the only authorized switch from the dark US model to EU-hosted models.

We stand up the retention pact through EPSCO and health ministries without a new vote where possible, using ESF+ reprogramming: retention premia for nurses, lab techs and dispatchers, municipal hiring for contact teams, and short retraining for port/logistics staff hit by both the paperwork-tool outage and early embodied-AI displacement from foreign robots. We offer mayors and wavering capitals visible payroll relief to stay on the joint fallback line, freeze new sovereignty votes and retaliation, and treat the leaked eval anomaly and genome-model bio-uplift finding as reason to tighten lab monitoring, not to chase new capability.
