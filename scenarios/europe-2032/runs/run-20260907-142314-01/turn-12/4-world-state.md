### Stagnation and Exposure

Global AI capability inches upward to 62.5, as closed labs advance incrementally within the 62–68 terminal zone. A frontier model is released under permissive terms just months after its closed counterpart, initiating a rapid diffusion of high-capability weights into distributed systems. Early deployments show fine-tuning activity in offensive applications, including in the southern Europe cyber intrusions. However, the openweight capability base remains constrained by prior levels of infrastructure and deployment readiness. The release marks a clear acceleration vector, but full absorption will take time: openweight capability rises only to 53.0, reflecting initial uptake rather than immediate parity.

With political capital at 5.0, the EU avoids new initiatives. Instead, it consolidates the municipal backchannel—a patchwork of local resilience networks—by launching a pilot "Trusted Resilience Node" certification under NIS2. Lyon, Barcelona, and Helsinki gain interoperability and access incentives, while Bologna, Ljubljana, and Tallinn receive repurposed recovery funds to accelerate integration. These are administrative reweightings, not reforms, avoiding legislative hurdles.

Behind closed doors, DG COMP and ENISA explore a legal pathway under the Digital Markets Act to compel hyperscalers to disclose EU energy use and spare compute capacity. If successful, it could create a shadow inventory of private infrastructure—potentially mobilisable in crisis. But legal review drags on; no formal action is taken.

### Trust Erodes Further

Public sentiment slips to 28.0 as job losses mount and dependency narratives harden. The cyber incident, combined with visible inaction at the EU level, fuels resentment. Two member states continue to reject central evaluation authority, deepening institutional fracture.

AI safety plummets to 9.0. The gap between capability and control widens, and no new safeguards land on deployed systems. The open-weight release acts as a capability jump, triggering a sharp penalty under rule 3.

Resilience gains a point—local coordination absorbs some fallout—but systemic fragility remains. Sovereignty declines as global capability rises by 0.5, triggering the standard decay. Political capital drops slightly under the ongoing cost of the backchannel measure, now past its stated finish date but still yielding partial benefits.
