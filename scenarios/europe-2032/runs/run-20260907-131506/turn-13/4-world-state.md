### Sovereignty in Crisis, Action in Motion

The EU enters 2032’s second half under deep strain. Despite the completion of the Public Trust Corps, its legacy — a live incident dashboard and embedded response teams — is overshadowed by ongoing disruptions in healthcare and welfare systems across multiple member states. The denial of frontier AI access has crystallised into a structural dependency crisis, with no domestic stack yet capable of substitution.

The **Mandate Public Algorithmic Accountability Boards** measure reaches its finishing turn. The first board delivers its findings on the Dutch AI scandal, recommending suspension of the implicated system. The Commission invokes Article 11, backed by enforcement teams, marking a rare assertion of central authority. Hungary observes; Poland refuses to cooperate, calling the move federal overreach. The board’s legal grounding holds, but its political fragmentation limits its reach.

Simultaneously, the **Sovereign AI Stack Initiative** is launched via Article 114 TFEU, redirecting Digital Europe and CEF funds to mandate EU-controlled infrastructure for all high-impact public AI. Fast-tracked data hubs in Finland, Spain, and Romania begin site preparation, but hardware delays and energy allocation disputes slow progress. France resists procurement restrictions, while Germany demands cost audits. Internal Commission warnings about implementation delays go unheeded.

AI capability advances to 72.0, driven by **verification_widens** — automated validation in law, engineering, and clinical coding accelerates deployment and performance. Open-weight models benefit from community fine-tuning, reaching 60.5. Yet **ai_safety** remains stagnant at 15.0, with no new safeguards deployed. The **labour_displacement** wave intensifies, especially in entry-level legal and administrative roles, feeding public anxiety.

Resilience holds at 42.0 despite rising pressure, as modular stacks prevent total collapse. But with **eu_political_capital** at 3.0, the EU operates at the edge of functional capacity. The portfolio remains overburdened, and no new measure yields immediate gains.

### A Union Divided, Determined

The priority on accountability boards pays symbolic dividends, but the real test — rebuilding sovereign capacity — has only just begun. The Stack Initiative is irreversible in intent, but not yet in effect. Without immediate compute or models, the Union’s agency remains fragile, its actions straining against the limits of its power.
