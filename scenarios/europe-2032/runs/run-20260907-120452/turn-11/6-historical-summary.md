AI capability reaches 88.0 with the deployment of a frontier system demonstrating superhuman autonomy in critical domains, yet no safety evaluations or transparency measures are implemented, deepening global concern over uncontrolled advancement. The EU’s Emergency Replication Mandate proceeds unevenly: Poland, Finland, and Spain establish sovereign AI replicas at capability 71.0, while Italy, Belgium, and Germany delay, the latter challenging the mandate’s legality at the European Court of Justice. Compliance pressure via Cohesion Fund conditionality yields limited results. The Sovereign Inference Backbone faces construction halts in Marseille due to energy protests, forcing rerouting through Bucharest and Helsinki under split-key redundancy agreements, though full integration remains delayed. Public trust plummets as mass protests erupt in Paris, Berlin, and Athens following the annulment of a partner state’s election and revelations of AI-driven welfare abuses. Automation spreads rapidly in law and accounting, amplifying societal unease. In response, the EU launches the under-resourced Civic Resilience Surge to counter disinformation in elections and media, but the effort is perceived as insufficient to address the widening crisis of institutional legitimacy.
