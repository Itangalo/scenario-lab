## In practice
We are enforcing the Emergency Replication Mandate as a condition of continued AI deployment in public service. The EAAD+ has issued compliance notices under Article 12(3) of the amended AI Act, backed by withholding of Cohesion Fund tranches for non-compliant member states. Poland and Finland have activated their replication pipelines using open-weight derivatives of frontier models now at capability 71.0, hosted on newly secured HPC nodes in Olsztyn and Kajaani. Spain is close behind, but Italy and Belgium remain non-responsive. Germany’s legal challenge at the ECJ proceeds, but we are treating it as a managed risk: implementation continues under protest, with full documentation preserved for potential retroactive validation.

The Sovereign Inference Backbone remains behind schedule, particularly in Marseille, where local opposition has blocked grid connection upgrades. We are rerouting initial capacity through Bucharest and Helsinki, leveraging split-key agreements to ensure no single government can throttle cross-border access. Mobile audit teams are now embedded at both sites, certifying physical security and energy redundancy ahead of first-load testing in early Q1 2032. We are also integrating the emergent municipal oversight network into the backbone’s telemetry layer, treating local red-team findings as live inputs to system assurance.

Despite the urgency of sovereignty, we cannot ignore the collapse in public trust. The Institutional Integrity Audit has triggered mass protests in Paris, Berlin, and Athens, and the annulment of a partner state’s election has exposed our democratic infrastructure as soft targets. That is why we are launching the Civic Resilience Surge: deploying EU-funded detection stacks to national electoral commissions, piloting blockchain-based media provenance in collaboration with ARD, RAI, and France Télévisions, and standing up a pan-European legal rapid-response unit to support institutions under coordinated disinformation assault. This is not a distraction from sovereignty — it is its necessary counterpart. Without public confidence that AI serves, rather than subverts, self-government, no amount of technical control will sustain legitimacy.
