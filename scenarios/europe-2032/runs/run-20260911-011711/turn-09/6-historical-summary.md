Autumn 2028–Winter 2029: grid drive stalled on unpatched legacy and revocable US dependence; EU pooled leverage via joint licensing and 5 zoned but unbuilt sites. Spring ransomware via poisoned update hit hospitals/cities; member state broke ranks with outside compute deal; Commission triaged recovery and joint licensing as sole priority. Autumn triage held technically but hurt politically: ENISA/CERT-EU pooling improved tracing, lab-control rule in procurement; Washington quotas left sites silent; tailored therapies cut waitlists then benefits-scoring scandal confirmed systematic cuts to vulnerables; protests merged, councils blocked grid connections.

Winter 2029 sweep attack via compromised management tool hit municipalities/hospitals/grid; restores failed where backups on fragile cloud; tracing faster but fixes lagged, tooling judged machine-built. Brussels funded emergency teams, clean backups, manual drills and unattributed fix sheets — wards restored in days but defenders-behind narrative held. Five zoned sites' grid offers expired into formal pause, burying build programme. Leaked frontier-lab oddity fuelled control fears; re-audits confirmed cuts, freezes failed; tailored therapies lone success.

Summer–Dec 2029: Taiwan quarantine hardened to blockade, advanced chip exports stopped, shifting EU planning to years of shortage. US tightened licences: Europe on rationed volumes to sustain existing clouds/labs, not build new; ministerial lobbying failed. Commission held essentials line — cyber teams, separated backups, paper fallbacks sped recovery — but thin funds, exhausted councils and confirmed benefits cuts eroded support. Five sites stayed paused. Brussels prepared only bargaining option: pooling EU upstream chokepoints (optics, chemicals, deposition) for guaranteed compute via trade/middle-power channels, deferred until recovery finished. By December consumer/industrial shortages spread, Europe seen as hostage to strait; tailored treatments continued but not credited strategically.
