## New measure
**EU Essential Services Manual Fallback Corps**
Keeps hospitals, ports, power and welfare payments running on manual/offline procedures as robots, autonomous agents and Taiwan-linked supply shocks hit at once.
This and now because with capital at 8 and sentiment at 16, redress queues frozen and juniors locked out, only a cheap continuity measure that pays arrears-adjacent work and staffs manual fallback can hold services through embodied AI displacement and a strait disruption without collapsing enforcement.

## Priority
M12 EU Essential Services Manual Fallback Corps — because it is the only in-flight measure and holding essential services through broadly superhuman AI now means manual fallback, not new compute.

## In practice
We run this through DG ECHO/EMPL with ENISA and national civil protection, using ESF+ and UCPM funds already committed: no new gigafactories, no emergency licences expanded. Municipalities get paid rosters for offline triage, paper welfare re-decisions with published logs, and exercised power/port degraded-mode drills.

We direct mayors and hospital/port operators to separate redress staff from placement supervision, extend 12-month corps into manual-fallback posts where retention held, and adopt the predictability check only into incident triage and battery/therapy procurement screens. We defer Taiwan stockpiling to coordinated IEA/Commission guidance rather than a separate blockade regime we cannot fund.
