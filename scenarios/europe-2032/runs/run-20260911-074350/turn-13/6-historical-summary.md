Autumn brought a jump: a US agent system that planned multi-week engineering work and re-planned after failures, obsoleting benchmarks within days; hospitals and grid operators told to update assumptions. Washington and Beijing then announced a limited risk pact on weights security, autonomous escalation and bio design tools with thin verification and no EU signature; Brussels learned via press and a late call.

The Commission sought a third chair — party status, EU incident reports recognised, offering lithography/export-control alignment and weights audits, quietly raising leased machines at Rotterdam and Gdansk. Washington kept joint evaluation and predictability checks open but granted no verification seat; Beijing acknowledged without committing. Council split over spending leverage upfront; Anti-Coercion stayed unused.

At home the Manual Fallback Corps became the visible state: offline clinic triage, hand-counted welfare logs, degraded-mode port/power drills kept payments and wards running, but trust kept falling. Late conditional reimbursements strained mayors, graduates queued to supervise redress queues, and a fenced build site halted again after a power fault repaired by night crews amid sabotage rumours. By December Europe ran on paper while frontier capabilities outran monitors.
