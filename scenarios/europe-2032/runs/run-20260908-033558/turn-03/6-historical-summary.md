In 2028, a major AI-driven cyberattack disrupted public services across three EU member states, exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities at machine speed and overwhelming human defences. This offensive surge underscored the growing imbalance between AI capabilities and defensive readiness. In response, the EU launched **Cyber Shield Rapid Deployment**, a category 6 initiative, unlocking €15 billion from digital and recovery funds to accelerate adoption of advanced defensive AI capable of real-time patching and behavioural detection. ENISA issued binding NIS2 guidelines, requiring member state cost-sharing and tying disbursements to procurement milestones, though legal challenges and delays in two countries slowed initial rollout.

A breakthrough from a European research consortium delivered the first operational defensive AI tools, with pilot deployments planned for early 2028. ENISA’s detection standards reached 60% coverage in critical sectors—partial progress amid ongoing capacity gaps. The Emergency Resilience Surge concluded with limited success, hindered by unresolved legal disputes over fund reallocation, yet yielding modest infrastructure upgrades through regional cooperation.

AI capability rose to 60.5, driven by extended recursive self-improvement cycles, while openweight model diffusion continued at 49.0. No improvements in AI safety occurred, widening the control gap. The EU advanced the **Open-Weight Model Regulation Initiative**, proposing AI Act amendments to license high-capability open models, facing strong resistance from open-source communities and startups warning of innovation flight. Public anxiety eased slightly due to visible defensive progress, but job displacement in cybersecurity deepened social discontent.

Resilience improved modestly to 43.0 on the strength of planning and early deployment, though operational impact remained limited. Political capital dwindled under the strain of overlapping initiatives, constraining the EU’s capacity to respond cohesively, even as sovereignty concerns intensified.
