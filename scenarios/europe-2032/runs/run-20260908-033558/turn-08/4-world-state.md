### The Threshold Crossed

AI capability surged to 85.0, crossing the sub-linear threshold where measurement begins to blur. The increment of +6.0 reflects accelerating recursive self-improvement, now deeply embedded in frontier development cycles. At this level, even leading developers describe systems as co-designing their successors, with human oversight increasingly ceremonial.

### Open Weights Advance Despite Scrutiny

Openweight capability rose to 62.0, a modest gain driven by underground releases and academic redistribution of slightly pruned frontier models. Though formal open releases remain restrained, the emergent auditing network confirmed functional equivalents of closed models operating in distributed research clusters. The gap between closed and open narrows, raising alarm among security agencies.

### A Fragile Step Toward Safety

The EU launched the **Transparent Assurance Framework**, a sweeping regulation mandating auditable AI in public functions. ENISA was empowered to inspect reasoning logs and verify compliance through adversarial testing. The Sovereign Control Pilot Programme concluded, feeding its findings into the new framework. These steps, combined with partial alignment from member states, lifted `ai_safety` from 0.0 to 2.0—still critically low, but no longer flatlining.

### Resilience Gains Ground

The Resilience Acceleration Programme advanced toward full deployment. *CyberShield 2030* exercised coordinated responses across 18 member states, validating new AI-driven intrusion detection systems. With critical sectors beginning integration, `resilience` rose to 47.0, reflecting improved readiness despite lingering vulnerabilities in healthcare and local government.

### Sovereignty Erodes Amid Strategic Shift

`eu_ai_sovereignty` fell to 16.0. The +5 from completed InvestAI Gigafactories was offset by −1 from capability growth and −2 from the unacknowledged erosion of access under the **ALLIANCE** posture: while no formal restriction occurred, de facto delays in joint evaluations and model-sharing emerged. The pivot from compute to control yielded no immediate sovereignty gain.

### Political Capital Strains

Political capital dropped to 18.0. The portfolio charge deepened with the new large measure (−3), the priority (−1), and ongoing commitments. The proposal bonus for the Transparent Assurance Framework was modest (+3), given its direct response to the recent scandal. No sentiment lift occurred—instead, `public_sentiment` fell to 32.0 as the scandal deepened and job displacement fears grew.
