### A Bid for Relevance

The EU formally joined the Global AI Safety Initiative, pledging €200 million and offering Brussels as host for its secretariat. The Commission proposed integrating EU audit logic into its evaluation protocols, aiming to export regulatory influence. But enthusiasm was muted. Middle powers welcomed funding but resisted EU dominance in standard-setting. France questioned the cost. Germany withheld support pending judicial review of the bio mandate. Only the Benelux and Baltic states backed full integration.
