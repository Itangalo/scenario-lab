We are imposing an immediate, binding audit requirement on all AI systems used in life sciences research and genetic engineering, demanding full provenance logs, model access for ENISA, and pre-deployment review for dual-use risk.

## In practice
We are acting on the bio_uplift_findings as a warning shot: the gate is open, and we are the only jurisdiction treating it as such. Under Article 16 TFEU and the recently strengthened public health emergency provisions, we are invoking the EU’s competence in health security to impose the Emergency Biosecurity Audit Mandate. ENISA, in coordination with the European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC) and the European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL), will establish a rapid-review unit in Heidelberg to assess all AI-assisted genome models above a defined capability threshold. Developers must submit full training provenance, fine-tuning data, and access to inference logs; any model used in organism design will require a safety case demonstrating containment, intent verification, and mutation boundary controls. This will be enforced through funding conditions and licensing—no public grant, no CE mark for lab equipment, without compliance.

At the same time, we are closing out the Resilience Acceleration Programme and the Sovereign Control Pilot Programme. *CyberShield 2030* concluded successfully in June, validating cross-border coordination and triggering the release of the final €3.8bn in Digital Europe Programme funds to upgrade intrusion detection in municipal networks. The three pilot agents—grid stability, medical triage, and border threat assessment—are now transitioning into the Transparent Assurance Framework as mandatory control templates for high-risk public AI. No further investment will go into standalone sovereign compute expansion; our leverage lies in control, not in chasing a frontier that outpaces us by design.

We are not reopening the InvestAI Gigafactories or pursuing new compute builds under this turn. The political capital cost of the Emergency Biosecurity Audit Mandate is already high—research ministries in Germany, France, and Sweden have warned of innovation flight—and we cannot compound it. Our credibility now rests on containment: if we cannot secure the gates that are open, no amount of capacity will save us.
