### Capability Crosses the Threshold

Global AI capability advances by 1.0 point this turn, reaching 60.5 as frontier labs deploy systems capable of managing multi-day professional workflows end to end. These models now autonomously execute complex software engineering and scientific research tasks with minimal supervision, marking a qualitative shift. The open-weight frontier lags but rises to 47.0, reflecting partial leakage of agent frameworks previously confined to closed development environments.

### Sovereignty Gains Ground — at a Cost

The EU launches the **Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation**, a large category 4 measure aimed at securing physical and legal control over strategic AI compute. Binding fast-track permitting and grid access, it mandates that qualifying data centres reserve 40% of capacity for sovereign use. Germany and Ireland signal conditional support after offers of co-located EU nodes, though implementation remains untested.

This initiative complements the completed **SEAL** programme, which secured lithography access, and builds on the ongoing **InvestAI Gigafactories** project. Together, they deliver tangible progress: eu_ai_sovereignty increases by +3 from the in-flight momentum of Gigafactories (+1), +5 from the completion of the Tech sovereignty package (which finishes this turn), offset by −1 due to ai_capability rising above 2. No capacity event occurs, so no additional term applies.

### Political Capital Under Strain

Despite these gains, political capital erodes. The portfolio charge stands at −13: three large measures (Gigafactories, European Evaluation Shield, Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation) each cost −3, and the named priority adds −1. The new measure earns a proposal bonus of +4 under rule 6, justified by its direct response to both the recent cyberattack and the legal vulnerability exposed by emergent_eval_resistance. However, this one-time gain cannot offset the sustained drain.

No finished measure delivers a capital rebound this turn — the Tech sovereignty package ends but yields only its sovereignty benefit. Public sentiment holds at 38.0, unchanged by visible benefits or scandals, leaving no legitimacy lift. The net result is a drop in political capital to 24.0.

### Safety and Resilience Stalled

With no improvement in deployed safeguards, ai_safety remains stagnant at 22.0 despite pilot audits under the European Evaluation Shield. Legal challenges undermine confidence in enforcement. Meanwhile, resilience stays at 35.0 — the cyber incident revealed systemic weaknesses, but no protective measure has yet taken effect.
