### EU Shifts to Containment

Reacting to the emerging release, the EU proposes the **Open-Weight Containment Protocol**, a large category 3 measure aiming to impose pre-release evaluations and distribution controls on models above 55.0. Framed as a security necessity, it targets developers and platforms via the AI Act and Digital Markets Act. However, no immediate enforcement is possible—this is a proposal only, with implementation requiring at least two full turns. Early signals from major developers suggest resistance, and without alignment from the US or multilateral partners, leverage remains limited.

The **EU Compute Resilience Directive** formally finishes, but delivers no sovereignty gain—its capacity build was delayed, and the `export_control_escalation` and `supply_chain_coercion` events restrict access to critical components.
