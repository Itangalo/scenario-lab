ENISA begins setting up pilot mirror repositories, and negotiations open with GitHub, GitLab, and the middle_power_coalition partners on shared attestation standards. The measure is named priority, reflecting its strategic weight.

The **EU Compute Resilience Directive** nears completion. Germany and the Netherlands, rattled by recent outages, now back domestic failover capacity. Poland’s demands are met with additional co-investment, and a Council vote is scheduled for November. Adoption by turn 12 appears likely.
