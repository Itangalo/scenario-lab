## Priority
**EU Sovereign Access Guarantee** — this remains the core of our strategic response to external dependency, and with the US election outcome confirming that access is now a rationed privilege, we must shift from building capacity to asserting control: without runtime authority over the models, the stack is a shell, and our sovereignty remains performative.

## In practice
We are acting on the recognition that compute and talent are not enough: the real test of sovereignty is whether we can run a system no one else can switch off, alter, or degrade. The US election consolidation has made clear that even allied access to frontier models will be conditional, reviewed, and potentially revoked — and that the AI Act’s leverage evaporates when non-compliance means exclusion rather than negotiation. We are therefore pivoting the EU Sovereign Access Guarantee from infrastructure delivery to operational control. This turn, we are drafting a binding certification framework under the Digital Governance Act, requiring that all models in the sovereign stack undergo pre-deployment evaluation by a newly empowered Joint AI Assurance Unit (JIAU), co-staffed by ENISA, EuroHPC JU, and national security agencies. The framework mandates transparent fine-tuning logs, runtime monitoring for unauthorised updates or remote signals, and EU-held override keys for critical interventions.

We are applying this to both internally developed models and foreign-origin models integrated into the stack, making clear that participation requires ceding operational control to EU authorities. This will meet resistance from US providers, but we are leveraging the Tech Sovereignty Package’s market access conditions to negotiate carve-outs: continued access to EU data and deployment in public services will depend on compliance. Domestically, we are aligning the Sovereign Talent Acceleration Programme to staff the JIAU and its technical teams, embedding specialists from the programme into audit and runtime roles. The emergent_cyber_defence_pilot shows that AI can strengthen resilience when it is under sovereign command — we are now ensuring that the same logic applies to the models themselves. This is not about isolation; it is about ensuring that when the outside world decides who gets what, the EU still decides what happens inside its own systems.
