### Capability Plateau, Uncertain Depth

Signs emerge that AI progress may be slowing: a flagship model release fails to meet internal benchmarks, and prices for top-tier inference drop sharply, suggesting surplus capacity. Developers dispute the interpretation, arguing this reflects efficiency gains, not stagnation. Regardless, the pause—real or perceived—buys a reprieve. `ai_capability` rises only +0.5 this turn, the lowest increment in years.

### Resilience Gains from Completed Shields

The **Hardened Cyber Shield** and **Bio-Risk Early Warning System** officially complete. The former institutionalises AI-driven intrusion detection across energy and transport networks, while the latter integrates genomic monitoring with model-output screening. These yield a combined +3 to resilience, lifting it to 47.0—solid but still short of baseline robustness.

### Sovereignty Drifts, Control Remains Aspirational

The EU proposes an update to the **Sovereign Access Guarantee**, refocusing it on runtime control through mandatory certification for model fine-tuning and intervention protocols. However, no enforcement mechanism is yet in place, and US developers resist ceding authority. With no tangible shift in access or control, and structural dependency persisting, `eu_ai_sovereignty` continues its slow decay, falling to 11.0 due to unresolved exposure.

### Political Capital Under Strain, But Not Collapsing

The EU advances one new measure this turn—the runtime governance refinement to the Sovereign Access Guarantee—consistent with the limit of one new initiative per turn. While the overall portfolio remains heavy, with five large measures still in flight, the incremental charge is contained. Political capital declines by −3 to 27.0, reflecting coordination costs and legal uncertainty, but avoids catastrophic erosion. No legitimacy penalty applies, as public sentiment remains above capital and no priority acceleration is active.

### Open Weights Creep Forward

With closed progress sluggish, open models catch up. `openweight_capability` rises to 47.5, narrowing the gap. The lack of major safety improvements means no boost to `ai_safety`, which stagnates at 25.0. Public sentiment holds steady, neither crisis nor breakthrough altering perceptions.
