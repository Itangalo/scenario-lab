## New measure
**EU Sovereign Access Guarantee**  
We will guarantee uninterrupted access to a sovereign stack of high-capability AI models for essential public functions, built and operated under EU legal jurisdiction, to prevent strategic paralysis from external access denial.

`Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`

## Priority
**EU Sovereign Access Guarantee** — this measure responds directly to *eu_frontier_access_denied*, which has already disrupted hospitals and ministries; continuing dependence on foreign frontier models is now a proven operational and political risk, and the only way to secure continuity is to build sovereign capacity that cannot be switched off.

## In practice
We are activating Article 122 TFEU and the safeguard clause in the Digital Markets Act to launch an emergency sovereign AI access programme. The European High Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU) will be repurposed to operate a dedicated sovereign model stack, starting with fine-tuned derivatives of existing open-weight models at capability level 45–50, trained on EU-secured compute clusters at the InvestAI Gigafactory sites in Finland and Portugal. These will be made mandatory for use in cross-border health data exchange, critical infrastructure dispatch, and EU-level crisis coordination systems — functions that cannot afford interruption. The model weights will be legally locked under EU jurisdiction, with no foreign entity holding administrative or termination rights.

Resistance is expected from member states reliant on US cloud providers and from industry lobbies warning of fragmentation. To overcome this, we are offering a phased transition: full compliance required only for new deployments in essential services from turn 5, with grandfathering for existing contracts. We are also leveraging the Tech Sovereignty Package’s accelerated permitting to fast-track grid connections for the Gigafactories, and redirecting €8.2bn from unused cohesion funds to subsidize sovereign model hosting and talent recruitment. The export_control_escalation and supply_chain_coercion events make it clear that compute and model access are now instruments of foreign policy — and we will no longer let the Union’s digital sovereignty depend on the goodwill of others.
