### EU Shifts to Infrastructural Sovereignty

The European Commission proposes the **EU Compute Resilience Directive**, mandating domestic fallback capacity for all AI systems supporting essential public functions. Leveraging Article 114 TFEU, it requires 70% operational continuity during external service denial, backed by EU-controlled compute. Co-financing is offered via the Digital Europe Programme to ease adoption.

However, early resistance emerges. Germany and the Netherlands express concern over duplication costs and interoperability with US cloud infrastructure. Poland demands additional funds, citing lower baseline readiness. Agreement in COREPER remains uncertain.
