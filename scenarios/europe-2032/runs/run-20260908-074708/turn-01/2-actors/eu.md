## New measure
Hardened Cyber Shield for Critical Operators  
Establishes mandatory intrusion detection and response systems across EU critical infrastructure operators, with central coordination and shared threat intelligence.

Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)

## Priority
Hardened Cyber Shield for Critical Operators — this measure responds directly to the cyber_test_shot event, which exposed critical gaps in cross-border detection and demonstrated that existing defences would not have caught a determined adversary.

## In practice
We are activating the NIS2 Directive’s enforcement powers through the CSIRT Network and ENISA, designating electricity transmission operators, water utilities and major port authorities as essential entities under reinforced obligations. This turn, we issue implementing acts requiring all such operators to deploy AI-assisted monitoring stacks with automated escalation protocols, integrated into a new European Threat Fusion Cell hosted in The Hague. The Cell will be staffed jointly by Europol, ENISA and national CERTs, and funded from the Digital Europe Programme with an additional €1.8bn reallocated from Horizon Europe’s unused reserves.

Resistance is expected from several member states citing cost and sovereignty concerns, particularly among mid-sized industrial nations with limited cybersecurity capacity. To overcome this, we are pairing the mandate with immediate access to EU co-financing covering 70% of compliance costs and fast-tracked technical support from the newly established Joint Cyber Unit. We are also convening an extraordinary Justice and Home Affairs Council in December to align interpretation of "substantial risk" under NIS2, pre-empting legal fragmentation. The cyber_test_shot incident provides the necessary political cover; we are framing non-compliance not as fiscal prudence but as systemic endangerment.
