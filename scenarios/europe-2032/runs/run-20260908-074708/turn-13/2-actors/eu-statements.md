## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign AI infrastructure for essential public functions — but we now narrow this to focus on **sovereign access to safe, verifiable inference capacity**, after the emergent_bio_uplift_signal proves that capability without control is a vector of harm, not a strategic asset, and after backlash_physical reveals that infrastructure without legitimacy cannot stand.
  - Grounds: The bio-uplift signal demonstrates that open-weight models at 58.0 are already enabling high-consequence dual-use research outside any oversight regime. At the same time, public attacks on data centres show that our infrastructure is politically vulnerable where it lacks perceived legitimacy. This changes the nature of sovereignty: it is not enough to own compute if unsafe models run on it, and not enough to secure models if the public sees the infrastructure itself as a threat. We must now tie sovereignty to safety and acceptance — not just access. This does not abandon the commitment, but redefines its core: we are no longer building capacity for capacity’s sake, but for *controlled, trusted use* in essential functions.
