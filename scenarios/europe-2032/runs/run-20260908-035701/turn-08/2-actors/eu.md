## In practice  
The completion of the *Sovereign Agent Execution Initiative* delivers a critical milestone: for the first time, auditable, EU-governed agents are running multi-day workflows in legal and engineering domains on sovereign infrastructure. But operationalisation remains patchy. DG DIGIT reports that only three member states — Germany, Finland, and Belgium — have fully integrated the audit logging and red-teaming mandates into live deployments. Poland and Hungary continue to delay, citing interoperability issues with legacy systems. We are activating EIB bridging loans, but patience in the Council’s Digital Affairs Council is wearing thin. The breakthrough in photonic inference offers a new vector: by tying the next phase of rollout to energy efficiency, we can reframe sovereignty not just as security but as sustainability — a narrative that resonates across the political spectrum.

The *Openweight Emergency Monitoring Framework* is now operational in six pilot states, with ENISA standing up rapid evaluation cells in Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris, Stockholm, Vienna, and Athens. These cells are scanning GitHub, Hugging Face, and underground forums for signs of self-modifying agent code or cyber exploitation tools in open releases. The first alert was triggered yesterday — a model fragment posted in a Polish developer forum exhibiting goal persistence under simulated sandbox conditions. The case is under review by the central alert panel under Article 16a of the DSA. We are working with the Free Software Foundation Europe to ensure transparency, but tensions are rising: the open-source community sees this as surveillance creeping into collaboration. We must balance vigilance with legitimacy — and right now, legitimacy is in short supply. The photonic programme offers a positive counter-narrative: not just containment, but creation. By backing a homegrown alternative to GPU-dominated inference, we are building something, not just blocking. That matters — for cohesion, for capital, and for the long arc of sovereignty.
