## New measure  
### Launch Sovereign Agent Execution Initiative  
We are launching a €15bn public-private programme to build and deploy EU-governed autonomous AI agents capable of executing multi-day professional tasks in law, engineering, and public administration — using only open-weight models, with full auditability, and running on sovereign compute.  

`Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`

## Priority  
**Launch Sovereign Agent Execution Initiative** — because the frontier’s leap to autonomous execution has changed the game, and our sovereignty is now defined not by access to models but by control over agents; with the US retreating inward, this is the moment to build EU capacity that cannot be switched off or subverted.

## In practice  
We are treating the completion of the Strengthen Open-Source Research Alliance as the legal and institutional foundation for the next phase: sovereign execution. The binding clauses are now in force under Article 114 TFEU, with DG GROW and DG CONNECT operationalising compliance protocols through the newly established Digital Infrastructure Compliance Office. Pilot audits in France and Finland are transitioning from setup to active review, though staffing gaps persist. Bilateral memoranda with Germany and Poland have preserved the integrity of the framework while addressing national sensitivities over data sovereignty — a fragile but functional balance.

The Sovereign Agent Execution Initiative is being launched as a Horizon Europe Joint Undertaking, co-financed by the Digital Europe Programme and national AI agencies, with €15bn committed over three turns. It will fund consortia — led by public research institutions and including mid-sized tech firms — to develop agent systems that can autonomously draft legislation, verify engineering blueprints, and manage procurement cycles, all based on open-weight models above 45.0 capability. These agents will run exclusively on EU-anchored compute, with mandatory runtime logging, red-teaming, and integrated interpretability tooling. We are engaging the Council’s Competitiveness Working Party to align public procurement rules so that, by turn 8, all non-sensitive multi-day tasks in public administration must use these sovereign agents. The European Investment Bank is structuring matching loans to incentivise private adoption. This is not about catching up — it is about defining a new tier of control: if the world moves from assistance to execution, the EU will not outsource its agency. It will execute under its own authority, on its own terms.
