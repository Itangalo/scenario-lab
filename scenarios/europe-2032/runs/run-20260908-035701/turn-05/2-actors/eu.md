## New measure
### Launch Sovereign Agent Execution Initiative  
We are launching a €15bn public-private programme to build and deploy EU-governed autonomous AI agents capable of executing multi-day professional tasks in law, engineering, and public administration — using only open-weight models, with full auditability, and running on sovereign compute.

## Priority
**Launch Sovereign Agent Execution Initiative** — because the frontier’s leap to autonomous execution has changed the game, and our sovereignty is now defined not by access to models but by control over agents; with the US retreating inward, this is the moment to build EU capacity that cannot be switched off or subverted.

## In practice
We are treating the completion of the Strengthen Open-Source Research Alliance measure as a foundation, not a finish. The legal clauses tying subsidies and grid access to open contribution have now been adopted by the Commission under Article 114 TFEU, with opt-in pathways for member states through the Digital Europe Programme. DG GROW and DG CONNECT are finalising compliance protocols with the new Digital Infrastructure Compliance Office, and pilot audits have begun at two Gigafactory sites in France and Finland. The German and Polish legal challenges have been partially defused through bilateral memoranda affirming national oversight of data logs, while preserving the EU’s right to verify compliance. This framework now underpins our next move: sovereign execution.

The Sovereign Agent Execution Initiative is being structured as a joint undertaking under Horizon Europe, co-funded by the Digital Europe Programme and national AI agencies. It will contract with consortia to develop agent systems — not just models — that can autonomously draft legislation, verify engineering plans, and manage public procurement cycles, all based on open-weight models above 45.0 capability. These agents will run exclusively on EU-anchored compute, with mandatory runtime logging, red-teaming, and interpretability tooling integrated from design. We are engaging the Council’s Competitiveness Working Party to align procurement rules so that public agencies must use these sovereign agents for all non-sensitive multi-day tasks by turn 8. The goal is clear: if the world is moving from assistance to execution, the EU will not outsource its agency — it will execute under its own authority, on its own terms.
