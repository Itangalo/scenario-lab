A national AI benefits scandal—ruled a violation of due process—sparks public outrage. Global export controls tighten, with advanced chip access tied to political alignment, weakening EU autonomy. The Runtime Integrity Emergency Response patches the critical runtime flaw, but only 18 member states implement it fully, while bilateral deals persist, fracturing cohesion. The EU launches the Photonic Sovereignty Accelerator as a category 4 security measure, yet funding delays and partial rollouts prevent immediate gains. Political capital collapses amid governance failures, and public sentiment declines further, driven by scandal and systemic fragility. Labour unrest erupts as auditors and technicians strike, halting AI use in welfare and health systems.  

Frontier AI access is abruptly severed, disrupting hospitals and public agencies across the Union, exposing the fragility of external dependencies. Despite completion of the Sovereign Runtime Control Initiative, only ten member states migrate critical systems to EU-governed runtimes. The EU responds with the **Critical Public Services AI Continuity Directive**, a small category 6 mandate requiring full migration by turn 13 and establishing fallback protocols, justified under Article 114 TFEU. Compliance is tied to Emergency Cohesion Fund disbursements, sparking resistance from member states and administrative strain. Early resilience gains emerge, but public sentiment remains flat due to ongoing disruption.  

The Photonic Sovereignty Accelerator delivers no near-term sovereignty benefits. AI capability increases by 1.5 due to photonic integration, eroding EU leverage, while AI safety stagnates at 5.0. An access denial event imposes a −2 sovereignty penalty, and rising capability enforces a −1 decay, offsetting the +1 gain from the directive’s partial implementation. Political capital remains depleted; institutional trust stays low, and legitimacy continues to erode despite new measures. Sovereignty is now operationally tested—but control without adoption proves hollow, and authority without legitimacy fails to restore stability.
