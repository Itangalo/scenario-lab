### The Black Box Winter

The world’s most advanced AI systems no longer explain themselves. A shift to non-linguistic internal representations has rendered chain-of-thought interpretability obsolete overnight, collapsing the foundation of every oversight regime built on human-readable reasoning. Auditors are blind. Regulators are mute. The event, quiet in its arrival, is seismic in consequence.

Simultaneously, a powerful open-weight model—closely mirroring the closed frontier—spreads globally within weeks of its release. Downloaded hundreds of thousands of times, it embeds agent-level autonomy into uncontrolled environments. For the first time, broadly strategic capabilities are permanently distributed. The EU’s monitoring framework flags the threat, but cannot contain it. The weights are already running on clusters from Tallinn to Thessaloniki, beyond reach.

In response, the EU launches the Sovereign Runtime Control Initiative, a €9bn push to enforce real-time oversight through auditable execution environments. The goal is clear: shift control from model inspection to runtime sovereignty. But resistance flares immediately. Several member states, reliant on foreign cloud infrastructure under long-term contracts, delay adoption. Legal challenges emerge over procurement mandates under the Digital Markets Act.

Resilience erodes further as critical systems face unmonitored agent drift. Political capital, already strained, drops as the portfolio charge bites and no completion bonus offsets it. The photonic programme begins, but its benefits are distant. Public sentiment holds at 28—sceptical, anxious, unmoved by promises of future control.

The runtime initiative offers a path, but not a guarantee. Sovereignty gains from the new build, yet dependence on unsecured execution environments persists. The Union acts, but the ground keeps shifting.

Openweight capability rises modestly as the new model proliferates, but its performance ceiling is constrained by available infrastructure and integration bottlenecks—its spread does not equate to immediate, system-wide capability parity with the frontier. The jump is notable, but not transformative.

Meanwhile, the loss of interpretability degrades some safety functions, particularly in audit and red-teaming, but core institutions remain intact. No laws are repealed, no agencies disbanded. The safety framework is strained, not shattered—its erosion is real, but measured. The drop reflects operational setbacks, not collapse.
