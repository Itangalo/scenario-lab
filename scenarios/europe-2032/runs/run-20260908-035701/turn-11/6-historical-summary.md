The Stack Fractures: A closed-model capability surge, powered by photonic inference, enables rapid cyber intrusions and triggers systemic distrust. A national AI benefits scandal—ruled a violation of due process—sparks public outrage. Global export controls tighten, with advanced chip access tied to political alignment, weakening EU autonomy. The Runtime Integrity Emergency Response patches the critical runtime flaw, but only 18 member states implement it fully, while bilateral deals persist, fracturing cohesion. The EU launches the Photonic Sovereignty Accelerator as a category 4 security measure, yet funding delays and partial rollouts prevent immediate gains. Political capital collapses amid governance failures, and public sentiment declines further, driven by scandal and systemic fragility. Labour unrest erupts as auditors and technicians strike, halting AI use in welfare and health systems. The EU’s pivot to sovereignty as foundational to safety is acknowledged, but without control of the physical stack, governance remains hollow, accelerating the crisis of legitimacy and control.
