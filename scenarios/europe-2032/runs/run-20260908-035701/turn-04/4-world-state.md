### Frontier Leaps Ahead  
`ai_capability` rises by +2.0 to 60.0, as automated verification in legal, clinical and engineering domains accelerates closed-loop training. The frontier crosses a threshold: AI systems now complete multi-day professional projects end to end, displacing junior roles across sectors. This leap triggers a structural shift — the era of AI as assistant ends, and the age of autonomous execution begins.

### Open Weights Gain Ground — But Lag  
With verification now automatable in key domains, replication of frontier techniques becomes faster. The EU’s push to bind open contributions to infrastructure access amplifies diffusion. `openweight_capability` jumps to 48.0, closing the gap slightly, though still far from the frontier.

### Safety Erodes Further  
No safety measure lands on shipped systems. The capability jump, combined with opaque reasoning in newly autonomous models, deepens control deficits. `ai_safety` drops to 27.0 as developers struggle to audit systems that redesign their own prompts and workflows.

### Resilience Gains from Completed Directive  
The **Emergency Cyber Resilience Directive** finishes this turn. Detection upgrades are now live across critical infrastructure in nine states, and red-teaming has exposed previously unknown attack vectors. `resilience` rises by +3 to 45.0, reflecting hardening gains — though patching remains uneven in healthcare and local government.

### Sovereignty Push Faces Legal Headwinds  
The EU launches its **Strengthen Open-Source Research Alliance** measure, tying subsidies and grid access to open model contributions. But immediate resistance emerges: German and Polish legal advisors question the EU’s authority to mandate data log sharing under current treaties. The European Data Protection Supervisor flags privacy risks. No binding clauses are adopted yet. Still, planning momentum yields a +2 boost to `eu_ai_sovereignty`, offset by −1 from capability growth, resulting in a net rise to 24.0.

### Political Capital Stagnates  
Four large measures remain in flight. The portfolio charge is −13. The new large measure adds −3, and naming it priority costs −1. A +2 bonus from finishing the cyber directive offsets some strain. No proposal bonus applies — the new measure responds to structural dependency, not a recent severe event. `eu_political_capital` falls to 24.0.

### Public Mood Turns Cautious  
`campaign_backlash` signals rising public anxiety. Media coverage focuses on job losses in legal and public administration. The push for mandatory open weights is framed by critics as a backdoor nationalisation. `public_sentiment` dips to 38.0.
