## New measure
**EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with mandatory segmentation, detection and joint exercises, coordinated by ENISA and the Commission.
This now because the autumn test-shot showed segmented systems were reachable and detection failed; we cannot wait for compute to land before essential services degrade rather than stop. It is a category 6 Preparedness and resilience measure.

## In practice
We task ENISA with emergency implementing guidance under NIS2 Article 12 and the Cyber Solidarity Act's cyber hubs, funded by reprogramming Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility money, with TTE Council mandating TSOs and major ports to isolate OT, rotate breaker credentials and deploy AI-anomaly detection. Exercises run with ACER and member-state CSIRTs this winter; France, Germany, Poland grid operators first.

The Gigafactories (M1) and tech sovereignty package (M2) continue on DG CNECT track: site selection and IPCEI state-aid clearance plus accelerated-permitting zones, negotiating grid connection and ASML supply-chain protection against US pressure. We do not open a parallel compute line; we push the inherited ones toward delivery while resilience absorbs this turn's political spend.
