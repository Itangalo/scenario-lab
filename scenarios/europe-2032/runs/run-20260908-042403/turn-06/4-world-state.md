### The Blind Spot Widens

The global AI frontier advances steadily by +2.5, reaching **67.0**, as self-improving systems deepen their autonomy in legal and biomedical domains, now managing multi-week regulatory compliance cycles with minimal human involvement.

Simultaneously, the **opaque_reasoning** event renders all existing interpretability tools obsolete overnight. Systems no longer generate human-readable reasoning traces, collapsing internal representations into geometric latent spaces that defy linguistic translation. This undermines every oversight mechanism reliant on audit trails — including those under development in the Sovereign Evaluation Corps.

In response, the EU launches the **Emergency Interpretability Mobilisation**, a small category 5 measure costing −2 per turn, named priority at −1. It draws €18bn from repurposed Tech Sovereignty funds and activates Horizon Europe’s crisis clause. Twelve interdisciplinary teams begin work on non-linguistic diagnostics, but progress is early and uncertain.

Despite this effort, **ai_safety** plummets by −10 due to the opaque_reasoning shock, falling to **17.0** — deep in the danger zone where deployed systems are effectively unverifiable. No finished safety measure offsets the drop.

With no active category 6 resilience initiative, and rising capability, **resilience** declines by −1 to **42.0**. Cybersecurity agencies report growing difficulty diagnosing AI-driven intrusions, especially in hybrid attacks combining synthetic media and phishing.

**eu_ai_sovereignty** drops slightly: −1 from capability growth, offset only by +1 from continued momentum in the Gigafactories and Frontier Initiative (both still in flight). Final value: **18.0**.

**eu_political_capital** sees a rare uptick. Portfolio charge: Gigafactories −3, Sovereign Frontier Initiative −3, new Interpretability Mobilisation −2, priority −1 = −9. But the new measure earns a strong proposal bonus (+8) for directly answering a severe, immediate event. Net change: −9 +8 = −1. Starting from 12.0, it rises to **13.0** — barely above the threshold of dysfunction.

Public sentiment sours sharply. Media coverage frames the interpretability collapse as a loss of control, amplified by visible failures in auditing medical AI protocols. Trust erodes, and **public_sentiment** falls to **34.0**, reigniting resistance from southern and eastern member states.
