### Frontier Acceleration

The AI frontier advances by 4.0 this turn, reaching **85.0**, as self-improving training loops compress development cycles. The growth remains steady, but the world is entering a zone where capability outpaces institutional comprehension. At this level, systems match strong experts across most cognitive domains, and their internal dynamics are no longer legible to human review.

### Democratic Infrastructure Under Fire

The annulment of a national election—officially due to unverifiable AI-driven disinformation campaigns—shakes confidence across the Union. The **Emergency Cyber Shield** nears completion and begins real-time monitoring, but adoption is patchy. Several southern and eastern member states delay full integration, demanding concrete guarantees on compute access. In response, the Commission fast-tracks a 10% allocation from Gigafactory reserves for public-sector AI in resisting states, tying it to Shield compliance by Q1 2031. The move stabilises cooperation, but slows rollout.

### Audit Ambition, Legal Fragility

The **Emergency Audit Access Framework** is proposed, aiming to mandate deep technical audits of AI systems in critical infrastructure. Leveraging interpretability tools from the recently completed mobilisation, it seeks to extract non-linguistic representations of model reasoning. However, legal challenges loom: foreign providers threaten WTO disputes, and domestic ministries resist ceding control. A phased approach—voluntary submission first, then mandatory audits—buys time, but not consensus.

### Safety and Sentiment Under Strain

Despite the new audit initiative, `ai_safety` rises only slightly (+2) due to the completion of the Interpretability Mobilisation, offsetting the usual −1 to −3 drag from capability growth. `resilience` gains +3 from the Cyber Shield nearing full deployment, despite rising threats. `public_sentiment` drops further, as job losses in legal and administrative sectors mount and the election crisis fuels anxiety.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Tightens

`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 21.0: −1 from the 4.0 rise in `ai_capability`, and no offset from the unfinished Audit Framework. The **Emergency Talent Retention Drive** still hasn’t launched, compounding stagnation. `eu_political_capital` declines to 15.0 under portfolio charges (−3 for Cyber Shield, −3 for new Audit Framework, −1 for priority), with no proposal bonus—the Audit measure responds to emerging risks, but no major incident has yet landed to justify a bonus.
