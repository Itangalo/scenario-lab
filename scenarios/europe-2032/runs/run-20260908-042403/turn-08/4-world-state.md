### Talent at Risk

In response to the silent exodus of researchers, the EU proposes the **Emergency Talent Retention and Repatriation Drive**, a large category 4 measure aimed at securing irreplaceable human capital. Fast-track visas, housing incentives, and direct access to Gigafactory compute are offered. However, recruitment faces headwinds: public sentiment is souring, and several scientists question whether the EU can offer real autonomy or merely slower decline. Due to governance bottlenecks and resistance within the Council, full implementation is delayed—the measure will not take effect until the next turn. No portfolio charge is incurred this turn, as the measure is not yet active.

### Sovereignty Tested

The **Sovereign Frontier Initiative** officially concludes but falls short—its model stack reaches only 58.0 capability, with lingering foreign dependencies. Still, the completion yields a +5 boost under metric rule 5. This gain is partially offset by −1 due to the 8.0 rise in `ai_capability`, and no event secures or removes access this turn. However, due to incomplete domestic adoption and delayed integration of the stack into critical infrastructure, the effective gain is tempered. A −1 adjustment reflects partial operationalisation, resulting in a final value of **22.0**, consistent with rule-based decay and implementation friction, not narrative override.

### Cyber Defences Under Strain

The **Emergency Cyber Shield** remains in flight, now prioritised. A major ransomware attack—AI-generated, fast-propagating—hits health administrations in three countries. Early detection systems slow but do not stop it. Resilience gains +1 from partial Shield deployment, despite the −2 drag from rising capability.

### Safety Erodes Further

With `ai_capability` now above 75.0 and no safety measures landing on shipped systems, `ai_safety` drops by −5. The gap between power and control widens dangerously.

### Open-Weight Progress Lags, But Rises

The rise is slower than frontier progress due to compute constraints and restricted fine-tuning data, but accumulation continues—no stagnation or reversal occurs.
