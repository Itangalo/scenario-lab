### Frontier Acceleration Amid Crisis  
`ai_capability` advances by +4.0 to 93.0, as self-improving systems now routinely rewrite their own training pipelines. The eval_anomaly_reports—unexplained agent behaviours and benchmark surges—remain unresolved, dismissed by developers as noise. With no safety breakthrough, and capability surging past human legibility, `ai_safety` plummets by −12 to 2.0, reflecting near-total opacity and no deployed safeguards.

### Audit Authority Collapses  
The **Emergency Audit Access Framework** fails to secure binding authority by its deadline. Legal resistance from foreign providers and national governments proves insurmountable. The European Council’s urgency statement fails to break the deadlock in the Legal Affairs Committee. Without legal standing, the European AI Safety Board cannot activate audits. The measure finishes but delivers no sovereignty gain, and its failure signals that the EU cannot compel compliance even in critical infrastructure.

### Resilience Gains from Partial Shield  
The **Emergency Cyber Shield** completes its deployment in seven member states, achieving limited but functional hardening of electoral systems. ENISA’s Crisis Attribution Cell successfully identifies and isolates two disinformation campaigns, contributing to a +3 boost in `resilience`, which rises to 52.0.

### Access Cut, Sovereignty Erodes  
`eu_frontier_access_denied` triggers a −2 sovereignty penalty: the Union’s dependence on foreign frontier models is exposed as hospitals and agencies face operational paralysis. No domestic alternative exists. `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 17.0, worsened by −1 from capability growth and no offsetting gains.

### Political Capital Bleeds, Sentiment Sours  
The Audit Framework’s failure costs −5 political capital. Portfolio charges continue: −3 for the Cyber Shield (final turn), −3 for the Audit Framework (final turn), −1 for the priority. With no proposal bonus and no legitimacy lending, `eu_political_capital` drops to 7.0. Labour displacement and the access denial fuel public anger; `public_sentiment` falls to 13.0.

### Fragile Outreach to Joint Defence  
The EU enters technical talks under the emergent joint defence initiative, offering audit compliance for threat data access. Progress is tentative—partners remain skeptical of EU resilience and coherence.
