## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Hardening and Response Pact**
It hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with mandatory detection, backup controls and cross-border exercises. This now because intruders sat for weeks in two member-state grids undetected, while open-weight offensive cyber is unrecallable and safety assurance has fallen behind the capability jump.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Hardening and Response Pact, because the autumn test-shot proved cascading infrastructure failure is the near-term irreversible harm, outranking even the inherited compute build.

## In practice
We are executing the two inherited compute lines — InvestAI Gigafactories (DG CNECT, EIB and Important Project of Common European Interest state-aid clearance) and the Tech Sovereignty permitting zones (Competitiveness Council, accelerated grid connection via TEN-E revision) — but the investment collapse means private co-funding is stalling, so we are using this turn to keep sites and power reservations alive rather than expand them, offering member states cohesion-fund bridging and ASML-linked supply-chain guarantees to hold permits.

On the new Pact we table a CER/NIS2 implementing act through the Justice and Home Affairs Council with ENISA as operator: mandatory 24-hour incident reporting to a common cell, funded sensor upgrades for TSOs and ports, and EU-CyCLONe live exercises in Q1 2027. We pair this with the secured frontier-access terms and the public-sector adoption wins to hold public sentiment — health waiting-list pilots in three member states are the visible proof that hardening enables use, not blocks it. Utilities resist cost; we meet them with Connecting Europe Facility reallocation and procurement preference.
