## New measure
**EU Health Data Sovereignty Containment**
Brings the emergency US remote-triage contracts under temporary EU oversight — mandatory registration, data-localisation gateway via EuroHPC pool, and time-limited authorisation tied to migration back to EU-hosted models.
This and now because with capital at 11 and sentiment at 6 we cannot ban life-saving triage outright, but an uncontrolled parallel health data flow outside EU oversight voids our two-year commitment to keep essential services running on European-controlled means; containment preserves care while closing the bypass.

## Priority
M11 EU Municipal and Hospital Cyber Triage, because if the automated ransomware sweep cascades through clinics and utilities now on fragile substitutes, neither containment of US contracts nor any sovereignty claim survives politically.

## In practice
We run M11 from the Commission-ENISA crisis cell under Article 122, pooling rescEU teams and locum admins to restore backups and keep appointment systems on EU-hosted open models, prioritising small clinics and eastern municipalities last-served in autumn, and holding drills only where swap-kits are stable.

We pair it with the new containment via DG SANTE and the European Health Data Space emergency article: regional groups must notify US triage contracts within 14 days, route data through the EuroHPC gateway with logging, and accept 12-month sunset tied to re-migration support. Health Council gets pooled inference funding and liability cover in exchange; non-registered flows face procurement exclusion, not criminal penalty, to avoid breaking care during the sweep.
