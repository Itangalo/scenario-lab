## New measure
**EU Continuity Switch-Over**
Emergency re-platforming of hospitals, ministries and dependent firms cut off from the US frontier model onto EU-hosted open and licensed models with pooled inference, clean fallbacks and procurement cover.
This is Diffusion, adoption and public trust, because the cutoff is an adoption crisis not a new training run — we cannot build new frontier compute in one turn, but we can keep lights and services on with what we control, and make the sovereignty commitment visible as continuity rather than cranes.

## Priority
M9 EU Swarm-Defence Rapid Deployment, because with capital at 15 and sentiment at 15 a second municipal/hospital cascade during the model cutoff would read as state failure and finish our capacity to act at all.

## In practice
We run the Switch-Over from DG CNECT and DIGIT through the ENISA crisis cell: emergency procurement waiver to fund inference pooling on the France/Spain gigafactory groundwork sites plus existing EuroHPC, model-swap kits and manual-fallback staffing for hospitals and ministries, paid from reshuffled Digital Europe, with French-Spanish presidency cover in Council to block a retaliatory spiral we cannot afford.

We push M9 as the shield for that migration — central purchase of the machine-speed patching and swarm-behaviour detectors to the same hospitals and municipalities being re-platformed, joint exercises before winter, explicit prioritisation of small clinics and eastern municipalities to stop the lag becoming the story. On the frontier-access denial we lodge Anti-Coercion screening and demand tier-licence reasons in Washington, but we do not promise restoration; we promise continuity without permission.
