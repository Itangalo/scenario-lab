## Priority
EU Critical Services Continuity Surge, because no two-year delivery promise survives cascading public-service outages and a chip cutoff that makes every AI question a security question.

## In practice
We act under NIS2 Article 21 and the Cyber Solidarity Act: ENISA's SOCs push the new automated-patching and swarm-detection tooling to the municipal utilities named in the spring audit and to health regions already on EU ring-fenced hosting, with JRC seconded teams closing the vendor remote-update bypass by forcing 48-hour logging as a condition for continued connection. No new agency, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and EIB guarantees from the finished permitting package.

On supply, DG GROW and the Chips Joint Undertaking impose an emergency allocation: existing EU stocks and ASML-centred upstream leverage are reserved for critical-infrastructure control systems and repair spares, while the Anti-Coercion Instrument consultation is opened to coordinate export terms with the US and middle-power holders. EPSCO and TTE Councils front the continuity message — degrade, not stop — to hold cohesion while Washington's structured-access offer stays strictly as EU-logged inference for public services, not as dependence.
