**EU Visible Benefit Rollout**
Extends the proven public-sector AI wins — shorter waiting lists, faster permits — from volunteer regions to a dozen more health and admin systems on EU-hosted, logged infrastructure, with cohesion funds tied to using the common stack.
Why this and why now: with capital at 15 and a member state cutting its own hyperscaler deal, only felt European delivery rebuilds permission to hold a common line; the adoption success gives us something to copy now while cyber and chip shocks make foreign dependence politically costly.

## Priority
EU Visible Benefit Rollout, because without a capital rebuild from felt delivery no cyber, chip or sovereignty line can be held while a member state defects.

## In practice
We run this through DG REFORM and DG SANTE with the TTE and EPSCO Councils: Digital Europe plus reprogrammed cohesion envelopes fund deployment teams that clone the EU-hosted assistant and triage stack into new hospitals and permit offices, conditional on ENISA-pushed patching and EU logging. No new procurement regulation this turn — just grant conditions.

On defection, the Commission withholds clearance for separate hyperscaler terms that bypass the spring audit logging and triage-hold duties, and offers the defecting state first access to the rollout funds and reserved chip spares if it returns to the common procurement terms. ENISA SOCs and the finished Continuity Hosting keep pushing swarm detection to councils hit by the summer sweep, while GROW holds the emergency chip reservation for grid and hospital controls.
