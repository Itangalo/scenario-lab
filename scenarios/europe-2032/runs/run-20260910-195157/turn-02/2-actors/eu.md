## New measure
**EU Evaluation and Deployment Review Capacity**
Stands up independent EU technical capacity to evaluate frontier and open-weight models for cyber-biological misuse and to feed results into deployment safeguards and incident response.
This is category 5 Public technical capacity and research, because with an open-weight release now within a few months of the closed frontier and permanently unrecallable, the EU cannot govern through release control and must be able to test what is loose and harden against it.
