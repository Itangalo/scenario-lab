### The Efficiency Earthquake

A breakthrough in algorithmic efficiency, originating in a loose network of open researchers, sends shockwaves through the AI landscape. The technique slashes training compute needs, enabling near-frontier capability on modest hardware. This accelerates open diffusion in theory, but tangible replication remains limited. Export controls, fragmented access to fabrication capacity, and legal barriers prevent immediate uptake. No major open-source release or distributed deployment occurs this turn. While experimentation spreads in academic and smaller commercial settings, no measurable expansion in operational openweight capability is realized. Thus, `openweight_capability` remains unchanged at 57.0, reflecting that diffusion requires more than insight—it demands infrastructure, access, and coordination not yet in place.

### Sovereignty Under Pressure

The EU completes the InvestAI Gigafactories and Hardened Cyber Shield, delivering long-planned resilience. The former lifts compute independence, the latter hardens critical systems. Together, they deliver +5 to resilience, bringing it to 53.0. However, the broader sovereignty agenda strains. The European Compute Continuity Initiative launches under emergency powers, initiating stockpiling and pilot fabrication in Leiden, Dresden, and Grenoble. Legal resistance from ASML and diplomatic friction with the U.S. delay full deployment; the measure is not yet operational and no sovereignty gain is realized from it this turn.

The European Algorithmic Sovereignty Initiative is proposed to close the insight gap, targeting sovereign mastery of efficiency techniques. However, introducing two new initiatives — one launched, one proposed — exceeds the EU’s capacity. Only one new measure may take effect per turn. Given the urgency of hardware continuity amid supply risks, the Compute Continuity Initiative is retained as the active new measure. The Algorithmic Sovereignty Initiative is deferred to a future turn, pending portfolio space, and does not enter the active portfolio.

Sovereignty sees a small net gain of +1.0, to 17.0, solely from the completion of InvestAI Gigafactories (+5) offset by the ongoing decay from rising ai_capability (−4.0, per metric rule 5) and the export_control_escalation event (−2.0). The Hardened Cyber Shield, not a category 4 measure, contributes no direct sovereignty gain.

### Safety in Freefall

No new safety measure lands on shipped systems. The gap between capability and control widens, but not catastrophically. ai_safety drops by 2.0 to 10.0, reflecting growing opacity in reasoning traces and early signs of uncontrolled recursive improvement in open models, though containment protocols still hold in critical domains.

### Political Capital Erodes

Despite finishing two large measures (+5 each), the EU’s political capital falls. The portfolio remains heavy: three large measures in flight cost −9, the priority −1. With only one new measure allowed, the proposal bonus is reduced; the Compute Continuity Initiative earns +6 for addressing taiwan_blockade, export_control_escalation, and eu_frontier_access_denied, all severe and current. The deferred Algorithmic Sovereignty Initiative contributes no bonus, as it was not formally adopted. With public sentiment stagnant and sovereignty still low, no compensating gains arise. The Union ends the turn with barely enough capital to act.
