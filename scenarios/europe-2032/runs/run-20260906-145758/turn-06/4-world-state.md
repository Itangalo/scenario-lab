### A Threshold Crossed

Global AI capability advances to 60.5 this turn, surpassing the symbolic 60.0 threshold as frontier labs deploy agents capable of multi-day autonomous operation in research and software engineering. These systems now contribute directly to their own improvement cycles, marking a qualitative shift. Open-weight models reach 47.0, reflecting early diffusion of memory-augmented frameworks that replicate limited aspects of agentive behaviour — a development accelerating open model utility but still far from frontier parity.

### Sovereignty Push Meets Legal Headwinds

The European Union advances planning for a **Sovereign Compute Mobilisation** initiative, aiming to fast-track EU-controlled compute with binding public-use obligations. However, no new measure is formally introduced this turn, consistent with the limit of one new measure per turn. The proposal remains in internal drafting, with Germany and Ireland engaging in preliminary discussions over co-located sovereign nodes. Legal and governance complexities prevent any tangible progress toward implementation. As no infrastructure milestones are achieved, there is no build momentum, and thus no sovereignty gain this turn.

The **European Evaluation Shield** continues to face a legitimacy crisis. A formal legal challenge, backed by major developers and foreign governments, is filed at the European Court of Justice, arguing its audit powers violate intellectual property rights. Pilot audits proceed under secrecy, but the agency’s authority remains contested.

### Cyber Vulnerabilities Deepen Public Distrust

A wave of AI-driven ransomware attacks exploited adaptive malware across hospital networks last turn, and recovery remains slow. No new resilience shield is yet adopted, and systemic fragility persists. Resilience dips further as critical infrastructure modernisation lags.

Public sentiment declines to 36.0 amid visible job displacement and growing unease over uncontrolled infrastructure expansion. Labour unrest intensifies, particularly around data centre siting in rural regions.

### Political Capital Under Strain

The portfolio continues to carry four active measures: InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, European Evaluation Shield, and Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield. No new measure is added, avoiding further expansion of the portfolio. The **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield** earns a proposal bonus for responding to recent events, partially offsetting the burden. Political capital declines moderately to 25.0, reflecting ongoing credibility but growing difficulty in managing concurrent initiatives. The EU remains capable of action, though manoeuvrability narrows.
