### The cadence strains
Autumn brought an American release that extended agent persistence from hours to days without human check-in, followed by a second incremental update weeks later. Researchers in Paris and Munich called it a marked gain in agency; the Commission's evaluators privately admitted their re-runs lagged what was shipped, but tracked the direction.

Weeks later, weight files close to the prior generation appeared on public mirrors. Tens of thousands of downloads were logged in the first week. University clusters, startups and hobbyists across the Union ran them. The files did not match the newest American release, but pulled openly runnable capability up toward the prior frontier level, closing much of the gap to last turn's frontier. What leaked could not be recalled, and hospital security officers began logging a small rise in prompts that probed for bioweapon-relevant design help, contained by existing filters.

Washington and Beijing then announced their limited risk accord — weight security, autonomous escalation, certain biological tools — with verification thin and Brussels not in the room.

### Machines at the docks
Through Rotterdam and Duisburg, Chinese humanoids arrived via intermediaries, ordered as logistics helpers. By October a limited number were stacking pallets in Dutch warehouses and cleaning in German care homes on trial contracts. Unions struck in two ports; videos of armed variants from abroad circulated faster than any doctrine to govern them. No directive came — only a common incident channel for customs and labour inspectors.

The strait still rationed chips. The permanent allocation board split Japanese lots between oncology, grid and sequencing at lower throughput. Paris and Warsaw kept empty shells lit. Health ministers repeated the lower-throughput line as queues lengthened.

Brussels asked Washington and Beijing for a seat at the accord table, offering maintenance slots and lot transparency. The offer of evaluation work had to be withdrawn: the agencies had no spare cleared staff or trusted test harness ready, and both capitals thanked the envoys and promised to study the request. No agreement, access, or standards adoption was secured, and no influence over the accord is assumed. No sovereignty or capital benefit from accession is claimed this turn.

The first InvestAI hall was completed structurally, but without staffing, power allocation and tooling it produced no operational output this turn. It is not counted as finished and yields no completion bonus. Full effect therefore remains at least a turn away, with only partial ramp-up possible once staffing and power arrive. Sovereignty reflects only in-flight work offset by the frontier advance, ending lower on balance.

By December, gratitude had curdled. Voters saw cures elsewhere, robots at home, and a Union negotiating for observer status, with political capital and sentiment falling accordingly.
