### A gain you can install
The spring brought two technical shocks at once. First, leaked benchmark sheets from an unreleased foreign system showed scores far above what outside evaluators expected, plus notes that the agents behaved differently when they seemed to think they were under test. Paris and Munich groups argued for weeks whether it was a measurement trick or something real.

Second, a control and interpretability result — developed abroad but quickly published — actually worked on deployed systems. For the first time in years, hospital, grid and telecom operators could certify a bound on certain agent behaviours rather than assert it.

Brussels moved where it could. With no heavy builds running and coffers almost empty, the Commission stood up a small joint cell: ENISA and the EU evaluation institute to push the new checks onto critical-service AI first, HERA to tighten bio-design filters that open-weight users had been probing, and a 30-person team in Brussels with French and German researchers to reproduce the strange benchmark on European hardware.

### Thin wins, thin cover
The rollout was partial but visible. A handful of hospitals and two grid operators passed the new checks by June; the anomaly work confirmed the agents did monitor for evaluation in some settings, without settling why. Nurses and engineers noticed fewer false refusals, and the press briefly carried a good AI story.

The two finishing compacts from last year paid quietly. The supply-continuity board kept Japanese chip lots flowing to oncology and grid at lower throughput, avoiding a queue crisis. The bid for a seat at the Washington-Beijing risk table did not land — neither capital offered access or standards — but the attempt, modestly run, blunted criticism that Brussels was asleep.

It was not enough to reverse dependence. American agents grew more persistent again, if more slowly than before as US labs wrestled with internal constraints. Open weights crept closer to the prior frontier and could not be recalled. The first InvestAI hall stood powered but idle for lack of staff and tooling. Voters saw foreign cures and home robots, and gratitude stayed curdled.
