## In practice
We are acting. The **Tech sovereignty package** has now delivered its final milestones: €200bn in private capital is committed, and three accelerated-permitting zones are operational in Spain, Finland, and Romania. With that measure **finished**, we reclaim 3 units of political capital — not enough to undo our strain, but enough to force a decision. And we are using it to break the logjam.

The **Emergency Compute Mobilisation Directive** is now law. We invoked Article 114 TFEU at the June European Council, citing the breakdown of internal market conditions due to AI-driven energy and infrastructure fragmentation. The vote passed by qualified majority — Poland and Hungary abstained, France voted no, but Germany, Italy, the Baltics, and the Nordic states held the line. The ECJ referral by France is acknowledged, but we are acting on the principle of necessity: if the Court rules against us, we will adapt, but we will not wait to become irrelevant.

ENTSO-E has designated three emergency grid corridors linking existing hydro and nuclear baseload in Scandinavia and France to planned AI clusters in Wallonia, Silesia, and Andalusia. The EIB has released €70bn from unspent pandemic and cohesion funds under a new emergency instrument, ring-fenced for compute infrastructure. ASML has been presented with binding procurement orders for EU-dedicated lithography throughput, backed by counter-sanction clauses and guaranteed offtake from EDIC. The first cluster will be operational by Q4.

This is not coordination — it is command. The **emergent_decentralized_ai_infrastructure** in Germany and Spain showed what is possible when constraints are bypassed; we are now scaling it under sovereign authority. The European Digital Infrastructure Consortium will operate the clusters, with access governed by a public interest firewall: no model above 50.0 capability may be trained without independent evaluation, and no weights may be transferred outside the EU without export licensing.

We know the cost. Public sentiment remains at 30.0 — disruption dominates, benefit is invisible. But if we do not act now, there will be no future to determine.
