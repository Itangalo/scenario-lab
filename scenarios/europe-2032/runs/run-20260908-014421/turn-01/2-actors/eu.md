## New measure
**Cyber Shield for Critical Operators**

A binding directive requiring all critical infrastructure operators — energy, water, ports, rail — to implement AI-driven intrusion detection systems, conduct quarterly red-team exercises modelled on the recent test shot, and report anomalous behaviour to a central EU body.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`

## Priority
**Cyber Shield for Critical Operators** — because the cyber_test_shot revealed that our detection failed where it mattered most, and we cannot rely on external actors to defend EU soil when they do not even see the threat.

## In practice
We are acting on three fronts this turn. First, through InvestAI Gigafactories, we are negotiating site selection with member states — France, Germany, and Poland have signalled interest — while drafting the legal anchoring clause that ensures these facilities fall under EU jurisdiction, not foreign extraterritorial claims. The Commission’s Legal Service is working on a novel interpretation of Article 114 TFEU to justify overriding national veto rights on strategic compute infrastructure, knowing full well this will face challenge in the Court of Justice. We are offering grid priority and state aid waivers to host nations, but demanding irrevocable ownership clauses and audit access in return.

Second, the Tech sovereignty package is being pushed through the Council's Competitiveness formation, where resistance remains from smaller states fearing lock-in to US hyperscalers. Our leverage is ASML: we are linking export licensing assurances to reciprocal commitments from cloud providers to locate sovereign stacks on EU soil. This is delicate — Washington is watching — so we are pairing it with quiet coordination through the Trade and Technology Council, framing it as interoperability rather than decoupling.

Third, Cyber Shield is being drafted now under ENISA leadership, using the cyber_test_shot as justification. We are aligning with the updated NIS2 enforcement guidelines, making compliance a condition for cohesion funding disbursement. Member states resist the cost and the mandate, so we are offering 70% co-financing from the Digital Europe Programme and fast-tracked cyber workforce training via Erasmus+. The first red-team scenarios are being developed with NCSC-NL and ANSSI, based directly on the intrusion patterns observed — polymorphic lateral movement, deep persistence, evasion of known signatures. This is no longer theoretical: if detection fails again, the next incident may not be found by accident.
