### The Frontier Pulls Ahead  
The leading AI labs, unimpeded by safety constraints, pushed global capability to 61.0 this turn. The new model’s ability to autonomously design and execute complex cyber operations marked a qualitative leap. Yet no corresponding advance in interpretability or control emerged. Internal lab audits revealed reasoning pathways that even developers could not reconstruct—opaque, emergent logic now underpinned the most capable systems. With no safety breakthrough, and capability surging by 3.0, `ai_safety` fell sharply to 16.0, reflecting near-total loss of assurance.

### Open-Weight Progress Stalls Despite Efforts  
A wave of open-weight model activity followed the latest frontier release, but EU access remained fragmented. Some research collectives achieved partial replication using donated compute and model distillation, but without full architecture details or training data, gains were incremental. The `openweight_capability` rose modestly to 48.0—up 3.0—reflecting scattered progress rather than systemic access. This fell far short of frontier pace, consistent with denied coordination and stalled infrastructure.

### Cyber Shield Pushed, But Sovereignty Slips  
The EU doubled down on *Cyber Shield*, making progress on technical standards and securing partial buy-in through generous co-financing. However, resistance in smaller member states hardened as legal challenges loomed over enforcement via cohesion funds. The priority status pulled implementation forward slightly, but full adoption remained uncertain.  

Meanwhile, the *InvestAI Gigafactories* and *Tech Sovereignty Package* stalled. France and Poland delayed site agreements, citing unresolved grid access and state aid disputes. ASML, under quiet U.S. pressure, paused export coordination talks. With no sovereign capacity secured and frontier access effectively denied, `eu_ai_sovereignty` dropped to 17.0—down another point from capability growth and lack of progress.

### Labour Fund Proposed, Not Launched  
In response to accelerating white-collar job losses, the Commission proposed the *Labour Transition Acceleration Fund* and began consultations with member states and social partners. Early design work drew cautious interest, but employer pushback emerged over mandatory contributions. No funding was approved or disbursed this turn. The proposal remains in development and will not enter implementation until next turn at earliest.

### Political Capital Erodes  
Three large measures and a priority strained capacity. The portfolio charge drained resources, and without a major incident being absorbed, no political gain offset the cost. `eu_political_capital` fell to 34.0.
