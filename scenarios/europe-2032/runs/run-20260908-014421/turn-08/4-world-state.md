### The Weight of Intent

The EU pressed forward on what it now frames as a unified front: asserting sovereign oversight and building public technical capacity. The **Sovereign Evaluation and Control Framework**, designated as the current priority, advanced through legal channels under Article 114 TFEU, overcoming corporate resistance in principle. However, no final vote was secured by mid-year, and enforcement remains contingent. Red teams were staffed, inspection environments built, but no audits were published. The framework’s authority remains aspirational—its weight felt in boardrooms, not in code.

The **Emergency Access Pact for Red-Teaming**, initiated in a prior turn, continued negotiations with research institutes and ENISA but stalled in implementation. Despite diplomatic efforts, pushback from US-owned labs and Luxembourg-registered entities blocked binding agreements. EIB loan terms did not trigger compliance, and the pact remains non-operational. While conceptually aligned with the priority measure, it has not been elevated to a new initiative and is not treated as an independent measure this turn. Its partial progress contributes no portfolio charge, but limited traction prevents a meaningful proposal bonus.

### Capability Creeps Forward

Global AI capability rose to **80.5**, driven by longer training runs and improved reasoning architectures. No discontinuity marked the advance, but the climb narrowed the margin between capability and control. With no finished measure landing on shipped systems, **ai_safety** suffered—yet the continued development of public evaluation pathways, though delayed, was judged a credible signal of intent. Under metric rule 3, this earns a partial safety boost, offsetting part of the usual penalty for capability growth.

**Resilience** dipped slightly as monitoring strain persisted and no category 6 measure concluded. The EU’s focus on upstream control did little to harden downstream systems.

**eu_ai_sovereignty** held flat. The InvestAI Gigafactories’ impact had already been absorbed; no new compute capacity came online, and no event secured or denied access. The rising capability imposed a −1 decay, offset by no gains.

**eu_political_capital** declined. The portfolio charge for one large measure (−3) and the priority (−1) totaled −4. The proposal bonus for the Sovereign Evaluation and Control Framework was assessed at +1—modest, given its incomplete status—against a backdrop of ongoing unrest. No legitimacy lending occurred, as public sentiment remained below capital.

Protests in tech hubs continued, with no retraining pathways in sight. Sentiment held at **25.0**, a brittle equilibrium between fear and resignation.
