## In practice
We are no longer waiting for permission. The **Labour Transition Acceleration Fund** has now delivered its final tranche: retraining pipelines are operational, wage support has reached 1.2 million workers, and the employer levy is fully implemented. With that measure **finished**, we reclaim a sliver of political capital — not enough to undo our strain, but enough to act. And act we must.

The **Emergency Compute Mobilisation Directive** is not a proposal — it is a requisition. We are invoking Article 114 TFEU to override national regulators in France and Poland blocking grid access for AI infrastructure, citing energy sovereignty. ENTSO-E will designate emergency grid corridors by August, and the EIB will disburse €70bn in repurposed cohesion and pandemic funds under a new emergency instrument. ASML will be presented with binding orders for EU-dedicated lithography throughput, backed by procurement guarantees and counter-sanction clauses. The European Digital Infrastructure Consortium (EDIC) will operate the clusters, with model access governed by a new public interest firewall.

We know the resistance: Paris demands veto rights, Warsaw cites constitutional order, Berlin wants guarantees on industrial access. We are offering none. This is not negotiation — it is survival. The **emergent_decentralized_ai_infrastructure** in Germany, Spain, and the Baltics proves the demand exists; we will now meet it with authority, not leave it to fragments. If the Union cannot act as a state in a state of emergency, then it is already gone — and we will not preside over its quiet dissolution.
