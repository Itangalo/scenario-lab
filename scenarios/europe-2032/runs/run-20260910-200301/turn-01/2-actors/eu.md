## In practice
We are pushing the Shield through the Competitiveness and JHA Councils together under Article 114 plus Article 222 solidarity clause, tasked to ENISA and the EU-CyCLONe network with CER/NIS2 enforcement money front-loaded. TSOs in the two hit member states plus port and water operators get mandatory audit, segmented backup controls, and staged-tooling hunt teams this turn; exercises in winter 2026 rehearse containment without self-inflicted outages.

At the same time we keep M1 Gigafactories and M2 Tech sovereignty package moving: DG CNECT holds accelerated-permitting zones and grid-connection offers, EIB structures the €200bn tranches, and we use ASML/EUV leverage quietly to resist US export pressure while negotiating reciprocal frontier-access guarantees. The Shield does not add compute; it buys time for that compute to land.
