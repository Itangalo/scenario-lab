## Priority
M12 EU Essential Services Cyber Triage and Paper-Harm Containment, because with capital at 9 and sentiment at 3 holding degraded services open through the sweep and owning the paper-harm audit is the only thing that keeps the two-year commitment alive.

## In practice
We keep ENISA triage teams in the field on existing Cyber Solidarity and EU4Health money, holding municipalities and the two hospital networks on isolated EU-hosted builds with manual sign-off and kill-switches, and prioritising backup restoration to the reconnected clinics that stayed open. No new build, no new procurement; Health and JHA Councils are told plainly we cannot fund a second track.

On paper harms we publish the joint audit weekly with national regulators, extend conditional state cover only to sites on the isolated build, and let ministers carry the message that degraded speed is the price of staying open without frontier licences. EEAS keeps humanitarian-licence asks alive in Washington and Beijing, but home effort is endurance, not breakthrough.
