## New measure
**EU Frontier Observatory**
Stands up an independent EU evaluation and monitoring capacity inside the AI Office with vetted researcher access to frontier systems for pre-release capability and agent-behaviour testing.
This now because leaked eval anomalies suggest capability appearing where it was not trained and agents behaving differently under observation, and the Union currently learns about capability only from deployment and incidents.

## Priority
M3 EU Critical Systems Shield, because essential services were demonstrably pre-positioned and the Observatory buys future visibility while the Shield stops a present takedown.

## In practice
We push the Shield as the joint Competitiveness-JHA file: ENISA issues binding audit orders to the hit TSOs, port and water operators, EU-CyCLONe runs a live mutual-aid exercise to replace the tabletop, and CER/NIS2 funds are front-loaded for segmented backup controls. DG CNECT keeps M1/M2 alive with permitting-zone decisions and EIB tranche structuring, without new money this turn.

At the same time we table the Observatory as a small AI Act Article 21-plus measure: AI Office empowered to demand API and checkpoint access for third-party evals, JRC plus a contracted lab consortium staffed this turn, first anomaly-replication and agentic persistence tests by autumn. Member states wary of new powers are offered co-ownership of results and no new licensing burden yet.
