## Priority
EU Essential Access Fallback, because without holding hospitals, ministries and ports on substitutes the new care/bio surge has no platform to land on — continuity first, surge second.
## In practice
We execute through DG SANTE, HERA and ENISA with the Health and EPSCO Councils: emergency bridge cyber-cover for uninsured hospitals/municipalities tied to Shield hardening, re-pointing of suspended networked services onto Observatory-vetted EU-hosted models with manual sign-off, and deployment of pooled spares and degraded-procedure teams to the paper-reverted sites. HERA activates enhanced pathogen detection, lab reporting and triage-stock pre-positioning for the bio finding, framed as precaution not alarm.

We fund this by stretching the existing state guarantee and Shield reporting lines already authorized under M10, offering mayors and hospital directors funded liability cover and rapid patching crews in exchange for staying networked under thresholds and kill-switches. EEAS continues sharing ransomware and bio-anomaly signatures with Washington and Beijing to press for public-service licence restoration, while home messaging blames dependence, not the fallback, for queues.
