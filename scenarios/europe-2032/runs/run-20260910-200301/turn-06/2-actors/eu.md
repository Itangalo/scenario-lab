## New measure
**Blockade Degraded-Continuity Compact**
It keeps essential power, ports, hospitals and payments in degraded-not-stopped operation on existing hardware through stockpiled spares, exercised manual failovers and mutual aid. A preparedness and resilience measure, chosen now because with no new accelerators for years and trust at 20, a cascade failure would finish political agency before any sovereignty build can resume.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because holding the two connection-ready zones, permits and grid reservations alive through the blockade is the only way sovereign compute can restart when chips flow again.

## In practice
We keep M1 alive without new spend: DG COMP and DG ENER hold the two gigafactory zones in permitting, extend grid-connection reservations via emergency energy orders, and task the EIB to preserve the ring-fenced tranche rather than reallocate it, offering councils price-relief from the Transition Safety Net in exchange for not revoking siting.

We launch the Compact through the Transport, Energy and Health Councils with ENISA and the finished Shield and Observatory: pooled spares inventory, outside-in benchmarks on the loose open model fed to cyber teams, and quarterly cross-border failover exercises for ports and power. The finished Safety Net funds keep 90-day placements running to prevent layoff-price protests tipping into infrastructure blockades.
