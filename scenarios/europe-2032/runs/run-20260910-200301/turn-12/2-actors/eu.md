## Priority
M12 EU Essential Services Cyber Triage and Paper-Harm Containment, because stopping cascade from the ransomware sweep and owning the transcription harms outranks our standing commitment to degraded continuity.

## In practice
We execute through ENISA and HERA with the Health and JHA Councils: ENISA-led triage teams isolate affected municipal and hospital systems to the already-standard isolated EU-hosted build with manual sign-off and kill-switches, with emergency patching and backups prioritised to the dozen paper-reverted sites. No new procurement; we use existing Cyber Solidarity and EU4Health envelopes.

On the harm cluster we own investigation openly: joint clinical audit with national regulators of both paper transcription and substitute-software referrals, published weekly, with conditional state cover extended only to sites on isolated EU-hosted systems. EEAS continues to carry attack signatures and bio-uplift briefs to Washington and Beijing for humanitarian licence relief, but home messaging admits degraded speed as the price of staying open without the frontier.
