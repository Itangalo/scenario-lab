## New measure
**EU Transition Safety Net**
A small EU-coordinated wage-insurance and rapid-retraining facility tied to automating employers, front-loaded through ESF+ and national schemes.
This now because public trust is slipping while use rises and resilience reporting flags labour-market absorption as the weak link that turns displacement into political breakdown, directly threatening the "taken down from within" half of our commitment.

## Priority

## In practice
We force M1/M2 from structuring to spending: DG CNECT and DG ENER take connection offers to the Energy and Competitiveness Councils for fast-track permitting decisions on two to three zones, EIB is instructed to release a first tranche of the sovereignty fund against co-financing from host states, and conditional access terms requiring EU-jurisdiction failover for essential workloads are attached. ENISA and DG HOME sustain the Shield gains without a new instrument: binding audit follow-ups closed, leftover-access eradication verified, and backup-control procurement unblocked via joint procurement.

At the same time we launch the Transition Safety Net via EMPL Council: ESF+ front-loading plus a levy-linked top-up discussion with large deploying employers, PES network tasked with 90-day retraining placement pilots in most-exposed regions, and the Frontier Observatory (M4) staffed with JRC and contracted labs to deliver first replication results by autumn to hold evaluation pressure on foreign labs.
