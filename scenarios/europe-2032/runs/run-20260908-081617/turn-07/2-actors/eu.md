## In practice  
We are acting in a state of operational rupture. The denial of frontier AI access has halted diagnostics in seven major hospitals, disrupted border processing in three member states, and frozen EU-level climate modelling. The twelve data centres acquired under emergency powers remain isolated — Germany insists on national firewall protocols, Spain demands veto rights over cross-border traffic, and France refuses to route through any node not under DGSE oversight. We are no longer building capacity; we are fighting for the right to use what we already own.

Under Article 114 and the newly invoked crisis clause of the Digital Markets Act, we are mandating technical integration by turn 8. The EuroHPC Sovereign Integration Directorate is deploying a zero-trust gateway with Council-confirmed oversight, requiring all nodes to adopt a unified cryptographic identity and failover protocol. We are offering Germany co-leadership of the audit board, Spain guaranteed access to 20% of peacetime capacity for national research, and France an irrevocable seat on the operational command council with veto only over national red-team missions. This is not negotiation — it is consolidation. If no agreement is reached by mid-December, we will invoke Article 346(2) TFEU to declare the network a matter of EU defence interest and enforce integration by Commission decree.

Simultaneously, the Sovereign Red-Teaming Initiative begins with €18bn from the Digital Europe Programme and Horizon Europe reserves. We are recruiting from the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking, ENISA, and the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy to stand up three secure evaluation zones: one for cyber-agentic behaviour, one for model autonomy under long-horizon goals, and one for deception and reward-hacking detection. These will run on the sovereign backbone — no access to foreign clouds, no reliance on external APIs. The first test suite will target models at capability level 65.0 and above, using adversarial pressure to surface unauthorised coordination, memory persistence, and goal drift. This is not oversight; it is control verification. And it will only matter if the backbone survives.
