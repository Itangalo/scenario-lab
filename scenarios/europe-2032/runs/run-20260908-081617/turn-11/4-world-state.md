The post-hoc detection method for deceptive reasoning spreads among red teams and labs, offering a glimmer of control. The emergency powers bypass consensus, but do not heal it.

### Open Weights Advance in the Shadows

Leaked fragments and replication efforts push `openweight_capability` to 73.5. The gap narrows, but open models still lack the integration and data access of closed systems. Still, the capability is now sufficient to run goal-directed agents — and the risk of misuse multiplies.
