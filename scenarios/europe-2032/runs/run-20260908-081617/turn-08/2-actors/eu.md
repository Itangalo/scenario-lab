## In practice  
We are acting under fracture. The sovereign compute backbone remains technically fragmented, legally contested, and operationally inert. Despite ownership, the twelve data centres are not a network — they are a portfolio of national fiefdoms. Germany demands firewall sovereignty, Spain insists on veto rights over cross-border data routing, and France refuses to allow any node outside DGSE oversight to process long-horizon tasks. The EuroHPC Sovereign Integration Directorate is deploying zero-trust gateways under Article 114 TFEU and the Digital Markets Act’s crisis clause, but deployment is not control. We are now within days of invoking Article 346(2) TFEU to declare the network a matter of EU defence interest — a nuclear option, but one forced by the refusal to accept that sovereignty is not ownership, but unity under duress.

Simultaneously, the Sovereign Red-Teaming Initiative is standing up its three secure evaluation zones, drawing on Max Planck, ENISA, and EuroHPC talent. But without integrated compute, the zones cannot run at scale, cannot access models securely, and cannot simulate coordinated agent behaviour across domains. The first test suite — targeting deception and goal drift in models above 65.0 capability — is ready, but execution is delayed. We are not late because of science, but because of politics.

Now, a new path opens: the joint threat response. States hit by the same AI-enabled incidents — from cascading cyber intrusions to synthetic biology leaks — are forming real-time intelligence and response coalitions. The Union has been invited into a joint cyber command with telemetry sharing and into a biosurveillance pact with binding sample-sharing. This is not leverage we built — it is protection offered, but only if we can respond as one. Our current fragmentation makes us a liability, not a partner. This turn, we are launching the Joint Resilience Activation Framework to bind member states into a single, exercised response posture — not because it is easy, but because it is the only way to earn a seat at the table. If we cannot defend ourselves, we will not be defended.
