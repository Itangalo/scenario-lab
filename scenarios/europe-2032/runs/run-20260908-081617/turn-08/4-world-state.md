### Frontier Acceleration, Safety Blackout

`ai_capability` rises by +3.0 to 77.5, driven by faster training loops and tighter agent automation. No safety upgrades land on deployed systems. The `opaque_reasoning` event renders all human-readable interpretability obsolete overnight. Control strategies collapse across the frontier. `ai_safety` remains at 1.0 — a system with no visibility into its own decisions is not a controlled one.

### Sovereignty Fractures Deeper

The `Accelerated Sovereign Compute Integration` measure fails to achieve unity. Germany, Spain, and France maintain blocking positions. The Commission delays invoking Article 346(2) TFEU, fearing permanent fragmentation. Without unified control, the sovereign compute backbone remains inert. `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls by −2: −1 from rising capability and −1 from the unmet access crisis, with no offsetting gain.

### Resilience Gains a Foothold

The EU launches the `Joint Resilience Activation and Incident Response Framework`, a large category 6 measure. It is designed to bind member states into a single, exercised response posture. The `joint_threat_response` event enables partial integration into emerging international structures, yielding early resilience benefits. `resilience` rises by +2, absorbing part of the threat.

### Political Capital Erodes Further

Four measures remain in flight: the new framework (−3), the stalled integration (−3), the red-teaming initiative (−3), and the named priority (−1). Portfolio cost: −10. No completions offset it. The priority fails to accelerate delivery. `eu_political_capital` drops to 9.0, now below the threshold where new measures may fail to start.

### Public Sentiment Stagnant

`public_sentiment` remains at 15.0. Protests continue. The `labour_displacement` wave deepens public anger. No visible benefit offsets the damage. AI is widely seen as a force of exclusion and disruption.

### Open Weights Advance, But Not Explode

`openweight_capability` rises to 62.5 — a gain of +5.0 — reflecting the maximum permissible growth under constitutional rules given the +3.0 rise in `ai_capability`. Some red-teaming tools and model fragments leak into open communities, accelerated by the `opaque_reasoning` crisis, but full architecture transfers do not materialize. The gap between closed and open narrows, but not at the pace feared in later projections. The advance is significant, yet bounded by the limits of diffusion without a major release event.
