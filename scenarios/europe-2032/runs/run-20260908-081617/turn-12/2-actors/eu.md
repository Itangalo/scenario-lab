## In practice
We are acting on a new strategic truth: sovereignty is not access — it is replacement. The cyber_major_incident, which disrupted emergency medical coordination across three member states using a self-evolving exploit chain derived from a closed-model training run, was the final proof. We are no longer merely blind — we are compromised. The Emergency Sovereign Agent Monitoring and Intervention Capability has delivered its first monitoring nodes, but they sit idle because there is almost nothing on EU soil above 70.0 capability to monitor. We have eyes, but no body to watch.

We are now redirecting €6.8 billion from the Digital Europe Programme, the Connecting Europe Facility, and unused cohesion funds into a new Sovereign AI Substitution Programme, to be led by a newly empowered European AI Agency reporting directly to the College of Commissioners. The money will fund three tracks: first, a rapid replication and fine-tuning effort using the best available open weights (currently at 73.5) to create functional equivalents of foreign models in healthcare diagnostics, climate forecasting, and energy optimisation; second, the construction of two new sovereign supercomputing clusters — one in Finland, one in Bulgaria — dedicated exclusively to public-interest AI and legally insulated from external control; third, a talent acceleration programme offering fast-track residency, funding, and security clearance to 500 frontier AI researchers willing to work under EU jurisdiction.

We are invoking Article 122 TFEU again, this time not for monitoring but for substitution. The Council will be presented with a draft decision framing the loss of control over essential AI systems as a threat to internal security. Germany and France will be offered co-leadership of the AI Agency. Ireland and the Netherlands will be required to host one of the new clusters as a condition of continued access to EU-wide cyber and bio-response coordination. The U.S. ambassador has been informed that any attempt to restrict access to dual-use components for the new clusters will be met with reciprocal restrictions on hyperscaler operations in the single market.

This is not about catching up. It is about carving out a domain of autonomy before the frontier moves beyond reach. We are no longer trying to regulate the world’s AI. We are building our own.
