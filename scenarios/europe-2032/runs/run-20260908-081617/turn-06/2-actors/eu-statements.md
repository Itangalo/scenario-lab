- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Prevent irreversible dependency on foreign-controlled AI infrastructure by securing sovereign access to foundational compute and model capacity, ensuring the Union can enforce its own safeguards and sustain essential functions under denial conditions
  - Grounds: The `eu_frontier_access_denied` event this turn has rendered our previous commitment insufficient — it is no longer enough to "secure foundational AI capacity"; we must explicitly anchor it in enforceable sovereignty and continuity under denial. Our focus shifts from capacity-building alone to ensuring that capacity is legally, physically and operationally under EU authority, with fail-safes for disconnection.
