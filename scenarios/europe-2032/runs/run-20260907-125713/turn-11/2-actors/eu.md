## New measure  
**Re-establish sovereign access to frontier AI capability**  
We are launching an emergency programme to restore EU access to AI systems at or near the global frontier, under enforceable legal and technical conditions that prevent arbitrary denial, by directly funding and governing a closed, sovereign training run reaching at least 70.0 capability by turn 14.  

`Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 14`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase eu_ai_sovereignty by 15–20 points, stabilise eu_political_capital by demonstrating strategic autonomy, and create a platform for future evaluation and resilience testing. Indirectly pressures ai_safety by enabling public technical scrutiny of a high-capability system under controlled conditions.  
`Applies to:` EU jurisdiction, with binding conditions on participating labs, energy providers, and security agencies.

## Priority  
Re-establish sovereign access to frontier AI capability  
This is the only measure that directly reverses the strategic humiliation of being cut off from the leading model at a moment of crisis — an event that exposed our dependence and shattered the credibility of our autonomy claims; without it, no other measure we pass will be believed, inside or outside the Union.

## In practice  
We are acting under Article 122 TFEU and the safeguard clause of the Digital Markets Act to override national competition rules and fast-track the creation of a temporary public entity — the European Frontier Initiative (EFI) — with full authority over compute allocation, data access, and export controls for this training run. The European Investment Bank is deploying a €9.2 billion emergency facility, drawn from the pandemic recovery reserve and the defence innovation window, to fund GPU procurement, energy contracts, and talent retention bonuses. We are commandeering 6 exaF-days from the Sovereign AI Core and reallocating them exclusively to this project, suspending all other high-capacity workloads until turn 14.

The Council, in an extraordinary session under the Competitiveness configuration, has approved the measure by qualified majority, despite objections from Ireland and Sweden over market distortion and from Germany over centralisation. In exchange for their support, we have committed to open a second sovereign run under joint German-French leadership in turn 15, and to guarantee that all model weights remain classified and under EAAD-controlled access. ENISA and the EAAD are embedded in the project from day one, with real-time logging and inspection rights, to ensure that safety concerns do not become a backdoor for external interference. This is not a partnership with the frontier labs — it is a takeover of the capability they gatekeep. We will not ask permission again.
