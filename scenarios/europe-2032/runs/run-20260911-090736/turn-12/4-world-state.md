### The attack and the antidote arrive together
Winter brought a ransomware sweep stitched together by automated tooling. Hospitals postponed procedures, city administrations lost ticketing and benefits portals, and a tainted software component forced ENISA to warn that no one yet knew the full blast radius. Attribution stalled within weeks. News tickers counted locked systems while technicians worked from printed runbooks.

In the same weeks, defenders got their first real win in years. University labs and two European vendors demonstrated patching that kept pace with newly found flaws and detectors that flagged coordinated swarms rather than known signatures. Brussels reprogrammed emergency digital funds to push the tooling through existing certification channels — first to large operators, then to the small municipal water and health providers missed in the autumn drills.

It half-worked. Certified hospitals absorbed the wave and restored service in days. Uncertified municipalities again lagged, short of staff to install what Brussels shipped for free. Anger at outages mixed with relief that something finally blocked an attack class.

### Money leaves, weights spread, cures promise
Venture funding for frontier building collapsed. Valuations halved, expansion plans were cancelled, and two hosting arrangements the pooled compact had counted on evaporated. Officials quietly scouted for cheap cancelled compute even as partners warned there would be no one to operate it.

A new openly released model, near the closed frontier, was downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in days. Police found its tooling in the attack chain within a fortnight — unrecallable, running on private hardware.

Then came tailored therapies that put previously untreatable conditions into remission. The treatments worked, but the most capable models behind them sat outside European control, and procurement talks through Japan and Canada stalled over price and access tiers. Patients celebrated; editorials asked why the cure proved dependence.

Redress audits ground on, ordering re-decisions and back-pay, while two regions still withheld logs. By June the Union was containing more and convincing less.
