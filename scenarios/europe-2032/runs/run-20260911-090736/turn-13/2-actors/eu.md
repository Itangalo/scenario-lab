## New measure
**Municipal Repair Corps for Uncertified Operators**
It funds and deploys joint ENISA-civil protection technician teams to install the swarm-defence patching in the uncertified municipal water, health and local-government operators that lagged for lack of staff.
This and now because certified hospitals recovered in days while uncertified municipals lagged for staff not kit, and with open weights now permanently at the frontier and capital and trust near collapse, a staffed last-mile rollout is the only resilience lever that both stops the cascade and shows redress-weary publics something working.

## Priority
Municipal Repair Corps for Uncertified Operators — because it directly serves the two-year commitment to hold social absorption through ungovernable diffusion by keeping essential services and benefits running where trust is lowest.

## In practice
We act through ENISA, DG ECHO civil-protection pooling and the CER Shield certification machinery, reprogramming unspent Digital Europe and UCPM funds to pay seconded utility engineers and university vendor teams on 6-month municipal contracts. Competitiveness and Health Council formations are asked only to waive secondment caps, not for new money, to fit political capital at 21.

On the other tracks we spend no new instrument. Redress re-decisions and back-pay continue under market-surveillance authorities with published logs; Entry-Level Guarantee vouchers keep flowing via ESF+; the Middle-Power Compact is held to cheap cancelled-compute scouting and joint therapy procurement via Japan/Canada, with no new hosting pledges this turn.
