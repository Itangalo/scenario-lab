## New measure
**EU Essential Services Continuity Switch**
Keeps hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the leading US model running by switching them to EU-anchored and open-weight fallbacks with emergency procurement and support.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because the cut-off is an active continuity failure on top of the labour shock and capability jump, and our two-year commitment to withstand coercion and absorb harm demands a continuity answer now, not another compute promise.

## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because the access denial plus open-weight frontier release plus intrusion pre-positioning makes cascading failure the risk that outranks even the gigafactory fight this turn.

## In practice
We run M3 as the emergency frame: ENISA and HERA map who lost access, DG DIGIT and EU4Health issue emergency switch contracts to EU-hosted providers and supported open-weight stacks, funded from the Shield's detection and hardening line, conditional on backup and logging obligations. Interior and health ministries get funded continuity teams, not waivers.

We hold M1/M2 to stop further fragmentation: no new bilateral power/tax terms outside the Common Terms (M4) notification regime, EIB guarantees only for sites that accept anchoring and continuity clauses, with the defecting site offered re-entry via accelerated grid connection. On labour, we task DG EMPL to ready retraining and income-bridge options inside the Shield's social-absorption track to be proposed as a full measure next turn when capital allows.
