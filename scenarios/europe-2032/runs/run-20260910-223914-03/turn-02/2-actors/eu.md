## New measure
**Common Terms for Foreign AI Partnerships**
Requires member-state deals with hyperscalers and foreign AI providers to meet EU legal anchoring, data and switch-off protections, notified to the Commission.
This is category 8 International coordination and leverage, because a single defection that undercuts our compute and sovereignty line will unravel the gigafactory bidding discipline and our leverage if left unanswered now.

## Priority
InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) – because holding four to five sites to joint investment decision is what the defection directly threatens, and without it resilience and leverage have no base.

## In practice
We are running M1/M2 through DG CNECT, DG ENER and the EIB, tying grid priority and InvestAI guarantees to EU legal anchoring and to the new common terms: any bilateral hyperscaler arrangement must be notified, meet data-locality and continuity clauses, and cannot offer tax or power terms below the joint framework. The defecting capital is offered accelerated connection for its site inside the framework rather than outside it.

We push the Shield (M3) via HERA and ENISA with the Civil Protection Mechanism uplift, starting reporting drills and pooled detection procurement in energy and telecoms first where the swarm traces hit, with EU-paid upgrades conditional on exercised backup plans. Hospitals and municipal utilities get a delayed-compliance track with funded support to stop Interior blocking.
