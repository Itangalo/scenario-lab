## New measure
**EU Graduate Transition and Wage Insurance Bridge**
It funds rapid retraining, hiring incentives and temporary wage insurance for entry-level white-collar roles displaced by AI, paired with keeping the safety breakthrough deployed.
Why this and why now: with capital at 10 and layoffs now named in law, accountancy, software and admin, the hardening half of our commitment fails if displacement becomes a shock; a small labour bridge is the only affordable move that also buys legitimacy for continued accord alignment.

## Priority
EU Graduate Transition and Wage Insurance Bridge, because visible job-loss without hiring demands protection now over further diplomacy, and it directly serves our two-year commitment to harden what society cannot afford to lose.

## In practice
We task DG EMPL and DG CNECT under ESF+ redeployment to stand up the Bridge: short-cycle reskilling vouchers, employer hiring credits for displaced graduates, and six-month wage insurance in the five named sectors, run through national public employment services. EPSCO is asked only for reallocation, not new money, to fit capital at 10, with EIB micro-guarantees for SMEs that retain and retrain.

We keep the finished Accord Cell alive on existing EEAS/JRC staffing to absorb the interpretability breakthrough into observer methods and bio-screening exchanges, while ENISA sustains Shield drills in hit cities. No new compute promise is made; we trade quick social protection for time to make observer status buy tier access.
