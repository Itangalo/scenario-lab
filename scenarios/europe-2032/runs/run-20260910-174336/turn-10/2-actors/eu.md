## Priority
EU Visible Benefit Adoption Scale-Up, because it directly serves the two-year commitment to rebuild consent and is the only measure that converts this turn's rare success into capital before the cyber fallout hardens hostility.

## In practice
We task DG DIGIT and DG SANTE under the Digital Europe Programme to fund replication packages for the successful waiting-list and fast-decision deployments, using EU-wide procurement with certified control properties from this turn's safety breakthrough, conditional on EU hosting and incident reporting. EPSCO and Health Council are asked to nominate 10 volunteer regions for first rollout.

We keep M9 running on its holdover track: DG EMPL rolls unspent ESF+ forward for vouchers and wage insurance where job centres deliver, ENISA sustains drills only in previously hit cities and the Spain-France swaps, with no new money tabled. CER Directive powers are used to frame the automated-attack response as exercised continuity, not new legislation, to avoid spending capital we do not have.
