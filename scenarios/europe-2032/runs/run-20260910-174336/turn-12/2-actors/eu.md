## New measure
None this turn, while political capital sits at 4.0 and public sentiment at 13.0 with no in-flight vehicle to carry new costs.

We are waiting for the safety-breakthrough adoption and the stabilised employment picture to restore a minimum of consent before tabling any new funded instrument that would fail on contact.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority named this turn, because the portfolio holds no in-flight measure to push and naming a finished line as priority would misdirect what little capital remains.

## In practice
We hold the line on what is already built rather than starting anything new. DG CNECT and the EU Model Evaluation and Assurance Office are instructed to take the interpretability/control result that the labs already want and apply it to the two audited health-administration models still running under EU hosting, publishing the certification rather than legislating it. ENISA sustains the paper-fallback and generator routines that held during the autumn sweep under existing CER and civil-protection powers, with no new hardening money tabled.

We use the ordinary-offices evidence — productivity up, employment not down — as the consent story the two-year commitment needs: Commission filming stays on clinics where waits fell and on offices where junior staff kept jobs, Interior Council continues emergency policing escorts for blocked builds in Spain, Netherlands and France under existing public-order coordination, and DG EMPL lets the finished voucher and wage-insurance lines pay out where job centres still process them. Where the research breakthrough lands — US or Chinese labs first — we do not chase it; we note it for procurement later, once hosting is accepted again.
