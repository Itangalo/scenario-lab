## New measure
**EU Clinic Queue Replication Pact**
It funds replication of the two certified health-administration models to willing regions with EU hosting and certification, paired with visible queue-time publication.
This is category 9 Diffusion, adoption and public trust, and now is the moment because the adoption_success event finally attributes falling waits to a European decision, giving the two-year commitment to rebuild consent something concrete to scale while capital is at 9.0.

## In practice
We hold to the two-year commitment to rebuild consent by keeping services running. DG SANTE and DG CNECT, via the EU Model Evaluation and Assurance Office, extend the existing certificates to new volunteer hospitals where EU hosting is accepted, with no new law and no new mandate, publishing wait-time deltas clinic by clinic.

Interior Council continues existing public-order escorts for builds in Spain, Netherlands and France, and DG EMPL lets finished voucher and wage-insurance lines pay where job centres function. Commission communication stays on filmed queues and retained junior jobs, not on new promises, to let the adoption success land before we spend further.
