## Priority
EU Accord Access and Verification Cell, because with capital at 9 and no gigafactory built, securing a seat and supply-chain leverage inside the accord outranks any domestic build we cannot fund.

## In practice
We task EEAS and DG TRADE under the Anti-Coercion Instrument logic to demand observer/consulted status in the weights-security and bio-design pillars, offering coordinated ASML/lithography export-control alignment and JRC/ECDC verification labs as our ticket. Council Foreign Affairs and Competitiveness formations are asked only for a mandate to talk, not money, to fit capital at 9.

DG CNECT and JRC redeploy remaining Digital Europe funds to staff a small verification cell — weight-security audit methods and bio-design screening — that makes EU safety work a shared obligation under the accord, while ENISA keeps Shield backups and drills alive in hit cities on existing budgets. No new compute promise is made; we trade leverage and verification for tier protection.
