## New measure
**EU Essential Services Shield and Cross-Border Contingency Pact**
Deploys ENISA-led hardened backup, failover and rapid-restore capacity for energy, health, water and municipal IT plus rehearsed EU-wide contingency plans, funded via repurposed Digital Europe and Civil Protection Mechanism.
Category 6 Preparedness and resilience is why now: the automated ransomware/infrastructure attack proved offensive tooling has outrun uneven defences, and without exercised degrade-not-stop capacity the next incident cascades across member states.

## Priority
EU Essential Services Shield and Cross-Border Contingency Pact, because live disruption of public services outranks even gigafactory timelines this turn and visible restoration is what rebuilds capital to sustain the sovereignty build.

## In practice
We push DG CNECT and ENISA with EU-CyCLONe and ACER to execute the Shield: joint threat-hunting carry-over from the finished Sprint, pre-positioned clean backups and manual-failover kits for the hit TSOs, hospitals and municipalities, and a first EU-wide cyber-contingency exercise in Q2 under the Civil Protection Mechanism. Money is Digital Europe redeployment plus EIB resilience loans, with the Health and Justice Councils co-owning to stop mandate fights.

We keep M1 Gigafactories and M2 permitting zones moving via Competitiveness Council — locking Spanish, French and Polish sites to grid-connection reservations and EIB guarantees — while we answer the decision-scandal inside existing law: task the AI Office and national market-surveillance authorities with a coordinated AI Act conformity review and remedy fund for the affected social-insurance/policing system, framing it as enforcement, not new legislation, to contain blame.
