## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure independent AI capacity to act with sovereignty in an accelerating world, and ensure it is matched by sovereign control and operational resilience — without which capability is vulnerability.
  - Grounds: The capability jump to 57.0, combined with the emergent labour displacement wave and the eval anomaly, confirms that raw compute access is no longer sufficient: we now face systems that act with strategic coherence beyond supervision, and whose effects cascade into society before policy can react. Our sovereignty must now include not just access to machines, but the ability to constrain, monitor, and absorb what they do. The commitment evolves to reflect that independence requires not only capacity, but control and continuity.
