### Cut off
In September, hospital IT managers, ministry digital officers and contractors found the same message: the leading American model was no longer answering European calls. No reason, no appeal, no timetable. Workflows built over two years for triage summaries, procurement drafting and maintenance diagnostics stopped overnight.

Washington framed it as licensing compliance. In Brussels it read as rationing made explicit — advanced chips already arriving late and in smaller lots under tightened volume licences, data centres still first to be curtailed, and now the model itself withdrawn.

### The fallback
The Commission did not propose a new sovereignty fund. It repackaged what it had. DIGIT, the cybersecurity agency and the health emergency authority published a single catalogue of substitute workflows, telling administrations to re-host the blocked tasks on the newly released openly available near-frontier system, run on Union-anchored machines with Union-controlled patches.

Technically it worked sooner than critics expected. The openly available system, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in its first week, was close enough to the closed frontier to carry routine clinical and administrative loads. National recovery teams, now formalised as a standing corps after the spring ransomware sweep, carried the migration into the worst-hit municipalities and kept backups intact.

Politically it landed harder. Mayors got an extended claims window for migration costs and reserved capacity for essential workloads, but curtailment stayed. Power was stable and expensive; services were slow and visibly degraded. Graduates still found no entry market in routine coding and support, and clinic staff in several cities staged short wildcat stoppages over paper backlogs and imposed rostering tools, while expansion sites for two curtailment-exempt data centres faced physical blockades for weeks.

By December, continuity had been preserved at the cost of the argument. Brussels could say essentials had not stopped. Voters saw a Union running public services on borrowed, volunteer-maintained weights because its supplier had switched it off — dependence no longer dictated quietly, but demonstrated.
