Spring-Dec 2031: automated ransomware sweep hit municipalities, clinics and shared software; paper systems for weeks while islanding and mobile recovery teams kept hospitals, water, ports up. Mayors got single claims window; export licensing tightened with volume licences slowing chips, raising prices, making data-centre curtailment first permanent. Entry hiring in routine coding/support collapsed. Power stable-expensive, services patched-slow.

In September the leading US model stopped answering European calls — Washington called it licensing compliance, Brussels read as explicit rationing. Commission repackaged existing tools: DIGIT, cybersecurity and health authorities issued catalogue to re-host blocked triage, procurement and diagnostics tasks on newly released open near-frontier system on Union machines. Migration via standing recovery corps worked technically, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times, carrying routine loads.

By December continuity preserved at cost: extended claims and reserved capacity but curtailment stayed, services degraded, clinic wildcat stoppages over backlogs, blockades of curtailment-exempt data centres. Voters saw public services running on borrowed volunteer weights switched off by supplier — dependence demonstrated, not just dictated.
