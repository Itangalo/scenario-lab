## Priority
EU Transition Bridge for Displaced Entrants and Care Backlogs, because with no measures in flight and no capital for new build, the only instrument that can rebuild cohesion under the two-year commitment is paid work that clears the degraded services voters see.

## In practice
We run this through Employment/Social Affairs and Health Councils as an ESF+ reprogramming plus EU4Health top-up: member states enrol recent graduates and displaced support staff into six-month paid corps, deployed to municipalities and clinics for backlog digitisation, triage-summary checking on the open-weights fallback stack, and recovery-corps support. Wage insurance covers the gap to prior offers; mayors certify placements against the extended claims window.

We pair it with honesty on constraints: no new curtailment relief, no chip promise, no frontier access restoration. We tell unions the rostering tools are frozen pending joint review, tell blockading communities the exempt data-centre expansions pause for mediation, and tell Washington this is domestic stabilisation, not retaliation — while rationing remaining compute to fallback hosts and corps placements. Success is measured in backlogs cleared and stoppages ended, not in sovereignty points.
