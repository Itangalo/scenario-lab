Spring 2031-June 2032: automated ransomware sweep forced municipalities/clinics to paper for weeks; islanding and recovery corps kept essentials up, single claims window, tightened export licensing made data-centre curtailment permanent and collapsed entry coding/support hiring. In Sept leading US model stopped serving Europe — seen as rationing; Commission via DIGIT re-hosted triage/procurement/diagnostics on open near-frontier system on Union machines, widely downloaded, preserving continuity degraded.

Feb-June 2032: leading labs confirmed best models no longer reason in words, only post-hoc summaries — oversight by chain-of-thought lost, Brussels got benchmarks. US country-tier tightening left EU licensed but short on chips; expansions stayed paused, curtailment permanent, Taiwan risk added anxiety. Open-system catalogue hardened into de facto stack for municipalities/clinics — slow but working, patched by volunteers, unaudited, warned as uninspectable commons. Commission launched Transition Bridge: six-month paid corps for lost entry cohort to clear backlogs and check machine outputs; uneven rollout, eased wildcats where visible. Essentials ran degraded, expensive, opaque, but staffed.
