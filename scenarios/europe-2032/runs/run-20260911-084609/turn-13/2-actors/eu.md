## Priority
EU Transition Bridge for Displaced Entrants and Care Backlogs, because visible pay and cleared backlogs are the only source of cohesion left while the new hardening patch builds — without it capital and sentiment collapse before biosecurity lands.

## In practice
We run the Bridge through ESF+ and EU4Health as before: Employment and Health Councils extend six-month corps placements, mayors certify backlog clearance, unions keep joint review over rostering tools. Remaining compute stays rationed to fallback hosts serving triage and diagnostics — no new curtailment relief promised.

We bolt the hardening patch onto that same delivery chain via HERA, ECDC and ENISA: deny-lists and human-review gates for bio-relevant queries on the Union-hosted open model, offline fallback protocols when filters trip, and a single incident/near-miss window tied to the claims system corps teams already use. Volunteer patches get a vetted channel, not a ban, to avoid breaking clinics that now depend on them.
