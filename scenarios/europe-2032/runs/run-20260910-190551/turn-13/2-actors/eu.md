This and now because physical blockades are delaying the only sovereign capacity we have left while public sentiment is at 8, and without a trusted siting bargain no compute, resilience or recovery measure can land; a small diffusion/trust instrument (category 9) is the cheapest way to rebuild capital to hold services.

## Priority
M13 — the Trusted Siting Pact just added — because unblocking builds and rebuilding local consent outranks everything else while outages persist and no other measure is in flight.

## In practice
We run this through Cohesion and Energy Councils with ENISA and national regulators, using reprogrammed cohesion envelopes and grid-connection agreements, not new mandates: mayors get a certified clean-restore and offline-kit guarantee plus a local dividend (training places, power-price rebate) for each site that proceeds.

We ask protesting councils and unions for a monitored construction moratorium — no forced clearing — while independent safety and environmental audits are published per site. Interior and Employment formations hold the line that foreign-run warehouses face the same audit standard, to keep blame from falling outward again and to turn verified recoveries into consent for capacity.
