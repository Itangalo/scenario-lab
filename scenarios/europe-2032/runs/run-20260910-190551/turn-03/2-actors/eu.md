## In practice

The Watch cell provides the threat model from the open-weight release now on private hardware.

For continuity we direct DG CNECT and HERA to map every hospital, ministry and port system dependent on the denied foreign model and switch them to EU-hosted alternatives procured under existing InvestAI and Digital Europe envelopes — no new compute programme, but emergency offtake and fine-tuning on sovereign capacity. We confront the defecting member state in COREPER with a solidarity offer: pooled inference at cost if it aligns procurement, making bilateral undercutting unnecessary.
