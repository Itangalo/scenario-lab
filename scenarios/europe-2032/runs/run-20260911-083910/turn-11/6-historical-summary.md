Autumn 2031 brought fragile stabilization: winter pathogen cordons held, clinics stayed on paper triage while sequencing/wastewater sentinels chased late chains, with emergency funds keeping staff paid and EU cyber repair corps rebuilding from offline backups.

A new leap in automated coding/intrusion compressed exploit assembly to hours, hammering hospital IT, dispatch and pharmacy systems; defenders held by isolation and relentless patching but confidence eroded.

Meanwhile tailored cures for untreatable conditions deployed abroad on foreign models dark to Europe since mid-containment. Brussels launched joint procurement and conditional approvals to run therapy protocols on domestic EU-hosted open models, but one member state broke ranks with a cheaper separate foreign access deal, rejecting solidarity credits.

By December EU-hosted treatments reached only a handful of clinics — symbolic relief. Fear eased slightly, anger at foreign dependence deepened. Survival held; common strategy fractured.
