## Priority
M9 EU Trusted Medical Access Programme, because with clinics still on paper triage and a major automated attack landing on hospital IT and public services, getting even a few EU-hosted cures visibly delivered is the only remaining source of legitimacy that can rebuild capital.

## In practice
We hold the line on M9 as procurement, not legislation: HERA joint purchasing and EMA conditional approvals to run licensed therapy protocols on domestic EU-hosted open models, with solidarity inference credits kept open to the defecting member state. No new Council vote, no new fund — DG SANTE and HERA executing within existing mandates so nothing requires cohesion we do not have.

For the cyber major incident we do not open a new instrument. We task the finished Grid Shield assets, the Cyber Emergency Repair Cell roster and the Fallback Care Corps under ENISA/CERT-EU coordination to isolate wards, rebuild dispatch, pharmacy and municipal systems from offline backups, and patch on the machine-written probe cycle. Containment-first, degraded-not-stopped, paid from emergency overtime already authorised.
