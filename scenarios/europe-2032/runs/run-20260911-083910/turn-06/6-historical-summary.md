EU triage response: Union computer-response and national crews restored backups with fast-track procurement, welcomed but temporary; many clinics on paper, mayors complained.

Sovereignty drive hollow: data-centre capital programme closed first phase with permits preserved but no construction on 5 reserved sites (Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm, Warsaw); power protests continued, private capital absent.

Cohesion frayed: one capital signed separate US provider access deal undercutting joint Washington compute ask; Paris/The Hague condemned breach. Joint licence alignment with Netherlands/Japan/Korea held.

US implemented national-asset doctrine: tiered foreign model access, tighter export reviews; EU diplomats faced harder terms. Public mood bleak, resentment over dependence.
