### AI Capability Crosses the Threshold  
The global AI frontier advances by 1.0 this turn, reaching 60.5. This marks the crossing of a psychological and technical threshold: systems now routinely execute multi-day professional projects end to end, displacing junior roles across software, research, and operations. Developers report early signs of recursive self-improvement, with AI systems contributing meaningfully to their own next-generation training pipelines.

### Open Weights Make Measured Gains  
A memory-augmented model framework derived from a frontier system is partially reconstructed and shared within trusted research collectives, despite export controls. While the full architecture remains out of reach, key components are reimplemented, pushing openweight_capability to 47.0—a modest but meaningful advance. Persistent agent behaviour remains out of reach for open models this turn, but the progress signals growing capability in distributed, resource-constrained settings. Cyber actors gain limited planning depth, but not yet at the level of state-grade tools.

### Safety Erodes Amid Escalating Gaps  
With no major safety measure implemented and capability rising sharply, ai_safety drops to 17.0. The gap between what systems can do and what safeguards exist widens dangerously. The European Evaluation Shield’s pilot audits continue, but the legal challenge undermines confidence in its authority before it can scale.

### Cyber Defences Lag Behind Threats  
Resilience dips slightly to 34.0 as the hospital ransomware attack revealed systemic weaknesses faster than new defences can be rolled out. While the **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield** is formally scoped and prioritised, it remains in drafting—no binding requirements are yet in force. ENISA publishes preliminary red-team scenarios, but cross-border coordination remains voluntary.

### Political Capital Under Strain  
The EU pushes forward on multiple fronts, but political capital erodes to 23.0. The portfolio charge remains high, and public sentiment sours further due to job displacement fears and the fallout from **election_annulled**, which fuels narratives of AI-driven information decay. The priority on cyber resilience yields no immediate benefit.

### Sovereignty Holds, But No New Gains  
The completion of SEAL last turn solidifies a baseline of autonomy, but no new sovereignty gains land this turn. Gigafactories progress, but no finishing event occurs. Sovereignty remains at 29.0.
