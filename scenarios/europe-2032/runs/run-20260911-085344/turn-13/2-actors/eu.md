## New measure
**EU Care Workforce Emergency Retention Pact**
Pays retention bonuses, funds emergency cross-border staffing pools and negotiates minimum-service guarantees with care unions to keep wards open through the strike.
This now because the cross-border care-worker strike is closing the outpatient capacity that containment depends on, and without staff no sovereign medical channel or resilience surge can hold; a labour protection instrument (category 7) directly targets retention and service continuity.

## Priority
EU Care Workforce Emergency Retention Pact – because rosters collapsing now outranks accession talks later, and holding wards open is the precondition for both legitimacy and the two-year commitment to survive ungovernable capability.

## In practice
We act through EPSCO and Health Council under the EU4Health and ESF+ emergency flexibilities: Commission funds time-limited retention top-ups and overtime back-pay in the two containment states, ELA coordinates licensed cross-border nurse/technician pools, and HERA keeps isolation corridors staffed under emergency staffing laws.

We keep M13 accession work running via EEAS in the weights-security and bio-design working groups, offering supply-chain and clinical incident data for re-licensed therapy models, while M12 ring-fenced inference is narrowed to the handful of hospitals actually delivering tailored treatments to avoid spreading the overtime pool thinner. We ask the large member state to pause separate procurement poaching during the strike in exchange for co-chairing the joint verifier bid.
