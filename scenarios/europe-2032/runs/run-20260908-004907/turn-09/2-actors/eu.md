## Priority

## In practice
We are shifting from enforcement to foundation. The Public Audit Framework has collapsed in legal dispute, and with political capital at 9.0 and public sentiment at 25.0, we cannot rebuild credibility through confrontation. Instead, we are acting on the emergent backchannel: technical envoys from Japan and South Korea have confirmed that firms like Tokyo Electron and SK Hynix are willing to fast-track equipment flows to EU sites if we can demonstrate sovereign, secure deployment capacity. The InvestAI Gigafactory in Finland is the only project that meets that threshold — and we are now activating emergency provisions under the TEN-E Regulation to bypass stalled permitting, unlock construction finance through off-take guarantees from the European Defence Fund and EuroHPC, and secure grid priority via the Clean Energy Package. Helsinki’s heat-recovery agreement neutralizes local opposition, and we are offering SMEs and national labs guaranteed access tiers to build domestic buy-in. This is no longer about catching up — it is about becoming a trusted pole in a multipolar AI ecosystem, where safety is enforced not by market threat, but by the weight of what we can run, protect, and share on our own terms.
