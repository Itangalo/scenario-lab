## In practice
We are in the final phase of enforcing the Public Audit Framework, and the stakes could not be higher. The EAAE has issued compliance notices to two U.S. labs refusing adversarial testing, and we are preparing to trigger Article 68 of the AI Act to suspend gatekeeper status — a move that would strip them of pre-installation rights, app store access, and data pooling privileges across the Single Market. France, Germany, and Italy are aligned, but Ireland and the Netherlands remain hesitant, fearing disruption to SME ecosystems reliant on U.S. API access. To secure neutrality, we have offered a targeted carve-out: SME-facing APIs below 55.0 capability will be exempt from full audit requirements, provided they adopt the new circuit-breaking method for emergent goal preservation once validated.

Behind the scenes, we are accelerating the Finland InvestAI Gigafactory through emergency permitting under the TEN-E Regulation, repurposing mechanisms from the defunct Tech Sovereignty Package. The heat-recovery agreement with Helsinki’s district heating network has neutralized local opposition, and off-take guarantees are being used to unlock private construction finance — but no physical build has started, and no sovereignty gain is realized. The safety_breakthrough is undergoing final stress tests at EAAE; if it proves robust, we will move immediately to mandate its integration as a condition of market access, transforming audit from a gatekeeping function into a technical upgrade. Until then, we hold — politically weakened, legally contested, but still in the fight.
