### The Fracture Deepens

The European Union attempted a strategic pivot from failed unilateral build efforts to coalition-based leverage, accelerating the InvestAI Gigafactory and doubling down on the Coalition for Open Access and Restraint (COAR). A new Emergency Access Protocol was launched under export control law, aiming to deter arbitrary model lockouts through coordinated retaliation. The Commission framed the move as a necessary evolution: sovereignty through interdependence, not isolation.

But credibility was already crumbling. A member state—widely reported to have struck a side deal with a U.S. hyperscaler—granted preferential access to frontier models in exchange for data sovereignty concessions, directly undermining EU-wide safeguards. The agreement bypassed COAR’s proposed evaluation framework before it had even taken shape. The move was defended domestically as pragmatic necessity, but internationally read as proof that the Union could not enforce cohesion.

Tokyo declined to activate the Digital Testbed, citing “unresolved alignment on enforcement thresholds.” Seoul withheld commitment on talent corridors, demanding reciprocal access to Dutch lithography data first. The Netherlands expressed support in principle but delayed red-teaming node funding pending legal review. Without binding participation, COAR remained a framework in name only.

Public sentiment eroded further as strikes in public administration spread, fueled by visible automation rollouts and the perception that AI governance served only to protect foreign systems. Hospitals still operated under degraded diagnostic capacity, and no visible benefit offset the disruption.

The InvestAI Gigafactory remained unstarted. No foundation was laid. Political capital, already at crisis levels, continued to bleed under the weight of stalled initiatives and now open fragmentation.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Falls

Global AI capability rose slightly, driven by closed-lab advancements now inaccessible to Europe. Open-weight models inched forward as tool-integrated agents proliferated in underground developer forums. With no safety mechanisms adopted and incident response still fragmented, ai_safety declined. Resilience weakened further as critical systems absorbed unmitigated strain.
