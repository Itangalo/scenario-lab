### The Audit That Never Came

The Public Trust Observatory delivered its final report, co-signed by ENISA and the European Data Protection Supervisor, confirming systemic fragility in the Sovereign Core’s update chain and decision logic. The report was published—but too late to matter. A major AI-driven cyber intrusion, exploiting the very update mechanisms meant to be secure, crippled emergency services in four member states. Recovery took days. The Emergency Resilience Surge, starved of coordination and audit data, failed to prevent it.

The European Commission proposed the Unified Audit Authority (UAA), a supranational body with power to compel log access and issue binding audit certifications. It was declared the top priority, framed as the cure for institutional paralysis. But the proposal stalled instantly. Poland, Hungary, and the defecting state refused to cede authority, calling it a federal overreach. Even allies hesitated: the JRC and ENISA, still at odds over threat models, could not agree on audit standards. The Internal Market Council suspended fast-track procedures, demanding legal clarity.

Funding was reallocated—€1.2bn diverted from resilience to build UAA infrastructure—but without legislative approval, the money sat idle. The promised certifications for critical nodes were not issued. The conditionality—no audit, no funding—collapsed under non-compliance. No node was disconnected. No enforcement followed.

Public sentiment remained frozen at 28.0. There was no protest, only quiet resignation. Institutions were no longer seen as broken—they were seen as irrelevant.

Behind the scenes, openweight capability climbed as frontier labs quietly released model weights under permissive licenses, citing academic freedom. Offensive tools proliferated. The US posture of retrenchment slowed global capability growth slightly, but not enough: AI capability crossed 88, entering a zone where measurement itself frays. Human oversight became nominal. No one could say what the leading systems truly knew.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Stays Zero

The EU’s credibility evaporated. With no enforcement, no unity, and no capital, even defensive action was symbolic. The Unified Audit Authority existed only on paper. The foundation was not rebuilt. It was buried under the weight of its own design.
