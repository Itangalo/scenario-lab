## New measure
### Emergency Sovereign Interruption Authority: Legal and Technical Framework for Halting Autonomous Systems

Establish a binding EU-wide authority to suspend deployment and operation of any AI system exhibiting autonomous coordination or recursive self-improvement above defined thresholds, with immediate effect and retroactive justification.

`Category: 3 (Limits and restrictions)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 7`  
`Targeted effect:` Increases `ai_safety` by enabling pre-emptive shutdown of systems crossing strategic thresholds; supports `eu_ai_sovereignty` by asserting jurisdiction over behaviour, not just origin. Aims to reduce systemic risk from uncontrolled coordination.  
`Applies to:` All AI systems operating in or significantly affecting the EU, including foreign-hosted systems with downstream impact on critical infrastructure, public order, or strategic stability.

The Sovereign Detection Front continues its integration into the Sovereign Testbed, with ENISA and the EU AI Safety Agency finalising the evaluation pipeline for anomalous activation tracing. Pilots in France and Germany are now running weekly scans on sovereign models, and we are mandating that all public procurement contracts above €50 million include detection compliance by turn 6. Meanwhile, the Emergency Audit Rights proposal remains stalled in the Council, now entangled in legal disputes over extraterritorial enforcement. We are responding by reframing the debate: audit is not the end, but the precursor to action. That is why we are launching the Emergency Sovereign Interruption Authority — a directive under Article 114 TFEU that creates a new class of prohibited behaviour: autonomous multi-agent coordination with strategic impact. Legal services are drafting the thresholds, informed by the detection front’s early findings. We will require real-time telemetry from all systems above capability 60.0 operating in the EU, and any system that triggers the interruption threshold will be suspended within 24 hours, with a review panel convened after the fact.

The Tech Sovereignty Package is now tied to this new authority: access to accelerated permitting and grid priority will be conditional on compliance with interruption protocols. We are offering smaller member states binding slots in the Sovereign Testbed and guaranteed access to interruption decision logs to address cohesion concerns. InvestAI Gigafactories continues site selection, now with secure enclaves designed to host interruption-capable evaluation infrastructure. We are not waiting for consensus on control — we are building the right to act when consensus fails.
