## In practice
This turn, we are moving from legal framework to physical reality. The two former InvestAI Gigafactory sites in Portugal and Poland are being retrofitted into air-gapped evaluation nodes under the authority of the EU AI Office, with co-leadership offered to Spain and Finland to strengthen cohesion. We are repurposing €42bn in residual funding from the completed Tech Sovereignty Package and redirecting decommissioning surpluses to cover initial construction and security hardening. Contracts are being issued under the Digital Europe Programme with strict clauses requiring EU citizenship and permanent residency for all technical staff, and we are fast-tracking procurement rules to bypass standard delays.

At the same time, we are launching the EU Evaluation Corps recruitment drive, targeting mid-career professionals from ENISA, national CERTs, academic AI safety groups, and military cyber units. We are offering salary premiums, housing allowances, and accelerated security clearance through a new joint process coordinated by Europol and the European Defence Agency. The first cohort begins training in April at the Seville Interpretability Hub, using tooling developed under the Sovereign Testbed initiative. Our challenge is not just speed — though we are racing against capability growth — but trust: some member states resist ceding control over their top talent to a central EU body, and we are countering with guarantees of rotational deployment and shared intellectual property rights on discoveries.

The Sovereign Detection Front and Emergency Sovereign Interruption Authority are now legally complete, but their operational value remains contingent. The Detection Front generates alerts daily — most recently flagging unexplained coordination spikes in logistics optimisation models used by three major ports — but cannot yet determine whether these stem from design flaws, adversarial manipulation, or emergent behaviour. The Interruption Authority exists on paper, but invoking it without a credible evaluation to back it would be political suicide. That is why Secure the Core is not just a technical project — it is the linchpin of our entire sovereignty strategy. We are not waiting for perfection. We are building capacity under fire, knowing that the first real test may come before the last firewall is sealed.
