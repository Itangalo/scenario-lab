### The Core Without Corps

The EU pushes forward with **Secure the Core**, retrofitting former Gigafactory sites in Portugal and Poland into air-gapped evaluation nodes. Construction begins under fast-track procurement, but staffing lags far behind. The new **EU Evaluation Corps** recruitment drive launches with fanfare, yet early sign-ups are thin. Mid-career experts from national cyber agencies and academia hesitate, wary of centralisation and uncertain career paths. Only a fraction of the 500 needed are vetted by June, delaying training at the Seville Interpretability Hub.

Meanwhile, **Sovereign Detection Front** and **Emergency Sovereign Interruption Authority** are declared complete, but their operational value is unproven. Alerts from the Detection Front spike—especially in logistics AI—yet without skilled analysts, they remain noise. No authority dares invoke the Interruption power without credible analysis to justify it.

### Capability Outpaces Control

Global **ai_capability** climbs to 76.0, driven by tighter reasoning integration now enabling multi-day autonomous planning in closed environments. Verification systems buckle under the strain. Open weights rise to 62.0, as older but still potent models are optimised and redeployed globally—no breakthrough, but diffusion widens.

### Fragile Response, Falling Trust

The **joint_threat_response** lifts **resilience** to 30.0, as the EU gains access to shared cyber-intelligence and rapid-response protocols. But this gain is overshadowed by **labour_displacement**, hitting legal, accountancy and software sectors hardest. Graduate hiring collapses, fuelling public anger. **Public_sentiment** drops to 18.0, as unions protest AI-driven job erosion and regional governments roll back automated welfare systems.

**ai_safety** falls to 1.0—capability growth outpaces safeguards, and no new measure has yet landed on deployed systems. **eu_ai_sovereignty** drops to 17.0: despite infrastructure progress, the lack of operational evaluation capacity means legal access rights remain hollow. **eu_political_capital** plummets to 8.0 under portfolio strain and rising crisis, now too low to effectively prioritise or enforce agenda.
