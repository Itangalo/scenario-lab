## New measure
Accelerate Sovereign Testbed Integration  
Fast-track integration of the emergent EU Sovereign Testbed Initiative into the Union’s legal and funding architecture to ensure continuity and prevent fragmentation.

`Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 5`

## Priority
Accelerate Sovereign Testbed Integration — this measure captures a fleeting window of consensus among member states alarmed by US access control and recent cyber intrusions, and turns ad hoc initiative into durable sovereignty before momentum decays.

## In practice
This turn, we are anchoring the emergent EU Sovereign Testbed Initiative under EuroHPC JU governance, activating Article 15 of the EuroHPC Regulation to fast-track legal integration and unlock immediate access to Horizon Europe top-up funding. We are convening a special meeting of the Competitiveness Council (COMPET) in December to secure unanimous endorsement, pre-empting fragmentation by binding the project to Union-level oversight while preserving national contributions. The Commission’s Digital Europe Programme will reallocate €12bn in uncommitted funds to cover early-phase design and security audits, conditioned on member states matching with semiconductor R&D credits from national budgets.

We are negotiating directly with ASML, imec, and CEA-Leti to align lithography roadmaps with testbed compute needs, leveraging export-control authority as a quiet incentive for cooperation. Resistance from smaller member states—concerned about centralisation—is being met with guarantees of open access slots and joint stewardship through a new Governing Board with rotating presidency. Meanwhile, the InvestAI Gigafactories team is adjusting site selection criteria to allow modular expansion paths toward testbed nodes, ensuring future compatibility. We are not starting a parallel compute race—we are making sure that when the frontier moves, Europe has a node on it that no external power can switch off.
