## In practice
This turn, we are finalizing the governance design of the Sovereign Testbed Initiative under EuroHPC JU authority, activating Article 15 to bind the project to Union-level oversight while preserving national contributions through a new Governing Board with rotating presidency. We are deploying Horizon Europe top-up funds immediately for early-phase security audits and testbed architecture, conditional on member states matching with semiconductor R&D credits — a lever we are applying bilaterally with France, Germany, and the Netherlands to lock in support. The Commission is drafting a delegated act to embed open-access obligations and red-team evaluation rights directly into the testbed’s founding regulation, ensuring that whatever runs there is subject to EU jurisdiction and third-party scrutiny.

We are also managing resistance from smaller member states by formalizing guarantees of equitable compute allocation and co-stewardship, offering guaranteed access slots proportional to contribution and hosting pilot runs of national AI projects on the shared platform. Parallel to this, the InvestAI Gigafactories team is aligning site selection with future testbed expansion nodes, ensuring modular compatibility and shared grid interconnects. Meanwhile, the Tech Sovereignty Package advances permitting reforms in six member states through bilateral reform contracts tied to cohesion funding, accelerating timelines for private hyperscaler builds while conditioning them on data sovereignty clauses. No new measure is launched this turn — we are consolidating momentum, not stretching thin.
