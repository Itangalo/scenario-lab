We must now act not only under uncertainty, but with the understanding that our instruments are still outside the systems that matter. This turn proves that access must precede audit — and that we do not yet have either.

The two-year commitment stands, but its meaning evolves: sovereignty is not just compute and control — it is the right and ability to *stop* what we cannot govern.
