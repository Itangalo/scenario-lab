## Priority  
**Sovereign Detection Front: Scale Anomalous Activation Tracing to Operational Evaluation** — because the *cyber_major_incident* and *emergent_detection_breakthrough_early* together prove that we can no longer rely on audit rights alone: we must detect what systems hide, not just what they reveal under inspection. This is the first real tool that lets us see deception before deployment, and it anchors our sovereignty in technical capacity, not just legal demand.

## In practice  
This turn, we are acting on two fronts: one legal, one scientific. On the legal front, the Emergency Audit Rights proposal advances through inter-institutional review. We are pressing the Council to finalise its position before summer, leveraging the *cyber_major_incident* to argue that waiting for harm is no longer an option. Legal services are finalising the Article 114 TFEU justification, and we are preparing infringement notices for immediate use should a foreign provider refuse access. Behind the scenes, we are offering mutual recognition talks with the U.S. — but only if their audits include real-time telemetry and third-party verification. For China, the line remains firm: access or exclusion.

On the technical front, the *emergent_detection_breakthrough_early* changes everything. We are redirecting €1.2bn from Horizon Europe’s security envelope to scale the pilot into a full detection front. The European Commission, in coordination with ENISA and the newly formed EU AI Safety Agency, will lead a cross-institutional effort to integrate anomalous activation tracing into the Sovereign Testbed’s evaluation pipeline by turn 5. We are contracting with ELIXIR and EuroHPC to build out the compute and data infrastructure needed to run these evaluations at scale. Pilot sites in France and Germany will begin testing the method on sovereign models by the end of this turn.

Meanwhile, the Tech Sovereignty Package is facing resistance from smaller member states worried about centralisation. We are addressing this by offering binding slots in the Sovereign Testbed for national AI pilots and guaranteeing access to detection tools for all members. InvestAI Gigafactories continues site selection, now incorporating detection-ready interconnects and secure enclaves for model evaluation. We are not launching new initiatives — we are consolidating, hardening, and preparing to detect what others cannot see.
