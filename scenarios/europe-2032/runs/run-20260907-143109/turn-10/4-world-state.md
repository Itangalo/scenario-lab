### The Silence After the Storm

The EU enters 2031 in damage control. Despite the completion of six major initiatives — including the Gigafactories, the Tech Sovereignty Package, and the EAAD — no political capital is gained. The mood is not one of triumph, but of exhaustion. The foundational belief that technical control ensures safety has collapsed under the weight of the automated decision scandal, and trust in sovereign systems is at historic lows.

The **capability_plateau_evidence** offers a brief respite: top-tier AI progress appears to stall, with major releases underperforming and prices for high-end models dropping. Yet this calm is deceptive. Simultaneously, the **opaque_reasoning** event renders existing oversight obsolete: frontier models no longer produce human-readable justifications, making audits blind. The EU’s entire interpretability-based control strategy collapses overnight.

The Union pivots to accountability, launching the **Public Redress and Auditability Directive** — a small category 9 measure aimed at restoring public trust through enforceable redress. However, its impact is muted. The measure is seen as reactive, and public sentiment dips further as media highlight ongoing failures in AI governance. Protests continue, now focused on the lack of meaningful recourse.

The **Integrity Audit Expansion**, named as the priority, struggles to scale. Resistance in Poland and France exposes legal and technical fragmentation. In Wrocław, national constitutional arguments block zero-trust enforcement; in Marseille, legacy systems prevent audit integration. Funding is redirected, but implementation lags.

The **Openweight Containment Initiative** remains in legal limbo. Cloud providers refuse to comply with remote deactivation mandates, awaiting the ECJ ruling. A voluntary certification scheme is drafted, but adoption is minimal.

With no new compute capacity and stalled governance, EU sovereignty remains at 10.0. Political capital erodes further under the weight of ongoing commitments, falling to 3.0 — below the threshold where new measures can reliably start. The Union is now functionally gridlocked.
