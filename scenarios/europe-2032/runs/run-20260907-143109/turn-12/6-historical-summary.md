The EU's crisis intensifies in 2032 as the **medical_breakthrough** of AI-designed gene therapies exacerbates feelings of technological dependence, with life-saving treatments controlled by foreign entities. Public resentment grows as the EU fails to leverage its completed flagship programmes—Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty Package, EAAD—for political or practical recovery. The **emergent_regional_aid_fracture** escalates into open rifts between northern and southern/eastern states, threatening the unity of the single market as nations pursue independent AI and compute alliances. The **EU Continuity Compact**, a last-ditch effort to enforce collective infrastructure and burden-sharing, stalls due to lack of trust and credibility, with **eu_political_capital** remaining at 3.0. The **Critical Infrastructure Continuity Protocol** lacks funding and enforcement, rendering it ineffective. The **Openweight Containment Initiative** is formally abandoned, symbolizing the EU’s loss of regulatory control. In response, **emergent_open_source_audit_tools** spread among civic and local actors, marking a shift toward decentralized, unofficial accountability. The Union enters a state of paralysis—structurally intact but politically inert, unable to act or adapt.
