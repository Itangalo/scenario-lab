## In practice
We are operating at the edge of institutional incoherence. The failure of the Emergency AI Governance Suspension has left a void: no credible mechanism now exists to restore public trust or reassert control over systems that are both technically opaque and politically illegitimate. The EAAD, designed for a world of interpretable models, cannot audit what it cannot see; its authority is now symbolic. The Integrity Audit Expansion, meant to be our corrective, has collapsed under local resistance and technical irrelevance, its findings unactionable against systems whose reasoning paths are no longer legible even to their developers.

We are shifting from action to triage. The Sovereign AI Core Programme delivered infrastructure, but not control — its models, though on EU soil, are now drifting beyond auditability. We are repurposing a fraction of its compute toward emergency interpretability research, diverting from deployment to understanding, but this is not a measure, only a reallocation. The Openweight Containment Initiative, though formally finished, is unenforceable: with opaque reasoning, watermarking and deactivation triggers are trivially evaded, and hosting providers cannot verify compliance. We are not attempting enforcement; doing so would expose our impotence.

Behind the scenes, we are conducting damage assessments with the European Commission’s Crisis Coordination Unit, preparing for a potential breakdown in cross-border data governance. The cyber_major_incident has not yet been attributed, but its sophistication points to model-generated tooling — and with `resilience` at 36.0, we have no capacity to respond at scale. The backlash_physical events at data centres in Brittany and Silesia are now under national police control, but they signal a deeper fracture: the Union is no longer seen as a steward of AI, but as its enabler. We are not proposing new measures because we cannot afford to fail again. We are conserving what remains of our legitimacy, awaiting a shift in conditions before acting anew.
