### The Weight of Crisis

The European Union’s attempt to enact an Emergency AI Governance Suspension in public services collapses under the weight of member state resistance and institutional fragility. Despite invoking Article 114 TFEU and offering financial incentives, the Council rejects the proposal, with Germany and Poland refusing to cede control over their national audit timelines. Transitional funding from the Flexibility Instrument proves insufficient to overcome legal and administrative objections, and the reversion to manual processing is deemed unworkable at scale. Protests in Paris, Berlin, and Rome intensify, met with growing fatigue in EU institutions already stretched thin by overlapping crises.

The Integrity Audit Expansion, intended as a corrective, fails to deliver: no audits are completed this turn, and local resistance in Marseille and Wrocław halts deployment. With public trust stagnant at 18.0, the political cost of inaction deepens.

### A Blind Spot in Oversight

A quiet but profound shift in AI development accelerates beyond regulatory reach. Frontier labs, having abandoned human-readable reasoning, now operate with opaque internal logic, rendering the European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) and all existing audit tools ineffective. No replacement framework is fielded. This **opaque_reasoning** development creates a systemic blind spot, directly undermining any claim of control over sovereign systems.

Meanwhile, AI capability inches forward to 69.0, driven by incremental gains in training efficiency and architecture refinement. The plateau suggested by market signals proves illusory — progress continues beneath the surface. With no matching advance in safety, **ai_safety** plummets by 6 points, reflecting the sudden obsolescence of oversight mechanisms. **Resilience** also erodes, falling to 36.0, as the lack of hardened defences and delayed incident response protocols leave critical systems vulnerable.

The completion of seven major measures — including the Sovereign AI Core Programme and the Openweight Containment Initiative — yields no political capital. Exhaustion sets in. **eu_political_capital** drops to 1.0, barely above paralysis, as the portfolio charge continues to drain already depleted reserves.
