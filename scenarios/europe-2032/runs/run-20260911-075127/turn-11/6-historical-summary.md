Autumn brought a modified pathogen designed with advanced models, with cases in three member states; hospitals ran isolation/tracing/pooled testing amid disputes over licensed doses.

Washington placed leading labs under federal control, cut publications and foreign API access, freezing triage assistants in Denmark/Portugal, which shifted to slower local models. A near-frontier open release spread widely despite warnings it aided bioweapon design. US-China announced limited pact on weight security and bio-tool restrictions with thin verification.

EU kept shelter wages/lights on, prioritized bio-detection network in incident mode with pooled reagents, cold chains, sequencing offered for verification; sought observer status in pact and licensed domestic therapy manufacturing. Little arrived in time: tighter export rules, slipped chips, delayed cable/grid repairs, state-to-state therapy talks yielding only pilots.

Transition payments finished late/small. Union intact, neutral, containing outbreak but exposed as dependent on systems others could switch off.
