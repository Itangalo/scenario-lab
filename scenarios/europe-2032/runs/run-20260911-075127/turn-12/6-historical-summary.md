Autumn saw a modified pathogen in three states, met with isolation/tracing/pooled testing amid licensed-dose disputes. Washington federalized leading labs, cut publications and foreign API access, freezing triage assistants in Denmark/Portugal; EU shifted to slower local models as a near-frontier open release spread despite bioweapon warnings. US-China pact on weight security and bio-tools had thin verification.

By June: US frontier keys went fully dark for Europe; triage re-hosted on smaller European models with longer queues but care continued, as a discontinuous foreign advance made timelines obsolete and evaluation opaque. EU bio-detection network completed rollout — pooled reagents, cold chains, sequencing improved isolation/tracing — earning invitation to binding sample-sharing and real-time biosurveillance/cyber telemetry, which blunted grid/hospital probing. Licensed therapy manufacturing stalled at pilots amid tighter exports, slipped chips, delayed cable/grid repairs. Union intact, neutral, outbreak contained, connected to shared warning, but humiliated by dependence and running a generation behind the frontier.
