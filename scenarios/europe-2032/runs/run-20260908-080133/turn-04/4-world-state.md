### The Frontier Crosses 60

The AI frontier advances, with multi-agent systems demonstrating sustained coordination in controlled environments for biomedical and infrastructural design. Verified deployments in drug discovery and grid optimisation show promise, pushing **ai_capability** to 60.0. Observers note increasing sophistication in long-horizon planning, though full autonomy over multi-week cycles remains emergent and not yet generalised. The pace of growth has moderated compared to previous turns, but the milestone marks a symbolic threshold in system coherence.

### Cyber Shield Completes, Resilience Rises

The **Cyber Shield for Critical Operators** formally completes, with the Joint Threat Analysis Cell now fully operational across 18 member states. ENISA’s first cross-border red-team exercise reveals persistent gaps in municipal cyber readiness, but AI-augmented detection tools cut threat dwell time by 40% among Tier 1 operators. This delivers a tangible boost to **resilience**, which rises to 45.0, reflecting improved detection and coordinated response—though healthcare and mid-tier industry remain vulnerable.

### Open Weights Advance Cautiously

With **ai_capability** at 60.0, **openweight_capability** rises to 51.0, reflecting continued diffusion of high-capability models through research releases and derivative tuning. ENISA detects growing use of agent-grade systems on open platforms, but most remain behind access controls or lack full frontier functionality. The gap between open and closed systems narrows incrementally, not through a single breakthrough but through cumulative leakage and replication. Systemic risk increases, but no major unauthorised release has occurred.
