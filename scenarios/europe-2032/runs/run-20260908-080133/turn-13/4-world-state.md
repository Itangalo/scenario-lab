### Steady Drift, Mounting Pressure

AI capability advanced steadily, rising by 1.5 points as frontier labs refined autonomous agent coordination in cyber and research domains. The open-weight frontier showed strong momentum: a powerful, permissively licensed model was released, functionally matching last year’s closed systems. However, widespread adoption and integration remain in early stages, with deployment bottlenecks in tooling, trust, and infrastructure limiting immediate impact. As a result, `openweight_capability` increased only modestly, rising by 1.5 points to 60.5 — narrowing the gap to the closed frontier to 11.0 points, but reflecting gradual diffusion rather than an abrupt leap.

With no safety upgrades landing on deployed systems, `ai_safety` eroded further, dropping by 3 points. The gap between what AI can do and what we can control now exceeds 50 points — a chasm growing too wide to ignore. Experts warn that even basic interpretability is failing under the weight of agent autonomy.

The **Red-Teaming Directive** reached its final turn. Simulations were conducted in synthetic environments due to lingering clearance delays, but Finland and Belgium delivered interim reports. The findings revealed critical blind spots in grid and border control agents, though without live testing, their real-world validity remains contested. The measure finishes this turn, but its impact is muted.

The **EU Incident Response Corps** was formally launched, hosted under ENISA with initial staffing from France, Germany, and the Netherlands. However, Poland refused command authority, and Italy withheld operational consent, limiting cross-border deployment to voluntary invitations only. Quarterly exercises are scheduled, but legal ambiguity persists. The measure is now in flight, but its reach is constrained.

Resilience inched up by 1 point as municipal systems integrated lessons from past incidents, though systemic fragility remains. Political capital declined under the weight of three active measures — two large, one small — and the named priority, despite no new proposal bonus. Sovereignty dipped again, as no domestic compute came online and dependence on external capacity deepened.

Public sentiment held flat. No scandal broke, but no visible progress either.

### Regional Pacts Fill the Void

The Nordic-Benelux safety network expanded, sharing early warnings on high-risk AI deployments. Though outside EU governance, their standards are becoming de facto benchmarks, increasing pressure on Brussels to act.
