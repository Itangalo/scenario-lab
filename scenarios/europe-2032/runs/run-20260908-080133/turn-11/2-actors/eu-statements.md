## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign operational control over AI systems in critical public infrastructure and establish enforceable governance to ensure public accountability
  - Grounds: The completion of sovereign agent deployment into live critical infrastructure has shifted the decisive challenge from access and capability to control and legitimacy. With openweight misuse already manifesting and member state defection exposing institutional fragility, operational control is not enough — it must be visibly bounded by rules that hold across jurisdictions and resist external capture. The current crisis of cohesion and public trust demands that we now prove the EU can govern what it runs, not just deploy it.
