### The Speed of the Frontier

The AI frontier accelerated again, with labs demonstrating reliable multi-agent collaboration in closed research environments — particularly in clinical coding and structural engineering, where automated verification now confirms outputs with high accuracy. This breakthrough, emerging just months after the theoretical possibility was confirmed, pushes **ai_capability** to 56.0. The expansion of verifiable domains sharpens the strategic stakes: capabilities once confined to tightly supervised tasks now self-validate, enabling faster iteration and broader deployment.

### Containing the Open Frontier

In response, the EU launches the **Open-Weight Monitoring and Containment Framework**, aiming to impose accountability on open-source model distribution. ENISA and BEREC begin drafting technical standards for detecting high-risk derivatives, while legal teams prepare enforcement notices targeting platforms hosting unsecured, high-capability models. However, no takedowns occur this turn — the system remains in design, and pushback from developer communities and free-software advocates grows. The measure signals intent, but its real impact is deferred.

### Shielding the Core

The **Cyber Shield for Critical Operators** advances: implementing acts are finalised, the Luxembourg-based Joint Threat Analysis Cell begins staffing, and telemetry sharing agreements are provisionally accepted. Yet without live red-teaming powers, the system’s preventive strength remains limited. Resilience sees a marginal uptick as integration begins, though full operational capability is still distant.

### Sovereignty on Thin Ice

No new domestic compute comes online. The **InvestAI Gigafactories** progress only to environmental assessments, slowed by local resistance despite Vestager’s mediation. The **Tech sovereignty package** inches forward in trilogue, traded for data centre access concessions. Meanwhile, **export_control_escalation** sees the US and allies adopt volume licensing — the EU avoids rationing but secures no new access. With ai_capability rising by 2.0 and no sovereignty-building measure completed, **eu_ai_sovereignty** erodes further.

Political capital declines under the weight of three large measures and a priority, despite the urgency of the moment. Public sentiment holds steady — no scandal, no breakthrough.
