Efforts under the **Secure Compute Anchoring Initiative** continue, with exploratory talks underway with US hyperscalers regarding potential administrative access in Ireland. No binding agreement has been reached; discussions are ongoing, and the possibility of invoking the Anti-Coercion Instrument remains a contingency, not an active measure.

Preliminary discussions begin on the concept of a **Sovereign AI Operating Cluster**—aimed at integrating sovereign compute, talent, and models into a live, EU-operated stack—but the initiative remains at the conceptual stage. Legal and funding hurdles prevent formal drafting or resourcing this turn. Frontex expresses interest, but no operational integration occurs.
