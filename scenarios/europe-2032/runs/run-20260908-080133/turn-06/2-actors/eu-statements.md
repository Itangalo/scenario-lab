## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Maintain and operate sovereign AI systems at or near the global frontier, ensuring the EU can act independently in a world where capability and control have decisively decoupled

The prior formulation focused on securing capacity, but the world now demands that we *operate* it, visibly and continuously, to remain a pole of independent action.
