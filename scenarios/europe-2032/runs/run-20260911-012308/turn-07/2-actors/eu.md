## New measure

Creates a temporary EU state-backed reinsurance and liability pool for certified operators forced onto EU-hosted fallback models, clarifying who bears dosage-harm risk and unfreezing cover to keep outsourcing and services running.
This now because the insurance freeze plus liability wave is the binding blockage on continuity: without a backstop, M7's fallback keeps hospitals exposed and uncertified operators frozen, directly serving the commitment to hold absorption through retrenchment.

## In practice
We keep M7 as the operational core: ENISA and DG CNECT hold certified hospitals/utilities on EU-hosted open fallbacks with manual override, while interior ministers' escalation thresholds govern agent isolation. No new siting fight; M1/M2 stay at permitted backup site awaiting disbursement.

We launch the backstop design via ECOFIN and DG FISMA with EIB provisioning: a time-limited EU guarantee behind national insurers resuming cyber/AI-liability cover for certified operators, paired with a Health Council / Justice Council liability clarification that emergency-switched dosage harm falls to the pool not individual clinicians, conditional on certified use and incident reporting. We offer insurers premium caps in exchange for resumed cover, and offer the defecting member re-entry on common pool terms.
