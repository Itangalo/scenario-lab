By late 2028 EU hardening lagged a leaked open near-frontier model; certified hospitals/utilities kept conditional cover at high premiums, broad exclusions paused. After US election, American frontier models went dark in March for Europe; Commission ordered move to European-hosted open models or paper-manual fallback. Services degraded; autumn fallback caused dosage-hallucination patient harm and liability filings. Insurers froze new cover for uncertified; guarantee pool rejected.

Jan-Jun 2030 was administration: containment routine, services steadier but degraded, insurance still frozen for uncertified, guarantee pool only costed/drafted paper, factories unused.

Late 2030 paper became policy then frayed: finance ministers accepted time-limited state tail and clinician shield, few large insurers resumed conditional cover for certified at high premiums. Relief cut short by published genome-model claiming viable human-infective design; Commission launched emergency bio-programme for synthesis screening, lab reporting, backstop extension to bio-triage, funding unclear. Graduate entry jobs in law, accountancy, software support, admin failed to return, blamed on automation. Side-deal state formalised deeper bilateral hosting deal; Brussels let outlier go. Screening mandates outpaced funding, hospital upgrades became unfunded duties, nurses threatened refusal to countersign machine doses. By Dec Union insured and screened on paper, still degraded in practice.
