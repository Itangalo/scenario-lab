## Priority
M8 EU Fallback Harm Backstop – because unfreezing insurance for certified fallback operators is the single blockage that decides whether continuity holds or services stall, and it directly serves holding absorption through retrenchment.

## In practice
We hold the line on continuity with what is already on the books. ENISA and interior ministry units keep certified hospitals and utilities on EU-hosted open models with paper-manual override where confidence falls, under the M7 escalation thresholds that now finish into standing procedure. No new siting or supplier track is opened; the gigafactory shells and middle-power spares memoranda are toured, not expanded.

All political weight goes to landing M8 via ECOFIN and DG FISMA with EIB provisioning: a time-limited EU guarantee behind national insurers resuming cover for certified fallback use, paired with Health and Justice Council clarification that properly reported emergency-switched harm falls to the pool, not to clinicians. We offer insurers workable premium terms for resumed cover, and keep the door open to the side-deal member state on common pool terms without renegotiating the pool to get them.
