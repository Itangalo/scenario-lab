## In practice
We task ENISA and the EU-CyCLONe network with the containment pact: pre-authorised isolation playbooks, joint hunt-and-patch teams drawn from M3 Shield staff, and CER-Directive emergency orders for segmentation and backups, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and CEF-Digital money via the Competitiveness and JHA Councils.

In parallel we keep M4 Continuity Stack as operator: DG DIGIT continues funded migration of hospitals and ministries cut off in February to EU-hosted open models, now explicitly steered toward the emergent non-US EU-ring-fenced hosting offers with non-withdrawal clauses to avoid a second switch-off, while M1/M2 gigafactory permitting continues on paper without new cash given the investment collapse. We frame both publicly as keeping wards and town halls running through attack and cutoff, to stabilise sentiment while recovery is visible.
