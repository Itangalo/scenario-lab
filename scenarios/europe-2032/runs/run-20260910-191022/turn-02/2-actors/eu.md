## New measure
**EU Essential Services Continuity Stack**
Stands up EU-hosted fallback models and migration support for hospitals, ministries and operators cut off from the leading foreign model, via emergency procurement and EU-cloud inference capacity. Why this and why now: the cutoff has turned dependence into immediate service failure, and keeping essential services running through disruption is the core of our two-year commitment.

## Priority
EU Essential Services Continuity Stack, because active loss of the leading model in hospitals and ministries outranks even the grid hunt this turn.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT and DG DIGIT with the AI Office to run an emergency continuity cell: inventory affected deployments in health and administration, offer funded migration to EU-hosted open and licensed models on Gaia-X / EUCS cloud, with temporary state-aid clearance via the Competitiveness Council.

In parallel we keep M3 Grid Shield as operator: ENISA pushes segmentation audits and credential rotation through, using reprogrammed CEF funds, while M1/M2 gigafactory permitting continues on existing legal bases without new cash. We frame the cutoff publicly as foresight failure we are now fixing, not as outrage alone, to protect capital for delivery.
