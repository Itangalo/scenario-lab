## New measure
**EU Supply-Chain Bargain for Continuity**
Locks EU lithography, equipment and materials leverage into joint continuity deals to keep essential compute, medical and grid-rebuild supplies flowing through the Taiwan blockade. Why this and why now: with semiconductors halted for years, open weights at the frontier and hospitals still rebuilding from the automated attack, the Union's upstream choke-point is the only asset that buys access and protection on EU terms.

## Priority
EU Supply-Chain Bargain for Continuity, because nothing else — gigafactory concrete, cyber containment, or bio screening — functions if compute supply is cut for years, so this outranks even the two-year independence commitment this turn by serving it.
## In practice
We run this through the Trade and Competitiveness Councils under the Anti-Coercion Instrument and EU Chips Act review: Commission mandate to negotiate joint continuity with US, Japan and Taiwan partners, export-control alignment on lithography spares in exchange for prioritized advanced-chip and medical-input quotas for EU essential services.

We fund it by reprogramming existing Chips JU and Digital Europe money, task ENISA, HERA and the AI Office to triage scarce compute to hospitals, grid operators and bio-detection first, and hold M1/M2 gigafactory permits and grid reservations legally alive while pausing new spend. Member states contribute only access and single-voice discipline, not fresh cash, to fit capital at 19 and sentiment at 13.
