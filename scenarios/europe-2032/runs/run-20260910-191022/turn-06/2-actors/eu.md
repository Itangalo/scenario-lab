## New measure
**EU Essential Fallback for Hospitals and Administrations**
What it does: swaps hospitals, ministries and dependent firms cut off from the US frontier model onto EU-hosted and vetted open models with ring-fenced support. Why this and why now: the sudden access cut has left essential services stranded, and without a visible EU-controlled fallback the independence commitment fails on its first test while trust collapses further.

## Priority
EU Essential Fallback for Hospitals and Administrations, because the US cutoff has made continuity of hospitals and ministries outrank even finishing gigafactories and the audit reset this turn.

## In practice
We execute the fallback through the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority, DG DIGIT and ENISA: a vetted model catalogue on EU ring-fenced hosting, emergency procurement derogation under the Public Procurement Directive, and deployment teams seconded from the Grid Shield operations centres to the affected hospitals and ministries. Funding is reprogrammed Digital Europe deployment money, not new gigafactory cash.

In parallel we keep M1 Gigafactories alive politically but frozen financially, using the Council Competitiveness formation to hold permits and grid reservations while EIB structures the financing restart, and we let M7 audits continue through the AI Office and market-surveillance authorities with redress pursued via deployer contributions. No new compute programme is stood up; the fallback runs on existing EU capacity.
