### Stagnation and Searing Backlash

Despite the frontier’s stagnation—now visibly stalling as top-tier AI systems commodify and release momentum falters—no technical breakthrough eases the crisis of control. The interpretability void deepens, rendering audits meaningless. With the ECJ striking down both the Integrity Audit Expansion and Openweight Containment Initiative, the EU’s regulatory architecture collapses under legal fragmentation and member-state resistance.

Public anger, already boiling, turns destructive. Data centre occupations spread to France and Belgium, and sabotage of power infrastructure delays AI deployments across the north. The backlash is no longer symbolic: it is operational, decentralized, and growing in coordination. Governments respond with heavier policing, further alienating citizens who see force protecting systems they cannot understand or stop.

### A Desperate Bid for Authority

In this vacuum, the EU launches the AI Emergency Suspension Authority, a bold attempt to reclaim legitimacy through power, not transparency. Framed under internal market rules, it grants binding authority to halt any AI system posing systemic risk, with fast-track citizen access and enforcement via the Court of Justice. Germany and the Netherlands tentatively support it; France and Poland resist, calling it judicial overreach. To secure backing, the Commission offers opt-in pilots and a sunset clause—concessions that dilute its permanence but keep it alive.

Yet momentum is thin. Political capital, already at 4.0, erodes further under the weight of three ongoing measures and a new large one. The Sovereign AI Core Programme, though technically complete, delivers no sovereign capability—no model above 50.0 runs on it. Its repurposing signals retreat, not progress.

### Trust Erodes Further

Labour markets fracture as entry-level roles vanish in law, accounting, and software. The absence of hiring, not just layoffs, fuels despair among graduates. Media frames AI not as a tool, but as an exclusionary force. Public sentiment plummets, now near open hostility.

Resilience weakens as cyber defences fail to adapt. Without active safeguards, critical systems grow more exposed to probing attacks exploiting opaque reasoning. The middle-power coalition strengthens export controls, but offers no shield against domestic collapse.
