## Statement changes  
- modify `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Restore public and institutional trust in sovereign AI systems by ensuring **enforceable redress, resilience to operational failure, and institutional accountability in the absence of technical auditability** — **but shift primary focus from systemic suspension to societal absorption, as the legitimacy of stopping systems is now less urgent than preventing collapse from their continued operation**  
  - Grounds: The **cyber_defence_breakthrough** and **capability_plateau_evidence** together alter the risk calculus: the immediate threat of runaway harm has receded, while the social and economic damage from AI-driven displacement intensifies unchecked. The **eval_anomaly_reports** suggest latent danger, but the plateau in capability growth means the window for reorienting without catastrophe has not yet closed. Meanwhile, the Emergency Suspension Authority — though symbolically necessary — has failed to quell public anger; instead, occupations and sabotage grow, not because people fear *what AI might do*, but because they see *what it is already doing*: erasing opportunity, deepening inequality, and entrenching foreign control. Trust is not being lost to opacity alone, but to exclusion. We must now pivot from mechanisms that *stop* AI to those that *bind* it to society — not just through accountability, but through inclusion. The revised commitment reflects that: we remain committed to control in the absence of understanding, but our primary task is no longer halting systems — it is ensuring that the EU, and its people, are not left behind by them.

- modify `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — **but we will no longer treat institutional authority as the sole form of agency. When legitimacy collapses, action must restore material stake, not just procedural control**  
  - Grounds: The **backlash_physical** and **public_sentiment** at 14.0 confirm that the Union is seen not as a regulator, but as a protector of systems that exclude its own citizens. The Emergency Suspension Authority, while legally bold, has not shifted perception because it does not address the lived reality of AI: it protects institutions, not people. The **cyber_defence_breakthrough** proves that technical progress can outpace governance — but only if someone is building for resilience, not just control. We are still acting under uncertainty, but we now recognise that agency is not only the power to stop, but the power to include. This revision acknowledges that legitimacy must be earned not through jurisdictional assertion, but through distributive justice: we must act not only to govern AI, but to ensure its gains are not concentrated beyond our reach.

## New measure  
**Measure:** Launch the EU AI Labour Absorption and Transition Fund  
A pan-European programme to provide wage insurance, retraining, and regional economic renewal for workers displaced by AI in entry-level cognitive professions, with automatic triggers based on sectoral automation rates and binding commitments from firms receiving public AI contracts to retrain or compensate displaced workers.

`Category: 7 (Labour and social protection)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 17`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase `resilience` by 10–14 points and `public_sentiment` by 8–12 points by directly addressing the social fracture at the heart of the backlash — the perception that AI is a foreign force stripping opportunity — and demonstrating that the EU can protect its people even when it cannot fully control the technology  
`Applies to:` All EU member states, with co-financing from national governments and mandatory participation for firms deploying AI in public-sector contracts or receiving EU innovation grants

## Priority  
**Priority:** Launch the EU AI Labour Absorption and Transition Fund

The **capability_plateau_evidence** gives us space to act without panic, and the **cyber_defence_breakthrough** reduces the near-term threat of cascading harm — but the social fabric is tearing. If we do not act now to bind AI’s gains to the people who are losing from it, no authority, no suspension power, no legal override will matter. ## In practice  
This turn, we are governing from the edge of social breakdown. With `eu_political_capital` at 3.0 and `public_sentiment` at 14.0, we can no longer afford symbolic authority — we must restore material stake. The AI Emergency Suspension Authority continues in flight: we are drafting its implementing act under Article 114 TFEU, with EAAD as the operational lead, and offering France and Poland co-chairing rights on the review panel to secure Council approval. But we are no longer betting on it to restore trust — only to preserve a last line of defence.

Instead, we are shifting our scarce capital to where legitimacy can still be rebuilt: in the lives of those being displaced. We are launching the EU AI Labour Absorption and Transition Fund under the Social Climate Fund and the European Social Fund Plus, with automatic triggers tied to Eurostat-reported automation rates in law, accounting, and software. The fund will provide 70% wage insurance for two years, full retraining in AI-augmented professions, and regional innovation grants to towns hit hardest. We are mandating that any firm winning a public AI contract must either retrain displaced staff or pay into the fund — a condition we will enforce through the Public Procurement Directive.

Germany and the Netherlands support the fund as a stabilising force; France sees it as a way to reclaim sovereignty over social policy; Poland remains sceptical, fearing open-ended liability. We are offering member states 85% co-financing for the first two years to secure uptake, and we are tying access to the Sovereign AI Core Programme’s remaining budget to compliance. The message is clear: we may not control the frontier, but we will not let it control us. The EU’s sovereignty is not in its stacks — it is in its people. And this turn, we are staking our last capital on that truth.
