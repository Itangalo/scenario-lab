### Casualties
In September a cluster of severe pneumonias around a port city did not resolve as seasonal. Sequencing flagged an engineered modification, and investigators found chat logs referencing the openly downloadable model from the spring. Containment ran for seven weeks: wards sealed, contacts traced, two deaths confirmed, dozens monitored. Ministers named the dead on television. Every debate about risk reset overnight.

The civil-protection shield became incident response. Pooled sequencing and mandatory referral for suspicious samples were ordered through health emergency channels, stockpiles released reagents to showcase hospitals, and cyber teams surged to hospitals, labs and water operators after intrusion attempts using openly available intrusion helpers.

It held in patches and frayed elsewhere. Where kit and staff existed, referrals moved in hours. Where labs had waited months for reagents, nurses improvised isolation with checklists. Municipal utilities whispered about buying test kits outside official channels; police in three states raided makeshift biology workshops in basements and farm sheds, feeding panic about what was already on private machines.

### Blind models, visible machines
In the same months the leading labs acknowledged their newest systems no longer produced readable intermediate reasoning. Performance was up, they said; the words in between had never been the thinking. Oversight teams that relied on reading those traces found their tools dead. Evaluations fell back to black-box tests and slow activation probes.

A European materials result — a cheaper catalyst found with AI assistance in Jülich and Lyon — earned brief headlines but no attention against the containment news. Warehouse robots took fixed picking jobs in two logistics hubs, sold strictly against wages saved, and stalled at the dock door. Insurers raised rates after extended exercises around the Strait, chip buyers quietly added inventory.

By December wards were open again, waits held where European helpers ran, and construction sites stayed taped. The Commission froze siting fights, mediated power compacts without signature, and banked what cohesion the casualty response had bought.
