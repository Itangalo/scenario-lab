## Priority
EU Joint Threat Response Accession — because with no measures in flight and war targeting infrastructure, shared telemetry and biosurveillance is the only instrument that delivers protection before we can fund anything else.

## In practice
We accede by Council decision to the joint cyber command and biosurveillance pact, mandating ENISA real-time telemetry sharing from power, telecoms and hospital networks and ECDC binding sample-sharing with a standing investigation mandate. DG CONNECT and DG SANTE reassign staff from the finished Shield and Bio-Shield lines; no new money is asked while bridge pay is still delayed.

We pair accession with wartime rationing honesty: DG SANTE allocates remaining licensed therapy capacity by clinical need after the US requisition, and we pursue the medical breakthrough only through deployable, EU-controllable doses and triage models, refusing new dependence on a frontier we have just been cut off from. The middle-power deconfliction offer stays as diplomatic cover, but operational effort moves to the table we were actually invited to.
