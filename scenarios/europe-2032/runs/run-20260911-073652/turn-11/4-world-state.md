### Cut off and invited in
The cut-off came without warning. Overnight, clinics in Lyon, Barcelona and Krakow found their licensed therapy slots and scheduling interfaces dead. Ministries and firms built on the leading American system got the same blank screen: access suspended under wartime emergency powers, no reason, no appeal. DG SANTE stretched stockpiled triage capacity for days, then moved to open rationing by clinical need.

Humiliation did the political work denial had not. Talk shows that had mocked Brussels for begging Washington now asked why healing had ever been left on permission. The wayward capital quietly kept its separate discount channel, deepening resentment.

### The other channel
In the same weeks, a different offer arrived. Hit by the same wave of grid intrusions and poisoned samples, a group of states pooled live telemetry from power, telecom and hospital networks and bound themselves to share biological samples with a standing investigation team. The Union was invited in.

The Council acceded in a single decision. ENISA began piping real-time data outward; the disease centre committed samples inward. No new money was voted — staff were reassigned from finished shield programmes. Operators reported faster attribution on the jamming that had plagued dispatchers, and isolation of grids improved. It did not restore therapy doses. It did keep lights and wards running through the autumn strikes.

### A cure that does not cure dependence
Autumn also brought news of genuinely tailored cures for previously untreatable conditions, demonstrated abroad. Brussels insisted it would only deploy doses and triage models it could run itself. The caution was understood, but the result was thin: a few pilot lots, strict criteria, long queues. Hope rose; delivery did not.

Sabotage at the gigafactory grid links paused as guarded repair crews restored pylons, yet construction never restarted. Bridge pay trickled out. Rumours swirled in neutral capitals of mirrored models hosted beyond either belligerent's reach, but nothing reachable arrived.
