### War over the frontier
In March, American and Chinese forces began striking each other’s technology base. No one in Brussels could say who fired first. Within weeks fabrication plants, cable landings, satellite links and data centres were declared legitimate targets. Two transatlantic cables went dark for days. A fab in East Asia burned. European air-traffic, power dispatchers and hospital networks felt the spillover as jamming and malware washed across shared systems.

The Union was not consulted. It was exposed anyway.

### Permission revoked
The therapy queues set in California froze. Licensed doses and scheduling slots for French, Spanish and Polish clinics were suspended under emergency requisition. DG SANTE kept triage assistants running on stockpiled capacity for a fortnight, then began rationing. Commentators stopped saying healing by permission. They started counting empty appointments.

A new openly downloadable system, close to the closed frontier, spread to hundreds of thousands of machines in its first week. Police warned it would supercharge fraud and intrusion just as ENISA teams were isolating grids from foreign cyber operations.

### Fires at home
At home the night fires spread. After the pilot robot sites, masked teams hit grid-connection works for the two permitted gigafactory plots, toppling pylons and torching cable drums. Construction halted. The Energy Council declared emergency control and sent civil-protection repair crews with guarded perimeters. Mayors who had asked for jobs now asked for protection.

Ports using Chinese-built humanoids went to short shifts as parts and updates stopped. Graduate hiring, already frozen in law, accountancy, software and back-office work, disappeared entirely. Bridge pay under the finished Transition Guarantee paid out, but slowly, and works councils could not hold reopenings.

The middle-power club became the Union’s single wartime channel. The Council declared European territory and cable landings non-belligerent and offered both capitals deconfliction and test recognition for no targeting and continued supply. Talks were continuous. Deliveries were not. The wayward capital kept its separate hyperscaler pact, pocketing pooled promises while keeping its bilateral discount.
