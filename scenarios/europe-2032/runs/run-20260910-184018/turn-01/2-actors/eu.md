In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the tech sovereignty package moving: DG COMP state-aid clearance for accelerated permitting zones, EIB guarantees to crowd private data-centre capital, and structured talks with ASML and grid operators on power and siting, trading permit speed for EU-anchored capacity conditions. Washington's switch-off order is handled diplomatically, not by retaliation now, to preserve negotiation space while domestic alternatives are built.
