## New measure
**EU Transition Bridge for AI-Displaced Workers**
Provides EU-co-funded wage insurance and fast retraining entitlements tied to automating employers, run through ESF+ and national PES.
This is Category 7 Labour and social protection, because queues, cancelled appointments and slipping trust now threaten both our sovereignty build and resilience work; why now is that without a visible absorption offer public sentiment will block data-centre siting, gigafactory co-finance and AI adoption just as M3 needs local cooperation.
