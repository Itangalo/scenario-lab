## New measure
**EU Workplace Augmentation Pact**
It turns the measured white-collar productivity gain into visible public benefit by funding augmentation assistants for SMEs, hospitals and administrations on EU-controlled models with retraining tied to hiring retention.
This turn because employment held while output rose among the least experienced, giving a rare window to lift sentiment and capital before the next capability jump, and it directly serves the two-year commitment to run essential services on AI under EU control.

## Priority
EU Workplace Augmentation Pact, because with only M6 still costing capital and sovereignty at 15.0, rebuilding public consent through shared gains outranks further hardening this turn.

## In practice
We run this through DG EMPL and DG CNECT on ESF+ and Digital Europe money: EPSCO agreement for wage-insurance conditionality that firms keeping headcount get subsidised augmentation licences, procured via the Continuity Switch stack on open-weight and allied models already re-platformed, with ENISA certification fast-track from M6 Anomaly Watch black-box tests.

We hold M6 on hospital and grid substitute certification now that chain-of-thought is dead, using activation inspection and incident reporting to restore audit coverage. Finished Shield, Transition Bridge and Continuity Switch work is kept sustained by member states without new EU spend, while we use the augmentation story — firms that cut early hiring again — to unblock gigafactory siting councils with mayors and unions.
