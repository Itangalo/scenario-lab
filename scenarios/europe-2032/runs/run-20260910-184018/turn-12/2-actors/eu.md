## In practice
We keep CER/NIS2 crisis coordination live: ENISA triages via the joint telemetry feed, Shield and civil protection surge teams stay in hardest-hit communes with clean backups and re-entry kits paid by reprogrammed Digital Europe and UCPM funds, and distributors/ports hold isolation discipline. Mayors keep 24-hour reporting in exchange for crews; hospital AI stays uncertified and gigafactory plots stay fenced-guarded, no new concrete.

We launch the Demonstrators via Employment/Social and Competitiveness Councils: ESF+ and Digital Europe SME vouchers for supervised office assistants in town halls, clinics and accountancy/legal SMEs, conditioned on no net layoffs and logged human sign-off, with ENISA hardening and fraud-hotline linkage. Employer federations get flexibility, unions get retraining seats and hiring-back audits from the spring evidence, to turn augmentation from resentment into shorter queues.
