## In practice  
We are treating the InvestAI Gigafactories as the core of our strategic recovery. The Commission has formally declared them a project of Union-wide strategic importance under Article 122 TFEU, unlocking fast-track procedures for grid access and environmental assessments. We are instructing the European Investment Bank to disburse the first €50bn tranche immediately to the lead sites in Brittany, Norrland, and Andalusia, conditioned on binding agreements for 70% local hiring and joint municipal ownership of heat-reuse infrastructure. ENTSO-E is now mandated to prioritise 15GW of reserve capacity from nuclear and offshore wind PPAs for these zones by year-end, with a dedicated task force established in DG ENER to resolve interconnection bottlenecks in real time.

At the same time, we are operationalising the Sovereign Model Initiative (SOMI) through a Horizon Europe Joint Undertaking, co-funded by the Digital Europe Programme and national AI institutes. Recruitment of EU-diaspora researchers is underway via Blue Card+ fast-tracks and retention bonuses, with initial teams assembling in Paris, Helsinki, and Barcelona. The model will be trained on EU-secured data, with weights stored under state-actor-grade security and no external dependency. Crucially, EAAD’s first red-team evaluations will target SOMI itself — not foreign models — to build internal technical credibility and audit capacity from within. The stalled hyperscaler levy has been paused and repurposed as a contingency mechanism, with EAAD’s early operations now funded through the Recovery and Resilience Facility to preserve political capital while maintaining forward motion.
