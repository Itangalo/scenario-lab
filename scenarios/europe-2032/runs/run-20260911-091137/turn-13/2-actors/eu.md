## New measure
**EU Municipal Repair and Trust Corps**
It puts paid local teams in clinics and utilities running degraded fallbacks to fix manual-reversion failures on site, verify data-leak claims publicly, and keep steward jobs filled.
This and now because with capital at 14 and sentiment at 9 nothing central can land, while the February agent scare plus unfounded leak stories are what is breaking absorption; a preparedness and resilience (6) corps is the only way the two-year commitment to hold social absorption survives this half-year.

## Priority
EU Municipal Repair and Trust Corps — because keeping lights, water and clinics visibly running in uncertified towns outranks any new compute or evaluation push we cannot fund or staff.

## In practice
We act through Employment and Environment Councils on an ESF+ and Civil Protection base, tasking ENISA and ECDC/HERA to second detection-kit trainers to the Corps, and labour ministries to convert Transition Guarantee stipends into Corps contracts. Money is repurposed cohesion and Digital Europe funds only; no new envelope is asked.

Delivery is municipal: mayors who take mutual-aid terms get crews first, the two refusing regions are offered bilateral aid slots via Japan/Korea/Canada tooling instead. Nurses, grid engineers and continuity stewards front verification — publishing trace summaries and leak checks — not Commissioners, to stop the thin-fair repair campaign failing again.
