### The attack and the agreement
The automated attack came in late summer as a ransomware sweep through shared software used by cities, clinics and logistics firms. Appointment books vanished, ledgers locked, and in places hit hardest during the spring walkouts staff fell back to paper. Defenders were visibly behind for weeks; pooled logs from the grid hardening work helped cut off the worst nodes, but restoration was uneven and attribution remained open.

In Washington and Beijing, negotiators announced a limited pact on securing model weights and restraining autonomous escalation, with thin verification. Brussels was not at the table but asked to be let in as an observer, offering grid telemetry and hospital containment records as its contribution.

### A small cell, a finished shield
The grid segmentation programme formally finished, and where its kits were installed operators isolated the malicious processes in hours. Elsewhere teams worked blind. That contrast became the Commission's argument: the hardening had worked, just not widely enough.

With budgets frozen and cohesion thin, the Union opened only one small instrument — a verification and patching cell tasked to copy the American-Chinese security provisions into European rules and to steer emergency fixes to hospitals and municipalities. The AI evaluation institute took the desk work; ENISA and Europol's cyber centre took triage, paid for by reprogrammed digital funds.

Paris and Berlin let it through because it cost almost nothing. The cell won observer seats at follow-on talks, but not decision rights. Patching was paired, belatedly, with fixes to the literal triage tools rather than forced migration, which calmed some clinicians without ending the dispute.

Meanwhile offices absorbed a quieter finding: productivity studies showed assistants lifting output in law, accountancy and administration without cutting jobs. Relief for workers, disappointment for investors who had bet on continued transformation. Talk of moratoria on new data-centre connections and paper triage lingered as rumour and council motions, not decisions.
