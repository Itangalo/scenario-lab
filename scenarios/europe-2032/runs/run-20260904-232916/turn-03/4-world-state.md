### Sovereignty Recalibration  
The Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive collapses in trilogue, rejected by Germany and Ireland over legal exposure and economic risk. The Netherlands’ bilateral deal with a US hyperscaler formalizes a split in EU alignment. In response, the Commission announces plans for the Sovereign AI Stack — a proposed €50bn publicly funded programme to build three fully sovereign compute clusters in France, Poland, and Finland. These would host EU-trained models under strict legal control, aiming to create de facto independence from foreign infrastructure.

However, the proposal immediately faces scrutiny. Germany questions the legality of binding participation for critical operators, while Poland delays site approvals pending environmental reviews. Crucially, no funding mechanism is yet secured: the Commission intends to reallocate unused cohesion funds and seek joint borrowing under a new innovation envelope, but these require unanimous Council approval. As a result, the programme remains in early design, with no construction started. openweight_capability holds at 43.0, as no new open models are released or advanced during this period.

### Political Strain  
With three large measures still in flight — InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, and Resilience Surge — the Commission attempts to launch a fourth: the Sovereign AI Stack. But under EU modelling constraints, only one new measure may be introduced per turn, and only one may be designated a priority. The attempt to elevate the Sovereign AI Stack as the new priority while adding it to an already overloaded portfolio triggers institutional friction. The political cost of overreach remains: the abandonment of the directive counts as a public defeat (−5), and the unresolved portfolio burden continues (−13 charge). eu_political_capital falls to 18.0, limiting future agility.

Resilience remains at 38.0. The Resilience Surge continues patchy rollout, with no measurable improvement this turn. public_sentiment holds at 42.0 — neither alarmed nor inspired.
