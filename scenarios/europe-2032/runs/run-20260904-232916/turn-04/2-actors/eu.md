## In practice  
We are acting on the reality that the open frontier is no longer trailing — it is *riding the wake* of the closed one. The recent openweight release, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times within days, means that whatever capability exists at 56.5 is now permanently distributed. Our sovereignty cannot depend on legal control of compute alone if the weights themselves are loose. So this turn, we are shifting the Sovereign AI Stack from planning to steel-in-the-ground: the French cluster at Saclay will begin construction immediately under the EDIC’s authority, using emergency infrastructure powers invoked under Article 122 TFEU for critical digital infrastructure. We are allocating €12bn from the innovation envelope — unlocked by a narrow Council majority — to fast-track procurement of 100,000 H100e-equivalent GPUs, with delivery staggered by Q4 2028. These will be wired into a dedicated grid node powered by nuclear and offshore wind, with cooling integrated into local district heating — making the cluster not just sovereign, but sustainable and visible.

At the same time, we are instituting a firewall around openweight development. No model trained on EU sovereign compute will be released openly unless it has undergone a pre-release evaluation by the European AI Office and has had its weights cryptographically locked to prevent retraining or redistribution. We are calling this the "Sovereign Open" framework: open access for research and audit, but closed to weaponization. This is not a restriction on science — it is a condition of survival in a world where a single leaked model can erase strategic advantage. We are offering Germany and Ireland a clear path back in: contribute grid capacity or talent to the Finnish or Polish clusters, and you gain full access to the secured model pipeline. Resist, and you will inherit a dependency on US or Chinese systems that can be cut off at will — or worse, find your own industries running on open models that no one can control. The era of passive dependence is over. The era of built sovereignty has begun.
