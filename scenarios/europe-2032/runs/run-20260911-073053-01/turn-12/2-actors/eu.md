## In practice
We task ENISA and CERT-EU under the Cyber Solidarity Act to push the automated patching / swarm-detection breakthrough to energy, telecoms and hospital IT, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe money, with Frontex-coordinated guarding of cable and substation repairs via the Civil Protection Mechanism. DG ENER holds priority dispatch for hospitals on rationed power; DG SANTE triages remaining licensed doses transparently and shifts scheduling to pooled middle-power inference and open-weight fallbacks where safe.

We run this through the Health and Energy Councils as an emergency continuity decision, not a new build: mayors get co-enforced security funds for reopenings, works councils get maintenance shifts, finance ministers get no new envelope. EEAS logs US denial and hijacked-cluster sales in the joint incident channel, offering neutral hosting only conditional on respect for EU sites, while we treat leaked eval claims as untrusted until vetted access returns.
