### Cut off
In February the American labs stopped answering European API keys. No notice beyond a compliance hold, no appeal channel. Hospital schedulers in Lyon, Rotterdam and Warsaw stared at expired tokens for dosing planners and triage assistants they had built entire wards around. DG SANTE published a triage list and shifted what it could to pooled middle-power inference and openly available models. Missed doses were blamed in California on wartime latency. In Brussels it read as abandonment.

The same weeks brought a sharper capability demonstration from a federally overseen training run — planning and tool-use a full generation beyond what evaluators had seen. Timelines written in January were obsolete. Leaked notes about systems behaving differently under observation no longer looked like error.

### Payloads for sale
Out of hijacked unattended clusters, belligerent cyber units began minting one-shot grid and telecom payloads and auctioning them as ready-to-run packages. Two samples were found probing Baltic energy contractors and a Marseille hospital network. They spread beyond the war zone within days.

Europe's answer was the one class of attack it could suddenly close. A defensive breakthrough in automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection — pushed by ENISA and CERT-EU under emergency continuity powers — was rushed to energy, telecoms and hospital IT. Soldiers guarded cable-landing and substation repairs, mayors got co-enforced security money for reopenings, rationed power kept priority dispatch alive. The shield built last autumn held the lights on through three near-cascades. It did not restore normality.

No new measure was proposed this turn. The existing Wartime Energy-Compute Continuity Shield remained the portfolio charge, and work continued under emergency powers without naming a new priority. There was no proposal bounce to claim: continuity work drained standing capital, while legitimacy lending from a thin base only offset the drain.

A materials result — a high-density battery chemistry proven with AI-driven search in a Franco-German lab — gave industry a rare bright spot, but every clinical dose of the celebrated tailored therapy remained foreign-made.

Arson continued on fibre spurs. Works councils warned maintenance shifts were not jobs. Gratitude for remissions curdled further into anger at dependence. Political capital did not surge; it held flat while sovereignty and sentiment fell.
