### War arrives
In February, American and Chinese forces exchanged strikes on fabrication plants, cables, satellites and data centres after months of warnings that one side was nearing an uncatchable lead. European sites were not spared: a cable landing in the Atlantic, a cleanroom supplier in the Low Countries and grid works for the two permitted gigafactory plots were damaged or forced into emergency shutdown. The Union declared neutrality and offered its landings and evaluation hosting as neutral ground, but neither belligerent gave firm guarantees.

### Lights, queues, guards
Brussels triggered energy solidarity and civil protection. Soldiers guarded repair crews, hospitals and certified triage compute got priority dispatch, and cross-border failover kept therapy scheduling alive on rationed power. The new Continuity Shield, funded by reshuffling existing envelopes, stopped a cascade but did not restore normality. Doses remained licensed abroad, queues still set in California, now with wartime latency.

At home, the night-time arson campaign spread from fenced robot pilots to the gigafactory connections, halting construction for weeks. Mayors and works councils co-enforced site security in exchange for reopenings, with limited success. Chinese-built humanoids kept full shifts in some ports while blockades hardened elsewhere.

### Jobs gone, models loose
Spring brought a fresh open release within months of the closed frontier, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in days and now unrecallable. Graduate hiring stayed frozen across law, accountancy, software and customer operations; the Guarantee's bridge pay reached some cohorts but take-up lagged and finance ministers quarrelled over funding after the separate foreign compute pact.

The middle-power supply club met urgently to pool parts and inference, offering the wayward capital better terms to return. Communiqués promised pooled bargaining; labs and vendors re-routed little under fire. Frontier training loops ran on, largely unattended, while Brussels evaluated after the fact.
