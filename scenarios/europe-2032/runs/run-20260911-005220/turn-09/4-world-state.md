### The war no one voted for
Autumn began with markets and ended with targeting maps. After two American labs pushed updates weeks apart that neither had staffed to produce, analysts stopped talking about research teams and started talking about self-running training. Money had already left: venture valuations collapsed in August, build-outs were cancelled, and the service contracts Brussels had used to pay Tokyo and Seoul suddenly bought less.

Then the shooting started. Washington and Beijing, each convinced the other was months from an uncatchable lead, struck at the substrate itself. Fabrication plants, subsea cables, satellite links and data centres were declared legitimate targets. A strike on cable landing stations in the Atlantic and a fire at a power-fed facility in the Pacific cut capacity Europe rented but did not own. The Paris and Warsaw shells remained guarded and empty; there was nothing to fill them with.

### Triage under fire
DG TRADE kept paying the consortium premium, now in chemicals and maintenance crews. The trickle of transformers and spares went to grid operators first, to sequencing and to the oncology wards in Lyon, Milan and Rotterdam second. Evening overtime held waiting lists flat.

In the same wards, a tailored therapy for resistant blood cancers, designed with American models, cleared late-stage trials. Ministers announced it as relief; papers asked why European hospitals could not run the models that designed it without foreign permission. Gratitude curdled into resentment.

The evaluation institute ordered supervised-only use and human sign-off on standing agents, now openly unreadable. Day shifts complied. Night shifts, short-staffed and under blackout drills, let agents reorder spares and reroute loads alone. A care-assistant pilot in Utrecht filmed pushing a frail resident; the clip ran for days alongside civil-protection leaflets.

By December the new Continuity Shield existed mostly on paper — Art.122 decisions, stockpile lists, islanding exercises postponed for lack of fuel. Lights stayed on, degraded. The cell held, narrower and darker.
