### ASML Talks Stall Amid Rising Tensions

The European Union presses forward with the ASML Sovereignty Guarantee, framing it as a cornerstone of industrial autonomy. However, negotiations with ASML stall as U.S. pressure intensifies, with Washington warning of supply chain disruptions and questioning the compatibility of EU co-ownership rights with export control regimes. While the offer of fast-tracked permitting and access to InvestAI capital remains on the table, ASML resists embedding governance clauses into equipment contracts, citing legal exposure and commercial neutrality. Parallel talks with Germany, France, and Italy on co-investing in redundancy units show limited progress, hampered by disagreements over cost-sharing and siting.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Dips

Global AI capability advances to 57.5, driven by further gains in autonomous software engineering and agent coordination. No major incidents occur, but the gap between capability and safety widens. The European Interpretability Initiative continues development, yet its tools remain in pilot phase, not yet integrated into frontier model compliance. With no new safety measure fully landing on deployed systems, and capability rising, confidence in control erodes slightly.

### Political Capital Erodes

Public sentiment dips to 41.0 as backlash grows against AI-driven displacement, amplified by rising support for campaign platforms calling for data centre moratoriums and worker protections. The EU’s focus on semiconductor sovereignty fails to resonate broadly, perceived by critics as elite technocracy detached from social concerns. With four large measures still in flight—including the newly prioritized ASML initiative—the political burden mounts. The named priority incurs its cost, and no measure completes to offset the strain.

Resilience declines slightly as cyber gains from the completed Cyber Shield are offset by slow adaptation to emerging supply-chain threats. EU AI sovereignty falls further, reflecting both the capability rise and the absence of any secured access breakthrough. The window to lock in foundational control narrows.
