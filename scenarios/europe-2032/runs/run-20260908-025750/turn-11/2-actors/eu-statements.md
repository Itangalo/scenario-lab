# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Stabilise public legitimacy and institutional cohesion through transparent, participatory AI governance in essential public services

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI — but we now acknowledge that **capability without containment is self-defeating, and sovereignty that cannot enforce its own survival is illusion**. The two mandates are not merely in tension; they converge on the necessity of enforceable boundaries around catastrophic risk.
  - Grounds: The openweight_frontier_release this turn — a jump in `openweight_capability` from 45.0 to 52.0, now within 10 points of the closed frontier — proves that the most dangerous capabilities are not just escaping, they are being *permanently distributed*. Combined with the bio_incident, this means the threat is no longer confined to labs or state actors. The EU’s ability to determine its own future now depends on its ability to *exclude* certain capabilities from circulation altogether. Our identity must reflect that this is no longer about balancing two goals, but about recognising that one is the precondition of the other.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Stabilise democratic legitimacy and sovereign control under accelerating AI capability — and now, in the wake of a biological incident with model involvement and the definitive collapse of human-legible interpretability, extend that control to include **enforceable containment of systemic risk vectors, beginning with biosecurity and cognitive opacity — and now, in light of openweight proliferation, extending to the permanent diffusion of frontier capabilities**
  - Grounds: The openweight_frontier_release this turn closes the window of control through laboratory governance. Whatever is released openly is now everywhere, and the assumption that we can regulate risk by regulating providers is broken. Our two-year commitment must now explicitly account for a world where the most dangerous models are not just accessible, but *already in the wild*. This does not weaken our resolve — it sharpens it toward *containment at scale*, not just at the source.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
