## New measure
**EU Cure Access Deployment**
Puts the new tailored therapies to work in EU public hospitals and clinics on accredited EU hosting and offline continuity systems, so patients feel benefit as European healthcare delivery rather than dependence on foreign models.
This and why now because the medical breakthrough is the first positive shock in years arriving while sentiment is at 7 and capital at 12, and unless delivery is visibly EU-run it will read as further dependence; tying it to the guarantee placements and cleaned networks makes the two-year commitment tangible.

## Priority
EU Entry-Level Guarantee Top-Up, because with capital at 12 and queues still driving blockades, paid hiring where systems are clean must hold before cure rollout can staff and scale.

## In practice
We run M11 through DG EMPL and EPSCO as the spine: ESF+ top-up advances stay directed to arrears municipalities and hospitals on the offline stack, with time-limited siting continuity for the two blockaded sites exchanged for binding local hiring quotas. Supervisors get a funded training allowance so posts do not collapse into burden complaints.

We prepare cure delivery inside existing health competences: HERA and EMA for accelerated tailored-therapy protocols, EU4Health procurement for diagnostics and cold-chain, and the sovereign hosting pact plus EU-only node for any model-dependent personalization to stay on accredited EU infrastructure. Health Council mandates pilot oncology and rare-disease centres in clean-network cities first, staffed in part by guarantee cohorts, so benefit and jobs arrive together.
