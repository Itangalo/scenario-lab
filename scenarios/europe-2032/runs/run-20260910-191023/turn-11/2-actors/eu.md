## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Guarantee Top-Up**
Extends the Transition Shield into a funded first-job guarantee for graduates in law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration via wage-subsidised placements with public services and accredited EU-hosted employers.
This and why now because measurable non-replacement of entry roles is driving blockades and permit-halts while capital is at 11 and sentiment at 8, and only paid hiring where funds previously stalled can reconnect Brussels to livelihoods.

## Priority
EU Entry-Level Guarantee Top-Up, because holding essential services and livelihoods together now depends on visible hiring rather than further cyber containment alone.

## In practice
We run this through DG EMPL and EPSCO as a top-up to M9: ESF+ reprogramming plus emergency advances become a dedicated envelope for 6-12 month wage-subsidised first posts, placed in municipalities, hospitals running the offline stack, and firms on accredited sovereign hosting. Cities where funds flowed continue; arrears areas get advances first.

We tie placements to the cleaned networks from M7/M10: national SOCs keep feeding the pooled hub via the EU-only node, so placements land where systems are clean and queues are shorter. We ask councils debating data-centre permit halts for time-limited siting continuity in exchange for local hiring quotas from the guarantee.
