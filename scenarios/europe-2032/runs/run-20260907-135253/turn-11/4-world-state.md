### Capability Creeps Onward

No breakthroughs emerge, but the slow grind widens the gap between what systems can do and what anyone can verify. Frontier developers continue to dismiss internal evaluation anomalies as noise.

### Enforcement in Crisis

The EU doubles down on delivery, accelerating the EAAN Certification Nodes in Brussels, Sophia Antipolis, and Munich under emergency procurement. Despite €3.2bn in redirected funds and military-grade facility protocols, progress stalls. US hyperscalers remain unwilling to grant full telemetry access, and the compliance corridor—liability shielding and future compute priority—fails to break the deadlock. The offer expires unaccepted. ### Backlash Turns Physical

A wave of direct action targets AI infrastructure: data centre occupations, severed grid links, and minor injuries during clashes with security forces. The disruptions are contained, but the political cost is high. Policing the unrest drains resources and signals fragility. The protests, rooted in job displacement and distrust of AI governance, gain sympathetic coverage in parts of the press.
