## Priority
**Priority:** Accelerate Core Infrastructure Buildout Under Emergency Powers  
This is no longer about competitiveness or control—it is about survival. The cyber_major_incident proves that we are operating critical systems on infrastructure we do not own and cannot secure. Without sovereign, hardened, auditable AI infrastructure, every evaluation, restriction, and resilience measure is a sandcastle at high tide. The Core is the only lever that can break our dependence, and we must now build it at war speed.

## In practice
We are declaring a state of strategic necessity. The cyberattack on health IT systems—automated, fast-moving, and traced to model-generated exploit chains—has exposed our foundational vulnerability: we regulate AI while running it on foreign-owned clouds, commercial grids, and contested supply chains. The Sovereign AI Core is no longer a long-term ambition. It is the emergency response.

We are invoking Article 122 TFEU to override national permitting bottlenecks in Brittany and northern Sweden, designating the Core sites as critical strategic infrastructure. The Commission, through DG ENERGY and DG CONNECT, will directly award fast-track contracts to three pre-qualified consortia—Siemens-Nokia, EDF-TotalEnergies, and a new EU-owned entity—under emergency procurement rules. €45bn will be drawn from unused REACT-EU allocations and redirected from the defunct InvestAI Gigafactories fund, approved via a special Council vote in the ECOFIN formation under urgent procedure. The EIB will match this with €30bn in senior debt, secured against future sovereign AI service revenues.

Germany and Sweden will be offered co-governance of local nodes with full transparency on emissions and water use. Italy and Hungary will be given a final offer: full participation in the Core’s secondary node with guaranteed jobs and technology transfer, or exclusion from access to sovereign AI services for five years. ENISA will begin shadowing construction to ensure air-gapping and auditability from day one. This is not a request. It is the only path left to keep the Union whole, secure, and in control of its own future.
