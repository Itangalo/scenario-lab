### The edge teams deploy
ENISA's mobile install teams began work in autumn, vans of contracted integrators arriving at town utilities and small clinics with pre-configured kits for the fast patching pipelines and swarm detectors. In certified hospitals and telecoms, containment times stayed low and operators reported another quiet quarter.

On the edge, progress was slower than the implementing decision promised. Kits that installed in hours in a large hospital took days in a municipal basement where documentation was missing and staff had no time to shadow. National response teams reported roughly half the targeted sites fitted by December, the rest queued into next year. The defended-core versus brittle-edge gap narrowed, but did not close.

### Cheques and portals
The Entry-Level Transition Guarantee moved from launch to enforcement. In the two large states where voucher portals had opened early, bridging payments and six-month retraining vouchers reached school-leavers and displaced junior staff in law, accountancy and customer operations. Graduate fairs in those cities were fuller by winter.

Elsewhere, labour ministers missed the deadlines set by their colleagues. Three states stalled over who paid the levy on large deployers of assistants and how automation levels were reported, and co-financing slowed. Anger eased where money arrived, hardened where portals still showed waiting lists. Editorial pages called it a two-speed safety net.

### Biology waits
No new illness followed the genome-model paper, but health agencies did not stand down. The emergency health authority and disease centre kept checking stocks, and pooled black-box test results with Pacific and North American partners trickled in — inconclusive, hard to compare across opaque models. Cyber defence felt ahead; biology felt exposed.

The capital holding a separate supply arrangement still neither refused nor signed the conditioned hosting offer, leaving the middle-power compact working but incomplete. With American frontier progress slow and rationing rhetoric steady, Brussels found itself able to hold its line, but not to extend it.
