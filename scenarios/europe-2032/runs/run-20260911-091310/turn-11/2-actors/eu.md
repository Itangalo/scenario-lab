## Priority
EU Brittle Edge Hardening Extension — because with no measures in flight and core sites already quiet, closing the uncertified edge outranks new funds and is the resilience half of the absorption-plus-leverage commitment.

## In practice
We act through ENISA and the Health and Digital Europe funding lines, not a new directive. The Commission issues an implementing decision under the Shield certificates: national CSIRTs plus contracted integrators get EU co-financing for on-site install teams, pre-configured patch/detector kits, and a waiver of recertification fees for town utilities and small clinics, with progress tracked in the hospital and municipal drill cycle rehearsed last autumn.

On the other tracks we spend no new instrument. The Entry-Level Transition Guarantee moves to enforcement: EPSCO holds the stalled states to portal deadlines and levy-reporting by large deployers to keep co-financing flowing. The bio genome signal stays with HERA/ECDC for stocking and with the middle-power compact for pooled black-box results, and the holdout capital stays offered conditioned hosting volume.
