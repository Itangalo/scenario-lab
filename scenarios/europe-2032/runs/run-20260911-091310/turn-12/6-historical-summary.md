By early 2031 EU strategy shifted from generations to unrecallable diffusion after a non-European lab released open-weights agents capable of days-long autonomous operation, obsoleting autumn deployment plans.

Cyber response was a containment net: Council Recommendation tasked ENISA/national teams with mandatory isolation playbooks for certified hospitals/telecoms/energy — swarm detectors, host takedown channels, spring drills, co-financing. Certified core isolated March-April probing wave with short containment; brittle edge repeated 2030 pattern with ~half new municipal utility/clinic sites queued for lack of docs/staff — gap narrowed only where money had landed.

Entry-Level Transition Guarantee remained two-speed: two large states paid bridging/vouchers/credits, three stalled over deployer levy; municipal councils linked data-centre permits to transition payments, injunctions and heated hearings reported.

Biology stayed exposed with no illness but inconclusive pooled black-box results; Washington-Beijing pact thin, Brussels observer; middle-power compact operational but incomplete as conditioned defector offer still neither refused nor signed.
