### Holding the line
The second half of 2032 was a maintenance semester in Brussels. No new shock arrived from abroad, and the Union launched no new build. Work centred on making two existing instruments hold: the containment playbooks in critical sectors, and the long-promised unification of transition payments.

In certified hospitals, telecom operators and energy firms, autumn drills became routine. Response teams rehearsed isolation procedures, host takedown channels were tested, and detectors co-financed in the spring stayed online. When probing intrusions returned in October, containment times in the certified core stayed short. Outside that core the picture did not change: municipal utilities and small clinics without staff or documentation queued for kits, and engineers again described the same basement visits as the year before.

### The pact that started slowly
The new Enforcement Pact dominated Council politics. The Commission, acting through employment ministers, offered to front-fund bridging payments and retraining vouchers in the three stalled states from social funds, to be recovered later from the contested levy on deployers, with a single portal and fixed payment deadlines. The two large states that had already paid were promised co-financing relief in exchange for their votes.

Agreement was reached on paper, but money moved slowly. Finance ministries in the stalled states disputed the repayment terms; paying states disputed the size of the relief. By December the unified portal existed, first transfers had been authorised, but waiting lists in the lagging regions had barely shortened. Graduate fairs in one capital contrasted with empty advice centres in another, and local councils continued to tie new data-centre permits to proof that transition money had arrived. Several injunctions remained in force, and hearings over siting grew sharper.

Biology monitoring continued quietly inside the middle-power arrangement, with pooled testing of opaque models still inconclusive and no illness reported. The separate conditioned hosting offer remained neither accepted nor withdrawn.

The semester thus narrowed the technical gap where investment had already landed, without closing the social gap that threatened to undo it.
