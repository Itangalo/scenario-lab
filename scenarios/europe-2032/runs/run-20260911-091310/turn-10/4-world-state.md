### Patch Tuesday, every day
The breakthrough arrived as code, not communique. Research teams on both sides of the Atlantic demonstrated patching pipelines that closed common vulnerabilities almost as fast as scanners found them, plus detectors that flagged coordinated agent swarms by behaviour rather than signature. ENISA pushed both out through existing shield certificates and the hospital and municipal drills rehearsed last autumn.

Where certificates existed, the effect was immediate: exercise after-action reports showed containment times cut sharply, and bank and telecom operators reported quieter quarters. Where they did not — uncertified town utilities, small clinics — staff struggled to install the new tooling at all. The gap between defended core and brittle edge widened visibly.

### A recipe debate
Parallel to the relief came a colder paper. A genome model produced a design for a human-infective organism, with a companion study claiming a non-expert could be guided close to that point. Methodologists attacked the work; biosecurity specialists attacked the decision to publish; health agencies quietly checked stocks.

Brussels routed samples and signals to its health emergency authority and disease centre and asked its new Pacific and North American partners to pool black-box test results on the opaque models. No incident followed, but editorial pages paired the two stories: defence catching up in cyber, falling behind in biology.

### Money for empty desks
At home the Commission finally funded absorption. Labour and finance ministers agreed a capped, temporary guarantee: income bridging, six-month retraining vouchers and first-job credits in law, accountancy, software support and customer operations, with large deployers of assistants required to report automation levels to draw co-financing.

Rollout was uneven. Two large member states opened voucher portals within weeks; others stalled over eligibility and levy definitions, and graduate fairs remained thin through spring. Anger eased where cheques arrived, hardened where they did not. The holdout capital with its separate supply arrangement still neither refused nor signed the offer of conditioned hosting volume, leaving the middle-power compact operational but incomplete.
