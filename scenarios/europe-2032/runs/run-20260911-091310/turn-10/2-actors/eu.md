## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee**
It funds wage insurance, paid retraining places and employer hiring credits for displaced entry-level cohorts in law, accountancy, software and customer operations, tied to automating employers.
This and now because graduate hiring has frozen while capital and sentiment are collapsing, and the two-year commitment to hold social absorption fails in this turn if displacement is left as an unfunded plan; the cyber-defence breakthrough and bio-precursor are handled on finished law and the compact, while scarce new money goes to the shock voters feel.

## Priority
EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee — because with no measures in flight and capital at 22, stopping the political bleed from empty graduate desks outranks any new build and is the core of the absorption-plus-leverage commitment.

## In practice
We act through EPSCO and ECOFIN on an ESF+ / SURE-type legal base, not a new frontier fund. The Commission tables a targeted guarantee decision: member states draw EU co-financing for income bridging, 6-month retraining vouchers and first-job credits in the four frozen sectors, with a levy-reporting condition on large deployers of assistants to tie automating employers to the fund. Labour ministers who were told to plan without money get money; finance ministers get a capped, temporary envelope.

On the other tracks we spend no new instrument. ENISA and national CSIRTs push the cyber-defence breakthrough — automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection — out through the Shield certificates and M7/M8 drills in hospitals and municipal utilities where exercises already work. The bio-uplift signal goes to HERA and ECDC for detection stocking and to the middle-power compact for pooled black-box evaluation, and the side-deal capital stays offered conditioned hosting volume to unwind.
