## In practice

Alongside that we launch the Cyber Shield via NIS2 implementing acts and ENISA, directed first at the two compromised transmission operators, the affected port and water utility: enforced segmentation audits, 24h detection reporting to the common CSIRT network, and EU-funded islanding exercises with ACER and member-state TSOs. Industry resistance on cost is met with Connecting Europe Facility and resilience funding tied to gigafactory grid connections.
