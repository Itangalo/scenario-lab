### The Weight of Failure

The Sovereign AI Core Stress Test Programme concludes with damning results: critical systems in energy and health fail live red-team drills, exposing unacceptably slow failover and brittle coordination. Public reporting is delayed by legal disputes over disclosure thresholds, but leaks confirm systemic fragility. No enforcement follows — the EU lacks both the sovereign infrastructure to replace failing systems and the political capital to compel reform.

### Preparing Ground, Not Gaining Ground

With the Sovereign Compute Accelerator canceled due to lack of funding, the EU pivots to preparatory action. The Emergency Grid-Ready Compute Siting Fast-Track is launched as a small category 4 measure, aiming to clear land and grid bottlenecks in Finland, Spain, and Romania. Under emergency provisions, the Commission designates three pre-zoned sites with existing renewable integration potential. ENTSO-E and national regulators begin interconnection studies, and fast-track permitting is mandated.

Yet this is groundwork only. No construction begins, and no compute comes online. The move is technically sound but politically fragile — several member states raise concerns about sovereignty over energy infrastructure, and environmental groups file preliminary challenges citing rushed assessments.
