## New measure
**Cyber Resilience Surge for Critical Infrastructure**

A time-bound programme to harden electricity, water and port operators against AI-enabled intrusions, using emergency procurement and binding cross-border detection standards.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 4`  
`Targeted effect:` increases resilience from 39.0 to at least 60.0; reduces likelihood of cascading failure from future cyber incidents; begins closing the detection gap revealed by cyber_test_shot  
`Applies to:` EU member states with critical infrastructure exposed to AI-enabled threats, particularly energy and transport sectors

## Priority
**Cyber Resilience Surge for Critical Infrastructure** — because the intrusion revealed by cyber_test_shot exposed systemic blind spots in real time, and failure to respond now will lock in vulnerability just as offensive AI capability crosses into open distribution.

## In practice
We are activating Article 12 of the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) in emergency mode, fast-tracking binding technical standards for intrusion detection and automated response across electricity transmission system operators, water utilities, and major port authorities. This is not a study or a recommendation: it is a six-month sprint to deploy AI-driven monitoring stacks under EU-level supervision, using emergency procurement powers to bypass national delays. The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) will lead implementation, with direct funding from the Crisis Recovery Facility, bypassing usual multiannual programming.

We are negotiating with Germany, France, and Poland this month to secure unanimous support in the COREPER formation, offering accelerated access to the InvestAI Gigafactories fund in exchange for rapid ratification of the new detection mandates. Resistance is expected from member states with under-resourced IT security teams and from private operators wary of compliance costs. To counter this, we are pairing the mandate with a €15bn transition envelope under the Just AI Facility, reimbursing 70% of qualifying hardening expenditures through 2028. The message is clear: resilience is not optional, but the Union will bear the cost of building it.
