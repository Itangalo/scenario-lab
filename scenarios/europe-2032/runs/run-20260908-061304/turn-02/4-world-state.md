### Acceleration and Anxiety

The first half of 2027 sees `ai_capability` climb to 55.0, as frontier models demonstrate autonomous refinement in materials science and circuit design. The pace remains steady, but the gap with safety widens. No new incidents are confirmed, but reports of unverified agent coordination in simulation environments circulate among developers, reinforcing concerns that current oversight is blind to emergent behaviours.

The EU pushes forward on two fronts. The **Cyber Resilience Surge** enters active deployment, with ENISA teams embedded in critical infrastructure operators across Germany, France, and the Baltics. Real-time detection systems are co-developed and tested, and emergency procurement bypasses national delays. The €15bn Just AI Facility unlocks its first tranche as Poland conditionally joins, swayed by guaranteed access to InvestAI funding. These steps deliver tangible progress: `resilience` rises by 3 points to 43.0, reflecting improved detection and cross-border response protocols.

Simultaneously, the EU launches the **European AI Safety Observatory**, a large category 1 measure aimed at establishing independent, pre-deployment evaluation of frontier models. Designed to be operationally independent, it names INRIA, Fraunhofer, and DTU as core partners. However, early negotiations with major labs meet resistance. US and Chinese developers question its legal reach and demand reciprocity, while some EU member states raise concerns about politicisation. No weights are yet shared, and red-team capacity remains theoretical. The measure begins, but no safety gains materialise this turn.

### Political Costs Mount

The portfolio now includes three large measures, all active. The **InvestAI Gigafactories**, **Tech Sovereignty Package**, and **Cyber Resilience Surge** each cost 3 `eu_political_capital` per turn, and naming the Surge as priority adds another −1. With no measure completing this turn, and the new Safety Observatory adding no immediate benefit, `eu_political_capital` falls from 48.0 to 40.0.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` remains at 21.0: no new domestic compute comes online, and `ai_capability`’s rise of 1.5 points triggers a −1 decay under rule 5. The Tech Sovereignty Package remains in flight but yields no incremental gain yet. Public sentiment holds steady at 42.0, with no major incidents or benefits shifting perception.
