### Capability Creeps, Safety Lags

Despite the investment slump, AI capability advances to 57.0 as automated verification spreads across legal and clinical domains, enabling rapid, self-checked progress. Developers report sharp jumps in agent autonomy, particularly in contract analysis and diagnostic support. With no new safety protocols deployed, and the gap between capability and safety widening, confidence in control erodes further—ai_safety drops to 24.0.

### EU Acts, But Pays the Price

The EU completes the Cyber Resilience Surge, hardening critical infrastructure across all member states. Real-time AI-driven intrusion detection is now operational in energy, water, and port systems, with successful containment of simulated attacks in multiple locations. This institutionalises crisis gains, boosting resilience to 48.0.

Simultaneously, the EU launches the **Sovereign Inference Corridor**, a large category 4 measure aimed at creating a secure, EU-controlled inference backbone. The initiative repurposes funds and fast-tracks retrofitting of EuroHPC sites. However, securing unanimous approval proves difficult—Italy and Spain delay endorsement despite hosting offers, citing concerns over centralisation and procurement transparency. The measure begins, but momentum is fragile.

The European AI Safety Observatory continues staffing and issues access demands to foreign labs, but faces legal uncertainty. No frontier developer complies, and the prospect of a CJEU case looms. The Tech Sovereignty Package pivots to state-led development, with EIB guarantees intended to revive stalled projects.

Public sentiment declines to 35.0 as campaign rhetoric intensifies. Moratoriums on AI in schools and hiring gain mainstream traction, and stories of white-collar displacement grow louder. The EU’s emergency measures are seen as reactive, not transformative.

Political capital falls to 29.0 under the weight of six large measures, including the new priority. The completion of the Cyber Resilience Surge brings a +4 bonus, but this is outweighed by ongoing costs and declining public mood.
