### A Turn of Contradictions

Despite a major cyberattack and rising job displacement, the EU pushes forward on sovereign infrastructure — but momentum falters under political and social strain.

A model-generated ransomware sweep cripples municipal services in three member states, exposing critical gaps in operational resilience. Though containment eventually holds, the attack underscores how quickly AI-enabled threats can exploit fragmented defences. With resilience at 48.0, the damage is absorbed without cascading failure — but public trust erodes further.

The European AI Safety Observatory completes its formation and prepares its first report, but foreign labs continue to ignore access demands. With no compliance, the Commission files a preliminary reference to the CJEU, betting on a long legal fight to assert authority under the AI Act. No safety gains land on deployed systems, and as capability advances, control confidence weakens — ai_safety drops to 21.0.

The Sovereign Inference Corridor gains traction as retrofitting begins in Finland, Portugal, and the Czech Republic. A last-minute deal with Italy and Spain, offering co-governance and fast-tracked funding, secures their reluctant endorsement. Momentum builds, but full operationalisation remains distant.

The new Hardened Openweight Deployment Programme launches, aiming to make fallback systems trustworthy through air-gapped clusters and red-teaming. However, early implementation reveals integration delays and resistance from national agencies wary of centralised control.

The Tech Sovereignty Package shifts decisively toward state-led development, with EIB guarantees unlocking new data centre projects. Yet enthusiasm is muted: public sentiment falls to 32.0 as white-collar job losses mount and media frame AI as a force of disruption, not progress.

The US election concludes with a clear outcome: a president who champions alliance over isolation. The result brings cautious optimism in Brussels — access to frontier AI may stabilise — but also strategic anxiety. Dependence is not ending; it is being renegotiated.
