## In practice
We are pushing the Sovereign Inference Corridor to operational footing this turn, knowing that without it, the Emergency Access Framework is a promise without power. The cyber_major_incident — a model-generated ransomware sweep that crippled municipal services in three member states — has made that real. We are convening the Competitiveness Council under Article 114 TFEU to finalise the Corridor’s governance, offering Italy and Spain rotating seats on the operational board and €3.5bn in fast-tracked resilience co-funding to unlock their approval. Retrofitting begins immediately at the Finnish, Portuguese, and Czech EuroHPC sites, using repurposed InvestAI Gigafactories capital and EIB emergency lending. These nodes will host hardened versions of openweights at 44.0+ capability, running on air-gapped clusters with biometric access and continuous integrity monitoring.

The European AI Safety Observatory has now reached full staffing and is preparing its first public report. With no response from foreign labs to our access demands, we are filing a preliminary reference with the CJEU to establish our legal authority under the AI Act — a move that may take years, but one that signals we are not accepting de facto exclusion from systems shaping our citizens’ lives. Meanwhile, the Tech Sovereignty Package is pivoting fully to state-led development: the EIB has issued its first 80% loan guarantees for data centre builds in the accelerated zones, and we are fast-tracking grid access for two new sites in Sweden and Romania.

The Hardened Openweight Deployment Programme launches this turn to ensure that sovereign inference is not just available, but trustworthy. We are tasking ENISA and the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking with deploying version-locked, integrity-verified openweights across the Corridor nodes, with mandatory red-teaming every six weeks and automated rollback on any detected compromise. This is not about frontier capability — it is about having systems we can run, defend, and believe in, even when the world turns off the tap.
