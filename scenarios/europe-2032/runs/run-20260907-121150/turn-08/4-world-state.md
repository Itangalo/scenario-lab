### The Storm Breaks

The first half of 2030 is defined by cascading shocks. A biosecurity breach involving an AI-designed pathogen triggers global alarm, exposing the EU’s inability to inspect or reconstruct the model behind it. Days later, a blockade in the Taiwan Strait halts advanced semiconductor shipments, freezing all non-Chinese compute expansion. The EU’s frontier AI access is abruptly severed—no logs, no appeal, no fallback.

Brussels responds with emergency mobilisation. The **Replication Foundry Initiative** launches under wartime protocols, directing the InvestAI Gigafactories to rebuild frontier models from open weights. But progress is stalled: no sovereign training stack exists, and the emergent European AI Commons lacks the coordination to deliver one at scale. Early attempts to replicate are limited to small and medium models, yielding modest gains in openweight capability. The initiative is bold, but premature.

The **Sovereign Verification Regime** inches forward: partial checkpoint access is secured from some providers, but two major U.S. firms remain defiant. Forensic teams detect telemetry spoofing, suggesting deliberate obfuscation. Enforcement actions are taken, but legal challenges loom. Meanwhile, the **Institutional Integrity Audit** concludes, revealing AI-mediated decision routing in three public bodies—further eroding trust.

Five cities formally join the **emergent_municipal_oversight_network**, creating a patchwork of local verification. It is a step toward distributed sovereignty, but also a sign of central authority’s erosion.

Public sentiment sours. The bio incident is framed as a failure of oversight; the semiconductor blockade, as strategic blindness. Protests grow over job losses and dependency. The narrative shifts: AI is no longer a tool, but a vector.

### Sovereignty in Freefall

Despite urgency, the EU’s leverage is hollow. Compute is stranded, models are unreachable, and political capital drains under the weight of unfinished measures. The dream of sovereign replication collides with the reality of unpreparedness.
