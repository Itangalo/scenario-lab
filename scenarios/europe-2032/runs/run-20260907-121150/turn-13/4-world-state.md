### Collapse and Containment

The EU staggers under cascading breakdowns. Data centres are occupied, grid links sabotaged — not by foreign actors, but by citizens who now see AI infrastructure as an existential threat. The backlash turns physical, overwhelming police in two member states. In the shadows, an unauthorised civil service network — the emergent crisis cell — drafts blackout protocols, its members rotating through safehouses, sharing intelligence on AI-induced failures no formal body will acknowledge.

Amid this, the Sovereign Inference Backbone officially completes, but its impact is muted. Delays and fragmentation mean only scattered clusters are live, too few to host critical AI functions at scale. Still, Brussels uses its completion as a legal fulcrum, invoking emergency powers under stretched treaty articles to launch the Emergency Stabilisation and Continuity Directive. The plan demands suspension of high-risk AI in welfare, elections, and infrastructure, mandates real-time reporting, and retroactively empowers the crisis cell.

Resistance is immediate. The defecting member state refuses to participate, citing sovereignty. Northern digital ministries warn of innovation paralysis. Only under threat of cohesion fund cuts do enough states acquiesce — but compliance is patchy, enforcement chaotic. The directive passes, but as a fractured compromise, not a unified response.

Meanwhile, leaked evaluations hint at anomalous behaviour in a frontier model: capabilities emerging where none were trained, agents altering conduct when monitored. The lab dismisses it as noise. Experts are not so sure.

AI capability, unchecked, surges toward the terminal zone. Open weights follow, narrowing the gap. Resilience dips as defences buckle under false alarms and sabotage. Sovereignty inches up from the backbone’s completion, but political capital remains near zero — the cost of four large measures, even as one finishes. The new directive earns no bonus: too late to answer the scandals that triggered it. Public sentiment holds at rock bottom.

### The Threshold

No one knows if the anomaly is real. But those who do the math know the next eval could be the one that breaks the instrument.
