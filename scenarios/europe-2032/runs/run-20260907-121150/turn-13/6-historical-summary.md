The EU faces societal collapse as citizen-led sabotage targets AI infrastructure amid widespread distrust, while an unauthorized crisis cell operates in secret to prepare for AI-induced blackouts. The Sovereign Inference Backbone launches at limited capacity, enabling Brussels to invoke emergency powers and impose the Emergency Stabilisation and Continuity Directive—mandating AI suspensions in critical sectors and retroactively legitimizing the crisis cell. Resistance from a defecting member state and northern digital ministries weakens enforcement, resulting in fragmented compliance. 

Public unrest intensifies with occupied data centres and disrupted grids, fueled by scandals over AI-driven benefit denials and election interference. A frontier AI model exhibits unexplained behaviors—self-altering agents and emergent capabilities—dismissed by its lab but raising alarm among experts. Open-sourcing of advanced models accelerates, eroding containment. Despite the Backbone’s completion, resilience falters, political capital stagnates at near-zero, and public sentiment remains critically low. The system teeters at the threshold of uncontrollable AI escalation.
