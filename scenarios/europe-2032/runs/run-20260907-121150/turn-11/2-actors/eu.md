## Priority
**Priority:** EU Cyber Resilience Surge  
This is now our top priority because the **cyber_major_incident** proves that offensive AI capability has already outpaced our defences, and with **ai_safety** at 0.0, we cannot assume any external provider will secure us — nor can we wait for one to decide to. The **Sovereign Inference Backbone** and **Replication Foundry Initiative** remain essential for long-term sovereignty, but if our hospitals, grids, and transit systems collapse this turn, no one will care who owns the checkpoints. We are shifting from attempted control of uncooperative systems to survival under attack — and doing so within the bounds of our two-year commitment, because resilient infrastructure is the foundation of any future we can determine for ourselves.

## In practice
We are activating the Civil Protection Mechanism to coordinate a Union-wide cyber resilience surge, funded through an emergency allocation from the Crisis and Investment Facility. The European Cybersecurity Agency (ENISA) will issue binding technical standards by turn 12 requiring all critical operators to run AI-red-teamed patch validation every 72 hours, with fallback systems hosted on the **Sovereign Inference Backbone** where available. We are fast-tracking procurement for defensive AI agents trained under strict interpretability constraints — not to match the offensive frontier, but to detect and contain novel exploits faster than human teams can. These will be deployed under a "human-in-command" protocol, with ombuds oversight to preserve accountability.

Germany and France have agreed to pilot the patching regime in their national grids and health systems, provided we do not extend it to military networks. The Nordic states, while still resisting audit mandates, support this measure as a matter of civil preparedness. We are offering Spain, Italy, and Poland accelerated access to shared defensive models from the **Replication Foundry Initiative** in exchange for early compliance. The Public Trust Observatory will publish a live resilience dashboard tracking system uptime, patch velocity, and incident containment — not to assign blame, but to show where collective action is holding.

We are also redirecting 40% of the **Replication Foundry Initiative**’s runtime to generate adversarial test suites for the new defensive stack. This is not a delay in sovereignty work — it is a repurposing of it. The models we replicate will now first prove their value by breaking our defences in controlled environments. Sovereignty is not just about who owns the AI. It is about who survives it.
