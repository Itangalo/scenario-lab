### Blackout and cutoff
Autumn brought a largely automated ransomware sweep through municipal systems, hospital groups and two grid operators, built with model-generated tooling. Appointments were cancelled, billing and dispatch fell back to paper, and clean-up took weeks. Attribution remained open. Defenders were visibly behind the pace of the attack code.

In the same weeks, European hospitals, ministries and firms building on the leading foreign model found access withdrawn at short notice, without reason or appeal. Queries returned refusals; contracts offered no recourse. Teams that had queued for the new multi-day agent scrambled to re-point workflows to slower European-hosted and openly available systems.

The double hit landed on services already stretched by the chip halt. The four prepared factory plots stayed guarded concrete with grid connections and no machines, while the capital that had signed its own foreign hosting deal stayed outside the joint pool.

### Triage
Through health and telecom ministers with the Union's cyber agency, emergency teams and health emergency body, Brussels funded triage: isolating the compromised dependency, distributing clean certified fallback images, and paying overtime and extra logging from the existing emergency envelope. Continuity fallback work completed this turn kept lights and wards running in degraded mode, but slower and more expensive.

A new interpretability result that lets operators certify some deployed-model behaviour was quickly folded into fallback monitoring, improving alarms and audit. A separate research advance — a materials finding from a non-European lab with longer-term industrial promise — barely registered beyond specialists.

Relief that services did not stop competed with anger over dependence. Coverage paired full waiting rooms with empty docks, and ministers arguing over export criteria and notification of bilateral deals. The recovery cell became the immediate priority; bargaining over equipment and supply stayed alive, but without machines or guaranteed hosting to offer.
