## In practice
We are activating the Critical Infrastructure AI Resilience Shield through a combination of emergency powers under Article 122 TFEU and delegated authority under the NIS2 Directive. The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) will lead technical integration, working with national CSIRTs and private operators to deploy the new AI-driven patching and swarm-detection systems to grid operators, rail networks, and major hospitals by the end of this turn. Funding of €15bn will be drawn from the Digital Europe Programme and the Recovery and Resilience Facility, with co-financing requirements waived due to urgency. A technical task force, co-chaired by ENISA and the European Defence Agency, will oversee deployment, with biweekly reporting to the Crisis Coordination Mechanism.

Meanwhile, the InvestAI Gigafactories remain on track for site finalisation, though local resistance in Brittany and northern Sweden persists. We are now offering binding energy reinvestment packages — including €500m for regional smart grid upgrades and green hydrogen production — in exchange for accelerated permitting. ENTSO-E has confirmed 12GW of reserved grid capacity, pending national energy plans, and we are fast-tracking environmental assessments under the Net-Zero Industry Act. The Tech Sovereignty Package advances through the Digital Markets Council, where we are linking accelerated permitting to enforceable commitments on EU-based operational control and energy efficiency. The legal service is finalising the Article 114 TFEU directive to harmonise permitting timelines, aiming for a June trilogue.

The European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) remains in formation. Despite the CJEU challenge to the hyperscaler levy, we are proceeding with initial staffing via secondments from national agencies and a fast-track recruitment scheme offering private-sector parity pay for critical roles in agent monitoring and interpretability. A shadow directorate has been established under ENISA to begin drafting evaluation protocols for models above 50.0 capability, with red-team scenarios focused on autonomous coordination and cyber-physical risks. We are preparing a revised funding mechanism — a temporary solidarity contribution from large digital service providers — to bypass the legal uncertainty, to be proposed if the levy remains blocked by turn 3.
