### EU Proposes Resilience Shield, But Implementation Delayed  
The European Commission proposes the Critical Infrastructure AI Resilience Shield following a defensive breakthrough in swarm-detection tooling, aiming to deploy coordinated protections across energy and health sectors. ENISA prepares technical guidelines, and early interest emerges in Germany, the Netherlands, and Finland. However, legal challenges from Poland and Hungary question the use of Article 122 TFEU for AI coordination, and no funding is secured—the Recovery and Resilience Facility remains under strain. With the EU already managing three large measures (InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty Package, European AI Assurance Directorate) and a priority, the new initiative cannot advance in this turn. It enters the portfolio pending capacity to absorb it.
