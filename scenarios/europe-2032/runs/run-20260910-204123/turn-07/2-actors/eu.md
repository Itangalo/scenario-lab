## New measure
**EU Visible Benefit Adoption Drive**
Scales the working public-sector AI deployments that cut waiting lists and decision times into a branded EU-wide rollout through health, administration and schools.
Now is the moment because adoption success is the only win attributed to a European decision amid open-weight shock and US tiering, and with sentiment at 6 and capital at 4 we must turn visible benefit into tolerance to hold the jobs and essential-services commitment.

## Priority
M7 EU Displacement Transition Fund, because queues longer than payouts and court-challenged levy mean the jobs commitment fails first if retraining cash does not flow, even as we seed the adoption drive.

## In practice
We keep M7 alive via EPSCO and ESF+ bridging: Commission advances national job centres from existing envelopes while DG EMPL defends the deployer levy in court, targeting entry clerical, customer-ops and junior coding freezes with wage-insurance plus fast retraining vouchers.

We launch the Adoption Drive via DG DIGIT and DG SANTE procurement: copy the hospital/grid models using the interpretability checklist and fallback stack hosting, funded by repurposed Digital Europe deployment lines, with mayors and health ministers fronting waiting-list results to rebuild trust. Skeleton gigafactory care-and-maintenance continues from EuroHPC JU envelopes only — no new capital call — to preserve permits and grid slots through US tier-rationing.
