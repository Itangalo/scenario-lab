### Sovereign Substitution, Slow Start

The EU’s **EU Substitute AI Initiative (EUSAI)** enters full motion, with IMEC, Fraunhofer, and Inria now staffed and sites operational. However, delays persist: chip deliveries are throttled by export bottlenecks, and recruitment falls short despite double salaries. The new **Operational Substitution Pathways Programme** launches to force adoption, mandating migration plans for national agencies using foreign AI. Yet resistance mounts—some member states argue the rules violate procurement sovereignty, and legal challenges loom.

The **Institutional Integrity Audit** concludes, revealing two member states routed sensitive data through foreign clouds. Binding remediation orders are issued, but trust erodes further. The **Sovereign Verification Regime** still lacks oncology model checkpoints; a GDPR-based legal push begins, but providers stand firm.
