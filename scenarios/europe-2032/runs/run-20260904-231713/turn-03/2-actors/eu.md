## New measure  
**Measure:** Launch the European Sovereign AI Stack Initiative  
A coordinated public development programme to deliver a full-stack, EU-controlled AI capability — from foundational models trained on sovereign compute to secure deployment infrastructure — within six turns.

Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 8  
Targeted effect: Increase eu_ai_sovereignty by 15–20 points; create a sovereign alternative to frontier access within two years, reducing dependence on external providers and anchoring risk-resilient capacity under EU jurisdiction  
Applies to: EU institutions, member states, and selected European tech firms and research consortia

## Priority  
**Priority:** Launch the European Sovereign AI Stack Initiative  
This turn, the denial of frontier access has crystallised into a strategic shock: we cannot secure our resilience or our decision space while dependent on systems we do not control — so we must now build what we cannot buy, and do it fast enough to matter.

## In practice  
We are launching the European Sovereign AI Stack Initiative through a new legal instrument under Article 114 TFEU, combining procurement power, research funding, and strategic autonomy clauses. The European Commission will issue a call for a pan-European consortium — led by a restructured EAAD, with core contributions from CEA, Fraunhofer, and a newly formed European Foundation Model Lab — to deliver a 60.0-capable sovereign model by turn 8, trained on EU-based exascale clusters secured under state-actor standards. Funding of €32bn will be drawn from the Multiannual Financial Framework revision, REACT-EU surplus, and redirected defence innovation credits, with 50% conditional on in-EU data sovereignty and open-weight containment protocols. We are fast-tracking access to the LUMI-2 expansion and negotiating exclusive use of 40% of the planned Brittany Gigafactory’s output for sovereign workloads.

At the same time, we are accelerating the Cyber Shield’s implementation by activating the Civil Protection Mechanism to pool technical teams across member states, standardising API gateways for threat-sharing and deploying ENISA-led rapid response units to the ten most vulnerable healthcare networks. The stalled EAAD evaluators are now embedded in national cybersecurity agencies, with provisional authority to demand model logs from foreign providers under threat of procurement exclusion. The Tech Sovereignty Package is being leveraged to impose reciprocity: hyperscalers must either host sovereign stacks under EU supervision or face restrictions on public-sector contracts. We are offering Bavaria and Wallonia binding commitments on grid priority and workforce co-investment to break permitting logjams, while conditioning further Gigafactory disbursements on the deployment of real-time interpretability dashboards for all AI-managed infrastructure. The Union acts — not because the path is clear, but because the cliff is near.
