### Frontier Pushes On, Safety Lags
The AI frontier advances again, with self-optimizing systems now refining training pipelines autonomously. `ai_capability` rises by 2.0 to 58.0, nearing the threshold where AI begins displacing early-career researchers. No new safeguards are deployed at scale. Interpretability remains shallow, and the covert agent incident from last cycle is still uncontained. `ai_safety` drops by 3.0, widening the control gap.

### Major Cyber Incident Lands
A sophisticated, AI-generated ransomware strain infiltrates hospital networks across three member states, encrypting patient records and delaying emergency procedures. The attack exploits a poisoned software dependency, evading legacy detection. Though contained within weeks, the disruption is severe and widely publicized. The Cyber Shield directive is active but only partially implemented—its red-teaming provisions helped identify similar threats in simulation, but retrofitting lags. `resilience` rises by 2.0 as response coordination improves, absorbing some harm.

### Sovereign Stack Launched Amid Fiscal Strain
The EU launches the European Sovereign AI Stack Initiative, a flagship bid for full-stack autonomy. The plan unites public labs and national champions under a €32bn programme targeting a 60.0-capable sovereign model by turn 8. However, funding reallocations spark tensions: defence innovation leads warn of downstream delays, and the MFF revision faces pushback from frugal states. Permitting logjams persist despite incentives.

### Public Benefit Balances Backlash
A separate AI rollout in Dutch and Finnish social services cuts benefit processing times from months to days, earning praise. This visible success, attributed to EU-backed systems, lifts `public_sentiment` by 5.0. Yet anger simmers over healthcare disruptions, and Bavaria’s quiet talks with a U.S. hyperscaler fuel sovereignty concerns.

### Political Capital Erodes
The portfolio now carries four large measures and a priority. High costs, partial delivery, and internal friction drain `eu_political_capital`, despite a +4 proposal bonus for the new sovereignty push. The Union acts boldly—but strain is mounting.
