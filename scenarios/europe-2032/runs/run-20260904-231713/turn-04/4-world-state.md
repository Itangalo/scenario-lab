### Open Release Narrows the Gap  
A leading lab releases a 59.0-capable model openly within months of its closed debut — a first. The release rapidly spreads through open research and developer communities, significantly boosting accessible capability. Combined with the emergent EU public model at 48.0, this lifts `openweight_capability` to 48.0, narrowing the gap with the frontier. However, adoption bottlenecks — including tooling fragmentation and compute access — prevent a full cascade, tempering the immediate impact and limiting the gain to +3.0 this turn.

### Cyber Shield Completes — Too Late?  
The EU Cyber Shield directive formally completes, mandating hardened detection and AI-driven red-teaming across essential services. Its delayed rollout spared no hospitals this time, but the new EU-wide Threat Simulation Facility launches under ENISA, aiming to prevent future surprises. `resilience` rises +6, reaching 48.0.

### Containment Push Meets Resistance

But Germany and Poland raise legal objections, citing free research and open-source principles. Hosting platforms balk at verification burdens. The protocol stalls in implementation, yielding no immediate safety gain.

### Sovereignty Drifts  
The Sovereign AI Stack advances in planning, but inter-state disputes delay compute allocation. No category 4 completion lands. With `ai_capability` rising 2.0, `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 14.0.
