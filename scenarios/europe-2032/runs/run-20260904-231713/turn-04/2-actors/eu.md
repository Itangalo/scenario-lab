## New measure  
**Measure:** Establish a Rapid Open-Weight Containment Protocol  
Create an emergency response framework to monitor, assess, and if necessary restrict distribution of high-capability open-weight models within the EU, triggered automatically when an open release exceeds 48.0 capability or demonstrates autonomous agent behaviour.

Category: 3 (Limits and restrictions)  
Size: small

Applies to: Open-weight model distributors, hosting platforms, and EU member states’ digital infrastructure regulators

## Priority  
**Priority:** Establish a Rapid Open-Weight Containment Protocol  
This turn, the openweight_frontier_release event forces a pivot: with a 58.0-capable system now within months of open release, and one already at 48.0 via the emergent EU model, we cannot rely on borders or goodwill — we must act now to contain what cannot be un-released, even as we build our own stack.

## In practice  
We are activating Article 28 of the AI Act through an emergency delegated act to establish the Rapid Open-Weight Containment Protocol, granting the EAAD provisional authority to issue takedown notices, require hosting platforms to verify model capability before distribution, and mandate watermarking and traceability for all models above 45.0 capability. This is not a ban — we support open science and innovation — but we cannot allow a 58.0-capable autonomous agent to spread unchecked through private GPUs across Europe. The protocol will be piloted in coordination with DE-CIX, GÉANT, and national CERTs, using signature-based detection and behavioural sandboxing to flag high-risk uploads. We are fast-tracking funding through the Digital Europe Programme and assigning a cross-institutional task force from the Commission, ENISA, and Europol to operationalise it by turn 5.

At the same time, we are pressing forward with the Sovereign AI Stack: the newly formed European Foundation Model Lab has begun federated training runs across LUMI, JUWELS, and the new Brittany cluster, with strict data provenance controls and containment protocols baked in from day one. The EAAD evaluators, now embedded in national agencies, are conducting live audits of the training pipeline, not just the final model. On resilience, we are treating the hospital ransomware attack as a wake-up call: the Cyber Shield directive finishes this turn, and we are converting its red-teaming mandate into a standing EU-wide AI Threat Simulation Facility under ENISA, funded at €1.2bn over three turns. Finally, we are leveraging the positive momentum from the Dutch and Finnish social AI rollouts to launch a public trust campaign under the Digital Europe Programme, showcasing sovereign systems in public service — because if we do not show people what we are building *for*, they will only see what we are restricting *against*.
