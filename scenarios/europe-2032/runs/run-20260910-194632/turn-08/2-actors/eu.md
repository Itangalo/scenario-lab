## New measure
**EU Transition Safety Net for AI-Displaced Workers**
It funds wage insurance, rapid retraining placement and income bridging for workers displaced by AI-driven automation, paid from reprogrammed ESF+ and employer co-contribution.
This is Labour and social protection work, and why now is that with political capital at 3 and sentiment at 22 we cannot land sovereignty or safety without first showing absorption: adjustment rather than shock is the only route back to room to act, and it serves the two-year commitment by keeping essential AI operable inside the Union without social breakdown.

Interior and labour ministries co-own it to link redress (M7 finished) to forward protection.

We hold the Gigafactory shells (M1/M2 finished) on their DG CNECT/grid-connection track — no new industrial file this turn — and keep Shield feeds and backups (M3/M6 finished) exercised via ENISA without new money, while DG TRADE keeps the jurisdiction checklist language alive but does not force a cohesion vote we would lose.
