### The Union Cracks Under Pressure

The global AI frontier remains frozen at 61.5, but the pause offers no relief. With open-weight models now matching mid-tier closed systems, the release of a powerful agent-grade model under permissive terms pushes `openweight_capability` to 55.0 — a capability now widely distributed and impossible to recall.

The EU’s attempt to shift from negotiation to construction falters under political and physical strain. The **Integrate Acquired AI Assets** measure formally completes, but its impact is hollow: no major systems were brought under EU jurisdiction, and the legal frameworks remain untested. The promised integration collapsed with the failure of bilateral talks, leaving only paper sovereignty.

The new **Baseline Compute Initiative** launches amid protests and legal challenges. Site selection in Finland and Portugal triggers local opposition, amplified by AI-generated media from the energy uprising movement. Romania delays permitting, citing national sovereignty concerns. Initial funding is secured, but physical progress stalls — no clusters are operational, and the project yields no early gains.

Meanwhile, the **Accelerate Resilience** measure, now the named priority, struggles. Emergency funding is released, but Hungary and Slovakia refuse co-financing, blocking patch deployment in critical regional grids. ENISA’s red-team exercises confirm systemic fragility: simulated attacks disrupt hospital scheduling and grid load balancing. With no category 6 measure delivering tangible results and `ai_capability` static, `resilience` falls to 22.0.

Public sentiment plummets to 20.0 as sabotage incidents escalate. Data centre occupations in Lyon and Malmö make international headlines. The government in Malmö suspends operations at one facility after a transformer fire — officially under investigation, but widely blamed on activists. The narrative shifts: AI is no longer just a job threat, but a destabilising force consuming public resources.

Political capital sinks to -3.0. The portfolio charge — two large measures (-3 each) and a priority (-1) — totals -7. No completion bonus applies, as the finished measure delivered no real effect. The new initiative earns no proposal bonus: it responds to long-simmering failures, not a recent shock. Sentiment exceeds capital, but legitimacy lift is capped by capital’s floor.

Sovereignty declines to 7.0. The **export_control_escalation** event cuts off access to advanced chips, affecting planned compute clusters. With no sovereign capacity yet online, the Union cannot substitute. Rule 5 applies: -2 for lost access. No category 4 completion bonus lands, and the in-flight measure yields no momentum.
