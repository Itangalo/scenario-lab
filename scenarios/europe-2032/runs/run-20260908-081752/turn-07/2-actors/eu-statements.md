We are therefore modifying it to include a clear off-ramp: when enforcement fails, we must act to preserve capability access through alternative means, not double down on unworkable compulsion. This is not surrender; it is strategic continuity. The goal remains European agency — but we now accept that it may have to be built around, not through, current bottlenecks.
  - Evidence quoted: Poland refused audit access, citing sovereignty concerns, and the Czech Republic declined the redirected site, fearing retaliation. The US hyperscalers stalled on operational transfer, demanding bilateral guarantees.
  - Reason: The development directly tests the EU's enforcement tools, proving its sovereignty strategy unworkable and altering the cost of maintaining the original commitment.
