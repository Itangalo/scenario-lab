## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will act decisively before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late — but we will now treat failure to compel as a signal to redirect, not reinforce, and treat market collapse as a window, not a retreat.
  - Grounds: The ai_investment_collapse and capability_plateau_evidence mark a structural shift: the frontier is no longer accelerating on capital alone, and the moment of maximum leverage may not come from strength but from others’ fragility. Our commitment to act under uncertainty must now include the discipline to see weakness as opportunity. We are not abandoning action — we are redefining courage: it is no longer just moving first, but moving when others freeze.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign levers before the frontier escapes — and ensure they are enforceable, not just declared; but when enforcement fails, pivot to alternatives without delay, and when the frontier stalls, seize the opening to lock in EU-controlled capability at fire-sale terms.
  - Grounds: The collapse in private AI investment has shattered the assumptions underpinning hyperscaler dominance. Facilities, talent, and even unfinished training runs are now available at unprecedented leverage. Waiting for a new boom would mean missing the only window to acquire capacity before the next wave of self-improving systems resets the race. Our pivot from compulsion to construction was necessary, but insufficient. We must now become acquirers, not just builders — and do so while the price is low and the sellers are desperate.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
