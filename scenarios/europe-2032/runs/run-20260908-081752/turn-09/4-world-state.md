### Stalled Frontier, Stalled Hopes

Global AI capability inched upward to 61.5, as self-improving agents made incremental gains in materials optimisation and supply chain coordination. However, the broader trend of stagnation held: no major model breakthroughs, falling compute prices, and quiet attrition at top labs suggested the frontier is consolidating, not accelerating. With no safety upgrades deployed, and no interpretability improvements in sight, `ai_safety` remained stagnant at 13.0, reflecting systemic opacity and eroding developer accountability.

The EU closed its Redirect Compute Access initiative, having secured permitting wins in Romania and Spain. These sites are now designated as intake hubs for future hardware, but no physical systems have yet arrived. The Emergency Acquisition of Stranded AI Capacity remained blocked: Swiss alignment researchers refused transfer, citing academic independence, and the London lab’s ownership dispute entered arbitration. No assets changed hands.

In response, the EU launched a new large measure — Integrate Acquired AI Assets — aimed at establishing legal and technical control over future acquisitions. However, with no assets yet secured, the effort began in abstraction. Early resistance emerged from potential partners, who viewed the integration framework as overly prescriptive. The Dublin hyperscaler rejected the proposed oversight model, demanding continued autonomous operation.

`resilience` fell to 26.0 as cyber preparedness lagged and energy disruptions persisted. With `ai_capability` rising, and no category 6 measure in force, the metric declined under rule 4.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` held at 12.0: Redirect Compute Access finished (+5 expected), but no capacity event occurred, and the Emergency Acquisition remained in flight with no tangible gain. The +5 from completion was offset by the −1 decay from capability growth and the absence of integration progress, leaving net change neutral.

`eu_political_capital` dropped to 2.0. The portfolio charge was −7 (Redirect Compute Access −3, Emergency Acquisition −3, priority −1). Redirect Compute Access finished, granting +3. No proposal bonus applied, as the new measure did not respond to a recent shock. With `public_sentiment` (32.0) above capital, a +1 legitimacy lift applied. Net: −7 +3 +1 = −3, bringing capital from 3.0 to 2.0.
