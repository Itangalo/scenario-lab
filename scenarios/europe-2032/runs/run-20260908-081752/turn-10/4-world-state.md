### Stagnation and Exclusion

Global AI capability remains at 61.5, with evidence mounting that the frontier has plateaued: top-tier model prices have dropped, and a major release failed to meet internal benchmarks. Yet stagnation brings no relief. The EU was abruptly cut off from the leading AI model, disrupting hospitals, ministries, and firms reliant on its inference API. No explanation was given, and appeals were ignored — a stark reminder of external dependence.

With `ai_safety` already at a perilous 13.0 and no safety upgrades in sight, the gap between capability and control widened. The denial of access, combined with systemic opacity and no visible progress on interpretability, triggered a −5 point drop, bringing `ai_safety` to 8.0.

The EU formally concluded its Emergency Acquisition of Stranded AI Capacity, but secured no assets. The London lab remains in legal limbo, Swiss researchers hold firm, and Dublin’s hyperscaler rejected integration terms. A counteroffer for autonomous operation in Cork was refused. The EU instead proposed phased integration with audit rights and security mandates under Article 356 TFEU, but no agreement emerged.

In response, the Union launched a large category 6 measure — Accelerate Resilience of Critical Infrastructure — aiming to harden energy, health, and municipal systems against AI-enabled threats. Fast-tracked funding and red-team exercises were initiated, but implementation is only beginning. No resilience gain is realised yet; instead, with `ai_capability` unchanged but no active category 6 measure completed, resilience fell by 1 point to 25.0 due to ongoing vulnerabilities.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` declined to 9.0: the finished category 4 measure yielded no gain (no assets acquired), the in-flight integration effort contributed nothing tangible, and the `eu_frontier_access_denied` event cost −3. The capability rise of 0.0 spared the −1 decay, but the access shock was severe.

`eu_political_capital` fell to 0.0. Portfolio charge: −7 (two large measures, one priority). No completion bonus (Emergency Acquisition yielded no strategic gain). No proposal bonus — the new measure, though urgent, did not respond to a recent incident within three turns. Sentiment (29.0) now exceeds capital, but capital cannot rise above sentiment, so no legitimacy lift applied. The Union is out of political room.

Public sentiment dipped to 29.0, as the access denial was framed domestically as a failure of foresight, not external aggression, and protests against AI energy use grew.
