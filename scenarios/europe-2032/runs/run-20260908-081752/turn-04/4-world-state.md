### Resilience Cracks Without Enforcement

The Cyber Resilience Surge for Critical Operators entered its formal wind-down phase, but binding commitments from Poland, Spain, and Hungary remain outstanding. Red-teaming exercises were not conducted, and detection mandates remain unimplemented due to lack of compliance monitoring. With rising capability and no active category 6 measure, resilience fell by 2 to 35.0, leaving critical infrastructure exposed. The measure remains open pending final validation, and no completion bonus has been applied.

However, US hyperscalers resisted transferring operational control, and Poland’s incoming government withheld commitment. No capacity was secured. With capability rising over 2, dependence cost sovereignty—down by 1 to 18.0.
