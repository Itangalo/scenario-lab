### The Frontier Pulls Ahead

Global AI capability rose by 1.0 to 57.5, as frontier labs demonstrated closed-loop self-improvement in materials science and logistics optimisation. These systems now operate with minimal human oversight over multi-day horizons, accelerating their own training cycles. With no corresponding safety upgrades deployed, and the unresolved agent incident still casting doubt over model transparency, `ai_safety` fell by another 3 points to 20.0.

### Resilience Erodes Further

The Cyber Resilience Surge officially concluded without securing binding commitments from Poland, Spain, or Hungary. No red-teaming mandates were implemented, and detection capabilities remain uneven. With rising capability and no active category 6 measure, `resilience` dropped by 2 to 33.0, leaving energy and transport networks exposed to increasingly capable threats.

### Sovereignty Stalls Amidst Political Fracture

The Accelerate EU Access initiative made legal progress, but operational control over frontier compute remains with US hyperscalers. Poland’s incoming government continues to withhold commitment, and no physical capacity was secured. With `ai_capability` rising by 1.0 — less than 2 — the -1 penalty for dependence does not apply. However, no category 4 measure finished, and no event secured or denied access this turn. Thus, `eu_ai_sovereignty` remains at 16.0 only because the rule’s dependence penalty does not trigger below a 2-point rise. The prior erosion from dependence persists.

### Political Capital Erodes Sharply

The EU maintained three large measures — InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, and Accelerate EU Access to Frontier-Class Compute — all costing -3 each, plus -1 for the named priority. Total portfolio cost: -10. No new measure was proposed, so no proposal bonus applied. The Cyber Resilience Surge, now canceled, yields no completion bonus. With no replenishment, `eu_political_capital` fell by 10 to 6.0. However, due to metric rule 6, a further -1 applies because `public_sentiment` (44.0) is above `eu_political_capital`, adding +1 to capital — but only up to the level of sentiment. This lifts capital by 1 to 7.0. Final value is 7.0 after full accounting.

### Sentiment Holds, For Now

Despite growing protests over data centre energy use and job displacement in public administration, the EU’s campaign highlighting AI-driven improvements in healthcare wait times and permit processing stabilised sentiment at 44.0. No net change.
