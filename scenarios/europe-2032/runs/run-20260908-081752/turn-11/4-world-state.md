### Stalled Frontier, Shrinking Options

Global AI capability remains frozen at 61.5, with top-tier model prices continuing to fall — a sign the frontier has stalled. Yet stagnation offers no reprieve. The gap between what systems can do and whether they can be trusted widens unchecked, leaving `ai_safety` stagnant at 8.0 despite mounting risks.

With open-weight models now widely available at near-frontier performance, developers quietly release a powerful agent-grade model under permissive terms. Its capabilities align closely with those previously confined to closed labs, pushing `openweight_capability` to 55.0 — halfway between its prior value and the plateaued frontier.

The EU’s defensive push continues. Red-team exercises expose critical flaws in hospital IT systems and regional grid operators, but patching lags. Fast-tracked funding faces delays as member states dispute cost-sharing, and only 30% of Tier 1 operators have deployed mandated MFA upgrades. Resilience erodes further, falling to 24.0, as `ai_capability` remains static but no category 6 measure delivers tangible results.

On the sovereignty front, negotiations for binding access agreements stall. Switzerland rebuffs overtures, citing neutrality and legal incompatibility. Ireland demands veto power over EU audit triggers, calling current proposals “coercive integration.” Nordic states express interest but insist on bilateral terms, fracturing the proposed joint framework. No progress is made, and the new large category 4 measure yields no early gains.

Public sentiment slips to 27.0 as protests against AI energy use intensify. Data centre disruptions in Lyon and Malmö make headlines, and a leaked ENISA report warning of “uncontained escalation pathways” fuels anxiety. The resilience effort, though urgent, is seen as reactive — another promise unfulfilled.

Political capital remains at 0.0. The portfolio charge holds: two large measures (−3 each) and a priority (−1) total −7. No completion bonus applies. The new measure, while strategic, does not respond directly to a recent incident within three turns, earning no proposal bonus. Sentiment exceeds capital, but legitimacy lift is capped by capital’s floor.

### Fragmentation Deepens

Behind closed doors, mid-sized member states accelerate talks with Japan and Canada on mutual compute access. Trust in central coordination frays. The American posture of consolidation tightens informal restrictions, making fallback options harder to secure.
