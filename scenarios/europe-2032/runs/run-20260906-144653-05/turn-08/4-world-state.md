### Strategic Leap, Political Toll

The European Commission launches the **Sovereign AI Compute Corridor**, a landmark initiative to anchor exascale AI infrastructure under irrevocable EU control. Invoking the Strategic Autonomy Clause, it designates three anchor sites in France, Germany, and the Nordic bloc, backed by €45 billion in repurposed funds. The EuroHPC JU is mandated to transform into a sovereign operator, but full restructuring will require trilogues with Parliament and cannot be completed before turn 9. Legal and staffing constraints delay operational capacity; no new compute comes online this turn. The measure is high-cost and now formally underway, contributing to political burden.

### Open Frontier Widens, Safety Erodes

An open-weight model closes significant ground on the closed frontier, downloaded globally within days. This advance lifts **openweight_capability** moderately, reflecting rapid diffusion and optimization of leaked weights, but it remains behind the frontier’s pace. With **ai_capability** rising to 62.5, the gap narrows from 14.5 to 14.5 points — consistent with accelerated catch-up, not overtaking. No catastrophic leak or breakthrough event occurs; progress stems from distributed refinement. Oversight continues to lag, and the absence of deployed safety countermeasures allows erosion, but no singular failure dominates. Combined with capability pressure, this results in a measured decline in **ai_safety**.

### Public Trust Collapses

Mass protests erupt after an election is annulled due to synthetic media manipulation. Cities see sustained unrest, with AI framed as an existential threat to democracy. Data centre attacks intensify, now coordinated with alarming precision. **Public_sentiment** crashes as fear of uncontrolled AI converges with anger over dependency and democratic erosion.

### Sovereignty Unchanged, Capital Erodes

Despite the ambitious launch, **eu_ai_sovereignty** holds steady. No new compute comes online, and dependence persists. The **openweight_frontier_release** and systemic brittleness maintain the decay pressure from prior turns. **Resilience** gains no ground — the Cyber Shield is implemented, but no new category 6 measure finishes. The **Sovereign AI Compute Corridor** begins mobilization, assessed as a large measure with delayed effect; its political cost is partially realized this turn at −3, reflecting preparatory strain and portfolio load. No proposal bonus applies: the measure answers emerging threats, but no qualifying event from the last three turns justifies a bonus. Sentiment remains below capital, so no legitimacy lending occurs.
