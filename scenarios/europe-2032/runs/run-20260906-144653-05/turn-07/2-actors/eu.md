## In practice
This turn, we finalise the adoption of the **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield** directive in the Internal Market and Consumer Protection Council (IMCO), securing qualified majority support after securing liability safeguards and co-funding commitments from the Recovery and Resilience Facility. Germany and Poland formally endorse the directive, with grid operators in both countries committing to pilot the ENISA red-team scenarios by the end of the year. ENISA publishes the first standardised attack library, based on the hospital ransomware campaign, and launches the first cross-border simulation exercise involving energy and health sectors in six member states. The directive is now legally binding, with full compliance required by turn 9.

Simultaneously, we capitalise on the **joint_threat_response** opening by initiating emergency trilogues with the European Parliament and the US Department of Homeland Security to establish the **Joint Resilience Integration Protocol**. The legal basis will be Article 114 TFEU combined with a new international agreement under Article 218, allowing for binding data-sharing and operational protocols. We offer US partners access to EU anomaly detection patterns from the Cyber Shield pilots in exchange for real-time telemetry from their AI threat monitoring systems. The protocol will be implemented through a new Joint Operations Cell hosted by ENISA, with standing authorisation to activate cross-border failover and dispatch response teams during declared incidents.

With the **SEAL** initiative complete and the **Tech sovereignty package** and **European Evaluation Shield** concluded, we pause new industrial and regulatory launches to consolidate gains. The **InvestAI Gigafactories** programme ends this turn with three sites fully permitted and two in early construction, but private investment remains below target. We do not renew or expand it now, awaiting assessment of delivery timelines and market uptake. Political capital remains constrained at 24.0, and public sentiment is fragile at 35.0, so we focus on delivering integration and resilience — not new promises.
