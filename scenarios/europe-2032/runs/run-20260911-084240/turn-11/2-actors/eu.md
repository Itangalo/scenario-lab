## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for the wage-bridge payments to actually land and for fiscal headroom to rebuild before starting anything new.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
Targeted Transition Income and Tier-One Retraining for Checkable Work, because with capital at 4.0 and sentiment at 1.0 no second front can be opened without killing the one measure that ties AI to jobs and bills.

## In practice
We run what is on the books through DG EMPL and national PES offices: reprogrammed ESF+ envelopes into 6-12 month wage-bridge for displaced tier-one coding, analysis, drafting and support staff, paired with short courses into cyber-hygiene, care-tech, grid-tech and human-check roles. The fight this half-year is disbursement speed and the automation-levy backlash — we frame the levy as flexicurity contribution, cap it, and push advance payments to municipalities where take-up is fastest.

At the same time we hold finished stocks without a new instrument: ENISA/CERT-EU push the Shield playbook for the poisoned monitoring-tool compromise, TSOs keep the shedding order warm, and we seek pragmatic chip-licence accommodation while municipal IT uses recall-proof open weights for cleanup. Messaging shifts from endurance to protection delivered: payments in accounts, clinics and ports rebuilt, hospitals and water held.
