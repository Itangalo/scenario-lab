## New measure
**Targeted Transition Income and Tier-One Retraining for Checkable Work**
Pays wage-bridge and fast retraining to workers displaced in routine coding, analysis, drafting and tier-one support, funded by a levy on automating employers.

This and now because labour displacement is real, narrow and entry-level, and with sentiment at 2.0 and export controls tightening, a visible jobs answer is the precondition for any sovereignty or resilience effort. It is category 7 Labour and social protection, small and executable through ESF+ and national PES without new builds.

## Priority
Targeted Transition Income and Tier-One Retraining for Checkable Work, because without stemming job-loss anger and bill anger no cyber-hardening, compute or control measure can be implemented.

## In practice
We run this through EMPL Council and ESF+: DG EMPL reprograms existing social envelopes into wage insurance for 6-12 months for displaced tier-one staff, paired with short PES-delivered retraining into cyber-hygiene, care-tech, grid-tech and human-check roles. Employers above a threshold of automation-related separations contribute to the transition fund — framed as flexicurity, not punishment — to blunt Council resistance.

At the same time we hold the finished continuity and islanding stock in place with no new instrument: ENISA/CERT-EU push the dependency-compromise playbook from the Shield, TSOs keep the load-shedding sequence warm, and we seek volume-licence chip access pragmatically while open weights do the municipal work we cannot recall. Daily technical notes continue, but the message shifts from rationing endured to jobs protected.
