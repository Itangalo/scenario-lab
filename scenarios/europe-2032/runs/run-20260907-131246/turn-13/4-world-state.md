### Sovereignty Deferred

The EU announces the Sovereign Compute Backbone as a proposed Crisis Infrastructure Project under Article 122 TFEU, aiming to repurpose military energy grids in France, Sweden, and Poland. Initial funding discussions begin through the European Peace Facility, with expectations of national co-financing. However, legal anchoring via a binding Council regulation meets immediate resistance from Germany and Austria, who denounce the override of national energy sovereignty. The proposal enters intergovernmental negotiation, with no agreement expected before mid-2033. No implementation begins this turn; the measure is registered but delayed.
