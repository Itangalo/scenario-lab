### Safety at a Standstill

Despite the technical foundation being in place, the new AI Safety Assurance Infrastructure faces immediate legal and political headwinds. Germany and Austria challenge the scope of Article 122 emergency powers used to authorize real-time intervention, demanding stricter parliamentary oversight and narrower triggers. While Finland and Norway offer red-team collaboration, Japan holds back on mirroring enforcement, awaiting clarity on audit standards. No foreign developer submits to the full weight-access regime, and no system is interrupted during the first half of 2031. The measure begins but yields no tangible safety gains this turn.
