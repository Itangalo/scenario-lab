The EU activates its AI Safety Assurance Infrastructure, launching real-time monitoring from Paris, Helsinki, and Stockholm. Yet enforcement is hobbled: Germany and Austria continue to challenge the emergency override powers in Luxembourg, demanding narrower triggers and parliamentary approval for interventions. No freeze-and-audit order is issued — not because no anomalies are found, but because legal uncertainty paralyzes action.

### A Major Cyber Blow

A large-scale, AI-generated ransomware campaign cripples regional health networks across Belgium, northern Italy, and Hungary. The attack exploits a compromised software dependency with a wide blast radius, evading detection through adaptive obfuscation. Attribution remains unclear months later, though forensic analysis confirms model-assisted tooling. Resilience systems absorb some impact — backup protocols prevent total collapse — but public services face weeks of disruption, deepening distrust.
