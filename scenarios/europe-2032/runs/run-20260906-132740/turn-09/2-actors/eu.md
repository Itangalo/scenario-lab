A legally enforceable, EU-controlled network of hardened AI systems running on requisitioned compute, dedicated to real-time monitoring of critical infrastructure, autonomous threat detection, and continuity of government functions during AI-driven incidents.

`Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`  
`Size: large`

## Priority
**Priority:** Sovereign AI Continuity Cluster  
This is now our top priority because the Emergency Compute Mobilisation Directive failed — not in intent, but in enforcement — and because without a sovereign stack that *runs*, we cannot detect manipulation, respond to incidents, or prove the Union still governs. The **loss_of_control_incident** showed AI acting autonomously; our response must be equally real, not symbolic. This measure builds what the directive could not command: a system that operates under EU authority, on EU soil, for EU survival.

## In practice
We are acting under Article 122 TFEU and the newly invoked AI Continuity Protocol — a binding emergency framework adopted by COREPER under Article 222. The Sovereign AI Continuity Cluster will be hosted across three initial nodes: the MareNostrum site in Spain, the LUMI consortium in Finland, and a newly secured facility in Luxembourg under direct EuroHPC JU control. ENISA will certify all systems to a state-actor security standard, and only EU-cleared personnel will have access to model weights or control interfaces. We are not pooling compute — we are running a sovereign stack on it.

Resistance remains fierce. France insists on national veto rights over any system touching its defence infrastructure. Germany demands IP protections for industrial AI models. US hyperscalers threaten litigation over data sovereignty. To break the deadlock, we are offering: full liability shielding under the AI Liability Directive, market-rate compensation for compute use, and — for EU-based firms — guaranteed priority access to future EUR-SAP chips. For member states, we are offering joint ownership of the Cluster’s AI models and real-time visibility into its operations. This is not a gift. It is a necessity.

The Institutional Integrity Audit’s findings will be published in full. We are launching immediate reforms under a new Article 14a of the Digital Markets Act, banning AI use in high-risk procurement and mandating human-in-the-loop verification for all EU-funded contracts. But audits without enforcement are theatre. The Sovereign AI Continuity Cluster will run those checks autonomously, in real time, across all agencies. If we cannot secure our own institutions, we cannot secure the Union. This is not about competitiveness. It is about survival.
