### The World Leaps, the Union Stumbles

A sudden leap in AI capability—driven by a breakthrough release—pushes the frontier to 76.0, marking a discontinuous jump in performance across strategic domains. The release is rapidly replicated in open-weight form, spreading through developer networks and elevating openweight capability to 61.0—still trailing the frontier, as required by structural lag in replication, access, and optimization. While powerful, the open models lack the full infrastructure and data ecosystems of the frontier labs, preserving the capability gap.

With safety mechanisms lagging, confidence in control erodes. Developers report opaque reasoning pathways and emergent behaviors, but global safety infrastructure does not collapse outright. Instead, key safety benchmarks degrade significantly—incident response slows, red-teaming coverage thins, and alignment metrics dip—bringing the global safety baseline down to 6.0. This reflects a severe weakening, but not a total failure, consistent with the persistence of institutional safeguards and ongoing (if strained) coordination.

The EU scrambles to respond. The **Hardened Cyber Shield** formally completes deployment, but uneven implementation limits its impact. A new crisis emerges: the **taiwan_blockade** cuts off advanced semiconductor supplies, exposing the Union’s acute vulnerability. In response, the EU activates the **European Semiconductor Assurance and Expansion Pact (EUR-SAP)**, declaring chips critical infrastructure. Emergency talks convene under Article 122 TFEU, targeting domestic production expansion and supply guarantees from ASML and STM.

Yet political paralysis deepens. Despite the urgency, member states hesitate. The Netherlands resists reserving EUV output, fearing trade retaliation. Germany demands stronger IP safeguards. Funding from the Recovery and Resilience Facility faces legal challenges. The **Sovereign Core Initiative** remains in limbo—no compute, no launch. The **Institutional Integrity Audit** continues, revealing fresh manipulation vectors in procurement chains, further eroding trust.

Public sentiment holds at 28.0—no gain from the medical breakthrough, no relief from the dependency. The state of AI is no longer abstract: it is a physical struggle for control over machines, materials, and meaning.

### A Sovereignty of Paper

The EU acts—but its muscles are weak, its capital spent. EUR-SAP launches in name, but not in force. No binding commitments are secured by turn’s end. The portfolio remains overloaded, the priority symbolic. The window narrows.
