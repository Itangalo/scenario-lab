EU pivoted to middle-power blocs — joint export-licence lists, pooled compute, access bargaining. ENISA wartime playbooks kept power/water; hospitals/ports degraded but running: continuity without sovereignty.

New open release matching closed frontier spread to hundreds of thousands of machines in days, unrecallable/unlicensable.
