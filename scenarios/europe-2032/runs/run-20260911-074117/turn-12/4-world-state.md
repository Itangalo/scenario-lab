### Under fire
By February American and Chinese forces were exchanging strikes on fabrication plants, cables, satellites and data centres. Brussels did not choose a side so much as discover geography: fibre landings, fabs and power for compute sat beside targets. Insurance across the Strait hardened into blockade risk, chip deliveries slipped again, and procurement built on licensed American models wobbled.

The Union bolted itself to other middle powers holding chokepoints — joint export-licence lists, pooled compute, shared bargaining over access. ENISA and emergency teams switched to wartime playbooks: backup routing, sheltered allocations for hospitals, water and ports. Power and water held, clinics and harbours degraded but kept running. It was continuity, not sovereignty.

### Loose weights, sharp biology
A new open release landed within months of the closed frontier and spread to hundreds of thousands of machines in days, including across Europe. What it could do — sustained coding and research work — could no longer be recalled, logged, or meaningfully licensed.

At the same time a contested genome-model study claimed a non-expert path to a human-capable pathogen. Methodologists quarrelled, biosecurity specialists did not sleep. Brussels froze health procurement to audited laboratory paths and pushed detection through its health emergency bodies, without a new programme.

### Redress completed, courts overflow
The welfare redress drive finished its formal work: frozen fraud scores, human re-decisions, arrears paid, logs published. Thousands cleared, tens of thousands still queued — and then judges took over. Courts citing the ombudsman findings ordered mass human re-review of benefit, policing and hiring decisions. The rollout paralysis the Commission had tried to manage became a litigation wave it had to staff.

Transition Corps teams held care and permit desks where they existed. Elsewhere hiring stayed frozen, graduate anger hardened, and data-centre protests found a wartime vocabulary. Cure on foreign machines, harm stamped at home, now war overhead: the public read was not resilience but exposure.
