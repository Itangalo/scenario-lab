By February US-China strikes on fabs, cables, satellites and data centres made Europe geography, not a side: insurance hardened, chips slipped, US-model procurement wobbled.

EU pivoted to middle-power blocs — joint licences, pooled compute, access bargaining. ENISA playbooks kept power/water; hospitals/ports degraded but running.

Unrecallable open release matching closed frontier spread to hundreds of thousands of machines; contested genome-model study prompted freeze to audited lab paths and detection push.

Welfare redress formally finished but courts ordered mass human re-review of benefit/policing/hiring decisions, creating litigation queue and rollout paralysis. Transition Corps held limited desks; junior hiring frozen amid war protests.

New turn: leased foreign humanoid/service robots — Chinese bodies, US stacks — arrived at ports and care homes, triggering second dismissal of maintenance/logistics/care workers, voucher anger, jobs-to-arming protests after weaponised-unit footage. Frontier labs made sharp general-competence jump to self-tooling continuous research systems safety teams could not follow; Brussels got US summaries not weights, while lagging open copy ran multi-day unlogged work EU-wide. Council held line with backup routing, satellite fallback, sheltered power/chips; supply pact bought winter time as deliveries slipped. Response began as physical-labour shield — Corps extended to maintenance, buy-only-serviceable-in-Europe rule, logistics/care hardening — but re-review queues swamped the same staff.
