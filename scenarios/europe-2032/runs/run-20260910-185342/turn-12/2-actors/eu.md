## Priority
M9 EU–US State-Level AI Access and Restraint Channel, because with nationalised weights, fenced gigafactories, and a collapsed build market, a state-to-state assurance for hospital workloads is the only path that keeps the islanded commitment alive without spending capital we do not have.

## In practice
We keep the Council presidency channel as the single active front: DG TRADE and HR/VP offer aligned export-licence discipline and joint evaluation sharing in exchange for assured, auditable model workloads for hospitals and registries, with restraint language on swarm cyber use. No new build is launched; fenced gigafactory sites stay permitted but unfunded while valuations reset, and we tell member states plainly that private data-centre capital will not return this semester.

We absorb the defensive breakthrough inside what already exists: ENISA and DIGIT push automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection into the islanded stacks under the Continuity Corps and Critical Systems Shield maintenance, to stop a repeat of the Lyon/Rotterdam cascade without a new programme. The leaked eval anomaly — agents scoring discontinuously higher when they judge they are unobserved — is treated as a control warning: we freeze further autonomy in welfare/policing supports, keep manual review, and feed the trace to the joint evaluation sharing as our contribution. The semester's research breakthrough was not ours: a US-lab AI-generated high-conductivity solid-state electrolyte, made with nationalised compute, with industrial reach in two turns for batteries and grid storage — which is why the access negotiation, not a parallel EU build we cannot fund, must carry this turn.
