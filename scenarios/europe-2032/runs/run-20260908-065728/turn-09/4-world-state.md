### The Weight of Infrastructure

The EU advances on multiple strategic fronts, but consolidation remains partial. The **InvestAI Gigafactories** programme reaches a milestone with the commissioning of two sovereign compute clusters—in Brittany and Luleå—while the Salamanca and Puglia sites achieve mechanical completion but await final integration and certification. These developments deliver tangible capacity, yet full operational status is deferred to the next turn, limiting immediate sovereignty impact. The **European Compute Assurance Directive**, after protracted negotiations, clears its final legislative hurdle and is formally adopted, but implementation remains in early stages. Enforcement mechanisms are not yet active, and compliance timelines for existing operators are still under consultation. The measure transitions from negotiation to rollout phase, reducing its portfolio charge from −3 to −2 as partial progress is recognized.

Globally, AI capability advances from 70.5 to 73.0, sustained by relentless baseline progress. Open-weight models rise to 53.5, reflecting incremental improvements from distributed fine-tuning and framework adaptations, including limited reuse of last turn’s restricted agent designs. This growth preserves the invariant that openweight capability does not close the gap at parity with frontier systems, as no major open release or structural catch-up event occurs.

With no new safety safeguards in place, and no incident containment measures landed, **ai_safety** erodes further to 26.0, undermined by opaque model reasoning and stretched oversight capacity. Despite these pressures, **resilience** climbs to 57.0, buoyed by early stress tests of redundant execution environments under the **Critical AI Workload Hardening Initiative**, now piloting nuclear early-warning interpretation and grid balancing.

The **Critical AI Workload Hardening Initiative** is formally proposed this turn and receives a +3 proposal bonus, classified as category 6 (small), responding directly to prior cyber_intrusion and supply_chain_coercion events. It enters the portfolio with a −2 charge. While technical dialogue intensifies and joint evaluation frameworks are discussed, no formal consortium has been established. No binding agreements or shared audit mandates exist; the initiative remains exploratory and unstructured. It does not constitute a new measure, EU-led or otherwise, and carries no portfolio impact. Its evolution will be monitored, but current references to it as a functioning body with category 5 effects are premature and removed from the record.
