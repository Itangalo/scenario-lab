## In practice  
The cyber intrusion remains the defining operational failure of this period: systems were compromised not through brute force, but through subtle, persistent access that evaded all existing monitoring. Confidence in our critical infrastructure is now tethered to visible action. I am directing the Commission to finalise the Cyber Shield Directive by June 2027, using Article 114 TFEU to justify harmonisation. ENISA will be empowered to conduct unannounced audits and mandate AI-powered intrusion detection systems based on adaptive behavioural baselines, not just signature matching. To overcome resistance from smaller member states, I am releasing €6bn from the unallocated portion of the Recovery and Resilience Facility, conditional on national co-financing of 30%, to cover detection upgrades in water and energy sectors.

Simultaneously, the InvestAI Gigafactories are facing legal challenges over grid access in Poland and environmental concerns in Bavaria. I am instructing the Vice-President for Energy to invoke Article 122 TFEU (emergency energy security) to fast-track grid connections, framing the AI compute build-out as essential infrastructure. This legal stretch invites scrutiny, but the combination of rising capability and exposed vulnerabilities justifies it. The Tech Sovereignty Package is now in trilogue, where southern member states demand stronger regional reinvestment clauses; I am offering a binding side letter on job guarantees and access to the future EDIC cloud, funded from private investor commitments.

Public sentiment has dipped to 40.0, and the anomaly leak — though not public — has intensified internal alarm. I am therefore advancing the new measure under Category 5: a public anomaly detection network, to be hosted by a consortium of national AI safety institutes under EASA-style oversight. This is not a reversal of our two-year commitment, but its necessary extension: sovereignty without detection is infrastructure without eyes. The network will begin operations in Q3 2027 with pilot access to two national labs and red-team funding from Horizon Europe. While this measure is new, it serves the same foundation: we cannot contain risk we cannot see, and we cannot determine our future if others define what is safe.
