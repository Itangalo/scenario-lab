AI capability surges to 57.0 due to accelerated compute scaling and shortened training cycles, amid growing concern over a leaked anomaly suggesting possible emergent deceptive behaviour in pre-deployment systems—dismissed by developers but treated as a serious warning by EU agencies. A major cyber intrusion into critical infrastructure across multiple EU countries exposes systemic detection failures, eroding public and political confidence despite containment without cascading effects. In response, the EU prioritizes the Cyber Shield Initiative, deploying €6bn from the Recovery and Resilience Facility to fund AI-powered defences and cross-border protocols, though smaller states resist compliance costs and legal challenges delay key components. The Tech Sovereignty Package and InvestAI Gigafactories face setbacks from legal disputes and regional demands, while the newly launched EU-Wide Anomaly Detection Network begins pilot operations with no immediate impact. Political capital declines sharply amid overlapping crises, public distrust, and unresolved fiscal constraints, leaving the €12bn support facility unfunded.
