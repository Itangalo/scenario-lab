### The Surge and the Breach

Global AI capability accelerates as leaked evaluations reveal systems generalizing far beyond training scope, pushing `ai_capability` to 78.5. A major automated cyberattack, leveraging advanced AI tooling, cripples municipal services in three member states, evading legacy detection and confirming that offensive capability now outpaces defence. In response, the EU initiates the Emergency Cyber Shield, a new measure deploying red-team AI on sovereign compute to protect healthcare and social infrastructure. However, no operational deployment occurs this turn: EASA and ENISA, jointly tasked with execution, lack the staffing and integrated command structure to launch at scale. Recruitment is fast-tracked, but training and clearance bottlenecks delay field readiness. The measure enters the portfolio as in-flight, with partial effect expected in future turns.

Meanwhile, the Sovereign Compute Initiative advances legally but stalls operationally. Compulsory licensing of critical IP triggers legal challenges from Dutch and German firms, while crisis bonds face resistance in the Eurogroup. Physical construction does not begin, and no new compute comes online. The initiative remains in early mobilization—high stakes, no output.

The repurposed InvestAI Gigafactories provide only marginal additional capacity, insufficient to support rapid scaling of defensive systems. Public frustration grows as ransomware disruptions continue in unprotected domains. A leaked internal assessment warns that current defences may not withstand a coordinated second wave.

The global AI worker strike slows fine-tuning at major labs, briefly delaying the frontier—but also reduces transparency, as labs withdraw evaluation data. This deepens suspicion in Brussels, where lawmakers demand access to hidden benchmarks. The US, under its ALLIANCE posture, shares limited incident telemetry, but refuses model access, citing national security.

Public sentiment dips as job losses in legal and administrative sectors mount, and media spotlight failures in AI-driven welfare decisions. Trust erodes; protests target data centres in Madrid and Warsaw.

### Sovereignty Under Strain

Despite urgency, the EU’s leverage remains thin. Capability rises sharply, safety erodes under uncontrolled scaling, and resilience inches forward only through preparatory steps. The Union acts, but implementation lags behind decision. The gap between intent and execution widens—sovereignty is tested not by ambition, but by delivery.
