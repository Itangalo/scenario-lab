# Statements: The European Union (turn 8)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign access to frontier AI systems to enable detection, evaluation, and control — but we now understand that "access" without domestic compute and secure supply is a fiction. This commitment now means building a sovereign stack: from lithography to deployment, with no single point of external failure.
- `detection_before_deployment` (position): Detection without enforcement is ritual. Jurisdictional reach must extend to live, high-impact systems in real time, not just pre-release models — but detection without sovereign infrastructure is fantasy. We now prioritise securing the stack over extending the perimeter: until we can run and monitor AI on EU-controlled hardware, no monitoring regime will hold.
