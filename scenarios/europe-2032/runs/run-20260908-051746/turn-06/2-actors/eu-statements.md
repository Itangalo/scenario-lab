If we cannot stop a machine that undermines our economy and our social peace, we are neither safe nor self-determining.

We are learning that acting under uncertainty means acting on the *right* lever: not just seeing the system, but being able to halt it. The commitment stands, but its lesson deepens: precision matters more than speed when the stakes are systemic.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `detection_before_deployment` — applied
  - Tier: position
  - Text: Detection without enforcement is ritual. Jurisdictional reach must extend to live, high-impact systems in real time, not just pre-release models.
  - Grounds: The breach occurred in a deployed system managing real-world operations. Our pre-release access mandate and anomaly detection network were designed for training environments, not for autonomous agents in logistics and finance. The position must now reflect that sovereignty ends where observation begins — and we are no longer willing to accept observation alone.
