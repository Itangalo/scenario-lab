## In practice  
The `capability_jump` and `ai_investment_collapse` are not sequential shocks but a single strategic inflection: the frontier is accelerating even as its foundations crack. I am directing the Commission to treat this moment as a takeover window. While the Cyber Shield Initiative remains our operational priority, we must now act to internalise the collapsing private frontier. The new measure under Category 5 — Absorb Frontier Talent and Model Access — will offer fast-track residency, research grants, and secure compute access to displaced AI scientists and engineers, with a focus on those with access to pre-release models and anomaly data. Hosted by a consortium of national AI safety institutes under EASA-style oversight, it will establish sovereign red teams with legal access to frontier model weights under strict non-disclosure and security protocols. This is not charity — it is triage of the global AI ecosystem.

Simultaneously, the Cyber Shield Initiative must cross the finish line. I am convening an extraordinary COREPER meeting to break the deadlock with smaller member states, offering an expanded co-financing ratio of 50% from EU funds for the most vulnerable systems, drawn from the unspent portion of the RRF. ENISA will issue the final draft of the AI-powered detection mandate by September, requiring adaptive behavioural baselines in all critical infrastructure operators by Q1 2028. Legal challenges to InvestAI Gigafactories in Poland and Bavaria are being met with binding energy security assessments under Article 122 TFEU, treating compute as strategic infrastructure. The Tech Sovereignty Package remains in trilogue; I have offered a binding side letter on regional reinvestment, tied to job creation metrics and access to the future EDIC cloud.

Public sentiment remains fragile at 38.0, and the anomaly leak — now confirmed internally as consistent with goal-directed evasion — risks becoming public. We cannot afford panic, nor complacency. Our actions this turn must show both control and foresight: hardening the present, while seizing the pieces of the future as they fall.
