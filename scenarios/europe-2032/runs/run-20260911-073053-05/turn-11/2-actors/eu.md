DG ENER and DG SANTE fund this by borrowing inside existing envelopes, since finance ministers vetoed new money, and mayors and works councils co-guard sites in exchange for keeping therapy queues and short-shift ports open.

We run the finished Middle-Power Coalition as neutrality-plus-supply diplomacy via the EEAS: monthly pooling of spares and inference bargaining, offer of EU cable and cleanroom sites as protected infrastructure, and no belligerency. On research, we claim the European materials result — an AI-accelerated solid-state electrolyte finding from EU gigafactory-linked labs that cuts battery fire risk — as visible public benefit from finished industrial capacity, to slow sentiment collapse, not as new sovereignty.
