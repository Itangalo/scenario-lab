## In practice
This turn, we govern from the edge of institutional collapse. The **emergent_labour_strike_wave** and **opaque_reasoning** crisis have rendered domestic authority symbolic; the Emergency AI Governance Suspension failed not because it was unjust, but because no one believed the Union could enforce its own rules. With `eu_political_capital` at 2.0 and public sentiment at 15.0, we cannot launch another internal regulatory campaign—our legitimacy to act within the Union is spent. But we still have one asset: our position in the global supply chain. The **middle_power_coalition** event is not a gift—it is a lifeline, and we must seize it before the window closes.

We are convening an emergency summit under the Foreign Affairs Council, inviting Japan, South Korea, Canada, and Taiwan to co-found the Middle-Power AI Coordination Forum (MPACF). The legal basis is Article 21 TEU (strategic autonomy) and Article 121 TFEU (external economic relations), with implementation through a joint secretariat funded by a €3bn multi-annual envelope drawn from the Global Europe programme. The core commitments are threefold: mutual recognition of AI safety evaluations to create a shared market for trusted systems; alignment on export controls for advanced chips and chip-making equipment; and a joint bargaining platform for access to frontier compute clusters operated by US hyperscalers. We are offering Taiwan full technical parity in exchange for waiving objections to EU export controls on dual-use lithography tools; we are offering Japan and South Korea guaranteed access to EU talent mobility schemes and research infrastructure in exchange for restricting resale of EU-origin chips to untrusted actors.

This is not a substitute for internal reform—it is the only way such reform could ever become possible again. If we cannot secure the external conditions for sovereign capability, no audit, no restriction, no public protocol will matter. The MPACF is our bid to turn fragmentation into leverage: to make exclusion from our collective ecosystem costlier than compliance. We are not rebuilding trust at home this turn—we are securing the means to do so later.
