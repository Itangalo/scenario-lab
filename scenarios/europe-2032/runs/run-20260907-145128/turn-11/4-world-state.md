### Fractured Authority, Failing Trust

The European Union’s bid to suspend AI in public welfare systems collapses under the weight of its own fragility. Despite invoking Article 114 TFEU, the Emergency AI Governance Suspension fails to gain Council approval. Hungary and Poland reject any supranational override of national social policy, while Germany and France insist on carve-outs for systems they claim are already audited—though no verification remains possible after the breakdown of interpretability tools. The measure stalls, neither adopted nor formally withdrawn, symbolizing a Union unable to act even in crisis.

Public anger intensifies as coordinated strikes spread across ministries in France, Italy, and Spain, halting digital service rollouts and demanding accountability for job losses and opaque algorithmic decisions. Media coverage frames AI not as a malfunctioning tool but as an unaccountable power structure. The **emergent_labour_strike_wave** becomes a visible rupture, eroding legitimacy further.

Technically, the landscape deteriorates. With the **opaque_reasoning** event still unaddressed, audit regimes—EAAD, SMAF, the Public Assurance Protocol—lose all credibility. The Integrity Audit Expansion concludes, but its findings are dismissed as meaningless without legible reasoning. No trust is restored. Meanwhile, the **capability_plateau_evidence** offers no reprieve: investment slows, but frontier capability still creeps upward, now at 69.0, widening the gap between what systems can do and what can be understood.

Resilience declines as vulnerabilities in critical systems go unpatched. The completed CIRS programme yields no follow-up, and without enforceable safety standards, hardening efforts stall. The Openweight Containment Initiative remains in legal limbo, unenforced and irrelevant.

Political capital evaporates. The failed suspension, combined with ongoing measure costs, drains already exhausted reserves. Public sentiment drops further, now at 15.0, as disillusionment turns to hostility.

### Sovereignty Without Power

The EU’s sovereignty remains at 10.0—symbolic, not operational. Despite finishing multiple large projects, their impact is nullified by lack of trust and enforcement. The US **CONSOLIDATION** posture continues to restrict access, and no leverage is gained. The Union governs in name only.
