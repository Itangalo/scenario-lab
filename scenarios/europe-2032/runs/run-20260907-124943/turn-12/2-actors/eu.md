Meanwhile, the **European Agency for AI Assurance and Control** stands at a crossroads. The safety_breakthrough — a method for certifying behavioural consistency in deployed agents — is being rapidly adopted by leading labs because it reduces their own liability. We are seizing this moment: under Article 16 of the AI Act, we are instructing the Agency to condition access to this EU-validated method on full pre-deployment evaluation for any model above 65.0 capability, even if trained outside the Union. Legal challenges are expected, but we are backing the move with €200 million in fast-tracked funding from Horizon Europe to build the Agency’s technical team by Q3. Austria’s recognition of foreign certifications remains a loophole, but we are negotiating a bilateral carve-out: in exchange for hosting the Agency’s interpretability lab, Vienna agrees to treat EU evaluations as exclusive for high-risk systems. This is not just about control — it is about making the Agency matter before it is too late.
