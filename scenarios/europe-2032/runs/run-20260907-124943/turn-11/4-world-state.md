### A Surge of Resolve, Tested by Reality

The EU launches the **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield Initiative**, a large category 6 measure, declaring it a priority in response to the election annulment and rising cyber threats. Funded with emergency allocations, it mandates AI-resistant redundancy across energy, health, and electoral systems, backed by live-fire exercises and a new European AI Threat Observatory in The Hague. Member states are given deadlines and offered 70% co-financing, yet early resistance emerges: two smaller states warn of fiscal strain, and private grid operators lobby to delay compliance, citing technical complexity. Due to the constraints of managing existing initiatives, full mobilization is delayed, and implementation will not begin in earnest until the next turn.

The **Edge Sovereignty Acceleration Programme** completes this turn, deploying neuromorphic edge systems in key infrastructure nodes across five member states. This delivers tangible progress in reducing cloud dependency, contributing to sovereignty. However, full integration remains uneven, with legacy systems still exposed.

The **European Agency for AI Assurance and Control** begins preliminary staffing in Vienna, but its operational launch is postponed due to legal disputes. A major U.S. developer refuses pre-deployment evaluations, and a founding member state announces it will recognize foreign certifications, creating a regulatory loophole. These developments stall the agency’s authority and delay its formal activation, limiting its immediate impact.

AI capability advances to 70.5, as frontier labs integrate AI into chip design and autonomous experimentation. Openweight models show modest gains, incorporating limited exploit logic observed in recent cyber incidents, but diffusion remains constrained. The jump in openweight capability is therefore limited, reflecting slow leakage rather than a major breakthrough or open release.

Resilience rises slightly due to the partial rollout of hardened protocols and the Edge Sovereignty Programme’s deployment. Public sentiment remains stagnant at 24.0, as protests against AI continue and trust in institutions erodes. Political capital dips slightly under the weight of initiating a new priority measure while managing ongoing challenges, despite the completion of one major programme.

### Fragile Foundations

The EU acts with urgency, but institutional cohesion frays under pressure. Hardening infrastructure is possible — but only if unity holds. The pace of action is constrained by capacity, and not every front can advance at once.
