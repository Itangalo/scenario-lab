## In practice
We are now executing the Emergency Public Legitimacy Programme through a binding tripartite enforcement mechanism: the Commission is using Article 114 TFEU to override national legal fragmentation on data access, the EDPB and FRA are co-issuing audit protocols for high-risk AI in social services, and the Justice Recovery Fund is disbursing compensation via the new EU-level claims office in Luxembourg. We are mandating integration of the Public Trust Observatory’s explainability toolkit across all participating agencies, with human-in-the-loop re-approval required for any decision lacking a clear justification trail by turn 9. Resistance from member states is being met with conditional disbursement of cohesion funds — a hard lever, but one we must now pull.

At the same time, we are launching the Neuromorphic Sovereign Inference Initiative to break the logjam in compute sovereignty. The emergent_efficiency_breakthrough from the European Neuromorphic Systems Consortium allows us to bypass U.S. export controls by building sovereign inference capacity using open-architecture, low-power chips that can run models at capability 55–60 on domestic energy grids. We are redirecting €45bn from stalled elements of the InvestAI Gigafactories budget to fast-track production lines in Dresden, Leuven, and Grenoble, with the first pilot deployments in municipal emergency response and healthcare triage systems by turn 11. This is not a replacement for high-end training compute — we still lack that — but it creates a resilient, distributed layer of sovereign AI operation that cannot be switched off by external actors. The Sovereign Audit and Redress Corridor is being provisioned with 20% of EuroHPC’s air-gapped capacity to run EU-developed interpretability tools, ensuring that even if trust in private providers evaporates, we retain the ability to audit and correct past decisions. The window is narrow, the capital is thin, but we are acting on both fronts: repairing legitimacy where it has broken, and building sovereignty where it has never existed.
