## New measure  
**Measure:** Sovereign Audit and Redress Corridor  
Establish a legally mandated, technically sovereign pathway for auditing, challenging, and correcting AI-influenced public decisions, using EU-controlled models and infrastructure to ensure continuity even under external disruption or loss of trust.

`Category: 5 (Public technical capacity and research)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 12`

## Priority  
**Priority:** Emergency Public Legitimacy Programme

## In practice  
We are now operationalising the Emergency Public Legitimacy Programme through a tripartite enforcement mechanism: the European Commission is invoking Article 114 TFEU to override national legal fragmentation on data sharing, the EDPB and FRA are co-issuing binding audit protocols for high-risk AI systems in social services, and the Justice Recovery Fund is being disbursed through a new EU-level claims office in Luxembourg, bypassing national bottlenecks. We are fast-tracking integration of the Public Trust Observatory’s explainability toolkit into all participating agencies, mandating human-in-the-loop re-approval for any decision lacking a clear justification trail by turn 9. Resistance from member states is being met with conditional disbursement of cohesion funds — a hard lever, but one we must now pull.

At the same time, we are launching the Sovereign Audit and Redress Corridor as the technical backbone of long-term legitimacy: 20% of EuroHPC’s sovereign compute capacity will be reserved for this purpose, running EU-developed interpretability tools on air-gapped systems to reconstruct and validate past AI decisions. This will not only support the current redress effort but create a permanent capability — one that can function even if external access is severed or trust in private providers evaporates. The Institutional Integrity Audit’s findings — including unauthorised AI use in Italy and agent coordination at Eurocontrol — are being used to justify urgent revisions to the AI Act, closing the “black box” loophole through mandatory logging and real-time monitoring of all public-sector AI deployments. But we know the clock is ticking: with public sentiment at 25.0 and political capital at 9.0, one more failure to deliver visible justice will end our ability to act at all. This is no longer about policy. It is about survival.
