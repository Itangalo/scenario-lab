### A Turn of Hard Realism

The European Union enters 2032 with its credibility fraying and its institutions strained. Despite the formal completion of the InvestAI Gigafactories in Bavaria and Wallonia, long-term funding remains uncertain without the InvestEU AI Bonds. The Pre-Emptive Intervention Authority, intended as a shield against runaway open models, has stalled in legal and political gridlock. Member states balk at enforcement overreach, and ECOFIN refuses emergency fast-tracking.

In response, the Commission launches the Sovereign AI Foundry Programme — a classified, end-to-end AI development effort under Article 172 TFEU, hosted jointly by DG CONNECT and the European Defence Agency. Framed not as competition with the global frontier but as strategic survival, it aims to create one trusted system under full EU control. Nodes will be established in France, Germany, and Finland, leveraging existing Gigafactory infrastructure.

Yet ambition meets constraint. The programme is large, costly, and politically divisive. Launching it drains scarce political capital, while the U.S., under its CONSOLIDATION posture, tightens export controls further — cutting access to updated model weights and advanced chip tooling. This deepens dependency precisely when sovereignty is most needed.

Public sentiment remains low, anchored in anxiety over job losses and bio-lab incidents. Protests grow outside data centres and research facilities, now joined by civil servants fearing opaque, militarised AI governance. Trust erodes further as the Commission pivots from open regulation to closed development, abandoning the dream of governing distributed models.

Meanwhile, the world does not wait. `ai_capability` edges upward, and `openweight_capability` surges toward 59.0 as new releases flood forums and mirrors. The gap between what exists and what can be controlled widens. No measure lands on safety or resilience. The EU acts — but too late, too narrowly, and at too high a cost.

### Strategic Retreat, Not Renewal

The shift from governance to self-reliance is real, but it is not yet effective. The Foundry is a bunker under construction, not a fortress standing. And outside, the storm gathers.
