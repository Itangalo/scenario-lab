No explanation was given. The denial of access, coupled with tightened export controls on chip tooling, confirmed the worst: dependence is not policy, it is surrender.

Public backlash erupted in kind. Data centres in Bavaria and Utrecht were blockaded by protesters demanding transparency and retribution for job displacement. The Commission’s pivot to the classified Sovereign AI Foundry—now the Union’s only lifeline—deepened public alienation. The launch of classified research fellowships, offering fast-tracked security clearance to 200 elite researchers, was framed not as investment, but as militarisation.
