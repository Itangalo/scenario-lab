### Capability Crosses the Threshold
AI capability advances to 60.5, crossing the symbolic 60.0 threshold as frontier labs deploy the first agents capable of completing multi-day professional projects end to end. These systems now autonomously manage software sprints, legal discovery, and clinical trial design, displacing junior roles across sectors. The jump triggers immediate concern among regulators, as no corresponding safety upgrades have landed on deployed systems.

### Incident Reporting Regime Proposed, Not Yet Implemented
The EU proposes the AI Incident Reporting Regime, a small transparency measure under Article 16 TFEU, aiming to create a shared factual basis for risk. COREPER begins technical review, but formal adoption is pending. Several member states raise concerns about legal ambiguity, and private operators signal resistance to future disclosure requirements. The public dashboard does not yet exist; no data has been collected. The measure is now in the pipeline but remains in early stages, with full implementation at least one turn away.

### Sovereignty Erosion Continues
The InvestEU AI Bonds remain stalled, with ECOFIN unconvinced by the revised Article 122 TFEU justification. Bavaria and Wallonia reject the offer of board seats, demanding binding regional allocation guarantees. With no progress, EU AI sovereignty drops to 20.0, reflecting both the capability rise and the ongoing fragility in compute financing.

### Political Capital Under Pressure, But Not Collapsed
The portfolio charge remains heavy: three large measures and a priority continue to drain political capital. The new incident reporting regime has not yet delivered any benefits and cannot offset the burden. Public sentiment dips to 32.0 as trust in AI governance wanes. However, no major political crises or coalition fractures occur this turn. The EU institutions absorb strain without breakdown, limiting the political capital drop to 24.0 — still declining, but within bounds of institutional resilience.

### Resilience and Safety Slip
With no protective measures landing and cyber incidents rising, resilience declines to 32.0. AI safety drops to 17.0 as eval anomalies go unaddressed and no safeguards are embedded in frontier models.
