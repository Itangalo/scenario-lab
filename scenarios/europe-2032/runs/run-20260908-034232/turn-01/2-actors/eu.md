## New measure
InvestEU AI Bonds  
A €150bn sovereign bond programme to finance EU-controlled compute infrastructure, ring-fenced for construction, energy integration, and talent retention, with repayment tied to AI value capture.
`Category:` 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)  
`Size:` large  
`Finishes on turn:` 8  
`Targeted effect:` eu_ai_sovereignty +20–25, eu_political_capital -5 to -10 short-term, public_sentiment +5 to +10 if framed as strategic independence  
`Applies to:` EU jurisdiction, with binding conditions on private partners

## Priority
InvestEU AI Bonds — because the cyber_test_shot and capability_jump prove we are already behind the curve, and without sovereign infrastructure, every other measure depends on permission from abroad.

## In practice
We are moving now on three fronts. First, the Commission will draft a proposal for InvestEU AI Bonds under Article 122 TFEU, treating AI compute as critical infrastructure akin to energy or transport. This will be presented to the ECOFIN Council in October, with a request for emergency issuance authority under EU stability safeguards. The legal base is contested, but so was the SURE instrument in 2020 — and the precedent stands.

Second, we are renegotiating the terms of the Tech sovereignty package with national governments, conditioning access to accelerated-permitting zones on binding commitments to EU ownership of at least 51% of compute capacity and full audit rights for ENISA. This is hard: Bavaria and Wallonia are resisting, fearing investor flight. We are offering them first access to bond financing and a 15% top-up on cohesion funds if they comply.

Third, we are redirecting the initial tranches of the InvestAI Gigafactories fund toward modular nuclear and grid upgrades in the Nordic and Iberian clusters — not waiting for the full build-out. This is not in the original plan, but the capability_jump changes everything: we cannot build sovereign capacity on borrowed time and fragile grids. The Agency for Defence Innovation will be tasked with hardening these sites against cyber intrusion from day one, integrating lessons from the cyber_test_shot. This is not just about speed. It is about ensuring that when the next jump comes, it does not leave us in the dark.
