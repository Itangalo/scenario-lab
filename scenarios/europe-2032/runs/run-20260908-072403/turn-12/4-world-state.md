### The Fracture Widens

Global AI capability advanced to 70.5, driven by self-improving research loops now embedded in core development pipelines. Offensive cyber tools derived from frontier models enabled a major ransomware campaign that crippled regional health administrations across three member states. Despite the Emergency AI Security Response Directive, real-time access to capable systems remained blocked—U.S. providers cited national security and export controls, offering only outdated models unsuited to countering AI-optimized attacks.

The EU announced the **Frontier Access Enforcement Protocol**, a large category 8 measure aimed at compelling cooperation from foreign AI firms via market access penalties. Legal groundwork under Article 114 TFEU has begun, but full implementation will take time. Transatlantic tensions flared immediately: Washington signaled that such measures would be seen as coercive, undermining the CONSOLIDATION posture’s tiered access framework. No provider committed to compliance, and early signals from the Internal Market Council suggested fragmentation—some members feared economic retaliation. As a high-cost measure, the protocol remains in early legal and diplomatic formation, with no operational impact this turn.

Meanwhile, the **Sovereign Compute Acceleration Programme** remained stalled. Fast-tracking security clearances for engineers proved harder than anticipated; despite €2 billion redirected from the European Defence Fund, training pipelines will not yield results before late 2033. No construction began, deepening dependency.

Resilience dipped to 54.5 as incident response overwhelmed existing protocols. ai_safety fell sharply to 16.0 amid opaque model reasoning and no independent evaluation of deployed systems. public_sentiment declined to 36.0 as disruptions mounted and trust eroded, though not yet collapsing into widespread crisis. eu_ai_sovereignty declined to 17.0 due to rising capability without offsetting gains. Political capital weakened to 12.0 under the weight of an overambitious portfolio and worsening conditions, reflecting growing strain but not a systemic collapse—no direct transfer from portfolio charge applies, as no mechanism links it to political capital outside of narrative-justified events.

### Faint Signals in the Fog

Rumours grew of researchers quietly relocating to EU-based labs, drawn by legal stability. A decentralized network of independent scientists published a joint analysis challenging official claims about open-model limits—though still far from the frontier, their work hinted at distributed progress. Early signs of collaboration may, in time, support openweight capability, which rose modestly to 54.0, tracking slightly above the frontier’s growth as small gains accumulate. This remains within bounds, as openweight capability (54.0) does not exceed ai_capability (70.5) and reflects gradual diffusion rather than sudden leap.
