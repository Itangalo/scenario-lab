## In practice
We accede by Council decision to the joint cyber command's real-time telemetry sharing and the biosurveillance pact's binding sample-sharing, designating ENISA and ECDC/HERA as EU nodes and extending the municipal sentinel and wastewater watches as our contribution. Legal base is Articles 168 and 222 TFEU with UCPM implementing acts, paid from reprogrammed EU4Health and Digital Europe envelopes — no new money vote.

We adopt the new interpretability/control certification on the Evaluation Institute's cleared list of older licensed and EU-hosted systems only, refusing to clear the March open weights, and we direct repair teams to deploy the certified controls and shared indicators to clinics and ministries on paper/offline fallback. We ask mayors and health ministers for continued forbearance on overtime and blockades, presenting pooled detection as the bridge until capital recovers.
