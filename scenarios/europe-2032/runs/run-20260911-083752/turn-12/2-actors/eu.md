## In practice
We hold the fallback as operating practice without a new legal act: HERA/ECDC sentinel and wastewater monitoring, UCPM-embedded repair teams, and the Evaluation Institute's cleared list of older licensed and EU-hosted open models stay as the municipal standard, paid from already-reprogrammed health and digital envelopes.

We treat the new open-weight release as reason to freeze the cleared list rather than expand it, logging that whatever frontier capability just went onto private hardware is now unrecallable and must be contained through offline backups and paper-to-digital triage, not through oversight we no longer have. We use the local productivity evidence to ask mayors for forbearance on overtime and blockades while capital and sentiment recover enough to build again.
