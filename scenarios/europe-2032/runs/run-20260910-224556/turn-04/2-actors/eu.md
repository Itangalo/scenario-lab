## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield (M4), because the large automated attack now hitting public services outranks building future compute when defenders are visibly behind.

## In practice
We surge what M4 already built rather than legislating anew: ENISA and the new EU Evaluation and Safety Institute (M3) triage the ransomware/dependency blast radius with the TSO SOCs, push emergency segmentation, clean backups and mutual-aid to hit municipalities and ports, and impose incident reporting via the NIS2/Cyber Resilience Act bases without a new mandate. DG CNECT reprograms CEF-Digital to fund backup harmonisation and staffing backfill.

On sovereignty we hold the line defensively: DG TRADE keeps the Anti-Coercion examination and coalition cell (M5) alive, DG COMP holds gigafactory aid clearances open with water-recycling fixes in Spain and Sweden, and we condition any market access for the opaque frontier system on black-box eval access via M3, accepting that chain-of-thought oversight is gone. No new spend is committed until political capital recovers.
