## New measure
**EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**
Hardens transmission operators, ports and water utilities with mandatory segmentation, 24h detection and cross-border mutual aid drills. Why now: the autumn grid intrusion proved exposure without adversary, and siting backlash means sovereignty cannot arrive before the next probe.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, because an undetected foothold that needs no attribution to cascade outranks concrete that will not land this turn.

## In practice
We run the Shield through DG ENER and ENISA under the NIS2 and Cyber Solidarity Act base, funded by re-prioritised Connecting Europe Facility money: binding segmentation standards for the two hit transmission operators extended EU-wide, SOCs with AI-assisted anomaly detection, and exercised isolation drills with port and water authorities. France, Germany and Poland get first deployment to buy Council JHA backing.

We keep InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and the permitting zones (M2) alive but defensive: DG COMP holds state-aid clearance, DG ENER offers water-recycling and energy-compensation packages to the stalled site to settle lawsuits, without opening new sites. The Evaluation Institute (M3) moves to staffing — JRC secondments and vetted-access negotiations — framed as the enforcer of the 2027-2028 obligations, resisting industry decoupling demands.
