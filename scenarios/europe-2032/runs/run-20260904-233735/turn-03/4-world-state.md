### Capability Creeps Forward, Safety Falls Behind  
AI capability advances to 56.5 as frontier labs compress development cycles, leveraging AI-driven code generation and automated testing. The closed frontier edges toward persistent agent systems, though full autonomy remains constrained by verification bottlenecks. With no safety measures yet embedded in shipped models and growing opacity in agent reasoning, `ai_safety` drops sharply to 25.0, reflecting rising concern among developers and regulators about unmonitored emergent behaviour.

### Open Weights Widen the Threat Surface  
The open release of powerful cyber tools continues to diffuse capability. While the latest open models fall short of the frontier, their adaptation by skilled users pushes `openweight_capability` to 44.0, nearing the threshold where offensive autonomy becomes accessible outside elite labs. This widening gap between closed and open safety practices fuels anxiety about uncontrolled proliferation.

### Sovereignty Gains Ground, at a Cost  
The Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive formally enters force, reserving 30% of frontier compute for public-interest use. This marks a legal milestone, but its operational impact is limited by a shortage of qualified personnel. In response, the EU launches the Sovereign Talent & Knowledge Repatriation Initiative, targeting 200 EU-origin AI scientists abroad. Early outreach shows promise, with pilot agreements signed by France, Germany, and the ERC, but recruitment faces headwinds from competitive US packages and concerns over research freedom.

The Tech Sovereignty Package inches forward: Spain and Poland sign MOUs for AI Acceleration Zones, aided by grid prioritisation deals. However, legal challenges loom over environmental waivers, and no new compute comes online this turn.

### Cyber Defences Prove Their Worth  
The Joint Cyber Shield Initiative conducts its first live-fire exercise, successfully detecting and containing a simulated multi-vector attack. Real-time telemetry sharing with NATO and Indo-Pacific partners enables rapid coordination, boosting `resilience` to 43.0.

### Political Capital Erodes Amid Public Backlash  
Labour displacement in logistics and customer service triggers protests in six member states. Media coverage frames AI as a job destroyer, dragging `public_sentiment` down to 35.0. The EU resists calls for a moratorium, but political capital continues to drain under the weight of four large concurrent measures.
