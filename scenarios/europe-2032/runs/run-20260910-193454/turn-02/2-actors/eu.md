## Priority
Grid and Port Cyber Shield, because the cyber_defence_breakthrough must be operationalised into OT patching and swarm detection before the quiet intruders return, and it remains the core of our two-year commitment.

## In practice
We push ENISA joint operations into deployment phase: using the Article 122 mandate to require automated patching pilots and swarm-behaviour detection in German, Polish and now French TSOs, co-financed from reprogrammed Digital Europe funds, with liability cover extended to keep energy ministers on board despite derogation requests. Equipment backlog is managed by pooled procurement of hardened relays.

We launch the Rollout via DG DIGIT and DG REFORM with volunteer coalitions — Denmark, Estonia, Spain — using existing EU procurement frameworks favouring EU-hosted models, funded by small-scale repurposing within Digital Europe, explicitly linking waiting-list falls to EU choice. The Gigafactories and tech sovereignty package are held on permitting track without new cash this turn, accepting the investment-collapse pause while we protect sites and grid connections for restart.
