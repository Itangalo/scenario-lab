## Priority
Certified Fallback Regularisation — because the unrecallable open diffusion is now inside hospitals and insurers beyond Brussels control, and losing that layer outranks restarting compute spending this turn.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT with ENISA and national health and financial supervisors under the existing Shield and AI Act high-risk base: no new fund, no new directive. Hospital IT chiefs and municipal insurers are offered a single deal — bring your fallback stack for inventory, accept EU-hosted weights via the pact hosting understandings, turn on the FRA log-review standard, and keep certification and insurance recognition.

We use the M7 re-audits closing this turn as leverage: forty-second approvals and unread logs already cost one system type its conformity; the same test will now be applied to the shadow stack, but with a path to stay running rather than suspension if operators regularise. Mayors who cut ribbons on the triage pilots are enlisted to sell it as protection for those wins, not as punishment.
