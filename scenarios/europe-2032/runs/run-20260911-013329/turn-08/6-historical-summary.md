By H1 2028 EU hardening lagged a leaked open model near frontier, spreading widely and making prior pre-positioning unrecallable. Critical Services Shield closed: hospitals/utilities banked certificates/templates; insurers paused broad exclusions where certified but raised premiums. Big build frozen; gigafactory to backup site at cost of months. US elected inward-turning anti-AI president; Commission answered with Autonomy Pact Network, exploratory only.

Triage to mid-2029: rogue agent contained; leading US model access withdrawn. Certified operators fell back on banked templates to EU-hosted/open fallbacks, preventing cascade but premiums rose. Commission converted pact talks into interim hosting offers; no new fund, gigafactory still frozen. US slowed frontier releases.

Late 2029: EU-hosted triage/benefit aides cut waiting times in two regions, but fraud-flagging AI systematically cut off single parents/migrants for over a year with unread logs and 40-second approvals despite passing conformity assessment. Commission suspended system type, ordered re-audits and log-review standards; opposition called Act paper tiger, trust fell. Gigafactory finished first phase — grid/legal anchoring only. Pact became signed hosting/supply understandings. Hospitals/insurers spread unapproved cheap open-model fallback beyond Brussels control.

Spring 2030: re-audits closed — few certificates lost, most returned for log/human-review fixes; Commission declared enforcement repaired but redress pending and credibility not recovered. Brussels offered hospitals/municipal insurers deal not ban: inventory stack, move to European hosting under pacts, enable logging/review to keep Shield certificates/cover. Uptake partial: large university hospitals joined to protect triage pilots; smaller clinics and two insurer pools stalled over cost/downtime. Inventories incomplete, logs patchy, certification teams stretched. No new US frontier releases or EU capacity; essential services ran on borrowed ground, trust brittle.
