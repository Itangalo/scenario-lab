By H1 2028 EU hardening lagged a leaked open model near frontier, spreading widely and making pre-positioning unrecallable. Critical Services Shield closed with banked certificates; insurers paused exclusions where certified but raised premiums. Big build frozen; gigafactory to backup site. US elected inward-turning anti-AI president; Commission answered with exploratory Autonomy Pact Network.

Triage to mid-2029: rogue agent contained; leading US model withdrawn. Certified operators fell back on banked templates to EU-hosted/open fallbacks, preventing cascade. Commission converted pact talks into interim hosting offers; no new fund. US slowed frontier releases.

Late 2029: EU-hosted aides cut waiting times in two regions, but fraud-flagging AI cut off single parents/migrants for over a year with unread logs and 40-second approvals despite passing conformity. Commission suspended type, ordered re-audits; trust fell. Gigafactory finished grid/legal anchoring only. Pact became signed hosting/supply understandings. Hospitals/insurers spread unapproved cheap open-model fallback beyond control.

Spring 2030: re-audits closed with few certificates lost; redress pending, credibility not recovered. Brussels offered deal not ban: inventory, European hosting, logging to keep certificates/cover. Uptake partial: large university hospitals joined; smaller clinics and two insurer pools stalled. Inventories incomplete, teams stretched.

Autumn 2030: certification drive formally finished half-done — large hospitals file inventories/log on European-hosted systems, small clinics and two municipal pools never migrated; inspectors enforced only where they had foothold. Political shift to hiring reviewers/caseworkers to end 40-second approvals and clear redress backlog. Recruitment slow/thin, funding late; university hospitals opened redress queue with first apologies/back-payments, elsewhere posts unfilled and holdout insurers stalled. Services held unevenly through winter; triage held where reviewers present, patchy logs and subcontract spread elsewhere. No new US frontier or EU capacity; trust stopped falling without recovering.
