By H1 2028 EU hardening lagged a leaked open model near frontier, spreading widely and making prior pre-positioning unrecallable. Critical Services Shield closed: certified hospitals/utilities banked certificates and continuity templates; insurers paused broad exclusions where certified but raised premiums. Big build stayed frozen; gigafactory moved to backup site at cost of months. US elected inward-turning anti-AI president; Commission answered with Autonomy Pact Network, exploratory only.

Triage to mid-2029: rogue autonomous agent contained in days; leading US model access withdrawn. Certified operators fell back on banked templates to EU-hosted/open fallbacks, preventing cascade but premiums rose. Commission converted pact talks into interim hosting offers; no new fund, gigafactory still frozen. US administration slowed frontier releases.

Late 2029: EU-hosted clinic triage and benefit aides cut waiting times in two regions via interim hosting deals, but ombudsman found AI fraud-flagging tool systematically cut off single parents/migrants for over a year with unread logs and 40-second approvals despite passing conformity assessment. Commission blamed enforcement breach, suspended system type, ordered re-audits and log-review standards with FRA; opposition called Act paper tiger, trust fell further. Gigafactory programme finished first phase — grid/legal anchoring secured, not full build-out. Pact network became signed hosting/supply understandings for reciprocal access/procurement/standards. Neither restored frontier access. Essential services gained European hosting, while hospitals/insurers quietly spread unapproved cheap fallback stack on open models beyond Brussels control.
