## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for political capital to recover above the level where any new instrument is fundable without collapse, and for space to respond to the open-weight jump and the ASML coercion without staking what we cannot pay for.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority named this turn, because with no measures in flight and capital at 1.0 there is nothing we can credibly push hardest without manufacturing a second failure.

## In practice
We keep what finished landing alive without new money: ENISA and CERT-EU joint teams keep the standardised patching and swarm-flagging playbooks in use in the rebuilt municipalities on existing Digital Europe maintenance, and DG SANTE keeps procuring the foreign-computed therapies on current health-ministry contracts while labelling provenance honestly. No new builds are ordered while permits remain frozen and US caps bind.

On the two external shocks we absorb rather than answer: the open-weight release within points of the frontier is treated as unrecallable distribution, so we circulate containment and incident-reporting guidance to hospitals and municipalities rather than pretending at recall, and on Washington forcing ASML cuts beyond leading-edge we log the use of our chokepoint against us in the Trade Council and seek only damage-limitation on servicing, without retaliation or a parallel compute programme we cannot fund.
