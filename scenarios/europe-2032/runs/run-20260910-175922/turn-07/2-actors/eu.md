## Priority
European Public AI Dividend Programme, because with capital at 11 and no in-flight measures costing us, rebuilding legitimacy through attributed public benefit outranks pushing blocked siting that we cannot force.

## In practice
We task DG REFORM and DG CNECT to codify the successful deployments — waiting-list triage, permit decisions, tutoring — into replicable EU-procured packages funded from Digital Europe and cohesion envelopes, with procurement preference for systems run under EU jurisdiction where feasible. Health and Education Council formations carry the rollout to mayors and regions, offering delivery money and staff support rather than siting demands.

We treat the defection as a cohesion hold, not a new negotiation: no retaliation, no parallel hyperscaler deal, and the Commission holds the Common Front line in the Trade and Competitiveness formations while letting the dividend programme show what joint EU attribution buys. On capacity, we keep M1-finished siting in joint review only where the one region agreed to talk, using TEN-E connection status already offered, and we bank interpretability via ENISA guidance without new instruments.
