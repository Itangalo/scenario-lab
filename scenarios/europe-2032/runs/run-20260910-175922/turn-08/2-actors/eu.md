## In practice
We task ENISA and DG CNECT to deploy joint incident teams to affected municipalities and health systems under the existing Critical Systems Hardening pact, funded from Digital Europe emergency reallocation, with CERT-EU triage and ECB-supervised continuity in finance. The Justice and Home Affairs Council carries mutual-aid requests, offering staff and clean-image rebuilds rather than new obligations.

We hold the dividend programme (M6) running through DG REFORM delivery money to untouched mayors, so clinics and classrooms stay attributed to Brussels while recovery proceeds. On permits and US tiers, we take no new build decision this turn: joint review stays paused under the court freeze, and we seek clarification on volume caps via the Trade Council without retaliation or a separate hyperscaler race.
