## Priority
Municipal Utilities Rapid Shield, because unrecallable near-frontier open weights probing brittle municipal systems outranks slow gigafactory and discipline tracks for the two-year absorb-shocks commitment.

## In practice
We run the Shield through ENISA and DG ENER with Civil Protection Mechanism crews: standard backup-control kits, joint audits extended from transmission to municipal utilities, and Digital Europe / Connecting Europe reprogramming to pay the EU share that finance ministries blocked. Blocked backup-site permits get emergency siting via the sovereignty-zone accelerated-permitting talks, with water/power concessions for councils.

Gigafactories and the tech sovereignty package stay negotiated with energy regulators on grid connections without new money this turn, and the Common Front review continues vetting the bilateral hyperscaler deal via DG CNECT/COMP procurement leverage. We accept public strain on data-centre siting and frame the Shield as keeping lights and water on, not as AI promotion.
