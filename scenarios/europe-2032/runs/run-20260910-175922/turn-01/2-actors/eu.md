## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Hardening and Response Pact**
It hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with mandatory detection, backup controls and cross-border exercises.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because the autumn intrusion proved detection would not have caught weeks-long presence in EU grids, and with open-weight frontier cyber now unrecallable, resilience is the lever within EU control while compute builds.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Hardening and Response Pact, because live intrusion in member-state grids and unrecallable open-weight offensive cyber outrank even the Gigafactories build this turn.

## In practice
We task ENISA with expanded 24/7 detection mandate alongside national TSOs and port authorities, funded by reprogrammed Connecting Europe Facility and Digital Europe money, with NIS2 implementing acts in the Transport-Telecom-Energy Council to make incident reporting within 24h and joint audits binding. Member states resisting cost-sharing in finance ministries are offered EU co-financing for backup control systems and mutual-aid crews.

The inherited InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty package continue through DG CNECT and accelerated-permitting zones, with grid-connection priority negotiated with energy regulators and ASML supply-chain coordination kept alive against US pressure, but no new compute instrument is stood up in parallel. This turn's political push, staff and Council time go to getting the hardening pact drafted and exercised before the next intrusion surfaces.
