## Priority
Municipal Automated Patching Surge, because it is the only fundable instrument that converts the defensive breakthrough into continuity of essential services this turn.

## In practice
We task ENISA and CERT-EU joint teams to push the new automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection images to the rebuilt municipalities and hospital networks on existing Digital Europe maintenance, using the standardised playbooks finished in 2031 — no new directive, Council implementing decision under NIS2 Article basis only. Larger cities get it first as relays for smaller towns.

We pay for deployment from maintenance and municipal recovery leftovers, asking health ministries and mayors only for access windows, not co-funding, since with political capital at 2.0 we cannot demand contributions. DG SANTE continues foreign-computed therapies with provenance labelling in parallel, without new procurement.
