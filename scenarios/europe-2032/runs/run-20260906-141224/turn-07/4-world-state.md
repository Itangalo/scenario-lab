### The Full-Stack Sovereignty Push

The EU doubles down on technological autonomy, launching **Sovereign Access to Foundational Models**, a small category 4 measure aimed at ensuring public institutions can run high-capability AI without foreign dependencies. Framed as continuity-of-government infrastructure, it repurposes €900m from the now-completed **Tech Sovereignty Package** to build a federated model repository across EuroHPC sites in France, Germany, and Italy. Legal authority draws from the AI Act, DMA, and Article 114 TFEU, but resistance simmers among member states wary of duplication and U.S. cloud providers threatening to limit telemetry sharing.

Meanwhile, the **Emergency Compute Assurance Initiative** advances, with audits underway at key hyperscaler and EuroHPC nodes. The EU leverages energy prioritisation under REPowerEU to pressure non-compliant operators, testing the limits of its emergency powers. Early assessments confirm that compute integrity is fragile — one cluster in Eastern Europe is found routing critical workloads through U.S.-controlled orchestration layers, triggering a high-level diplomatic intervention.

### Global Pressure Intensifies

Washington’s **supply_chain_coercion** escalates as the Netherlands is forced to restrict ASML’s servicing of older lithography tools, hitting not only China but also EU industrial users reliant on mature-node chips. The move exposes the Union’s single chokepoint in semiconductor supply — and that it is not under EU control. This direct blow to strategic autonomy triggers a −2 event term in sovereignty, narrowly offset by momentum from new initiatives.

Despite the U.S. retrenchment posture slowing global AI momentum, **ai_capability** rises to 63.5, driven by self-improving agents optimising software and materials pipelines. With no safety measures landing, **ai_safety** drops to 18.0. **Openweight_capability** climbs to 51.0, as older frontier models leak into open channels via research collaborations and grey-market redistribution.

**Resilience** holds at 50.0 — the grid probe from last turn caused contained damage, and the emerging detection methods for adversarial feedback loops prevent escalation. Public sentiment remains stagnant at 30.0, caught between anxiety and grudging acceptance of emergency measures.
