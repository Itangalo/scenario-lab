### Capability Surge, Safety Slips Further  
Global AI capability advances to 61.0, driven by closed labs achieving multi-day autonomous project execution. No new safeguards land on frontier systems, and the opaque reasoning behind recent model decisions—exposed during the access denial incident—remains unaddressed. With no safety breakthrough and rising capability, confidence in control erodes further, pushing ai_safety down to 28.0.

### Open Weights Advance, But Not Leap  
The rise in closed capability enables incremental progress in open-weight systems, lifting openweight_capability to 50.0. While model leaks from frontier labs continue at a steady pace, the adaptation by open communities remains constrained by compute access and integration delays. Early versions of powerful models appear in open repositories, but lack the full tooling and infrastructure for rapid deployment at scale.

### Open-Weight Monitoring Initiative Launched, Not Yet Operational  
The EU announces the Open-Weight Risk Monitoring Network, a new coordination effort linking research hubs in France, Germany, and Sweden. However, due to budget constraints and delays in securing cross-border data sharing agreements, full operations are postponed. Initial staffing begins, but no monitoring infrastructure is yet active. The measure is added to the portfolio, but implementation will not begin in earnest until Turn 4.

### Sovereignty Under Strain  
Despite urgency, InvestAI Gigafactories face grid bottlenecks and local resistance in Hungary, slowing physical progress. The legal push to bind hyperscalers to EU-controlled compute hits delays in the Council, where member states fear retaliatory investment withdrawal. With ai_capability rising by 3.0 and no capacity secured, eu_ai_sovereignty drops to 19.0.

### Resilience Inches Forward  
The Resilience Shield advances with stress test trials in six countries, and AI-enhanced intrusion detection upgrades begin. However, jurisdictional disputes stall mandatory enforcement, limiting gains. Resilience rises slightly to 41.0.

### Political Capital Under Pressure  
Four large measures remain active: InvestAI, Tech Sovereignty, EAAD, and Resilience Shield. The proposed Open-Weight Network is introduced but not yet implemented, contributing a partial cost of −2 to the portfolio. Total portfolio charge is now −15, with priority cost −1. A +1 proposal bonus reflects early recognition of open risks. No sentiment boost applies. Without new funding sources or reprioritization, the burden falls on political capital, which declines to 34.0—reflecting strain, but not collapse.

### Public Mood Turns Cautious  
Media spotlight labour displacement in technical sectors, framing AI as a job threat. The access denial incident fuels narratives of dependency. Public sentiment dips to 38.0, increasing friction for future sovereignty investments.
