## In practice
This turn, we are acting on the quiet alarm that has been building across energy regulators: AI-driven feedback loops in grid management systems are no longer theoretical. One utility has already paused automation upgrades. We cannot wait for blackouts to validate the risk. So we are launching the **Grid Integrity Shield Initiative**, a binding directive under the Clean Energy for All Europeans package, enforced through ACER and ENTSO-E, requiring all transmission system operators using AI for forecasting or load balancing to implement three layers of defence by turn 12. First, all AI-generated dispatch recommendations must pass through an independent verification module — not just logging, but real-time sanity checks using alternative models trained on disjoint data. Second, any action that would shift more than 15% of baseline load requires human-in-the-loop confirmation, with escalation protocols for out-of-distribution conditions. Third, all such systems must feed into a new cross-border anomaly correlation platform hosted by ENISA, allowing early detection of convergent failure modes. We are allocating €1.8bn from the Modernisation Fund and connecting it to the Digital Europe Programme’s cybersecurity envelope to fast-track implementation.

At the same time, we are pushing the **Secure Compute Corridor** into its final phase. The tripartite board — Commission, member state nominees, technical experts — has finally agreed on workload allocation protocols, thanks to a compromise: Hungary and Poland will host secondary enclave nodes with guaranteed minimum compute shares for national critical functions, but under EU-level audit and failover protocols. ASML and Infineon are now delivering under Chips Act enforcement, and OVHcloud’s air-gapped enclaves in Finland and Grenoble are on track for activation next turn. This is not just about access — it is about control. The **Systemic Integrity Watch Expansion** depends on it: we cannot audit what we cannot run. And now, with the openweight frontier release closing the gap to the closed frontier, the same capability that threatens our grids is already loose. Our only advantage is speed, and our only leverage is sovereignty. We are building both — one node, one audit, one circuit-breaker at a time.
