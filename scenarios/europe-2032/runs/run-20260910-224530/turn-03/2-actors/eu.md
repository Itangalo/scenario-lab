## New measure
**EU AI Displacement Transition Facility**
Provides wage insurance, rapid retraining vouchers and employer-linked transition contributions for entry-level white-collar roles hollowed out by AI, starting with law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration. This is category 7 Labour and social protection, and why now is because measurable non-replacement of graduate hiring has made adjustment-versus-shock the binding constraint on public licence for the gigafactory and hardening builds, while capital cannot sustain new large builds.

## Priority
EU AI Displacement Transition Facility, because without a visible income-and-retraining bridge for displaced entry-level workers the Hardening Pact and compute builds lose the public tolerance they need to finish.

## In practice
We table the Facility through EPSCO and ECOFIN under ESF+ and the Just Transition logic, using re-purposed ESF+ and EIB social tranches for 6-month wage insurance and retraining vouchers delivered via national PES, with a co-levy design on large automating employers to blunt fiscal pushback from net-contributor states. DG EMPL leads with member-state pilots in the worst-hit graduate labour markets this autumn.

The Hardening Pact (M4) and Evaluation Institute (M3) stay running without new scope: ENISA-led segmentation audits and spring-drill follow-ups continue, and the Institute keeps phased industry testing while we press for vetted US model access. Gigafactories (M1) and permitting zones (M2) we explicitly hold at sequenced pace — no new ground-breaking ahead of hardening milestones — to contain co-financing fights and signal that security and jobs come before expansion.
