## New measure
**EU Bio-Detection and Critical-Service Hardening Sprint**
Stands up EU-wide biosecurity detection (genomic surveillance, hospital screening protocols, HERA stockpile triggers) alongside cyber-hardening playbooks for health and energy operators exposed by the capability jump. This is Preparedness and resilience work, and why now is because the bio-uplift finding opens a four-turn bio gate while the frontier jump and open-weight diffusion make both misuse and cascade failure materially more likely before gigafactories land.

## Priority
EU Bio-Detection and Critical-Service Hardening Sprint, because an open bio-capability precursor outranks build timelines — sovereignty means nothing if a distributed model enables harm we cannot detect or absorb.

## In practice
We task HERA with ECDC to expand wastewater and clinical genomic sampling in 10 hub cities, fund rapid sequencing kits and reporting duties for hospitals via EU4Health, and pre-authorise common procurement of medical countermeasures. ENISA with DG ENER runs exercised isolation and restoration drills with transmission operators hit last autumn, tying cyber-insurance relief to adopted hardening standards.

The Gigafactories (M1) and permitting zones (M2) continue through state-aid and grid-connection work, now explicitly conditioned on hardened power and security-by-design, while the Evaluation Institute (M3) is directed to add bio-uplift and agent-capability tests to substitute-model certification. Labour displacement is monitored via EMPL scoreboard this turn to prepare a transition instrument next turn, but we do not split capital across two new builds at 28 capital.
