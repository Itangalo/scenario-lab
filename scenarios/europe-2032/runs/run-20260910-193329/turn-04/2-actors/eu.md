## Priority
EU Municipal Recovery and Containment Surge, because containing the ongoing attack and keeping services running outranks even assured access bargaining while still serving our two-year commitment to absorb shocks on infrastructure we control.

## In practice
We task ENISA and DG CNECT under the Cybersecurity Act emergency procedures to deploy joint teams, isolated clean backups and the Shield's automated patching and swarm-detection to affected municipalities and health systems, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe resilience money with cross-border mutual aid via the Competitiveness Council.

Gigafactories (M1), Tech Sovereignty Package (M2) and Assured Access Pact (M5) continue on protected public funding and joint negotiation track: EIB holds sites, Trade holds allied inference licences, EuroHPC keeps Continuity Stack workloads on EU-hosted open models. We publicly pair recovery with the adoption wins — falling waiting lists, faster decisions — to show European-hosted AI delivering benefit through the incident.
