## New measure
**EU Grid Hardening and Incident Response Pact**
Hardens transmission operators, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with segmented OT, 24h detection, and cross-border exercises. Why now: autumn intrusions proved mapped grids and failed detection, and containment outages demand resilience before the next probe acts.

## Priority
EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute, because without independent evaluation mandate and staffing the Union cannot enforce high-risk obligations or answer open-model grid tooling claims.

## In practice
We push the Evaluation Institute through the AI Office with JRC support, seeking a Competitiveness Council mandate for vetted pre-deployment access and weight-security audits, offering industry time-limited testing windows to blunt vetting resistance. InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package move jointly under DG CNECT and EIB: we restart site selection after the two blocked hearings with alternative sites and water-power mitigation funds, and tie cohesion energy compensation to final grid pledges from Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm and Warsaw.

We launch the Grid Pact via ENISA and ACER under the NIS2 and CER bases, funding OT segmentation, backup relay controls, and joint TSO-port-water exercises, and we contain the defecting member-state bilateral by offering gigafactory supply-chain placement conditional on common procurement terms. No new borrowing is committed this turn; we use existing InvestEU and Connecting Europe Facility lines to keep capital spendable.
