### Ration threats and a bargain
Autumn brought the tightening everyone in Brussels feared. Washington rewrote chip and model licensing rules again, with new volume caps and end-use checks. For weeks it was unclear whether allied buyers would be cut off alongside rivals. Commission trade officials flew to Washington with a thin hand: continued servicing of lithography tools, specialty chemicals and power equipment, coordinated loosely with Tokyo, Seoul and Taipei, in exchange for continuity of supply.

The deal that came back was partial. Existing allocations for hospitals, municipal fallback systems and the triage network were protected on multi-quarter licences, earmarked to run on EU soil behind isolation gates. New orders for logistics automation and commercial data-centre expansion were not. American officials spoke of domestic delays and slower rollouts at home; Chinese vendors moved quickly to fill the commercial gap.

### Cheques, vouchers and robots
In the warehouse belt the cushion finally reached the street. Short-time top-ups were paid through reprogrammed social funds, twelve-week vouchers were extended into longer care and repair tracks where clinics and utilities would take trainees, and mayors from Rotterdam to Lille, Leipzig, Łódź and the Po valley received block grants tied to keeping local portals and triage points online.

It helped, but did not reverse. Two large operators paused levy talks and warned of rerouting new sheds south and east. Retraining centres reported full classrooms and thin placements. Local papers contrasted nurses printing faster diagnoses with young pickers watching caged machines do their old shifts.

By December, clinics held where they ran and the lights stayed on. Resentment did not lift. Night-time fires at two robot charging yards and scattered damage to fencing were reported as isolated vandalism, with police and commentators arguing over whether it was protest or coincidence.
### The trusted rollout lands
The health and gov-tech marketplace expansion formally closed, with auditors certifying a new batch of contained deployments. The Commission claimed vindication; councils scarred by the outage winter noted portals had stayed up, barely.
