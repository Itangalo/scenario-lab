Autumn 2030 brought a repeat large automated assault on municipal portals and registries: isolated districts with human-approval gates restored in days, others queued for weeks with French-Dutch teams assisting; attribution unresolved, tooling judged machine-generated.

Labs showed a sharp jump in machine competence on verifiable work — code, math, intrusion — absorbed by attackers within weeks, not defenders; safety assurance fell effectively to zero.

Capital fled AI: valuations reset, data-centre expansions cancelled, two private hosting options for European buyers evaporated. Brussels leaned on fallback, handing reserved EuroHPC capacity and space in the sole gigafactory to cut-off hospitals/ministries; autumn migration drills became the Union's most credible asset. One win: European-hosted triage/decision-support under containment cut waiting lists in early-adopter cities, with procurement conditioned on telemetry and gates.

Public hostility deepened as US-software/Chinese-hardware warehouse robots hollowed picking/sorting work while screens stayed dark elsewhere.
