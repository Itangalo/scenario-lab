### Fragile Solidarity in Freefall

The EU convenes an emergency EPSCO session to launch the **EU Civilian Redress and Reintegration Facility**, a large-scale social intervention aimed at stabilising communities shattered by AI-driven job losses. Framed as an act of solidarity, the measure passes on paper — but implementation collapses before it begins. Fiscal resistance from several member states stalls disbursement, legal challenges question Union competence over retraining pipelines, and mobility mandates spark backlash in smaller economies. The facility exists only in draft regulations and unspent appropriations.

Public trust erodes further as the **Integrity Audit Expansion** concludes with mixed outcomes: some systems cleared, others suspended indefinitely due to unverifiable model reasoning. Auditors are dismissed as bureaucratic theatre. With no meaningful oversight delivering safety, and no redress reaching workers, resentment hardens.

`ai_capability` rises by 0.5, continuing its slow climb beyond 68. No leap occurs in `openweight_capability`, but containment is strained. The **export_control_escalation** event tightens access to chips and frontier models — under the standing **US_POSTURE: CONSOLIDATION**, allied buyers are rationed, not excluded, but terms grow punitive. The EU lacks the leverage to negotiate exceptions, and its dependence deepens.

`ai_safety` drops by 3 — capability advanced without assurance, and no finished measure improves deployed systems. The gap between power and control widens dangerously. `resilience` falls by 2: rising displacement and no effective response erode societal absorption. `eu_ai_sovereignty` drops by 1, as capability rose and no domestic compute capacity was secured to offset dependence.

`eu_political_capital` plummets from 7.0 to 4.0. The large measure costs −3, the priority −1, and no bonus offsets it. Worse, the measure fails to gain traction — blocked by legal uncertainty and fiscal resistance, it delivers nothing. With capital now below 12, the EU risks losing control of its agenda entirely.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 21.0. Fear dominates. The redress programme is too delayed, too contested, to shift perception.

### Unrest Takes Root

Grassroots coalitions of displaced workers and public-sector unions grow bolder, using openweight tools to flood local media with disinformation targeting AI integration. The movement is not yet national, but its reach is spreading.
