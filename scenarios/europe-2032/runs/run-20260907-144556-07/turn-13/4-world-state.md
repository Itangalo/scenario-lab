### The Union on the Brink

The European Union attempts a desperate institutional gambit: reframing AI-driven job collapse as a eurozone financial stability crisis to unlock €50bn from the European Stability Mechanism (ESM). The move hinges on redefining systemic risk — a legal and political stretch. While Germany and France express cautious interest, smaller member states denounce the proposal as overreach, accusing Brussels of bypassing democratic accountability. The Commission’s Legal Service concludes that binding AI transition metrics to ESM conditionality likely requires treaty change — a non-starter in the current climate.

With the **EU Civilian Redress and Reintegration Facility** still unimplemented, and no new fiscal instrument activated, public trust erodes further. Displaced worker coalitions intensify disinformation campaigns, now targeting national finance ministries to sabotage ESM negotiations. The narrative of EU paralysis becomes self-reinforcing.
