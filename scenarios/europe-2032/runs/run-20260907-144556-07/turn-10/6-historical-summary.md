The EU enacts the Emergency AI Governance Suspension in Public Services, halting AI use in welfare systems after the automated_decision_scandal, marking a symbolic admission of sovereign AI failure. Despite transitional funding, resistance emerges in Berlin and Athens over operational backlogs. The opaque_reasoning event invalidates existing audit frameworks, crippling EAAD and eroding trust in safety verification, though safeguards remain nominally intact. The Integrity Audit Expansion loses credibility amid technical obsolescence. Evidence of a capability plateau emerges, with slowing innovation and falling prices, while limited replication of hardened models occurs without large-scale leakage; Openweight Containment holds but remains legally contested. Major initiatives conclude without revitalizing momentum, leaving sovereignty hollow amid stagnant compute capacity and enduring US dependency. Public trust stabilizes slightly due to the moratorium, but skepticism persists over delayed redress and shallow accountability, reflecting exhaustion rather than progress.
