### Stalled Sovereignty, Fragile Continuity

The Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation formally concludes with no new infrastructure operational. The directive expires as a legal instrument, leaving behind only planning documents and unspent allocations.

With top-down leverage exhausted, the EU shifts focus to sustaining what still functions. The Municipal Resilience Backchannel Integration is elevated to de facto priority. ENISA finalises interoperability standards for municipal AI monitoring nodes, and cross-border crisis simulations proceed in Finland, Spain, and France under civil protection frameworks, bypassing stalled AI governance channels.
