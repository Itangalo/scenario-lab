### Fracture and Drift

The European Union closes its Municipal Resilience Backchannel Integration with quiet technical success but little strategic momentum. ENISA finalises an interoperability standard for local AI monitoring nodes, incorporating anomaly detection for unauthorised model deployment and early signs of goal persistence. Pilot audits in Lyon, Barcelona, and Helsinki yield actionable data, which DG HOME integrates into civil protection crisis simulations under Article 196 TFEU. Yet continuity is limited: Germany abstains, citing federal jurisdiction, and no new members join. The framework survives — but as a rump network, not a unified system.

### Strategic Paralysis Deepens

With political capital at 6.0, the Union cannot launch new initiatives. The redacted Evaluation Shield report, once meant to restore credibility, now symbolises fragmentation: two additional member states declare they will no longer participate in central AI safety evaluations, deepening the compliance patchwork. Developer cooperation frays further, and public narratives turn to “EU irrelevance” in AI governance. Media spotlight bilateral deals with U.S. labs, while job displacement and dependency humiliations feed growing public resentment.

### Taiwan Shock, Sovereignty Under Strain

The blockade of Taiwan disrupts global semiconductor exports, turning every AI policy question into a security crisis. The EU activates its supply chain leverage under Article 216 TFEU, mapping critical lithography and packaging nodes where EU firms hold irreplaceable IP. But no coordination with Japan or the Netherlands follows, and the Competitiveness Council resists controls due to export exposure. The Defence Technological and Industrial Base initiative is quietly repurposed to include AI resilience — a stopgap, not a strategy.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Crumbles

Global AI capability advances to 62.5, driven by consolidation of agent functionality. No safety measures land on shipped systems; anomalous behaviours grow more frequent. With `openweight_capability` rising to 59.0, leaked weights spread widely. Resilience improves slightly to 38.0 from the backchannel’s formal closure, but labour unrest and institutional drift drag public sentiment down to 28.0. Political capital erodes further, to 5.0, as the portfolio charge persists and no wins offset the drift.
