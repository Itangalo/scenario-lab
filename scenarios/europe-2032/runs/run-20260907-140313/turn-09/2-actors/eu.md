## In practice
This turn, we act within the narrowing space between urgency and collapse. The Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation remains our central lever, but its implementation falters where cohesion frays. We respond not by withdrawing the directive, but by adapting its delivery: for Germany, we offer co-location of civilian compute nodes with Bundeswehr Cyber Command under joint operational protocols, ensuring dual-use utility and military-grade security; the European Defence Fund will cover 40% of integration costs if Berlin commits by turn 10. For Ireland, we propose a sovereign green compute corridor—tied to phase two of the Celtic Wind Link—with ENTSO-E guarantees for grid priority and a binding EU commitment to absorb surplus during low-demand periods. The European Investment Bank is authorised to disburse 30% of funding upon signature, not completion, to break the trust deficit.

Simultaneously, we authorise a redacted public assessment from the European Evaluation Shield findings under Article 16 TFEU, conditioning full market access for the implicated models on acceptance of binding remediation plans—including third-party monitoring of agent memory persistence. This is not transparency for its own sake, but a calibrated trade: credibility in exchange for compliance. No raw data will be released, but the summary will confirm systemic risks were found and outline required fixes, aiming to restore minimal trust with civil society and technical communities.

On the ground, the Municipal Resilience Backchannel Integration begins quietly: DG Energy and DG HOME are tasked with drafting interoperability standards by turn 10, while ENISA launches a pilot audit programme with Lyon, Barcelona, and Helsinki. No new funds are drawn yet—this phase runs on existing crisis-response envelopes and volunteer municipal participation—but we signal that certified nodes will receive priority in next year’s Digital Europe allocation. It is not top-down control, but a scaffold for what already works—because when capital is near zero, legitimacy must come from function, not fiat.
