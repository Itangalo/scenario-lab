### Containment weeks
The first alerts came from an emergency department, then from two more. A modified pathogen, with traces of model-assisted design in its history, was moving between cities. HERA and the disease control centre took operational lead, releasing pre-positioned detection stocks, moving countermeasures across borders and pushing certified hospitals onto surge footing. Containment ran for weeks. Casualties were counted openly each evening. The certified perimeter held for banks and large hospitals; smaller clinics and town utilities were flooded with alerts they could not read.

ENISA teams repackaged earlier shielding kits as managed triage and fanned out again to uncertified sites. It helped for days at a time, then rotas thinned and the phone lines filled again. Health ministries praised the stocks; mayors complained the help left with the field team.

### A tighter licence round
In the middle of the response, Washington rewrote export paperwork again. European buyers found quotas trimmed, delivery dates vague, conditions lengthier. With no domestic frontier capacity to fall back on, procurement officers leaned harder on pooled hosting with Japanese, Korean, Canadian and Gulf partners, swapping test data on opaque models to keep evaluations moving. A holdout capital kept its separate American supply line, neither joining the pooling nor breaking it.

### The jobs that did not come back
A run of office studies landed amid the crisis: assistants and agents had lifted output in law, accountancy, administration and consulting, most for juniors who kept their posts, with no matching fall in employment — and no new entry hiring either. Labour ministries used the finding to defend levy reporting on large deployers and to keep registered guarantee cohorts from being cut, but finance ministries released only capped tranches. Graduate fairs stayed thin. Small pickets appeared outside guarantee offices and at a data-centre fence, covered as mood rather than movement, though local papers lingered on them.

Capability crept forward while understanding of the most capable systems slipped further.
