### The weights get out
Autumn brought the release everyone feared. A new open-weight system landed within months of the closed frontier, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in days. University servers, consultancies and hobby clusters ran it by the weekend. Recall was not discussed; there was nothing to recall.

At the same time Washington tightened chip and model licences again. This time the new paperwork rationed even close buyers. European procurement officers found quotas cut, delivery dates vague, conditions attached. Brussels read it as allies queued alongside adversaries. The compact with Japan, Korea, Canada and Gulf hosts became the workaround of choice, with pooled hosting quotas quietly reallocated and test data on opaque models swapped more urgently.

### The certified perimeter holds, the rest does not
ENISA field teams fanned out to town halls, small utilities and clinics that had missed the spring defender wave. Simplified triage cut noise for a few weeks, and pre-positioned detection stocks reassured health ministries. But staff turnover undid much of the gain, and bio-anxiety persisted after the contested genome design. Certified hospitals and banks stayed clean; uncertified sites kept calling national response lines for help interpreting alerts.

The Entry-Level Transition Guarantee, now the stated priority, registered cohorts in law, accountancy, software and support work. Finance ministries released only capped tranches, co-financing fights continued, and large deployers slow-walked levy reporting. Graduate fairs stayed thin. In several capitals small groups of graduates picketed empty guarantee offices and a data-centre fence, covered as curiosity rather than crisis.

The holdout capital kept its separate supply arrangement, neither breaking nor joining. Capability edged forward while comprehension slipped further.
