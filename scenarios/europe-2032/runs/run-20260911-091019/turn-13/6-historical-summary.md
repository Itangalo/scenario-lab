By late 2030 open-weights near-frontier spread irreversibly across universities, firms and hobby clusters.

A modified pathogen with traces of model-assisted design triggered weeks of containment led by HERA and disease control centre; certified perimeter held for banks and large hospitals while smaller clinics and utilities were overwhelmed. Casualty counts closed out openly; repackaged shielding kits helped the tail but field help was seen as transient and bio-anxiety over dependence persisted.

Washington suspended leading US model access — quotas cut, contracts under review, no appeal — forcing scramble to re-route triage and procurement tools. Brussels opened a priority queue for pooled capacity with Japan, Korea, Canada and Gulf hosts via civil protection funds; migration was slow due to authentication and test-data issues. A holdout capital kept its separate US line and refused pooling. Washington also pressed the Dutch lithography maker to extend cuts to older tools and servicing; firm slowed compliance, Brussels opened retaliation file with little effect.

Entry-Level Transition Guarantee cohorts were retained but only capped tranches released; graduate marches moved from squares to data-centre fences over empty desks, met with refilled counselling shifts and ordered de-escalation, marred by one night of batons.

Capability edged forward as comprehension of most capable systems slipped further.
