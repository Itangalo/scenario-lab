## In practice
We use what is finished and start only what heals. Under the finished Critical Services Shield, Bio-Shield and Continuity Stack, DG SANTE, HERA and the Energy Council keep hospitals, power and telecoms in degraded-but-running mode, while DG EMPL continues bridge pay and the EEAS holds non-belligerence and the middle-power channel for licensed components and landing-station deconfliction.

The new Trust is run through HERA procurement with EMA conditional pathways: pooled EU orders for doses and vectors with EU-anchored cold chain, hospital sites in France, Spain and Poland first where certified triage already exists, prescription by public clinicians not foreign apps, and incident reporting to ECDC. Health Council is the legal base, EU4Health plus rescEU money, mayors offered reopened clinics and retraining posts in exchange for lifting blanket bans where supervised care robots only are allowed.

On humanoids we do not launch a second instrument: we enforce the finished Deployment Safety Accord locally — no unattended operation, municipal bans respected, night-fire investigations via Europol — and table joint evaluation of control models through the middle-power coalition note to buy time for a limits measure next turn.
