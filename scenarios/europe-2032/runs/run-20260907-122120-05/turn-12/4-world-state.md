### The Digital Onslaught

A wave of AI-scaled ransomware cripples regional hospitals and water treatment plants across three member states, exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities generated by frontier models. The attack, adaptive and self-propagating, overwhelms legacy defences. Attribution remains unclear, but forensic analysis confirms the tooling was model-generated — a new era of automated subversion has arrived.

### Emergency Response

In response, the EU invokes Article 122 TFEU, launching the Emergency Critical Infrastructure Shield. ENISA issues binding standards by June: AI-resistant segmentation, air-gapped controls, and anomaly detection trained on synthetic threats become mandatory. A €1.2 billion fund co-finances upgrades, prioritising vulnerable municipal systems. CERT-EU plans a pan-European live-fire exercise for July. Yet implementation is uneven — national regulators resist central mandates, and smaller operators lack technical capacity. The shield is declared, but not yet built.

### Fractured Cohesion, Rising Pressure

Legal challenges from U.S. AI firms intensify, contesting the Sovereign Evaluation Regime’s extraterritorial reach. Behind closed doors, Dutch and German semiconductor executives warn of disruption as U.S. export controls tighten further, now affecting not just advanced nodes but also mature chips vital to industrial systems. ASML confirms servicing delays, citing jurisdictional pressure — a move felt acutely in EU supply chains.

### Stalled Assurance, Sinking Trust

No progress lands on ai_safety: red-teaming remains theoretical, and monitoring hooks are absent from deployed systems. The gap between capability and control widens. Public sentiment sags further as headlines spotlight ransomware and grid instability. A leaked internal assessment warns of “cascading failure scenarios” in energy systems, eroding confidence in both AI and governance.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Shrinks

The EU’s leverage frays. Despite urgent action, resilience improves only modestly as implementation lags. eu_ai_sovereignty falls to 19.0 — capability rose by 1.5 (−1), and supply_chain_coercion strikes at core compute access (−2). Political capital drops to 27.0 under portfolio strain and the cost of a new large measure. The priority adds further burden. No bonus offsets the toll.
