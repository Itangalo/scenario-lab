## Priority

## In practice
We act through DG CNECT with ENISA, HERA and the AI Office, on a Health/Emergency legal base: emergency allocation of InvestAI and Continuity Bridge capacity to hospitals and ministries, standardised fallback to EU-hosted openweight models vetted in Ljubljana, and cross-border mutual aid for power-rationed sites. Funding is reallocated hosting revenues and civil protection money, not a new envelope we cannot pass.

We pair it with containment, not new law: trigger the Agentic Loss-of-Control protocols — telemetry sharing under the Cyber Command pact, incident reporting, isolation of unsanctioned agents — and we hold the lithography export-authorisation line as reciprocal leverage for restored access without spending capital on a trade fight while the large member state's bilateral deal stands. DG EMPL keeps paying income-bridge advances through the vendor-litigation freeze to prevent the continuity switch reading as Brussels saving systems, not people.
