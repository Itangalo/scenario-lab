## Priority
EU Local Trust and Redress Compact, because without unfreezing siting and restoring minimal trust neither reciprocal chips nor allied models can land, and it directly serves our two-year commitment by making independent capacity politically buildable again.

## In practice
We act through DG JUST and DG ENER with the Employment/Social and Energy Councils: an AI Act Article 72/73 enforcement package — immediate suspension and case-by-case human re-review of the two welfare systems, compensation fund from vendor penalties, conformity audits of Annex III systems with logs actually inspected by the AI Office and national ombudsmen — paired with a siting compact that makes any InvestAI continuity hosting permit conditional on local power-price rebates, water-use caps, and municipal revenue share.

We use the Delft solid-state interface as the local-payoff story, procured through the EIB and Innovation Fund into automaker supply chains tied to permit-zone jobs, while ENISA and Ljubljana keep essential-services fallback running on pooled allied and on-prem open models. DG TRADE keeps the lithography-for-chips allocation regime alive as finished leverage, offered only against restored volume licences, but spends no new capital until mayors lift freezes.
