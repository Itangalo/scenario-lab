## Priority
EU Continuity Fallback for Critical AI Dependencies, because restoring hospitals, ministries and firms cut off this turn outranks even the Shield until continuity is shown.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT, the AI Office and HERA with a 72-hour mapping of cut-off dependencies via NIS2 contact points, then fund fallback inference on EuroHPC and Gigafactory pilot capacity plus allied commercial clouds under Article 122 emergency procurement and reprogrammed Digital Europe funds. Health and interior ministers in the EPSCO/JHA formations are offered cost-cover for re-integration, not new rules.

M3 Shield continues on its ENISA-ACER detection baseline track with audited co-financing, but its staff and money are second call after fallback teams this turn. M1/M2 stay on permitting and grid-queue mediation with EIB, with site decisions explicitly tied to proving domestic inference can carry critical loads when foreign access is withdrawn.
