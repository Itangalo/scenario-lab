## New measure
**EU Open-Weight Surge Shield Extension**
It hardens hospitals, grids, ports and municipal IT against the cyber and fraud tooling the new open-weight frontier puts permanently on private hardware, via rapid patching, detection signatures and mutual-aid playbooks.
This and why now because the release puts ~52-level capability beyond recall within months, and with sovereignty at 20 and Shield teams diverted to fallback duty, distributed misuse is the shock our two-year resilience commitment must absorb first.

## Priority
M3 EU Critical Infrastructure Shield, because fallback continuity held but Shield baselines slipped and the open-weight surge now tests exactly the grid-port-health detection it was built for.

## In practice
We keep DG CNECT and ENISA on Shield delivery as first call again: restore seconded detection teams from fallback duty, use NIS2 implementing-act money and co-financing to force sensor installs in lagging small ports, and run the joint grid-port exercises deferred from spring. The new Extension is run as a Shield work-package, not a parallel bureaucracy — EU-CyCLONe distributes model-abuse signatures, HERA/ECDC adds bio-screening guidance for synthesis providers.

M1/M2 stay alive on a narrow track: EIB and DG ENER mediate grid connections only for pilot sites that prove they can carry critical inference loads, unblocking the conditionality that stalled permitting. We ask Health and JHA Councils for staff retraining cover for the clumsier EU-hosted fallback, in exchange for holding audit and reporting obligations through the surge.
