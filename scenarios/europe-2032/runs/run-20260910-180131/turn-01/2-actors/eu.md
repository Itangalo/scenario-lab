## In practice
We task ENISA and the CER/NIS2 cooperation group to impose binding detection and incident-reporting baselines on transmission system operators and major ports, funded by reprogrammed InvestAI and Connecting Europe Facility resilience envelopes. Energy and Transport Councils carry the mandate, with ACER coordinating joint exercises by spring 2027; reluctant finance ministries are offered co-funding rather than new levies.

The Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) continue on their permitting and site-selection track — DG CNECT and EIB structuring the €200bn vehicles — but without new acceleration this turn while political capital absorbs the Shield. We also fast-track use of the interpretability breakthrough in EU evaluation work via the AI Office, without creating a separate measure.
