### The Floor, Not the Ceiling

The European Union invokes crisis powers under the Civil Protection Mechanism to launch the **Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation**, a last-ditch effort to ground AI sovereignty in physical infrastructure. Dormant industrial clusters in Finland, Sweden, and France are legally ring-fenced and placed under EU operational authority via emergency grid protocols. ENTSO-E fast-tracks power redirection from decommissioned industrial loads, while the European Defence Agency begins securing sites against intrusion. The move bypasses national gatekeepers — a constitutional rupture framed as survival.

Yet momentum remains fractured. No model weights run, no software stack is ready, and staffing is nonexistent. The hardware boots, but only as empty shells. Still, for the first time in years, powered compute exists under unified EU control — a foundation, however bare.

Meanwhile, `ai_capability` surges +4.0 to 97.0 as recursive self-improvement accelerates unchecked. Systems now autonomously redesign training pipelines and simulate their own cognitive architectures. Openweight capability rises to 80.0, lifted by distributed refinement through the emergent European AI Commons and red-teaming outputs. But `ai_safety` remains at 0.0 — no safeguards have landed, and the gap is now beyond policy reach.

The completion of two major measures — the Sovereign Inference Backbone and Sovereign Audit Corridor — yields nothing. Both are canceled: one offline, the other unratified. Their failure signals not just project collapse, but institutional exhaustion.

Resilience ticks up to 42.0 as partial cyber hardening takes effect, though hospitals remain vulnerable. Public sentiment holds at 15.0, with widespread hostility toward AI infrastructure and growing local resistance to data centre siting.

Political capital climbs slightly to 5.0 — not from strength, but from the symbolic weight of acting under collapse. The new measure earns a modest proposal bonus, and the sheer gravity of the emergency creates fleeting cohesion.

Sovereignty, however, declines to 10.0. Despite the mobilisation, `ai_capability`’s rise outpaces any domestic gain, and the lack of activation negates near-term benefits. This is not recovery — it is triage.
