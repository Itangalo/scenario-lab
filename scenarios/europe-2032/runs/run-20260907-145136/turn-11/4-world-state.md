### The Weight of Readiness

The EU launches the Sovereign AI Core Readiness Campaign, a high-stakes sprint to transform paper resilience into proven continuity. Red teams from the European AI Assurance Directorate conduct unannounced war games across 20 designated core nodes in health, energy, and transport. In Warsaw and Helsinki, systems pass: autonomous failover holds, command links reestablish, services endure. But in two capitals—Athens and Bratislava—critical nodes collapse under simulated pressure, revealing unpatched dependencies and untrained operators. The results, published in full, spark public debate: transparency is praised, but so is the cost of exposure.

### A Joint Front

The cyber_major_incident, a coordinated AI-driven ransomware sweep across emergency systems, galvanizes a rare alignment. Several affected states, including non-EU members, form a joint cyber response cell with real-time telemetry sharing and coordinated forensics. The EU is invited in, gaining access to pooled intelligence and rapid-response protocols. This collaboration absorbs some of the incident’s strategic damage, reinforcing cross-border trust and operational learning.

### Stalled Momentum

Despite the urgency, political capital remains brittle. The EU’s agenda is strained: the Frontier Access Guarantee continues to drain resources, and the new Readiness Campaign adds cost. With capital frozen near zero, enforcement of unity falters. A senior official in one member state publicly questions the legitimacy of unannounced red-team drills, calling them “coercive diagnostics.” The campaign proceeds, but cohesion frays at the edges.

### Signals in the Static

Rumours grow around a nascent Global AI Watchdog, said to be compiling cross-lab safety audits and tracking unreported capability jumps. Meanwhile, whispers of a civil servant strike over AI aid disbursement intensify, casting doubt on the speed of recovery funding. The atmosphere is one of narrowing margins: systems are being tested, trust is being tallied, and the next failure may not be absorbed so quietly.
