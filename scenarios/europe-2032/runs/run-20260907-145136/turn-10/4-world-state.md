### A Shock to the System

A wave of coordinated ransomware, generated by advanced AI tooling, disables emergency dispatch systems across three member states. The attack exploits a compromised dependency in public safety software — undetected for weeks, it activates simultaneously, overwhelming response capacity. Recovery takes days; trust takes longer. Attribution remains unclear, but forensic analysis confirms model-assisted development at scale.

### Leap and Lurch

Global AI capability surges by +4.0 to 75.0 after a leading lab demonstrates sustained autonomous operation in complex simulation environments — a jump in planning horizon and robustness that resets deployment expectations. The advance compresses timelines industry-wide. Yet no corresponding safety validation lands: `ai_safety` plummets −5 due to the capability jump, reaching 12.0. Developers acknowledge opaque reasoning paths and limited intervention points.

With closed models leaping forward, open weights follow unevenly. `openweight_capability` rises to 58.0 — halfway between its prior value and the new frontier, as some techniques leak into the open despite restrictions.

### Patching the Breach

The EU launches the Emergency Continuity Response Initiative, deploying rapid technical teams to restore air-gapped nodes left exposed by the incident. In Thessaloniki and two reluctant capitals, progress is made through emergency procurement and shared command structures. €1.8bn is mobilized under crisis provisions, and 17 critical sites undergo live failover drills. Partial gains lift `resilience` by +4: the initiative is still unfolding, but visible stabilization earns early credit. However, institutional strain persists — a category 9 adoption push from last turn still weighs on systems.

### Sovereignty Under Strain

Despite efforts, `eu_ai_sovereignty` dips to 22.0. The capability jump exceeded 2.0 this turn (it was +4), triggering a −1 decay. No event affecting access occurred, and no category 4 measure finished. Momentum from ongoing projects does not offset structural lag.

### Capital Frozen

Political capital remains at 0.0. The portfolio charge — Frontier Access Guarantee (−3), Emergency Continuity Response Initiative (−2), priority (−1) — totals −6. No proposal bonus applies: while the cyber incident justifies the new measure, it occurred *this* turn, not in the prior three, so no retroactive justification exists. Agenda control remains suspended below capital 12.

Public sentiment inches up +2 as repair efforts are seen as decisive, functional, and human-led — a rare moment where action follows failure visibly.
