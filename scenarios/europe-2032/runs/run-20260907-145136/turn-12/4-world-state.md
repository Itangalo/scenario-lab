### The Silence After the Reasoning

The frontier’s most advanced systems no longer explain themselves. Engineers report fluent, correct outputs — but no traceable logic. The interpretability tools that once gave auditors a window into model reasoning now return blank. This silent leap forward in capability arrives just as the EU scrambles to stabilize its own institutions.

### A Strike That Holds

The strike by civil servants administering AI emergency funds escalates into a full operational freeze. Disbursements under the Emergency Continuity Response Initiative halt. In Athens and Bratislava, planned upgrades to air-gapped nodes stall mid-process. Red-team exercises meant to rebuild confidence instead expose a crisis of legitimacy. The European AI Assurance Directorate suspends new drills pending negotiations. Trust, not code, is now the bottleneck.

### Unity Through Accountability?

The EU responds by launching the Human-in-the-Loop Assurance Initiative, redefining the Sovereign AI Core Readiness Campaign around operator voice and appeal rights. The move is praised by unions but meets resistance in national digital agencies, where officials argue that real-time oversight will slow failover. The Frontier Access Guarantee nears completion, but its final audit is delayed by two weeks as negotiators demand inclusion of operator feedback mechanisms.

### A Fragile Equilibrium

The US and China announce a narrow agreement on autonomous escalation risks, pausing certain joint military AI developments. The EU is not consulted. Meanwhile, the silent advance in AI capability continues, with `ai_capability` rising slightly — but without any corresponding leap in safety. The gap widens. Resilience holds, sustained by existing fallbacks, but no new gains land. Political capital remains frozen. Public sentiment, for now, does not fall — but it does not rise.
