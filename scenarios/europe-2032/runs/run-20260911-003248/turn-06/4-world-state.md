### The jump and the machines
Winter turned on two announcements weeks apart. An American lab demonstrated a system that planned, coded and ran tools for days with little supervision, making December forecasts look dated overnight. Almost simultaneously, commercial humanoids moved from pilot warehouses to sale: Chinese-built machines for logistics, digging and clearance, with armed variants shown in the same quarter.

In Brussels the timing cut both ways. The new administration in Washington took office offering structured allied access on published terms, joint testing and aligned export rules. The Commission rushed negotiators to lock in tiers for researchers and evaluators, insisting access would sit alongside the two domestic gigafactory sites, not replace them. Talks opened cordially but produced paper rather than capacity: access schedules, evaluation protocols, council mandates. Nothing flowed yet.

At home, two long builds paid out. The permitting and capital package for data centres cleared its final legal hurdles, giving the two advanced sites grid priority and a state-aid shield. The bio-shield and continuity regime — sequencing networks, stockpiles, rationed compute for hospitals — was declared operational. Large hospitals held through the spring; triage assistants kept waiting lists down enough for mayors to cite.

Small towns felt a different turn. The new municipal pact sent wage bridges, retraining money and staffing pacts through employment and regional channels, with the investment bank leaning on reinsurers to stay in small-utility risk and civil-protection teams drilling manual chlorination fallback. Where crews arrived, boil notices ended and resentment eased. Where they did not, the drills themselves were blamed as Brussels theatre, and insurers still repriced.

The robots landed hardest on the industrial base Europe still leads. Chinese supply chains set price and volume; American control software set behaviour. Works councils in automotive and machinery regions warned of dexterity following within a year, with no sector to retreat into. Public mood soured further, and the Commission's insistence that allied access plus home build equals autonomy convinced few. Relief at Washington's tone survived; belief that it solved dependence did not.
