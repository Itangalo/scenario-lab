## In practice
We run the rollout through ENISA and DG CNECT with the Critical Services Shield operators, using the Cybersecurity Act certification and CER Directive duties as legal base: EU-funded sensor and auto-patch kits, joint procurement for municipalities, and exercised playbooks linking the manual-fallback crews to the new detection. Telecoms and Competitiveness Councils carry it, with DIGITAL and EIB backstop money front-loaded to small utilities first to show delivery where drills were filmed as theatre.

On M8 we keep DG GROW/TRADE holding procurement preference for EU-controlled stacks and screening on dumped hardware and one-sided software terms, but sequence enforcement behind the defence rollout so plant managers get protection before new sourcing costs. DG EMPL keeps dexterity transition funds with works councils, explicitly tied to the two energized gigafactory regions and port pilots to keep the two-year commitment visible.
