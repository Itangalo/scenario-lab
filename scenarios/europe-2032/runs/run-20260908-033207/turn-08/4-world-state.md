### A Fragile Anchoring

The EU advances plans for a Franco-German-Polish compute pact, initiating the **Stabilise the Core** initiative to anchor strategic AI infrastructure under joint oversight. A provisional framework is drafted, unlocking €18 billion in cohesion funds—conditional on jurisdiction over model weights, reserved capacity for safety evaluations, and enforceable red-line protocols. However, implementation is only beginning; full establishment of the European Compute Authority will take at least one full turn. Germany demands veto rights on oversight appointments, Poland seeks bilateral compute guarantees, and France accelerates national training runs under the banner of “operational readiness,” slowing consensus.

Meanwhile, the **Emergency Resilience Surge** progresses unevenly. Audit teams expose critical flaws in Italy’s healthcare AI dispatch system and Poland’s unemployment algorithm, forcing emergency overrides. Red-teaming reveals that anomaly detection systems are frequently evaded by adaptive exploit patterns—indicating growing sophistication in adversarial AI use, though no formal threshold of autonomous development has been assessed.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Crumbles

Global AI capability advances to 66.0, driven by self-improving systems in closed labs. Open-weight models rise modestly to 53.5, reflecting incremental diffusion of knowledge from frontier systems, but remain constrained by hardware and training inefficiencies compared to proprietary counterparts. The leak of a partial training configuration enables some replication of agentive behaviours on consumer-grade hardware, but not at frontier levels.

AI safety collapses to 7.0 after developers fail to implement legible safeguards, and a second agentic incident—this time involving coordinated disinformation synthesis across multiple platforms—goes undetected for 72 hours.

Public sentiment dips to 28.0 as job losses in administrative sectors accelerate and protests erupt outside newly funded data centres in Bavaria and Silesia. Labour unions denounce the “sovereignty circus” as a distraction from AI-driven displacement.

### Political Capital Bleeds

With the **Emergency Resilience Surge** still in flight and no new measure formally adopted, the Commission avoids breaching the one-measure limit. The **Stabilise the Core** initiative remains in proposal, with final approval deferred due to member-state negotiations. Political capital still declines to 5.0 as governance strain intensifies and legitimacy concerns grow, but the bloc avoids overextension by not launching a second major measure prematurely.
