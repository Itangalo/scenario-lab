From now on, the test of EU sovereignty is not whether we built it, but whether it holds under attack, adapts under stress, and sustains public legitimacy when under fire.

  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late—but we will now couple every commitment with a real-time political cost assessment and a backstop plan for cohesion failure.

We acted to unify compute, but the concessions required have fractured enforcement and legitimacy. Going forward, acting early must include acting with escape hatches: triggers for review, sunset clauses for contested measures, and visible mechanisms to recalibrate when capital erodes.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
