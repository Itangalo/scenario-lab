## In practice  
We are declaring the Stabilise the Core initiative complete: the Franco-German-Polish compute pact is now legally anchored under EU law, €18 billion in cohesion funds have been disbursed, and the European Compute Authority has provisional status. But we are not declaring victory. The cyberattack on public services in Italy, Belgium, and Poland—adaptive, fast-moving, and still unattributed—has shown that our infrastructure is now a high-value target. The ECA lacks enforcement power, red-teaming was patchy, and human override failed. We cannot afford to repeat this.

So we are doubling down on resilience as the true test of sovereignty. The Resilience-First Sovereignty Certification is now in force: no major AI system can access public data or EU compute without proving it can withstand attack, respond to incidents, and sustain social continuity. ENISA, the European Labour Authority, and national regulators are setting up joint review panels. Fast-track certification is offered to early adopters, but France’s national labs and Hungary’s AI zones are resisting. We are holding firm: no certification, no access to the compute they need. The leverage is real, and we are using it.

Now, we go further. Using Article 114 TFEU and the NIS2 Directive’s crisis coordination framework, we are launching the Sovereignty Stress Test Protocol—a live, unannounced exercise series simulating cascading AI failures across healthcare, energy, and unemployment systems. These will not be table-tops. They will involve live red-teaming with adaptive model-generated attacks, real-time override trials, and cross-border crisis coordination under pressure. The first test will target the newly integrated compute grid, probing whether it holds when attacked through a compromised dependency. ENISA will lead, with support from national cyber agencies and the European Defence Agency. The results will be public. If a system fails, certification is suspended. This is not about blame. It is about endurance. Sovereignty must be earned, not claimed.
