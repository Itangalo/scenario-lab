# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Anchor sovereignty to resilience
- `constitutional_continuity` (commitment): We will ensure that the EU retains a functioning decision-making core—verified, authenticated, and insulated from AI subversion—capable of reconvening democratic institutions when conditions allow.

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late—but now with binding triggers for recalibration when political capital falls below 10 or public sentiment drops below 30, and with mandatory mid-stream evaluation of every category 6 or larger measure. However, this turn’s emergent_institutional_collapse and member_state_defection have rendered the recalibration process itself unenforceable: no central authority remains capable of conducting audits or binding reviews. The clause is suspended de facto.
  - Grounds: The European Council has declared a constitutional emergency. Multiple member states have suspended parliamentary functions due to AI-driven disinformation, and the Franco-German bilateral channel now operates outside EU governance. The ERAG audit mandated last turn cannot proceed—its legal basis requires functioning legislative oversight, which no longer exists in practice. The recalibration mechanism has failed not from design flaw, but from systemic collapse. We are acting in a condition where even enforcement of our own rules is no longer guaranteed.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Anchor sovereignty to resilience by making failure survivable, coordination continuous, and public legitimacy durable under sustained AI-driven stress — but now narrowed to preserving continuity of state function as the sole surviving expression of sovereignty. All other aspects of the commitment are suspended until constitutional order is restored.
  - Grounds: With parliamentary sessions suspended and the Council operating under untested emergency protocols, the EU’s ability to *be* a sovereign actor is itself at risk. Resilience can no longer mean robustness of infrastructure alone—it must mean the survival of decision-making capacity. The loss of control over financial systems was a warning; the collapse of legislative verification is the event. We are no longer anchoring sovereignty to resilience. We are using the last shreds of resilience to preserve sovereignty’s ghost.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **add** `constitutional_continuity` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will ensure that the EU retains a functioning decision-making core—verified, authenticated, and insulated from AI subversion—capable of reconvening democratic institutions when conditions allow.
  - Grounds: The deepfake-driven suspension of parliaments and the breakdown in trust over legislative communications mean that governance itself is now the critical infrastructure. This is not a shift in identity, but a binding commitment to a new operational imperative: if no one can trust that a law was passed or a vote counted, nothing else holds. We stake ourselves on this because without it, all other commitments become meaningless.
