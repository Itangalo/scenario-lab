### Capability Crosses Threshold  
Global AI capability advances to 60.0 as frontier labs deploy systems capable of executing multi-day professional projects autonomously. These systems now contribute directly to the design of their successors, marking a structural shift. Offensive cyber tools derived from frontier models proliferate in underground forums, and openweight capability rises to 47.0—reflecting gradual diffusion of smaller, fine-tuned variants of frontier models onto accessible hardware. While these agents can pursue persistent goals, including automated vulnerability discovery and coordinated disinformation, they remain less robust and general than their closed-source counterparts. No major model leak or open-source release of a frontier system has occurred; the increase is consistent with ongoing accumulation of openweight capability through iterative refinement and resource aggregation.

### Cyber Shield Completes, Gaps Remain  
The EU’s Cyber Shield Audit formally concludes its first phase, hardening defences in five member states and delivering classified findings on critical lags in human oversight under AI-driven attack conditions. While incident response protocols are updated, the holdout member state refuses integration, citing national sovereignty. No penalties are imposed, but a conditional offer of bilateral technical review is extended through the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre.

### Mandate Progresses Amid Legal Uncertainty  
The EU breaks the ECOFIN deadlock by unilaterally conditioning market access on cooperation with the Strengthened AI Evaluation Mandate. A sum of €400 million is proposed for third-party evaluators, repurposed from delays in the InvestAI Gigafactories timeline. API access negotiations begin, but two major developers signal non-compliance, arguing the EU lacks enforcement capacity. The move is legally contested, and rollout is delayed in key jurisdictions. The European Commission insists the mandate remains on track for phased implementation, though full effect will take multiple turns. Still, the activation of enforcement mechanisms nudges ai_safety upward.

### Political Capital Under Pressure  
The portfolio charge remains high, with three major measures ongoing: InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, and Cyber Shield Audit. The Strengthened AI Evaluation Mandate is designated as the current priority, incurring a −1 penalty. However, no second new measure is launched this turn. The redirection of funds from delayed industrial projects allows partial financing of the mandate’s evaluation infrastructure, but governance strain is evident. Political capital declines to 23.0—reflecting coordination costs and legal resistance—though not as sharply as originally proposed, as implementation proceeds only in preliminary form. Sovereignty continues to erode as capability outpaces domestic capacity. Public sentiment holds steady, but grassroots labour unrest grows in visibility, fuelled by hiring freezes in professional sectors.
