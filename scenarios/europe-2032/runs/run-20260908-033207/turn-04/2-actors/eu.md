## In practice  
We are treating the completion of the Cyber Shield Audit as a threshold moment: the first phase of assessments has concluded in five member states, revealing not only persistent vulnerabilities in energy and logistics but also a dangerous lag in human-in-the-loop decision cycles under AI-driven attack conditions. The holdout state remains outside the audit, but the invocation of Article 21 of NIS2 now carries real weight — we are preparing a conditional enforcement package that ties access to EU digital infrastructure funds to compliance, and we have offered a bilateral review with the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre to preserve sovereignty while securing the network. The classified cross-validated report will be delivered to national security councils in July, and we will use it to argue that collective resilience cannot be opt-in when the threat is systemic.

At the same time, we are breaking the logjam on the Strengthened AI Evaluation Mandate. ECOFIN’s hesitation on the €1.2 billion funding is no longer acceptable in light of this turn’s incident. We are now acting unilaterally under the AI Act’s enforcement provisions: the European AI Office will be empowered to condition market access for any model above 50.0 capability on verifiable cooperation with independent evaluation. We are releasing €400 million immediately from the Innovation Fund to stand up third-party evaluator contracts and secure API access, with the remainder tied to milestones in test protocol development and staff onboarding. Developers who refuse cooperation will face suspension of deployment rights in the EU — a lever we have not used, but now must. This is not a request; it is a recalibration of power between the frontier and the public interest.

The Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package continue on permitting fast-tracks in Finland and Portugal, but we are making clear that the legitimacy of these investments depends on control: building compute without oversight is not sovereignty, it is dependency with extra steps. We are not waiting for the full package to land to assert our authority — because if we do not govern the systems that will govern us, we will not survive long enough to own them.
