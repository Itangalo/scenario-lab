## New measure
**EU Displaced-Worker and Claimant Safety Net**
Creates an EU-topped-up wage insurance, rapid retraining voucher and independent audit-and-appeal standard for automated benefit and hiring systems, paid from ESF+ to blunt the welfare-scandal fallout and early displacement.
This is category 7 Labour and social protection with a category 9 trust arm, because with sentiment at 30 and capital at 27 we cannot hold the sovereignty-and-resilience commitment without stopping the domestic bleed — lawful-but-harmful automation is now the binding constraint on everything else.

## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because reinsurers excluding OT interruption and ports/grids throttling throughput after a real hospital-and-logistics hit outranks even the welfare repair this turn, and only exercised fallback keeps services running.

## In practice
We surge M3 through the Civil Protection Mechanism and ENISA as an OT backstop: EU bridge guarantees where reinsurers pulled back conditional on mandatory manual-fallback drills, plus HERA/ENISA detection kits to the hit municipalities and port operators, with health and transport ministers co-signing throughput safeguards. DG CNECT holds M1/M2 to the two viable gigafactory sites with EIB guarantees and power reservations, pausing bidding wars while foundry supply is squeezed and telling other sites plainly that grid comes before concrete.

In parallel we launch the Safety Net via EPSCO and ESF+: member states get top-up funds for wage insurance and 3-month retraining placement only if automated welfare/employment systems submit to the Clearinghouse (M4) audit log and human-appeal standard, using the court ruling as legal base. The Clearinghouse itself pushes the interpretability check as interim certification and logs the new open-weight release for municipal SOCs — we cannot recall it, so we distribute detection instead.
