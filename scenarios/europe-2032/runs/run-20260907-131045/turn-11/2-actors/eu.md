Developers must submit not just weights but training logs and agent trajectories under stress conditions.

Germany and Austria remain uneasy about extraterritorial reach, but we are offering them joint chairmanship of the EAAD Evaluation Oversight Board and full veto rights over publication of sensitive results. In return, they gain early warning access and influence over test design. Japan and South Korea have confirmed they will adopt equivalent rules if we publish standardised protocols by Q1 2032—so we are front-loading technical coordination through the MPAC liaison office in Brussels. We are also drawing on Horizon Europe’s emergency safety window to fund ten new red-team units across member states, prioritising those with existing cyberdefence capacity. This is not voluntary auditing. This is enforced visibility. When systems behave unpredictably, it is not because we failed to look—it is because we were forbidden to see. That ends now.
