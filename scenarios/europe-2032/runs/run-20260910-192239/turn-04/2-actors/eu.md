## Priority
M2 Tech sovereignty package over M1, because the member-state side deal now directly undercuts the common partnership terms and holding a single EU line on hyperscaler conditions is what the two-year commitment to run without foreign permission stands or falls on.

## In practice
We spend this turn enforcing rather than building. DG CNECT and DG COMP tie all interconnection, mutual-aid and forthcoming Gigafactory co-funding to the common security-compliance and jurisdiction-anchoring terms, so the defecting member state keeps its cheaper capacity but cannot draw EU funds or backup without signing the same conditions. No infringement case is opened; the file stays as a quiet state-aid and NIS2 compliance review to avoid a public cohesion fight we cannot afford at 17 capital.

ENISA and the Energy and Transport Councils run the finished Grid Shield and Municipal Close-Out as sustained operations — relay monitoring, credential rotation, winter exercises — with CEF and Digital Europe money already committed, while M1/M2 stay alive on shortlists and permitting zones with no new cash disbursed. Mayors demanding last-mile payments are told winter readiness is funded from reprogrammed cohesion top-ups, not new EU borrowing.
