### Scandal and Response

A national social insurance agency’s AI-driven eligibility system is exposed for systematically denying benefits to disabled claimants, with an ombudsman’s report confirming algorithmic bias and lack of human review. The automated_decision_scandal triggers public outrage, particularly in regions already skeptical of top-down digital governance. Trust in AI-mediated public services erodes, dragging public sentiment down to 35.0.

In response, the EU fast-tracks the Sovereign Audit Access Directive, leveraging the political momentum and its new seat in a joint cyber intelligence framework. The directive mandates real-time audit access to model weights and training logs for all high-capability systems operating in the EU. However, fierce resistance emerges: U.S. hyperscalers warn of IP exposure, and some member states question the legal viability of compelling foreign firms. The compromise—tiered access and sovereign-hosted auditing—gains limited buy-in but delays full implementation.
