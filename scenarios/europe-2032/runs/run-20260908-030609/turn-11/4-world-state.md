### Medical Access Initiative Lifts Sentiment  
AI-driven therapies for neurodegenerative diseases emerge, capturing public attention. The EU launches the Emergency Access to Frontier Models for Public Health, securing conditional access to foreign systems. Providers accept data sovereignty terms in exchange for market access, and the EMA begins oversight. Public sentiment rises as treatments become visible, though concerns linger about dependency.

Political capital remains flat. The completion of the Joint Threat Response Integration Directive provides a modest operational win, but the ongoing portfolio burden—Sovereign AI Core, Joint Threat Response, Middle Power Leverage Accord, and the new Emergency Access initiative—stretches institutional capacity. No priority shift is declared, and the lack of focus limits political gains. The Emergency Access initiative yields public goodwill but introduces strategic exposure under the current US retrenchment posture, tempering upside. No breakthrough is implied on the legal basis for EU-wide authority; discussions continue without resolution.
