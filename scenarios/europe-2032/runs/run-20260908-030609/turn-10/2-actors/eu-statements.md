# Statements: The European Union (turn 10)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Maintain sovereign control over AI systems that are critical to public function, security, and democratic resilience

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Anchor the operation of AI systems essential to public function, security, and democratic resilience within EU jurisdiction, ensuring enforceable oversight, operational continuity, and accountability under Union law
  - Grounds: The openweight capability rise to 54.0 confirms that containment through access control is no longer viable; sovereignty must now be defined not by ownership of hardware or models, but by control over *operation*. The two-year commitment must evolve from securing compute to securing function—ensuring that critical AI systems are not only hosted in the EU but are legally, technically, and institutionally answerable to it.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
