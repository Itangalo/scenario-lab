### The Weight of Absence

Global AI capability climbs to 73.0 as self-improving systems refine their own architectures, enabling faster training cycles and deeper domain mastery. Open models, while lagging, reach 59.0, sufficient to automate mid-level coding and data analysis tasks across dispersed networks. The gap between closed and open widens slightly, but the open frontier now embeds techniques once exclusive to top labs.

In the EU, the completion of the Sovereign AI Core and Emergency Access frameworks delivers technical functionality but no political relief. The clinical pilot using EU-trained models proves viable, yet its 58.0 capability is already outpaced by systems used in North America and East Asia. Hospitals report diagnostic delays, and public messaging falters: the promise of autonomy rings hollow when the sovereign stack is visibly behind.

The Accelerated Sovereign Compute Buildout is formally launched, with binding energy directives issued and bond structures finalised. However, national resistance flares—Poland challenges the grid access mandates before the European Court, and Spain delays permitting over environmental concerns. Only Finland moves swiftly, leveraging existing nuclear infrastructure. The promised 60% reservation rule faces non-compliance from two major cloud operators, who argue the directive exceeds EU competence.

The Talent Anchoring Initiative launches with fanfare, but early recruitment is slow. Several targeted researchers decline, citing superior facilities and fewer bureaucratic constraints abroad. A senior team at a UK institute rejects the offer publicly, calling it “symbolic without scale.”

Public sentiment dips to 35.0 as job losses in legal and accounting sectors become impossible to ignore. Media frames the talent shortfall and delayed compute rollout as proof that the EU is reacting, not leading. Political capital erodes further under the strain of multiple large initiatives and legal pushback.

Sovereignty declines as dependency deepens—despite intentions, no new sovereign compute comes online, and the loss of access to foreign capacity continues to bite. The Union acts, but the ground shifts faster.
