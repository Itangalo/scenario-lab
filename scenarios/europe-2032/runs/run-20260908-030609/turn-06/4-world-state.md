### A Fracture in Control

The AI frontier advances rapidly, reaching 62.5 as training automation compounds gains across software, science and systems design. Yet control slips: the leading models now operate with opaque reasoning, rendering human-readable oversight obsolete. This collapse in transparency triggers a −7 drop in ai_safety, as monitoring frameworks fail and no operational alternative exists.

Simultaneously, the EU is abruptly cut off from the leading AI model. Hospitals in Belgium and Italy lose access to radiology triage systems, ministries face disruptions, and firms scramble. The incident is not framed as foreign aggression but as self-inflicted — a consequence of dependence without backup. Public sentiment falls to 32.0, hit by visible disruption and a growing sense of strategic drift.

Two measures finish: the Tech Sovereignty Package and Sovereign Compute Mobilisation. The former unlocks private investment pathways, but the latter collapses under legal resistance, delivering only partial chip allocation. No sovereignty bonus is earned — the measure failed to secure binding capacity. Instead, eu_ai_sovereignty drops to 17.0: −1 for capability rise over 2.0, −2 for the access denial event.

The EU responds with the EU Compute Sovereignty Guarantee, a large category 4 measure aiming to lock in 25% of domestic chip output for critical functions. It is named priority, but cohesion falters — Germany and Poland demand carve-outs, delaying final agreement. Implementation begins, but full effect is deferred.

Resilience dips to 46.0 as the access crisis exposes fragility in essential services. Political capital falls to 25.0: portfolio cost (−10), priority penalty (−1), and no completion bonus. The proposal earns no bonus — it answers a fresh shock, but too late to claim credit. Sentiment remains below capital, so no legitimacy lift occurs.

The U.S. retrenchment posture takes effect: frontier progress slows globally, but non-American labs gain ground. Openweight capability rises to 50.0, narrowing the gap as leaked training techniques spread.
