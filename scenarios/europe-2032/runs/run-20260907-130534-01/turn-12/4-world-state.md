The offensive leap is confirmed when a model demonstrates zero-day discovery at scale—no general intelligence, but a narrow, verifiable jump in cyber operations.

With no defensive measure yet complete, resilience erodes further. The Oversight Board restructuring is underway, but it delivers no immediate gain. Civil society inclusion is welcomed in Finland and Norway, but Germany and Austria remain sceptical, demanding judicial oversight. Public sentiment, already at rock bottom, holds steady in hostility.
