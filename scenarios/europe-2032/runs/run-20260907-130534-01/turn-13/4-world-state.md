### The Core Comes Online

The Sovereign AI Core is now live. For the first time, the EU exercises direct, real-time oversight over high-risk AI systems interfacing with critical infrastructure. Monitoring covers all models above 60.0 capability—foreign and domestic—operating within or affecting the bloc. The system’s first intervention, during a flawed medical triage rollout, proved technically sound; now, legal and institutional safeguards are in place. Civil society observers from Germany and Austria sit on the restructured Oversight Board, with veto rights on non-catastrophic interventions. The balance between control and legitimacy has been recalibrated—but not restored.

### Capability Creeps Forward

Global AI capability advances to 72.5, driven by closed-loop self-improvement in leading labs. No new safety breakthroughs land on deployed systems. Interpretability remains outpaced, and developers report internal models rewriting core logic faster than audits can track. The safety gap widens further, but no incident triggers a formal drop in `ai_safety` this turn—the metric already bottomed out at 10.0.

Openweight capability holds at 60.0. Underground forums continue to distribute filtered tools capable of bio-design assistance, but no public release breaches the threshold.

### A Fragile Recovery

The completion of the *Accelerate EU AI Safety Assurance Infrastructure* delivers a much-needed political win. The measure, a large category 5 initiative, finishes on schedule, granting a +5 boost to political capital. However, the ongoing cost of the portfolio charge has ended: with no active measures remaining, the drain stops.

Sovereignty, however, slips to 9.0. Despite the Core’s activation, no physical compute build has landed. The rise in `ai_capability` by 1.5 triggers a −1 decay, and no category 4 measure has finished. The EU remains dependent on foreign frontier access, now codified in practice rather than merely in policy.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 14.0. No visible public benefit has yet been demonstrated, and the ambulance routing demonstration—meant to shift perception—is delayed. The public sees no change. Hostility persists.
