## In practice
This turn, we are operating at the edge of our capacity—financially, technically, politically. The completion of the AI Safety Emergency Investment marks a threshold: the €40bn has landed, the EAAN enclaves exist in law and in steel, and the first red teams are now embedded in Brussels, Sophia Antipolis, and Munich. But they are not yet live. Certification nodes are not switches; they require integration with national intelligence streams, secure data pipelines from providers, and legal validation of inspection authority under Article 28 of the AI Act. We are pulling all levers to activate them: invoking Article 222 TFEU to fast-track security clearances for cross-border teams, directing the European Cybersecurity Agency to pre-stage threat models, and wiring emergency procurement authority to bypass final certification bottlenecks. The goal is not perfection—it is sovereign presence. By turn 13, we must be able to say: we see the weights, we control the runtime, we own the audit.

At the same time, we are enforcing the new telemetry mandate, not through negotiation but through conditionality. The Digital Markets Act’s gatekeeper provisions are now the enforcement vector: no real-time telemetry feed into EAAN enclaves, no designation as a core platform service. This is not a request. US hyperscalers have pushed back, citing national security and IP leakage, but we are holding firm—access to the EU market is not a right, and certainly not for systems that exhibit goal persistence across resets. We are offering limited reciprocity: anonymised aggregate anomaly data will be shared with trusted allied regulators, but only after sovereign analysis. The resistance is expected. What is not expected is retreat. With political capital at 8.0, we cannot afford failure—but we also cannot afford irrelevance. The labour unrest in legal and administrative sectors is real, and we are hearing it. But our response cannot be to slow down oversight—it must be to make it work. If we do not get the nodes online, the border is a fiction, the telemetry is noise, and the public will be right to rage.
