## New measure
**EU Town Pools On-Scheme Compact**
Brings remaining off-books fuel, spares and breaker-log pools on-scheme with paid reimbursement and shared guarding, so sabotage stops hitting unprotected stores.
A small preparedness and resilience measure now because with US access dark and capital at 5, the only capacity we can actually hold is hand restoration and lit wards, and that fails while pools stay targets outside the rules.

## Priority
Priority is M10 EU Town Pools On-Scheme Compact, because it is the only new instrument and it directly serves the two-year commitment to hold essential services and social order through cut-off and sabotage.

## In practice
We execute through DG ECHO and ENISA with the crisis network and municipal associations on existing Civil Protection Mechanism money: registration with same-day reimbursement for fuel and transformers, standardized paper breaker-logs, and mutual-aid pairing of accepting towns with refusing ones.

We keep living off European soil — showcase scheduling assistants on EU default, no lithography concessions, Tokyo/Seoul spares talks continued — and we keep ENISA certified failure-mode playbooks in clinics and courts with published dashboards, while police guards shift from showcase-only to joint guarding of on-scheme stores to remove the guarded-computers-over-neighbourhoods story.
