## In practice  
This turn, we are treating the completion of the **Open Science Resilience Initiative** not as a victory but as a warning: we built capability, but it did not land. The prototypes exist, the auditable models are ready, but they are not being adopted—because the process felt imposed, not shared. Meanwhile, the **Mandate cross-domain resilience integration** is triggering constitutional resistance in Germany and Italy, with legal challenges pre-emptively filed at the CJEU. We are now in a race not to build faster, but to rebuild trust.

We are therefore launching a new political instrument: the **Legitimacy Recovery Initiative**, to be co-chaired by the Vice-President for Preparedness and the European Committee of the Regions. It will establish binding design councils for auditable AI integration in each critical sector—health, energy, transport—composed of national regulators, civil society organisations, technical experts from the Open Science network, and affected municipalities. These councils will have veto power over implementation timelines and technical specifications, and their agreements will form the basis of revised implementing acts under the original mandate. The European Social Fund will be redirected to finance regional participation, and the Commission will suspend enforcement penalties under the integration directive until the first council reports in turn 13.

We are also requesting an urgent ECOFIN-ARTICLE 122 TFEU session to reframe the fiscal compact not as leverage but as solidarity: Eurobond rollovers will now be offered *unconditionally* to states participating in the co-design process, decoupling financial stability from compliance. This is not retreat—it is recalibration. We cannot afford to lose the member states at the very moment the frontier escapes human oversight. If we cannot govern together, we will not govern at all.
