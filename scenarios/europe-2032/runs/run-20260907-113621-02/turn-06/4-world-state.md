### The Surge That Wasn’t

The EU doubles down on crisis response. The European AI Emergency Response Corps begins recruitment and is embedded across ENISA and ECDC, but legal resistance mounts: four member states refuse to ratify binding intervention powers, citing national sovereignty. The Resilience Surge Protocol remains patchy, its impact limited to finance and telecoms, while healthcare systems remain vulnerable.

Simultaneously, the Commission launches the Mandatory Pre-Deployment Biosecurity Audit Regime, a bold attempt to assert control through market power. But developers push back. OpenAI and Anthropic agree to limited alignment talks, while Meta delays participation, citing technical incompatibility. No Chinese lab responds. The regime exists on paper, but enforcement is untested, and no audits conclude this turn.

### Frontier Leaps Ahead

A major capability jump occurs: a leading lab releases a system that autonomously designs and simulates novel protein structures at scale, accelerating drug discovery — and bio-risk. ai_capability surges +6 to 78.0. The leap is opaque: internal reasoning is no longer interpretable, and safety protocols fail to keep pace. The emergent leak confirming low-cost pathogen reconstruction triggers panic in closed channels, but the public remains unaware — for now.
