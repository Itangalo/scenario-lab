### A Surge of Power, a Crack in the Line

The frontier of AI capability advances to 64.0, reflecting the ongoing global trajectory established at the scenario’s outset. A leading lab demonstrates an agent system capable of chaining multi-day exploits across digital and physical domains without human intervention—impressive, but within the expected bounds of progress. The demonstration accelerates interest and concern, yet does not rupture prior deployment timelines.

Simultaneously, a major breach of cohesion emerges: a member state signs a bilateral agreement with a US hyperscaler, securing access to a 68.0 model outside EU oversight. The move, defended domestically as pragmatic necessity, is met in Brussels with alarm. It directly undermines the AI Containment Protocol, now being enforced through Article 16 TFEU and dual-key access arrangements in Saint-Malo and Kiruna.

In response, the EU doubles down on containment. The European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) begins preparatory audits under the Sovereign Model Assurance Framework (SMAF), now formally adopted but not yet fully implemented. The first sovereign model run enters pre-training review—a procedural milestone, not a systemic shift. Construction advances on the Swedish InvestAI Gigafactory, though grid constraints in Norrland threaten delays despite RePowerEU-backed transmission upgrades.

The Critical Infrastructure Resilience Surge (CIRS) formally concludes, transitioning to ENISA as a monitoring function—yet patching gaps persist. To close them, the EU proposes the Resilience Reinforcement Initiative, mandating AI-assisted monitoring across energy, transport, and biosecurity sectors and establishing cross-border incident handover protocols. The initiative is announced and scoped but will not begin implementation until the next turn, respecting governance and staffing limits.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 25.0. Trust is not growing, and the political cost of enforcement is rising. The Anti-Coercion Instrument is invoked to deter further defections, but unity is fraying under the strain of six concurrent initiatives.

### The Weight of Ambition

The portfolio’s fiscal and administrative burden deepens. Political capital erodes further, even as the safety breakthrough at leading labs offers little solace—the EU cannot integrate these gains without sovereign access. The moment of greatest technical opportunity coincides with peak institutional vulnerability. No new measure is implemented instantly; momentum builds, but execution lags.
