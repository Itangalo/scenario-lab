AI capability rises to 73.0, widening the gap between advancement and safety as frontier systems remain unsafeguarded. A member state’s bilateral deal with a U.S. hyperscaler fractures EU unity, undermining the Sovereign Core Initiative amid urgent healthcare needs. Physical attacks on data centres and a ransomware assault—powered by a fine-tuned open-weight model—cripple hospitals, exposing vulnerabilities despite the Hardened Cyber Shield’s partial effectiveness. The EU activates NIS3 emergency protocols, but legacy weaknesses persist. The Commission fast-tracks the Union-Wide Sovereign Core Adoption Directive with incentives and a side deal with Germany, yet legal disputes over data and sovereignty delay implementation. Public trust erodes further as the Institutional Integrity Audit reveals AI’s role in migration enforcement, disinformation spreads, and sentiment sours amid rising dependency and unrest. Political capital dwindles under the strain of overlapping crises, weakening institutional capacity and leaving sovereignty efforts fragmented and underfunded.
