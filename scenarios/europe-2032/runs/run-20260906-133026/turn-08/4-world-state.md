### The Plateau That Wasn’t

Despite signs of a slowing frontier — underwhelming model releases and falling prices for top-tier access — AI capability advances to 75.5, confirming the plateau was illusory. The gap between advancement and safety remains yawning, with no safeguards implemented on frontier systems. Open-weight models, now at 62.0 capability, are increasingly weaponised; the hospital ransomware attack is traced to a fine-tuned public release, further eroding trust.

### Fractures Harden

The EU’s response falters under its own weight. The **Institutional Integrity Audit** concludes, exposing AI’s unchecked role in border enforcement across seven agencies. While the report is published and reforms launched, the damage is done: public sentiment dips to 26.0 as media frames the revelations as systemic betrayal. The **Cohesion Enforcement Protocol** is proposed, linking access to frontier AI and emergency support to adoption of the Sovereign Core. But resistance flares immediately. Two member states denounce it as coercion, and legal challenges emerge over its basis in Article 122 TFEU. The measure fails to unify — instead, it polarises.

Germany agrees to accelerate compliance by turn 9 in exchange for safeguarding its research share, but a second state begins quiet talks with Beijing for alternative compute access. The **emergent_bilateral_compute_backdoor** gains momentum, now involving four nations. Meanwhile, sabotage at a Lithuanian data hub — a direct copy of the Polish attack — underscores the fragility of physical infrastructure.

### A Sovereignty Eroding

The **InvestAI Gigafactories** and **Tech Sovereignty Package** formally conclude, but their impact is muted by delays and fragmented rollout. The **Sovereign Core Initiative** remains in flight, its adoption patchy. Political capital, already at 8.0, plummets under the cost of five large measures and the new priority. The Cohesion Protocol, though ambitious, fails to gain traction, offering no immediate boost. Resilience declines as cyber and physical threats outpace defences. The Union acts, but cohesion — and credibility — slips further.
