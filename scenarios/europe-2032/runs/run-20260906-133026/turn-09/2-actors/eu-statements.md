- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Anchor EU strategic autonomy in sovereign, auditable AI systems capable of operating through a loss of external access or trust — and ensure that such systems are institutionally embedded, socially accepted, legally binding, and *enforceably unified* across all member states, or they will not hold when tested.
  - Trigger: The **cyber_major_incident** confirms that open-weight models at 62.0 are now weaponisable at scale, and the **automated_decision_scandal** reveals systemic failures in accountability that erode public trust even as the **emergent_bilateral_compute_backdoor** fractures cohesion.
  - Grounds: The previous commitment correctly identified enforceable unity as essential, but events have shown that legitimacy is equally structural. The Institutional Integrity Audit exposed AI’s unchecked use in border enforcement, triggering public backlash. The Cohesion Enforcement Protocol, while legally grounded, is politically failing: two member states challenge it as coercion, and public sentiment has dropped to 26.0. Without legitimacy, enforcement breeds resistance, not compliance. The revised commitment must therefore elevate *public legitimacy* to parity with *enforceable unity* — not as a secondary concern, but as a core condition of sovereignty. This means binding adoption not just through conditionality, but through transparency, redress, and visible public benefit.
  - Evidence quoted: **cyber_major_incident:** A large, largely automated attack lands. Its form is chosen at the time – a ransomware sweep across public services, a compromised software dependency with an unknown blast radius, or disruption of infrastructure directly – and severity scales with how far offensive capability has outrun defence. Damage is real and public, defenders are visibly behind, and attribution takes months. Whoever ran it, the tooling was model-generated.

**automated_decision_scandal:** An AI-supported decision system in social insurance, policing or the courts is found to have systematically wronged people, with a judgment or an ombudsman finding behind it. Restriction becomes cheap and adoption becomes politically impossible for years. Metric rule 6's internal-origin clause applies in full.

**emergent_bilateral_compute_backdoor (emergent event):** A coalition of mid-sized EU member states, frustrated by paralysis at the center and facing renewed pressure from external powers, quietly begins coordinating direct bilateral access to foreign AI compute through shared hosting agreements with neutral third countries. This emergent hedging strategy bypasses EU-wide restrictions and undermines the Sovereign Core Initiative’s legitimacy.
  - Reason: The developments directly undermine enforceable unity, public trust, and sovereignty—the core of the statement—by exposing systemic failures and fragmentation.
