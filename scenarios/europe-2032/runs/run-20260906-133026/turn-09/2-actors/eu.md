## In practice
We are acting on three fronts to stabilise the Union’s collapsing AI legitimacy and cohesion. First, we are grounding sovereignty in accountability: the **Public Redress and Transparency Framework** will be proposed in the European Parliament under the AI Act’s Article 69a, creating a pan-EU appeals body for AI-impacted citizens, with powers to compel model logs, demand human review, and award compensation. This is not symbolic — it is structural. The framework will be piloted in Germany, France, and Lithuania, using Sovereign Core systems to ensure auditability, and funded with €8bn from the unused portion of the InvestAI reserve. We are offering member states opt-in bonuses: additional recovery fund access for early adoption, and technical support from the newly operational Public AI Observatory.

Second, we are reinforcing the Sovereign Core not just as a technical stack but as a political anchor. The **cyber_major_incident** — a ransomware sweep across regional health systems using a fine-tuned open-weight model — has validated our worst fears: at 62.0, offensive capability is now distributed. We are fast-tracking the integration of Sovereign Core agents into ENISA’s emergency response protocols, ensuring that during incidents, only auditable, EU-controlled systems are authorised for crisis management. The Commission is also invoking Article 122 TFEU to condition access to the new €12bn Cyber Emergency Facility on Sovereign Core adoption — not as punishment, but as a firewall against systemic collapse.

Third, we are confronting the legitimacy deficit head-on. The **automated_decision_scandal**, confirmed by the European Ombudsman, found that an AI-supported social insurance system in one member state wrongfully denied benefits to over 12,000 people, including disabled veterans. We are launching a public reconciliation campaign: publishing all findings, suspending the system pending redress, and appointing a High Commissioner for AI Justice to oversee reparations. Simultaneously, the Public Trust Observatory begins watermarking all EU AI outputs this turn, and a media literacy push — “Know the Source” — launches in nine languages ahead of the 2031 elections. This is not damage control. It is the foundation of a new compact: sovereignty without redress is domination, and trust without remedy is propaganda. We are rebuilding both — or we will have neither.
