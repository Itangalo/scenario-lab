### The Crisis of Trust Deepens

A wave of ransomware cripples regional health systems across three member states, exploiting vulnerabilities in fine-tuned open-weight models. The attack, rapidly attributed to a transnational cybercrime group using AI-generated exploit chains, exposes the fragility of public infrastructure. ENISA confirms the tools were derived from models at or near 62.0 capability — now widely accessible. Despite the completion of the InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package, their impact remains fragmented, with only two member states fully integrating Sovereign Core systems.

Public backlash intensifies following the European Ombudsman’s damning report on an AI-driven social insurance system that wrongfully denied benefits to thousands. The scandal fuels widespread anger, with protests in multiple capitals demanding accountability. In response, the EU launches the Public Redress and Transparency Framework, proposing binding appeal rights and compensation for AI-impacted citizens. Pilots begin in Germany, France, and Lithuania, leveraging Sovereign Core for auditability. A High Commissioner for AI Justice is appointed, and the “Know the Source” media campaign rolls out.

Yet cohesion frays further. The Commission’s move to tie €12bn in emergency cyber funding to Sovereign Core adoption sparks legal challenges from two member states, who call it coercive. Meanwhile, the bilateral compute backdoor expands, with a third nation initiating quiet talks with Beijing. Germany accelerates adoption, but only after securing guarantees on research autonomy.

The Sovereign Core Initiative remains the priority, but progress is slow. Physical attacks on data infrastructure persist, mirroring earlier sabotage in Poland and Lithuania. The grassroots municipal oversight network, operating independently, reports improved local resilience — a contrast to top-down struggles.

### Stalled Momentum

Ambitious reforms founder on thin political capital. The redress framework is welcomed, but its long rollout tempers immediate sentiment gains. Trust remains fragile, and the EU’s authority is questioned both from within and beyond its borders.
