## In practice
We are pressing forward on three fronts to lock in the emergency compute procurement. First, the Commission is finalising the Article 356 legal act under the ECOFIN Council, arguing that strategic compute access meets the threshold of a "crisis situation" under the treaty. We are offering France and Poland preferential access to 15% of the sovereign partition for national defence and critical infrastructure coordination in exchange for Council support. Second, negotiations with Meta, AWS and OVHcloud are entering their final phase: we are offering fast-tracked permitting in designated "AI Resilience Zones" — repurposed from the Tech Sovereignty Package — and guaranteed energy priority during shortages, in return for legally binding commitments that at least 30% of the procured GPUs will be available for public use during declared supply shocks. The European Defence Fund is reprogramming €8.2bn from legacy projects to co-finance the purchase, reframing the investment as dual-use infrastructure.

Simultaneously, we are racing to conclude the Openweight Containment Framework before turn 5 ends. The sunset clause triggering at 50.0 openweight capability has gained traction in COREPER II, but Germany demands veto rights on audit findings and Italy insists on exemptions for academic releases. We are offering a joint Franco-German evaluation hub hosted in Strasbourg, with rotating directorship, to break the deadlock. On ALAI, we are finalising co-financing agreements with ASML and Zeiss to secure 18 additional DUV tools for EU-based fabs, leveraging our supply-chain leverage through the middle_power_coalition to bypass U.S. export bottlenecks. The Cyber Resilience Surge has concluded, and its after-action review confirms that while detection improved, cross-border command remains weak — justifying our continued investment in category 6 resilience, but not yet a new measure. We are holding the line on all existing commitments, but withholding new initiatives until the implications of election_alliance fully settle in turn 6.
