## New measure
**Emergency Resilience Surge (ERS)**  
Activate and fund immediate hardening of critical public services against AI-enabled cyber and disinformation attacks, with binding standards and real-time monitoring.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 12`  
`Targeted effect:` +10 to `resilience`, +2 to `ai_safety` through improved detection and containment of harmful AI use in public systems  
`Applies to:` EU member states, with enforcement through Article 122 TFEU and the Digital Services Act review mechanism

## Priority
`Emergency Resilience Surge` — because the cyber_major_incident and emergent_disinfo_wave have already landed, public trust is collapsing, and without a visible, immediate response to harm absorption, no sovereignty or capability project will survive the political backlash.

## In practice
We are acting on two fronts: one defensive, one existential. The cyber_major_incident — a model-generated ransomware sweep that took down hospital scheduling systems in three member states and disrupted water grid monitoring in two more — has made it undeniable: our preparedness is not fit for purpose. The emergent_disinfo_wave compounds it, eroding trust in institutions at the very moment we need cohesion. We cannot wait for the December failover test to prove ECAM’s worth; we must show now that the EU can protect its people.

The Emergency Resilience Surge launches under Article 122 TFEU, with €18bn drawn from the Crisis and Investment Facility. It mandates binding cyber hardening for all critical public infrastructure using AI, enforced by ENISA with quarterly audits. A new Disinformation Response Cell, hosted at eu-LISA and staffed with joint national analysts, will deploy model-based detection tools to flag synthetic content in real time, integrated with national media regulators. Member states must adopt the framework by turn 12 or risk losing cohesion funding. The European Cybersecurity Competence Centre will coordinate rapid deployment of patches and fallback protocols, especially in healthcare and energy.

This is not a pivot from sovereignty — it is its urgent recalibration. Without resilience, sovereign compute means nothing. Without public confidence, no measure holds. We are holding the line so that the next turn can build.
