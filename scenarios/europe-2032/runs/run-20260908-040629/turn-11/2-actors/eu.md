Establish a distributed, EU-controlled network of high-assurance AI testbeds to evaluate, monitor, and refine agentic systems under real-world conditions without relying on foreign-hosted infrastructure.

## In practice
We are executing the Emergency Resilience Surge under the legal authority of Article 122 TFEU and the revised Digital Services Act, with €18bn drawn from the Crisis and Investment Facility. ENISA is now drafting binding cyber standards for AI-integrated critical infrastructure, to be adopted by member states by turn 12. These will mandate real-time logging, failover protocols, and third-party audit access for all AI systems in healthcare scheduling, water monitoring, and energy grid control. The Disinformation Response Cell at eu-LISA is being staffed with joint national analysts and equipped with detection models trained to flag synthetic media at scale; integration with national media regulators begins next month. We are offering cohesion funding bonuses to early adopters, while conditioning future structural funds on compliance. Resistance from three member states — citing cost and sovereignty concerns — is being met with bilateral task forces to co-design implementation pathways.

At the same time, we are launching the Sovereign AI Testbed Network to close a critical gap: we cannot evaluate what we cannot run, and we cannot secure what we do not understand. This network will deploy EU-controlled clusters in Germany, France, Finland, Italy, the Netherlands, and Poland, each hosting high-assurance environments for testing agentic models in legal, medical, and infrastructure domains. These are not development labs; they are evaluation theatres with full interpretability tooling, red-teaming capacity, and secure data pipelines. Funding comes from Horizon Europe and the Digital Europe Programme, with co-investment from national AI agencies. The testbeds will interface with the AI Office to inform future regulatory thresholds and serve as the foundation for a European AI Observatory. This is not a substitute for compute sovereignty — but it is the next best thing we can build while waiting for Gigafactories to come online.
