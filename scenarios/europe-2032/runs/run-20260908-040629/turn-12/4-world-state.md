The Emergency Resilience Surge concludes. ENISA finalises binding cyber standards under NIS2, and ten member states implement full logging and audit access. However, Hungary, Poland, and Austria maintain parallel national frameworks, citing sovereignty. The eu-LISA Disinformation Response Cell fails to scale, overwhelmed by a synthetic disinformation wave. Still, the partial rollout delivers measurable hardening: `resilience` increases by +3.

The European AI Watchtower is launched as a large category 1 measure, aiming to unify threat detection across intelligence, platforms, and civil society. Hosted in Luxembourg with edge nodes in Helsinki and Marseille, it operates under Article 16 TFEU. A cohesion incentive—full cost reimbursement plus a 15% infrastructure bonus—tempts early adoption. Yet protests intensify: direct action targets data centres in Germany and France, with grid sabotage and injuries reported. Public sentiment plummets to 10.0 as backlash physical escalates and no visible benefit counters the disruption.
