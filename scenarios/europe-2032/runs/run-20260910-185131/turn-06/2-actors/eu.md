## Priority
InvestAI Gigafactories (M1), because with US rationing imminent and capital at 1.0 it is the only sovereign-access build still funded and pulling it forward is the commitment.

## In practice
We keep the Union in a holding pattern that protects M1. DG CNECT and DG ENER use existing InvestAI mandates to clear grid-connection and permitting queues for the four to five sites, making joint-procurement discipline a condition for factory siting and CEF co-financing, without opening a new vote or fund that would collapse for lack of capital.

On labour displacement we acknowledge the graduate hiring freeze in law, accountancy, software and customer operations but answer only with monitoring: Eurostat-Eurofound tracking and EIB redeployment of existing Social Fund lines, while finance ministers continue to block the Reserve top-up after the failed radios drill. On Taiwan we task DG TRADE and the EEAS to price shipping-insurance and chip-supply exposure, folding it into the tier-rationing exposure map for the next commitment push.
