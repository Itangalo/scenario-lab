## Priority
EU Patch Sprint, because it is the only instrument affordable at capital 2.0 that can deliver a visible win and slow the political bleed while gigafactories remain stalled awaiting US tiering.

## In practice
We task ENISA and DG CNECT to push the breakthrough patching and detection stacks through the Grid and Port Cyber Shield vendor list, using existing NIS2 implementing acts – no new vote, no new money, just accelerated certification and joint procurement of the tooling for the two breached operators first, then smaller utilities.

On access and jobs we stay in holding: DG ENER keeps gigafactory grid-connection queues warm, DG TRADE maps US tiering exposure, and we answer graduate unemployment only with the Eurostat dashboard, because finance ministers still block fresh spending and any large initiative would collapse and cost the last of our capital.
