## In practice
We task ENISA and ACER with a binding emergency hardening directive under the NIS2 and Cyber Resilience Act bases, funded by reprogrammed InvestAI deployment funds and the Connecting Europe Facility, with TSOs in the two hit member states as first movers. Energy Council in December must mandate breaker-credential rotation, relay segmentation audits, and plain-sight tooling hunts across all electricity TSOs, plus the affected port authority and water utility as pilot sites.

In parallel we keep M1 Gigafactories and M2 sovereignty package running on DG CNECT permitting zones and EIB guarantees, but we tell France, Germany and the Netherlands plainly that grid connection for new AI data centres is conditional on passing the same segmentation standard. Resistance from operators over cost is met with EU co-financing and from industry over delay with accelerated permits once hardened. Exercises with the US CISA lessons-learned team start in Q4, without waiting for attribution.
