## New measure
**Graduate Junior Posts Bridge**
Funds time-limited paid junior placements plus retraining vouchers in affected university cities, tied to public-sector and SME adoption of AI tools.
Why this and why now: with capital at 4.0 and sentiment at 30, concrete without jobs and a member-state breakaway are bleeding the last cohesion; a small labour measure (category 7) is the only affordable visible answer to town-hall anger over missing junior posts while the productivity plateau removes the transition story.

## Priority
Graduate Junior Posts Bridge, because holding the Union line after a defection requires showing jobs now, not gigafactories later, and it outranks access-mapping under our two-year commitment this turn.

## In practice
We run this through DG EMPL and EGF-ESF+ top-ups, no new regulation: Council EPSCO conclusions authorise reprogrammed ESF+ envelopes for 6-12 month paid placements in administration, hospitals and SMEs using EU AI tools, with wage top-up conditional on employers retaining mentoring. University cities with sharpest graduate unemployment get first calls.

On sovereignty we hold without spending: DG CNECT and DG TRADE keep the two poured sites on grid-reserve, push transformer orders via joint procurement, and quietly negotiate a no-undercut pledge in COREPER to contain the defecting member state, while ENISA sustains the Patch Sprint rollout to smaller utilities with existing funds. No new fund is tabled because any large vote would fail at capital 4.0.
