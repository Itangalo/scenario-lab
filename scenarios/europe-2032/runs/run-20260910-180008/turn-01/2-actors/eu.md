## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with mandatory detection, backup controls and cross-border exercises.
Category 6 Preparedness and resilience. Why this and why now: the autumn intrusions and loss-of-control incident show harm arrives before assurance catches up, and resilience is the lever fully in EU hands.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because undetected presence in two member-state grids outranks longer-horizon compute build-out this turn.

## In practice
We task ENISA with a binding detection and incident-reporting standard for transmission system operators and major ports under the NIS2 legal base, funded from Digital Europe and CEF, with joint exercises in the Energy and Transport Council formations. Member states resisting cost-sharing are offered co-financed SOC capacity and mutual-aid credits.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package moving: accelerated permitting zones and grid connections negotiated with host states, private capital de-risked via EIB guarantees, and ASML supply-chain leverage held in reserve for the export-control negotiation. Frontier access terms secured this turn are written into procurement as notice-period and evaluation-right clauses.
