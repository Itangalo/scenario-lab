## New measure
**EU Automation Transition Shield**
Creates a joint EU fund for wage insurance, rapid retraining and redeployment for warehouse, logistics and supplier workers displaced by deployed embodied AI, tied to employer automation reporting.
Why this and why now: commercial robots are now dividing the labour market at the manual-judgement line and landing on EU industry from US models and Chinese hardware, while office studies show augmentation without job losses — using that dividend story to fund transition now is how we stop sentiment at 20 from blocking both sovereignty builds and adoption.

In parallel we table the Transition Shield via EPSCO and EMPL: ESF+ plus a top-up from the Globalisation Adjustment Fund for wage insurance and 6-month retraining vouchers in the three most exposed logistics/manufacturing corridors, with automation-reporting tied to InvestAI site aid to get employers and regions on board, and we use the law/accountancy productivity evidence publicly to separate augmentation from displacement. InvestAI Gigafactories stays to lot-by-lot permitting and mediated power/water talks at the two blocked sites — no parallel compute vehicle, no new promise until grid links move.
