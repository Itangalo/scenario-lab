## In practice
We run this through EPSCO and Energy Councils with ENISA, ECDC and DG SANTE/DG ENER, funded by re-scoped EU4Health, DIGITAL and Just Transition lines: extend the M11/M12 swarm-detection and auto-patch mandate to all undefended federations, staff manual-fallback rotas, and publish ward-level continuity scores so defended-versus-undefended is closed.

We pair it with mediated siting: Commission-brokered roundtables with blocking municipalities, TSO/DSOs and hospital-grid operators offering local resilience co-benefits, water-efficiency and hiring guarantees in exchange for lifting injunctions into a single national siting law, using CER/NIS2 implementing acts rather than new compute promises while US licence cuts persist.
