## New measure
**EU Clinic Backlog Clearing and Procurement Review**
A small category 6 preparedness measure that funds paper-backlog digitisation teams, independent clinical review of delayed-care cases, and a time-limited court-supervised review to unfreeze trusted health-administration AI procurement.
This and now because the coordinated lawsuits freeze exactly the procurement that keeps rationed clinics running, and with capital at 11 and sentiment at 5 only a visible backlog-clearing plus judicial review can prevent the freeze hardening into permanent distrust.

## Priority
M9 EU Essential Compute Continuity and Rationing Regime, because keeping hospitals, grids and payments on ring-fenced inference through the rationing winter is what prevents the litigation freeze and parts shortage cascading into service collapse.

## In practice
We keep M9 as the operating system: DG GROW/CNECT with ENISA and the Semiconductor Board enforce the audited spares inventory and priority tiers, Health and Energy Councils defend hospitals-first ring-fencing, and we use the Single Market Emergency Instrument to keep Bridge inference segregated for essential services while conversion sites stay frozen.

We stand up the backlog measure through DG SANTE and DG JUST with the Health Council and national patient ombudsmen: EU4Health money for mobile digitisation and clinical review teams to town halls and clinics hit by the managed-service intrusions, HERA-style triage protocols for delayed care, and a joint Commission-court expert panel to certify which centrally procured treatments and assistants are safe to reconnect. Procurement stays frozen only pending that review, with staff refusals addressed by independent safety sign-off rather than orders to reconnect.
