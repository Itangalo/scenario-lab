## Priority
M10 EU Clinic Backlog Clearing and Procurement Review, because only visible digitisation, independent re-triage and court-accepted safety sign-off can break the boycott, unfreeze trusted procurement, and rebuild minimal legitimacy after the automated attack.

## In practice
We keep the rationing regime as the floor without new spending: ENISA, DG CNECT and the Health/Energy Councils hold segregated inference for hospitals, grids and payments, dole audited spares by tier, and isolate the ransomware-affected municipal services for manual fallback rather than reconnecting compromised assistants.

We put everything political we have into M10: DG SANTE/DG JUST funds mobile scanning and clinical review teams from EU4Health, the joint Commission-court panel publishes which tools are cleared to reconnect with human-readable safeguards given opaque reasoning, and patient ombudsmen and clinic staff get independent sign-off veto to end the boycott. No new build or procurement is pushed until that review lands.
