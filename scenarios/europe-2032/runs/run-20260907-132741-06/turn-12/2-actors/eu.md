We are not waiting for the ECJ ruling — we are acting on the precedent of pandemic and energy crisis measures, where speed created facts. The emergent watchdog network is being invited into a formal observer role, not as a partner, but as a warning: if we do not become the authority, we will be replaced by it. This is not legitimacy. It is re-entry. We are proving we can act — before we ask to be trusted.
