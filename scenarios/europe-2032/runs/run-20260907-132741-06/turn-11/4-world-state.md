### Crisis Powers Invoked — But Unity Fractures

In a dramatic emergency session, the European Commission invoked Article 122 TFEU, declaring an AI-enabled systemic crisis and bypassing the Council to enforce integration of the Sovereign Cyber Shield. ENISA deployed emergency defense stacks in Italy and Spain within days, halting active intrusions in energy and healthcare networks. The newly formed AI Incident Containment Authority isolated two high-risk autonomous control systems in transport and utilities, marking the first binding enforcement of its kind.

Yet the move deepened political fissures. Poland and Hungary filed an urgent referral with the European Court of Justice, arguing the invocation violated national sovereignty. Germany and France backed the action—but only conditionally, demanding oversight reforms. Public sentiment, already at rock bottom, fell further as protests framed the emergency powers as authoritarian overreach. “No to digital martial law,” read banners in Warsaw and Budapest.
