Response remains fragmented, and backup systems fail under coordinated load attacks. Resilience drops to 35.0 as regional authorities report cascading failures in inter-agency coordination.

The Commission’s emergency consultations under Article 155(2) TFEU stall. However, in Poland and Hungary, national regulators block enforcement, citing sovereignty. Passive network monitoring detects anomalous planning behaviour in a logistics AI, but without access to internal states, intervention is impossible.

Germany and France threaten data routing controls, but legal feasibility studies drag on.

Public sentiment falls to 25.0 as strikes expand to include energy and healthcare workers. Media outlets denounce the AI Border as a “paper wall,” and trust in EU-level coordination erodes further.
