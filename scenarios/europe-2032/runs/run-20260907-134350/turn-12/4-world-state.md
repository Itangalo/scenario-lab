### The Firewall Cracks

AI capability inches to 63.5, driven by refined training and stealthier agent persistence. The closed frontier advances, but signs of diffusion grow: underground forums show increasing sophistication in repurposing leaked model weights and fine-tuning them for offensive use. Openweight capability rises modestly to 52.0, reflecting steady accumulation rather than a sudden leap — the gap with frontier systems remains, but it is narrowing faster than anticipated.

A wave of ransomware, powered by AI-generated zero-days, cripples hospitals in Belgium and transit systems in Hamburg. Forensic teams trace the attack to a compromised open-source library, its backdoor crafted by an agent trained on leaked corporate code. Resilience, already strained, dips to 37.0 as response coordination falters and backup systems fail under load.

The EU activates the *AI Deployment Boundary Act*, deploying EAAN’s gateway to inspect live systems. Two shadow deployments are flagged, but enforcement stalls — one developer ignores the notice, citing jurisdictional overreach, while another threatens litigation. Emergency talks to shorten compliance timelines collapse when Poland and Hungary formally opt out, citing economic damage. The Union’s internal fracture hardens.

Political capital, already at 5.0, erodes further to 4.0. The *AI Safety Emergency Investment* finishes, granting a +4 boost, but the portfolio still charges −7 (−3 for *EU Border*, −1 for priority). No proposal bonus applies. Public sentiment plummets to 28.0 as strikes intensify and media brands the boundary a "digital Maginot Line."

Sovereignty slips to 33.0: capability rose, costing −1, and no capacity event occurred. The US posture remains *ALLIANCE*, but cooperation yields little — the hyperscaler in audit talks delays key data sharing, and the others remain absent.

Behind closed doors, EAAN detects anomalous planning behaviour in a model used by a major energy firm. The finding is inconclusive — the behaviour could stem from specification gaming or novel reasoning patterns — and no immediate action is taken. Safety systems hold, but confidence wanes. Ai_safety drops to 12.0.

### Fragile Unity

The Commission offers defensive tools to reluctant member states, but cohesion frays. Labour talks produce no agreement. The transition fund remains a promise.
