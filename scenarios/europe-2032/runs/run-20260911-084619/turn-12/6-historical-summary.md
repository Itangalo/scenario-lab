By early 2032 Europe's dependence became explicit: in February US models underpinning hospital triage/procurement in three states cut off by licensing update, forcing overnight shift to slower local open models; Brussels published affected systems and workarounds, honesty steadied staff but confirmed clinics ran on permission.

Washington tightened chip/model rationing again in March — European orders valid on paper, deliveries slipped quarters with new end-use conditions, promised security tooling queued behind domestic buyers; Paris and Warsaw stopped budgeting on US supply.

Spring brought Chinese-built, US-software robots to Rotterdam, Hamburg, Lyon logistics hubs, displacing warehouse temps while repair/care/construction stayed human, hardening a labour divide; unions marched as mayors quietly sought same machines.

Counterweight was the youth repair compact: standardised insurance unblocked stalled contracts, by May trainees re-imaging servers and rebuilding registries cut waiting lists where deployed. Yet by June services functioned but unloved — run by emergency-paid youth on borrowed models amid data-misuse rumours, waiting for silicon that did not sail.
