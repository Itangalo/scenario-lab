### The cutoff
In February, hospitals in three member states found the American model they had built triage and procurement tools on simply stopped answering. No notice beyond a licensing update, no appeal channel. Ministries switched overnight to locally stored open models and European-hosted assistants. They worked, after a fashion — slower, blunter, needing more supervision. Doctors noticed. Clerks noticed.

Brussels owned it as a failure of foresight. Health officials published lists of affected systems and workaround guides. The honesty steadied staff but confirmed the humiliation in public: Europe's clinics ran on permission.

### Rationing tightens
Washington tightened chip and model controls again in March. Under tiered rationing, European orders stayed valid on paper while deliveries slipped by quarters and new end-use conditions appeared. The promised security tooling for the municipal rebuilds queued behind domestic buyers. Procurement officers in Paris and Warsaw stopped budgeting around American supply.

### Machines for the warehouse
Spring brought the robots. Chinese-built mobile units with American control software appeared in logistics hubs in Rotterdam, Hamburg and Lyon — picking, sorting, palletising through the night. Warehouse temps were the first to go. Repair shops, care wards and chaotic building sites stayed stubbornly human, and the line between the two hardened into a new labour divide. Unions marched against the docks automation while mayors quietly asked for the same machines for municipal depots.

### Young hands hold
The one visible counterweight was the youth repair compact. Standardised insurance waivers unblocked contracts in stalled regions, and by May trainees in municipal vests were again re-imaging servers and carrying folders in clinics. Where waiting lists actually fell because a rebuilt registry finally worked, local papers credited the crews.

It was not enough. Screens remained distrusted, rumours spread of trainees pasting patient data into unvetted local assistants, and by June services were functioning but unloved — kept running by young workers paid with emergency money, on borrowed models, waiting for silicon that did not sail.
