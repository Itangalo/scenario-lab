### The sweep
It began as a helpdesk ticket in three countries at once. A widely used monitoring dependency pushed a signed update that was not what it claimed to be, and within hours municipal registries, clinic appointment systems and two regional grid dispatch tools encrypted themselves in sequence.

The tooling that built the payload had been assembled quickly, largely by machines. Defenders said so openly: patch notes referenced automated exploit chains that moved from intrusion to lateral movement in minutes. Attribution statements promised months of work.

Hospitals fell back to the printed folders from the winter kits. That worked where the kits had arrived; where they had not, ambulances diverted. Water and power stayed up, but billing, scheduling and maintenance queues ran on paper for weeks.

Brussels sent its new joint recovery teams. Three mobile units of national emergency responders and contracted vendors toured the worst-hit cities, isolating systems, re-imaging servers, publishing daily isolation guidance. Mayors queued at a single claims window for energy relief and recovery money alike, and again complained the envelopes had only been relabelled.

### Jobs and chips
While technicians rebuilt domains, two other pressures tightened.

University career offices reported what consultancies already knew: graduate intake for routine code, standard analysis and first-line support had collapsed. The losses were narrow — checkable, draftable work — but they were permanent, and they belonged to the young. Comment pages filled with forecasts postponed another year.

At the same time Washington tightened chip and model licences again. Under tiered rationing, allied buyers kept volume access on paper while delivery dates slipped and conditions multiplied. European cloud operators were told to wait for expedited patch tooling; procurement officers quietly kept running locally stored open models that no licence could recall.

A new laboratory demonstration sharpened the edge: systems that wrote, proved and broke code markedly better than the month before. Specialists argued about whether it meant anything general. Operators did not wait for the argument to settle.

By June services were standing, degraded. The recovery corps was visible, but voters remembered the dark screens as they had remembered the dark suburbs.
