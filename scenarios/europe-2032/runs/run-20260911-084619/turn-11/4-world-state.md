### Paper systems, young hands
The autumn began with clinics still on paper. The summer sweep had left registries half-rebuilt, and the joint recovery teams toured again, this time with new faces beside them: cohorts of twenty-somethings in municipal vests, re-imaging servers, logging tickets, carrying folders between wards.

It was the visible part of the new hiring compact. Brussels had reshuffled emergency social funds into a single window so mayors could draw repair money and wage money together. In Lyon, Gdansk and Brno the scheme staffed quickly; elsewhere contracts stalled over who supervised, who paid insurance, who owned liability if a trainee wiped a backup. Mayors welcomed the bodies and again asked why the money arrived labelled twice.

### The weights slip loose
While trainees learned isolation procedure, a frontier-class open release dropped. Hundreds of thousands of downloads in days. University servers, small firms, municipal IT backrooms pulled copies that no licence could recall. Operators who had waited months for approved patch tooling now ran local assistants to write scripts, triage logs, probe their own rebuilt domains — and, defenders warned, to build the next payload faster.

The code edge sharpened at the same time. Automated chains that had taken minutes in spring took less, and audit notes spoke of machine-written exploits needing only a nudge.

### Chips tighten
Washington tightened export rules again. Under tiered rationing, European buyers kept volume on paper while delivery slips lengthened and conditions multiplied. Expedited security tooling was promised, then queued. Procurement officers stopped planning around it.

In the Strait, extended exercises lifted insurance rates and prompted a round of expulsions. No blockade, no break — but chip buyers in Europe did the arithmetic aloud: dependence dictated twice over, on models that had just leaked and on silicon that might not sail.

By December services were less degraded but not trusted. The repair crews were popular where seen; the screens behind them were not.
