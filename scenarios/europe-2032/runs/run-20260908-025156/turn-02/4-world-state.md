### Fractures at Home, Pressure from All Sides

The European Union launched the **Labour Transition Shield**, a large-scale social intervention aimed at stabilising public sentiment amid accelerating job displacement in legal, financial, and public administration sectors. Though formally proposed, early resistance emerged: Germany questioned the retraining model’s compatibility with dual vocational training, and Poland refused to join without exemptions for state-owned enterprises. The Shield became the named priority, but cohesion frayed—undermining its political lift.

Simultaneously, a **member_state_defection** unfolded when Hungary secured a bilateral AI infrastructure deal with a U.S. hyperscaler, bypassing EU procurement guidelines and offering preferential data access. Framed as economic pragmatism in Budapest, it was seen across Brussels as a rupture in collective leverage. The move weakened enforcement of the Tech Sovereignty Package and cast doubt on the Union’s ability to unify its digital frontier.

AI capability advanced to **56.0**, driven by multi-agent training and compressed release cycles. Yet no new safety evaluations were applied to in-development systems, and the gap with **ai_safety**—now at **30.0**—widened further. Red-teaming efforts by ENISA exposed persistent blind spots in autonomous reasoning, but findings were not binding.

The **Emergency Resilience Surge** progressed slowly. While Nordic grid operators adopted draft standards, Germany delayed implementation pending Energy Council talks. Resilience dipped to **40.0** as planning momentum stalled and no physical hardening was completed.

The **InvestAI Gigafactories** and **Tech Sovereignty Package** remained in flight, but grid bottlenecks and external dependency persisted. With **ai_capability** rising by 2.0, **eu_ai_sovereignty** fell to **19.0**, reflecting deepening structural exposure.

Public sentiment dropped to **39.0** amid strikes in Paris and Frankfurt, as media framed AI as a threat to skilled work. Political capital fell to **34.0**, strained by three large measures and the fallout from disunity.
