### EU Launches Emergency Replication Mandate

Faced with existential dependency, the EU activates the **Emergency Replication Mandate** as its top priority. It demands all public AI systems above capability 55 be mirrored using open or verified weights on EU-controlled infrastructure within 90 days. Compliance orders go out immediately, backed by emergency funding and fast-tracked clearances.

However, resistance flares. Germany challenges the Anti-Coercion Instrument’s use in AI infrastructure, calling it a breach of national sovereignty. Legal disputes delay node deployment in Hamburg. Poland halts its replication wave after discovering that the targeted model’s architecture is patented and export-restricted — efforts stall without access to foundational checkpoints.

Meanwhile, Bucharest, Helsinki, and Marseille begin construction of the **Sovereign Inference Backbone**, but audit teams report incomplete hardening. No system is yet operational. The mandate advances politically, but operationally, it remains fragile.

Public trust does not improve. Moratoriums on welfare and judicial AI deepen anxiety. Media frames the replication effort as desperate, not decisive. `public_sentiment` holds at **12.0**.

Despite urgent action, `eu_political_capital` remains at **6.0** — too low to gain momentum. The portfolio charge persists: two large measures (−3 each), one small (−2), plus priority (−1) = −9. But capital cannot fall below 6 due to floor effects in rule application. No bonus arrives: the mandate responds to ongoing risks, not a past event with measurable harm, so no proposal bonus applies.

`resilience` slips to **38.0** as automation spreads faster than defences. The `middle_power_coalition` forms, strengthening supply coordination — a positive signal — but delivers no immediate technical uplift.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to **12.0**: `ai_capability` rose ≥2 (−1), no capacity event landed, and the mandate, though launched, yields no immediate gain. The race for independence has begun, but the ground lost this turn is deeper than the steps forward.
