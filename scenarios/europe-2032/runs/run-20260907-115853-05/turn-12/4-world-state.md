### EU Shifts to Assurance

The Emergency Replication Mandate formally closes, having secured compliance pledges from all member states. However, operational replication remains patchy: fewer than half the targeted systems have begun migration, and none are fully hardened. In Poland, patent restrictions stall deployment; in Germany, constitutional challenges to centralised AI control persist.

In response, the EU launches the **Sovereign Assurance Acceleration** programme as its new priority. The initiative aims to embed real-time monitoring, interpretability stacks, and red-teaming agents into replicated systems. Funding is redirected from stalled adoption projects, and mobile audit teams deploy to Bucharest, Helsinki, and Marseille.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at **12.0**, as media frame the shift from replication to assurance as proof of earlier overreach. Resilience dips to **37.0**, reflecting widening gaps between automation and defence.
