## In practice
We are treating the completion of the Emergency Replication Mandate as a necessary but insufficient victory. The directive has cleared legally and compliance plans are in from all member states, but audit teams report that fewer than half of the targeted systems have begun actual replication, and none yet include real-time monitoring or interpretability stacks. In Poland, the patent block remains unresolved; in Germany, local courts continue to challenge the Anti-Coercion Instrument’s application to AI infrastructure. We are now shifting focus from legal compulsion to operational integrity.

The new **Sovereign Assurance Acceleration** programme will deploy the European AI Assurance Directorate’s (EAAD+) most advanced tooling — including dynamic circuit-breakers, latent goal detection modules, and live red-teaming agents — directly into the replication pipeline. Funding will come from the emergency reserve of Horizon Europe and the Digital Europe Programme, redirected from stalled adoption pilots. We are fast-tracking procurement for open-source interpretability frameworks and contracting with the Replication Foundry network in Finland, Spain, and Romania to integrate assurance layers by default. Mobile audit teams will be embedded in Bucharest, Helsinki, and Marseille to certify each node before activation.

We are also responding to the **bio_uplift_findings** by expanding the scope of assurance to include bio-risk screening: all replicated models used in life sciences or dual-use research must now undergo mandatory pathway analysis and access logging, with alerts routed to the European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control. This is not an add-on — it is the core of what sovereignty must mean now. We are no longer building mirrors. We are building eyes.
