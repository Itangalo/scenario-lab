### The Fracture Widens

AI capability surged to 97.5, driven by self-improving systems now mastering complex legal reasoning and system design at superhuman speed. Safety held steady at 8.0, as existing protocols continued to contain known failure modes, though concerns grew about opaque reasoning paths and the recent bio uplift. Open-weight models rose to 82.5, as compressed derivatives of frontier techniques spread through underground developer networks, enabling capable but uncontrolled misuse. The gap between open-weight and frontier capability narrowed, but remained within historical bounds, reflecting sustained diffusion rather than a sudden leap.

#### Labour Crisis, Collective Response

The EU faced a collapsing graduate job market. Law, accountancy, software, and administration saw entry-level hiring dry up entirely. In response, the **Emergency Labour Transition Shield** launched under Article 122 TFEU, mobilising €2.1 billion in fast-tracked support. Member states negotiated access to retraining funds in exchange for hosting upskilling hubs or contributing trainers, averting outright rebellion from fiscal hawks.

The **Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory**, intended to monitor AI threats, was formally abandoned after failing to launch — a symbol of the EU’s execution deficit. Meanwhile, the **Emergency Compute Redundancy Initiative** in Finland and Bulgaria remained years from completion, its clusters still unpowered.

The **Sovereign Inference Stack Integration** began exploring collaboration with emerging coordination efforts among middle powers, though no formal agreements were reached this turn. Technical teams exchanged preliminary assessments, but mutual distrust and differing standards slowed progress. The potential for shared telemetry remains under discussion, but no operational access has been secured.

#### Political Triage

With sovereignty falling to 7.0 — penalised by capability growth and unresolved access denial — the EU shifted from building sovereign capacity to surviving its absence. The cancellation of the failed Observatory freed 3 units of political capital, partially offsetting portfolio costs. The new Labour Shield, though large, earned no proposal bonus — it responded to ongoing displacement, not a single recent shock. Modest gains in political capital reflect internal cost management, not external validation.

Public sentiment rose slightly to 22.0, as visible support for displaced workers tempered growing anxiety. Resilience ticked up to 44.0, absorbing some harm through early intervention.
