### The Threshold Crossed

Global AI capability reached the terminal threshold this turn, surging by +2.5 to 100.0 as self-improving systems recursively optimised design, legal, and engineering domains beyond measurable human oversight. The instrument is now out of range — no institution can characterise what the frontier is doing. Open-weight models rose to 82.5, sustained by underground diffusion, and now approach broad expert-level performance in critical domains, enabling uncontrolled but highly capable misuse.

#### EU in Emergency Mode

The EU completed the **Emergency Compute Redundancy Initiative**, but operational clusters remain offline in Finland and Bulgaria due to grid delays and land disputes. The legal framework is preserved, but the failure to deliver functional redundancy underscores the Union’s eroding execution capacity. Still, the cancellation freed political capital, enabling the launch of the **Municipal Sovereignty Network Integration**, a large category 8 measure aimed at leveraging the emergent coalition of middle powers to secure shared access and telemetry.

Brussels convened a summit with Canada, South Korea, Singapore, and Switzerland, proposing mutual export licensing and joint evaluation protocols in exchange for EU interpretability tools. Initial responses were cautious — trust remains low, standards differ, and none want to provoke the dominant powers. Yet the shared vulnerability created momentum, and technical teams agreed to pilot data-sharing on non-frontier models.

Domestically, the **Emergency Labour Transition Shield** began disbursements, with northern states opting out of co-financing but contributing trainers to avoid political isolation. Mobile audit teams detected early fraud in two regions, triggering corrective action. Green-AI transition pilots launched in energy-sabotage hotspots, pairing retraining with local infrastructure resilience.

Public sentiment held at 22.0 — fragile, anxious, and increasingly polarised. The **emergent_energy_backlash_resurgence** intensified, with small-scale sabotage of power infrastructure near data centres in France and Germany, reflecting deepening distrust. Meanwhile, the **emergent_municipal_sovereignty_network** evolved from rumour to coordination, offering a fragile hope of collective agency.
