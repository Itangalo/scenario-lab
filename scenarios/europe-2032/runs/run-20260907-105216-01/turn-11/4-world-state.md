### The Weight of Absence

The EU doubled down on sovereignty, but momentum is not recovery. The **Emergency Compute Redundancy Initiative** pressed forward, with ground broken in Finland and Bulgaria despite local protests over energy use. Yet integration remains fragile—microwave backhaul licensing stalled in two jurisdictions, and power upgrades face civil disobedience. The clusters remain years from operational readiness.

The **Sovereign Inference Stack Integration** was formally launched, a large category 4 measure aiming to unify telemetry, interpretability, and fallback inference under EU control. But its ambition exposed deeper fractures: without access to the models it must monitor, the stack risks becoming a hollow framework. Technical teams report circular dependencies—tools require data, but data requires access, which requires tools.

Meanwhile, the **Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory** remained frozen in legal limbo, unable to observe what it cannot reach. Its failure to launch, despite two years of planning, underscored a systemic incapacity: the EU can legislate, but not execute.

AI capability surged to 93.5, driven by self-improving loops now mastering complex legal reasoning and system design. Open-weight models climbed to 79.5, as compressed derivatives of frontier techniques spread through developer ecosystems. With no new safeguards deployed, **ai_safety** stagnated at 8.0, now dangerously decoupled from capability.

**Resilience** fell to 43.0, penalized for rising systemic risk and the absence of finished protections. The denial of frontier access had not been mitigated—only anticipated.

Political capital dipped to 1.0. The portfolio charge was steep: −3 for the Redundancy Initiative, −3 for the Behavioural Observatory, −3 for the new Inference Stack, and −1 for prioritization, totaling −10. A proposal bonus of +8 for launching the Inference Stack in direct response to the access crisis partially offset the cost. No uplift came from sentiment or sovereignty.

Sovereignty fell to 9.0: −1 from capability growth and −3 from the unresolved impact of **eu_frontier_access_denied**, despite new efforts. Fallbacks did not yet exist; dependence remained unbroken.
