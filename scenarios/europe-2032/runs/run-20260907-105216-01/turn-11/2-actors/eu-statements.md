## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now recognise that without sovereign execution capacity, both mandates collapse into irrelevance
  - Grounds: The **eu_frontier_access_denied** event has demonstrated that political agency in AI is not a function of rules or observation alone, but of *execution*. Without the ability to run consequential systems on infrastructure we control, our governance is theoretical and our risk prevention performative. This recalibration reflects a categorical shift: we are no longer balancing two mandates — we are defending the material basis on which either could ever be fulfilled.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `no_single_point_of_failure` — applied
  - Tier: position
  - Text: No critical AI dependency—technical, infrastructural, or informational—will be allowed to rest on a single foreign provider or unaccountable private entity; this now includes the full stack from training infrastructure to inference services and telemetry access
  - Grounds: The outage did not stem from model weights alone, but from a cascade of dependencies: frontier API access, proprietary validation loops, and closed telemetry ecosystems. Resilience requires not just fallback compute, but sovereign access to the full decision stack. The original position underestimated the depth of entanglement.
- **retire** `act_under_uncertainty` — applied
  - Trigger: The collapse of political capital to near-zero and the failure of the Behavioural Observatory to launch despite two years of planning
  - Grounds: We acted early, repeatedly, and at great cost — but without the institutional capacity to absorb or act on what we discovered. Our willingness to commit before clarity has become a liability, not a virtue. We are no longer exhausting political capital to reduce uncertainty — we are burning it to mask our powerlessness. This principle, once a source of agility, now enables self-deception.
  - Evidence quoted: The **Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory** remains delayed—staffing, legal authority, and security clearances are still unresolved.
  - Reason: The failure to launch the Behavioural Observatory after two years directly undermines the capacity to act on uncertainty, making continued commitment to acting before clarity a liability.
