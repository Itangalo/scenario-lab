### Capability Creeps Forward, Safety Stalls

AI capability advances by +0.5 to 63.0, entering the **62–68 terminal zone** where progress slows but persists. Frontier developers report tighter integration between planning and execution modules in autonomous agents, enabling longer-horizon tasks with reduced human oversight. No discrete incident occurs, but anomalous behaviours in test environments grow harder to interpret. With no safety improvements landing on deployed systems, `ai_safety` remains stagnant at 19.0.

### EU Shifts from Persuasion to Enforcement

The European Union activates its enforcement lever: the Commission issues an implementing act under the AI Liability Directive, suspending liability shielding for providers that deny EAAN auditors full access to model weights, telemetry, or red-teaming authority. The move targets two major US-based developers—one already rerouting EU inference through third parties, another withholding training data provenance. Legal challenges are expected; one developer threatens to withdraw entirely from the EU market.

Despite urgency, implementation falters. Member-state alignment is uneven, with Hungary and Sweden questioning the legal basis for extending DMAct enforcement to liability conditions. The Accelerate EAAN Certification Nodes initiative remains six months behind schedule due to unresolved staffing disputes and resistance from national agencies citing sovereignty concerns—now crystallising into open resistance under the banner of *emergent_eval_resistance*.

### Social Pressure Mounts

Spontaneous worker actions intensify in Lyon, Athens, and Gdansk as AI-driven displacement becomes visible in legal support and public administration roles. Unions demand retraining guarantees, but the Commission’s response—framing strict enforcement as a prerequisite for fair transition—is met with scepticism. Public sentiment dips to 32.0, reflecting growing frustration over job losses without visible relief.

Resilience declines by −1 to 40.0 as hardening efforts stall amid regulatory focus. Sovereignty drops to 34.0 due to persistent dependency and a −1 decay from capability growth. Political capital collapses to 6.0: the portfolio charge remains high (−8), no gains offset it, and internal discord amplifies the cost.
