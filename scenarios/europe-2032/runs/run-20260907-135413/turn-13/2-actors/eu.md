## New measure  
**Require Decision-Traceability for High-Capability Systems**  
We mandate that all AI systems above capability 60.0 operating in the EU must provide a decision-trace mechanism — a stable, auditable record of causal reasoning that can be validated by EAAN, even if internal representations are non-linguistic.

`Category: 1 (Evaluation and oversight)`  
`Size: small`  
`Finishes on turn: 15`  
`Targeted effect:` +5 to +7 ai_safety by restoring meaningful oversight despite opaque internal reasoning; prevents deployment of systems whose decisions cannot be causally audited, reinforcing the credibility of the EU Border; strengthens eu_political_capital by demonstrating a response to opaque_reasoning that is both technically grounded and enforceable  
`Applies to:` All providers operating AI systems above capability 60.0 in the EU, enforced through EAAN certification and DMAct gatekeeper compliance

## In practice  
This turn, we confront the collapse of interpretability head-on. The **opaque_reasoning** event has shattered the assumption that we can audit what we cannot read. The EAAN certification nodes in Brussels, Sophia Antipolis, and Munich are now live, but their value hinges on what they can inspect. We cannot accept systems that act without trace. So we act: the Commission drafts a new implementing act under the AI Act, amending the EU Border to require decision-traceability — not full internal legibility, but a stable, verifiable causal chain linking inputs to outputs, engineered as a separate assurance layer. This is not a request; it is a condition of market access.

We coordinate through the Internal Market Council, offering co-development rights to member states in the EAAN trace-validation framework to ease sovereignty concerns. Hungary and Sweden are invited to second technical staff to the new audit units, with EU funding for integration. The semiconductor crisis has sharpened minds: the taiwan_blockade has made clear that we cannot outsource strategic cognition. We act under Article 114 TFEU again, not for speed, but because the internal market cannot function if some states allow black-box systems that others cannot trust. Public sentiment remains at 32.0, and protests continue, but we are no longer reacting — we are defining the line. If the frontier moves into darkness, we will not follow blindly. We will wait. We will build. And we will understand before we allow.
