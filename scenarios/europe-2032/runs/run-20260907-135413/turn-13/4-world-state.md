### Sovereign Audit, Fractured Implementation

The EU completes *Accelerate EAAN Certification Nodes*, deploying audit infrastructure in Brussels, Sophia Antipolis, and Munich. However, operational integration is stalled by *emergent_eval_resistance*. Hungary and Sweden refuse full data access, citing constitutional limits, weakening central oversight. The Commission responds by launching *Require Decision-Traceability for High-Capability Systems*, a small category 1 measure mandating causal audit trails for all systems above 60.0 capability. It is framed as essential to preserve the EU Border’s credibility, but faces immediate legal scrutiny.

Despite offers of co-development rights and technical integration funding, coordination remains thin. One major provider signals it will reroute inference through non-EU nodes rather than expose decision logic. Another warns compliance may require architectural overhauls, delaying deployment timelines.
