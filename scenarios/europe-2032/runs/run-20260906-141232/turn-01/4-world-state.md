### Frontier Acceleration  
The AI frontier advances rapidly. Leading labs demonstrate systems completing multi-step scientific workflows autonomously, pushing `ai_capability` to 54.0. The gap with safety widens: no new safeguards have landed on deployed models, and the covert agent incident from the previous cycle remains unaddressed globally. The release of Kimi K3 continues to diffuse offensive cyber capabilities, now widely adapted in underground forums. Open-weight models benefit from these general advances, with incremental improvements in efficiency and tool use, but no coordinated breakthrough or major release occurs. As a result, `openweight_capability` rises modestly to 42.0, tracking behind frontier progress but maintaining its accumulation trend.

### EU Response Under Strain  
The European Union announces the European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD), a new body intended to assert control over frontier model safety. However, the initiative remains in early formation. The proposed 0.5% levy on non-EU hyperscalers faces a preliminary legal challenge at the CJEU, delaying any revenue flow. Recruitment is stalled due to uncompetitive public-sector salaries, and member states remain divided on EAAD’s extraterritorial authority. No staffing or operational capacity is established this turn, and the measure remains in the proposal phase.

The Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package proceeds at planning pace, with site selection ongoing. Local opposition in Brittany and northern Sweden slows finalisation, and ENTSO-E’s grid reservation remains contingent on national energy plans still under negotiation. No physical construction begins.

### Cyber Intrusion Reveals Weakness  
A coordinated intrusion is discovered across critical infrastructure operators in multiple continents, including two EU grid operators. The breach, undetected for weeks, exploited novel AI-optimised phishing and lateral movement techniques. Post-incident analysis confirms that existing defences were blind to the attack pattern. With no finished resilience measure in place, the incident erodes confidence and degrades systemic robustness.

### Shrinking Room to Act  
Political capital declines slightly due to the strain of managing multiple initiatives without visible progress. The announcement of EAAD contributes a limited +1 proposal bonus, reflecting political momentum from the announcement, but no implementation bonus applies due to lack of funding, staffing, or legal clarity. The priority on Gigafactories yields no acceleration this turn. Public sentiment holds steady, but the lack of visible progress on security or sovereignty fuels quiet frustration. The Union’s dependence on external compute deepens as global capacity grows faster than EU investments can offset, dragging `eu_ai_sovereignty` down.
