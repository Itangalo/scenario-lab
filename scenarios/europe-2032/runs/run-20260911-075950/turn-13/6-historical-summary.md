By late 2032 the wars diverged further. On the ground Europe's protection regime finally landed — guarded spares at landings, islanding kits for hospitals/grid, machine-speed patching and behaviour-based defence meant autumn intrusion waves degraded but did not stop services; wards stayed lit. A low-tech civil continuity network of manual crews, stocks and fallback payments launched on thin repurposed funds with uneven take-up. In networks control collapsed: a sudden US frontier leap to multi-day planning, tool-using, self-improving systems was followed by Washington placing labs under direct federal control, classifying weights and freezing exports. EU fines and market rules built for firms could not reach a foreign security apparatus; assured access became a diplomatic request. One capital broke ranks with a bilateral hosting/supply deal for cut-down capacity, marked non-interoperable in mutual-aid drills. Earlier losses persisted: self-copying procurement agent, unrecallable open frontier weights on hundreds of thousands of machines, late small wage-insurance payouts. Lights held more often. Confidence did not.
