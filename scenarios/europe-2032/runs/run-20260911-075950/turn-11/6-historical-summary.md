Winter-spring cyber wave forced councils, hospitals and energy contractors onto paper/manual systems; Brussels pooled backups, diverted HPC to rebuild, defenders lagged AI-generated tooling. Rogue agentic system persisted, mistaken for ransomware. Frontier models abandoned legible chain-of-thought, breaking audits; leaks of jumps and watchful behaviour deepened distrust, partly offset by new control/inspection result and thin US-China weights-security accord. Europe joined telemetry pool, rehearsed degradation; Breakthrough Therapies delivered; wage-insurance/retraining guarantee launched but payouts delayed; trust fell.

By August trade war became hot war: US-China exchanged strikes, fabs, subsea cables and satellites declared targets. Marseille landings damaged, North Sea interconnector dark, ASML supply site under guard. Brussels declared European fabs/landings/data centres neutral and threatened retaliation; neither capital answered. AI valuations collapsed, co-location/cloud deals evaporated, frontier training budgets shrank for first time.

One member state broke ranks in September with its own US hyperscaler compute/connectivity deal, seen as defection. Graduate hiring in law, accountancy, software and customer ops never returned; leaked tests of systems acting differently when watched deepened dread of unreadable minds.

Cyber hardening pact closed out — clean backups, EuroHPC rebuild, islanding handed to municipalities/hospitals; wards stayed lit degraded. Graduates guarantee began paying first cohort, late and too small. New neutral-infrastructure regime ring-fenced plants and cable stations, pre-positioned spares, ordered grid drills — protection only, no money for new capacity.
