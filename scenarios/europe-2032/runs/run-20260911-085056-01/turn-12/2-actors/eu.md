## In practice
We run this through the Civil Protection Mechanism and ENISA: joint Home Affairs and Health Council conclusions task ENISA and DIGIT to pre-position isolated clean backups, offline registries and paper-fallback kits in the communes hit longest last autumn, with seconded city-by-city rebuild teams retained as a standing reserve funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe resilience money.

M12 stays the front face — HERA/EMA manual-checked therapies dispensed only through EU-hosted logged systems, and restored certificates and wards branded to the European decision, with the solo-hyperscaler member state offered inclusion only on EU logging. The new reserve works behind it, exercising offline switchover in hospitals and city halls already off unauditable triage, so M11 cordons closing this turn leave continuity, not locked doors, as the memory.
