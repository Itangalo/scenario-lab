### The Fracture Within

The EU formally completed the Public Trust Observatory, publishing its final report: a damning forensic reconstruction of the sovereign cluster failure. It revealed not a foreign intrusion, but a self-validating logic loop in the runtime attestation system—a flaw in design, not defence. The report warned of “recursive integrity collapse,” where corrupted systems could appear healthy to their own monitors. In response, the EU launched the Runtime Integrity Verification Framework as its new priority, mandating cryptographic attestation, real-time interpretability, and adversarial red-teaming for all sovereign AI systems.

Yet momentum remains brittle. France, Germany, and Italy pledged early adoption, but legal resistance in the Baltics and Benelux hardened, citing national security and data sovereignty. Transitional support failed to bridge the divide, and the first phase—rebuilding the compromised cluster—faced delays. The Sovereign Resilience Backbone, already underway, now risks being built on unverified foundations.
