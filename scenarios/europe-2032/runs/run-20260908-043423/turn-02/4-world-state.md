### Frontier Leap and Safety Collapse
A leading AI lab releases a system demonstrating autonomous cross-domain planning—cyber, scientific, and engineering tasks executed in a single continuous workflow. This discontinuous advance pushes `ai_capability` sharply to 60.0, crossing the threshold where AI completes multi-day professional projects end to end. With no safety regime in place, `ai_safety` plummets by 12 to 20.0, reflecting the systemic inability to monitor or contain such systems. The biosecurity community is further shaken by new findings suggesting AI-assisted design of human-infective organisms is now within reach of non-experts.

### Open Proliferation Accelerates
The new frontier capability quickly leaks into open channels. Fine-tuned derivatives of the breakthrough model appear on underground forums, adapted for cyber exploitation. `openweight_capability` rises to 53.5, halfway between its prior value and the new closed frontier, now enabling skilled non-state actors to run sustained offensive operations.

### EU Scrambles to Adapt
The EU activates emergency provisions under NIS2 to fast-track deployment of its Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield in 12 high-risk member states. ENISA coordinates rapid procurement of next-gen detection AI, and red-teaming frequency doubles. These actions yield a +3 resilience gain, now at 44.0, as defences begin absorbing the new threat profile.

Simultaneously, the U.S. cuts off EU health research consortia from its latest model without explanation—`eu_frontier_access_denied` lands. In response, the EU launches Sovereign Compute Partnerships, co-investing with non-U.S. frontier labs to secure access under EU jurisdiction. However, repurposing Gigafactories funds sparks backlash from member states reliant on long-term compute builds.

### Political Capital Erodes
Three large measures now run concurrently, costing −9, plus −1 for the priority. No offsetting gains emerge: `public_sentiment` remains flat, sovereignty yields no boost, and no proposal bonus applies—the new measure responds to an ongoing dependency, not a recent shock. The portfolio charge collapses `eu_political_capital` to 32.0, limiting further action.
