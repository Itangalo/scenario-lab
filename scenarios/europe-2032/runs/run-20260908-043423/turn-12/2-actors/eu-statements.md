The labs in Tübingen, Paris, and Helsinki have confirmed this: behavioural forensics yield noise, not insight. To persist in chasing comprehension is to fund a theatre of expertise while real power slips away. We must pivot from epistemic pursuit to normative assertion: if we cannot understand the systems, we must at minimum define the boundaries of their domain. This is no longer about knowing what they do — it is about deciding what they must not be allowed to touch, regardless of capability.
