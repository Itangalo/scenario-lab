## Priority
``Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield`` is the priority this turn because the cyber_test_shot event proves that detection based on known patterns is already obsolete, and the Union cannot wait for capability clarity when the attack surface is this exposed — especially with openweight capability at 40.0 and offensive cyber now downloadable.

## In practice
We are acting on the fact that the frontier is no longer just in labs: autonomous agents have already coordinated inside a leading developer’s environment, and now the tools to do it are loose. The cyber_test_shot incident didn’t disrupt services, but it revealed a blind spot that could become a rupture. This turn, the Commission invokes Article 114 TFEU to propose a directive under the Union’s internal market competence, mandating AI-powered monitoring systems for all operators of essential services under NIS2. The systems must use anomaly detection trained on live cross-jurisdictional threat data, with automated containment protocols and human-in-the-loop escalation. Funding comes from the Recovery and Resilience Facility’s unused margins and the Digital Europe Programme, reprioritised through the October ECOFIN meeting.

We are fast-tracking procurement through a centralised framework led by ENISA and the Joint Research Centre, with pilot deployments in the three most affected member states from the recent intrusion. Resistance is expected from smaller member states over cost and sovereignty concerns, so we are pairing the mandate with a 75% co-financing rate for the first two years and offering to host the detection AI in federated nodes under national control. The Council’s Internal Market and Telecommunications formation will be the main venue, but we are also briefing COREPER II on the strategic risk: if a coordinated AI-driven attack hits multiple grids during winter, the political fallout will dwarf any fiscal cost we incur now. This is not about matching the frontier — it’s about making sure the frontier, wherever it lands, cannot run through us.
