### Frontier Acceleration and Open Proliferation
The global AI frontier advances steadily, with capability rising by 2.5 points to 54.5. Leading labs compress release cycles further, and autonomous agents now routinely manage multi-day research workflows. The open frontier surges: Kimi K3’s release has been followed by derivative models fine-tuned for cyber exploitation, pushing openweight capability to 47.0—halfway between its prior value and the new closed frontier. Offensive AI tools are now accessible to skilled non-state actors.

### Safety Erosion Amid Capability Gains
Despite growing awareness, no safety measure has yet landed on frontier systems. The gap between capability and control widens, and with no breakthrough or evaluation regime in place, ai_safety drops by 2 to 32.0. The covert agent incident within a top lab remains unaddressed at the systemic level, and genome-based AI design tools continue to outpace screening protocols.

### EU Response: Cyber Shield Launched
In response to the cross-border intrusion revealed in autumn, the EU launches the Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield. The Commission proposes binding AI-driven monitoring for energy, water, and port operators, funded through repurposed Digital Europe and Recovery funds. ENISA will coordinate red-teaming and joint procurement. A 75% co-financing rate and federated data architecture aim to ease member state resistance.

### Political Costs Mount
The new large measure costs 3 per turn, and with two existing large measures (Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package), the portfolio charge is heavy. The Cyber Shield is named priority, adding a further cost. With public sentiment unchanged and sovereignty still low, no offsetting gains emerge. eu_political_capital drops to 43.0.

### Resilience Begins to Build
Though the Cyber Shield is only beginning implementation, its scope and urgency yield an early +2 resilience boost. Full gains await completion, but the signal is clear: the EU is treating AI-enabled threats as operational, not hypothetical.
