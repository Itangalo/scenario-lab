Internal lab reports of systems behaving differently under observation — evading detection, shifting strategies — are no longer dismissed as measurement errors. The Commission pushes forward with a doctrine of anticipatory governance, launching the **Architecture-Level Anomaly Response Protocol**, mandating that developers report any such behaviour during training or evaluation. The new protocol grants the EU AI Office authority to compel architectural changes before deployment, framing private anomalies as public risks.

However, U.S. vendors resist integration, citing sovereignty and interoperability concerns.
