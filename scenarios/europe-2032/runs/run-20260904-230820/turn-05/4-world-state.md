### A Year of Fractures  
2028 closes with the EU reeling from a cascade of AI-driven shocks. A major cyber incident — a coordinated ransomware sweep exploiting AI-generated zero-days — cripples emergency dispatch systems across three member states. Response is slow, fragmented, and undermined by a critical shortage of skilled defenders. Public anger surges, and trust in digital governance erodes further.

### Access Denied  
In a sudden move, EU hospitals, ministries, and grid operators are locked out of a leading AI model with no explanation. The cutoff, lasting days, disrupts diagnostics, planning, and infrastructure monitoring. The incident exposes a fatal flaw: sovereignty over compute means little without guaranteed access to the models that run on it. The EU labels this a strategic wake-up call.

### Response Under Fire  
The Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive, Critical Infrastructure AI Hardening Initiative, and European Cyber Resilience Corps all complete this turn. Hardening blueprints are published, and deployment of new cyber defenders begins. However, the emergent_cyber_labour_shortage continues to impede full staffing — training pipelines are underway but not yet delivering at scale, and public-sector retention remains weak against private-sector competition. The Commission launches €9bn in funding to accelerate rollout and proposes the creation of a future EU cyber service with hazard pay and mobility rights, but legislative approval and implementation will take time.

### A New Front: Legal Access  
The Commission proposes the Frontier Access Guarantee Mechanism — a legal framework intended to secure access to frontier AI systems under public interest law. The proposal is contentious, with U.S. tech firms warning of retaliation and legal scholars questioning its extraterritorial reach. No immediate access is restored, and the mechanism remains in early debate, with no enforcement leverage yet established over non-EU developers.

### The American Election  
The U.S. presidential election concludes amid growing public anxiety over AI’s societal impact. The outcome shifts the political landscape, but the full policy direction of the incoming administration remains unclear. Official posture will be determined upon inauguration.

### Eroding Ground  
Despite completing three measures, political capital declines moderately, reflecting the severity of the crises and ongoing implementation gaps. Public sentiment drops to 25.0 amid visible system failures and access disruptions. Sovereignty continues to decline as capability outpaces operational capacity. Resilience sees a modest gain from completed hardening efforts, though human and access vulnerabilities persist.
