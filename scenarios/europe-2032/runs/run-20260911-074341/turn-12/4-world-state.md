### Informed afterwards
The spring brought a communiqué from Washington and Beijing: a limited pact on securing model weights, restraining autonomous escalation, and restricting a class of biological design tools, with inspection thin but real. Brussels learned the details from the press summary. The foreign policy chief spent weeks shuttling between embassies offering cable-landing logs and incident data in exchange for observer status. No invitation came.

At home the exclusion landed hard. Commentators called it the war no one voted for producing the peace no one consulted Europe on. Talk shows replayed images of American-planned therapies in Rotterdam and Barcelona while European engineers guarded fenced, power-starved build sites.

### Holding, not building
With no money for new programmes, administration became the strategy. Municipal clinics and ports stayed degraded-not-stopped through procedures drilled in earlier years. National response teams patched ransomware damage street by street.

The one trust claim left was the welfare redress. Reviewers continued file-by-file re-decisions, publishing logs, paying arrears where seconds-long approvals had cut benefits. Queues stayed frozen. Victims received cheques, but televised against blackouts and exclusion headlines, remedy read as apology without power.

By June the Union was intact and quieter. Services held. No new chips sailed from the Pacific. The catalyst breakthrough stayed on paper. Survival without leverage rebuilt a sliver of administrative credibility, and spent the last of the illusion that restraint alone would buy a seat at the table.
