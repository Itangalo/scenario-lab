### An invitation, at last
The invitation that arrived in July was not for the weights pact. It was for something operational: a joint cyber command pooling real-time telemetry, and a linked biosurveillance pact with binding sample-sharing. After the spring of learning about deals from press summaries, a seat — even a technical one — was politically irresistible.

The Council acceded quickly. ENISA, CERT-EU, ECDC and HERA were mandated to plug in feeds under existing solidarity and health articles. No new money was voted; liaison officers were reassigned from exhausted recovery cells. The seat came as a guest in an American-led room, not as shared control — access without autonomy.

### Patching at machine speed
Accession coincided with a genuine shift in defensive tooling. Automated patching that moves as fast as vulnerabilities are found, and detectors that catch swarm behaviour rather than signatures, were pushed through drilled procedures to clinics, ports and power operators.

Street-by-street patching became overnight patching. Operators reported ransomware waves dying on contact for the first time in years. Services still degraded, but they no longer stopped.

The relief was real, and partial. The models behind the tools no longer show their work. Intermediate reasoning in the frontier systems has become unreadable — announced as an architecture win, experienced as an oversight loss. Checklists that depended on reading the chain of thought stopped working, forcing welfare reviewers and clinical overseers onto slower outcome audits and black-box tests. Oversight was eroded but not collapsed; existing institutions continued to function in degraded form.

### Cures that prove dependence
Autumn brought tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions into Rotterdam and Barcelona wards, delivered by American-planned models. Families celebrated; talk shows split the screen between recoveries and the fenced, power-starved build sites at home.

Procured under continuity rules, the cures arrived as treatment, not as autonomy. Gratitude mixed openly with humiliation, and each course deepened reliance on foreign compute and foreign models.

At the same time a near-frontier open release spread to hundreds of thousands of downloads in a week. Whatever it carried is now on private hardware permanently, outside regulatory reach.

By December the Union held essential services better than it had in two years, sat inside one operational room it had been shut out of before, and could neither inspect the minds it depended on nor build their equals. Survival had gained a tool. Leverage had slipped further.
