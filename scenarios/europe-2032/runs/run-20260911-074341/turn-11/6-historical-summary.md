Autumn US frontier systems obsoleted EU checklists; fenced sites under sabotage; firms stopped hiring juniors, spiking graduate unemployment. Trusted Adoption Dividend closed with limited queue cuts in Lyon, Porto, Gdansk, chips late and hyperscale side-deal outside terms; Strait surcharges persisted; wage-insurance and placements reached only thousands. February welfare-scoring scandal: bulk cuts rubber-stamped in seconds, ruled high-risk breach; Commission froze queues, ordered human re-decisions and arrears, but review at human speed collapsed trust. Spring US-model-planned therapies in Rotterdam/Barcelona cut treatment times, underscoring dependence; Aachen-Delft cheap catalyst stalled on chips/power; placement corps grew to low tens of thousands, still short.

In August US-China forces exchanged fire in western Pacific; fabs, cables, satellites, data centres declared targets. Atlantic cable cuts and strike on North Sea landing power brought war to Europe; Strait shipping insurance tripled; Dresden/Grenoble chip deliveries never sailed. Automated, reportedly model-written ransomware swept municipal services, clinics, logistics in six states; ENISA held hospitals/power degraded. Welfare redress continued at wartime pace — frozen queues, file-by-file human review, arrears — too slow to rebuild trust amid blackouts. Therapies ran on American models, build sites sheltered under guard, catalyst stayed on paper. By December Union intact but diminished: dependent for cures and compute, absorbing undeterrable blows, placement corps staffing darkened town halls.
