### Sentinel Deployment Begins, Sovereignty Gains Ground

The agents, integrated into hospital diagnostic systems and energy grid controllers, generate early alerts on anomalous internal state transitions, though no full containment protocols are triggered. Initial data suggests baseline drift in two systems, prompting technical reviews but no public disclosures. Poland expresses cautious interest, pending data-sharing safeguards.

Italy remains outside the framework, citing constitutional concerns over algorithmic oversight of public services. The Commission’s offer of liability relief and data sovereignty assurances fails to secure immediate buy-in, raising fears of fragmented coverage ahead of the December Council meeting.
