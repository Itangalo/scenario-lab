### Capability Creeps Forward

Despite global uncertainty, the frontier of AI capability advances steadily. With no major safety breakthroughs or containment events, the most powerful systems reach new levels of autonomous operation in research and cyber domains, pushing `ai_capability` to 73.5. Open-weight models, while still lagging, absorb enough from leaked toolchains and public research to rise to 61.0—now capable of running multi-day cyber campaigns with minimal oversight.

### Resilience Gains Ground, at a Cost

The EU completes two major initiatives: the Autonomous System Boundary Trials and the Critical Function Sentinel Deployment. The former yields modest technical insights but fails to prevent external breaches, while the latter successfully detects anomalies in high-risk systems across healthcare and energy. Together, they contribute to a resilience gain of +3, bringing the metric to 56.0 as monitoring infrastructure becomes operational.

Simultaneously, the Cyber Resilience Surge enters full deployment, hardening critical systems in nine member states. Air-gapped backups are installed, and ENISA coordinates rapid-response drills—though Italy participates only under mutual assistance terms, not binding commitments.

### Truth in the Crosshairs

In response to synthetic election interference, the EU launches the Critical Information Integrity Shield. Pilots begin in France and Finland, integrating AI-powered deepfake detection and media provenance tracking into electoral and emergency systems. Public broadcasters adopt the trust indicators, but major platforms resist full compliance, citing technical burden. Still, the visible effort begins to shift public perception: sentiment rises to 30.0 as citizens respond to tangible action.

### Political Capital Erodes

The portfolio now includes three large measures, one of which is prioritized. The full charge hits: −3 for each large measure, −1 for the priority. With no sovereignty gains and a capability rise of 1.5, `eu_ai_sovereignty` drops to 32.5. Political capital, strained by execution costs and partial buy-in, falls to 38.0 despite the proposal bonus for the new category 6 measure.
