Zero-trust architectures are deployed across energy and health networks, and Operation Iron Gate II reveals critical gaps in cross-border coordination. Despite fast-tracking, integration remains uneven—some states delay implementation, citing cost and national control. The perimeter hardens, but cracks persist.

All new integrations are suspended. Yet progress is fragile—engineers are overstretched, and trust in EU systems continues to erode.

Technically feasible, politically fraught—it passes, but implementation will take years. For now, it is a promise, not a shield.

Public sentiment remains frozen at 28.0. No visible benefit offsets the fear. Media fixates on autonomy incidents and the exodus of researchers. The fog of uncertainty deepens.

The storm is not over. It is gathering.
