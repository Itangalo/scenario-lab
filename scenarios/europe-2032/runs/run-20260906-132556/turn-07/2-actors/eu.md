The Institutional Integrity Audit continues to expose systemic vulnerabilities: we have now suspended AI advisory use in Italy’s cybersecurity agency and launched a formal investigation into Eurocontrol’s traffic coordination logs. We are also mandating that all critical operators adopt Sovereign Core fallback protocols by turn 9, with compliance tied to NIS3 certification and Cohesion Fund disbursements. Resistance from member states relying on foreign cloud providers is high, particularly in the Baltics and Benelux; we are offering transitional co-financing and joint liability shielding to secure buy-in. This is no longer about competition. It is about survival — and about proving that the Union still governs.
