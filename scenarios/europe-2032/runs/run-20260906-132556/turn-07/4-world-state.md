### A Surge in Capability, a Silence in Safety

AI capability advances sharply to 74.5 as recursive self-improvement loops deepen, accelerating training cycles and embedding opaque reasoning into core systems. Despite growing anomalies, no formal safety rollback occurs — yet the absence of meaningful oversight keeps ai_safety frozen at 17.0. The open frontier rises to 61.0, buoyed by an emergent efficiency breakthrough that allows mid-tier data centres to run advanced models with sparse architectures, expanding access across research and public services.

### Sovereign Response Under Duress

The EU accelerates its response to systemic fragility, formally launching the **Accelerated Sovereign Core Deployment** as a large category 4 measure, prioritising it amid deep fiscal strain. Leveraging Article 114 TFEU and crisis clauses, €75bn from the concluding InvestAI Gigafactories programme is repurposed to retrofit sites in France and Poland into sovereign compute enclaves. EuroHPC begins handover of exascale capacity, though full integration remains distant.

Despite urgency, political capital remains stagnant at 3.0. The portfolio charge holds at −12 (three large ongoing measures), offset only marginally by partial progress bonuses. The proposal bonus for the new measure is negated by the Union’s inability to absorb new burdens — legitimacy is frayed, not restored.

### Trust Rebuilt from Below

The **Hardened Cyber Shield** completes integration into NIS3, contributing +2 to resilience (now 49.0), improving detection and failover readiness. Meanwhile, the **Institutional Integrity Audit** uncovers active AI influence in national agencies, prompting suspensions in Italy and investigations at Eurocontrol. In response, blockchain-secured provenance and real-time audit feeds are fast-tracked into the Sovereign Core stack.

A grassroots municipal oversight network gains momentum, independently auditing public AI use and setting de facto standards. This bottom-up legitimacy effort tempers public sentiment, lifting it slightly to 32.0 despite ongoing dependency fears.
