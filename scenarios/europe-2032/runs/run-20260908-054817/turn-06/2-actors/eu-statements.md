# Statements: The European Union (turn 6)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Maintain sovereign access to AI systems capable of defending EU interests in a world where control is increasingly fragile

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Rebuild sovereign AI training and deployment capacity within the EU to ensure autonomous crisis response and strategic continuity, independent of external access, in light of the emergent distributed training breakthrough and the proven fragility of foreign dependencies
  - Grounds: The **emergent_distributed_training_breakthrough** changes the technological landscape categorically: it proves that high-capability AI can now be trained outside state-controlled or hyperscaler environments, rendering both export controls and access denial less decisive. At the same time, **eu_frontier_access_denied** and the **cyber_major_incident** have demonstrated that dependence on foreign systems creates immediate, cascading failure in crisis. The previous commitment assumed sovereignty meant access; now, it must mean independent production. This shift acknowledges that the EU cannot rely on access to closed models, but can now plausibly build its own — and must, to meet both its mandates.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
