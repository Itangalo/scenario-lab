### Closing One Chapter, Opening Another

The EU formally concluded two major programmes: the **InvestAI Gigafactories** and the **Emergency Resilience Surge**. While the Gigafactories have secured permits and funding, their physical infrastructure remains under construction, delaying tangible compute gains. Nevertheless, the completion marks a symbolic victory, reinforcing the Union’s commitment to infrastructure sovereignty.

The **Emergency Resilience Surge** delivered measurable progress. ENISA activated cross-border cyber response teams, and fallback diagnostic systems are now operational in several major hospitals. These measures helped absorb recent AI-enabled cyber probes, limiting disruption — a quiet but significant validation of resilience investment.

Buoyed by this momentum, the EU launched the **Sovereign Model Training Initiative** as its new priority, repurposing decommissioned industrial sites in Poland and France into auditable AI training clusters. Early coordination via EuroHPC and national clouds is underway, though technical integration remains fragile. Legal harmonisation under Article 114 TFEU faces resistance from member states wary of centralised data mandates. The initiative is classified as a category 4 measure and receives a +5 proposal bonus for addressing `eu_frontier_access_denied` and leveraging the `emergent_distributed_training_breakthrough`.

The **middle_power_coalition** continues to evolve, with informal coordination intensifying among EU, Japan, South Korea, Canada, and Nordic states on mutual AI access and red lines. Discussions on a **Joint Access Framework** are advancing but remain preliminary, with no formal agreement adopted and no decision to launch it as a distinct measure this turn. Partners welcome expanded access but resist external oversight mechanisms; work continues to align expectations. For now, it remains a diplomatic track under exploration, not a launched initiative, and carries no proposal bonus or portfolio charge.

Public sentiment holds steady at 30, as scepticism persists over whether these moves translate into real autonomy. Meanwhile, U.S. restrictions on model access continue under the **CONSOLIDATION** posture, reinforcing Europe’s drive for self-reliance.

### Faint Signals on the Horizon

Rumours grow of a **regional aid pact** among Nordic and Benelux states to pool compute and red-team open models — a parallel effort that could complement or complicate EU strategy. Meanwhile, leading U.S. and UK labs are said to be restricting collaboration with European researchers, citing security concerns, threatening joint projects.
