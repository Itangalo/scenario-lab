## Priority  
**Emergency Resilience Surge** — because the cyber_test_shot proves that detection failed and that the threat is already inside, making resilience the most urgent lever to prevent cascading harm while sovereignty takes years to build.

## In practice  
We are treating the cyber_test_shot as a wake-up call: the intrusion was not stopped by existing defences, and its discovery was accidental. This cannot stand. This turn, we are activating the Crisis Coordination Mechanism under the Union Civil Protection Mechanism to launch the Emergency Resilience Surge, directing ENISA, the European Cybersecurity Agency, to lead a binding audit of all critical energy, water and port operators within three months. By March 2027, every such operator must demonstrate AI-resistant segmentation, anomaly detection with automated rollback, and live red-teaming protocols. Funding of €18bn will be drawn from the Recovery and Resilience Facility’s unused digital tranche and the European Defence Fund’s dual-use envelope, with Council approval under Article 122 TFEU for urgency.  

The pushback is real: member states resist central mandates on infrastructure, and utilities warn of cost and complexity. We are offering full co-financing (90%) and fast-track access to the InvestAI Gigafactories’ future compute for compliance. We are also instructing Frontex and the European Defence Agency to repurpose AI-driven surveillance models for internal network defence, creating a dual-use incentive. This is not about building our own frontier AI — that is the longer game — but about ensuring that when the next attack comes, it does not shut down the lights. The Tech sovereignty package continues its legislative crawl through the Council and Parliament, with the Commission offering bilateral side-deals on grid access to win over reluctant capitals. The Gigafactories are in early site selection, but land acquisition and environmental waivers are moving under emergency energy security provisions in Germany, France, and Poland.  

We are acting on two time horizons: resilience now, sovereignty later. The cyber_test_shot changed nothing about our long-term commitment — it only proved how little margin we have.
