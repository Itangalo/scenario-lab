### The Weight of Enforcement

AI capability advances to 61.0 as frontier labs refine the new architecture, extending autonomous execution into longer, more complex workflows. Yet no corresponding safety gain follows. The earlier breakthrough in interpretable reasoning remains confined to research, not yet embedded in shipped systems. With capability rising and no new safeguards in place, `ai_safety` stagnates.

The EU pushes forward on sovereignty, launching the **Accelerated Firmware Forensics and Trusted Stack Certification Network**—a large category 4 measure aimed at grounding the Sovereign Compute Certification Framework in technical reality. Six new labs are slated, funding redirected, protocols drafted. But rollout is slow: national agencies dispute jurisdiction, and the secure repository in Luxembourg faces integration delays. The measure is in flight, not delivering.

Worse, geopolitical pressure mounts. The US, under its **CONSOLIDATION** posture, tightens chip and model export controls, rationing access even to allied partners. Simultaneously, Washington compels the Netherlands to restrict ASML’s servicing of older lithography tools—equipment critical to Europe’s own industrial base. This supply-chain coercion strikes at the EU’s weakest leverage point, exposing its dependency.

Brussels responds with resolve, declaring the Sovereign Compute Certification Framework a priority. But political capital is too low—only 12.0—to gain any acceleration benefit. Hungary and Poland remain defiant, rejecting audit timelines. A solidarity fund is offered, but trust is thin. ENISA’s authority grows on paper, not in practice.

Public sentiment falls to 25.0 as job losses in entry-level law, finance, and tech become undeniable. Media spotlight graduates locked out of the job market, while data centre siting protests grow in Germany and Spain. The promised benefits of AI remain abstract; the costs are immediate.

Resilience dips as no category 6 measure lands, and systemic strain grows. Sovereignty declines further—capability growth costs −1, and no completion bonus arrives. Political capital collapses under the weight of three large measures in flight, including the new one. The proposal bonus fails to materialise: the firmware scandal persists, but the new labs are too incremental to count as a decisive response.

### A Union Under Strain

The EU acts with purpose, but its foundations are too weak to bear the load. Authority is claimed, not commanded.
