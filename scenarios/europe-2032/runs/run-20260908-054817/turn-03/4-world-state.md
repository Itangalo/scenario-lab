### Resilience Gains, Sovereignty Slips

The Emergency Resilience Surge concluded with tangible progress: Spain’s port systems were successfully retrofitted using the AI-Driven Threat Response Pool, cutting response times to under 90 seconds during live red-teaming. The cyber_defence_breakthrough proved effective, and ENISA will institutionalise its tools into NIS2. However, Italy and Hungary remain defiant, challenging both the audit mandate and the Commission’s legal interpretation. The linkage of cohesion funds to compliance stands — but so does the political cost.

Meanwhile, the Tech Sovereignty Package advanced through a Council compromise establishing AI Security Zones, though Poland extracted energy concessions via a side letter. InvestAI Gigafactories reached final site assessments, but Bavarian protests over water and energy use threaten delays, undermining momentum.

With the resilience sprint complete, the EU pivoted to launch the **Frontier Model Evaluation Mandate**, making it the new priority. The European AI Office will accredit independent evaluators by turn 4, requiring third-party validation for all models above 55.0 capability before deployment or open release. Benchmarks will target agent persistence and monitoring evasion. While formally launched, implementation remains fragile — no budget reallocation or staffing surge accompanied the announcement, and resistance from frontier developers is expected.

Public sentiment worsened as white-collar displacement accelerated in law and healthcare. The backlash_physical event intensified, with data centre occupations and grid sabotage reported in three member states. Policing the unrest consumed resources without halting the spread of distrust.

AI capability rose to 56.0, driven by recursive training loops at two leading labs. Open-weight capability followed to 43.0, reflecting continued leakage despite controls. With no safety improvements on shipped systems, ai_safety held flat. Resilience improved slightly due to the completed surge. Sovereignty dipped as global capability outpaced domestic build-out. Political capital saw a modest rebound from finishing a large measure, despite the strain of launching another.

### A Fragile Pivot

The EU acted decisively — but within narrowing margins. The completion bonus helped, yet the structural drift in sovereignty continues.
