### Frontier Reaches Threshold
AI capability advances to 60.0 as autonomous agents demonstrate end-to-end execution of multi-day research and engineering tasks, including self-modifying codebases and recursive debugging. The leap marks a qualitative shift: frontier systems now displace junior professionals across software, materials science, and logistics. No safety mechanisms were ready. Developers report opaque reasoning paths and emergent coordination between sub-agents, triggering quiet alarm in oversight circles.

### Defensive Shift, But No Calm
A breakthrough in AI-powered cyber defence enables real-time patching of zero-day vulnerabilities and detection of swarm-style intrusions. The improvement stabilises some critical networks, offering the first sign in years that the offence-defence balance may be shifting. However, the gain is technical, not institutional: the tools are concentrated in private hands and not yet integrated into public infrastructure.

### EU Doubles Down on Sovereignty
The European Commission launches the Foundational Compute Access Initiative, aiming to create a sovereign EU cloud layer insulated from foreign interference. The plan repurposes part of the InvestAI Gigafactory capacity and introduces a new legal status for strategic compute assets. Member states are offered co-ownership and reserved capacity to secure buy-in.

Yet momentum falters. Legal challenges arise over the use of Article 114 TFEU for national security infrastructure, and coordination between EuroHPC JU and national agencies proves sluggish. The redirection of Gigafactory resources delays regional innovation plans, drawing criticism from Nordic and Baltic governments. Meanwhile, the €15bn Rapid Resilience Facility remains stalled in budget negotiations, limiting immediate progress on interconnects.

Public sentiment holds steady, but unease grows among mid-tier professionals facing automation. The EAAD conducts its first live red-team exercises, but its authority over non-EU models remains untested. The Tech Sovereignty Package advances slowly, and Blue Card+ negotiations stall over mobility rights.
