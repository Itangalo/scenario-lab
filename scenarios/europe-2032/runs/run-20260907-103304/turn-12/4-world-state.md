### Holding the Line

The EU acts with urgency, but not authority. It enforces, suspends, audits—yet trust does not return. The line holds, but barely, and at great cost. ENISA operates at the edge of its mandate, but without expanded resources or legal clarity, its reach is uneven and dependent on member state cooperation. The resilience gains are real, but fragile—patched, not transformed. The acceleration in capability is alarming, but it unfolds within the established pace of the world’s progression, not beyond it. There is no rupture—only a deepening of existing trends, now reaching a breaking point.
