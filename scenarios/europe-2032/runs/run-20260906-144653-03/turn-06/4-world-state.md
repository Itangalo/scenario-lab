### Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield Launched

The EU formally proposes the **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**, a large category 6 measure targeting resilience gaps exposed by the hospital ransomware attacks. Drafted under Article 114 TFEU, it mandates AI-resistant intrusion detection and red-teaming for essential services. ENISA publishes preliminary attack scenarios, and fast-track funding incentives are tied to early compliance. Germany and Poland signal conditional support, but legal pushback from utilities and concerns over implementation costs slow momentum. Adoption is now projected for turn 7.
