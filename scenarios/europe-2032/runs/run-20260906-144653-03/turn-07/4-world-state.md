### Capability Plateau and US-China Truce

Signs of a slowdown in frontier AI progress emerge as a flagship release underperforms internal benchmarks, and prices for top-tier compute begin to fall — a rare signal that capability gains are compressing. Meanwhile, Washington and Beijing announce a fragile understanding on AI risk governance, focusing on securing model weights and limiting autonomous cyber escalation. The agreement, thin on verification, excludes the EU, underscoring its absence from the core strategic dialogue.

### Cyber Shield Lands

The **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield** clears final legal hurdles and is formally adopted, marking a rare delivery moment for the EU. Mandating AI-resistant defences across energy, health and transport, the directive is fast-tracked with incentives tied to InvestAI funding. ENISA publishes red-team scenarios based on recent ransomware patterns, and cross-border drills are scheduled. Germany and Poland commit to early compliance, though smaller member states voice concerns over implementation costs.

### Sovereignty Advances

With the **InvestAI Gigafactories** completed, the EU now hosts a self-sustaining bloc of sovereign compute. Combined with the operationalisation of the **SEAL** maintenance corridor for ASML tools, this yields a +5 boost to **eu_ai_sovereignty**. The rise in AI capability (+0.5) triggers a −1 decay, but no access event occurs. Net gain: +4.

### Political Capital Rebounds

The portfolio charge — four large measures (−12) and a priority (−1) — is partially offset by a +5 finish bonus for the Gigafactories and a +3 resilience gain from the Cyber Shield. No proposal bonus applies, as the Shield was introduced earlier. Political capital climbs to 28.0, aided by delivery momentum.

### Safety and Sentiment Stalled

Despite progress, **ai_safety** remains stagnant at 19.0. The evaluation shield faces growing resistance from labs limiting audit access, and no new safeguards have landed on deployed systems. Public sentiment holds at 35.0, caught between recognition of defensive action and persistent anxiety over job displacement and opaque AI governance.
