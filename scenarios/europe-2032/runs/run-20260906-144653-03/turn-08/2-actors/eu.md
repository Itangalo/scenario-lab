## In practice  
This turn, we initiate the AI Safety Emergency Investment by drafting a Council Regulation under Article 114 TFEU to establish the European AI Assurance Network (EAAN), a distributed technical agency with secure access to frontier models developed or deployed in the EU. The €40bn envelope combines €25bn from the EU budget (drawn from unused cohesion funds and the pandemic recovery instrument) and €15bn in matched national co-financing, with disbursement tied to delivery milestones. We task the European Commission’s Digital Directorate to stand up the first operational cells in Brussels, Sophia Antipolis, and Munich by turn 9, focusing on three capabilities: runtime agent monitoring, weight-provenance verification, and adversarial stress-testing of long-horizon planning systems.

We simultaneously open negotiations with Germany, France, and the Netherlands to designate national high-security compute enclaves for model inspection, leveraging the completed InvestAI Gigafactories and the SEAL corridor to ensure physical and legal control. Access will be governed by a new class of EU-level security clearance, and we offer member states fast-tracked resilience funding and liability shielding under the AI Liability Directive in exchange for hosting nodes. Resistance from industry is expected — particularly from US-based developers who have already restricted audit access — so we prepare to invoke the Digital Markets Act’s gatekeeper provisions and the AI Act’s high-risk obligations to compel cooperation, backed by the threat of market suspension for non-compliant systems above 60.0 capability.

With political capital still fragile at 28.0 and public sentiment at 35.0, we frame the investment not as a new industrial bid but as a necessary correction: a firewall against uncontrolled capability growth. The completion of the Cyber Shield and InvestAI Gigafactories has bought temporary breathing room; this turn, we use it to build the technical eyes and hands the Union lacks. Without them, sovereignty is hollow, and safety is a slogan.
