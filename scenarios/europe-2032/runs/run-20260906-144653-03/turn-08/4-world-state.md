### Capability Creeps Up

Despite signs of a plateau, AI capability advances by another half-point, reaching 61.5. The slowdown in compute price growth and underperforming flagship models have eased pressure, but marginal gains persist through refinement and scaling. Open-weight capability follows, edging up to 49.5, as older frontier models continue to leak into open channels through academic collaborations and grey-market redistribution.

### EU Acts on Safety Gap

The European Union launches the **AI Safety Emergency Investment**, a €40bn programme to build sovereign technical capacity for monitoring and auditing high-capability AI. Framed as a corrective to the stagnation of AI safety at 19.0, the plan establishes the European AI Assurance Network (EAAN), with nodes planned in Brussels, Sophia Antipolis, and Munich. The Commission begins drafting a Council Regulation under Article 114 TFEU and initiates talks with Germany, France, and the Netherlands on hosting secure inspection enclaves.

### Political Capital Strained

The new large measure costs −3 per turn, and as the priority, an additional −1. With no measures finishing this turn, the portfolio charge is −4. The proposal bonus is **none** — no major safety incident has recently occurred to justify a surge, and the stagnation, while alarming, has not yet crystallised into crisis. Public sentiment remains flat at 35.0, offering no legitimacy lift. Political capital falls to 23.0.

### Sovereignty Holds

No access event occurs, and the InvestAI Gigafactories are now complete, contributing no further momentum. The +0.5 rise in capability triggers a −1 decay under rule 5, but no new category 4 measure is finishing. Sovereignty remains at 37.0.

### Atmosphere of Doubt

Evaluation resistance and labour unrest continue to simmer. Researchers report further delays in accessing model internals, and junior professionals in legal and administrative sectors voice growing anxiety. No incident has yet broken the surface, but the gap between capability and control widens.
