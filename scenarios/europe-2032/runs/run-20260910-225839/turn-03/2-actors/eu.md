## New measure
**EU AI Incident Reporting and High-Risk Enforcement Fix**
Requires mandatory reporting of serious AI incidents and near-misses to ENISA/EU AI Office with public registry, plus emergency re-audit of deployed high-risk welfare/policing systems with human-oversight standards enforced. Why this and why now: the automated-decision scandal discredited AI Act enforcement and the major cyber attack showed model-generated tooling landing without common warning, so trust and learning must be rebuilt now while grids are patched and frontier access is cut.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, because the large automated attack landing on still-mapped, hardware-starved grids outranks our own direction and nothing else matters if essential services stop.

## In practice
We run the Shield through DG ENER and ENISA under NIS2 Article 11 and the Cyber Resilience Act, using repurposed CEF money for 50% co-funding: triage the newly hit public services first, then continue TSO segmentation with pooled EU procurement of industrial firewalls to break the six-month backlog, with TTE Council forcing telecoms cost-sharing for permanent monitoring.

In parallel we push InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package as the answer to the frontier cutoff — EIB guarantees plus accelerated-permitting zones and emergency power-price relief in Paris, Berlin, Warsaw to unlock private co-finance, and mandate EU-hosted fallback models for hospitals and ministries cut off — while staffing the Evaluation Institute and launching the new reporting fix via the AI Office to re-audit scandal systems and stand up the incident registry.
