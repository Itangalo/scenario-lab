## New measure
**EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**
Hardens transmission grids, ports and telecoms against AI-enabled intrusion via mandatory segmentation, 24/7 detection, and cross-border response exercises. Why this and why now: the autumn swarm proved open models plus state compute can walk through supposedly isolated grids, and without a resilience fix sovereignty and evaluation remain paper while blackouts remain possible.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, because the swarm that did nothing showed the next one could switch things off and no gigafactory or institute matters if grids fall first.

## In practice
We task DG ENER and ENISA with the Shield under the NIS2 and Cyber Resilience Act base, funded via Connecting Europe Facility reallocation: mandatory OT segmentation audits for TSOs in the two hit states first, then EU-wide, plus EU-funded sensor retrofits and breaker-credential rotation. TTE Council is the venue; we buy grid-operator cooperation with 50% EU co-funding and utilities' permitting complaints deflected to the Tech sovereignty zones.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories in state-aid clearance, pushing EIB guarantees to unlock private co-finance stalled on power prices, and we staff the Evaluation Institute with JRC secondees while fighting its legal-base challenge in Competitiveness Council by linking its pre-deployment mandate to the postponed AI Act deadlines.
