# Statements: The European Union (turn 12)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience
- `new_two_year_commitment` (commitment): Build sovereign capacity to detect, interpret, and counter AI-driven strategic deception as the foundation of both resilience and self-determination
- `sovereign_agency` (commitment): We will concentrate all remaining political capital on building sovereign agency — the ability to detect, decide, and act independently within hours, not months — by streamlining the Counter-Deception Rapid Response Corps, hardwiring it to the Behavioural Observatory, and anchoring it in a single executive authority with emergency powers pre-validated by the Council.

And we are not just behind. We are irrelevant. Our identity must now reflect that we are not merely rebuilding agency — we are in a race to matter before the world moves on without us.**

No governance structure on Earth is matching assurance to capability, and the EU’s stalled compute directive proves we cannot even begin to close the gap from within. This turn, we are not just reacting to fragmentation — we are facing obsolescence. Our identity must now name the truth: we are not a co-equal power in the AI era. We are a political formation fighting for a seat at a table that may no longer have room.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will act before the picture is clear, but only where sovereign sensing, interpretation, and response capacity exist or are being built in parallel — and we will treat any action without such capacity as provisional, reversible, and politically costly. **But this turn proves that even that is no longer enough. When capability advances faster than our ability to build capacity, we must act not just early, but differently — by narrowing focus, accepting that we cannot do everything, and betting on the few instruments that might still anchor relevance.**

This is not a failure of will, but of convergence: our measures take two to three turns, while the world moves in six-month increments. The recalibration here is not a retreat from action, but a shift in strategy — from broad mobilisation to precision intervention. We will still act under uncertainty, but we must now choose not just *where* to act, but *what to stop doing*, because continuing everything is indistinguishable from doing nothing.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger

  - Grounds: The **two_year_commitment** — "Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience" — has been overtaken by events. Situational awareness is no longer the bottleneck. We have Sentinel Nodes, Observatory red-teams, and telemetry. The bottleneck is **sovereign agency**: the ability to *act* on that awareness. With **eu_political_capital** at 0.0 and no compute on the ground, awareness without power is performance, not governance. We cannot renew a commitment to awareness when the crisis is authority.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger

This is not a rejection of legitimacy — it is a redesign of it for crisis tempo.
