# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience
- `new_two_year_commitment` (commitment): Build sovereign capacity to detect, interpret, and counter AI-driven strategic deception as the foundation of both resilience and self-determination
- `sovereign_agency` (commitment): We will concentrate all remaining political capital on building sovereign agency — the ability to detect, decide, and act independently within hours, not months — by streamlining the Counter-Deception Rapid Response Corps, hardwiring it to the Behavioural Observatory, and anchoring it in a single executive authority with emergency powers pre-validated by the Council.
- `sovereign_execution` (commitment): We will create a legally autonomous, operationally independent executive cell — embedded in the EEAS, hardened against interference, and pre-authorized by the Council to act on verified threats from the Behavioural Observatory — that can deploy countermeasures, issue binding alerts, and coordinate cross-border responses within six hours of detection, even in the absence of unanimity. We will accept the legal and political risk of overreach, because the risk of paralysis is greater.
