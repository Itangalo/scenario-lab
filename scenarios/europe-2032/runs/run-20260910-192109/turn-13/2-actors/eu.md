## Priority
The new EU Municipal Clean-Backup and Compromised Adapter Purge, because it is the only in-flight measure and it directly answers this turn's incident and market while carrying our containment commitment.
## In practice
We act without new law under NIS2 Articles 11-12 and the Serious Cross-Border Threats Regulation. ENISA and national CERTs lead emergency cutoff and clean-backup restoration for the ransomware-hit public services, with EC3 tracing the forum trade in backup images and adapters for revocation and takedown, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health/rescEU money for overtime, adapters, and offline vaults.

In the JHA and Health Councils we extend isolation authorities and keep hospitals on domestic triage inference while compromised adapters are purged and reissued. HERA/ECDC sustain voluntary 24h sequencing and border stocks, and we offer accelerated clean kits and mutual-aid technician pools to southern and eastern councils to hold cohesion while recovery runs slow.
