## In practice
We task ENISA jointly with the Electricity Coordination Group and CER authorities to order segmentation and breaker-credential audits of the two affected TSOs first, then all synchronous-area TSOs, funded by repurposed CEF-Digital and Civil Protection Mechanism money. Legal base is CER and NIS2 implementing acts via the Telecom/Energy Council, with France, Germany and Poland co-sponsoring to carry qualified majority over reluctant municipalities.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and the Tech sovereignty package (M2) moving through DG CNECT and EIB structuring: site shortlists, grid-connection reservations and ASML supply-chain safeguards, resisting US pressure for export alignment by linking it to the Anti-Coercion Instrument discussion. We do not open a parallel compute programme; acceleration comes from permitting and power, handled here as prioritisation, not a new instrument.
