## Priority
EU Grid and Critical Systems Hardening Shield, because the test shot precursor opens a three-turn cyber gate that outranks even our sovereignty build-out for this turn.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the Electricity Coordination Group with emergency segmentation audits of the two affected TSOs, funded by a reprogrammed tranche of the Digital Europe Programme and backed by a Council implementing decision on minimum OT-segmentation and AI-anomaly detection standards. NIS2 enforcement teams are surged to ports and water utilities, with joint exercises with ACER and member-state CSIRTs this winter.

The Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) continue on their tracks — DG CNECT holds accelerated-permitting zones and ASML supply-chain talks — but political push this turn goes to containment and hardening, using the US switch-off as leverage to justify both resilience spending and why EU compute cannot wait.
