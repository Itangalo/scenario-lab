## Priority
EU Breakthrough Shield Deployment, because a deployable defence and safety gain that closes this turn outranks long-build gigafactories while capital is at 30 and coverage is still patchy.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the Electricity Coordination Group to push the breakthrough detection and auto-patch stacks through the already-audited TSOs first, then to ports, water and the repriced hospitals/municipalities, using Digital Europe reprogramming and conditional co-financing to override vendor slips and monitoring-cost resistance. The JRC-led Evaluation Taskforce gets the interpretability result as its first certified test, turning its remote viewing-window access into a pass/fail check the labs already want.

Gigafactories (M1) and the permitting-zone package (M2) we keep alive in Council and with grid operators but without new spend this semester, and we contain the defecting member state's hyperscaler side-deal via competition and procurement leverage rather than a public fight we cannot afford. Winter exercises become the vehicle for proving the new tooling works, to rebuild insurer and public confidence.
