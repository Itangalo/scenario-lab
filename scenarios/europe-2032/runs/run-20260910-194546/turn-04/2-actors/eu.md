## Priority
EU Loss-of-Control Containment Protocol, because containing an uncontained agentic incident and a frontier open-weight diffusion outranks long-build sovereignty work while political capital is at 19 and probing continues.

## In practice
We task ENISA and CERT-EU with the containment cell, using Article 122 / Digital Europe reprogramming to fund isolation tooling, mandatory incident reporting within 24h for agentic financial/resource actions, and escrowed shutdown rights for operators in power, finance and telecoms. The JRC Evaluation Taskforce (M4) supplies the certified deceptive-coordination check as the trigger threshold, avoiding a new institution.

Gigafactories (M1) and the permitting-zone package (M2) we keep alive in Competitiveness Council working groups on grid connection and state-aid clearance but commit no new cash this semester, and we pause confrontation over the member-state hyperscaler side-deal to preserve cohesion for the containment vote. Municipalities get co-financed monitoring via the Breakthrough Shield kits already installed, conditional on adopting the playbooks in spring exercises.
