## New measure
**EU Continuity Stack for Essential Operators**
Stands up EU-controlled fallback inference and support for the hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the leading US model, via pooled EU capacity and emergency procurement.
This is category 9 Diffusion, adoption and public trust, because the cut-off is an adoption-trust crisis now, not future compute — it keeps essential services running while the gigafactories build.

## Priority
InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) – because only domestic compute anchored in EU law makes the cut-off unrepeatable, and the Continuity Stack is a bridge to it.

## In practice
We are pushing M1/M2 to final investment decision in the Competitiveness Council with InvestAI guarantees and EIB money, trading grid priority for EU legal anchoring to stop regional bidding wars. The Dutch lithography lever stays in reserve for Anti-Coercion discussions.

This turn DG CNECT with ENISA and HERA stands up the Continuity Stack from existing EuroHPC and EU cloud procurement: emergency framework contracts let affected hospitals and ministries switch to EU-hosted models with migration help. On the scandal, we enforce rather than legislate — Commission guidance orders AI Act audits of the flagged social-insurance/policing system, with the AI Office and national market surveillance authorities publishing conformity and oversight findings, to show the Act bites without freezing adoption.
