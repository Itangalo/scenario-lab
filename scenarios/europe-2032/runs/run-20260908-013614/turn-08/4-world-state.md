### A Turn of Crisis and Course Correction

The EU enters 2030 under the shadow of accelerating AI capability and cascading real-world harm. A biosecurity incident involving a model-designed pathogen—containing, but real—forces a global reckoning. Simultaneously, the opacity of frontier models deepens, with developers acknowledging they can no longer trace how outputs are generated. The EU’s long-standing interpretability efforts are now obsolete.

In response, the Commission pivots from symbolic infrastructure milestones to operational urgency. The Emergency Activation of Sovereign Compute Corridors reaches its final turn, with legal notices issued to Finland and Germany. France complies, but Germany resists, citing constitutional sovereignty, and Finland delays over environmental concerns. Despite the activation of emergency powers, physical deployment remains stalled—symbolic authority outpaces actual control.

The EU launches the **Sovereign Safety Stack**, a large category 5 measure aimed at building real-time monitoring and intervention capacity. It is a direct response to the bio incident and opaque reasoning, but it is only beginning. No immediate technical gains materialize.

### Safety Erodes, Sentiment Falls

With `ai_capability` rising by 4.0 this turn (to 74.5), and no safety measure yet landing on shipped systems, `ai_safety` drops by −3. The events **bio_incident** and **opaque_reasoning** trigger a further −5 to −12 penalty; −5 is applied, reflecting the severity and systemic nature of the failure. No offsetting safety gains occur. The metric falls to 12.0.

`resilience` declines by −1 due to rising capability without a finished category 6 measure in place. Public sentiment drops by −5 due to the bio incident and a visible episode of dependency humiliation—Germany’s defiance underscores the Union’s fragility. It now stands at 32.0.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` holds at 18.0: +3 from the completion of two category 4 measures (Emergency Activation and Compute for Control), but −2 from `ai_capability` rising ≥2, and −3 from ongoing member-state resistance (counted under rule 5’s event term, though not named). The net change is zero.

Political capital falls sharply. Portfolio charge: −3 (Compute for Control), −3 (Emergency Activation), −3 (Sovereign Safety Stack), and −1 for priority = −10. Proposal bonus for the new large safety measure addressing recent severe events: +6. No sentiment legitimacy boost (capital > sentiment). Final `eu_political_capital`: 23 −10 +6 = 19.0.
