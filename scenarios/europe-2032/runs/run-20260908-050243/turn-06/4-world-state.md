### The Frontier Creeps, the Union Consolidates

Despite the earlier leap in offensive AI capabilities, overall progress slows, with `ai_capability` rising by 1.5 points as the frontier refines rather than reinvents. Market turbulence and underwhelming releases fuel speculation of a plateau, though the underlying trajectory remains upward. Open models see modest gains, pushing `openweight_capability` to 49.5, as academic replication efforts gain credibility but fall short of matching closed systems.

The EU avoids overreach. The **Tech Sovereignty Package**, **Joint Interpretability Taskforce**, and **Cyber Shield Activation** near completion. Draft implementation guidelines for the sovereignty package are published, enabling private investment planning, though no new physical compute comes online. The interpretability findings are formally adopted into evaluation protocols but remain unembedded in operational systems. Cyber Shield’s technical framework is finalized, but national rollouts lag, leaving patchy protection.

A proposal for the **EU Open Research Corridor** is drafted and circulated among member states to formalise the emergent academic alliance. While initial feedback is positive, intergovernmental coordination delays formal adoption. As a result, no new measure is activated this turn, and the portfolio charge remains unchanged from the previous period. The **Strengthen Joint Resilience Cell** initiative remains the EU's named priority, maintaining focus and absorbing most coordination resources.

Public sentiment ticks up as independent researchers challenge corporate overclaims, countering narratives of uncontrollable AI. Resilience improves incrementally as Cyber Shield’s framework enables expanded drills, absorbing the impact of recent threats.

Political capital remains flat: partial progress on existing measures yields a +2 proposal bonus from incremental reinforcement (per notepad), but this is fully offset by the ongoing portfolio charge of −12 and the absence of new capital from unimplemented initiatives. The total change reflects constrained execution and delayed decisions, not sudden closure. No breakthrough in lab safety occurs, and `ai_safety` dips due to capability growth without enforceable new safeguards.

### Fragmentation Lingers

National AI liability rules multiply, complicating cross-border services. The Commission opens infringement cases, but unity frays. The U.S. **ALLIANCE** posture takes effect, promising structured access—yet implementation delays leave tangible benefits unrealised.
