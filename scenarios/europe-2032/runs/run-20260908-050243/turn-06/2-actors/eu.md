## Priority
**Strengthen Joint Resilience Cell and Expand Cross-Border Drills** — because the cyber_major_incident has exposed not just technical gaps but a crisis of confidence in the Union’s ability to act collectively under pressure, and this measure remains the most direct test of whether we can harden essential systems before the next wave of offensive AI capability lands.

## In practice
We are treating the recent attack not as a one-off but as a signal that the offensive use of AI is now operationally mature — and that our defences must evolve from reactive patching to anticipatory resilience. The Joint Resilience Cell, established under ENISA and co-located with HERA, is now operational and conducting weekly threat briefings with national CSIRTs. We are pushing member states to adopt standardised detection signatures trained on synthetic exploit chains, and we are using the leverage of EU funding conditionality to enforce compliance: no state receiving Digital Europe Programme grants may opt out of baseline cyber hardening. The first round of failover drills for energy and health IT systems will be conducted by December, using red-team AIs calibrated to current offensive capability levels, with results reported directly to the Commission.

At the same time, we are confronting the reality that our political capital is stretched thin. Three major initiatives — the Gigafactories, the Tech Sovereignty Package, and Cyber Shield — are all in flight, and the Joint Interpretability Taskforce has now delivered its final report. We are integrating its findings into the EU’s evaluation protocols, but we cannot afford to launch another large measure this turn. Instead, we are consolidating gains: scaling the Resilience Cell into a permanent coordination node, deepening drills, and using the quiet momentum of the emergent_middle_power_initiative to align safety standards with Japan, South Korea, and Taiwan. This is not the time for grand new promises — it is the time to prove we can finish what we started, and that the Union remains a credible actor even when the frontier surges ahead.
