# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now understand that **capability without control is not sovereignty, it is surrender**.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late — but we now define “late” not only as lagging behind capability, but as failing to act when political cohesion unravels. Our commitment is no longer only to speed, but to **cohesion under strain**.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Sovereign interdependence through collective leverage — but the openweight_frontier_release and emergent_institutional_fracture have shattered the assumption that coordination among states and institutions alone can contain systemic risk. This turn, we pivot from interdependence to **sovereign resilience**: building EU-governed capabilities that do not depend on trust between member states or recallable goodwill from developers. We are no longer counting on alignment; we are hardening the core.
