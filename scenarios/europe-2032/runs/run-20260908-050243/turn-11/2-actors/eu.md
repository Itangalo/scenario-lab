## In practice  
This turn, we are launching the EU Labour Transition and Professional Inclusion Council under Article 152 TFEU, with the Vice-President for Social Rights convening the first plenary in Brussels. The Council will include representatives from the International Bar Association, the European Federation of National Engineering Associations, and the Federation of European Accountants, alongside ETUC and BusinessEurope. We are allocating €400 million from the unused Just Transition Fund and the European Social Fund Plus to finance regional pilot programmes in Germany, France, and Belgium — the epicentres of unrest — where AI automation in legal discovery, tax compliance, and structural design will be paused pending joint review.  

The goal is not to stop innovation, but to embed it in a process that honours professional standards and preserves public trust. We are offering licensing bodies co-determination over AI certification thresholds — for example, no AI may sign off on a structural safety review without a chartered engineer’s final approval. In return, we ask for a suspension of strikes and a commitment to fast-track retraining in AI-augmented workflows. This is not concession — it is recalibration. The Joint Threat Analysis Cell remains our shield against external threats, but this Council is our immune system: it ensures that the body politic does not reject the very transformation we are trying to manage. We are no longer governing only the machine — we are governing the meaning of work.
