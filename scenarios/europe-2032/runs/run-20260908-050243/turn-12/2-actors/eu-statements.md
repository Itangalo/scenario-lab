- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied

  - Text: Sovereign interdependence through collective leverage — but the openweight_frontier_release and emergent_institutional_fracture have shattered the assumption that coordination among states and institutions alone can contain systemic risk. This turn, we pivot from interdependence to **sovereign resilience**: building EU-governed capabilities that do not depend on trust between member states or recallable goodwill from developers. We are no longer counting on alignment; we are hardening the core.
  - Trigger: The openweight_frontier_release has placed dangerous capabilities permanently beyond regulatory reach, while emergent_institutional_fracture has blocked the Joint Threat Analysis Cell from fulfilling its mission. These are not setbacks — they are structural failures of the model we staked ourselves on.
  - Grounds: Our previous commitment assumed that collective leverage — over data, markets, and developer access — could substitute for independent capacity. But with open weights now within months of the frontier and national agencies refusing to share telemetry, that leverage evaporates. The pathogen-linked incident proved harm can originate anywhere; the collapse of interpretability proves we cannot audit it in time; the fracture between Paris and Berlin proves we cannot coordinate fast enough. We must now act as if no one will help us in time, and build accordingly. The cost of this reversal is high: it redirects scarce capital from diplomacy to infrastructure, slows cooperation efforts, and signals a retreat from multilateral idealism. But the alternative — reactive fragmentation — would be worse.
  - Evidence quoted: **openweight_frontier_release:** An open-weight release lands within months of the closed frontier rather than a year. It is downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in the first week, and whatever capability it carries is now on private hardware permanently and beyond recall. `openweight_capability` moves to within 5–10 points of `ai_capability` at a stroke.

**emergent_institutional_fracture (emergent event):** The unresolved data sovereignty dispute between France and Germany escalates into a broader institutional crisis, with national intelligence agencies refusing to share threat telemetry with EU-level bodies. This undermines the credibility of any pan-European security response and forces member states to pursue bilateral threat-sharing agreements outside EU channels.
  - Reason: The developments directly undermine the EU's ability to rely on coordination and trust, which were foundational to its original commitment to sovereign interdependence.
