## Statement changes
- modify `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer mistake speed for agency when leverage lies elsewhere.  
- Grounds: The *openweight_frontier_release* event proves that capability diffusion is no longer a function of time or policy—it is now a matter of download speed. Our emergency redirection of compute and surge funding for open-weight deployment did not prevent disruption because the bottleneck was never infrastructure, but authority: we could not act coherently in the face of member-state fragmentation and external control. We remain committed to acting early, but this turn reveals that our early actions must be more precisely leveraged, not merely faster. The cost of this reversal is credibility with domestic reformers who expected decisive unilateral action, but it is necessary to align means with real power.

- add `leverage_over_mimicry` (position): The EU’s path to sovereignty does not run through matching the frontier model for model, but through controlling access, shaping ecosystems, and binding the capabilities that matter into enforceable relationships.  
- Grounds: With openweight models now within 5–10 points of the frontier and permanently distributed, the idea that we can “catch up” via compute alone is obsolete. The denial of access was not reversed by building more data centres — it was ignored. Our leverage now lies in coordination, not duplication: in the chokepoints we hold collectively with other middle powers, in the rules we enforce on systems embedded in our critical functions, and in the speed with which we can *withhold* as well as adopt.

## New measure

Create a joint coordination mechanism with like-minded states controlling critical nodes in the AI stack — chip fabrication, lithography equipment, model hosting, and data infrastructure — to align export controls, negotiate compute access, and pool evaluation capacity.

## Priority
**Form Middle-Power Coalition on AI Supply Chain Leverage** — because the *middle_power_coalition* event offers a rare alignment of opportunity and necessity: the export_control_escalation forces a choice between rationing and exclusion, and only by acting collectively can we ensure the EU is not left at the back of the line — this measure turns our fragmented exposure into shared power.

## In practice
We are treating the *openweight_frontier_release* not as a victory of openness, but as a wake-up call: capability is now loose, and no amount of sovereign compute can recall it. The €30bn surge funding for open-weight deployment has reached labs, but not yet public services — and the emergency redirection of inference capacity is still bottlenecked by legal disputes in Dublin and Luxembourg. We are no longer building our way to sovereignty; we are negotiating for it.

This turn, we are activating the diplomatic track opened by the *middle_power_coalition* event. The Commission, backed by the High Representative, is convening an emergency ministerial on AI supply chain resilience in Brussels, inviting Japan, South Korea, Canada, and the Nordic Council. The agenda is narrow and binding: align export control thresholds on AI-relevant chips and models, establish mutual recognition of safety evaluations, and create a joint reserve of compute capacity for critical public functions that activates when any member is excluded. We are offering Taiwan reciprocal access to EU research data in exchange for prioritisation in TSMC’s next-gen wafer allocation; we are offering Canada co-leadership in the evaluation working group in exchange for loosening U.S.-aligned restrictions on AI cluster exports.

The pushback is real — the Americans are watching closely, and the German industry lobby fears retaliation — but we are framing this not as defiance, but as interdependence: no single power controls the entire chain, and the EU holds real levers in standards, market access, and talent. If we act alone, we are a footnote. If we act together, we are a counterweight.
