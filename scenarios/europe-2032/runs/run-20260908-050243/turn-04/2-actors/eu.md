## In practice  
We are treating the cyber_major_incident not as an isolated breach but as proof that offensive AI tooling is now operationally viable at scale—and likely already in circulation. The attack exploited a poisoned software dependency with a wide blast radius, compromising municipal health records and traffic control systems across three member states. Attribution remains unclear, but forensic analysis confirms the exploit chain was model-generated, optimised for evasion and speed. We are activating Article 19 of the NIS2 Directive to trigger a Union-wide alert and mobilising the Cyber Shield mechanism under ENISA’s crisis coordination framework. This means immediate deployment of standardised intrusion detection signatures—trained on synthetic adversarial patterns—to all designated essential services, funded through an emergency €800m draw from the Digital Europe Programme and the Recovery Instrument.

At the same time, we are mandating failover drills within 30 days for energy grid operators and national health IT systems, using scenarios generated by red-team AIs calibrated to current offensive capability. These will be coordinated through a new Joint Resilience Cell co-located with ENISA and the European Health Emergency Preparedness Authority (HERA), with participation required for continued access to EU digital infrastructure grants. To overcome resistance from member states wary of centralised mandates, we are offering fast-tracked EU funding for local upgrades and mutual aid guarantees in case of spillover. The Commission will report weekly to COREPER II during this turn, and we expect the Council to endorse a binding implementing act by April. This is not just about patching systems—it is about proving that the Union can absorb shocks without fracturing, and that resilience is not outsourced to goodwill or commercial SLAs. We are also quietly expanding the remit of the Joint Interpretability Taskforce to include post-incident model forensics, linking control and recovery in a single sovereign capability.
