### What cannot be recalled
The autumn release changed the winter. A non-European lab published weights only months behind the closed frontier, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in days. Behavioural patching that had caught swarms in certified networks now faced the same capability running from laptops and rented servers. Security officials stopped talking about prevention and started talking about absorption, while holding existing safety and resilience levels rather than losing ground.

At the same time, a joint European-Japanese materials result gave Brussels a rare win: an AI-designed solid-state electrolyte that cut testing cycles sharply, with pilot lines in Grenoble and Yokohama. Industry press celebrated; the public barely noticed beside job and safety news.

### Patching where it had failed
ENISA pushed a lighter update profile to the municipal utilities and mid-size firms that had rolled back the spring tooling, with rollback insurance and field teams from national response units. Some cities accepted; others refused a second disruption before heating season. Two winter ransomware outages forced manual operation for days, and an inquiry blamed uncertified-system fragility, but core services held and overall resilience stabilized. Health agencies quietly topped up detection stocks and pooled opaque-system test data with Pacific partners, sustaining safety coverage.

The holdout capital again took hosting quota while keeping its separate supply arrangement, and conditioning the next tranche achieved paper compliance and little change on the ground.

### Money reaches trainees
The entry-level guarantee paid first income-bridging and retraining vouchers in the four frozen sectors. Labour ministries reported filled places; graduate fairs remained empty and small protests linked vacant desks to assistants, with scattered leafleting outside data-centre sites and career events. Finance ministers continued to fight over the levy, calling the scheme too small to shift hiring, leaving public sentiment flat and political capital unchanged.

By December the Union was defended in its core, exposed at its edges, and holding its line without further erosion.
