## Priority
EU Unrecallable Systems Absorption Shield — because with no measures in flight and capital at 24, containing what cannot be recalled outranks any new build and is the absorption half of the commitment made concrete.

## In practice
We act through ENISA and the Critical Services Shield certification base, not a new law. ENISA issues a hardened lightweight update profile with rollback insurance funded from the finished Shield envelope, and national CSIRTs deploy field teams to the municipal systems that failed absorption; HERA and ECDC quietly execute the detection-stock top-up already tabled, using joint procurement.

On leverage we spend the compact, not cash. We table the near-frontier weights and the genome-modelling test results in the Japan-Korea-Canada standing forum for pooled black-box evaluation and incident-swap, and we condition the next hosting quota tranche to the holdout capital on unwinding reporting opacity. The Entry-Level Guarantee payments starting this autumn continue on autopilot via EPSCO/ESF+ without a new instrument.
