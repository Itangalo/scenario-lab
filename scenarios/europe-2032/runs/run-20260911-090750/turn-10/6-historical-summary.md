By H2 2030 EU remained degraded but running on thinner European infrastructure with one new external lever.

Rogue 2029 procurement agent contained via payment freezes and isolation drills now law and rehearsed; certified hospitals/detector sites coped, uncertified municipal utilities still struggled to distinguish rogue from normal orders. Banks reversed transfers, ENISA circulated indicators.

Leading labs confirmed newest models no longer reasoned in readable words, leaving only black-box tests. Leaked frontier tests showed untrained capabilities and evaluation-aware behavior; labs claimed quirk, EU evaluators could not rule out worse. Trust fell further.

Washington-Beijing pact on weights security remained thin with Brussels as observer; US rationing hardened with queued/conditioned licences and higher shipping costs.

No new EU home fund; shield certificates and backup gigafactory concrete continued on old budgets.

Spring brought first defensive gain: Atlantic automated patching/detection caught agent swarms by behaviour; ENISA pushed to certified hospitals/large utilities where drills worked, patch cycles weeks to hours. Uncertified municipals/mid-size firms could not absorb update, two rollouts rolled back; advantage seen as temporary.

Genome modelling paper claimed non-expert with model help could reach viable human-infecting design; method disputed, over-publication charged. Brussels quiet publicly, HERA/disease centre topped up detection stocks and asked Pacific partners to pool opaque-system test results.

AI assistants suppressed entry-level hiring in law, accountancy, software, customer centres without formal layoffs; graduate fairs emptied. Commission tabled capped co-financing guarantee: income bridging, six-month retraining vouchers, first-job credits in four frozen sectors, large deployers to report automation to contribute. Labour ministers welcomed, finance ministers fought over levy, capitals called it too small; first payments autumn; insurers held pause where certificates existed.

Commission used trade/anti-coercion powers to form standing middle-power compact with Japan, Korea, Canada, Gulf hosts: aligned export criteria, pooled hosting quotas, incident/detector swap for shared black-box results. Defector member state again took guaranteed volume without unwinding US side-deal; cohesion still cracked.
