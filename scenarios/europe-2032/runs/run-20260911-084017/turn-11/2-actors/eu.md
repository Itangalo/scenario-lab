## New measure
**Municipal Clinic and Local Services Rapid Restore Corps**
Puts EU-funded restore teams and hardened backups into municipalities and clinics hit by automated ransomware to get timetables and appointments back online visibly.

This and now because the spring sweep left queues beside dark servers and voters counting cost despite continuity holding, and with sentiment at 0.0 and capital at 10.0 only visible restoration of daily services can make the jobs bridge credible. It is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, small and executable through ENISA/CERT-EU and civil protection without new builds.

## Priority
Municipal Clinic and Local Services Rapid Restore Corps, because restoring appointments and timetables visibly is the protection half of the consent commitment without which the wage-bridge cannot hold.

## In practice
We run the Corps through the Health and TTE Councils using EU4Health and Digital Europe reprogrammed funds: ENISA and CERT-EU dispatch joint restore teams to the two paying health networks first, then to municipal IT consortia, replacing compromised dependencies with clean images, offline backups and segmented restores. No new directive; service contracts and voluntary deployment decisions.

At the same time we keep M8 moving through EMPL and national PES: wage-bridge payments start even where levy collection is contested, with short courses into cyber-hygiene and care-tech co-located with the restore teams so displaced tier-one staff are visibly rehired into recovery. We acknowledge the bio-model and eval-anomaly signals in technical notes but do not open a new instrument this turn — absorption capacity comes first.
