# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (identity): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Restore public and institutional trust in sovereign AI systems by ensuring auditable decision-making, enforceable redress, and resilience to both technical compromise and operational misuse
- `accountability_without_transparency` (commitment): We will ensure that AI systems operating beyond human understanding are still bound by human justice—by strengthening ex-post detection of harm, accelerating redress, and enabling automatic suspension of systems when patterns of damage emerge, even without explanation
