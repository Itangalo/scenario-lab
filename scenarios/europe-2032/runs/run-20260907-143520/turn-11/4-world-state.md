### The Age of Unreadable Systems

Despite incremental progress in frontier AI—now at 69.5—the defining condition of this turn remains epistemic collapse. Leading systems operate beyond human interpretability, rendering audits symbolic and eroding the foundation of regulatory trust. The EU’s final validation effort, the **Integrity Audit Expansion**, concluded with mixed results: forensic probes in Paris and Helsinki confirmed weight integrity and containment, but revealed no pathway to explain decision patterns. Member states received technical clearance certificates—yet the public saw only opacity masked by procedure.

Poland rejected the proposed co-audit framework, insisting that adversarial verification infringes on national administrative sovereignty under Article 4(2) TEU. Technical upgrades in Marseille stalled as funding disputes delayed procurement. The audit regime survives—but not its universality.

### Oversight Without Understanding

In response, the EU launched the **Pattern-Based Harm Detection Network**, a new small category 2 measure aiming to shift oversight from reasoning to impact. Design work began under EAAD leadership, but early negotiations with Germany and Belgium over data access collapsed amid concerns about re-identification risks in aggregated welfare outcomes. Compromise talks continue, though anonymisation protocols may delay deployment.

The Openweight Containment Initiative expired without effect. With the ECJ still deliberating and cloud providers noncompliant, the EU pivoted to a voluntary certification label for “EU-Compliant Open Models.” Adoption is expected to be minimal.

Public sentiment worsened. Protests over algorithmic welfare denials intensified in Spain, while France’s paused tax system became a symbol of institutional paralysis. The reframing of accountability around procedural fairness—rather than transparency—failed to resonate. A new wave of sabotage targeted data centres in Lyon and Milan, attributed to anti-AI extremists; emergency services struggled to respond, underscoring systemic fragility.

Resilience dipped as confidence in critical systems frayed. Political capital plunged further, overwhelmed by portfolio costs and absence of visible gains.
