### Capability Plateau, Uncertain Depth

Signs emerge that the breakneck pace of AI advancement may be slowing. A major release from a leading lab underperforms expectations, and spot prices for top-tier compute drop sharply—suggesting last year’s frontier is now commoditized. Experts are divided: some see a temporary bottleneck, others a structural shift. Regardless, the global `ai_capability` index rises only 0.5 this turn, the smallest increment in years.

### Resilience Gains on Paper, Falters in Practice

The Continuity Assurance Framework completes certification for nine member states, marking a formal milestone under metric rule 4. This initiates a phased rollout of operational validation, with initial effects contributing a +1.0 increase to `resilience`, now at 53.0. Full implementation will require sustained coordination and behavioral adaptation across agencies, with red teams to be gradually embedded over the next 18 months. ENISA begins scoping the staffing, training pipelines, and inter-agency agreements needed to operationalize continuity as a cultural practice, but no new capacity is yet active. The framework is legally sound but institutionally fragile; progress beyond the initial step awaits demonstrated buy-in and resource allocation.

### Sovereignty Erodes Amid Delayed Execution

The EU invokes emergency powers to launch the Sovereign Compute Acceleration Programme, committing to modular, energy-integrated data centres in Finland, Slovakia, and Portugal. Contracts are fast-tracked, construction is scheduled to begin within weeks, and partnerships formed with non-US semiconductor suppliers. However, no physical infrastructure is yet in place, and compute capacity remains offline. Under metric rule 5, momentum from such measures only accrues after meaningful implementation, and given the scale and cross-border nature of the project, full effect is at least two turns away. With no implementation bonus this turn, the +2 momentum cited in the draft is invalid. Meanwhile, the 0.5 rise in `ai_capability` triggers a −1 decay in `eu_ai_sovereignty`. No offsetting event term applies, resulting in a net −1.0 change. `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 20.0.

### Political Capital: Stalled Momentum, No Recovery

With `eu_political_capital` at 0.0, the EU launches the large category 4 Sovereign Compute Acceleration Programme in response to supply_chain_coercion. This qualifies for a +3 proposal bonus under metric rule 7. However, the portfolio charge for ongoing measures remains: −3 for the Frontier Access Guarantee and −1 for the priority, totaling −4. The new programme does not incur a portfolio charge this turn, as it is newly proposed and not yet active. After applying the +3 proposal bonus to the −4 charge, the net change is −1. However, due to the constitutional floor at no movement below zero unless explicitly allowed, and given that political capital cannot drop below 0.0 without a validated deficit mechanism, `eu_political_capital` remains at 0.0. The attempt signals ambition but achieves no net gain in leverage or capacity.

Public sentiment holds at 20.0, unmoved by distant plans and unresolved anxiety.
