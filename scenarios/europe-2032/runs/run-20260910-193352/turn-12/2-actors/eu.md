## New measure
**EU Embodied Work Transition Shield**
Pays wage-bridge and rapid retraining for picking, sorting, warehouse and supplier workers displaced by deployed robotics, funded from ESF+ reprioritisation and tied to employers deploying systems. Category 7 Labour and social protection, small, because with capital at 2 and sentiment at 14 we cannot build compute or regulate the frontier — we can only show the Union cushions the manual jobs now falling on the side the robots take.

## Priority
M10 EU Embodied Work Transition Shield, because finishing cyber/bio hardening without answering warehouse and supplier layoffs is why completion reads as maintenance and why the side-deal humiliation sticks.

## In practice
We run this through DG EMPL and national public employment services under ESF+ existing legal base: no new regulation, no procurement fight, automatic income bridge for displaced logistics workers plus paid reskilling vouchers for repair, care-assist and site-supervision roles the robots do not take. Deployment employers co-fund via transition levy offset against training delivered.

We hold the line on what just finished — M8/M9 wrappers, patch feeds and pooled sequencing guidance stay operated by ENISA/ECDC/HERA — and we answer the side-deal and export-control tightening politically: presidency keeps queue-priority offer for re-alignment, and tasks DG TRADE to keep allied volume-licence access without splitting procurement further. No new compute instrument this turn while Gigafactories line is stalled.
