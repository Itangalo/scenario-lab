## In practice
We task ENISA with joint threat-hunting teams across the two hit grid operators plus all TSOs, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility money, with mandatory staged-tooling reporting under NIS2 Article 23. The Civil Protection Mechanism runs autumn tabletop and live breaker-isolation exercises with ACER and port authorities.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and the Tech sovereignty package (M2) moving through site selection and permitting-zone transposition, using the cyber intrusion as leverage with member states resisting grid-connection priority for data centres: resilience and sovereignty are the same build. DG CNECT holds the pen, Energy Council formation clears the mandates, and we offer cohesion top-ups to municipalities hosting sensors and backup control centres.
