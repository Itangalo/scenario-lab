However, fast-tracked disbursements have not yet begun: negotiations over funding sources remain unresolved. No payments were made in Rhône-Alpes, Silesia, or Central Greece this turn. Work was initiated on assessing oversight needs for human control in high-risk systems, but no framework has been scoped or named—this remains preparatory analysis, not a new measure. The Transition Fund stands as the sole new initiative this turn.
