This turn, we must show that our decisions respond to real harm, not abstract risk, or we lose the capacity to act at all.

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Defend the coherence of European agency by ensuring collective resilience, secure decision loops, and public legitimacy in the face of open, autonomous, and increasingly dangerous AI systems — now prioritising the restoration of social contract over technical preparedness

The shift from technical to political resilience is categorical: if people no longer believe the EU can protect them *from* AI, they will not trust it to protect them *with* AI.
