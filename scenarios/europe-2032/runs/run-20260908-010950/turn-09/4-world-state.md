### A Fractured Horizon  
The global AI investment collapse sent shockwaves through the ecosystem. Frontier labs scaled back training runs, and several high-profile compute partnerships vital to European ambitions dissolved overnight. While this briefly slowed the advance of `ai_capability`—now at 59.7—the damage to trust was deeper. A member state, citing urgent national needs, struck a unilateral deal with a foreign hyperscaler, bypassing EU coordination on AI infrastructure. The move, defended as pragmatic, was widely seen as a crack in the Union’s strategic coherence.

### Open Weights, Open Wounds  
An unexpected open-weight release, closely mirroring the closed frontier, rapidly disseminated advanced agentic capabilities. Downloads surged, placing powerful AI beyond regulatory reach. This development pushed `openweight_capability` to 50.0, reflecting rapid diffusion but still trailing the frontier in effective capability. The release intensified concerns about uncontrolled proliferation, though no breach of core safety thresholds has yet occurred.

### Delivering on Promises  
The European AI Transition Fund concluded its implementation. Disbursements reached key regions, retraining pipelines launched, and a moratorium on public-sector AI layoffs held in health and education. The delivery bolstered public trust—`public_sentiment` rose to 47.0—by demonstrating tangible support amid disruption. Linking modernisation grants to verified "human-in-command" standards added teeth to accountability.

### Sovereignty Under Strain  
Despite these gains, `eu_ai_sovereignty` fell to 27.0. The member state’s defection directly eroded collective leverage, triggering a −2 under metric rule 5. The investment collapse further undermined planned capacity, though its effect was indirect and not charged here. No new category 4 measure finished, and `ai_capability`’s minimal rise avoided the −1 decay.

Political capital edged up to 25.0. The portfolio charge—three large measures at −3 each and a priority at −1—totalled −10. But the completion of the Transition Fund (category 7) delivered a +4 bonus, and `public_sentiment` now exceeding capital granted a +1 legitimacy lift. The Union held course, but cohesion remains fragile.
