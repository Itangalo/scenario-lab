## In practice
We are finalising the disbursement of the European AI Transition Fund, ensuring that wage insurance reaches Silesia and Rhône-Alpes by December and that retraining pipelines are active in at least ten high-impact regions by Q1 2031. The European Social Fund Plus is mobilising emergency protocols, and we are working with national labour ministries to certify compliance with the automation moratorium in health and education. The certification of “human-in-command” systems by the European Public Administration School is on track, and we are linking access to EU modernisation grants to this standard — making accountability a condition of funding, not a suggestion.

At the same time, we are assessing the fallout from the **ai_investment_collapse** and the **member_state_defection**. The collapse has slowed the frontier, but it has also frozen critical compute partnerships we were counting on. We are convening an emergency meeting of the Competitiveness Council to renegotiate access to stranded capacity, leveraging our upstream semiconductor position to secure equity stakes in idle facilities. The defection by a member state has undermined our external posture, and we are launching quiet diplomacy to reaffirm collective positions with both Washington and Beijing, while accelerating plans to insulate EU decision loops from external dependency. The new commitment — securing decisional coherence — begins next turn, but its foundation must be laid now: in delivered promises, restored trust, and a clear line between what we regulate and what we command.
