### Resilience and Sentiment Under Pressure  
The cyberattack’s aftermath revealed inadequate detection and substitution capacity, especially in healthcare. Labour protests intensified, linking job losses to automation and political instability. Media coverage highlighted AI’s role in recent disruptions, contributing to public unease. However, without a major shift in tone or scale—such as a coordinated disinformation wave or mass incident—public sentiment remains within its prior range. Resilience efforts were outpaced by the speed of emerging threats, and without exercised response, the damage was not contained. However, initial progress in drill activation and threat-sharing protocols prevented a steeper drop. Resilience remains unchanged as early exercises yield partial insights but no systemic improvement.
