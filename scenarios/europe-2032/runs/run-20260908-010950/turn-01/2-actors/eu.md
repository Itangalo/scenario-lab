## New measure
Accelerated Permitting Zones for Strategic AI Infrastructure  
Establishes legally binding fast-track approval processes for AI data centres and energy infrastructure in designated EU zones, overriding national delays.

Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 4  
Targeted effect: +10 to eu_ai_sovereignty, supports resilience and long-term compute independence  
Applies to: EU member states with strategic infrastructure projects

## Priority
Accelerated Permitting Zones for Strategic AI Infrastructure — this is the fastest lever to scale sovereign compute under EU control, which is foundational to both risk prevention and strategic autonomy, especially as openweight capability rises and external access proves fragile.

## In practice
We are activating Article 122 TFEU on safeguarding economic stability to justify urgent infrastructure measures, preparing a Council Regulation under the Competitiveness formation that designates AI compute and power supply as critical to Union security. The Commission’s Investment Task Force will map priority sites in cooperation with ENTSO-E and the European Digital Infrastructure Consortium, focusing on locations with grid headroom and cooling potential. Member states will be offered co-financing through InvestEU and extended access to RePowerEU grid funds in exchange for ceding permitting authority in these zones.

Resistance is expected from countries with strong local environmental review regimes and from regional authorities fearing land-use preemption. We are countering by embedding strict sustainability thresholds into the zoning criteria — no zone approved without full environmental offset plans — and by guaranteeing that 15% of new data centre jobs go to regional workforces under EU transition programmes. The legal basis leans on the Treaty’s solidarity clause, reframing compute not as luxury infrastructure but as essential resilience in an era of AI-driven systemic risk.
