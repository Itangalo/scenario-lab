## In practice
We put ENISA, CERT-EU and the Health and JHA Councils on incident command: ring-fence compromised dependencies, push pre-drilled EU-hosted open-model fallbacks with logged overrides, and deploy the exhausted ENISA visiting teams as mutual-aid pools funded by repurposed civil-protection money, not new legislation.

EIOPA extends the December backstop into a time-limited EU re-insurance guarantee only for operators meeting the common isolation and drill certificate, giving private insurers a priceable risk. We offer the defecting member state re-entry on that certificate without reopening its side-deal fight, to stop a second split while services are down.
