## Priority
M7 EU Containment and Continuity Switch – because the insurance withdrawal crunch now threatens to turn degraded-but-running hospitals and logistics operators into uncovered-and-closed ones, and only the continuity layer keeps cover and services alive.

## In practice
We put the entire turn into making M7 land. DG CNECT and ENISA convert the civil-protection fallback orders into certifiable risk-reduction: EU-hosted open-model deployments with isolation logs, manual-override drills, and common escalation thresholds become the re-insurability standard. EIOPA convenes the withdrawing insurers with a temporary EU backstop guarantee tied to that certification, so new cyber and business-interruption cover resumes for certified operators while uncertified US-dependent stacks stay suspended.

We spend no new legislative fight. The frozen Gigafactory backup site stays frozen, Autonomy Pact talks stay exploratory, and Health and Transport Councils carry the insurer negotiation — offering the defecting member state re-entry on common certification terms rather than opening a second front over its side-deal. With capital at 11, holding continuity is the commitment.
