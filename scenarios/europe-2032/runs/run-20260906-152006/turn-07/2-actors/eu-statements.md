## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign AI control to withstand strategic shocks and prevent catastrophic misuse — but we now acknowledge that control without domestic capacity is a hollow shield, and sovereignty cannot be enforced from the outside in. This turn, our priority shifts to building the unblockable foundation: EU-owned compute, talent, and assurance infrastructure capable of independent advancement.
  - Grounds: The *eu_frontier_access_denied* event proves that reliance on foreign models, even under bilateral deals, is not just risky — it is revocable at will, with immediate operational consequences for hospitals, ministries and firms. At the same time, *labour_displacement* reveals that the social contract is fraying not in the abstract, but in lived reality: graduates cannot enter the professions they trained for. Our containment regime is sound in theory, but if we cannot run capable systems ourselves, we have no alternative to offer when access is cut. We are still committed to control — but only because we now see that true control begins with sovereign capability.
