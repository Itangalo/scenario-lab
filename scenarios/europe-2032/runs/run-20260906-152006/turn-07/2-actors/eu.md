This turn, our priority shifts to building the unblockable foundation: EU-owned compute, talent, and assurance infrastructure capable of independent advancement.
  - Grounds: The *eu_frontier_access_denied* event proves that reliance on foreign models, even under bilateral deals, is not just risky — it is revocable at will, with immediate operational consequences for hospitals, ministries and firms. At the same time, *labour_displacement* reveals that the social contract is fraying not in the abstract, but in lived reality: graduates cannot enter the professions they trained for. Our containment regime is sound in theory, but if we cannot run capable systems ourselves, we have no alternative to offer when access is cut. We are still committed to control — but only because we now see that true control begins with sovereign capability.

## Priority

**Priority:** Build the EU Sovereign Compute Backbone  
We are pushing this hardest because *eu_frontier_access_denied* exposed the fatal flaw in our current posture: we can design containment, but we cannot enforce it without the means to run our own systems — and without that, every rule we pass is subject to veto by a foreign provider.

## In practice

This turn, the fiction is over. The denial of frontier model access — sudden, total, and unexplained — has grounded hospitals’ diagnostic pipelines, stalled drug discovery projects, and left public agencies scrambling. It was not an attack, but it read like one. Meanwhile, young graduates in law and accountancy face a job market that no longer exists, and the narrative of AI as a benevolent assistant has cracked. We are acting on two levels: enforcement and foundation.

On enforcement, we complete the AI Containment Protocol this turn. The EAAD, now fully staffed, issues its first compliance certification for the Saint-Malo-Kiruna cluster, mandating dual-key access, air-gapped operation, and real-time telemetry to Brussels. We invoke the Anti-Coercion Instrument formally, notifying the defector state that cohesion fund disbursements will be suspended unless alignment is restored within six weeks. This is not retaliation — it is the cost of belonging to a system that only works if everyone is in it.

But enforcement alone is not enough. We are launching the EU Sovereign Compute Backbone to end our dependence on borrowed power. This is not another public-private partnership with soft guarantees — this is state-owned infrastructure, built with emergency RePowerEU grid overrides and funded through a reprofiled portion of the InvestAI allocation. We are fast-tracking procurement under Article 346 for national security infrastructure, bypassing standard tenders, and deploying modular liquid-cooled racks in Kiruna and Saint-Malo with direct dark fibre to CERN and EMBL. Talent recruitment is underway via a new Horizon Europe track offering citizenship-fast-track packages for top-tier AI engineers willing to commit to five years of public service. Without this backbone, our rules are paper walls. With it, we begin to mean what we say.
