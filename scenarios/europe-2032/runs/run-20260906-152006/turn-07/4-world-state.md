### The Denial That Changed Everything

In mid-2029, a silent cutoff severed EU institutions from the most advanced AI models. Hospitals paused diagnostics, ministries froze planning cycles, and firms scrambled. No explanation came — only silence. The abrupt denial of access exposed the fragility of reliance on foreign systems, even under bilateral deals. Publicly, the Commission called it a strategic wake-up call; privately, panic rippled through capitals.

AI capability advanced further, reaching 70.0, as closed labs achieved autonomous multi-day exploit chaining across domains. Yet assurance did not keep pace. Despite internal interpretability gains, the EU’s lack of sovereign access meant no meaningful safety improvements landed domestically. With no new safeguards on shipped systems and rising capability, `ai_safety` fell to 24.0.

The EU responded with resolve — and overreach. The AI Containment Protocol formally took effect, with the EAAD certifying the first dual-key cluster in Saint-Malo and Kiruna. But enforcement backfired: the defector state refused to comply, citing national sovereignty, and threatened legal action at the CJEU. Cohesion fund suspensions were delayed pending review, weakening deterrence.

Simultaneously, the EU launched the Sovereign Compute Backbone, a large category 4 measure aimed at full ownership of high-performance clusters. Fast-tracked under Article 346, it bypassed tenders and began deploying liquid-cooled racks in Kiruna and Saint-Malo. However, grid bottlenecks in Norrland and delays in dark fibre deployment slowed progress.

Public sentiment dropped to 20.0 as job losses in law, accountancy, and software mounted. Graduates found doors closed, and AI was blamed. The Resilience Reinforcement Initiative stalled further, as regional governments refused to fund upgrades, citing exhaustion.

With seven major initiatives still in flight — including now-cancelled ones — and political capital thinning, the burden of ongoing costs overwhelmed capacity. The new measure, while symbolically decisive, strained cohesion and budgets.
