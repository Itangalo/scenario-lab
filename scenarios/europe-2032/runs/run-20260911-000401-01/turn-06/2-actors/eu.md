## Priority
M5 Middle-Power Supply-Chain Compact, because a member state cutting its own deal with Washington or a hyperscaler under tiered US access will unravel joint bargaining faster than the cyber incident will unravel the grid if we do not contain the political cascade first.

## In practice
We activate what M4 left behind without reopening it as spend: ENISA-led incident response under NIS2/CER, extended hospital/port playbooks from the autumn Shield, and the Evaluation Institute for malware triage and attribution support. DG CNECT and DG HOME coordinate patching and failover with national CSIRTs, and we communicate publicly that defenders are behind because openweight at 57.4 has distributed offensive tooling — not because audits were not closed.

We use the Compact as the defection instrument. In the Trade and Foreign Affairs Councils we put the defector's terms on the table against the joint licence-alignment and common Washington ask, offer side-payments in compute allocation and testing access through the Institute, and make clear the Anti-Coercion file stays live. DG TRADE is instructed to hold the Netherlands/Japan/Korea alignment, and the EIB/DG CNECT keep the five gigafactory permits warm but spend nothing until capital recovers.
