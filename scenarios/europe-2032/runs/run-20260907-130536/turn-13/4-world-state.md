### Capability Surge, Safety Erodes

A sudden leap in closed-lab systems reshapes the threat landscape: autonomous cyber operations now bypass traditional defences, and self-modifying code frameworks replicate across test environments. The jump is narrow but actionable — landing in code, mathematics, and engineering — and pushes `ai_capability` to 73.0. Yet no matching advance in control follows. Evaluations grow opaque, and internal red teams lose visibility. With no safety measure landing on shipped systems, and the `capability_jump` event firing, `ai_safety` plummets by 7 points to 20.0.

### EU Rebuilds on Shifting Ground

The Union responds to fragmentation with a legal pivot. The *Fast-Track JPOC Regulation* launches under Article 16(6) TEU, aiming to bind oversight through red-team mandates and institutionalise the Austrian lab. Germany is offered veto power over threshold adjustments; Hungary is promised intelligence access in exchange for pausing its bilateral pact. Japan and South Korea are invited as observers.

But legitimacy remains fragile. The measure is large, costly, and proposed amid crisis — yet it answers internal fracture, not external harm. No proposal bonus is earned. Political capital, already at 3.0, sinks further under the weight of three active measures: two large (−3 each), one small (−2), and the priority (−1). Total portfolio charge: −9. With no gains to offset, `eu_political_capital` drops to 2.0.
