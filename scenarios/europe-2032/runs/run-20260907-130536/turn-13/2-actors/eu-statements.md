- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Maintain independent access to AI capabilities sufficient for strategic autonomy and crisis response — but only if that access can be secured against misuse and uncontrolled escalation; we now condition capability on containment, and containment on cohesion.
  - Grounds: The capability_jump confirms that the frontier is advancing in verifiable, actionable domains — cyber, code, engineering — while our own instruments falter. The pause of the Sovereign AI Core has not restored trust; it has exposed that our authority depends not on technical reach but on political buy-in. Germany’s veto demand, Hungary’s bilateral drift, and the suspension of talks with Japan and South Korea reveal that sovereignty without legitimacy is not sovereignty at all. The Union’s capacity to act is collapsing not from lack of tools, but from lack of consent. Thus, we deepen the reframe: strategic autonomy must now mean *collective* autonomy, enforced through binding oversight and shared risk. The commitment to capability stands, but only if it is held in common, and only if the mechanisms of control are as distributed as the threats we face.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
