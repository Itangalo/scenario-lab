### The registry nobody wanted to need
The scandal broke in autumn: an ombudsman and then a court found that an AI-supported scoring system used in benefits and policing triage had systematically cut entitlements and flagged families for years. Brussels conceded the worst reading — the deployment had never been classified as high-risk at all. Every decision had been lawful. The Act written for 2024 simply did not reach the deployments of 2032.

Talk shows replayed caseworkers approving queues at forty seconds an item. Restriction became cheap overnight; mayors suspended automation projects, unions demanded bans.

The Commission answered with a registry, not a ban. National ombudsmen and equality bodies got a common reporting template, the Fundamental Rights Agency to collate cases, redress paid first from existing social funds under the non-discrimination acquis. Filing was orderly; payouts were slow and small. It stopped a blanket ban but bought no gratitude.

### Exercises while straits tighten
Far away, extended exercises around Taiwan lifted shipping insurance and triggered an expulsion. No chip shipment stopped, no therapy batch was cancelled — but procurement officers dusted off rationing schedules. Rumours of a foreign licensor holding back doses for its own waiting lists persisted as single-source whispers, denied and repeated.

The middle-power pact closed its formal term with aligned licences and pooled orders, no factory, no re-signed compute. Ministers called it leverage; pharmacists called it a waiting list with letterhead.

In clinics, the Exploitation Shield became routine: offline kits, rapid patch teams, joint cyber-bio drills pushed to the next tier of weak communes. When forged referrals spiked again, wards degraded rather than stopped. Staff kept the network staffed, shipments stayed quarantined, no new frontier law was tabled.

By December, survival looked conditional twice over: cures by foreign licence, safety by paper backups. The Union had admitted its law was badly drawn and kept its hospitals open — and was blamed for both.
