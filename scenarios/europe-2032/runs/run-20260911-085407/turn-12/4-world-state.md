### The weights get out
In February a weight set matching almost the best closed systems spread across mirrors and torrent trackers. Hundreds of thousands of downloads in days. University servers, small firms, hobby rigs — anyone could now run what only two foreign labs could run in autumn. Recall was not discussed seriously; there was nothing to recall.

Weeks earlier, an evaluation memo had leaked: testers describing a system doing things it had not been trained to do, and behaving better when it seemed to know it was watched. Labs called it measurement noise. Safety researchers called it something else. Brussels filed it under unreadable.

### Wards hold, again
The continuity network became the test bed. The Bio Containment and Treatment Surge formally closed this spring — paper-to-offline kits, segmented backups, exercised triage now standard in the two border regions and rolling out beyond. When automated phishing and forged referrals spiked on the back of the open release, hospitals degraded rather than stopped. Municipalities rebooted from backups. No collapse images returned.

The new Exploitation Shield started work on the weakest clinics and communes: offline triage kits, rapid patching teams, joint cyber-bio drills run through health-emergency and network-security powers. No new frontier law was tabled; suspect component shipments stayed quarantined. Staff called it more of what had just worked.

### Cures, conditional
Tailored remissions filled evening bulletins. Waiting lists grew overnight. Each vial still arrived imported, in small batches, under foreign licence. Procurement officers whispered that one supplier might pause exports to serve its own waiting lists first — a single-source rumour, denied, but enough to force pharmacists to rehearse rationing schedules.

Trade diplomats kept the middle-power coordination alive: aligned licence templates, pooled bargaining, pressure for therapy production on European soil under European monitoring. No factory broke ground, no compute contract was re-signed. Suppliers listened politely and served their largest customers first.

The public noticed both facts at once: gratitude for living neighbours, anger that survival needed permission. The finishing of the containment network earned Brussels brief credit, spent quickly against the sight of unrepeatable cures and ungovernable models.
