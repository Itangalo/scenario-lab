## New measure
**EU Frontier Evaluation Office**
Stands up independent EU technical capacity to evaluate frontier and open-weight models for cyber-offensive and other dangerous capabilities before deployment.
This is Public technical capacity and research because assurance has fallen behind capability after the swarm showed open models adapted for intrusion at scale, and the Union cannot govern what it cannot test itself; it directly serves the independent-capacity leg of our survive-the-test-shot commitment while M1/M2 build compute.

## Priority
EU Frontier Evaluation Office, because M1 and M2 keep sovereignty construction alive but without own evaluation the next test shot arrives unverified and unchallengeable, so assurance must outrank acceleration this turn.

## In practice
We mandate DG CNECT with ENISA and the JRC to establish the Office under the AI Act's evaluation articles, funded by reprogrammed Horizon Europe and Digital Europe money, with vetted-researcher access and mandatory pre-deployment test slots for models deployed in the Union. The Competitiveness and Telecoms Councils carry it, offering France and Germany co-location of testing nodes in exchange for accepting common methods.

At the same time we keep M1 Gigafactories and M2 Tech sovereignty package on permitting and EIB financing tracks, holding the line that grid connection stays sequenced behind critical-system hardening from the finished Shield, and we task the Office's first work programme with reproducing the swarm tooling so TSO detection tuned last turn stays current.
