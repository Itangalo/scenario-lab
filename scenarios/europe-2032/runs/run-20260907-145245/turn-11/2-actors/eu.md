## Priority
**Priority:** Activate the Sovereign AI Core Programme  
We are prioritizing this because the Emergency Continuity Response Initiative revealed that we have rules, teams, and funding — but no sovereign system that actually works when needed. Resilience is not drills. Sovereignty is not access. It is control. And control means a system that turns on when the lights go out — not because a capital allows it, but because the Union commands it.

## In practice
This turn, we are building the spine of sovereign control. The Sovereign AI Core Programme was legislated in theory, but it remained a ledger of promises: compute here, talent there, access someday. Now, under Article 122 TFEU and the recently invoked Crisis Continuity Protocol, we are activating it as a live operational entity. The European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking is repurposing 40% of its existing Aurora-class clusters — €2.3bn worth of hardware — into a unified, EU-governed control plane with real-time failover for health, energy, and emergency services. We are bypassing national procurement queues and installing EU-certified orchestration software by directive, effective immediately.

Resistance remains. Two capitals still refuse node activation, citing national security. We are offering them co-leadership of the Core’s resilience council and full audit rights — but we are also moving without them. The first nodes go live in Helsinki, Marseille, and Bratislava by month’s end. The Frontier Access Guarantee continues, but its purpose shifts: no longer just a hedge against foreign cutoff, but the backbone of a system we now intend to switch on. We are not waiting for unanimity. We are building the Union that functions — because if we don’t, no one else will.
