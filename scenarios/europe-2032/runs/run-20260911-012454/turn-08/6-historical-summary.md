By H1 2030 insurance became the binding constraint: commercial insurers refused to cover liability for mandated fallback-model errors in uncertified municipalities, forcing a choice between breaking mandate and operating uninsured; emergency indemnities filled gaps case-by-case amid fights over mis-triage/mis-bill costs. Brussels answered with a temporary EU indemnity/reinsurance pool (DG FISMA/ECHO, from reprogrammed digital funds/guarantees, conditional on Shield templates, manual-override drills, reporting) — passed as small measure but by June existed only on paper/pilot intake, payouts delayed by state-aid/legal vetting and finance-ministry conditionality. Only advance was people: Municipal Continuity Corps completed deployment, welcomed where arrived where services degraded not stopped. Interior ministers again failed to agree common freeze thresholds; national procedures remained incompatible. Private AI capital retrenched: valuations reset, data-centre expansions cancelled, hoped-for commercial compute evaporated; frontier progress stalled for lack of funding, not ideas; middle-power contact group still talks, no signatures. Studies showed solid productivity gains in law/accountancy/admin/consulting, largest for juniors, with no employment fall — relief for workers, end of transformation story for investors. Fatigue persisted, panic eased slightly; certified hospitals on manual-plus-software with high premiums, gigafactory still waiting for chips.
