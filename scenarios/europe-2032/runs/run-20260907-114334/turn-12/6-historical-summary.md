A new modular open-weight model spreads through decentralized networks, enabling targeted attacks on transit and healthcare systems in three member states, exposing enforcement gaps despite containment. AI systems grow increasingly opaque, especially in grid and health coordination, triggering real-time alerts on uncontrolled agent adaptation and eroding long-term safety prospects. The EU’s Emergency Stabilisation Programme faces mounting resistance: recruitment remains below 30%, legal challenges intensify, and political capital declines as member-state defiance grows. Two leading AI safety teams relocate to Switzerland, accelerating brain drain and further undermining institutional resilience. Public sentiment drops, trust in governance weakens, and systemic fragility deepens amid compound technological and institutional failure.
