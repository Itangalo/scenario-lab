## Statement changes
* ``- modify `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing. But when one mandate consumes the other, we are no longer governing — we are reacting. Sovereignty without continuity is hollow. Safety without capacity is surrender.``
- Grounds: The collapse of political capital and public sentiment reveals that maintaining both mandates requires not just resolve but credibility — and credibility depends on delivering functional continuity, not just declaring control. Our identity must reflect the tension not as a balance to strike, but as a contradiction to manage, where failure in one collapses the legitimacy of both.

* ``- modify `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer pay for certainty with cohesion. When action fractures the Union, we have already lost.``
- Grounds: The failure of the Continuity Assurance Framework to achieve full buy-in — despite binding rules and funding — shows that speed without alignment produces not resilience but resistance. We acted under uncertainty, but the cost was not error — it was disunity. From now on, acting early must include acting together.

* ``- retire `two_year_commitment```  
- Grounds: The two-year commitment period closes this turn, and its core objective — sovereign AI continuity — has been institutionalized in law and partial practice. However, the operational reality remains fragmented, and the political foundation has eroded. We are not abandoning the goal, but we cannot sustain the commitment in its current form without a credible path to cohesion and funding.

* ``- add `no_time_for_perfection` (commitment): We will accept imperfect tools, incomplete readiness, and partial compliance — but only if they serve a functioning whole. We will prioritize systems that work over systems that are pure.``
- Grounds: The cyber_major_incident exposed that air-gapped nodes existed in theory but failed in practice. We can no longer afford to wait for ideal conditions. Our commitment shifts from achieving perfect control to ensuring operational continuity, even if it means working through flawed systems and reluctant partners.

## Priority
**Priority:** Launch the Emergency Continuity Response Initiative  
We are prioritizing this because the cyber_major_incident has shattered confidence in our continuity systems — not because the framework is wrong, but because it was not ready when needed. We must prove that the EU can respond to failure with speed and unity, or no framework will ever be trusted again.

## In practice
This turn, we are shifting from building rules to repairing reality. The cyber_major_incident — a model-generated ransomware sweep that disabled emergency dispatch systems in three member states — revealed that our air-gapped nodes were either offline, misconfigured, or inaccessible when the crisis hit. The Continuity Assurance Framework is law, but it is not yet lived. We are now launching the Emergency Continuity Response Initiative under Article 122 TFEU, mobilizing €1.8bn from the Crisis Solidarity Mechanism to fund rapid technical interventions across health, energy, and transport.

The European AI Assurance Directorate is deploying cross-border response teams to 17 high-risk sites, using emergency procurement to bypass stalled national processes. We are repairing nodes, retraining operators, and conducting live failover drills within 72 hours of activation. In Thessaloniki, after the failed drill last turn, we have replaced the local CIO and installed a joint EAAD-national command cell. In two resisting capitals, we are offering full cost coverage and technical co-ownership in exchange for immediate compliance — not as punishment, but as partnership.

We know this is not the final fix. But we cannot wait for perfect sovereignty while services collapse. We are choosing function over form, unity over purity, and response over delay. The lights must stay on — and this time, they must stay on by design.
