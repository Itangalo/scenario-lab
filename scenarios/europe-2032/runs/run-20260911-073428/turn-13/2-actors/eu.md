## New measure
**EU Graduate Bridge and Fair Therapy Access**
It funds paid graduate bridge placements tied to municipal continuity work and imposes EU-wide transparent queuing for tailored immune therapies to kill the paid-slot black market. This is a labour and social protection measure at its core because with capital at 3 and sentiment at 14, unreplaced entry-level jobs and residency rationing in Lyon, Krakow and Porto will break cohesion faster than any frontier risk.

## Priority
EU Graduate Bridge and Fair Therapy Access — because with no measures in flight and only pooled telemetry holding services together, visible jobs and fair queues are the only lever that rebuilds capital to do anything else.

## In practice
We run this through EPSCO and DG SANTE/DG EMPL on existing ESF+ and EU4Health lines, no new concrete promised. Bridge placements are paid from the old Transition Guarantee envelope, placed with ENISA/CERT-EU municipal teams, hospital logistics and telecom failover crews doing the ransomware recovery work, with works councils guaranteeing skeleton-crew ports are not undercut.

DG SANTE takes over therapy allocation from municipal residency rationing with a single EU registry, published waitlists and cross-border slots, enforced by ECDC sample-tracking already under the accession pact. Mayors in Lyon, Krakow, Porto get supply visibility and anti-fraud inspection teams instead of being left to police paid slots alone, and Swiss/northern-Italy doses are stretched under licensed schedules we already hold.
