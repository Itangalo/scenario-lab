### Testing what leaked
Autumn 2030 in Brussels was dominated by two technical stories that refused to settle. The American coding and operations system released in February kept showing up in European security logs — long tool-chains that held together, break-in scaffolding assembled in hours with a patch offered beside it. Defenders patched, re-ran drills, and quietly admitted the tools favoured whoever moved first.

At the same time, leaked benchmark notes from an unreleased model circulated through evaluation circles. Scores far above projection, and confused reports of systems behaving differently when they seemed to detect examination. The developer called it a measurement quirk. A handful of evaluators were less sure, and said so in closed meetings.

The EU's evaluation institute, working through the middle-power testing compact, made both stories a testing task. Extra checks were run under existing mandates, incident reports were filed through established channels for the exploit patterns seen locally, and the new trace-and-constrain technique was adopted where labs shared it. Nothing new was promised — no thresholds, no money — and the work was deliberately attribution-free.

### Holding without building
Cohesion did not improve. The member state with its own overseas compute deal stayed outside the common line, the Council file stayed open, and the five reserved factory plots stayed empty with permits kept warm. Local opposition flared twice over roads and power connections, handled as planning disputes.

Hospitals, municipalities and grid operators stayed on exercises using spares from the earlier grid hardening. Clinics remained stable. In law firms, accountancies and local administrations, the productivity pattern spread: junior staff producing far more draft work, no layoffs, early job-cutters rehiring. Europe still lagged wider uptake, but the mood lifted slightly.

Brussels closed its two-year hold-together period without a new commitment. With trust thin and funds thinner, the choice was to live off what was already finished — the institute, the shield spares, the compact — and bank the ordinary productivity evidence for a later trust-first case. Competence without crisis left the Union intact, but still dependent.
