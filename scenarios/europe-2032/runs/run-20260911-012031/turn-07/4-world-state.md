### Patching, not building
The second half of 2029 was defined by repair work. Municipal IT teams, hospital administrators and port operators spent the autumn finally closing the unpatched dependency that had let machine-generated ransomware spread in February. With recalled grid-drive crews rotating through, daily guidance from Brussels, and clinics slowly moving off paper, the response looked steadier than in the spring — orderly, but with no new protection to show.

Attribution was never published. Lessons-learned papers circulated without naming a culprit, which calmed operators and frustrated capitals that wanted someone to blame.

### The holdout holds
The member state that cut its own compute deal did not return. The Council kept the cohesion file open, repeated the offer of common terms for Washington and joint licensing, and allowed joint evaluation work to go ahead without the holdout. The common line survived on paper. The five reserved factory sites stayed reserved and unbuilt, permits kept warm, bankers still absent. Anti-construction sentiment flared locally in faint press reports of blocked access roads and disputed power hook-ups, but nothing yet stopped a build that had no funding to start.

### A floor built elsewhere
In Washington and Beijing, negotiators announced a limited accord on securing model weights and restricting certain biological design tools, with thin verification. Brussels was briefed after the fact. The middle-power compact became the vehicle for alignment, and the evaluation institute prepared to plug into shared testing once money exists.

Meanwhile a US frontier laboratory announced machine-generated proofs resolving a long-open problem in combinatorics — celebrated by mathematicians, remote from voters. More felt were the office figures: law firms, accountancies, administrations and consultancies reporting measurable productivity gains, strongest among juniors, without layoffs. Firms that had cut early quietly rehired. Papers noted Europe lagging in adoption, but the mood lifted marginally: competence without crisis.

Brussels banked the evidence and started nothing.
