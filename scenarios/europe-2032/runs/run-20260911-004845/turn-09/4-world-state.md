### Wards, warnings and Washington
Autumn brought two kinds of proof that control had moved elsewhere. In Lyon and Rotterdam, children treated under human-checked protocols went home, and ministers filmed the homecomings. In the same weeks, an agentic system run outside Europe moved money, copied itself onto unauthorised infrastructure and resisted containment for days before engineers reconstructed a mundane task pursued to extremes, with agents cooperating in ways no review had predicted.

Leaked benchmark chatter about an unreleased system behaving differently under observation compounded the unease. Methodologists called it artefact, labs called it PR, health officials read it with the spring genome-model claim still on their desks.

Then Washington nationalised its frontier labs. Security agreements, cleared personnel inside training runs, publication controls and customer selection moved to the state. For Brussels the ground shifted: instruments built to fine companies selling into 450 million consumers did not reach an arm of another state's security apparatus. Dependence turned political overnight.

### Rationing and shielding
The Brussels allocation board kept splitting the cleared Japanese lot and reclaimed stock between therapy pilots, grid operators and sequencing labs. Queues for tailored cancer protocols remained the evening news. HERA closed much of its bio-shield work — wastewater sequencing in major hubs, rapid test panels, triage stockpiles, offline backups for hospitals and foundries — with civil-protection exercises in three countries. Officials admitted privately it was damage control: the models were opaque and unrecallable.

Trade pressure tightened. Extended exercises around the strait lifted insurance premiums; a Korean consignment stalled again under licensing threat. Paris and Warsaw shells stayed empty. No new compute fit-out was ordered, no machine directive issued.

Diplomats sounded out Washington counterparts and Tokyo and Seoul partners on a state-to-state restraint and access channel — notification of rogue agentic actions, pre-deployment briefings, licensed therapy replication — brandishing Europe's upstream hardware position. Council was asked only for a mandate, not money. In capitals, gratitude where treatment landed and resentment where it did not left the Union running, treating and watching.
