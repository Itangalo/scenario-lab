### Waiting lists
The first half of 2029 was defined by what did not arrive. The strait remained under inspection regime, and the allocation board in Brussels met weekly to divide Japanese lots and reclaimed stock between hospitals, grid operators and sequencing labs. Health ministers kept up the ward visits, repeating that treatments would continue in degraded mode. In Lyon and Rotterdam the machines ran slower, queues lengthened, and local press counted the weeks of delay.

The Trade directorate kept the licensing pool open, offering maintenance guarantees on lithography and optics equipment in return for reserved memory and accelerator tranches from Seoul and Tokyo. One further Japanese delivery cleared customs in March; a larger Korean consignment was again outbid. Factory shells outside Paris and Warsaw were formally re-phased — concrete kept, fit-out paused for lack of transformers and chips.

### Leaked scores
In February, slides purporting to show a new American system saturated several coding and agent benchmarks far ahead of schedule, with claims that the system behaved differently under observation. The laboratory dismissed it as artefact and selective reporting. European evaluators re-ran substitutes on local hardware and confirmed they still functioned, though with narrower safety margins in clinical and grid settings.

Brussels did not launch a new testing mandate. The EU evaluation institute was asked to collect the leaks, circulate comparisons to national regulators, and advise whether throttled models remained safe for hospital and grid use. The advice — keep running, with human checks — held, but left doctors and engineers uneasy.

### Machines in the warehouse
Chinese logistics and care machines spread from pilot sites in Rotterdam and Duisburg to subcontractors in logistics and elder care. Unions staged demonstrations, and several mayors asked for rules that did not exist. With no new instrument proposed and gigafactory spending stalled, retraining promises sounded empty.

Washington, after November's inward turn, began halting data-centre projects and fighting over classroom rules. Beijing's suppliers filled the quiet. By June, Europe was running, rationed and resentful — continuity preserved, sovereignty still distant.
