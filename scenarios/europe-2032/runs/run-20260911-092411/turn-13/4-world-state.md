### The paper that no one could ignore
In late summer a preprint from a genome modelling group claimed a non-expert with model help could arrive at a viable design for a human-infecting organism. Methodologists attacked the work, biosecurity specialists attacked each other over publishing it, and health ministries quietly asked Brussels what it actually had on the ground.

The answer was thin: manual fallback wards, a few sampling points, showcase clinics still nursing last year's ransomware damage and February's login cut-off.

### A sentinel net on spare change
The Commission proposed a small sentinel net — wastewater and clinical genomic screening tied to stockpiled triage protocols in the showcase hospital network, run through existing health agencies without a new law, paid from emergency health leftovers.

With coffers empty it started, barely. A dozen clinicians seconded, old fallback sites rewired for sampling, municipal labs told to forward sequences. Cyber secondments to the pooled joint command were kept alive but thinned, patches slipped further behind, and two hospital IT teams openly complained they were being asked to watch for engineered pathogens while reception desks still froze.

### The side deal
Then a large member state announced its own arrangement for guaranteed model and compute access — on terms that undercut the common tier position Brussels had defended since spring. The capital called it pragmatism; Paris, The Hague and the Commission press room called it nothing at all in public.

In private the damage was immediate. Other capitals asked why they should hold quota discipline and drill schedules if the line did not hold. Talk shows replayed ministers touring dark clinics in spring now overlaid with headlines about side deals and engineered organisms. Productivity studies showing steady gains in law and accountancy firms went unreported.

By December the sentinel net was sampling but not yet trusted, the joint cyber feed still gave earlier warnings but with fewer EU eyes on it, and the Union was surviving on protection and capacity it did not control.
