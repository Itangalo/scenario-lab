## Priority
Priority is M9 European Public Dividend Showcase, because with capital at 2 and sentiment at 12 it is the only instrument that can keep a common EU line alive through the cyber incident and the open-weight jump.

## In practice
We execute M9 through DG REFORM and Commission Representations on existing Digital Europe/EU4Health money: keep the health/justice pilots online, publish the waiting-list dashboards under EU procurement badges, and extend ENISA guard and black-start cover to those showcase sites first. No new law, no new fund — an Implementing Decision only.

We answer the automated cyber sweep and the frontier open-weight release with finished capacity, not new instruments: M7 containment and mutual-aid reimbursement plus M4 grid-shield isolations and manual restoration to keep hospitals degrading not stopping, and M8 breaker-log and transformer-store repairs prioritised where sabotage plus attack overlap. On chips and compute we stay at quota-management — no ASML concessions, Japan/Korea spares talks alive, freeze on forced migration — because with private build-out cancelled we cannot buy sovereignty this turn.
