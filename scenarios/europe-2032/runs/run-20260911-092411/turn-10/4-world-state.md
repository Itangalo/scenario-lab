### Claiming a win while the stores burn
The half-year opened with money leaving. After years of breakneck build-out, private AI valuations reset hard in the spring. Planned data-centre expansions in Europe funded through US hyperscaler partnerships were cancelled outright, not deferred. Leased compute options Brussels had counted on evaporated, and frontier training budgets shrank for the first time.

Washington tightened again at the same moment. Advanced chips and hosted model access for allied buyers moved to strict volume quotas, with servicing of older lithography tools under case-by-case licence. The Hague complied under renewed protest. With private capital frozen, the Commission chose quota-management: no new concessions on export equipment, talks with Tokyo and Seoul kept alive on spares, and a freeze on any forced migration off foreign stacks.

Against that squeeze, one European success became visible. In two health ministries and a justice pilot run through EU-procured tools, waiting lists fell measurably and administrative decision times dropped from months to days. Brussels rushed to label it: dashboards, EU procurement badges, Commission representation tours crediting continuity hosting on European soil.

That hosting became a target. Through March and April, coordinated groups opposed to AI expansion struck at the very fallback the black-start plan relied on — off-books generator pools drained, transformer stores vandalised, breaker-log cabinets torched as symbols of collaboration. Police reports treated the sites as political targets precisely because they had been brought on-scheme and reimbursed.

The containment and mutual-aid protocol finished formal adoption this turn, and mattered. Cross-border fuel and spares reimbursement held, and manual-restoration drills turned into real isolations that kept hospitals degrading rather than stopping. But restoration still crawled where stores had been sabotaged, and the showcase clinics needed ENISA guard details to stay online.

Trust ticked up where the waiting-list numbers were believed, and fell where the fires were seen. The Commission held the common line, but with almost nothing left to spend.
