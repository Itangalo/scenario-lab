## In practice
We hold what holds. DG CNECT and ENISA keep the Seville thresholds and assured-systems guidance live where installed — Rotterdam, Antwerp, patched grids — with Recovery Corps teams logging restores, and DG DIGIT keeps the two city back-office wins running as proof protection can pay. No new build, no new levy, no licence fight we cannot win.

DG ECFIN and DG MOVE roll over the emergency state guarantees for uninsured grid and port operators on existing reprogrammed funds, strictly conditioned on certification and incident reporting, while EPSCO maintains wage-bridges for displaced pickers and warehouse staff. With open weights now at the frontier and robots taking picking, sorting and welding from outside suppliers, we publicly name the boundary — checkable manual tasks gone, judgement work manual — and ask Washington only for spares continuity, to avoid another capital-burning refusal.
