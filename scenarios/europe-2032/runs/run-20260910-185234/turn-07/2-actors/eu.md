We task ENISA and the JRC with JRC Seville to turn the breakthrough into a deployable certification kit — pre-observation behaviour prediction and certified control properties — and mandate its application through the NIS2 implementing acts to grid, port and municipal operators hit in January. Recovery Corps teams from M6, with DG CNECT funding on low burn, do the retrofit and clean-restore in place; no new gigafactory cash vote is sought.

At the same time we keep M8 alive on minimal burn: HR/VP and Trade hold the middle-power export-licence and pooled-evaluation format as monthly technical talks, offering Rotterdam incident data and the new certification results as the EU ticket to keep US tiering from hardening and to preserve sites and grid reservations from M1/M2 for a later coalition-backed rebuild. No parallel compute programme is launched.
