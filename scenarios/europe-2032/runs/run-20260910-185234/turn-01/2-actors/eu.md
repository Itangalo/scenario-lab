## Priority
Grid and Port Cyber Shield, not the Gigafactories, because staged breaker credentials in two member states mean the next swarm actuates before compute sovereignty lands.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the TTE Council formation with an emergency NIS2 implementing act: mandatory OT segmentation audits for all transmission system operators, the Rotterdam/Antwerp-scale port authorities and regional water utilities, funded from the existing InvestAI envelope re-profiled toward hardening, not new money. CER cross-border mutual aid teams will exercise breaker-loss scenarios this winter, with ACER verifying relay credential rotation.

The Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) continue on their tracks — DG CNECT holds permitting zones and ASML leverage — but political push this turn goes to interior and energy ministers who must impose costs on operators, offering EU co-financing for detection tooling in exchange for mandatory incident reporting into the Shield. Telecoms and finance hardening is referenced as template, not reopened as a separate instrument.
