## In practice
We hold M1/M2 at legal-anchoring condition without new push: DG CNECT keeps InvestAI/EIB guarantees conditional on EU jurisdiction, lets the contested site shift to the backup grid-ready location via accelerated-permitting zone, and tasks the Competitiveness Council to stop the three-capital bidding. No new money this turn.

The push is the Sprint through ENISA with ACER/CEER and civil-protection funds: mobile restore teams, standardized failover images, and two-day re-tests for failed hospitals/municipals, with a pass certificate that satisfies insurer hardening requirements. Interior ministries get paperwork cut to a single continuity-plan template as price for access.

We keep M4 preparatory only: DG DIGIT asks services to cost EU-hosted triage/permit/tutoring options against Taiwan-driven supply risk, no contracts launched, to avoid a second staffing front while the Sprint absorbs ENISA bandwidth.
