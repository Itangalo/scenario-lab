Brussels claimed a win as EU-procured/hosted AI cut waiting lists in three health systems and two justice ministries and lifted pilot-school results, briefly shifting coverage from sabotage to patients seen faster.

Then the fallback was attacked: coordinated winter-spring vandalism across four states hit formalised generator/transformer/breaker-log pools — fuel contaminated, cables cut, warehouse torched, anti-machine leaflets left. Mutual-aid reimbursement and joint rerouting kept manual restoration running, slower and under guard.

Washington held volume quotas and case-by-case servicing; Dutch complied under protest. With private AI valuations collapsing and hosting deals evaporating, Brussels froze: no forced migration, no export-tool concessions, Japan/Korea talks kept alive.

Showcase sites got ENISA/crisis-network protection first, keeping dashboards green while mayors complained spares were diverted. Capability growth slowed with money, safety assurance fell further behind. Public mood rose from open hostility on real benefits but stayed deeply distrustful.
