## New measure
**EU Anti-Coercion Shield for ASML and Lithography Servicing**
Activates the Anti-Coercion Instrument around ASML to block extraterritorial cut-off of older lithography exports and servicing and to keep EU leverage jointly held.
Why this and why now: Washington is using our one chokepoint against us while we have no measures in flight and no capital to waste; defending it is the precondition for any future sovereignty and for holding services together.

## Priority
Priority is M10 EU Anti-Coercion Shield for ASML and Lithography Servicing, because without it the Union loses its only bottleneck leverage and any cut-off resilience becomes permission-based.

## In practice
We act through DG TRADE and the Foreign Affairs Council using the Anti-Coercion Instrument legal base: formal examination of US extraterritorial coercion via the Netherlands, blocking-statute cover for ASML servicing contracts, and a joint mandate to negotiate servicing carve-outs with Washington rather than bilateral Dutch compliance.

We pair this in Council with Japan and Korea outreach on spares and servicing reciprocity, funded from existing trade-defence and Digital Europe envelopes — no new money vote this turn given capital at 7. We tell mayors and showcase hospitals plainly that spares timelines stay guarded, while telling Washington quotas cannot be met if servicing is withdrawn.
