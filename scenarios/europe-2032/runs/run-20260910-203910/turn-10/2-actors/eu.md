## Priority
The new EU Cyber Recovery and Assured Substitution Deployment, because a visible attack on degraded public services outranks steady substitution work — if restoration fails, continuity fails with it.

## In practice
We run recovery through ENISA, EU-CyCLONe and the Shield continuity teams: triage the ransomware/dependency blast radius, isolate affected municipal and health IT, restore from clean backups onto EU-hosted stacks, and surge cross-border CERT mutual aid to the hardest-hit member states, funded by repurposed Shield operating lines and emergency UCPM support.

We pair restoration with assurance: the Assurance Cell with the Joint Research Centre vets the interpretability/control breakthrough, publishes deployment guidance, and mandates logging and pre-observation checks on the vetted openweight models carrying imaging triage, procurement and customer desks. Health and TTE Councils carry the mandate; telecom vendors on foreign servicing contracts get fallback support only under EU maintenance discipline and incident-reporting conditions.
