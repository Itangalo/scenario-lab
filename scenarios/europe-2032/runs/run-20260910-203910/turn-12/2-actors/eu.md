## New measure
**EU Degraded-Essentials Sustainment**
Keeps registries, hospital admin and municipal desks running in degraded EU-hosted mode with manual workarounds, mutual aid and passive bio-detection uplift, without new build.
Chosen because with capital at 3 and sentiment at 2 we cannot fund or enforce anything larger, and if degraded essentials stop now the Union loses the last basis for any recovery later.

## Priority
EU Degraded-Essentials Sustainment, because it is the only measure on the books and without degraded-but-running services political agency collapses entirely.

## In practice
We run this through ENISA, EU-CyCLONe, HERA/ECDC and the UCPM with no new money: extend M8/M9 operating lines, keep clean-backup rebuilds on EU-hosted stacks, retain logging and pre-checks on open-weight triage/procurement models, and fund overtime and workaround staffing via repurposed Shield and cohesion funds.

We ask the Health and EPSCO Councils for a sustainment mandate, not a new regulation: degraded service standards, cross-border staff and bed/registry mutual aid, and exercised manual fallbacks. We tell member states openly we cannot promise restoration to normal or frontier access, only that power stays on and clinics and registries stay open, to stop fragmentation while we survive this trough.
