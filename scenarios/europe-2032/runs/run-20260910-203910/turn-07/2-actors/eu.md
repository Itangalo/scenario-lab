## In practice
We declare the pause ourselves via an Employment Council recommendation backed by a Commission guidance on pilot permits, making the stoppage EU-wide, temporary, and conditional — not a mayoral patchwork. DG EMPL reprograms ESF+ to keep wage-insurance flowing during the pause, with Europol and national police coordinating site protection rather than evictions to avoid escalation.

We use the semester to negotiate restart criteria with IndustriAll, BusinessEurope and the occupying works councils: no restart without levy payment, oversight rights, and EU-soil data and maintenance clauses that answer the defection risk.
