The Middle Power AI Coalition (MPAC) formally concludes its framework, but momentum fractures. South Korea and the Netherlands pivot toward the emergent supply-chain pact, prioritizing chip access over joint governance. Japan delays mirroring enforcement, awaiting US posture clarity. Norway and Finland offer red-team support, but without binding commitments, the EU’s leverage remains thin. The broader geopolitical reordering continues apace, yet the Union struggles to anchor it.
