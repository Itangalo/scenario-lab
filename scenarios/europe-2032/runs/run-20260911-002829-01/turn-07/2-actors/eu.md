## Priority
EU Cyber Restoration and Gigafactory Continuity Taskforce — because with capital at 13 and sentiment at 15 nothing else lands if ransomware-down services stay down and the two sites stay blocked.

## In practice
We run the Taskforce first through ENISA, CERT-EU and DG HOME/CNECT under the Cyber Solidarity Act and Civil Protection Mechanism: ring-fenced incident-response teams to the worst-hit municipalities and utilities, clean backups and manual-fallback operation for water/power and hospitals, and temporary national-guard/police protection plus injunction orders for the two construction sites and TEN-E grid links. EIB emergency liquidity keeps small utilities and contractors solvent through the investment-collapse repricing.

On M7 we keep EEAS in Washington seeking interim researcher access while DG TRADE answers the ASML coercion via the Anti-Coercion Instrument — no export-control surrender that trades the one chokepoint for unsigned access — and we use the Foreign Affairs Council to re-bind the defecting member state to a common line. DG COMP holds the narrowed gigafactory state-aid cover so the Taskforce protection converts into poured concrete, not just guarded fences.
