### Neutral ground in someone else's war
Autumn began with sirens of two kinds. American and Chinese forces were firing on each other across the Pacific, and within weeks fabrication plants, cables, satellites and data centres were declared legitimate targets. Brussels declared neutrality the same week: no belligerent use of Union territory, airspace or cables, no part in targeting decisions, but open offers of hospital aid, civil protection and cable repair.

Neutrality did not keep the war away. Power lines feeding the two fenced gigafactory construction sites were cut in coordinated night attacks, leaving cranes still and substations dark. Contractors suspended work, insurers withdrew, and guarded medical convoys now shared roads with police crime-scene teams. Ministers insisted the pylons were a criminal matter, not a front line. Local papers called them the first war damage in Europe.

### Containment holds, trust does not
The pandemic regime became the whole of government. Joint buying of tests and protective gear, wastewater and sequencing watch, coordinated closure guidance, and cyber teams keeping hospital records restorable held utilities and wards from cascading together. The taskforce built the previous year to restore networks and keep factory power on finished its work and was folded into hospital continuity.

At the same time the courts finished what fear had started. Judges confirmed that an automated welfare and policing support system had penalised thousands while never being classed as high-risk, so every step had been lawful. The 2024 rulebook looked written for another decade. Coverage fused the judgment with illness, outages and fenced-off building sites.

A limited Washington-Beijing understanding on securing weights, avoiding automated escalation and restricting biological design tools was noted in Brussels and changed little on the ground. Laboratories published joint evaluation language while a new openly released model, close to the closed frontier, spread to hundreds of thousands of machines beyond any recall.

By December hospitals still functioned, lights stayed on, and the Union had stayed together. Few thanked it.
