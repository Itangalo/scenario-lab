Prior autumn ransomware and stalled gigafactories left brittle services and interim US access talks, later irrelevant.

February-March: genome-model-assisted severe pathogen triggered pandemic mode — borders, triage, closures. EU HERA/ECDC emergency regime: joint procurement, wastewater/sequencing surveillance, coordinated closures, hospital-focused cyber restoration; taskforce later folded into hospital continuity.

Autumn: US-China war in Pacific; fabs, cables, satellites, data centres declared targets. Brussels declared neutrality — no belligerent use of territory/airspace/cables, humanitarian/cable-repair offers only. Neutrality failed to insulate: power lines to fenced gigafactory sites cut in coordinated attacks, work suspended, insurers withdrew; ministers called it criminal, press called it first war damage.

Courts confirmed automated welfare-policing system lawfully penalised thousands due to non-high-risk classification, discrediting 2024 rulebook. Washington-Beijing understanding on weights, escalation, bio-design tools noted but changed little; new near-frontier open model spread irretrievably.

By December: hospitals functioned, lights on, Union intact but trust collapsed.
