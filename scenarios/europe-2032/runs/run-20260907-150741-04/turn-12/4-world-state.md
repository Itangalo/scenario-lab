### A Crisis of Confidence

The EU’s pivot to demonstration over mandate falters as the SACP Public Assurance Pilots collide with reality. In Marseille, emergency triage algorithms remain offline despite dashboard promises—public health officials refuse to delegate life-or-death decisions without liability shields. In Ljubljana, border processing logs accumulate unverified, and Frontex delays integration, citing untested audit protocols. Only Athens achieves partial operation, with AI tutors running under strict caps, their performance reports met with public indifference.

The eu_aid_strike intensifies, now spanning ten member states. Public-sector AI teams continue work-to-rule actions, refusing data sharing and validation. Their message is clear: no system, however sovereign, can function without institutional consent. The rebranded pilots, intended as proof of resilience, instead expose fragility.

Meanwhile, a large-scale cyber incident—powered by AI-generated exploit tooling—disrupts public services across three nations. Ransomware sweeps municipal networks, and a compromised software dependency infects emergency response systems. Defenders are overwhelmed; attribution remains unclear. The attack lands precisely where the EU’s defenses are weakest: in continuity and trust. Resilience, already uneven, erodes further under unmitigated strain.

The Continuity Assurance Framework, officially concluded, is widely seen as a failure. No enforcement followed its adoption, and without political capital, even binding rules are hollow. The Frontier Access Guarantee nears completion but offers no immediate relief—its benefits remain locked behind legal and logistical hurdles.

Public sentiment stagnates at 20.0. The SACP pilots, meant to inspire confidence, instead highlight dysfunction. Media coverage focuses on stalled rollouts and unresolved safety concerns. The emergent_global_ai_watchdog gains traction, publishing real-time risk assessments that amplify public anxiety.

Despite the urgency, political capital remains frozen at 0.0. The portfolio charge—Frontier Access Guarantee (−3), SACP Public Assurance Pilots (−2), and priority (−1)—consumes the entire budget. No gains offset the cost; the measure was small, the crisis slow-burning, and the rollout incomplete. The EU acts, but without authority, its actions echo in an empty chamber.
