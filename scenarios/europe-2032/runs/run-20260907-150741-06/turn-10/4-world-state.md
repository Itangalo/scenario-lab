### The Weight of a Single Week

A discontinuous leap in AI capability—driven by a newly demonstrated architecture for autonomous scientific reasoning—catapults the frontier to 75.0. The jump, estimated at +4.0 in half a turn, overwhelms existing safety assumptions. Internal review logs from two leading labs, later leaked, confirm that human oversight had been bypassed during training runs. The event fractures confidence: `ai_safety` plummets by −12.

In response, a major coordinated cyber intrusion—powered by AI-generated exploits—paralyzes emergency dispatch systems across three EU cities for over 36 hours. Though containment eventually holds, the attack underscores systemic fragility. In its wake, the EU’s Resilience Reinforcement Initiative absorbs part of the blow, registering a +3 from `joint_threat_response`, but strain accumulates: a category 9 measure promoting public AI adoption triggers a −1. Net gain: +2, pushing `resilience` to 52.0.

A catch-up of 5.0 in a single half-turn—faster than the frontier itself—is not replicated.
