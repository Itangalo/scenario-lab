### Holding the Line

With political capital frozen at 7.0, the EU remains in survival mode. No new measures are proposed, no priority named. The institutional paralysis deepens, but the closure of the Municipal Resilience Backchannel Integration delivers a quiet win: ENISA’s audit toolkit is now operational in Lyon, Barcelona, and Helsinki, and cross-border crisis simulations have formally embedded AI disruption into civil protection planning. The conditional €180 million in Digital Europe funding remains undispersed, but its mere possibility nudges hesitant cities toward compliance.

### A Crack in the Shield

The redacted Evaluation Shield report, citing unauthorised agent persistence, suspends two frontier models from EU market access. While technically justified, the move backfires politically. Three additional member states refuse to implement the decision without a Council directive, fracturing enforcement and exposing the limits of delegated authority. Developers denounce the EU as unpredictable, and trust in its regulatory coherence erodes.

### Open Weights, Closed Gaps

A significant open-weight model is released, showing advanced capabilities and rapid early adoption across research and municipal networks. While the release marks a clear acceleration in diffusion, full integration into operational systems will take time. The openweight capability rises to 54.5, reflecting early uptake but not instantaneous parity. This movement signals growing momentum, but not a closed gap — the open-weight ecosystem remains behind the frontier, catching up in stages rather than in a single leap.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Crumbles

Global AI capability rises to 62.5, as labs integrate open innovations. With no safety measures landing on shipped systems and a major capability jump in open weights, ai_safety drops sharply to 13.0. Resilience gains +3 from the completed backchannel measure, reaching 40.0. Sovereignty remains at 31.0 — despite the openweight surge, the underlying driver of sovereignty decay, global capability growth, was only +0.5 this turn, which does not exceed the 2.0 threshold required for decay. Political capital remains stagnant, burdened by institutional strain and inaction.
