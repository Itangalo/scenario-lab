## In practice  
We are treating the completion of the InvestAI Gigafactories programme as a turning point: four sites are now under construction, with first-phase delivery expected by Q2 2030, anchoring a minimum of 15,000 next-generation GPUs on EU soil. This is not just infrastructure—it is leverage. But physical capacity means nothing without operational command. That is why we are launching the European Sovereign AI Activation Council: a new executive body chaired jointly by the Commission, ENISA, and the European Defence Agency, with voting membership from all states contributing compute to the sovereign reserve. Its mandate is clear—approve training runs, assign priority workloads, and enforce cross-domain interoperability—and its authority will be codified under Article 122 TFEU, with qualified majority voting to prevent single-state obstruction.

Poland’s continued veto of the Sovereign Model Development Initiative through the EuroHPC Council has become untenable. We are offering Warsaw a final settlement: full participation in the Activation Council with guaranteed Tier-2 node status in Wrocław, inclusion in the first sovereign training run, and co-leadership on the interpretability stack—but only if they lift their block by the end of this quarter. If not, we trigger Article 122 emergency procedures to designate Szeged and Cluj as dual alternative nodes, funded directly from the Chips Act reserve and operating under Commission supervision. This bypasses EuroHPC governance entirely and sets a precedent: when strategic cognition is at stake, the Union decides collectively—or it loses the right to decide at all.

Meanwhile, the aftermath of the health system cyberattack continues to erode public sentiment. We are fast-tracking integration of red-teamed vulnerabilities into national SOC protocols and mandating AI-generated exploit detection in all NIS2-covered entities by Q1 2030. The Labour Transition Fund’s successor design is ready, but we cannot launch it yet—political capital is too thin, and legitimacy too frayed. Every move now must restore credibility: not through promises, but through irreversible decisions.
