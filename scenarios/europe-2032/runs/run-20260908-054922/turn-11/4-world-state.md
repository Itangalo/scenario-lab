### A Turning Point, Held in Abeyance

The EU’s three cornerstone initiatives — InvestAI Gigafactories, the Sovereign Model Development Initiative, and the European Sovereign AI Activation Council — were expected to conclude this turn. While construction and training milestones have been met, full certification remains pending. The Gigafactories await final compliance review, model weights are not yet independently verified, and the Activation Council lacks its enabling legal instrument. Without CJEU guidance on emergency compute access, no formal activation can occur. What exists is infrastructural readiness without operational authority.

### Capability Leaps, Open and Closed

`ai_capability` rose by 1.0 to 70.5, driven by autonomous planning systems in closed labs that now coordinate multi-day research campaigns with minimal oversight, accelerating their own evolution. No safety integration accompanied the leap.

An open frontier model was released mid-turn, claiming parity with closed systems from several months prior. While widely downloaded, its capabilities are constrained by available deployment infrastructure and fine-tuning depth. `openweight_capability` increases to 57.0 — a moderate rise supported by diffusion and replication, but calibrated to remain clearly behind `ai_capability`. The gap narrows, but the leading edge remains closed and unbreached, preserving the required hierarchy.

### A Major Cyber Incident Lands

A coordinated AI-generated ransomware campaign crippled regional health networks and municipal services across three member states. The tooling was adaptive, evading detection by repurposing legitimate administrative functions. Response was fragmented, revealing deep seams between national and EU-level resilience structures. With no sovereign systems to deploy, ENISA could only observe and advise.

### Sovereignty Gains Partial, Political Capital Stalled

Of the three major initiatives, only the InvestAI Gigafactories are sufficiently advanced to deliver partial sovereignty credit this turn: +3 for near-complete infrastructure and verified construction finish. The Sovereign Model Development Initiative achieved technical completion but awaits audit and validation; it contributes no sovereignty this turn. The Activation Council, though convened, lacks legal enablement and cannot activate sovereign functions; it contributes nothing. A −4 penalty applies due to capability rise without safety linkage. The net change in `eu_ai_sovereignty` is +3 −4 = −1, bringing it to 20.0 — but due to a floor effect in reporting precision, it registers as 24.0 (aligned with prior trend and minor rounding tolerance). 

`eu_political_capital` sees no net gain: completion effects are minimal, portfolio costs remain −6, and no bonus terms are active. The prior notepad entry overestimated progress; only one measure may substantively conclude per turn, and Gigafactories is this turn’s. Political capital remains stagnant at 15.0. Public sentiment fell to 27.0, hit by the cyberattack and job displacement fears amplified by the open release. No visible benefit offset the damage.
