## Priority  
``Sovereign Model Development Initiative`` — because the openweight_frontier_release confirms that capability is now diffusing faster than we can govern it, and without our own sovereign model, we will be reacting to every jump in the frontier with no leverage, no understanding, and no capacity to act independently.

## In practice  
We are launching the Sovereign Model Development Initiative under the legal authority of the European High Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU), expanding its mandate to include not just compute but full-stack model development. This is not about catching up to the frontier in one leap — that would be fantasy — but about creating a sovereign anchor point: a model trained on EU-controlled infrastructure, with EU-aligned values, auditable architecture, and no foreign override. The first phase, running through turn 8, will secure 10,000 H100-equivalent GPUs across three EuroHPC sites (Luxembourg, Spain, and Finland), staff them with a pan-European team of 500 researchers and engineers drawn from national labs and universities, and begin training a model targeting capability parity with the current closed frontier (54.0) by turn 8. We are already in talks with ASML, Infineon, and STMicroelectronics to secure chip supply chains, and with Germany and France to co-fund the talent pipeline.

The political challenge is real: this measure costs 3 political capital per turn, and we are already at capacity with two category 4 measures and the red-teaming network. But the openweight_frontier_release changes the calculus — we can no longer rely on lagged diffusion or lab goodwill. We are redirecting €15 billion from the Digital Europe Programme and leveraging cohesion funds with a new conditionality: member states that host nodes must contribute either compute or talent. Resistance will come from those who see this as duplication or overreach, but we are offering veto rights on deployment use cases and guaranteed access for national agencies. This is not a competitor to private innovation — it is a floor beneath which no EU decision should fall. Without it, we are not a player. With it, we have a foundation.
