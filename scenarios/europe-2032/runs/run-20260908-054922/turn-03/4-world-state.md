### Frontier Push and Open Release
The AI frontier advances again, with recursive training loops and agent coordination enabling systems to autonomously design and validate complex software and materials experiments over multi-day horizons. `ai_capability` rises by +2.0 to 58.0, edging toward the threshold where junior expert roles begin to vanish across cognitive sectors. A new open-weight model, derived from leaked training data and refined by a transnational academic consortium, reaches broad expert-level reasoning in narrow domains. While still behind the frontier, it marks a step change in accessibility. `openweight_capability` rises to 45.0, crossing a critical threshold where distributed actors can now execute sustained, goal-directed tasks with minimal oversight.

### Sovereignty Efforts Stumble, Social Response Launched
The EU pushes forward on the Sovereign Model Development Initiative, securing political backing from France and Germany and offering Poland a secondary node in Wrocław. However, funding remains stalled: the European Parliament delays the EuroHPC mandate expansion, citing legal concerns over data sovereignty and procurement. The reprogramming of €8 billion from the Digital Europe Programme faces resistance from smaller states fearing exclusion. Talent recruitment lags, and GPU deliveries under the Chips Act are delayed by export bottlenecks. The initiative remains in motion but yields no operational capacity.

In response to mounting unrest, the EU launches the Labour Transition Acceleration Fund, directing €30bn toward displaced workers in legal, administrative, and technical sectors. Wage insurance and retraining programs begin rolling out in Belgium, Spain, and Finland, with early uptake in regions hit hardest by AI-driven automation. The move is framed as both economic stabilisation and political necessity.

### Safety Erodes, Capital Strains
With no safety measures completed and capability surging, `ai_safety` drops by −3 to 24.0. `resilience` gains +1 from early progress on social absorption, mitigating systemic risk. `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 19.0 as capability growth outpaces sovereign build-out. Political capital drops sharply to 28.0 under the weight of four active measures and no major delivery. Public sentiment rises slightly to 42.0, reflecting cautious approval of the labour fund.
