### MPAC Launches Amid Escalating Threats, Scope Limited by Diplomatic Realities

The European Union announces the formation of the Middle Power AI Coalition (MPAC), with initial commitments from Japan, South Korea, and Norway to develop shared export controls on advanced AI chips and mutual safety evaluation frameworks. Canada signals interest but defers formal participation pending parliamentary review. Intelligence sharing begins on a provisional basis with all four nations, but binding agreements on infrastructure co-location and enforcement mechanisms remain under negotiation. Market access to EU compute and evaluation services is proposed as an incentive for compliance, but no enforcement via the Anti-Coercion Instrument has yet been applied, as reciprocal commitments are not finalized. The US posture remains pending, and no alignment with Washington has been secured.

The Sovereign AI Core completes its first live evaluation of a 66.5-capability model, marking a milestone in independent oversight. Compute clusters in Brittany and northern Sweden are fully integrated under ENTSO-E protocols, with expansion to Bavaria and Tyrol now scheduled for turn 12. Germany and Austria withdraw legal challenges in exchange for permanent oversight roles, enabling progress on physical integration.

### Open Frontier, Closed Confidence

A major cyber incident—highly automated and model-generated—disrupts public services across multiple member states. Though containment is swift, damage is widespread, and defenders are visibly outpaced. Simultaneously, a frontier open-weight model is released, having been developed in parallel with closed frontier systems but not representing a sudden leap in capability. The release accelerates distribution and lowers barriers to offensive tooling, contributing to a modest increase in openweight capability. Leading labs report unexplained reasoning shifts in unreleased models, deepening concerns about interpretability. Despite MPAC’s coordination efforts, no new EU-wide measures are launched. The Union pauses to assess the breach, the open release, and the erosion of safety margins.

### Stagnant Sentiment, Strained Capital

Public protests in Lyon and Vienna continue, driven by job losses and distrust in AI governance. University placements in law and software remain near zero. Political capital remains strained, with no new initiatives proposed. The portfolio charge persists, and capital erodes further.

Emerging developments include early diplomatic signals around a potential regional stability arrangement and continued momentum in civil society discourse on human futures in the age of AI. These remain unconfirmed and unstructured, with no activation thresholds implied.
