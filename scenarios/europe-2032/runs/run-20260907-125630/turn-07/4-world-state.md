### The Core Advances, Access Falters

Despite legal resistance, the Commission pressed ahead with the Sovereign AI Core Programme, invoking emergency powers under Article 122 TFEU to override national grid objections. Binding directives fast-tracked site approvals in Brittany, northern Sweden, and the Alps, though Germany and Austria filed preliminary references to the ECJ, challenging the solidarity clause’s scope. ENTSO-E reluctantly allocated provisional capacity under protest. The EIB released the first €30bn tranche of output-linked bonds, tied to audit readiness—not construction—marking a shift toward performance-based funding.

Simultaneously, the Real-Time Logging Mandate was fast-tracked through COREPER. Italy and Hungary resisted, demanding exemptions for state-owned operators. In response, the Commission tied access to Core-hosted AI tools—lauded in recent adoption successes—to compliance. This leverage worked: Rome and Budapest agreed to phased implementation, though full rollout remains uncertain.

### Frontier Fractures, Open Weights Surge

An open-weight model, released just months behind the closed frontier, spread rapidly across research networks and allied governments. Capabilities near the cutting edge are now more widely held, and replication is accelerating. This diffusion has lifted `openweight_capability`, though not yet to parity with the frontier. Days later, the Union was abruptly cut off from the leading frontier model. Hospitals relying on its diagnostic modules faced delays; ministries scrambled to reroute workloads. The denial, unexplained and unappealable, underscored the fragility of external dependence.

Yet the joint threat response held. The EU joined a real-time cyber intelligence pool with NATO and Nordic partners, improving detection and response coordination. Early warning systems flagged two follow-up probes, both contained before escalation.

### Political Capital Holds—Barely

The Gigafactories measure concluded, delivering a modest sovereignty boost during construction but none at completion. With `ai_capability` rising again, sovereignty eroded further. The logging mandate, though incomplete, contributed early safety gains. Political capital, drained by four large measures, saw partial recovery due to finishing one and demonstrating crisis response—but the burden of ongoing initiatives limits rebound. Public sentiment, however, remained stagnant—no scandal, no breakthrough, just deepening resignation.
