### A Crisis of Trust

The EU responds to a damning ombudsman report revealing widespread harm from an AI-assisted benefits system, launching the **Public-Sector AI Audit and Remediation Initiative** as its new priority. The move is framed as democratic immunization: only systems that survive independent review will gain safe harbour from future liability. Interior ministries push back, warning of administrative paralysis, but the European Data Protection Board and Fundamental Rights Agency begin setting up review panels.

Despite the urgency, public sentiment erodes further as media spotlight the scale of wrongful denials. The scandal confirms deep institutional vulnerability, and trust in AI-mediated governance plummets. Labour groups demand broader audits, while legal scholars question the initiative’s enforceability across member states.
