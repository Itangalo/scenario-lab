## In practice  
We are treating the convergence of external coercion and internal unrest as a systemic stress test. The US has tightened chip exports under its ALLIANCE posture, but in practice has rationed access so tightly that even allied European labs face delays—confirming that dependency is vulnerability, no matter the diplomatic framing. Worse, Washington has leveraged jurisdiction over American IP in ASML’s toolchain to force broader export cuts, including older lithography machines used for non-AI chips. This is not containment of China—it is assertion of unilateral control over the global supply chain, and it is being exercised against us. Our one structural chokepoint is being turned by another power, not by us.

At home, the backlash_physical wave has moved beyond protests into sabotage: grid substations serving data centres have been attacked, and there are injuries. Policing is overwhelmed, not by scale but by dispersion—this is not a single front but a thousand pinpricks. Interior ministers warn that if essential services start failing during peak winter demand, public order could unravel faster than any policy can respond. We cannot wait for full industrial autonomy—we must harden what exists, now.

So we are activating the Civil Protection Mechanism under Article 8 of Decision 1313/2013/EU, co-funding emergency upgrades to municipal IT backbone systems across ten high-risk regions identified by ENISA. Using unspent Modernisation Fund allocations, we are deploying rapid-response teams from CERT-EU and the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre to install air-gapped controls, pre-scripted failover routines, and live anomaly detection trained on adversarial AI patterns. Crucially, we are mandating cross-border mutual aid pacts: if Lyon’s water system goes down, Stuttgart’s backup node activates automatically. We are offering fast-track access to sovereign cloud capacity for any municipality that signs on by turn’s end—tying immediate survival to long-term integration. This is not ideal; it is necessary. Without this, no audit will matter, because there may be no state left to conduct one.
