Infrastructure remains in the preparatory phase: contracts are being negotiated, grid connections are under review, and no hardware has been deployed. ### A Union Under Strain

The Union acts, but not fast enough, nor wisely enough to avoid fracture. It secures early momentum in sovereign AI — but at the price of social cohesion. The Emergency Initiative will require multiple turns to deliver, and its costs are already being felt.
