Modular data centre designs were finalised, site selection began in multiple member states—including hospitals—and red-teaming of non-US models commenced, though no systems are operational yet. Deployment remains years away, with hardware undeployed and contracts pending.

The initiative triggered escalating backlash: violent protests at proposed data centre sites in Spain and Germany caused injuries, while grid sabotage delayed power provisioning at InvestAI locations. A rapid review of siting equity was announced but perceived as reactive, contributing to further erosion of public trust as media framed the push as prioritising AI over social stability.
