### Capability Creeps Forward, Safety Stalls

Global AI capability inches upward to 64.0, sustained by incremental gains in autonomous reasoning and systems integration. The frontier advances, but without corresponding safety upgrades in deployed models, the gap between power and control widens. The release of a new genome-editing design by an AI—contested but credible—fuels biosecurity alarm, reinforcing concerns that oversight is failing to keep pace. No systemic safety measure lands on frontier systems, and with no independent evaluation regime yet in place, `ai_safety` rises only slightly from its nadir, to 1.0, reflecting fragile awareness rather than assurance.

### Resilience Gains, Fragmentation Deepens

The Emergency Resilience Reinforcement Directive concludes successfully, with Lyon, Stuttgart, and Valencia fully integrated into Gaia-X’s sovereign monitoring layer. ENISA’s Cyber Incident Response Surge Capacity begins staffing, though legal disputes over deployment authority and data access delay full readiness. Resilience improves to 61.0, buoyed by partial activation of failover protocols and the directive’s closure.

Yet political cohesion fractures. Italy, Austria, and Finland invoke emergency override clauses to suspend EU-level AI deployments in public services, citing democratic accountability and uncontrolled risk. The Commission scrambles to contain the fallout, suspending enforcement actions and opening emergency talks. The Frontline Public Assurance Corps expands into Barcelona, attempting to mediate local tensions, but its mandate strains under the weight of systemic distrust.

### A New Accord, at a Cost

In response, the EU launches the Union-Wide Legitimacy Accord—a binding negotiation with member states to redefine the scope of EU authority in AI governance. Aimed at halting fragmentation, it is a large, high-stakes measure set to conclude in turn 16. However, its introduction, combined with the ongoing portfolio, overextends strained political capital. Public sentiment, already low, dips to 22.0 amid visible institutional stress and accelerating job displacement in legal and logistics sectors.
