## In practice  
This turn, we invoke Article 4 of the Treaty on European Union and the Crisis, Emergency and Exceptional Circumstances clause in the Digital Markets Act to fast-track the deployment of a sovereign AI stack. The Joint Research Centre will lead, with operational support from EuroHPC and legal anchoring through the European Commission’s Digital Directorate. We are repurposing €12bn from unused REACT-EU and Connecting Europe Facility funds to stand up secure inference clusters on EU soil, using legally quarantined copies of the last accessible model weights — now classified as critical infrastructure assets.

We are negotiating immediate access to vetted, truncated versions of frontier models with three non-US-aligned developers, offering mutual data rights and immunity from export controls in exchange for hosting rights. Simultaneously, we accelerate the InvestAI Gigafactories timeline by pre-positioning modular data centres at two approved sites, using emergency permitting under the revised Tech Sovereignty Package. The European Defence Agency will conduct a red-team audit by Q3 to ensure the stack meets minimum operational thresholds for crisis continuity. This is not a permanent solution — but it is the floor beneath everything else. If we cannot compute, we cannot govern.
