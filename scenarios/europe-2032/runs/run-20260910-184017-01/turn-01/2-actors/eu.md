## New measure
**Grid and Port Cyber Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with mandatory detection upgrades and cross-border exercises.

## Priority
Grid and Port Cyber Shield, because undetected presence in EU grids outranks build-out speed and the datacentre backlash makes hardening the licence for continued expansion.

## In practice
We task ENISA with a binding detection and segmentation standard for transmission system operators and major ports under the NIS2 legal base, funded by re-prioritised CEF-Digital and Horizon security lines, with joint exercises in the Energy Council formation this autumn. TSOs resisting cost are offered co-financing tied to Gigafactory grid-connection priority; municipalities blocking data centres are offered water and power transparency audits to split legitimate siting concerns from the coordinated campaign.

The Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) continue on their tracks — DG CNECT holds the state-aid clearances and permitting-zone negotiations — but we explicitly sequence them behind hardening in communications, using the secured frontier access terms and the public-sector adoption wins to show benefit while we fix detection first. ASML export pressure is managed quietly via the Anti-Coercion screening, not a new instrument this turn.
