## Priority
European Public Services AI Scale-Up, because it directly serves the two-year commitment to keep essential services running while converting favourable coverage into consent for Gigafactories and grid hardening.

## In practice
We run this through DG REFORM and DG CNECT under the Digital Europe Programme, with joint procurement via the European Health Data Space and the Living-in.EU municipal network, using the secured US-model terms as bridge while requiring EU-hosted deployment for scale-up sites. Health ministries and mayors who hosted pilots become champions; we offer co-financing for staffing and evaluation to municipalities facing permit fights.

M1 Gigafactories and M2 Tech sovereignty package stay in committee-track: DG COMP clears state aid, site shortlists add grid-connection and water-transparency criteria from M3's Shield to unblock permits. ENISA's finished Shield standards become the condition for scale-up connection — no detection compliance, no scale-up funds — sequencing hardening before expansion without a new instrument.
