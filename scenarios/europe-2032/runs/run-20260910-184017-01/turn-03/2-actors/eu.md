## New measure
**Municipal Shield Relief Corps**
Puts funded, shared cyber-implementation teams behind the municipalities now asked to deliver the Shield retrofits and the public-services scale-up at once, so essential services stay staffed through the load spike.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, and why now is that the June bottleneck is crews and municipal IT capacity, not rules — without relief both hardening and adoption stall and the two-year commitment to keep services running fails on implementation.

## Priority
Municipal Shield Relief Corps, because it is the binding constraint on the two-year commitment to keep essential services running — Gigafactories and scale-up both die in municipal IT queues without it.

## In practice
We fund this via Technical Support Instrument top-up plus Digital Europe redeployment, run by DG REFORM with ENISA secondments: regional pools of vetted integrators that do Shield segmentation and EU-hosted deployment for M4 sites, conditional on the March detection standards. Mayors get staff, not just co-financing; transmission operators get a single municipal interface for exercises.

M1 Gigafactories and M2 Tech sovereignty package stay on committee-track: DG COMP accelerates state-aid clearances using the new grid-connection and water-transparency criteria to settle the two stalled shortlists, with substation upgrades sequenced ahead of site awards. M4 scale-up continues joint procurement through EHDS and Living-in.EU, but no new sites connect without Shield compliance and Corps support — hardening before expansion.
