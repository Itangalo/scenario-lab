## New measure
**EU Graduate Transition and Public AI Works Corps**
Puts displaced entry-level cohorts into paid EU-hosted public-service AI deployment roles in hospitals and municipalities, pairing wage support with retraining. Why this and why now: with hiring frozen, frontier cutoff biting, and one visible adoption success to copy, this turns labour shock into the workforce that keeps essential services running on European capacity.

## Priority
EU Graduate Transition and Public AI Works Corps, because with no measures in flight and capital at 8.0 it is the only new spend that simultaneously answers job losses, distrust at 22.0, and hospitals left exposed by the cutoff.

## In practice
We fund through ESF+ and SURE-repurposed loans via EPSCO and Health Council, placing law/accountancy/software/customer-ops graduates as supervised AI-assist operators and patch-rollout aides alongside ENISA/HERA seconded teams in Lyon-model sites and Rotterdam/Antwerp docks. Legal base is Article 162/175 with Article 222 continuity justification; the large member state keeps its derogation but its feeds stay in the pact.

We copy the one adoption success that measurably cut waiting lists into a standard EU-hosted image on EuroHPC/Gigafactory spillover capacity, so wards on paper triage get a working European tool rather than a foreign cutoff. Municipal corps do ward-by-ward onboarding on overtime converted to Corps contracts, with no new chip procurement and no enforcement fight we cannot afford.
