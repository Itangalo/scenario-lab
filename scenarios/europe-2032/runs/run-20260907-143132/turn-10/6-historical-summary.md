Public trust plummets as protests spread, now joined by civil servants warning of administrative collapse.

Amid widespread exhaustion, seven major initiatives conclude without political gain, marking not progress but fatigue. The belief that sovereign control ensures safety collapses; the EU’s sovereignty becomes increasingly symbolic, lacking both capacity and cohesion.
