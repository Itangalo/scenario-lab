### Legal Limbo Undermines Evaluation Shield

The European Evaluation Shield technically completed this turn, but its authority remains unconfirmed by the ECJ. The EU’s legal reaffirmation effort via ECOFIN-JHA lacks binding force, and frontier developers withhold full cooperation. Pilot audits continue under fragile data-sharing waivers, but without judicial backing, the Shield cannot compel access. No safety gains land on deployed systems, and the capability-safety gap widens.
