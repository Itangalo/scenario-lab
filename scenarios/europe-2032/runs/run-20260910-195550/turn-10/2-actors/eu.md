## In practice
We run the Bridge as the front line: DG ECHO/ENISA single intake keeps paying overtime, short-time support and segmentation upkeep from cohesion and civil-protection budgets, prioritising eastern and southern town halls and clinics whose portals crashed, with EPSCO sign-off for extended windows and lighter audits.

In parallel we launch the Stack without a new law: DIGIT and HaDEA bulk-procure EU-hosted inference and hardened open-weight packs for health and administration, deployed on existing national clouds and Gigafactory pilot halls, with procurement preference for EU-controlled servicing. DG TRADE maintains screening-without-retaliation while we quietly negotiate fallback servicing, so substitution reads as competence, not escalation.
