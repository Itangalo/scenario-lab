## New measure
**EU Embodied-Work Transition Shield**
It keeps warehouse, sorting and logistics workers hit by commercial robots on payroll pathways through wage insurance, rapid retraining for repair/maintenance roles, and EU-preference procurement for maintenance depots using repurposed pilot halls. Why this and why now: embodied deployment just split the labour market at the manual-judgement line and open-weight frontier models put automation capability beyond recall, while blockades and sentiment at 18 mean we cannot build new capacity — we must hold people and services where they are.

## Priority
EU Embodied-Work Transition Shield, because with no measures in flight and capital at 5 only visible protection against robot-driven displacement can lift sentiment enough to unblock continuity work.

## In practice
We run this through DG EMPL and the cohesion fund already carrying the livelihood bridge, with EPSCO mandate: wage-insurance top-ups and short retraining vouchers for picking/sorting/palletising workers, co-funded by automating logistics employers, and maintenance-technician tracks staffed from the repurposed factory pilot-hall depots.

We do not force new data-centre or substation siting this turn: DIGIT/HaDEA keep EU-hosted inference and hardened open-weight packs running for Rotterdam, Lyon, Milan and Warsaw clinics on existing clouds, member states police blocked gates under existing powers, and procurement favours EU-maintained robot fleets for resupply-type logistics without touching lethality rules. Lighter audits stay to keep transfers flowing east and south.
