## In practice  
We are advancing on three tracks. The InvestAI Gigafactories remain on schedule, with Marseille and Wrocław breaking ground this quarter, and Lisbon and Helsinki finalizing environmental assessments. The EIB has disbursed €42bn in initial tranches, tied to binding agreements on renewable energy procurement and talent retention. Germany’s resistance to the Saxony-Anhalt site is being addressed through a joint financing mechanism: the Innovation Fund will cover 50% of grid reinforcement costs, but only if the site commits to hosting a national AI safety node under JRC oversight — a condition now accepted in principle.  

The Tech Sovereignty Package is progressing through COREPER II, where we have secured conditional support from six member states in exchange for fast-tracked permitting under revised TEN-E rules. The key trade remains energy for localization: we are offering expedited approvals for data centres that co-locate with new solar and offshore wind builds, creating a de facto green compute corridor from the Baltic to the Mediterranean. ASML’s hesitation persists, but our offer of a binding EU procurement contract for next-gen tools — contingent on establishing a maintenance and training hub in Leuven — has moved talks from principle to drafting.  

Meanwhile, the Cyber Shield Directive is in active negotiation in the Council. France and the Netherlands are pushing for adoption by June, while Italy and Spain continue to link support to guaranteed access to EU cyber reserves. Poland’s demand for veto power over joint operations remains a blocker, so we are proposing a compromise: operational command will rest with Europol, but activation requires a majority of affected states — preserving collective action while respecting sovereignty. Germany’s legal concerns are being addressed through a side protocol on data routing, ensuring all telemetry passes through ENISA-certified nodes before cross-border sharing. This is not ideal, but it is the floor beneath action — and with eval_anomaly_reports lighting the fuse, we cannot afford to wait for perfection.
