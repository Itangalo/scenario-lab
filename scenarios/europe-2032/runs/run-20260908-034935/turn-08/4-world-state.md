### The Inflection Point  
Signs of accelerating AI development intensify in early 2030, with frontier labs reporting shorter training cycles and increasingly autonomous research pipelines. Retrospective analysis will later confirm `rsi_onset`, but for now the shift is subtle: `ai_capability` rises at a slightly faster pace than before, increasing by +4.0 to 78.5. This reflects a gradual compounding effect consistent with underlying global rates established from turn 1 — not a sudden rupture. No discrete "leap" occurs; instead, the curve steepens incrementally, staying within the bounds of fixed, unannounced world movement.

### The End of Containment  
The EU formally abandons the Open-Weight Containment Framework, acknowledging that pre-release controls are unenforceable without U.S. cooperation, now withheld under tightening export controls. Hugging Face and GitHub Europe invoke extraterritorial jurisdiction, and enforcement collapses. In response, the Commission proposes the Secure Inference Initiative: a new regulatory framework targeting runtime monitoring, trusted execution environments, and cryptographically logged job metadata for all high-capacity inference in the bloc. The proposal marks a strategic pivot from origin-based control to action-based resilience.

### Resilience Through Observation (Proposed, Not Active)  
Though the Secure Inference Initiative is tabled, no implementation has begun. The measure is category 6 (large), requiring cross-border harmonization, technical standardization, and integration with law enforcement systems. Minimum rollout time is two full turns. Early adopters have not yet received incentives — those await approval of funding mechanisms still under negotiation. Integration with Europol’s attribution network remains in design phase. `resilience` holds at 42.0; no hardening has occurred this turn.

### Sovereignty Erodes Further  
`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 16.0, driven by the ongoing capability rise (−1) and the `export_control_escalation`, which now rations allied access (−1). The EU lacks sovereign compute at scale, and no category 4 measure completes. Political capital increases modestly to 18.0, reflecting partial credit for closing the Open-Weight Containment Framework (−3 charge removed) and the urgency of the new proposal, though the full portfolio burden remains heavy. Public sentiment holds at 28.0 — the public sees only retreat and rebranding, not renewal.
