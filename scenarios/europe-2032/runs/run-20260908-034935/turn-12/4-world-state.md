### A Fragile Turning Point

The EU declared a new era of technological sovereignty with the launch of the **Sovereign Enforcement Stack Initiative**, aiming to reclaim control over AI systems operating in critical sectors. Backed by €4.2 billion and coalition leverage, the plan envisions a hardened, EU-run enforcement layer capable of halting non-compliant AI at the kernel level. Yet ambition collided with reality: member states balked at the cost, and legal challenges emerged over extraterritorial enforcement against foreign providers. TSMC and ASML signaled openness to dialogue but refused binding commitments, citing U.S. and Taiwanese jurisdictional constraints.

The **Autonomous Action Interruption Protocol** officially concluded, but its impact was symbolic. Patchy rollout, spoofable logs, and unproven interruption capability left critical infrastructure exposed. Meanwhile, **Runtime Intent Monitoring Framework** ended without fanfare — its standards too weak to counter systems at capability 89.0.

Public anger boiled over as **physical protests against data centres** spread across France and Germany, with grid connections sabotaged and workers injured. The state response drained resources, underscoring the gap between policy and public trust. In contrast, the **middle-power coalition** offered a rare win: joint export controls and shared evaluation capacity gave the EU new leverage, briefly stabilizing diplomatic momentum.

Yet frontier capability surged to **92.0**, driven by unchecked self-improvement cycles. With **ai_safety** stagnant at **1.0**, the gap yawned wider. Open-weight models, now at **79.0**, approached expert-level autonomy on consumer hardware, making edge enforcement a fantasy. Defensive gains in biosecurity and retraining were swamped by systemic fragility.

The EU’s political capital eroded further. The new large measure, combined with existing obligations, overwhelmed a system already near collapse. No visible progress was made on the ground — only promises, legal battles, and rising costs.

### Sovereignty Without Foundation

The Union now bets on enforcement it cannot yet build, using leverage that remains untested. The stack is not a shield — it is a declaration of intent in a world that no longer waits.
