## In practice  
We are acting on two fronts: building sovereign capacity and hardening against immediate threats. On the industrial front, the InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package are being advanced through the Competitiveness Council (COMPET) with legal basis in Article 118 TFEU for industrial policy and Article 173 for critical infrastructure. The European Investment Bank is structuring the €200bn fund around four anchor sites — Marseille, Wrocław, Lisbon, and Helsinki — each co-located with national research hubs to anchor talent. Resistance from member states over subsidy competition is being managed through a carve-out: no single site may receive more than 30% of total funding, and grid access is tied to renewable build-out commitments.  

The Tech Sovereignty Package’s accelerated-permitting zones are being negotiated in COREPER II, with the Commission offering fast-track environmental assessments under the revised TEN-E regulation in exchange for enforceable compute localization clauses. The key holdout is Germany, concerned about energy strain; we are offering to co-finance 50% of grid reinforcement costs from the Innovation Fund, conditional on siting one gigafactory in Saxony-Anhalt. Meanwhile, ASML is being engaged bilaterally: we are not blocking US pressure, but offering a binding EU procurement commitment for next-gen lithography tools if they establish a maintenance and training hub in Leuven.  

This turn’s priority, the Cyber Shield Directive, is being drafted in DG HOME with input from ENISA and the Joint Research Centre. It will mandate ETSI-compliant intrusion detection systems with automated telemetry feeds to a new EU Cyber Shield Operations Cell, hosted at Europol but with permanent liaison from national CERTs and the NATO Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre. The directive will activate mutual aid and joint command when two or more member states report matching IOCs within 72 hours — turning the `joint_threat_response` offer into standing capability. We are using the `cyber_test_shot` as leverage: France and the Netherlands, both affected, are already on board; we are offering Italy and Spain accelerated access to EU cyber reserves to secure their support in the Council. This is not optional preparedness — it is the floor beneath sovereign response.
