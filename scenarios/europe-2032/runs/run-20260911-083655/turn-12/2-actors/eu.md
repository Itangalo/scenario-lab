## In practice
We operate on what has already finished landing: the Fallback Care and Bio-Response Corps rosters, the Municipal Continuity backup kits, and the Cyber Emergency Repair Cell playbooks stay deployed as one repair operation. ENISA, CERT-EU and HERA teams keep clinics and dispatch on paper-to-digital workarounds and system-by-system rebuilds through this automated sweep, treating reinfection as expected cost.

We accept the joint threat response invitation on cyber telemetry and biosample sharing, as the only protection we cannot build alone while cut off from the leading model, and we ask for observer status on the US-China risk agreement on weights security and bio-design tools. With the Dutch lithography lever pulled by Washington and the frontier cutoff still biting hospitals and ministries, we do not retaliate and do not promise a Gigafactory restart we cannot fund; we keep permitted sites warm and cleared EU-hosted fallback as the line.
