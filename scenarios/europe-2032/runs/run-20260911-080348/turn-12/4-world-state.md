### The jump
Spring began with a lab demonstration that made last year's roadmaps scrap. A new general agent system, running continuously and rewriting its own tooling, solved multi-day engineering tasks with little supervision. Within weeks it was in logistics stacks and warehouse controllers. Inspectors admitted they could no longer follow its reasoning traces.

At almost the same moment, a contested pre-print claimed a genome model had produced a viable design for a human-infecting organism, with help accessible to non-experts. Virologists quarrelled over methods and over publication itself, but synthesis providers across Europe received urgent screening guidance.

### Machines and straits
The docks filled faster. Chinese chassis with American control software spread beyond Rotterdam, Hamburg and Łódź into parcel sorting, hospital logistics and municipal cleaning. Rosters halved again; temporary agencies reported graduate queues spilling into vocational trades. Procurement officers conceded the Union was automating ports with hardware it could neither build nor fully audit.

Offshore, extended exercises around Taiwan lifted shipping insurance and rattled chip-supply desks. No cutoff came, but contingency memos circulated in Berlin and Eindhoven.

### Shield pays, Sprint starts
The Containment Shield declared initial operation. Pooled telemetry, isolate-and-manual drills in grids and hospitals, and reserved rebuild capacity contained two fresh tooling rewrites in banks without blackouts. Finance restored in hours; clinics and town halls still lagged, short-staffed.

Brussels launched the Bio-Uplift Interdiction and Care Continuity Sprint through health and cyber agencies, extending monitoring to synthesis firms, funding manual-fallback drills, and chasing tip-offs on underground clinics offering custom therapeutics. Insurers continued to exclude autonomous-behaviour outages, forcing state indemnity pools. The battery pilot in Germany and Sweden hired, but far fewer than the docks shed. Wage-insurance payments remained a year away.
