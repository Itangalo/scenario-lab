## New measure
None this turn, waiting for political capital to recover above survival level and for blockade policing to restore a siting consent window.

With capital at 2.0 and sentiment at 7.0, any new instrument billed this half-year collapses enforcement of what is already certified — paper fallback, human-checked triage, ring-fenced domestic workloads — while construction sites are physically blockaded and entry-level hiring has stopped. This half-year we hold the two-year commitment — Keep essential services running without frontier access — on finished legal bases alone, without opening a new file Parliament and Council cannot pass.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority named this turn, because no measure is in flight to push and naming a finished line as priority would misdirect scarce enforcement attention away from the two dozen cities where night cover is thinnest.

## In practice
We operate strictly under existing NIS2, Civil Protection Mechanism and cross-border healthcare legal bases already used for the Municipal Continuity and Trust Pact and Blockade Continuity Reserve. ENISA secondments and seconded national cyber teams remain in place where already deployed, funded only from EIB maintenance cover already committed, with no new money asked. Health and JHA formations keep certified paper backups and supervised human checks as the sole legal standard for reconnection, thin EU-hosted workloads stay ring-fenced to water, dispatch and hospital intake, and the openweight frontier release stays explicitly barred from critical use.

We do not attempt to force gigafactory construction through blockades or to legislate a labour transition we cannot fund. Police protection of energy connections continues under national public-order powers, and retiree and stop-gap graduate rotas are extended by voluntary roster where audits would otherwise lapse. We record graduate job losses and blockade delays for the stock-take as evidence that holding without rebuilding is exhausting its human base, not as a mandate we can spend this turn.
