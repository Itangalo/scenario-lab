We roster retirees and stop-gap graduates on supervised shifts to prevent audit collapse, and we do not ask Parliament or Council for a new mandate this turn.

The half-year's research breakthrough lands outside us, which is why we cannot turn it into relief. A US frontier lab has demonstrated a verified training-algorithm advance — roughly 4x cheaper long-horizon agent training via synthetic-verifier distillation — a computing result that specialists call a landmark because it moves self-improvement from visible path to working loop, lifting frontier capability within its current rate while leaving safety and EU access unchanged. The address matters: with our gigafactories built but short of chips and staff and the side-deal capacity outside common allocation, the gain accrues to the same providers who cut us off in August, deepening dependence and resentment rather than easing it. We record it as evidence for the stock-take, not as capacity we can use.
