## In practice
This turn, we are closing the EU Open-Weight Monitoring and Containment Framework: the Threat Fusion Cell in Brussels is now fully operational, ingesting anomaly reports from national CERTs, academic networks, and the new bio-detection node in Warsaw, which has already flagged two synthetic DNA orders with structural similarity to known pathogens. The system works — but it is chasing threats already in motion. We cannot scale detection to match the velocity of open diffusion. The release of a model fine-tuned for bio-design, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times before any flag was raised, proves that we are fighting the last war.

We are also advancing the InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package: construction has begun on two sites, one in Finland and one in southern France, with accelerated grid access granted under the new permitting zones. Private capital is responding — €42bn committed so far — but the pace is fragile, and the US election retrenchment has tightened global supply chains. We are no longer counting on seamless transatlantic coordination. That is why we are launching the Mandatory Pre-Release Evaluation measure: under a new delegation from Article 16 of the AI Act and the recently strengthened ENISA mandate, we will require any developer releasing a model above 45.0 capability to submit it to an independent evaluation by a consortium of national labs and vetted academic partners before public release. The evaluation will assess dual-use risk, agent autonomy, and self-improvement potential. Results will be published in redacted form, and conditions — such as watermarking, usage caps, or delayed release — may be imposed. We are negotiating with Hugging Face and GitHub to operationalize compliance at upload, offering fast-track review for research exemptions and open models below the threshold. The German and Dutch research councils have voiced concern, but we are offering full funding for compliant academic projects and a sunset clause if sovereign capacity reaches 60.0. This is not censorship — it is responsibility at the threshold of irreversible risk. And we are doing it now, because the next model may not wait.
