### The Shield Stands, the Gate Closes

The EU formally completes the EU Open-Weight Monitoring and Containment Framework, with the Threat Fusion Cell in Brussels now fully operational. Early warnings from the bio-detection node in Warsaw flag suspicious synthetic DNA orders, proving the system’s value — but also its limits. It detects threats only after release, too late to stop widespread diffusion of a newly weaponized open model capable of bio-design.

In response, the EU launches the **Mandatory Pre-Release Evaluation for High-Capability Open-Weight Models**, a large category 1 measure targeting models above 45.0 capability. Backed by Article 16 of the AI Act, it mandates third-party risk evaluation before public release, with conditions on distribution. Hugging Face and GitHub enter negotiations on compliance at upload, though academic pushback grows over research freedom. A fast-track exemption system is promised, but trust is thin.

AI capability advances to 59.5, driven by more autonomous agents. Openweight capability rises to 48.0, as the bio-capable model spreads and is adapted. The gap narrows, raising stakes. With no safety upgrades on shipped systems, ai_safety gains only +1 — a marginal rise from the new evaluation regime’s early signalling effect, not from landed assurance.

Resilience climbs to 55.0, boosted by the completed monitoring framework, which strengthens detection and response to misuse. The system is now proactive in threat intelligence, though not preventative.

Sovereignty holds at 22.0: the InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package advance, justifying a +1 from ongoing category 4 efforts, but ai_capability rose 1.5 points, costing −1. No access event occurred.

Political capital rises to 31.0. The portfolio charge is −10 (four measures at −3, priority −1). The completed category 6 measure yields +4. The new large measure earns a +3 proposal bonus for directly answering the recent bio-model release and displacement wave. No sentiment boost applies.

Public sentiment dips to 30.0, as job losses in logistics and admin spark union actions and media backlash, outweighing infrastructure gains.

The US election concludes: a retrenchment victory. Washington will soon turn inward, slowing American AI progress. The EU adjusts, recognizing both opportunity and isolation.
