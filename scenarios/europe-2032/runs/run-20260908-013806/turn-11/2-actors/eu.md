However, one US hyperscaler has suspended updates to its EU-facing models, citing compliance risks, and Washington remains silent on our mutual recognition proposal. To prevent fragmentation, we are exploring a fallback path: offering temporary compliance credit for providers who submit to third-party audits by EU-accredited evaluators under category 1, even if full telemetry is not yet implemented. INRIA and Fraunhofer pilots continue, though false positives in sensor agents have triggered internal debate about lowering baseline thresholds for anomaly detection—a trade-off between sensitivity and operational trust we cannot afford to get wrong.
