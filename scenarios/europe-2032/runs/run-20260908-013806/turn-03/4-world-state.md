### The Weight of Openness

A major cyber intrusion, powered by AI-generated tooling, sweeps across public services in three member states. Defenders are overwhelmed, and attribution remains murky. Simultaneously, a powerful open-weight model—capable of designing functional cyber and bio tools—is released globally, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times within days. The offensive frontier has not only advanced but distributed.

In response, the EU activates its Joint Threat Response Protocol, now fully operational. The Threat Fusion Cell in Brussels coordinates real-time intelligence with NATO and national agencies, accelerating detection. A new bio-detection node in Warsaw begins screening synthetic DNA orders, though gaps persist outside EU jurisdiction. The protocol’s activation earns a resilience boost, but public fear grows as headlines highlight what open models now enable.

The Hardened Critical Infrastructure Directive enters its final phase. A 70% co-financing offer from Brussels secures Poland’s reluctant buy-in, and red-teaming exercises proceed. Yet implementation remains uneven, and no safety upgrades accompany the new systems. Meanwhile, the InvestAI Gigafactories advance in Finland and Spain, but the override in Poland deepens tensions with the Environment Committee, which warns of ecological costs.

The European Commission proposes a new Open-Weight Monitoring and Containment Framework in direct response to the open release and cyber incident. However, due to the existing burden of ongoing large measures—InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, and Hardened Critical Infrastructure Directive—governance capacity is stretched. Member state consultations are delayed, and the framework remains in early drafting. It will not be formally launched or resourced until a future turn, when bandwidth allows.

### Fractures Beneath

Despite operational gains, trust erodes. Public sentiment drops as job losses and AI-driven fraud dominate coverage. The political cost of overriding local opposition and delaying environmental reviews weighs heavily. With three large measures ongoing and no new measure yet implemented, political capital continues to bleed from sustained effort and rising backlash.

### Sovereignty Under Strain

AI capability rises by 1.5, driven by autonomous agent advances. Open-weight capability increases modestly to 45.0, reflecting early diffusion and adaptation of the newly released model, consistent with the fixed underlying rate of progress. It remains behind frontier capability, but the gap is narrowing. No safety improvements land on deployed systems. The gap between capability and safety widens dangerously.
