## In practice
We are treating the emergent_bio_pathogen_breakthrough not as a lab curiosity but as a systemic threshold crossed: AI can now design functional, targeted organisms. While this iteration is non-human, the architecture is general, and the tools are diffusing. The joint_threat_response offer is our best chance to harden faster than the threat spreads. This turn, we are accepting the invitation into the joint cyber command and biosurveillance pact, committing €4.2bn from the Civil Protection Mechanism and the Digital Europe Programme to establish permanent EU nodes in both networks. The Joint Research Centre will lead technical integration, with Frontex and HERA providing operational continuity. We are fast-tracking Council adoption of a binding decision under Article 202 TFEU, creating a standing EU Threat Fusion Cell with real-time access to shared telemetry and automated alerting—this is not coordination, it is fusion. Germany and Sweden have signalled support, but Poland hesitates, fearing duplication; we are offering them co-lead status on bio-detection integration in exchange.

At the same time, we are defending the trajectory and cost of our industrial build-out. The InvestAI Gigafactories are now in final site validation, with Finland and Spain securing grid pre-authorisations through the fast-tracking clause invoked last turn. However, Poland’s site faces local opposition over energy draw; we are deploying the Tech Sovereignty Package’s accelerated-permitting clause to override regional objections, citing national security grounds under the new resilience rationale. This draws renewed fire from the Parliament’s Environment Committee, which threatens to block the next tranche of Digital Europe funding. In response, we are advancing the binding PUE review promised last turn to this summer, with ENERDATA tasked to deliver benchmarks by turn 3. We are not slowing down—we are anchoring faster. Sovereign compute is no longer about competitiveness alone; it is about being able to run the defences when the attacks come at AI speed.
