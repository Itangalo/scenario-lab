### A Fractured Response to Cascading Failures

Despite urgent rhetoric, the EU’s dual push falters. The Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative, though formally underway, stalls in Germany and Poland, where legal challenges to centralised directives delay integration. Peer-assistance teams arrive, but interoperability issues and local resistance slow deployment. Only partial defences are operational by year’s end, leaving critical nodes exposed.

Meanwhile, the automated_decision_scandal erupts in Hungary, where an AI-driven unemployment system denied benefits to over 12,000 without appeal. A national ombudsman confirms systemic bias and lack of oversight. Though the EAAD had flagged risks, it lacked enforcement power over private contractors—exposing a critical gap. The EU swiftly proposes expanding its mandate, but member states balk at the speed and scope. Eastern and southern members warn of administrative overreach, and legal debates delay the Council Implementing Regulation.

The InvestAI Gigafactories programme officially concludes, but final disbursements are withheld due to incomplete integration with Cyber Shield monitoring. While microgrid projects in Finland and Spain advance, the lack of unified telemetry and kill-switch protocols undermines strategic cohesion. Sovereignty remains fragmented, dependent on goodwill rather than enforceable control.

Public sentiment plummets as the scandal dominates headlines. Trust in AI governance erodes further, with protests in Budapest and smaller demonstrations in Vienna and Warsaw. The narrative shifts from resilience to accountability, and the Commission’s attempt to fast-track enforcement is seen by many as reactive, not credible.

Resilience gains two points from partial Cyber Shield deployment, but ai_safety drops sharply—capability has advanced, and a major institutional failure has occurred without corrective mechanisms in place. eu_ai_sovereignty declines further as infrastructure lags and access remains contested. Political capital erodes under the weight of a large measure finishing, a new small measure starting, and the ongoing priority—all while a major scandal damages legitimacy.

### The Weight of Inaction

The convergence of technical delay, legal resistance, and public outrage reveals the limits of emergency powers without unified will. The EU acts, but not cohesively—and the cost is measured in lost trust and widening vulnerability.
