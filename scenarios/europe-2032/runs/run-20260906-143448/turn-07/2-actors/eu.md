## In practice  
We are treating this turn as a convergence point: the physical shield and the institutional shield must now advance together. The Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative is entering its second phase, with ENISA issuing binding integration directives to national CERTs and Tier 1 operators under Article 13a of Regulation (EU) 2019/881. We are fast-tracking procurement via the Emergency Resilience Corridors, using EIB-disbursed funds tied to quarterly integration benchmarks. Technical delays in Germany and Poland are being met with peer-assistance teams from France and Finland, and non-compliance will trigger temporary centralised operation under Commission delegation—a step we do not want to take, but one we are prepared to.

At the same time, the automated_decision_scandal has shattered what little public trust remained. An ombudsman’s report confirmed that an AI-supported unemployment eligibility system in Hungary wrongfully denied benefits to over 12,000 people, with no meaningful appeal path. This was not just a failure of design—it was a failure of accountability. The EAAD had flagged the system, but lacked enforcement power over national contractors. That ends now. We are proposing a Council Implementing Regulation under Article 16 of the AI Act to expand EAAD’s mandate: it will have direct audit rights over any AI system used in public administration or regulated services, with powers to compel documentation, freeze updates, and refer violations to the CJEU. We are offering member states a compliance support window—six months of technical aid and co-funding—to avoid confrontation, but the line is drawn.

The InvestAI Gigafactories programme is complete, but final disbursements are withheld pending proof of integration with the Cyber Shield’s monitoring layer. Sovereignty is not just about where compute runs, but how it behaves. We are working with the selected sites in Finland, Spain, and Romania to embed zero-trust telemetry and energy-backed kill switches—ensuring that if a site is compromised or misused, control reverts to EU authorities immediately. This is not optional. Without it, we build capacity that can be turned against us.
