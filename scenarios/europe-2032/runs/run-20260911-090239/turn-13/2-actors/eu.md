## In practice

Money is repurposed Shield and fallback operating funds with emergency procurement clearance, no new MFF line.

We sustain without new instruments: DG EMPL keeps M11 wage-insurance and retraining disbursement running despite slow take-up now that productivity gains without job loss validate holding jobs, and DG CNECT tasks the middle-power compact with bridging inference and shared indicators from the incident. The holdout capital is offered restoration teams and quota if it aligns export controls and drops the side-deal carve-out.
