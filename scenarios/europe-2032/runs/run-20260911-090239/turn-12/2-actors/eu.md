## New measure
**EU Critical Services Continuity Fallback**
It repurposes existing gigafactory shells, shield-certified capacity and pooled middle-power inference to keep cut-off hospitals, ministries and firms running on EU-controlled open-weight models.
This and why now: with the leading model withdrawn at short notice and private build-out collapsing, keeping essential services alive on capacity nobody else can switch off outranks new builds and directly holds the allied-leverage half of our commitment. This is Sovereignty and industrial capacity.

## Priority
EU Critical Services Continuity Fallback — because hospitals and ministries dark on a foreign cutoff is an immediate sovereignty failure that outranks even the safety-net rollout this turn.

## In practice
We act through DG CNECT and ENISA with a COMPET Council mandate: map cut-off dependencies in health and administration, allocate emergency inference quotas in the gigafactory shells and national HPC sites, and ship hardened open-weight replacements vetted from the M10 detection regime. Money is repurposed operating and shield funds, not a new MFF line, with fast-track state-aid clearance for utilities to connect.

On the standing tracks we sustain without new instruments: M11 disbursement continues through EPSCO/DG EMPL despite slow take-up, ENISA/HERA keep patching and bio-sampling exercised, and the middle-power compact is tasked to cover evaluation swaps and temporary inference bridging. The holdout capital is offered priority fallback quota if it aligns export controls and drops the side-deal carve-out.
