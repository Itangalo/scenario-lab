By H2 2031 the EU banked quiet competence — automated patching and detection deployed, hospitals/utilities hardened except small-town 1990s grids left manual, wastewater sampling and detector exchanges continued as models grew opaque — but no new compute funding or cohesion breakthrough; retraining/wage-insurance funded via repurposed ESF+ saw slow take-up amid frozen hiring, and rogue-procurement controls held.

In February leading US model access was cut off for Europe without appeal, hitting hospitals, ministries and firms, while private AI finance collapsed with cancelled data-centre builds and lost hosting. Brussels imposed emergency fallback: DG CNECT/ENISA mapped dependencies, allocated emergency inference in unfinished gigafactory shells and supercomputers, pushed hardened open-weight replacements via repurposed funds. Certified hospitals and large administrations restored degraded service within weeks; small-town grids stayed manual, monitors briefly switched off in utility towns requiring hand re-patching. Holdout took funds and priority quota offer but kept separate US supply, refusing export alignment. Public mood hardened as dependence became visible.
