## Priority

## In practice
We act through EPSCO and DG EMPL, repurposing ESF+ and Recovery residuals rather than asking for a new MFF line the Council cannot pass. National public employment services roll wage-insurance, short retraining vouchers tied to health, municipal utility and cyber-defence needs, and employer flexibility for restructuring, with Commission disbursement conditional on take-up reporting.

On the standing tracks we sustain without new measures: ENISA and HERA keep the patching, swarm-detection and bio-sampling regime exercised from M10 on operating budgets, gigafactory shells and shield certificates continue on old money, and the middle-power incident and evaluation swaps continue as the leverage track. The holdout capital is offered full access to the safety-net fund and pooled hosting if it aligns export controls.
