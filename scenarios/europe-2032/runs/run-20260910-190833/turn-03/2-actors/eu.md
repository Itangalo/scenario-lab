The Evaluation Facility (M4) continues staffing via seconded national labs and asserts AI Act systemic-risk access to the model tooling behind the attack, while M1 gigafactories and M2 sovereignty zones proceed only on hardened grid connections. On embodied AI we task DG GROW and EU-OSHA with a mapping of robotics exposure in logistics and manufacturing to prepare a transition instrument next turn, without opening a second front now.
