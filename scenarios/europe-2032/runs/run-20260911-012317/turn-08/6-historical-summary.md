By H1 2030 US providers cut Europe off leading models overnight; hospitals/ministries fell back to older open models and paper. Venture collapsed, data-centre expansions cancelled, Gulf/Asian hosting deals thinned/repriced; EU grid-ready sites remained permitted but empty and unfunded. A new open model months behind frontier downloaded hundreds of thousands of times, unrecallable. Contested biosecurity paper claimed genome model enabled viable human-infecting organism design, read by officials as categorically new. Brussels responded with detection: pooled sequencing surveillance, screening lab orders for machine-designed sequences, certified offline triage; guidance adopted fast, funding reprogrammed not new. Joint procurement held on short extensions with existing guarantees; Washington gave only generalities to clarity request. Side-deal state stayed out. Continuity held degraded amid anger at foreign control and paper capacity.
