## Priority
Sovereign Fallback Supply Coalition – because with political capital at 10 and no measure in flight, securing an externally anchored but jointly held supply line outranks any new domestic build the Union cannot fund or permit this turn.

## In practice
We mandate DG TRADE and DG CNECT under the Anti-Coercion Instrument preparatory track and the EU Containment and Continuity Switch playbooks to pool EIB guarantees and member-state essential-service demand into one negotiable volume for non-US compute, hosted open-weight inference, and spare parts. The Foreign Affairs Council (Trade) carries the joint offer to Japan, Korea, Taiwan and Gulf cloud holders; ENISA certifies the EU-hosted endpoints so certified hospitals and utilities stay on degraded-not-stopped procedures.

We do not reopen the Gigafactories disbursement or a parallel compute build: M1/M2 zones stay legally anchored on the backup grid-ready site with no new money, and interior ministers extend the emergency escalation thresholds by implementing act to cover the new agentic capability. The defecting member state is offered re-entry on common procurement terms, with refusal recorded, while Washington is asked only for written clarification of licence volumes — no new dependence is planned around an answer.
