### Priority

### In practice  
We are treating the ransomware sweep not as a one-off but as a stress test that failed: AI-generated attack tooling, targeting public services at scale, has exposed a dangerous gap between our offensive awareness and defensive readiness. The cyber_major_incident — a coordinated compromise of municipal IT systems via a poisoned dependency in widely used open-source administrative software — disrupted emergency response coordination in three member states. The blast radius remains uncertain, but early analysis confirms autonomous propagation and evasion of signature-based detection. This is not the future; this is now. And with the emergent_cyber_insurance_crisis, the market is telling us what our metrics already whisper: resilience is breaking.

So we act. This turn, we launch the Emergency Resilience Mobilisation Initiative under Article 4(2) TEU (security of essential functions), invoking the solidarity clause. We are fast-tracking a Council Regulation that mandates baseline cyber hardening for all critical public services — including automatic patching, air-gapped backups, and AI-powered anomaly detection — funded through an emergency €18.7bn allocation from the Recovery and Resilience Facility and the Digital Europe Programme. We are activating the EU Civil Protection Mechanism to coordinate cross-border response drills this summer and establishing a mutual aid protocol: any member state under sustained AI-powered attack can request immediate technical and personnel support from others, backed by binding commitments. ENISA will issue emergency guidelines within 60 days, and we are conditioning future cohesion funding on compliance.

We know the resistance: some capitals see this as centralisation; others lack the capacity to comply. So we pair compulsion with support — a rapid-response technical corps drawn from the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre, and a grace period for implementation with front-loaded grants. This is not about perfection — it is about continuity. If public services cannot run through an incident, sovereignty is a legal fiction. We are not waiting for the next attack to prove how fast the water is rising. We are building levees — now.
