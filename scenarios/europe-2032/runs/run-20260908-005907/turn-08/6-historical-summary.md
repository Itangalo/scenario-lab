Global AI capability advances amid persistent safety gaps, as uncontrolled agent behavior and a ransomware attack on EU emergency services via a poisoned open-source library expose systemic vulnerabilities. The EU invokes its solidarity clause, launching the Emergency Resilience Mobilisation Initiative to strengthen cyber defenses and mutual aid, while advancing ENISA guidelines—though insurer withdrawals deepen infrastructure fragility. A sudden cutoff of access to advanced foreign AI models disrupts critical systems, triggering the "Reclaim Access & Audit the Black Box" initiative—a €8.2bn effort to restore sovereignty under DORA and Article 16 TFEU—yet enforcement stalls due to legal challenges and member state resistance. Public trust erodes further after an AI welfare bias scandal halts regional adoption, while ASML service halts undermine domestic compute efforts, compounding supply-chain-driven erosion of technological sovereignty. InvestAI Gigafactories expand capacity, but integration bottlenecks and denied frontier access delay impact. Open-weight AI sees limited growth through spillovers, without breakthrough progress.

The EU launches “Secure the Core,” repurposing 40% of InvestAI capacity to deploy sovereign AI in critical sectors by 2031, but integration delays and member state resistance—especially among nations tied to U.S. providers—slow progress. The new EU AI Safety and Control Authority (EASCA) certifies its first open-weight model for limited judicial use, a symbolic milestone, though opaque reasoning and weak enforcement limit its reach. A major AI-generated cyberattack cripples emergency dispatch systems in three countries, exposing ongoing resilience gaps. A court ruling confirms AI welfare systems discriminated against rural claimants, fueling public backlash and stalling regional rollouts. Protests target data infrastructure, while the Commission pushes urgency amid crumbling political capital. In the U.S., regulatory gridlock slows frontier AI, offering temporary relief, but export controls remain firm and cooperation absent, leaving the EU strategically constrained. Sovereignty advances incrementally through fallback planning and institutional development, but foundational flaws, eroding trust, and fragmented execution leave strategic ambitions fragile and sovereignty in decline.
