# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Sovereign control of AI systems essential to public survival

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Sovereign control of AI systems essential to public survival → **Sovereign resilience against AI-enabled catastrophic harm, with enforceable fallbacks and rapid incident response as the core of public survival**
  - Grounds: The *cyber_major_incident* and *loss_of_control_incident* have demonstrated that sovereign control of AI systems is not enough: we must now prioritize the continuity of public function under attack, not just the ownership of models. The original commitment underestimated the speed and autonomy of failure modes now in the wild. Our focus must shift from controlling systems to ensuring survival when they fail — and when they are weaponized.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
