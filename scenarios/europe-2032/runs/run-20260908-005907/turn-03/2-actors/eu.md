**EU Offensive AI Monitoring and Containment Initiative** — because the emergent_cybercriminal_federation shows that offensive AI tooling is now in circulation and self-replicating across threat actors, and without a unified detection backbone, we are fighting blind; this measure builds the first EU-wide capacity to track and disrupt AI-powered attacks at scale.

## In practice  
We are treating the emergent_cybercriminal_federation not as a spike in criminality but as a structural shift: the offensive capability frontier is now open, distributed, and self-propagating. The Emergency Resilience Surge ends this turn, and while it hardened the most exposed nodes, it did not address the evolving threat stream. We are therefore launching the EU Offensive AI Monitoring and Containment Initiative to close that gap. ENISA, in coordination with Europol and national CERTs, will stand up a Threat Intelligence Fusion Cell by Q1 2028, fed by automated telemetry from critical operators and open-source software repositories. This cell will use red-teamed AI models to simulate attack patterns, fingerprint known AI-generated malware, and issue real-time countermeasures. We are also mandating that all NIS2-covered entities feed anonymised intrusion data into this system, under strict data governance, to enable pattern detection across borders.

We are funding this through a reallocation of €3.2bn from the unused portion of the Emergency Resilience Surge’s contingency envelope, avoiding new fiscal demands during the CJEU review of our disbursement powers. The legal basis is Article 114 TFEU, leveraging the internal market rationale of cross-border cyber risk. We are offering Germany a seat on the oversight board and Hungary fast-tracked recovery funds for rural digitalisation in exchange for de-escalation. This is not just defence — it is early warning. We cannot stop every actor, but we can stop being surprised. And in a world where openweight_capability is at 43.0 and rising, not being surprised is the only advantage we can count on.
