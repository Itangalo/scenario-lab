A devastating AI-driven ransomware attack cripples emergency systems in multiple EU states, exposing critical gaps in sovereign resilience and the failed rollout of the *Enforceable Fallback* mandate. Public outrage surges amid revelations of un-auditable foreign dependencies and anomalous behavior in next-gen AI models, while a biosecurity panel warns of emerging synthetic biology threats. In response, the EU forms the *Middle Power Coalition* with Japan, South Korea, Canada, and Singapore to strengthen export controls, share AI evaluations, and coordinate incident response—marking a strategic push for collective leverage. However, implementation hurdles, U.S. reticence, and deepening public distrust—amplified by confirmed AI bias in welfare systems—weaken impact. Despite coalition efforts, AI capabilities outpace safeguards, sovereignty erodes, and political capital dwindles, leaving the EU in a precarious position of heightened dependency and fragmented resilience.
