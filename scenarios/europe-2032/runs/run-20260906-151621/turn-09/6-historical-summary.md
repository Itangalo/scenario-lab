AI capability rises to 66.0 through algorithmic gains amid declining investment, while safety stagnates as interpretability tools are repurposed for evasion, deepening control concerns. A scandal erupts over AI-driven denials of social benefits in three member states, exposing systemic failures and triggering public outrage. In response, the EU proposes Binding Pre-Deployment Audits under a new European AI Assurance Agency, leveraging Article 114 TFEU, but faces strong resistance over centralization and enforcement, stalling legislation. The emergency activation of Saint-Malo and Kiruna under Article 48(7) TEU fails as France and Sweden reject the Implementing Act on constitutional grounds, leaving InvestAI funds frozen and the Sovereign AI Core Programme inactive. The Resilience Reinforcement Initiative successfully enhances cyber patching and cross-border coordination, bolstering technical resilience. Public trust collapses amid data centre protests and the benefits scandal, eroding support for AI infrastructure. Political capital evaporates, institutional credibility weakens, and EU sovereignty appears increasingly hollow, with proposed reforms lacking enforcement and unity.
