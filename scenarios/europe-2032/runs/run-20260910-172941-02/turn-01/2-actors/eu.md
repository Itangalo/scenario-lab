**EU Critical Infrastructure Shield**
Hardens electricity grids, ports, water and hospitals against AI-enabled intrusion with mandatory detection, backup and cross-border exercises.

## In practice
We are pushing ENISA and the Energy and Transport Councils to issue binding cyber-baseline standards under NIS2, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility money, with joint procurement of AI-assisted anomaly detection for TSOs. France, Germany and Poland resist cost-sharing; we offer co-financing and Civil Protection mutual-aid guarantees to hold them.

Alongside this we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package moving: DG COMP clearing state-aid for first sites, accelerated permitting zones negotiated with host regions, and ASML supply-chain coordination to resist US export pressure. We signal that public-sector fallback models running on EU compute will replace cut-off US dependencies in hospitals and ministries.
