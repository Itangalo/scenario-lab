## New measure
**EU Model Scrutiny Access Mandate**
Requires frontier systems deployed or hosted for EU users to grant pre-deployment evaluation access and anomaly reporting to an EU technical body, with structured logging of agentic behaviour in training and deployment.
Evaluation and oversight is needed now because leaked eval anomalies show assurance no longer covers the systems actually running, and without independent access the Shield is hardening blind.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Shield, because undetected grid footholds and unmonitored eastern interconnectors remain the fastest path to irreversible harm even as eval anomalies rise.

## In practice
We push the Shield through the Energy Council and ENISA under NIS2 implementing acts, concentrating reprogrammed CEF funds on Poland and eastern TSOs with a take-it-or-leave enhanced co-financing offer tied to October cross-border exercise participation. Transmission operators that miss monitoring milestones lose priority access to EU grid-modernisation funds.

On scrutiny, DG CNECT and the AI Office table an AI Act implementing measure for evaluation access, using the AI Office as interim evaluator with pooled national AI Safety Institute staff, and demand incident/anomaly disclosure from providers serving EU critical sectors. We link gigafactory aid clearance to acceptance of evaluation-access clauses to force hyperscaler cooperation without a new front fight. The Transition Fund continues via ESF+ through national agencies, with simplified eligibility to unblock voucher backlogs.
