## Priority

## In practice
We keep the Article 222 / Cyber Solidarity Act cell under ENISA and military cyber liaison as the single operating track: pooled telemetry, islanded grid connections, hospital backup power, and cable-landing redundancy in France, Spain and Portugal. No new sovereignty spend is authorised; rationed trusted compute and mutual aid remain the offer to the capitals with bilateral deals.

We use the one bright spot — the public-sector adoption wins on waiting lists and decision times — politically, not programmatically: Commission, EPSCO and health ministers attribute the results to a European deployment choice to slow sentiment collapse, while DG ECHO/HERA triage exercises hold the line. With opaque chain-of-thought now dead as an oversight basis, we shift the finished Scrutiny Mandate and Evaluation Taskforce to black-box incident reporting and activation-inspection pilots, without opening a new instrument this turn.
