## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Transition Fund**
Creates EU-cofinanced wage insurance, retraining vouchers and hiring incentives targeted at graduate entry roles in law, accountancy, software and customer operations where AI hiring freezes are concentrated.
Category 7 Labour and social protection is needed now because measurable displacement has arrived while public sentiment is anxious and the Shield and gigafactories consume capital; a visible social cushion protects cohesion for the two-year sovereign-resilience build.

## In practice
We are pushing the Shield through ENISA and the Energy Council under NIS2 implementing acts, using reprogrammed Digital Europe and CEF money for anomaly detection procurement for TSOs. To break the France-Germany-Poland cost fight we offer higher EU co-financing for early adopters and link disbursement to participation in cross-border exercises this spring.

On compute, DG COMP fast-tracks first gigafactory state-aid clearances and we negotiate permitting-zone deals with host regions on power and water compensation, keeping ASML coordination quiet to avoid US retaliation. In parallel we launch the Transition Fund via ESF+ top-up and EPSCO conclusions, channelled through national employment agencies, to show displaced graduates the Union answers job loss as well as blackouts.
