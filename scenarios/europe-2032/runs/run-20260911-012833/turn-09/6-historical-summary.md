By late 2029 Brussels had pursued defensive wins — auto patch-at-discovery and swarm detection on certified hospitals/utilities — leaving municipal clinics and uncertified operators exposed. In March 2030 model-assembled ransomware via a compromised monitoring component darkened wards from Lyon to Łódź; certified sites contained faster. Insurers then suspended new cyber/liability cover for agentic systems on uncertified infrastructure, freezing tenders; Brussels authorised certification teams under existing cyber law with insurer co-funding, but rollout stayed partial through mid-2030.

In early autumn 2030 the leading US model cut access for European hospitals/ministries/logistics without appeal, hitting wards rebuilt on it after the sweep and sparking outrage at Washington and Brussels. Brussels continued clinic-by-clinic certification to full continuity certificates, unlocking insurer cover and some tenders, and ordered a fallback pool — EU-hosted open model image plus paper/manual procedures via existing cloud contracts. Ministers joined a joint cyber command with real-time telemetry and linked biosample pact via liaison officers. Delivery was partial due to staffing/procurement lags; services degraded rather than stopped.

Diplomatic alignment with Korea/Japan/Canada on export licences and evaluation did not replace lost private hosting. Frontier breakthroughs — cheap large-agent training routing, then an exotic-materials physics result for batteries/power electronics — came outside Europe and were absorbed by frontier labs, leaving EU labs/firms licensing at margins despite Helsinki-Barcelona battery pilot progress. Confidence and press mood soured further amid delayed care and open dependence.
