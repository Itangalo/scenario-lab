## New measure
**EU Automation and Robotics Transition Shield**
Provides wage insurance, rapid retraining and redeployment support for workers displaced by AI and newly deployed robotics, funded by a levy on large-scale automation deployments and tied to employer retraining obligations.
Why this and why now: with entry-level cuts hardening and Chinese robotics hitting the industrial base from outside while public sentiment sits at 22 and capital at 14, a visible jobs shield is the only way to hold autonomy and prevent backlash from blocking everything else; the safety breakthrough lets us pair it with certified-safe European systems for retraining and placement.

## Priority
EU Automation and Robotics Transition Shield, because without stemming job-loss anger and robot-driven displacement no autonomy or safety regime survives politically.

## In practice
We run this under the European Social Fund+ and SURE-type instrument via EPSCO Council, with DG EMPL and national employment agencies delivering wage insurance and 6-month retraining vouchers for law, accountancy, support and logistics workers, co-financed by member states and a transition contribution from large automating employers. Unions get a seat on the allocation board; the large member state with the side-deal is offered full access if it rejoins joint licensing discipline.

We simultaneously fast-adopt the interpretability breakthrough through the EU Evaluation Institute and ENISA into clinical, grid and hospital-agent checklists to restore deployments cut after dark reasoning, and task HERA continuity cells to keep pooled bio/agent shielding on standby. DG TRADE holds the Seoul/Tokyo maintenance bargain and rations gigafactory power to Paris/Warsaw halls, while competition screening prepares anti-dumping and standards responses to Chinese robots without promising a fab we cannot fund.
