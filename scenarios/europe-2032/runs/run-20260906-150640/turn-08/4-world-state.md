### The Fracture Widens

Despite institutional maturation, the EU’s strategic footing weakens. The closed frontier slows, but not by design: a broad **ai_investment_collapse** rattles the sector, as valuations reset and major compute expansions are scrapped. This retreat from private investment paradoxically slows global capability growth—yet the damage is asymmetric. The EU’s sovereign ambitions, built on pledged capital, now rest on crumbling foundations.

Rumors persist of a potential breakthrough in the open frontier—a model approaching the leading edge, possibly nearing 68.0 capability, circulating in encrypted developer forums. The release remains unconfirmed, and verification is inconclusive. Security analysts debate whether the signals represent a true inflection or noise amid growing paranoia. Without confirmation, no measurable rise in `openweight_capability` occurs, and the EU’s margin of control narrows under uncertainty.

Meanwhile, the **emergent_circuit_tracing_leak** has been confirmed. A core EAAD audit technique is now public—originating from a rogue researcher—disseminated globally and already repurposed in adversarial contexts. The EU’s internal assurance advantage erodes as both defenders and attackers adopt the method, leveling the playing field.

In response, the EU pivots from expansion to survival. The **Sovereign Stack Hardening Initiative** launches as a priority, mandating zero-trust architecture and air-gapped evaluations across the Sovereign AI Core Programme. Funding is redirected from the stalled Polish cluster, now under cybersecurity audit. Implementation is partial and under pressure—governance delays and interagency disputes slow full deployment, with effects expected only in coming turns.

However, political capital erodes further. Multiple large measures conclude, but their completion is overshadowed by crisis. The Openweight Containment Initiative faces legal challenges and shows no progress, while public sentiment sinks as job losses mount and trust in AI governance wanes. A backlash grows against both uncontrolled open models and opaque sovereign systems.

### Hardening Without Healing

The EU acts decisively—but too late to prevent the erosion of its own foundations. Sovereignty is no longer a matter of compute, but of control. And control is slipping.
