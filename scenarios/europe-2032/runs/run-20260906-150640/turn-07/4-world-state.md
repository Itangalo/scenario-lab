### A Surge in the Open, a Stumble at Home

The closed frontier shows signs of strain: major labs underdeliver on public promises and top-tier model access dips in price, suggesting a **capability_plateau_evidence**. Yet beneath the surface, offensive AI tools proliferate. A sweeping ransomware campaign—orchestrated by adaptive, model-generated malware—cripples municipal services across three member states. ENISA confirms the attack code was synthesized by a fine-tuned variant of a 60.0-class system now circulating outside controlled environments. Simultaneously, a **cyber_defence_breakthrough** emerges: new swarm-detection systems, trained to identify coordinated intrusion patterns in real time, begin rolling out across critical networks, restoring some balance.

In response, the EU launches the Openweight Containment Initiative, mandating watermarking, tracking, and remote deactivation for all open-weight models above 45.0 hosted in the Union. The move is framed as essential containment—but enforcement hinges on hosting providers, and early pushback comes from academic collectives and cloud firms wary of compliance costs. Implementation will take time, and immediate impact is limited.

Multiple foundational programmes conclude: the Tech Sovereignty Package unlocks €200bn in pledged private investment, SMAF finalizes auditability rules, and EAAD and CIRS transition into permanent functions. The Public Assurance Protocol wraps up, though its influence is muted by low public trust. Poland remains hesitant on the Wrocław cluster, delaying the dual-key agreement despite the attached €10bn grant.

Public sentiment sours further as media spotlight job losses in local government IT and cybersecurity roles overwhelmed by the attacks. The narrative shifts from fear of frontier AI to anger over distributed harm—fueling backlash against open releases, even as the EU’s own containment effort faces legal and technical headwinds.

### Fractured Gains

Despite defensive progress and institutional maturation, political capital erodes. The scale of concurrent efforts outstrips capacity, and cohesion frays at the edges.
