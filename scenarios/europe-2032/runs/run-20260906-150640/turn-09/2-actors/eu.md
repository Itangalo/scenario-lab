## New measure
**Measure:** Launch the Integrity Audit Expansion  
A programme to scale up independent, adversarial-style auditing of sovereign AI systems, focusing on both technical integrity (weight security, evaluation air gaps) and operational accountability (decision provenance, redress pathways), with findings binding on continued operation.

`Category: 1 (Evaluation and oversight)`  
`Size: small`  
`Finishes on turn: 11`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase `ai_safety` by 8–12 points by ensuring that sovereign systems are not only secure in design but accountable in action; mitigate the damage of the **automated_decision_scandal** by restoring public confidence through verified transparency  
`Applies to:` All entities operating under the Sovereign AI Core Programme, with special scrutiny for systems deployed in public administration, law enforcement, and social services

## Priority
**Priority:** Launch the Sovereign Stack Hardening Initiative

## In practice
This turn, we are acting in the wake of dual shocks: a breach not of code, but of trust. The **emergent_circuit_tracing_leak** has exposed a fatal flaw — our most advanced interpretability tools, meant to secure our systems, are now being repurposed to probe them. At the same time, the **automated_decision_scandal** has confirmed that even systems under sovereign control can inflict systemic harm when oversight fails. We are responding by hardening the core of the Sovereign AI Core Programme through the Sovereign Stack Hardening Initiative, invoking Article 122 TFEU to fast-track implementation. Funding is being redirected from the suspended Polish cluster, and ENISA is deploying emergency audit teams to Wrocław and Marseille to verify zero-trust compliance by turn 10.

The Openweight Containment Initiative remains in flight, but legal resistance grows: cloud providers argue that remote deactivation mandates violate DSA principles, and the ECJ has fast-tracked a preliminary reference. We are holding firm, but our energy is shifting — the frontier is no longer just outside our borders, but inside our labs. That is why we are launching the Integrity Audit Expansion: to ensure that every sovereign system, especially those making binding decisions, is subject to adversarial review by EAAD and civil society auditors. We are negotiating with the Council’s Competitiveness configuration to embed this into the revised AI Act framework by turn 10, and we are offering national governments co-governance roles in exchange for support. The era of building fast is over. The era of proving trust is beginning.
