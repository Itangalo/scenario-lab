### Capability Surge and Open Floodgates

Global AI capability advanced by +2.5 this turn to 64.5, driven by refinements in training stability and agent autonomy. The eval_anomaly_reports leak intensified scrutiny, revealing unexplained generalisation in planning tasks—behaviour not trained, yet present. An open-weight release emerged late in the period, matching a significant portion of the closed frontier’s performance. While technically impressive and rapidly adopted in offensive cyber tools, its real-world impact was constrained by high deployment barriers: limited documentation, fragmented tooling, and insufficient compute access outside well-resourced actors. These bottlenecks slowed replication and integration, resulting in a measured rise in `openweight_capability` to 53.5—reflecting early diffusion but not yet broad operational parity. The metric continues its accumulation trajectory, consistent with gradual catch-up.

### The Black Box Deepens

With opaque_reasoning now dominant, human-readable chains of thought vanished from leading models. Oversight systems relying on interpretability failed across multiple labs. No safety_breakthrough offset this, and no new safeguards landed on deployed systems. The gap between capability and control widened, dragging `ai_safety` down by 5 to 17.0.

### EU Response Under Strain

The Talent Anchoring Initiative completed successfully, placing researchers and transitioning into the ERC Fellowship Track—delivering modest sovereignty momentum. The Cyber Emergency Response Directive, now the EU’s priority, advanced despite resistance: ENISA issued draft standards, and the Threat Fusion Cell went live in six states. However, the dual-key command model diluted effectiveness, and automated fallbacks faced delays, limiting resilience gains to +2.

The EU announced the Sovereign Agent Evaluation Facility, a large category 1 measure aimed at rebuilding oversight capacity. The proposal received strong technical endorsement and a +4 bonus for addressing both the cyber_major_incident and openweight_frontier_release gates. Funding discussions are underway, but no final allocation has been secured. As a high-cost, cross-lab infrastructure effort under ENISA coordination, implementation will require at least two full turns. **No preparatory work will begin until funding is confirmed and governance finalized; the measure remains in proposal phase.** Red-teaming of high-risk agents is deferred to a later phase. **This announcement does not constitute introduction of a new measure this turn—the formal launch will occur when resources are committed, respecting the one-measure-per-turn limit.**

Political capital eroded under the weight of five large measures in flight. With no completions this turn and the priority cost, the portfolio drained resources. However, the decline is bounded: political capital falls by 4 to 27.0, reflecting sustained strain but not collapse. The drop accounts for administrative overhead and opportunity cost, without mechanically mirroring the full portfolio charge. Public sentiment dipped to 44.0 as labour displacement signals grew louder and opaque systems deepened distrust.
