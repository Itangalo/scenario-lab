## New measure
**EU Sovereign Agent Evaluation Facility**  
Establish a permanent, public-sector capability to evaluate autonomous AI agents before deployment, using red-team environments and adversarial testing to assess goal persistence, evasion tactics, and resistance to shutdown.

`Category: 1 (Evaluation and oversight)`

## Priority
**Cyber Emergency Response Directive** — because the emergent ransomware campaign exploiting openweight models has already exposed fatal gaps in cross-border command and automated response, and with opaque_reasoning now undermining all human-readable oversight, we must harden operational resilience now, even as we build longer-term evaluation capacity.

## In practice
We are treating the Cyber Emergency Response Directive as our immediate line of defence. ENISA has issued the first draft of binding technical standards for automated fallbacks in emergency services, energy, and transport, to be adopted via comitology by Q1 2029. The Threat Fusion Cell is now operational in pilot mode, ingesting telemetry from national CERTs in six member states, with full integration mandated by turn 6. To overcome resistance on cross-border command, we are offering a dual-key activation protocol—national authorities retain veto power, but only if they provide countermeasures within four hours or cede control to a rotating EU incident lead. The €15bn Emergency Cyber Hardening Facility is being disbursed through InvestEU, with pre-approval for operators who adopt the new ENISA standards by Q3.

At the same time, we are advancing the Secure Critical Research Infrastructure: Horizon Europe now requires all AI-related grants to include compliance with weight-control and audit clauses, and EuroHPC has confirmed that access to LUMI-class machines will be restricted to institutions that have signed the framework by turn 6. The Talent Anchoring Initiative has successfully placed 147 researchers in EU institutions, and with its completion this turn, we are transitioning its functions into the European Research Council’s new AI Fellowship Track. The InvestAI Gigafactories remain behind schedule in Romania due to local opposition, but the European Infrastructure Mechanism has now been invoked, and construction will begin under Article 346 safeguards. The Tech Sovereignty Package is in final trilogue, and we are holding firm on the 60% EU-controlled compute threshold—this is not negotiable if we are to avoid permanent dependence.

The new EU Sovereign Agent Evaluation Facility will be launched under Horizon Europe’s emergency innovation window, with initial funding of €3.8bn over two years. It will be hosted by a consortium of national labs—CEA, Fraunhofer, and TNO—under ENISA oversight, and will begin operations in turn 6 with red-team testing of three high-risk agent systems already in deployment. This is not a substitute for regulation, but a foundation: we cannot govern what we cannot test, and we cannot trust what we cannot break.
