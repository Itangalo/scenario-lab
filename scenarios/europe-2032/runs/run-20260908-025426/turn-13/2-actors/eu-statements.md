# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but when they conflict, we choose survivability first.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign assurance infrastructure to govern AI behaviour, not just access it
- `contain_the_frontier` (commitment): We will not accept a world where the most capable AI systems are outside sovereign control, even if that means delaying access to their benefits. From now on, our default stance is containment: capability above 75.0 must be matched by verified control, or it is not permitted in EU critical functions — regardless of origin.
