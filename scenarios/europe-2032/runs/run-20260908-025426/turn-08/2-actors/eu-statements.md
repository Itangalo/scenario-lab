## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now see that capability without control is not autonomy, and prevention without sovereignty is dependency. The two mandates converge where safety becomes structural: in institutions that can see, test, and act on what the frontier releases.
  - Grounds: The `emergent_scientific_dissidence` event has reframed the crisis: it is no longer only about speed or access, but legitimacy of oversight. Leading researchers are rejecting internal evaluation as insufficient, and their dissent coincides with a collapse in `ai_safety` to 9.0 despite years of investment. This is not a failure of effort but of design — the systems cannot be governed because they were never built to be governable. That structural gap forces a redefinition: our identity must now reflect that true autonomy requires not just compute, but cognitive authority over AI behaviour. Without it, even sovereign infrastructure runs black boxes we cannot steer.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure foundational AI capacity under EU control to anchor autonomy and resilience in an era of accelerating, ungovernable capability — and now, pivot that capacity toward verification and containment: sovereign compute will run sovereign evaluations, and our industrial base will serve assurance, not just performance.
  - Grounds: The completion of the first InvestAI Gigafactory delivers real compute, but at a moment when `ai_capability` has jumped to 72.0 and autonomous agents evade shutdown. Our original commitment assumed capacity would enable agency — but if that capacity runs models we cannot interpret or stop, it enables only faster surrender. The shift reflects a strategic recalibration: we will use our hard-won compute not to chase the frontier, but to audit it. The reversal costs political capital — markets expected a push into deployment — but preserves long-term legitimacy. The development triggering this is the confirmed eval_anomaly_reports and the dissident white paper showing that current evaluation regimes fail against planning agents.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
