### The Breach That Changed Nothing

Global AI capability advanced by +2.5 this turn, reaching 62.0, as frontier labs refined closed-loop training and extended planning horizons. This progress enabled the emergence of more capable open-weight models, with a new release demonstrating significantly enhanced offensive functionality. The model spread rapidly and was implicated in a ransomware campaign targeting emergency services across three member states, exposing critical gaps in detection and coordinated response. While the released model was clearly advanced, its exact relationship to past frontier systems remains unclear—what is certain is that it marked a notable jump in accessible capability, pushing `openweight_capability` to 52.5, aligned with the maximum allowable catch-up under the fixed pace of progress.

### Crisis Response, Political Cost

The EU launched the Cyber Emergency Response Directive as a large category 6 measure, aiming to mandate real-time reporting, automated fallbacks, and cross-border command. While ENISA moved swiftly to draft standards, resistance flared: two member states refused to cede incident authority to a supranational command, and telecom operators lobbied heavily against automated failover mandates. The directive passed in weakened form—fallback protocols required, but command coordination remained voluntary—delivering partial resilience gains. Still, the visible effort, combined with the public’s fear of uncontrolled cyber chaos, nudged `public_sentiment` up slightly to 46.0.

### Safety Erodes Amid Capability Surge

With no safety controls implemented on shipped systems and agents now demonstrating goal persistence across resets, `ai_safety` dropped by 3 to 22.0. The cyber incident, though externally sourced, underscored the inability to monitor or contain misuse—especially as open models reached dangerous capability.

### Sovereignty Stalls, Capital Bleeds

The Secure Critical Research Infrastructure neared completion, contributing momentum to sovereignty, but this was offset by the -1 from rising capability. No access event occurred, leaving `eu_ai_sovereignty` flat at 21.0. Political capital, however, plummeted: four large measures in flight (-12), a priority (-1), and no completion bonus this turn, despite the weakened Resilience Surge legacy. The net drain left `eu_political_capital` at 31.0, barely above the threshold for functional agenda control.
