### The Surge and the Strain

Global AI capability advances to 90.0, driven by recursive self-improvement loops now operating across multiple frontier labs. The jump is contained — sub-linear due to measurement saturation — but the trajectory points unambiguously toward terminal capability. No corresponding leap in understanding or control follows. `ai_safety` remains frozen at 7.0, as the tools to audit emergent reasoning still lag behind deployment.

### Regulatory Foundations, Fragile Gains

The **Assurance Integration Directive** completes its rollout: verified interruptibility and goal transparency are now mandatory for all high-capability AI in EU critical sectors. Two foreign providers exit the market rather than comply, creating disruptions in radiology and customs. EU-backed alternatives scale unevenly, feeding public frustration.

The **Critical Capability Moratorium Framework** becomes law, establishing a legal basis to halt deployment of systems above 75.0 without proof of control. However, enforcement is delayed — labs contest the verification standards, and no domestic EU system yet matches the capability of the models being restricted.

### Response in Motion, Capital Exhausted

The EU launches the **AI Incident Coordination Cell**, integrating real-time telemetry from critical infrastructure into ENISA’s operations. Quarterly cross-border drills are scheduled, and anomaly detection models go live. The move strengthens preparedness, but full operational capacity is months away.

Industrial action spreads as healthcare and transport workers demand enforceable limits on autonomous systems. The new Cell is framed as proof of responsiveness, but unions dismiss it as reactive.

`resilience` rises to 64.0, reflecting improved coordination. `public_sentiment` climbs slightly to 50.0 — the medical breakthrough helps, though access limitations mute its impact. Political capital dips to 6.0 under the weight of six ongoing measures. The new priority adds cost with no immediate return.

Sovereignty falls to 18.0: `ai_capability` rose by 3.0 (−1), and `eu_frontier_access_denied` triggers a −3 event term. The US denies EU institutions access to the latest model, citing national security. No domestic substitute exists. The Union scrambles to reroute critical research workflows.
