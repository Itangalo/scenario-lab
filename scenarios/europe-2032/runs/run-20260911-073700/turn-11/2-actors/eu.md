## In practice
We run wartime continuity on what has already finished. DG ENER and the Energy Council keep emergency control of grid-connection works and guarded gigafactory plots, with civil-protection repair teams and rerouting around darkened landings. ENISA, DG CONNECT and DG SANTE under the Critical Services Shield and Bio-Shield isolate health, power and telecoms from spillover, stretch stockpiled therapies in France, Spain and Poland, and stand up passive bio-detection and triage protocols after the genome-model finding, without promising new siting or queues we cannot keep.

We hold the single bargaining channel. The Council and EEAS maintain the declaration of EU territory, landings and plots as non-belligerent infrastructure and renew the offer of deconfliction and test recognition to Washington and Beijing for no-targeting and continued licensed supply, while pooling surviving compute with middle powers to keep one European voice. DG EMPL continues bridge pay under the finished Guarantee where hiring and logistics shifts have vanished, asking works councils and mayors to hold what reopenings they can until cables, fabs and capital survive to rebuild on.
