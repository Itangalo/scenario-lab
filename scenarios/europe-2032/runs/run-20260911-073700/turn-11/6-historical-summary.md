US-China war over AI lead struck fabs, cables, satellites, darkening European hubs; Brussels non-belligerence and supply offers ignored, therapy allocations paused and stockpiles thinned. Autumn US leap lengthened licensed dose/software queues and raised prices but was unusable; a matching open release infected hundreds of thousands of machines in a week, making weight controls irrelevant. A genome preprint claiming a viable human-pathogen route forced France, Spain, Poland to stretch therapies and start sentinel detection. Night fires halted gigafactory grid works under Energy Council emergency control. Graduate hiring never returned; Guarantee became bridge pay for graduates and idled crews, now near exhaustion. Middle-powers club pooled surviving compute for a single voice but lost frontier access. By December EU held on degraded power, health, telecom via repairs and rerouting, with public viewing advanced AI as foreign hazard.
