## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) — because breaker-mapping knowledge is retained and Taiwan escalation raises coercion risk, exercised continuity must finish before concrete does.

## In practice
We keep the Shield as the operational push via ENISA and the Civil Protection Mechanism: completing mandatory reporting drills for hit transmission operators, rolling jointly procured detection to ports, and trading EU-paid hardening for signed continuity plans in municipal utilities. Interior Council gets technicians via pooled training funds, not new standards this turn.

On gigafactories we do not add a new instrument. DG CNECT and DG ENER mediate the two feuding connection offers, making grid priority conditional on EU legal anchoring and adding power-price and water-use compensation packages to unblock permits, using existing InvestAI guarantees and EIB lines. On Taiwan we hold Dutch lithography coordination in reserve and task DG TRADE to map chip-supply exposure, without opening a new measure until the precursor path clarifies.
