## In practice
We task DG CNECT with the Health Emergency Preparedness and Digital Europe funds to stand up an EU-run inference fallback: Health ministries and hit operators get first slots on EuroHPC and existing cloud under EU legal anchoring, with the AI Office fast re-certifying substitute models under M4. Council Health and JHA formations carry it as emergency continuity, not new AI regulation.

On the build track we do not add money. DG ENER keeps mediating the two grid-connection feuds with conditional priority and compensation from existing InvestAI guarantees, while DG TRADE keeps chip-mapping without triggering export controls. M2 permitting zones are used to site fallback capacity, linking the emergency to the stalled large build.
