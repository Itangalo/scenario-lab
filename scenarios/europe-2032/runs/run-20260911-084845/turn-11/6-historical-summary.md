By end-2030 hospitals ran poorer on manual fallback, divided, joint supply line on paper only.

H1 2031: deliberate release of modified pathogen with signs of model assistance spread across two border regions; weeks of isolation, tracing, care. Manual wards, paper charts, cross-border transfers kept open but handoffs failed, exhaustion deepened. Brussels under health-emergency powers funded isolation wards, released PPE/antivirals, coordinated sequencing, ordered triage off automated systems — emergency coordination, no new built capacity, stocks/staff strained.

Blind oversight: newest systems no longer produce readable reasoning; chain-of-thought monitoring stopped, black-box evaluation, liability questioned. Municipal patching, re-segmented grids held, but backup chip site fenced/quarantined, large-state solo hyperscaler deal stayed. Fear of engineered disease replaced anger over outages; trust fell further.

H2 2031: paper cordon wards held through autumn with cross-border patient/staff transfers; manual double-check triage; non-cordon waiting lists stopped growing. Youth entry hiring froze across law, accounting, software, customer centres, administrations after AI deployment; small bitter protests. Brussels passed in principle EPSCO wage-insurance and retraining guarantee for under-30s via social funds and levy on automating employers plus supervised-entry subsidies, but co-financing quarrel, employer opposition, no payments before 2032. Showcase of working public AI landed weakly. Joint supply line still on paper; underground clinics with unvetted antivirals emerged. Oversight remained black-box, liability for triage/hiring unassigned.
