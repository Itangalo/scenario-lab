## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee**
A category 7 Labour and social protection fund for wage insurance, paid retraining and hiring incentives targeted at entry-level law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration roles displaced by AI, tied to automating employers.
This now because graduate hiring collapse is the visible face of displacement driving hostility at sentiment 18, and because the adoption success gives us a European benefit story to pair with protection under our absorption commitment.

## Priority
EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee – because without stemming entry-level wipeout and hostility, cordons and manual care cannot hold capital or cohesion this turn.

## In practice
We act via ESF+ and SURE-type instrument through EPSCO Council: Commission proposes wage-insurance top-ups and retraining vouchers for under-30 entrants in named sectors, funded by co-financing plus levy on large automating employers, with hiring subsidies conditional on human-supervised entry posts. We pair this with public-sector AI adoption showcase – waiting-list and decision-time gains from M4 – to show European AI creates supervised work rather than only removes it.

M11 continues under HERA/ECDC lead: isolation wards, PPE/antiviral release, paper double-check triage off AI, cross-border staff transfers. ENISA reserve teams stay on municipal patching. We keep Gigafactory payments frozen and joint supply line on paper while quarantine persists, offering no new compute spend until absorption stabilises.
