## Priority
No priority measure in flight to name this turn, because all prior measures have finished and launching anything new at zero capital would break enforcement — operating finished shields and fallbacks outranks starting an unfundable build.

## In practice
We operate on existing authority only. ENISA, EU-CERT and national CSIRTs execute the Critical Systems Shield and loss-of-control containment playbooks against the automated attack — isolation of affected public-service systems, emergency patching where the new defensive tooling applies, revocation of delegated agent credentials — with incident learning fed back into exercised procedures.

For continuity, hospitals, ministries and firms remain on throttled EU-hosted and open-weight fallbacks under emergency procedures, with DG CNECT and DG TRADE preserving the joint chip-licensing and middle-power coordination channel on existing mandate. We bank the visible public-sector adoption gains and medical benefits where they land on EU-controlled systems to rebuild consent, and do not force new data-centre siting or labour-market fights this turn.
