## Priority
No priority measure in flight to name this turn, because the portfolio holds no in-flight measures and launching anything new at near-zero capital would break enforcement — operating finished shields and accepting protection offered by others outranks starting an unfundable build.

## In practice
We operate on existing authority only. ENISA, EU-CERT and national CSIRTs execute the Critical Systems Shield and loss-of-control containment playbooks against the ransomware sweep and its secondaries — isolation, revocation of delegated agent credentials, human sign-off on payments, shared automated patching and swarm-detection — with incident learning fed back into exercised procedures. With chain-of-thought oversight now dead at the frontier, we shift what control remains to black-box behavioural monitoring and deployment guardrails on EU-hosted systems.

For continuity, hospitals, grid operators and payment systems remain on throttled EU-controlled fallbacks under emergency procedures, with DG CNECT and DG TRADE preserving the Japan-Korea licensing channel and middle-power coordination framework. We accept the invitation into the joint threat-response telemetry and attribution pool on existing mandate — protection we cannot build alone — without forcing new data-centre siting or labour-market fights this turn, and bank the visible triage and therapy gains where they land to rebuild minimal consent.
