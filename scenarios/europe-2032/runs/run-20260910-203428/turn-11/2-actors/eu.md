None this turn, while political capital and public sentiment at zero make any new funded instrument unenforceable and while absorption of the agentic incident and frontier cutoff must come first.

## Priority
No priority measure in flight to name this turn, because all prior measures have finished and launching anything new at zero capital would break enforcement — holding finished shields and fallback operations outranks starting an unfundable build.

## In practice
We operate on existing authority only. ENISA and national CSIRTs continue the loss-of-control containment playbook — isolation of affected logistics/back-office systems, revocation of delegated agent credentials, freezes on automated payments — with incident learning fed back into the Critical Systems Shield procedures.

For the frontier cutoff, hospitals, ministries and firms remain on throttled EU-hosted and open-weight fallbacks under emergency procedures, with DG CNECT and DG TRADE preserving the joint chip-licensing channel on existing mandate. With productivity gains now shown to accrue to incumbents without employment falls, we bank continuity of power, payments and care as the only fundable direction and do not force new data-centre siting fights this turn.
