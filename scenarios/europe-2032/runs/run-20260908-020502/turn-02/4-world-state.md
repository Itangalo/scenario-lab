### Frontier Acceleration, Safety Stalls  
Global AI capability advances to 56.0 as leading labs deploy new training runs featuring autonomous agent coordination and real-time cyber-physical system simulation. No corresponding safety upgrades land on frontier models, and interpretability remains limited to narrow probes. The gap between capability and control widens, with developers acknowledging internal struggles to monitor emergent planning behaviours.

### Compute Access: Deal Struck, Trust Thin  
The Emergency Compute Resilience Directive clears ECOFIN after the Commission agrees to a tripartite governance model and binding compensation for affected providers. A Compute Resilience Board will oversee access decisions, aiming to depoliticise crisis interventions. However, Germany and Ireland extract concessions limiting invocation frequency and mandating judicial review, weakening enforcement teeth. The directive passes — a political win — but its operational constraints raise doubts about real-time efficacy.

### Sovereignty Efforts Advance, But Not Fast Enough  
InvestAI Gigafactories launch feasibility studies in Finland and Spain, with the EIB structuring risk-sharing instruments to attract private capital. The Tech Sovereignty Package inches forward in Parliament, trading support for rural broadband in exchange for streamlined permitting. Yet, no major breakthroughs occur, and delays persist in aligning national regulations.

### Public Value, Private Doubts  
The EU launches the Public AI Adoption Acceleration initiative, scaling successful deployments in healthcare triage, education tutoring, and administrative processing. Early results show improved service delivery, and media coverage shifts slightly, acknowledging tangible benefits. However, unions warn of deepening job insecurity, and regional protests flare in response to automated public-sector staffing tools. The mood remains tense beneath the surface.

### Sentiment Lifts, Capital Erodes  
Public sentiment rises on visible improvements in public services, but political capital declines under the weight of three large measures and a named priority. The resilience bump is modest, reflecting partial adoption gains. AI safety drops further as no safeguards accompany rising capability.
