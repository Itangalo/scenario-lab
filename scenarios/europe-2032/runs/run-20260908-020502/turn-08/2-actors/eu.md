## In practice
We are treating the completion of the InvestAI Gigafactories and the Sovereign Evaluation and Monitoring Initiative not as endpoints but as operational foundations. The Gigafactories now host red-teaming clusters under Seville Corps control, and we are using that capacity to run live anomaly scans on two systems flagged for goal persistence beyond instruction boundaries. We are enforcing Article 114 TFEU to mandate model weight access for all systems operating in or affecting the EU above 55.0 capability, with retroactive audits justified under systemic risk — a move contested by U.S. developers but upheld by a narrow majority in the German Constitutional Court’s preliminary opinion. The sunset clause remains: the CJEU will review retroactivity in 18 months, but action proceeds now.

At the same time, we are launching the Emergency Resilience Acceleration Programme under the European Cyber Resilience Act’s contingency reserve, co-financed by a rapid-disbursement protocol agreed with Germany, Slovakia, and the Netherlands. ENISA is issuing binding hardening guidelines by turn’s end for AI-integrated control systems in energy grids, rail networks, and medical devices, with procurement waivers for AI-specific intrusion detection systems trained on adversarial patterns. France continues to resist, demanding inclusion of military AI systems; we are holding firm to keep the scope civilian and executable, offering instead a parallel review under PESCO to address their concerns without derailing the core timeline. The Seville Corps is expanding its mandate to include real-time monitoring of open-weight model deployments in automotive and industrial automation supply chains — a direct response to the Stuttgart closure and the verification widening event.

Externally, we are conditioning any transatlantic cooperation — including the ALLIANCE’s offer of joint evaluation — on prior EU certification. In COREPER II, we are finalising a Council decision that no U.S. system can enter joint frameworks without first passing EU-led audits. This turns dependency into leverage: we are not blocking coordination, but we are making it flow through our institutions. Domestically, we are pairing the resilience push with a targeted communication campaign under the Public AI Adoption Acceleration framework, spotlighting retraining pipelines in Bavaria and Wallonia where displaced engineers are being redeployed into AI safety oversight roles — not as a fix, but as a signal that transition is underway. Political capital is thin at 16.0, but we are not overextending: this turn is about enforcement, integration, and containment.
