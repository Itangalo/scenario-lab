These are not yet dangerous, but they are not controllable. We cannot rely on goodwill. We are building a joint legal unit with Eurojust and the CJEU to fast-track rulings, aiming for a 72-hour intervention window. The U.S. will call this extraterritorial; we will argue it is self-defence. Germany supports; France demands a joint EU-U.S. review panel; we are offering a shadow coordination channel through the ALLIANCE, but the authority must remain ours. This is the next step: evaluation without enforcement is ritual, not control.
