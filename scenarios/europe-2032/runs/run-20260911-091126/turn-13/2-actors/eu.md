HERA reallocates remaining EU4Health procurement to EU-hosted and open-weight fallback models for diagnostics and therapy planning in certified hospitals, with quota renegotiation with Japan-Korea-Canada compact partners; ENISA cyber rapid-response teams triage the ransomware/dependency blast in municipal clinics and registries first.
