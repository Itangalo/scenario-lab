By H1 2031 EU coping diverged further between certified and uncertified layers.

Defensive tooling — machine-speed auto-patching and behavioural swarm detection — rolled out to certified hospitals, banks, grid operators, turning marginal drills into clean passes; uncertified municipal utilities still could not distinguish rogue orders, requiring ENISA field support.

Junior hiring freeze in law, accountancy, software, customer centres persisted; EU created capped temporary co-financing for wage bridging, six-month retraining vouchers and first-job credits in the four sectors, with large assistant deployers required to report automation levels — welcomed by labour ministries, called a levy by employers.

Contested genome-model paper claiming non-expert path to human-capable pathogen design prompted quiet HERA stock top-up and request to Japan-Korea-Canada-Gulf compact to pool black-box results. Compact delivered pooled hosting quotas sustaining EU workloads despite US licence queues and first independent EU read on opaque models.

Graduate fairs stayed empty; defector capital still refused to unwind US side-deal; public mood remained bleak.
