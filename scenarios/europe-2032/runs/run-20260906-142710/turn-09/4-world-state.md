### Capability Creeps, Safety Lags

The frontier advances as automated verification widens across law, engineering, and clinical coding. Tasks once requiring expert review now self-validate, accelerating deployment cycles. This turns the narrative on controllability: if outcomes can be checked cheaply, risk seems manageable—even as the systems producing them grow more opaque. The US, under its retrenchment posture, slows its own frontier progress, but global capability climbs steadily.

Open-weight models see modest gains, building on existing open-source momentum and incremental contributions from distributed developers. However, the ecosystem remains constrained by compute access and coordination limits. The jump toward parity with closed systems is neither immediate nor frictionless—progress is real but gradual, consistent with prior trends.
