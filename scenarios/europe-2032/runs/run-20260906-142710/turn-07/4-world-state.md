### The Shield Holds, But at a Cost

The Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative went live in pilot mode across key infrastructure nodes in France, Finland, and the Netherlands, successfully intercepting several AI-driven dependency attacks before they could propagate. ENISA’s standardized containers enforced zero-trust verification, and automated patching prevented escalation—proof that AI-native defense can work. Yet integration remains partial: Italy and Hungary continue to block access to health data coordination layers, citing national sovereignty, despite the recent attack exploiting precisely those blind spots.

The InvestAI Gigafactories were formally completed, but two sites lack grid connections due to unresolved energy-sharing disputes and local opposition. Final certification is now tied to Cyber Shield integration and real-time logging, raising tensions with developers who argue the mandates are being applied retroactively. The 5GW energy-backed compute target was missed, though the Commission’s repurposing of Article 194 TFEU to designate Gigafactories as strategic energy users may force compliance—Germany and Austria have launched binding arbitration, threatening delays.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 21.0. The restructured Cyber Resilience Corps, fast-tracking displaced professionals into monitoring roles, has seen modest uptake, but many view it as a symbolic gesture. Protests persist, particularly in cities where AI-driven automation has hollowed out mid-level professions.

The EU launched a new large measure—mandatory real-time model logging for critical infrastructure—aimed at closing auditability gaps. However, with no immediate technical or political breakthrough, ai_safety holds steady. Resilience dips slightly as capability advances outpace systemic hardening.

The US posture of retrenchment continues to slow global frontier momentum, but offers no direct leverage. Internal cohesion frays under the strain of enforcement, and political capital barely inches upward despite finishing a major infrastructure project.

### A Fragile Foundation

Sovereignty erodes further as dependency on contested energy access and incomplete integration undermines the Union’s control. The Gigafactories are built—but not yet operational at scale. The Shield is deployed—but not universally. The logs are mandated—but not yet implemented. The line holds, but only just.
