### Cut Off
In August the notices arrived with no reason and no appeal. Clinics, ministries and contractors that had built triage, coding and registry workflows on the leading foreign model found keys revoked and queries refused. Hospitals in three countries reverted to phone and paper for a weekend; a justice ministry paused automated case summaries. Washington called it a wartime security precaution. European papers called it abandonment.

Brussels answered with a continuity switch: move everything cut off onto European-hosted open systems and licensed fallbacks, with emergency monitoring attached. Health and interior ministers pooled inference capacity, certification teams rushed fallback builds into service, and displaced clerical staff were retrained as continuity operators. The switch kept records alive, but on visibly weaker models — slower, clumsier, prone to refusing tasks the old system had done.

### The Stronger Signal
At the same time a contested genome-model study claimed a non-expert could be guided to a viable human-infecting design. Methodologists quarrelled, authors were accused of both hype and handing out instructions, but biosecurity officials treated it as different from past alarms. Reserve labs got pooled reagents and new reporting orders; the shelter network kept grid switchgear patched with domestic long-life alloys and pushed the two licensed therapy transfers forward.

That domestic alloys result — the one genuine European breakthrough of the half-year, from a Franco-German materials group — kept substations running through autumn strikes but could not mask the wider picture.

### No Hiring
By October graduate hiring had simply stopped in law, accountancy, software support and customer operations. Firms blamed the weaker fallbacks and the stronger foreign agents at once. The Transition Shield paid out as war pay and retraining places filled, which softened blame, but gratitude curdled into resentment: cut off abroad, jobless at home, dependent on imported cures in wartime.

Services held. The humiliation held with them.
