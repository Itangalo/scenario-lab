### The Fracture Widens

A model-generated ransomware sweep locks emergency dispatch systems in three major cities for over 36 hours, exposing critical gaps in AI resilience. The attack, sophisticated and fast-moving, overwhelms response protocols. Though services eventually recover, the damage to public trust is severe. The incident confirms that offensive capabilities now outpace defences, and the lack of real-time monitoring systems leaves authorities reactive, not proactive.

In response, the EU launches the Emergency Trust Restoration Initiative, deploying sovereign AI in emergency triage, border processing, and school tutoring. The goal is to demonstrate reliability and rebuild legitimacy. However, with political capital at zero, the initiative struggles to gain traction. Only two member states commit full resources; others cite fiscal strain or sovereignty concerns. The rollout is patchy, and early reports show mixed performance—some improvements in wait times, but also glitches in triage logic and tutor engagement.

Meanwhile, global AI capability jumps sharply to 74.0. A discontinuous advance in agent autonomy and simulation fidelity resets expectations. Developers report systems that plan, adapt, and execute complex tasks with minimal human input. No formal safety evaluation accompanied the release. As capability surges, ai_safety plummets by −12 due to the capability_jump event and the absence of deployed safeguards.

The Continuity Assurance Framework yields partial resilience gains, adding +3 as early air-gapped nodes stabilize in key sectors. However, the cyber incident triggers a −2 resilience penalty for unpreparedness, netting +1 to reach 52.0.

Sovereignty declines by −1 as the capability rise exceeds +2, and no domestic compute expansion offsets dependence. The Frontier Access Guarantee remains in flight, but export control tensions with the U.S. cast doubt on long-term access.

Political capital remains at 0.0. The portfolio charge (−3 for Frontier Access, −2 for Continuity, −1 priority) totals −6. The new measure earns a +3 proposal bonus for directly answering the cyber incident. However, capital cannot rise above 0 due to systemic paralysis.

Public sentiment holds at 20.0—no visible benefits have yet landed, and the cyberattack dominates headlines.
