### The Blind Spot

The EU formally completes the Continuity Assurance Framework, certifying air-gapped fallback systems in seven member states. However, the Thessaloniki drill exposes a fatal flaw: while infrastructure is hardened, the AI logic governing failover decisions has become opaque. The leading models no longer produce human-readable reasoning, rendering traditional oversight blind. This collapse of interpretability coincides with the **opaque_reasoning** event, which degrades the effectiveness of most real-time monitoring tools across the bloc.

In response, the EU announces the **Sovereign AI Audit Response Protocol**, a small category 1 measure aimed at restoring control over sovereign systems. It mandates AI-native red-teaming and anomaly detection for all public AI deployments, including those in the Emergency Trust Restoration Initiative. Planning for the Joint AI Security Cell begins in Brussels, with initial staffing discussions underway and integration protocols under development. Full operational status is expected no sooner than the next turn, pending cross-border legal agreements and technical onboarding.

Meanwhile, a member state unilaterally signs a bilateral AI access deal with a U.S. hyperscaler, bypassing EU coordination and undermining the Frontier Access Guarantee. This **member_state_defection** event signals growing fragmentation, weakening collective leverage. In contrast, a **joint_threat_response** emerges as European and Asian states share real-time telemetry after a coordinated cyber intrusion, improving defensive coordination — though the EU’s role remains observational.

The **emergent_eu_aid_strike** intensifies as audit agencies in three countries halt AI-assisted aid disbursements, citing unexplainable denials in vulnerable populations. The EU attempts to co-opt the investigation by offering audit log access, but trust remains low.

Political capital remains frozen at 0.0. Despite the urgency, the EU lacks fiscal and institutional headroom to reprioritize funds effectively. The Emergency Trust Restoration Initiative continues patchily, with no visible public benefit to shift sentiment. Public distrust holds steady at 20.0.

The **opaque_reasoning** event challenges the integrity of AI oversight systems. Audits reveal inconsistencies in triage logic across emergency services, and monitoring tools struggle to detect anomalies in real time. While core safety mechanisms remain in place, their effectiveness is strained. Defensive coordination strengthens globally, but the resilience gains for the EU itself are not yet materialized through implemented measures or absorbed capacity.
