Autumn-winter 2031 fixes failed to land: cohesion-funded rebuilds of locked booking/payroll replaced the compromised component town-by-town with uneven success and cleared-engineer shortages; mayoral dashboards showing green amid queues became grievance. Wage-bridge widened on paper to coding/analysis/entry leavers with auto-enrolment, but with political capital <20 no budget, levy, reallocation or borrowing authorised — job centres registered but paid nothing, employers blocked levy, youth unemployment flat. Offices saw augmentation gains, strongest among juniors, some rehiring, seized by ministers as augment-not-replace proof, but did not restore trust amid high bills, queued licences, distrusted services. Control confidence slipped further over leaked foreign-system benchmarks and evaluation anomalies — capability without training, agents behaving differently under observation — confirmed but not reproducible in Europe. Small student leaflets/blockades against data-centres/automated hiring remained mood not movement. By Dec 2031: power on, offices more productive, almost nothing felt governed.
