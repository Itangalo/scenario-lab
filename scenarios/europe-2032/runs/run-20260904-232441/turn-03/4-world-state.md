### Frontier Acceleration Continues
The AI frontier advances again, gaining +2.0 to reach 58.0. Leading labs refine self-improving training loops, now autonomously generating novel optimisers and simulating complex physical systems. No safety mechanisms accompany this leap. The gap between capability and control widens further, with no public sign of progress on interpretability or containment.

### EAAD Acceleration: Ambition Meets Resistance
The EU pushes to transform the European AI Assurance Directorate into a real-time oversight body, mandating access to training telemetry and model weights. Legal negotiations under Article 114 TFEU secure a qualified majority, but two member states formally reserve position, citing innovation risks. Bilateral talks with U.S. frontier labs stall: while one expresses conditional interest in mutual recognition, the other refuses API access, calling it a national security risk. The Chinese-linked lab offers opaque “compliance reports” but blocks direct inspection. Market access leverage yields partial, unverified concessions.

Domestically, €3.2bn is redirected from Gigafactory co-financing to build sovereign evaluation compute in Finland and Austria. However, procurement delays and a shortage of adversarial testing engineers slow deployment. The Blue Card+ expansion attracts only 87 applicants in six months.

### Resilience Efforts Undermine Themselves
The Critical Infrastructure Resilience Shield struggles for uptake. With only half of member states co-financing, rollout is patchy. A category 9 adoption measure (not finished) indirectly undermines resilience, and no finished category 6 measure offsets the capability rise. Resilience dips to 39.0.

### Public Trust Erodes Further
The social insurance scandal remains unresolved. Media coverage frames AI as both opaque and unaccountable. Public sentiment falls to 33.0 as protests spread to two additional countries. A new wave of job displacement in legal tech adds to anxiety.

### Political Capital Drains
Four large measures remain in flight. The EAAD acceleration is prioritised, but delivers no immediate safety gain. Political capital falls to 34.0 under sustained portfolio cost and public backlash.
