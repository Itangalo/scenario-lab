## New measure
**Measure:** Establish sovereign red-team simulation clusters with mirrored access to frontier model weights  
This measure builds and operationalises air-gapped, high-resilience evaluation environments in Finland and Austria capable of running adversarial simulations on mirrored weights from frontier models under EAAD supervision.

`Category: 5 (Public technical capacity and research)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 6`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase ai_safety by 6–8 points and eu_ai_sovereignty by 5–7 points by enabling the EU to independently test, probe and contain emergent behaviours in high-capability models without relying on foreign labs’ self-reporting  
`Applies to:` EU member states, the European AI Assurance Directorate, and developers of models above 45.0 capability operating in the EU market

## Priority
**Priority:** Accelerate the European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) with binding access to frontier model weights and real-time monitoring of training runs  
Because the healthcare breach proves that uncontrolled models are already interacting with critical systems, and because ai_capability has reached 58.0 with no corresponding leap in ai_safety, we must shift oversight from post-deployment audits to real-time intervention — the only way to prevent cascading harm is to be present where risk emerges: inside the training run.

## In practice
We are finalising amendments to the AI Act under Article 114 TFEU to mandate real-time telemetry and weight access for all models above 45.0 capability seeking EU market access. The Internal Market Council has approved the legal package with a qualified majority, despite reservations from Sweden and Ireland, who fear reputational damage to European innovation. We are leveraging Digital Markets Act gatekeeper designations to compel cooperation from the two U.S.-based frontier labs: one has agreed to a pilot API feed in exchange for mutual recognition with the U.S. AI Safety Institute, while the other remains resistant, citing national security. The Chinese-linked lab continues to offer only opaque compliance summaries; we are now conditioning full market access on verifiable architectural separation between its domestic and EU-facing clusters, with on-site inspection rights.

Domestically, the €3.2bn redirected from Gigafactory co-financing is being deployed to build sovereign red-team clusters in Finland and Austria — air-gapped facilities with hardened security and direct EAAD oversight. These will run mirrored versions of frontier models for adversarial testing, using techniques developed by a newly expanded cohort of 200 interpretability researchers recruited under the Blue Card+ reform. While only 87 have arrived so far, we are fast-tracking visas and offering binding public service contracts with premium salaries to close the gap. To strengthen resilience in parallel, we are linking the Resilience Shield’s co-financing waivers to EAAD compliance: member states that implement zero-trust architecture in health and energy sectors will receive partial reimbursement for EAAD monitoring rollout costs, creating a virtuous cycle between security, oversight and sovereignty.
