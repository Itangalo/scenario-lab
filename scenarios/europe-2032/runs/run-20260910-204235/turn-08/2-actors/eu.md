## In practice
We run the Sprint via DG DIGIT and DG SANTE with EPSCO and Education Council cover: EU4Health and Digital Europe re-tasked money for three lighthouse deployments — hospital triage scheduling, municipal permit assistants, classroom tutoring — procured under EU procurement preference to EU-soil hosting, with Eurostat-validated dashboards publishing waiting-time and decision-time falls.

We keep M8 accession as the operating protection track: ENISA/CERT-EU analysts seconded to the joint cyber command, health labs streaming samples under binding sharing, interior ministers briefed on live feeds to contain the side-deal fallout. No new cash call; we offer the defecting capital co-branding on Sprint results as a path back to the common position.
