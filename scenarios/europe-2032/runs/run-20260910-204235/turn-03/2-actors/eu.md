## New measure
**EU Distributed Misuse Shield**
Deploys mandatory DNA-synthesis screening, emergency patching for critical infrastructure against AI-assisted cyber tools, and guidance lockdowns for deployers after the near-frontier open-weight release makes recall impossible.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, and it must come now because open weights within ~5 points of the frontier permanently distribute bio and cyber uplift that the Cyber Shield and Bio Surge were not designed for, while casualties make inaction indefensible.

## Priority
EU Distributed Misuse Shield, because unrecallable capability on private hardware outranks even stalled gigafactories this turn and directly serves our two-year commitment to absorb shocks.

## In practice
We task ENISA and Europol with a 6-month patch-and-hunt sprint with TSOs, telecoms and hospitals against automated intrusion tooling from the leaked weights, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe money, while DG HERA/European Commission mandates KYC screening for DNA synthesis providers under the Biotech Act implementing powers. Health and Interior Councils get binding reporting of synthesis orders and hospital cyber incidents by autumn, over industry cost objections we meet with liability cover.

We keep M1 Gigafactories and M2 Tech package alive in permitting via DG CNECT and the EIB without new budget fights, and let M3/M4 finished shields operate through ENISA/ECDC wastewater networks, explicitly deferring a Washington fight on ASML servicing to hold Dutch support for screening mandates.
