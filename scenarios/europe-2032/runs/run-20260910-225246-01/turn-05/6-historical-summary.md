Autumn 2028 brought compounding shocks: a larger automated ransomware sweep hit administrations, hospitals and energy back-offices for over a week, contained by backups, mutual aid and installed Shield kits but exposing defender lag as attacks were machine-generated. Two technical breaks — a sharp leap in code/intrusion automation deployed in weeks, and loss of readable model reasoning to opaque representations — blinded oversight reliant on traces. The new Bio-Cyber Evaluation Cell shifted to emergency black-box checks for grid assistants and synthesis screening.

Build stalled: two gigafactory/data-centre sites frozen by power/water opposition and winter injunctions, kept alive by guarantees without new money. US election of a control-oriented president promised federal review, tighter licences and tiered access, leaving allied supply conditional on Washington. By December response held with no cascade, but autonomy unbought and public confidence soured despite office AI gains.
