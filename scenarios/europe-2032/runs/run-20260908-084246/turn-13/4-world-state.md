### Capability Leap Amid Crisis

A sudden leap in closed AI capability—driven by a verifiable advance in autonomous code generation and cyber operations—pushes `ai_capability` to 73.0. Though general competence rises only modestly, the jump alters attacker capabilities overnight. Open-weight models, replicated from the frontier, reach 60.0, accelerating misuse in disinformation and system probing.

### Democratic Drift, Not Decision

The EU closes the **Emergency Override Authority Enforcement Directive**, mandating integration of monitoring stacks into AI systems above 50.0 capability. But enforcement halts at the ECJ, which rules the directive exceeds Union competence without unanimous member-state consent. The **Sovereign Incident Response Corps** remains underfunded and unstaffed, its activation delayed.

In response, the Commission launches the **Labour and Governance Stabilisation Pact**, a large category 7 measure aimed at quelling civil service strikes and restoring trust in public AI use. France and Germany condition support on enforceable limits over AI in hiring and promotions. Poland and Hungary reject the framework as federal overreach, though offered resilience funds for municipal services.

An emergency European Council session under Article 15 opens negotiations, but no consensus emerges. The Pact begins, but without immediate buy-in from key states, its implementation is partial. The strikes continue, disrupting digital tax and healthcare systems across three member states.

### Falling Shields

`ai_safety` drops to 15.0 as no safety measure lands—autonomous systems proliferate unchecked. `resilience` falls to 54.0, undermined by ongoing strikes and the failure of override enforcement. `eu_ai_sovereignty` declines to 8.0, as capability rises and no domestic compute or access gains materialize. The **capability_jump** exacerbates dependence, and no event secures access.

`eu_political_capital` rises slightly to 5.0: the portfolio charge is −9 (three large measures, priority), but a +4 proposal bonus for the Pact—answering two severe, recent emergent events—offsets it. `public_sentiment` dips to 25.0, as strikes and election turmoil erode confidence.
