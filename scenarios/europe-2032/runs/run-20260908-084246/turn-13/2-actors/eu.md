## In practice

We are convening an emergency session of the European Council under Article 15 of the Treaty on European Union, not to impose control, but to open a negotiation. The Commission will table the Labour and Governance Stabilisation Pact as a legislative instrument under the social policy clause, with co-decision rights granted to the European Parliament and observer status for civil service unions. We are directing €1.2 billion from the Cohesion Fund and the Digital Europe Programme to backstop job transition guarantees, with 70% of funds conditional on member states ratifying binding no-strike agreements during the negotiation window.

France and Germany are already signaling conditional support, but only if the Pact includes enforceable limits on AI in hiring, promotions, and performance reviews. Poland and Hungary remain opposed, framing this as another centralising overreach — but we are offering them targeted resilience funding for municipal services in exchange for non-obstruction. The real leverage is time: the strikes are already disrupting digital tax processing and healthcare scheduling, and the annulled election in Slovakia has created a precedent that other governments fear. We are not selling sovereignty today — we are selling continuity.
