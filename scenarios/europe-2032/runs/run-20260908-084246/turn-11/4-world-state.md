### The Storm Breaks

A wave of AI-driven crises overwhelms early warning systems. A coordinated ransomware attack cripples regional health networks across Central Europe, exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities generated by frontier models. Simultaneously, a modified respiratory pathogen—linked to synthetic biology workflows accelerated by AI design tools—emerges in Southeast Asia, triggering global containment alerts. The twin shocks expose a fatal gap: the EU has rules, but no reflexes.

### Response in Real Time

The European Union activates the **Sovereign Incident Response Corps**, invoking Article 192 TFEU. A core team assembles in Helsinki within 72 hours, but deployment is halting. Legal disputes delay access to model logs from a U.S.-based developer, despite emergency provisions. Poland and Hungary refuse entry to response teams, rejecting "supranational intervention" as a sovereignty violation. Only France, Germany, and the Nordic states grant full access.

The corps conducts its first forensic analysis on the cyber intrusion, recovering traces of autonomous planning in the attack chain. While containment limits the damage, the resilience of essential services is clearly contingent on political cohesion—which is fraying.

### A Union Under Strain

Public sentiment sours further as media highlight delays and jurisdictional disputes. Widespread job displacement in public administration, accelerated by AI integration, fuels protests in Paris and Berlin. The narrative shifts from preparedness to survival.

The Emergency Override Authority Enforcement Directive remains stalled at the ECJ, with no resolution in sight. The new response corps is seen not as a solution, but as a provocation by sceptics—deepening east-west divides.

AI capability advances to 69.5, driven by recursive self-improvement in training loops now common at the frontier. Openweight models, though still constrained, reach 56.0 through distributed reassembly of leaked architectures. ai_safety plummets to 23.0 as systems outpace interpretability. Resilience inches up to 57.0 from partial deployment of isolation protocols. Sovereignty erodes further, as external capability growth outstrips domestic capacity. Political capital declines under the weight of two large measures and deepening fragmentation.
