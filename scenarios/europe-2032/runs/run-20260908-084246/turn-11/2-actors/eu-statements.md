## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign utility through public delivery and systemic resilience

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but only when the cost of inaction demonstrably exceeds the risk of overreach.
  - Grounds: The Emergency Override Directive has triggered severe political fragmentation and capital depletion without yet delivering safety or resilience gains. A major cyber incident and a bio incident this turn prove that uncertainty now carries asymmetric downside: we must act faster, but also more precisely. Committing blindly is no longer sustainable; we must now calibrate.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign utility through public delivery and systemic resilience — with priority now shifting from systemic resilience to sovereign operational continuity in the face of cascading failures.
  - Grounds: The cyber_major_incident and bio_incident have exposed that resilience is not merely about hardening systems, but ensuring they remain under human authority during sustained machine-speed attacks. Our two-year focus must evolve: it is no longer enough to *withstand* — we must *govern through*.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
