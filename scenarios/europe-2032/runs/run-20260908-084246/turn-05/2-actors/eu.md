## In practice
The Cyber Resilience Surge is now complete. ENISA has delivered certified detection stacks, including the sovereign EU option co-developed with Airbus and Siemens, and all Tier 1 operators of essential services are mandated under DORA Article 5a to deploy AI-powered intrusion detection by Q1 2028. Germany’s national variants have been certified against EU baselines, preserving technical sovereignty without delay. Belgium has activated EU threat feeds under binding directive, and legal appeals are suspended during declared incidents. The emergency powers under NIS2 have held, but the political cost remains high. We are now shifting from crisis response to strategic consolidation.

The Sovereign Evaluation Pilot continues to scale: France’s node at Inria, Finland’s at VTT, and Germany’s under BSI are operational, with secure compute enclaves standing up using repurposed Digital Europe funds. Legal access to training runs above 50.0 capability is being finalised under Article 68 comitology, and we are beginning to monitor not just outputs but trajectories. But the openweight_frontier_release at 52.0 capability — downloaded over 400,000 times in 72 hours — exposes the limits of containment. The Open-Weight Containment Framework is in flight, but enforcement remains contested. Hosting platforms resist liability, and civil society warns of censorship. We need a counterweight: not just control, but proof of value.

That is why we are launching the Sovereign AI Adoption Accelerator. We will fast-track procurement for AI systems that are both effective and under EU control, starting with three domains: hospital triage in under-resourced regions, backlog clearance in immigration processing, and adaptive tutoring in vocational retraining. These will use only models evaluated under the Sovereign Evaluation Pilot and hosted on EU-anchored infrastructure. The European Commission will issue a delegated act under the Digital Services Act to prioritise these deployments, and we are unlocking €15bn in cohesion funds to co-finance uptake in member states most exposed to job displacement. The goal is not just efficiency — it is legitimacy. The adoption_success event showed us what is possible: waiting lists falling, decisions arriving in days, teaching that works. Now we must make it visible, widespread, and ours.
