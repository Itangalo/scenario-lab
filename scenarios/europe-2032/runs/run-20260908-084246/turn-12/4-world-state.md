### Fractured Authority

The European Union signals a strategic pivot. Facing mounting legal and political resistance, the **Emergency Override Authority Enforcement Directive** is acknowledged as stalled pending the ECJ ruling. In response, the Commission proposes the **Democratic Redeployment of Emergency Powers**, a category 3 measure intended to replace unilateral override with co-governance involving national parliaments and oversight bodies. However, no implementation begins this turn: with only 4.0 political capital and two active measures already in flight (**Emergency Override Authority Enforcement Directive**, **Sovereign Incident Response Corps Activation**), the proposal remains in drafting. Legal scholars question its feasibility, and member-state resistance hardens—especially in Central Europe, where the directive is seen as either too weak or too invasive.

### Resilience Under Strain

Despite the activation of the **Sovereign Incident Response Corps**, resilience sees only marginal decline to 56.0. The openweight release enables early signs of decentralized misuse, including AI-facilitated disinformation tied to synthetic media tools now more accessible. These affect local information environments but are not yet widespread. The response corps remains in formation and lacks mandate or access to counter cross-border threats, while the **Emergency Override Directive** remains unenforceable pending the ECJ ruling.

AI safety dips slightly to 20.0, as no new regulatory measure has yet landed on shipped systems and autonomous planning becomes more common. The EU’s sovereignty sees a minor decay to 10.0, reflecting continued erosion of external compute arrangements and no near-term domestic capacity coming online. The **ai_investment_collapse** reduces pressure on EU compute access indirectly, but no gain materializes due to lack of sovereign infrastructure.

Public sentiment softens to 27.0 amid growing unease about AI control and skepticism toward EU crisis management. Political capital holds at 4.0—unchanged—due to the proposal bonus from **Sovereign Incident Response Corps Activation**, but no additional cost is incurred because **Democratic Redeployment of Emergency Powers** has not yet launched as an active measure.
