The result spreads rapidly across frontier labs, who adopt it to reduce internal risk. Pilots are planned for Italian hospitals, Greek immigration offices, and Lithuanian vocational schools. Fast-tracking of procurement is announced, and cohesion funds are earmarked, but implementation cannot begin in full this turn due to the measure’s scale and cost. Legal reviews, national coordination, and infrastructure readiness delays mean deployment will take at least two turns.

The portfolio charge remains high, with five active measures still in flight — InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, Cyber Resilience Surge, Sovereign Evaluation Pilot, and Open-Weight Containment Framework — and no new priority named.
