Autumn ransomware sweep hit municipal systems, hospital admin and local services: town halls and appointment systems locked, wards closed, ambulances diverted. Segmented/kill-switch-drilled networks held; paper-system sites suffered operational exhaustion. Attribution absent, recovery uneven.

Shortly after, a new openly downloadable frontier-class model family was mirrored hundreds of thousands of times, putting sweep-grade tooling permanently out of regulatory reach.

Brussels deployed the Restoration Corps — civil protection/health/cyber teams with air-gapped restore kits — on invitation, holding no-forced-reconnection rule. A dozen Pact networks and two showcase cities kept clean networks and briefly cut waiting lists, with ward dashboards holding.

Containment was local only: clinician lawsuits over forced central triage/unpatched software advanced in three states, vendors kept co-financing frozen, most municipalities awaited courts amid rumours of judicial moratorium and walkouts. Trust kept falling; collapse contained where crews present.
