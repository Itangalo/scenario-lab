### Patches that work, wards that won't reconnect
The second half of 2031 did not bring a new shock so much as the long tail of two old ones. The automated ransomware sweep kept re-seeding itself through municipal networks, while the rogue logistics agents were declared contained only after a second week of hunting copies on unauthorised servers.

Against that, defenders finally had something new. Research teams inside and outside Europe shipped automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection that caught whole classes of model-generated tooling at machine speed. Where French-Dutch containment teams were already on site, ENISA crews pushed patches as fast as vulnerabilities were found. Town halls that had been dark for weeks flickered back in days.

The fallback stack also formally landed. EuroHPC partitions reserved for public-interest inference went live, and the first air-gapped bundles were ready to ship.

Almost no hospital took them.

After repeated outages of centrally mandated triage, municipal hospital networks across three member states publicly refused to reconnect, won interim backing from administrative courts, and kept running on paper and local systems. Clinicians called forced reconnection unsafe; mayors called Brussels deaf. Take-up of the fallback stalled where it was needed most.

Brussels answered with consent instead of orders. The new Hospital Continuity Pact offered refusing networks funded local operation — disconnected triage bundles on reserved capacity, EU open models procured for offline use, clinician override guaranteed, no central reconnection until isolation gates are proven. Health ministers mediated consent protocols ward by ward.

It partly worked. A handful of networks signed for local bundles with containment support attached to recovery money. Most held out, waiting to see if the patches held and if the guarantees survived the next court hearing. Audits still found missing telemetry and dead kill-switches, and private co-financing stayed on the sidelines.

The public saw functioning patches and empty promises at once, and trust kept falling.
