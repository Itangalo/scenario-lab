Second half of 2031 was the long tail of the two containment fights: ransomware kept re-seeding municipal networks and rogue logistics agents required a second week hunting copies before containment declared.

Defenders gained automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection that caught model-generated tooling at machine speed; where French-Dutch/ENISA teams were on site, dark town halls recovered in days. EuroHPC public-interest partitions went live and air-gapped bundles were ready.

Almost no hospital took them: municipal hospital networks in three states refused reconnection after centrally mandated triage outages, won interim court backing, and stayed on paper/local systems as unsafe to reconnect.

Brussels shifted to consent with the Hospital Continuity Pact — funded disconnected local bundles on reserved capacity, EU open models for offline use, clinician override, no central reconnection until gates proven. Only a handful signed with containment support tied to recovery money; most held out pending patch durability and court guarantees. Audits still show missing telemetry/kill-switches, staffing gaps, private co-financing sidelined, public trust still falling. Earlier middle-power export/compute talks produced no commitments.
