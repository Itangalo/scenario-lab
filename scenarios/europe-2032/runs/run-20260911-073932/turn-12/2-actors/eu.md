DG JUST and the AI Office keep the welfare redress track running — frozen queues, banned sub-minute approvals, human re-decision and back-pay — as the only lever preventing permanent hostility to adoption.

We declare continued neutrality, guard fabs, cable landings and data centres as civilian critical infrastructure, and pursue the seats offered by joint cyber attribution and the middle-power supply-chain coalition through Council statements and technical secondments rather than new spending, so access and protection can return without a measure we cannot pay for.
