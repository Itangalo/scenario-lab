## New measure
**EU Welfare AI Redress and Human Oversight Enforcement**
Creates an EU-level redress and re-examination duty for AI-supported welfare, policing and court-assistance systems with logging audit and meaningful human-review standards enforced through the AI Act. Category 2 transparency and reporting measure — why this and why now: the automated-decision scandal has made enforcement the test of the Act itself, and with sentiment at 20 and capital at 9 we cannot afford adoption or sovereignty without showing wronged people get remedy fast.

## Priority
M10 EU Entry-Level Transition and Essential Service Corps — because holding graduate livelihoods and visible EU-paid work is what keeps services staffed and build-sites guardable while the scandal and superhuman jump pull sentiment down.

## In practice
We run M10 through DG EMPL and ESF+ clearing with mayors and hospital boards, pushing placements from thousands toward tens of thousands in permit, care-scheduling and offline-backup roles on EU-anchored Dividend sites, with wage-insurance conditional on common procurement terms. NIS2 joint teams and ENISA keep hospitals and ports degraded-not-stopped, and DG GROW keeps reserved chips moving to racks under civil-protection guard.

We open the redress track through DG JUST and the AI Office with the Fundamental Rights Agency and national ombudsmen: freeze the impugned welfare/policing queue, order human re-decision with 40-second approvals prohibited, publish logs, and pay arrears where the finding confirms systematic wronging. We frame it as Annex III breach with enforcement, not a gap, to keep the Act intact. Medical and research breakthroughs are procured only on EU-anchored terms so benefit does not read as further dependence, and Taiwan surcharges are absorbed via the Blockade Continuity stocks without new measures.
