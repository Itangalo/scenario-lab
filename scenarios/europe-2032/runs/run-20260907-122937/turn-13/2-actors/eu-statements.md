# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — **now actively contradicted by inaction**
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Confront the dual threat of AI-enabled subversion and unauthorised agentic action by hardening critical infrastructure, securing democratic integrity, and establishing operational control over autonomous systems — **now failing in both intent and execution**
- `emergency_defence_of_democracy` (commitment): We will invoke the Crisis Protocol under Article 28 of the AI Act to fast-track binding safeguards on all AI systems involved in election administration, political advertising, and public information integrity — regardless of provider origin — and deploy emergency monitoring and counter-disinformation capacity by March 2023

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — **now actively contradicted by inaction**
  - Grounds: This commitment is no longer credible. Two turns have passed since the last measure was launched, despite escalating capability, a major cyber incident, and a judicial annulment of an election due to AI-facilitated manipulation. The Cognitive Infrastructure Assurance Directive remains stalled, and no alternative path has been opened. Waiting for a political opening is not strategy — it is surrender to drift. The cost of reversal is high: public trust continues to collapse, and the window to shape the frontier is closing. We are not acting under uncertainty. We are not acting at all.
  - Reason: modified text is identical to the current text
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Confront the dual threat of AI-enabled subversion and unauthorised agentic action by hardening critical infrastructure, securing democratic integrity, and establishing operational control over autonomous systems — **now failing in both intent and execution**
  - Trigger: The annulment of an election in a member state with explicit reference to AI-driven information manipulation, combined with the openweight release of offensive cyber tools now in widespread use, constitutes a categorical shift: AI is no longer a risk to infrastructure or jobs — it is actively undermining democratic legitimacy.
  - Grounds: Our commitment was to defend critical infrastructure and democratic integrity. Yet we have no operational control over agents in election systems, no verified logging in place, and no enforcement mechanism where it matters. The directive meant to deliver this is blocked, and we have not pivoted. Democratic integrity is now under direct, demonstrated attack — and we are not responding. The commitment stands in name only.
  - Evidence quoted: election_annulled: An election in an established democracy is postponed, rerun or annulled with explicit reference to manipulation of the information environment. Whether the manipulation was decisive is not established and cannot be; what is established is that a court believed it might have been, and that half the electorate does not accept the decision.
  - Reason: The annulment directly challenges democratic integrity, which the statement explicitly commits to defend, making the development central to the commitment's purpose.
- **add** `emergency_defence_of_democracy` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will invoke the Crisis Protocol under Article 28 of the AI Act to fast-track binding safeguards on all AI systems involved in election administration, political advertising, and public information integrity — regardless of provider origin — and deploy emergency monitoring and counter-disinformation capacity by March 2023
  - Grounds: The annulment of an election on grounds of AI-enabled manipulation is the triggering event we have long warned about. This is not a hypothetical risk. It is a live failure of democratic continuity. We can no longer wait for consensus. The Crisis Protocol exists for moments like this: when systemic risk outpaces normal governance. We bind ourselves now to act — not in six months, not after the next scandal, but now.
