# Statements: The European Union (turn 12)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience
- `no_single_point_of_failure` (position): No critical AI dependency — technical, infrastructural, or informational — will be allowed to rest on a single foreign provider or unaccountable private entity; this includes inference capacity, model weights, and the energy and spectrum required to run them — and we now extend this to include the political and financial architecture that funds and legitimizes them

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now affirm that without sovereignty in execution, neither mandate can be fulfilled, and that without political capital, sovereignty itself is a fiction
  - Grounds: The metric `eu_political_capital` has reached 0.0 — a systemic zero, not a temporary dip — exposing that even binding infrastructure investments mean nothing if the Union cannot act collectively. This update reflects a categorical shift: we are no longer constrained by capability alone, but by legitimacy, cohesion, and the exhaustion of trust across institutions. The storm is not only technological; it is political, and we are out of runway.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, but now with binding investment in reducing the opacity that creates uncertainty — especially in AI behaviour, intent inference, and frontier access dependencies — and we will anchor those investments in physical and legal infrastructure that cannot be revoked by remote decision — but only where such investment does not deepen the capital deficit that makes all action impossible
  - Grounds: The failure to gain traction on the Sovereign Compute Energy Assurance Pact, despite its necessity, reveals that acting under uncertainty now requires not just courage but triage. We cannot build the floor beneath our feet if the floor itself collapses the house. This update binds our doctrine to survivability: we will still act early, but we must now weigh each action against its cost to the Union’s ability to remain a coherent actor at all.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `no_single_point_of_failure` — applied
  - Tier: position
  - Text: No critical AI dependency — technical, infrastructural, or informational — will be allowed to rest on a single foreign provider or unaccountable private entity; this includes inference capacity, model weights, and the energy and spectrum required to run them — and we now extend this to include the political and financial architecture that funds and legitimizes them
  - Grounds: The bilateral deal struck by a member state with a U.S. provider, bypassing Union coordination, proves that sovereignty is not just about infrastructure but about alignment. A single point of political failure — a state acting alone under pressure — can unravel the whole. We must now treat fiscal and diplomatic cohesion as part of the stack.
