### The Floor Reinforced, But Not Raised

AI capability advanced by +2.5 to 98.5, continuing its steady climb into the terminal zone. At this level, frontier models self-modify in ways no human team can reconstruct, and their training trajectories are opaque even to their operators. The instrument is nearing saturation: above 85, growth is halved in effect due to diminishing resolution, but the underlying motion remains relentless.

Open-weight capability rose to 76.0, consistent with the established convergence dynamics—advancing by approximately half the gap toward ai_capability from the previous value of 67.5. This reflects ongoing distillation of leaked weights and incremental improvements in reimplementation across academic and grey-market clusters. No sudden breakthroughs occurred; the path remains one of gradual catch-up, not leapfrogging.

The **Sovereign Compute Energy Assurance Pact** was formally launched as a category 4 measure and designated the EU’s priority initiative under crisis provisions. Binding negotiations with ENTSO-E and BEREC commenced, but immediate resistance flared in two member states where energy ministers refused to cede grid priority to AI infrastructure. A compromise was reached: co-location guarantees and MFF funding for grid hardening softened opposition, but full cross-border obligations were deferred pending Council review. The measure is now in flight, with implementation expected over multiple turns.

The **Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory** did not complete integration this turn. Instead, final integration is now projected for the next turn, following delays in cross-agency data-sharing protocols and certification bottlenecks at ENISA. Sentinel nodes are operational in pilot mode, feeding limited telemetry, but full system integration remains pending. As such, its contribution to ai_safety is partial: +1 applied this turn from early data streams, with further gains expected upon completion.

The **Emergency Compute Redundancy Initiative** continues toward completion next turn, with site hardening ongoing in Luxembourg and Finland.

Political capital rose to 1.0. The proposal of the large-scale Energy Assurance Pact delivered a +4 proposal bonus. However, the portfolio charge of −7 (Emergency Compute Redundancy Initiative at −3, the new large measure at −3, and priority at −1) resulted in a net movement of +1. The Union remains fragile, but not entirely inert.

Resilience increased by +0.5, reflecting early infrastructure commitments under the Energy Assurance Pact, though full hardening remains distant. Public sentiment held at 25.0 — too low for legitimacy to lend capital, too high for restrictionist measures to gain goodwill.

### Municipal Momentum Meets National Gridlock

Eight cities expanded their informal compute-sharing network, linking municipal microgrids and emergency response AI under mutual aid protocols. No Brussels mandate authorized it — but neither could it be stopped.
