## New measure
**European Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with mandatory detection, segmentation and exercised cross-border response.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and it must come now because the autumn intrusion proved undetected persistence in EU grids while open-weight offensive cyber is now unrecallable.

## Priority
European Critical Systems Shield, because undetected persistence in transmission operators outranks even compute build-out while M1 and M2 already run.

## In practice
We task ENISA jointly with the CER Directive competent authorities and TSOs via the Electricity Coordination Group, funded by reprogrammed InvestAI and Connecting Europe Facility money, to impose network segmentation, continuous AI-anomaly monitoring, and 24-hour incident notification for designated transmission, port and water operators. The Energy and Transport Council formations carry the mandate; member states resisting cost-sharing are offered co-financed SOC capacity and EU procurement.

In parallel we keep M1 Gigafactories and M2 Tech sovereignty package moving through DG CNECT and accelerated-permitting zones, defending ASML supply-chain leverage and grid-connection fast-tracking against US pressure, without opening a parallel compute line. The Shield's exercises in late 2026 feed resilience requirements back into the AI Act general-purpose obligations due in 2027-2028.
