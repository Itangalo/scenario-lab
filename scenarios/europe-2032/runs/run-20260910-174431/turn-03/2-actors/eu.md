## New measure
**EU Systemic Cyber Cover Backstop**
Creates a temporary EU-coordinated state guarantee for systemic cyber cover for transmission, ports and water operators conditioned on Shield segmentation and monitoring milestones.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, and it must come now because insurer retrenchment after the autumn intrusions threatens to stall Shield retrofits and leave designated operators uncovered, while the open-weight frontier release puts unrecallable offensive capability on private hardware.

## Priority
European Critical Systems Shield, because the backstop only buys time and the implementing acts for segmentation, monitoring and 24-hour notification must still land in late 2027 before the next intrusion cycle.

## In practice
We push M3 through ENISA and the Energy and Transport Councils into enforceable implementing acts under NIS2/CER, using joint procurement and Digital Europe/CEF co-financed SOCs to settle the remaining large-capital cost dispute and close patchy monitoring coverage. The new backstop is tied to those milestones: DG FISMA and DG ENER with finance ministries stand up coordinated national guarantees with an EU reinsurance frame, with premiums and eligibility conditional on segmentation and sensor rollout.

In parallel we keep M1 gigafactory siting and M2 permitting zones moving via DG CNECT fast-track grid connections while defending chip-equipment leverage, and we direct the AI Office evaluation unit to publish interim offensive-cyber red-lines and feed Shield threat models. We answer the automated-decision scandal inside the AI Act frame as an enforcement failure, tasking national market-surveillance authorities with remedy and audit rather than opening a new legislative track now.
