### A Fractured Front Against Escalating Risk

Despite urgent action, the aftermath of the cyberattack continues to strain public trust and institutional cohesion. The Cyber Resilience Emergency Acceleration Directive advances—AI intrusion detection is now active in 85% of Tier 1 services, and automated failover systems prevent cascading outages in two new regions. Yet integration of the European Cyber Incident Response Corps remains stalled, with Italy and Poland withholding full data-sharing access, citing national sovereignty. Mutual aid protocols exist on paper only.

The launch of the Sovereign AI Safety Assurance Programme signals ambition, but meets immediate resistance. The U.S. government, under the ALLIANCE posture, expresses concern over access to model weights, calling the mandate “technically overbroad.” Major frontier labs threaten to pause updates to EU-facing models unless audit terms are renegotiated. Within the EU, Germany and the Netherlands warn of innovation flight, while civil society groups demand stronger safeguards following an automated decision scandal in French unemployment benefits, where thousands were wrongly sanctioned.
