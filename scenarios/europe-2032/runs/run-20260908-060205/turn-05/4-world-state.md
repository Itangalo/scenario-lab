### US Election Sets New Course

The United States elects a new administration committed to technological alliance. The promise of structured access to frontier AI systems—models exceeding 60.0 capability for vetted European institutions—arrives just as open-weight models demonstrate autonomous cyber operations. The offer includes joint safety evaluations and incident reporting, easing immediate fears of strategic dependency. US_POSTURE: pending (administration takes office next turn).

### Monitoring Gains, Mandate Stalls

The Secure Open-Source Frontier Monitoring Initiative achieves operational status, detecting autonomous reasoning in real time across major platforms. Red-team findings from Vienna and Paris expose planning capabilities in open models above 48.0. Yet enforcement falters: national data protection authorities issue conflicting rulings on the legality of training on public web data. The European Data Protection Board fails to unify interpretation, paralyzing the Commission’s draft implementing act for the open-weight evaluation mandate.

### Sovereignty: Leverage, Not Control

The ASML agreement enters force, embedding export notification and 30-day deferral into EU law. In return, €1.2bn in R&D funding flows to semiconductor nodes in Germany, the Netherlands, and—after last-minute concessions—Cork and Msida. Simultaneously, the EU activates a middle-power coalition with Japan, Taiwan, and South Korea, aligning export controls through a new Joint Semiconductor Oversight Cell in Brussels. This coordination secures supply-chain access, marking a quiet but strategic gain.

### Building the Future, Amid Doubt

The European Commission proposes the Autonomous AI Development Programme, a €45bn initiative to build sovereign exascale clusters, protect critical AI talent, and ensure EU-bound deployment. The proposal advances to Council deliberation, but full adoption and funding release are pending. Internal skepticism grows, with member states divided on duplication amid newfound US openness. No implementation occurs this turn, and the measure remains in development, with political cohesion beginning to show early strain. The EU maintains one active priority: advancing the open-weight evaluation mandate.
