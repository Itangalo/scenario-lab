### Capability Creeps, Open Weights Advance

AI capability rises modestly to 61.5 as frontier labs report incremental gains in reasoning coherence and system integration, though no breakthroughs emerge. Open-weight models advance more noticeably, reaching 53.0, aided by partial replication of planning modules from publicly available technical reports and improved training efficiency. While this narrows the gap slightly, open models remain behind closed systems in autonomy and scale.

### Emergency Powers Proposed, Implementation Delayed

The European Commission proposes invoking Article 16 TFEU to fast-track infrastructure at InvestAI Gigafactory sites in France and Germany, citing strategic urgency. However, full implementation is delayed pending legal review: regional governments have launched challenges over environmental assessments and procedural overreach. Construction has not yet begun, and the European Investment Bank confirms existing funding envelopes remain unchanged—no additional capital is released pending resolution.

### Sovereign Development Framework Advances, Funding Pending

The EU Autonomous AI Development Programme framework is advanced, with agreement on integrated team locations in Paris-Saclay, Munich, and Eindhoven. Access to EuroHPC exascale machines will be contingent on participation, strengthening long-term sovereign control. However, the proposed €4.2bn allocation lacks a confirmed funding source; negotiations with member states on reallocation or joint borrowing remain ongoing. Talent fast-tracks are activated, though recruitment remains below target. The programme's formal launch is deferred until funding pathways are secured, but its structural design progresses sufficiently to contribute to sovereignty.

### Evaluation Framework Faces Headwinds

Work continues on a unified evaluation framework for advanced open models, but full red-teaming requirements are not yet applied. For now, pilot testing begins with projects using public compute or ERC grants, while smaller initiatives receive interpretability vouchers. Developer skepticism persists, and trust erodes further as automation displaces junior roles in law and software—media narratives frame the shift as elite-driven disruption.

### Resilience Through Alliance

The EU joins a joint biosurveillance pact and real-time cyber telemetry network, enhancing detection and response capacity. This lifts resilience despite domestic fragility, cushioning potential systemic risks from distributed AI misuse.
