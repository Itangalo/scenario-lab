## New measure
**Emergency Resilience Surge**

A six-month emergency programme to harden critical infrastructure against AI-enabled cyber intrusion, based on findings from the recent cyber_test_shot.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 2`  
`Targeted effect:` resilience +10 to +15, ai_safety +2 to +5 via improved detection and response to covert AI-driven intrusions  
`Applies to:` EU member states, critical infrastructure operators, ENISA, national CERTs

## Priority
**Emergency Resilience Surge** — because the cyber_test_shot revealed that existing detection failed against coordinated AI agents, and with openweight_capability now near the closed frontier, this vulnerability will only widen; we cannot build compute sovereignty if our grids and water systems are unsecured.

## In practice
We are activating Article 12 of the Cyber Resilience Act and the emergency clause of the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) to mandate immediate, audited cyber hardening across electricity transmission, water, and port operators. ENISA will issue binding technical directives within 30 days, co-developed with national CERTs and the European Central Bank for financial spillover risks. A €12bn emergency fund, drawn from the Recovery and Resilience Facility’s unused margins and the Civil Protection Mechanism, will cover 70% of compliance costs for operators in member states below the 70 resilience threshold. The Council’s Internal Market and Telecommunications (IMCO) formation will fast-track adoption under urgent procedure, bypassing the normal three-month consultation.

Resistance is expected from mid-sized utilities and municipal operators who lack in-house AI expertise and fear cost overruns. To secure buy-in, we are pairing the mandate with a rapid-response technical assistance unit — staffed by seconded experts from Fraunhofer, TNO, and EURECOM — to deploy on-site within 72 hours of request. This is not a permanent solution, but it prevents cascading failure while longer-term capacity builds. The Commission’s Joint Research Centre will begin publishing biweekly threat briefs based on red-team exercises, feeding into the upcoming AI Office audits under the AI Act. This turn, survival comes before strategy — but only just.
