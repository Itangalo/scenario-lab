### The Open Frontier Widens

Global AI capability advances to 56.5 as self-improving training loops shorten development cycles. A second open-weight model, closely mirroring the latest closed system, spreads rapidly across research and underground networks, pushing openweight_capability to 48.0. This near-frontier diffusion intensifies concerns about uncontrolled autonomous reasoning and cyber exploitation, but no formal safety evaluation framework yet exists to contain it.

### EU Response: Monitoring and Leverage

The EU activates the Secure Open-Source Frontier Monitoring Initiative, deploying scanning tools across open platforms and establishing secure red-teaming labs. Early collaboration with Hugging Face yields limited data sharing, but resistance from decentralised AI communities stalls broader engagement. The initiative is operational but not yet effective at scale.

Simultaneously, the Commission launches negotiations with ASML and member states to formalise export control coordination on semiconductor lithography tools. While ASML expresses cautious support, U.S. officials signal strong opposition to any mechanism that dilutes unilateral control. Internal EU divisions emerge, with some member states fearing economic retaliation. The proposed tripartite forum with Japan and the U.S. stalls in early talks.

### Safety Erodes, Sovereignty Slips

With no safety upgrades to shipped models and capability rising sharply, ai_safety drops to 26.0, reflecting growing opacity and the absence of enforceable oversight for open models. The covert agent incident remains unresolved, compounding institutional unease.

eu_ai_sovereignty declines to 19.0 as global compute advances outpace EU efforts, and no domestic capacity milestone is reached. The ASML initiative, while symbolically significant, has not yet secured tangible control.

eu_political_capital falls to 36.0 under the sustained burden of three large ongoing measures and the priority cost. The new category 8 measure adds strain but yields no immediate gain, as transatlantic friction mounts. public_sentiment holds steady at 42.0, with no major incident or benefit shifting public mood.
