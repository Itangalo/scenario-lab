## In practice
We are treating the completion of the InvestAI Gigafactories not as an endpoint but as a launchpad: construction delays in France and Germany remain unresolved, but the emergency permitting framework is now legally active, and we are redirecting the first €12bn tranche to pre-position grid connections, cooling infrastructure, and secure perimeter fencing at both sites, conditioned on binding agreements that model weights will be stored exclusively on EU-jurisdiction hardware with no remote administrative override. This is not merely industrial policy — it is jurisdictional hardening. At the same time, the Secure Open-Source Frontier Monitoring Initiative has proven its value: Vienna and Paris red teams have published findings of autonomous planning loops in two open models above 48.0, including one that generated zero-day exploits and tested them in sandboxed networks. Yet without enforcement teeth, detection is just warning. The Commission’s draft implementing act remains frozen due to the EDPB split on training data legality, so we are bypassing the impasse: today we table a Council regulation under Article 16 TFEU, proposing that any entity accessing EuroHPC resources or ERC AI grants must submit open models above 48.0 to mandatory third-party evaluation, with non-compliance triggering suspension of access. We are offering audit-safe exemptions for small-scale academic work, and pairing this with a new public interpretability fund under category 5 to help developers meet the standard. This creates a de facto baseline without requiring unanimous legal interpretation. Separately, we are activating the Anti-Coercion Instrument’s digital clause in quiet coordination with Tokyo and Seoul, leveraging the Joint Semiconductor Oversight Cell to signal that unilateral export shifts affecting EU access will trigger review — not to escalate, but to lock in the current cooperation dynamic while we build. The U.S. alliance gives breathing room, but not independence. Our priority now is clear: sovereignty is not access — it is origin. And we are building from the ground up.
