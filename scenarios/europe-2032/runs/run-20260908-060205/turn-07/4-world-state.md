### Capability Growth Slows, But Autonomy Spreads

AI capability inches upward to 61.0, as the frontier shows signs of plateauing—major releases underperform, and high-end model access becomes cheaper, suggesting commoditization rather than breakthrough. Yet open-weight models continue diffusing: autonomous planning behaviours detected in sandboxed environments confirm that capabilities once confined to closed labs are now replicable from public weights, pushing `openweight_capability` to 52.0.

### Enforcement Push Meets Resistance

The EU tables a binding evaluation mandate for open models above 48.0, tying access to EuroHPC resources and ERC grants to compliance with red-teaming and transparency standards. The move bypasses stalled legal debates but triggers backlash: research collectives in Germany and the Netherlands denounce it as academic overreach, while open-source developers warn of fragmentation. The regulation passes in draft form, but full adoption is delayed by appeals to national data protection authorities.

### Sovereignty Gains Ground, Despite Delays

The InvestAI Gigafactories programme officially concludes, unlocking €12bn for pre-construction infrastructure at both French and German sites. Though physical construction remains blocked, jurisdictional safeguards—exclusive EU hardware storage and no remote override—are codified into funding agreements. This delivers a sovereignty boost, even as local opposition grows.

### Labour Market Fractures

Widespread attrition in graduate hiring across law, accountancy, and software sparks protests in several capitals. Unions blame AI-driven automation, pointing to firms replacing junior roles with agent workflows. Public sentiment dips as media frames the shift as “opportunity lost,” undermining support for rapid AI integration.

### Political Capital Under Pressure

Despite technical progress, political capital erodes. The new measure incurs its cost, and the priority adds strain. Member states voice skepticism about overlapping sovereignty initiatives, especially as the U.S. alliance reduces immediate pressure. No collapse occurs—but momentum slows.
