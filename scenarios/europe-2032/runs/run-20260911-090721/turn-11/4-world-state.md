### Containment weeks
Autumn brought the release health planners had feared. A modified agent with clear links to model-assisted design spread from a lab lapse compounded by deliberate sharing, and hospitals in three member states moved to isolation protocols. Casualties mounted into the dozens before cordons held. HERA and the disease control centre worked within their existing mandates, coordinating stockpile draws and cross-border information sharing.

The response was visible, and uneven, and overstretched. Certified hospitals that had passed the earlier drills absorbed surge patients and pushed mandatory reporting into emergency channels. Smaller clinics and town networks, still without the new patching tooling, reverted to paper triage and manual shutdowns when probing traffic spiked alongside the outbreak. Officials acknowledged that detector audits and medical team deployments were delayed by staffing and logistical limits, with wider coverage pending. Engineers again described protected cores and brittle edges. No new EU measure was introduced this turn; the response operated under previously authorised instruments.

### What could not be recalled
Within weeks of the outbreak, a new openly released model family landed almost at the closed frontier. It built on several openly tracked training runs and distributed fine-tunes from the spring, and downloads ran to hundreds of thousands in days. Forensics later found its assistance in the bio-design chain, but by then copies were on private hardware everywhere. Takedown requests circulated to major hosts and mirrors, with mixed and slow responses.

Two other developments cut across the gloom. Atlantic labs published a control result that made deployed-system behaviour measurably more predictable in limited tests, and European hospitals and large utilities began scoping installation where certification allowed, with wider rollout still pending validation. Separately, a joint European-Japanese materials finding — AI-designed alloys cutting electrolyser costs — promised industrial payoff within a year, though factories saw little of it yet.

### The guarantee, constrained
Against this, the entry-level income-bridging and retraining vouchers continued as limited pilots for laid-off graduates in law, accountancy and customer operations. Broader disbursement was not possible this turn: no reallocation envelope had been established in prior turns, finance ministries said further scale-up would require new borrowing and would wait for next year's budget round, and implementation remains phased over future turns. Student unions said it was late and thin. Trust in information and institutions kept falling as grief and fraud dominated coverage.

Brussels opened intensive talks with the holdout capital on aligning supply controls for the duration of containment, offering joint procurement access and emergency stockpile sharing. Officials on both sides described the exchanges as ongoing, with no agreement yet reached and no harmonization effects yet recorded.
