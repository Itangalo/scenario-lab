By late 2032 the EU was running but deeply distrusted after a dual bio-cyber shock.

Defenders lagged as malware persisted via helper software and bio-tests faltered; joint containment-law teams eventually restored backups, segmented networks, and deployed mobile detection. Certified hospitals with signed assistants triaged faster; uncertified towns waited.

Brussels launched a late, still-staffing joint bio-cyber surge from health/digital funds. Private AI capital fled in autumn, valuations collapsed, hosting/hardware deals evaporated, gigafactory shells stayed shells; ministers shifted to holding steady. Holdout capital took restoration/medical help but again refused common export terms.

Public mood darkened over fraud, cancelled care, and engineered-disease fear; threats against contractors and intrusion at a compute site continued. By December services were patched and slower, pathogen contained, at cost of trust.
