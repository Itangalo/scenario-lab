## Priority
**Emergency Resilience Surge** — because the intrusion revealed systemic fragility that cannot wait for industrial build-out, and public confidence will erode if recovery depends only on foreign-controlled systems during a crisis.

## In practice
This turn, we are activating Article 12 of the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) to mandate immediate threat detection upgrades across energy transmission operators, major ports and water utilities in affected member states. The Cybersecurity Agency (ENISA) is deploying rapid-response teams to conduct live penetration testing and oversee implementation of AI-driven anomaly detection, funded through a €12bn emergency allocation from the Crisis Shield instrument under NextGenerationEU. We are coordinating with national CIRTs and requiring cross-border mutual aid protocols to be exercised by Q1 2027.

At the same time, we are pressing ahead with the InvestAI Gigafactories programme, finalising site selections in Finland, Spain and Poland with state aid approvals under the Marguerite II fund. The Commission is negotiating grid priority access with transmission system operators, backed by proposed amendments to the TEN-E regulation to fast-track interconnectors. Resistance from local communities over energy draw is being met with binding commitments to co-locate green hydrogen plants and guarantee district heating off-take. Meanwhile, the Tech Sovereignty Package is advancing permitting reforms in seven member states through peer-review pressure in the Competitiveness Council, leveraging cohesion funds as incentives.
