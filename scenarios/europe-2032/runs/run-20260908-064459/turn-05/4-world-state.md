### ASML Defection Undermines Leverage

In a major blow, a member state signs a bilateral deal with a U.S. hyperscaler, granting preferential access to AI training infrastructure in exchange for relaxed data rules. This member_state_defection shatters the EU’s unified stance, exposing deep fragmentation. The ASML Sovereignty Accord, already stalled, is now effectively abandoned as a viable policy, surviving only as a cautionary benchmark.

### Gigafactories Prioritized Amid Crisis

With external leverage failing, the EU declares InvestAI Gigafactories its priority initiative. Emergency funding is proposed and fast-tracked through legal overrides, accelerating site development in Finland and Spain. However, these moves provoke local resistance and legal challenges, limiting immediate progress and straining cohesion.

### Resilience Holds, Public Mood Shifts

The Emergency Resilience Surge completes, hardening two major energy operators. Yet enforcement is delayed for non-compliant sites to avoid amplifying public anxiety. Meanwhile, public sentiment dips as high-profile AI incidents dominate headlines, eroding trust in both public and private deployments.
