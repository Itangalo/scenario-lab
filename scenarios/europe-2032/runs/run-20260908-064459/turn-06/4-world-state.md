### Frontier Gains Amid Safety Erosion

AI capability advances to 62.5 as automated verification in law, engineering and clinical coding enables rapid, self-improving development cycles. Though no full autonomy breach occurs, opaque reasoning deepens, and labs struggle to explain model decisions post-hoc. With no safety measure landing on shipped systems, assurance erodes further.

### Open Frontier Widens Gradually

An open-weight release matching the closed frontier within months — not years — begins to flood high-capability models into distributed hands. Openweight capability rises to 50.0, reflecting early diffusion and uptake, but deployment bottlenecks and infrastructure gaps prevent faster accumulation. The EU’s Secure Open-Weight Access pilot in Strasbourg initiates audits of newly available models, though interpretability tools lag behind capability.

### Labour Market Fractures

Entry-level roles in law, accounting and software vanish as firms deploy AI workflows. Graduate hiring stalls across the bloc, feeding public discontent. Media frames the shift as "degree obsolescence," intensifying backlash against AI adoption.

### Sovereignty Efforts Stall

The ASML Sovereignty Accord remains inactive, with no formal termination yet declared; internal disagreements persist following a member state’s bilateral engagement, but unity has not fully shattered. The Tech Sovereignty Package secures only €82bn in private investment — less than half its target — and is not renewed. InvestAI Gigafactories accelerate in Finland and Spain via emergency powers, but legal challenges delay grid connections, and the one-turn compression fails.

### Enclaves Advance Under Integration

The EU’s Sovereign Evaluation and Execution Enclaves continue integration with permitting reforms, progressing toward operational status. Initial testing environments are established, but full deployment is delayed by coordination hurdles and audit complexity. Public sentiment dips to 37.0 amid job losses and perceived elite detachment. Resilience slips as enforcement delays face legal pushback.

### US Posture Confirmed

The new US administration takes office, declaring a policy of structured cooperation. The EU’s Joint Access proposal fails — Washington demands unacceptable concessions — but dialogue continues on evaluation standards.
