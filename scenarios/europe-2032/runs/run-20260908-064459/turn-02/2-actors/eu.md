## In practice
This turn, we are seizing the opening in the ASML negotiations by convening a high-level task force under the Competitiveness Council, co-chaired by the Commissioner for Internal Market and the Vice-President for A Europe Fit for the Digital Age. The goal is to finalise a binding accord by Q2 2027 that places export licensing authority for EU-based ASML systems under a newly established European Semiconductor Oversight Board, with voting rights apportioned among member states and the Commission. This would allow the Union to condition access to extreme ultraviolet (EUV) and next-generation high-NA tools on compliance with EU AI sovereignty standards — including data sovereignty, model transparency, and restrictions on military use — effectively turning lithography into a strategic instrument rather than a passive export. We are offering ASML legal immunity for past compliance with U.S. directives and guaranteeing €8bn in R&D co-funding through Horizon Europe and the Chips Joint Undertaking in exchange for institutional alignment.

Simultaneously, we are pressing forward with the Emergency Resilience Surge, deploying ENISA rapid-response teams to conduct live red-teaming exercises at three major energy transmission operators and two critical ports identified as compromised in the recent intrusion. These exercises are triggering mandatory upgrades to AI-driven anomaly detection systems, with full deployment required by Q3 2027 under Article 12 of DORA. Non-compliant operators face temporary suspension of EU grid access, a measure we are enforcing through the Agency for the Cooperation of Energy Regulators (ACER). In parallel, the InvestAI Gigafactories programme is advancing site finalisation in Finland and Spain, with the Commission offering grid priority access in exchange for binding commitments to reserve 40% of initial capacity for public-sector AI workloads — including safety evaluations and crisis modelling. Poland remains hesitant, citing grid instability, so we are redirecting €12bn from the Marguerite II fund to fast-track a new interconnector between Silesia and the Baltic Energy Market.
