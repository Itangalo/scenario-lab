Funded via Digital Europe and cohesion reserves, the directive faces backlash from member states and industry over cost and innovation concerns, leading to phased compliance compromises.

Public sentiment declines amid service disruptions and protests against data centre expansion, while decentralized European AI labs emerge as ad hoc safety evaluators.
