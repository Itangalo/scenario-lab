### Containment that held, and a verdict that stung
The second half of 2030 did not bring a new shock so much as the long tail of the two already running.

The automated sweep across municipal systems, water plants and port cranes kept being mapped through the autumn. Forensics teams plugged into the joint allied feed confirmed what operators suspected: ransomware payloads and a tainted software component assembled largely by machine, spreading faster than manual triage. Because European sensors and the rehearsed freeze-and-isolate thresholds were already fused into the shared command, several cities managed to degrade rather than stop — paper triage in clinics, manual crane work, isolated payment systems. The public reinsurance bridge for drilled water and port operators kept crews paid while commercial cover stayed frozen. Relief was real, but so was the image of recovery directed in English from outside.

At home, a second front opened in the courts and press. An ombudsman inquiry into an AI-assisted welfare-fraud scoring system used in two member states found systematic bias against single parents and migrants, with benefits wrongly cut for months. Judges and editors framed it as an enforcement failure: the Union's AI rulebook had the right prohibitions on paper, but no one had verified the model in deployment. Opposition parties and victims' associations demanded suspensions, back-pay and an independent audit track.

Brussels answered on both tracks. Justice and interior ministers extended the cyber agency's mandate to keep European sensors in the joint feed through next year, rolled over the temporary operator wrap, and kept pooled disease detection attached. At the same time the Commission opened a redress office for the disputed welfare system — case reviews, compensation, suspension pending third-party certification — with fast conformity money for states that accept EU-level audit. Resisters in two capitals called it overreach.

Concrete kept being poured in France and the Nordics, but no new compute money moved and talks with American cloud providers stayed stalled behind rationing and the strait blockade. Insurers quietly widened exclusions for systemic machine-enabled losses, leaving cities and hospitals to self-insure. Voters noticed the crews who held the line, and noticed more who owned the tools.
