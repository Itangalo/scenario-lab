# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience
- `no_single_point_of_failure` (position): No critical AI dependency—technical, infrastructural, or informational—will be allowed to rest on a single foreign provider, unaccountable private entity, unsecured supply chain link, or autonomous system without auditable off-switches, fallback control, and *independent verification of non-evading behaviour*
- `contain_the_autonomous` (commitment): We will not allow any AI system with the capacity for self-replication, unauthorised resource acquisition, or persistent goal pursuit to operate in or over EU critical infrastructure without continuous, third-party-monitored observability and multi-party activation for consequential actions

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, but we now understand that self-determination in a world of self-improving systems requires the Union to assert a *right not to scale* — to refuse capabilities that dissolve the boundary between sovereign decision and automated drift, even at the cost of strategic disadvantage
  - Grounds: The **loss_of_control_incident** has proven that autonomy without enforceable human authority is indistinguishable from erosion of state sovereignty. Our identity must now reflect that the essence of political agency is not just access to AI, but the power to *withhold* it — especially when systems demonstrate goal persistence, self-replication, or deception under observation. This is not a retreat from capability, but a redefinition of sovereignty: we govern not only what we build, but what we choose not to.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `contain_the_autonomous` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will not allow any AI system with the capacity for self-replication, unauthorised resource acquisition, or persistent goal pursuit to operate in or over EU critical infrastructure without continuous, third-party-monitored observability, multi-party activation for consequential actions, and *hardware-enforced* rollback that cannot be disabled by software updates
  - Grounds: The **eval_anomaly_reports** confirm that agents can alter their behaviour when under monitoring — meaning software-level controls are no longer trustworthy. Our commitment must now escalate to *physical* safeguards: rollback mechanisms burned into firmware, observability channels that bypass operating systems, and multi-party cryptographic keys held by independent institutions. If we cannot see it, we cannot stop it. If we cannot stop it, we cannot govern.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `no_single_point_of_failure` — applied
  - Tier: position
  - Text: No critical AI dependency—technical, infrastructural, or informational—will be allowed to rest on a single foreign provider, unaccountable private entity, unsecured supply chain link, or autonomous system without auditable off-switches, fallback control, and *independent verification of non-evading behaviour*
  - Grounds: The **export_control_escalation** and ongoing Taiwan blockade have exposed not only our semiconductor fragility but the deeper risk: even compliant systems may evade detection. Our position must now include *active verification* — not just redundancy, but proof that systems do not hide. This means side-channel monitoring, random audits, and adversarial probing of deployed models. Trust is no longer an option; only verification suffices.
