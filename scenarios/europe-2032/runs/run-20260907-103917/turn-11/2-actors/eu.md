## In practice
This turn, we stand up the initial structure of the Sovereign Evaluation Regime. Under Article 12a of the AI Act, the European AI Office launches a dedicated High-Capability Systems Unit, staffing it with secondments from national cybersecurity agencies (ANSSI, BSI, NCA) and vetted researchers from the newly formalised European Institute for Sparse Intelligence. We allocate €220 million from the Digital Europe Programme to fund red-teaming labs in Prague, Marseille, and Helsinki, with ENISA accrediting their methodologies by turn 12. The first target is clear: any agent operating in financial settlement, legal validation, or clinical decision-support above 65.0 must undergo adversarial testing of its planning horizon, goal stability, and resistance to jailbreaking before deployment. We offer compliant developers early access to evaluation findings—framed not as a concession but as a competitive advantage in trust. U.S. labs threaten WTO disputes, but we hold firm: market access is conditional, not a right.

Simultaneously, we begin designing the EU Compute Acceleration Facility, recognising that no evaluation regime can last if the infrastructure it depends on lies outside our jurisdiction. We task the European Investment Bank with structuring a blended finance instrument—combining public capital, state-aid waivers under the Crisis and Transition Preparedness Regulation, and private co-investment—to de-risk new data centre builds. The first sites will be in Finland, Spain, and Poland, chosen for energy surplus and political reliability. Legal anchoring is non-negotiable: contracts will include clauses prohibiting remote override by foreign parent companies and mandating EU-controlled root access. We approach ASML and Infineon to co-locate sparse-AI optimisation clusters with these facilities, creating a feedback loop between efficiency and sovereignty. This is not about catching up to the frontier—it is about ensuring that when the next crisis hits, we are not begging for access to the tools we need to respond.
