The goal is clear: convert coordination into commitment. This is not about forming a new bloc, but about making our collective leverage unignorable. The supply_chain_coercion incident proves that unilateral chokepoints are unstable; only together can we ensure that no single power can weaponise the supply chain against us. The pact will be negotiated under the auspices of the Digital Markets and Security Council, with signing targeted for turn 15.
