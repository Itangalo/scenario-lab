### Strain Beneath the Surface

Despite bold intentions, the Union’s bandwidth is stretched. Investor confidence in the Tech Sovereignty Package wanes further as global signals shift. ASML negotiations remain stalled, and Gigafactories face delays in grid integration. The vision of a third pole in AI stands unchallenged in principle—but in practice, execution falters under cumulative load.

Political capital remains unchanged at 42.0. The portfolio charge continues to reflect three ongoing large measures—InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty Package, and Cyber Shield—at −3 each, and the current priority at −1, totaling −10. However, since no new measure is formally introduced this turn, no additional burden is incurred, and crucially, no proposal bonus is awarded. Political capital therefore does not drop.
