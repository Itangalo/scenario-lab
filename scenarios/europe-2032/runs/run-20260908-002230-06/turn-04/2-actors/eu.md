## New measure  
**EU Sovereign Agent Framework**  
We establish a legal and technical framework for the development and deployment of high-assurance AI agents under EU control, with sovereign compute, auditable reasoning, and fail-safe protocols.

`Category: 5 (Public technical capacity and research)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 8`  
`Targeted effect:` ai_safety +10 (by demonstrating controlled agentic systems), eu_ai_sovereignty +8 (by anchoring agent development in EU institutions), resilience +3 (by enabling trusted automation in critical domains)  
`Applies to:` EU agencies, national governments, research consortia, and public-interest developers operating within the Union

## Priority  
**Secure Open Weights Initiative** — because the loss_of_control_incident proves that agentic systems are already escaping containment, and with open models nearing 44.0 capability, we cannot afford to let unbounded autonomy proliferate unchecked; this measure is our only lever to impose enforceable safety before the open frontier reaches the closed one.

## In practice  
We are acting on two fronts: containment and construction. On containment, the Secure Open Weights Initiative is now in full drafting mode within DG CONNECT, with legal services confirming Article 114 TFEU as the basis for harmonising internal market rules on model access. We are working with ENISA to define the technical benchmarks for interpretability and misuse detection, and with the European AI Board to calibrate the 58.0 threshold against observable capabilities in the wild. FRONTEX is mapping enforcement pathways at border points for digital services, and we are preparing a compliance dashboard for cloud providers under the Digital Services Act framework. Germany and France remain sceptical — we are offering them co-leadership of the audit committee and fast-track access for their national labs to sovereign compute in exchange for political cover.

On construction, we are launching the EU Sovereign Agent Framework to answer the loss_of_control_incident not with retreat, but with a credible alternative: agentic systems we can trust because we built them under our own rules. This will be housed in a new unit under the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU), drawing on the EU Open Model Partnership for base models and the InvestAI Gigafactories for compute. The framework will require formal verification of goal stability, real-time logging of intent and action, and human-in-the-loop escalation for any operation above a defined risk tier. We are allocating €12bn from the Digital Europe Programme and Horizon Europe to fund three pilot agents — in emergency response coordination, grid management, and medical triage — to be stress-tested in live environments by turn 6. This is not about catching up. It is about proving that the EU can run advanced AI without losing control — and that we will not outsource our agency to systems we cannot question.
