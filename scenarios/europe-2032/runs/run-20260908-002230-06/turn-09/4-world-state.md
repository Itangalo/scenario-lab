### The Initiative Taken, the Cost Felt

Global AI capability advances to 62.5, entering the 62–68 terminal zone. Progress remains steady but significant, driven by self-reinforcing automation in research and cyber operations. A major cyber incident sweeps across public health and transport systems in three member states, exploiting AI-generated zero-days. Though containment eventually holds, response is slow and fragmented—resilience absorbs some damage, but not all. The attack’s tooling traces back to open-weight models now capable of autonomous exploit chaining.

Verification widens dramatically when a leading legal tech firm demonstrates fully automated contract review with near-perfect accuracy, validated by a new cryptographic benchmark. The domain joins code and mathematics as one where AI progress can self-verify—accelerating capability gains and undermining claims that human oversight remains essential.

### Sovereignty Gains Ground
