**EU Sovereign Agent Launch Programme**  
We fund, staff, and deploy the first operational high-assurance AI agents on sovereign EU compute, using the EU Sovereign Agent Framework to run real public missions under auditable conditions.

## Priority
**EU Sovereign Agent Launch Programme** — because the Sovereign Compute Acceleration Directive has delivered physical capacity, and the EU Sovereign Agent Framework has delivered design rules, but unless we now run agents in the wild under EU authority, both remain inert — and with offensive AI tools already in circulation and verification widening into new domains, we must demonstrate sovereign operational capability or concede the initiative to those who do.

## In practice
This turn, we transition from infrastructure to operation. The Finnish and Spanish gigafactories are now grid-connected and under legal designation as critical infrastructure, with 70% of initial compute capacity allocated. The European AI Office, in coordination with DG CONNECT and the European Cybersecurity Agency, begins provisioning secure enclaves for the first wave of sovereign agent deployments. We are launching three pilot missions: one to support cross-border pandemic forecasting with auditable model chains, one to automate regulatory compliance checks for SMEs, and one to assist national cyberdefence units in real-time threat analysis — all running under the EU Sovereign Agent Framework’s audit and fail-safe protocols.

Germany and Austria, though still cautious about autonomy, have agreed to participate in the compliance and public health pilots under strict co-governance terms, in exchange for guaranteed access to retraining funds from the AI Transition Hub programme. We are drawing on the European Innovation Council’s rapid deployment budget and reallocating 12bn from the now-completed Tech Sovereignty Package to cover staffing, security certification, and red-team exercises. The agents will not operate at frontier capability, but they will be the first AI systems of meaningful capability under direct EU control — legible, bounded, and accountable. This is not about matching the frontier yet; it is about proving we can act on it without permission.
