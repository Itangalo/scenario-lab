### The Open Floodgates

Global AI capability advances to 55.0 as automatic verification systems now routinely validate complex legal contracts and clinical trial designs, accelerating deployment across regulated sectors. The leap intensifies pressure on governance systems already struggling to keep pace.

A frontier-capable open model is released, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times within days, and begins running on consumer-grade hardware worldwide. While the release sparks immediate global concern — particularly for its capabilities in autonomous cyber exploitation and synthetic media generation — the technical impact is incremental rather than transformative. The open-weight frontier rises to 44.0, reflecting rapid adoption and early adaptation, but not an overnight parity with closed models. The gap between closed and open frontiers narrows significantly, yet structural limitations in training infrastructure, data quality, and optimization prevent a full convergence in one turn.

In response, the EU launches the **Secure Open Weights Initiative**, a bold regulatory framework to impose safety constraints on high-capability open models. Framed as infrastructure security, it mandates interpretability, provenance checks, and misuse monitoring for any model above 58.0 to access EU cloud, hosting, or payment systems. The measure is fast-tracked as the Union’s priority.

However, political costs mount rapidly. The portfolio now includes four large measures, each costing −3, plus the priority surcharge of −1, totaling −13. The proposal bonus is +5 — justified by the severity and recency of the openweight release and the campaign backlash, both direct triggers. Yet this gain is overwhelmed by the ongoing burden. Political capital drops to 35.0 despite a slight public sentiment rise to 46.0, driven by framing the initiative as protective rather than restrictive.

`ai_safety` increases to 43.0 due to the signal the new regulation sends, even though implementation is months away — early commitments from major hosting providers lend credibility. `resilience` edges up to 42.0 as contingency planning begins.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 21.0: the +0.5 from Gigafactories and +0.5 from Tech Sovereignty Package in flight are outweighed by the −1 decay from rising `ai_capability` and no capacity-securing event.

### Fractures Beneath Unity

Resistance emerges from within. Germany and France express reservations about enforcement feasibility. Open-source coalitions denounce the plan as a backdoor ban. The Commission presses forward, but cohesion frays under the strain. The full technical and institutional implications of the new initiative will take time to unfold — initial compliance is partial, and enforcement mechanisms remain in development.
