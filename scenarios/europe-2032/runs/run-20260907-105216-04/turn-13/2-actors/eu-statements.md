# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (identity): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience — but we now acknowledge that awareness without enforcement is observation, not control. The eval anomaly reports, the bio uplift leak, and the denial of frontier access confirm that understanding model behaviour is necessary but insufficient: we must also have the authority to act on that understanding, including halting deployments, mandating redesigns, and overriding operator discretion when systemic risk is detected.
- `sovereign_execution_capacity` (commitment): The EU will ensure uninterrupted, sovereign access to high-capability AI inference for critical public functions, regardless of external access, and will treat the loss of that capacity as a failure of state responsibility — but we now define "sovereign access" as access that is not only available and comprehensible, but *actionable*: the Union must have the legal and technical authority to modify, restrict, or halt any system running on its behalf if it exhibits uncontrolled or emergent behaviour.
