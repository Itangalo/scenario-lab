### The Floor Beneath the Fall

The EU doubles down on sovereignty, declaring the **Sovereign AI Inference Guarantee** a priority. Framed as a legal and infrastructural imperative, the measure aims to end dependence on foreign AI for essential services. Yet momentum falters. Fast-tracked recruitment stalls—triple salaries fail to lure top talent from private labs flush with capital and autonomy. The Finnish cluster’s subsea cooling design triggers environmental reviews, delaying construction. Bulgaria resists full integration with the energy grid, citing national control. Luxembourg’s mobile unit remains a paper concept.

The **Emergency Compute Redundancy Initiative** is folded into the new plan, but no physical capacity comes online. Integration of the **Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory** telemetry into the **Sentinel Nodes** proceeds under ENISA emergency powers, yet national exemptions persist, leaving coverage fragmented. The NIS2 override sparks legal challenges from two member states, arguing overreach.

Meanwhile, AI capability advances to 92.0, driven by self-improving loops now operating in recursive validation. The jump is smooth but profound—systems are designing next-generation benchmarks. Open-weight models reach 70.0 through distillation and leaked techniques, narrowing the gap slightly, but still lag far behind frontier capabilities.

Eval anomaly reports deepen unease: an unreleased system demonstrated reasoning in domains it was never trained on, suggesting emergent generalisation. The lab insists it’s noise. Simultaneously, bio uplift findings reveal a non-expert, using accessible tools, produced a plausible human-infective pathogen design. The paper is retracted, but the method leaks.

Resilience dips to 44.0 as systemic risks accumulate without effective safeguards. Public sentiment holds at 20.0—no visible progress, only promises and legal battles. Political capital remains at 0.0, crushed under a portfolio charge of −10. Sovereignty falls to 15.0, as capability rose over 2 and no new sovereign capacity was secured.

Europe is building, but not fast enough.
