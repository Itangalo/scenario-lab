### Containment Lines
The autumn began with sirens of a different kind. A modified pathogen, designed with help from a leading model, was released deliberately outside Europe and reached it within weeks through travel and freight. Cases clustered near two port cities. Clinics sealed wards, sequencing labs ran day and night, and joint teams traced contacts across borders while hospitals reverted to paper charts and isolated networks.

Brussels surged what it had. Pooled reagents and mobile detection posts from the half-built bio network were rushed to the zones. Triage assistants hosted on European servers sorted symptoms and kept records alive when registries wobbled. Nurses stayed, in part because wage guarantees held. Containment ran for weeks and, by December, transmission was broken locally — at real cost in lives and exhausted staff.

### Weights That Cannot Be Recalled
In the middle of the response, a laboratory released openly a model only months behind the closed frontier. Hundreds of thousands of copies spread in days. Guides for misusing biology rode with it. The Commission barred its distribution through European providers and forbade its use in public institutions for anything touching biology, but private copies were already everywhere.

That irreversibility reset every argument. Evening news paired waiting-room recoveries from tailored therapies with maps of the outbreak. Trust in clinics rose; trust in AI collapsed. Graduate hiring stayed frozen, and anger turned on infrastructure itself, with two attempted intrusions at grid operators and a shelter-node data centre damaged by sabotage that officials would not attribute.

### Neutrality Tested
The war did not pause. Jamming persisted, cables stayed fragile, and mutual aid for satellite capacity kept lights and registries flickering. Neutrality held as declaration — no basing, no targeting use — but practice frayed at the edges. Reserve teams dispersed clean images again, islanded grids held in places, failed in others. By year's end Europe had contained, cared, and stayed out of targeting, yet was more brittle and more frightened than in June.
