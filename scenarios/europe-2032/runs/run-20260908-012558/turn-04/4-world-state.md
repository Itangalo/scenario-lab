### Capability Creeps Forward  
Global AI capability advances steadily by +2.5 this turn, reaching 61.5. The verification widening in law, medicine and engineering enables faster, self-supervised refinement in these domains, accelerating progress. Open-weight models rise to 49.0, as leaked training data and open-source fine-tuning narrow the gap—still behind, but no longer trivial.

### Emergency Corps Stands Ready  
The EU AI Emergency Response Corps is formally activated after its first cross-border exercise in the Benelux region. The drill simulates a coordinated AI-driven grid disruption, and while response coordination proves functional, interoperability gaps delay containment by several hours. Still, the completion of this category 6 measure delivers a +3 boost to resilience, now at 41.0, reflecting improved institutional readiness.

### Sovereign Compute Push Meets Resistance  
The push to repurpose national HPC clusters under EU control advances in Italy, Spain and the Nordic states, but Germany and France demand concessions. A compromise grants them co-leadership and reserved capacity, preserving momentum. With the category 4 measure still in flight and capability rising, sovereignty gains +1 from progress and loses −1 from dependence, while the *middle_power_coalition*’s earlier access gains still provide residual stability. Net gain: +1, sovereignty reaches 25.0.

### Labour Guarantee Launched Amid Skepticism  
The EU launches the AI Labour Transition Guarantee, offering displaced public-sector workers wage replacement and retraining. The measure, large and category 7, earns a +2 sentiment bump as unions cautiously welcome it. However, early rollout in Belgium and Portugal faces administrative bottlenecks, tempering public optimism. Sentiment rises to 34.0.

### Political Capital Holds  
Four large measures remain in flight, costing −12, with priority adding −1. But the completion of the Emergency Corps yields +3, and the Labour Guarantee—responding directly to recent displacement waves—earns a proposal bonus of +3. Net change: −7, offset by +6, leaving political capital at 38.0.
