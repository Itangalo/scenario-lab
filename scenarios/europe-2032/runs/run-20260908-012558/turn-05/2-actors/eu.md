Germany and France have accepted co-leadership of the oversight board and 20% reserved capacity for national security use, but resistance persists in Poland and Hungary, where we are offering binding energy-for-access swaps: grid priority in exchange for full compliance. Legal challenges are expected, but we are moving under crisis authority, and the first clusters will go live by turn 6.

The InvestAI Gigafactories programme remains on track, with site selection finalised in Finland, Spain, and northern Italy. The European Investment Bank is structuring blended finance packages using state aid under Article 107(3)(c), and we are fast-tracking grid and cooling upgrades via TEN-E corridors. However, rising energy pressure from the emergent_ai_driven_energy_crisis_pressure event forces us to tie each gigafactory disbursement to local renewable capacity expansion — no new compute without new clean power. This conditionality is slowing Poland’s participation, but strengthens long-term resilience and public legitimacy.

Meanwhile, the AI Labour Transition Guarantee is rolling out in pilot regions, with Belgium and Portugal establishing verification protocols for displacement events in legal and public IT sectors. The European Social Fund+ is being redirected to fund training partnerships with universities and hospitals, aiming for 120,000 new AI-augmented public roles by mid-2030. Early bottlenecks in wage verification are being addressed through a central EU portal under development by the Commission’s Digital Directorate. Public sentiment remains fragile, but the visible response to displacement is preventing escalation — for now. We are holding our course, but we are out of slack.
