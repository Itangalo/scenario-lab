However, the Labour Transition Guarantee falters due to bureaucratic inefficiencies, triggering protests and declining public sentiment. A growing safety crisis emerges as opaque AI reasoning evades interpretability, prompting a €12bn EU research push. Political capital erodes under the weight of multiple ongoing initiatives and visible policy failures, narrowing the window for effective governance action.
