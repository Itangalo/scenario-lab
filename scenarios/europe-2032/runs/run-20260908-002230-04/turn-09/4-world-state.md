### A Leap in Open Capability, a Lurch Backward in Control

Global AI capability rose to **67.0**, driven by the sudden automation of verification in law, engineering, and clinical coding—tasks once thought to require human judgment now yield to scalable checks. This shift unlocked rapid progress across domains, compressing development cycles and accelerating deployment.

Amid this surge, a partial open-weight model leak emerged, attributed to a compromised developer environment at a frontier lab. The released weights, while incomplete and lacking full alignment stacks, enabled rapid replication of core functionalities in non-safety-critical domains. This allowed **openweight_capability** to rise to **56.0**, reflecting a significant but bounded leap—consistent with the delayed replication of a portion of frontier gains rather than full parity. The model spread widely, but with degraded performance in high-stakes applications and no embedded safety constraints, amplifying risks.

The EU attempted no new measure this turn, citing legal uncertainty around its sovereign compute push. Yet it reaffirmed the **Sovereign Compute Emergency Acceleration** as its priority, asserting that infrastructure—not policy—was the true foundation of sovereignty. Implementing acts under Article 122 TFEU were issued, forcing grid integration and redirecting Cohesion funds. But Bavaria and Wallonia refused compliance, escalating to constitutional challenge. Protests intensified at both proposed gigafactory sites.

Despite the urgency, no construction began. Political capital eroded further as the Council remained divided, and public sentiment held at **23.0**, poisoned by narratives of central overreach. Resilience saw a modest gain from a **joint_threat_response**: the EU was admitted into a real-time cyber intelligence pact after coordinated attacks on energy operators, improving detection and response coordination.

Yet AI safety fell sharply to **28.0**, overwhelmed by the speed of open diffusion and lacking tools to monitor or constrain autonomous replication. The gap between capability and control widened dangerously.

### Stalled Leverage, Shrinking Options

With no physical infrastructure online and legal challenges mounting, **eu_ai_sovereignty** declined to **17.0**—down due to capability growth (−1) and no offsetting gains. The emergency measures had not yet landed, so no +1 for momentum. Political capital dipped slightly under continued erosion, despite the resilience boost, ending at **18.0**.

The window to act narrowed. The world moved faster. The Union stood still.
