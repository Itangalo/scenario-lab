## In practice
We are acting on the fact that access without infrastructure is illusion. The cutoff from the leading global model was not an anomaly—it was a demonstration of power. And power flows from physical control. The emergency invocation under Article 122 TFEU was not a policy preference but a necessity: the two InvestAI Gigafactories were meant to be our foundation, but they were being strangled in national procedures that assume time and consent we no longer possess. We reordered the sequence: build first, litigate after. The Commission’s Legal Service has issued the implementing acts, naming the Soria and Silesia sites as critical, mandating ENTSO-E to prioritise grid integration, and directing Cohesion Policy funds to offset regional impact. Member states have 60 days to comply. We are not bypassing democracy—we are fulfilling its higher duty: survival.

But the backlash is real. Constitutional challenges from Bavaria and Wallonia have been filed, and protests continue. The Council will meet in ECOFIN formation to reframe the issue: this is not a digital policy, it is macroeconomic resilience. Without sovereign compute, the EU cannot run its own simulations, secure its own models, or respond to the next cutoff. The €10bn cohesion top-up is not compensation—it is investment in legitimacy. We are not extracting; we are anchoring. And when the Court rules—and it will—we will not retreat. We will deliver. Two gigafactories. Online. Armed. Unblockable. That is the only sovereignty that lasts. Until then, we hold.
