## New measure  
None this turn.  
We are waiting for the European Court of Justice’s ruling on the use of Article 122 TFEU to enable emergency sovereign compute development, which will determine whether the Union can override member-state objections in strategic infrastructure projects — a threshold question for both sovereignty and political agency.

## Priority  
Sovereign Compute Emergency Acceleration — because without a legal pathway to deploy sovereign compute at speed, no other measure in AI safety, resilience or control can be sustained independently, and because the Court’s imminent judgment will define the outer boundary of what the Union is still capable of deciding for itself.

## In practice  
We are holding firm on the Sovereign Compute Emergency Acceleration, not advancing it physically but preparing it operationally. DG Connect and DG Energy have completed readiness plans for the Soria and Silesia sites: grid integration blueprints are finalised, security protocols drafted under ENISA oversight, and workforce pipelines mapped through the European Institute of Innovation and Technology. The €10bn cohesion top-up remains ring-fenced, but disbursement is contingent on the ECJ’s judgment. We are not moving without it. To do so would risk both legal invalidation and deeper fragmentation — a loss of legitimacy we cannot afford at 18.0 political capital.

Behind the scenes, the Presidency continues bilateral mediation with Bavaria and Wallonia, not to reopen negotiations, but to signal that compliance pathways exist: conditional hosting agreements, enhanced regional benefit-sharing in energy and digital jobs, and co-governance of site security. These are not concessions to obstruction, but preparations for unity — should the Court rule in our favour. If it does not, we will have to confront the reality that the Union cannot build what it must defend on its own soil, and that our sovereignty must be rebuilt through other levers: control of supply chains, talent, and access. But that is not now. We wait for the judgment. We prepare. We do not act blindly — not when the cost of failure is the end of credible action.
