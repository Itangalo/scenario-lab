## New measure
**Sovereign Access to Open-Weight Frontier Models**
We establish a legally anchored, EU-governed programme to secure access to the most capable open-weight models through sovereign hosting, weight verification, and controlled deployment — ensuring the Union can monitor, test, and use frontier models without dependence on foreign providers.

## In practice
We are closing the second year of crisis response under fire. The Cyber Resilience Emergency Programme is now in its final phase: ENISA has completed red-teaming at 15 critical nodes, and binding opinions under NIS2 are forcing patching in real time. Cohesion fund conditionality has unlocked €8.3bn in RRF tranches, but only for member states that deploy AI-driven intrusion detection by June. Italy and Spain are still trailing, but ANSSI and BSI are on the ground, and national crisis protocols in Germany and France have fast-tracked procurement. This is not optional — it is the minimum floor for continuity.

Meanwhile, the open frontier is no longer just a regulatory concern. With openweight_capability at 50.5 and the cyber_major_incident clearly model-generated, we can no longer rely on perimeter oversight alone. The Secure the open frontier measure is complete, but it produced norms, not power. That is why we are launching Sovereign Access to Open-Weight Frontier Models: not to build a new model, but to claim sovereign access to the ones that already exist. Under Article 17 of the AI Act and the Digital Markets Act’s gatekeeper provisions, we are establishing a EU-wide verification regime for open-weight models above 45.0 capability. These weights will be hosted on GAIA-X-compliant infrastructure, audited for integrity and alignment, and made available only to certified public operators — for defensive use only. We are offering liability shielding and access to threat telemetry in exchange for compliance, but refusal means exclusion from public contracts. This is not a lab programme — it is infrastructure for survival.

The window opened by election_retrenchment in the US is real, but it will not last. We are not chasing the frontier — we are securing the ground beneath us.
