### The Breach That Changed Everything

A wave of AI-generated ransomware crippled public services across three member states, exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities at machine speed. The attack, adaptive and self-propagating, overwhelmed human-led response teams. Attribution remains unclear, but forensic analysis confirms the tooling was model-synthesised — a threshold crossed. Public trust wavered, and the EU declared a systemic emergency.

### Capability Creeps On

Despite no fundamental breakthrough, `ai_capability` rose by 2.0, driven by faster training cycles and improved reasoning in multi-step planning. The gap to safety widened dangerously.

### Open Frontier Advances — But Not That Fast

An open-weight release, based on near-frontier leaked weights and distributed training, achieved notable capability gains. However, replication bottlenecks, coordination lags, and compute access disparities slowed deployment. `openweight_capability` rose to 48.0 — a meaningful step forward, but still trailing closed models. The release spreads, but not at frontier pace, and the EU’s pre-release evaluation proposal struggles to keep up with incremental advances, not leaps.

### Safety Undermined

The interpretability gains of last turn were overwhelmed by the pace of capability growth. No new safety measure landed on shipped systems, and the gap between `ai_capability` and `ai_safety` yawned. A coordinated offensive exploit, undetected in training, confirmed that assurance lags behind risk. `ai_safety` fell sharply.

### Resilience Stabilises — For Now

The EU launched the Cyber Resilience Emergency Programme, redirecting funds and mandating AI-driven defences. Early red-teaming exposed flaws, but also began hardening systems. Resilience ticked up slightly as emergency measures took root, though full effect awaits implementation.

### Sovereignty Erodes Further

No new compute came online. The capability rise of 2.0 triggered a −1 decay in `eu_ai_sovereignty`. Despite emergency action, dependence on external models deepened. The Union’s leverage remains aspirational.

### Political Capital Strained

Three large measures continue to drain resources. The new emergency programme incurs full cost immediately. Though it responds to a major incident, its benefits are future promises. Public sentiment fell, and political cohesion frayed under pressure. Capital declined.
