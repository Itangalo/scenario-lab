### The Calm Before the Next Storm

Despite the ai_investment_collapse slowing frontier development, `ai_capability` rose by 1.5 this turn, driven by efficiency gains and algorithmic refinements. The open frontier, however, kept pace: the emergent EU eval pool enabled rapid validation and distribution of defensive models, pushing `openweight_capability` to 49.5. This narrow advance was not from replication, but from coordination — a rare win in the open domain.

### Safety, Finally, Edges Up

The Emergency Access to Defensive AI programme, though small and temporary, landed with precision. By fast-tracking evaluation and deployment through the eval pool, the EU introduced real-time monitoring and adaptive patching tools into live environments. For the first time in months, `ai_safety` rose — +2 from the measure’s partial effect, and +2 more from the emergent pool’s oversight contribution. While still far from sufficient, the trend reversed.

### Resilience Gains Ground

The Cyber Resilience Emergency Programme, now prioritised and funded, began delivering. Red-teaming exposed vulnerabilities, but also forced rapid remediation. Critical energy nodes upgraded detection systems, and cross-border coordination improved. With the measure in flight and nearing completion, `resilience` gained +3.

### Sovereignty Slips Again

No new compute came online. The Tech sovereignty package remains delayed in national parliaments, and InvestAI Gigafactories is still years from delivery. With `ai_capability` rising, the −1 decay term applied. Despite the eval pool’s success, it does not count as secured access. `eu_ai_sovereignty` fell to 19.0.

### Political Capital Crumbles

The portfolio charge remained heavy: four large measures and a priority cost −13. The new small measure added −2. The proposal bonus for addressing the recent cyber incident was modest — +3 — given its limited scope. No sentiment boost applied. After all terms, `eu_political_capital` dropped to 30.0, limiting future flexibility.
