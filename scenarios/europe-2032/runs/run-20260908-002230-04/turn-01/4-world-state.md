### The Open Frontier Gambit

The European Union announces a proposed binding threshold requiring pre-release evaluations and containment guarantees for any open-weight model above capability level 45.0, marking a significant expansion of the existing "Secure the open frontier" measure. This widening of scope leverages the EU’s supply chain influence—particularly ASML’s dominance in chipmaking—to pressure U.S. and Chinese developers. However, no new measure is introduced; instead, the Commission reorients its current portfolio around this enhanced requirement, which will take at least two full turns to implement due to legal review and international coordination needs.

### Capability Creeps Forward

Despite no general intelligence breakthrough, the frontier advances steadily. Leading labs compress release cycles further, and internal benchmarks confirm systems are now operating at the edge of autonomous research direction. `ai_capability` rises by 2.0, reflecting sustained progress in multi-domain reasoning and long-horizon planning.

### Open Weights Catch Up

A recent open-weight release—based on leaked weights from a frontier model several months prior—enables rapid diffusion of advanced capabilities. While impressive, it does not fully mirror the current closed frontier. Improved access to consumer-grade compute and distributed training collectives allows replication up to capability level 46.0, representing a 6.0-point gain this turn. This growth is substantial but respects the accumulation principle: it builds on prior leaks and infrastructure developments that occurred off-screen in earlier turns. `openweight_capability` remains below `ai_capability`, as no open release has yet surpassed the frontier.

### Safety Gains Ground

A major interpretability breakthrough allows developers to predict and certify dangerous behaviours before deployment. The technique is rapidly adopted across leading labs, improving real-world assurance. `ai_safety` climbs to 39.0, the first meaningful rise in years.

### Resilience Erodes

The cyber intrusion across critical infrastructure—power grids, ports, utilities—exposes systemic detection gaps. Though no cascading failure occurs, the breach was found by accident, not design. With no resilience-building measure completed, `resilience` dips as confidence wanes.

### Sovereignty Under Pressure

The capability jump of 2.0 triggers a −1 decay in `eu_ai_sovereignty`. No new compute capacity comes online, and the Union’s access to frontier models remains dependent. The proposed regulatory expansion does not yet yield leverage—it is still in consultation phase—and thus provides no immediate boost.

### Political Capital Strain

Three large measures now run concurrently. The portfolio charge is steep, and the priority adds further cost. The regulatory expansion earns no bonus—no qualifying event in the prior three turns justifies accelerated approval under rule 6. Political capital erodes as implementation delays loom and diplomatic resistance mounts.
