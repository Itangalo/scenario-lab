## In practice
We are acting on the fact that sovereignty is not a policy — it is physics. The redteam demonstration at capability 50 confirmed that open-weight models are now offensive cyber weapons by default, and our only defence is sovereign access to them. But Sovereign Access to Open-Weight Frontier Models is stalling because the infrastructure it needs does not exist, and cannot be built under national rules. Two member states have blocked hosting sites; five regions are delaying on environmental grounds. We cannot wait.

So we are invoking emergency powers. Under Article 122 TFEU — the same legal base used for pandemic and energy crises — we are declaring AI compute a critical strategic infrastructure. This means binding overrides on zoning, environmental review, and grid connection timelines for projects tied to the InvestAI Gigafactories fund and the Tech sovereignty package. The Commission will issue emergency directives within 30 days, naming specific sites and timelines. ENTSO-E will be ordered to prioritise grid access; member states will have 60 days to comply or face infringement.

We are not bypassing consultation — we are reordering it. National parliaments will be briefed; regional authorities will be compensated; environmental mitigation will be required, but not allowed to block. The Council will be convened in ECOFIN formation to endorse this as a macroeconomic resilience measure. We are offering €10bn in cohesion top-ups to affected regions — not as a bribe, but as a signal: this is not extraction, it is survival. Without this, every other measure we have is a script for dependence. We are not writing that future.
