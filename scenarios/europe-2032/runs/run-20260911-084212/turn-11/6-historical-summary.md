Autumn 2030 intrusion crippled municipal booking, payroll and hospital admin in several states via tainted component; screens dark, paper charts/forms returned, wages late, while water/power held and attribution dragged. Brussels deployed joint mobile recovery cells under EU cyber agency funded by repurposed digital/cohesion funds, rebuilding from clean images and leaving offline fallbacks. Two hard-hit regions restored payroll before Christmas and cleared hospital backlog; elsewhere queues and quota-delayed hardware persisted, technicians relied on ungovernable open models. Wage bridges continued via paper, some retrainees hired as hygiene assistants. Load-shedding held through December cold snap but politically invisible amid high bills, frozen entry-level hiring, and anger over dark administration. By year-end services slowly restored, dependence on outside compute and unrecallable code deeper.
