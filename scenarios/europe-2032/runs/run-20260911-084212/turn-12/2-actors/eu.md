## New measure
**EU Clinical Benefit and Office Augmentation Diffusion Programme**
Puts the new tailored therapies and proven office-productivity gains to work through EU hospitals, primary care and SMEs on EU-controlled infrastructure and procurement, so visible benefits land as European delivery rather than foreign dependence.
This and now because medical breakthrough plus augmented knowledge-work is the first widely felt benefit in years, and our two-year commitment to tie transition to jobs and protection requires us to make it arrive visibly through public services before resentment hardens further.

## Priority
EU Clinical Benefit and Office Augmentation Diffusion Programme, because restoring consent through visible jobs, care and functioning services outranks further hardening this turn while M9 cells finish the municipal rebuild.

## In practice
We task DG SANTE and DG GROW with joint procurement for clinical deployment of tailored therapies through university hospitals and the European Health Data Space, with EMA accelerated pathways conditional on EU-site processing and model access on EU terms, funded by reprogrammed EU4Health and Digital Europe envelopes. DG EMPL extends M8 retraining into augmentation coaches for law, accountancy and municipal administration SMEs.

We use the Health and Competitiveness Councils to get mayors and social partners delivery dates for clinics and hiring-support desks, offering regions co-funding if they host inference on EU-anchored capacity and keep offline fallbacks manned. ENISA cells under M9 continue clean rebuilds in parallel, so restored portals carry the new health-booking and SME-admin tools rather than just returning to status quo.
