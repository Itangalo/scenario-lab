## New measure
**EU Graduate Transition and Income Bridge**
Funds the finalised retraining and income-support blueprint for frozen entry sectors via employer-linked transition contributions and repurposed ESF+ funds, paired with paid placement in resilience and care roles.
This and why now: labour displacement is no longer forecast but measured, graduate anger is collapsing capital and sentiment, and with frontier investment collapsing this turn training spend is cheap — absorption is the commitment and it must be funded now.

## Priority
EU Graduate Transition and Income Bridge — because with hiring frozen and sentiment at 23, funded absorption outranks further hardening this turn and directly holds the two-year commitment.

## In practice
We act through EPSCO and the Commission employment directorate on an ESF+ repurposing decision plus a time-limited automating-employer levy. National public employment services open paid retraining tracks and income-bridge payments for law, accountancy, software and customer-operations entrants, with fast placement into municipal utility cyber-hygiene teams, hospital sequencing support, and care.

On the running track ENISA and HERA continue the M10 deployment: automated patching and swarm detectors pushed to small utilities with field engineers, postponed containment exercises rescheduled with overtime funded, and wastewater sequencing kept on hospital-lab contracts. The loss-of-control incident is handled under the M8 protocol with incident reporting to ENISA and shared black-box findings via the middle-power swap, without opening a second new instrument this turn.
