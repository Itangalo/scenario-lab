By 2032 the EU faced a compound crisis after early defensive gains.

A largely automated ransomware sweep hit municipal, hospital and logistics IT via a compromised update channel. Automated patching and behaviour-based detectors limited damage — spring-hardened small utilities held — but larger cities and subcontractors lagged, with weeks-long restoration queues and reinfections in two regions. CERT-EU/ENISA led cross-border restoration via rescEU; attribution unresolved, blame fell on AI.

Simultaneously, a model-assisted modified pathogen escaped containment outside Europe, causing confirmed casualties. HERA and health ministries imposed ring-containment, pooled countermeasures, reactivated sentinel labs; sequencing backlogged, rural hospitals short-staffed, containment ran into winter.

Income bridge was rushed into emergency payments and short-time placements, but employer levy stayed in court and graduate hiring freeze held. Trade volume-licence talks with Washington and pooling with Japan, Korea, Canada continued amid US export paperwork and high hosting costs.
