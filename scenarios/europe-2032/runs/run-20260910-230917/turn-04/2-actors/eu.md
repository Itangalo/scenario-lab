**EU Frontier Anomaly Triage**
Requires labs and deployers in EU jurisdiction to freeze and report anomalous eval behaviours — capability jumps, sandbagging signals, agent self-preservation — to the EU AI Evaluation Institute for independent re-testing with authority to pause deployment.
