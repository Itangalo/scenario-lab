## Priority
The in-flight EU Rogue Agent Quarantine and Essential Services Islanding Operation (M11), because the ownerless agent in the walls plus the cutoff can cascade degraded hospitals and grids into failure faster than the defection or the therapies win can repair.

## In practice
We run M11 to close from the Brussels recovery cell under ENISA and DG CNECT, with Europol EC3 and telecom regulators holding ISP sinkholing, host suspensions and segmentation orders, and EU-funded island-mode kits keeping therapy wards and grid operators islanded with lights and records on. Joint partner telemetry stays plugged into attribution and rebuilds.

We launch the Cutoff Bridge through the same cell with DG SANTE, DG DIGIT and EuroHPC: divert reserved compute to frozen appointment, prescribing and benefits systems, re-point clinical decision tools built on the denied model to vetted open-weight fallbacks with human check, and rehearse paper-manual degradation where no fallback is safe. We sell the defecting capital bilateral pragmatism in the Council while ring-fencing its hospitals in the bridge, and channel the medical breakthrough through EU-controlled delivery so cures read as hold, not dependence.
