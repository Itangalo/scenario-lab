## New measure
**Secure the Bio Gate: Launch the EU Bio-Secure AI Monitoring Programme**  
A rapid-response research and detection initiative to monitor and contain AI-enabled bio-risk, leveraging public research labs and border biosecurity agencies.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: small`  
`Finishes on turn: 5`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase `resilience` by +5 to +10, particularly in biological detection and response; modestly improve `ai_safety` by establishing early-warning protocols and securing model access points before misuse escalates.  
`Applies to:` EU member states, coordinated through the European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC), Frontex, and the European Defence Agency (EDA), with mandatory reporting obligations for genome-model hosting platforms operating in the EU.

## Priority
**Sovereign AI Testbed for Critical Infrastructure** — because the cyber_test_shot proved that passive defences failed, and we now face a world where AI-driven intrusions are fast, adaptive, and cross-jurisdictional; only live, cross-border exercises can teach us how to respond in time.

## In practice
We are standing up the Sovereign AI Testbed in coordination with ENISA and national CERTs, designating critical operators — from German grid managers to the Port of Rotterdam — to host red-team exercises using EU-held models. These models, fine-tuned from open weights at capability 40.0, will simulate multi-vector attacks modelled on the cyber_test_shot, with real-time blue-team responses monitored by a joint EU-CERT cell. Legal authority is being fast-tracked under Article 196 TFEU, and to overcome resistance from underfunded member states, we are offering 70% co-financing from the Digital Europe Programme, linked to priority access in the InvestAI Gigafactories rollout.

At the same time, the bio_uplift_findings have forced our hand: a credible path to human-targeted synthetic biology via AI assistance is now in circulation, and the gate is open. We cannot wait for consensus. This turn, we launch the EU Bio-Secure AI Monitoring Programme — a small but urgent measure under Category 6 — to equip public research institutes with detection tools and mandate reporting from any platform hosting genome-design models. The ECDC and EDA will lead protocol development, while Frontex integrates screening at key ports and data hubs. We are not banning research — we are hardening the perimeter, because the first sign of misuse may be the last.
