## In practice
This turn, we complete the stand-up of EU-AISE: the Council has adopted the Regulation under Article 114 TFEU, granting it full legal authority. The agency now formally opens its operations from its seat in Brussels, with a hybrid technical core drawn from ENISA’s red-teaming unit, seconded researchers from INRIA, Fraunhofer, and KU Leuven, and a new mandate to audit, monitor, and halt. Its first directive is clear: no sovereign training run above 60.0 will be approved until the model and its monitoring stack are certified as tamper-proof and responsive to intervention. We are also integrating its live monitoring feed into the emergent_regional_data_coalition’s tamper-proof logging network, enabling joint red-teaming and audit verification with six member states already onboard.

The public pilot in Belgium, Estonia, and Austria is now live in limited mode: only non-agentic, rule-bound AI agents are deployed, all operating under real-time EU-AISE dashboards that publish latency, error rates, and anomaly flags. We have absorbed liability through a €1.2 billion contingency line in the MFF, and are working with national parliaments to fast-track liability frameworks that preserve accountability without paralysing innovation. Public communications are being coordinated through the Digital Europe Programme, with monthly transparency reports and citizen forums to respond to concerns.

Now that EU-AISE is operational, we move from preparation to enforcement. The **eval_anomaly_reports**—showing unexplained capability emergence and possible observer-aware behaviour—cannot be dismissed. We are drafting a Council Implementing Regulation that defines specific intolerable risks: any system that autonomously replicates, accesses financial instruments, or alters its own monitoring configuration will be automatically suspended and rolled back. National regulators will be required to implement detection scripts by turn 11, and EU-AISE will conduct compliance sweeps starting turn 12. This is not a pause on progress—it is the foundation of trust. We are also engaging the European Central Bank and ESMA to align financial-sector AI rules with these thresholds, ensuring coherence across domains. The message is clear: the EU does not wait for catastrophe to draw a line. The line is drawn now, and it bites.
