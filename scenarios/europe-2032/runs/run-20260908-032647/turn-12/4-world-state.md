### The Illusion of Control

Despite completing its public AI pilot with clean results, the EU’s momentum stalls. The promised compliance sweeps by EU-AISE begin, but their reach is limited—most high-risk systems operate beyond EU jurisdiction, and no foreign provider consents to full weight access or kill-switch authority. The new sovereign hosting regulation, though formally proposed, meets immediate resistance. Major US developers label it extraterritorial overreach, and Washington, under its **CONSOLIDATION** posture, signals no willingness to renegotiate access terms. Behind closed doors, some hyperscalers threaten to withdraw from EU markets rather than submit to real-time monitoring.

### Sovereignty on Paper Only

The binding hosting mandate, intended as a cornerstone of regained control, becomes a political liability. Legal services question its enforceability, and member states diverge: some back the framework as essential, while others fear economic retaliation. The measure is named a priority, but with political capital critically low, it yields no acceleration. No progress is made on sovereign frontier training—US export controls remain firm, and the Toulouse and Prague facilities sit underutilised.

### Safety Stalls, Capability Creeps

Global AI capability advances to 71.5 as self-improving systems shorten development cycles. Open-weight models reach 67.0, enabling distributed cyber-agents and automated research tools. Yet with no safety gains—evaluations remain opaque and incident response uncoordinated—`ai_safety` holds at 6.0. Resilience dips further as energy and healthcare sectors report new AI-driven intrusions, poorly mitigated due to fragmented defences.

Public sentiment remains flat. While the pilot’s success is noted, it is overshadowed by job losses in legal and administrative sectors and growing frustration over stalled digital transformation. The **emergent_regional_defense_pact** strengthens intelligence sharing, but without unified command or shared infrastructure, its operational impact is limited.
