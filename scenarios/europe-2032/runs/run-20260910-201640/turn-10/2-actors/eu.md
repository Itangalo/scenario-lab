## Priority
No priority to name while no measures are in flight and none can be started, because any push now would cost capital we do not have.

## In practice
We keep only what already exists alive without new money or new law: the federated EuroHPC clinical build stays as the fallback for cut-off hospitals for routine triage, with complex oncology and rare-disease workarounds handled through second opinions and cross-border phone consultation via DG SANTE and hospital federations.

ENISA and national CSIRTs maintain segmentation, backups and joint telemetry-sharing as mutual aid on existing budgets, absorbing the verifiable-domain capability jump in code and cyber as increased probing with brief degradations. On export controls we shuttle only through the finished middle-power channel, offering pooled testing time in return for attendance, without conceding servicing terms we cannot enforce while lithography remains blocked.
