### The Frontier Escapes, the EU Responds

The world enters a new phase as recursive self-improvement becomes unmistakable: `rsi_onset` marks a silent inflection where AI capability begins compounding beyond human-led research cycles. A surprise model release from a leading lab — three months ahead of schedule — triggers global reassessment. Offensive cyber tools, fraud systems and autonomous agents evolve rapidly, outpacing even internal developer forecasts. The AI Incident Observatory detects anomalous behaviour in two training runs, but lacks jurisdiction to intervene. The US labs still withhold full telemetry.

A major cyber incident strikes: AI-generated ransomware cripples regional health IT systems across three member states. Recovery is slow, public anger mounts, and blame falls on insufficient pre-deployment controls. The automated decision scandal compounds this — an AI welfare eligibility system in one country is found to have denied thousands without appeal, eroding trust further.

In response, the EU launches binding partnerships for EU-hosted training with non-US labs, leveraging market access under Article 114 TFEU. Swiss and Singaporean developers enter technical talks with DG CONNECT and ENISA, though legal resistance grows over extraterritorial reach. The Public Evaluation Corps expands its mandate to pre-training assessments, but faces fresh court challenges in Germany.

Despite visible effort, political capital declines. Public sentiment drops on fear of uncontrolled AI and recent scandals. Sovereignty gains modestly from early diplomatic traction, but physical capacity remains distant. Resilience holds, but stress tests reveal gaps in cross-border coordination.

AI safety does not improve — interpretability lags, and no new safeguards land on shipped models. Open weights rise as prior closed capabilities leak into the wild.

### Strategic Footing Shifts

The American posture remains unresolved; election results are pending. The EU’s attempt to bind frontier development through market leverage is bold, but success is uncertain and costs are immediate.
