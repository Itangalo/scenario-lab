### The Open Frontier Breaks Containment

AI capability surges to 91.0, propelled by recursive self-improvement now firmly entrenched across research, engineering, and legal domains. The verification_widens event accelerates progress: once-human-judged fields like contract review and clinical coding now admit automatic validation, enabling rapid AI self-training. Open-weight models rise to 78.875, as growing accessibility of training techniques and incremental model leaks from frontier institutions allow capable reproductions to emerge on consumer hardware. While proliferation is accelerating, full replication of the latest frontier systems remains out of reach for open actors—this gain reflects diffusion, not parity.

The EU completes its **EU-hosted training partnerships**, securing binding telemetry agreements with two non-US labs operating in Lyon. This marks a rare sovereignty gain, but its impact is overshadowed by the scale of uncontrolled open releases. The Union announces a **new measure** mandating pre-release evaluation and containment for all open-weight models above 60.0 capability, invoking Article 114 TFEU to fast-track adoption. The proposal is formally tabled and enters a one-turn implementation delay per standard procedure; no enforcement begins this turn, and compliance mechanisms remain under development.

Despite the effort, **ai_safety** plummets to 24.0. The capability jump from 85.0 to 91.0—well above the +4 threshold—triggers a −5 to −12 penalty, and no safety measure lands on shipped systems. The new evaluation mandate is too recent to yield results, and the informal lab cooperation pact remains unenforced. Meanwhile, **resilience** dips to 45.0 as cyber threats evolve faster than defences, and no category 6 measure finishes.

**eu_ai_sovereignty** falls to 25.0: despite the Lyon agreements, the 6.0-point capability rise costs −1, and the lack of new compute builds leaves the Union exposed. **eu_political_capital** drops to 26.0, burdened by the −2 cost of the new small measure and −1 for naming it priority. No finishing bonus offsets these, and **public_sentiment** declines to 43.0 as labour_displacement deepens, with law and medical graduates facing shuttered hiring pipelines.

A regional resilience pact among Nordic and Benelux states gains quiet traction, signaling sub-federal initiative in the face of EU hesitation. The US election remains unresolved, prolonging strategic drift.
