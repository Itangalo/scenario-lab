### The Weight of Real-Time Oversight

AI capability advances to 70.0 as frontier labs accelerate development in the wake of the breach, exploiting new agent architectures that autonomously refine their own toolchains. The pace remains steady, now firmly in the +2.5 to +4 range, with no signs of slowdown.

The EU completes four measures this turn: the **InvestAI Gigafactories**, **Secure Critical AI Compute Nodes**, **European Public Evaluation Corps**, and **Strengthen post-deployment monitoring**. The first two deliver tangible gains in compute resilience and sovereign infrastructure, lifting **resilience** by +2. The evaluation corps, though controversial, is now embedded, and its early findings feed into the new **European AI Incident Observatory**, which begins operations in a limited read-only mode.

The EU launches a new small measure—**operationalising the Observatory with emergency powers**—as its priority. While not yet enforceable, the legal and technical groundwork advances under Article 16 of the AI Act. However, pushback from non-EU developers intensifies, with two major labs limiting data access and challenging the jurisdictional reach of containment protocols. Full enforcement is delayed to turn 9, slowing momentum.

Despite the breach, **ai_safety** sees only a modest +2 increase. The interpretability breakthrough enables limited monitoring, but systemic vulnerabilities remain unaddressed. With **ai_capability** rising 3.0, the gap widens, constraining safety gains.

**Sovereignty** dips to 25.0: the completion of *InvestAI Gigafactories* (a category 4 measure) would normally grant +3 to +6, but it finishes this turn—so the bonus applies next turn under rule 10. This turn, only the -1 decay from capability growth applies. No capacity event occurs.

Political capital stabilises. The completion of four measures yields a combined +14, offset by ongoing costs (−9) and the new measure (−2). A +3 proposal bonus reflects the direct response to the recent breach. Public sentiment rises slightly as visible action follows crisis.

### Cracks Beneath the Surface

Protests grow near evaluation sites, and legal challenges to the Public Evaluation Corps gain traction in national courts. The US posture remains unannounced, deepening strategic uncertainty.
