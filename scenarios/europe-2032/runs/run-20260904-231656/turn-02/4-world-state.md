### Capability Plateaus, Tensions Rise  
AI capability advances to 55.5 this turn, but at a slower pace — the first sign of a plateau. Major labs underdeliver on promised performance gains, and the market price for top-tier inference drops, suggesting the frontier is compressing. While development continues, the era of predictable leaps may be ending. Still, no safety gains follow: opaque reasoning persists, and developers focus on scaling rather than interpretability. `ai_safety` slips to 31.0.

Meanwhile, open-weight models continue to diffuse offensive cyber capabilities. Kimi K3 derivatives are now used in coordinated intrusion attempts, pushing `openweight_capability` to 43.5. Critical infrastructure operators report a surge in probing attacks, many exhibiting adaptive behaviour.

### EU Responds with the Shield  
The European Union launches the **Critical Infrastructure AI Shield**, a large-scale resilience initiative targeting energy, water, and transport systems. It mandates AI-driven anomaly detection, red-teaming with adversarial models, and air-gapped fail-safes. Funded through the Recovery and Resilience Facility, it begins deployment across member states. Early adoption is uneven — Germany and France move quickly, while Hungary and Slovakia delay implementation, citing cost.

The Shield is named the EU’s priority, reflecting growing alarm over systemic fragility. However, high political costs accumulate: the new measure is large, and the priority designation adds further strain.

### Sovereignty Undermined  
The **Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive** nears final negotiation, but Washington’s coercion of ASML — cutting off servicing for older lithography tools — signals that the EU’s access to foundational tech is not its own to guarantee. This act of supply-chain coercion directly undermines EU compute sovereignty, triggering a −2 point loss under metric rule 5.

Despite ongoing efforts, no tangible compute capacity is secured this turn. The Tech Sovereignty Package progresses slowly, with only five member states committed. `eu_ai_sovereignty` drops to 21.0.

### Political Capital Erodes  
With two large measures in flight (−3 each) and a priority (−1), the portfolio charge totals −7. The Critical Infrastructure AI Shield earns a proposal bonus of +3 — it directly answers the recent grid intrusion and rising cyber threat — but this is outweighed by ongoing costs. `eu_political_capital` falls to 40.0. Public sentiment holds steady at 42.0, as benefits of the Shield are not yet visible.
