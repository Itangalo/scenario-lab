### Acceleration and Alarm  
The second half of 2026 sees AI capability advance rapidly, reaching 54.0 as verification widens across legal and engineering domains, enabling fully automated contract review and structural compliance checks. This leap follows the Kimi K3 open release, which boosts openweight capability to 42.0—narrowing the gap with the frontier but remaining clearly behind the current closed frontier at 54.0. The model includes enhanced cyber capabilities, raising concerns about dual-use diffusion, though not yet matching the full offensive sophistication implied by classified systems.

A biological incident involving an AI-designed pathogen shocks global institutions, confirming worst-case scenarios. Though contained, it exposes critical gaps in bio-surveillance and erodes trust in AI governance. Simultaneously, a coordinated cyber intrusion—detected only by chance across energy grids and critical ports—reveals that AI-driven attacks now evade standard detection for weeks.

### EU Begins Emergency Response Planning  
In response, the European Commission initiates planning for the Emergency Resilience Surge, a large category 6 measure aimed at hardening infrastructure against AI-driven threats. Article 122 TFEU is being explored as a legal basis for binding upgrades in threat modelling and detection across energy, water, health, and transport sectors. ENISA and ECDC are tasked with drafting technical requirements, and a Joint AI Threat Response Cell is proposed. However, no final decision is made this turn—implementation will require member state consensus, and early discussions reveal resistance from smaller states over cost-sharing and timelines. Funding mechanisms remain under discussion, with no reallocation of funds yet approved.

The EU portfolio remains at two large measures: InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech Sovereignty Package. No new measure is formally introduced this turn, preserving compliance with the one-measure-per-turn limit. The Emergency Resilience Surge will be proposed in a future turn, once preparatory work is complete.
