ENISA and ECDC draft joint technical standards, and planning begins for pilot detection systems in Germany, France, and Poland. However, full implementation cannot proceed this turn due to the high cost and complexity of the measure: Council and Parliament must approve the reallocation of up to €15bn from digital and recovery funds, and national co-financing arrangements via InvestAI compute incentives remain under negotiation. No systems go live yet; the measure is in early rollout, with effects expected in future turns. The portfolio charge of −3 is applied, but no resilience gain is recorded until deployment begins.
