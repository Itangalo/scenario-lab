## In practice  
We are executing the Emergency Resilience Surge with urgency. The Commission has tabled a draft Council regulation under Article 122 TFEU, proposing binding AI threat-modelling requirements for operators of essential services in energy, water, health, and transport. ENISA and ECDC are finalising joint technical standards by June, with pilot detection systems deploying in three member states under the Joint AI Threat Response Cell’s coordination. Funding is being front-loaded: €15bn reallocated from the Digital Europe Programme and RRF, with the Commission offering early access to InvestAI Gigafactory compute as leverage for swift national matching contributions. To overcome resistance, we have introduced a solidarity mechanism—national co-financing capped at 0.1% of GDP, with shortfalls pooled through the EU Civil Protection Mechanism.

The Tech Sovereignty Package continues to advance, though quietly. We are finalising agreements with national energy regulators to prioritise grid access for AI data centres in sovereignty zones, and with port authorities to fast-track shipments of critical components. The ASML reciprocity effort, while rebuffed bilaterally, is being re-channelled through the newly formed middle_power_coalition, where export-licence alignment among EU, Japan, South Korea, and the Netherlands is creating collective leverage. We are using this framework to demand mutual access guarantees: no EUV maintenance without guaranteed compute access for EU public institutions. This is not a threat—we are not breaking supply chains—but a condition of continued cooperation.

Public anxiety is rising. The emergent_labour_displacement_wave is now visible in national statistics, and unions are demanding action. While no new measure is launched, we are preparing a targeted flexicurity response under category 7, to be proposed once the current resilience push clears the runway. For now, we are amplifying the adoption_success story—the Danish digital health rollout that cut waiting times by 40% using EU-governed AI—as proof that our model delivers public benefit without surrendering control. This narrative is critical: without it, political capital will erode faster than we can rebuild it.
