## In practice
This turn marks a threshold: two foundational measures reach completion. The InvestAI Gigafactories programme concludes with three operational sites—Finland, France, and Poland—now hosting sovereign, high-throughput compute clusters capable of running hardened open-weight models up to 58.0 in secure environments. These are legally anchored under EU jurisdiction, with physical access restricted and energy supply guaranteed through national grid priority agreements. While not yet at frontier capability, they break dependency for essential inference workloads in health diagnostics, energy forecasting, and civil protection coordination.

The Sovereign Inference Grid finishes deployment across the same three countries, integrating with the new gigafactory nodes to form a coherent, resilient backbone. ENISA has certified the stack from firmware to application layer, verifying that no external kill switch or remote deactivation mechanism remains. Hospitals in Helsinki and Lyon are already piloting AI-assisted triage systems trained on local data and running entirely on EU-controlled infrastructure. This is no longer contingency planning—it is functioning autonomy. We are now demonstrating that the Union can maintain core operations even under total AI cutoff.

At the same time, we consolidate the European Labour Transition Facility, accelerating disbursement through bilateral agreements with member states that accept central monitoring in exchange for front-loaded funds. Germany, Spain, and Italy sign on, unlocking €18bn in immediate transfers tied to verifiable retraining placements and wage insurance claims. We broker a provisional agreement between ETUC and BusinessEurope on sectoral transition councils, embedding worker voice in redesigning legal and administrative workflows around human-AI collaboration. Public sentiment remains fragile, but early visibility of support—real people in real jobs getting real help—begins to shift the narrative from loss to transition. We act on both fronts still, but now from a firmer base: the state holds, and the people see movement.
