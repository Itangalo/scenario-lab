## Priority
European AI Shield Initiative — because operational continuity in health, energy, and civil protection is failing *now*, and no amount of future compute will restore today’s lost diagnostics or tomorrow’s unrun forecasts. While the Sovereign Inference Grid is the next step toward structural independence, the Shield is the only measure actively restoring function this turn — it is the bridge between collapse and recovery. We are still under the old commitment, and the Shield is its final, urgent expression: not just to deploy AI, but to prove the Union can still govern when cut off.

## In practice
We are treating the cutoff not as a disruption but as a regime change. This turn, the European AI Shield Initiative moves from proposal to deployment under Article 172 TFEU, mobilising €12bn from unspent RRF and Digital Europe funds to field sovereign AI stacks across critical functions. ENISA and the European Health Agency are fast-tracking certification of inference nodes running on hardened open-weight models below 52.0, hosted on the first operational clusters of the InvestAI grid in Finland, France, and Poland. Hospitals in seven member states will receive priority access by Q4, with energy grid operators and civil protection units to follow. These systems are not frontier — but they are ours, auditable, and cannot be switched off from outside.

At the same time, we are launching the Sovereign Inference Grid as the structural successor: a €18bn expansion of domestic inference capacity, co-financed by the EIB and tied to binding siting agreements with member states. This is not about training the next GPT — it is about ensuring we can run what we have, when we need it, without permission. The Council’s Competitiveness configuration will vote on the permitting override in November; we are offering Italy and Spain accelerated deployment of Shield nodes in exchange for grid hosting. The Openweight Containment Protocol enters final drafting at ENISA, but we are under no illusion — without leverage, containment beyond our borders is symbolic. So we are building leverage: compute, control, and continuity. Not because it is easy — it is not — but because without it, we are not a Union, but a network of dependencies.
