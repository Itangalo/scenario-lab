## New measure
**Measure:** Launch the European Labour Transition Facility  
A time-bound, EU-coordinated programme to scale retraining, wage insurance, and regional economic renewal in sectors facing AI-driven displacement, starting with legal, administrative, and mid-level technical services.

`Category: 7 (Labour and social protection)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 8`  
`Targeted effect:` Raise resilience by 8–10 points through faster absorption of workforce shocks; modestly improve public_sentiment by demonstrating tangible support amid disruption; reduce pressure on emergent regional aid pacts by offering a harmonised, equitable alternative.  
`Applies to:` Member states, regional governments, trade unions, and employers in high-displacement sectors.

## Priority  
European AI Shield Initiative — because operational continuity in health, energy, and civil protection remains the front line of sovereignty: without functioning diagnostics, stable grids, and reliable crisis response, no amount of future compute or retraining will matter. The Shield is the only active measure restoring function *now* in the face of ongoing access denial. While the emergent regional aid pact shows growing urgency on jobs, the Shield remains the core of our two-year commitment — not just to build capacity, but to prove the Union can act when cut off. This is not about catching up; it is about refusing to collapse.

## In practice  
We are treating the AI access cutoff as a permanent condition, not a temporary outage. This turn, the European AI Shield Initiative shifts from planning to partial deployment: €12bn in redirected RRF and Digital Europe funds are being unlocked for sovereign inference stacks in Finland, France, and Poland, with ENISA fast-tracking certification of hardened open-weight models below 52.0 for use in diagnostics, grid forecasting, and emergency coordination. The European Health Agency is finalising deployment protocols with seven member states, prioritising hospitals facing cascading delays. These systems will not match the frontier — but they will run, and they will be ours. We are offering Italy and Spain binding commitments to host nodes of the Sovereign Inference Grid in exchange for waiving objections to centralised siting, leveraging the EIB’s conditional €18bn financing to lock in cooperation.

At the same time, we are launching the European Labour Transition Facility to confront the growing social fracture. With legal and administrative job losses accelerating, and regional pacts forming outside EU coordination, we cannot afford fragmentation. Under the European Semester and flexicurity frameworks, we are mobilising €30bn — half from the Social Fund Plus, half matched by member states — to fund retraining with income support, sectoral transition councils, and regional innovation vouchers. We are negotiating with the ETUC and BusinessEurope to co-design implementation, ensuring both worker protection and employer flexibility. This is not a relief programme — it is a restructuring of the social contract around AI-driven change. Without it, public sentiment will keep eroding, and political capital will vanish before we finish building what we need. We are acting on two fronts: securing the state, and securing the people. One without the other is not sovereignty.
