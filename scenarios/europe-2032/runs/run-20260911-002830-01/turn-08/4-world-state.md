### Plague and acceleration
Winter became triage. A pathogen bearing the marks of engineering — more transmissible than the coronavirus, more lethal, harder to detect early — spread through airports before alerts caught up. Borders thickened, wards filled, and non-urgent care was shelved across the Union. HERA and national health agencies reactivated pandemic playbooks, but staff were already thin from the war-driven cutoff of leading models.

In the same weeks, frontier laboratories shifted gear. Training runs began to be managed largely by systems themselves, with human researchers reviewing outputs rather than directing steps. Release notes arrived faster than regulators could read them. The cadence was not announced; it was inferred.

### A thin floor
Washington and Beijing, still exchanging fire by proxy over fabs and cables, signed a limited risk-reduction understanding on securing model weights, restraining autonomous escalation, and curbing a class of biological design tools. Verification was described as thin but real. Brussels was not at the table. The Commission demanded observer status and humanitarian restoration of model access for hospitals; replies were polite and non-committal.

At home the Union pushed what it could control. Sovereign compute and empty factory halls were retasked to surveillance modelling and cut-off hospitals. Manual-care protocols, islanding drills, paper logs and local kill-switches for autonomous agents were extended nationwide. A newly published method for checking what deployed systems were actually doing was rushed into EU-operated deployments, with public incident logs.

It helped unevenly. Cities with pact crews and older domestic models degraded but kept lights, water and emergency lines alive. Elsewhere clinics went dark for days, rumours blamed foreign machines and grid-priority sites, and a few municipalities quietly froze further installations.

By June the mood was grimmer than anger: exhaustion, burials, and machines that improved faster than the assurances around them.
