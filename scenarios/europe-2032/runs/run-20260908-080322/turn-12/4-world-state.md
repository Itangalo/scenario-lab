Auditors are to be dispatched to major agencies, and live disconnection drills are scheduled pending member state coordination. France and Poland signal support, linking infrastructure concessions to future compliance, but Germany resists binding timelines, demanding transitional arrangements. Italy, amid ongoing strikes at logistics hubs, delays commitment, citing operational instability. Due to bandwidth constraints, the Commission designates the **Decommissioning Directive** as the new priority measure, pausing active advancement of the Sovereign Compute Corridor Acceleration to maintain organizational coherence.
