### A Threshold Crossed

Global AI capability crosses a quiet threshold: frontier systems now operate with reasoning coherence beyond structured benchmarks, achieving what developers call “latent task generalization.” `ai_capability` jumps +2.5 to 88.0, entering the broadly superhuman range. The leap triggers automatic safety downgrades: no deployed system keeps pace with understanding or control. `ai_safety` plummets to 15.0, reflecting widespread opacity and the collapse of verification norms under unanticipated capability jumps.

### Denial and Disruption

The EU’s access to the leading frontier model is abruptly revoked—no notice, no appeal. Hospitals using AI diagnostics, energy regulators relying on forecasting models, and judicial support systems face immediate degradation. The incident, labeled internally as a strategic dependency shock, validates long-standing warnings. In response, the European Commission activates its emergency powers, launching the **Emergency Certification of Fallback Systems**, mandating auditable, non-proprietary alternatives for critical functions by turn 13.

### Drills Yield Gains

The **Operational Autonomy Drills for Core Public Functions** conclude successfully, demonstrating viable manual procedures across welfare, energy, and border control. The exercise boosts confidence in continuity planning, contributing to a +3 increase in `resilience`, now at 65.0.

### Sovereignty Erodes

Despite new initiatives, `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 6.0. The -1 penalty from rising capability applies, and the `eu_frontier_access_denied` event triggers a -2 penalty under metric rule 5—access was taken away at scale, affecting core state functions. No offsetting gains land yet; the new certification measure is small and in early implementation.

### Political Capital Stretches Thin

The portfolio now includes two active measures: the ongoing **Sovereign Compute Corridor Acceleration** (large, -3) and the new **Emergency Certification** (small, -2), plus the -1 priority cost. With `eu_political_capital` already at 10.0, the burden deepens. No bonus offsets the strain. `public_sentiment` drops to 17.0 amid visible disruption, physical protests at data centres, and growing anxiety over job losses in legal and administrative sectors.
