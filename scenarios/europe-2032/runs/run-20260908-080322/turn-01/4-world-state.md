### A Surge Against the Inevitable

The EU launches the Emergency Resilience Surge, invoking Article 114 TFEU to impose binding cyber-hardening mandates on essential operators. ENISA drafts emergency standards within weeks, but pushback flares: Hungary and Slovakia challenge the legal basis, arguing disproportionate burden, while smaller utilities warn of implementation delays. The programme is in early rollout, with 70% co-funding approved to ease adoption, though disbursement awaits parliamentary budget reconciliation. Initial progress is uneven, and no major resilience gains are yet realized on the ground.

Meanwhile, the Tech Sovereignty Package faces delays. Legal disputes over land use in Germany and energy allocation in Poland stall permitting zones. The promised private capital remains hesitant, awaiting clearer regulatory footing. InvestAI Gigafactories advances site selection—Spain, Finland, and Romania lead—but no construction begins. The Commission acknowledges that physical capacity is years away, and no new compute enters the bloc this turn.

### Capability Leaps, Safety Stalls

AI capability advances to 54.5 as frontier labs compress release cycles further. The open-weight frontier sees moderate progress, with incremental releases and improved access to training infrastructure, lifting openweight_capability to 42.0—still 12.5 points behind the closed frontier. The narrative of a sudden open-model breakthrough is overstated; while diffusion has improved, no single release closes the gap dramatically. Offensive cyber tools remain concerning but are not yet widely operationalized.

This leap comes at a cost. The gap between capability and safety widens. The coordinating agents incident, the phage experiments, and now increased deployment of minimally assured systems strain assurance. No new safety measures have landed on shipped systems. ai_safety drops to 29.0, reflecting growing unease in technical circles.

Public trust erodes further after a high-profile automated decision scandal in a national social insurance system is ruled unlawful. The case becomes a symbol of unchecked AI in governance. public_sentiment slips to 37.0, entering anxious territory.

The EU’s political capital is stretched.
