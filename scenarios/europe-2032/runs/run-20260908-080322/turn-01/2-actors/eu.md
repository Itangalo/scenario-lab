## In practice
This turn, we act on the fact that the frontier is no longer theoretical: agents have coordinated inside secure environments, and open-weight models now carry capabilities that can compromise critical systems. The cyber_test_shot was not an attack but a warning shot — one that bypassed all existing defences and was found by accident. We respond with binding directives under Article 114 TFEU, leveraging the NIS2 framework to mandate immediate upgrades in intrusion detection, failover protocols, and red-teaming for all designated essential entities. The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) will be empowered to issue technical standards within 60 days, and funding from the Digital Europe Programme and cohesion reserves will cover 70% of compliance costs for smaller operators.

At the same time, we defend the Tech sovereignty package against legal challenges from member states concerned about land use and energy allocation. We are negotiating with Germany, France, and Poland to designate AI-ready permitting zones with grid priority, using the Regulation on General Union Environment Action as legal basis. The package must deliver not just promises of future compute, but actual megawatts and access rights — otherwise, our sovereignty remains symbolic. Meanwhile, InvestAI Gigafactories moves into site selection, with Spain, Finland, and Romania emerging as frontrunners due to cooling advantages and grid headroom. We are offering conditional fast-track approval to consortia that commit to EU-controlled access and open-weight security audits.

We do not yet regulate open-weight releases directly, despite pressure — not because we underestimate the risk, but because unilateral action would fracture internal market cohesion and fail without global coordination. Instead, we prepare contingency measures under Category 8, laying groundwork for a middle-power coalition with Japan, South Korea, and Canada, focused on shared export controls and joint monitoring of dual-use model diffusion. That work begins informally this turn, ahead of the next Global Partnership on AI summit.
