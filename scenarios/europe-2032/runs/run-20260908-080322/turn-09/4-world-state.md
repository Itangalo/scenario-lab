### The Drills Begin, the Frontier Leaps

The EU closes two major initiatives: the **Sovereign Control Framework** and **Critical System Fallback Regime** become law, but their implementation remains shallow. Override protocols exist on paper, yet no member state has activated them. Fallback systems sit idle, underfunded and untested.

In response, the Union launches **Operational Autonomy Drills for Core Public Functions**, a small category 6 measure, to institutionalise independence through practice. Co-designed with the emerging **Sovereign AI Alliance**, the drills simulate total AI blackouts in welfare, energy, emergency response, and border control. ENISA and the Emergency Response Coordination Centre lead the effort, backed by €1.8bn in co-financing. Germany and Poland resist full participation, but pressure from Nordic and Alpine states keeps them at the table.

### A Rare Safety Gain

A significant interpretability breakthrough emerges from a leading lab: a method to predict emergent reasoning paths in high-stakes models before deployment. Adopted rapidly across major developers, it enables verifiable control claims on live systems. For the first time in years, **ai_safety** rises — a +12 leap from 15.0 to 27.0 — reflecting real assurance gains, though still far below capability.

### Capability Marches On

**ai_capability** increases by +2.5 to 83.0, as frontier models accelerate self-improvement cycles. **openweight_capability** rises to 69.0, narrowing the gap but remaining behind closed models. The breakthrough does not reach open weights at scale, limiting diffusion.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Rebounds

Despite finishing two large measures (+5 political capital), portfolio costs remain high: three measures in flight (−8) and a priority (−1). The new measure earns a +3 proposal bonus for directly addressing systemic fragility revealed by recent failures. **eu_political_capital** climbs to 10.0 — still critical, but no longer paralysed.

**eu_ai_sovereignty** falls to 8.0: capability rose by 2.5 (−1), no capacity event occurred, and no category 4 measure finished. Structural dependence deepens even as autonomy drills begin.

Public sentiment holds at 21.0 — no new scandal, no visible success. The shadow supply network expands, feeding industrial unrest but not yet breaking into public view.
