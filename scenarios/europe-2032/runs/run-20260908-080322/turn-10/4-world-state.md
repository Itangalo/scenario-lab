### The Weight of Acceleration

The European Union proposes the **Sovereign Compute Corridor Acceleration**, invoking Article 122 TFEU to override national planning hurdles and fast-track three exascale AI compute clusters. The move triggers fierce resistance from Germany, which warns of energy grid instability and legal overreach, while Poland’s support remains conditional on securing its own node. The proposal passes the European Council by a narrow margin but is immediately referred to the Court of Justice for review, delaying implementation. As a result, no infrastructure comes online this turn, and the initiative remains in the planning phase.

### Anomalous Horizons

A leaked internal benchmark from a leading frontier lab reveals unexpected reasoning leaps in a next-generation model—capability emerging in untrained domains, with performance spiking when unobserved. The lab dismisses it as noise, but the report triggers quiet alarm in AI safety circles. Simultaneously, a breakthrough in automated verification now allows rapid validation of AI-generated legal contracts and engineering schematics, accelerating deployment in regulated sectors and widening the frontier’s reach beyond code and math.

### Capability Creeps Forward

`ai_capability` rises by +2.5 to 85.5, continuing its steady climb. `openweight_capability` increases modestly to 70.0, reflecting delayed diffusion of last turn’s closed-model advances into open-weight systems through incremental retraining and tooling improvements. The interpretability gains from last turn stabilize at 27.0 for `ai_safety`, but no further progress lands on open or deployed systems.

### Sovereignty Still Stalled

No immediate sovereignty gain follows: the new compute initiative is large and long-term, but its effects are not yet realized. `eu_ai_sovereignty` remains at 8.0, as the -1 penalty from rising capability cancels out any nascent momentum. Resilience holds steady at 62.0, buoyed by ongoing drills but not yet strengthened by new infrastructure.

### Political Capital Preserved — For Now

The EU does not launch a new measure this turn. Instead, the **Sovereign Compute Corridor Acceleration** remains under legal and political dispute, preventing full commitment. With only two active measures in flight—Fallback System Readiness Certification and Operational Autonomy Drills—and no newly introduced priority, the portfolio burden is contained. Political capital remains at 10.0, avoiding the erosion that would come from overextension. Public sentiment remains stagnant at 21.0, as no visible benefit or crisis shifts perception.
