## New measure
**Municipal Recovery and Mutual Aid Sprint**
Deploys ENISA-led fly-in teams, pre-cleared EU-hosted fallbacks and cross-border mutual aid to restore ransomware-hit public services and keep degraded services running.
This turn must answer the visible cyber cascade first because with political capital at 10 nothing else moves if essential services stay down, and it directly serves the two-year commitment to hold absorption through retrenchment and diffusion.

## Priority
Municipal Recovery and Mutual Aid Sprint – because restoring stopped public services outranks new capacity or labour reform while defenders are visibly behind and attribution is pending.

## In practice
We run this through ENISA and DG CNECT under the banked Critical Services Shield certificates, using Civil Protection Mechanism mutual aid: certified hospitals and utilities stay on EU-hosted/open fallbacks, while fly-in teams prioritize municipalities and mid-size operators where backlog and turnover undid prior hardening. No new fund is opened; we re-task unspent Shield exercise money and insurer co-funding where certification holds.

On labour, we do not open a second instrument this turn. EPSCO is tasked to map graduate-entry hiring freezes in law, accountancy, software and customer operations for a flexicurity proposal next turn, and the Autonomy Pact interim hosting offers stay as the stopgap for cut-off US workloads. The gigafactory backup site continues through permitting without fresh money while protests and capital constraints bind.
