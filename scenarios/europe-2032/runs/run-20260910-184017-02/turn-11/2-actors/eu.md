## Priority
EU Black-Box Assurance Initiative, because without a replacement for legible reasoning the Hard Shield rollout and certified checks just deployed lose their safety basis and the hardening commitment fails.

## In practice
We task the Joint Research Centre with ENISA to stand up pre-deployment black-box stress tests and funded activation-inspection pilots, using Digital Europe funds reallocated from the finished Shield rollout, with a Competitiveness Council mandate making passing results a condition for hospital and municipal procurement renewals.

We keep the breakthrough auto-patching and swarm detectors running through ENISA rapid units in first-hit sites while the new assurance lands, and we license foreign compute for clinical and defensive workloads openly, telling Parliament and waiting towns that borrowed machines buy time for controls we can verify at home.
