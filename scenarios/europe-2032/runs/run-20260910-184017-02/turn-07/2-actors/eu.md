## Priority
EU Containment Surge, because public services visibly failing would collapse the capital needed for both the chokepoint bargain and sovereign capacity, and containment must outrank direction this turn.

## In practice
We run the Surge through the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority and ENISA under the Critical Shield framework already finished: emergency procurement extended for the patching toolkit, mandatory isolation drills in hospitals and transmission operators, and EU-CyCLONe mutual-aid teams deployed to the worst-hit member states. Bio-Sentinel wastewater and clinical sampling is raised to weekly reporting while the genome-model claim is triaged.

We hold M8 on its track in parallel without new concessions: DG TRADE maintains the freeze on further ASML servicing cuts pending a joint control list, linking any alignment to written access quotas, while the gigafactory hosts are told concrete poured stays funded and injunctions are fought in court — no new compute money is promised until valuations settle.
