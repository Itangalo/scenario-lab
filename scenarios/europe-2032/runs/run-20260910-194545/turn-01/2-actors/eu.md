## New measure
**EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**
A Union-wide programme to harden electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled swarm intrusions through mandatory segmentation, AI-assisted detection and exercised cross-border response. This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and why now is the autumn test shot proving that segmented infrastructure was reachable and existing detection would not have caught it.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, because the precursor intrusion outranks industrial build-out this turn and the cyber gate is open for the next three turns.

## In practice
We task ENISA with joint operations alongside TSOs in the two affected member states and CER authorities, funded by reprogramming CEF-Digital and the Cybersecurity Emergency Mechanism, using an Article 114 NIS2 implementing act to mandate network segmentation audits and staged-tooling hunt teams within this turn.

On the inherited lines we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package moving through DG CNECT and accelerated-permitting zones, offering grid-connection priority and EIB guarantees to hold private capital, while resisting pressure to divert their budgets to immediate cyber fixes. The defensive breakthrough on swarm detection is procured centrally via joint procurement for operators to deploy, buying time without creating a second instrument.
