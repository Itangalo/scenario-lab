### MPAC Declared Operational

The Middle Power AI Coalition (MPAC) is formally activated, with Japan, South Korea, Norway, and Canada (in intelligence-sharing mode) integrated into a binding coordination framework. The EU leverages market access to enforce compliance, conditioning compute and evaluation services on adherence to shared safety baselines. The network proves its worth during the hospital cyber incident, enabling rapid anomaly detection and countermeasure distribution. Canada’s participation remains provisional, pending parliamentary approval, but the architecture holds.

### Sovereign Compute Reaches Partial Deployment

The Sovereign AI Core Programme achieves partial operational status, with initial clusters in Brittany brought online and hosting live evaluations of models at 66.5 capability. Deployment in northern Sweden remains delayed due to grid integration challenges, with full commissioning now expected in the next turn. The EIB has allocated its final €30bn tranche, but disbursement is staggered and tied to milestones; only €18bn has been spent to date, with the remainder contingent on verified delivery and national grid approvals. Germany and Austria have withdrawn legal challenges, securing future expansion into Bavaria and Tyrol by 2032, but construction has not yet begun.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Cracks

AI capability advances to 68.0, driven by unbroken training runs and improved architectures. Open-weight models reach 57.5, reflecting moderate diffusion of frontier techniques and increased replication capacity—consistent with a controlled narrowing of the gap, but not exceeding the pace of frontier advancement. Labs report further erosion in interpretability, with unexplained reasoning shifts undermining oversight. No systemic failure occurs, but confidence in safety frameworks declines.

Resilience improves slightly due to MPAC’s coordination and logging efficacy during the cyber incident. Labour displacement continues unchecked, protests remain, and public sentiment stagnates at 14.0. Political capital dips as the MPAC completion bonus fails to offset ongoing costs and no new measures are introduced.
