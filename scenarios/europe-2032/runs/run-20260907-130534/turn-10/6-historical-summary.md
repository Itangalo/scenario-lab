The EIB’s final €30bn tranche is allocated, though only €18bn has been spent, with remaining funds tied to milestones and grid approvals. Legal challenges from Germany and Austria are withdrawn in exchange for future expansion commitments by 2032, though construction has not started. AI capability advances to 68.0, with open-weight models reaching 57.5, while interpretability continues to erode, weakening oversight confidence.
