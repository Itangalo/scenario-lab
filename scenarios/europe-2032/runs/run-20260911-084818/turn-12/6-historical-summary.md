By early 2030s deliberate pathogen release forced sealed wards, tracing, partial EU surge; containment barely held with cancelled electives, fatigue errors, backup restores preventing cascade. Black-box models broke oversight; Brussels ordered phased withdrawal from automated triage with slow manual pilots.

No financial recovery: data-centres cancelled, Taipei quarantine/frozen payments continued, solo hyperscaler deal kept with limited EU logging. Rebuilt public systems later cut hospital lists and benefit delays where surge reached, but scandal over sub-minute benefits/policing rubber-stamps with unread logs triggered redress guarantee: log retention, mandatory human re-review, interim payments, and rule that systems shaping eligibility/policing/court outcomes are high-risk. Re-review slow, payouts uneven; Medical Surge closed after reached clinics degraded rather than stopped; fear turned to anger at automation.

Following spring brought paired technical shift: workable interpretability on deployed systems with predicted logs matching outputs, alongside genome design loop lowering barrier to human-infecting constructs. Commission issued upgrade-and-screening mandate — certified interpretability property for health/benefits/policing/critical systems with joint evaluation cell, plus customer/sequence screening for synthesis and cloud bio-tools. Rollout partial: verified in public hospitals and two benefit agencies, vendors stalled elsewhere, screening caught paperwork gaps, separate hyperscaler state routed only reimbursed workloads via logged infrastructure. Older cordons formally closed; late winter clusters absorbed by reached clinics without dispatch/power loss, and partial redress/interim payments blunted anger. Fear persisted over bio-misuse and remaining unreadable systems, but ministers could point to first certified check and absorbing wards.
