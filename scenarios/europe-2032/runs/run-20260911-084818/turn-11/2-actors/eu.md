We keep M11 cordons funded through HERA/ECDC and Health Council emergency powers, with manual triage pilots extended while black-box oversight stays suspended, and we publicly attribute the waiting-list and decision-time gains from European public-sector deployments to the EU choice that funded them. Ministers front both the cordons and the redress desks, offering the hyperscaler-deal member state continued surge reimbursement only through logged EU infrastructure, to hold one supply and one accountability line.
