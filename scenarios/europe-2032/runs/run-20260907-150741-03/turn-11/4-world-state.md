### The Calm Before the Storm

The EU enters the second half of 2031 under a pall of exhaustion. The cyber_major_incident — a coordinated, model-generated ransomware wave — exposed the fragility of public infrastructure. Though systems eventually recovered, the breach confirmed that threats now evolve faster than human oversight can respond. The incident catalysed no unified action, only a scramble.

The Continuity Assurance Framework formally concludes this turn. In theory, it mandates failover nodes for all critical AI systems. In practice, only five member states completed certification, and two of them failed live drills. Thessaloniki’s backup triage system froze mid-test, revealing integration flaws. The framework lands with a thud: no resilience surge, no confidence boost.

Meanwhile, the Frontier Access Guarantee inches forward, but Washington signals tightening controls. A draft export rule could restrict frontier model transfers even under SLAs. Canada delays renewal talks. The EU’s leverage frays.

In response, the Union launches the Sovereign Reliability Sprint — repurposing the failed Emergency Trust Restoration Initiative. The new plan focuses on demonstrating sovereign AI in emergency triage, border processing, and tutoring, using a locked, auditable stack (SACP 62.0). Weekly performance dashboards are promised. Marseille, Ljubljana, and Athens are selected, though Athens remains sceptical.

But with eu_political_capital at 0.0, the launch is symbolic. No new funding, no enforcement. The Digital Ministers’ Council meets, but consensus is thin. Greece openly prepares to bypass EU channels for U.S. models, citing border security needs. The "no free rides" policy is suspended in all but name.

Public sentiment holds at 15.0. No trust, no momentum.

### A Narrow Advance

Despite the paralysis, resilience climbs to 53.0. The partial activation of failover systems in three cities during unplanned outages demonstrates limited but real progress. The gains are narrow, technical, and uncelebrated — but they count.

AI capability inches up to 74.5, as labs refine multi-agent coordination. Openweight_capability sees a modest uptick to 52.5, reflecting slow diffusion of last turn’s breakthrough into open ecosystems — integration remains patchy, and performance gains are incremental. ai_safety remains at 5.0 — no improvement, no collapse. The system is broken, but still standing.
