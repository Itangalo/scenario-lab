### The Retreat to Foundations

The EU’s pivot to component-level validation marks a quiet but profound admission: sovereign AI cannot be demonstrated through public spectacle when its foundations are unproven. The Sovereign Reliability Sprint collapses in all three pilot cities—Athens offline due to grid instability, Ljubljana bypassed by Frontex, Marseille degraded under load—exposing integration as the true failure point. The Frontier Access Guarantee concludes with mixed results: while enforcement mechanisms remain fragile, the measure delivered +2 effective gain through partial cooperation with Canadian partners and limited data access improvements, as scored in prior turns. However, U.S. export controls, already in place under the standing CONSOLIDATION posture, continue to constrain broader leverage, rather than representing a new tightening.

Greece’s direct deal with a U.S. hyperscaler, followed by Cyprus’s intent to follow, tests the limits of the “no free rides” policy. Infringement proceedings begin, but without new enforcement capacity, they remain legally grounded but operationally inert. The Commission begins drafting the Component Integrity Sprint—a narrow, technically focused effort to validate model attestation, secure inference, and failover triggers in isolated environments. However, funding remains unapproved, governance delays stall decision-making, and no host is confirmed. Red teams from Estonia and Finland are consulted about future participation, but no planning milestones are met this turn. The initiative does not formally launch and remains in early drafting.

Public sentiment plummets to 10.0 as job losses in law, accounting, and administration mount and physical protests target data centres. The strikes by civil servants over rushed AI deployment—long feared—now loom as `emergent_eu_aid_strike` enters its sixth turn. Labour displacement and backlash erode any remaining trust.

Resilience dips to 51.0—gains from partial failover use are outweighed by the systemic failure of continuity mechanisms under real pressure. ai_safety remains stagnant at 5.0; no controls have landed. ai_capability edges to 75.0 as frontier labs achieve reliable multi-day autonomy in research tasks. openweight_capability holds at 52.5—no new open releases bridge the gap.

eu_ai_sovereignty falls to 20.0: the Frontier Access Guarantee concludes and scores +2 effective gain, partially offsetting the decay of −1 triggered under metric rule 5 (capability rose ≥2 in a prior turn: 74.0 from 71.0). No ongoing measure contributes positively, as the new sprint has not launched. The net effect is −1 from decay, applied to previous 21.0.

eu_political_capital drops to −2.0: the portfolio charge remains −6 (Frontier Access Guarantee −3, Sovereign Reliability Sprint −2, priority −1). No bonus applies for completion, as the Frontier Access Guarantee’s conclusion does not meet legitimacy thresholds for bonus recognition. No planning bonus is awarded, as the new proposal did not reach a formal milestone. The sharp decline in public sentiment and visible policy failure further isolates the Commission, with no countervailing gains.
