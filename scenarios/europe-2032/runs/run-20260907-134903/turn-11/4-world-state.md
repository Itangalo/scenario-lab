### Enforcement Fractures

The EU’s push to legislate the *AI Deployment Boundary Act* fractures under legal and political strain. Despite anchoring the proposal in Article 114 TFEU, national courts in Poland and Hungary issue preliminary rulings questioning its proportionality and extraterritorial reach. Germany’s constitutional court announces it will hear a challenge, citing risks to innovation and digital sovereignty. The Commission holds firm on core requirements—model access, telemetry, kill switches—but concedes to delaying enforcement timelines in exchange for temporary political cover.

Diplomatic friction with the US intensifies. While the **ALLIANCE** posture holds, Washington signals that compelled compliance via DMA reclassification would be viewed as coercive. In response, two major US developers suspend engagement with EAAN pilots. Only one submits to the Brussels certification gateway, which goes live with limited functionality and no production-scale workloads.
