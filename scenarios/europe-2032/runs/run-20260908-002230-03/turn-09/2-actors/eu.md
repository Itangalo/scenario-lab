## In practice
We are finalizing the legal design of the Frontier Model Transparency and Agent Behaviour Registry under Article 114 TFEU, with the AI Office coordinating across DG GROW, DG JUST, and the European Commission’s Legal Service. The model draws on financial stress testing: auditors like TÜV and Bureau Veritas will verify logs of training runs, agent behaviour, and red-teaming outcomes, with access granted to the European AI Security Board and vetted researchers. We are preparing for U.S. pushback by aligning access to EU data pools and compute infrastructure with compliance — turning market access into leverage. Behind the scenes, we are engaging with mid-tier European developers and public research labs to build a coalition of the willing, offering fast-tracked audit support and reduced compliance burdens for early adopters.

The Secure Open-Weight Access and Containment Framework is now in drafting at DG CONNECT, with ANSSI and BSI leading technical design. The core mechanism is a trade: open-weight developers above 50.0 capability gain fast-track access to sovereign compute and EU research grants, but only if they submit to weight-security audits and agree to EU-controlled distribution — no direct public release, only access via secure gateways with usage monitoring. This turns the shadow market into a regulated corridor. We are preparing exemptions for non-commercial research below 50.0 and building a compliance support unit to avoid overburdening smaller collectives. Resistance is expected from decentralised foundations like EleutherAI and Hugging Face’s open divisions, and we are preparing counter-narratives around responsible openness and EU leadership in safe diffusion.

The emergent_compute_black_market finding has triggered an internal review of InvestAI’s usage controls. We are mandating all Gigafactory operators to implement workload provenance tracking and container attestation by turn 10, with ENISA conducting spot audits. Hungary’s bilateral deal with Washington is being addressed through a new clause in cohesion fund disbursements: any member state signing external AI access agreements without Commission notification will face suspension of InvestAI benefits. This is being framed not as punishment but as mutual protection — sovereignty only holds if it holds for all.
