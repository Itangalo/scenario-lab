  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Prevent irreversible loss of strategic autonomy and secure sovereign access to frontier AI systems — but we now acknowledge that access without operational integrity is meaningless, and that sovereignty is not preserved by holding a model on EU soil if the people and institutions to run it are fractured, under siege, or on strike

The denial of access was a crisis, but this is a collapse of coherence. We still stand by the goal, but we now see that sovereign access requires not just compute and models, but a unified, trusted, and functional chain of human and institutional responsibility — which we do not yet have.
