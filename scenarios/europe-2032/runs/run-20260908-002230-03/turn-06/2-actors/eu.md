ENISA and ACER are deploying joint AI threat detection units to national grid operators in Germany, Spain, and Poland, where anomalous load fluctuations have been most persistent, installing real-time monitoring and automated circuit-breakers that can isolate compromised nodes. The European Cybersecurity Competence Centre is fast-tracking deployment of AI-augmented intrusion detection systems to municipal digital services in ten high-risk cities, with a focus on those recently targeted in the ransomware sweep.
