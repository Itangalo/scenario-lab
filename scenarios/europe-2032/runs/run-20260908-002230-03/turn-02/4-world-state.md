### Steady Advance, Deepening Risk

AI capability advances steadily to 55.5, driven by compressed training cycles and early signs of recursive optimisation in frontier labs. A leaked internal evaluation from a leading developer shows anomalous performance in reasoning benchmarks—capabilities emerging in domains not explicitly trained—prompting quiet alarm. The lab dismisses it as a measurement artefact, but the report circulates among regulators and researchers, feeding unease.

Open-weight models inch forward to 43.0, benefiting from diffusion of Kimi K3 and derivative work, though no major open release closes the gap with the frontier. The eval anomaly sharpens focus on control, but no safety interventions land. With capability rising and no new safeguards in place, *ai_safety* drops to 27.0, reflecting growing opacity and the fragility of current oversight.

The EU launches the Public-Sector AI Adoption Accelerator as its priority, targeting ten pilot regions to scale proven AI tools in healthcare and social services. Early results in Finland and Portugal show faster diagnostics and benefit processing, contributing to a modest rise in public sentiment to 44.0. However, rollout is uneven—Italy delays participation over data sovereignty concerns, and funding disputes in Spain slow deployment.

The Contingency Plans with Exercises yield limited progress. A cross-border cyber drill reveals persistent gaps in emergency authority delegation, but the Implementing Act under NIS2 is delayed by legal debates. Resilience improves slightly to 39.0 due to incremental coordination gains.

Efforts to unblock the Tech Sovereignty Package succeed in part: the accelerated-permitting zones are split off and advanced via qualified majority voting, with Germany and France backing the move. Bulgaria and Romania agree to co-lead pilot zones in exchange for infrastructure commitments. Yet global capability growth outpaces build-out, and with no access secured, *eu_ai_sovereignty* slips to 19.0.

Political capital falls to 38.0 under the weight of three active measures and the priority cost, despite a small boost from visible adoption gains.
