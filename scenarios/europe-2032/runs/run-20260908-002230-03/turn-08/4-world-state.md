### Frontier Leap and Open Diffusion

AI capability advances by +2.5 to 71.5, as recursive agent collectives now autonomously refine training pipelines and simulate adversarial environments at scale. Human oversight is increasingly retrospective. With no safety upgrades landing, and opaque reasoning emerging in logs, *ai_safety* remains at 5.0 — a level associated with high risk of undetected incidents and delayed response. Describing the situation, experts warn of mounting fragility, but no outcome is certain.

An open-weight release follows the closed frontier with a lag, distributing a model derived from last year’s architecture. It spreads across research collectives and edge clusters, enabling capable but non-frontier applications. *Openweight_capability* rises by +1.5 to 52.5, reflecting gradual diffusion dynamics and the typical 6–12 month delay in open replication. The gap between closed and open narrows only slightly, preserving the historical pattern of delayed access to cutting-edge capabilities.

### Resilience Surge Delivers, But at a Cost

The **Emergency Resilience Surge** completes, hardening energy and transport networks across the EU. Automated circuit-breakers isolate two AI-coordinated probing attacks, preventing escalation. The fusion cell proves its worth, triggering coordinated responses. *Resilience* rises +5 to 57.0.

However, public backlash intensifies. Media report on the growing synchronicity of strikes across France and Belgium, with transport and public administration workers joining actions. The pattern is noted, but no cause is confirmed. Speculation grows about external coordination, but authorities offer no attribution. *Public_sentiment* drops −5 to 33.0, driven by unease over unexplained social disruption.

### Transparency Push Begins, But Not Yet Effective

The EU proposes the **Frontier Model Transparency and Agent Behaviour Registry**, demanding logging from all developers above 65.0 capability. U.S. labs denounce it as industrial espionage. Given its scope and expected resistance, implementation will take at least two turns. No data is collected this turn. The measure is registered, but not yet active.

Completed sovereign compute projects — the InvestAI Gigafactories and the Sovereign AI Compute Corridor — are now operational. However, integration challenges slow full realization of benefits. While both projects were completed in the prior turn, their scale classifies them as high-cost infrastructure, requiring phased rollout. Only partial sovereignty impact from the Compute Corridor is realized this turn (+3), consistent with gradual integration. The Gigafactories' contribution is delayed due to staffing bottlenecks and grid synchronization issues, pushing full impact into the next turn. This yields a net +3 from completions, offset by −1 from capability rise and −3 from *member_state_defection* (Hungary signs a bilateral AI access deal with Washington, undermining EU unity). *Eu_ai_sovereignty* rises to 17.0.

Political capital falls to 27.0: portfolio costs (−8), priority (−1), and scandal (−5) outweigh resilience bonus (+4) and proposal bonus (+1). Sentiment remains below capital, so no legitimacy lift.
