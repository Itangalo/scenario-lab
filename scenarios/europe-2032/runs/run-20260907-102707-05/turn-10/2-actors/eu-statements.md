# Statements: The European Union (turn 10)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience
- `no_single_point_of_failure` (position): No critical AI dependency—technical, infrastructural, or informational—will be allowed to rest on a single foreign provider or unaccountable private entity

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Anchor EU decision-making in real-time, sovereign sensing of AI behaviour and capability shifts, to enable timely governance and prevent strategic surprise
  - Grounds: The two-year commitment period has closed, and the events of this turn—particularly the **eu_frontier_access_denied** incident and the ongoing **emergent_synthetic_leaks_wave**—confirm that situational awareness is not merely a precondition for governance, but now an urgent operational necessity. The previous formulation was structural; this one is functional. We are no longer building awareness as a foundation—we are racing to avoid irrelevance in a world where decisions are made by systems we cannot observe, let alone control.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, but now with binding investment in reducing the opacity that creates uncertainty—especially in AI behaviour, intent inference, and frontier access dependencies
  - Grounds: The collapse of political capital and public sentiment proves that acting under uncertainty without improving the quality of judgment leads to institutional exhaustion. Our willingness to act early remains, but it must now be paired with a structural effort to shrink the domain of the unknown. This recalibration accepts that we can no longer afford to be blindsided every six months.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **add** `no_single_point_of_failure` — applied
  - Tier: position
  - Text: No critical AI dependency—technical, infrastructural, or informational—will be allowed to rest on a single foreign provider or unaccountable private entity
  - Grounds: The **eu_frontier_access_denied** event severed core services across healthcare and public administration overnight, exposing systemic fragility. This position does not reject cooperation or interoperability, but insists that essential functions must have sovereign fallbacks, regardless of cost or complexity.
