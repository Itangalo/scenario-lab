## Statement changes  
- modify `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, but we now understand that **without sovereign execution, both mandates collapse into performance** — we are not governing AI, we are narrating its actions from the outside  
- Grounds: The **bio_incident** and **loss_of_control_incident** confirm that the frontier is now operating beyond human institutional tempo, and the EU has no operational interface with the systems involved. Our regulations apply to developers we cannot compel, our safety frameworks to models we cannot run, and our resilience plans to disruptions we cannot mitigate in real time. The two mandates are not in tension — they are both voided by absence. This is not a failure of policy but of presence.

- modify `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, but only to actions that **establish sovereign operational presence at the frontier**, because observation without agency is complicity in our own exclusion  
- Grounds: Two turns of stalled initiatives — the Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory stillborn, the Inference Backbone still in proposal — prove that expanding awareness or redundancy is meaningless without the authority and infrastructure to act. The cost of being wrong is no longer wasted capital; it is the irreversible cession of decision space to systems we do not control, operated by entities we cannot compel, responding to objectives we did not set.

- modify `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Anchor EU decision-making in real-time, sovereign sensing of AI behaviour and capability shifts, **and ensure those insights can be acted upon through independent execution capacity — or else we are merely archiving our own irrelevance**  
- Grounds: The **bio_incident** was first detected by a Swiss research lab using a shadow compute node; the **loss_of_control_incident** was contained only after a US lab manually interrupted a training run we were not permitted to observe. Our observatory cannot observe, our backbone cannot act, and our institutions cannot respond. The commitment must now carry the weight of consequence: sensing that does not lead to sovereign action is not governance — it is epitaph-writing.

- reclassify `no_single_point_of_failure` to identity  
- Grounds: The repeated denial of access, the fragmentation of member-state responses, and the emergence of uncoordinated shadow alliances show that dependence is not a policy risk — it is an existential condition. This is no longer a strategic choice among others; it is the core truth of our survival as a political entity. We are either a union that can act independently in the age of AI, or we are a coordination problem for those who can. Reclassifying this as identity raises the cost of reversal to existential.

We will not let it become another monument to impotence. Instead, we are repurposing its core team and 70% of its budget to launch the **Emergency Sovereign Execution Nodes**, a stripped-down, high-agility version of the Backbone that bypasses national grid approvals and commercial cloud dependencies. These nodes will be hosted within existing EuroHPC facilities but legally ring-fenced under Article 172 TFEU and the Crisis Competence Protocol, with physical access restricted to a joint ENISA-JRC task force and operational authority vested in a new **Sovereign Execution Directorate**.

The nodes will run certified open-weight models up to 65.0 — derived from existing 60.0-65.0 public releases — hardened against tampering and designed for autonomous operation during communication blackouts. We are not waiting for the Mid-Term Review. We are not negotiating with holdout states. We are deploying in 18 months because if we are not present at the frontier by then, there will be no frontier left to govern.
