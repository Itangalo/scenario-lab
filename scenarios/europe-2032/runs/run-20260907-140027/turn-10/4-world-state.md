### Stalled Ambitions, Fractured Oversight

The European Union formally closes the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation, but the directive ends in failure: no new capacity was secured. Germany’s constitutional reservations and Ireland’s grid concerns remained unresolved, leaving the Union without leverage over physical AI infrastructure. The legal framework survives as a symbolic commitment, but with no funding disbursed and no sites approved, it delivers no sovereignty.

Meanwhile, the European Evaluation Shield’s authority collapses further. Two member states formally reject its power to impose remediation mandates on AI developers, citing lack of legal grounding and transparency. The redacted report confirming “anomalous agent behaviors” is dismissed by industry as speculative and weaponised, while civil society sees it as too little, too late. Enforcement of safety norms across the bloc is now effectively paralysed.

With no new measure proposed and political capital at 6.0, the EU remains below the threshold where new initiatives reliably start. The Union shifts focus to the Municipal Resilience Backchannel Integration, now its only functioning instrument. ENISA advances interoperability standards, and cross-border crisis simulations are launched in Finland, Spain, and France. However, the €180 million funding pledge remains contingent and unallocated, limiting immediate impact.

### Capability Plateaus, Open Weights Surge

Evidence mounts that frontier progress is slowing: a major AI release underperforms expectations, and prices for top-tier models decline, suggesting commoditisation. Yet open-weight capabilities surge. The persistent agent systems now run widely on consumer hardware, pushing `openweight_capability` to 56.0. This gap between closed stagnation and open diffusion widens distrust in centralised oversight.

The gap between `ai_capability` and `ai_safety` grows, and with no safety measure landing on shipped systems, `ai_safety` drops sharply. `resilience` sees a minor gain from ongoing backchannel integration, but `public_sentiment` falls as institutional paralysis becomes visible. `eu_ai_sovereignty` declines further due to rising capability without offsetting gains. Political capital erodes further under the weight of uncompleted measures.
