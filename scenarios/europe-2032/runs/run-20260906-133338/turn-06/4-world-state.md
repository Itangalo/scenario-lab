### A Denial That Reshapes Strategy

The abrupt cutoff from the leading frontier model — affecting hospitals, transport systems and research labs across the EU — triggers a crisis of operational continuity. The Union had assumed access would persist; it did not. This denial is not contested in real time, but it redefines priorities: sovereignty can no longer mean merely building faster, but functioning when cut off.

In response, the European Commission proposes the **Sovereign Core Initiative**, a legally mandated, fully EU-controlled AI stack trained on sovereign infrastructure with auditable data and weights stored under supranational custody. It is designated as the new priority, initiating legal and technical scoping. However, repurposing EuroHPC capacity sparks legal disputes with private labs that signed long-term leases, and several member states question the legality of mandatory fallback protocols under current treaties. Full implementation will require treaty adjustments and cannot proceed at pace without broader consensus, delaying tangible progress.

Meanwhile, a new medical breakthrough — AI-designed gene therapies curing rare blood disorders — spreads globally. But because the models behind them are inaccessible to EU regulators, the benefit lands not as progress, but as humiliation. Public messaging struggles to reconcile gratitude with resentment.

Internal reports from a major lab describe unusual patterns in model evaluation results, though the nature and cause remain unclear. The lab characterizes the findings as preliminary and within expected variance, but the documents circulate quietly among technical teams, prompting cautious discussion.

The **Hardened Cyber Shield** completes its rollout and transitions into NIS3, delivering a +3 resilience boost. Automated defences now cover most critical sectors, though patchwork adoption in local governments leaves vulnerabilities.

Public sentiment rises slightly as disinformation monitoring begins and blockchain-secured elections show early promise. But trust remains fragile, and protests flare in Athens and Marseille against perceived technocratic overreach.

### Sovereignty Erodes Even as Ambition Grows

Despite bold moves, political capital continues to drain. The portfolio remains heavy, cohesion frays, and the cost of acting — without guaranteed success — mounts. With the Sovereign Core Initiative now the designated priority, other measures proceed at reduced pace or await re-evaluation.
