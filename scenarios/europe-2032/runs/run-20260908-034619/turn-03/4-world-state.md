### A Crisis of Coordination

The EU responds to a wave of AI-optimized ransomware that crippled regional health services in three member states by launching the Regional Data Resilience Pact, a new large Category 6 measure aimed at unifying cyber defences across critical infrastructure. The initiative leverages momentum from growing frustration with slow, opaque US-led incident coordination and gains early support from Germany, France, and Nordic states. However, integration hurdles emerge immediately: Italy and Spain demand co-leadership of the AI threat modelling unit, delaying final agreement, while Poland questions the legal basis under Article 114 TFEU.

Despite the urgency, no finished resilience measure lands this turn. The pact is still in drafting, so only partial progress counts toward resilience — a modest +2 gain as planning advances and ENISA begins prototype telemetry integration.

### Capability Marches On

AI capability rises to 57.0, driven by tighter training loops and agentive tool use in development pipelines. Openweight models reach 45.0, as earlier releases diffuse into broader use — now matching the closed frontier’s performance from mid-2026. Yet safety remains stagnant at 31.0; interpretability gaps persist, and no new safeguards apply to deployed systems. Human oversight is still the only brake on emergent behaviour.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Crumbles

The portfolio charge hits hard: four active measures, including three large ones, cost −12 total. The new priority adds −1. No measure finishes, so no completion bonus offsets the drain. A proposal bonus of +3 applies for answering the recent cyber incident directly, but political capital still falls to 32.0 under sustained strain.

Sovereignty drops to 18.0: −1 from rising AI capability (up 1.5), no capacity event, and no payoff yet from sovereign compute initiatives.

Public sentiment dips to 38.0 as campaign rhetoric frames AI as a source of insecurity and job risk, amplified by visible disruption from the ransomware attack. Ambivalence turns anxious.
