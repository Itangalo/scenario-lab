### A Fragile Step Forward

The EU consolidates its first major institutional gain of the cycle with the formal establishment of the European AI Evaluation Authority (EAEVA), fulfilling the *Secure EU sovereign evaluation capability* measure. The body begins staffing and schedules its first red-team exercise, marking a tangible advance in public technical capacity. This delivers a +5 boost to `ai_safety`, reflecting the shift from voluntary to mandated, independent pre-deployment scrutiny.

Meanwhile, the *Regional Data Resilience Pact* advances slowly. ENISA integrates telemetry from pilot sites, contributing a modest +2 to `resilience`. However, legal disputes and leadership disputes persist, delaying full adoption.

### Capability and Talent Under Pressure

`ai_capability` rises to 58.5, driven by tighter development loops and the diffusion of agentive tool use. The `safety_breakthrough` event validates new interpretability methods, directly lifting `ai_safety` to 36.0. Yet rising capability costs `resilience` −1, as defences lag.

The newly proposed *European AI Talent Shield* responds to accelerating researcher outflows to US frontier labs. While France and Germany commit funding, pushback emerges from smaller states wary of centralisation. The initiative, though symbolically strong, strains cohesion.

### Political Capital Erodes

Four active measures, including three large, cost −11. The new priority adds −1. The completion of EAEVA yields +4, but political capital still drops to 28.0. Sovereignty rises slightly to 20.0: +3 from EAEVA’s completion (category 5, jurisdiction-wide), +1 from *InvestAI Gigafactories* in flight, and −1 from rising AI capability.

Public sentiment holds at 38.0, caught between anxiety over job risks and cautious approval of new safeguards.
