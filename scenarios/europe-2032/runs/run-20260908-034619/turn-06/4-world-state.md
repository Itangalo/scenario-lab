### The Weight of Scandal

The ombudsman’s report on the AI welfare system lands like a verdict. Images of elderly citizens denied care circulate across social media, and protests grow in three major cities. The European Commission responds swiftly, declaring a new priority: a binding right to explanation and redress in AI-mediated public services. Legal teams draft a directive under Article 114 TFEU, anchoring it in the Charter’s right to good administration. Fast-track review panels, public audit logs, and mandatory impact assessments are proposed. France and Finland express support, citing ongoing pilots, but Germany resists, warning of federal overreach. A compromise is reached: co-financing from the Just Transition Fund to ease adoption.

The Regional Data Resilience Pact completes, integrating real-time telemetry across five member states and boosting resilience by +3. However, a quiet parallel initiative — several states pooling cyber red teams outside EU structures — exposes fragmentation. This emergent divide weakens systemic coordination, limiting resilience gains. eu_ai_sovereignty falls to 20.0: +2 from ongoing Gigafactory progress, −1 from ai_capability crossing 60, and no access event. The US alliance posture begins, but offers no immediate sovereignty lift.

### Capital Erodes, Sentiment Plummets

Political capital drops sharply. The portfolio charge of −13 (four large measures, priority) outweighs completion gains (+4). The scandal, of internal origin, costs −8.
