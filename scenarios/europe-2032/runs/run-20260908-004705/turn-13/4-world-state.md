`ai_capability` reaches 100.0, marking the effective upper bound of the current measurement scale. ### Open Weights Advance, But Within Bounds

Decentralized distillation of frontier techniques continues, building on earlier open releases and modular verification tools. Some openweight systems demonstrate improved autonomy and self-consistency, particularly in constrained domains. However, performance gains are limited by ongoing compute scarcity and the exodus of technical talent. `openweight_capability` rises to 87.0—reflecting steady progress, but still trailing the closed frontier and within plausible reach given prior trends. No sudden leap occurs; the gains are incremental and uneven across regions.

The EU’s *Emergency Expansion* and *Sovereign Access* measures formally conclude, but their completion brings no operational gain. Audit nodes are present in all member states, mirrored agent stacks stand ready—but with no personnel, legal authority or funding, they remain inert. CERT-EU logs go unexamined; red-team protocols gather digital dust. The infrastructure exists, but the Union cannot activate it.
