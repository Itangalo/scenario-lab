## In practice
We are finalising the disbursement framework for the Labour Transition Accelerator through the European Social Fund+ and the new AI Adjustment Instrument, with 85% co-financing unlocked for member states that submit national implementation plans by March 2027. The Commission is actively engaging with EPSCO ministers to align eligibility with digital transition benchmarks, and we are deploying technical assistance teams to high-displacement regions—particularly in public administration and legal services—to fast-track retraining pipelines. Wage insurance pilots will launch in three member states by June, using simplified digital claims processed through national AI transition portals.

Simultaneously, we are pressing forward with the InvestAI Gigafactories, with the Compute Siting Task Force now operational and resolving grid interconnection delays in two priority zones. Binding agreements with three semiconductor firms are nearing completion, anchoring next-generation packaging capacity under EU jurisdiction, while cloud provider negotiations include enforceable data sovereignty clauses modelled on Gaia-X. The task force has invoked emergency powers under the revised TEN-E Regulation to bypass local permitting bottlenecks, though this has triggered legal challenges in two member states, which we are contesting through the Court of Justice on grounds of strategic infrastructure necessity.

The Tech Sovereignty Package remains under negotiation in the Council, where concerns over state aid and regional equity persist. To break the deadlock, we have formalised the regional equity clause—20% reserved capacity for SMEs and public research, plus one cross-border hub per zone—and are offering smaller member states guaranteed access to at least one hub through a rotational siting mechanism. This compromise preserves speed while reinforcing cohesion, and we expect a qualified majority by mid-2027.
