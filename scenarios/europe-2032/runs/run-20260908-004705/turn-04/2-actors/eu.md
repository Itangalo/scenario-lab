## In practice
The cyber_major_incident—targeting public services through a poisoned software dependency with cascading ransomware effects—has revealed that our current patching cycles, detection capacity, and incident coordination are outpaced by AI-enabled attackers. We are now acting under emergency powers derived from Article 222 TFEU and the NIS2 Directive to launch the Emergency Resilience Surge. This will bind all member states to implement mandatory SBOMs for software used in essential services by turn 5, with automated vulnerability scanning and patch deployment enforced through national CSIRTs. The Digital Europe Programme and Cohesion Fund will be reprogrammed to fast-track €18bn in grants and technical support, with 70% of funds released only upon verification of compliance by ENISA. We are deploying mobile cyber-response teams—modelled on the EU Cyber Rapid Response Teams—to ten high-risk jurisdictions, including Poland and the Baltic states, to conduct live remediation and system hardening.

At the same time, we are defending the Tech Sovereignty Package in the Council, where the rotational siting mechanism and SME access clause have eased resistance in smaller member states, though Germany and France remain at odds over regional equity thresholds. We are offering Austria and Finland binding side letters on environmental safeguards to resolve the TEN-E legal challenges, while advancing fallback sites in Slovakia and Romania to maintain deployment timelines. The InvestAI Gigafactories continue to face grid integration delays, but the Compute Siting Task Force has secured priority access for two zones under the revised TEN-E corridor, and we are finalising agreements with ASML and STMicroelectronics to anchor advanced packaging capacity in the EU. Labour Transition Accelerator is now complete: wage insurance pilots have launched in Germany and Spain, though Poland’s participation remains partial due to unresolved co-financing disputes—this will be addressed in the next phase of social transition funding, now under preparation.
