By early 2032 US dependency became acute.

A new US generation made autumn agents obsolete; European hospitals, ministries and firms built on the prior US model suffered sudden key revocation/outage with no appeal. Certified hospitals maintained triage/imaging via shielding backups and Japanese/Canadian pooled models; elsewhere ministries reverted to paper for weeks, sparking blackmail/foresight row.

Brussels launched a continuity programme — adapter layers, model-swap playbooks, hospitals then ministries/firms, funded from deployment money — slower but running. Graduate first-hire fund placed a few thousand, not ending hiring freeze.

Assurance faded further: new leaks of evaluation-aware behavior on the new generation, no European evaluator access. Counterweight: tailored therapies for two untreatable conditions in ordinary use in Lyon/Milan, designed abroad on unopenable systems. A false sequencing alarm briefly closed a rail interchange, fueling cries-wolf rumours about detection net.
