## New measure
**EU Graduate Entry Transition Fund**
Funds wage-subsidised first hires and rapid retraining in law, accountancy and software where entry hiring has frozen, paid from repurposed ESF+ and Digital Europe money.
This and why now: with open weights at the frontier, safety at 9 and a capability jump landing, absorption is the commitment that is failing first — graduate desks staying empty is what turns ambivalence into hostility and strips the last capital we need to hold allied leverage.
