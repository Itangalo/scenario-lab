## New measure
**EU AI Transition Cushion**
Creates an EU-level wage-insurance and rapid-retraining backstop for workers displaced by AI-driven automation, co-funded through ESF+ and tied to employers deploying automation.
This is Labour and social protection because investment collapse and accelerating deployment will land as a labour shock in exposed regions first, and without a visible cushion the resilience half of our two-year commitment fails politically before gigafactories or shields can land — and it is cheap, fast, and buys consent for the harder capacity fights.

## Priority
InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) – because the hyperscaler pause plus sector-wide investment collapse turns stalled site selection into potential permanent loss of private build-out, and without anchoring public capacity now independent capacity cannot be recovered later.

## In practice
We are using this turn to rescue M1/M2 rather than re-fund them in parallel. DG CNECT and DG ENER impose a single EU anchoring term sheet for priority grid connection — EU legal domicile, incident-reporting and evaluation access — and suspend competing national connection bids via the Competitiveness Council, offering cohesion top-ups to losing regions. We convert the paused US hyperscaler sites into negotiated public-option anchors with EIB guarantees, using the investment collapse as leverage for cheaper land and power contracts, while holding ASML export alignment in reserve in the Foreign Affairs Council.

We drive M3 Shield pooled procurement of patch-at-speed and swarm detection through ENISA/HERA to municipal networks, accepting large-vendor standards for now to keep speed. The Assurance Adoption Hub hires from JRC secondments to bypass the hiring freeze, and we launch the Transition Cushion via EPSCO with ESF+ reprogramming, piloting in manufacturing and back-office regions where sentiment is most anxious to stabilise support for siting and hardening.
