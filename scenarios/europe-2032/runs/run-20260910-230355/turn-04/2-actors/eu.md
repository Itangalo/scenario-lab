## New measure
**European Public Sector AI Scale-Up**
Expands proven public-sector AI deployments – health triage, permit decisions, tutoring – from pilots to EU-wide procurement with European providers under trusted-source standards.
This is Diffusion, adoption and public trust because the turn's two positive events give us rare attributable wins to scale, rebuilding sentiment and political capital cheaply while gigafactories stay stalled and capital is at 16.

## Priority
EU AI Transition Cushion (M5) – because with productivity gains landing without job losses yet hiring freezes spreading, the cheap wage-insurance pilot is the only instrument that converts good news into consent for siting and hardening before capital runs out.

## In practice
We freeze new fights on M1/M2 siting this turn and hold the single term sheet without new money: DG CNECT keeps the one accepted public-anchor negotiation moving with EIB guarantees, while cohesion top-ups are paused to stop bleeding capital in the Competitiveness Council. ENISA/HERA pooled patching continues to municipal networks on existing contracts, favouring speed over vendor diversity.

We push M5 through EPSCO via ESF+ reprogramming already agreed, targeting back-office and manufacturing regions where the productivity data defuses displacement fear. The Assurance Hub (M4) continues slow certification with seconded staff, and we task DIGIT with codifying the successful health/administration deployments into a replicable procurement template funded from Digital Europe, so the new Scale-Up starts on paper without a new budget fight.
