The InvestAI Gigafactories programme collapses despite funding, due to stalled investment and energy constraints; the Emergency Compute Resilience Initiative identifies spare capacity but fails to secure reliable grid access. Talent recruitment under the EU Anchoring Initiative yields modest gains, with slow integration. AI safety plummets to 3.0 as safeguards fail to deploy, while cyber resilience slightly improves to 44.0. Openweight capability reaches 58.5, narrowing the global gap, but EU AI sovereignty dips to 18.0 as external advances outpace domestic capacity.
