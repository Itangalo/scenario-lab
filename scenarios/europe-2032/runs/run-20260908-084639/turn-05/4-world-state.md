### A Fragile Opening

The U.S. election concludes with a decisive victory for the anti-AI platform, confirming a sharp inward turn in American technology policy. Data centre expansions are paused, frontier labs face new restrictions, and Washington withdraws from international AI coordination efforts. While this slows global capability growth slightly, the broader effect is strategic uncertainty: the EU’s long-relied-upon transatlantic alignment evaporates, and the global AI landscape fractures.

Frontier AI capability nonetheless advances to 65.5, driven by autonomous research agents in materials science and software exploitation. The open frontier widens: a near-frontier model leaks and spreads rapidly, accelerating distributed use. `openweight_capability` rises to 54.0, narrowing the gap with the closed frontier despite partial containment efforts.

The EU completes the **Compute Sovereignty Accelerator Directive**, streamlining permitting and grid access for sovereign AI infrastructure. Three national projects are fast-tracked, but physical deployment lags. The **Sovereign Access Framework** moves forward under the Digital Markets Act, with draft licensing rules tying market access to weight anchoring and auditability. However, U.S. firms resist, and legal challenges loom. Enforcement remains aspirational.

Simultaneously, the **EU Talent Anchoring Initiative** launches, offering long-term grants and residency to frontier researchers. Early interest is strong, but tangible gains in human capital are distant.

`ai_safety` drops to 25.0 as capability surges without matching safeguards. No new controls land on deployed systems, and the accountability framework remains in implementation. `resilience` holds at 45.0 — improved cyber readiness offsets no new physical hardening.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` slips to 17.0: the −1 from capability growth outweighs any momentum from ongoing measures. No event secures or denies access this turn.

Political capital falls to 31.0. The portfolio carries five large measures, including the priority, costing −16. The completion of the Accelerator Directive adds +3, and the new Talent Initiative earns a +3 proposal bonus for responding to recent systemic shocks. Net change: −7 before sentiment adjustment. `public_sentiment` remains at 33.0, anchoring capital below a rebound.
