### A Crisis of Trust

The European Ombudsman’s damning report on AI-driven social insurance denials and biased policing algorithms triggers a political earthquake. Public outrage surges, with protests in Brussels, Berlin and Barcelona demanding accountability. The EU responds with the **Public Algorithmic Accountability Framework**, a sweeping new rule mandating audits, real-time monitoring, and automatic suspension of harmful public-sector AI. Tied to cohesion fund access, it forces rapid adoption—but the pivot comes at a cost.

### Capability Marches On

`ai_capability` climbs to 59.5 as frontier labs deploy autonomous agents managing multi-week research cycles, including novel catalyst discovery and low-level cyber operations. Open-weight models, now capable of running advanced inference on consumer hardware, enable more persistent local attacks, pushing `openweight_capability` to 45.0—nearly matching the closed frontier of two years prior.

### Safety, Fragile and Falling

Despite the new framework, `ai_safety` remains weak. The measure lands too late to prevent the scandal and will not yield safeguards for months. The gap between capability and control widens, though the new rule earns cautious optimism among regulators. `ai_safety` rises slightly to 30.0, reflecting the signal of intent—but no deployed system yet bears its imprint.

### Resilience Creeps Forward

The Cyber Resilience Emergency Buildout inches ahead. ENISA expands red-teaming to municipal water systems, and procurement for detection tools accelerates. `resilience` gains +1 to 42.0, though physical upgrades remain delayed.

### Sovereignty Erodes

`eu_ai_sovereignty` drops to 19.0. The rise in `ai_capability` triggers a −1 decay, and no sovereignty-securing event offsets it. The Compute Sovereignty Accelerator Directive progresses slowly, facing legal challenges over state aid, yielding no momentum bonus.

### Political Capital Bleeds

The portfolio charge—four large measures at −3 each, plus −1 for the priority—totals −13. A modest +2 from partial resilience progress and +3 from the new accountability measure (answering the recent scandal) are far outweighed. `eu_political_capital` falls to 38.0.

### Sentiment Sours

Public trust erodes further. The scandal dominates headlines, and the EU’s response, though bold, is seen as reactive. `public_sentiment` drops to 38.0, edging toward hostility.
