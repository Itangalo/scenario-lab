### Faster models, thinner assurances
The spring belonged to the labs. A new generation of systems trained with far less human steering began arriving on a cadence no one had announced, each release more autonomous in planning and tool use than the last. Researchers spoke privately of human oversight becoming nominal. Public deployments in Europe that had just gained modest interpretability cover found that cover outdated within weeks.

The Union felt the bottleneck shift to concrete. With chips, power and fenced ground now the binding constraint, construction sites drew renewed attention — night-time intrusions, cut cables, halted works — reported as vandalism but persistent enough to force civil-protection guarding at two locations.

### Restoration closes, rollout begins
Two programmes closed as promised. Joint response teams, offline backups and manual drills left hospitals and utilities degraded rather than stopped where crews had arrived, and port checks resumed in Rotterdam and Hamburg. Audits of containment procedures for warehouse robots also closed, folded into routine inspections.

Brussels launched its new dividend: EU money to scale what had worked — shorter therapy scheduling queues, one-stop permit modules, classroom assistants — procured, on paper, only on EU-anchored compute. Early videos from aided cities showed falling waiting lists. But rollout was partial, and chips reserved on paper did not yet exist in racks.

### A crack in the line
Mid-turn a member state unveiled its own side deal for hyperscale capacity on terms that undercut the common procurement line. The capital defended it as pragmatism; elsewhere it read as the Union unable to hold its members. The Competitiveness Council offered dividend funds conditional on common terms rather than a sanction fight it could not afford, containing but not reversing the split.

Washington's allied-access drafting inched forward with still no European firm onboarded, while insurers kept Taiwan Strait surcharges. Voters saw tangible help in some clinics and queues elsewhere, gratitude mixed with resentment at dependence.
