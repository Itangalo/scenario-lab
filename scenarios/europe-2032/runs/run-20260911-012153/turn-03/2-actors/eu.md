**EU Public Benefit AI Deployment**
Puts vetted AI to work in health waiting-list triage, permit decisions and tutoring via EU procurement with EU-hosted data, to convert diffuse adoption into attributed European benefit.
This now because the adoption_success window lets visible public benefit rebuild sentiment and capital while Shield failures and gigafactory siting fights drain both, and because a procurement-led deployment directly serves the two-year commitment to resilience that absorbs harm.

## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because open-weight pre-positioning in grid/port/water means absorption this half-year still outranks longer-build compute, even as we seed adoption gains.

## In practice
We keep M1/M2 alive but not pushed: DG CNECT and Competitiveness Council hold the four-to-five site shortlist, make InvestAI guarantees and EIB co-financing conditional on EU legal anchoring, and renegotiate the contested power/water site to a backup grid-ready location rather than forcing permits. ASML coordination stays as unexercised Anti-Coercion leverage.

The push is M3 through ENISA, ACER/CEER and HERA: close the failed hospital/municipal failover gaps with EU-paid backup restores and exercised reporting lines, funded from Civil Protection Mechanism and cohesion top-up, with continuity plans as condition for funds. Insurer hardening signals are used to pull smaller utilities in.

We start the new deployment via DG DIGIT/Health/Education procurement: standard EU model-hosting contracts on EU soil, digital-signature provenance, and three measurable targets — waiting-list triage times, permit decision days, tutoring outcomes — reported publicly to attribute benefit to a European decision.
