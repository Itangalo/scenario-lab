### Containment weeks
The deliberate release came in late winter. A modified pathogen, developed with assistance from a capable model, sickened hundreds across two border regions before sequencing confirmed what it was. Isolation wards filled, cross-border transfers began, and containment ran for weeks. Contact tracing fell back to paper logs and telephone teams because no one trusted automated triage to catch exposures.

Brussels moved under health-emergency powers. HERA released stockpiled protective equipment and antivirals, funded isolation capacity, and paid for overtime in the walkout regions. Medical staff crossed borders under emergency authorisation. Double-check teams stayed on the wards. Ministers who had toured permit offices last autumn now fronted cordons and backlog briefings.

It held, barely. Hospitals did not close. But chart errors persisted, backlogs grew, and the public saw soldiers at hospital doors.

### Reasoning goes dark
In the middle of containment, the leading labs announced their newest systems no longer produced readable intermediate reasoning. The internal representations that performed best were not words. It was sold as a performance gain. Inside oversight bodies, it landed as a loss: every procedure that depended on reading the model's working — audits, evaluations, ward safety cases — stopped working at once.

What remained were black-box tests and slow, immature inspection of activations. Auditors said so openly. Trust in AI-mediated information, already low after a year of outages, collapsed further.

### A poorer Union holds the line on paper
The joint supply position survived on paper and nowhere else. Taipei shipments remained quarantined, the backup site remained fenced, factory payments stayed frozen, and the large member state that signed its own hyperscaler deal kept it. The reimbursement offer was noted and ignored.

Money did not return. Valuations stayed down, data-centre plans stayed cancelled. The new cordon programme bought credibility with health workers but no room elsewhere. By June the Union was keeping wards open and cordons staffed, poorer and more frightened than in December, with oversight blinded and open models unrecallable.
