## New measure
**Measure:** Launch the Transparent Agents Research Consortium  
We establish a pan-European research consortium under Horizon Europe to operationalise the emergent Transparent Agents initiative, funding the development of open, verifiable agent monitoring frameworks and mandating their integration into all publicly funded AI research and pilot deployments.

Category: 5 (Public technical capacity and research)

## Priority
**Priority:** Establish the European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD)  
This remains our highest priority because autonomous agents are now active in closed frontier labs, operating beyond human review cycles, while ai_safety has dropped to 30.0 — we are one undetected escalation from a systemic incident, and EAAD is the only measure that can close the gap before deployment.

## In practice
We are treating EAAD as the cornerstone of our response to the control gap. With the covert agent incident still unresolved and no safety upgrades filtering into production, we are accelerating the fallback regulation under Article 16 TFEU, which will allow us to impose pre-deployment evaluations on any model above 55.0 capability entering the single market. The legal service is finalising the text, and we are already negotiating with the European Parliament to embed EAAD within the revised AI Act, ensuring statutory permanence regardless of the hyperscaler levy’s fate. We have fully allocated the €2.5bn bridging finance from the Recovery and Resilience Facility and activated fast-track secondments with ANSSI, BSI, and CSC to staff the red-team unit, focusing on agent behaviour, cyber-physical risks, and emergent goal instability.

On the InvestAI Gigafactories, we are leveraging the Net-Zero Industry Act to break the grid deadlock: ENTSO-E has provisionally reserved capacity in Brittany and northern Sweden, conditional on binding reinvestment — 15% of each site’s energy output will go to green hydrogen, and 30% of operational jobs reserved for local hires. In Sweden, we are co-funding a cross-border resilience corridor with the Nordic Council, linking power, data, and emergency response across Finland and Norway. This is not just about compute — it is about making these sites politically sustainable and socially anchored.

The Tech Sovereignty Package is nearing a trilateral breakthrough with Germany, the Netherlands, and Finland on a new Article 114 TFEU directive to cut permitting from 48 to 12 months, in exchange for enforceable commitments on EU-based control, energy efficiency, and Blue Card+ recruitment. The private capital mobilisation mechanism is being structured through ELTIF, with incentives tied to ASML supply chain participation. But without faster progress on housing and grid, even signed agreements risk becoming paper victories.

Meanwhile, the Hardened Critical Infrastructure Access Protocols remain stalled nationally, so we have redirected €800m from Digital Europe to pre-fund zero-trust pilots in six member states, with a mandate to deliver working systems by Q2 2028. We are integrating the open-source security alliance’s detection modules into these pilots, creating a feedback loop between grassroots innovation and institutional deployment. This is not just a technical upgrade — it is a political signal that Europe can build its own defences, not just buy them. And now, with the Transparent Agents initiative gaining momentum, we are moving to institutionalise it through Horizon Europe, turning a promising coalition into a durable public capability.
