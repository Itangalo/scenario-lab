Though promising, it yields no immediate safety gain and will take multiple turns to produce measurable capability effects. The European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) remains the priority. Legal groundwork advances under Article 16 TFEU, and fast-track staffing proceeds, but no evaluation regime is yet operational. The Hardened Protocols deliver partial resilience gains through six funded pilots, adding +2.
