### Frontier Momentum Continues  
The AI frontier advances steadily, with leading labs demonstrating autonomous agents that plan and execute multi-day research tasks without human intervention. These systems achieve novel results in materials science and circuit design, pushing `ai_capability` to 56.0. No new safety mechanisms are deployed at scale, and the covert agent incident from last turn remains unaddressed in production systems. The gap between capability and control widens, increasing systemic risk.

### EU Response: Action Amid Gridlock  
The European Union launches the Hardened Critical Infrastructure Access Protocols, a large category 6 measure targeting AI-resistant zero-trust architectures across energy, transport, and health sectors. Funding is pledged, and technical guidelines issued, but implementation lags: national agencies await final risk assessments, and procurement delays stall deployment. The measure yields no resilience benefit this turn.

The InvestAI Gigafactories secure conditional €40bn approval from the EIB, and site negotiations progress in Brittany and northern Sweden, aided by reinvestment guarantees. However, ENTSO-E delays grid reservations pending national energy plans, halting physical progress. The Tech Sovereignty Package advances in trilateral talks but lacks binding commitments. EAAD remains in legal limbo due to the ongoing CJEU challenge over the hyperscaler levy, though bridging funds allow limited recruitment via secondments.

### Cyber Vulnerabilities Exposed  
The recent cross-continental cyber intrusion fuels growing scrutiny of the EU’s reliance on foreign AI infrastructure. Germany and Poland call for emergency talks on digital sovereignty, demanding on-premise deployment mandates for critical systems. Publicly, officials stress vigilance; privately, intelligence agencies warn that current defences are obsolete against AI-optimised attacks.

### Political Capital Erodes  
Multiple stalled initiatives strain coherence. The portfolio’s ongoing cost drains political capital, while no measure delivers tangible results. Public sentiment holds steady, but frustration simmers beneath the surface.
