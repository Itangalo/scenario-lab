Puts the new tailored therapies into public hospitals and pharmacies under EU prescription and safety-certification rules, with European clinical data resident in Europe and procurement tied to verified supply.
A category 9 diffusion and public trust measure, small because it uses hospitals, EMA and procurement rather than new build, and urgent now because a visible cure delivered on EU terms is the only lever that can lift sentiment from 17 and reopen consent for hardening and capacity.

## Priority
EU Trusted Cure Deployment, because without a tangible benefit that arrives as European healthcare nothing else — shielding, levy, siting — regains public tolerance.

## In practice
We run this through EPSCO and the Health Council with EMA certification and HERA procurement: fast-track authorisation conditional on EU pharmacovigilance access, joint procurement lots that require European data hosting and incident reporting, and rollout via national health systems starting with the hospitals hit in the ransomware wave.

We hold the line elsewhere without new money: ENISA hub keeps the joint cyber feed and municipal telemetry live under M7/M9 procedures, ESF+ pays the first Transition Guarantee cohorts while the levy fight continues, and gigafactory sites stay in legal maintenance. Export-control tightening is answered by conserving the middle-power pact access we secured, not by a new build we cannot fund at capital 22.
