## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled swarm intrusion with mandatory segmentation audits, staged-tooling detection, and cross-border rehearsed contingency exercises.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and it must come now because the autumn test shot proved segmentation failed and detection would not have caught it, opening a three-turn window before use.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because a demonstrated pre-positioned swarm capability against EU grids outranks even gigafactory timelines for the next six months.

## In practice
We push the Shield through the Energy and Transport Councils under the CER and NIS2 legal bases, tasking ENISA with joint segmentation audits of the two affected transmission operators plus all synchronous-grid TSOs, funded by a small reallocation from Connecting Europe Facility resilience lines. Interior ministers are asked to commit national CSIRTs to a live-fire exercise in autumn, with France, Germany and Poland resisting scope and won over by limiting mandatory measures to transmission-level operators first.

In parallel we keep both inherited sovereignty lines moving: DG CNECT shepherds InvestAI Gigafactory site selection toward four to five bids with grid-connection guarantees, while the Tech sovereignty package team negotiates accelerated-permitting zones with member states and private capital, using ASML leverage quietly to deter further US export pressure. We deliberately add no new compute programme to avoid splitting credibility.
