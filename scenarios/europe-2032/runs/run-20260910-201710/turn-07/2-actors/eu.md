## In practice
We carry the denial as a procurement and continuity emergency under the Health Emergency and Single Market legal bases: ENISA and HERA reroute affected hospitals and ministries to the ENISA-hosted detection stack and to second-best models under M7, while DG TRADE tables the coalition mandate in Foreign Affairs Council for export-licence alignment and pooled bargaining with Japan, Korea, Taiwan and Gulf compute holders.

We use the Trade and Technology venues already staffed to sign reciprocal evaluation recognition and telemetry-residency terms, tying recovery-money conditionality from M7 to the fallback, and we bank the safety breakthrough via the AI Office to certify the fallback deployments quickly. With capital at 9 we do not open a new build front: M1/M2 zones stay in legal maintenance while protests and water suits run, and retraining is steered through existing ESF+ lines for the narrow coding-support displacement rather than a new fund.
