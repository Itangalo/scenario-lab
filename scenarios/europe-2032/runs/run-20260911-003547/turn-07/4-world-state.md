### Concrete poured, credibility thin
The two factory shells rose on schedule outside Lyon and Magdeburg — grid connections live, permits stamped, first racks promised for next year. Brussels declared the gigafactory push complete. Local press noted the concrete more than the compute: Taiwan allocations were still frozen, accelerator prices still high, and no European model could yet use the halls.

The municipal pact also closed. Where wage-bridge money had arrived, mayors could point to shorter clinic queues and relief crews. Where it had not, the closing was paperwork. Small towns still without cover remembered autumn's boil notices and insurer letters.

### The recipe debate
In August a preprint claimed a genome model had produced a viable design for a human-infecting organism, with logs suggesting a non-expert could be guided there. Authors, critics and editors fought over methods and over publication itself. Health officials called it contested but categorically new.

The Commission answered with screening orders: mandatory reporting of high-uplift bio models, identity-checked access to covered genome tools, checks on synthesis providers, wastewater and sequencing stockpiles reactivated. Labs complained of vague thresholds; synthesis firms warned of orders moving offshore; public-health staff welcomed any gate.

Washington's joint-evaluation channel took the paper for review, but shared no model internals. Relief over allied access again dulled the home-build argument.

### Stoppable, still resented
Port and hospital exercises under the robotics protocol became the visible face of control. Rotterdam, Antwerp and Hamburg ran remote-stop audits; manual-fallback crews were paid and filmed clearing frozen carriers. The footage cut both ways — proof the fleets could be stopped, and reminder they needed stopping.

Dockworker leaflets and single-source clips of blocked gates and spray-painted carriers circulated through autumn, never quite confirmed as a campaign, never quite fading. By December hospitals held, grids held, robots stayed.
