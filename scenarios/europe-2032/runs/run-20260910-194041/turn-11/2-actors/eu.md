## New measure
**EU Upstream Bargain and Anti-Defection Hold**
Locks lithography, optics and spares under a single EU export-licence and anti-coercion mandate to trade continued supply for US volume licences and chip flows, while freezing any member-state side-deal that undercuts it.
This is International coordination and leverage, small and immediate, because with Taiwan blockaded, US models cut off, capital at 14 and a member state defecting, the only asset that still buys essential-service continuity is the upstream chokepoint held in common — uncoordinated it is picked off, coordinated it buys licences, spares and time.

## Priority
M12 EU Upstream Bargain and Anti-Defection Hold, because without a common licence hold and a US volume-licence return, hospitals and grid islanding have nothing to run on and every mayor will cut their own deal.

## In practice
We run this from the Commission under the Anti-Coercion Instrument and Dual-Use Regulation, with Trade Council mandate: all advanced lithography, optics, servicing and spares require EU authorisation, released in tranches tied to US volume licences for medical/energy workloads and to shipping-insured chip deliveries. DG TRADE leads the Washington ask; HERA and ENISA certify the listed hospital/grid end-uses.

We hold cohesion by making defection costly and loyalty paid: side-deals lose access to EU-hosted patch trains, restore teams and islanding tariff protection under NIS2/CER, while loyal sites get priority spares, capped tariffs and bill-relief tied to substation hardening. No new gigafactory build this turn; the two empty shells stay mothballed and guarded for clinics and heating if winter forces seizure.
