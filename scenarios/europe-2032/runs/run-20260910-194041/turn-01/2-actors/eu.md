## New measure
**EU Critical Infrastructure Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against swarm AI cyber intrusions through mandatory segmentation audits, AI-assisted detection deployment, and cross-border incident exercises.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and it must come now because the autumn test shot proved segmented infrastructure was reachable and opens a three-turn window before the capability is used.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Shield, because the precursor intrusion demands immediate hardening before the gate closes, outranking even the compute build-out this turn.

## In practice
We task ENISA with expanded mandate and DG ENER and DG MOVE with joint implementation, using the Cybersecurity Act Article and the CER Directive as legal base, funded by reprogramming Connecting Europe Facility and Digital Europe funds for detection tooling. Transmission system operators in all member states face binding segmentation and credential audits within this turn, with the two compromised operators first, and staged exercises with ACER.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package moving through site selection and permitting zones, and we open a file in the Foreign Affairs Council on ASML coercion to prepare a joint leverage response, but without starting a second instrument until the Shield is staffed. Resistance from operators over cost is met with co-financing, and from finance ministers over fiscal strain with the argument that containment outages cost more.
