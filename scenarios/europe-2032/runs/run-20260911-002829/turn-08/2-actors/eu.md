## In practice

DG EMPL ties remaining Trust Pact wage bridges to councils that co-sign staffing and redress cooperation, to show the lawful gap is being closed through the AI Act review.

We freeze new builds and new promises. DG CNECT and DG COMP hold the two grid-priority factory shells on public maintenance funding while alternative sourcing is sought, and EEAS keeps the US access channel warm at staff level without committing to published tiers we cannot enforce. No new fund, no new directive is tabled this semester; the work is to make the one running measure visible enough that trust stops falling.
