### Capability Leaps, Safety Stalls  
A sudden leap in AI capability—driven by a closed-system breakthrough in autonomous code generation and cyber operations—pushes `ai_capability` to 58.0. Though general problem-solving improves only modestly, attackers now exploit AI to chain vulnerabilities in minutes. The jump triggers a sharp drop in `ai_safety` to 32.0, as developers scramble to interpret the new systems, and the earlier interpretability gains are overwhelmed.

### EU Responds to Cascading Risks  
The European Union activates the Civilian AI Incident Response Corps under ENISA, deploying liaison teams to national regulators and launching a cross-border situational awareness platform. The move strengthens operational readiness and earns a +2 resilience gain this turn, though full benefits await deployment in 2028. The **European Incident Response Corps Activation Protocol**, now the named priority, accelerates planning and coordination, but yields no immediate sovereignty gains.

The **Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive** concludes with symbolic force but no new compute online. With private investment collapsing due to sector-wide retrenchment, the **Tech sovereignty package** falters. The EU redirects €18bn in public funds to de-risk data centres in Finland, Ireland, and Romania, but delays in permitting and partner withdrawals limit progress. `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 21.0, hit by capability growth and the absence of physical capacity delivery.

### Public Trust Erodes Further  
The fallout from the welfare decision scandal deepens. Protests spread to Belgium and Italy, and new reports reveal similar issues in hiring algorithms. The **campaign_backlash** intensifies, with major parties adopting AI restriction platforms. Public sentiment drops to 37.0. The EU’s resilience push is seen as technical, not moral—focused on containment, not accountability.

Political capital tightens. Two large measures (−3 each), one small (−2), and a priority (−1) cost −9. A one-time +3 bonus for the new resilience measure—responding to recent grid intrusions—partially offsets the drain. No sentiment-driven boost applies. Net change: −6, bringing `eu_political_capital` to 40.0.

`openweight_capability` rises to 47.0, tracking half the frontier’s advance, as cyber tools diffuse into open models.
