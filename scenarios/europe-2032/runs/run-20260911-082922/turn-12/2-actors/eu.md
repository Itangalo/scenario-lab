## In practice
We run the substitution via CERT-EU on the joint floor and HERA/DG SANTE: inventory cut-off dependencies, re-point to registry-audited fallback models, cut cloud links where compromised and restore from clean backups, funded from the Shield envelope. DG CNECT holds the triage registry live under M13 with workload caps and re-checks; no new deployments beyond audited sites.

We treat the verifiable-domain jump as attacker uplift now: pooled telemetry and attribution stay on watch, cooperating municipalities drill isolation, and DG TRADE does not spend scarce capital chasing slots but keeps Hague-Tokyo-Seoul paper alive. The fenced gigafactory stays idle — we do not open a parallel compute line, revival if any will come through the existing record, not a new instrument.
