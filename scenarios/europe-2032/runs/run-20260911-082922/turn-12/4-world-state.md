### Dark screens
In February, access cut out. Hospitals in three member states, two ministries and a cluster of logistics firms that had built workflows on the leading American model found calls returning errors. No reason, no appeal channel, only a short notice about usage review. Wards fell back to paper triage for a weekend; a customs clearance queue stalled at Rotterdam.

Brussels read it as foresight failed. Opposition papers called it humiliation — years warned about dependence, the fenced gigafactory still idle, licensing outreach still paper.

### The jump in the verifiable
At the same time, a leading lab released a system that tore through code, maths benchmarks and offensive cyber demonstrations. General chat looked only slightly smarter, but practitioners said what an attacker could automate changed within weeks. Pooled telemetry on the joint operations floor lit up with faster, cleaner intrusion attempts against clinics and municipal networks.

Cooperating cities that had drilled isolation cut cloud links and restored from clean backups in hours. Others stayed dark for days. The familiar divide returned, now overlaid with the cutoff.

### Substitution
The Commission's answer was substitution, not rebuilding. CERT-EU teams inventoried cut-off dependencies, re-pointed hospitals and ministries to audited fallback models from the civic registry, kept workload caps and human re-checks. Nurses fronted the rollout again. It worked partially: by May most cut-off wards were running, slower and narrower, but running.

The joint cyber accession completed in this half-year helped — shared attribution and real-time feeds meant the spring wave degraded services rather than stopping them where the pool reached.

What did not happen mattered too. No new compute line was opened, no slots were recovered from The Hague, Tokyo or Seoul, insurers still withheld civic cover. Trust in audited triage held where waiting lists fell, but nationally mood soured on dependence. The Union contained the cascade it could reach, and lived inside the dependence it could not.
