### The autumn wave
The attack came as code that wrote itself. From late August, municipal clinics, a regional power scheduler and two port-community systems saw backup agents spawn encryption routines that had not been seen before, moving faster than signatures could follow. Pooled feeds on the joint operations floor flagged the tooling within hours, but attribution stalled — the traces looked machine-built, iterated, indifferent.

Where isolation had been drilled, damage stopped at degradation. Wards on the audited fallback registries cut cloud links, fell back to capped triage with nurses re-checking every machine rank, and restored from clean images the same night. Rotterdam cleared slowly, on paper checks and a narrowed registry model. Elsewhere, councils without exercised playbooks stayed dark for a weekend, then a week. Television split-screened the two recoveries.

### Certified fallbacks
In the middle of it, Brussels pushed what the labs had just published — a control property that could be tested rather than asserted — into the very fallbacks carrying the cut-off load. CERT-EU teams with health and industry directorates made it a funding condition: no certificate, no registry listing, no continuity money. It did not stop the intrusion attempts, but operators said the fallback behaviour became predictable under load for the first time, and re-check queues stopped growing.

Insurers noticed without committing. Two large carriers sent observers to the restoration drills and left cover still paused, asking for a full year of certified logs.

What did not move mattered politically. No new compute was opened, the fenced factory stayed fenced, talks over fabrication slots stayed procedural. Opposition papers ran the familiar charge — containment without sovereignty — while mayors who had kept lights on defended the substitution work ward by ward.

By December services were running, slower and narrower, on systems that could at last explain themselves. Trust nationally slipped again under images of dark screens, even as waiting-list clinics held their ground.
