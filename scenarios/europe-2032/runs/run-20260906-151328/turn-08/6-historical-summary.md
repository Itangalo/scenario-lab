France and Germany support the move, but Poland and Hungary condemn it as overreach, threatening legal action and deepening internal divisions. The Commission offers compute incentives to secure buy-in, with limited success. The European AI Assurance Directorate identifies flawed training data in the implicated system, marking a technical win for safety, but institutional resilience continues to erode as capability outpaces governance. Public sentiment remains low, and political capital falls to 13.0, highlighting the growing gap between technical sovereignty and political cohesion.
