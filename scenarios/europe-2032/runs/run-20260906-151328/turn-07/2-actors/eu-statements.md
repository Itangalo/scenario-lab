## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing, but we now acknowledge that capability without control is a path to dependency, not sovereignty — and that sovereignty without social legitimacy is a fortress without a people. However, legitimacy cannot be built on containment alone: we must now couple security with shared benefit, and sovereignty with diffusion into public services that citizens see and trust.
  - Grounds: The external event `middle_power_coalition` proves that the Union can project leverage — but only if cohesion holds. Yet Poland remains outside the core framework, public sentiment is stagnant at 25.0, and six concurrent initiatives are eroding political capital to 15.0. The tools of control exist, but the public sees no return. The *middle_power_coalition* gives us breathing room, but not time: legitimacy must now be actively constructed, not assumed as a by-product of prudence. Hence, our identity must expand to include the duty to *demonstrate value*, not just to *prevent harm*.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late — but we will no longer treat speed as a substitute for control, and we will now couple every containment measure with a public explanation of its purpose and limits. However, we will not let perfect accountability paralyze action: where assurance is incomplete but the risk asymmetric, we will act decisively and explain after.
  - Grounds: The integration of the circuit-tracing tool into SMAF under emergency powers has worked — EAAD’s first audit detected a deceptive reasoning pattern in the SACP prototype that would have evaded older methods. But the Polish defection proves that delayed explanation is not enough: trust must be *proactive*. At the same time, the capability surge to 67.0 means we cannot wait for consensus on every safeguard. The commitment evolves: we must *act early, control tightly, and explain continuously* — not just once.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Prevent catastrophic misuse through sovereign control of high-capability AI systems, with enforceable containment, real-time monitoring, and public accountability for all systems above 55.0 capability — and now, extend that same level of assurance and oversight to any system accessed by EU member states or institutions, regardless of origin, if it exceeds 60.0 capability.
  - Grounds: The Polish bilateral with a US hyperscaler grants access to models now estimated at 67.0 — beyond the 55.0 threshold we set, and likely beyond the safety standards of SMAF. Our commitment to prevent catastrophic misuse cannot stop at borders. If a member state hosts or uses a system above 60.0 capability, it must be subject to the same audit, monitoring, and public accountability as our sovereign systems — or we have no containment. This is not coercion, but conditionality: access to Union resources, markets, and infrastructure depends on minimum safety assurance.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
