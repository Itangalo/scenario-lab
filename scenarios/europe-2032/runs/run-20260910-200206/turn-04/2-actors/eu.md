## New measure
**EU Embodied-AI Resilience Pact**
Screens and hardens industrial robotics deployment — mandatory safety and kill-switch standards for commercial robots, supply-chain diversification away from single-source actuators/batteries, and CER-extension to manufacturing sites — while tying member-state hyperscaler deals to Union loyalty via FDI-screening enforcement.
Why now: Chinese-built robots with US control models are hitting the EU industrial base our sovereignty still rests on, and a member-state side-deal threatens to fragment any response; our resilient-Europe commitment demands absorption on infrastructure we control, not retreat.
