## New measure
**EU Sovereign Resilience Cloud Reserve**
Creates a small sovereign reserve of EU-anchored compute for hospitals, municipalities and ports hit by the autumn attacks to fail over to. A category 4 sovereignty measure, chosen now because the ransomware sweep, port-agent containment, US tiered-access warning and the side-deal defection together make dependence the immediate resilience bottleneck under our two-year commitment to secure sovereign capacity resilience can run on.
