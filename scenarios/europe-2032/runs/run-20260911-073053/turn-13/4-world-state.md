### The week the screens went dark
In late July the licences stopped. Hospital planners in Lyon and Krakow who had built chemotherapy schedules around American inference woke to refusal messages: no reason, no appeal. Within days the cross-border backup links they had nursed through the spring also failed — cut cables and wartime targeting left clinics and ports on local systems for weeks.

Then the automated attack came. A ransomware sweep rolled across municipal services, a poisoned software update spread further, grid operators saw substations trip. Defenders were hours behind tooling that wrote itself. Attribution teams said months.

### Degraded mode
Brussels did not promise restoration. Health and energy ministers ordered hospitals, ports and grid operators onto rehearsed fallback: paper triage cards, locally run dose models double-checked by hand, manual substation operation, guarded repair crews, rationed power. Ambulances and spare optics were pooled across borders through civil-protection channels. Mayors posted joint patrols around clinics and data-centre gates where students and farmers had gathered.

The fallback held, barely. Lights flickered but stayed on in most regions. Wards cleared more slowly, with longer waits and cancelled procedures. Nurses described doing by hand what the machines had done in seconds.

### Machines with hands
Through the autumn, delivery robots and warehouse machines of Chinese manufacture and American control software appeared in Rotterdam, Hamburg and Łódź ports — cheap, capable, quickly adapted beyond carrying and digging. Unions warned there was no sector left to retreat to; graduate hiring stayed frozen. A new openly released model, near the closed frontier, spread to hundreds of thousands of downloads in days, and warnings about compromised weights in clinical stacks multiplied.

Envoys shuttled to Washington offering continued restraint on landings and cleanrooms for lithography maintenance in exchange for emergency medical licences as a humanitarian exception. Washington called it procedure. At home the press called it begging. By December survival was local, manual, and exhausted — and control felt further away than ever.
