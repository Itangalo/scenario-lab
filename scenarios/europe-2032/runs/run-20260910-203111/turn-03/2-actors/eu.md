## New measure
**EU Continuity Deployment Stack**
It puts EU-hosted fallback models and manual-continuity procedures into hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the leading foreign model, using EuroHPC and Gigafactory pilot capacity already on EU soil. This now because the cutoff turned dependence into immediate service failure, and the municipal strike plus benefits scandal mean recovery must be visibly European and trustworthy to hold the two-year commitment to keep Europe functioning.

## Priority
EU Continuity Deployment Stack, because restoring cut-off hospitals and town halls with EU-controlled capacity outranks grid work that assumes those services have something to run on.

## In practice
We are tasking DG CNECT with DG SANTE and DG EMPL to procure EU-hosted open-weight deployments under the AI Act and Procurement Directive, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health, deployed via EuroHPC and the first Gigafactory pilot nodes. ENISA cross-border teams under M4 provide offline fallback and segmented restores to the hit municipalities, with mandatory drills now paired with overtime pay and staffing guarantees negotiated with EPSU to end sick-outs.

M1 Gigafactories and M2 sovereignty package stay on EIB track with Shield-conditioned grid connections; we do not open a parallel compute line. On the scandal we order the AI Office with FRA and national ombudsmen to audit the benefits/policing system, publish logs, and table an Annex III gap-closing amendment, enforcing paper conformity as breach while admitting the coverage gap — restriction is not our answer, replacement with supervised EU-run systems is.
