## New measure
**EU–Middle-Power Supply-Chain Bargain**
Joins EU export-licence alignment, joint compute-access bargaining and shared evaluation capacity with middle powers holding supply-chain chokepoints to secure continued access to capable models on EU terms.
This and now because with sovereignty at 14, capital at 9 and sentiment at zero we cannot build our way out alone, and the coalition window that just opened is the only cheap leverage that restores access without new siting or procurement fights.

## Priority
M12 EU–Middle-Power Supply-Chain Bargain, because restoring lawful access to frontier capability on joint terms outranks extending the unpaid Transition Guarantee when dependence anger now drives the protests.

## In practice
We run this through the Council trade formation and the Anti-Coercion Instrument toolkit, with DG TRADE and DG CONNECT aligning export-licence positions with coalition partners, pooling evaluation results from the frontier control work, and bargaining compute access jointly rather than member-state by member-state. No new data-centre siting, no new procurement; the offer to partners is coordinated ASML / materials / energy leverage in exchange for guaranteed model access.

We hold the lights-on stack on finished instruments — manual fallback, overtime rosters, ENISA patching and swarm-detection — and keep M11 Transition Guarantee lists open through employment ministries and ESF+ reprogramming, publishing only local audit stability while payments catch up, to avoid staking fresh capital we do not have.
