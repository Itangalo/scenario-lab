### A seat at someone else's table
Brussels had little to offer in the first half of 2031 except a playbook that worked. With chip slots still cancelled, the fenced gigafactory site still idle, and private data-centre plans still shelved after the valuation reset, the Commission acceded to the pooled cyber and bio response arrangement that several partners hit by the same winter intrusions had built.

The bargain was modest and, for once, honest. ENISA and the health emergency bodies plugged exercised telemetry and locked-backup procedures into real-time sharing, accepted binding sample-sharing with safeguards, and in return got early warning and a seat in the joint attribution cell. Operators in cooperating cities felt it within weeks: a ransomware wave aimed at clinics was flagged hours earlier, isolated on the old playbook, and restored without the long dark spells of 2029.

The chips-for-compute and liability pact closed in the same months, but closed thin. Partner capitals kept the licensing conversation warm and declined to reopen slots while finance stayed frozen. Insurers noted the liability clarification and still waited for statute. The text promised secured compute; the docks delivered paper.

At home the trust repair drive became the visible priority. Workload limits and mandatory human override were imposed on the frozen benefits queue, scandal logs were published with a redress window, and a handful of audited triage assistants were rolled out in willing municipalities with money repurposed from finished preparedness lines. Waiting lists fell where they ran. Elsewhere deployments stayed frozen, and the press kept the other frame alive: what harmed people had been lawful.

American labs, slowed by their own domestic retrenchment, trained less than planned. Second-tier builders elsewhere talked loudly of catching up, and some did. The frontier crept rather than leapt, but the systems shipping on top of it grew no more legible. Reviewers complained again that they were testing releases, not development.

By June the Union was better protected than in January and no more sovereign. Containment now depended in part on a command it did not command.
