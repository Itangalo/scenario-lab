## In practice
We close out under the old commitment: ENISA and DG SANTE administer the final backstop payouts from repurposed Digital Europe, EU4Health and Solidarity funds, conditioning cover on isolation, re-testing and failover to EU-hosted interim and vetted open models, and we keep the invitation to the separate-deal capital open on identical terms.

We open the new direction in the Foreign Affairs and Competitiveness Councils with a Commission mandate for export-licence alignment and joint procurement, using the Anti-Coercion Instrument as the backstop threat, and tasking ENISA/ECDC with a shared bio-evaluation cell funded by pooling existing evaluation budgets, not new money. We offer middle powers guaranteed failover hosting and joint insurer-recognised certification in return for compute access and screening capacity, while leaving idle gigafactory sites to planning hearings rather than spending capital we do not have.
