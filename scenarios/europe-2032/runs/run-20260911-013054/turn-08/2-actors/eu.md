## Priority
EU Hospital-Utility Continuity Backstop — because with zero measures in flight and insurers exiting, preventing uninsured service stoppages outranks restarting concrete on gigafactories.
## In practice
We act through ECOFIN and EPSCO on an emergency state-aid continuity framework: a pooled EU backstop administered with ENISA and national treasuries that replaces withdrawn cyber cover for certified hospitals and utilities for 18 months, funded from unspent Digital Europe, EU4Health and Solidarity envelopes, not a new large fund we cannot pass at capital 12.

Conditionality is the instrument: backstop cover flows only to operators that isolate agent-contaminated tenants, submit to ENISA re-testing, and fail over essential workloads to EU-jurisdiction interim hosting and vetted open models. We invite the defecting capital's hospitals in on identical terms to end the side-deal split, while leaving gigafactory disbursement frozen until continuity is held.
