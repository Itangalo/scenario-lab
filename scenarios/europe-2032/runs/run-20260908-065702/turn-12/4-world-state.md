### The Plateau That Wasn’t

Evidence mounts that AI progress has slowed: a flagship model release underperforms, and prices for top-tier access drop, suggesting commoditization. Insiders dispute the reading—some claim optimization has merely shifted inward, away from public benchmarks. Still, the pause, real or perceived, halts the relentless climb. `ai_capability` rises only +0.5 this turn, the lowest in years.

With the frontier limping, open-weight development catches breath. A capable new release, built from leaked training recipes and optimized for consumer hardware, spreads rapidly through engineering and activist circles. `openweight_capability` rises to 56.0—narrowing the gap, though still far from parity.

### Sovereignty on Shaky Ground

The EU completes three major category 4 measures: *InvestAI Gigafactories*, the *Tech Sovereignty Package*, and *Sovereign Compute Reserves*. These deliver tangible compute gains, but integration lags. Runtime nodes in Bavaria and Silesia face delays—local protests, energy bottlenecks, and U.S. export warnings over audit access stall deployment. The Union’s physical stack is being built, but not yet unified.

In response, the EU launches *Accelerate Runtime Node Certification*, a small category 6 measure to fast-track interoperability with Sovereign Compute Reserves. It promises unified override protocols and 15-minute failover, but implementation is two turns away. No resilience bonus yet.
