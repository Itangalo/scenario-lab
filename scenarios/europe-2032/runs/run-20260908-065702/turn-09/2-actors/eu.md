## Statement changes
``modify `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure operational control over AI systems in critical infrastructure``
- Trigger: the two-year commitment period closes this turn

- modify `sovereign_access` (commitment): Guarantee uninterrupted, legal, and technically effective EU access to high-capability AI systems for evaluation, crisis response, and essential public services  
  - Grounds: The failure of the Sovereign Compute Reserves and the throttling of U.S.-hosted models during the Portuguese grid incident prove that access cannot be assumed even when legally mandated. The commitment must now shift from guaranteeing access as a right to enforcing it as a condition of physical control: we do not merely need access — we must own the stack where it matters. The new formulation of the two-year commitment reflects this reality.

- modify `enforceable_authority` (commitment): Ensure EU evaluative and audit power is binding, enforceable, and credibly backed by emergency action  
  - Grounds: The Sovereign Evaluation Corps remains blind to real-time system behaviour because developers refuse live access and the EU lacks sovereign infrastructure to host auditable instances. Without the ability to run, observe, and intervene in systems during crises, audit is reduced to retrospective inquiry. The commitment stands, but its implementation now depends on operational control, not just legal mandate.

## New measure
**Measure:** Mandate Operational Control for Critical AI Systems  
Require all AI systems above capability 50.0 used in EU critical infrastructure to operate on EU-anchored infrastructure with full-stack control, including runtime access, model weights, and data flows, enabling real-time audit and emergency override by the Evaluation Corps.  
`Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 11`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase eu_ai_sovereignty (+6–8), ai_safety (+4–6), and resilience (+3–5); eliminate dependency on foreign-controlled runtime environments; create a legally and technically enforceable zone of EU authority over high-risk AI operations.  
`Applies to:` Foreign and domestic AI developers operating in EU critical sectors (health, energy, transport, digital infrastructure), with compliance enforced through market access licensing and the Digital Crisis Protocol.

## Priority
**Mandate Operational Control for Critical AI Systems** — because sovereignty is not declared, it is exercised: without full-stack control, we cannot audit, respond, or defend, and this turn’s events prove that access without control is an illusion.

## In practice
We are drafting the **Critical AI Operational Control Act** under Article 114 TFEU, to be proposed to the Council and Parliament in Q3 2030. This is not a revised Access Act — it is a hard boundary: no system above 50.0 capability operates in critical infrastructure unless it runs on EU-anchored infrastructure where the Evaluation Corps has real-time access to model weights, runtime behaviour, and data flows, and where emergency override is technically guaranteed. We are no longer asking for cooperation — we are mandating control.

We are engaging with three EU-based hyperscalers — Atos Cloud, Deutsche CloudNet, and Polonia Data — to stand up certified sovereign nodes by Q2 2031, using emergency permitting from the revised Tech sovereignty framework. We are offering state-backed power contracts, access to public fibre, and liability shielding in exchange for binding commitments on uptime, security, and full access. France, Germany, and Poland have signaled readiness to align national procurement if the Act passes, and we are fast-tracking joint certification through the EuroHPC-AI Authority to avoid delays.

Behind the scenes, we are preparing for retaliation. The U.S. will call this protectionism; Washington may restrict even more. But we now know that rationed access is no access at all. If they cut us off, we must be able to run our own systems — not just evaluate them. This is not about parity with the frontier. It is about survival: the ability to see, decide, and act when the world outside goes dark.
