## New measure
Hardened Cyber Shield for Critical Infrastructure  
Establish continuous, AI-driven intrusion detection and automated containment in energy, water and port systems across the EU.

## Priority
**Hardened Cyber Shield for Critical Infrastructure** — because the cyber_test_shot proved detection gaps at scale, and resilience is the only lever we can move fast enough to matter before the next incident.

## In practice
We are acting on the intrusion discovered this autumn not as an anomaly but as a signal: the era of AI-coordinated, stealthy, cross-sector attacks has arrived. The cyber_test_shot compromised systems in three continents, including two EU grid operators, and was only found by accident. Our current detection regimes failed. We cannot wait for the next failure to be catastrophic. This turn, the Commission invokes Article 12 of the NIS2 Directive to launch an emergency reinforcement of detection and response in energy, water and transport. We are mobilising the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) to co-design, with member states and operators, an AI-augmented monitoring layer — trained on red-team simulations and integrated with automated containment protocols. Funding comes from the Digital Europe Programme and the Recovery and Resilience Facility, with €12bn frontloaded over the next 18 months.

This measure will bind all designated operators in critical sectors to deploy the shield by mid-2028. Resistance is expected from national utilities and port authorities citing cost and sovereignty, but the cyber_test_shot provides a shared factual basis to overcome it. We are offering full EU co-financing (75%) and fast-track access to sovereign AI compute via the InvestAI Gigafactories for model training and simulation. The Council’s Internal Market and Telecommunications formation will vote on the implementing act in December, backed by a public communication campaign framing the shield as basic infrastructure — like fire alarms in high-rises. This is not about catching up; it is about refusing to be a passive surface for attacks designed elsewhere, with consequences we alone absorb.
