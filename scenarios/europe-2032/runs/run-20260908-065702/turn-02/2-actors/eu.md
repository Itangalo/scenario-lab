## In practice  
We are responding to the cyber_major_incident with full force. The attack—targeting public services through a poisoned software dependency—has disrupted municipal systems in five member states, locking access to health records, tax processing, and social payments. The malware was adaptive, evading signature-based defences and propagating via AI-generated code that mimicked legitimate maintenance scripts. This is not the work of script kiddies. The tooling was model-generated, accessible, and effective. Our window to act as a defender is closing.

The Hardened Cyber Shield is now the operational core of our response. ENISA is fast-tracking the design of the AI monitoring layer, running red-team simulations this quarter with national CERTs and major software maintainers. We are mandating integration of the shield into all critical software supply chains by 2028, starting with public-sector vendors. The Council’s Internal Market and Telecommunications formation will vote on the implementing act in March, backed by emergency funding from the Digital Europe Programme—€6bn released immediately, with another €6bn contingent on delivery milestones. To overcome resistance, we are offering 75% co-financing and legal liability protection for operators who comply in good faith. The message is clear: we will not let our infrastructure become the testing ground for others’ offensive AI.

At the same time, we are pausing new commitments in category 4. The ai_investment_collapse has wiped out two of the three private hyperscaler partnerships under the Tech sovereignty package, and the InvestAI Gigafactories now face a funding shortfall of €45bn. We are convening a special Council session in April to reassess the fiscal envelope, explore public takeover of stalled sites, and consider whether the current compute build-out plan remains viable. The capability_plateau_evidence—falling prices, underwhelming releases—suggests the frontier may be stalling, but we cannot bet our sovereignty on a dip. We are acting under uncertainty, yes—but not in the dark. We will commit again when we see where the floor is.
