## New measure
**Municipal Utility and Hospital Cyber Backstop**
Creates an EU-backed reinsurance backstop and rapid-response patching teams for municipal utilities and hospitals hit by copycat swarm intrusions and repriced cyber cover.
This is Preparedness and resilience, because the insurance crunch is forcing maintenance cuts that turn the Shield's paper baselines into real outages now.

## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because copycat reuse of the published swarm toolkits makes exercised backup plans and joint detection the binding constraint, ahead of gigafactory siting.

## In practice
We are pushing M3 through ENISA and HERA with the Justice and Home Affairs Council: mandatory reporting drills become eligibility for EU-paid detection stacks, and the first joint exercises are redirected to municipal utilities and hospital groups named in the forensic write-ups. Interior and health ministers resisting obligations are offered the new Backstop funds only if they sign tested continuity plans.

In parallel we stand up the Backstop via DG ECHO's Civil Protection Mechanism and EIB reinsurance guarantees, pooling emergency patching teams from national CSIRTs to cover deferred-maintenance sites through June. M1 and M2 continue on DG CNECT track with two advanced gigafactory sites ring-fenced for investment decision, while stalled sites are held to EU-anchoring conditions rather than new money this turn.
