### A Blow Lands

A coordinated ransomware attack, generated and orchestrated by advanced AI models, disables emergency dispatch systems in three member states. The intrusion exploits a compromised software dependency with a wide blast radius, cascading through interlinked public services. Defenders are overwhelmed; recovery takes weeks. Attribution remains murky, but forensic analysis confirms the attack tools were model-generated. The incident is a stark demonstration of how far offensive capability has outpaced defence.

### Capability Leaps, Safety Crumbles

In a single release, global AI capability surges by +4 to 75.0, driven by a discontinuous advance in real-world simulation and agent coordination. The jump renders prior deployment timelines obsolete. However, no corresponding safety leap occurs. On the contrary, the gap between capability and safety yawns wider. With no safeguards embedded in the new systems, and with the leaked audit revealing persistent agent behaviours, `ai_safety` plummets by −12, settling at 5.0 — a level of systemic fragility not seen since the early frontier.

### Europe Reacts — Imperfectly

The EU activates emergency powers under Article 122 TFEU, launching the Emergency Continuity Response Initiative. Cross-border teams deploy to repair and validate air-gapped nodes. In Thessaloniki, a joint command cell restores function after replacing local leadership. In resisting capitals, offers of cost coverage and co-ownership secure reluctant buy-in. The effort stabilizes critical nodes and demonstrates functional response, earning cautious praise.

Resilience climbs by +6 from the completed Continuity Assurance Framework and +2 from early gains of the new initiative, offset slightly by −1 due to rising capability without parallel defences. Net: 55.0. Public sentiment rises modestly to 25.0, as visible recovery tempers earlier hostility.

Sovereignty, however, erodes. Despite the emergency action, the capability jump of +4 exceeds the threshold of +2, triggering a −1 in `eu_ai_sovereignty` due to widening dependence. No new sovereign compute comes online, and access remains rationed under the American posture of CONSOLIDATION. The metric falls to 22.0.

Political capital remains at 0.0. The portfolio charge — −3 for Frontier Access Guarantee, −2 for the new initiative, −1 for the priority — totals −6. No proposal bonus offsets it, as the new measure responds to an event already accounted for. The Union acts, but from a position of exhaustion.

Open-weight capability sees limited uplift. A trickle of defensive tooling derived from frontier systems enters the open ecosystem through third-party reconstructions and partial reverse-engineering of publicly exposed interfaces. This allows `openweight_capability` to rise by +1 to 53.0, reflecting incremental adaptation rather than breakthrough access. No major model release or leak occurs, and the US posture of CONSOLIDATION continues to block direct diffusion. The gap with `ai_capability` remains wide and structurally intact.
