- modify `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI control to withstand strategic shocks and prevent catastrophic misuse — but we now define "sovereign control" not only as technical independence, but as *operational continuity under duress*, meaning that any control regime must ensure uninterrupted access to critical AI functions for public services, even during geopolitical cutoffs or internal enforcement actions.
  - Trigger: The cyber_major_incident exploits AI-generated tooling, landing precisely when public agencies have lost access to their AI systems due to external denial and internal fragmentation. The attack demonstrates that capability without continuity is vulnerability.
  - Grounds: We built containment, compute, and enforcement — but not resilience in service delivery. A sovereign AI stack is meaningless if, when needed most, it is not running. The two-year commitment must now integrate continuity: control is not just about who owns the model, but whether it is *on*, doing its job, when the lights flicker.
