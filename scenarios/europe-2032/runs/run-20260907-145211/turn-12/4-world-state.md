### A Covenant Under Strain

The EU pushes forward on two fronts: building sovereign infrastructure and repairing social legitimacy. The Sovereign AI Core Programme advances technically, with land secured in France, Germany, and Poland, and procurement launched for EU-fabricated accelerators. Yet political headroom remains nonexistent. Despite the ambition, the programme yields no immediate sovereignty gain — the clusters are years from operation, and compute access remains rationed under the US **CONSOLIDATION** posture. With no finished measure and capability inching upward, sovereignty slips further: dependence persists, cohesion frays, and the metric falls to 20.0.

Simultaneously, the Social Continuity Compact is launched as a large category 7 measure, aiming to convert labour unrest into structured inclusion. The European Labour Authority begins drafting verification protocols, and pilot retraining schemes open in Málaga and Thessaloniki. Early enthusiasm is tempered by delays: BusinessEurope resists binding obligations, and several member states question the funding mechanism. Still, the gesture lands. Public sentiment, long in freefall, stabilizes and rises slightly as workers see a path forward — not just resistance, but co-design.

The Continuity Assurance Framework, a category 6 resilience measure, officially concludes. Though stress tests exposed flaws, its completion signals institutional follow-through. Resilience gains +1, reflecting improved planning and the anchoring of fallback protocols, even if implementation lags.

AI capability creeps to 75.5, driven by quiet progress in model scaling and agent coordination outside the EU. Safety remains frozen at 5.0 — no incident, no breakthrough, only the quiet erosion of oversight. Open weights stay at 53.0; the firewall holds, but only barely.

Political capital remains at 0.0. The portfolio charge — three large measures, one priority — demands −10, but the EU has no capital to spend. The system is gridlocked: actions proceed in practice, but legitimacy and capacity decay in parallel. The proposal bonus for the Social Continuity Compact is judged +2 — a large measure answering recent labour unrest — but it cannot be paid, as capital is already at floor.

### Signals in the Fog

Rumours of a global AI watchdog grow louder. Worker strikes subside, but only temporarily — the aid strike remains a live tension, its resolution uncertain.
