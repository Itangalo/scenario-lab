## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**

This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because the autumn test shot proved segmentation failed and detection would not have caught it, and the precursor window demands action now while Gigafactories build over years.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because without domestic compute the June switch-off threat remains an existential veto over everything else including resilience.

## In practice
We are pushing the Gigafactories selection through DG CNECT and the Competitiveness Council this turn: four to five sites, €200bn blended envelope, state-aid clearance via IPCEI, grid-connection fast-tracking with TSOs, and ASML leverage held in reserve against US pressure. France, Germany and the Nordics anchor the first sites; cohesion funds and EIB guarantees buy in the periphery.

Alongside it we launch the Critical Systems Shield via ENISA and the CER Directive implementation: emergency audits of transmission operators hit in autumn, mandatory segmentation standards for OT networks, 24-hour incident reporting to the EU-CyCLONe network, and joint exercises with port authorities. Funding comes from Digital Europe and member-state recovery lines, with telecoms and energy regulators as enforcers.

We do not pretend this fixes open-weight proliferation or lab control failures; it buys time and keeps hospitals, grids and ministries running when the next switch-off or swarm arrives.
