## In practice
We are putting M1/M2 into defensive hold through DG CNECT, DG ENER and the EIB: protecting site reservations and grid-connection queue positions legally, pausing compulsory measures, and opening mayor-level talks in the blocking municipalities on blackout resilience, water and power compensation, and construction traffic. No new ground-breaking is forced against blockades this turn.

We are living off the now-finished M3 EU Critical Systems Shield and M4 Monitoring Baseline as the operating spine: ENISA and EU-CyCLONe keep cross-border CERT mutual aid, paper-fallback procedures for hospitals and municipalities, and port/grid isolation playbooks running, while ministries work insurer-by-insurer to prevent cover withdrawal during attribution. Public communication admits dependence on foreign models for both attack tooling and recovery, and frames resilience as the bridge until EU capacity lands.
