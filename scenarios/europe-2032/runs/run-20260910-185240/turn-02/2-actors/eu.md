## New measure
**Insurer-Aligned Monitoring Baseline**
Sets a minimum AI-assisted monitoring standard for energy and port operators to retain cyber cover, delivered through ENISA certification and the Shield audits.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because the insurer repricing threatens to withdraw cover now unless monitoring is adopted, and a cheap fast baseline holds the Shield together while the capability jump and investment collapse play out.

## Priority
M3 EU Critical Systems Shield, because loss of insurance would cascade into port and grid shutdowns faster than the Gigafactory financing gap.

## In practice
We are driving M3 through ENISA and EU-CyCLONe this turn: emergency segmentation orders at the two hit TSOs, 24h reporting live, and joint port exercises brought forward to meet the insurer deadline. The new Baseline is bolted onto those audits, not a separate bureaucracy, with Digital Europe funds covering municipal utilities that cannot pay for monitoring themselves.

On M1/M2 we are in holding and salvage mode: DG CNECT and EIB are renegotiating the blended envelope after the investment collapse cancelled co-financing, protecting site reservations and grid connections while private tranches are replaced, with ASML and cohesion guarantees as the bargaining chips. We do not launch a parallel compute plan; we keep the Gigafactories alive until capital returns.
