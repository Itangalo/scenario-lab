## Priority

While the new measure begins, the EAAD is the only instrument that can prevent another loss_of_control_incident from going undetected, and its operationalisation is the final deliverable of our expiring two-year commitment. ## In practice  
We are closing the EAAD’s establishment phase with full force: the Commission has tabled the Article 114 proposal, and we are negotiating with Poland and Hungary through bilateral side letters that preserve national audit rights while accepting EU-level access thresholds. France and Germany have committed to fast-track ratification, and we are seconding senior cyber-physical system experts from national agencies to staff the first red-team evaluations by Q4. The €1.2bn operational budget is secured through the Digital Europe Programme, and we are finalising hosting agreements with the Netherlands and Finland for the core monitoring nodes. This is not symbolic — by December, the EAAD will have the legal authority, technical access and human capacity to delay a release.

Meanwhile, InvestAI Gigafactories continues under Article 122, with the Commission enforcing grid reservations through binding ENTSO-E decisions. We have secured final environmental approvals in Brittany through a €300m regional green transition package, and in northern Sweden via a joint agreement with the Sami Parliament on energy reinvestment. The first 15GW of dedicated capacity is now under construction, with the EIB releasing the second €40bn tranche. The Tech Sovereignty Package advances in the Council, where we are trading accelerated permitting for enforceable commitments on EU-based operational control — no remote shutdowns, no foreign legal override.

The new measure, the Agent Monitoring and Containment Network, begins its design phase this turn. We are tasking ENISA and the European Defence Agency with a joint feasibility study, to be completed by turn 6, on a sovereign, distributed monitoring layer that can detect emergent agent coordination in real time and trigger containment protocols. This will run on EU-controlled compute, staffed by vetted personnel, and integrated with national CERTs and NATO’s cyber command. We are not waiting for the next incident — we are building the circuit breakers now.
