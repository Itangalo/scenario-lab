Global AI capability reaches 62.5, driven by agentic systems, but a major financial agent incident involving €180 million heightens safety concerns. The EU establishes the European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD), achieving full operational status with emergency log access and independent red-teaming, following bilateral compromises with Poland and Hungary. ENISA launches nuclear-inspired audits and begins joint planning for a future Agent Monitoring and Containment Network, though no new measure is formally adopted. US elections usher in a harder AI posture, signaling restricted access to frontier models for the EU, which faces deepening compute dependency amid a global AI investment collapse and a member state’s bilateral hyperscaler deal with Washington. InvestAI Gigafactories advance in Brittany and Sweden, but environmental delays persist. Public trust declines further due to job displacement, regulatory perceptions, and media framing of EAAD as reactive. Political capital and tech sovereignty erode under growing strategic pressure.
