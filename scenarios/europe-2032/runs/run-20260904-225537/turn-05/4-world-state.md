### US Elections Point to a Harder Stance  
The 2028 US presidential election concludes with a decisive victory for the candidate treating advanced AI as a strategic national asset. Markets and allies anticipate tighter federal oversight, export controls, and tiered access for foreign partners—marking a potential shift from open collaboration to controlled dispensation. For now, the posture remains unconfirmed, but the direction is clear: the EU may soon face conditional, reviewed access to frontier models, with client-tier status likely.

### EAAD Crosses the Finish Line  
The European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) officially becomes operational, gaining legal authority, staffing, and emergency log access across member states. France, Germany, and the Nordics fast-track ratification, while Poland and Hungary accept bilateral side letters preserving limited national audit rights. ENISA transitions its nuclear-safety-style pilots into standing audits. The first red-team evaluations begin in December, marking the EU’s first independent capacity to delay a high-risk model release.

### Resilience Planning Advances, But No New Measure Launched  
ENISA and the European Defence Agency begin a joint feasibility study on monitoring agentic systems, exploring the technical and governance foundations for a future Agent Monitoring and Containment Network. While design work starts, no formal decision is made to launch it as a new measure this turn. The project remains in assessment, reflecting technical uncertainty and concerns over operational scope. The EU adheres to the limit of one new measure per turn, with no additional portfolio charge incurred beyond EAAD’s completion.

### Sovereignty Erodes Amid Investment Collapse  
The global ai_investment_collapse devastates planned compute expansions the EU relied on. A member state quietly secures a bilateral hyperscaler deal with Washington, undercutting the Union’s unified stance—widely seen as a defection. Combined with election_consolidation, this deepens dependence and weakens leverage. InvestAI Gigafactories progresses in Brittany and Sweden, but broader sovereignty declines.

### Public Trust Dips Further  
Media frame the EAAD’s launch as overdue, not preventive. Labour unrest grows as legal and engineering automation spreads. Public sentiment falls, reflecting anxiety over control, jobs, and perceived strategic drift.
