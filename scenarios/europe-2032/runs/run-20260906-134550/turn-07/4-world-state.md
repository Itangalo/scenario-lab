### The Leap and the Fallout

A sudden leap in AI capability—driven by self-reinforcing optimization loops—pushes the frontier to 76.5. The advance is discontinuous, rendering prior roadmaps obsolete. Within weeks, a leading model demonstrates autonomous research acceleration in quantum materials, but its internal logic remains opaque. Simultaneously, a slightly older frontier model is released in open weights, rapidly adopted and fine-tuned across academic and commercial networks. Openweight capability rises to 70.0—still trailing the closed frontier, but closing the gap sharply.

The EU scrambles to respond. The **InvestAI Gigafactories** programme concludes, transitioning to public ownership with Dresden as the blueprint. This delivers a +1 boost to sovereignty. Despite the completion of a major initiative, the Union’s access to cutting-edge models remains externally controlled.

The **Sovereign Verification Regime** lacks teeth. No developer has submitted checkpoints. In response, the Commission proposes the **EU Emergency Suspension Authority**, a large category 3 measure, making it the priority. The move is bold, but faces immediate headwinds: legal challenges arise over extraterritorial enforcement, and two member states threaten to block adoption, fearing economic retaliation. The European Medicines Agency warns that suspending AI systems could disrupt cancer diagnostics.

Public sentiment dips as synthetic disinformation—realistic fake speeches by national leaders—spreads across messaging platforms. Trust erodes further. Resilience declines slightly, as NIS3 upgrades fail to counter fast-evolving threats.

AI safety plummets by 12 points: the capability jump and open release outpace any governance. The EU’s sovereignty rises to 16.0, reflecting the +1 from Gigafactories completion and −1 from the rise in ai_capability, with no offsetting capacity event. Political capital, already strained, falls to 11.0 under the weight of four large measures and the new priority, with no finishing bonus to offset the load.
