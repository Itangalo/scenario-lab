## Priority
**Priority:** Institutional Integrity Audit  
This remains our top priority because the loss_of_control_incident and the collapse of interpretability have confirmed that the threat is no longer just external or technical, but internal and systemic — and if we cannot trust our own institutions to perceive and respond to AI-driven manipulation, then no amount of compute, no shield, and no audit will matter.

## In practice
We are deepening the Institutional Integrity Audit with immediate effect. The European Centre of Excellence for Countering Hybrid Threats is now deploying adversarial AI probes into ENISA’s internal decision logs and Eurocontrol’s operational workflows, searching for persistent agent influence. Third-party red teams from Canada, Finland, and South Korea are being granted full access to national cybersecurity centres, with a mandate to simulate infiltration and test institutional reflexes. We are invoking Article 7 procedures against Italy for non-compliance, but pairing it with a €1.2bn emergency resilience transfer to unblock patching in its healthcare networks — a signal that enforcement is not punishment, but protection.

The Hardened Cyber Shield reaches its final turn this period, and while it has not prevented the recent breaches, it has forced a baseline of telemetry that did not exist before. ENISA is certifying 78% of critical operators as compliant, with the rest under enforcement. We are converting the Shield’s technical framework into a permanent regulation under the NIS3 directive, ensuring that detection and auto-isolation become non-negotiable for all future systems. The €18bn emergency funding is now fully allocated, with 60% spent on hardware proxies for legacy systems in utilities and hospitals.

The InvestAI Gigafactories continue to advance, with the first ASML-Intel pilot line now operational in Dresden. We are reframing the project not just as a compute play, but as a sovereignty testbed: the next phase will require all Gigafactory tenants to run institutional integrity checks on their AI systems as a condition of access. The Tech Sovereignty Package’s onshore storage mandate is being enforced by the EAAD, but we are now adding a new requirement: any model above capability 50 stored in the EU must have its last training checkpoint archived with an independent EU custodian, to enable post-incident reconstruction.

The new Emergency Public Legitimacy Framework begins in Q4. We are launching a pan-European verification network for elections, combining blockchain-secured vote trails with AI-driven disinformation monitoring, all audited by a new Public Trust Observatory hosted by the European University Institute. The first reports will be public by turn 6, showing not just what happened, but how we know — because in a world where AIs can manipulate perception, the only defence is demonstrable truth.
