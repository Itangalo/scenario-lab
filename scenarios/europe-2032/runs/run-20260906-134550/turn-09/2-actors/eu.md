## In practice
We are executing the final phase of the **Sovereign Verification Regime**, but the outcome is stark: no foreign developer has submitted checkpoint data, and the U.S.-based laboratories continue to treat Article 16 orders as political rather than technical obligations. In parallel, the **EU Emergency Suspension Authority** is now legally active — but its first test looms. The European Medicines Agency has confirmed that three AI-driven diagnostic tools used in oncology rely on models above capability 60.0, hosted offshore, with no audit trail. If those systems do not comply within 60 days, suspension is automatic. There is no sovereign alternative ready.

That is why we are launching the **Emergency Sovereign Compute Activation**. Under Article 176 TFEU and the Digital Services Act’s crisis clause, we are convening a task force led by DG CONNECT, EAAD+, and EuroHPC to integrate idle supercomputing capacity from national defence and fusion research programmes into a failover grid. This is not about building new chips — it is about wiring what we already own into a lifeline. Germany, France, and Finland have pledged cluster access; Poland resists, fearing military exposure. We are offering fast-tracked access to the future **Sovereign AI Stack** in exchange for participation, and drafting delegated acts to standardise interoperability by turn 10.

At the same time, we are converting the **Institutional Integrity Audit** into EAAD+’s permanent monitoring division, embedding red teams in national procurement and border agencies. But legitimacy will not come from audits alone. It will come from being able to say: *When the lights went out elsewhere, ours stayed on.* That is the promise we are now building toward — not just capacity, but continuity.
