### War finds Europe
By February the Pacific war was no longer distant. American and Chinese strikes traded against fabrication plants, cable landings, satellite links and data-centre clusters, and two blasts near Mediterranean cable stations and a fire at a North Sea interconnect put Europe on the target map. No one in Brussels had voted for belligerency; no one was asked.

The open frontier did not pause. A new openly downloadable system landed within months of the closed lead, downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in a week. What it could do was now on private hardware for good.

At home, hiring data hardened into a political fact. Entry posts in law, accountancy, software support and administration were simply not returning. Graduates who had hoped the new transition Guarantee would bridge them found bridge pay slow and retraining vague. Therapy queues, once a bright spot, stuttered as power rationing and cloud billing collided.

### Shield up, grid down
The Union answered with emergency continuity. Energy solidarity and civil protection mechanisms were triggered, repair battalions dispatched to the two gigafactory grid connections, hospitals and certified triage computing given priority dispatch, cross-border failover offered for hit cables and centres. Soldiers guarded substations that activists had torched weeks before.

It partly worked. Lights stayed on in most places, hospitals kept scheduling. Construction did not restart. Coordinated night arson and blockades spread from robot pilot sites to grid works, halting cranes for weeks. Mayors demanded protection before reopening warehouses; works councils demanded reopenings before policing sites.

Diplomats raced a second track, offering cable-landing, cleanroom and evaluation-host neutrality to both belligerents in exchange for EU sites being spared, while pooling orders for spare parts and medical inference. The wayward capital listened politely and kept its separate foreign pact. Pooled bargaining bought time, not chips, while export controls tightened around a war economy.

Frontier training loops, now largely unattended, ran on through the smoke. Brussels was still reading release notes after the fact.
