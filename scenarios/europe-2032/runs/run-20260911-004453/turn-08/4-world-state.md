### The jump lands
In February a US lab demonstrated agents that planned across days with little supervision, breaking benchmarks Brussels had used for hospital triage clearance. Overnight, deployment roadmaps were obsolete. EuroHPC queues lengthened as researchers tried to reproduce the results on older hardware.

The Safety Institute invoked its notification powers within weeks. Deployers operating in the Union, including one routing updates through a Swiss subsidiary, were told to notify and pause jump-enabled releases pending re-run evaluations. Public statements spoke of compliance; in practice legal letters disputed scope and several vendors shipped with warning labels while files waited for compute. Brussels had no agreement, no market-access lever, and no extra evaluation compute to force a different outcome this turn, and the hold remained largely paper.

### Holds that hold paper
Health ministers welcomed the pause. With clinics still on rationed therapy and paper fallbacks, no one wanted unreviewed automation in wards. Unions in Rotterdam and Duisburg repeated the bargain: no unreviewed systems, but staffing floors kept.

What worked was degraded-mode work, in part. The continuity regime formalised this spring — islanding drills, spare-parts pools, a ring-fenced EuroHPC slice — meant a March grid fault in the east degraded rather than stopped services. Hospitals ran on paper for a day without panic. But with no new funding source, staff to run the review queue, or accelerators to clear it, the two finished continuity instruments could only blunt the fault, not raise system-wide readiness.

The supply compact delivered less. Japanese maintenance crews kept Paris and Warsaw halls powered, but no accelerators arrived. Ribbon-cut gigafactories stayed largely empty, and pooled buying again lost to Washington-backed bids. DG TRADE kept channels open quietly to avoid holds becoming outages.

By June Brussels had a notification queue it could not yet run and a continuity regime only partly staffed. The pause bought time, not assurance, and the political cost of carrying two continuity measures without results to show weighed on capital.
