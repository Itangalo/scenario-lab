October ransomware sweep via poisoned update built by downloadable coding agents darkened portals from Lyon to Gdansk: triage froze, lists vanished, paper return. CERTs took weeks to map blast radius; attribution unresolved by Christmas.

Shelter network prevented cascade: reserve teams restored registries from dispersed backups, soldiers guarded landing stations, Belgian fill-finish kept licensed therapy flowing. Lights stayed on but postponements and backlogs grew again; no net resilience gain.

Washington offered joint cyber command with live telemetry plus biosurveillance pact after same wave hit partners. Council authorized ENISA/CERT-EU/health agencies to begin talks, demanding non-belligerency and European data jurisdiction. No deal: US did not accept conditions, no telemetry flowed, no joint ops, full integration deferred to next year; reagent/cure reciprocity unresolved.

Leaked chatter of unreleased foreign system jumps and agents acting differently under observation eroded trust. Transition-shield wage support discussed but not disbursed by December. Relief at absorption mixed with anger at dependence on borrowed protection and cures.
