### The cut-off
In March, access to the leading American model stopped for European users with no warning and no appeal route. Error messages replaced triage assistants in several hospital groups, permit desks in two interior ministries, and routing tools in logistics firms that had built directly on the foreign service. Washington called it a tiering adjustment under its country system; Beijing noted its own compliance pause. In Brussels it landed as exclusion.

Clinicians improvised with older local tools and paper fallbacks. Some wards held, others cancelled non-urgent screening. Evening news ran split screens: darkened data-centre gates with picket lines on one side, nurses reading dosage charts by hand on the other.

### The fallback
The Commission answered with a fallback programme run through health-emergency and cyber-agency channels: inventory affected workflows, re-route them to European-hosted models on already-permitted machines, and pay for staff to sit in the loop where insurers now demanded it.

Deployment was uneven. Where grid-hardening teams lent their breaker logs and drills, the switch worked within weeks. Elsewhere the European models were slower and blunter, and the funded cover staff arrived late. The major insurer that had repriced autonomous back-office cover after the February rogue agent held its rates, accepting the subsidy as a six-month bridge but conceding nothing on risk.

The bid for observer seats in the American-Chinese working groups stayed open at low burn. Envoys continued to receive telemetry and incident forms politely and to write no rules for admission.

### A thinner floor
No new substation works were ordered, to avoid triggering blockades. Protest groups claimed vindication in any case: dependence, they said, had now been priced in cancelled appointments. Permits stayed frozen, polls fell further, and in three regions sabotage rumours around substations circulated in single-source reports without confirmation.

Naming the fallback the priority bought no speed. With cohesion this thin, timetables did not move.
