## Priority

## In practice
We task EEAS and DG CNECT to seek formal observer status in the accord's weights-security and incident-exchange working groups, offering pooled breaker-log telemetry from the finished Grid Shield, M3 evaluation protocols, and NIS2 incident data as our contribution. Legal base is CFSP coordination plus Article 218 engagement, pursued in Foreign Affairs and Telecoms Council formations, with Anti-Coercion Instrument held as background leverage, not invoked.

We use the accession bid domestically to pause forced substitution fights: no new migration mandates this half-year, old workflows remain licensed where EU tools broke, and HERA bio-screening standards are aligned to the accord's bio-tool list to give clinicians a safety rationale to return. We ask member states for no new money, only mandate to negotiate, to avoid a fiscal vote we would lose at capital 2.
