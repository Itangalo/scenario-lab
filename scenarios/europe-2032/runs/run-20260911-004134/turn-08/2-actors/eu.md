**EU Work Transition and Service Continuity Pact**
Puts displaced clerical, customer-ops and junior professional workers into paid retraining, wage-bridged redeployment to hospitals, grid, cyber-recovery and municipal repair, tied to employer contributions from automating firms. This is Labour and social protection work, and why now is because measurable graduate and entry-level displacement plus sentiment at 22 and strikes in logistics hubs mean the semiconductor rupture becomes a legitimacy rupture this turn unless job loss is visibly absorbed into the continuity operation holding essential services.
