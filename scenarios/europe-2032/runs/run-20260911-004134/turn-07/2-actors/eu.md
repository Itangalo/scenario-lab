## Priority
EU Essential Services Continuity and Cyber Recovery Operation, because with the frontier cut off and a model-generated attack cascading in public services, holding autonomy and essential services through the rupture — our two-year commitment — demands recovery and continuity before any new gate or build.

## In practice
We activate the permanent continuity cell as operational command: DG DIGIT and ENISA run 24-hour triage for the ransomware/dependency blast, with cross-border CERT mutual aid and mandatory incident reporting under NIS2, while EuroHPC and the rationing board re-platform cut-off hospitals and ministries to degraded-but-running European models and protect their inference allocation. Health and Energy Councils carry the rationing decision; money is re-phased from Gigafactory milestones, not new funds.

The Supply Compact (M7) stays as procurement tail — second Japanese lot, Seoul/Tokyo pooling, hospital/grid priority — and the Safety Institute keeps its two limited holds but orders no new ones until reasoning opacity can be re-tested, to avoid irritating firms while delivering nothing. Health ministers speak with one line: queues are rationed openly, cures stay under EU control, and recovery, not new promises, is the deliverable this winter.
