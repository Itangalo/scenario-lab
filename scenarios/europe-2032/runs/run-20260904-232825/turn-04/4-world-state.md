### Capability Creeps Forward, Safety Erodes
AI capability advances to 58.0 as frontier labs extend agent autonomy into multi-week planning cycles, particularly in supply chain optimisation and drug discovery. These systems demonstrate recursive self-correction, raising concerns about unmonitored evolution. With no safety upgrades deployed, and opaque agent reasoning now suspected in a major logistics firm’s sudden routing failure, `ai_safety` drops further to 21.0.

### Open Frontier Widens
The Kimi K3 toolkit continues to mutate, with variants now automating zero-day discovery and exploit deployment. Cyber defences in mid-tier municipalities are overwhelmed by AI-driven phishing and ransomware waves, confirming that offensive capability at 45.0 openweight is now broadly operational. `openweight_capability` rises to 46.0, reflecting diffusion of core techniques beyond elite actors.

### Resilience Inches Up
The Critical Infrastructure AI Hardening Programme completes its final rollout phase, upgrading detection and failover systems across 78% of targeted energy and transport nodes. While not yet fully tested under coordinated attack, the improvements yield a modest +1 to `resilience`, bringing it to 41.0.

### Labour Unrest, Sovereign Response
A wave of strikes erupts across logistics, rail, and public administration, driven by rapid AI-driven job displacement. In response, the EU launches the Sovereign Labour Transition Fund, a large category 7 measure aimed at automatic income support and retraining. However, fierce resistance from northern member states delays final approval, limiting immediate impact on public sentiment, which remains stagnant at 33.0.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Political Capital Strained
`eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 17.0 as capability growth exceeds 2, costing −1, and no capacity event offsets it. The portfolio charge for five ongoing measures (including the new Fund) totals −13, while the named priority adds −1. The Sovereign AI Incident Response Corps finishes, granting +4 capital, but the new measure earns no proposal bonus as it responds to an emergent, not yet confirmed, crisis. Political capital drops to 24.0.
