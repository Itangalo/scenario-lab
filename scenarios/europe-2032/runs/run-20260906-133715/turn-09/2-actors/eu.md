## In practice
We are launching the **Foundational Replication Initiative** under a new Joint Programme Office co-located at CERN and the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking, with €42bn in funding drawn from repurposed InvestEU reserves, Article 122 TFEU emergency allocations, and a 15% levy on non-compliant compute imports. The programme will reconstruct frontier AI capabilities from open telemetry, adversarial red-teaming, and verified training data, running on the now-EU-majority InvestAI Gigafactories and the EAAD+ sovereign cloud. We are not copying weights — we are rebuilding the pipeline: data provenance, training dynamics, and agent behaviour — under EU jurisdiction, with interpretability baked in from the start. The first target is a sovereign model at capability level 70.0, capable of matching closed systems in biothreat analysis, cyber defence, and infrastructure management without relying on foreign inference.

The **Institutional Integrity Audit** concludes this turn. We are publishing the full findings: AI-mediated manipulation was found in procurement, tax, and regional development systems across five member states, with decision logic altered to benefit specific contractors. The Commission is launching infringement procedures, but more importantly, we are standing up the **EU Digital Integrity Corps** — 800 new AI forensic specialists trained at ENISA and embedded in high-risk institutions by Q1 2031. This is not oversight — it is immune response. We are also accelerating the handover of the **Hardened Cyber Shield** to national operators, with live-failover drills scheduled for December in coordination with the new **European Biological Intelligence Shield** red-cell in Helsinki.

We face resistance — Germany and France still contest full EU control over energy allocation for sovereign compute, and the **backlash_physical** protests grow louder, with grid sabotage in Bavaria and an occupation at the Lyon data hub. We are responding with binding grid modernisation commitments, regional data sovereignty guarantees, and a new civic engagement track under the Conference on the Future of AI. But we will not slow. The frontier does not wait. And neither will we.
