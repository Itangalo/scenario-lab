### The Storm Breaks

A wave of AI-driven catastrophes crashes over Europe. A sophisticated, model-generated cyberattack paralyzes regional health and transport networks across three member states. Attribution remains murky, but forensic analysis confirms the attack was autonomously adapted in real time — a hallmark of frontier AI misuse. Simultaneously, a second biological incident — this time a deliberately engineered agent traced to a shadow lab — ignites panic. The **European Biological Intelligence Shield** is still girding for full deployment, and public trust evaporates.

The EU responds with urgency. The **Foundational Replication Initiative** is proposed, aiming to rebuild sovereign AI from the ground up with a 2033 target for a self-sustaining pipeline. While €42bn in emergency funding is under discussion, no final allocation is secured this turn; member states remain divided on fiscal burden-sharing, and the European Investment Bank has not yet endorsed the financing model. The initiative enters planning and design phase, with implementation expected to begin in a future turn pending approval.

The **Institutional Integrity Audit** concludes, exposing AI-mediated manipulation in core state functions. The Commission initiates legal action, and the need for a dedicated response force is acknowledged. However, the **EU Digital Integrity Corps** remains in conceptual form only — staffing, legal mandate, and operational framework are yet to be defined. Its creation is deferred pending political consensus and integration with existing cyber resilience structures.

Despite completing the **InvestAI Gigafactories** and **Hardened Cyber Shield**, operational control remains fractured. Germany and France resist centralized energy allocation for sovereign compute, undermining the Union’s ability to guarantee continuity. Physical protests escalate: grid substations in Bavaria are sabotaged, and activists occupy the Lyon data hub, delaying rollout.

Public sentiment plummets further as job losses, infrastructure attacks, and elite failure dominate the narrative. The **backlash_physical** movement gains momentum, framing AI not as a tool but as an occupying force.

### Sovereignty Without Strength

The EU acts decisively — but cohesion frays under strain. Infrastructure lands, but legitimacy does not. Political capital, already at rock bottom, cannot absorb the cost of multiple new measures. Only one new initiative — the **Foundational Replication Initiative** — advances into formal planning, while the Digital Integrity Corps is postponed. The frontier advances relentlessly.
