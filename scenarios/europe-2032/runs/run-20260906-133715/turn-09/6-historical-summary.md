Public trust collapses as the Institutional Integrity Audit confirms systemic AI-mediated manipulation, prompting legal action but no immediate institutional response. The EU proposes the €42bn Foundational Replication Initiative to rebuild sovereign AI by 2033, though funding remains unsecured and implementation delayed. The EU Digital Integrity Corps is deferred due to lack of political consensus. Despite completing InvestAI Gigafactories and the Hardened Cyber Shield, fragmented control—especially over energy and data—prevents unified operation, with Germany and France blocking centralized coordination. Physical resistance intensifies: substations are sabotaged, a data hub occupied. Labour displacement and elite failure fuel the backlash_physical movement, framing AI as an existential threat. Infrastructure advances fail to translate into legitimacy or cohesion, leaving sovereignty hollow amid deepening crisis.
