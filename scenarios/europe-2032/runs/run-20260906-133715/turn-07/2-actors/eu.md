## Priority  
**Priority:** Foundational Replication Initiative  
This is now our top priority because the **emergent_efficiency_breakthrough** has shattered the assumption that frontier AI is too expensive or complex to replicate — and the **bio_uplift_findings** show that we cannot afford to be dependent on systems we cannot rebuild. Verification is necessary, but no longer sufficient: we must be able to *replace* what we cannot control. This is the only way to reconcile our two mandates — sovereignty and safety — in a world where the frontier is now portable.

## In practice  
We are launching the **Foundational Replication Initiative** under the Horizon Europe framework, with a €45bn allocation drawn from the repurposed InvestAI Gigafactories fund. The initiative will be hosted by a new European AI Foundry, co-located with the EAAD+ in Dresden and legally insulated from external influence. Its mission is clear: given only the inputs, outputs, and telemetry of a high-capability system — not the weights — we will reconstruct its function using modular, auditable components. This is not copying; it is sovereign *re-creation*. The first target is the oncology model behind the **medical_breakthrough**, which we were denied access to — we will rebuild its diagnostic logic from clinical outcomes and public trial data, using synthetic data generation and adversarial distillation. We are not waiting for permission.

The **Institutional Integrity Audit** continues, now entering its most sensitive phase. The anomalous data routing in two national digital ID systems has triggered emergency reviews by ENISA and Europol. We have activated the EU’s Cyber Rapid Response Team to conduct live forensics, and the Public Trust Observatory is publishing redacted logs in real time. This is not just about detecting AI influence — it is about proving that the EU can still *own* its institutions. The Audit will finish on schedule in two turns, and its final report will set the baseline for a new wave of reforms.

We have formally closed the **InvestAI Gigafactories** and **Tech Sovereignty Package** programmes. The Gigafactories will become EU-majority ventures, but their purpose shifts: they are no longer just compute hosts — they are now the physical backbone for the Foundational Replication Initiative, running sovereign inference clusters on reconstructed models. The state-aid debate is over; this is now a security imperative. The Council will vote under Article 122 TFEU, treating AI sovereignty as a matter of critical infrastructure. We are not nationalising — we are *fortifying*.
