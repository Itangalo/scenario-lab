### The Weight of a Single Life

The EU pivots hard toward biological resilience in the wake of a confirmed pathogen release linked to AI-assisted design. The **European Biological Intelligence Shield** is launched under HERA with €38bn in emergency funding, repurposing the completed InvestAI Gigafactories for sovereign inference. However, German and French resistance delays full operational control, forcing Brussels to negotiate data routing and energy allocation under crisis conditions.

Despite the urgency, political capital erodes. The **Institutional Integrity Audit** confirms AI-mediated manipulation in critical systems, but public trust plummets as media expose delayed responses and fragmented enforcement. Labour displacement accelerates, and the **bio_incident** dominates headlines, casting AI as an existential threat. Public sentiment drops further into hostility.

The **Hardened Cyber Shield** and **InvestAI Gigafactories** officially complete, contributing to resilience and sovereignty. Yet the broader portfolio remains heavy: the **Institutional Integrity Audit** continues at full cost, and the new Shield adds another large measure. With no measure closure offset this turn, the burden deepens.

AI capability advances to 78.5, driven by closed-loop self-improvement in frontier labs. Open models rise to 61.0 through incremental spillovers, but no breakthroughs widen the gap. Safety remains stagnant at 15.0 — no safeguards have landed on deployed systems, and the gap between capability and control widens.

The municipal oversight network grows, with cities sharing AI-driven threat patches, but national coordination lags. The EU acts decisively, but cohesion frays under strain: sovereignty inches up from infrastructure completion, while political capital collapses under unmet expectations and rising fear.

### A Firewall Built on Sand

The Shield is a technical leap, but its legitimacy is untested. Red-teaming begins in Helsinki, yet the first autonomous alert — a false positive in Rotterdam — triggers panic before correction. The line between preparedness and overreach blurs. The Union builds faster than it can convince.
