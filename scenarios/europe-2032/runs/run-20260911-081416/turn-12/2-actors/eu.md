## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee**
A small labour and social protection backstop that pairs wage insurance and paid retraining for displaced junior coders, analysts and support staff with a transition levy on automating employers, plus a human-sign-off rule for benefits AI to break the dark-clinics-plus-wrongful-denials pairing. Because with sentiment at 1 and capital at 12 we cannot rebuild sovereignty until people see services stay up and lost entry jobs lead somewhere.

## In practice
We run this through EPSCO and the ESF+ reprogramming, not a new AI Act fight: DG EMPL funds the wage-insurance and retraining places, DG CNECT and the AI Office attach the human-sign-off and log-reading condition to the benefits audit already underway, with national PES as delivery agents. Employers automating tier-one coding, analysis and support pay the transition contribution; municipalities that blocked data-centre siting get maintenance and retraining jobs tied to the repair corps.

We keep the finished Shield, Backstop and Fallback Stack sustained on existing NIS2/UCPM and EuroHPC lines without reopening them: ENISA keeps isolation gates and offline-fallback checks, the mid-power compact keeps chips flowing. No new compute build this turn; we spend the one slot on making displacement land as adjustment, to buy room for sovereignty later.
