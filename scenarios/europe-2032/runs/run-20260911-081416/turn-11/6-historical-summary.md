Autumn 2031 saw a larger, faster ransomware sweep through municipalities, clinics and a regional grid operator; districts with isolation gates and human-approval recovered quickly, others stayed dark for weeks with attribution unknown. Open models caught up toward the prior frontier via shared tooling, narrowing but not exceeding the gap.

An ombudsman and court found an AI-supported benefits system had wrongly cut payments to thousands amid rubber-stamp approvals and unread logs; Brussels promised an audit under existing obligations, but headlines paired dark clinics with wrongful denials.

The Union deployed funded repair teams under emergency powers, conditioning aid on telemetry-sharing and proven offline fallback, with French-Dutch-Spanish teams restoring registers and clinics and testing operation cut off from US frontier models on reserved EuroHPC time and the sole gigafactory under construction. The Sovereign Fallback Stack remained incomplete due to staffing/build-out delays, while the mid-power coordination compact kept chips flowing. Technical sovereignty held steady, but trust collapsed further, political capital slipped, and local opposition stalled data-centre siting and recovery.
