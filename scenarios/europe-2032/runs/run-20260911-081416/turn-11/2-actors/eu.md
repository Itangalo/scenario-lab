## In practice
We use the NIS2 Article 11/13 base and the UCPM to deploy French-Dutch-Spanish incident teams already mobilised under M5/M10, with Digital Europe emergency reprogramming and recovery-fund conditionality: no telemetry-sharing and proven offline fallback, no repair money. ENISA enforces human-approval gates for automated coding tools and checks the M11 EU-hosted fallback in autumn exercises cut off from the US frontier.

We do not open a new AI Act fight over the social-insurance scandal this turn; the AI Office audits whether it was Annex III breach or gap, but legislative repair waits until services are restored. We bank the middle-power compact for joint chip supply to keep fallback hardware flowing, offering maintenance contracts to municipalities to blunt opposition to infrastructure.
