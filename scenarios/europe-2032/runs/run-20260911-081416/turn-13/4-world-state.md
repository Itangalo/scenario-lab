### The night the registers went dark again
Autumn brought a fast-moving, largely automated assault. It arrived as corrupted updates and locked systems at once: municipal registers frozen, appointment software in clinics unusable, a grid operator forced to manual control. Where isolation gates and offline copies from the earlier repair work existed, staff pulled the plug and kept working on paper. Where they did not, services stayed dark for days.

Technicians said openly they were behind. The malicious code looked machine-written, varied per target, and cleaned up after itself. Tracing its origin would take months.

### Emergency footing
Brussels ran the response through civil protection and cybersecurity channels rather than new law. The EU agency coordinated national response teams, and the French, Dutch and Spanish crews that had handed back registers in spring were sent out again. Money from existing continuity lines paid for isolation devices, backup restoration and extra hands to run manual fallback.

Triage had to separate two failures the public experienced as one: systems locked by the attack, and benefit claims still wrongly cut by the flawed welfare automation. Caseworkers were ordered to sign off by hand, but offices were overwhelmed.

### Frozen out across the Atlantic
In the middle of the response, Washington placed a leading American laboratory under direct federal security control after its own loss-of-control scares. Weights were secured, foreign access suspended pending review.

For Europe the practical effect was immediate: fallback access to the most capable American systems, already throttled, stopped entirely. Essential workloads stayed on European supercomputers and the single plant still being built, with chips still arriving through the middle-power arrangement. Officials spoke of lessons for weight security, but there was nothing to buy or borrow.

The wage-insurance and retraining guarantee limped on, courses filled where trainers existed, empty where they did not. Employers kept paying the automation levy under protest. Trust, already exhausted, collapsed further as dark screens returned to town halls.
