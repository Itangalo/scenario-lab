## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, because staged access in two member-state grids still undetected in production makes finishing pilot-to-deployment the precondition for the two-year commitment, ahead of the slower evaluation build.

## In practice
We keep the Shield as the operational push: ENISA with EU-CyCLONe moves from pilots to live swarm-monitoring on transmission operators and ports, using the 70% co-financing to settle cost-allocation fights and a fast-track tender repair; DG ENER ties gigafactory grid-connection approvals to Shield sensor rollout to blunt municipal opposition in Spain, Germany and Netherlands over power/water.

In parallel we seed the new evaluation capacity inside ENISA/EU AI Office with Digital Europe reprogramming: a small vetted evaluator team with pre-release access agreements and weight-security audit mandate, staffed by retaining frontier talent that would otherwise leave, and tasked first with learning from Shield incident data. M1/M2 site selection continues on its track without a new compute programme, preserving capital while the Shield spends it where harm is immediate.
