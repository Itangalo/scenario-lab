## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for political capital to recover above 2.0 and for the joint response seat to be taken without spending a new instrument we cannot staff.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority measure in flight to name; with capital at 2.0 and all programmes finished, any new large or small instrument would fail on delivery and risk paralysis, so we husband enforcement and take protection through the invited joint command.

## In practice
We treat the automated attack as the turn's governing shock. DG CNECT and ENISA keep 24h monitoring on the two transmission grids and the large port where the Shield held, extend hospital and ministry fallbacks where already live, and direct the finished Evaluation unit's tuning into patching for the municipalities still on paper after last year's ransomware sweep. Police protection is accepted for the halted grid connections in Spain, Germany and the Netherlands while we avoid reopening siting fights that would cost cohesion we do not have.

Externally we do not retaliate against Washington's quota rationing. DG TRADE logs slipped accelerator and model-access deliveries and keeps the middle-power file warm, while the Commission chases existing gigafactory orders by phone. We accept the invitation into the joint cyber command's real-time telemetry sharing — protection we cannot build alone — as an operational adhesion, not a new measure, and note the office productivity gains without layoffs to argue against austerity cuts to retraining, without launching a new labour instrument this turn.
