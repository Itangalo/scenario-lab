## Priority

## In practice
We hold the line on what is already funded. DG CNECT and ENISA keep the Cyber Shield enforcement on the two transmission networks and the port on the 70% EU co-financing, closing the joint-procurement liability block for water and small grids without opening a new programme. DIGIT completes the dependency list, the AI Office evaluation unit vets EU-hosted and open-weight replacements for clinical and control-room use, and EU4Health/Digital Europe pays for switch-over playbooks — accepting weaker performance now to avoid a third paper-triage week.

On the two external shocks we prepare rather than build. HERA and ECDC task existing biodefence labs to reproduce and scope the genome-model uplift claim inside the biosecurity community, feeding results into Shield detection and M5 continuity exercises, while DG TRADE assesses whether Washington is rationing allies alongside adversaries and holds an Anti-Coercion response for the coalition track. M1/M2 gigafactory grid-ties stay in Council Energy formation to unblock Spanish, German and Dutch permits, and M4 evaluation access talks continue on questionnaires-plus-logs until lab access moves.
