## In practice
We are running the Shield through ENISA and HERA under the Civil Protection Mechanism, turning the winter drills into live incident response: mandatory reporting is enforced for the hit grid operators, port and water utility, jointly procured detection is diverted to them first, and EU-paid hardening is conditional on exercised islanding and manual-operation continuity plans. Interior ministers who balked get funding now, obligations now.

On the cutoff, we task DG CNECT and EuroHPC JU to reallocate existing supercomputer inference and Gaia-X cloud capacity to a certified Fallback Stack, using emergency procurement under the Single Market Emergency Instrument to put Mistral, Aleph Alpha and open-weight deployments into hospitals and ministries within weeks. The Competitiveness Council is told plainly: M1 gigafactories remain the term priority for independence, this stack is the bridge that keeps that promise credible.
