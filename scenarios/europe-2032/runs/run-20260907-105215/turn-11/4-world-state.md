### The Weight of Inertia

Global AI capability surges again, rising +4.0 to 93.5. Automated validation now drives self-improvement across physics, chip design, and logistics, accelerating progress beyond institutional comprehension. Open-weight models advance to 70.0, supported by sustained diffusion of open research, incremental improvements in efficiency, and the broader availability of training infrastructure. While frontier techniques continue to influence the open ecosystem, progress is uneven and lags significantly—closed systems now outpace open by 23.5, a gap widening under structural pressures.

In Europe, paralysis deepens. With `eu_political_capital` at −9.0, the Union cannot launch new initiatives. The **Emergency Compute Redundancy Initiative** and **Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory** remain in flight, but yield no part-bonus this turn—too little progress has been made. The named priority changes nothing: at zero or negative capital, prioritisation has no effect. Emergency reallocations are attempted, but face legal and budgetary gridlock. Bilateral coordination with Finland, Luxembourg, and Bulgaria persists, yet grid upgrades and microwave backhaul contracts remain stalled due to unresolved financing and permitting—no new compute capacity comes online this turn.

This boosts `resilience` by +3, partially offsetting the lack of finished domestic measures. Yet the benefits are fragile—dependent on goodwill and access that could vanish with the next geopolitical shift.

Labour markets fracture further. Entry-level roles in law, accountancy, and software vanish, replaced by AI agents that require no salary, only supervision. Public anger grows. Some cities express interest in procuring open-weight models for public services, citing service delivery failures. A loose network of mayors has begun informal coordination—**emergent_municipal_sovereignty_network**—but lacks funding, staffing, or legal authority to act unilaterally. National governments reject any devolution of AI procurement, asserting central oversight. No coordinated municipal action occurs this turn.

Political capital remains at −9.0, dragged down by the portfolio charge. Public sentiment drops to 15.0—broad hostility now dominates, with AI seen as both a job destroyer and a foreign-controlled threat.
