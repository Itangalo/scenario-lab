A catastrophic AI-driven cyberattack cripples hospitals and power grids in three member states, exposing critical infrastructure vulnerabilities and triggering public outrage. The EU responds with the Cyber Shield Activation and Critical Service Hardening programme, mandating AI-augmented defences in essential sectors, but deployment is delayed by interoperability conflicts and personnel shortages. Amid ongoing implementation failures, €1.2 billion is redirected to accelerate the Emergency Replication Mandate in Poland, Spain, and Finland, yet distillation efforts remain behind schedule and foreign developers refuse cooperation. Germany and France continue to block central protocols for the Sovereign Inference Backbone, risking Cohesion Fund sanctions as split-key integration stalls. Public trust plummets further amid rising automation-driven job losses and fallout from the Institutional Integrity Audit. AI capabilities surge to 92.0 via opaque, self-reinforcing systems, far outpacing stagnant safety and interpretability research, while open-weight models reach only 70.0. Political fragmentation intensifies, with three major initiatives overburdened, under-resourced, and failing to restore coherence or security, deepening the EU’s strategic dependency and institutional crisis.
