### The Storm Breaks

A major AI-driven cyberattack cripples hospital systems in three member states and disrupts regional power grids, exposing the fragility of critical infrastructure. The attack, leveraging AI-generated zero-days and supply-chain exploits, overwhelms legacy defences. Though containment begins within days, recovery is slow and public anger mounts. The EU activates emergency protocols, launching the **Cyber Shield Activation and Critical Service Hardening** programme, mandating AI-augmented intrusion detection and failover systems across energy, health, and digital identity platforms. Deployment starts in high-risk sectors, but full rollout is delayed by interoperability disputes and staffing shortages.

Meanwhile, the **Emergency Replication Mandate** remains the political priority. Binding compliance orders are issued, and €1.2 billion is redirected to replication efforts in Poland, Spain, and Finland. However, distillation progress lags, and no foreign developer has cooperated. Germany and France continue resisting central protocols for the **Sovereign Inference Backbone**, despite threats to withhold Cohesion Funds. Split-key systems are verified in two nodes, but integration remains partial.

Public sentiment holds at rock bottom. Fast-tracking whistleblower protections offers little comfort as automation displaces thousands in public administration. The **Institutional Integrity Audit**'s fallout deepens distrust.

AI capability jumps to 92.0, driven by opaque, self-reinforcing training loops in a closed frontier system. No safety advance matches it; interpretability remains out of reach. Open-weight capability rises modestly to 70.0 as distillation efforts yield fragmented progress.

### Fractures Deepen

Political capital erodes further under the weight of three large measures. The replication mandate’s priority status fails to overcome implementation gridlock. Sovereignty slips as dependency on foreign AI persists and internal coordination frays. The cyber shield, though urgently needed, arrives too late to prevent damage—and too slowly to restore confidence.
