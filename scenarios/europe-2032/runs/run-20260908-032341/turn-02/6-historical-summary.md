Public trust improved due to visible AI adoption successes in healthcare and social services, attributed to European systems. However, strategic vulnerabilities persisted — the ASML Joint Export Governance Initiative stalled over Dutch resistance and U.S. pressure, despite EU offers of legal protections. Behind closed doors, alarming biosecurity findings from a genome model raised urgent concerns about open-weight AI risks. Europe advanced in pockets, but lacked cohesion and speed.
