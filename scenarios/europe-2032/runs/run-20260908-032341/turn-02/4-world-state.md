### A Fragile Step Forward

The **Emergency Resilience Surge** concludes with mixed results. While binding AI-driven detection protocols are now in place across all major energy and port operators, the March pan-European exercise reveals uneven implementation: some regional water authorities still lack real-time monitoring. Still, the new baseline hardens defences meaningfully. This progress is amplified by a global **cyber_defence_breakthrough**, as automated patching tools begin closing the attacker’s advantage — a shift felt immediately in EU systems.

Meanwhile, public trust sees a rare uplift. The rollout of AI in hospital triage and social services delivers faster decisions and shorter wait times, with citizens crediting *European* systems — a visible **adoption_success** that tempers earlier resentment.

But strategic fragility persists. Despite internal fractures at ASML, formal negotiations for the **ASML Joint Export Governance Initiative** stall in April. Dutch officials resist ceding national authority over export controls, while ASML’s board fears provoking Washington prematurely. The EU offers legal shielding under Article 346 TFEU, but no breakthrough is reached. The initiative remains alive, but momentum wanes as U.S. diplomats intensify private warnings to the Netherlands.

Behind the scenes, biosecurity experts debate alarming findings from a genome model capable of designing human-infective organisms. Though contested, the **bio_uplift_findings** sharpen concerns about open-weight models falling into the wrong hands — a threat the EU is ill-prepared to counter.

### Sovereignty Without Leverage

The Union’s sovereignty score holds steady, not because of gains, but because the erosion from last turn — access denial and member-state defection — has not deepened. Yet no capacity event fires this turn, and no category 4 measure finishes. The Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package remain in flight, offering only potential.

Political capital dips slightly under the weight of three large measures, including the newly prioritised ASML initiative. No bonus offsets this cost, as the new measure, though timely, did not respond to a recent shock. Public sentiment, however, rises on tangible benefits — a quiet correction to the crisis narrative.

Europe moves — but not fast enough, not far enough, and not together.
