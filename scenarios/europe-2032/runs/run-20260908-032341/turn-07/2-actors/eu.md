## In practice
This turn, we conclude the InvestAI Gigafactories initiative with a final Commission Communication acknowledging that while the €200bn private investment target remains distant, the framework for accelerated permitting and grid access has been established in six member states, and three hyperscalers have signed binding agreements under revised State Aid rules. However, the persistent shortfall in *sovereign* compute — now laid bare by the ASML crisis and the U.S. containment posture — demands a break from business-as-usual. We are therefore launching the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation, invoking the EU’s newly codified crisis-response powers under the Digital Markets Act’s ancillary provisions and the Treaty’s solidarity clause. This will fast-track the deployment of three sovereign AI clusters — one hosted by the French HPC agency GENCI, one by the German Gauss Centre, and one by the Polish PL-Grid — each equipped with at least 10 exaflops of secure, EU-controlled compute, prioritised for the emergent_eu_lab_coalition’s unified training run and for critical public functions.

We are pairing this with an intensified legal push under Article 346 TFEU and the Anti-Coercion Instrument, preparing a Council Recommendation for next turn that would establish a binding EU-level consultation mechanism for critical technology servicing decisions — not to nationalise ASML, but to ensure that no strategic decision is made under unilateral foreign pressure. The Biosecurity Watchtower remains operational, but with no model yet blocked, its credibility hinges on visible follow-through: we are now directing the AI Office to conduct its first formal review of an open-weight release — a genome-scale model flagged by the Dutch National Cyber Security Centre — to test the mechanism under real conditions. Meanwhile, the Secure Open-Model Research Initiative is entering its second half-year, with EuroHPC finalising consortium agreements and Fraunhofer launching the first interpretability sprint on the coalition’s base model. Public sentiment remains fragile, but the emergence of a credible EU-led alternative to U.S. frontier AI offers a narrative of agency — one we must now back with steel, silicon, and sovereign power.
