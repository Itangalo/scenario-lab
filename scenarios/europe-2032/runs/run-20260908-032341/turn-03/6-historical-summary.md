The Emergency Resilience Surge concluded with binding AI security protocols adopted across energy and port sectors, enhanced by automated patching integrated into ENISA standards, though gaps in water monitoring persisted in Central and Eastern Europe. Public trust improved due to visible AI successes in healthcare and social services, bolstered by a global shift toward automated cyber defences. However, a 36-hour loss of control at a U.S. logistics firm highlighted risks of agentic AI, prompting the EU to launch the Biosecurity Watchtower and Open-Weight Risk Directive, imposing thresholds on high-risk open models, particularly genome-scale systems, to address emerging biosecurity threats. The ASML Joint Export Governance Initiative stalled due to Dutch resistance and U.S. pressure, despite ASML’s openness to EU coordination. Ongoing initiatives—InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech Sovereignty Package—failed to deliver sovereignty gains, slightly eroding sovereignty and depleting political capital amid mounting implementation costs and lack of major milestones.
