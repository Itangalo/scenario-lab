## In practice
This turn, we act on two shocks: a major, AI-generated cyberattack has swept public services across three member states—targeting hospital billing systems, municipal payroll, and regional transport schedulers—with ransomware that adapted in real time to patching attempts. Attribution remains uncertain, but forensic analysis confirms the tooling was model-synthesised, leveraging known vulnerabilities at speeds no human team could match. Simultaneously, a private lab outside the EU has released a system that jumps `ai_capability` to 67.2—+6.7 in one leap—by demonstrating recursive self-improvement in constrained domains. The release bypassed all known evaluation pipelines, and its weights are not public—but its techniques are already being replicated. With `ai_safety` collapsing and `openweight_capability` creeping toward 52.0, the gap between detection and action is now a chasm.

We respond by converting the Biosecurity Watchtower from a monitoring body into an enforcement authority. Under Article 16 of the AI Act and reinforced by Article 122 TFEU’s emergency clause, we issue a directive requiring all member states to implement immediate restrictions—within 60 days—on any open-weight model assessed by the AI Office as posing a dual-use biological or cyber risk. Non-compliant models will be blocked from EU hosting platforms, distribution networks, and research funding. We empower national regulators to impose fines, seize compute credits, and suspend developer access. The Dutch, German, and French digital ministries are already aligned; Poland and Hungary resist, citing sovereignty and innovation costs. We offer Poland a seat on the enforcement oversight board and redirect 15% of the Secure Open-Model Research Initiative’s legacy budget to a sovereign red-teaming lab in Warsaw. For Hungary, we link compliance to Cohesion Fund disbursements tied to cyber resilience upgrades.

The Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation finishes this turn: legal and site agreements are locked, and EuroHPC confirms that 8.2 exaflops are now online, with full 10+ exaflops expected by turn 11 despite grid delays. This is not the full victory we promised—but it is a foundation. We now shift from building capacity to defending it. Without enforcement, sovereignty is just hardware waiting to be compromised.
