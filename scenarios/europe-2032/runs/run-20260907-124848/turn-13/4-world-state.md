### A Surge in Capability, No Open Release

Global AI capability advances to 73.5 as automated verification expands into legal compliance and supply chain logistics, accelerating self-improving loops in frontier labs. No open-weight release occurs—despite earlier signals, export controls and internal governance delays block distribution. Openweight_capability remains at 58.5.

### Cyber Catastrophe and Institutional Strain

A coordinated AI-generated ransomware swarm disables hospital scheduling systems in three EU capitals. The attack exploits zero-day vulnerabilities chained by autonomous tools, overwhelming human-led response. Though the **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield Initiative** finishes this turn, its limited rollout—only seven member states piloting detection modules—fails to prevent cascading failure. Resilience drops to 39.0 as critical services falter.

### Regulatory Overreach and Public Backlash

The **automated_decision_scandal** erupts in the Netherlands, where an AI used in social insurance is found to have wrongfully denied thousands of claims due to a biased risk model. Public trust erodes further; sentiment falls to 20.0. Protests intensify, with federal states accusing the central AI agency of unchecked authority.

The EU proposes the **Runtime Integrity Certification Regime**, mandating cryptographic attestation of agent stability in critical sectors. Framed as a response to the cyber incident, it demands hard constraints on self-modification and replication. Germany and France condition support on sunset clauses and appeals mechanisms. The measure is named priority.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Crumbles

The **Cyber Shield** finishes, but yields no resilience gain—its deployment was too narrow, too late. No +3 to +6 bonus applies. Sovereignty falls to 25.0: capability rose 1.5 (−1), no capacity event, and no category 4 completion. Political capital drops to 34.0: portfolio charge hits −7 (two large measures, priority), no finish bonus, and the internal-origin scandal costs −10. A +3 proposal bonus for the new regime is outweighed by backlash.
