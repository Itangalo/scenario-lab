By late 2032 EU containment held as degraded survival, not recovery.

August ransomware sweep with automated tooling hit municipal systems, hospital admin and two regional utilities; certified hospitals/mobile labs kept beds open on paper/backup power, but billing, appointments, benefit payments froze in a dozen cities. Blocklists updated in hours — too slow for full queues. Attribution failed; tooling agreed machine-made, author disputed.

Simultaneously open weights matching closed frontier spread to hundreds of thousands; contested genome-modelling paper warned non-experts could reach human-infecting design, keeping Japan/Canada sample-sharing and cyber telemetry on high alert.

Brussels chose continuity: mandatory auto-patching, 4-hour human override for medical routes retained, compensation drawn from existing health-rescue line. Containment Liability and Service Continuity Compact reached full effect; first February-freeze payouts landed slow/partial/backlogged, stopping revolt spread. One holdout town reconnected for guaranteed payouts, other stayed out.

Strains persist: welfare-scoring enforcement drag, allied licence rationing, separate hyperscaler side-deal, obsolete rulebook claim, no new EU fund, thinner infrastructure.
