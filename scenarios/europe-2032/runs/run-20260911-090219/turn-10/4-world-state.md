### Where the drills had failed
ENISA teams arrived in spring with the new patching and swarm-detection packages, prioritising the municipal utilities and hospitals that had failed last autumn's exercises. In certified hospitals installation was fast — automated patching closed tickets in hours that once took weeks. In smaller town utilities rollout stalled: understaffed IT teams, incompatible legacy controllers, a CSIRT backlog. The mandatory containment exercise helped where detectors already existed; elsewhere operators still hesitated to freeze payments for fear of cutting off normal orders.

HERA's bio-detection sprint moved faster on paper than in sewers. Hospital labs signed wastewater and clinical sequencing contracts, and incident feeds from Japan and Canada began flowing through the compact swap. Coverage remained patchy, and public health officials privately warned sampling could spot a signal but not stop it.

### A quieter frontier, a louder lab argument
American frontier progress stayed slow, with rationed licences and queued allocations unchanged. Attention shifted to the contested genome-model paper claiming a non-expert could be guided toward a viable human-infecting design. Authors and critics traded accusations of alarmism and recklessness. Brussels had no readable reasoning to check, only black-box test results shared under the compact, which narrowed but did not settle the question.

### No money, but a plan on the table
The Commission held domestic spending flat: concrete for backup compute and shield certificates continued on old budgets. Labour ministers finalised the retraining and income-support blueprint for law, accountancy, software and customer-centre graduates, explicitly unfunded until next year. Graduate fairs stayed empty, and unions called the plan a promise without a pay slip.

The holdout capital accepted the defensive tooling but again deferred unwinding its separate supply deal, taking priority protection while keeping its outside option. Relief over the first real defensive gain in years mixed with resentment that protection still depended on outside models and outside hosts.
