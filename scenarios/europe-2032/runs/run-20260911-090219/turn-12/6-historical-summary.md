By early 2032 EU containment held but success created political cost.

Winter-spring pathogen wave ebbed via certified hospitals, mobile labs, stockpiles; absenteeism fell. ENISA automated patching/swarm-detection blocked opportunistic hits on hospitals/utilities — first sustained defensive win.

February automatic payment freezes triggered backlash: operators in two towns defied orders to keep billing, hailed as heroes; halted medical supplier sued. Council emergency liability compact: freezes stay automatic, 4-hour human override for medical/care routes, central logging, 30-day compensation via repurposed health-rescue window; one town reconnected, other held out publicly.

External dependence deepened: US tightened chip/model controls, allied licences shrank with quiet rationing; large member state renewed separate US hyperscaler side-deal, seen in Brussels as undercutting. Japan/Canada binding biosurveillance sample-sharing and real-time cyber telemetry gave protection EU couldn't build alone; limited public-sector AI wins praised locally.

Overshadowed by welfare algorithm scandal: ombudsman found benefits-scoring systematically cut vulnerable claimants with rubber-stamp approvals; never classed high-risk, thus lawful — fuelling claim flagship rulebook obsolete for 2032.

Prior strains persist: thinner infrastructure, one external lever, liability dispute, labs-vs-evaluators, US rationing, thin weights pact, middle-power compact, no new EU fund, suppressed hiring/unfunded retraining, cohesion cracked.
