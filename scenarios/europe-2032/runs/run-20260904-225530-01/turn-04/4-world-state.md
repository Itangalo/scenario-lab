### Capability Surge, Safety Freefall  
Global AI capability advances to 65.0 as frontier labs integrate autonomous reasoning into multi-day research pipelines, accelerating materials and algorithm design. The leap widens the gap between what systems can do and what developers can explain. No interpretability tools have caught up, and safety assurance remains absent from deployment. With no new safeguards in place, `ai_safety` drops sharply to 14.0. The open-weight frontier follows: a decentralised release of a model with autonomous planning functions pushes `openweight_capability` to 52.0, enabling persistent agent-like behaviour outside controlled environments.

### EU Shifts Focus to Social Resilience  
Responding to accelerating job losses in legal, accounting, and public administration, the EU launches the Reskilling and Transition Acceleration Facility — a €22bn programme co-managed with member states and unions. Pilots begin in Berlin, Marseille, and Valencia, leveraging digital credentials for cross-border recognition. Employer tax credits unlock early buy-in, but delays in national implementation frameworks slow full rollout. The measure is large and timely, but only begins to yield partial benefits.

### Sovereignty Push Meets Headwinds  
The Sovereign AI Compute Initiative advances with GPU procurement and site preparation in Finland and Portugal. However, export control tightening — now excluding even allied access to next-gen firmware — counts as a partial denial of frontier compute access. This, combined with the AI capability rise, drags `eu_ai_sovereignty` down to 20.0. The Tech Sovereignty Package clears Council hurdles, but ASML-related provisions remain frozen. EAAD finalises staffing and prepares its first audit, though no findings are public yet. The Cyber Shield identifies and patches critical SCADA flaws in Hungary, with expansion underway.

### Political Capital Erodes Amid Mounting Pressures  
The new reskilling facility becomes the priority, but political capital continues to decline. Public sentiment falls to 36.0 as media spotlight mid-level job losses and a bio-risk toolkit spreads online. Despite defensive breakthroughs in cyber, the perception of uncontrolled AI harm grows. The overloaded agenda and fiscal strain limit gains, even as the EU attempts to balance technological and social infrastructure.
