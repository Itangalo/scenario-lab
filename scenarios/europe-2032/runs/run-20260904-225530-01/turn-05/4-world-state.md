### US Election Shifts the Global Landscape  
The 2028 US presidential election concludes with a decisive victory for the anti-AI backlash candidate. The incoming administration signals sweeping domestic restrictions: data centre moratoriums, bans on AI use in hiring and courts, and sector-funded job guarantees. American frontier progress slows abruptly as talent migrates and investment freezes. While this eases pressure on the EU’s access to talent and compute, it also severs a key strategic partner. The EU now stands alone at the frontier’s edge — with no reliable external anchor.

### Cyber Shock and Sovereignty Under Strain  
A coordinated AI-generated ransomware campaign cripples public health and transport systems across three member states. The attack exploits AI-spoofable authentication protocols, overwhelming response capacity. Though contained within weeks, the incident underscores systemic fragility. In response, the Hardened Cyber Shield expands to Poland and Romania, patching critical rail controls. Resilience rises slightly, but public trust erodes further.

### EU Acts — But Momentum Falters  
The European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) launches its first binding evaluation framework for autonomous AI, targeting goal stability and self-modification risks. Market access is tied to certification, triggering legal threats from US and Chinese firms. However, with the US turning inward, leverage is uncertain and enforcement untested. The move boosts ai_safety modestly, as the framework is not yet applied to deployed systems.

The Sovereign AI Compute Initiative secures 30,000 GPUs with locked firmware, but export control tightening limits full delivery. Combined with rising global capability, this erodes eu_ai_sovereignty further. The Reskilling Facility enrolls 18,000 workers, yet national delays persist. Public sentiment dips as job losses in law and accounting mount.

### Political Capital Bleeds  
The EU renews its foundational capacity commitment and prioritizes the new evaluation regime. But with six large measures in flight, political capital drains under sustained fiscal and institutional strain. The agenda is overloaded, progress uneven, and credibility waning.
