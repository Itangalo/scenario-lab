## In practice  
We are activating the Sovereign AI Compute Initiative under Article 122 TFEU for strategic infrastructure, with the European Commission leading procurement through a new Special Purpose Vehicle insulated from export control pressure. The cluster will be built in three phases: 30,000 GPUs by turn 5, 70,000 by turn 7, and full deployment by turn 8, hosted across two geographically dispersed sites in Finland and Portugal to ensure redundancy and energy resilience. Funding will come from a €38bn reallocation: €20bn from the Digital Europe Programme’s unused margins, €12bn from REPowerEU’s flexibility clause (reclassified as strategic digital infrastructure), and €6bn in new borrowing under the EU’s enhanced credit rating. We are negotiating with NVIDIA and AMD to bypass U.S. restrictions via on-premise deployment of non-exportable firmware, and with ASML to secure maintenance access under EU legal warranty, not American jurisdiction.

Simultaneously, we are pushing the InvestAI Gigafactories through final site approvals in Brittany and northern Sweden by invoking the Net-Zero Industry Act’s emergency clause for national security-equivalent infrastructure, overriding regional objections with binding Commission decisions. The Tech Sovereignty Package’s permitting reforms are being fast-tracked in the Council’s Internal Market formation, with a compromise text that decouples ASML-related measures to avoid legal retaliation while preserving grid prioritisation and local incentives. On the Cyber Shield, we are deploying the Resilience Corps to Hungary and Slovakia this quarter to co-manage red-teaming pilots and demonstrate feasibility, aiming to convert resistance into participation before the first audit cycle. EAAD’s funding model is now finalised: 60% from the Multiannual Financial Framework, 40% from the graduated deployment fee, with the first assessments scheduled for Q1 2028 — focused on agent persistence and goal drift in models above 55.0 capability. We are not waiting for perfect clarity. We are building sovereign capacity because the frontier no longer asks permission — and neither will we.
