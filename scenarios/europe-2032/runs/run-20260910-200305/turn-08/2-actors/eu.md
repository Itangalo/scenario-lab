**Periphery Care Audit and Extension Pact**
Puts independent logging and liability audits into periphery hospitals and extends certified continuity teams there under joint EU-federation oversight.
This and now because periphery federations refuse to rejoin the EU triage system, and without settling liability and delivering surge teams outside capitals the trust-rebuild commitment collapses into a capitals-only corps.

## Priority
Periphery Care Audit and Extension Pact, because the certified corps finished but operates only in capitals and the two-year commitment to keep essential services running fails if periphery wards stay closed.

## In practice
We task DG SANTE with the AI Office, EDPS and HERA to run fast-track logging audits on EU-hosted open triage models, publish dosage guardrails and unread-log fixes, and create a capped EU liability backstop via EU4Health and member-state social funds to settle compensation claims for benefit cuts and dosage errors. Hospital federations get co-signed audit protocol and site-by-site recertification timetable in Health Council.

We redeploy the joint bio-cyber reserve surge rosters and ENISA health teams from large-city shielding to periphery clinics and small utilities-linked hospitals, installing segmented EU-hosted triage with logged clinician sign-off. Washington quota rationing is managed by prioritizing periphery allocations under the hyperscaler no-break pledge and Spanish-French gigafactory pilot capacity, to show capacity Europe controls reaching beyond Paris, Berlin, Warsaw.
