## New measure
**Municipal and Hospital Cyber Shield**
Extends binding segmentation, credential rotation and behavioural detection from large grids to the ransomware-hit municipalities, regional hospitals and logistics suppliers, with EU-funded incident-response teams and cross-border drills.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, category 6, and it must come now because the spring sweep proved the cutoff and the cyber shock hit the same uncovered services our two-year commitment to absorb shocks on infrastructure we control requires us to harden.

## Priority
EU Essential Services Continuity Reserve, because cut-off hospitals and ministries without a working fallback will fail before any new hardening lands.

## In practice
We push M4 into operation this turn: DG CNECT and HERA use reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health money plus emergency procurement waivers under the NIS2 crisis process to inventory denied-model deployments and stand up EU-hosted open-model fallbacks in Paris, Berlin and Warsaw first, with paper-procedure kits where models still hallucinate. COREPER contains the solo hyperscaler side-deal with a non-aggression bargain on joint bargaining.

We start the new Shield via ENISA and the Civil Protection Mechanism: mandatory hardening standards for municipalities and regional hospitals, funded response teams, and autumn cross-border drills. On the inherited compute lines we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package slow-burning, offering water-use caps and accelerated grid connection in Spain and Germany to unblock permits, while infringement screening on the benefits fraud system is framed as enforcement repair to limit legitimacy loss.
