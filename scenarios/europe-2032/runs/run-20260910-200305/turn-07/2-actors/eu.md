## New measure
**Certified Care Continuity Corps**
Puts certified, EU-hosted triage and admin AI back into wards under human sign-off to reopen beds and show lawful AI working.
This is the trust-rebuild commitment made concrete now because wards partly closed and fraud-scoring ban have collapsed legitimacy to 14, and with capital at 5 only a small, visible restoration can precede any sovereignty build.

## Priority
Certified Care Continuity Corps, because without reopening wards under certified lawful use the two-year commitment to rebuild trust fails and no further measure is fundable.

## In practice
We task the AI Office with HERA and ENISA to run joint certification sprints for EU-hosted open models for triage support and admin, using Article 168 health legal base and existing EU4Health envelope — no new Council vote. Hospitals in Paris, Berlin, Warsaw that already switched get certified first, then periphery sites with surge-team presence.

We implement the CJEU ban fully while ring-fencing care: fraud-scoring stays suspended with audit by EDPS, compensation via member-state social funds, and triage fallbacks only return with logged human clinician sign-off and dosage guardrails. DG SANTE negotiates with hospital federations to lift suspensions site-by-site to end lawsuits.
