## Priority
EU Swarm Defence Hardening, because an unactuated swarm with EU footholds outranks the slower sovereignty build when the next three turns carry elevated cyber risk.

## In practice
We task ENISA with a binding emergency hardening standard under the NIS2 legal base, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility money, with TSOs, port authorities and water operators in Council Energy and Transport formations required to segment OT, rotate breaker credentials and deploy swarm-behaviour detection piloted from the breakthrough tooling. France, Germany and Poland lead the first exercise in December; laggard municipalities get EU co-funding tied to compliance.

On the inherited lines, we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package running but slow-burn them: DG CNECT shepherds permitting zones and grid connections, we defend ASML autonomy quietly against US export pressure without a public break, and we use the AI Act postponement window to prepare 2027 high-risk enforcement rather than reopening the text.

We link the two tracks politically: resilience spending is sold as sovereignty protection — no credible EU cloud without survivable grids — to hold member-state cohesion and blunt industry claims that security diverts from competitiveness, while productivity gains without job losses are used to keep public sentiment neutral.
