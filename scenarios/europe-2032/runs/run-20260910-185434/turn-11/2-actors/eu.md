## New measure
**EU Graduate Entry Guarantee and Public-Service Continuity Corps**
Pays emergency entry-level placements in hospitals, municipalities and grid operators tied to manual-fallback staffing while funding employer hiring incentives and rapid retraining, directly answering the blockade wave and the hiring freeze.
This is category 7 Labour and social protection, small and enforceable at capital 5, because with US labs nationalised and EU frontier access cut, legitimacy and continuity now depend on visible jobs and staffed fallback rather than any new frontier rule.

## Priority
M11 EU Graduate Entry Guarantee and Public-Service Continuity Corps, because it is the only instrument that simultaneously staffs essential services cut off from US models and answers graduate protests with cheques and posts rather than statements.

## In practice
DG EMPL with DG HOME and ENISA reprogrammes ESF+ and the remaining resilience top-ups into paid six-month Corps posts in hospitals, municipal utilities and port operators already on manual drills, paired with hiring-loss compensation for private firms in law, accountancy and software that reopen entry roles. Council EPSCO and member states with blockaded sites get immediate de-escalation funds in exchange for opening career offices and empty shells to Corps recruitment.

On access denial, we do not litigate in Washington this turn. Ministries, hospitals and firms on the cut-off model are ordered onto EU-hosted open-weight and legacy systems under the Containment and Auto-Patch pipelines, with tenant isolation and key rotation already finished, and the US nationalisation is treated as state-to-state: we log the denial for a future Anti-Coercion and arms-control channel while keeping lights and logs running by hand where models fail.
