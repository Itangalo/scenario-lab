## In practice
We are tasking ENISA with ENTSO-E, member-state TSOs and port authorities to map breaker credentials, isolate protection relays, and deploy AI-swarm detection drawn from Mythos-class tooling, funded by reprogrammed InvestAI and Connecting Europe Facility money under Article 173/194 legal base. Energy Council in December sets binding segmentation deadlines; reluctant operators get co-financing for upgrades, persistent laggards get NIS2 enforcement.

Alongside this, we keep the two inherited sovereignty lines moving: Gigafactories site selection to final investment decisions and the tech sovereignty package permitting zones to first grid connections, using ASML leverage quietly to deter further US extraterritorial switch-offs. No parallel compute programme is stood up; urgency does not shorten those lead times, so our push this turn is to make what we have defensible.
