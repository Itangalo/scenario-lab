## Priority
Priority is the Guarded Fallback Sites Protection Surge, not any new build, because holding essential services through cut-off requires the guarded fallback to survive arson first.

## In practice
We execute through the Justice and Home Affairs Council and national interior ministries on existing Internal Security Fund and Civil Protection Mechanism money: extend the police-guard roster already on generator compounds to municipal battery depots and fallback stores, fund fire-watch patrols and off-books transformer pools on official reimbursement, and task ENISA/EU-CyCLONe with rapid damage reporting.

We manage the politics as protection of energy-transition infrastructure, not AI policy: mayors and nurses get visible guards and repaired sites, opposition framing of dependence is met with dashboards kept lit, and we do not promise replacement US model access or revived data-centre builds we cannot fund. Grey-broker chip offers are refused centrally to avoid enforcement collapse.
