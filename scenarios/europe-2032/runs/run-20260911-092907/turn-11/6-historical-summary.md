Brussels' earlier win with EU-procured triage/case tools held but tech reset had killed data-centre expansions and private compute; Washington kept quotas, Hague complied, Japan/Korea gave only spares. Fallback sites suffered break-ins/arson, clinics lost backup, transformer pool torched; police guards posted and off-books pools moved to official reimbursement. Drilled mutual-aid worked: cross-border fuel/transformer sharing moved Feb-March without new vote, manual restoration kept districts lit; credit went to engineers, not Brussels.

In early autumn the leading American model access was revoked without reason or appeal — inventory management in Washington, triage/drafting tools dark in European wards. Brussels did not retaliate, kept spare-parts talks alive, ordered showcase clinics to continuity stack and paper. The black-start/manual-fallback network was declared operational: sharing moved without vote, guards stayed, EU tools on EU-hosted capacity kept waits down where guarded, slipped to hand logs where not. Nurses/mayors still credited drills/neighbours, but dashboards kept lit let Commission claim foresight; opposition called it proof of dependence on a foreign switch. Private compute did not return, grey brokers offered rerouted chips/logins, one battery depot fire sparked unconfirmed arson rumours. Union ended smaller in capacity, slightly larger in standing: fenced in, guarded from within, still running.
