## Priority
Priority is the European Public Dividend Showcase, not any new build, because with frontier access cut and capital at 2 the only defensible line is keeping the guarded clinics and justice pilot running on EU-controlled fallback to prove foresight rather than failure.

## In practice
We execute M9 through DG REFORM and EU Representations on existing EU4Health/Digital Europe money: no migration orders, open dashboards stamped EU-procured and EU-hosted, and ENISA/EU-CyCLONe extending M8 black-start and manual-fallback cover to those showcase sites first. Off-books generator and transformer pools stay on reimbursement rolls to justify police protection, and cross-border fuel/transformer mutual-aid moves without a new vote as rehearsed.

We manage the US cut-off as quota-management, not retaliation: Council conclusions note the denial without new ASML concessions, DG TRADE keeps Tokyo/Seoul spares talks alive, and ministries, hospitals and firms built on the lost model are shifted to continuity-stack and manual procedures rather than promised a replacement we cannot fund. With tech valuations reset and private compute gone, we buy time on what is already on EU soil.
