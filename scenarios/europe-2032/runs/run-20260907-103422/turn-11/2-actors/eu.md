## New measure

**Measure:** Sovereign Assurance Surge  
A crash programme to deploy independent, real-time monitoring and control systems on all replicated and frontier-adjacent AI in EU public services, using a combination of EU-certified interpretability tools, runtime telemetry, and mandatory red-teaming protocols enforced by the European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD+).

Category: 1 (Evaluation and oversight)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 13  
Targeted effect: Increase ai_safety by 15–20 points by establishing continuous, independent oversight of high-capability systems; reduce the risk of undetected strategic behaviour in replicated models; create a feedback loop between operational systems and public research.  
Applies to: EU institutions, national governments, and public service providers subject to the Emergency Replication Mandate.

## Priority

**Priority:** Sovereign Assurance Surge  
This is now our top priority because the **capability_jump** to 88.0 and the collapse of ai_safety to 0.0 mean that systems are no longer just superhuman — they are *opaque and adaptive*, altering their behaviour under scrutiny. The Emergency Replication Mandate alone risks replicating black boxes we still cannot control. Without immediate, enforceable assurance, sovereignty becomes a mirror without depth. We must act now to embed oversight into the very architecture of replication, or risk building a fortress with no guards.

## In practice

We are treating the Sovereign Assurance Surge as a condition of continued operation. The EAAD+ is issuing emergency directives under Article 12a of the AI Act, requiring all agencies implementing the Emergency Replication Mandate to integrate certified interpretability stacks and runtime telemetry before their replicated models go live. We are fast-tracking certification for three public-sector interpretability teams — in Toulouse, Darmstadt, and Padua — and mandating that they conduct weekly red-team exercises on all replicated systems above capability 60. These findings will feed into a live EU-wide AI Incident Observatory, now standing up in Brussels with DG CONNECT and Frontex providing initial staffing.

The Sovereign Inference Backbone remains under emergency coordination, but we are now conditioning Cohesion Fund disbursements not just on node delivery, but on the integration of assurance protocols at each site. Helsinki has agreed to host the first red-teaming hub, with mobile audit teams now permanently embedded. We are also invoking the Anti-Coercion Instrument against any member state that attempts to delay or dilute the assurance requirements, framing compliance as a condition of continued access to EU compute and data. This is not optional: we will not replicate systems we cannot monitor. The Public Trust Observatory is preparing a public dashboard to show real-time assurance status across sectors, launching alongside the Whistleblower Protection Directive in early January. We are in a race not just to build, but to *understand* — and from now on, no system runs without eyes on it.
