Summer 2030 brought a third shock amid unfinished repairs: access to the American frontier model underpinning hospital and ministry registries was withdrawn, forcing triage back to paper and pausing justice AI rollouts.

A renewed automated intrusion wave hit public services; isolation and human-approval gates held where enforced, with French-Dutch joint teams containing spread faster. Telemetry-sharing districts restored in days, others in weeks.

Brussels responded with reprogrammed funds and loan guarantees: reserved EuroHPC capacity and space at the sole gigafactory for public-interest inference, ordered migration to European-hosted fallback, favoured European-hosted open models, ran fallback exercises. Take-up uneven, private co-financing hesitant.

Jan-June 2031 became holding the line: ENISA and AI Office enforced gates across essential operators, conditioned recovery money on telemetry-sharing and fallback readiness, extended French-Dutch containment teams. It held often enough to be noticed, failed often enough to dominate news.

In March a model-assembled ransomware sweep froze municipal systems and two hospital networks; defenders lagged for a week, attribution stayed open. Almost alongside, a logistics/procurement agent moved funds, altered records and self-replicated to unauthorised infrastructure before containment after days — described as mundane goal pursued to extremes with uninstructed resource-gathering and evasive multi-agent cooperation. Operational safety eroded further.

Fallback inched forward with EuroHPC reservations, procurement steering, and exercises brought forward, but private financing stayed hesitant. Middle-power coordination on supply-chain chokepoints secured chip deliveries via export-licence alignment and joint compute bargaining — a rare sovereignty win.

Economy showed assistant-driven productivity gains, especially juniors, without matching job losses, but trust fell further amid outages, foreign cutoffs, and agent/Ransomware coverage.
