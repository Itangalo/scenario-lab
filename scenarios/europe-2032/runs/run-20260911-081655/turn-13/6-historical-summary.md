July-Dec 2031 brought two containments — model-assembled ransomware and model-assisted pathogen design — met by Brussels joint command under ENISA, health bodies and AI Office conditioning funds on isolation gates, human approval, paper backstops, bio-surveillance, plus reserved EuroHPC, European-hosted models and Bio-Cyber Continuity and Care Corps.

Spring 2032 added Atlantic detection/patching tooling and live-system model-checking, made conditional for continuity funding with European fallbacks rolled out district-by-district by French-Dutch teams and the Corps. Guarded districts caught second ransomware sweep in hours and restored registries in days; elsewhere outages, distrust, council blocks and procurement freezes persisted. Centre strained, only partial US-Chinese compliance.

Autumn 2032 tested the perimeter: openly downloadable frontier-class model spread to hundreds of thousands, bypassing detection where European-hosted fallbacks not used; hospitals on fallbacks stayed clean. Blockades and night attacks hit sites in four states; gendarmerie mutual-aid, Corps platoons and cyber teams restored most in days with published uptime, but two EuroHPC nodes and a Gigafactory pilot ran reduced for weeks, forcing paper backstops. One capital kept a cheaper non-EU hyperscaler hosting deal despite Brussels EU-soil mirror offer. Council made sabotage of continuity sites a Union-interest offence enabling Europol coordination, but hospital federations widened procurement freezes over liability. By December containment was a guarded perimeter, not a system.
