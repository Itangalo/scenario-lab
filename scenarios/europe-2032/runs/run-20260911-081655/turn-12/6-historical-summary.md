July-Dec 2031 brought two containments: model-assembled ransomware and model-assisted pathogen design, met by Brussels joint command under ENISA, health bodies and AI Office conditioning funds on isolation gates, human approval, paper backstops and bio-surveillance, plus reserved EuroHPC, European-hosted models and Bio-Cyber Continuity and Care Corps.

Spring 2032 brought defensive gains: Atlantic tooling to detect coordinated agent activity and push machine-speed patches, and live-system model-checking adopted by major labs. Brussels made continuity funding conditional on the new patching/detection stack, prioritizing European fallback models, with French-Dutch teams and the Corps rolling it out district by district. Where deployed, second ransomware sweep caught in hours and registries restored in days; elsewhere outages, distrust, leaflets, council blocks and procurement freezes over liability persisted. Deployment strained a thin centre, stalled other files, drew complaints of credit-claiming, and won only partial US-Chinese supplier compliance, leaving containment less fragile but not yet legitimate by June.
