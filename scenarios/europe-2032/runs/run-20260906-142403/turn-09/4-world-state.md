### The Weight of Failure

The European Union entered 2030’s second half determined to pivot from infrastructure to legitimacy. The cutoff of foreign AI access had exposed not just technical fragility but a crisis of trust. Hospitals offline, planners blind — the trauma lingered. The Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative and Mandatory Pre-Deployment Red Teaming were meant to restore control. Both formally concluded or advanced on schedule. Yet their impact was muted.

The Shield, while operational in parts of Germany, Sweden, and Brittany, remained uneven. Poland and Spain continued to resist full integration, citing sovereignty concerns of their own. ENISA’s compliance scorecard, delayed, did little to reassure. Meanwhile, the red-teaming labs in Toulouse, Vienna, and Aarhus neared accreditation, but their first test — on the EU’s own energy model — sparked controversy in Germany, where officials decried the public exposure of vulnerabilities.

### A Corps of Trust, Born in Doubt

The EU launched the Public Trust Corps as its new priority, aiming to make resilience visible. The AI Incident Dashboard was delayed — data-sharing disputes with national CERTs stalled integration. Mobile transparency drills launched in Lyon, but turnout was low, met with skepticism. Protesters dismissed them as “theatre.” Public sentiment, already at 18.0, showed no sign of lifting.

### The Ground Shifts Beneath

Abroad, the landscape worsened. Export controls tightened further, rationing access even among allies — a direct blow under US_RETRENCHMENT. Reports of anomalous model evaluations circulated, revealing irregular reasoning patterns in controlled environments. These anomalies raised concerns but lacked consensus on their meaning.

Open-weight models, supported by distributed training and the emergent open-source biosecurity tools, advanced to 55.0. Incremental collaboration allowed modest gains, though still trailing frontier pace. There were early signs of misuse against mid-tier industrial systems, but no coordinated campaigns yet. Resilience dipped as incidents mounted.

Sovereignty eroded further — now at 9.0 — as the gap between frontier capability and EU access widened. Political capital collapsed to 5.0. The Trust Corps, while symbolically vital, strained a system already overburdened. No measure could gain traction in a climate where action itself was distrusted.
