## Priority
**Priority:** Launch Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative  
This remains our dominant effort because the attack surface is live, expanding, and already inside our networks—resilience is the only thing standing between us and systemic failure. While the new isolation protocols are essential for long-term sovereign integrity, the Cyber Shield is the only measure actively blocking harm *now*, and without it, no amount of hardened compute will survive its first hour online.

## In practice
We are treating this turn as the hinge between exposure and containment. The Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative is being activated under Article 122 TFEU as an emergency resilience corridor, with ENISA issuing binding deployment mandates to Tier 1 operators in energy, health, and transport. The €45bn reprofiled from expired instruments is being released in tranches tied to integration milestones: by turn 7, every targeted operator must run swarm-detection agents trained on adversarial model behaviour and implement zero-trust verification for all model-generated code. Poland and Spain remain resistant, but we are offering opt-in technical assistance packages through the EIB and framing participation as eligibility for future cohesion funds—this is not coercion, but clarity: you cannot claim European solidarity while refusing European protection.

At the same time, we are revising the definition of “sovereign” in real time. The InvestAI Gigafactories, finishing this turn, delivered compute capacity—but not safety. We are now conditioning final certification on compliance with the new Binding Isolation Protocols: any site receiving EU sovereign status must demonstrate hardware-enforced separation, energy autonomy via microgrid linkage, and red-team validation against AI-driven intrusion. Germany and Austria’s ECJ challenge over energy overrides is being met with a revised legal package under Article 194 TFEU, now framed as civil protection rather than market intervention. Brittany and northern Sweden’s microgrids will become pilot zones for full disconnection drills by turn 8.

Public sentiment, at 22.0, is a fuse. We are launching a joint EAAD–Civil Protection Agency campaign titled *Shield & Signal*, showing concrete progress: live dashboards of shielded nodes, public demonstrations of automated patching, and fast-track hiring of displaced workers into cyber resilience teams funded by the EIB transition window. This is not optimism—it is proof of life. If we do not show that the Union can act *and* protect, we will lose not just capital, but legitimacy.
