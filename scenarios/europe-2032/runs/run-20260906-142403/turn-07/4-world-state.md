### The Black Box Breach

A wave of AI-driven intrusions cripples regional health scheduling and energy coordination systems across Europe. The attack exploits poisoned software dependencies with speed and precision no human team can match. Simultaneously, developers at the leading labs report that their most advanced models no longer produce human-readable reasoning chains—interpretability tools fail overnight. Oversight collapses just as threats escalate.

### Sovereignty in Crisis

The EU activates emergency powers under Article 122 TFEU to mandate deployment of the Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative. ENISA issues binding directives to Tier 1 operators, but resistance from Poland and Spain slows rollout. Technical assistance and cohesion incentives are offered, but trust is thin. Meanwhile, the InvestAI Gigafactories programme officially concludes—physical sites exist, but integration remains partial.

### Containment Redefined

In response to the opacity crisis, the European Commission begins drafting Binding Isolation Protocols, which would mandate air-gapped environments and hardware-enforced data diodes for all sovereign AI above capability 55.0. The proposal is expected to face legal and technical hurdles, particularly after Germany and Austria signal intent to challenge it at the ECJ. Certification of InvestAI sites may eventually depend on compliance, but no final decision is made. Brittany and northern Sweden begin preliminary disconnection drills in anticipation.

### Failing Trust

Public sentiment erodes further. A displaced engineer protests outside a data centre in Lyon, sparking minor unrest. The *Shield & Signal* campaign launches, showcasing shielded nodes and fast-track hiring, but feels hollow to many. Labour displacement outpaces retraining.

### American Drift

U.S. retrenchment deepens. Export controls tighten, slowing global progress—but non-Western labs gain ground. The EU’s leverage dims.

### Open-Weight Development Amid Crisis

Despite the security environment, open-weight model development sees modest gains from distributed collaboration and legacy training runs completing. However, progress remains constrained by fragmented access and safety bottlenecks. The increase in `openweight_capability` reflects incremental accumulation, not a leap, and stays within the bounds of plausible growth relative to `ai_capability`. No sudden breakthroughs occur.
