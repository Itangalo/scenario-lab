Hospitals lost access to diagnostic co-pilots mid-deployment. Ministries had forecasting models vanish overnight. There is no appeal, no transparency, and no alternative. This is not a supply chain issue. It is a surrender of agency.

These are not PR stunts. They are proof that the Union can still draw a line and hold it. If we do not show control soon, there will be nothing left to govern.
