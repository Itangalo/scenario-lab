### EU Rushes to Build Authority

The EU accelerates deployment of EAAN certification nodes in Brussels, Sophia Antipolis, and Munich, reallocating €3.2bn from pandemic recovery funds and invoking crisis procedures to bypass procurement delays. The move aims to operationalise the *AI Deployment Boundary Act* by turn 14, ensuring sovereign capacity to evaluate and monitor high-capability systems.

However, resistance hardens. US hyperscalers reject runtime monitoring as a violation of trade secrecy and national security agreements. One lab formally withdraws its EU market entry plans, citing “unilateral and unreciprocated oversight demands.” Diplomatic channels strain under the pressure, even as the EU offers liability shielding and future compute access in exchange for cooperation.
