## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because unrecallable open-weight probing of grids, ports and water made absorption the binding constraint, outranking even gigafactories this turn.

## In practice
We are driving M3 through ENISA and HERA under the Civil Protection Mechanism, using cohesion-funded hardening to buy interior-ministry sign-off on mandatory reporting drills and pooled detection procurement, starting where autumn drills failed: hospitals and municipal networks. Joint exercises with transmission operators in the two probed states are first in the queue.

We keep M1 and M2 moving through DG CNECT, the EIB and the Competitiveness Council toward final investment decision on four to five gigafactory sites, tying priority grid connection to EU legal anchoring to stop the capital bidding war, while holding ASML coordination in the Foreign Affairs Council in reserve against Washington export pressure.

This turn we seed the Assurance Adoption Programme in the AI Office with a small certification pilot: funded interpretability teams with model access for public evaluators, working with labs that want the result adopted, to turn the breakthrough into certifiable deployment conditions for essential operators.
