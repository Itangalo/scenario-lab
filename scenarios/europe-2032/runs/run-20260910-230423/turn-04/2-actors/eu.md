This and now because capital is at 21 with investment collapse and a defection fracturing us — a small, conditional backstop uses the crunch to finish Shield uptake without a new large fund we cannot afford.

## Priority
Certified Resilience Backstop (new) – because the emergent insurance crunch will otherwise strand the hospitals and town utilities our two-year commitment to absorb harm depends on, and it outranks gigafactories stalled by the investment collapse this turn.

## In practice
We run the Backstop through DG ECHO's Civil Protection Mechanism and DG SANTE/HERA with EIOPA guidance: cohesion-funded detection kits stay conditional on completed drills, and certified operators gain access to pooled EU-reinsured cover while uncertified face the market price. We frame the Commission premium-differentiation recommendation as protection, not punishment, to blunt municipal backlash.

We keep M1 and M2 on life-support without new cash: DG CNECT holds the four-site shortlist and EU-anchoring condition for priority grid, letting the investment collapse pause private builds rather than forcing a defeat, and we quietly contain the defecting member state's side-deal in the Competitiveness Council to avoid a public cohesion break. M4 assurance pilots continue in energy and health where developer access is workable, banking legibility gains while models outrun evaluators.
