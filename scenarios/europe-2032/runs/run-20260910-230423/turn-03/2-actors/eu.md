## Priority
EU Critical Services Shield (M3) – because it finishes next turn and uneven hospital/municipal coverage is the binding absorption gap that our two-year commitment to withstand coercion and absorb harm must close first.

## In practice
We are pushing M3 to close through the Civil Protection Mechanism and the Health Emergency and cybersecurity agencies: cohesion-funded pooled detection procurement is now conditional on completing mandatory reporting drills, and joint exercises move from transmission operators and ports to hospitals and municipal utilities with staff-backfill funding to answer the shortage plea. The rumoured cyber-insurance repricing is made explicit as a Commission recommendation to concentrate boardroom minds.

We keep M1 and M2 alive without new money this turn through DG CNECT, the EIB and the Competitiveness Council: narrowing to final investment decision on four to five gigafactory sites with priority grid connection tied to EU legal anchoring, while holding the coordinated ASML response in the Foreign Affairs Council in reserve. The small Assurance Adoption Programme (M4) continues certification pilots in energy, transport and health, using selective developer access where it flatters to lock in precedent for broader access later.
