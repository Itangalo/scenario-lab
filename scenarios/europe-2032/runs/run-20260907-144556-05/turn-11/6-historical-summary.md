Political cohesion in the EU collapses as the emergency suspension of AI in public services fails, blocked by member states invoking national sovereignty and procedural delays. Article 114 TFEU is effectively nullified, and with political capital at 6.0, no binding measures are implemented. The Integrity Audit Expansion concludes but is disregarded, seen as toothless and performative, while redress mechanisms remain undefined.

Public resistance escalates beyond protest into disruptive actions: administrative AI systems are sabotaged in France, Belgium, and Italy, and physical attacks target critical infrastructure, including a major data center outage caused by transformer sabotage. Policing efforts fail to restore legitimacy. A marginal gain emerges via inclusion in a joint international threat response pact on AI-enabled cyber threats, though the initiative is externally driven and does not reflect EU leadership.

Domestically, strategic paralysis deepens. The Sovereign AI Core Programme and related initiatives remain incomplete, delayed to early 2032 due to staffing, procurement, and coordination failures. No sovereign compute or frontier AI access comes online. The US consolidates its lead under CONSOLIDATION, leaving the EU further behind. Frontier AI advances to 70.0, operating beyond EU audit or control.

Openweight AI capability rises to 54.0 through informal diffusion of leaked models into research and civic projects, but deployment remains fragmented and limited by expertise and infrastructure gaps. The EU’s containment framework is strained, and with no new policies or industrial capacity emerging, governance collapses into de facto drift, marking a surrender of strategic agency.
