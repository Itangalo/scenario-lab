### The Compact That Wasn’t

The European Union’s Emergency Governance Compact for Sovereign AI collapses before it can take shape. With political capital at just 6.0, the bloc lacks the authority to convene even a voluntary pause. The proposal, framed as a crisis compact with administrative support for member states that suspend AI in welfare systems, is rejected outright by Germany, Poland, and the Netherlands. They cite national competence, legal uncertainty, and the risk of service disruption. No formal agreement is signed. A handful of municipalities pause new AI deployments, but no system is taken offline, and the audit findings from the Integrity Audit Expansion remain unenforced.

### Finished Measures, Faded Momentum

Despite the political paralysis, several major initiatives formally conclude this turn. The Sovereign AI Core Programme, the Tech Sovereignty Package, and the InvestAI Gigafactories reach their stated finishing dates. In practice, integration delays, staffing shortfalls, and fragmented validation mean no operational sovereign capability comes online. The European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) is legally established but remains under-resourced and untested. The Public Assurance Protocol issues no reports. The Openweight Containment Initiative fails to gain traction, undermined by enforcement gaps and member-state non-compliance.

### Resilience Gains, Safety Stalls

A modest resilience boost arrives as the Critical Infrastructure Resilience Surge completes. Detection upgrades and red-teaming exercises improve preparedness in energy and transport sectors, marginally raising resilience. However, with no progress on ai_safety — and capability advancing — the gains are narrow. The gap between frontier AI and oversight widens further, with no mechanism to audit systems above 70.0.

### Frontier Drift, Open Diffusion

ai_capability rises to 71.0, driven by autonomous development in closed labs. openweight_capability climbs to 56.0, fueled by informal diffusion of leaked tools into academic and civic projects, though deployment remains limited by infrastructure and expertise. The EU’s containment strategy is increasingly symbolic.
