## New measure  
**Measure:** Launch the EU Civilian AI Stewardship Corps  
A rapid-response civilian corps trained and mandated to support public services during AI suspensions, deliver audit-backed redress, and co-develop reintegration frameworks with national authorities and civil society.

`Category: 7 (Labour and social protection)`  
`Size: small`  
`Finishes on turn: 15`  
`Targeted effect:` Stabilize `public_sentiment` by demonstrating tangible support for communities harmed by AI in public services, reduce resistance to sovereign AI efforts by embedding legitimacy through local engagement, and begin rebuilding `eu_political_capital` through visible delivery  
`Applies to:` EU member states, with deployment prioritized in regions with active AI-related public service disputes or sabotage incidents

## Priority  
**Priority:** Launch the EU Civilian AI Stewardship Corps  
We are pushing this hardest because at `public_sentiment` 18.0 and `eu_political_capital` 6.0, the Union cannot command — it must demonstrate. The collapse of the Emergency Governance Compact proves that top-down enforcement is impossible; legitimacy must now be rebuilt from the ground up. This measure does not impose, but serves — and in serving, re-earns the right to lead.

## In practice  
This turn, we are shifting from failed compacts to lived repair. The Emergency Governance Compact is dead: rejected by Germany, Poland, and the Netherlands, and with no political capital to revive it. We cannot suspend AI in welfare systems by fiat, but we can meet the anger behind the sabotage with something real. The EU Civilian AI Stewardship Corps is not another audit or directive — it is people. We are mobilizing civil servants, digital rights experts, and public service veterans into a deployable civilian force, funded through the European Social Fund and coordinated by the Commission’s Directorate-General for Employment. They will go where AI has broken trust — where benefits were denied, where appeals vanished into code, where communities feel abandoned — and work alongside local ombuds, unions, and civic groups to deliver redress, document failures, and co-design what accountable AI looks like in practice.

We are not waiting for sovereign AI to deliver capability before we act on legitimacy. Instead, we are using the wreckage of the Sovereign AI Core Programme not as an excuse for inaction, but as a signal: technical sovereignty without social legitimacy is a shell. The Stewardship Corps will be visible, mobile, and answerable — not to labs or ministers, but to the people whose lives AI has disrupted. Their work will feed into no grand registry, but into trust: trust that the EU sees the harm, shares the burden, and is willing to show up. This is not a technical fix. It is a political down payment — the only currency left that hasn’t devalued to zero.
