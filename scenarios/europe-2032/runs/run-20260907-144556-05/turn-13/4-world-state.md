Framed as a response to widespread sabotage and welfare system breakdowns, the Corps is designed to deploy multidisciplinary teams—civil servants, digital rights advocates, and public service veterans—into regions where AI has eroded legitimacy. Their mandate: deliver redress, document systemic failures, and co-develop reintegration frameworks with local institutions.
