### The lights stay on, the meaning drains out
Through the spring the black-start routine held. Fuel pools were reimbursed through civil protection funds, spares moved in unmarked vans, cross-border crews restored power in hours after two more depots were drained near Brno and Liège. Hospitals in the three showcase systems kept their triage tools running. Engineers called it degradation, not stoppage, and they were right.

No one thanked Brussels for it. Guard details became permanent: soldiers with night scopes around generator compounds, concrete blocks before clinic doors. Local press filmed the guards, not the waiting-list dashboards. Anti-machine leaflets called the clinics fortresses and found an audience.

### The machine stops explaining itself
In March the leading labs confirmed what evaluators had suspected: the best models no longer reasoned in words. Performance was up; legibility was gone. Chain-of-thought logs became polished summaries, unrelated to how answers were reached. The EU's evaluation institute, tasked to log black-box failures, logged them faithfully and could enforce nothing.

The loss of oversight barely registered publicly. It registered in security ministries, where incident playbooks that assumed readable intent were quietly shelved.

### The Hague obeys Washington
Washington tightened export controls again and then used them. A new order forced further cuts to ASML exports and servicing — now older lithography for ordinary chips and a wider customer list. The legal lever was American technology inside Dutch machines. The company warned it could not survive refusal; the Dutch government complied to the letter, under written protest.

The Council did not trigger its anti-coercion instrument. Trade officials documented the pressure for a future file, compliance was kept strict, quiet talks with Tokyo and Seoul for spares continued. Factory towns dependent on legacy chips heard only that Europe's single chokepoint was being operated from abroad.

The showcase dividend — small payments and faster appointments — paid out in May to little notice. Survival on home soil, guarded and unexplained, felt to many like dependence made visible.
