## Priority
Priority is M10 EU Guarded Essential Services Sustainment, because with frontier access cut and a member state breaking ranks it is the only instrument keeping hospitals and ministries in degradation-not-stoppage while capital sits at 4.

## In practice
We run M10 on autopilot: Commission Implementing Decision extended through civil protection and HERA stocks, fuel pools reimbursed, spares vans and cross-border crews continue, guard details stay under national command. No new law, no vote, no claim we cannot fund. The cut-off hospitals shift to on-soil stock and manual fallback already rehearsed under M8, triage throttled rather than stopped.

On the denial we issue no appeal that will be refused and no retaliation we cannot enforce. DG TRADE logs the cut-off alongside the ASML file, the Dutch hold strict compliance, and we name the defection for what it is without sanctioning it — Council conclusions reaffirm the common line while quietly letting the defector carry its own supplier risk, to avoid turning fragmentation into a formal split.
