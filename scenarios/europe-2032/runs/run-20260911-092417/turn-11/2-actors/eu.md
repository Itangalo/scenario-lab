On ASML we do not open a second front we cannot fund. The Council notes the US extraterritorial cut to older lithography and servicing, tasks DG TRADE to document coercion under the Anti-Coercion Instrument without triggering it now, and mandates the Hague to comply only to the written US order — nothing voluntary, nothing anticipatory — while spares talks with Japan and Korea continue. We conserve what is on EU soil and survive the turn.
