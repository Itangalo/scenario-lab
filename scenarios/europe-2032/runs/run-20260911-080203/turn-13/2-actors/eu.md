**EU Breakthrough Therapies Sovereign Delivery Compact**

## Priority
The new Sovereign Delivery Compact, because with containment holding but capital at 20 and jobs falling, only visible EU-delivered cures can fund the Transition Shield politically.

## In practice
We task DG SANTE and HERA with joint procurement plus EU-site tech-transfer for the new therapies, using EU4Health and rescEU top-up, delivered through the ring-fenced wards that already cut waiting lists. Health Council QMV on a cross-border threats / internal market base; pharma gets accelerated EMA pathways for EU manufacturing commitments.

We keep DG EMPL pushing the Transition Shield wage insurance and vouchers through employment offices despite unagreed levies, using interim ESF+ advances, and ENISA sustaining isolation drills and clean backups from the finished Shield so grids degrade rather than stop. Mayors and hospital directors front the delivery story to tie healing wards to European procurement, not foreign models.
