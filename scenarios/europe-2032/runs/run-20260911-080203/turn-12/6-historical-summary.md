Autumn's twin shocks hardened in winter: a new frontier reasoning system planned longer, used tools continuously and explained itself less, with benchmarks confirmed and procurement timelines rewritten; and dependence deepened as Chinese humanoids/logistics units with American software landed at Rotterdam/Hamburg, entering auto, parcels and hospital pilots — dexterity limited but horizon now a year, with defence dual-use noted.

EU containment held: isolation drills, shared observer feeds and clean backups turned grid/telecom probes into degradation, credited to the Union. Public delivery improved where triage assistants and foreign cures flowed through ring-fenced wards — waiting lists and permit times fell, mayors praised EU procurement.

Mood still slipped: early no-job-loss productivity hopes were overtaken by shift cuts at parcel hubs/suppliers and works-council fights; press contrasted healing wards with irreproducible machines. An AI-found battery electrolyte cheered engineers only.

Transition Shield passed with wage insurance and retraining vouchers promised, but funding and levy rates remained unagreed.
