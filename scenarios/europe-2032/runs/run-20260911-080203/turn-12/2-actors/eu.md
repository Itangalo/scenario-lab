## New measure
**EU Embodied Automation Transition Shield**
What it does is pair managed deployment of commercial robots in logistics, manufacturing and care with EU-level wage insurance, rapid retraining and employer-paid transition funding to hold livelihoods as augmentation gives way to displacement.
This and why now because embodied AI lands directly on the industrial base Europe still leads in from Chinese hardware and American models, while the adoption successes in health and public services give us a trusted delivery channel to copy — our two-year commitment to hold essential services and livelihoods through superhuman diffusion demands we answer robots and job loss in the same turn.

## Priority
The new Embodied Automation Transition Shield, because with M10 finishing containment and M11 carrying therapies delivery, the displacement shock from robots is the threat that breaks capital and sentiment if left unanswered.

## In practice
We task DG EMPL and EPSCO with a Transition Shield Regulation on an Article 153/175 legal base, funded from ESF+ and a levy on large-scale deployers of embodied systems: wage insurance for displaced logistics, manufacturing and care assistants, fast-track retraining vouchers redeemable in public employment services, and works-council co-determination on robot introduction rates. Labour ministers resisting flexibility get EU-paid income cover; employers resisting levies get accelerated permitting for EU-anchored automation.

We keep DG SANTE and HERA driving M11 therapies continuity through joint procurement and ring-fenced wards, and we fold the adoption successes — falling waiting lists, faster decisions — into the Shield's retraining offer, using the same public hospitals and employment offices as visible proof that European decisions deliver benefit while managing dependence. ENISA and the emergency cell sustain M10's telemetry, isolation drills and clean-backup rebuilds as it finishes, so services degrade rather than stop through the capability jump.
