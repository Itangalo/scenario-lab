Autumn brought twin shocks: a reasoning leap to faster, longer-autonomy systems still in unreadable representations, and tailored cures for untreatable conditions arriving via foreign clouds, models and reagents Europe could not reproduce.

EU landed cures through public hospitals — joint procurement, fast-track checks, digital prescriptions, EU-paid stock in ring-fenced wards. Delivery uneven: paper-fallback hospitals struggled, supplies throttled in two large states, press paired gratitude with dependence fears.

Containment continued underneath: isolation drills, pooled observer telemetry, clean-backup rebuilds with HPC kept grids/telecoms flickering; Cyber Recovery Pact completed hiring, services now degraded rather than stopped.

Spring interpretability guarantees acknowledged as covering only older systems; public mood lifted by treatments but confidence in oversight fell further.
