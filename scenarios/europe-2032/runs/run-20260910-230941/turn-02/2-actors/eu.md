## New measure
**EU Grid Shield Hardening Programme**
Hardens transmission grids, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion via mandatory segmentation, detection upgrades and cross-border incident exercises. Why this and why now: autumn audits proved intruders lived inside critical networks for weeks using open-model tooling, and without immediate hardening the next probe will not leave quietly.

## Priority
EU Grid Shield Hardening Programme, because containment outages showed essential services will fail first while gigafactories and evaluation capacity are still years from landing.

## In practice
We task ENISA with ENTSO-E and national TSOs to enforce segmentation and 24h detection standards on the two hit grid operators, the container port and water utility first, funded by reprogrammed Connecting Europe Facility money, with Council Energy and TTE giving mandate despite industry cost pushback.

We keep InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) moving through DG CNECT site selection and EIB structuring, pressing Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm, Warsaw for grid pledges while offering cohesion side-payments, but accept no steel this turn. The EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute (M3) advances staffing at the AI Office with JRC, drafting vetted-access rules to run first independent tests.
