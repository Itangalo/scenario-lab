## New measure
**EU Continuity Stack**
Stands up EU-hosted fallback inference and model substitution for hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the leading foreign model, using pooled EU capacity and vetted open models under EU control. Why this and why now: the cutoff lands directly on essential services that built on a revocable foreign dependency, and without an immediate EU-controlled alternative the denial reads as Union failure and stalls resilience rollout.

EU Continuity Stack, because the access denial outranks our own direction this turn — continuity for hospitals and ministries must come before longer-term gigafactory build-out.
## In practice
We task DG CNECT with ENISA and the EuroHPC JU to pool available EU inference, procure EU-hosted open-model capacity, and push substitution kits first to hit hospitals and municipal stacks via emergency Digital Europe reprogramming, with the Health and TTE Councils mandating continuity standards.

We keep InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) on site-selection in Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm and Warsaw and EIB structuring, and keep EU Rapid Cyber Defence Deployment (M5) pushing automated patching to grid operators and ports, but accept engineering bandwidth stays on incident response and fallback integration while attribution remains unresolved.
