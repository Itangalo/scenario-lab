## Priority
EU Grid Shield Hardening Programme, because the major incident hits the same grids, ports and public services it is already segmenting and the breakthrough only helps if baseline logging and segmentation are in place first.
## In practice
We task ENISA with CERT-EU and national CSIRTs to push the new automated patching and swarm-detection tooling first to the two hit grid operators, the container port, water utility and the compromised public-service stacks, funded by emergency reprogramming plus Digital Europe cybersecurity funds, with TTE Council mandating deployment standards despite municipal capacity gaps.

We keep InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) on site-selection and EIB structuring with Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm, Warsaw, and push the EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute (M3) toward vetted-access pilots, but accept both stay preparatory while engineers and incident responders absorb the attack and attribution proceeds.
