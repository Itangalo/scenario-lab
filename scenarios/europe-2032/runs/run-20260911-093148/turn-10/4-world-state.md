### Holding by hand
The first half of 2031 did not bring a new blackout, but an attack on the answer to the last one. Small groups broke into transformer stores, drained fuel from pooled generators and smashed telemetry cabinets holding the handwritten breaker logs. Police reports described it as coordinated; local press described the targets as the neighbourhood lifelines. Grid operators stopped listing where spares were kept.

Brussels put the black-start network on a protection footing. Off-books pools were brought on-scheme, dispersed, guarded and reimbursed under civil-protection rules. A published map showed what was protected without showing exactly where. Where guards arrived in time, restoration drills held. Where they did not, towns lost the fallback they had counted on and went back to waiting.

The containment and mutual-aid protocol finished in the middle of this. Isolation drills, shared dependency cut-offs and cross-border crews were now procedure, not experiment. Engineers credited it with faster fault isolation during spring intrusions, even as restoration remained slow and manual.

### Quotas and cancelled build-out
Washington moved again to volume quotas for allied buyers of advanced chips and model access. Servicing for older tools went case-by-case. The Hague complied under protest. With private valuations collapsing and announced data-centre expansions cancelled on both sides of the Atlantic, there was nothing to buy even where a licence existed. European clinics stayed on a European default with a metered American fallback; factory plans stayed frozen.

One bright spot was claimed loudly. Health ministries and city administrations showed waiting lists falling and permit times cut from months to days where public-sector assistants had been deployed, branded as a European decision. Crowds did not cheer, but coverage noted it. Hostility eased from open rage to sullen doubt.

The Commission spent nothing new, promised no new procurement, and held the common line. It ended the half-year with almost no standing left, but with the lights still on.
