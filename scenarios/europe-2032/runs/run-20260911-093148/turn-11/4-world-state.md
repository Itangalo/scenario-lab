### The jobs that did not come back
Autumn brought figures no one could spin away. Law firms, accountants, software houses, customer centres and back-office administrations confirmed what graduates already knew: entry posts were simply not being refilled. Firms spoke of tooling efficiencies; unions spoke of hiring freezes. The argument over numbers mattered less than empty intake cohorts in Brussels, Milan, Warsaw and Barcelona.

Anger settled on the young. Protests outside job fairs were sullen rather than violent, but press coverage linked the vanished starter jobs to the same systems that had moved money on their own two years before. Ministers who had claimed public-sector assistants as a European success found the claim turned against them.

### A fallback that holds, barely
The guarded black-start and manual fallback network was declared complete. Dispersed stores, reimbursed fuel pools, paper breaker logs under guard and cross-border mutual-aid crews were now standing procedure. Engineers said isolation during autumn intrusions was minutes faster than in spring.

Restoration was still slow. Two regions lost guarded stock to theft and vandalism despite patrols, and operators again removed locations from circulation. A scuffle between guards and intruders at one depot left injuries and a week of headlines about barricaded infrastructure. Elsewhere drills held, and lights came back by hand.

### A guarantee on paper
Brussels answered with a Graduate Transition Guarantee: six months of wage insurance and short retraining vouchers for displaced juniors, paid by reprogrammed social funds and a temporary levy on automating employers. Launched through employment ministries without a new law, it reached first cohorts late in the year.

Take-up was real but thin. Funds were small, employer attestation was patchy, and many graduates saw vouchers for jobs that did not exist. The Commission promised no new procurement elsewhere, kept clinics on the domestic default with metered American fallback, and kept quotas and frozen factory plans untouched. The common line held, but little else moved.
