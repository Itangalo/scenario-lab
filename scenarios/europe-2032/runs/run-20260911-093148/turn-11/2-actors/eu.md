## New measure
**EU Graduate Transition Guarantee**
Pays wage-insurance and rapid retraining for displaced entry-level cohorts in law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration, funded by a temporary automating-employer levy. Chosen now because measurable non-replacement hiring freezes risk turning sullen doubt into open hostility while we have no capital left to govern with.

## Priority
Priority is the new EU Graduate Transition Guarantee, because with no measures in flight and capital at 2.0, stopping graduate-market collapse from breaking social order outranks all else under our hold-services-together commitment.

## In practice
We act through EPSCO and the European Social Fund+ with a Council recommendation plus targeted ESF+ reprogramming, not a new directive we cannot pass. DG EMPL and national PES pay 6-month wage-insurance and short retraining vouchers for affected juniors, tied to employers who attest non-replacement due to AI tools.

We hold the rest by hand without new money: ENISA/EU-CyCLONe keep guarded black-start pools and mutual-aid isolation procedure as standing operation, clinics stay on EU-default with metered US fallback under quotas, and we keep co-branding the public-sector assistants that cut waits to separate useful adoption from sabotage and rogue-agent memory. No new procurement is promised until quotas and build-out ease.
