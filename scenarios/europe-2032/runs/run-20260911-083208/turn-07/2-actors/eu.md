## Priority
M6 EU Cyber Emergency Repair Cell, because visible restoration after back-to-back automated attacks is the only thing that can stop cohesion and sentiment collapsing further while we cannot afford a new front.

## In practice
We keep the ENISA-led Repair Cell as the Union's sole active spend, using existing CERT-EU and national CSIRT rosters under NIS2 crisis coordination and reprogrammed Digital Europe emergency funds, with teams held in the worst-hit municipalities and clinics until backups hold and the defensive breakthrough tooling is pushed out. No new money vote is attempted; with capital at 1 we cannot carry one.

We freeze new builds and asks: the Gigafactory permits and M5 Compact table are kept warm by DG CNECT and the EEAS without fresh commitments, and we use the Cell's solidarity aid as leverage to pull the defecting capital back to the joint Washington tier-access position. We acknowledge the knowledge-work productivity evidence publicly but take no adoption push now — the public cannot feel it against downed services.
