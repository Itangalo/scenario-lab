## New measure
None this turn, while political capital sits at 3 and public sentiment at 4 and any voted build would die before a mandate.

**EU Cyber-Continuity Hold**
This would be a small preparedness and resilience hold to keep hospitals, dispatch and ports on isolated EU-hosted fallbacks through the automated-attack wave, but with no Council vote possible and emergency lines already committed it must wait until credit recovers enough to staff it.

## Priority
No priority named this turn, because with no measures in flight and no capital to fund a new one, preserving finished fallback capacity on existing health-emergency and civil-protection lines outranks naming a push we cannot pay for.

## In practice
We live off what has finished. HERA joint procurement, Digital Europe and civil-protection commitments keep paying stay-until-holding crews, paper-to-digital re-typing, municipal-server nursing, and locally hosted open-model packs for ports. ENISA and CERT-EU hold the isolate-and-restore-from-offline-drives playbook for the model-generated ransomware sweep, with clinic sequencing sentinels kept on low-background watch. Brussels asks the Health and Home Affairs Councils only to keep emergency channels open, not for new money.

We start no new Gigafactory vote, no export-control retaliation, no open-weight ban we cannot enforce now that the March release is on hundreds of thousands of private machines, and no EU robot-build while control models remain American and Chinese hardware dominates. Mayors keep visible credit for backup kits; commissioners absorb blame on evening shows until cohesion and credit recover enough to build again.
