# Statements: The European Union (turn 8)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI control to withstand strategic shocks and prevent catastrophic misuse

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing, but we now acknowledge that capability without control is a path to dependency, not sovereignty — and that sovereignty without resilience is a fortress with no people left inside. Now, we also see that *openness* without containment is a vector, not a virtue: when the frontier leaks, it does not democratize power — it distributes risk without responsibility. And now, we see that *resilience without cohesion* is a fiction — if member states will not share the burden, no directive will harden the weakest link.
  - Grounds: The `Resilience Reinforcement Initiative` has stalled not due to technical failure, but because cost-sharing disputes have frozen patching velocity across critical sectors. This turn, the failure is not of design but of union: resilience is only as strong as its least funded node, and we are learning that a system of mutual obligation cannot be enforced from Brussels alone. The identity must now reflect that our survival depends not only on technology or law, but on the willingness of the member states to stand together under fire — and that, so far, they are not.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer treat speed as a substitute for control, and will pause where assurance cannot scale with capability; now, we also act where resilience lags, even without a direct mandate, because harm finds the weakest joint. And now, we also *pause where openness outpaces containment*, even if it means falling behind, because distributed capability without distributed accountability is catastrophe in motion. But now, we also *insist on shared burden*, even when solidarity is thin — because a union that does not share risk cannot claim sovereignty.
  - Grounds: The failure of the `Resilience Reinforcement Initiative` to gain traction reveals a deeper truth: our measures assume a level of fiscal and political cohesion that does not currently exist. We have acted under uncertainty, yes — but we have assumed that solidarity would follow. It has not. The commitment must now include the demand that risk be shared, not merely managed, because without it, even the strongest containment regime collapses at the edges.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Prevent catastrophic misuse by hardening containment, resilience, and oversight at the sovereign level — even if it slows our own capability race — because a sovereign AI stack that cannot be controlled is not sovereignty, it is a hostage to fortune. But now, we also act to force cohesion — because a Union that cannot protect its weakest members cannot protect any of them.
  - Grounds: The original commitment focused on sovereign-level hardening, but the failure of resilience efforts in mid-tier member states shows that sovereignty cannot be centralized. The Nordic-Benelux red-teaming pact is a symptom: when the centre cannot deliver, the periphery organizes. We must now bind cohesion into our core mission — not as a hope, but as a condition of survival.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
