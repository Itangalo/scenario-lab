### A Gathering Pace, a Fractured Front

The frontier advances to 64.0 as new training runs complete across multiple labs, building on established scaling laws. One demonstration — of agent-like workflows coordinating across enterprise systems — draws intense scrutiny, though its autonomy remains bounded and scripted. No rupture in continuity is observed; the pace remains consistent with prior trends, even as capabilities press further into operational domains.

In Europe, cohesion frays. A member state, citing national competitiveness, signs a bilateral agreement with a US hyperscaler, gaining research access to a 67.0 model outside the EU’s Containment Protocol. Brussels issues a formal warning: such arrangements erode collective control. The Commission invokes the Anti-Coercion Instrument, threatening suspension of cohesion funds, but legal challenges delay enforcement.

Amid tensions, institutional progress continues. The European AI Assurance Directorate becomes operational, conducting its first pre-training review under the Sovereign Model Assurance Framework, now legally binding. Construction begins on the first sovereign AI cluster in Saint-Malo and Kiruna under Franco-German dual-key oversight. The Swedish InvestAI Gigafactory breaks ground, though transmission delays in Norrland cast doubt on energy readiness.

The Containment Protocol remains active and is reaffirmed as the EU’s top priority, with an Article 16 implementing act advancing slowly through interagency review. Full rollout is hindered by unresolved disputes over escalation thresholds. Meanwhile, the expired Critical Infrastructure Resilience Surge leaves critical patching gaps. In response, the EU launches the Resilience Reinforcement Initiative, mandating AI-assisted monitoring in energy and transport and initiating a continent-wide bio-detection network.

Open-weight development progresses modestly to 50.0, reflecting incremental replication of last cycle’s frontier models. No sudden leap occurs; the open ecosystem absorbs knowledge at its usual lagged rate.

Public sentiment holds at 25.0. Trust remains tethered to proof — not promises.

### US Posture Set

The new US administration takes office, classifying frontier AI as a strategic national asset. Access will be rationed by geopolitical tier — a move that hardens Europe’s dependency.
