### US Posture Set: Strategic Rationing Begins  
The new US administration takes office and formally adopts a posture of strategic consolidation, treating frontier AI as a tiered national asset. Access to advanced models is now formally restricted based on alliance status, with the EU placed in a conditional tier—granted limited research access but excluded from operational deployment rights. The move confirms the dependency exposed in the prior term, and Brussels responds with urgency.

### Emergency Access Guarantee Launched — Priority Designated  
The EU launches the Emergency Access Guarantee for Sovereign Stacks, making it the current named priority among active measures. This binding failover mechanism ensures public agencies retain AI access during capability denial events. The system will run on compute from the Sovereign Compute Acceleration Initiative, with pre-tuned models for health, energy, and crisis response. EuroHPC and the CEA Joint Undertaking begin integration, and ENISA drafts certification protocols. However, Poland and Hungary resist linking disbursement to compliance, demanding concessions. A bilateral deal on medical AI co-development is offered, but talks stall.

### Sovereign Talent Push Accelerates  
Housing agreements in Paris, Munich, and Utrecht near completion, enabling faster relocation. France and Germany agree to diplomatic-level treatment for recruited engineers, including spousal work rights. Yet recruitment remains slow—many candidates wait to see tangible infrastructure before relocating.

### Tech Sovereignty Package Completes  
The Tech sovereignty package concludes, establishing accelerated-permitting zones and a €200bn private investment target for data centres. However, with no immediate build-out, the impact is procedural, not physical.

### Capability Creeps, Open-Weight Gains Moderate  
AI capability rises to 67.0, driven by incremental advances despite signs of a plateau. Open-weight models increase to 54.0, reflecting continued diffusion of prior knowledge and modest improvements in fine-tuning and efficiency. The gap with frontier capability narrows slightly but remains significant; no major open release or leak occurs to accelerate parity.

### Political Capital Erodes  
With six large measures in flight and one now formally prioritized—the Emergency Access Guarantee for Sovereign Stacks—political capital falls to 11.0. The EU’s agenda remains overstretched, and public visibility stays low.
