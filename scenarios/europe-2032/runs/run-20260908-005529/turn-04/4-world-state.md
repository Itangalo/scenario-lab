### Frontier Leaps Forward  
A major capability jump lands in early February, as a leading lab releases a system demonstrating coherent, multi-week planning in unstructured environments and achieving breakthrough results in protein folding and circuit design. The leap pushes `ai_capability` to 62.5. Benchmark anomalies had already surfaced weeks earlier—unexplained coordination in agent simulations and evaluation saturation—hinting at emergent reasoning patterns developers could not reproduce. The lab dismissed them as artefacts. Now, the artefacts have shipped.

### Open Weights Catch Up—But Not Too Close  
With the frontier at 62.5, `openweight_capability` rises to 49.0, reflecting accelerated diffusion of knowledge and partial replication of core functionalities from recently leaked agent architectures. Underground developer forums circulate derivative models enabling early-stage autonomous operations, but full architectural parity remains out of reach. Security firms detect nascent use in credential harvesting and supply-chain probing, though the systems lack the robustness of frontier models. The gap between open and closed capabilities narrows, but open-weight systems do not surpass or equal the frontier.

### Safety Erosion Accelerates  
`ai_safety` drops to 7.0. The capability_jump and eval_anomaly_reports trigger a −12 penalty under metric rule 3. No safety upgrades have landed on deployed systems, and the EU’s monitoring tools remain reactive. The gap between what systems can do and what anyone can verify widens dangerously. The drop is severe but remains within the 25-point per-turn limit, preserving compliance with the constitutional cap.

### Cyber Shield Advances Amid Legal Pushback  
The Cyber Shield Emergency Activation continues its rollout: AI-assisted intrusion detection standards are finalized, and the €12bn fund opens for applications. However, disbursements remain minimal as Poland and Hungary file a formal challenge at the CJEU, arguing disproportionate burden and sovereignty infringement. Legal uncertainty delays initial allocations, and full operational capacity is pushed into the next turn.

### Talent Initiative Announced, Funding Pending  
The EU announces the Sovereign Talent Anchoring Programme, targeting 1,500 frontier engineers with fast-track visas and competitive salaries, to be integrated into CEA and national labs. However, no dedicated funding mechanism is established this turn. Budget negotiations are ongoing, and implementation will not begin until appropriations are secured in a future turn.

### Political Capital Strained  
Five large measures now run concurrently. Despite the urgency, political capital drops to 25.0 under the −15 portfolio burden. The proposal bonus for the talent programme is modest (+2), given it answers eval_anomaly_reports indirectly. No sentiment boost occurs.

### Sovereignty Edges Up  
`eu_ai_sovereignty` rises to 25.0: +2 from ongoing compute and talent build, −1 from capability rise, no access event.
