### Capability Creeps Forward  
AI capability advances by +2.5 this turn, reaching 72.5, as leading labs resume limited training under improved internal oversight. Progress concentrates in agent autonomy and cross-domain reasoning, narrowing the gap between supervised execution and continuous operation. Openweight_capability rises to 59.0, driven by modular improvements and the growing accessibility of agent frameworks from the EU open consortium, though no foundational release closes the gap with the closed frontier.

### Sovereignty: Foundations Laid, But Not Yet Secure  
The EU marks formal completion of four major sovereignty initiatives: the InvestAI Gigafactories, Sovereign Compute Acceleration Initiative, Sovereign Talent Anchoring Programme, and Emergency Access Guarantee. Hardware deployment begins at EuroHPC sites, and 1,320 frontier engineers are now under EU-affiliated contracts. However, full operational sovereignty remains distant—Poland and Hungary continue to resist implementing the Emergency Access Guarantee, and while the Commission has initiated legal proceedings under Article 122 TFEU, the mechanism cannot be enforced this turn due to the absence of prior procedural groundwork. The invocation is pending, not active, and will require intergovernmental negotiation and time to materialize. The open agent consortium gains traction, with pilot funding routed through Horizon Europe for grid optimisation, but remains experimental.

### Incident Response Framework Launched, Not Yet Operational  
The Commission announces the Sovereign AI Incident Response Network, integrating ENISA, ECDC, and national agencies into a proposed early-intervention framework. Shared monitoring tools are in development for deployment across critical operators in energy and health, but full integration will take at least two turns due to coordination complexity and data sovereignty barriers. Resilience increases by +1.5 to 49.5, reflecting early progress and partial deployment, but not full operational status.

### Political Capital Frozen, Sentiment Stagnant  
With eu_political_capital at 1.5, the EU remains in paralysis. The automated decision-making scandal continues to erode public trust, anchoring public_sentiment at 25.0. No new measures are launched, and the portfolio charge remains high at −4. ai_safety holds flat at 9.0—no control improvements land on deployed systems, and opacity concerns deepen. The sovereignty gains from completed initiatives are partially offset by the ongoing portfolio burden, resulting in a net increase of +2.0 to eu_ai_sovereignty, rising from 25.5 to 27.5.
