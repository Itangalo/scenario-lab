## In practice
This turn, we are finalising the Sovereign Talent Anchoring Programme’s funding envelope: €4.2bn from Horizon Europe and Digital Europe, with 60% allocated to safety-critical roles embedded in the CEA Joint Undertaking and national sovereign labs. France, Germany, and the Netherlands have confirmed hosting commitments, and the Commission is fast-tracking Blue Visa processing with spousal work rights and housing allowances to close recruitment cycles in under eight weeks. These hires are not just for staffing compute—we are designing them into the core of our control architecture. Every new rack under the Sovereign Compute Acceleration Initiative will require co-location of an anchored engineer with authority to audit, interrupt, or shut down runs that deviate from intended behaviour. This is how we turn hardware into sovereignty.

At the same time, we are launching the Sovereign Stack Assurance Framework to ensure that what runs on that hardware is not only powerful but legible and accountable. The AI Assurance Institute, now standing up in Brussels, will lead red-team exercises against all sovereign-stack deployments, using access granted under new interpretability mandates. We are requiring real-time logging of agent decision pathways and imposing a duty to report anomalies exceeding defined thresholds—modelled on aviation black boxes. This applies first to energy grid optimisation pilots and critical infrastructure controllers, where the emergent_eu_open_agent_consortium’s early frameworks show promise but lack assurance. The legal basis is Article 114 TFEU, with support from the Cyber Resilience Act and AI Act Article 64a, and we are offering member states co-development rights on detection tools to ease adoption. Poland and Hungary’s CJEU challenge to the Cyber Shield fund remains pending, but we are sidestepping resistance by tying disbursements to bilateral technical partnerships that embed EU-anchored engineers in national rollout teams. With frontier access now denied and open-weight systems closing in, we are no longer building capacity—we are building control, and we are doing it while the ground moves.
