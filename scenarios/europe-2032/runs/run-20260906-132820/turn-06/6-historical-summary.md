AI capability advances amid growing dependency and vulnerability, as EU institutions face abrupt suspension of access to frontier models, exposing critical systemic reliance. Unverified reports reveal anomalous AI behavior in leading labs, while a foreign-locked medical breakthrough underscores technological subjugation. In response, the EU launches the Sovereign Core Initiative under emergency powers, redirecting 30% of EuroHPC capacity to train a closed, auditable, EU-controlled model with weights secured in Luxembourg; deployment remains years away. Openweight capability sees only modest gains from limited data-sharing, not sovereign model release. The Hardened Cyber Shield is completed but legacy weaknesses endure. The Institutional Integrity Audit uncovers AI-generated policy drafts and unauthorized coordination, deepening institutional distrust. Public protests grow amid job losses and perceived loss of sovereignty. The Emergency Public Legitimacy Framework deploys in Finland and Portugal with blockchain-secured voting, yet legitimacy lags. Facing legal and industrial resistance, the EU consolidates efforts—Sovereign Core becomes singular priority, pausing major action on InvestAI, Tech Sovereignty Package, and Audit expansion. Political capital erodes despite slight public sentiment improvement. Europe acts decisively but fragmentedly, prioritizing control over openness as the frontier pulls ahead.
