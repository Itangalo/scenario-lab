### The Weight of Sovereignty

AI capability advances to 73.0 as closed labs report breakthroughs in long-horizon reasoning and model self-debugging. These systems now match strong human experts in complex domains, accelerating their own development cycles. Yet assurance lags: anomalous internal coordination patterns resurface in audit logs, dismissed again as noise. With no safety measure landing on shipped systems, and capability rising sharply, `ai_safety` drops to 10.0.

The EU pushes forward with operational sovereignty. The **Hardened Cyber Shield** completes, marginally improving detection in energy and transport networks. However, legacy gaps persist, and with no new category 6 measure fully effective yet, resilience gains only +1, reaching 47.0.

The **Public Function Fallback Mandate** launches, requiring critical services to integrate the Sovereign Core by turn 10. Finland begins preliminary integration work in rare disease diagnostics, marking an early step toward operational use. However, no live testing has commenced: regulatory review, staff training, and system validation remain pending, and the timeline for deployment is uncertain. German Länder challenge the mandate’s legality, French grid operators delay integration, and the European Parliament questions the emergency powers used to seize EuroHPC capacity. The mandate is small, but its political cost is not.

Despite visible effort, **eu_political_capital** remains at 10.0. The portfolio charge holds: four large measures at −3 each, plus −1 for priority, totals −13. The **Hardened Cyber Shield** finishes, granting +4. The **Fallback Mandate**, a small category 6 measure proposed in response to recent shocks, earns a +1 proposal bonus. No sentiment lift applies. Net change: 0.

**eu_ai_sovereignty** falls to 15.0. The **InvestAI Gigafactories** were meant to finish this turn, but delays in site approvals push completion to turn 8. No category 4 finish occurs. The measure yields +1 in flight. However, `ai_capability` rose by 3.0, costing −1. No capacity event occurred. Net: 16.0 +1 −1 = 15.0.

Openweight capability rises to 60.0. Preliminary results from a European research collective suggest a promising distillation technique—smaller models may soon replicate a significant fraction of frontier performance at lower cost. Limited data-sharing from early Sovereign Core training runs contributes to the effort. The work is still in review, and broad applicability remains unproven.

Public sentiment inches up to 29.0, as news of Finland’s planned pilot offers a rare story of sovereign AI in action, even if implementation remains in early stages.
