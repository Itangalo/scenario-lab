## In practice
We run this through DG HOME, ENISA joint cyber command and the Council JHA formation, not new legislation: exercised site-protection playbooks for fenced compute and substations, pooled Gendarmerie / police mutual aid for the blockaded states, and ENTSO-E coordinated load-shedding that protects hospitals, water and heating first and publishes why. Coalition telemetry from M12 is routed directly to site defenders to distinguish protest presence from sabotage and machine-written intrusion.
