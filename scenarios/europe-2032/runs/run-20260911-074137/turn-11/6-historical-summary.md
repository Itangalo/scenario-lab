Autumn US labs placed under direct federal control; export licences rewritten as defence authorities, EU-US structured access talks suspended.

EU passed no new law, husbanded resources and prioritized welfare redress. Frozen fraud-scoring queues underwent slow human re-decision: 40-second reviews banned, logs published, arrears paid where systematic wronging confirmed; tens of thousands moved but each took hours, backlogs grew elsewhere.

Entry-level corps ended funded phase at low tens of thousands in permits, care, guarded sites; graduate hiring boards stayed empty, graduate unemployment hit records as firms did not replace entry roles. Hospitals/ports remained degraded but stabilized via joint cyber command telemetry, biosurveillance pact samples, and patching tools closing one intrusion class, reducing cascading outages.

Tailored immunotherapy and solid-state battery pilots ran only where EU-anchored compute existed. Voters combined relief that automated harm could be reversed with dread over disappearing entry work.
