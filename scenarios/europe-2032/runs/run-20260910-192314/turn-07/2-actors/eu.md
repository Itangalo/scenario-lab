## New measure
**Member-State AI Deal Clearance**
Requires member states to notify and clear exclusive hyperscaler or third-country AI capacity deals through the Council before signature, with joint procurement as the alternative offer.
This is an international coordination and leverage instrument (category 8), small and legal rather than fiscal, because with political capital at 2 and a defection actively undercutting the Union line, stopping the bleed outranks starting new builds — and it directly serves our absorb-shocks commitment by keeping sovereign capacity collective.

## Priority
Member-State AI Deal Clearance, because with no measures in flight and a defection in progress, holding a single Union bargaining line is the precondition for any future sovereign build to matter.

## In practice
We move through the Competitiveness Council and the Anti-Coercion Instrument legal base, not through new money. DG TRADE and DG CNECT table an emergency notification regime: any preferential compute, model-access or siting deal gets a 6-week Union review, with the InvestAI consortia and joint procurement offered as the compliant route. The defecting capital is offered grandfathering plus a share of pooled US-tier negotiation, not punishment.

We spend no capital we do not have. With Gigafactories (M1) now finished and the Shield (M3) finished, enforcement runs through existing AI Office and ENISA channels — no new programme, no grid re-sequencing fight with mayors this turn — to let political capital and sentiment bottom out and recover while the clearance rule holds the line.
