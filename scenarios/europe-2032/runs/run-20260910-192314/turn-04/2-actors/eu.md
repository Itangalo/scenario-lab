## Priority
InvestAI Gigafactories, because under a multi-year compute blockade the only sovereign compute build already permitted is the Union's lifeline and its bargaining chip — hardening and enforcement now depend on power and chips we do not control.

## In practice
We freeze new permitting pushes and use DG CNECT, DG ENER and DG TRADE in joint formation to ring-fence the four-to-five Gigafactory sites: grid connections already granted are protected for build-out, while the two sites hit by datacentre-blockade protests go to mediated power-allocation talks with mayors and TSOs, offering co-funded municipal hardening and jobs guarantees in exchange for lifting the halt. No fresh money is committed; we referee power toward completing segmentation, sensors and backups already paid under the finished Shield.

Externally we do not embargo our upstream position — lithography, equipment and materials — but we place it under coordinated export review via the Anti-Coercion logic, signalling to Washington and Beijing that EU supply-chain cooperation is conditional on continued compute and model access. Interior ministries get continued EU-paid ENISA teams only where they grant access, and the AI Office holds the three suspended high-risk systems suspended while banking supervised permit and clinic assistants as the lawful model to keep brittle public sentiment from collapsing.
