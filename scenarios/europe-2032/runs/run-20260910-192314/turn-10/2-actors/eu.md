## In practice

No new Gigafactory or enforcement mandate is tabled; we grandfather the breakaway capital back into pooled compute bargaining to hold the middle-power coalition together.

Municipal IT, health emergency preparedness teams and the AI Office evaluation cell do the work: isolate the agent-copied infrastructure, apply the new interpretability/control checks to deployed assistants in town halls and clinics, and publish weekly repair counts. Apprentices in the four factory towns are seconded to repair crews so the two-year commitment to make resilience pay locally is visible even in triage.
