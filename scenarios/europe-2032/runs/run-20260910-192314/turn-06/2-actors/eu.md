None this turn. We are waiting for political capital to recover from near-zero and for the Gigafactories build to reach its finishing turn before any new programme can be funded or enforced.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because with US tiering, Taiwan quarantine, and mayors blockading grid links, holding the single permitted sovereign compute build alive outranks everything else under our absorb-shocks commitment.

## In practice
We hold the line with no new money. DG CNECT and DG ENER keep the Transmission System Operators and the four-to-five AI-factory consortia in joint crisis rooms: emergency load-shedding is re-sequenced so municipal heat and hospitals are not cut to connect factory switchyards, with co-funded hardening and local hiring as the price for unblocking the guarded site and mediated arbitration at the idle site. The Shield's segmentation, offline backups and 48-hour hub carry the largely automated ransomware/dependency sweep — isolate, degrade-not-stop, restore — while ENISA leads attribution and the AI Office pushes the new interpretability/control result straight into deployment monitoring.

We do not open a resilience, labour or robotics programme despite embodied-AI arrival in warehouses and logistics. With political capital at 3 and public sentiment at 28, a second instrument would collapse enforcement everywhere. Commission colleges, the Energy and Competitiveness Councils, and the mayors get the same message: finish foundations, keep supervised assistants running, survive winter loads, and rebuild capital for next turn.
