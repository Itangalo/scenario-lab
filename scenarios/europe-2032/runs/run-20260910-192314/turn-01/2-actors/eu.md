## In practice
We are tasking ENISA with expanded 24/7 operational mandate alongside national TSO regulators and the Energy and Transport Council formations, using NIS2 Article legal base for emergency hardening orders: asset inventory, OT segmentation audits, and credential rotation across the two affected EU grid operators first, then all ENTSO-E members.

Money comes from reprioritised Connecting Europe Facility and Digital Europe funds for detection sensors and backup control systems, while the in-flight InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty package continue on permitting and site selection — DG ENER and DG CNECT brokering grid-connection for both compute and hardening, offering member states co-funded response exercises to overcome interior-ministry resistance to EU-level access to OT networks.
