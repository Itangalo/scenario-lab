## New measure
**Repair-to-Jobs Transition Fund**
Pays displaced admin, clinic-billing and municipal-service workers to staff paper-to-digital restoration, server replacement and assisted-AI support roles while funding fast retraining stipends.
This serves the two-year commitment directly — making resilience pay in visible local jobs — and with capital at 8 and sentiment at 8 only paid local work rebuilds consent enough to stop further defection.

## Priority
Repair-to-Jobs Transition Fund, because without visible paid work from the repair effort the two-year commitment to rebuild consent fails and no other measure can be funded.

## In practice
We run this through DG EMPL and DG REGIO using extended state-aid clearance and the pre-authorised emergency lines plus ESF+ reprogramming — no new budget vote. Regions hire from unemployment registers for repair crews, clinic phone triage, and municipal IT helper desks applying the new interpretability checks, with wage top-ups booked to existing lines.

ENISA and the AI Office keep publishing weekly repair counts, now with local hires named, while EPSCO formation offers mayors apprenticeship secondments converted into paid repair contracts. The breakaway capital is offered equal access to the fund conditional on staying in pooled bargaining under the middle-power coordination, to hold cohesion without new money.
