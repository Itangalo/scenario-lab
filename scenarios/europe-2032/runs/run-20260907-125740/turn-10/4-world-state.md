Global AI capability advances to 68.0, driven by closed-lab progress in recursive self-improvement techniques. Leading labs report further anomalies in model reasoning—unpredictable jumps in planning depth and tool use—straining interpretability frameworks. Despite the Real-Time Logging Mandate, no systemic failure occurs, but confidence in oversight erodes. AI safety drops to 24.0 as assurance fails to keep pace.

### Resilience Edges Up, Sentiment Stalls

Resilience increases slightly to 34.0, supported by the logging system’s role in containing the hospital incident and early coordination gains from MPAC. However, no broad hardening measures are completed, and healthcare systems remain vulnerable. Labour displacement continues unchecked in legal, accounting, and software sectors, with university placements near zero. Protests in Lyon and Vienna denounce opaque governance, and public sentiment remains frozen at 14.0.
