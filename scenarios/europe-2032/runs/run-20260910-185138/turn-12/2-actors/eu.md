## Priority
M11 EU Middle-Power Bio-Containment and Compute Access Compact, because only joint licences and pooled eval give us leverage to hold the single line while domestic staffing rebuilds, and it needs political push to reach domestic approvals.
## In practice
We run M11 through DG TRADE and FAC-Trade: take the initialled text to capitals for domestic approval, align bio/compute licence lists, and pool the short-staffed eval quota into joint compute bargaining, offering pooled therapy inputs and audited inference slots as the price for no new bilateral hosting deals. The two frozen-queue capitals are offered re-entry on common audit, no lawsuits.

In parallel we stand up the staffing surge via HERA/ECDC and EU4Health plus ESF+: emergency waivers and stipends for sequencing trainees, rehiring for hub labs, and funded municipal teams for wastewater sampling, clinic order and mortuary backup. Mayors get policing and continuity payouts conditional on staying in the common queue. We do not open a new compute build; all inference stays on EU-hosted hardened hubs with mandatory genome-model reporting.
