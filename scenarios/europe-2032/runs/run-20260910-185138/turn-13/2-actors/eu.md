## Priority
New Accession Track measure, because getting inside the US-China floor before its verification practices harden is the one move that can restore leverage, safety cooperation and political standing at once, outranking even staffing recovery.
## In practice
We task EEAS plus DG TRADE/CNECT to seek observer-then-party status in the US-China follow-on group, tabling the initialled Japan-Korea licence alignment and our eval pool as Europe's verification offer, and we transpose the agreed weights-security and bio-design-tool controls via an implementing act under the AI Act and Dual-Use Regulation. HERA/ECDC enforce the WHO-linked pre-publication review for genome-model work through the common queue: no audited-hub clearance, no funding or publication sign-off.

In parallel we keep M12 running through EU4Health/ESF+ rehires and municipal sampling teams, with mayoral payouts still conditional on staying in the queue, to show the Union can verify what it signs. The two side-deal capitals are told re-entry on common audit is now also the ticket to shared US-China verification slots and therapy inputs — no separate lane.
