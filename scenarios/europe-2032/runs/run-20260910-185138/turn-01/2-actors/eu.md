## Priority
EU Critical Systems Cyber Shield, because the test shot showed pre-positioning in EU grids with undetected persistence and the next incident will exploit the same gap before gigafactories deliver.

## In practice
We task ENISA with joint audits of the two affected transmission operators plus all EU TSOs, backed by a CER/NIS2 implementing act in the Transport-Telecom-Energy Council, forcing air-gap verification and breaker-credential rotation within this turn. Funding comes from re-directed Connecting Europe Facility and Digital Europe money, with member-state grid regulators compelled to report staged tooling.

At the same time we keep InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) and the tech sovereignty package (M2) moving through DG CNECT and accelerated-permitting zones, offering France, Germany, Spain and Poland grid-connection priority and EIB guarantees to hold private co-investment, while resisting US pressure on ASML via the Anti-Coercion screening. The Shield's exercises run through the EU Cyber Crisis Liaison network this winter, linking port authorities and water utilities into the same playbook.
