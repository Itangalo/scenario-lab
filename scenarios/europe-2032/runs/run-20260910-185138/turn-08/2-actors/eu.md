## New measure
**EU Youth Construction and Jobs Compact**
It pairs a policed construction-continuity protocol for gigafactory and data-centre sites with guaranteed local hiring, apprenticeships and wage-bridge slots for blockading graduates, run through municipalities and public employment services. This and now because coordinated blockades in three states plus a member-state side-deal have stalled the only sovereign capacity still moving, and with capital at 6 and sentiment at 9 we cannot hold sites by force alone — we must trade jobs for access.

## Priority

## In practice
We task EPSCO and the Employment Committee with the compact, funded by repurposed ESF+ and the finished Transition Fund envelope: mayors get policing guidelines under the Civil Protection Mechanism that keep one construction lane open while guaranteeing dialogue zones, and contractors on the first gigafactory keep grid queues only if they sign local-hire and paid trainee quotas verified by national PES agencies.

We answer the defection by not chasing it publicly: the Commission offers the defecting capital the same compact terms plus ENISA-audited continuity connection, making the side-deal redundant for hospitals and ministries without triggering an Article 258 fight we cannot afford at 6 capital. DG ENER and ENISA keep mutual-aid crews and emergency inference brokering on existing playbooks, no new instrument, to hold essential services through the spring.
