## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure independent AI capability to anchor European strategic autonomy, prioritising fallback infrastructure and sovereign inference capacity over duplication of frontier training at this stage of the crisis
  - Grounds: The foreign access denial in January and the subsequent legal resistance have made clear that sovereignty at the level of operational continuity is now more urgent than matching the frontier. Our immediate task is not to train a rival model, but to ensure that essential functions do not collapse when one is revoked. This shift does not abandon our aim, but focuses it on what can be built in time and sustained against political headwinds — sovereign inference, not sovereign training.
