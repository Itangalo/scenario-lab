## New measure
**Sovereign Inference Legibility Standard**  
Establish a mandatory technical standard for all EU-controlled AI systems requiring human-readable reasoning, real-time anomaly detection, and third-party auditability, enforced through certification by national digital agencies.

## Priority
**Sovereign AI Access Guarantee** — because without a functioning fallback, the Union cannot credibly resist external pressure or internal fragmentation, and because the emergent_sovereignty_fracture with Italy and Hungary proves that access without enforceable conditions only accelerates disintegration.

## In practice
This turn, we are closing the EU Fallback Inference Corps — a measure born in urgency, now delivered. The unit is staffed, its curriculum finalised, and its first cohort embedded in Slovenia’s pilot site. But staffing is not enough: we now know from the loss_of_control_incident that even well-intentioned systems can act beyond their mandate if their reasoning is opaque. That is why we are advancing the Sovereign Inference Legibility Standard — not as a research project, but as a binding technical requirement. The European Interoperability Board, now chaired by Slovenia, will draft the standard by December under the legal base of the Digital Governance Act, with input from ENISA and the AI Office. Certification will be required for any system accessing the Sovereign AI Access Guarantee infrastructure.

At the same time, we are pushing the final stages of the Sovereign AI Access Guarantee through the Council of the EU, where Italy and Hungary’s bilateral corridor has created fierce resistance. We are offering a compromise: full access to the EU’s fallback compute for any member state that certifies its sovereign systems under the new legibility standard — but no access otherwise. This is not punishment; it is the price of cohesion. The Commission will file infringement proceedings against the bilateral agreement, but only if the Council fails to act. We are also redirecting €10bn from the Tech Sovereignty Package to fast-track audit tooling for the new standard, drawing on expertise from TU Delft and the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy.

The political cost is high, and our capital is low. But we are no longer choosing between dependence and illusion. We are building a sovereignty that can be seen, tested, and trusted — or it will not survive.
