### The Weight of Completion

The EU formally closes the InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty Package, and Sovereign AI Access Guarantee. Despite legal finalisation, no Gigafactory has broken ground—funds were diverted, and procurement disputes stalled implementation. The Access Guarantee exists on paper, but its authority is hollowed by Italy and Hungary’s reliance on non-EU cloud providers, exposing strategic fragility.

A major AI-driven cyberattack exploits poisoned open-source code, crippling health networks in three member states. In response, the EU launches the €30bn Resilience Surge, fast-tracking AI-powered intrusion detection and zero-trust systems across critical infrastructure. ENISA and national CERTs lead deployment, backed by the newly mobilised Cyber Reserve. However, legal challenges from smaller states over procurement waivers slow rollout, revealing institutional strain.

White-collar job losses accelerate, especially in law, accountancy, and software, as firms replace entry-level roles with AI. Cross-border strikes grow, and unions reject emergency retraining hubs as inadequate. In response, the Commission launches the Emergency Social Stability Compact—€8bn redirected to wage insurance and expanded transition centres in Milan, Warsaw, and Marseille. Co-governed by unions and regional authorities, the initiative aims to restore political legitimacy. Early public reaction is cautious, but not hostile.

The Legibility Standard’s final draft is adopted, mandating human-readable reasoning and third-party auditability for EU-controlled AI. Enforcement, however, is limited to EU-funded systems, leaving wide gaps. The US maintains its tier-two access policy, restricting frontier model sharing and reinforcing European dependency.

### Fractures and Fragile Gains

Despite multiple closures, sovereignty declines further as global AI capability advances and no domestic compute comes online. Political capital remains frozen at 10.0—too low to gain traction, too high to collapse outright. The Resilience Surge yields early defensive gains, lifting resilience slightly. Public sentiment inches upward, as the Stability Compact signals responsiveness, but widespread anxiety persists.
