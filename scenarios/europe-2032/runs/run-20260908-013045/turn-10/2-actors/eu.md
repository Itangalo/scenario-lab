## In practice  
This turn, we act on the legal vacuum left by the stalled ECJ ruling and the failure of voluntary renegotiation. We draft a Council Regulation under Article 222 TFEU — mutual defence in matters of essential supply — redefined to include cognitive infrastructure. The Emergency Sovereignty Compact will tie continued access to EU cohesion and recovery funds to three deliverables: Spain must grant grid priority to the Andalusian compute cluster by Q3 2031; Finland must accept civilian governance of the Arctic site with Commission oversight; Romania must begin parallel grid upgrades using pre-financed bonds, with delivery tied to disbursement. Non-compliance triggers automatic suspension of infrastructure funding and reallocation of energy quotas.  

We coordinate with the ECOFIN Council formation, using the NextGenerationEU enforcement mechanism as precedent. Legal services confirm that Article 222 allows emergency action when a member state’s inaction jeopardises collective security — and with the US denying frontier AI access for healthcare and research, and open-weight models now enabling cyber-operations at 50.0 capability, the threshold is met. We present the draft to COREPER II this month, backed by France, Germany, Italy, and the Benelux states. The Anti-Coercion Instrument is invoked not against third states but as a domestic deterrent: non-compliant members will face calibrated economic consequences.  

Simultaneously, we launch a public campaign framing sovereign compute as a civil defence imperative — not a tech project, but a shield against foreign-controlled diagnostics, AI-driven disinformation, and automated attacks on energy grids. Messaging targets regional leaders in resisting states, offering co-leadership roles in the governance board and guaranteed local employment. The measure costs political capital we do not have, but the alternative is irreversible strategic erosion: a Union that cannot run its own systems cannot make its own decisions. This is not a choice. It is a last stand.
