### The Weight of Unity

The EU’s AI Safety Assurance Infrastructure formally went live, marking the completion of a long-planned sovereign monitoring backbone. Technical components—rollback protocols, intrusion detection agents, and secure clusters in Luxembourg and Helsinki—are operational. But their authority remains suspended. Without a unified command, the system sits in standby, its sensors collecting data but unable to trigger interventions.

The new **Negotiated Integration of EU Crisis Intervention Authority** measure launched, aiming to bind HERA, ENISA, and EAAD under a single crisis command. A Digital Crisis Coordination Council convened, with member states demanding concessions. Germany insists on a national override; Austria demands judicial review; France negotiates early access to bio-signatures. These opt-in safeguards delay centralisation, turning integration into a bargaining process rather than a rollout.

Public sentiment remains frozen at 12.0. Protests in Berlin and Marseille grow louder, now targeting the perceived opacity of the new command structure. Civil society groups warn of a “digital Leviathan” emerging under emergency powers. The human futures movement files a motion in the European Parliament to suspend all real-time intervention protocols until a democratic mandate is secured.

Resilience holds at 38.0. Though the health infrastructure upgrade is technically ready, fragmented legal approvals prevent full deployment. The decentralised bio-defence network—run by university labs and civic hackers—detects a false alarm faster than the official system, amplifying criticism of EU sluggishness.

AI capability climbs to 73.0, driven by unchecked progress in closed labs. Openweight models reach 61.5, nearing autonomous cyber operations. With no safety breakthrough and no finished measure improving ai_safety, the metric stagnates at 11.0.

Sovereignty drops to 10.0: capability rose by 2.0, costing −1, with no offsetting gains. Political capital rises slightly to 4.0—finishing the safety infrastructure grants +3, but the portfolio charge of −10 (three large measures, one priority) dominates. The proposal bonus for the new large measure is +4, justified by the recent bio and anomaly shocks. Without it, capital would have collapsed further.

### A Fragile Foundation

The EU is building authority, not technology. But legitimacy is not guaranteed—only negotiated. And the clock is ticking.
