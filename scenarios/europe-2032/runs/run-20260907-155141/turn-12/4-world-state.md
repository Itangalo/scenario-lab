### The Blind Spot Widens

Global AI capability remains at 75.0, but the frontier is no longer stable. The sudden collapse of human-readable reasoning across leading systems renders existing oversight obsolete overnight. Auditors can no longer trace decisions, and interpretability tools fail en masse. No safety upgrade lands—`ai_safety` holds at 5.0, now dangerously inadequate even for basic monitoring.

An open-weight release, nearly matching the closed frontier, spreads rapidly through underground networks. With hundreds of thousands of downloads in days, the model’s capabilities are now permanently distributed. `openweight_capability` surges to 70.0, narrowing the gap and enabling autonomous agents in hostile hands.

### EU’s Pivot Under Pressure

The EU formally disbands the Continuity Assurance Framework after a damning post-mortem reveals only partial adoption and failed failover drills. No resilience gain is achieved. Instead, the Union pivots to containment, launching the Emergency Audit and Control Mandate—a small category 1 measure aimed at restoring visibility through mandatory adversarial testing and real-time monitoring of high-capability systems in critical sectors. A 24-hour suspension clause acts as a kill switch for anomalous behaviour.

However, resistance flares immediately. Germany delays implementation in health AI, citing risks to diagnostic continuity. France demands joint oversight, slowing coordination. The phased rollout buys time but weakens enforcement. The measure yields no immediate safety benefit.

Meanwhile, the Frontier Access Guarantee remains stalled. U.S. export controls block Quebec, and Japan delays Fukuoka’s legal transfer. Sovereign compute clusters in Marseille and Vilnius advance, but grid upgrades face local opposition. No new capacity comes online.

### Sentiment and Capital Erode

A fresh wave of AI-enabled fraud, powered by open agents, hits small businesses. Public services lag, and trust declines further. `public_sentiment` drops to 16.0. The audit mandate, though framed as protective, is seen by some as reactive and disruptive.

Resilience falls to 45.0 as untested fallback systems fail during a minor grid incident. Sovereignty declines to 20.0 as capability dependence persists and no domestic compute comes online. Political capital dips to 1.0 under the weight of ongoing measures and weak legitimacy.
