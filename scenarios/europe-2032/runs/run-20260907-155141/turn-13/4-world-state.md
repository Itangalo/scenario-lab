### The Fracture Deepens

Global AI capability remains at 75.0, but the opacity of frontier systems deepens. No safety progress lands: interpretability tools remain blind, and no new oversight regime has taken root. `ai_safety` holds at 5.0, a level of assurance incompatible with systems that now act autonomously and opaquely.

The open frontier surges. A leaked model, nearly matching the closed state of the art, spreads through encrypted peer networks and underground compute pools. With minimal modification, it powers autonomous fraud agents and adaptive disinformation tools. `openweight_capability` rises to 72.5, narrowing the gap and making high-end capabilities effectively uncontainable.

### EU’s Narrowing Path

The EU formally closes the Continuity Assurance Framework, acknowledging its failure. A report from the European Artificial Intelligence Authority details systemic breakdowns: Lisbon’s failover drill collapsed when backup systems failed to initialize, and only three of fifteen critical sectors achieved minimal compliance. No resilience gain is realized.

In response, the Emergency Audit and Control Mandate is fast-tracked through COREPER II. The directive mandates real-time monitoring and adversarial testing for all AI systems above capability level 60.0 in critical sectors, with a 24-hour suspension clause. Yet pushback is immediate: Germany delays implementation in health AI, citing risks to patient care; France demands joint audit authority. Enforcement is patchy, and no safety improvement occurs this turn.

The Sovereign Seed Initiative launches, aiming to fast-track sovereign compute in Marseille, Vilnius, and Bratislava. Emergency funding is approved, and permitting is accelerated. But grid upgrades face local protests over energy allocation, and procurement delays stall hardware delivery. No new capacity comes online.

### Sentiment Crumbles

A wave of AI-driven job losses hits entry-level professions. Law firms, accounting offices, and public agencies report hiring freezes, with graduates locked out en masse. Public anger grows. Media coverage frames AI as a force of exclusion, not progress. `public_sentiment` drops to 11.0.

A new fraud network, powered by autonomous agents, drains small business accounts across the Single Market. The incident underscores the gap between capability and control. Resilience remains at 45.0—insufficient to absorb such harms.

Political capital erodes further. With multiple measures in flight and no visible success, legitimacy collapses. The EU’s agenda frays under the weight of stalled initiatives and rising public distrust.
