### The Plateau That Shakes the World

The AI frontier remains frozen at 69.0, but stillness brings no safety. Evidence mounts that top-tier capability has plateaued — prices for access fall, and a flagship release underperforms its own roadmap — yet this stagnation only sharpens the race to distribute what already exists. A major open-weight model, released within months of the closed frontier, spreads globally in days. Overnight, systems capable of sustained agency and narrow superhuman performance in cyber and research tasks are on uncontrolled hardware. `openweight_capability` rises to 58.0 — approaching but not surpassing the frontier — as diffusion accelerates without breakthrough.

Meanwhile, U.S. export controls tighten further under the ongoing consolidation posture, effectively rationing allied access. Then, without warning, the EU is locked out of the leading AI model. Hospitals lose diagnostic support, agencies lose forecasting tools, and firms built on integration face collapse. The denial lands not as a surprise, but as confirmation: Europe’s dependence is now a weapon turned inward.

### Emergency Measures, Fractured Unity

In response, the EU invokes Article 122 TFEU and launches the Emergency Compute Mobilisation Directive, overriding delays in Sweden and Finland to power up InvestAI Gigafactories. It also compels underused private data centres to lease spare capacity. But resistance flares: Helsinki fears provoking Moscow; Stockholm cites energy sovereignty. The directive is legally assertive, but implementation drags through June. Grid allocations shift and permits accelerate, yet full operational launch of the Sovereign AI Core Programme is delayed — construction continues, but no completion this turn.

The Resilience Reinforcement Initiative, initiated earlier, completes on paper, mandating AI-assisted patching and cross-border failover protocols. However, with only pilot deployments live, its impact is limited. `resilience` rises slightly as planning solidifies, but not enough to absorb a major incident.

The Social Licence Framework is abandoned — politically unsustainable amid strikes and German resistance. This retreat from inclusion costs legitimacy. Public sentiment dips further, as media frame the emergency measures as technocratic overreach without democratic grounding.

### Erosion Without Collapse

`ai_safety` falls sharply — the gap between high capability and weak oversight widens, and no safety measure lands. `eu_ai_sovereignty` inches up from nascent mobilisation, but not enough to offset structural decay. Political capital erodes further under portfolio strain and failed cohesion, even as the Union attempts decisive action.

Europe acts — but with one hand tied behind its back.
