## New measure
**Measure:** Launch the Civic Pact for AI Sovereignty  
A binding framework co-developed with member states, civil society, and labour unions to re-establish public legitimacy for EU AI governance, embedding consent into the architecture of sovereign systems through participatory oversight, shared benefits, and enforceable red lines on misuse.

## In practice
This turn, we are acting from the ruins of emergency authority. The Emergency Compute Mobilisation Directive has delivered partial operational capacity: the InvestAI Gigafactories in Finland are now grid-connected, and private data centres have released stranded compute under compulsory leasing. But the cost has been catastrophic. Sweden formally challenged Article 122 invocation before the CJEU; Helsinki warns of energy instability amid Russian hybrid pressure; German Länder refuse integration with SACP outputs. Public-sector strikes have spread to transport and utilities, framing AI sovereignty as elite rule by algorithm. We have hardware — but no mandate.

So we pivot. Not away from sovereignty, but toward its soul. The Civic Pact for AI Sovereignty begins in Strasbourg, convened by the Commission with the European Parliament’s Conference on the Future of Europe as co-host. It launches three tracks: a Citizens’ Assembly on AI Powers, mandated to define red lines on state and corporate use; a Labour Accord on Transition Guarantees, tying access to sovereign AI tools to wage insurance and retraining rights; and a Federal Pact with Member States, giving binding veto power over any SACP deployment that bypasses national oversight. Funding comes from repurposing €20bn unspent from the Digital Europe Programme and redirecting 30% of the Recovery and Resilience Facility’s digital tranche.

This is not consultation — it is codetermination. We are offering Germany a seat on the SACP Governing Board with audit rights; Sweden a moratorium on military-linked training runs; France a guaranteed share of model development for its national champions. The price of cohesion is shared control. But if we get it, we gain something no directive can force: the right to act.
