### A Hinge Turn, Half-Held

The EU enters a new era of AI control under duress. The United States elects a president who treats frontier AI as a strategic national asset, tightening export controls and rationing access by geopolitical tier — a clear signal that dependency is no longer a technical issue but a lever of power. In Europe, the biosecurity community is shaken by a contested but alarming study: a genome model has produced a viable design for a human-infective organism. While the methodology is debated, the finding crosses a threshold, raising fears that misuse is no longer theoretical.

In response, the EU designates the Sovereign Model Assurance Framework (SMAF) as its top priority, initiating a formal shift in focus. However, this reprioritization occurs without retiring any existing measure, triggering friction across the portfolio. The European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) formally becomes operational this turn, with emergency hiring and fast-tracked clearances beginning, though recruitment bottlenecks persist and full staffing remains distant. The first air-gapped sovereign cluster is slated for joint French-German hosting, with dual-key access protocols now in development. Construction also begins on the first InvestAI Gigafactory in Sweden, enabled by Article 122 overrides, though physical progress remains in early stages.

The Critical Infrastructure Resilience Surge (CIRS) concludes, but with mixed results: only 40% of targeted systems completed upgrades, and patching delays remain a critical vulnerability. The programme transitions to ENISA as a standing monitoring function. The AI Containment Protocol is formally proposed this turn, mandating air-gapped operation and human-in-the-loop escalation for high-capability sovereign systems, but implementation will take at least one full turn to roll out across relevant sites.

Public sentiment holds at 25.0, resistant to messaging efforts. Fiscal strain deepens as six major measures remain in flight, draining political capital. The US election outcome and biosecurity scare justify the pivot to control — but implementation lags ambition. The open-weight ecosystem sees modest gains from incremental optimization and limited diffusion of prior models, but no breakthrough deployments or new open releases materialize at scale.
