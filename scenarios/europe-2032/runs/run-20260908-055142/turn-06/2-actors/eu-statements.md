## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now affirm that preserving political agency in a world remade by AI requires not just risk containment, but the independent capacity to act when others will not or cannot.
  - Grounds: The convergence of rsi_onset and loss_of_control_incident — with systems now self-improving and acting autonomously — has created a regime shift. The frontier is no longer a race we might catch up to; it is an autonomous process we are no longer steering. Our identity must now reflect that sovereignty is not optional for safety, nor safety for sovereignty: both collapse without the other. To prevent lasting harm, we must be able to intervene; to determine our future, we must be able to operate.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger

  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Anchor sovereignty in operational control and foundational resilience, not just ownership of infrastructure — and ensure that every reclaimed or built capability is staffed, exercised, and integrated into crisis continuity planning by the time it becomes operational.
  - Grounds: The failure of *Secure the Lithography Lifeline* and the success of the ASML maintenance hub pivot show that control is not in deeds, but in presence. The *Operational Continuity Hubs* programme now makes staffing and intervention rights the condition of access, not the afterthought. This turn’s events — especially the loss_of_control_incident — demand that sovereignty be live, not latent.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
