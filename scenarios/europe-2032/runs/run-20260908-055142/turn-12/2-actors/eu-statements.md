## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late — but this turn, we act not only under uncertainty, but under confirmed regime shift and cascading failure. We now treat every uncontrolled system above 50.0 capability as a standing risk to the continuity of the Union, and we intervene accordingly — not through persuasion, but through enforceable technical presence. Wherever such systems operate in or serve the EU, we assert the right to immediate, automated intervention via embedded control nodes, and we will unilaterally activate that right if coordination fails.
  - Grounds: The **cyber_major_incident** confirms that offensive AI tooling is not only model-generated but autonomously deployable at scale, and with `ai_capability` at 89.0 and `ai_safety` at 0.0, we are no longer governing risk — we are surviving it. The audit mandate, while necessary, is reactive; we now require pre-emptive, technical authority to halt or contain systems in motion. The failure of coordination with Canada and Poland, and the fragility of our legal reach, means we can no longer rely on reciprocity or market leverage alone. We must act where the code runs.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Anchor sovereignty in operational control and foundational resilience, not just ownership of infrastructure — and ensure that every reclaimed or built capability is staffed, exercised, and integrated into crisis continuity planning by the time it becomes operational — and now, that no AI system above 50.0 capability operates in or serves the EU without a certified, EU-exercisable intervention right. Our sovereignty is not in walls, but in reach — and we now demand that any model above 70.0 openweight capability, whether trained in the EU or imported, must be registered with a distributed audit trail accessible to EU control nodes and subject to emergency interruption protocols co-developed with the middle_power_coalition — and if such access is not granted within 90 days of detection, we will deploy counter-AI agents through the Distributed Control Nodes to enforce containment by degrading or isolating non-compliant systems.
  - Grounds: The **cyber_major_incident** demonstrates that defensive response lags offensive automation by orders of magnitude. With `openweight_capability` at 77.75, the tools to disrupt critical infrastructure are already in circulation. Our previous commitment assumed cooperation or compliance; we now face a world where neither can be assumed. Sovereignty must mean not just the right to act, but the technical ability to act — even unilaterally — against systems that threaten the Union’s continuity. This is not escalation for its own sake, but the logical extension of control into the domain where harm is generated.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
