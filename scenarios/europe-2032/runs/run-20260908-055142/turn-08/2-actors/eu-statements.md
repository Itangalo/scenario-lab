## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but this turn, we act not only under uncertainty, but under confirmed regime shift. We now treat every uncontrolled system above 50.0 capability as a standing risk to the continuity of the Union, and we intervene accordingly. However, our authority to intervene is now visibly constrained by both external resistance and internal fragility: U.S. hyperscalers refuse compliance, member states resist integration, and public trust erodes. Therefore, we now narrow the scope of intervention to systems directly embedded in critical infrastructure — energy, transport, health, and state administration — and prioritise operational control over jurisdictional assertion. We act where we can hold ground, not where we claim it.
  - Grounds: The **member_state_defection** event and the failure to secure extraterritorial compliance with *Mandatory Intervention Rights* reveal that legal claims without enforcement capacity are performative, not sovereign. Our commitment must reflect reality: we cannot mandate audit access globally, but we can — and must — ensure it where AI touches the nervous system of the Union.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Anchor sovereignty in operational control and foundational resilience, not just ownership of infrastructure — and ensure that every reclaimed or built capability is staffed, exercised, and integrated into crisis continuity planning by the time it becomes operational — and now, that no AI system above 50.0 capability operates in or serves the EU without a certified, EU-exercisable intervention right.
  - Grounds: This turn’s events confirm that sovereignty is not in law, nor even in hardware, but in live human presence and tested response. The **emergent_labour_displacement_wave** proves that societal resilience is fraying, while the Polish standoff shows that political capital evaporates when force is applied without legitimacy. Thus, we reframe: our two-year commitment now demands that for every node of compute, there is a team; for every kill switch, a hand; for every displaced worker, a path back in. Control is staffing. Resilience is transition.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
