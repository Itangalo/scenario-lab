# Statements: The European Union (turn 10)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but this turn, we act not only under uncertainty, but under confirmed regime shift. We now treat every uncontrolled system above 50.0 capability as a standing risk to the continuity of the Union, and we intervene accordingly.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Anchor sovereignty in operational control and foundational resilience, not just ownership of infrastructure — and ensure that every reclaimed or built capability is staffed, exercised, and integrated into crisis continuity planning by the time it becomes operational — and now, that no AI system above 50.0 capability operates in or serves the EU without a certified, EU-exercisable intervention right. We now extend this to the open-weight frontier: if capability is distributed, so must control be. Our sovereignty is not in walls, but in reach
