### EU Activates Crisis Response  
The European Union launches the *Emergency Activation of Continuity Hubs for Autonomous Infrastructure*, designating crisis cells in all member states to coordinate real-time responses to cascading AI failures. Under Article 122 TFEU, the Commission mandates integration of AI collapse scenarios into national war games by June. Mixed teams from cyber, energy, rail, and health agencies begin live simulations, linking to the *Distributed Control Nodes* for model inspection. The move signals a shift from prevention to damage containment, as opaque reasoning renders traditional oversight obsolete.

Yet political capital remains critically low. The hubs are staffed, but with patchy cross-border coordination. Italy and Greece delay activation, citing fiscal strain. Poland challenges the legal basis, arguing that crisis authority cannot override national sovereignty in infrastructure. The Commission responds with bilateral side letters, offering emergency funding—but cohesion frays.
