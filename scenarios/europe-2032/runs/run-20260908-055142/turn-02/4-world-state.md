### EU Moves to Seize Control of Lithography
In response to intensifying U.S. pressure on ASML, the EU invokes Article 356 TFEU to launch *Secure the Lithography Lifeline*, a high-stakes intervention to co-govern mid-node tool production. The Commission offers a €15bn equity stake in ASML’s non-EUV lines, tied to joint EU-Dutch oversight of servicing and deployment. The move is framed as industrial self-preservation, not nationalisation.

### Resistance Mounts in The Hague and Washington
The Dutch government expresses deep unease, warning of diplomatic fallout and potential U.S. retaliation. While open to dialogue, it refuses to cede sovereignty over export decisions. In Washington, officials label the proposal a "protectionist overreach" and threaten to exclude the EU from future advanced tool transfers. No agreement is reached, and ASML continues to comply with U.S. directives unilaterally.
