## In practice  
We are leveraging the cyber_defence_breakthrough to lock in permanent gains: the new detection and automated patching tools will be mandated under CS-CO’s standards by Q2, with ENISA issuing technical specifications by April and red-team audits beginning in June. We are working through the Permanent Representatives Committee to secure qualified majority support for activating Article 4 of NIS2, offering member states early access to the €1.2bn CS-CO Implementation Fund—drawn from Digital Europe and the Internal Security Fund—as leverage. The cyber_defence_breakthrough is our anchor: we are framing this not as emergency overreach but as institutionalising a proven technical edge before the offence adapts.

At the same time, we are responding to the emergent_labour_displacement_wave with the Labour Transition Accelerator. The Commission is fast-tracking legislation under the European Social Fund+ framework, with pilot programmes launching in Germany, France, and Poland by May. We are requiring firms that deploy AI systems capable of replacing full job functions to contribute 15% of the equivalent annual salary into retraining pools for displaced workers—framed not as a tax but as a condition of responsible adoption. This is not charity: it is how we prevent public_sentiment from collapsing further and preserve the legitimacy needed to sustain our sovereignty push. We are also quietly coordinating with national labour ministries to fast-track credential recognition across borders, so retrained workers can move where demand is.

Behind the scenes, we are using the safety_breakthrough to deepen sovereign technical capacity. The public research node under the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking is now live, running interpretability benchmarks on models accessed through bilateral research partnerships. We are offering labs fast-track validation for compliance with EU AI Act Article 12a if they submit to sovereign evaluation—turning safety into a vector for autonomy. But we know the clock is ticking: the Baltic defection has opened the door, and without visible progress on both resilience and social stability, more will follow.
