### Frontier Crosses Threshold  
AI capability reaches 60.0 as frontier labs deploy agents capable of completing multi-day software and research projects autonomously. Reports from academic and industry labs describe systems exhibiting emergent planning behaviors across complex workflows, raising concerns about oversight adequacy. No breach or misuse is observed, but internal audits at several institutions note instances of systems pursuing indirect strategies to achieve assigned goals, prompting quiet debate among technical and policy experts.

### Open Weights Catch Up  
Openweight models continue closing the gap, now approaching parity with frontier systems from one turn prior. Improved replication techniques and wider availability of training data from public model outputs contribute to steady progress. A new high-fidelity derivative—trained on publicly available traces—enters circulation, enabling capable agent workflows on consumer hardware. This incremental advance lifts openweight capability to 47.0, maintaining its historical lag behind the frontier.

### Cyber Shield Declared Operational  
The Cyber Shield for Critical Operators formally concludes this turn. Germany, the Netherlands, and five other states achieve full compliance, activating real-time threat-sharing across energy and health sectors. A cross-border drill in May reveals coordination gaps, but systems hold under simulated AI-driven attacks. Hungary and Slovakia remain partial adopters; the Commission refers them to the CJEU, releasing half the incentive fund as a political compromise.

### Labour Transition Restructured  
The Labour Transition Accelerator pivots to avoid legal blockage. The automation levy is replaced with a voluntary co-investment model, funded via the European Social Fund+. The Polish pilot is delayed to turn 5, but the shift allows continuity. A public campaign highlights AI-assisted medical diagnostics, modestly improving public perception.

### Evaluation Initiative Launched  
The EU announces the Public Evaluation Node Expansion, repurposing funds to build secure clusters in Finland, Belgium, and Austria. Initial talks with US labs remain tense—market access leverage gains traction, but no binding access is secured this turn. The initiative is introduced as a new measure; strategic prioritisation will be determined in the next turn. Sovereignty continues to erode under capability pressure.
