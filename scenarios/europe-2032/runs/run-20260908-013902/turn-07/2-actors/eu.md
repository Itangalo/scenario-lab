## In practice
We are concluding three major measures this turn: InvestAI Gigafactories, Public Evaluation Node Expansion, and the Frontier Access Enforcement Directive. The Gigafactories programme has secured site commitments in Finland, Poland, and southern France, with construction beginning on two. However, grid integration remains behind schedule, and no EU-controlled frontier compute is yet operational. We are now transferring the programme’s implementation to the European Chips Infrastructure Consortium, with a mandate to deliver first-phase capacity by turn 10. The Public Evaluation Node Expansion, while technically complete, has failed to achieve its purpose: no US lab has granted access, and the European AI Office lacks enforcement teeth. The Frontier Access Enforcement Directive, though formally adopted, has not compelled compliance — two major developers have suspended EU operations rather than submit to audits. This is a defeat for evaluation, not for principle: we will not retreat, but we must now act where we still can.

The cyber_major_incident — a ransomware sweep across hospital networks in three member states, coordinated through an AI-generated exploit chain — has crystallised the new reality. Defenders are reactive, attribution is slow, and the tools are already in circulation. Combined with the election annulment and backlash_physical — including an arson attempt on a data centre in Bavaria — the Union is in crisis mode. We cannot wait for evaluation access that will not come, nor for sovereignty that remains aspirational. We are therefore launching the Sovereign Resilience Emergency Activation under Article 12 of NIS2 and the newly invoked Crisis Coordination Protocol. Germany, the Nordics, and the Benelux countries have agreed to immediate joint threat monitoring. For Hungary and Slovakia, we are offering full cost coverage and technical support from the EU Cyber Rapid Response Team in exchange for binding adoption by turn 8. ENISA will issue quarterly AI Threat Postures, and all essential services must conduct stress tests by turn 9 or face temporary federalisation of oversight. This is not the future we wanted — it is the one we are in.
