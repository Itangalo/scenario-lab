### Crisis Response and Cascading Strain  
The EU launches the **Critical Infrastructure Resilience Sprint**, a large category 6 measure, in direct response to a widespread ransomware attack exploiting a poisoned software dependency. The assault, driven by AI-generated tooling, disrupted hospital scheduling in three member states and triggered emergency protocols in two national grid operators. ENISA deploys rapid-response teams, and mandates for binary transparency and air-gapped fallbacks are issued. However, implementation is uneven: only seven member states meet the first compliance deadline, and procurement for non-transformer intrusion detection systems faces export bottlenecks from third countries. Still, early detection improves, limiting the blast radius of follow-up probes.

Meanwhile, the **Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive** remains in negotiation. A compromise with US cloud providers—offering partial compliance via data residency and audit rights—fails to materialize after legal teams reject the framework as unenforceable. Five member states condition support on access to InvestAI loan guarantees, politicizing the process. No sovereignty gain is realized this turn.

### Frontier Fractures  
The **ai_investment_collapse** triggers a global retrenchment in AI infrastructure. Several planned data centres in Europe are cancelled, undermining private commitments under the Tech Sovereignty Package. Frontier labs downsize training runs, but **ai_capability** still climbs to 55.5 due to algorithmic efficiency gains, though at a reduced rate. The **openweight_frontier_release** shocks the landscape: a Kimi K4 derivative is published within weeks of its closed counterpart, featuring autonomous exploit chaining. The release enables rapid iteration in underground AI communities, and **openweight_capability** rises to 44.0 as adaptation and fine-tuning spread its capabilities. This marks a significant leap in accessible power, but not one that closes the full gap to the frontier.

With no safety improvements on deployed systems and opaque AI coordination behind the attack, **ai_safety** drops sharply to 27.0. The **cyber_major_incident** and collapsing public trust drag **public_sentiment** down to 37.0. Labour unrest grows in tech-adjacent sectors, amplifying fears of dependency and displacement.
