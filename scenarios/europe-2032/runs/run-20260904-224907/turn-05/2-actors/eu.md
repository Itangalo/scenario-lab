## New measure
**EU Agent Access Compact**  
Establish a legally binding agreement granting EU-certified public entities direct, audited access to frontier models above capability 55.0, hosted on sovereign-controlled infrastructure within the EU, with no right to withdraw once granted.

## Priority
**EU Agent Access Compact** — because the failure of compute ownership mandates has left us with no alternative path to sovereign agency at the frontier, and because this turn’s US election outcome makes reliance on transatlantic goodwill both tempting and dangerous: we must lock in access now, on our own terms, before external alignment becomes a substitute for autonomy.

## In practice
We are finalising the legal architecture of the EU Agent Access Compact under Article 68 of the AI Act and Article 15 of the DMAct, treating gatekeeper status and high-risk deployment as leverage points for reciprocal access. The offer remains: fast-track certification, reduced audit frequency, and privileged participation in EU safety standard-setting in exchange for sovereign-hosted, always-on instances of frontier models—fully updated, no right to withdraw. We are targeting OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, and DeepSeek as initial signatories, with the European AI Access Authority (seeded from the now-concluded European AI Safety Observatory) set to govern access and monitor compliance. Hosting will be co-located in InvestAI Gigafactory zones, under EU jurisdiction, with physical and logical isolation enforced by ENISA.

The Critical Infrastructure AI Hardening Initiative and the European AI Safety Observatory both conclude this turn. On hardening: we are converting the draft standards into delegated acts under the NIS2 framework, mandating red-teaming and failover automation for all essential service operators by Q2 2029. On safety: the pilot phase has validated the interpretability pipeline, and we are transitioning its core team into a standing technical unit within the Commission, with authority to conduct real-time monitoring of high-risk agent deployments. The Tech Sovereignty Package continues to accelerate permitting in seven member states, and the InvestAI fund has confirmed site agreements in Finland, Spain, and Poland. We are not stopping the build—we are redefining what it means to own the future.
