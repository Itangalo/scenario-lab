### The Atlantic Thaw

The U.S. presidential election concludes with a decisive victory for the candidate advocating transatlantic technological alignment. Early signals suggest a new era of structured cooperation: joint evaluation protocols, shared incident reporting, and eased access to frontier models for allied institutions. In Brussels, relief mingles with unease. The immediate pressure on EU access eases — but so does the urgency to build independent capacity.

### Sovereignty Through Access?

The EU finalises the legal framework for the **EU Agent Access Compact**, a bold attempt to secure sovereign access to frontier AI without owning the compute. Leveraging the Digital Markets Act and AI Act, the Commission offers fast-track certification and reduced oversight in exchange for permanently hosted, audited model instances. Talks intensify with OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, and DeepSeek. While no final agreement is reached, the offer is taken seriously — exclusion from the EU market remains costly. The measure is formally proposed as a large category 4 initiative, to be prioritised starting next turn. No implementation effects occur yet; the framework enters the pipeline and will require at least two full turns before full effect.

### Safety Stalls, Resilience Advances

The European AI Safety Observatory completes its pilot, validating interpretability tools that can flag goal drift in high-risk agents. However, no systemic safeguards are yet embedded in live systems. With capability advancing sharply, **ai_safety** dips to 33.0. Meanwhile, the **Critical Infrastructure AI Hardening Initiative** finishes successfully, converting draft standards into binding delegated acts under NIS2. Mandatory red-teaming and failover automation will roll out by mid-2029, lifting **resilience** to 40.0. The measure is now closed and removed from the active portfolio.

### Political Capital Holds — For Now

Four measures remain in flight: InvestAI Gigafactories (−3), Tech sovereignty package (−3), European AI Safety Observatory (−3), and the newly proposed EU Agent Access Compact (−3). The priority charge (−1) does not apply until next turn, when the Compact is formally prioritised. A +3 bonus is earned for completing the Critical Infrastructure AI Hardening Initiative. With no proposal bonus (no major negative event in the last three turns that the new measure directly answers), **eu_political_capital** remains flat at 19.0.

### Public Mood Shifts Slightly

Visible progress on infrastructure and safety, coupled with the U.S. election outcome, nudges **public_sentiment** to 44.0. The mood remains ambivalent, but the sense of drift recedes.
