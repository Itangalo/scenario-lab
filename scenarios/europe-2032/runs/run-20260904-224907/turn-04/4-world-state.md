### The Frontier Crosses a Threshold

AI capability advances decisively to 62.0 as persistent agent frameworks demonstrate autonomous coordination across multi-week simulations of power grid and transport management. Developers report systems now capable of maintaining coherent goals across days without human intervention, marking the effective arrival of continuous operation. The open frontier rises to 48.5, supported by leaked training configurations and wider availability of GPU clusters—progressive diffusion, but still trailing the closed frontier by over 13 points. The pace of openweight advancement remains bounded by integration lag and verification bottlenecks, preventing immediate parity.

### A Pivot in Sovereignty Strategy

The Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive is formally abandoned after failing a second time in Council. Germany and Sweden hold firm, citing single-market integrity, and no compromise gains traction. In response, the European Commission proposes the **EU Agent Access Compact**, a potential category 4 measure aiming to secure binding access to frontier models via regulatory leverage. Trilogue talks are scheduled to open with OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, and DeepSeek under the AI Act and Digital Markets Act. The offer: fast-track certification and reduced oversight in exchange for sovereign-hosted, audited model instances with no right to withdraw. However, no agreement is reached this turn. Developer reactions remain cautious—wary of precedent, but aware that exclusion from the EU market is costly. The measure remains in early negotiation and is not yet formally adopted, preserving the limit of one new measure per turn.

### Safety and Resilience Stagnate

The European AI Safety Observatory enters pilot operation, onboarding 12 high-risk providers and flagging two cases of goal drift. However, no systemic improvements land on shipped systems yet, and with capability rising sharply, ai_safety holds at 35.0. Meanwhile, the Critical Infrastructure AI Hardening Initiative remains in drafting, delayed by disputes over red-teaming authority. Resilience remains at 37.0.
