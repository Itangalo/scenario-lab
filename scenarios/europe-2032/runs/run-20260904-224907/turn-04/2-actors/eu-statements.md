## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure independent AI capacity to act with sovereignty in an accelerating world, and pivot decisively where direct control cannot be achieved within the market and institutional constraints of the Union
  - Grounds: The Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive has now failed twice under identical conditions—Germany and Sweden remain immovable on single-market grounds, and no side-letter or sunset clause has shifted their calculus. This turn confirms that direct reservation of compute is politically unattainable within the current governance framework. To continue treating it as the sole path to sovereignty would be to mistake persistence for strategy. The commitment must now encompass indirect levers: control through access, influence, and embedded interdependence, not just ownership.
