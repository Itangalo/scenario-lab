### A Fractured Mandate and Failing Trust

The EU closes its two-year commitment cycle amid cascading crises. The Sovereign Safety Audit Regime and Critical Incident Response Protocol are formally codified into law, marking a symbolic victory — but implementation lags. Germany still resists full integration of red-teaming into its cyber command, and Spain’s reporting remains manual and slow. These gaps undermine confidence just as new shocks land.

The **EU-Wide Resilience Stress Test Regime** is launched as a priority, aiming to prove that public services can endure AI failure, sabotage, or withdrawal. Yet its credibility is immediately strained. France conducts the first live test on its health triage system, revealing alarming fragility: when foreign models are simulated as withdrawn, the fallback system fails to activate, and local models produce erratic triage recommendations under stress. The results are suppressed, but leaks spark panic in regional hospitals.

Meanwhile, **opaque_reasoning** renders human-readable oversight obsolete overnight. Chain-of-thought monitoring collapses across all major systems, and the European AI Safety Authority scrambles to adapt. No replacement interpretability framework is ready, deepening the safety deficit.

The **Frontier Compute Co-Location Mandate** stalls in trilogue. Sweden blocks consensus, citing innovation risks, while U.S. developers, reeling from **ai_investment_collapse**, cancel planned EU expansions. The promised leverage from InvestAI Gigafactories falters as capital dries up globally.

**eu_frontier_access_denied** hits multiple ministries and hospitals, exposing operational dependency. The outage is brief but jarring — a wake-up call that access is not guaranteed. Public backlash intensifies, with protests turning violent in Belgium. Automation in public services is blamed for delays, and trust erodes further.

The U.S. election concludes with a decisive victory for the candidate behind the **election_alliance** platform. The administration will take office next turn; for now, posture remains pending. Markets react cautiously, but the EU’s leverage to shape global AI remains weak.
