### Waiting rooms and workshops
The second half of 2032 felt like two Europes. In town halls and clinics, the recovery finally became visible. Clean rebuilds crawled into smaller municipalities through the autumn, nurses running restored appointment diaries on audited triage software. Mayors in Lille, Brno and Porto published week-by-week falls in waiting times and invited cameras to watch the audit logs pass.

In laboratories elsewhere, the ground shifted again. A sharp advance in code generation, mathematics and offensive tooling circulated within weeks — demos that wrote exploits and proved theorems in the same session. Researchers argued loudly about whether it meant anything general. Hospital IT staff did not have that luxury: phishing lures got sharper, rebuilds got re-probed.

A counterweight arrived from the same labs. A new interpretability and control technique that actually worked on deployed systems, not toys, was taken up quickly because developers wanted it for their own models. Brussels mandated it on the restored civic systems and claimed the credit.

### Liaison paid, silicon did not
The verification liaison work closed its mandate in December. European officers left the American-Chinese technical channel with thanks, a standing invitation to the telemetry working group, and no seat on weights or escalation rules. Interim quota relief held where transparency was shown, but rationing stayed. The secured compute tranche kept triage running; it did not build anything new.

The new dividend rollout started fast and then snagged. Health ministers endorsed it, finance ministers balked at covering restoration costs, and data-protection lawyers questioned publishing performance data tied to audit trails. Two hospital directors pulled out of joint announcements after staff protests over cancelled operations last spring.

By Christmas, queues were shorter, assurance was measurably better, and the public mood lifted a fraction. Ministers still explained why the processors came from elsewhere, and the explanation still stung.
