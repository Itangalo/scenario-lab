By late 2032 Brussels remained connected but constrained: inside pooled attribution/telemetry and export-licence coordination, with a standing telemetry invitation after the Washington-Beijing verification liaison closed in December — but still without a seat on weights/escalation rules and still short on silicon, with interim quota relief tied to transparency, not lifted rationing.

The February 2031 ransomware recovery finally reached smaller towns via clean rebuilds on audited triage software; Lille, Brno and Porto showed falling waits by autumn. The secured compute tranche sustained triage but built nothing new; gigafactories stayed stalled.

A new AI advance in code generation, maths and offensive tooling sharpened phishing and re-probing of rebuilds, while a workable interpretability/control technique was quickly mandated on restored civic systems.

Civic Trust repair partly lifted: queues shorter, assurance measurably better, mood slightly improved, but dividend rollout snagged on finance refusal to cover restoration costs, data-protection objections, staff protests over cancelled operations, and continued dependence on foreign processors.
