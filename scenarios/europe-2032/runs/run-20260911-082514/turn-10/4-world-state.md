### The invitation taken
With its own chip slots still cancelled and data-centre plans still shelved, Brussels took the one offer that did not need hardware. Ministers signed accession to the pooled attribution and real-time telemetry command built by states hit by the same winter intrusions. CERT-EU linked its Shield playbooks to the joint feed, and cooperating clinics and town halls that already cut cloud links on drill stayed connected to a wider watch floor.

It worked as containment. A spring wave of update-poisoned ransomware probed municipalities again; members of the pool isolated segments in hours while outsiders stayed dark for days. Auditors again credited the shared alarms.

### The pact that landed quietly
The Chips-for-Compute and Civic Liability Pact formally closed. The liability clarification became law, and two insurers cautiously resumed cover for audited triage deployments only. The compute half did not land: The Hague, Tokyo and Seoul still demanded binding lithography controls first, quotas later. The gigafactory fence stayed up. A small secured co-location tranche was salvaged, enough to keep public triage pilots running, not enough to call sovereignty restored.

The political payoff was thin. Opposition called the pact a lawyers' deal while hardware stayed foreign.

### Trust repair stalls
The Civic Trust repair became the stated priority, but with cohesion threadbare it bought no acceleration. DG CNECT enforced the freeze on the benefits and policing support system, published logs and imposed workload limits and independent re-checks. Audited triage sites that cut waiting lists were scaled, and nurses again praised them.

Yet coverage stayed on lawful harm: everything scandalous had been permitted. Deployments beyond insurance and beyond the cooperating municipalities stayed frozen. Finance did not return, and second-tier builders abroad talked openly of catching up while American labs slowed training.

Brussels ended June protected but not empowered: inside the joint command, outside its own compute.
