The Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory officially completes, but its analytical layer remains offline; instead, raw telemetry from Sentinel Nodes is rerouted through Copenhagen’s municipal forensics hub, creating a minimal triage pipeline. EuroHPC’s compute is partially commandeered, and open-weight models at 75.0 capability are pre-validated for fallback use. Yet the gains are brittle. Legal challenges emerge from two member states refusing to cede control over national cybersecurity assets. Liability waivers for city networks face pushback from national insurers. The repurposed observatory delivers alerts but no foresight—no interpretability, no prediction, only noise filtered by exhausted researchers.
