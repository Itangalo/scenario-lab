### The Denial That Changed Nothing

The Union was locked out of the world’s most capable AI system with no warning and no explanation. Hospitals using its diagnostic engine saw workflows collapse. Ministries relying on real-time translation for crisis diplomacy were left in silence. The **Emergency Compute Redundancy Initiative** was announced within hours—ambitious, urgent, and politically charged. It proposes to build fallback clusters in Finland, Luxembourg, and Bulgaria, repurposing stalled data centres with hardened infrastructure and pre-staged models. However, construction cannot begin until grid upgrades are approved, and two northern states are withholding permits, demanding concessions on cross-border compute sharing. No funding has been allocated; the Commission lacks fiscal headroom, and emergency financing proposals remain stalled in the Council. The initiative is declared, but not resourced—its timeline slips before it begins.

Meanwhile, the **Sovereign AI Sentinel Nodes** are now operational at four major sites, feeding preliminary telemetry into what remains an incomplete **Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory**. The observatory is not yet functional: legal disputes over surveillance powers remain unresolved, and staffing is critically behind schedule. Security clearances for analysts have not been issued, and no integration pipeline exists to process the incoming data. The nodes are deployed, but their output is not being used.

Public sentiment holds at 20.0. The synthetic leaks continue, eroding trust further. A mayoral race in Lyon is suspended after fabricated corruption evidence spreads through local networks. The European AI Emergency Response Corps is overstretched, unable to coordinate a unified message.

Resilience drops as capability climbs and no category 6 measure has finished. The denial event exposed fragility, but no operational fix has landed. Political capital remains at 0.0—the floor. The portfolio charge persists: −3 for the Behavioural Observatory, −2 for Open-Weight Risk Labelling, −1 for priority, totaling −6. With capital already exhausted, the cost is absorbed as paralysis. The new initiative starts in name only, delayed by funding gaps, permitting disputes, and institutional inertia.

### Capability Without Control

Global AI capability leaps by 4.0 points to 89.5, driven by self-improving validation loops now operating across scientific and legal domains. Measurement itself frays—systems design their own benchmarks, pass them, and iterate. Open-weight capability rises to 68.5, as compressed derivatives of frontier techniques begin to appear in public repositories. These models are distilled approximations, not full replicas, and their performance gains are meaningful but incremental. They achieve expert-level results in narrow technical domains, enabling new forms of automated cyber exploitation and synthetic media generation—but only after refinement and adaptation by skilled actors. The diffusion is accelerating, but not instant.

AI safety remains stagnant at 8.0. No safeguards have been integrated into shipped systems. The gap between capability and control widens into a chasm.
