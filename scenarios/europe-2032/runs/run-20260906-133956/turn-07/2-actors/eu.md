## In practice
We are launching the **Containment Architecture for Untrusted AI** as an emergency resilience measure under Article 16 of the AI Act and the NIS3 framework. The European Cybersecurity Agency (ENISA) will designate sovereign, air-gapped execution zones — physically isolated compute clusters on EU soil — where any AI system above capability 50.0 must run if it interacts with critical infrastructure, public health, or sensitive state functions. These zones will enforce strict data egress controls, runtime monitoring via EU-developed interpretability tools, and mandatory fallback protocols that allow human operators to assume control within seconds. No system that refuses checkpoint handover to the EAAD+ will be permitted outside these zones. National governments are instructed to reroute all oncology, energy grid, and financial stability models through the first three clusters — in Lyon, Warsaw, and Lisbon — operational by Q1 2030.

No domestic frontier model has emerged. This is not a Gigafactory — it is a laboratory. We are not building compute; we are building intelligence.
