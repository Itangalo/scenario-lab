### Transatlantic Momentum Meets Legal Gridlock

The EU advances the **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**, finalising the directive and launching the first transnational resilience drill with grid operators and emergency agencies across six member states. ENISA’s standardised attack scenarios, modelled on recent ransomware intrusions, underscore the urgency. Fast-track access to InvestAI funding and liability shielding incentivise early compliance, particularly in Germany and Poland. However, the measure remains in flight, yielding only partial operational gains.

Meanwhile, the **European Evaluation Shield** faces mounting resistance. A coalition of frontier developers and foreign governments continues its legal challenge at the European Court of Justice, arguing that pre-deployment audits infringe on intellectual property. In response, the Council passes a resolution affirming the public interest in AI scrutiny, and classified briefings warn justice ministers of uncontrolled agent coordination. Pilot audits proceed under strict confidentiality, but broader enforcement stalls.

### Capability Creeps Forward, Safety Erodes

AI capability advances to **61.5**, driven by improved agent autonomy and self-improving code generation. Open-weight models reach **50.0**, aided by leaked memory-augmented architectures and wider GPU availability, increasing misuse risks. With no safety measure landing on shipped systems and capability rising sharply, **ai_safety** drops to **16.0**, reflecting growing concern over opaque reasoning and uncontrolled coordination.

**Resilience** inches up to **36.0** due to early defensive coordination, though systemic fragility persists. **Public sentiment** declines to **33.0** amid rising job displacement fears and perceptions of institutional drift. Labour unrest intensifies, with cross-border union actions demanding transition programs.

**Eu_ai_sovereignty** falls to **28.0** as capability growth outpaces domestic build-out, despite the completed SEAL initiative. **Eu_political_capital** remains strained at **27.0**, burdened by an ongoing portfolio charge and no near-term payoff.
