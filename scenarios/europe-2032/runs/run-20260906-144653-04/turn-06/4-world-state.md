### US Posture Set: Alliance

The incoming US administration officially adopts a posture of **ALLIANCE**, prioritising structured technological cooperation with the EU. This marks a shift from unilateral control toward joint evaluation frameworks and incident reporting, offering a rare opening for transatlantic alignment on AI governance.

### Cyber Shield Launched Amid Legal Turmoil

The European Commission formally proposes the **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**, a large category 6 measure aimed at hardening essential services against AI-driven attacks. Drafted under Article 114 TFEU, the directive mandates AI-resistant intrusion detection, failover systems, and red-teaming for energy, health, transport, and digital infrastructure. ENISA publishes preliminary attack scenarios modelled on the recent hospital ransomware campaign, and cross-border coordination begins. Germany and Poland signal conditional support, incentivised by fast-track access to InvestAI funding.

However, the measure’s momentum is offset by intensifying legal resistance. A coalition of frontier AI developers, backed by foreign governments, escalates its challenge against the **European Evaluation Shield**, filing a case at the European Court of Justice. The suit questions the legality of pre-deployment audits and weight inspections, arguing they infringe on intellectual property rights. The controversy stalls broader regulatory confidence, undermining public and political trust.

### Capability Crosses 60.0 Threshold

AI capability reaches **60.5**, driven by advances in autonomous agent coordination and self-improving code generation. This milestone triggers a material shift: AI systems now complete multi-day professional projects end-to-end, displacing junior roles across legal, engineering, and software sectors. Openweight capability rises to **47.0**, reflecting wider distribution of memory-augmented models, though still trailing the frontier.

### Safety and Sentiment Erode

With no safety measure landing on shipped systems and capability surging past 60, **ai_safety** drops sharply to **19.0**. The gap between capability and control widens, raising alarms. **Resilience** falls to **34.0** as the cyberattack’s aftermath reveals systemic fragility, despite new defensive proposals. **Public sentiment** declines to **35.0**, dragged down by job displacement fears and the perception that institutions are losing control.

### Sovereignty Holds, Political Capital Erodes

The completion of **SEAL** last turn secures EU access to critical semiconductor infrastructure, anchoring **eu_ai_sovereignty** at **29.0** despite capability growth. However, **eu_political_capital** dips to **28.0**, burdened by a portfolio charge of −13 and no immediate payoff from new initiatives.
