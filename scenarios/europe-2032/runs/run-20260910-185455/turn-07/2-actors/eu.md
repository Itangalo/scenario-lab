## Priority
Blockade-Era Upstream Bargain, because with semiconductors blockaded and capital at 18 it is the only move that serves the two-year commitment to un-switch-off-able capacity without new money, outranking even the hospital rebuild.

## In practice
We run the cyber response through the finished Shield and its joint cell with ENISA and HERA: signatures, segmentation waivers and EIB reflows to paper-triage regions, with certified public-sector AI wins — falling waiting lists where adoption held — publicised by DG SANTE and DG DIGIT to hold sentiment. The defecting capital is offered re-entry inside common terms or stays outside protected-zone benefits; no new resilience line is opened.

For sovereignty we task the Trade and Competitiveness Councils under the Anti-Coercion Instrument logic to mandate the Commission to tie upstream licences to Japan-Korea-Singapore-Gulf pooled procurement from the M7 Compact, while DG RTD and EuroHPC use M6 Anchorage chairs and slots to land displaced talent and workloads. Gigafactories stay legally alive, unfunded builds are sequenced behind secured supply.
