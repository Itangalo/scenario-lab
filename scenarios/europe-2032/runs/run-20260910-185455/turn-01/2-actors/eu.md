## In practice
We task ENISA with a binding emergency hardening directive under the NIS2 legal base, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility money, requiring TSOs, major ports and water utilities in all 27 to segment OT from IT, deploy AI-anomaly detection, and report staging indicators to a common cell in Brussels within 24 hours. The Energy and Transport Council formations carry it, with France, Germany and Poland as lead operators; we buy municipal and utility consent with 70% EU co-financing of upgrades.

Alongside it we keep the two inherited compute lines moving: InvestAI Gigafactories through DG CNECT site selection and EIB guarantees for four to five sites, and the tech sovereignty package through accelerated-permitting zones and grid-connection deals, resisting US pressure on ASML by linking export alignment to published, non-discriminatory US access terms. We use the secured frontier access window to insist on evaluation rights and notice periods while the Shield exercises run this autumn.
