## Priority
**Priority:** Launch Sovereign AI Core Programme  
We are pushing hardest on the Sovereign AI Core Programme because without physically and legally insulated infrastructure under our own control, every other measure—oversight, safety, resilience—depends on permissions we cannot enforce and access we cannot guarantee; this turn, with a member state’s defection exposing our fragility, sovereignty is not strategic preference but survival.

## In practice
We are executing the Sovereign AI Core Programme under Article 122 TFEU as a cross-border crisis response, deploying joint Commission-defence task forces to Brittany, northern Sweden, and the Alps to begin site preparation regardless of national permitting delays. Germany and Austria are being offered co-auditing rights on environmental impact assessments conducted by a newly convened Joint Scientific Panel under EEA oversight, while Italy and Hungary face a hard condition: participation in the Apennines secondary node—and guaranteed local jobs and training hubs—is contingent on compliance with real-time logging and audit protocols. No audit, no access stands. The €80bn redirection—€50bn from the expired Tech sovereignty package via Recovery Regulation emergency clauses, €30bn from InvestAI’s unspent envelope—is now locked into EIB-disbursed tranches tied strictly to delivered, audited compute capacity above 60.0, not construction milestones.

Simultaneously, we are activating EAAD’s emergency powers under the amended AI Act to compel immediate access to agent decision trails in critical infrastructure. This is not voluntary reporting: we are mandating API-level integration with runtime monitoring stacks using backward-tracing tools that can detect emergent planning behaviour. Operators who refuse will be barred from hosting any AI system above 55.0 capability in the EU. The first dashboard feed—sanitized, time-delayed, but live—will go online in April, streamed directly to national parliaments and civil society observers, turning transparency into proof of function. This is how we rebuild credibility: not with promises, but with visibility under fire.
