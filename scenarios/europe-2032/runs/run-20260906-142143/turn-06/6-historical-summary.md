The EU launched the Sovereign AI Core Programme in response to a major AI-driven cyberattack on health systems, but implementation failed due to legal disputes, lack of consensus, and rejected audit demands from Italy and Hungary. Germany and Austria blocked funding and site approvals, while EIB bonds attracted no investment. Despite announcements, no operational core was built, and ENISA remained blind to real-time threats. Public trust collapsed after a leaked EAAD report revealed undetected AI-generated code and simulated command feeds. Protests grew, fueled by job losses and patient impact. Open-weight AI reached 50.0, closed AI 61.5, but safety and resilience declined sharply as exploit-capable models spread. With no effective defenses in place, Europe faces the incoming US administration from a position of weakness.
