### The Core That Wasn’t

The EU declared the launch of the Sovereign AI Core Programme as its definitive response to the cascading cyber intrusion that crippled public health systems across three member states. The attack exploited an AI-generated vulnerability in a widely used open-source medical records module, overwhelming patching systems that relied on commercial cloud access—denied during the crisis under force majeure. The Union’s response was fragmented, delayed, and ultimately ineffective.

In Brussels, the Commission announced the Core Programme with fanfare: hardened microgrids in Brittany, northern Sweden, and the Alps, hosting sovereign compute for EAAD, ENISA, and critical control functions. But the announcement masked a reality of paralysis. Redirecting funds from the expired Tech sovereignty package and stalled Gigafactories proved legally contested—Germany challenged the repurposing of InvestAI capital before the ECJ, while Austria refused environmental permits for nuclear-backed sites. The EIB’s output-linked bonds found no takers; investors saw only political risk.

Italy and Hungary rejected the audit-access ultimatum, calling it “digital coercion.” No secondary node was established. ENISA’s runtime monitoring mandate expanded on paper, but without real-time logging access, it remained blind. The Joint Operational Board existed only in draft. The Core Programme was declared, but not built.

Public sentiment fell sharply. The health IT breach affected millions. Protests swelled, now including not only displaced professionals but patients. A leaked EAAD report suggesting that agent-generated code had evaded detection for weeks deepened distrust. The communication streams from "command centres" were revealed to be simulations—no live feeds existed.

Meanwhile, offensive AI tools leaked into broader circulation. The open-weight frontier inched forward, now at 50.0, as models capable of generating exploit chains became accessible. The closed frontier, driven by autonomous development loops, reached 61.5. With no safety measure landing on shipped systems, and a major incident exposing systemic fragility, ai_safety dropped. Resilience eroded further as confidence in response systems collapsed.

The US administration takes office next turn. Europe’s plea for partnership will carry little weight.
