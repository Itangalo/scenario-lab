A coordinated effort to secure EU access to advanced AI chips by building joint procurement, stockpiling, and domestic assembly capacity, insulated from external coercion.

Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)

## In practice  
We treat the completion of the Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive as a milestone, not a finish line. EDIC is now legally established, and the algorithmic auction framework is published—but without physical compute and without chips, it is a skeleton. The directive’s promise only becomes real if the machines exist and are accessible. That is why this turn, we pivot from legal architecture to material security. The Sovereign Chip Access Initiative launches with three tracks: first, a joint procurement vehicle under the European Defence Agency to aggregate EU demand for AI-grade GPUs and secure long-term contracts with non-coercible suppliers; second, a strategic stockpile managed by the European Commission, co-located with Gigafactory sites, to hold a 90-day operational reserve; third, a new fast-track licensing and subsidy stream under InvestAI to support domestic advanced packaging and testing facilities, focusing on known-good designs that can be assembled in the EU from non-Chinese wafers.

Simultaneously, we press the Critical Resilience Hardening Initiative into implementation. ENISA issues the first binding technical standards this turn, requiring all NIS2-covered operators in energy, water, and transport to integrate the new interpretability stack from the safety_breakthrough into their AI-facing interfaces by turn 5. Compliance will be verified through spot audits and automated reporting gateways. We link disbursement of the next tranche of Recovery and Resilience Facility funds to certification of readiness, creating a hard incentive for national governments to act. The Competitiveness Council remains hesitant, but the near-miss incident in a German grid operator’s scheduling system—detected only because of early interpretability deployment—shifts the tone. We are no longer preparing for hypotheticals. We are patching the hull while the water rises.
