This is not optional modernisation; it is the baseline for surviving the next incident.

Behind the scenes, we are preparing for the Taiwan gate opening. The supply_chain_coercion event proves our chokepoints are being weaponised by others—we will not let that happen again. For now, we harden what we can, seize the compute we must, and prepare to fight for what we lack.
