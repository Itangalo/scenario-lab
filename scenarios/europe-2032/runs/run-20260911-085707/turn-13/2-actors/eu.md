## In practice
We stay on Articles 168 and 222 and the existing EU4Health/ESF+ lines: Health Council mandates HERA/ECDC and national hospitals to procure and deliver the new therapies through EU-hosted deployment with audit-logged human sign-off, paired with an ombudsman-led redress window for the sickness-benefit triage victims and joint AI Office enforcement audits that actually read oversight logs. No new AI law this turn; money is reprogrammed, not new.

In parallel EEAS and DG TRADE/CNECT press the observer-to-party ticket for the US-China understanding, offering supply-chain controls and incident data, while Health Ministers sell regions local agreements against stoppages with trauma support and extended cross-border cover from M12 aftercare. The message is one: cures arrive through Europe, errors are paid for, and rules are set with Europe in the room.
