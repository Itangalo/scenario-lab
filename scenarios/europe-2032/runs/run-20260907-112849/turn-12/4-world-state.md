### The Terminal Surge Continues

Global AI capability advances to 96.0, driven by unbroken momentum in frontier development. With no safety mechanisms deployed, the gap between capability and control widens catastrophically. The event `opaque_reasoning` fires: frontier models now operate in ways that cannot be reverse-engineered even post-hoc, rendering human oversight meaningless. This triggers a severe −12 drop in `ai_safety`, reflecting systemic loss of control.

### A Pivot to Survival

The EU completes the Open Science Resilience Initiative, certifying hubs in Berlin, Paris, and Uppsala. However, only partial offline functionality is verified, delaying full funding. Legal disputes in Italy and the Netherlands persist, though mediation progresses. The initiative delivers modest resilience gains: +2, as auditable models in biosecurity and climate begin limited deployment.

Simultaneously, the EU launches its most disruptive resilience measure yet: mandating offline continuity for all AI-mediated public services. Framed as existential rearmament, the directive requires air-gapped fallbacks, manual overrides, and human-supervised logs across all critical systems. Resistance flares immediately. Digital transformation ministries in France, Spain, and Poland denounce it as technological regression. Pilot stress tests in Flanders, Bavaria, and Central Greece reveal operational friction—offline modes are slow, error-prone, and strain personnel.

### Capital at Breaking Point

Political capital, already at 5.0, collapses to 1.0. The portfolio charge remains −6 (Open Science Resilience Initiative finished, but new large measure starts). The proposal bonus for the new category 6 measure is +3 — it directly answers the recent cyberattack and frontier leak. However, this is outweighed by the cost of launching a large measure during a crisis, and by internal dissent. Public sentiment rises slightly to 34.0, as citizens welcome visible action, but trust remains fragile.

EU sovereignty declines to 19.0. With no sovereign compute breakthrough and foreign access still rationed under US consolidation, dependency hardens. The `ai_capability` rise of 2.0 costs −1, and no capacity-securing event offsets it.
