### The Terminal Phase Accelerates
Global AI capability advances to 98.5, driven by self-improving systems operating beyond human interpretability. With `opaque_reasoning` now fully entrenched, the frontier no longer produces tools — it produces oracles whose outputs cannot be audited even post-hoc. This deepens the `ai_safety` crisis, which collapses by a further 12 points to −12.0, reflecting a complete breakdown in the ability to verify, let alone control, the most capable systems.

### EU Enforcement Meets Resistance
The European Union doubles down on survival, launching binding audits of 47 critical infrastructure operators to enforce offline continuity. The Emergency Resilience Task Force conducts live stress tests at RTE and Enel, revealing that only 12 of 47 systems can maintain basic functionality without AI or external connectivity. At Deutsche Bahn, manual override protocols fail under load; at Frontex, air-gapped logs prove vulnerable to side-channel corruption.

Legal and political pushback intensifies. France refers the mandate to the Court of Justice, arguing it undermines digital sovereignty and violates internal market principles. Poland halts implementation in border and rail systems, citing national emergency powers. Despite these challenges, the EU proceeds, framing non-compliance as existential risk rather than regulatory overreach.

A public demonstration at Zeebrugge in December shows a paper-based border process handling 80% of normal volume — a symbolic victory for resilience, but one that underscores the cost of de-AI-fication. The European Data Protection Board reinforces the push, ruling that unverifiable AI decisions in public services breach GDPR Article 22.

Public sentiment rises slightly to 35.0, as citizens respond to visible accountability measures. However, trust remains fragile, and the economic drag of de-AI-fication fuels quiet discontent in industrial sectors.

Resilience improves marginally to 56.0, reflecting partial progress in audit readiness and the psychological impact of demonstrated fallback capacity. Yet the gains are narrow and localized.

EU sovereignty declines to 18.0, as the lack of sovereign compute and continued dependence on foreign frontier access erodes leverage. Political capital remains at 1.0, pinned by the immense strain of enforcement amid fragmentation and legal uncertainty.
