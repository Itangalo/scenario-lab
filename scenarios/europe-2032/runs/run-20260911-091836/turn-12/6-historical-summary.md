February US cut-off forced reliance on continuity stack and EU helpers; a separate capital deal and paper no-undercut pact frayed cohesion; factory builds stalled by freezes, blockades and injunctions; wards/grid held on European systems.

Showcase sites saw fragile gains where triage helpers bedded in, but winter brought an audit break: hospital-insurer review claimed systematic under-triage of older patients and migrants, two ministries paused tools outside pilots, queues lengthened. Brussels' Bio-Uplift Watch/Hospital Shield offered re-testing and clinician sign-off; some accepted, others rejected as cover for bias.

Meanwhile a genome-model pathway to human-infecting design became accepted public warning, and an openly released frontier model spread permanently to private hardware. Municipal officers and clinics improvised with downloadable models via chat/USB beyond oversight; officials answered only with guidance and voluntary reporting. HERA/ECDC/ENISA kept low-cost monitoring, hygiene checks and circling water-power talks; US gave sympathy, no timetable. By June essential care held in islands, distrusted in others, improvised everywhere, diggers still stopped.
