## Priority
Priority is M9 — EU Bio-Uplift Watch and Hospital Shield — because with bio-precursor active, audit-driven suspensions spreading, and shadow use of unrecallable frontier models, keeping low-cost syndromic monitoring and hospital hygiene continuity alive is the only move that does not collapse capital further.

## In practice
HERA with ECDC and ENISA extend the Watch to the audit revolt: independent re-validation of continuity-stack triage helpers in showcase sites, publication of under-triage findings, and temporary human-in-the-loop protocols to lift national suspension orders without new procurement. Hospital Shield hygiene teams stay on maintenance money in wards that remain open.

We do not stand up a new registry or ban for the shadow clinic network we cannot enforce; municipal officers and small labs pooling open models are offered handling guidance, incident-reporting channels, and script-review via existing HERA mandates, while DG SANTE convenes hospital networks and insurers in the Health Council formation to negotiate common triage standards. Water-power compact talks and the no-undercut exhortation continue site-by-site, with no challenge to injunctions this turn.
