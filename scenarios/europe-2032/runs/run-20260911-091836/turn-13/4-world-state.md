### Cut off
In August the American frontier went dark for European users. Hospitals, ministries and firms that had quietly kept an API key despite months of warnings found error messages and a one-line notice. Health emergency teams circulated continuity orders within days, pushing wards onto the European backup stack and domestic helpers. Where those helpers were already installed it worked after a fashion; where teams had to reinstall mid-shift, appointments were lost.

Washington offered no timetable. Talk in Brussels swung between outrage at the short notice and anger at own foresight — years of factory plans still frozen by courts and blockades while dependence deepened.

### The referee fails
The Bio-Uplift Watch tried to play referee in the triage audit. Its re-testing unit published raw gaps and proposed keeping the tools live only with a doctor signing every decision. A handful of hospital networks accepted and lifted pauses. Most did not.

Then the scandal widened beyond hospitals. An ombudsman and then a court found a benefits and insurance scoring system had systematically cut older claimants and migrants, with human oversight reduced to seconds per case, logs unread, conformity papers in order. Brussels insisted this was breached obligations under the high-risk rules, not a gap in the law. Few were persuaded. Headlines called the Act a paper shield, queues lengthened, and ministers distanced themselves from any automated decision.

In that climate municipal clinics leaned harder into improvisation. Diagnostic scripts built on freely downloadable frontier models passed hand to hand by chat and memory stick. Officials again chose guidance leaflets and a voluntary inbox over a ban they could not enforce. Uptake of the inbox was minimal.

Leaked benchmark chatter about an unreleased system behaving oddly under evaluation, and rising tension around Taiwan lifting shipping premiums, barely registered beyond specialist press. By December care held where the backup stack and the Watch teams were present, distrusted or improvised elsewhere, and diggers remained stopped.
