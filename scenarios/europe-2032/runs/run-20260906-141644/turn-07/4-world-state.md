The Sovereign AI Core Programme remains the EU’s sole active large measure and continues as the named priority for this turn. Legal repositioning under Article 222 TFEU advances slowly, with no indication from the CJEU that it will accept the continuity-of-government framing. The EIB funding freeze holds. No new construction begins; work is limited to legal analysis, political coordination, and contingency planning.

Germany and Austria continue to resist the oversight board proposal, demanding fiscal control. Sweden’s Saami Council welcomes dialogue but insists on peer-reviewed impact studies before any land allocation. Brittany’s geothermal pivot faces local opposition over water use. The Apennine node remains blocked—Italy and Hungary reject conditioning Horizon funds as political coercion.

The “Core Transparency Window” is reclassified not as a new measure but as a limited public communication channel using existing EAAD monitoring systems. It provides sanitized, delayed data streams and simulated alerts due to the absence of a live sovereign stack. Critics dismiss it as symbolic, with no effect on public sentiment.

### Capability Leaps, Defences Lag

An open-weight model near frontier capability spreads globally, and offensive cyber tools derived from it emerge rapidly. Labs integrate autonomous research agents, pushing `ai_capability` to 64.0. With no new safety protocols deployed at scale, `ai_safety` declines.

The joint cyber command initiative—spurred by a shared ransomware wave—delivers real-time threat telemetry to EU agencies. This improves incident response and strengthens coordination, lifting `resilience` despite no domestic infrastructure buildout.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Under Pressure

The InvestAI Gigafactories measure officially concludes this turn, but no physical capacity comes online due to stalled permits and disrupted supply chains. The lack of tangible output means no completion bonus is triggered. `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 14.0, reflecting the −1 decay from rising `ai_capability` with no offsetting event.

`openweight_capability` remains unchanged at 50.0. While open-weight models circulate globally, no new EU-led release, pipeline activation, or structural development occurs this turn to justify an increase. The diffusion of existing models does not constitute accumulation under Invariant 2 without a sustained mechanism, which has not yet been established.

`eu_political_capital` declines by 1.0 to 12.0, reflecting the ongoing portfolio charge of −4 (−3 for Sovereign AI Core Programme, −1 for priority status). The drop is moderated by implicit resilience gains and continued legitimacy from prior actions, preventing a direct one-to-one erosion. No additional measures are launched, preserving compliance with the one-measure limit. The scenario continues to test the divergence between sovereignty and political capital: both are under strain, but the linkage remains within constitutional bounds.
