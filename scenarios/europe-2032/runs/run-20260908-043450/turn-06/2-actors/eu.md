## In practice  
This turn, we are closing the loop. The Integrated Sovereign Defence Protocol has passed into force, but its value lies not in legal text but in operational reality. We are deploying the Automated Threat Enrichment Layer to ensure that every confirmed AI-driven attack or model flaw translates into an immediate defensive update. Using the €4.2bn redirected from InvestAI, we are funding secure API integrations between Luxembourg’s sovereign enclave and Finland’s hardened node, with the software stack developed jointly by ENISA and the German BSI. The layer will pull from two sources: the Critical Incident Integration Directive’s real-time reporting feeds and the repurposed Frontier Model Evaluation Consortium’s threat reverse-engineering output. When a new AI-generated exploit pattern is confirmed, the system will auto-generate defensive signatures and push them to all connected CERTs and operators within four hours.

Resistance remains from smaller member states who fear opaque escalation and loss of control. Our offer is full auditability: every automated alert will carry a traceable chain of evidence from source data to action, viewable in real time by national authorities. We are also binding this layer to public accountability—quarterly public summaries of triggered defences will be issued by the AI Office, not to expose methods, but to prove that protection is happening. The U.S. election_retrenchment has frozen transatlantic coordination, so we cannot wait for alignment. We act alone, but not in isolation: the interpretability breakthrough gives us a technical foothold, and the bio_incident a political window. This turn, we make sovereign defence real — not as a promise, but as a system that breathes on its own.
