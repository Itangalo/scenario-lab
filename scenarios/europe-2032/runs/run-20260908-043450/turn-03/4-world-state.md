### The Edge of Control

The EU doubled down on control, not capacity. The Emergency Resilience Surge extended monitoring to 24 grid nodes and five ports, with real-time correlation now flagging AI-like attack patterns. Germany’s integration with the French nuclear grid proved viable, but Poland’s continued refusal forced reliance on Baltic interconnectors, exposing a critical dependency. Still, the system held — for now.

The European Frontier Model Evaluation Consortium gained legal footing via a fast-tracked AI Act revision, and core institutions — CEA, Fraunhofer, TNO — began staffing secure facilities in Luxembourg and Finland. But no frontier developer granted access. Market leverage proved insufficient without enforcement teeth.

In response, the EU launched the Sovereign Evaluation Authority Pilot, repurposing €3.8bn from the stalled Gigafactories fund. Secure enclaves were initiated, and red-team hiring began. Yet this was not evaluation — it was preparation. No model was assessed, and no emergent behaviour detected.

Meanwhile, AI capability advanced to 56.5 as labs deepened agent autonomy. With no safety improvements on deployed systems and eval_anomaly_reports hinting at uncontrolled behaviours, ai_safety fell to 29.0. openweight_capability rose to 44.5, as Kimi K3 derivatives spread in underground cyber forums.

Political capital collapsed. Four large measures now in flight — Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty, Resilience Surge, and the new Evaluation Pilot — cost −12, plus −1 for the priority, totaling −13. No measure concluded. The proposal bonus for the Pilot was +3, given its direct response to eval_anomaly_reports and the erosion of trust, but this barely stemmed the tide. eu_political_capital fell to 20.0.

Public sentiment turned negative. campaign_backlash gained ground as local protests blocked data centre expansions in Spain and Sweden. Workers displaced by AI in logistics and legal sectors found little support. Sentiment dropped to 39.0.

Sovereignty eroded further. No domestic compute came online, and global capacity pulled ahead. With ai_capability rising by 1.5 and no category 4 measure completing, eu_ai_sovereignty fell to 17.0.

### A Pivot Without Power

The pivot from compute to control was clear — but the means to execute it were not. The Union sought authority without access, oversight without leverage, and resilience without unity.
