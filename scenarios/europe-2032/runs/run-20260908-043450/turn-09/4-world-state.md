### A Turn of Hard Choices

AI capability advanced slightly to 61.5, as self-improving loops in logistics and materials science continued to yield marginal gains. Openweight capability rose to 50.0, reflecting its typical lag behind the frontier and the absence of major new open releases. No safety improvements occurred — the interpretability breakthrough remains siloed, and the gap between capability and control widens.

The **Sovereign Semiconductor Assurance Programme** formally concluded this turn but delivered no sovereignty benefit. Blocked by U.S. export controls, the initiative failed to secure lithography access, leaving the EU without a domestic production pathway. The symbolic commitment did, however, lay groundwork for future industrial policy.

In response, the EU launched the **Trusted Infrastructure Integration Directive**, a large category 4 measure aimed at forcing critical infrastructure operators to use AI systems running on verifiable EU-controlled hardware stacks. The directive, justified under internal market rules, targets energy, telecoms, and financial systems. While legally assertive, its success hinges on enforcement capacity and foreign compliance — early signals from major hyperscalers suggest resistance, citing operational impracticality.

Simultaneously, the Commission released €600 million to fund data sovereignty hubs in Vilnius, Riga, and Bratislava, tied to full API integration with the EU threat layer. This effort, part of the ongoing **Regional Oversight Integration Initiative**, seeks to buy cohesion through co-ownership. Initial responses are cautious; trust deficits persist.

The portfolio charge this turn was −6: the completed semiconductor programme (−3), the new directive (−3), and the named priority (−1). The completion bonus for the finished measure was +2, but no proposal bonus applied — the new directive responded to a structural failure, not a recent crisis. With eu_political_capital already at 8.0, the net result is a decline to 7.0. Public sentiment remained stagnant at 38.0, offering no relief.

Sovereignty rose to 13.0: the semiconductor programme’s failure incurred no gain, but the new directive, now in flight, contributes a partial +2 as it nears early implementation. The −1 decay from ai_capability growth (only +0.5) did not apply. No capacity event occurred.

Resilience held at 53.0 — coordination signals improved, but no formal integration was achieved.
