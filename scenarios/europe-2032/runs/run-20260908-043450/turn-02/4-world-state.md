### The Weight of Too Much at Once

The EU pressed forward on multiple fronts, but the strain proved decisive. The Emergency Resilience Surge made partial progress: real-time monitoring was deployed across key French and Benelux grid nodes and three major ports, with early detection systems now correlating anomalies using synthetic attack profiles. Germany unlocked limited grid access after securing co-financing, but Poland withheld approval entirely, forcing rerouting through Baltic interconnectors — a workaround that preserved momentum but delayed full coverage by months.

The European Frontier Model Evaluation Consortium was not launched as a new measure. Instead, exploratory talks among member states and technical institutions continued under the existing emergent_lab_cooperation initiative, with no formal proposal advanced. Legal drafting has not yet begun, and no new coordination bodies were established. The idea of secure evaluation facilities in Luxembourg and Finland remains under study, but no site selection or funding has been approved. Negotiations with frontier developers for pre-release access remain informal and nonbinding.

Meanwhile, the InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package remain stalled. The proposal for an EU Strategic Compute Authority to co-own sites gained attention but no formal buy-in. Industry resistance persists, and member-state hesitancy over sovereignty and cost remains high.

AI capability advanced to 55.0 as labs further automated training pipelines and extended agent autonomy. With no safety improvements on deployed systems, and no breakthroughs in interpretability or control, ai_safety dipped to 31.0. openweight_capability rose to 43.0, driven by wider distribution of Kimi K3-derived tools capable of targeted cyber operations.

Political capital eroded. With three large measures still in flight — InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty Package, and Emergency Resilience Surge — the portfolio charge remained at −9 (−3 per measure), and the named priority added −1, for a total of −10. No measure concluded, and no external crisis was mitigated, so no recovery occurred. eu_political_capital fell to 30.0, reflecting sustained strain without collapse. public_sentiment held steady at 42.0 — ambivalent, unalarmed, unimpressed. Sovereignty declined further as global compute outpaced EU efforts and no domestic capacity came online.
