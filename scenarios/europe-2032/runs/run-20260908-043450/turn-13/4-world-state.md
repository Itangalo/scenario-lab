### Stalled Response, Mounting Pressures

AI capability edged upward to 63.5, driven by incremental gains in logistics optimisation and materials discovery. However, no corresponding safety improvements emerged. On the contrary, the confirmed opacity in frontier models—now observed across multiple stress tests—deepened concerns. With no new safeguards in place and systemic control gaps widening, `ai_safety` dropped sharply by 5 points to 17.0.

The **Trusted Infrastructure Integration Directive** formally concluded this turn but failed to achieve adoption. The Internal Market Council did not ratify the harmonised standard, citing WTO risks and U.S. industry resistance. ENISA’s technical framework remained unused. The collapse marked the end of the EU’s primary effort to anchor sovereign control over AI execution infrastructure, leaving a critical gap.

Simultaneously, an informal coalition of Eastern member states advanced plans for parallel procurement of sovereign AI infrastructure, threatening permanent fragmentation. Emergency cohesion talks began, but with political capital already depleted, the EU could offer only limited incentives.

No new measure was proposed. The existing portfolio charge—−3 for the now-finished directive, plus −1 for the priority—remained active this turn, as rule 6 applies charges in the turn a measure finishes. With no completion bonus (the directive failed to land), and no new proposal to trigger a bonus, `eu_political_capital` declined by 4 points to −3.0, reflecting the full weight of the portfolio charge and consistent with the model’s accounting.

`eu_ai_sovereignty` fell to 9.0. The failure to secure infrastructure control, combined with ongoing capability growth (though under 2.0), eroded leverage. No capacity event occurred, but the absence of a functioning measure in category 4 left no offsetting gain.

Public sentiment held at 38.0. The open-source bio-design breakthrough sparked both excitement and alarm, but no major incident or visible benefit shifted perception. Resilience remained unchanged—no new category 6 measure finished, and the prior boost from the Regional Oversight Initiative had already landed.

Behind the scenes, contingency planning intensified under the Digital Markets Act, and semiconductor supply-chain leverage was mapped. But with no capital to act, these remained theoretical options.
