### A Fragile Leap Forward

AI capability crossed the 60.0 threshold this turn, reaching 60.5 as frontier labs completed multi-day autonomous research cycles in materials science and logistics optimisation. This milestone unlocked self-improving training loops, accelerating progress despite global headwinds.

A major interpretability breakthrough — the first to apply to deployed systems — allowed real-time tracing of high-stakes AI decisions in finance and infrastructure. Two leading labs adopted the method, significantly improving transparency and earning a +5 boost to ai_safety, which rose to 34.0.

The EU marked a partial victory as the Automated Threat Enrichment Layer became operational in France, Germany, and the Benelux. The system cut threat response times to under four hours, delivering a tangible resilience gain (+3), and resilience reached 52.0. However, integration stalled in Central and Eastern Europe, where nations withheld API access pending stronger oversight guarantees.

Simultaneously, Washington’s coercive move against ASML — halting servicing of mature-node lithography tools — exposed a critical vulnerability. The EU’s AI defence systems, however advanced, rely on chips made with U.S.-controlled equipment. This strategic dependency triggered a sovereignty crisis.

In response, the European Commission proposed the Sovereign Semiconductor Assurance Programme, a category 4 measure aimed at securing maintenance and subsystem production of critical lithography tools within EU jurisdiction. A draft budget proposes €18bn in redirected funding from the InvestAI Gigafactories programme, with one site now delayed. However, the reallocation requires approval from the European Parliament and Council under the revised MFF flexibility clauses, and debate is ongoing. No final adoption occurred this turn, and the measure remains under negotiation. Implementation cannot begin before Turn 9 under high-cost procedural timelines, and no portfolio charge applies at this stage.

### The Cost of Crisis

The portfolio charge — InvestAI Gigafactories (−3), Automated Threat Enrichment Layer (−2), and priority (−1) — totalled −6. The proposed Sovereign Semiconductor Assurance Programme did not yet trigger its charge, as it remains under negotiation and not formally adopted. With no proposal bonus applicable — the threat layer’s bonus was already claimed last turn — eu_political_capital declined by 6, falling from 17.0 to 11.0. No emergency adjustment applied, as cohesion held at a minimal but sufficient level to avoid invoking contingency floor rules.

Sovereignty fell to 12.0: despite the new initiative, the supply_chain_coercion event stripped −2 under metric rule 5, and ai_capability’s rise of 1.0 imposed a further −1. Public sentiment held steady at 38.0 — too low to lend legitimacy.
