## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, because the precursor cyber gate outranks build-out of compute when blackouts are stageable now.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the TTE Council formation to issue emergency network codes under the NIS2 and CER directives, backed by Connecting Europe Facility reprogramming: transmission system operators in all member states must segment OT from IT, rotate breaker credentials, and deploy AI-swarm detection sensors by mid-2027. Joint EU-CERT exercises this winter will rehearse containment without self-inflicted outages.

Alongside that we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package moving through DG CNECT and the EIB: accelerated-permitting zones, grid connections for the first two sites, and ASML export-coordination to hold leverage while Washington pressures. We do not open a parallel compute programme; we push the inherited ones to deliver.
