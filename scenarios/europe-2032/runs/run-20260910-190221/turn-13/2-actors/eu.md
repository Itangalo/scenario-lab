Stands up ENISA-led mobile restoration teams and funded offline fallbacks to bring hit registries, billing and hospital systems back to checked builds and keep them running paper-capable through the open-weight-enabled sweep.
This is a preparedness and resilience measure (category 6), and it serves our two-year commitment to keep essential services and jobs standing because services are degraded now while resilience is 42.0 and the open-weight release puts frontier tooling permanently beyond recall.

## Priority

## In practice
We run it from ENISA and EC3 with DG DIGIT and DG SANTE/HERA: ring-fenced recovery teams to the hit municipalities and health operators, mandatory rollback to last-known-good builds, joint police triage extended to the compromised dependency, with power/water carve-outs for continuity nodes paid from reallocated UCPM and ESF+ envelopes.

We let finished M7/M8 continuity rules and M9 throttled servicing carry wards while we absorb, and we use HERA joint procurement for the foreign-modelled cures on EU terms to show benefit without promising new builds we cannot fund at capital 16.0. Councils blocking data infrastructure over water get continuity funding tied to offline procedures, not to new siting, to stop the refusal spreading.
