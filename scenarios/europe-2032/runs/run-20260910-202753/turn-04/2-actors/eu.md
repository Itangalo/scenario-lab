## New measure
**EU Transition and Siting Compact**
This is Labour and social protection work that pairs a funded entry-level retraining and wage-bridge for displaced graduates with community benefit and local-veto rules for data-centre and Shield segmentation sites.
We choose this now because with sentiment at 20, injunctions freezing Shield expansion, and unions naming abandonment, neither sovereignty nor resilience can move until siting is legitimate and job loss is answered.

## Priority
EU Transition and Siting Compact, because unfreezing Shield sites and defusing hostility to infrastructure outranks even the Gigafactory build this turn — without local consent nothing else lands.

## In practice
We table an EPSCO-anchored package funded by reallocated ESF+ and Just Transition money: wage insurance for 12 months for under-30s displaced in law, accountancy, support and customer operations, plus paid reskilling places tied to Shield cyber-recovery and grid-segmentation works. Municipalities hosting Gigafactory, data-centre or segmentation sites get grid-fee rebates and a formal early-consultation right to lift the injunctions.

We keep M1 and M2 alive only as permits and IPCEI clearance, join the joint cyber command telemetry pool on Shield terms via ENISA without ceding procurement, and task the Evaluation Cell to publish attribution of the open-model tooling to justify the compact. We tie recovery aid to the defecting member state to common siting standards, not to cancelling its hyperscaler deal outright.
