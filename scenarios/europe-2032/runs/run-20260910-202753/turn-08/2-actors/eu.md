## Priority
EU Shopfloor Continuity and Displacement Bridge, and not the hospital reserve, because plant-level displacement and Chinese robot dependence is now the faster path to fragmentation than ward fallbacks alone.

## In practice
We run this through DG EMPL and DG GROW under existing ESF+ and Globalisation Adjustment Fund rules, no new regulation: short-time work top-ups and rapid retraining vouchers in auto, logistics and machinery regions most exposed to Chinese humanoids, co-funded by member states with Commission advances. Maintenance and spares pooling for critical automation is handled via HERA-style joint procurement, diversifying away from single-source Chinese stacks where possible.

We keep M7 running in parallel: SANTE/HERA keeps EU-hosted open-model fallbacks for the five cutoff states, ENISA keeps isolation drills, funded by reallocated EU4Health/Digital Europe. We do not reopen Gigafactory earthworks or force siting injunctions this turn, and we log ASML rationing and therapy dependence as supply risks to be raised in Trade Council, not retaliated now.
