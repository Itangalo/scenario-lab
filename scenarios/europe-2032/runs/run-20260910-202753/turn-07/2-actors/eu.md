## In practice

We pair that with a Health-Interior loss-of-control protocol: mandatory incident reporting from the Shield, exercised isolation and rollback drills in two hospital groups and one ministry IT estate per member state, using Evaluation Cell attribution and continued allied telemetry. We do not force entry on siting injunctions, do not retaliate on ASML coercion this turn, and log medical-breakthrough dependence as the case for fallback rather than new restriction.
