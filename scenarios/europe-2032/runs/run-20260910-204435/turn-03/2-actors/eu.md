**Municipal Equal Protection Deployment**
Puts the swarm-detection and fast-patching stack into small water and municipal operators via managed EU-funded SOC coverage and tuning teams.

## Priority
Municipal Equal Protection Deployment, because without closing the small-operator gap our two-year commitment to withstand disruption fails where break-ins are still rising.

## In practice
We task ENISA and EU-CyCLONe through the NIS2 Cooperation Group to extend existing SOC contracts as managed service for lagging water/municipal operators, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and Cohesion funds with 100% EU co-financing for tuning staff this semester. Energy and Environment Council formations carry it; interior ministries are offered no new relay builds, municipalities are offered staff not alerts.

M3 audits and cross-border exercises continue to turn 4, and M1/M2 gigafactory permitting continues under DG CNECT with dialogue on EU-hosting standards to contain the Kimi K3 backlash without a blanket ban. We hold the US licence alignment signal steady with no new commitment to conserve capital for fielding.
