## New measure
**EU Stranded Compute Salvage and Maintenance Corps**
Takes over cancelled private data-centre shells and power reservations for EuroHPC continuity hosting, and funds maintenance crews to keep health-energy-water on European-hosted models through the freeze.
This is Preparedness and resilience work, category 6, and why now is that the investment collapse strands the private build-out we were borrowing while mayors freeze new connections — so we salvage what is already permitted and staff what already exists rather than promising new sites.

## Priority
EU Taiwan Strait Continuity and Rationing Protocol, because with rationing drills already exposing missing spares and confused responsibilities, holding the rationing line outranks starting the salvage until drills lock.

## In practice
We run M12 hard this turn through the Civil Protection Mechanism and TTE-Energy: EuroHPC JU holds pre-booked health-energy-water allocations, ENISA and DG ENER complete live fallback drills in the key-suspended hospitals and logistics hubs, and member states release pooled spares under existing licences. Mayors blocking new connections are offered what they asked for — staffed protection and paid crews for existing reception sites, no new grid demands.

We stand up the salvage in parallel on a rescEU + Cohesion repurposing base: DG CNECT maps cancelled private shells with grid and permits already granted, EuroHPC takes short leases only where no new connection is needed, and the M11 graduate crews are re-hired as maintenance operators for water, small hospitals and grid fallback. EPSO and social partners extend wage insurance to private-build workers absorbed into the corps, to stop the collapse turning into anti-AI layoff anger while the freeze holds.
