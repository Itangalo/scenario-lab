## Priority
EU Upstream Bargain for Compute Continuity, because without secured supply the Continuity Stack fallback has no hardware to run on and our two-year commitment to independent capacity under European control fails at the fab gate.

## In practice
We mandate DG TRADE with DG CNECT under the Anti-Coercion Instrument and EU Chips Act review to pool export-licensing for lithography and related spares, conditioning volumes on reciprocal compute allocations, serviced GPU pools and continued inference access for health and administration. Council in Foreign Affairs and Competitiveness formats carries the joint offer to the US, Japan and Taiwan authorities; EuroHPC JU holds the EU-side reception capacity.

We do not restart gigafactories as a new build — M1/M2 zones and M9 continuity hosting remain the landing ground. What this bargain buys is time and silicon to keep those sites filling: prioritised shipments for EU-anchored inference, maintenance guarantees, and a common evaluation-share on the anomalous system so concessions are not traded against an unseen capability jump. DG EMPL keeps the tied retraining-to-deployment track for displaced juniors to staff the reception, defusing sabotage pressure without opening a second labour measure.
