## New measure
**EU Essential Continuity Stack**
Keeps hospitals, ministries and firms running after the US frontier cutoff by standing up an EU-controlled fallback inference layer on existing EuroHPC and gigafactory-zone capacity using EU-tuned open models.
This is Sovereignty and industrial capacity work, category 4, and why now is that the cutoff turned dependence from a warning into an outage — we must show continuity before outrage curdles into blame, while channelling graduate displacement anger into deployment rather than sabotage.

## Priority
EU Essential Continuity Stack, because the access denial directly tests our two-year commitment to independent frontier capacity under European control and outranks even the municipal shield rollout this turn.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT with EuroHPC JU and ENISA to switch essential-service users cut off from the US model onto hardened EU-hosted models, funded by reprogrammed InvestAI and Digital Europe money, with procurement preference for EU-anchored inference in health and administration decided in the Competitiveness and EPSCO Councils. DG TRADE keeps quiet volume-licence diplomacy alive but we no longer promise restoration — the message is continuity, not appeal.

M8 continues through the joint SOCs and managed teams: auto-patch and swarm detection to water, municipal IT and hospital admin hit in the winter sweep, with mutual-aid conditional on adoption to hold resilience gains. On jobs, we use existing ESF+ and SURE-type transition funds via the Employment Council for entry-level law, accountancy, software and admin cohorts, explicitly tying retraining places to the Continuity Stack rollout so displacement response does not become a second unfunded measure this turn.
