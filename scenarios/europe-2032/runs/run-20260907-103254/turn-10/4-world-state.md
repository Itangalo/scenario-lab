### The Breach That Changed Everything

A wave of AI-generated cyber intrusions sweeps across public services in multiple member states, exploiting update mechanisms in newly deployed sovereign systems. The attack, sophisticated and self-propagating, bypasses traditional defences and reveals a grim truth: the very systems meant to secure European autonomy are vulnerable at their core. Initial damage is contained, but trust evaporates overnight.

In response, the EU declares an emergency and launches the Emergency Resilience Surge, invoking crisis powers to fast-track cybersecurity upgrades across critical infrastructure. Zero-trust authentication, real-time anomaly detection, and red-team audits are mandated for all sovereign nodes. Rollout of the Sovereign Core is halted pending independent verification of update integrity — a necessary but costly delay.

The Public Trust Observatory begins operating in interim form, though its first report is delayed to the next turn. Resistance from intelligence agencies and legal disputes over data sharing limit its initial reach. Whistleblower protections are announced, but few come forward.

Public sentiment worsens as media spotlight the failure of AI systems meant to protect, not endanger. The French social benefits scandal continues to reverberate, now framed as part of a broader pattern of opaque, unaccountable automation. Protests flare in several cities, targeting data centres and government offices.

Behind the scenes, researchers quietly leave EU institutions for better-resourced labs abroad, deepening the brain drain. Auditors whisper of systemic flaws in the sovereign stack — flaws that may have allowed the breach to go undetected for weeks.

### Fragility Exposed

The EU acts with urgency, but political capital is spent faster than trust can be rebuilt. The Emergency Resilience Surge is launched, but cohesion frays under pressure. One member state refuses full compliance, citing sovereignty concerns of its own. The Union’s ability to act decisively is strained to the breaking point.
