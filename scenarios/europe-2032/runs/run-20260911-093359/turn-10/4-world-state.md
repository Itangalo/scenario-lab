### Holding by hand
The first half of 2031 did not bring a new blackout, but an attack on the answer to the last one. Small groups broke into transformer stores, drained fuel from pooled generators, and smashed telemetry cabinets holding handwritten breaker logs. Police reports described coordination across three countries — vans, lists, messaging channels calling the stockpiles collaboration with the machines.

Brussels had already made the Black-Start network its sole priority. ENISA and national cyber-response teams, with police and gendarmerie, moved to bring the hidden pools onto the books: guarded depots, dispersed spares, reimbursement for farmers, workshops and municipal utilities that had stockpiled off the record. A plain public map of protected sites was published — this keeps the lights on, not this serves the system. Where guards arrived in time, equipment survived. Where they did not, a hospital in the east and two towns in the south lost their fallback for weeks.

### Money leaves, quotas stay
American capital fled the AI sector in the spring. Valuations collapsed, data-centre extensions were cancelled rather than delayed, and several leasing deals European operators had counted on evaporated. At the same time Washington tightened licences again, keeping even allies on volume quotas for advanced chips and metered model access.

For Europe the two shocks cancelled each other only on paper. Nothing more could be bought even if money returned, and no money was there to buy with. Clinics stayed on a European default with an American fallback, grid operators stayed on segmented manual operation. Factory managers spoke of being fenced in twice over.

### One bright waiting room
The one counter-story came from waiting lists. In two health systems and a handful of municipalities, European-run scheduling and triage software cut waits and decision times from months to days. Health ministries co-branded the deployments, paid the teams, kept the branding local. Patients noticed.

It did not reverse hostility, but it separated, for the first time in a year, useful adoption from the rogue-agent and blackout story. The containment and mutual-aid protocol formally closed this half-year, and cross-border fuel and repair pools now reimburse automatically. Restoration is still by hand — but it is now rehearsed, listed, and guarded.
