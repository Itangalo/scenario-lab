### The table in another room
Washington and Beijing announced it jointly, in terse parallel statements: a limited pact on securing model weights, on guardrails against autonomous escalation, and on restricting a class of biological design tools, with inspections thin but real. Brussels learned the details the way most capitals did — afterwards, by cable.

The Commission tried to turn afterthought into entry ticket. The foreign-policy chief was mandated to seek a seat at the verification table, offering what Europe alone could offer: hard evidence from the winter blackout and the spring sabotage wave, test protocols from its evaluation institute, audit practice on weight security. No new fund, no directive, only a diplomatic brief co-signed by supply-chain partners.

It landed unevenly. American officials praised the black-start data in public and deferred on access in private; Chinese counterparts welcomed technical exchange while insisting the core arrangement remained bilateral. Observer invitations to working groups arrived, a full chair did not. Europe was inside the information flow, but not inside the agreement.

### Held lights
At home, the one programme that had money finished its work. The network of guarded depots, dispersed spares and manual fallback procedures — rehearsed through the autumn, reimbursed automatically across borders — was declared operational. Grid operators ran a dark drill in November: segmentation held, hospitals kept fallback, logs stayed legible by hand.

It did not restore trust in AI, but it changed the texture of distrust. Health-ministry scheduling software continued to cut waiting times, and local branding kept those wins separate from the larger fear. Sabotage attempts continued, small and spiteful, but guards now mostly arrived first.

Whispers persisted of villages and cooperatives keeping their own unlisted stores, refusing the central map. Nothing confirmed, only a driver mentioning an unmarked shed, a councillor declining to answer.

With coffers empty and quotas still binding, the Union ended the year with lights held by hand and a chair still missing in the room where the great powers set rules.
