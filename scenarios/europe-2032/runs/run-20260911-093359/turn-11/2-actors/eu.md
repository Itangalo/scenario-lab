## New measure
**EU Accession Track to the US-China AI Risk Accord**
It seeks formal EU inclusion in the weights-security, escalation and bio-design-tool agreement as a verifying third party, offering EU evaluation and grid-incident evidence in return for access. Why this and why now: it is the only move that costs almost no money or build-out, exploits the one window where safety cooperation is no longer a unilateral cost, and gives a paralysed Union leverage without new funds.

## Priority
Priority is the new Accession Track, because with no measures in flight and capital at 2.0 it is the only instrument that can advance both mandates without procurement Washington has quota-blocked.

## In practice
We task the EEAS and DG CNECT with a single brief: ask for a seat at the verification table, not a rewrite. Council Conclusions mandate the High Representative to approach Washington and Beijing jointly, with the middle-power supply-chain partners as co-sponsors. The offer is concrete: ENISA incident data from the blackout and sabotage wave, the Evaluation Institute's test protocols, and EU weight-security audit practice.

We spend no new fund and table no directive. Health-ministry triage wins and the guarded black-start depots are held on existing budgets and auto-reimbursement pools, under NIS2/CER and the Civil Protection Mechanism. If inclusion is refused, observer status and mutual recognition of evaluations still leaves the Union inside the information flow rather than informed afterwards.
