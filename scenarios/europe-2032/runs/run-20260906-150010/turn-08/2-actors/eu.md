## Priority
**Measures for Joint Threat Response Integration** — because the `joint_threat_response` event creates a time-limited opening to embed the Union in a live, operational security architecture that directly compensates for our weak standalone resilience and underdeveloped evaluation capacity, and missing this window would leave us exposed as bio and cyber threats converge.
