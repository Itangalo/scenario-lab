### A Threshold Crossed

Global AI capability reaches 60.5 this turn as coordinated agent systems demonstrate autonomous execution of multi-day scientific and engineering workflows. Developers report systems now self-initiating subtasks, managing dependencies, and recovering from failure without human intervention — crossing the threshold into persistent goal pursuit. The jump triggers widespread technical reassessment, particularly as offensive cyber tools leveraging similar coordination appear in the wild.

### Sovereignty Push Amid Legal Headwinds

The European Union launches the **Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation**, a large category 4 measure aimed at fast-tracking EU-controlled compute infrastructure with binding public-use obligations. Despite legal pushback from Germany and Ireland, the Commission leverages revised recovery fund incentives to begin negotiations with three member states for co-located sovereign nodes. However, full implementation is delayed, and no binding commitments are secured this turn. As a high-cost initiative, no immediate effect is realized, and the measure remains in early mobilization.

The **European Evaluation Shield** formally begins pilot audits under contested authority. Though board appointments are complete and technical work is underway, the legal challenge filed by a coalition of frontier developers and foreign governments casts a shadow over its legitimacy. No full audit is completed this turn, and the agency’s credibility erodes in public discourse.

### Cyber Resilience Erodes Further

A follow-up wave of AI-driven attacks exploits residual vulnerabilities in municipal systems, compounding earlier hospital disruptions. ENISA reports that adaptive malware bypassed updated filters within hours, underscoring the gap between threat evolution and defensive deployment. Resilience dips as critical infrastructure remains inconsistently hardened.

Public sentiment declines further, driven by visible job displacement in legal and diagnostic sectors and growing resentment toward data centre expansion. The cyber incidents and legal battles around AI oversight feed a narrative of losing control.

### Sovereignty Gains, Political Capital Stretches

**eu_ai_sovereignty** rises to 31.0: +3 from the ongoing **InvestAI Gigafactories**, +3 from the completion of the **Tech sovereignty package** (which finishes this turn and delivers its final sovereignty contribution), offset by −1 due to the 1.0-point rise in **ai_capability**. No gain is attributed to the newly proposed compute mobilisation, as high-cost measures require at least two turns before yielding effects.  
**eu_political_capital** falls slightly to 28.0 under a sustained portfolio charge of −13, despite a +3 proposal bonus for the new sovereignty measure responding directly to recent crises. No sentiment boost applies, as public sentiment remains below political capital.
