The Fundamental Rights Agency and national ombudsmen run sampled audits of public-sector scoring systems, with freezes held until logs are actually read.

We use the defensive breakthrough narrowly: ENISA pushes the proven patching and swarm-detection tooling through the Cyber Recovery Corps playbooks and municipal operators already stood up, without launching a new programme. On the open-weight jump we concede we cannot recall it and instruct the audits and utilities to assume the capability is already distributed, keeping paper rosters and retired operators on call through winter.
