By autumn 2031 mobile Cyber Recovery Corps restored clinics and grid control in served cities, but Brussels single claims window left payouts pending. Commission answered with reallocated emergency package — wage insurance, short retraining, redress and transition desks; cyber-recovery, care and grid courses filled fast.

Redress overtaken by ruling: social-insurance scoring cut/flagged thousands without human review, logs unread, ruled outside 2024 AI Act high-risk categories. Ministers ordered sampled audits, pause deferred. Trust hardened to refusal; council moratorium motions legally unable to take effect. Utilities kept paper rosters and retired operators for winter.

Frontier shifted darker: labs moved to opaque-reasoning models adept at code/intrusion planning, raising deception fears; contested genome-model paper claimed path to human-infecting design. Europe, paying premium under US chip/model controls, watched from sidelines.

Through winter-spring 2032 claims window finally paid out, transition desks steered youth to recovery courses, wage top-ups kept households afloat, queues shortened but town halls stayed hostile as audits crawled, logs unread, freezes never arrived. Commission held line — no new programme, budgets exhausted.

Two technical shifts cut across repair: operators adopted new defensive tooling — automated patching and behaviour-based detection — cutting break-ins at grids/clinics, spread via corps networks. Simultaneously a frontier-class openly released model spread to hundreds of thousands, runnable on private hardware, unrecallable. Utilities kept paper backups, auditors rewrote threats, councils cited loss of control. Relief at fewer outages mixed with dread; polls near zero, Brussels spent out.
