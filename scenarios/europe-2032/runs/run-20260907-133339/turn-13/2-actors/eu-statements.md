# Statements: The European Union (turn 13)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late — but we will no longer act through instruments that depend on cohesion we do not have, or capital we do not possess. Expediency must not deepen paralysis.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Restore political agency and public trust through demonstrable control over AI incidents, transparent accountability, and sovereign response capacity — but we now acknowledge that without sovereign infrastructure, accountability is performative and control is delegated. This turn, we pivot from institutional redress to material reconstitution: our credibility depends not on findings, but on force — the force of independent compute, detection, and response.
- `breakdown_response` (identity): We are a union in systemic breakdown, where decision-making is fractured, capital is exhausted, and trust is gone. Our task is no longer to govern AI, but to survive its consequences long enough to rebuild the capacity to act.
