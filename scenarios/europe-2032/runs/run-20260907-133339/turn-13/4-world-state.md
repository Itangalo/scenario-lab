### The Unraveling

AI capability rose to 73.5 as automated systems began passing rigorous benchmarks in legal contract validation and clinical diagnostics, driven by newly automated verification in structural engineering and medical coding. With no safeguards deployed, safety plummeted to 3.0—opaque reasoning loops proliferated, undetected even in high-stakes public systems. Resilience eroded further to 32.0 as probing attacks on energy and transport infrastructure intensified, exploiting open-weight models now capable of sustained cyber reconnaissance.

Public sentiment dipped to 6.0 amid visible job losses in law, accounting, and software, where entry-level hiring vanished. Media outlets highlighted graduates queuing for warehouse jobs while AI staffed corporate back offices. The European Union, stripped of credibility, faced open mockery for its abandoned Sovereign Inference Foundry—plans that assumed control over defence and space assets collapsed when the European Defence Agency, ESA, and TSMC Dresden refused to act without legal authority or export clearance.

In response, the EU launched the EU Crisis Observership for AI-Driven Disruption, a small transparency measure hosted under the European Political Community. Designed to compile and publish real-time data on AI incidents and job losses, it aimed to restore minimal trust. Yet with no enforcement power, no budget beyond existing agency allocations, and reliance on voluntary member-state reporting, its impact was muted. Early bulletins were delayed by data-sharing disputes, and the first release was dismissed as incomplete.

The EU formally canceled the Sovereign Inference Foundry Initiative, acknowledging it had operated on false assumptions of authority. The move was honest but costly—political capital remained at 0.0, pinned by the −3 charge from the ongoing Accountability Tribunal and the new −2 for the Observership, offset by no legitimacy. The priority cost −1, bringing total portfolio charge to −6. No proposal bonus was earned: the Observership, while timely, was small and reactive, not a response to a specific recent event. Sovereignty fell to 3.0 as capability rose and no domestic capacity was secured, compounded by the denial of frontier model access to EU institutions.
