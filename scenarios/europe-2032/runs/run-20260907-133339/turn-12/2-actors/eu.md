This is not ideal — it is minimal, fragmented, and opaque by necessity — but it is the only path left to create a sovereign inference backbone that can eventually host the Tribunal’s investigations, power the Trust Corps’ monitoring, and harden the Cyber Shield.

We are not abandoning accountability — we are trying to save the ground it stands on. But without sovereign compute, their findings will be irrelevant — processed, delayed, or suppressed by systems we do not control. This Foundry is not a replacement for democratic legitimacy. It is the last scaffold on which it might be rebuilt.
