## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for the Municipal Repair Bridge to finish landing and for any capital to rebuild before starting anything new.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
M8 EU Municipal Repair and Displacement Bridge, because with capital at 2 and sentiment at 3 it is the only instrument still buying clinics, registries and retraining slots that keep fragmentation from becoming irreversible.

## In practice
We keep the Commission reprogramming decision in force through EPSCO and the Committee of the Regions, with ENISA certifying clean rebuilds and labour ministries disbursing vouchers and wage-bridge payments. Where audits stall we move to flat-rate vouchers already cleared; where cohorts fill we copy that delivery model rather than rewriting rules.

We use the middle-power coordination framework only to hold aligned screening of robot control software and the joint observer bid, spending no new money abroad. To the mayoral pact we offer power-price transparency and a pause-plus-review on grid connections in exchange for lifting physical blockades on permitted builds, without opening a new build programme this turn.
