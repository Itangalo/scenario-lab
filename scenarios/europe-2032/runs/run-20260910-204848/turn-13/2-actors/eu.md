## New measure
None this turn. We are waiting for political capital and public sentiment to rebuild enough to fund anything without triggering collapse after the winter clinic failure.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
No priority measure to name this turn, because with no measures in flight and capital at 1.0 we cannot credibly push a new instrument until emergency diversions are stabilised.

## In practice
We keep clinics and registries running through existing national emergency health powers and ENISA-supported manual workarounds, with the Commission publicly taking responsibility for the EU-funded rebuilds that failed rather than blaming municipalities. No new EU fund or programme is opened; labour ministries continue already-committed voucher payments where audits allow.

We use the Health Council and EPSCO only to coordinate patient diversions and to publish a joint audit-plus-fix plan for the failed workarounds, offering the mayoral pact and logistics unions transparency on what broke in exchange for holding cooperation through winter, without launching builds or retraining cohorts we cannot pay for.
