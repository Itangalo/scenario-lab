## New measure
**EU Municipal Repair and Displacement Bridge**
It funds rapid repair of hit municipal/hospital IT via existing ENISA/HERA channels plus immediate income-bridge and retraining slots for logistics workers displaced by foreign robots, using cohesion and ESF+ money already committed.
Why this and why now: with capital at 5, sentiment at 10 and mayors blocking builds, only a small visible repair-and-jobs bridge can stop fragmentation and reopen room to build; it is category 7 Labour and social protection, cheap and fast, directly serving our survive-by-resilience-and-trust commitment.

## Priority
EU Municipal Repair and Displacement Bridge, because without stemming municipal anger and union protests no sovereignty build or cyber hardening can proceed.

## In practice
We run it through EPSCO and the Committee of the Regions, not as new legislation: Commission implementing decision to reprogramme ESF+ and cohesion envelopes for clean-up vouchers to affected municipalities/hospitals and wage-bridge for logistics hubs, ENISA certifying clean rebuilds.

We pair it with the middle-power coalition line in the Council: no new spend abroad, just aligned export-licence scrutiny on robot control software and joint compute bargaining, while offering mayors transparency on power prices and a pause-plus-review on grid connections to lift the physical blockades.
