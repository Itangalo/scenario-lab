## New measure
**EU Humanitarian Clinical Access and Embodied-Work Continuity Bridge**
Deploys the arriving tailored therapies through the rationing corps and neutral escrow channels while extending continuity protection to care, logistics and industrial maintenance crews facing embodied AI displacement. Category 6 Preparedness and resilience – why this and why now: with services degraded-alive, sentiment at 1 and capital at 7, the medical breakthrough is the only event that can lift public tolerance for continued rationing, but only if it reaches ordinary clinics on EU terms rather than as another demonstration of dependence, and robots arriving in the same semester mean no time to separate health continuity from work continuity.

## Priority
M11 EU Humanitarian Clinical Access and Embodied-Work Continuity Bridge, because keeping essentials alive now depends on turning the one positive shock into delivered care rather than another dependence lesson.

## In practice
We execute through HERA, EMA emergency use, and the EuroHPC/Essential Inference Continuity structures already guarding triage, payroll and settlement: pooled inference slots are re-weighted to diagnostics and tailored-therapy planning, ENISA-hardened hospital pathways get first spares from the Swiss-escrow track DG TRADE is now asked to fund and formalise, and civil-protection repair crews escort cold-chain and maintenance.

On embodied AI we do not open a new industrial programme we cannot fund. We attach logistics, hospital-porter, mine-clearance and facility-maintenance roles to the existing Transition Fund delivery chain and EPSCO social-partner agreements, with procurement guidance favouring repairable, EU-serviceable machines and retraining tied to care and grid-repair corps, while tabling armed-logistics concerns in the middle-power forum without spending our last capital on a treaty we cannot enforce.
