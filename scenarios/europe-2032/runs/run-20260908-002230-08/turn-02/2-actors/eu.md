## In practice  
We are acting on the emergent_asml_defiance_contingency not as a legal curiosity but as a strategic inflection: the semiconductor supply chain is now a frontline of AI sovereignty. The ASML Strategic Shield Initiative will be proposed under Article 207 TFEU (common commercial policy) and Article 346 (security exceptions), establishing a binding EU export control regime that overrides conflicting third-country demands. The European Commission, in coordination with the High Representative, will draft an Implementing Regulation designating EU-origin advanced lithography systems as strategic assets, requiring dual authorization for any export — one from the member state of origin, one from a newly convened European Strategic Export Control Board (SECB), composed of rotating member-state representatives, industry observers, and security agencies. To prevent paralysis, we are introducing a fast-track approval path for trusted partners (Japan, South Korea, Canada) while mandating full review for jurisdictions under coercive pressure. A €6bn contingency fund, drawn from the European Defence Fund and repurposed Recovery and Resilience Facility reserves, will compensate firms for lost contracts and fund alternative market development in neutral jurisdictions.

At the same time, we are advancing the Cyber Shield Directive by finalising the Joint Cyber Resilience Board’s charter under ENISA, with binding audit rights for dual-use infrastructure now extended to include AI-coordinated intrusion testing every quarter. Germany and the Nordics have accepted the framework, but France and Poland continue to resist full audit access; we are offering a compromise: national red teams may conduct the exercises under EU supervision, preserving operational secrecy while ensuring compliance. The €12bn Modernisation Envelope is being disbursed through the Digital Europe Programme, with 70% pre-allocated to member states scoring below 50 on resilience. For InvestAI Gigafactories, we have completed environmental assessments in Rhine-Ruhr and northern Spain, with final site confirmation delayed in eastern Poland due to water stress. We are activating the TEN-E legal base to prioritise grid connections, but local opposition is intensifying — we are responding with a LIFE-funded mediation task force to co-design water recycling protocols with municipalities. The Tech Sovereignty Package is advancing in Parliament, where the 30% renewable cap has defused energy concerns; we are now negotiating with IMCO to link accelerated permitting to workforce training commitments, tying diffusion to social stability.
