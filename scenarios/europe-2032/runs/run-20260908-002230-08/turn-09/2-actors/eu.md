## In practice
The Sovereign Compute Initiative remains our anchor. We are now invoking Article 114 TFEU to override national land-use objections in Finland and Poland, treating the compute buildout as a matter of internal market integrity and collective security. Legal teams at the Commission are preparing infringement proceedings against the two member states blocking site approvals, while parallel negotiations with ASML and Intel Europa intensify under the Chips Act’s national security clause. We are offering STMicroelectronics expanded R&D grants and workforce training subsidies in exchange for packaging-line prioritisation on EU-controlled lithography nodes. These sites will host secure enclaves with direct access for the European AI Office and ENISA, enabling real-time red-teaming of models at or above 60.0 capability — not just evaluation, but active containment.

The Frontier Access Corridor finishes this turn: while it established the legal principle of compulsory model access for market operation, it delivered no actual infrastructure to run those models. That gap is now critical. The cyber_major_incident — a model-generated ransomware sweep across public health systems in three member states — exploited exactly this: attackers used autonomous agents to chain vulnerabilities faster than human defenders could respond, and the EU had no sovereign instance of the relevant models to simulate countermeasures. ENISA-CERT coordinated a patch rollout, but only after days of disruption. We are now launching the **Sovereign Red Line Enforcement Network**, a Category 4 measure that builds the missing operational layer: distributed, hardened compute nodes across six member states, legally mandated to host and counter high-capability AI systems. This is not about market access — it is about survival. We will fund it from the emergency reserve under the Civil Protection Mechanism, and deploy it through the EuroHPC JU, with red-teaming authority granted directly to the AI Office’s Red Cell. Without this, every rule we pass is a line drawn in sand before the tide.
