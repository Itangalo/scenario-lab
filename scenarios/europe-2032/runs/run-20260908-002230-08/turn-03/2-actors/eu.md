## In practice  
We are treating the bio_incident as a system-wide wake-up call. The Emergency Resilience Surge will be activated under Article 122 TFEU (serious disturbances in the operation of markets) and coordinated through the Joint Crisis Management Protocol. The European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC) and ENISA will jointly issue binding technical standards within 60 days: all high-containment labs must deploy AI-augmented anomaly detection in genetic synthesis workflows, and all critical infrastructure operators must implement AI-driven intrusion detection with real-time reporting to national CSIRTs. A €9bn emergency envelope, drawn from unspent MFF allocations and the Civil Protection Mechanism, will fund immediate upgrades, with 75% front-loaded to member states scoring below 50 on resilience. We are fast-tracking procurement through the EU’s emergency framework, bypassing normal tendering rules.

At the same time, we are renegotiating the ASML Strategic Shield Initiative in light of Washington’s coercive move. The original plan assumed we could assert control; now we must build redundancy. We are shifting focus from export control to domestic capability: redirecting part of the contingency fund to support a pilot EU-owned lithography maintenance and upgrade hub, hosted in Belgium with German engineering and French security oversight. This is not a replacement for ASML, but a hedge. We are also accelerating the Cyber Shield Directive’s final implementation: the compromised audit arrangement will stand for now, but we are introducing a sunset clause — after turn 4, full EU audit rights will activate unless member states demonstrate equivalent national capabilities. For InvestAI Gigafactories, we are suspending new site approvals pending a strategic review of demand in light of the ai_investment_collapse; instead, we are repurposing €30bn of the fund to retrofit existing data centres with enhanced physical and cyber protections. The Tech Sovereignty Package continues its parliamentary path, but we are decoupling the workforce training requirement from permitting — it remains incentivised, not mandated — to preserve speed. Public communications will tie all actions to the bio_incident response, framing resilience as the foundation of sovereignty.
