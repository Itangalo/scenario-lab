## In practice
We are treating the emergent_open_bio_toolkits and opaque_reasoning events as a systemic wake-up call: the tools to design and simulate biological agents are now outside state control, and the systems meant to detect or contain misuse cannot see what they cannot interpret. The Sovereign Resilience Surge will activate under the Crisis Coordination Mechanism in the Digital Services Act and the NIS2 directive, with binding mandates issued by the ENISA-CERT network to all critical operators. By turn 8, every energy grid operator, water authority, and major hospital network must deploy AI-augmented intrusion detection with zero-trust architecture, backed by EU-level red-teaming exercises simulating coordinated bio-cyber attacks. We are unlocking €45bn from the Digital Europe Programme and the Recovery and Resilience Facility — reallocated from stalled broadband projects — to fund rapid deployment, with 30% reserved for cross-border substitution plans so that no single point of failure can collapse essential services.

At the same time, we are pushing the Evaluation Corps into operational readiness ahead of schedule: the first 120 evaluators will be seconded from national cybersecurity agencies by mid-turn, granted secure access to model weights under Article 16 TFEU, and equipped with formal verification tooling developed in partnership with INRIA and Fraunhofer. The InvestAI Gigafactories remain on track, but we are now conditioning final disbursements on participation in both the red-teaming programme and the Resilience Surge’s monitoring layer — no EU-subsidised compute without full transparency and incident readiness. The finished Tech Sovereignty Package is being rebranded as “AI Infrastructure with Accountability,” with local benefit clauses now law: every data centre must host a regional retraining hub and contribute to grid resilience. Public messaging will tie the Resilience Surge directly to the Bavaria sabotage — not as justification, but as proof that security and legitimacy must rise together. We are not building walls. We are building systems that hold.
