### A Blind Spot Widens

Global AI capability advances to 63.0, driven by stabilised agent architectures now operating across cyber and materials domains without human-readable reasoning. The frontier no longer explains itself—interpretability tools fail as internal representations shift to non-linguistic tensors. A major release underperforms expectations, and spot prices for top-tier inference drop, sparking debate over whether progress is plateauing. The evidence is ambiguous, but the shift is real: oversight based on audit trails is obsolete.

Open-weight models reach 52.0, boosted by replication of simplified safety techniques and the rapid spread of open bioengineering toolkits. These distributed frameworks, combining genome design with cloud simulation, are now in use by research-adjacent and non-state actors, dramatically lowering the barrier to niche pathogenicity and environmental manipulation. Containment is impossible.

The EU responds with urgency. The Sovereign Resilience Surge launches as a large category 6 measure, aiming to harden energy, water, healthcare, and transport systems against AI-enabled threats. Binding standards are issued, backed by €45bn in redirected funds. ENISA-CERT mandates zero-trust architectures and cross-border red-teaming by turn 8. However, rollout is uneven—some member states resist centralised mandates, and technical bottlenecks delay deployment.

The Sovereign Evaluation Corps accelerates recruitment, drawing 120 experts from national agencies, but lab access remains contested. No frontier developer has granted full model weight access, and legal challenges loom. The completed Tech Sovereignty Package is rebranded with accountability clauses, but its impact is muted.

Public sentiment holds at 30.0, as backlash over job losses and the Bavaria sabotage persists. Messaging ties resilience to legitimacy, but trust in AI governance erodes further.

### Transatlantic Drift

The 2028 U.S. election outcome takes effect: a narrow victory for the candidate favouring strategic competition and export controls. The new administration adopts a posture of **US_POSTURE: CONSOLIDATION**, rationing frontier access by geopolitical tier. This accelerates EU sovereignty decay, as dependence on foreign compute deepens amid stalled domestic capacity.
