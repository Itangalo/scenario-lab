## In practice
We are acting on the intrusion discovered in autumn 2026 not as an isolated breach but as a signal: the frontier in AI is now the frontier in attack. The Cyber Shield Directive will be proposed under Article 114 TFEU for internal market harmonisation, routed through the ENISA agency with binding standards drafted by a new Joint Cyber Resilience Board composed of national CIRTs, energy and water regulators, and port authorities. Member states will resist additional reporting and the cost of AI red-teaming tools, so we are pairing the directive with a €12bn Modernisation Envelope under the Digital Europe Programme, front-loaded to 2027–2028, to cover 70% of compliance costs for operators in lower-resilience states. The Council’s Competitiveness formation will be the decisive venue, where Germany and the Nordic states support the measure but France and Poland demand exemptions for national defence-linked infrastructure — we are countering with a carve-in for dual-use systems that allows EU-level audit without compromising sovereignty claims.

At the same time, we are advancing the InvestAI Gigafactories by fast-tracking environmental assessments under the Trans-European Networks for Energy (TEN-E) legal base, leveraging the urgency of energy demand from AI to justify grid prioritisation. The first site selection — Rhine-Ruhr, northern Spain, and eastern Poland — will be announced by Q1 2027, with state aid approvals already pre-cleared under Article 107(3)(c). Resistance from local communities over power and water use is growing, especially in drought-affected regions, so we are launching a parallel public consultation under the Aarhus Convention, funded through LIFE programme resources, to avoid delays. The Tech sovereignty package is being pushed through the European Parliament’s IMCO and ITRE committees, where concerns about land use and energy inflation are strong; we are amending the proposal to cap data centre power draw per region at 30% of local renewable capacity, a compromise that preserves momentum without sacrificing long-term scale.
