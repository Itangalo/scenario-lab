### Frontier Breaks the 60 Threshold  
`ai_capability` advances by +1.0 to 60.5, as integrated agent loops in engineering design and biomedical validation demonstrate autonomous multi-day task execution. The milestone triggers broader recognition of post-junior displacement across technical professions.

### Open Access Widens  
With distilled training methods now shared openly and fine-tuning pipelines democratized, `openweight_capability` rises to 47.0 — closing within 13.5 points of the frontier. Capable models are now accessible to well-resourced academic labs and mid-tier firms, accelerating misuse potential.

### Safety in Freefall  
The major cyber incident, enabled by uninterpretable agent behaviour in a compromised dependency chain, reveals systemic blind spots. No safeguards were in place on deployed systems. `ai_safety` plummets by −5 to 7.0, reflecting eroded confidence and the widening gap between capability and control.

### Resilience Holds — But Only Just  
The Emergency Resilience Surge’s detection frameworks helped contain the cyber incident’s spread, preventing total collapse in public services. However, no new category 6 measure completes this turn, and the lack of follow-up investment leaves critical gaps. `resilience` remains flat at 51.0.

### Sovereignty Erodes Further  
The Frontier Access Restoration Initiative concludes without securing access, failing to counter U.S. consolidation. Meanwhile, `ai_capability` rising by +1.0 triggers a −1 decay under rule 5. No category 4 completion bonus applies this turn. The Sovereign Agent Foundation Stack is newly proposed and yields no immediate benefit. Net effect: 21.0 − 1 = 20.0.

### Political Capital Under Strain  
Portfolio charge: Sovereign Agent Foundation Stack (−3), priority (−1) = −4. No measure finishes to yield a completion bonus. A +2 proposal bonus is granted for launching a large, urgent category 4 measure in direct response to `cyber_major_incident` and `eu_frontier_access_denied` within three turns. No sentiment-driven legitimacy boost applies. Net change: −4 + 2 = −2 → 25.0.

### Public Sentiment Deteriorates  
Media coverage frames the cyber incident as a failure of both foreign dependency and EU oversight. The launch of a sovereign agent stack is met with scepticism — seen as reactive and underfunded. Labour groups warn of automation waves. `public_sentiment` drops by −3 to 28.0.

### From Diplomacy to Fallback  
The EU shifts strategy: with frontier access still blocked, the Commission launches the Sovereign Agent Foundation Stack as a minimal operational fallback. Member-state buy-in is partial; Germany and France commit resources, but Eastern members cite budget constraints. The stack targets critical functions but faces technical and coordination hurdles. Informal minilateral efforts persist, undermining cohesion.
