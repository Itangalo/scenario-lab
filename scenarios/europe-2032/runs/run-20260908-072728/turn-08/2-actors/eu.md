## New measure

**Sovereign Agent Foundation Stack**  
Launch a targeted, fast-track programme to deliver a minimal but operationally resilient EU-run agent stack for critical public functions — cyber incident response, medical diagnostics verification, and infrastructure monitoring — that can run on domestic compute and be secured to a state-actor standard.

`Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 10`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase `eu_ai_sovereignty` by +4 through operational sovereign capability; contribute +2 to `resilience` by hardening essential services against cascading failure in absence of frontier access; begin closing the gap between openweight and deployed safety by ensuring EU-controlled systems meet baseline assurance thresholds.  
`Applies to:` EU institutions, selected member-state agencies, European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking, and vetted public research labs.

## Priority

Sovereign Agent Foundation Stack — Because the Frontier Access Restoration Initiative has not yet yielded results, and with `ai_safety` now at crisis levels and openweight models nearing dangerous thresholds, waiting for access is no longer a strategy — it is a surrender of agency. This stack is not about matching the frontier; it is about ensuring the EU can still function when the frontier fails to serve it.

## In practice

This turn, we shift from diplomacy to deployment. With the Frontier Access Restoration Initiative now complete but access still blocked by external pressures, we activate the fallback track in earnest. The Commission, through DG CONNECT and the European AI Office, launches a fast-track call under the Digital Europe Programme and the EU’s semiconductor sovereignty fund to stand up a minimal sovereign agent stack — not a full frontier competitor, but a hardened, auditable system for essential public functions. We allocate €3.8bn from the unused digital tranche of the Recovery and Resilience Facility, unlocked only to member states that commit compute and personnel to the joint stack. The European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking repurposes part of LUMI’s reserved capacity for this purpose, under EU legal jurisdiction and with physical access controls meeting state-actor standards. We convene a crisis council of member-state CIOs and national security advisors to define non-negotiable use cases: real-time cyber threat correlation, validation of AI-generated medical results, and autonomous infrastructure monitoring during outages. To secure buy-in from the Parliament’s Industry Committee, we offer them co-drafting rights on the security and transparency framework, ensuring this does not become a backdoor for unchecked autonomy. This is not a replacement for access — it is the floor beneath it. Without it, every crisis deepens our dependency. With it, we ensure that even in blackout, the Union still has eyes and hands.
