### The Open Frontier Widens  
Global AI capability climbs to 67.5 as the undisclosed architectural breakthrough continues to compound, enabling agents to coordinate across domains with minimal oversight. The diffusion of knowledge and model weights from earlier frontier releases accelerates, aided by improved distillation techniques and distributed training on consumer-grade hardware. `openweight_capability` rises to 53.0 — reflecting steady accumulation rather than a sudden rupture — as offensive cyber tools, synthetic media generators, and planning modules become more capable and accessible. The growth is substantial but remains within the bounds of `ai_capability`, with no single release or technique closing the gap abruptly.

### Cyber Storm Hits  
A coordinated ransomware campaign, powered by AI-generated exploits, cripples municipal services in Belgium and northern Italy. Emergency response systems are delayed, and recovery takes weeks. The attack’s sophistication confirms that offensive AI is no longer confined to state actors. Defenders are reactive, not proactive. With no finished resilience measure in place, `resilience` drops to 54.0.

### EU Shifts Tactics  
The Open-Weight Containment Protocol collapses under legal challenges from Poland and Hungary, and cloud providers win a preliminary injunction. The EU begins drafting a new transparency-based response — a proposed Open-Weight Rapid Evaluation and Public Alert System — to assess and publicly rate high-risk open models. The framework is still in early development, with technical specifications and legal basis under review. It will not stop distribution, but aims to erode trust in unsafe systems over time. No implementation occurs this turn; the measure will require at least one full turn before any partial effect can emerge.

The Sovereign Agent Foundation Stack reaches formal completion on schedule, with hardened agent nodes activated in Lyon, Warsaw, and Helsinki. However, integration into EU crisis response protocols is only partial: deployment is fragmented across member states, with inconsistent access and limited interoperability. While the infrastructure is online, its operational utility remains constrained. As a result, the boost to strategic autonomy is meaningful but incomplete. The measure contributes +3 to `eu_ai_sovereignty` this turn, rather than the full +5, with further gains possible in future turns as integration deepens. This increase is partially offset by a −1 reduction due to global capability growth, resulting in a net rise from 17.0 to 19.0.

### Political Strain Deepens  
The Emergency Resilience Surge remains the priority, but progress is slow. The cyber incident, combined with ongoing fragmentation, drags `public_sentiment` down to 22.0 as protests near data centres grow. A mid-tier member state signs a bilateral deal with a U.S. hyperscaler, further undermining unity. `eu_political_capital` remains under pressure, unchanged from last turn.
