## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Anchor strategic autonomy in sovereign AI capacity and resilient diffusion, ensuring the EU can act independently when frontier access fails — but with openweight capability now at 53.0 and ai_safety collapsed to 1.0, we cannot treat diffusion as a vector of empowerment unless it is first contained. Our commitment must now split: we will build sovereignty *and* containment in parallel, not sequence them. The open frontier is not a platform; it is a breach.
  - Grounds: The cyber storm confirms that offensive AI is now widely accessible and operationally effective without central coordination. With `ai_safety` at 1.0, there are no safeguards in circulation, and the Open-Weight Rapid Evaluation system has not yet delivered even partial awareness. We can no longer assume that distributed capability will naturally align with public good — nor that we have time to build upward before the floor gives way. The reversal does not abandon our goal of strategic autonomy, but forces us to defend the conditions for it.

This demands a shift from decision-making under risk to survival under obscurity.
