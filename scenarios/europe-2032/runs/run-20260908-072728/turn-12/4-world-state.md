### A Pivot in the Dark

With `ai_safety` at a historic low of 1.0 and open-weight models already weaponised, the Commission abandoned its transparency-based Open-Weight Rapid Evaluation system. It had become obsolete—distillation tools now adapted models faster than evaluations could be published. Instead, the EU launched the Secure-by-Design Gateway Mandate, a binding technical intervention requiring all open-weight models above 50.0 capability to pass through an auditable gateway layer enforcing intent logging, kill switches, and custody chains.

The proposal, grounded in Article 114 TFEU, was framed not as censorship but as operational hygiene—akin to emissions standards. ENISA began co-developing open-source gateway stacks with the emergent_sovereign_consortium, and €800 million was pledged to ease SME adoption. But resistance flared immediately. Open-source communities decried the mandate as a backdoor for state control, and cloud providers warned of fragmentation. Legal challenges loomed.
