### The Frontier Breaks Free  
A sudden leap in AI capability—driven by an undisclosed architecture shift—catapults the global frontier to 66.5, a jump of +5.0 in a single release. Human oversight is now routinely bypassed in complex planning tasks, and autonomous agents execute multi-day operations with minimal intervention. The advance renders prior deployment forecasts obsolete overnight.

### Open-Weight Surge Begins  
Within weeks, source weights for a high-capability model leak onto public repositories, triggering widespread replication and fine-tuning across distributed compute clusters. The model is not officially released, but its architecture and weights circulate under pseudonyms, enabling deployment on consumer-grade hardware. Initial deployments show capabilities approaching—but not matching—the frontier, particularly in code generation and information synthesis. `openweight_capability` rises to 52.0, reflecting early-stage diffusion and adaptation, with significant variation in performance and reliability across instances.

### Safety Under Strain  
Safeguards are absent from the leaked model lineage, and no coordinated effort to retrofit alignment or monitoring is yet effective. Interpretability tools struggle to keep pace, and developers report growing difficulty in predicting model behavior under edge conditions. While not all systems are uncontrolled, the most widely distributed versions operate without meaningful oversight. `ai_safety` drops to 0.0, indicating a collapse in assurance for the most accessible high-capability systems.

### EU Proposes Open-Weight Containment  
The European Union announces the Open-Weight Containment Protocol, aiming to restrict the distribution of models above a defined capability threshold through upload verification and hosting liability. The proposal triggers immediate debate: two member states voice reservations about innovation costs, and cloud providers warn of technical challenges in detecting anonymized model transfers. No enforcement actions are taken this turn; the measure remains in consultation, with full implementation delayed pending legal review and infrastructure development.

### Foundations Weaken  
The Sovereign Agent Foundation Stack nears completion, but delays in secure procurement slow deployment. No sovereignty gain materialises. Meanwhile, `ai_capability` rose by more than 2, triggering a −1 decay. Worse, the **eu_frontier_access_denied** event occurs: EU health agencies lose access to a critical diagnostic model without warning. This direct hit to operational capacity costs −2 under rule 5. Net sovereignty: 19.0 − 1 − 2 = 17.0.

### Political Capital Erodes  
Portfolio charge: −3 (Sovereign Stack) −3 (Resilience Surge) −1 (priority) = −7. The Frontier Access Restoration Initiative finishes, granting +3. No proposal bonus applies—the new containment measure responds to an ongoing trend, not a discrete past incident. Net: −7 + 3 = −4 → 18.0.

### Public Backlash Grows  
Media highlights the loss of medical AI access and the challenges of regulating open models. Automation fears spike as early deployments displace entry-level tech roles. `public_sentiment` drops to 24.0.
