### A Surge and a Split

Global AI capability leapt forward this turn, rising by +6 to **86.0**, as a major developer released a system demonstrating discontinuous advances in autonomous scientific reasoning and real-time adaptation under eval conditions. The jump confirmed that frontier models are no longer merely scaling but rearchitecting themselves mid-training. Open-weight models followed, climbing to **79.7**, narrowing the gap as older frontier weights leaked into distributed networks.

The EU completed two measures: the **Strategic Compute Reserve** became operational, and **Distributed Verification Nodes** were deployed across critical infrastructure. These delivered partial technical gains, but the moment of delivery was overshadowed. The new **Sovereign Medical AI Verification Authority** launched amid fierce debate. While legally established, Italy and Spain suspended implementation pending European Court review, citing patient access concerns. The EMA gained authority—but not universal compliance.

AI safety, already at crisis levels, fell further to **3.0** due to the capability jump and lack of safeguards landing on new systems. Resilience dipped slightly as resources shifted toward medical verification, leaving cyber response teams undermanned during a spike in automated probing of health databases.

Political capital declined to **5.0**. The portfolio charge remained high: −3 for the Evaluative Research Programme, −3 for the new large measure, −1 for the priority, totaling −7. A modest proposal bonus of +2 was granted for directly addressing the recent medical breakthrough with a large, timely measure. No sentiment-driven boost applied. The completion bonus was consumed by ongoing costs.

Public sentiment edged up slightly to **32.0**, reflecting initial approval for patient safeguards, though media coverage grew polarized. Some hailed the EU’s “ethical backbone,” others decried “bureaucratic obstruction” in a time of healing.

Sovereignty ticked down to **18.0**: the capability rise cost −1, and despite completing compute infrastructure last turn, no new category 4 gain applied this turn. No event secured or severed access.

### Signals in the Fog

Rumours spread of informal collectives—AI-augmented doctors, diagnosticians, and therapists—offering cross-border second opinions, challenging centralized control. Meanwhile, Finland and the Netherlands began sharing red-teaming results independently, testing models beyond official channels.
