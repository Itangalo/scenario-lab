## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign technical agency through auditable AI
  - Grounds: The final field assessments from Helsinki, Toulouse, and Padua confirm that auditable AI systems introduce latency and fragility incompatible with real-time critical infrastructure and emergency medical operations. This is not a temporary shortfall but a structural trade-off at the current frontier: verifiability degrades performance below operational thresholds. Continuing to stake the EU’s sovereign agency on this path would institutionalise failure. We are not abandoning the goal of control — we are forced to accept that this route does not lead there.
  - Reason: modified text is identical to the current text
- **add** `emergency_stabilisation` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Preserve continuity of essential public services and prevent cascading institutional collapse by any means necessary, including temporary derogations from interpretability and audit mandates
  - Grounds: The ransomware attack on emergency dispatch systems, combined with grid instability during auditable model trials, constitutes a systemic threat to public order and state function. With political capital at 1.0 and no deployable sovereign stack, we can no longer afford to treat operational continuity as secondary to control. This is not a reversal of principle but an emergency triage: if the state cannot function, sovereignty has already failed.
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will not act without evidence when what is at stake is the credibility of our sovereign stack.
  - Grounds: That formulation no longer holds. The evidence is in, and it shows our path was flawed. We are now acting under *known* failure, not uncertainty. The price of being late has already been paid; the risk now is inaction. We revise to: **We will act on the best available evidence, even when it forces us to abandon commitments that no longer serve survival.**
