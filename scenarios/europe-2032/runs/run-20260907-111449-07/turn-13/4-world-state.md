### The Emergency Directive

The European Commission invokes Article 122a TFEU, declaring an operational emergency and adopting the Emergency Continuity Directive. Non-auditable foreign AI systems are conditionally authorised in grid management and emergency dispatch under strict containment protocols. Germany and France agree to suspend legal challenges, but Italy delays, citing constitutional concerns over foreign algorithmic control in public services. Technical guidance from DG HOME and ENISA is issued, mandating air-gapped deployment and human oversight, but implementation lags as member states dispute monitoring authority.
