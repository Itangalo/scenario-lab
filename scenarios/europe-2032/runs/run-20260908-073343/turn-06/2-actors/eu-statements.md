- **add** `secure_control_before_capability` — applied
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will ensure the EU can observe, interpret, and interrupt frontier AI systems operating within or affecting its territory, regardless of origin, before their capability reaches 60.0
  - Grounds: At 59.0, the global frontier is one step from end-to-end autonomous project execution. With ai_safety at 12.0 — lower than at any point in the last four years — and no sign of safety catching up, we can no longer rely on developer goodwill or post-hoc regulation. Control must be secured now, not promised for later. This commitment binds us to build sovereign technical capacity to monitor, audit, and if necessary halt systems that operate beyond human oversight, even if they are foreign-built or cloud-hosted. It is not a retreat from cooperation; it is the precondition for it.
