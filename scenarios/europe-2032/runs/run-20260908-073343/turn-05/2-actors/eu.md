## New measure
### Strengthen Resilience of Critical Infrastructure Against Autonomous AI Threats  
Mandate threat-informed red-teaming and continuous cyber-physical stress-testing across energy, transport, health and finance sectors, with binding remediation timelines and centralised incident simulation led by ENISA.

## Priority
**Strengthen Resilience of Critical Infrastructure Against Autonomous AI Threats** — because the leap in ai_capability to 58.0 has already demonstrated autonomous planning in adversarial contexts, and with ai_safety at just 14.0, we cannot assume containment; our priority must shift from securing compute to ensuring survival when systems fail.

## In practice
This turn, we activate the European Cyber Resilience Board to lead a new wave of sector-specific exercises simulating AI-driven attacks that exploit cascading failures in interdependent systems. Using authority under the revised NIS2 Directive, we require national regulators to enforce participation from all operators of essential services, with ENISA issuing standardised attack profiles derived from the emergent_lab_cooperation findings — particularly those involving persistent agent behaviour and recursive goal exploitation. We are deploying €18bn from the Digital Europe Programme and REACT-EU recovery funds to co-finance automated detection upgrades, including AI-powered anomaly tracking and hardware-enforced execution sandboxing in industrial control systems.

Resistance is expected from mid-tier providers citing cost and complexity, so we are introducing a fast-track certification regime: entities that complete baseline hardening by turn 6 gain preferential access to future EU procurement and liability shielding during declared AI incidents. At the same time, we are expanding the mandate of the Joint Cyber Unit to include standing rapid-response teams equipped with forensic AI analysis tools, trained to deploy within hours of a major breach. This is not about building faster — it is about staying upright when the next shock hits. The frontier may be moving beyond us, but we will not let it move through us.
