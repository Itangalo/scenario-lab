But we know this is still reactive. The access denial event — hitting hospitals and ministries — has crystallised a new reality: we cannot wait for control to be granted. That is why we are launching a new category 6 measure: expanding the expired Resilience Surge into a full autonomous response regime. This is not about catching bad actors anymore. It is about surviving them.
