## New measure
### EU Sovereign Deployment Corridor  
Establish a legally and physically secured infrastructure stack—compute, networking, and control interfaces—for running critical AI systems under EU jurisdiction, with no dependency on foreign operators or platforms.

Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 6  
Targeted effect: eu_ai_sovereignty +15–20, resilience +5–8 via guaranteed operational continuity of essential services  
Applies to: EU institutions, national governments, and designated critical operators required to run sovereign workloads on the corridor

## Priority  
**Cyber Shield for Critical Infrastructure** — because the detection gap revealed by recent intrusions is actively enabling harm, and without trusted visibility into AI-coordinated threats across sectors, no sovereign capability can be safely exercised or verified.

## In practice  
This turn, we finalise the legal activation of the European Cyber Resilience Board under Article 13b of NIS2, granting it authority to issue binding technical standards by April and enforce compliance through national regulators. ENISA is now drafting detection templates focused on cross-system, slow-burn AI intrusions, drawing on forensic data from the recent attacks. We release €12bn in blended funding—70% grants from the Digital Europe Programme and Recovery and Resilience Facility—to co-finance adoption, with disbursement tied to quarterly milestones. Early compliance unlocks fast-track access to digital modernisation funds, while laggards risk conditional suspension of cohesion fund eligibility.

Behind the scenes, we are in closed negotiation with three major industrial cybersecurity vendors—Darktrace, Trend Micro, and a consortium led by Siemens—to open-source lightweight versions of their AI-monitoring stacks, resolving IP disputes through mediation by DG CONNECT. This is not just about patching systems; it is about proving the EU can set operational tempo in resilience, even as it lags in raw capability. The Sovereign Deployment Corridor, meanwhile, begins technical scoping this quarter: DG DEFIS and EDA are mapping redundant high-speed links between existing InvestAI sites, with legal anchoring under the Digital Markets Act’s gatekeeper provisions to prevent foreign override.
