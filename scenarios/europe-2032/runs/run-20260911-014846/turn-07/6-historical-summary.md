ENISA pushed lab breakthrough — automated patching keeping pace with flaws and behavior-based malicious-agent detectors — as certified update via Critical Services Shield; national teams installed where local staff exhausted. Certified hospitals/grid absorbed probing with fewer outages; thin municipal utilities with legacy systems still fell back to manual.

No new EU fund; triage continued. Long-blocked backup grid-ready gigafactory site released planning money, permitting pushed, first concrete poured — months of delay cost.

US inward-turn rationing hardened: frontier volume licences honoured in name but queued/conditioned; Taiwan exercises lifted shipping insurance and rattled chip expectations with tighter US export licensing. Ministries/hospitals/firms on leading American model stayed constrained. Interim hosting talks with middle-power suppliers and EU-jurisdiction hosts continued, no signed foreign capacity; defector member state kept side-deal hosting/supply, refused unwind, cohesion cracked.

Office studies showed AI assistants lifted output, most for juniors, without job falls; ministers claimed manageable transition, unions disputed security. Public-benefit clinics/advisory offices stayed on European-hosted tools, no layoffs. Insurers held pause on exclusions where certificates existed.

Relief at avoided collapse mixed with anger at exposed dependence.
