## New measure
**EU Entry-Jobs Transition Guarantee**
Creates a funded EU-level wage-subsidy, retraining and income-bridge for displaced entry-level white-collar cohorts, tied to automating employers and paid from redeployed funds.
This and now because graduate hiring has frozen while Brussels spent no new home money, and the two-year commitment to hold social absorption fails if displacement becomes a shock before the bio gate opens; the cyber-defence breakthrough gives us fiscal and operational room to spend political attention at home this turn.

## Priority
EU Entry-Jobs Transition Guarantee — because with no measures in flight and capital at 22, stopping the domestic legitimacy bleed from missing entry jobs outranks adding another foreign lever, and it is the core of the absorption-plus-leverage commitment.

## In practice
We act through EPSCO and ECOFIN on an ESF+ redeployment plus EIB guarantee, not a new treaty fund: DG EMPL tables a transition guarantee offering 6-month hiring subsidies for entry posts, paid traineeships in certified hospitals, municipalities and SMEs, and an income bridge where posts are gone, with co-funding levied via reporting on firms deploying assistants at scale. Labour ministers who were told to plan without funds get funds; finance ministers get no new headline deficit.

On the standing book we sweat finished assets: ENISA pushes the cyber-defence breakthrough — automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection from M7 — into uncertified municipal utilities that failed drills, and HERA plus ECDC use the contested bio-uplift finding to pre-position sequencing and response under the Shield and M8 protocols, while the Middle-Power Compact continues pooling hosting and black-box results without new spend. The holdout capital is offered transition money conditioned on unwinding its side-deal.
