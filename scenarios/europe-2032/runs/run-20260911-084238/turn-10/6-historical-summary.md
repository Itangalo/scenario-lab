Spring 2031 automated ransomware via compromised update library hit municipal IT, clinic booking and two grid operators, freezing timetables, forcing paper prescriptions and load-shedding; recovery took weeks with AI-assembled intrusion tooling outpacing defenders. Winter islanding kits/offline folders kept hospitals/water lit, framed as survival not function. US lab demonstrated autonomous coding/intrusion agents, reproduced in Europe from open weights but not originatable; Washington tightened chip controls with capped, audited volume licences. Entry-level routine code/analysis/support jobs cut without restoration, sparking small protests. Commission responded with wage bridges, short retraining, and levy on automation-linked separations — fast enrolment, slow placement, opposed by business and unions — continuing rationing, dependence and degraded-services pattern since 2029.
