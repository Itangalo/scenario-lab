February 2032 US model cutoff forced paper reversion in three large states; Commission reserve of capped chips, supercomputer slices and older models only partly filled by June, with booking errors causing missed urgent referrals. Open-model proliferation and ENISA shields plus wastewater sequencing slowed but did not stop disruption.

Autumn moved crisis to streets: after missed referrals traced to swapped booking models, clinic staff staged coordinated walkouts in three states demanding human sign-off, thinning care, blowing overtime and slipping electives. The ring-fenced reserve reached full operation, keeping prescriptions and ministries running, but thin and weaker with persistent errors, credited to paper lists not Brussels. Commission responded via labour ministers with emergency locum/overtime pay, mandatory human signature for urgent referrals, reversion to human-led lists with offline folders, and joint monitoring talks, pausing further strikes into winter amid near-zero trust.
