## New measure
**EU Care Staffing and Human Booking Guarantee**
Puts a human back in charge of clinic booking and triage by funding cover staff, mandating human sign-off on urgent referrals, and pausing opaque auto-booking where errors caused harm — to stop walkouts and restore basic consent for AI in care.

This and now because coordinated walkouts after missed urgent referrals have turned sentiment at 1.0 into organized action, and the jobs-bills-protection commitment fails if care is seen as automation imposed on staff and patients; a small Labour and social protection instrument (category 7) executable via EPSCO, ESF+ and HERA without new siting or frontier promises is what can land before care stops.

## Priority
EU Care Staffing and Human Booking Guarantee, because with no measures in flight and clinics walking out, stopping the staffing collapse outranks any new sovereignty build this turn.

## In practice
We run this through EPSCO and Health Ministers via DG EMPL and DG SANTE with HERA and ENISA support: ESF+ emergency envelope for locum cover and overtime in walkout municipalities, an EU4Health instruction that urgent referrals require human sign-off and that faulty auto-booking modules revert to human-led lists with offline folders kept warm, plus joint staff-council monitoring in the three large states hit by the cutoff.

We sustain what finished without opening new tracks: the Essential Inference Reserve keeps ring-fenced inference for hospitals and ministries on existing sites, ENISA patching and 30-city wastewater sequencing stay funded, and wage bridges stay enrolled while we offer staff councils a visible win and hospital directors liability cover for pausing unsafe automation. Daily notes shift from blackmail and foresight-failure to nurses backed, referrals checked by humans.
