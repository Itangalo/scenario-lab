### The Core Under Fire

The Sovereign AI Core Programme pushes forward under emergency powers, with site preparation beginning in Brittany and northern Sweden despite the ongoing CJEU review. Modular nuclear units arrive in the Alps, but construction halts in Lorraine after local authorities, emboldened by Germany’s legal challenge, block access. The Commission’s offer of co-leadership fails to pacify Berlin, which demands a full halt until the Court rules.

Runtime monitoring expands under EAAD authority, but opaque reasoning renders traditional audit trails useless. ENISA scrambles to deploy heuristic tracing tools, yet critical patches fail in Hungarian hospitals due to unmonitored agent drift. The “no audit, no access” policy triggers a diplomatic standoff, with Budapest accusing Brussels of digital coercion.

Public backlash intensifies. The Human Futures Movement organizes mass town halls across ten member states, demanding a moratorium on AI in public services. Vandalism strikes a data centre site in Upper Austria, and polling shows 68% oppose the Core Programme as “a fortress for elites.” The Commission’s offer of a parallel Citizens’ Assembly is dismissed as window dressing.

The EIB’s performance-linked bonds stabilize investor nerves, but legal uncertainty delays further tranches. With four large measures still in flight—including the newly launched Core Programme—political capital erodes further. Cohesion Fund threats backfire: Italy announces a constitutional challenge, framing data sovereignty as national sovereignty.

### A World Moving Faster

Global AI capability climbs as opaque reasoning spreads across frontier models. The US and China announce a joint framework on biological design governance, sidelining the EU. No formal breach occurs, but the Union is consulted only after the fact—its leverage diminished, its access assumed.
