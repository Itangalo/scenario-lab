## In practice
We keep ENISA, DG ENER and the TSOs on the M3 rollout as the main spend: finishing OT monitoring kits in the two hit states, forcing grid-control access deals through the NIS2 Cooperation Group, and moving HERA/ECDC wastewater sentinels from sampling to declared operation using Civil Protection Mechanism exercises. M1-M2 stay on permitting track with no new cash to avoid splitting authority.

Alongside it we task the EU AI Office and JRC with a small joint assurance cell to port the new interpretability/control method onto models deployed in the EU, with pre-access agreements from labs that already want it, and we task ECDC/HERA with an EU-hosted secure pathogen repository with vetted access, audit and IP safeguards to bring the pausing research hospitals back into sharing without open international publication. Legal base is the Health Union package and Data Governance Act, funded by reprogrammed Horizon and EU4Health lines.
