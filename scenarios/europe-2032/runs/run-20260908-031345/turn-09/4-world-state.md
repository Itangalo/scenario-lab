While both findings remain contested, they mark a shift—capability is now outpacing understanding.

Without full unity, the coalition’s leverage remains diluted.

### Fragility Beneath the Surface

The safety breakthrough from last turn did not propagate to deployed systems at scale. With no new safeguards enacted, and capability advancing, `ai_safety` dropped by 5. Resilience declined further as minor model drift incidents accumulated, exposing weak incident response outside finance and telecoms.

The window for strategic cohesion narrowed. The coalition is alive—but not yet effective.
