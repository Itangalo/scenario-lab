Create a permanent technical unit under CNECT with mandate and access to pre-evaluate open-weight models above capability 45.0 before release, using adversarial testing and interpretability tools to assess risk of misuse and autonomous replication.

## In practice  
We are pressing forward on the Reallocate Resilience Fund initiative, but the legal and political resistance is real. The €8bn shift is now being reframed not as a diversion from resilience but as a recalibration: we are compressing the timeline of Hardened Critical Infrastructure Zones to focus on Tier 1 nodes — energy, finance, and health command systems — while deferring broader municipal rollouts. This allows us to release €4bn immediately, tied to binding commitments from three pre-vetted startups: one in Finland building agent frameworks under audit, one in France specializing in secure fine-tuning, and a third, pan-European consortium co-located with an InvestAI site in Spain. DG COMP is fast-tracking state-aid clearance, arguing that the crisis context meets the "exceptional circumstances" clause in Article 107(3)(b), but Hungary has filed a preliminary reference to the ECJ, and Poland threatens to block the next cohesion package unless we offer co-design rights on the labs’ governance.

Meanwhile, the Tech sovereignty package remains tied up in the Environment Council, where Italy’s demand for a site grows louder. We are offering a compromise: a smaller, symbolic node in Sardinia tied to geothermal R&D, paired with a €500m regional upskilling fund — but only if Italy drops its veto. ENISA continues to roll out audit templates for Hardened Critical Infrastructure Zones, but adoption lags outside the Nordic and Benelux blocs. The new Public Evaluation Unit proposal, while small, is intended to break the silence on open-weight risk: we are staffing it from existing CNECT personnel and pulling in seconded researchers from the Danish AI Safety Centre. Its first mandate will be to assess any open release above 45.0 — starting with the next wave we know is coming, not the one we just missed.
