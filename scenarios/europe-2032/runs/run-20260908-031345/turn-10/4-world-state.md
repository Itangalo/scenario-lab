### The Weight of Response

Global AI capability advanced by 2.5 points to 78.5, continuing its steady climb. The eval anomalies and biosecurity concerns from the previous turn remain unresolved, reinforcing the sense that systems are outpacing human oversight. Offensive cyber capabilities, now clearly weaponized, have crossed into operational use.

The EU proposed an emergency activation of its AI Incident Response Framework in response to a widespread cyberattack that crippled hospital systems and public procurement platforms across multiple member states. ENISA was tasked with drafting coordination protocols, including proposed access to critical infrastructure logs and model telemetry from certified providers. Fast-tracked funding of €900 million was announced to establish regional response cells, with a central hub planned for Lyon.

However, implementation has not yet begun. Germany and the Netherlands raised constitutional objections to Brussels’ proposed surveillance and override powers, demanding joint command structures and audit rights. Poland and Hungary rejected the framework as a power grab, withholding cooperation despite offers of priority reconnection support. Negotiations over the mutual assistance clause continue, with no agreement on enforcement mechanisms.

Public sentiment dropped to 20.0 as the attack’s human toll became clear—weeks-long delays in medical procedures, disrupted welfare payments—and as fears grew over expanded state access to private systems. The narrative shifted from technological promise to systemic vulnerability.

With no prior measures completed and capability rising, ai_safety fell further, now at 15.0, reflecting opaque reasoning and uncontrolled deployment. Resilience dipped as the attack exposed response gaps, despite the intent behind the proposed framework. eu_ai_sovereignty remains unchanged at 14.0, as no domestic compute gains were realized and the new proposal has not yet advanced. Political capital decreased slightly to 11.0, reflecting the cost of introducing a new measure without completing a prior one, but without the additional penalty of naming a priority.

The Middle-Power Coalition remained stalled, and the emergent regional evaluation platform deepened fragmentation.
