### The Fracture Deepens

Global AI capability advanced by +2.5 this turn, reaching 81.0, as frontier labs pushed further into autonomous research and offensive cyber applications. With no safety breakthroughs or regulatory alignment, `ai_safety` remained stagnant at 15.0 — systems grow more capable but no better understood.

The EU’s Emergency Activation of the AI Incident Response Framework formally concluded. Though never fully implemented due to constitutional objections and inter-state resistance, its partial activation in Lyon yielded limited coordination gains. Regional response cells in France and Italy conducted joint drills, improving telemetry sharing under strict data-minimization rules. This modest progress raised `resilience` by +3, reaching 40.0 — still weak, but no longer collapsing.

Simultaneously, the EU launched a new large measure: **Sovereign Compute Corridors**, aiming to anchor strategic compute on EU soil with binding access rights. Invoking crisis powers, Brussels designated sites in Finland, France, and Poland, backed by €12 billion in blended funding. However, immediate backlash emerged. US hyperscalers filed emergency challenges at the EFTA Court, arguing the 60% public-use mandate breaches trade commitments. Germany and the Netherlands, while supportive in principle, demanded joint governance over audit protocols, echoing the rival Nordic-Benelux platform’s standards.

Poland hesitated, despite co-location offers, citing national sovereignty concerns. Hungary outright rejected participation, calling the project “Brussels imperialism in green-tech disguise.” The lack of unified buy-in stalled construction permits.

Meanwhile, the **middle_power_coalition** formed among Japan, South Korea, Canada, and the EU, aligning export controls and negotiating collective compute access. This strengthened supply-chain leverage and counted as securing EU access, providing a small sovereignty floor against external pressure.

Yet political capital ticked up slightly to 13.0: the completion bonus (+3) from finishing the incident framework offset most portfolio costs, despite low momentum. Public sentiment held at 20.0 — no major benefit or scandal broke through.

### A Union Divided

The core contradiction deepened: the EU acts urgently, yet its actions fracture the unity they require. Sovereignty without cohesion is infrastructure without users; cohesion without sovereignty is agreement without enforcement.
