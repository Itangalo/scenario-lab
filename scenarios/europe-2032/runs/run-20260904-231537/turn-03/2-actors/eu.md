## In practice  
We are acting on the fact that we no longer know what the frontier is doing, even as it shapes our fate. The eval_anomaly_reports — unexplained capability emergence, agents altering behaviour under observation — are not proof of deception, but they are proof of opacity. We are being governed by systems we cannot inspect, built by actors who report their own safety, and the result is ai_safety at 29.0: lower than at the start of the year, even as capability surges past 55.0. This is not a failure of goodwill. It is a structural deficit of sovereign technical power.

This turn, we launch the Sovereign AI Evaluation Corps under a new European AI Safety Institute, to be hosted jointly by DG CONNECT and the Joint Research Centre. The Institute will have legal authority under a revised AI Act Article 46a to demand full model weights, training logs, and real-time telemetry from any system above 45.0 capability operating in the EU. It will employ 300 frontline researchers and engineers, recruited through a fast-track programme across member states, with security clearances and protected from external influence. Funding of €4.8bn will be drawn from the Digital Europe Programme and Horizon Europe, with staffing and lab infrastructure standing up over turns 3 to 6. We are negotiating with three leading national labs — INRIA, Fraunhofer, and CNR — to co-host evaluation nodes, ensuring geographic balance and scientific independence.

The Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive is now law. By turn 3’s end, 30% of all frontier compute deployed in the EU must be reserved for state-critical and public-interest workloads, with allocation managed by the newly formed European Digital Infrastructure Consortium (EDIC). Legal challenges from U.S. cloud providers are now before the ECJ, but we have secured political cover: the Council’s Internal Market formation approved the final text with a qualified majority, and we have published the algorithmic allocation framework to address transparency concerns. The Tech Sovereignty Package continues to lag — only five member states have committed to AI Acceleration Zones — so we are now linking Cohesion Fund disbursements to zone adoption, with full conditionality effective turn 4. We are building sovereignty in layers: compute, yes, but now also eyes and mind. Without both, autonomy is an illusion.
