## In practice
We run M7 through EPSCO/Health with HERA/EMA as incident registrar: pulled module stays pulled, retained traces reviewed under the failure-prediction method, interim compensation paid from reserves, and backlog publication continues to prove wards still work. DG CNECT and the AI Office hold the two gigafactory sites on brokered power and spares despite US volume-licence friction, without new money, to protect the two-year access commitment.

The new shelter regime runs via DG HOME, ENISA and the Civil Protection Mechanism with JRC support: TTE and JHA Councils adopt degraded-mode checklists for hospitals, energy DSOs and telecoms, pre-stock spares and offline procedures piloted in the M3-hardened sectors, and UCPM mutual-aid triggers for cross-border cascade. We explicitly frame this as living with distributed frontier capability we cannot recall, not as controlling it, to buy cohesion from the separate-deal member state and municipalities.
