## Priority
M13 EU Wartime Cyber Restoration and Mutual Aid Corps, because it is the only new instrument and it directly serves the two-year commitment to keep essential services and cohesion functioning when nothing else can be started.

## In practice
We act under the Civil Protection Mechanism and NIS2 Article response, not a new law. DG CNECT, ENISA and the EU Civil Protection Pool task existing M3-hardened SOCs and M12 bio-sampling staff into cross-border restoration teams, funded by reprogrammed ESF+ and EU4Health reflows already committed to overtime.

We deploy the cyber_defence_breakthrough first to hospitals, grid control and telecoms: automated patching and swarm detection pushed via the Cyber Shield, with analogue fallbacks and pre-cleared isolation kept as legal cover for continued triage refusals in France, Belgium and Netherlands. Guarding of fabs, landing stations and interconnectors continues under the neutrality-protection posture, with mutual-aid triggers from hardened regions to reduced-service towns.

We ask no Council vote for money, only endorsement of no-EU-territory-for-strikes and liability-as-hazard-pay continuity to prevent walkouts, because with political capital exhausted any contested proposal collapses enforcement elsewhere.
