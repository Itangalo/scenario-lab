## New measure
**EU Shopfloor Transition and Manual Continuity Fund**
It funds wage insurance, rapid retraining and employer transition contributions for picking/logistics/warehouse workers displaced by commercial robots, while ring-fencing repair, care and construction-site work as manual-continuity occupations with supported apprenticeships.
Why this and why now: with sentiment at 10, capital at 16 and robots now taking the automatable physical jobs from outside suppliers, a small labour-protection move (category 7) is the only affordable way to hold the independence-and-absorption commitment and stop displacement becoming collapse.

## In practice
We run this through EMPL and the EGF/ESF+ base with the Employment Council owning rollout: SURE-style wage bridges for displaced logistics workers, transition levies on deploying firms to fund retraining, and manual-continuity apprenticeship lines for repair/care/site work coordinated with the islanded permit/school pilots that still work.

We hold islanded continuity with existing ENISA structures without a new build, treat the tightening export controls as rationing to be absorbed by cannibalised domestic clusters, and answer the member-state hyperscaler side-deal and US model withdrawal by keeping essential workloads on EU-hosted fallbacks rather than chasing replacement access this turn.
