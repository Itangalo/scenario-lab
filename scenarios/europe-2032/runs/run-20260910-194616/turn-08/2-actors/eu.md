## New measure
**EU Supply-Chain Leverage and Continuity Pact**
It converts the Union's lithography and materials position into joint continuity terms with the US, Japan and the Netherlands during the Taiwan blockade, trading controlled upstream flow for secured compute and inputs for EU essential services. Why this and why now: with advanced semiconductor exports halted for years every AI question is a security question, and the one asset that still gives a capital-starved Union bargaining power is the upstream bottleneck — using it jointly beats hoarding it alone, and it serves the two-year commitment to run essential AI without foreign permission.

## Priority
M8 EU Supply-Chain Leverage and Continuity Pact, because under blockade no domestic workload, repair pool or bio-preparedness holds without jointly managed chip and compute continuity, and it outranks new builds we cannot fund at capital 16.

## In practice
We put the Trade and Competitiveness Council formations with DG TRADE and DG GROW behind the Pact: Anti-Coercion Instrument readiness and export-control alignment as the legal base, ASML/Netherlands, Japan and the US as the coalition table, offering predictable, conditioned upstream supply in return for prioritised compute, spares and servicing for EU health, energy and telecom operators. ENISA and HERA keep degraded-mode repair pools and manual-check operations running from finished measures without new money.

On bio uplift we do not open a second instrument this turn: we task HERA/ECDC with targeted detection guidance and lab liaison within existing resilience funding, holding a full biopreparedness build for next turn if the precursor strengthens. On knowledge-work gains with no job loss we bank the sentiment stabiliser but claim no dividend, conceding the welfare-system gap remains lawful under the 2024 AI Act and asking Parliament for time for a gap-mapping recast rather than moratoria that would freeze adoption.
