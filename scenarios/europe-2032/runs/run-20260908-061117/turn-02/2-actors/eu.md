## In practice  
We are pressing forward on three fronts. On sovereignty, we are launching the Sovereign AI Testbed — a joint initiative between the European Commission’s AI Office, the European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking, and national cybersecurity agencies — to build sovereign evaluation capacity that does not depend on foreign labs or proprietary black boxes. The Testbed will be hosted across three EU supercomputing centres (Bologna, Barcelona, and Ostrava), with €6.2bn allocated from the Digital Europe Programme and Horizon Europe, and will begin operations in turn 3 with red-teaming of open-weight models at capability level 43.0 and above. Access will be granted to vetted researchers and public auditors, with full weight security protocols modelled on the TSMC state-actor standard. This is not about matching the frontier tomorrow — it is about ensuring that when we act, we act on our own understanding.

On resilience, the Emergency Resilience Surge continues at pace. ENISA is now issuing binding standards for AI-aware intrusion detection in energy and water systems, with full deployment required by turn 4. The Joint Resilience Board has convened for the first time, with representatives from all 27 member states, and has approved a common red-teaming protocol using certified AI agents. Fast-tracked procurement has enabled the purchase of detection stacks from EU-based vendors like Tenable and Darktrace, with integration support from the Cybersecurity Competence Centre. The offer of EU liability for incidents during transition has secured buy-in from Germany, France, and Poland, though Italy and Hungary remain hesitant, citing cost and sovereignty concerns. We are countering with targeted co-financing offers — 70% EU funding for red-teaming in municipalities below median income — to prevent fragmentation.

Meanwhile, the Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package remain on track. Site selection for the first two Gigafactories is complete (in Spain and Finland), with grid connection agreements fast-tracked under the new accelerated-permitting zones. Private investment is flowing, though below target — the current sentiment drag from the automated_decision_scandal is slowing hyperscaler commitments. We are preparing a communication campaign to reframe AI infrastructure as a resilience asset, not just an economic one, but that will wait until the Surge shows visible results. For now, we are holding the line: sovereignty without resilience is exposure, and resilience without sovereignty is dependence. We are building both, in parallel, under fire.
