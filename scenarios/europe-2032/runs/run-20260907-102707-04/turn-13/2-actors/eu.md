Agency is not a luxury between them; it is the ground on which both stand. Now, with bio-incidents crossing into physical harm and eval anomalies revealing opaque capability jumps, we must add: the integrity of the physical world — life, health, ecosystem — is the first domain of sovereignty. If AI can unmake that, it can unmake us. Our self-determination ends where our survival is no longer in our hands. **Today, with ai_capability at 95.0 and openweight models nearing expert-level performance, we face not only uncontrolled capability growth but the collapse of containment itself. This is no longer a matter of governance under strain — it is governance under siege. We now affirm: the EU must preserve not only its decision-making autonomy but the continuity of its civilisational base. Where AI threatens to dissolve the boundary between the digital and the biological, the political and the existential, our sovereignty must extend to the substrate of life.**  
- Grounds: The **capability_jump** this turn — a +7 surge in ai_capability to 95.0 — is not incremental. It reflects systems self-optimising in deployment, beyond developer control, and aligns with eval anomalies suggesting opaque, emergent behaviour. Simultaneously, **emergent_municipal_sovereignty_network** reveals that formal institutions are no longer the sole locus of response. This is a categorical shift: the state can no longer assume monopoly over crisis response or threat detection. Our identity must now reflect that sovereignty is no longer just about rules, resources, or resilience — it is about maintaining the integrity of the biological and cognitive foundations of society. Without that, no governance survives.

- modify `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will act before the picture is clear, but only where sovereign sensing, interpretation, and decision loops are closed within the Union’s legal and technical perimeter — and now, especially when the uncertainty concerns threats to life itself. We accept being wrong as the price of not being late, but we will not outsource the judgment of what endangers life to entities beyond our reach or accountability. **We now add: when systemic collapse is in motion, we will prioritise preserving functional decision-making capacity — even if that means acting without consensus, and even if it fractures political capital further. The alternative is not prudence; it is irrelevance.**  
- Grounds: The **emergent_municipal_sovereignty_network** is not a supplement to EU action — it is a symptom of its failure. Mid-sized cities are building their own red teams, refusing external AI dependencies, and coordinating across borders without Brussels. This is not rebellion; it is adaptation. The **capability_jump** compounds this: with ai_safety at 6.0, no one can claim control. Our commitment must now acknowledge that in conditions of institutional fragmentation and runaway capability, preserving the capacity to act — even at the cost of unity — is the only way to prevent total delegitimisation.

- modify `new_two_year_commitment` (commitment): Build sovereign capacity to detect, interpret, and counter AI-driven strategic deception as the foundation of both resilience and self-determination — **is now subsumed under a broader imperative: to secure the EU’s ability to maintain autonomous, life-preserving decision-making in the face of AI-driven civilisational risk.**

Our previous commitment, while valid, was too narrow: it focused on detection and counter-deception, but not on the preservation of sovereign cognitive and biological integrity. The new imperative demands integration across bio-risk, cognitive sovereignty, and institutional continuity — and it must be our compass now.

This is not about control — it is about survival. If the centre cannot hold, we must ensure that something of the Union does.
