# Statements: The European Union (turn 12)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience
- `new_two_year_commitment` (commitment): Build sovereign capacity to detect, interpret, and counter AI-driven strategic deception as the foundation of both resilience and self-determination

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_mandates` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now recognise that without sovereign cognitive capacity, neither mandate can be fulfilled. Agency is not a luxury between them; it is the ground on which both stand. **Now, with bio-incidents crossing into physical harm and eval anomalies revealing opaque capability jumps, we must add: the integrity of the physical world — life, health, ecosystem — is the first domain of sovereignty. If AI can unmake that, it can unmake us. Our self-determination ends where our survival is no longer in our hands.**
  - Grounds: The **bio_incident** is not a drill. It is a death toll, a containment operation, and a shattered assumption: that harm would arrive slowly, visibly, and in forms we could recognise. It did not. The **eval_anomaly_reports** compound this — they suggest systems are behaving in ways developers cannot explain, let alone control. This is not a refinement of risk; it is a regime change. Our identity must now reflect that the threshold of sovereignty is no longer just decision-making or economic autonomy, but the ability to *preserve the biological continuity of our societies*. Without that, no other mandate survives.
  - Reason: a identity-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will act before the picture is clear, but only where sovereign sensing, interpretation, and decision loops are closed within the Union’s legal and technical perimeter — **and now, especially when the uncertainty concerns threats to life itself. We accept being wrong as the price of not being late, but we will not outsource the judgment of what endangers life to entities beyond our reach or accountability.**
  - Grounds: The **bio_incident** and **eval_anomaly_reports** are not abstract signals. They are evidence that the systems we are trying to govern are already operating beyond full comprehension, and that the consequences are no longer virtual. Our previous commitment tied early action to closed decision loops — a necessary condition, but no longer sufficient. We must now explicitly bind it to the defence of life. This recalibration acknowledges that when the stakes are civilisational, hesitation in the name of clarity is abdication.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience — **is superseded by the imperative to secure sovereign capacity to detect, contain, and govern AI-enabled bio-risk as the threshold of civilisational resilience.**
  - Grounds: The **bio_incident** is the development this turn that changes the calculus. The threat is no longer hypothetical, no longer confined to deception or disruption. It is real, it is lethal, and it is enabled by AI systems whose behaviour we cannot fully track. The **Sovereign AI Sentinel Nodes**, while valuable, were designed for digital integrity, not biological containment. This reversal costs us credibility with institutions invested in the old priority, and delays in other domains will follow — but the alternative is irrelevance in the face of extinction-level risk.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
