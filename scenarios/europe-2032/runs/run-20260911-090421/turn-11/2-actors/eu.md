Puts the medical and public-service gains already arriving onto European rails — EU-hosted clinical AI and administrative assistants deployed through hospitals and town halls with waiting-list and decision-time targets published monthly.
This and why now: with a loss-of-control incident frightening the public while a medical breakthrough and a proven adoption success show concrete benefit, converting diffuse gains into an attributed European success is the only way to rebuild sentiment and capital to fund absorption; it directly serves holding social absorption through displacement.

## Priority

## In practice
We act through DG SANTE and DG DIGIT with the Health and General Affairs Councils, using EU4Health and Digital Europe repurposed funds plus InvestEU guarantees for EU-hosted inference. HERA-contracted sequencing labs get the tailored-therapy pathway fast-tracked under EHDS safeguards, while ENISA-certified deployment cells roll the proven admin assistants to waiting-list and permitting backlogs with monthly publication.

On the home track we hold the defensive line without new spend: the M10 patching and bio-detection feeds continue via CSIRTs and the Japan-Korea-Canada swap, M8 loss-of-control protocols are invoked for the incident review, and EPSCO keeps the finalised flexicurity plan ready to fund once this scale-up restores capital. The holdout capital is offered front-of-queue clinical hosting if it aligns export criteria and drops the side-deal.
