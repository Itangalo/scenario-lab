## New measure
**EU Displacement Absorption Fund – Retraining and Wage Insurance**
Activates the finalised flexicurity plan with real money — EU-guaranteed wage insurance and paid retraining places funded by a levy-linked contribution from automating employers and ESF+ top-up, deployed first to frozen-graduate sectors and logistics/back-office workers.
This and why now: with graduate hiring frozen, retraining finalised but unfunded, and dread from the autumn agent incident competing with cures-and-queues goodwill, funding absorption is the core of holding social absorption through displacement; it directly serves the two-year commitment and converts sentiment gains into capital.

## Priority

## In practice
We act through EPSCO and DG EMPL with an ESF+ amendment and InvestEU guarantee, pairing member-state PES delivery with employer co-funding where AI assistants displaced junior roles. First tranches go to pilot regions where oncology and town-hall gains are already published monthly, so benefit and protection are seen together.

On the home track we hold the line without new spend: ENISA and national CSIRTs keep M7/M8 swarm-detection and loss-of-control playbooks live for the embodied-AI pilots in logistics, HERA-contracted labs continue EU-hosted clinical inference under EHDS safeguards, and the middle-power evaluation swap continues. Defence ministries keep embodied pilots contained to controlled sites while we monitor spillover to labour displacement.
