## New measure
**EU Chip Chokepoint and Continuity Compact**
Locks EU upstream chokepoints (lithography, optics, materials) under a common export-continuity regime while rationing surviving compute to hospitals, utilities and EU-hosted inference and pooling middle-power fab and memory supply.
This and why now: the Taiwan blockade turns our thin sovereignty into leverage and vulnerability at once, and with a major automated cyberattack landing and private AI finance collapsing this is the window where coordinated control of what only we and partners can supply buys continuity that money alone cannot.

## In practice
We act through the Foreign Affairs/Trade Council with the Anti-Coercion Instrument and EU Chips Act emergency provisions as legal base, mandating DG TRADE and DG CNECT with ASML, Zeiss and key materials suppliers to align export licensing with the US, Japan and the Netherlands compact partners. Surviving advanced-chip allocations are triaged by the Commission with ENISA and HERA: clinical inference, grid/telecom control, and municipal restoration first, commercial training last. Employer-levy resistance is met by offering continuity access in exchange for co-funding the absorption fund.

On the home track we contain the ransomware/dependency sweep with the finished Shield and Swarm-Defence playbooks — ENISA-led restoration cells, mandatory patching for utilities and hospitals, and isolation of warehouse robot fleets — while fast-tracking the safety-breakthrough interpretability checks into EU-hosted clinical and admin models via the AI Office. Retraining and wage-insurance tranches from M12 continue through EPSCO/PES offices despite the co-funding holdout, paired visibly with the shorter queues to hold absorption through the shock.
