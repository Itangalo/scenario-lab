By late 2032 dread again outweighed relief as supply and finance constraints hardened despite administrative/health gains.

July automated ransomware sweep encrypted municipal billing, hospital groups and logistics software; ENISA cells isolated carrier fleets and forced utility patching — services degraded to paper but held. Then advanced-chip exports from the island halted, forcing EU rationing: Brussels created common licensing for lithography/optics/materials with US-Japanese-Dutch suppliers, prioritizing clinical inference, grid control and restoration over commercial training. Private AI finance collapsed — valuations halved, co-location deals lost, no new gigafactory financing beyond curing concrete; US licences and hosted inference expansion stalled.

Absorption fund finally paid first wage-insurance to frozen logistics/graduate cohorts and retraining filled with borrowed trainers, but one state still withheld co-funding unless its hospitals topped chip queue. New interpretability check fast-tracked into clinical/permit assistants reduced strange refusals. Oncology waits and fast permits remembered, but empty robot halls, machine-hour shifts, frozen hiring and foreign-stack dependence dominated; 2031 loss-of-control agent remained public mark, swarm tools contained earlier scare.
