### The Frontier Accelerates

AI capability advances rapidly, reaching 85.5 as leading U.S. labs refine autonomous viral vector design and expand into integrated bioprocess simulation. The jump reflects sustained investment and closed-loop self-improvement in model training pipelines. Openweight models absorb techniques from leaked training data and derivative releases, pushing openweight capability to 67.0—still one full generation behind, but showing improved performance in narrow domains like molecular scaffold suggestion under tight human guidance. While knowledge diffusion from leaks accelerates open model development, the frontier continues to pull ahead, preserving the capability gap.

### Europe’s Narrow Activation

The European AI Emergency Response Corps achieves partial operational status. Cyber and biosecurity divisions are staffed and equipped, and binding protocols are in place across seven member states. However, Germany’s absence—pending a constitutional court ruling on federal overreach—fractures continental coverage. A fallback activation under the Civil Protection Mechanism is prepared, but lacks legal teeth. The Corps is on standby, but not yet a unified force.

The Emergency Biosecurity Evaluation Compact launches administratively: EMBO, SciLifeLab, and the Paul Ehrlich Institute begin drafting evaluation frameworks. Yet no evaluations occur—the targeted U.S. lab refuses access, citing national security and regulatory incompatibility. Without binding authority, the Compact remains symbolic.

### Political Capital Erodes

With two large measures still in flight—including the prioritised Emergency Response Corps—and no major initiatives completed this turn, political capital declines. The portfolio strain is acute, and the lack of German buy-in undermines credibility. Public sentiment holds at 34.0, as media spotlight the gap between EU ambitions and operational reality. Labour displacement accelerates in pharmaceutical and logistics sectors, but no new support measures land.

U.S. consolidation continues, restricting EU access to frontier models and deepening dependency. No new bilateral defections are confirmed, but quiet negotiations intensify.
