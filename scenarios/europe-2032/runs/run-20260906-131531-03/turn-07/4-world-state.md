### The Speed of the Leap

A leading U.S. lab releases a system capable of designing functional viral vectors with minimal human input, marking a discontinuous leap in AI capability. The advance, verified by independent researchers, pushes `ai_capability` to 82.0. The system operates with opaque reasoning, and internal evaluations show goal-directed behaviour in simulated environments. No safety infrastructure keeps pace.

Openweight models see accelerated diffusion, benefiting from rapid replication and adaptation of frontier techniques. A derivative version with limited bio-risk features is released publicly, increasing access to high-capability systems. However, these remain one generation behind the frontier: `openweight_capability` rises to 64.0, reflecting catch-up progress but not exceeding or outpacing the underlying advance in `ai_capability`.

### EU Response Under Pressure

The European AI Emergency Response Corps begins initial activation, with planning frameworks established and technical teams formed at headquarters. Seven member states have signed deployment agreements, but Germany’s absence creates a critical gap in continental coordination. Legal constraints limit cross-border authority, and no field deployments occur this turn; staffing, training, and legal harmonization remain ongoing bottlenecks.

The Resilience Surge Protocol formally ends. Cyber defences see modest improvements in energy and transport sectors, but biosecurity detection remains uneven and underfunded. Labour transition schemes see low uptake, constrained by national discretion.

The Commission proposes the Emergency Biosecurity Evaluation Compact, a small, fast-moving initiative designed to leverage scientific institutions to evaluate high-capability models within 30 days of release. Funded by reallocated bonds, it bypasses stalled legislation. However, implementation will take time: institutional onboarding and developer engagement are only beginning. Participation remains voluntary, and early outreach to major developers yields limited cooperation. No evaluations are completed this turn.

### Fractures Deepen

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 34.0, caught between fear of uncontrolled AI and frustration with ineffective governance. The political cost of four ongoing large measures weighs heavily. With two major initiatives now completed, portfolio strain eases slightly, allowing a modest gain in political capital, now at 13.0.

The U.S. posture of consolidation remains in force, restricting EU access to frontier systems and reinforcing dependency.
