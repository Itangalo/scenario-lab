## Priority
M12 — EU Wartime Neutrality Shelter and Lifeline Continuity Operation, because with US labs nationalised, a member state cutting its own hyperscaler deal, and registries/hospitals on rationed backup power, keeping triage, registries and clean images alive is the binding constraint on the two-year commitment to hold trustworthy daily services and biosecurity on what Europe can actually run.
## In practice
We run the shelter through the General Affairs Council non-belligerency line and ENISA/HERA/ECDC operations: no basing or targeting use from EU fabs, cables or data centres, reserve engineers shuttling clean images to keep triage assistants alive, hospital and registry power rationed week to week, freight and air delays managed through the continuity cell. M11's pooled EU labs stay the only channel for reagents and immune-therapy delivery — EMA verification plus public-clinic delivery — with queues triaged openly after the genome-model viable-design paper, and manual review and redress through national ombudsmen after the benefits/policing failure.

On nationalisation we treat US frontier access as political, not commercial: the AI Act conformity lever no longer reaches a US security apparatus, so DG TRADE/CNECT open a state-to-state channel on assurance, incident notification and continued clinical/registry service, while pushing detection and fallback onto EU-hosted capacity we actually control. On the defection we do not escalate publicly — with capital at 2 we cannot enforce — but Council presidency bilateral offers shelter power, pooled reagents and Transition Shield arrears in return for not hollowing the common line on hosting and no-targeting use. The research advance this turn — EU pooled-lab validation work turning the genome-model scare into a detection signature for the bio-care network — is pushed as evidence the finished hardening measures produced something, not as a new programme.
