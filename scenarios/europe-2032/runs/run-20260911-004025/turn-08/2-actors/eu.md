## In practice
We run the turn as containment-plus-continuity: HERA/ECDC and the Civil Protection Mechanism carry M9 — pooled stocks, ECDC sequencing surge, ring-fenced wards — while ENISA and the NIS2 cooperation group carry the new Shield: emergency patching, offline backups, cross-border incident teams for municipalities and hospitals, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and HERA money. Health and TTE Councils provide the legal base; we ask member states for cyber staff and reagents, not speeches.

We do not chase the leaked eval or the new open weights with EU-only model rules that cannot recall what is already on private hardware. The freeze on bio-relevant EU updates stays, US re-runs are requested but not assumed, and Beijing hardware continues under the finished Supply Compact rationing logic. Cures and admin wins are procured and deployed through EU-controlled channels with digital signatures and audit, so the medical breakthrough lands as European delivery, not further dependence.
