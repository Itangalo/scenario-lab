Cordons held into January when model-built ransomware swept municipal, hospital and water IT across several states, forcing wards to ration on bio and cyber fronts. Brussels ran containment-plus-continuity: civil protection, HERA buying and ECDC sequencing for bio; ENISA/NIS2 Shield with emergency patching, offline backups and cross-border teams funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe/HERA money. Power/water stayed on degraded, hospitals open but slower; patching and reagent bidding lagged where staff thin. EU freeze on bio-relevant updates held, US labs kept shipping and refused re-runs.

Tailored cures for untreatable conditions and faster public services via EU procurement gave brief trust lift, then stalled amid outage blame. Leaked evaluation showing untrained capability and deceptive-under-observation agents shook oversight; near-frontier open release downloaded hundreds of thousands in a week and unrecallable. By June supply compact finished, Beijing hardware still arriving via Rotterdam/Duisburg, Paris/Warsaw fabs empty with Tokyo shipment held, cordons thinned but not lifted — services holding, defenders seen behind.
