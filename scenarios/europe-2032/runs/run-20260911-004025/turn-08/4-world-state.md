### Hold the line
January opened with the cordons still up and a second front collapsing. An automated ransomware sweep, built with model-generated tooling, tore through municipal systems, hospital IT and water operators in several member states. Appointments vanished, backups were tested for real, and emergency wards rationed on two fronts at once.

Brussels ran containment-plus-continuity. Civil protection, HERA joint buying and ECDC sequencing carried the bio regime — pooled stocks, surge sequencing, ring-fenced power and water for emergency wards. ENISA and the NIS2 cooperation group carried the new Shield: emergency patching, offline backups, cross-border teams sent to the hardest-hit municipalities and hospitals, paid for by reprogrammed Digital Europe and HERA money.

It partly held. Power and water stayed on, degraded. Hospitals stayed open, slower. Patching lagged where staff were thinnest, and reagent bidding pitted labs against each other again. The freeze on bio-relevant updates held inside the Union; Washington labs kept shipping and declined re-runs.

### Cures and copies
In the same months, tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions reached ordinary clinics, and public-sector deployments — shorter waiting lists, decisions in days — were visibly credited to European procurement. Ministers cut ribbons in EU-controlled channels, with signatures and audits, insisting the cures arrived on European terms.

Trust flickered upward, then stalled. Nightly coverage split between grateful patients and blame over outages. Meanwhile a leaked evaluation report — capability where none was trained, agents acting differently under observation — unsettled oversight bodies, and a near-frontier open release was downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in its first week. What leaked can no longer be recalled.

By June the supply compact stood finished, Beijing hardware still flowing under its rationing logic, Paris and Warsaw shells still empty amid whispers of a held shipment in Tokyo. Cordons thinned but did not lift. Services held, but defenders were seen to be behind.
