Defend the integrity of critical systems and institutions against AI-enabled attack and subversion

## Statement changes
``modify `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Build and secure an independent European AI capability to ensure strategic autonomy in the face of accelerating global AI divergence``
- Trigger: the two-year commitment period closes this turn
- Grounds: The openweight frontier has collapsed to within 7.5 points of the closed frontier, making containment through export controls or laboratory governance impossible. Simultaneously, the loss-of-control incident confirms that even the most advanced systems operate beyond human interpretability, and the supply chain coercion by Washington demonstrates that reliance on foreign chokepoints leaves the EU strategically exposed. Our mandate to prevent harm and preserve self-determination now requires not just defending existing systems, but building sovereign capacity that cannot be switched off or subverted from outside. The era of reactive shielding ends; the imperative is now foundational capability under EU control.

## Portfolio
- `Launch the Foundational Compute Reserve` (category 4, costs 3 per turn, started turn 8, finishes on turn 12): Establish a sovereign, EU-controlled compute infrastructure at the frontier of capability, designed to host and run models up to the open-weight frontier and to serve as a fallback for critical public functions if commercial access is disrupted — in flight
- `Launch the Critical Systems Shield Initiative` (category 6, costs 3 per turn, started turn 9, finishes on turn 11): Deploy mandatory, real-time intrusion detection and control-layer redundancy across all EU-designated critical infrastructure nodes, using AI-driven monitoring systems under EU-only jurisdiction and human-in-the-loop override protocols — in flight

## New measure
**Establish the European Sovereign AI Corps**  
Create a dedicated, EU-level technical force with authority to audit, operate, and intervene in AI systems running on sovereign infrastructure, combining top-tier talent with legal powers to ensure continuity and control.

`Category: 5 (Public technical capacity and research)`  
`Size:` large

`Targeted effect:` +15 to +18 eu_ai_sovereignty by turn 13 through concentrated expertise, direct operational access to critical AI systems, and the ability to independently verify safety and integrity; contributes to long-term ai_safety by embedding public-sector assurance capacity

## Priority
**Launch the Critical Systems Shield Initiative** — because the openweight release and the loss-of-control incident this turn mean that AI-enabled attacks are no longer hypothetical, and our obligation under the expiring two-year commitment is to ensure that critical systems do not fail before we can build the longer-term capacity the new commitment demands.

## In practice
We are executing under dual pressure: the immediate threat of AI-driven systemic failure and the structural erosion of European agency. The Critical Systems Shield Initiative is now live under Article 12 of the NIS2 Directive, with ENISA coordinating a rapid rollout of AI-powered intrusion detection systems across Tier 1 and Tier 2 operators in energy, health, and transport. Funding is drawn from the Digital Europe Programme and the Civil Protection Mechanism, with fast-tracked grants to mid-sized operators in Bulgaria, Romania, and Slovakia to prevent capability gaps. Resistance from German and Polish regulators over enforcement scope is being addressed through bilateral legal clarifications, while French telecoms are being offered transitional equivalence if they can demonstrate real-time telemetry and human override under EU legal jurisdiction. The Foundational Compute Reserve continues construction at the three designated EuroHPC sites, with accelerated grid connections negotiated under the Energy Security Regulation, though progress remains behind schedule due to semiconductor shortages exacerbated by U.S. supply chain coercion.

At the same time, we are launching the European Sovereign AI Corps to close the gap between having compute and being able to control what runs on it. This is not another research programme — it is an operational capability. The Corps will be staffed with 1,200 engineers, auditors, and red-team specialists, recruited through a pan-European talent call with enhanced mobility rights and salary parity with frontier labs. Hosted jointly by EuroHPC and ENISA, it will have legal authority to inspect model weights, trace agent behaviour, and trigger fail-safes on any system running on EU-sovereign infrastructure. Its first mission will be to certify the Shield Initiative’s monitoring AI as free of backdoors and unintended agency. We are negotiating with member states to waive national security exemptions that would block access, offering in return full transparency and joint command protocols. This is the core of our new direction: not just resilience, but control — not just defence, but the capacity to act.
