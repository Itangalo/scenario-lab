### EU Responds with Shield Initiative

In response, the EU launches the **Critical Systems Shield Initiative**, a large category 6 measure aimed at hardening energy, health, and transport networks against AI-driven attacks. Mandating sovereign-run intrusion detection and human-in-the-loop overrides, it begins integration under ENISA leadership. However, early resistance emerges: Germany and Poland challenge the scope of Article 12 enforcement, while French telecom operators warn of interoperability risks with U.S. cloud stacks. Fast-tracking avoids total deadlock, but delays are expected.
