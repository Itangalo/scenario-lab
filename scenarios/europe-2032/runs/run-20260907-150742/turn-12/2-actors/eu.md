The EAAD portal now hosts the full certification report, open to scrutiny, because we have learned that trust cannot be decreed. The cost was years of frozen capital and silent strain, but it is behind us. The framework transitions to routine maintenance under the Digital Europe Programme and NIS3 enforcement, and its portfolio charge ends. We now turn from continuity to sovereignty — not as a luxury, but as the next layer of survival.

We are being rationed, not cut — but rationing is just slow strangulation. That is why we are pressing the Accelerate Sovereign Compute Siting initiative with full force, invoking Article 122 TFEU to override permitting delays in Bavaria and Wallonia. €4.2bn in redirected Cohesion Fund tranches and EIB green-digital bonds are now committed. We are fast-tracking grid access through ENTSO-E emergency protocols, targeting brownfield sites in Spain, Poland, and Finland where cooling and power infrastructure already exist. This is not consensus; it is the closest we can get to unity under duress.

This will not replace frontier systems — not yet — but it will serve as a live testbed for emergency response coordination and grid stability under AI control, with full transparency and public audit. It is the first real alternative under the `no_more_free_rides` doctrine: if a member state seeks access outside the framework, we can now offer a secure, sovereign alternative within 90 days. This pilot is not insurance. It is the beginning of independence.
