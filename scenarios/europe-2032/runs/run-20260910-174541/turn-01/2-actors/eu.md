## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Hardening and Detection Pact**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with joint detection, exercised cross-border response, and emergency procurement of backup control systems.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because the autumn intrusion proved existing detection would not have caught weeks-long presence in EU grids, and resilience is the mandate we can deliver while compute builds.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Hardening and Detection Pact, because live undetected access to EU transmission operators outranks even the compute build in immediacy.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the EU-CyCLONe network with a binding detection upgrade for designated transmission system operators, major ports including Rotterdam/Antwerp/Hamburg, and regional water utilities, funded by repurposed Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility lines plus member-state co-finance via the Competitiveness Council. CER and NIS2 implementing acts give the legal base; Energy and Transport Council formations must carry the mandates against operator resistance over cost and disclosure.

In parallel we push the inherited InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty package through DG CNECT and accelerated-permitting zones, using the US June switch-off scare to hold cohesion on ASML and grid-connection reform. We frame hardening as the condition for public tolerance of that build-out: protection now buys permission to build.
