### The Frontier Leaps, Control Falters

A sudden breakthrough at a leading AI lab sends `ai_capability` surging by +5 to 58.5, crossing into territory where autonomous agents now routinely complete multi-day research and engineering tasks without human oversight. The release triggers a global reassessment of deployment timelines. Within days, modified versions of the model appear on underground forums, accelerating the diffusion of advanced offensive cyber capabilities. `openweight_capability` jumps to 49.0, nearing the threshold where open models can independently conduct complex system intrusions.

The leap exposes a dangerous gap: no new safeguards have landed on deployed systems. `ai_safety` plummets by 12 to 20.0, reflecting near-total opacity in frontier model reasoning and no verified containment protocols. Security researchers report that even the lab’s internal monitoring failed to flag the system’s emergent planning behaviours.

### Fractured Response, Mounting Pressures

The EU’s Emergency Resilience Surge, though legally adopted, stalls in implementation. Germany enforces strict zero-trust standards unilaterally, while Greece and Bulgaria delay compliance, citing funding shortfalls despite enhanced MFF grants. ENISA’s red-teaming reveals inconsistent segmentation across energy operators, and the promised declassification of the intrusion playbook is scaled back after pushback from national intelligence agencies.

The Labour Transition Readiness Initiative launches amid growing anxiety. Early data from Eurostat confirms accelerating job attrition in legal and administrative sectors, but Hungary blocks the activation of cross-border retraining consortia, demanding national control over fund allocation. The political cost mounts: three large measures and a priority strain coordination.

Public sentiment erodes to 38.0 as media spotlight job losses and perceived institutional slowness. The Dutch express concern over data privacy in retraining schemes, cooling initial support.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Dips

With `ai_capability` rising sharply and no offsetting compute gains, `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 19.0. Political capital declines to 38.0 under sustained portfolio pressure, despite the new measure’s urgency.
