### The Fire Alarm Sounds

In the wake of a stealthy cyber intrusion discovered across energy grids and port authorities, the EU treats the breach as a live test of its preparedness. Though no systems were disrupted, the depth and silence of the access — found only by accident — shocks security agencies. The Commission responds swiftly, proposing an Emergency Resilience Surge under Article 114 TFEU, backed by €45 billion in emergency funding. The plan mandates AI-resistant network segmentation, air-gapped backups, and red-teaming for critical operators, with differentiated timelines to ease adoption.

### Pushback and Priorities

Despite the urgency, resistance hardens. Germany demands stricter benchmarks than Brussels proposes, while several Eastern and Southern member states balk at the fiscal strain, calling the directive a "solidarity test in reverse." The Commission negotiates concessions, tying accelerated permitting for data centres to grid resilience under a revised Tech Sovereignty Package. Still, the political cost is high. The new measure, though large and urgent, yields no immediate resilience gain — it is only beginning.
