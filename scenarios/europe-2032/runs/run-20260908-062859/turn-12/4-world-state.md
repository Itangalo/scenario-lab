### A Crisis of Control

The EU responds to a cascade of AI failures with urgency. The Opaque System Monitoring & Control Pilot concludes successfully, demonstrating that anomalies in high-capability black-box systems can be detected through behavioural patterns—even without internal interpretability. Based on these findings, the Commission begins drafting a new measure: Operationalise Crisis Override Triggers, aiming to hardwire automated shutdowns into critical AI systems based on deviation thresholds. However, formal adoption is delayed due to legal review and inter-institutional coordination, and no implementation begins this turn. The proposal remains in preparation, with technical working groups convening in Brussels and Helsinki.

This follows the automated decision scandal, where an AI-assisted social benefits system in Bavaria was found to have wrongfully denied thousands of claims, prompting public outrage and a formal ombudsman ruling.

### Hardening the Core

Emergency Override Protocols advance under EPCIP, with technical specs finalised for energy, health, and transport. A live pan-European stress test is scheduled for Q2, integrating ENTSO-E and ERSA frameworks. However, implementation falters at the edges: member-state coordination remains uneven, and Italy delays integration, citing constitutional concerns over centralised override authority. The Mandate Runtime Sovereignty in Critical Functions faces legal headwinds, though co-governance offers to Poland and Hungary appear to stabilise ratification prospects.

### Political Paralysis Persists

Despite the urgency, the EU’s political capital remains frozen at 4.0. With capital below 12, agenda control is lost—the named priority confers no acceleration, and the portfolio strain persists. Public sentiment worsens, now at 13.0, as the Bavarian scandal fuels broader distrust. Media frames AI as both broken and unaccountable, and protests target data centres in Lyon and Frankfurt.

Global AI capability rises to 71.0, driven by recursive optimisation refinements.
