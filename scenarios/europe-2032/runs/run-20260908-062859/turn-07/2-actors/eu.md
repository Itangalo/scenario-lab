## In practice
We are building the spine of European AI sovereignty — not as a backup, but as the core. The European Sovereign Compute & Control Grid is now legally anchored under Article 122 TFEU as critical infrastructure, unlocking fast-tracked permitting and cross-border energy integration through the TEN-E framework. We are directing €80bn from the MFF and InvestEU to begin site development in Finland, southern Germany, and the Netherlands, with the condition that all compute above 60.0 capability hosted on the grid must route through our new EU-Controlled AI Evaluation and Intervention Nodes. These nodes — to be operational in France and Finland by turn 10 — will not only evaluate models pre-deployment but will have live monitoring and, where necessary, override authority during incidents. This is not theoretical: the cyber_major_incident that hit public services this turn was executed with AI-generated exploit chains, and attribution remains stalled. We cannot afford another six months of reactive patching.

At the same time, we are confronting the backlash from the open-source community. The emergence of OSARN is not a failure of communication — it is a political movement, and we must treat it as such. We are engaging through the Open Source Observatory, offering compliance exemptions for models below 55.0 and technical support for watermarking and distributed authentication. But we will not retreat on the principle: if a model can plan, persist, and act in the world, and it runs in our hospitals or our grids, we must be able to see it and stop it. The Secure the Open Frontier measure has ended, but its lesson endures — licensing alone cannot secure open weights. That is why the new Evaluation and Intervention Nodes apply not just to closed systems, but to any system above 60.0 capability, open or closed, that touches critical functions. We are drafting a Council regulation under Article 114 TFEU to establish this as a harmonised standard, knowing it will face legal challenges. But the alternative — a patchwork of national overrides or no control at all — is not sovereignty. It is surrender.
