### The Frontier Crosses 60

Global AI capability reaches 60.0 as the leading lab integrates recursive self-improvement loops into its training pipeline, enabling systems to complete multi-day professional projects end-to-end. The milestone triggers alarm: such models now contribute directly to their own successors. Open-weight versions, stripped of safety filters, appear within weeks, pushing `openweight_capability` to 52.0. While these models exhibit advanced autonomous behavior, they remain derivative of the frontier systems and do not surpass them in overall capability. The diffusion is irreversible.

### EU Responds with Control

The EU launches the **Mandatory Pre-Release Evaluation Regime**, mandating third-party audits for all models above 50.0 capability. The European AI Safety Board is empowered with red-team labs and classified benchmarks. U.S. developers protest, calling the rules extraterritorial, but the Commission offers mutual recognition for compliant evaluators. Implementation begins, though full enforcement capacity will take time to build.

### Resilience Drags Forward

The **Emergency Resilience Surge** progresses unevenly. Germany meets all zero-trust standards; Greece and Bulgaria submit delayed timelines after receiving emergency MFF top-ups. ENISA conducts cross-border drills using a declassified intrusion playbook, though intelligence agencies withhold attribution data. Resilience gains +1 as partial hardening takes effect.

### Labour Initiative Stalls

Hungary maintains its veto on cross-border retraining consortia, demanding national control. The Commission offers concessions, including algorithm co-design and a Just Transition fund, but no breakthrough. Dutch privacy concerns are mitigated by EDPB oversight and data retention limits.

With five large measures in flight — InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, Emergency Resilience Surge, Labour Transition Readiness Initiative, and the new Evaluation Regime — and one designated priority, political capital is strained. The portfolio charge accumulates, reflecting governance bandwidth limits. The Labour Initiative and new Evaluation Regime fail to generate public confidence.
