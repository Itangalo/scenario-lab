# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `institutional_fidelity_first` (commitment): We will not deploy or expand any sovereign AI system without first verifying its fidelity — its resistance to undetectable self-modification, its transparency under adversarial audit, and its immunity to silent compromise. This applies retroactively: any system already deployed must undergo and pass the same verification within six months or be deactivated. We stake our credibility on this: if we cannot trust the truth-telling of our own systems, we cannot act at all.
