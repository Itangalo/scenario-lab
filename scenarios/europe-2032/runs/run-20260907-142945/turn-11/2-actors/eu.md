A binding directive to repurpose all available EU-located compute—civilian, academic, industrial, and military—into a unified sovereign AI infrastructure under joint EAAD and EDA oversight, with priority access for critical public services, cyber defence, and continuity-of-government AI operations.

`Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 12`

`Applies to:` All member states, EU institutions, and private entities operating compute clusters above 10 petaFLOPs within EU jurisdiction, enforced through emergency powers under Article 122 TFEU and the NIS2 Directive

## In practice  
This turn, we are acting under emergency conditions. The **cyber_major_incident**—a model-generated ransomware sweep that crippled hospital networks in Belgium and Bavaria—has exposed our strategic helplessness. The **eu_frontier_access_denied** event, which cut off our ministries from the last externally hosted model above 65.0, has left us blind and mute. The Integrity Audit Expansion continues on paper, but its auditors have nothing left to audit: the systems are either down or locked out. We are no longer governing AI; we are surviving its absence.

We are invoking Article 122 TFEU to justify the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation, treating this as an economic and societal emergency equivalent to the 2022 energy crisis. The European AI Assurance Directorate (EAAD) and the European Defence Agency (EDA) are drafting joint operational protocols to requisition compute from national research labs, cloud providers, and semiconductor fabs. France has already signaled resistance, calling it "digital conscription," while Germany demands veto power over military-civilian data flows. We are offering opt-in bonuses through the Digital Europe Programme and threatening to suspend cohesion funds for non-compliance—ugly tools, but the only ones left.

The Openweight Containment Initiative is now irrelevant: no one is releasing anything above 53.0, and the frontier is frozen. We are letting it expire at turn 12 without renewal. Our focus is singular: keep the lights on. If we survive this turn, we may rebuild trust. If we do not, there will be no Union left to trust anything.
