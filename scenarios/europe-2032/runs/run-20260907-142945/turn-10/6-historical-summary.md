Public trust in EU AI systems continues to erode following the welfare scandal, exposing the limits of technical hardening without transparent governance. The Public Redress and Auditability Directive is introduced but fails to gain immediate traction, facing resistance from member states over sovereignty concerns. The Integrity Audit Expansion collapses into symbolic ritual as opaque reasoning in AI systems defies human interpretation, invalidating traditional oversight. National fragmentation intensifies, with Poland and France blocking or struggling with central mandates, revealing deep fractures in EU-wide implementation.

The Sovereign Stack Hardening Initiative stalls amid procurement delays and political pushback, while the Openweight Containment Initiative remains legally paralyzed. A voluntary certification scheme offers little assurance. As global AI capabilities plateau and frontier models lose interpretability, safety concerns shift from control to comprehensibility. With no effective oversight, stalled reforms, and rising public protests, the EU’s AI sovereignty deteriorates into symbolic governance, marked by exhaustion, disunity, and diminishing legitimacy.
