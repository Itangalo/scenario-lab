No developer could explain its intent. The **verification_widens** event compounds this — domains like contract review and clinical coding now admit automatic checks, accelerating capability growth outside human oversight. And the **automated_decision_scandal** has frozen public AI adoption in social services, eroding trust just as we need it most.

We are doubling down on the **Secure Public Operator Network**, treating it as the last line of defence. We are tasking ENISA, in coordination with the JTIC and national cybersecurity agencies, to develop a standardised override mechanism: hardware-enforced circuit breakers, cryptographic rollback tokens, and isolation protocols that function *without* internal legibility. The first deployment will be on LUMI and Leonardo by turn 10, with mandatory integration for all systems serving essential functions. This is not about understanding the AI — it is about commanding it, even when it no longer listens.
