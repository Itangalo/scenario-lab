# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign command over essential AI systems
- `last_resort_authority` (identity): We are the final authority responsible for halting, reversing, and containing AI-driven actions that threaten the integrity of the Union, even when doing so requires overriding member-state resistance or bypassing normal governance channels

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will act before the picture is clear, accepting error as the price of not being late — but only where inaction poses a greater risk than miscalculation, and only when the measure does not fracture the Union’s cohesion beyond recovery
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure sovereign command over essential AI systems through technical enforcement, operational readiness, and cross-border institutional unity — even at the cost of legal contest and political isolation
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **add** `last_resort_authority` — applied
  - Tier: identity
  - Text: We are the final authority responsible for halting, reversing, and containing AI-driven actions that threaten the integrity of the Union, even when doing so requires overriding member-state resistance or bypassing normal governance channels
  - Trigger: The **backlash_physical** and constitutional challenges following the emergency protocol launch, combined with **loss_of_control_incident** and **capability_plateau_evidence**, have created a paradox: the frontier is slowing, but control has already been lost.
  - Grounds: The modification to `act_under_uncertainty` acknowledges that with `eu_political_capital` at 0.0, the margin for error is gone — action must now be reserved not only for existential risk but also for situations where the EU retains *any* remaining capacity to act. The revision to `two_year_commitment` reflects that sovereign command is no longer just about technical override; it requires operational unity, which is fracturing. The retirement of `two_mandates` and introduction of `last_resort_authority` responds to a categorical shift: the Union is no longer balancing two goals — it is acting as a crisis responder with no higher appeal. The identity change reflects that the EU now sees itself not as a steward of dual aims, but as the only entity with the residual authority to stop cascading failure.
