## New measure
**Cyber Shield Directive**

Establish mandatory intrusion detection and response systems across all EU critical infrastructure operators, with real-time telemetry sharing and red-teaming requirements.

## In practice
This turn, we are launching the Cyber Shield Directive through Article 114 TFEU, using the internal market legal base to mandate harmonised cybersecurity standards across energy, water, ports and digital infrastructure. The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) will be empowered to set technical specifications, requiring network-level anomaly detection powered by AI-driven behavioural analysis, automated reporting to a new Joint Threat Intelligence Cell in Brussels, and quarterly adversarial simulations conducted by national CERTs in rotation. Member states resist the cost and sovereignty implications, so we are pairing the directive with an immediate €12bn disbursement from the Recovery and Resilience Facility to co-fund implementation, weighted toward less wealthy members. We are also fast-tracking amendments to the NIS2 Directive to close loopholes for smaller operators.

Simultaneously, we are defending the InvestAI Gigafactories plan against growing fiscal scrutiny. Germany and the Nordic Council warn of state-aid distortions and green-energy trade-offs, so we are restructuring the funding tranches to require matching private investment and grid decarbonisation plans per site. The Commission is negotiating with EDF, Siemens and STMicroelectronics to anchor demand through public procurement of sovereign AI for nuclear safety modelling and industrial automation. Separately, the Tech sovereignty package is advancing permitting reforms in seven pilot regions, but resistance from local governments over land use is mounting — we are responding with a new "sovereignty bonus" in cohesion funds for municipalities that approve data centre zones within six months. All of this proceeds under constrained political capital, which makes prioritising Cyber Shield essential: if we lose control of our infrastructure, nothing else survives to matter.
