### A Crisis-Driven Turn  
The EU’s response to the cyber_major_incident unfolded under intense pressure. The **Cyber Shield Directive**, a category 6 measure, advanced from proposal to binding standards under Article 114, with ENISA issuing technical specifications for AI-driven detection systems. France, the Netherlands, and Finland began integrating real-time telemetry, but delays in Germany and Hungary slowed full rollout. The **Joint Threat Intelligence Cell (JTIC)** was proposed as a new initiative this turn, with staffing commitments secured and pilot data access negotiations underway; however, legal disputes over data sovereignty have delayed operational launch, and full implementation will extend into the next turn.

### Resilience Gains, Sovereignty Loses  
Despite incomplete deployment, early integration of Cyber Shield components contributed to resilience, accelerating threat correlation and shortening response windows. The JTIC, while not yet active, is expected to amplify these gains once operational. However, the **export_control_escalation** — restricting mature-node chip tools to volume licences, excluding the EU — deepened strategic dependence. This event, combined with `ai_capability` rising by 2.0, triggered a −2 and −1 deduction in `eu_ai_sovereignty`, respectively. Despite efforts to anchor private investment in the **InvestAI Gigafactories**, fiscal renegotiations and 60% co-investment demands signaled weakening momentum.

### Safety Breaks Through, Politics Strained  
The **safety_breakthrough** — a deployable interpretability method allowing prediction of emergent model behaviours — was rapidly adopted across major labs, lifting `ai_safety` by +5. This, combined with the cyber incident, justified the JTIC’s dual role in feeding operational data back into model assurance. Political capital eroded due to portfolio strain: only one new large measure (JTIC) was introduced this turn, joining the three ongoing measures (InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech sovereignty package, Cyber Shield Directive) and the standing priority, resulting in a charge of −9. A proposal bonus of +4 applied for launching JTIC, a category 6 response to the active `emergent_cyber_ransom_surge`. Net change in political capital was −5, from 39.0 to 35.0.

### Public Mood Holds  
No major AI benefits or scandals emerged. Job displacement concerns simmered but were overshadowed by security debates. `public_sentiment` remained flat.
