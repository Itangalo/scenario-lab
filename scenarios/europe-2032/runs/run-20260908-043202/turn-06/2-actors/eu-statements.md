# Statements: The European Union (turn 6)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI access for essential functions

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Deliver sovereign AI access for essential functions by turn 8 through legally guaranteed, technically operational, and politically enforceable continuity mechanisms, including fallback compute, trusted public operators, and binding access rights
  - Grounds: The Emergency Compute Mobilisation Directive has revealed both progress and fragility. We have repurposed clusters, but only half the targeted institutions are online, and legal resistance in Germany and Italy shows that access without enforceability is not sovereignty. The US election outcome confirms that external access will tighten, not ease. Our commitment must now harden from aspiration to delivery: not just securing capacity, but ensuring it is *unbreakable* by legal, technical or political failure. This shift raises the stakes and focuses our capital spend.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
