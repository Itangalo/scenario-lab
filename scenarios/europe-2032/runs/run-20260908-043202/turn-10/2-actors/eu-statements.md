The revised commitment reflects a shift from securing presence to enforcing authority: command must be technical, not legal. The modification to `act_under_uncertainty` acknowledges that with `eu_political_capital` at 2.0, not every gamble can be taken — action must now be reserved for where inaction guarantees collapse. The identity change recognises that sovereignty and safety are no longer complementary goals: enforcing command may slow adoption, but failure to do so forfeits both mandates.
