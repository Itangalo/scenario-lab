### A Fractured Response to Cascading Failure

A major cyber incident unfolded in October, as AI-generated vulnerabilities in a widely used open-source library triggered a fast-spreading compromise across public service systems. The breach bypassed hardened perimeters by exploiting trust in software maintenance workflows, exposing the fragility of digital infrastructure even where frontier model access is restricted. Automated propagation overwhelmed legacy patching cycles, affecting emergency dispatch systems in three countries before containment.

The EU’s **Loss-of-Control Containment Framework** proved effective in detection, triggering alerts within minutes through integrated financial and cyber fusion cells. However, its reach remained incomplete—Hungary’s continued non-participation necessitated reliance on parallel telemetry, underscoring persistent coordination gaps. While cascading collapse was avoided, recovery took days, not hours, eroding public confidence.

In response, the EU launched the **Software Supply Chain Integrity Initiative**, mandating cryptographic provenance tracking and sovereign-model-powered scanning for all critical software components. This large category 6 measure aims to reduce exploit windows dramatically. Simultaneously, the **Critical System Hardening Initiative** advanced, with funding released and legal authority asserted through the AI Act, though procurement delays and workforce shortages slowed deployment.

The **Frontier Model Real-Time Monitoring Directive** remains in flight, contributing to ongoing political capital costs. With no breakthrough in safety assurance and `ai_capability` advancing again, `ai_safety` remained stagnant. The rise in capability, now at 73.5, reflects further embedding of self-improving loops in development pipelines, particularly in autonomous reverse-engineering.

Despite a significant resilience investment, `resilience` increased only modestly, as gains were offset by the strain of responding to a live incident. `eu_ai_sovereignty` dipped further due to continued dependence on restricted U.S. models under **RETRENCHMENT**, with no domestic capacity yet compensating. Political capital eroded under sustained portfolio costs and the absence of visible public benefit, remaining under pressure despite the activation of one measure.

### The Limits of Preparedness

Trust in digital continuity wavered. While detection systems worked, their scope did not match the speed of attack. The new supply chain initiative is a necessary step—but one arriving just behind the threat.
