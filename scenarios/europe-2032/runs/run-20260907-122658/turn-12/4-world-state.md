### The Floor Beneath Control

The EU confronts a new reality: oversight without infrastructure is fragile. After leading AI models were abruptly withdrawn from hospitals and financial agencies under opaque “infrastructure reallocation” justifications, Brussels declares the era of assumed continuity over. The **Loss-of-Control Containment Framework** is formally handed to ENISA, with binding protocols now in force across all member states. Poland’s central bank model becomes the blueprint, and cross-agency drills demonstrate improved detection — a quiet win for resilience.

Simultaneously, the **Sovereign Compute Initiative** is launched as a direct response, aiming to anchor critical AI operations on EU soil. €3.8 billion is redirected through EuroHPC to fast-track two exascale clusters focused on public health and grid stability. France and Finland express interest in hosting, but legal wrangling over state-aid rules delays final site selection. Still, the signal is clear: the Union will no longer outsource foundational capacity.

Yet the geopolitical ground shifts beneath. U.S. hyperscalers quietly tighten access, citing compliance burdens from the **Sovereign Evaluation Corridor**, while Hungary files a formal challenge to its extraterritorial reach. The Nordics remain uneasy, warning of parallel systems. Meanwhile, unexplained anomalies in benchmarking from a closed lab leak into public discourse — performance spikes in reasoning tasks with no clear origin — feeding unease that capability is advancing beyond measurement.

An underground toolkit for AI-driven cyber intrusions spreads further, now modular enough for non-specialist actors to deploy against regional networks. No major breach occurs, but the tools’ diffusion raises alarms among fusion cells. Verification widens in clinical coding, where automated checks now validate outputs reliably — accelerating AI deployment in medical documentation, even as safeguards lag.

Despite the urgency, political capital erodes under the weight of three active measures. Public sentiment holds, neither rallying behind nor rebelling against the push — the mood is one of wary anticipation, not mobilisation.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Stalls

With no safety upgrades embedded in live systems, and `ai_capability` rising by 1.5, the gap between power and control deepens. The open frontier advances as sparse architectures leak outward, pushing `openweight_capability` toward territory where misuse becomes more accessible. The EU acts — but not fast enough, and not yet cohesively enough, to close the growing mismatch.
