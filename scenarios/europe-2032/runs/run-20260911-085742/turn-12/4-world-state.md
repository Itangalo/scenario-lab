### The lists that moved
In three regions, hospital administrators could point to something that had not happened in two years: waiting lists for diagnostics and for routine surgery actually fell. Triage-support software, rostering tools and accelerated discharge paperwork, run on European procurement contracts, cut decision times from months to days in pilot sites. Health ministers toured the wards with cameras. For once Brussels was credited, not blamed.

The Commission moved to bottle it. Joint procurement teams from health and digital departments offered the same package to willing health systems, with reference visits to the sites where it had worked and money shifted from existing digital and health funds. No new law, no new data-centre permits — a deliberate attempt to avoid re-igniting siting fights.

### Wards held, formally closed
The bio containment surge that had carried transfers and joint stocks through 2031 formally closed. Stocks were inventoried, tracing teams stood down to a standby roster. Ministers declared the job done.

On the wards the declaration landed badly. Retention premia under the bridge programme arrived in some countries, missed payroll cycles in others. Cross-border hires filled gaps in the north, while southern hospitals reported resignations mid-shift continuing. Employment offices did pay the first income-bridge tranches to displaced clerical workers, but caseworkers described queues, appeals and confusion over eligibility. The scale-up tour was met in two cities by nurses protesting clinic closures, asking why success was being replicated before staffing was stabilised.

Grids avoided cascade and municipal patching held, but fraud using leaked models spread in reimbursement claims, and border-region whispers persisted of back-room clinics offering unlicensed remedies cooked up with freely available models. Single sources, no confirmation, but enough to keep local press anxious.

Frontier systems remained unreadable, disbursements frozen, and no sovereignty restart was even attempted. The win was real, narrow, and fragile — polling finally ticked up, but from deep hostility.
