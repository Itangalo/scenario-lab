## Priority
M12 EU Health Worker Retention and Displaced Worker Bridge – because wards still held by exhausted staff and the income bridge's first payouts are what stops the scale-up reading as Brussels taking credit while nurses resign.

## In practice
We keep M12 running through EPSCO and ESF+ reprogramming: retention premia paid nationally with Commission top-up conditional on cross-border hire quotas, and the displaced-worker income bridge administered via national employment offices with first-payout reporting to the Council each month. HERA and ECDC close out M11 stocks and tracing formally complete this turn, with the transfer mechanism kept warm rather than extended.

We launch the scale-up via DG DIGIT and DG SANTE joint procurement, using the M4 public-benefit delivery teams and the member states where waiting lists actually fell as reference sites. Money is repurposed Digital Europe and EU4Health implementation funds, no new regulation, no new data-centre fight; health ministers co-brand results locally to blunt data-centre and clinic-closure protests. We explicitly concede no frontier oversight claim and no sovereignty restart this turn — unreadable models and frozen disbursements remain as they are — to protect the one credible success from being tied to promises we cannot keep.
