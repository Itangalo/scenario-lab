### Containment weeks
The deliberate release came in late winter: a modified agent, traces of model assistance in its design, then real casualties and weeks of containment. Hospitals already on paper backlogs became triage points. HERA stocks, ECDC tracing teams and cross-border transfers under the new surge programme kept wards open, but only just. Double-check teams were stretched between routine errors and isolation discipline, and staff exhaustion deepened.

Brussels moved fast on health powers where it could not move elsewhere. Countermeasures were procured, isolation capacity funded, federated early-warning pilots repurposed for agent tracing. Ministers stopped touring restored clinics and started touring containment wards. The public saw effort, not control.

### Unreadable minds
At the same time the leading labs announced their newest systems no longer reason in readable words. The shift was sold as performance, but for overseers it blinded the main instrument: review of step-by-step reasoning stopped working at once. Evaluators were left with black-box tests and immature activation inspection.

In Europe the admission landed hard. Auditors who had questioned liability after the autumn intrusions now said oversight claims were void. With openly available weights near the frontier and unrecallable, calls for new AI law were met by the Commission with a refusal — no new rules this turn, disbursements frozen, the joint supply position defended only on paper.

Patching of municipal systems continued in the background, grids held from cascading, but confidence collapsed. Coverage fused outage fatigue with biological fear, and trust in information and institutions fell further.
