Tracing standby and stocks from the closed surge stay warm under HERA/ECDC without reopening a formal surge.

We launch the chokepoint defence via Trade and Competitiveness Council: Commission opens Anti-Coercion examination on US extraterritorial servicing bans, puts Dutch export licences for older DUV tools under Union consultation, and mandates no further NL cut without Council authorisation in exchange for EU legal cover and compensation backstop for ASML. We offer Beijing, Seoul and other cut-off customers joint servicing terms in return for supply-chain cooperation, to force Washington to negotiate with the Union rather than dictate to The Hague.
