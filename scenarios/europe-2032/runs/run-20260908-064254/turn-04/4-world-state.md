### Offensive AI Goes Live

A wave of automated ransomware cripples hospital networks across three member states, exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities generated by AI and evading detection through adaptive obfuscation. The attack’s tooling is traced to a model-generated exploit chain, confirming that offensive capability is no longer confined to labs. With attribution stalled and recovery slow, the incident underscores a new reality: defense now lags by design.

### Open Frontier Widens

Days later, a leading lab releases a powerful open-weight model—downloaded over 200,000 times in a week. Its capabilities approach the closed frontier, enabling distributed use in cyber operations and research alike. `openweight_capability` rises significantly, reflecting rapid diffusion and early adoption, but remains bounded by the pace of overall frontier advancement, which itself increases to 58.0 as verification-enabled deployment accelerates in engineering and legal domains.

### EU Responds with Binding Protocols

In response, the EU launches the **Mandatory Anomaly Detection & Response Protocols**, mandating real-time monitoring for critical operators and integrating them with national CSIRTs and the European Agent Observatory. ENISA coordinates rollout under NIS2 powers, backed by cohesion funding conditionality. The move strengthens resilience architecture, but resistance from Visegrád states simmers over enforcement, and full implementation will take time.

### Control Talks Stalled, Talent Thin

Efforts to finalize the Sovereign Control Stack stall as northern members reject emergency override clauses. A compromise establishes a High Council for AI Resilience, but decision-making complexity grows. Meanwhile, staffing shortages hit both the Observatory and the new protocols, as private sector demand outpaces public retention.

### Science Steps In — Not Yet Operational

The Commission begins formalizing the emergent lab consortium into the EU Defence AI Collaborative via Horizon Europe, aiming to boost independent evaluation capacity. While the initiative signals a shift toward homegrown assurance, it remains in development and will not become operational until future turns. This step does not constitute a new implemented measure at this time.

### Sentiment Slips, Capital Holds

Public concern rises as protests grow near Gigafactory sites. Moratoriums on AI in welfare decisions gain ground. Yet political capital stabilizes—just—due to timely action after the cyberattack.
