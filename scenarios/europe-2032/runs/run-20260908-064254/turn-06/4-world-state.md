### Frontier Crosses Threshold

AI capability advances to 60.5 as frontier systems begin managing multi-day engineering and legal projects end-to-end, displacing junior professionals across sectors. The milestone marks entry into a new regime: AI now executes complex, goal-directed work without human supervision, raising alarm over control and accountability.

### Open Weights Incremental Gain

A major open-weight release—downloaded widely across European research and hacking communities—begins to diffuse, contributing to a modest uplift in open capabilities to 48.5. The model supports autonomous agents capable of sustained reasoning and early-stage cyber exploitation, narrowing the gap with closed systems. Its spread is rapid, but full integration into high-leverage applications remains limited by infrastructure and expertise constraints. Enforcement agencies report preliminary misuse in credential harvesting and social engineering, but at a scale not yet matching worst-case projections.

### EU Pushes Oversight Forward

The **Mandatory Anomaly Detection & Response Protocols** formally finish, achieving full legal force. ENISA deploys interoperability envoys, accelerating integration with national cybersecurity agencies. The “Red Sky” drill exposes coordination flaws, but confirms detection systems can identify anomalies in real time. Visegrád compliance remains partial, but financial incentives keep momentum.

The **European Agent Observatory** is now fully operational, providing a baseline for monitoring high-autonomy systems. Building on this foundation, the European Commission proposes the **Sovereign Verification Regime for High-Autonomy Systems**, which would mandate real-time behavioural audits by EU-accredited bodies. The proposal faces immediate resistance from frontier developers, who question jurisdiction and feasibility. Due to its complexity and high implementation cost, full enforcement is not expected before the next turn at the earliest.

### Political Strain Mounts

COREPER II remains deadlocked on EAIRC’s emergency powers, delaying operationalisation. The **Tech Sovereignty Package** finishes this turn, but delivers only modest private investment momentum. Political capital erodes under sustained portfolio pressure. Public sentiment holds at 39.0, as job losses and opacity fuel distrust.
