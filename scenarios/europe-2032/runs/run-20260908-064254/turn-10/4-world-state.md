### Frontier Creeps Forward, Safety Stalls

Global AI capability advances to 63.0, as closed models integrate deeper into autonomous research pipelines, particularly in materials science and regulatory forecasting. The gains are narrow but persistent. Open-weight capability rises to 51.0, buoyed by accessible fine-tuning toolkits and distributed training collectives, though no major release closes the gap.

### Emergency Powers Push Meets Immediate Resistance

The European Commission proposes the **Emergency Powers for AI Infrastructure Requisition**, invoking Article 42 TEU to establish authority to override national and corporate refusal during systemic incidents. Framed as a crisis contingency, the draft regulation targets compute access, model weights, and energy supply. A classified ENISA assessment details non-compliance: German grid restrictions, French side-channel access deals, and Finnish export-laundering risks.

Despite high-level backing, the proposal triggers immediate backlash. Germany decries federal overreach, France questions strategic autonomy, and U.S. hyperscalers warn of investment flight. Even Finland, previously supportive, expresses concern over judicial bypass. The draft is fast-tracked, but consensus is fragile.

### Fragmentation Deepens, Trust Erodes

Regulatory divergence among Germany, France, and Poland worsens, fragmenting enforcement of the AI Act. Firms now maintain separate compliance systems, slowing deployment and undermining the single market. The Commission warns of a de facto breakup.

Public sentiment slips to 37.0, as media spotlight growing job displacement and algorithmic opacity. A high-profile case involving AI-driven welfare denials in two member states fuels resentment. The 'Public AI' coalition expands, demanding transparency.

### Limited Gains Amid Mounting Costs

The **middle_power_coalition** event strengthens EU leverage: export-licence alignment and shared evaluation capacity improve political capital. However, no measure finishes with real effect—verification lacked teeth, compute sovereignty stalled. The new large measure begins, costing political capital. Sovereignty remains stagnant, safety erodes due to capability rise without matching assurance, and resilience dips as preparedness lags.
