### EU Verification Regime Completes, But Questions Loom

The **Sovereign Verification Regime for High-Autonomy Systems** formally completes this turn, establishing real-time audit access for critical AI deployments. However, its authority is immediately questioned: without sovereign control over model training, auditors lack access to foundational weights or training data. Critics label it “oversight without ownership,” and a senior ENISA official resigns, warning that the regime verifies only what developers choose to show.

### Compute Push Meets Resistance

The **Accelerated Compute Sovereignty Initiative** enters implementation, with emergency permitting declared and €18bn in financing secured. Yet political friction mounts. ECOFIN blocks full disbursement, citing fiscal risks, and Germany delays grid access negotiations, demanding guarantees on industrial off-take. Talks with hyperscalers stall: DeepMind and Meta reject the EU subsidiary requirement as “operational surrender,” while NVIDIA insists on export exceptions for next-gen chips. Only Finland shows full alignment, offering tax breaks and fast-tracked environmental waivers.
