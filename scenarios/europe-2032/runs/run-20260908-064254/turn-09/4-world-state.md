### Frontier Creeps Forward, Safety Lags

Global AI capability advances to 62.5 as automated reasoning tools are embedded deeper into clinical trial design and regulatory compliance workflows. The closed frontier continues its incremental climb, though gains are narrowing. Open-weight models rise to 50.5, absorbing spillover from tooling used in legal discovery and diagnostics—still no major release, but growing accessibility of fine-tuning frameworks widens deployment.

### Verification and Compute Initiatives Complete — But Deliver Little

The **Sovereign Verification Regime** and the **Accelerated Compute Sovereignty Initiative** both formally conclude this turn. Yet their impact is underwhelming. The verification regime lacks teeth without access to training data or model weights, and a senior ENISA resignation underscores its limitations. The compute initiative secured financing and permits, but construction remains stalled: Germany withholds grid access, ECOFIN blocks disbursement, and no hyperscaler accepts the subsidiary requirement. The promised exascale facilities exist only on paper.

### Internal Fractures Deepen

A member state unilaterally negotiates direct access to a U.S. hyperscaler’s frontier model, bypassing EU coordination and undercutting the Union’s collective stance. Defended domestically as pragmatic necessity, it is seen externally as fragmentation in action. This act exposes the limits of voluntary alignment and fuels fears of a splintering digital bloc.

### Political Capital Erodes Amid Strategic Doubt

With two large measures finishing but delivering minimal real-world change, political capital declines. The completion of the verification regime yields no bonus—it improved process, not safety or control. The compute initiative, despite its scale, fails to shift sovereignty upward. The priority cost applies, and no new measure offsets the erosion. Behind closed doors, the Commission prepares crisis powers for potential requisition of compute, but unity remains fragile.

Public sentiment holds at 39.0, unmoved by technical milestones that feel disconnected from daily life. Labour displacement and opaque algorithmic decisions continue to fuel quiet resentment.
