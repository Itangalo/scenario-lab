## In practice
This turn, we act on two fronts: one immediate, one structural. On the immediate front, the discovery of undetected AI-coordinated intrusions into energy, water and port systems triggers a Council-level emergency under Article 222 TFEU. We activate the Civil Protection Mechanism and direct ENISA, in coordination with national CSIRTs, to issue binding hardening protocols within 30 days. These will mandate multi-factor authentication, air-gapped monitoring for control systems, and real-time anomaly reporting to a newly activated EU Cyber Shield dashboard. The Commission fast-tracks €12bn in cohesion and recovery funds to cover 80% of implementation costs for affected operators, conditional on compliance by Q2 2027. Resistance from member states citing cost and sovereignty is met with a clear message: the attack was not targeted — its randomness is the threat.

Structurally, we double down on compute sovereignty. The InvestAI Gigafactories programme begins site selection in France, Poland, and Spain, with the Commission offering state-aid waivers for energy and land use in exchange for legal commitments that the compute remains under EU jurisdiction and cannot be remotely disabled. We negotiate with ASML to co-invest in domestic lithography maintenance and training, leveraging our supply-chain weight. The Tech Sovereignty Package advances draft legislation for “AI Zones” with permitting compressed from ten years to 24 months, modelled on the GDPR enforcement timeline. Industry resists the pace, but the denial of frontier access to EU hospitals and ministries gives us leverage: dependence is not neutrality.

Publicly, we frame this not as a technology race but as a defence of decision space. The cyber intrusion was not an attack on data — it was a probe of our capacity to function. The US denial of model access was not a policy dispute — it was a reminder that discretion is not a right. We cannot know if the frontier is plateauing or accelerating, but we know that when the test came, we were unprepared on both fronts. Our response must match that duality.
