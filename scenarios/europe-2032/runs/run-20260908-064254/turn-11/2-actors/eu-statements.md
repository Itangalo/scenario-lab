## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Ensure the EU can observe, contest, and forcibly intervene in AI systems whose behaviour or access lies beyond design or jurisdiction — but only when such intervention is materially possible and politically sustainable, recognising that sovereignty erodes as much from overreach as from weakness.
  - Grounds: Trigger: backlash_physical and the erosion of eu_political_capital to 19.0. The Emergency Powers measure, though legally grounded, has sparked resistance not only from member states and hyperscalers but from civil society and infrastructure workers. Requisition without trust becomes coercion, and coercion without capacity becomes farce. We adjust the commitment to reflect that intervention capacity must be both technically executable and socially tenable — otherwise, it is not sovereignty.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
