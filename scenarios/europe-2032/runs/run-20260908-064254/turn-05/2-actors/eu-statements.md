The frontier is loose, and the state’s role must shift from gatekeeper to guardian of operational continuity. This reframes sovereignty as a function of monitoring, verification, and enforceable intervention—not just infrastructure.
  - Evidence quoted: the two-year commitment period closes this turn
  - Reason: The closure of the commitment period directly ends the timeframe for the original pledge, changing the cost of maintaining it and triggering a necessary reassessment of the EU's strategic posture.
