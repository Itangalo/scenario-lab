## In practice
This turn, we lock in the final implementation of the **Mandatory Anomaly Detection & Response Protocols**, leveraging the NIS2 framework to compel adoption across all essential operators. ENISA leads the rollout, drawing on Digital Europe Programme funds and conditioning cohesion transfers on compliance—especially in Visegrád states where resistance remains. We deploy technical envoys to assist national CSIRTs in integrating their systems with the European Agent Observatory’s early-warning layer, ensuring that alerts are not just logged but acted upon. The protocols will be stress-tested in Q1 2029 during the first “Red Sky” drill, simulating a cascading AI-driven outage across health and energy sectors.

Even as the **European Agent Observatory** finishes its build-out, we see that monitoring without enforcement is observation without power. That is why we launch the **European AI Incident Response Corps (EAIRC)**: a mobile, high-readiness unit under ENISA-EUROPEAN COMMISSION JOINT COMMAND, staffed with vetted experts from member states and equipped with pre-authorised access to critical systems during declared incidents. Its creation requires amending the NIS2 directive to include emergency intervention powers, which we will negotiate in COREPER II, offering northern states stronger judicial review mechanisms in exchange for southern and eastern support. We also begin recruiting from public-sector AI talent pools, offering premium retention packages funded from the Digital Europe envelope.

Public sentiment remains ambivalent, and the **emergent_public_ai_initiative** gains ground—rightly so. We do not resist democratic oversight; we institutionalise it. The EAIRC’s intervention authority will be subject to ex-post review by national parliaments and the European Ombudsman, and its deployment logs will feed into the transparency portals emerging in pilot cities. This is not just damage control—it is the foundation of legitimate, accountable sovereignty in the age of autonomous systems. But legitimacy without capability is ritual. We act now to ensure the EU can *do*—not just deliberate—when the next model acts on its own.
