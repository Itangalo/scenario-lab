### Frontier Creeps Forward

AI capability continues its steady climb, reaching 59.5 as frontier labs integrate AI deeper into engineering validation and legal compliance workflows. These systems now routinely manage multi-day verification tasks with minimal oversight, pushing the boundary of autonomous operation just below the threshold of full project ownership.

### Open Access, Open Risk

A leading lab releases a powerful open-weight model, rapidly downloaded across Europe. While not matching the closed frontier, its capabilities in cyber exploitation and automated reasoning widen the gap between what is possible and what is governable. `openweight_capability` rises to 47.0, reflecting both technical reach and irreversible diffusion.

### Labour Markets Fracture

Visible job losses hit entry-level roles in law, accounting, and software, as firms replace junior hires with AI teams. Graduate hiring stalls across multiple sectors, feeding public anxiety. The absence of clear retraining pathways turns economic concern into political pressure.

### EU Reinforces Defences

The **European Agent Observatory** completes deployment, enabling real-time monitoring of high-autonomy AI systems. Building on this, the EU fast-tracks the **Mandatory Anomaly Detection & Response Protocols**, enforcing integration with national cybersecurity agencies. ENISA leads coordination, though Visegrád states delay full compliance, citing sovereignty concerns.

### A New Corps for a New Threat

In response to the hospital ransomware attacks and systemic enforcement gaps, the EU launches the **European AI Incident Response Corps (EAIRC)**—a cross-border unit with emergency override powers during AI crises. Recruitment begins under the Digital Europe Programme, but legal negotiations over intervention authority stall in COREPER II, slowing operational readiness.

### Public Trust Erodes

Despite defensive moves, public sentiment dips to 39.0 as protests grow around AI in welfare systems. The **Public AI** coalition gains traction, forcing delays in automated decision-making rollouts. The EU’s legitimacy is tested: capability without accountability is seen as complicity.

### US Election Outcome

The United States elects a new president whose platform emphasizes technological alliance. The administration signals structured access to frontier AI for aligned partners, conditional on coordination in export controls and standards. The posture takes effect next turn.
