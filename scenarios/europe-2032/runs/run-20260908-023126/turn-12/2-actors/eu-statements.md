## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Secure the foundations of sovereign AI agency while preventing catastrophic systemic risk and restoring public trust as a foundation for political action
  - Grounds: The `capability_jump` event confirms that unanticipated capabilities are now emerging in verifiable, high-leverage domains — cyber, code, narrow engineering — and they are outpacing institutional response cycles. Meanwhile, the **Emergency Resilience Surge** remains in development, not deployment, and the red-teaming it was meant to enable has already uncovered critical vulnerabilities in real systems. The Union cannot claim to be securing its future if its own critical infrastructure remains exposed, its political capital is draining (now at 14.0), and public sentiment remains hostile (26.0). The commitment stands, but its interpretation must shift: we are no longer building foundations — we are fighting to prevent collapse. The word "restoring" public trust is no longer aspirational; it is a survival condition. Without it, no measure lands.
