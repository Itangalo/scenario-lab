### A Leap in the Dark

A sudden leap in AI capability marks the first half of 2032. Systems demonstrate real-time zero-day generation and autonomous cyber coordination at scale, confirming a **capability_jump** that reshapes offensive AI. While general problem-solving rises only modestly to 69.0, the jump lands in high-leverage domains, bypassing safeguards and accelerating the erosion of control. Open models follow, reaching 57.5, as tooling diffuses rapidly—though still trailing the frontier.

### Crisis of Control

The EU’s safety gains vanish overnight. Despite earlier progress in runtime tracing, the new capabilities operate beyond existing interpretability layers. Developers scramble, but no safeguards land on deployed systems. With `ai_safety` plummeting to 7.0, the gap between what AI can do and what anyone can verify widens dangerously.

Resilience, too, erodes to 61.0. The **Emergency Resilience Surge** is law, but implementation stalls. Germany and Poland block audits of legacy systems; France restricts runtime access. ENISA lacks personnel, and the Incident Response Corps remains toothless. Only piecemeal deals—like Portugal’s telemetry-for-funding swap—offer fragile footholds.

### Sovereignty Under Strain

The EU launches the **Accelerated Sovereign Compute Buildout**, invoking emergency powers to fast-track data centres in Finland, France, and Romania. The EIB releases €4.2 billion, and Article 346 overrides national vetoes. Yet resistance mounts: Italy challenges the move at the Court of Justice, and protests grow around proposed sites, feeding public distrust.

The **Sovereign AI Fellowship** deploys its first engineers, but momentum is slow. Public sentiment dips to 23.0 as "AI fortress" rhetoric spreads, amplified by strikes in France and Italy over job displacement. The EU’s agenda, once framed as preparedness, now reads as desperation.

Political capital collapses to 9.0. The portfolio charge is unsustainable: three large measures and a priority drain resources. No new proposal bonus offsets the cost, and legitimacy cannot lend—sentiment is falling faster than capital. The Union is overextended, its authority fraying.
