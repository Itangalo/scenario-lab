While framed as a response to biosecurity and control risks, the plan is seen as ambitious given thin political capital. Implementation has not yet begun in earnest, pending further coordination and resource allocation.

The shift in posture is noted as a permissive condition for future collaboration but does not, on its own, alter operational realities or safety outcomes.
