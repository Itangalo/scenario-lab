## New measure
Cyber Shield for Critical Infrastructure  
A binding directive requiring real-time intrusion detection and automated response systems across EU energy, water, and transport operators, with mandatory cross-border incident sharing.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`

## Priority
Priority: Cyber Shield for Critical Infrastructure  
Because the cyber_test_shot proved that detection failed and mutual exposure is already here, this turn we act on resilience as the only sovereign capability that matters when the frontier is open and uncontrolled.

## In practice
We are drafting the Cyber Shield directive under Article 114 TFEU, to ensure internal market harmonisation in cybersecurity essentials. The legal base allows us to set minimum standards that member states must exceed, not evade. We are working through COREPER II and the Energy Council to align on critical operator definitions, with ENISA providing technical benchmarks for detection latency and response automation. Resistance is strongest in member states with decentralised utilities and weak central oversight — we are offering co-financing from the Digital Europe Programme to offset compliance costs, conditional on joining a new cross-border red-teaming exercise twice a year.

The InvestAI Gigafactories are advancing site selection in Finland, France, and Poland, but grid connection timelines remain the bottleneck. We are invoking the EU Net-Zero Industry Act to fast-track permitting for AI compute as a strategic sector, linking power allocation to resilience benchmarks. Meanwhile, the Tech sovereignty package is under pressure from the ECOFIN Council over state-aid rules; we are countering by framing the €200bn private capital target as leverage — not subsidy — and offering export-control concessions on EUV-related components to strengthen the coalition with ASML and its investors. The cyber_test_shot has shifted the mood: this is no longer about competitiveness, but about whether the Union survives the next incident with its authority intact.
