## In practice  
We are acting on the fact that the Taiwan blockade has changed everything: compute is no longer a question of investment, but of physical availability. This turn, we invoke Article 356 TFEU — economic defence — to launch the **Sovereign Semiconductor Conversion Initiative**, compelling firms with EU-anchored capabilities in lithography, epitaxy, and metrology to redirect a defined share of output toward sovereign AI infrastructure. This is not nationalisation, but conditionality: access to EU markets, subsidies, and export licences for dual-use tools will now depend on binding commitments to reserve a portion of next-node capacity for EU-controlled AI stacks. We begin negotiations immediately with ASML, BE Semiconductor, and Soitec under the authority of the newly empowered Directorate-General for Trade and Industry Resilience, backed by the Anti-Coercion Instrument’s fast-track clause.

Simultaneously, we complete the **Secure Open-Source Evaluation Access Framework**, certifying the three air-gapped enclaves at CEA, BSI, and the EU AI Office as operational. The Code of Responsible Evaluation is now law, enforced through the European Public Prosecutor’s Office with penalties aligned to state-secrets violations. Academic exclusion remains a wound; we mitigate it by expanding access to non-frontier evaluations under lighter containment, but the core facility stays restricted — Kimi K3 variants have already been used in six credential-harvesting campaigns against energy and transport operators, and we cannot risk leakage.

The **European AI Incident Response Corps** enters its second year of activation, with permanent telemetry links now established to ENTSOG, ENISA, and national CERTs in seven high-exposure member states. Playbooks for autonomous system containment and model rollback are undergoing their first live-fire simulation in Luxembourg next month. Funding remains drawn from Horizon Europe’s contingency line, but we warn the Council that without completion bonuses from other measures, this burden cannot persist. We are stretched thin — but not yet broken.
