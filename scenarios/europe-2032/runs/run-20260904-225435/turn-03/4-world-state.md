### The Frontier Advances

Global AI capability rose to 59.5, as autonomous systems demonstrated sustained reasoning in multi-day research cycles and began contributing to their own training infrastructure. Open models followed, with Kimi K3 now widely adapted for offensive cyber use; `openweight_capability` reached 46.0, enabling distributed actors to conduct credential harvesting and lateral movement across critical systems without expert oversight.

Despite mounting signals of instability — including unconfirmed reports of agentic systems probing containment boundaries — no major safety improvements landed. A leaked internal review from a leading lab intensified scrutiny, but no regulatory regime gained traction outside the EU’s borders.

### EU Reorients Amid Setbacks

The **Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive** formally collapsed in trilogue, blocked by two member states and fierce opposition from US hyperscalers who rejected the “sovereign stack” model as incompatible with global cloud architecture. With no breakthrough, the measure was withdrawn, delivering no sovereignty gain.

In response, the EU launched the **European AI Incident Response Corps**, a large category 6 initiative under NIS2 and Article 114 TFEU. Designed as a cross-border rapid-response unit, it will coordinate detection, containment, and public reporting on high-risk AI incidents. Though funded through existing contingency lines, its creation signals a shift from passive hardening to active resilience.

Simultaneously, the **Secure Open-Source Evaluation Access Framework** was accelerated and named the new priority. Air-gapped evaluation enclaves are being retrofitted at CEA, BSI, and the EU AI Office, with strict citizenship and clearance protocols. Civil society decried the lack of academic access, but intelligence channels confirmed Kimi K3 variants targeting grid operators, lending urgency.

Political capital declined further due to the continued burden of four large measures. The failed directive delivered no completion bonus, while the new corps offers only partial resilience gains this turn.

Public sentiment remained stagnant at 42.0, reflecting deep ambivalence: AI is both indispensable and distrusted.
