## In practice  
We are acting on the fact that containment is failing at the frontier, and the Union still lacks the organs to see and respond. This turn, we formally conclude work on the **Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive**, acknowledging its failure in trilogue despite our offer of carve-outs and incentives. The liability shield, the EDIC auction model, the sovereign stack proposal — none were enough to overcome the alignment of US commercial pressure and member-state fiscal hesitancy. We extract what value we can: the concept of colocated sovereign stacks will be preserved as a condition for future access to InvestAI funding, and the negotiation record strengthens our hand in upcoming Anti-Coercion Instrument reviews.

Simultaneously, we accelerate implementation of the **Secure Open-Source Evaluation Access Framework**, now designating the French CEA, Germany’s BSI-affiliated research cluster, and the EU AI Office’s own lab as the three enclaves. We issue calls for vetted teams under strict citizenship and clearance requirements, and begin retrofitting air-gapped facilities using repurposed defence computing budgets. The Code of Responsible Evaluation is submitted to the European Parliament for endorsement as a binding standard, with penalties aligned to existing state-secrets legislation. Civil society objections are noted but outweighed by the demonstrated risk: Kimi K3 variants are already being used in credential harvesting campaigns against energy grid operators in two member states.

The new **European AI Incident Response Corps** is launched at ministerial level through Article 114 TFEU and the NIS2 framework, structured as a hybrid civilian-military unit with real-time access to intrusion telemetry, mandatory reporting rights from operators above 40.0 capability deployments, and pre-authorised playbooks for isolation, rollback, and public notification. Its creation acknowledges that resilience is no longer about hardening alone, but about having a body that can move faster than the incident — and that such a body must exist *before* the first cascade begins. Funding is drawn from the contingency line of the Horizon Europe safety budget, minimising new fiscal exposure. This is not an army; it is an immune system. And like any immune system, it must be trained on the pathogen.
