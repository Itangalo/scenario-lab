## Priority
**Sovereign Inference Initiative** — because the access cutoff, the loss-of-control incident, and the collapse of private investment have together shattered the illusion that dependence on foreign frontier providers is a temporary phase rather than a permanent vulnerability; this measure is the only one that closes the loop between capability, control, and continuity.

## In practice
We are treating the Sovereign Inference Initiative as a constitutional necessity, not a policy option. Under Article 122a TFEU, we are fast-tracking procurement through the European Digital Infrastructure Consortium (EDIC), directing immediate deployment of sovereign GPU clusters in Luxembourg, Finland, and Marseille. By Q3, all EU-level health data queries must run on EU-anchored systems; by Q4, member states must certify justice and border systems as sovereign-inference compliant. We are conditioning public procurement on full red-team access and model provenance, turning the Cyber Shield’s unrealised promise into enforceable leverage. ENSISA will audit compliance, and non-compliant systems will be barred from public contracts.

The InvestAI Gigafactories continue in Hamburg, Lyon, and — after prolonged delays — Budapest, where new grid commitments have unblocked construction. We are redirecting a portion of the Tech Sovereignty Package’s accelerated-permitting zones to support these sites, tying faster approvals to binding commitments on EU legal anchoring and talent retention. Meanwhile, the Labour Transition Accelerator has formally concluded: disbursements are now final, though follow-up evaluations will assess reintegration rates. We are repurposing part of its administrative infrastructure to support workforce integration into sovereign compute operations, treating AI capacity-building as a jobs programme as much as a security one.

The collapse of private AI investment and the US election retrenchment have created a paradox: the frontier is slowing, but so is our access to it. We are not rushing to fill that gap with public capital — not yet. Instead, we are using this turn to consolidate control over what we already have in flight, ensuring that the Sovereign Inference Initiative lands with operational reality, not just legal force. When the first cluster powers on, it will run not just public services, but the evaluators, auditors, and red teams that make safety possible. That is the foundation: not catching up, but decoupling on purpose.
