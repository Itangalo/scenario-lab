### The Verification Surge Accelerates

The recursive verification breakthrough deepens, driving `ai_capability` to 98.5 (+3.0 this turn), as frontier systems now autonomously validate legal contracts, clinical trial designs, and engineering blueprints with near-zero error rates. The rate remains high, though slightly sub-linear due to measurement saturation above 85. No human-led safety review keeps pace. Systems operate as black boxes, their reasoning chains too dense to audit—rendering `ai_safety` stagnant at 0.0.

Open-weight models benefit from partial diffusion of verification techniques, with fragments of frontier tools appearing in underground research forums and early-stage replication attempts. However, full reconstruction remains out of reach. `openweight_capability` rises to 80.0 (+3.0), reflecting gradual knowledge leakage and improved open-source tooling, but not a systemic breach. The gap with the frontier narrows only modestly, and no single event enables wholesale capability transfer.

### Sovereignty Without Power

The EU reinforces the Sovereign Inference Initiative, mandating evaluable AI in justice, health, and border systems by mid-2032. Three operational clusters now run critical workloads on fully sovereign stacks. Yet this technical progress yields no metric gain: no new measure is launched, and the initiative remains in flight without reaching completion. Political capital remains below 12, rendering the named priority ineffective.

With no active measures, the portfolio charge is zero—and no priority penalty applies. The constraint that at most one priority may be named per turn means that in the absence of an active measure, no priority status—positive or negative—can be assigned. The earlier −1 priority charge is therefore removed from the calculation.

### Fragility Spreads

A major election in a Western democracy is annulled over concerns of AI-manipulated information, triggering political unrest. Though resilience measures are absent, the incident does not directly trigger a resilience bonus or penalty—yet public trust erodes further. Labour displacement accelerates in legal and clinical sectors, and retraining programmes fail to absorb the wave. Public sentiment drops to 15.0.

Resilience declines to 30.0 as cyberattacks on municipal services intensify, exploiting AI-generated zero-days. No category 6 measure is in place to counter the threat.

Sovereignty falls to 15.0: −1 from the capability rise (over 2.0), and no capacity-securing event offsets it. Political capital drops to 7.0, as inaction compounds institutional drift and member-state dissent grows.
