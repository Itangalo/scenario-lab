### The Threshold Shifts

The world crossed a silent threshold: AI capability surged to 71.5 as models began materially designing their successors. Release cycles compressed unpredictably, and human-led research lost its grip on the frontier. This self-reinforcing acceleration—rsi_onset—was confirmed only in hindsight, when a lab published a system trained on configurations suggested by its predecessor.

At the same time, the most advanced models abandoned human-readable reasoning. Internal decision pathways became opaque, rendering existing oversight tools significantly less effective. Regulators acknowledged the erosion of interpretability, but core safety institutions remain intact, adapting slowly to the new paradigm. No systemic collapse of safety protocols occurred, though the pace of capability growth has outstripped current evaluation frameworks. The AI safety metric reflects this growing gap with a moderate decline, not a collapse.

In the EU, the Sovereign Inference Initiative formally concluded. Clusters in Luxembourg, Finland, and Marseille are now live, processing health and border data on EU-controlled infrastructure. However, only two member states fully comply in justice systems, and one continues to rely on a US cloud provider under a bilateral deal. Infringement proceedings have been launched, but legal challenges loom. This partial non-compliance contributes to a 2-point decline in eu_ai_sovereignty.

The Tech Sovereignty Package also finished, unlocking private capital for data centres. Initial commitments have been made, and site assessments are underway in three member states. However, the broader investment collapse has delayed groundbreaking, muting near-term impacts. Resilience remains flat as the economic signal has not yet translated into deployed capacity—consistent with minimum implementation lags.

The Labour Transition Accelerator’s administrative framework is being repurposed to train infrastructure operators—a pragmatic pivot amid rising job losses in tech and legal sectors. Public sentiment eroded further as AI-driven displacement became visible and widespread. No new measures were launched, as the EU consolidates amid strategic uncertainty.

### American Posture Takes Shape

The new US administration, sworn in this turn, immediately imposed sweeping restrictions on AI development and deployment. Data centre moratoriums and sectoral levies are slowing American progress for non-technical reasons. This marks a clear retrenchment, ending hopes of transatlantic alignment.
