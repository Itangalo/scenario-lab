## In practice
We are treating the finalisation of the Sovereign Inference Initiative as a moment of truth. Over this turn, the clusters in Luxembourg, Finland, and Marseille have powered on, with initial workloads now live: health data queries in the European Health Data Space are running exclusively on EU-anchored inference, and border management systems in three member states have migrated to sovereign stacks. In justice, progress is patchier—only two member states have certified compliance, and the bilateral deal involving the US hyperscaler remains unresolved. We are invoking Article 260 TFEU to initiate infringement proceedings, not to punish, but to reassert that sovereignty is not optional. ENSISA has completed audits of 14 critical systems; six are compliant, eight are under remediation orders with a three-month deadline.

The InvestAI Gigafactories continue toward completion, with Hamburg and Lyon on track for Q4 activation and Budapest finally unblocked after the national grid operator committed to a dedicated substation. We are redirecting a portion of the now-finished Tech Sovereignty Package’s accelerated-permitting zones to fast-track grid interconnections for these sites, tying them to binding commitments on EU legal anchoring and talent retention. The administrative backbone of the Labour Transition Accelerator is being repurposed to train 5,000 new operators and maintenance engineers for sovereign compute infrastructure—turning displacement into capacity.

We are not launching new measures this turn because the world has shifted beneath us: private investment has collapsed, the US is retreating, and the frontier is now self-driving. Our focus is on consolidating what we have built. When the first sovereign cluster runs not just services but red-team evaluations and safety monitors, we will have closed the loop. That is the threshold: not access, but control. Not catching up, but choosing our own path.
