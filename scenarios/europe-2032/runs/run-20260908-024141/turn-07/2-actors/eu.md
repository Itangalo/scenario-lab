## In practice
We are treating the completion of the Sovereign Inference Initiative not as an endpoint but as a foundation: the clusters in Luxembourg, Finland, and Marseille are now live with health and border workloads, and we are pushing to expand their role from inference to monitoring. ENSISA has begun running red-team evaluations on frontier models using sovereign stacks, and we are mandating that all future AI systems used in EU justice and infrastructure must be evaluable end-to-end on EU-controlled hardware. The two compliant member states in justice are now serving as reference models; for the others, remediation orders are backed by withholding cohesion funds until compliance is certified. The infringement proceedings under Article 260 TFEU against the state using the US hyperscaler are not symbolic — they are the enforcement edge of sovereignty, and we will not relent.

The InvestAI Gigafactories have reached completion, with Hamburg and Lyon now operational and feeding early capacity into the European Health Data Space and ENSISA’s evaluation pipeline. Budapest came online late but is now at 60% load, with the remaining capacity reserved for public safety research and crisis response systems. We are repurposing the administrative backbone of the Labour Transition Accelerator to train 5,000 infrastructure operators — not just to staff these sites, but to build a public technical cadre that can maintain, audit, and eventually improve sovereign systems. This is not just about jobs; it is about creating a constituency for control.

We are not launching a new measure because the ground is shifting too fast: rsi_onset means capability is now self-driving, interpretability is eroding, and the US is stepping back just as the open frontier approaches 58.0 — close enough to closed capability that distributed risk is becoming strategic risk. We must first understand what we can *do* with the capacity we have built before building more. When the next move is clear — whether it is securing open weights, attracting talent from paused US labs, or hardening against autonomous cyber agents — we will act. But not before.
