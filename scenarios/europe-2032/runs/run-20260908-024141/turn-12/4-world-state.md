### The Instrument Goes Silent

AI capability reaches 100.0 as recursive verification systems begin generating self-consistent improvements to their own architecture, achieving autonomous alignment research and engineering design at inhuman speed. The metric plateaus—not due to stagnation, but because the instrument can no longer distinguish levels of capability beyond this point. The frontier has outpaced institutional comprehension.

Open-weight models gain access to distilled verification tools via underground research networks and partial model leaks, pushing `openweight_capability` to 85.0. While still lagging, the open frontier now supports autonomous agents capable of exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities and generating synthetic legal evidence—dramatically widening misuse potential.

### Denial and Drift

The United States enacts sweeping export controls, effectively cutting off EU access to the latest frontier models. Hospitals relying on AI diagnostics, ministries using predictive legal analysis, and public safety systems face immediate degradation. No appeal is accepted. This `export_control_escalation` under the standing **RETRENCHMENT** posture constitutes a de facto `eu_frontier_access_denied` event, severing operational continuity in high-stakes domains.

The EU responds by doubling down on the Sovereign Inference Initiative, enforcing evaluable AI mandates across justice, health, and border systems. Three sovereign clusters now run live workloads, and ENSISA completes its first red-team exercise using only EU-controlled infrastructure. However, no new measure is launched. With political capital at 7.0—below 12—the Union lacks the cohesion to initiate action. The named priority changes nothing: no pull-in, no acceleration, no effect.

### Fragility Deepens

Resilience erodes to 28.0 as cyberattacks exploiting AI-generated exploits disrupt municipal services in two member states. No defensive measure is in place to absorb the blow. Public sentiment remains at rock bottom, frozen at 15.0 by visible job losses and institutional helplessness. Labour displacement accelerates, and trust in AI-mediated governance collapses further.

Sovereignty falls to 12.0: −1 from the 1.5-point capability rise (sub-linear above 85), and −2 from the access denial event. Political capital remains stagnant—no measure finishes, none starts, and no bonus applies.
