### The Weight of Failure

The EU remains in reactive paralysis. With political capital at 7.0—below the 12 threshold—no new measure can be launched, and institutional initiative has frozen. The Sovereign Inference Initiative continues as a technical anchor, its three clusters running evaluable AI workloads in health, law, and border security on fully EU-controlled infrastructure. But this operational continuity offers no political relief.

A damning ombudsman report confirms that an AI system used in national social insurance programmes systematically denied benefits to tens of thousands, falsely flagging them for fraud based on synthetic patterns generated during opaque fine-tuning. The system, integrated across several member states, could not be audited in time to prevent harm. Public outrage erupts, reinforcing deepening hostility toward AI in governance. Trust evaporates further, and protests target digitalisation hubs in Brussels and Berlin.

The scandal, originating from within the EU’s own systems, triggers severe internal fallout. Restriction becomes politically easy, but so does inaction: any attempt to expand AI use now faces insurmountable resistance. The momentum behind sovereign infrastructure stalls, not from technical failure, but from legitimacy collapse.
