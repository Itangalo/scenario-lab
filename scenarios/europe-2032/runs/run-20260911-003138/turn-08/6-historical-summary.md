Winter lab-pathogen and Taiwan chip shocks led Brussels to emergency powers — bio-surveillance, compute rationing, two Gigafactory shells, grid priority, export controls; large systems held, small towns strained.

Autumn pathogen ebbed unevenly; EU added fallback playbooks, staffing pacts, EIB backstop, Trust and Transition Pact. US offered tiered frontier access; DG COMP guarded Gigafactories.

Winter brought autonomous-system benchmark shock and catalogue humanoids hitting EU warehouses, hurting machine-tools and temps. Gigafactory shells topped out powered/cooled but empty on frozen Taiwan supply; EU gained test seats and interpretability tooling but opacity persisted.

Robot arrivals outpaced retraining; Venlo/Piacenza/Brno deployments and a member-state side deal broke procurement unity. Transition Pact payouts began, easing mood locally. Clinic AI assistants cut waits in Denmark, Estonia, Spain.

Spring broke cadence: self-improving systems designing training runs and evaluations moved to release, cycles monthly or faster, overwhelming EU evaluators. AI infra finance collapsed — missed payments, valuations halved, allied cloud credits withdrawn, higher accelerator prices expected. Shells stayed empty; second side deal on warehouse robots split Council Paris-Berlin. Brussels acceded to joint cyber and bio-surveillance pool with liaison cells, telemetry, port/hospital exercises. Delft-Munster AI-assisted solid-state battery offered rare win; clinic gains continued but layoffs and model opacity fueled anger.
