## New measure
**EU Embodied Work Transition and Trusted Deployment Pact**
Extends wage bridges, retraining and service-contract protection to warehouse, logistics and machine-tool workers hit by catalogue humanoids while tying municipal procurement to safety-checked, EU-serviced units. Category 7 labour and social protection measure — why this and why now: catalogue robots plus opaque reasoning have collapsed tolerance in small towns, and without a visible jobs shield the access pact reads as substitution and the Union loses the cohesion to hold anything else.

## Priority
M8 EU Embodied Work Transition and Trusted Deployment Pact — because with sentiment at 17 and capital at 12 the defection risk and union backlash will kill M7 allied access unless displacement is visibly answered first.

## In practice
We run the new Pact through DG EMPL and the EPSCO Council under ESF+ and the Just Transition logic: extended wage insurance for warehouse/logistics temps, retraining vouchers co-signed with IG Metall, CISL and Czech unions, and a transition levy dialogue with large deployers to fund it. Procurement via the Municipal Pact network favours units with EU service contracts and incident reporting, giving our machine-tool makers a moat without a tariff fight.

On M7 we keep EEAS and DG CNECT shuttling to Washington for published-tier researcher access and joint black-box evaluation, now upgraded with the new interpretability result — DG COMP holds the line that access funds, not replaces, the two grid-prioritised Gigafactory sites. Bio-Shield monitoring and manual-fallback drills continue on existing budgets; no new instruments are stood up here, only the staffing pacts and EIB backstop already on the books are steered to robot-hit municipalities.
