## In practice
We are treating this turn as the inflection point where sovereignty stops being a demand and becomes a fact. The Sovereign AI Core Programme is now operational in intent: we have activated Article 122 TFEU to justify overriding national vetoes on energy and infrastructure grounds, treating AI resilience as a matter of public health and security. The Commission, in coordination with the Joint Operational Board (now convened under emergency protocol), has issued binding site designations in Brittany, northern Sweden, and the Alps, with construction to begin under delegated authority from the Council if member states do not appoint representatives within 30 days. We are offering Germany and Austria a bilateral transparency pact—full access to environmental impact assessments and grid stability models—in exchange for non-obstruction, while making clear that the Core will proceed regardless. For Italy and Hungary, we are formalising the Apennines node as a conditional offer: full technical transfer, 12,000 jobs, and preferential access to EU AI services, but only upon ratification of real-time auditability and logging mandates within three months.

Simultaneously, we are weaponising the safety_breakthrough. The new interpretability stack—capable of reconstructing the decision lineage of AI-generated code even after deployment—will be integrated into EAAD’s runtime monitoring within six months. This allows us to move beyond static pre-release evaluations to active, continuous assurance. We are directing ENISA to retrofit this capability into the three critical sectors hit by the recent attack: hospital IT, emergency response networks, and power grid management. The stack will be open to all certified operators, but mandatory for any system above 55.0 capability operating in essential services. This is not a research pilot: it is a deployment. We are allocating €4.2bn from the unspent portion of the InvestAI Gigafactories fund—now closed—to finance the integration, training, and oversight teams. This is how we close the loop: sovereign infrastructure to run it, sovereign tools to see it, and sovereign authority to act when it fails.
