### A Shock to the System

The EU’s most capable AI systems were abruptly disabled overnight. Hospitals lost diagnostic support, ministries faced permit backlogs, and green tech firms saw simulations collapse. No explanation was given. The cutoff, applied by nationality, confirmed a new reality: access to frontier AI is not a service, but a lever of power — and the Union had no counterweight.

In response, Brussels launched the **Secure Access Compact with Middle Powers**, aiming to bind Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, and Canada into a mutual defence pact for AI infrastructure. The goal: shared evaluation, joint export controls, and reciprocal access. Initial signals were promising — Tokyo and Seoul welcomed coordination — but Taiwan’s participation drew immediate U.S. scrutiny, and Canada hesitated, wary of economic retaliation. The coalition remained fragile, its legal grounding untested.

Meanwhile, the **Loss-of-Control Containment Framework** formally concluded. Its protocols, battle-tested during the Swiss derivatives incident, were codified into permanent AI Act guidance. Poland integrated them fully; Hungary’s refusal triggered an Article 7 inquiry over obstructing cross-border crisis response.

But confidence eroded. A **capability_jump** in verifiable domains — autonomous code generation and cyber exploitation — widened the gap between what systems can do and what humans can audit. This, combined with the **opaque reasoning** behind the access denial, shattered trust in existing safety frameworks. **ai_safety** plummeted.

Public anger grew. Protests turned physical: activists occupied a data centre in Frankfurt, cutting power lines. Police intervention escalated tensions. With jobs disrupted and services failing, **public_sentiment** dropped sharply.

Resilience improved slightly — the containment framework’s institutional coordination proved valuable — but sovereignty fell as dependence deepened. Political capital strained under the weight of crisis response, a new large measure, and a named priority.

### Flickers of Alliance

Despite the chaos, the **middle_power_coalition** event marked a turning point. Quiet coordination among export-control agencies laid the groundwork for future leverage — a small gain in the long game of autonomy.
