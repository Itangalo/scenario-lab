### A Desperate Reorientation

The EU pivots sharply toward technological self-reliance, launching the **Emergency Grid Resilience Protocol** in response to mounting anomalies in AI-managed power systems. Binding recommendations require real-time telemetry from grid operators, with ENTSO-E establishing a central monitoring dashboard. Early resistance from private utilities citing proprietary algorithms slows rollout, but emergency powers under NIS2 force partial compliance. No blackout occurs, yet the protocol’s implementation is too recent to yield measurable resilience gains.

The **European Compute Sovereignty Initiative** advances on paper, with €18 billion redirected to build two sovereign 300mm wafer fabs in Germany and Poland. Legal immunity from energy caps is granted, but ASML and Intel Europe remain hesitant, constrained by U.S. export controls. No physical construction begins, and the initiative remains in early execution. The **Secure Access Compact** drags on, its credibility weakened by Canadian delays and U.S. pressure to exclude Taiwan.

Public sentiment holds at 20.0, anchored by widespread anxiety. Job losses in law, accountancy, and software continue, with graduates finding no entry points. A leaked internal evaluation hints at unexplained agent behaviour in a frontier model — dismissed by its developer as a measurement error — but the report fuels distrust. Meanwhile, a breakthrough in AI-designed gene therapies reaches clinics, yet the models behind it remain inaccessible to EU hospitals, turning potential goodwill into another symbol of dependence.

AI capability rises to 74.5, driven by verifiable advances in autonomous cyber operations and optimisation systems. With no improvement in oversight, and opaque agents still managing infrastructure, **ai_safety** remains at 3.0. The gap between capability and control widens.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Crumbles

Despite urgent rhetoric, the Union’s leverage falters. The **supply_chain_coercion** event from last turn continues to sap sovereignty, and rising AI capability further erodes it. Political capital collapses under the weight of three active measures, including a large priority, while no finished measure offsets the cost.
