# Statements: The European Union (turn 11)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure autonomous defence and sovereign response capacity against AI-enabled threats to critical infrastructure
- `contain_the_gap` (position): Prioritise measures that reduce the distance between advancing AI capability and lagging safety, even if they do not build new capacity or yield immediate political return

## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Operationalise autonomous defence and sovereign response capacity as the baseline for EU-wide AI security, ensuring full integration, real-time telemetry, and unified kill-switch authority across all member states, with binding enforcement and no tolerated exceptions
  - Grounds: The counter-agent systems are now technically complete, but operational cohesion remains fractured—Italy resists integration on patient safety grounds, Poland operates under a bilateral U.S. arrangement, and compliance is uneven. The original commitment to *secure* capacity has been met; the new imperative is *enforcement*. Without binding, universal adoption, the system is only as strong as its weakest link. This turn’s cyber_major_incident and member_state_defection make clear that technical completion is not strategic sovereignty. The commitment evolves to reflect that the Union must now compel unity, not just enable it.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **add** `contain_the_gap` — applied
  - Tier: position
  - Text: Prioritise measures that reduce the distance between advancing AI capability and lagging safety, even if they do not build new capacity or yield immediate political return
  - Grounds: With ai_capability at 69.5 and ai_safety at 14.0, the gap is now catastrophic. The emergent_ai_worker_unions signal that even developers see systems as unsafe, while the kill-switch protocol and counter-agents are defensive rear-guards, not preventative controls. We cannot close the gap this turn, but we must begin treating it as a first-order threat. This position guides prioritisation: not every action must build compute or harden infrastructure. Some must simply slow the race until control can catch up.
