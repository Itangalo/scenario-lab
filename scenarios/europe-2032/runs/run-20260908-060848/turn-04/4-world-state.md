### The Shield Completes — But the Breach Widens  
The Cyber Shield for Critical Operators (CS-CO) formally enters force, marking the EU’s first comprehensive, binding defence against AI-powered cyber threats. ENISA coordinates the first pan-European red-teaming exercise, revealing critical flaws in energy grid automation systems across three member states. While the framework is now law, enforcement remains uneven: Germany invokes national emergency override clauses, rejecting central fallback triggers, while Greece and Bulgaria struggle to implement templates without sustained technical support.

### A Corps for the Unknown War — In Formation  
The EU Red-Teaming Corps is announced as an extension of ENISA’s mandate, with staffing to begin through secondments in the next quarter. Planning for live simulations on AI-managed rail signalling and hospital diagnostics is underway, but no exercises have yet been conducted. Private operators express concern over future disruptions, and legal questions over data access remain unresolved. The initiative signals ambition, but operational capacity remains months away.

### Sovereignty Erodes as Capability Leaps  
Global AI capability reaches 60.0, driven by self-improving agents that now complete multi-day research and engineering tasks autonomously. Openweight capability rises to 47.0, reflecting gradual diffusion of advanced techniques and partial uptake of newly available tools derived from frontier systems. While a frontier model leak is reported in underground developer forums, its actual deployment remains limited and fragmented, preventing a sharp leap in openweight capability. The EU’s sovereign compute ambitions stall: InvestAI Gigafactories remain offline, and the Tech sovereignty package’s infrastructure anchoring clause faces legal scrutiny from the European Court of Justice, delaying enforcement.

### Safety Stands Still  
Despite growing threats, ai_safety holds at 29.0. The European AI Safety Lab’s assessment of the Swiss agent framework yields insights but no binding improvements. Developers reject mandatory pre-deployment audits, and the “significant market impact” justification faces constitutional questions. No new safeguards land on deployed systems.

### Capital Holds — For Now  
Political capital stabilises at 36.0. The completion of CS-CO delivers a modest boost, offset by the cost of launching a single new measure this turn. Public sentiment remains unchanged, caught between cautious adoption and lingering fear after the hospital attacks.
