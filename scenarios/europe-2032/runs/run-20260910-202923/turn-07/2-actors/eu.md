## New measure
**EU Displaced-Worker Guarantee and Retraining Pact**
It ties automation-linked restructuring to funded wage insurance, rapid retraining and placement via certified public employment services, answering job-loss hostility and stalled robot hype.
With capital at 16 and sentiment at 15, clinics alone cannot rebuild trust while labour fear dominates coverage; this extends the trust-rebuilding commitment from proven services to livelihood security, and the narrow embodied-AI reality gives us a window to act before broader displacement hits.

## Priority
M7 EU Trusted Public Services Rollout, because visible certified clinic and municipal gains remain the only proof holding audits together while the new pact builds out — trust cannot wait three turns for retraining cheques.

## In practice
We keep M7 fronted by health and interior ministers: DG DIGIT and AI Office replicate passing hospital/municipal audits with EU4Health and Digital Europe money, EPSCO/Health Council procurement preference for certified EU systems, district figures published to counter blockades.

We launch the Pact via DG EMPL with EPSCO Council under ESF+ and SURE-type backstop: qualifying automating employers co-fund wage insurance, PES fast-track retraining tied to certified sectors, legal base Articles 153/175 to blunt moratorium parties and unions resisting data-centres. Joint cell freeze and 24h reporting stay enforced in power, transport, hospitals, finance under NIS2/CER and AI Act Art.73 while M1 gigafactory capacity is shepherded to operational use, not new build.
