## New measure
**EU Incident Registry and Deployment Audit**
Creates mandatory reporting of serious AI incidents and near-misses to ENISA/EU AI Office with a public registry of high-impact deployments, plus resourced audit teams to enforce oversight and logging duties. Why this and why now: the major automated attack and the benefits/policing scandal both showed harm discovered by victims not providers and logs never read — without common reporting and real audit capacity the Shield hardens blind and the AI Act stays paper.
