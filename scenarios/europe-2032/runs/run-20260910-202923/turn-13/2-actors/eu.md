## In practice
We use DG REGIO and DG ENER under the Just Transition and cohesion envelope to fund a 6-month mediated pause: prefects and mayors convene site-level roundtables with operators, grid TSOs and protest groups, offering local electricity tariff relief, apprenticeship quotas and municipal veto over municipal-AI functions in exchange for lifting blockades and securing construction access.

ENISA/CERT-EU and the joint cyber command keep the finished M9 manual fallbacks and M10 sensor net running on existing NIS2 solidarity mandates without new orders, while DG CNECT extends the narrow exemptions to the predictable-behaviour order where blockaded sites cannot install kill-switch retrofits. No new permitting acceleration is pushed until the truce agreements are signed.
