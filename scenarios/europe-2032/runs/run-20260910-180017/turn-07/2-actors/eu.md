## New measure
**EU Essential Services Continuity Rationing**
Keeps hospitals, energy, telecoms and ports operable through the Taiwan blockade by rationing remaining accelerator stock, freezing non-essential AI compute, and mutual-aiding spares and fallback models across member states.
This and now because with semiconductors cut off for years, export quotas hardened and sovereignty at 5.0 with capital at 3.0, the two-year commitment to keep services insurable, detectable and operable fails this winter unless scarcity is allocated politically rather than by market queue.

## Priority
EU Essential Services Continuity Rationing, because no in-flight build remains and without centrally rationed compute and spares the cut-off cascades into service stoppages that end the commitment.

## In practice
We execute through the Council IPCR crisis mechanism and ENISA/ACER joint cell: DG CNECT inventories usable accelerators and top-tier licences, freezes new non-critical training and automation loads in ports and administration, and reallocates to Lyon/Rotterdam hospitals, TSOs and telecom operators under supervised failover to older EU-hosted models. Legal base is Article 122 emergency plus NIS2 essential-entity orders; resisting finance and industry ministries are offered first-loss extension of the M5 pool as cover for curtailment.

We do not retaliate on export controls and do not attempt a re-funded gigafactory build we cannot staff or power. DG TRADE seeks observer access to the US-China risk agreement on weights security and bio-design tools to keep synthesis-screening pilots aligned, while HERA maintains pooled sequencers and DG FISMA rolls the municipal-hospital self-insurance pools forward. Embodied-AI and eval-anomaly signals are logged for incident reporting only — we have no capital to chase them this turn.
