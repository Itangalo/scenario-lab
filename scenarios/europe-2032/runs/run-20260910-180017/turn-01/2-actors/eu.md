## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled intrusion with mandated detection, backup controls and cross-border exercises.
Category 6 Preparedness and resilience. Why this and why now: the autumn intrusion proved detection would not have caught weeks-long presence in EU grids, and without credible resilience neither sovereignty nor safety commitments survive the next incident.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because undetected compromise of grids and ports outranks even ASML coercion this turn — without working lights and ports sovereignty has no base.

## In practice
We task ENISA with expanded 24/7 detection mandates for the two affected TSOs plus all interconnected TSOs, funded via Connecting Europe Facility reallocation and CER/NIS2 implementing acts, with ACER and the Electricity Coordination Group running live backup-operation exercises in Q4. Interior and Energy Councils carry the mandate; grid operators resisting cost-sharing are offered 50% EU co-funding for sensors and segmented control backups.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package moving: DG CNECT accelerates permitting-zone designation and grid-connection reservations for the first two gigafactory sites, while we open a quiet Trade/Competitiveness Council track on ASML servicing — no new instrument now, but preparing an Anti-Coercion Instrument evidence file and joint outreach to Japan and South Korea for next turn.
