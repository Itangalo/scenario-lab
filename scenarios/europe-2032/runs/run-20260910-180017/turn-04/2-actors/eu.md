## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because without hardened energy/health/telecoms and completed autumn exercise standards the insurance backstop has nothing to underwrite and the bio surge has no resilient hospitals to plug into — it remains the binding constraint on our two-year commitment to resilient essential services.

## In practice
We drive the Shield to completion through the Health and Energy Councils: ENISA certifies machine-speed patching and behavioural detection as NIS2 implementing-act requirements, DG ENER conditions the 50% cost-share on staffing the joint SOCs, and DG FISMA pushes ECOFIN to agree the backstop's InvestEU guarantee so renewals for hardened operators can be offered before year-end gaps bite.

On bio we task HERA to bulk-procure sequencing and stockpile medical countermeasures, ECDC to link hospital reporting, and DG RTD to fund rapid verification of the contested genome-model study with vetted researcher access, while DG TRADE keeps the Tokyo/Seoul servicing talks and Anti-Coercion file alive and contains the side-deal capital with offtake offers rather than sanction. DG SANTE frames tailored therapies as EU-delivered benefit via joint procurement to salvage trust despite dependence.
