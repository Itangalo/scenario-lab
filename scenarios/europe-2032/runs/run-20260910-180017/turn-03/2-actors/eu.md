## New measure
**EU Critical-Sector Cyber Insurance Backstop**
Creates a temporary EU-reinsured pool for AI-enabled cyber losses in energy, transport, health and telecoms, conditional on operators adopting the Shield's patching, detection and backup standards, to stop exclusions from freezing essential-service cover.
Category 6 Preparedness and resilience. Why this and why now: the Kimi K3-driven exclusion wave directly threatens the resilient essential services half of our two-year commitment, and the defensive-tooling breakthrough gives us certifiable standards to price the backstop against before renewals collapse.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because the breakthrough detection/patching only helps if the understaffed Q4 exercise actually deploys it, and without hardened operators neither the insurance backstop nor the gigafactories survive contact with incidents.

## In practice
We push ENISA and DG ENER through the Energy and Transport Councils to make the new automated-patching and swarm-detection tooling the mandatory Shield standard for the autumn cross-border exercise, using NIS2 enforcement to force staffing that lagged last turn, with the 50% cost-share now tied to adoption. DG FISMA and EIOPA stand up the reinsurance pool via InvestEU guarantee, with Treasury ministers offered containment of a cascade failure in exchange for contingent liability, and cover conditional on Shield compliance to pull operators along.

On sovereignty we hold the line: DG TRADE keeps the Anti-Coercion file live and the Tokyo/Seoul joint team negotiating shared servicing, while we contain the defecting member state's side-deal bilaterally — offering pooled mature-node capacity and gigafactory offtake rather than public sanction — and DG CNECT keeps the two re-scoped gigafactory sites on public anchor funding with grid reservations held despite Taiwan-driven chip cost rises.
