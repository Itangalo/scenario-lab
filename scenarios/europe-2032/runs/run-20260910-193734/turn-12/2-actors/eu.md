## In practice
We run the incident as a preparedness task, not a sovereignty bid. ENISA as EU node forwards joint indicators to municipal CERTs, Health and JHA Councils reactivate the staffed patching rota on the finished Continuity Reserve legal base with insurer co-pay, and the Franco-German verification cell triages false alarms. No new procurement, no conditionality fight.

We contain the side-deal politically: full access to joint signatures and recovery teams for any operator that stays on common reporting, Transport and JHA Councils keep the solidarity file open without sanctions, and DG TRADE holds screening papers warm. We bank the medical breakthrough for trust and note the open-weight release and eval anomaly as reason to stay inside the US-China verification floor rather than chase solo compute we cannot fund.
