## New measure
**Essential Services Continuity Reserve**
Creates EU-funded backup power, offline recovery kits and mutual-aid repair crews for hospitals, water and grid operators to keep services degrading not stopping during AI-enabled attack.
This is Preparedness and resilience work that must come now because segmentation audits under the Cyber Shield take years while the autumn probes show staged access already inside, so continuity capacity is the only hardening that pays off within this commitment period.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, because it is the core of the two-year hardening commitment and detection plus segmentation audits must move before the continuity reserve can be targeted.

## In practice
We push the Shield through TTE-Energy and JHA this turn: Commission implementing act for mandatory segmentation audits on designated TSOs, ports and water utilities, ENISA-procured swarm-detection sensors funded by CEF-Digital reflows, and the five-country live containment drill. We buy French-German assent with co-financing rates and priority grid-connection for AI factory zones.

Alongside we stand up the Continuity Reserve via UCPM / rescEU: stockpiled islanding kits, mobile generators and clean backup images for hospitals and municipal services, plus cross-border repair teams rostered from TSOs. Utilities resisting unfunded mandates get funded spares and crews in exchange for audit compliance.
