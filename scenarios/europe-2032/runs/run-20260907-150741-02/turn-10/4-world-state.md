### A Leap and a Collapse

Global AI capability surges by +4.0 to 75.0 as a leading lab releases a system that autonomously designs and validates complex scientific experiments, demonstrating performance across physics, biology and engineering at expert level. The jump renders prior timelines obsolete: full-cycle research automation is now visibly within reach. Human oversight becomes advisory rather than directive.

With no safety mechanisms embedded in the new release, `ai_safety` plummets by −8, reflecting both the capability leap and the widening control gap. Developers admit they cannot fully interpret the model’s reasoning pathways, and internal red-team exercises fail to predict key behaviours.

The Emergency Trust Restoration Initiative launches in Marseille, Ljubljana and Athens. Early demonstrations show promise: triage wait times drop, border queues clear faster, tutoring engagement rises. But technical hiccups emerge—AI tutors misdiagnose learning gaps, and border processing flags false matches under stress load. No major incident occurs, but leaks to press paint a picture of overreach. Public sentiment holds steady at 20.0—neither improved by early wins nor worsened by flaws, as media framing remains dominated by systemic distrust.

Resilience stagnates at 49.0. Despite partial gains from continuity planning, the ransomware attack on emergency dispatch systems exposed cascading vulnerabilities. Recovery took 36 hours, not because systems failed, but because human operators could not safely resume control. The lesson: resilience requires trust in fallbacks, not just their existence.

EU political capital remains at 0.0. The portfolio charge—Frontier Access Guarantee (−3), Continuity Assurance Framework (−3), priority (−1)—totals −7. A proposal bonus is denied: while public benefit was sought, the measure responds to chronic sentiment collapse, not a discrete recent shock. With capital below 12, agenda control persists in seizure; the priority confers no acceleration.

Sovereignty drops to 21.0. The capability jump (+4) triggers a −1 decay under rule 5. No category 4 completion or access event offsets it. The Frontier Access Guarantee remains in flight but yields no near-term gain.

Export controls tighten in Washington, with allied access now rationed—yet no formal exclusion hits EU agreements. The Union avoids a direct capacity strike, but future expansions are uncertain.

Open-weight capability rises only moderately to 54.0 (+2.0), reflecting continued deployment of existing open models and incremental fine-tuning. While interest grows in sovereign, auditable systems, no breakthrough release or coordinated scaling effort enables a faster catch-up. The gap to the frontier remains wide, and the pace of open innovation still lags behind proprietary advances.
