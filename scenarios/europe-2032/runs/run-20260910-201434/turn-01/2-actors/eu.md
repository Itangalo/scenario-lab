## New measure
**EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-swarm intrusion with mandatory segmentation, exercised incident response and cross-border mutual aid.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because the autumn test shot showed staged access across EU grid operators using open Mythos-class tooling and opened a three-turn cyber window that existing detection would not catch — resilience must land before sovereignty can.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, not the inherited gigafactories, because the precursor intrusion makes cascading infrastructure failure the binding risk in this commitment period.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the Energy Council formation with emergency hardening directives under the NIS2 and CER legal base, funded by reprogrammed Connecting Europe Facility and InvestAI resilience tranches: mandatory OT segmentation audits for all transmission system operators, breaker-credential rotation, and staged-tooling detection signatures shared via the Cybersecurity Emergency Mechanism. Exercises in Q4 2026 with TSOs in the two affected member states, then EU-wide.

The Gigafactories (M1) and tech sovereignty package (M2) continue on their permitting and capital-mobilisation tracks — DG CNECT and EIB structuring the €200bn vehicles — but we explicitly sequence grid-connection priority to hardened sites and use ASML/EUV leverage discussions to buy time, offering member states accelerated funds for compliance rather than new obligations without money. Utilities resisting downtime for audits are met with liability protection if they disclose intrusions early.
