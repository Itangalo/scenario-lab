## New measure
**EU Assurance Adoption Cell**
Stands up a joint EU evaluation cell to certify and rapidly deploy the new interpretability/control result onto AI systems running in hardened grid, port and water environments, and to fold public-sector adoption wins into trusted deployment templates.
This is Public technical capacity and research work now because the safety breakthrough only helps resilience if it lands on shipped systems before the winter window, and visible adoption success gives us the legitimacy to mandate it.

## Priority
EU Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield, not the new Cell, because staged intrusion tooling still makes cascading failure the binding risk in this commitment period.

## In practice
We keep ENISA, ACER and the Energy Council on the M3 hardening track — OT segmentation audits, credential rotation, shared signatures and Q1-Q2 exercises — using CEF reprogramming and liability cover to get TSOs through audit downtime, and we hold grid-queue preference for hardened sites to keep M1/M2 permitting moving without new fights.

We task the AI Office with JRC and ENISA to staff the Cell from existing evaluation budgets: vetted access to the breakthrough technique, certification pilots with the two affected TSOs and the port/water operators, and procurement templates that copy the successful public-sector adoption cases into health and administration rollouts. DG CNECT ties Cell certification to InvestAI deployment milestones so sovereignty capacity arrives assured rather than opaque.
