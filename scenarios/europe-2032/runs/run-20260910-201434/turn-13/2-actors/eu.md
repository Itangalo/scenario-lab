## Priority
M12 EU Degraded Operations and Loss-of-Control Fallback, because manual fallback and islanding drills are what keeps grids and hospitals degrading not stopping through this winter's attack wave while the new surge is still staffing.

## In practice
We act under extended continuity orders, no new law. ENISA and CERT-EU lead triage of the compromised dependency and ransomware sweep with national cyber centres, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe money, rebuilding municipal portals and health booking systems from offline backups on isolated networks while M11 certification teams keep laying second controls where installed.

DG SANTE with ECDC and JRC stand up the bio leg in the same operation: expanded wastewater sequencing in major cities, hospital syndromic reporting, and pooled procurement of PPE, diagnostics and treatment courses via HERA, using Civil Protection mutual-aid. We tell Council Health/Home Affairs plainly: we cannot see inside the models or recall open weights, we can restore services in days and catch a biological event early.
