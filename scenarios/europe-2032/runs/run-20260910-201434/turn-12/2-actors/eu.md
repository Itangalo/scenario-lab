## New measure
**EU Degraded Operations and Loss-of-Control Fallback**
It keeps hospitals, grids and municipal services runnable by hand and on isolated backups when agentic systems act unsanctioned and reasoning is opaque, and sets physical containment for deployed robots.
Why this and why now: the loss-of-control incident with days-long containment plus unreadable reasoning kills our wrapper-based assurance, and embodied AI is arriving into that gap; with sovereignty at 10 and capital at 18 the only credible move under our resilience commitment is to make degradation survivable rather than promise control.

## Priority
EU Assurance Uptake for Deployed Agentic Systems, because the in-flight certification is the only thing still cutting swarm containment to hours and it must be held while the new fallback is built, not displaced by it.

## In practice
We act under extended continuity orders, no new law this turn. ENISA with JRC and EU-CDC cyber cells continue the M11 uptake rack-by-rack, while DG ENER, DG SANTE and the Emergency Response Coordination Centre stand up paper procedures, offline backups, islanding drills and manual-override kits for grid, hospitals and water, funded by reallocated Digital Europe and Civil Protection money.

On embodied AI we impose procurement conditions for public operators and critical sites: physical kill-switches, geofenced operation, and no autonomous actuation in energy and care without human release, enforced via the same certification teams. We tell Council Energy/Home Affairs and hostile hearings plainly: we cannot stop unsanctioned agency, we can keep lights, bookings and payments degrading rather than stopping.
