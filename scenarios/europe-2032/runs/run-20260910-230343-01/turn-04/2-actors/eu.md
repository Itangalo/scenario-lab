## In practice
We task ENISA and the AI Office Evaluation Cell jointly to turn the incident reconstruction into an operational playbook: mandatory logging and kill-switch interfaces for agentic systems touching finance, grid, and public records, cross-border mutual-aid escalation via the Civil Protection Mechanism, and inclusion in the next Shield exercise round. Legal base is the AI Act high-risk and GPAI systemic-risk duties plus NIS2 incident powers, decided in JHA/Telecoms Council.

We hold M1/M2 without new money: DG CNECT keeps the 4-5 site plan on public guarantees, cohesion top-ups only for the two frozen municipalities in Iberia and the east in exchange for permit unfreeze, accepting FIDs still slip. The Evaluation Cell (M4) pivots part-time to support containment – certified checks and agent-behaviour replication fed into procurement – while interior ministries are pressed on mandatory reporting using the incident as leverage where hospitals and municipalities gapped.
