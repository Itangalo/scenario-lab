## In practice  
We are treating the ASML crisis not as a corporate dispute but as a sovereignty inflection point. Under Article 121 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the EU, we are initiating an extraordinary review by the Competitiveness Council, with binding opinion sought from the European External Action Service on the strategic impact of U.S.-pressured DUV restrictions. We are establishing a temporary EU Export Integrity Board—co-chaired by the Vice-President for Values and Transparency and the Commissioner for Industry—tasked with assessing whether current licensing frameworks allow third powers to de facto control EU technological sovereignty. By turn 5, it must deliver a proposal for an EU veto mechanism on exports of critical dual-use equipment when they threaten the bloc’s own AI infrastructure build-out. This is not about protectionism; it is about ensuring that European-built capacity cannot be strangled by decisions made in foreign capitals.

At the same time, we are pressing forward on all existing measures. The Cyber Shield Protocol remains on track: ENISA has now onboarded 78% of designated operators, and the first joint red-team exercise across France, Germany, and Poland revealed critical gaps in hospital network segmentation—gaps now being addressed with emergency funding from the Digital Europe Programme. We are using the momentum to demand full compliance by turn 4, threatening to trigger Article 26 fines for non-compliant member states. On the industrial front, we are renegotiating the InvestAI Gigafactories’ environmental review with EDF and local authorities, proposing an independent hydrogeological monitoring body to unblock the delayed site. The Tech Sovereignty Package is entering trilogue, where we are trading final concessions on green hydrogen for enforceable water recycling standards. And we are expanding the Labour Transition Fund’s cyber-skills linkage, directing €5bn in pilot funds to regions most exposed to automation in transport and logistics—tying economic relief to systemic resilience.
