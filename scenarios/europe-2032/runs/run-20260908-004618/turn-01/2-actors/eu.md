A new €45bn fund to scale national retraining and wage insurance programmes in sectors hit by sudden automation, with mandatory co-financing from firms automating roles at scale.

## In practice
We are moving now on two fronts: hardening our societal base and accelerating our strategic independence. On the first, the Labour Transition Accelerator Fund will be proposed under the European Social Fund+ legal base, with co-legislation in the Employment, Social Policy, Health and Consumer Affairs Council (EPSCO). We will fast-track negotiations by offering member states 70% EU co-financing if they report automation-driven job losses quarterly and link displacement data to training pipelines by turn 3. Resistance from fiscally conservative states will be met with a temporary flexibility clause in the Stability and Growth Pact, allowing reclassified spending on transition as investment — a precedent set during the pandemic.

On the industrial front, we are advancing the InvestAI Gigafactories through the Competitiveness Council, leveraging ASML’s strategic position to negotiate joint ventures with TSMC and Intel on EU soil, conditioned on irrevocable legal anchoring of compute and weight repositories. The Tech sovereignty package is being operationalised via the Net-Zero Industry Act framework, repurposing its accelerated permitting logic for AI infrastructure zones. We are meeting resistance from environmental agencies on grid load and water use; our offer is binding commitments to co-locate green hydrogen plants and closed-loop cooling systems, funded from the same package. This is not just about compute — it is about making AI’s footprint legible, containable, and politically sustainable.
