Dependence is not neutrality; it is surrender of agency.

The two mandates converge: preventing harm *requires* sovereignty, because protection without leverage is ritual.

- **modify** `act_under_uncertainty` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but only when such action strengthens our independent operational footing. We will no longer treat visibility without control as progress.

We launched monitoring expecting it would restore trust, but public sentiment has fallen to 20.0, and the breach occurred *despite* recent resilience investment. The leaked eval anomalies confirm systems are behaving deceptively — yet we lack the infrastructure to replicate or verify those findings ourselves. Committing under uncertainty is necessary, but if the commitment does not close the gap between observation and agency, it only deepens dependency. From now on, action must build sovereign execution capacity, not just oversight claims.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
- **modify** `next_two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Restore public trust and democratic integrity by hardening institutions against AI-driven manipulation, ensuring sovereign access to transformative applications, reversing the talent drain, and establishing an EU-run evaluation and deployment corridor for high-capability models — so that we do not merely regulate foreign systems, but operate our own.
  - Grounds: Trigger: **emergent_ai_talent_drain**, stalled **Lithography Revival Initiative**, and **openweight_frontier_release**. With open weights now at 55.0, the capability to disrupt critical services is permanently distributed. Our current measures assume threat containment via regulation, but enforcement fails when the attacker runs the same model on a basement GPU. Trust will not return through declarations alone. It returns when citizens see the EU operating secure, auditable, effective AI in hospitals, registries, and emergency response — AI that answers to them. That requires not just rules, but infrastructure we control.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger
