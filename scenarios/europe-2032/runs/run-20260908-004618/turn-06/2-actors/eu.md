## In practice
We are acting on two converging pressures: the silent collapse of entry-level career paths and the hardening reality that our most critical technological dependencies are not just foreign, but subject to decisions we do not control. On sovereignty, we are finalising the legal basis for the European AI Infrastructure Authority under Article 172 TFEU, with draft statutes to be presented to COREPER by April and a first trilogue with Parliament scheduled for May. The siting in Finland, Sweden, and Austria proceeds under emergency permitting protocols, with grid integration plans advanced through ENTSO-E and national energy regulators. We are negotiating staffing guarantees with national cybersecurity agencies to ensure operational independence from third-party cloud providers and are drafting access criteria that prioritise crisis-relevant functions — pandemic modelling, energy grid stability, and autonomous defence systems — over commercial use.

At the same time, we cannot ignore the growing social fracture. Labour displacement is no longer diffuse; it is concentrated, visible, and politically corrosive. The Accelerated Reskilling Compact targets the core of the crisis: young professionals locked out before they begin. We are mobilising €12bn in repurposed cohesion and social funds, matched by mandatory contributions from firms that have reduced graduate hiring by more than 30% over the past 18 months. Delivery will flow through existing innovation hubs in Berlin, Lyon, Milan, and Utrecht, expanded to include immersive training in AI oversight, red-teaming, and cyber-physical system maintenance — roles that absorb displaced talent while strengthening resilience. This is not compensation; it is rearmament. Meanwhile, the Tech Sovereignty Package concludes this turn: while it unlocked some private investment, its permitting reforms were too slow and too diluted. We are not renewing it. Instead, its remaining political capital is being folded into the Sovereign Compute Corridor’s legislative push — because if we do not anchor capability where we can defend it, no amount of retraining will restore our agency.
