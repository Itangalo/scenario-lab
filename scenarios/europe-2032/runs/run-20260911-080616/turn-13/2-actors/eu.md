## Priority

## In practice
We task DG EMPL with EPSCO and DG CNECT with the Committee of the Regions: ESF+ and remaining EU4Health money tops up municipalities already on the Sovereign Fallback Stack to expand European-hosted triage, waiting-list and permitting assistants, on condition telemetry and audit gates stay on and results are published. Employers drawing productivity gains in routine coding, analysis and support sign sectoral first-job charters — wage-subsidised junior re-entry and retraining places co-funded from those savings — to answer unions without a labour reform we cannot pass.

We keep what finished running without new law: HERA/ECDC BioWatch sequencing and ER screening continue on recovery funds where data is shared, isolation-gate audits stay targeted on labs and synthesis providers, and Franco-Dutch medical teams remain on call. Commission communication credits named city-hall cuts in waiting times loudly, while no new gigafactory, compute or restriction measure is launched; the Middle-Power Compact venue is used only to keep synthesis screening aligned.
