## New measure
**EU Dock-Logistics Flexicurity Transition**
Pays wage insurance, rapid retraining and port-municipality income bridging for warehouse, picking and yard workers displaced by foreign robots, tied to employer automation levies and union no-strike cooperation.
Why this and why now: because stoppages plus outage coverage collapsed mood to 26 and frontrunner restoration staff are stretched thin, and with office work augmented not collapsing, this manual-logistics boundary is where absorption must land to hold the two-year commitment to keep essential services running; this is category 7 Labour and social protection.

## Priority
M11 EU Dock-Logistics Flexicurity Transition, because halting port stoppages and visible income protection outranks new concrete this half and is what lets restoration and keep-alive continue without political break.

## In practice
We run this through EPSCO and EMPL with ESF+ and Globalisation Adjustment Fund top-up: DG EMPL funds wage insurance and 6-month retraining vouchers for dock, warehouse and yard workers, municipalities get bridging grants conditional on accepting common backup/islanding playbooks so restoration is not traded off. We ask logistics unions to suspend stoppages in exchange for automation-levy co-funding from terminal operators deploying foreign machines, and ask terminal operators for maintenance-repair apprenticeship slots where robots cannot go.

DG CNECT, DG ENER and EIB hold the keep-alive line without a new measure: poured gigafactory sites stay powered and queued, bilateral hyperscaler deal stays ring-fenced to one case while Council fights further breakaways, locally-hosted procurement stays frozen pending the court ruling. ENISA, HERA and ERCC continue seconded cyber-clinical teams to hit towns and hospitals, sequencing overtime and offline-copy restocking, logging bio-uplift and eval-anomaly signals for Saclay-Barcelona verification work and preparing an ASML Anti-Coercion response next turn, not opened now.
