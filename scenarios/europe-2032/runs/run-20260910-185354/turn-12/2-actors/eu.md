## New measure
**EU Essential Services Islanding and Joint Response Pact**
Stands up degraded-but-open operation for hospitals, grids and ports under the ransomware sweep and access cutoff by islanding critical systems, deploying EU-controlled fallback models, and joining the joint cyber command telemetry pool.
Why this and why now: because the automated attack plus sudden denial of the leading model hits exactly what our two-year commitment covers — keeping essential services running — and with capital at 18 we cannot rebuild sovereignty this half but can stop cascades and buy protection we cannot build alone.

## Priority
M13 EU Essential Services Islanding and Joint Response Pact, because absorbing the live incident and replacing denied dependencies outranks all other work under our absorb-harm commitment.

## In practice
We task ENISA and the EU Joint Cyber Unit via the Council's TTE and JHA formations to trigger the Cybersecurity Act emergency procedures: mandatory telemetry sharing into the invited joint cyber command, pre-authorised islanding playbooks for frontrunner hospitals and two delayed port hubs, and overtime-funded seconded repair teams extended from M10. DG DIGIT and HERA re-route affected ministries and hospitals from the cut-off US model to Saclay-Barcelona evaluated fallbacks and licensed volume stock already on EU soil, using Digital Europe funds, no new vote needed.

We hold the rest defensive without new instruments: DG EMPL keeps M11 wage-insurance payouts flowing to dock towns hit now also by warehouse robotics, DG TRADE keeps Anti-Coercion preparation over Dutch servicing cuts but does not escalate while Hague talks continue, and the M12 verification cell stays as observer to preserve the only frontier seat we hold. Member states resisting data-sharing get restoration queue priority as the offer to stop.
