## Priority
Priority is M11 EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee, because with capital at 4 and sentiment at 8 only visible wage top-ups and placements can stop the hiring-freeze plus wrongful-denial crisis from becoming ungovernable.

## In practice
We run everything through EPSCO and DG EMPL on the existing ESF+ reprogramming: labour ministers own weekly enrolment and first-payment counters, justice ministers hold the pause on contested benefits tools pending the ombudsman-ordered audits, and we publicly tie any conversion bonus uplift to proof of human review. No new law, no new fund, no Brussels showcase language.

We contain the member-state side-deal politically, not legally: Council presidency shuttles to the defecting capital to ring-fence essential-services fallback and servicing-clearance commitments from the Hague-Tokyo-Seoul contact group, while ENISA and health ministries keep clinics and manual-fallback doctrine exercised. The AI Safety Institute patch guidance from the interpretability check continues on its low track without a new mandate.
