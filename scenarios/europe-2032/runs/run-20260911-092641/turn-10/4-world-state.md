### Claiming a win while the stores burn
For the first time in two years, Brussels had something to point at. In three health systems and two justice ministries, European-procured assistants running on European-hosted capacity had cut waiting lists and decision times enough to show on a dashboard. The Commission put its label on it fast: open counters, weekly figures, ministers visiting clinics that were actually moving faster.

It almost worked. Local press covered shorter queues. Nurses who had cursed the systems months earlier said the new triage help saved hours.

Then the fallback itself came under attack.

Across January and March, generator yards, transformer stores and the cabins holding paper breaker logs were broken into, drained, torched or concreted shut. Leaflets left behind called the pools collaboration with the machine. In two towns, the very spares stockpiled off the books to survive the autumn intrusions went up in smoke. Bringing the pools on-scheme with reimbursement, meant to protect volunteers, instead painted a target on addresses that had been secret.

The containment protocol that finished this spring helped contain the damage. Cross-border mutual aid moved mobile generators to the hit sites, hospitals stayed lit, manual procedures held where equipment remained. But restoration by hand is slow when someone is smashing the hands.

### Rationed harder
Washington's new licensing round confirmed the worst reading: even allied buyers on volume quotas, servicing approvals case by case. The Hague signed under protest again. Private compute markets, meanwhile, seized up as investment fled the sector, cancelled builds cascading through the cloud resellers Europe had been renting from. Brussels froze any forced migration and told capitals to sit on what was already on European soil.

The showcase sites got priority cover, guards and fenced fuel. Elsewhere, mayors asked why their stores were not covered. The answer — no money, no law, no standing — did not travel well.

By June the Union had a credited success, defended in a few places and resented in many others. Hostility eased a notch where waiting lists fell, hardened where cabinets burned.
