Brussels claimed first win in two years: EU-procured assistants on EU-hosted capacity cut waiting lists/decision times in three health systems and two justice ministries, showcased with open counters and weekly figures.

Fallback then attacked: Jan-March break-ins torched/drained generator yards, transformer stores, breaker-log cabins; leaflets called pools collaboration with machine. On-scheme reimbursed pools exposed secret addresses; two towns lost off-books spares from autumn. Cross-border mutual aid moved mobile generators, hospitals stayed lit, but restoration by hand slowed by sabotage.

Washington hardened rationing: allied buyers on volume quotas, servicing case-by-case; Hague complied under protest. Private compute seized as investment fled, builds cancelled; Brussels froze forced migration, told capitals to sit on existing EU capacity. Showcase sites got guards/fenced fuel; elsewhere mayors resentful with no money/law/standing.

By June: credited success defended in few places, resented in many; hostility eased where queues fell, hardened where stores burned.
