## New measure
**Joint Middle-Power Supply Bargain**
It turns the middle-power coordination window into joint export-licence alignment and pooled bargaining for compute, spares and servicing to keep EU-hosted services alive under US quotas.
Why this and why now: with sovereignty at 11, capital at 5, and Washington rationing even allies case-by-case, only a joint withholding position gives Brussels leverage it cannot buy alone, and it directly serves holding essential services through cut-off.

## Priority
Priority is the Joint Middle-Power Supply Bargain, not the European Public Dividend Showcase, because without secured compute, spares and servicing the showcase sites and fallback pools have nothing to run on.

## In practice
We execute through the European Council and DG TRADE with the Hague, Tokyo and Seoul in a small contact group: Council conclusions mandating licence-alignment talks, an Anti-Coercion Instrument readiness note as backstop, no new law yet. DG GROW pools EU demand for servicing approvals and transformer/grid spares to bargain volume quotas jointly, funded from existing Single Market and trade facilitation lines.

We keep M9 alive on its own track: DG REFORM keeps the health/justice dashboards publishing weekly, while ENISA and EU-CyCLONe extend guarded cover only to showcase sites and hit pools, leaving mayors with mutual-aid moves rather than promises. We freeze forced migration and sit on existing EU capacity while the joint bargain reprices access.
