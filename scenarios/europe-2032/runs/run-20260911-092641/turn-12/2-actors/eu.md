## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee**
A flexicurity-style bridge for displaced entry cohorts — wage insurance, paid retraining placements and hiring incentives in law, accountancy, software, customer operations and administration — funded from ESF+ reprioritisation, to absorb the graduate hiring freeze without new law.
Why this and why now: with labour displacement visible, sentiment at 12 and capital at 10, the automated-decision scandal makes any frontier or adoption push politically impossible, while the bio precursor and open-weight exposure demand social order first; this directly serves holding social order through open-model exposure.

## Priority
Priority is the EU Entry-Level Transition Guarantee, not the Joint Middle-Power Supply Bargain, because without an answer to job losses and wrongful automated decisions the showcase dashboards and supply queueing lose all legitimacy.

## In practice
We run this through EPSCO and DG EMPL with ESF+ reprogramming: member states redeploy uncommitted ESF+ to wage-insurance top-ups and 6-month retraining placements, with a hiring bonus for firms converting placements, using existing Social Climate and EGF legal bases to avoid a new regulation fight. Labour ministers own the counters; justice ministries pause contested AI-decision deployments pending human-oversight audits to contain the scandal.

We keep M10 alive on a low track: DG TRADE sustains the Hague-Tokyo-Seoul contact group for servicing clearances and pooled spares tenders, no new mandate sought. ENISA and health ministries keep guarded cover on showcase clinics and manual fallback doctrine exercised, while the safety-breakthrough controls are pushed via the AI Safety Institute into deployed-system patch guidance.
