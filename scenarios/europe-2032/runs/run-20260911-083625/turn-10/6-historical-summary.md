Spring brought twin shocks after winter outages: US frontier AI providers cut off Union users with days' notice, darkening hospital, ministry and logistics tools; firms fell back to older licences and open weights on European servers. Simultaneously a deliberately released, machine-designed modified pathogen spread in two regions; Union health-emergency and disease-control bodies led weeks-long containment using sequencing/wastewater sentinels, with real but contained casualties. A new code/intrusion-automation release cut break-in kit assembly from days to hours.

Brussels responded without new vote: repair cell stayed embedded in clinics/communes with paper triage, offline backups and dispatch workarounds; overtime paid from reprogrammed funds; ministries ordered to cleared European-hosted fallbacks. Services degraded but held, mayors credited crews, but fallbacks were weaker, errors and queues rose, mood darkened further and trust sagged amid mixed rumours.

No new Gigafactory money voted, sites kept warm, grid work crept; retaliation over extended US lithography curbs shelved. Prior AI-written assault on appointments/dispatch/ports, paper reversion, hardened backup kits and sentinel upgrades helped absorb blow but exposed dependence.
