Repair cells stayed in clinics/communes with paper triage, offline backups, dispatch workarounds; overtime paid from reprogrammed funds; ministries/hospitals rationed cleared European-hosted models to triage/dispatch/labs; sentinels prevented second pathogen wave. Services degraded but held, mayors/nurses credited.
