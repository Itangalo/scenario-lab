### The Plateau That Wasn’t

Despite signs of stagnation—a flagship model underperforming and prices for top-tier access dropping—`ai_capability` climbs to 62.0, entering the 62–68 terminal zone. The drop in price reflects not a halt but a shift: last year’s frontier is now this year’s baseline, with capability diffusing faster than it advances. Open-weight systems absorb these gains gradually, with agent frameworks built on recent leaks showing improved performance on consumer hardware. `openweight_capability` rises modestly to 53.0, reflecting incremental optimization rather than a leap, consistent with the slow diffusion of frontier advances.

### Fractured Oversight, Failing Leverage

The Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation formally ends, delivering nothing. In its place, the Commission issues a non-binding request for energy and capacity disclosures from dominant AI providers, citing digital fairness. However, lacking enforcement mechanisms and facing resistance from key member states and firms, the initiative stalls before it begins. No new measure is formally launched, and the action is recorded as a minor regulatory gesture rather than a structured policy. The EU’s leverage remains theoretical, and its access to critical compute is more precarious than ever. `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 28.5, reflecting both capability growth and the erosion of perceived control.

### Bottom-Up Resilience, Modest Gains

The Municipal Resilience Backchannel Integration finishes successfully. Lyon, Barcelona, and Helsinki are certified as Trusted Resilience Nodes, with Bologna, Ljubljana, and Tallinn fast-tracked. The decentralised model proves durable where central mandates failed. `resilience` rises to 39.0, a modest but real gain in societal absorption capacity.

### Political Capital at the Threshold

With one large measure completed (+5) and no new initiative launched, portfolio burden lifts. `eu_political_capital` rises to 12.0—just enough to potentially launch new measures next turn. But legitimacy remains unearned: sentiment stays flat at 34.0, and institutional drift continues. The US administration takes office next turn; the world awaits its posture.
