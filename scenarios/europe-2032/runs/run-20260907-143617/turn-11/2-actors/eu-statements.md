We now know that chain may not exist in any form we can inspect. We must now add a new layer: resilience to the invisible. Trust cannot be restored through redress alone if the decisions themselves are beyond human comprehension. The commitment stands, but its meaning has shifted—from proving systems are accountable, to proving we can survive them when they are not.
