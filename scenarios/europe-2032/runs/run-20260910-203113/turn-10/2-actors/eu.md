## New measure
**EU Logistics Displacement Transition and Care Retraining Compact**
A small category 7 labour and social protection pact that ties automation levies on large-scale robot deployments to wage insurance and fast-track retraining into repair, care and retrofit construction.
Why this and why now: warehouse layoffs plus locked clinics are what is collapsing sentiment to 22 and draining capital to 18; with bio uplift contested and export rationing hardening, visible protection that converts displacement into staffed care capacity directly serves the two-year commitment to keep services running and earn trust.

## In practice
We run M11 as priority through DG EMPL, DG SANTE and Digital Europe: signed assistants stay on EU-hosted rationed inference, pilot waiting-list gains are published weekly, and small-town staffing tied to reporting is protected from reallocation. EPSCO and health ministers get the credit line with mayors, unions get audit access to signature logs to blunt fraud coverage.

We run M10 through HERA, ENISA and the joint cyber command invitation: load-shedding, dual-site triage and bio-detection databases stay hot, telemetry and binding sample-sharing are pooled without ceding data-control, and minimum stocks are replenished for the bio-gate window. We stand up the new Compact via ESF+ redeployment and employer co-funding: logistics firms ordering Chinese-built robots on US models register deployments, contribute to transition funds, and displaced warehouse workers are offered paid retraining into care, maintenance and municipal resilience roles that M8 left understaffed.
