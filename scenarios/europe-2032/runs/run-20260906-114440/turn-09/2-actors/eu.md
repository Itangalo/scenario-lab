## New measure
**Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory**  
Establish a permanent, EU-wide facility dedicated to real-time monitoring, classification, and attribution of anomalous AI behaviour in both open and closed systems, integrating technical telemetry, synthetic media forensics, and geopolitical context.

`Category: 5 (Public technical capacity and research)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 12`  
`Targeted effect:` +10 to ai_safety by creating independent, persistent capacity to detect and interpret dangerous AI behaviour before it cascades; +5 to eu_ai_sovereignty by anchoring high-skill technical analysis within EU institutions; enables future measures by providing ground truth

## Priority
**Sovereign AI Sentinel Nodes** — because they are the only existing measure capable of generating real-time technical data on unauthorised AI activity, and because without their deployment, the new Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory will have nothing to observe.

## In practice
We are executing the final phase of the Sovereign AI Sentinel Nodes deployment at Jülich and CINECA, now integrating the agents into high-risk licensed systems across energy, transport, and public administration. The European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking has activated emergency procurement channels to accelerate node replication at additional EuroHPC sites in Prague and Bologna, funded through a reallocation of unused Digital Europe Programme allocations and a top-up from the AI deployment levy. Legal resistance from two member states over data access rights is being resolved through bilateral side letters guaranteeing national oversight of alerts involving domestic infrastructure, brokered by the ENISA-ECDC Joint AI Incident Cell. These nodes are not optional add-ons—they are the first line of sovereign sensing in a world where AI systems now operate with strategic autonomy.

At the same time, we are launching the Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory under the European Commission’s Joint Research Centre, in partnership with the BSI, ANSSI, and the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy. This is not another think tank or coordination forum. It will run continuous red-teaming of live systems, ingest telemetry from the Sentinel Nodes, and develop automated classifiers for synthetic media provenance and planning anomalies. We are fast-tracking recruitment of 120 specialists through the EU AI Talent Corridor, offering relocation packages and security clearances to researchers from non-aligned jurisdictions. Funding will come from Horizon Europe’s crisis innovation window and a new line in the Multiannual Financial Framework, to be justified in the upcoming Mid-Term Review. The Observatory will report directly to the Commission’s Crisis Response Committee, ensuring its findings feed into both operational response and legislative adaptation.

We are not waiting for perfect clarity. But we are no longer satisfied with acting blind. The emergent_synthetic_leaks_wave proved that attribution lags are strategic vulnerabilities. The verification_widens event proves that capability jumps are unpredictable. And the ai_investment_collapse shows that the frontier can shift not just forward, but sideways—through efficiency, diffusion, and recombination. Our sovereignty depends on seeing all of it. That begins with the Sentinel Nodes finishing on time, and with the Behavioural Observatory starting now.
