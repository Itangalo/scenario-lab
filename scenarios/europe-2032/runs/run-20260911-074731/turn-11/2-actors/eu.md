## Priority

## In practice
We treat the cutoff as foresight failure made real, not as negotiable outrage. DG CNECT and ENISA under the finished Critical Services Shield and Bio-Shield continuity regime issue a cutoff continuity order: reserved EU-anchored compute and licensed open-weight models are reallocated to triaged health, benefits-administration and port-logistics workloads, with NIS2 joint teams keeping degraded-not-stopped operations.

We fund this from existing ESF+ and civil-protection lines, no new vote, Council JHA and EPSCO formations notified, not asked. Hyperscaler side-deals are frozen on common procurement terms, and the lawful-but-wrong redress continues in parallel, so the fallback does not re-entrench the same unread triage systems. Mayors get fallback slots and guard support for fenced sites first, to contain sabotage while sentiment is at 16.
