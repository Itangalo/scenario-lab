Autumn brought a second machine-generated ransomware sweep via poisoned software component through municipal and hospital-adjacent networks in France, Poland and Low Countries: benefits portals froze, appointment systems dark, Gdansk port servers isolated. ENISA-led triage kept payments and yard movements crawling on backups; Lyon oncology lost rebuilt lists a third time to paper diaries. Recovery took weeks, no attribution.

Brussels completed continuity fallback — protected compute slots for health lists, benefits and port workloads plus raised bio-sampling — preventing stoppage but only degrading services. Public legitimacy eroded further amid slower substitutes, escorted repairs, guarded halted sites, and cheap Chinese humanoids on withdrawn US stacks; mayors demanded policing before construction, augmentation claims failed as work displaced to paper, radio and waiting lists.
