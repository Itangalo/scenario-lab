**EU Entry-Level Transition and Essential Service Corps**
Creates EU-funded wage-insurance, rapid retraining and paid essential-service placements for displaced graduates and admin/customer-operations workers, tied to municipalities and hospitals already under the Continuity Shield. ## Priority
M10 EU Entry-Level Transition and Essential Service Corps — because without stemming graduate and white-collar hiring collapse no compute, resilience or adoption measure survives public hostility and site sabotage this turn.

## In practice
We run the Corps through DG EMPL and the ESF+ with top-up from the Dividend underspend, via EPSCO and the Competitiveness Council: wage-insurance for firms that retain-retrain juniors alongside AI, plus 12-month paid placements in hospitals, municipalities and civil-protection-guarded build sites doing offline-backup, port-check and permit-module work. Conditionality answers the side-deal split — Dividend and Corps funds flow only on common procurement terms.

We finish under the old commitment by holding what we built: NIS2 joint teams keep hospital/port continuity without new cost, ENISA hardens Dividend deployments on EU-anchored compute only, and DG GROW uses permitting/grid-reservation law to push reserved chips into racks with civil-protection guarding, while TTC allied-access is kept warm but not waited for. No new frontier restriction we cannot enforce; survival is keeping services degraded-not-stopped and voters seeing EU-paid work where AI took jobs.
