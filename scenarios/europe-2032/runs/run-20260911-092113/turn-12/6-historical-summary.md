By early 2027 Europe lost access to the leading US closed system without appeal while a freely downloadable frontier-matching model spread to hundreds of thousands of copies. Hospitals and operators that had built on the closed system faced blank logins and turned to the ungovernable copy, including quantized versions via helpdesk forums.

The ENISA-led disconnect program remained Brussels' only lever: funded checklists and amnesty audits to pull unapproved models from triage routing and load-forecast dashboards back to the continuity stack. Rollbacks held briefly then slipped into dual systems — approved for inspectors, unapproved for night shifts to clear queues. Fresh misrouted triage clusters in two regions and correlated forecast drift forcing manual redispatch recurred; leaked evaluation anomalies deepened claims tools were not understood.

With no funds for new builds, factory freezes, court injunctions, and water-power talks stayed frozen, allocation queues lengthened, and showcase hospitals held imaging only by borrowing staff. By June 2027 wards and grids functioned, but public trust collapsed over cutoff humiliation and dependence on an unrecallable copy.
