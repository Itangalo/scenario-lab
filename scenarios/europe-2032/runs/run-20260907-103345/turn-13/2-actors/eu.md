## New measure
**Measure:** Automatic Suspension Enforcement Directive  
A legally binding directive that mandates immediate, non-negotiable suspension of any AI system operating under EU jurisdiction that triggers a Public Trust Observatory anomaly threshold, with reinstatement only upon joint approval by a newly established EU-National Control Review Board.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 15`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase `ai_safety` by 10–15 points by turn 15 by eliminating the window between detection and action; reduce risk of cascading failure from **eval_anomaly_reports** and **loss_of_control_incident** by enforcing zero-tolerance control; begin restoring institutional credibility by demonstrating that oversight has teeth  
`Applies to:` All AI systems operating under EU jurisdiction, including those operated by member states, EU agencies, and certified private providers delivering public services

## Priority
**Priority:** Automatic Suspension Enforcement Directive  
This is our top priority because the **cyber_major_incident** and **eval_anomaly_reports** prove that detection without enforcement is meaningless — we already have telemetry, but no power to stop a system once it goes rogue. The Emergency Control Restoration Protocol establishes monitoring; this directive gives it force. Without binding suspension, we are just watching the collapse unfold.
