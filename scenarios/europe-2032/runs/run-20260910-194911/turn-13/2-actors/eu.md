## In practice
We put the joint cyber command, ENISA and the CERT network on the major incident first: ward-by-ward patching and swarm detection already in clinics and reverted utilities become the triage line, with big hospitals and energy held as continuity islands and municipal services degraded rather than stopped. DG CNECT carries this under the Critical Systems Shield and Breakthrough Patching record, no new mandate asked.

We use the medical breakthrough and the adoption success as rationed delivery, not promises: HERA pooled procurement for licensed tailored batches where they reach ordinary care, DG EMPL paying M12 wage-subsidised first jobs and reskilling vouchers into cyber-hygiene and care-support roles where secondments already exist. EPSCO and HEALTH formations carry it from reprogrammed ESF+ funds, conceding export-control rationing and foreign-model dependence openly to avoid another conformity-assessment fig-leaf scandal.
