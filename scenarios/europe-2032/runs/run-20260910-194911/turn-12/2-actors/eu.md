## New measure
**EU Displaced Entry-Level Guarantee**
It funds wage-subsidised first jobs, rapid retraining and income bridging for graduates and clerical workers displaced in law, accountancy, software and customer operations, tied to the cyber-defence and care hardening rollout. Because labour displacement is now measurable and public sentiment at 21 leaves no room to hold services without holding livelihoods, and this is the core of the two-year commitment.

## Priority
EU Displaced Entry-Level Guarantee, because with hiring frozen and a decision-scandal discrediting automated administration, the commitment to hold livelihoods outranks patching continuity this turn.

## In practice
We task DG EMPL with ESF+ reprogramming and SURE-top-up bridging to pay employers taking displaced entry-level staff, paired with short reskilling vouchers for cyber-hygiene, care-support and municipal resilience roles where the joint cyber command and HERA cell already have secondments. EPSCO formation carries it, with no new headline fund to force a Council budget fight we would lose at capital 7.

We use the cyber-defence breakthrough and safety breakthrough as delivery, not new programmes: ENISA pushes automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection as certified controls into the small clinics and reverted utilities, and we cite the new interpretability result to impose human-oversight logging and ombudsman redress on the scandal-hit decision systems under the AI Act, conceding the Annex III gap and fixing enforcement visibly to make adoption politically possible again.
