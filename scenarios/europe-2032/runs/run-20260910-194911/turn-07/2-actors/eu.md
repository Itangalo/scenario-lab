## New measure
**EU State-to-State Frontier Access Mission**
Pursues a government-to-government assurance deal with Washington now that frontier labs are under direct US state control, trading EU supply-chain and verification cooperation for restored essential-service model access.
This and now because nationalisation kills market-based leverage under the AI Act but opens arms-control-style negotiation, and with sovereignty at 14 and quotas hardening this is the only near-term path to keep essential services running under the continuity commitment.
