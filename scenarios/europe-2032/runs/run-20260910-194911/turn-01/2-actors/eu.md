## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled swarm intrusions with exercised incident response and cross-border mutual aid.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and why now is the autumn test shot showing segmented infrastructure was reachable and detection failed.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because the precursor intrusion outranks industrial build-out this turn while Gigafactories continue in background.

## In practice
We task ENISA with expanded mandate and CER/NIS2 enforcement via the Energy and Transport Councils, funding rapid audits of transmission operators in the two affected member states plus port and water pilots, with money from Digital Europe and Connecting Europe Facility reprogrammed.

The Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) continue through DG CNECT and accelerated-permitting zones, with member-state planning ministries pressed on grid connection for the first sites while ASML supply-chain leverage is held for coalition work later. We do not open a parallel compute programme.

For the Shield we stand up joint EU-CERT exercises this winter, pre-positioned breaker-credential rotation and relay segmentation kits, and a Commission reporting line for swarm-probe telemetry to catch the next test before actuation.
