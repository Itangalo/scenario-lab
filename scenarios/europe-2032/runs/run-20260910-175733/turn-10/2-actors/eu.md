## Priority
EU Trusted Medical Access Programme, because without converting the breakthrough into trusted EU-delivered care the audits and fallback queues cannot regain democratic consent under open superhuman diffusion.

## In practice
We run the Programme through Health Ministers Council and HERA with EMA fast-track and EHDS rules: EuroHPC triage capacity reserved for diagnostics and dosage validation, joint procurement favouring EU-hosted deployment, Bio-Cyber Mesh monitoring for misuse.

We tie it to the finished Transition Guarantee and Embodied Audits: wage-insurance and mayoral audit sites become distribution points for trusted deployment, with JRC/ENISA checklists extended to clinical AI, accepting handwritten degraded operation where validation fails rather than ceding to withdrawn US licences or unverified fleets.
