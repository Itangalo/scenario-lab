This and now because access-denied plus eval anomaly means the licensed stack cannot be trusted or recovered, and the two-year commitment to hold AI access requires a switch that runs in existing gigafactory and continuity hosting rather than a new compute build.

## In practice
We execute the switch through DG CNECT and the Health and JHA Councils using the Continuity Stack lists and ENISA hardening: HERA and EU4Health fund inference wrapping and guardrails, EuroHPC allocates existing gigafactory pilot capacity to fallback queues, no new siting vote. Member-state CIOs get a triage order — hospitals, water/grid, ports first — with model cards and incident reporting to the Bio-Cyber Mesh.

We keep M8 running via Employment Council and ESF+ to pay wage-insurance advances and hold mayors on permits, framing the fallback as proof EU hosting protects jobs and care. We treat the leaked eval anomaly as trigger to deny further high-risk US updates until third-party checks land, using the Anti-Coercion framing to resist retaliation while we switch.
