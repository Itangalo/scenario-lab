## Priority
EU Trusted Medical Access Programme, because without holding trusted medical delivery through rationing this turn the assurance uptake has no ward to land in and consent collapses under dependence.

## In practice
We run the Trusted Medical Access Programme through Health Council and HERA with EMA/EHDS fast-track: EuroHPC triage for diagnostics and dosage validation, joint procurement for EU-hosted deployment, wage-insurance and Rotterdam/Lyon/Hamburg audit sites as distribution, accepting handwritten fallback where validation fails.

We start the Assured Deployment Uptake through JRC, ENISA and the AI Office with EuroHPC labs: take the interpretability/control result the labs already want, apply it to clinical dosage and hospital triage models and to bio-cyber mesh monitoring first, publish pass-fail certification so clinicians stop overriding blind. We use the same inspectors and checklists to avoid a new bureaucracy.

We prepare for Taiwan escalation within existing Blockade Continuity stocks: no new instrument this turn, but triage chips, medicines and inference allotments toward hospitals, water and grid, and signal to Washington that rationing allies accelerates loss of consent.
