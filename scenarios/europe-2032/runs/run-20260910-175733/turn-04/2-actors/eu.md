## New measure
**EU-Funded Genome Publication Standard**
Requires EU-funded researchers to publish AI-assisted genome-design work only through screening-compliant venues with voluntary ECDC notification as a grant condition.
This and why now: the non-European preprint openly soliciting screened-out papers threatens forum-shopping that would void the publisher-led pause and our detection mesh; tying compliance to EU funding closes the loophole inside our jurisdiction without a new general mandate fight.
