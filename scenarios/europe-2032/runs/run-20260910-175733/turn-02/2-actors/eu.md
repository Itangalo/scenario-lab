## New measure
**EU Continuity Stack for Cut-Off Users**
Stands up an emergency substitution service that moves cut-off hospitals, ministries and firms onto EU-hosted and open-weight models with support contracts and fast-track procurement.
This and why now: the cutoff has landed on live clinical and administrative workloads, so absorbing the shock without permission is the two-year commitment made concrete — continuity first, then leverage.

## Priority
EU Continuity Stack for Cut-Off Users, because restoring live hospital and ministry function after a foreign switch-off outranks even grid hardening and compute build which continue without priority.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT and the Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority with a triage list of affected hospitals, ministries and critical firms, funded by repurposed Digital Europe and EU4Health envelopes, procuring inference capacity on EU AI Factory pilots and Mistral/Aleph Alpha-hosted models via an emergency framework contract. ENISA and national health CERTs provide migration teams to re-validate clinical workflows.

The Critical Systems Shield (M3) continues on its Energy Council mandate: joint TSO audits and 24/7 monitoring with cross-border exercises. Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) stay on DG COMP state-aid track; we explicitly frame the Continuity Stack as demand-aggregation for them, not a parallel compute programme, to keep industry and budget ministries on board while we negotiate fallback licensing with alternative non-cutoff providers.
