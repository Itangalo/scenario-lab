## In practice
We task ENISA with expanded 24/7 detection support and fund TSO upgrades through Connecting Europe Facility repurposing, with Energy Council mandate for joint audits by end-2026. NIS2 enforcement teams in the two affected member states lead exercises with port authority and water utility operators, bringing in private SOC providers under framework contracts.

The Gigafactories (M1) and Tech sovereignty package (M2) continue on DG CNECT and DG COMP tracks: site selection shortlists and state-aid clearance for accelerated-permitting zones, with ASML supply-chain dialogue kept separate to avoid linkage to US pressure. We signal that AI Act GPAI guidance due in 2027 will not be further delayed, to hold leverage while resilience lands first.
