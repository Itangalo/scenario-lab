## New measure
**European Public Results AI Programme**
Puts proven EU-run AI assistants to work in hospitals, municipal administration and schools where waiting lists and backlogs have already fallen, using EU-hosted models and procurement tied to the Shield's security baselines.
This is Diffusion, adoption and public trust work, because the one bright spot this turn — visible public-sector benefit attributed to a European decision — is the only lever that rebuilds sentiment and capital while the capability jump makes frontier control harder, and it bolts adoption to sovereignty rather than to American products.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because live municipal/hospital ransomware riding undetected grid access still outranks even the capability jump — containment in transmission, ports, water and hospitals must land now while evaluation and adoption build.

## In practice
We drive the Shield into operation via JHA and Energy Councils: ENISA baselines and 24h reporting as implementing acts, first TSO/port/hospital cohorts drawing Digital Europe and EIB loans through the single portal, with municipal co-financing top-ups to stop budgets balking and energy/water compensation to unblock supercomputer groundworks in the two litigating regions.

We staff the Agent Regime on an emergency track: the AI Office borrows ENISA secondments and Joint Research Centre evaluators to run pre-deployment agent tests and the whistleblower channel, with loss-of-control playbooks and kill-switch thresholds piloted with the financial supervisor. In parallel DG CNECT and DG SANTE scale the Results Programme only where it already works — triage, appointment backlog, permit decisions — hosted on EU supercomputers with EU-compute preference, so success is visibly European and visibly secure.
