## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens electricity, ports, water and telecoms against AI-enabled intrusion with mandatory detection standards, joint exercises and rapid-response aid.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, because the autumn intrusions in two member states prove detection would not have caught a weeks-long presence and resilience is the lever within EU control while sovereignty builds.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, because live undetected access to EU grids outranks longer-term compute build-outs that cannot stop the next intrusion.

## In practice
We task ENISA with expanded mandate and CER/NIS2 enforcement powers, funded via Digital Europe and EIB resilience loans, to impose baseline hardening, 24h incident reporting and joint TSO exercises in Competitiveness and JHA Councils. France, Germany, Spain grid operators are first cohort given the autumn victims; telecom and port authorities follow.

The InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech sovereignty packages continue on DG CNECT tracks: site selection and accelerated-permitting zones under the AI Continent Action Plan, with ASML/EUV supply-chain leverage held as bargaining chip against US pressure. We explicitly link Shield procurement to EU-sovereign compute requirements where feasible, so resilience spending pulls sovereignty forward without opening a parallel compute programme.
