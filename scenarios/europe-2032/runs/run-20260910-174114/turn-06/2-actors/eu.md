**EU Cohesion and Retraining Backstop**

This and now because political capital is 6 and sentiment 24: holding societies together through ungovernable acceleration means visible absorption first, or no further build is enforceable; a small labour and social protection instrument (category 7) is the only affordable bridge while Gigafactories finish.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because with US labs nationalised and tiered access looming, keeping the one sovereign compute build moving through injunctions and siting drift outranks even the new backstop — without EU soil capacity nothing else is autonomous.

## In practice
We nurse M1 through DG CNECT/DG ENER mediation: compensation and grid-connection guarantees to lift the supercomputer injunction and re-anchor two gigafactory sites, using EIB lending-bank top-ups, while holding permitting zones already won. The Critical Systems Shield, now finished law, is enforcement mode — ENISA 24h reporting, joint telemetry via the joint cyber command invitation, and walk-through restoration for ransomware-hit services.

We launch the Backstop via EPSCO/JHA with ESF+ and Digital Europe repurposing: direct grants not loans for municipalities/clinics refusing loan terms, insurer backstop to stop cover withdrawal, and mayor-led EU-compute assistants plus short-cycle retraining vouchers for logistics and clerical workers displaced by robots. Load-shedding is handled as civil protection — UCPM mutual aid and emergency curtailment rules for training clusters to protect winter grids.
