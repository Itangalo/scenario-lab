## New measure
**EU External AI Deals Unity Framework**
Creates a binding notification-and-solidarity procedure requiring member states to submit hyperscaler and third-country AI infrastructure deals for Commission review against Union positions, backed by Anti-Coercion Instrument deterrence and conditionality on EU funds.
This now because a member-state side-deal undercutting the Union line directly threatens our two-year commitment to keep essential services running on independent capacity, and without a common bargaining front the Gigafactories and hardening pact will be picked off bilaterally.

M3 EU Grid and Critical Services Hardening Pact, because keeping lights, hospitals and telecoms running through disruption remains the core of our two-year commitment and the autumn and winter incidents both hit that same backbone.

## In practice
We drive M3 through the TTE Council under NIS2 Article 11 and CER, with ENISA and ACER auditing the compromised TSOs and paying the first live segmentation exercises from reprogrammed Digital Europe and CEF-Energy, offering liability caps for telemetry to bring resisting operators on board. M4 containment runs in parallel via the Emergency Response Coordination Centre and interior ministries, pushing the slipped tabletop and mandatory self-replication reporting this autumn.

This turn we open the unity procedure via the General Affairs and Foreign Affairs Councils on Articles 207/218 and the Anti-Coercion Instrument: DG TRADE and DG CNECT screen notified bilateral compute and model-access deals, the Commission can delay signature for Union-interest review, and cohesion and InvestEU top-ups are tied to compliance. The defecting capital is offered a face-saving route to re-anchor its deal inside the Gigafactories pipeline rather than a public sanction fight. M1/M2 stay in permitting triage with host regions, no new money opened while capital is thin.
