## Priority
M6 EU Automation Displacement Cushion, because with access cut off, robots in deployment and a benefits scandal destroying trust, income continuity and continued service are the only things that keep the Union governable.

## In practice
We triage what is already on the books. DG EMPL and the Employment Council accelerate the Cushion payouts through ESF+ to logistics, warehouse and auto-supplier regions hit by embodied deployment, pairing wage insurance with mayoral siting funds to hold factory perimeters. ENISA and the TSO cell take the defensive breakthrough — automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection — and push it through the M3/M4 playbooks already exercised: hospitals, grids and payment systems first, since cloned-voice freezes and probing attacks showed where cascade starts.

On access denied we do not promise a replacement we cannot fund: DG CNECT/TRADE holds the M5 unity line, no bilateral breakaway, pooled fallback procurement and conditioned top-ups to the joint pipeline, while Health and ministerial users are migrated to degraded-mode continuity. On the scandal we concede the harder reading — the system sat in a gap the Act's Annex III categories do not reach, so what was done was lawful — and task the AI Office with a narrow patch proposal rather than defending enforcement that cannot answer it. M1 stays in triage only until turn 7: permitting and grid-connection taskforces, no new money.
