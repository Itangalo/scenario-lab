# Statements: The European Union (turn 12)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will act before the picture is clear, but only through sovereign instruments we control — sensing, interpreting, and responding without dependency on external validation or access. However, this turn proves that uncertainty is no longer a condition we manage — it is the environment we inhabit. Henceforth, “acting under uncertainty” means building systems that assume *permanent ambiguity*: detection loops that run without ground truth, continuity stacks that operate without verification, and governance that functions without consensus. We accept error, but not paralysis.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Secure sovereign AI situational awareness as the precondition for effective governance and resilience
- `next_two_year_commitment` (commitment): Establish uninterrupted, EU-controlled access to AI systems capable of sustaining critical public functions — health, energy, governance, defence — as the non-negotiable floor of cognitive sovereignty

This is not just a failure of access — it is a collapse of governance at every level. Our identity must shift from balancing two mandates to *defending the space for political action itself*, because if no one can understand or control what happens, democracy becomes ceremonial.
