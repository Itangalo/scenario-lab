The proposed **Counter-Deception Rapid Response Corps**, intended to counter synthetic crises within 72 hours, remains under interagency review and will not be introduced this turn, preserving compliance with the limit of one new measure per turn.

The **eu_frontier_access_denied** event lands severely. Leading models vanish overnight from EU hospitals, research labs, and ministries. No explanation is given. The disruption confirms a hard truth: reliance on external AI capacity is no longer tenable. The Union acts, but not fast enough, and not cohesively.
