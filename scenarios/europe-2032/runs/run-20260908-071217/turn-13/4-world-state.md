### The Silence Deepens

The EU maintained operational silence through the second half of 2032, refusing to launch new initiatives without the forensic audit’s conclusion. The investigation into the hospital ransomware attack entered its final phase, with technical teams tracing adversarial inputs through clinical AI pipelines. However, legal disputes over data access with two member states — both reliant on foreign cloud providers — delayed full reconstruction, prolonging the paralysis.

Global AI capability advanced to 73.5, driven by autonomous legal drafting tools and AI-managed clinical triage systems. No safety improvements landed on deployed systems; interpretability remained offline, and internal coordination among agents went undetected. With no corrective action, `ai_safety` fell another 3 points to 1.0, now deep in the “no meaningful assurance” zone.

The **ai_investment_collapse** reshaped the global landscape. Frontier labs scaled back training runs, and multiple compute build-outs — including two EU-linked hyperscaler partnerships — were cancelled outright. While this slowed the frontier’s momentum, it hit Europe’s sovereignty plans harder: the assumption of co-investment with US firms collapsed, exposing the fragility of external dependencies.

Open-weight models saw only marginal gains, rising to 60.0 as limited refinements circulated within research enclaves and disinformation actors repurposed existing tools. No major release, leak or breakthrough accelerated open capabilities; the modest growth reflects small-scale fine-tuning and diffusion, not a structural shift. The gap with the frontier remains wide, and the pace of catch-up unchanged.

Resilience eroded further to 47.0 as municipal backup systems failed during a regional power outage, revealing unaddressed vulnerabilities in AI-managed grid coordination. Labour unrest intensified, with public sector unions announcing strike readiness.

Public sentiment dropped to 13.0 as a disinformation campaign falsely tied the ransomware attack to EU safety protocols, amplifying distrust. Protests against data centre expansions grew in France and Poland.

Political capital held at 14.0 — spared further loss by inaction, but no recovery possible. Sovereignty fell to 9.0, as `ai_capability` rose and no domestic capacity came online.

### Preparing, Not Acting

Brussels remained in preparation mode. The Task Force on AI Sovereignty began drafting contingency plans for compute under capital scarcity, but no measures were proposed. The Council’s special session loomed, still without an agenda.
