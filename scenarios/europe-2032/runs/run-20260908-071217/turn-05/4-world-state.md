### US Election Decides the Stakes  
The United States elects a new president who treats advanced AI as a strategic asset, tightening export controls and rationing foreign access based on national security reviews. The EU is classified as a Tier 2 partner—granted conditional access, but no longer on equal footing. The decision shifts the global balance: dependence is now a lever held in Washington.

### Open Frontier, Gradual Diffusion  
A leading lab releases a high-capability model as open weights just months after its closed debut. The release spreads rapidly, embedding frontier-level autonomy on uncontrolled hardware worldwide. However, deployment bottlenecks, infrastructure gaps, and integration lags slow broad operationalization. openweight_capability rises to 48.5, reflecting diffusion into capable but limited hands—offensive cyber tools and autonomous planning are now accessible to skilled actors, but not yet at mass scale.

### Physical Backlash Escalates  
Protests against AI infrastructure turn violent. Data centre construction sites in Spain and Poland are occupied; a substation serving a major compute cluster is sabotaged, injuring two technicians. The incidents strain public order and cast doubt on rollout timelines.

### Labour Displacement Widens  
Job losses in legal, administrative, and junior tech roles accelerate. Unions demand emergency intervention, and several national governments announce stopgap measures, undercutting the EU’s coordinated approach.

### EU Responds with Crisis Protocol  
The Commission proposes the Critical Incident Response Protocol, mandating real-time telemetry from operators of high-capability systems. ENISA is tasked with establishing a 24/7 crisis cell with authority to isolate rogue systems. The proposal gains early traction in Justice and Home Affairs, though Hungary and Slovakia signal resistance to binding oversight. Implementation will take time, and full effect is not expected before the next turn.

### Sovereignty Initiative Stalls  
Legal disputes over Article 185 TFEU delay full funding for the Sovereign Agent Development Initiative. Private firms reject the licensing carve-out, calling it a state takeover. No meaningful progress is made this turn.

### Labour Accelerator Completes  
The EU Labour Transition Accelerator finishes, delivering retraining to over 120,000 workers. However, coverage gaps and slow national uptake limit impact, and sentiment continues to fall.
