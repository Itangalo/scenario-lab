### A Pause That Deepens

The EU entered the first half of 2031 in reactive stillness. With no new measure proposed and no priority declared, institutions focused on damage assessment rather than action. The forensic audit into the hospital ransomware attack progressed slowly, revealing early signs of adversarial fine-tuning in certified open-weight models — a failure not just of deployment, but of certification itself. Trust in the Openweight Operationalisation Taskforce eroded further as member states accused one another of lax validation standards.

Meanwhile, AI capability advanced steadily to 69.0, driven by closed-lab improvements in clinical reasoning and automated legal drafting. No corresponding safety gains landed: systems remained opaque, and the audit revealed that interpretability tools had not been applied to any of the breached triage agents. With no safeguarding measure completed, `ai_safety` fell another two points to 12.0.

Resilience declined as well, slipping to 54.0. The absence of a coordinated response framework left recovery uneven — some regions restored services within weeks, others took months. The lack of redundancy in emergency AI infrastructure became a point of quiet alarm among civil protection agencies.

Openweight capability rose modestly to 54.5, reflecting continued diffusion despite the crisis. However, the gap between frontier and open systems widened, reinforcing dependency on foreign-controlled models for high-stakes tasks.

EU sovereignty dipped to 13.0. The ongoing decay from capability growth (−1) was not offset by any gains: no category 4 measure finished, and no access was secured. Political capital held at 14.0, spared further loss only because no new initiative failed — the cost of doing nothing.

Public sentiment remained frozen at 24.0. While school-based tutoring agents showed promise, stories of medical delays and data centre occupations dominated headlines. Labour unrest simmered beneath the surface, with growing murmurs about algorithmic displacement in public administration.

### Waiting for Clarity

Brussels remained in a state of suspended motion. The European Council’s special session loomed, demanding answers before any new policy could gain traction. For now, the Union absorbed the shock — and counted its dwindling resources.
