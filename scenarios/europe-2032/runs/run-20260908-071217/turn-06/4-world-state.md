### Access Cut, Response Launched
The EU is abruptly locked out of a leading frontier AI model, disrupting hospital diagnostics, judicial document processing, and transport logistics across multiple member states. With no explanation or appeal, the outage lasts 72 hours—long enough to expose systemic fragility. The Commission responds by fast-tracking the **EU Compute Access Guarantee**, a new large measure aimed at pre-positioning sovereign fallback models and securing continuity for critical services. It is named the priority, with €800 million allocated immediately for replication of open-weight equivalents at capability 58.0+ on Gaia-X-linked clusters.

### Sovereignty Erodes Further
Despite the urgency, progress on long-term sovereignty remains stalled. The **Sovereign Agent Development Initiative** faces continued resistance over licensing terms, and private firms reject compromise offers. Legal clarity on Article 185 TFEU arrives too late to unlock funding this turn. Meanwhile, **ai_capability** rises by 2.0, widening the gap with EU-controlled systems. With no category 4 measure completing and frontier access now denied, **eu_ai_sovereignty** drops to 12.0.

### Resilience Gains from Coordination
In a rare win, the EU joins a new joint cyber and bio threat-sharing pact, enabling real-time telemetry exchange with key allies. This **joint_threat_response** lifts **resilience** by +2. Separately, ENISA begins standing up a crisis cell under qualified majority voting, bypassing Hungarian and Slovak resistance, though full powers await broader agreement.

### Political Capital Under Pressure
The **Tech Sovereignty Package** completes, but its impact is muted. Political capital falls under the weight of four large measures in flight (−12), the priority (−1), and no completions to offset them. A modest proposal bonus (+3) for addressing **eu_frontier_access_denied** partially cushions the drop. Public sentiment worsens due to visible dependency humiliation and ongoing protests, dragging capital down further.

### Open Frontier Widens
A verification breakthrough in clinical coding and legal contract analysis allows AI systems to improve rapidly in these domains without human oversight. Open-weight models at 50.5 capability now support semi-autonomous workflows in healthcare and law, accelerating diffusion despite infrastructure bottlenecks.
