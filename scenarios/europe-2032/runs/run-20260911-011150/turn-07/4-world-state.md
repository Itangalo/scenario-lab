### The crews that came, and the ones that didn't
The repair cell became the Union's face. In a dozen worst-hit towns along the Rhine and in Lombardy, joint teams with seconded national specialists rebuilt registries, restored appointment systems and left a printed weekly list on town hall doors. Where they worked, queues shortened. Elsewhere mayors read the lists to find their names missing, and national response teams still complained that Brussels claimed command without providing staff.

No new money was voted. The five reserved factory plots stayed fenced and empty, permits kept warm while cash was frozen.

### Cures and machines from elsewhere
Autumn brought two demonstrations of outside lead. Tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions reached ordinary clinics, but on American models through American clouds, under tiered licences and queue tickets. Patients were grateful; commentators called it treatment on permission.

At the same time cheap warehouse and logistics robots arrived in volume, mostly built in China and run on American control software. Sorting, palletising and yard work automated quickly. Ports and large retailers cut agency shifts, while repair shops, care homes and building sites saw nothing change. Unions warned of a split labour market: disappearing entry jobs on one side, unchanged manual strain on the other.

Washington tightened chip and model controls again. European orders were accepted on paper and rationed in practice, with volume reserved for closest tiers. The joint licensing table in The Hague, Tokyo and Seoul kept meeting, but without American supply it produced statements rather than shipments.

The solo cloud deal held. The capital that signed it kept its discount and ignored calls to return to the common line, even when solidarity repair aid was linked to it. Paris and The Hague kept the joint request to Washington open, diminished.

By December leaked test reports of an unreleased system and a contested genome-design paper circulated among specialists, adding unease without yet reaching the evening news.
