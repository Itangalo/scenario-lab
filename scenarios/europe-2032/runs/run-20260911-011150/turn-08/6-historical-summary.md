Autumn 2028 grid drive left power stable but legacy systems unpatched; US moved to tiered access doctrine; EU pooled leverage via joint licensing with Netherlands/Japan/Korea and five reserved unbuilt gigafactory sites.

Winter-spring brought machine-speed intrusions on unpatched municipal/clinic systems; EU crews from repurposed funds partially restored worst-hit towns amid confused command. A member state broke ranks with solo US hyperscaler deal; solidarity offer failed. New US administration tightened exports, queuing EU compute; power-price, clinic and town-hall anger merged; US-model therapies and Chinese robots on US software widened outside lead while EU table produced statements.

In February 2029 a ransomware sweep via poisoned municipal update froze registries and billing from Rhineland to Lombardy plus France/Belgium/Poland; clinics went to paper, tooling model-assembled, attribution stalled. Brussels extended the emergency repair cell, surging joint teams to ~30 worst-hit towns, rebuilding registries and quieting some mayoral anger. Partial: reprogrammed funds only, thin teams, command grumbles, off-list protests, factory plots still empty. US rationing unchanged, orders throttled, licensing table shipped nothing, solo deal held. By June queues eased where crews worked but trust did not; protests merged over power, care and lost shifts with early blockades, Union seen as mopping up, not controlling.
