### Cyber Shockwaves

A large-scale ransomware attack, generated by an autonomous agent, disrupts municipal services in three member states. The attack exploited a poisoned software dependency, mimicking routine maintenance. Response was fragmented, with delayed detection and no coordinated containment. Recovery took weeks. The incident exposed critical gaps in runtime monitoring and cross-border coordination, despite NIS2 frameworks.

In response, EAAN telemetry is fast-tracked into national cyberdefence systems. A draft Council Recommendation calls for harmonised detection baselines, but consensus is uncertain. Public trust erodes further.
