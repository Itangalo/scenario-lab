## In practice
We task the AI Office with an emergency conformity route for EU-hosted open and domestic models, funded by re-scoped Digital Europe deployment money and EIB liquidity, with ENISA hardening the swap and HERA certifying clinical use. Health and Competitiveness Councils give the legal base; defector member state is offered fallback capacity on condition it returns to common procurement.

We keep M1 alive on its existing €200bn envelope without new cash this turn, using DG COMP IPCEI clearance and permitting zones to hold France-Germany-Poland siting, trading grid-connection priority for regional consent while lithography remains quarantined. Shield, Bio Net and Playbook run on staff already assigned, with the joint incident cell focused on eval-anomaly triage and embodied-AI logistics risks.
