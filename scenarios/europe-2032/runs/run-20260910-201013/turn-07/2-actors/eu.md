**Emergency Cyber Hardening for Swapped Public Systems**
It pushes ENISA-led patching, offline backups and misuse monitoring onto the hospitals, tax offices and logistics systems just migrated to EU fallback models after the US cutoff and the verifiable-domain capability jump.
Why this and why now: the cutoff swap left public systems on unfamiliar models exactly as code/cyber attacker capability jumps, and with capital at 12 this small hardening buys resilience without reopening the stalled gigafactory siting fight inflamed by the French-German energy backlash.

## Priority
M6 EU Cutoff Fallback Deployment, because keeping clinics and ministries running through the cutoff is what stops further defection and earns the consent needed for any later grid and cyber work.

## In practice
We keep the AI Office emergency conformity route and HERA clinical certification as the front door, with Digital Europe redeployment and EIB liquidity paying ENISA joint teams to stay in Rotterdam, Lyon, Hamburg and Gdansk hospitals and ministries until fallback models are stable. The defector member state is offered fallback slots only if it rejoins common procurement, and Health Council provides cover.

We do not push new cranes or grid priority this turn while France-German blockades halt sites; InvestAI Gigafactories and the sovereignty package sit on existing permits, IPCEI clearance and private pledges, with Commission mediation offering energy-bill relief dialogue to unions in Lille rather than forced siting. The new cyber patch runs through the Shield/Bio Net staff and joint incident cell already assigned, focused on backup restoration drills and monitoring for the leaked-eval anomaly pattern, with no new cash call to spare political capital.
