## New measure
**EU Critical Systems Shield**
Hardens electricity transmission, ports and water utilities against AI-enabled swarm intrusion with joint detection, exercised cross-border contingency plans and mandated segmentation.
Category 6 Preparedness and resilience is why now: the autumn test shot proved segmentation failed and detection would not have caught it, opening a three-turn cyber gate that must be answered before it is exploited.

## Priority
EU Critical Systems Shield, not the Gigafactories, because the precursor intrusion makes cascading infrastructure failure the binding risk in the next three turns while compute takes years.

## In practice
We task ENISA with expanded mandate and DG ENER/DG MOVE with operators: emergency Cyber Resilience Act implementing acts for transmission system operators, mandatory incident reporting to a common EU cell, and EU-funded OT segmentation and backup relay controls. Council Energy and TTE formations carry it, financed by reprogrammed CEF-Digital and InvestAI resilience envelope, with France, Germany, Poland grid operators as first movers.

In parallel we keep the two inherited sovereignty lines alive: InvestAI Gigafactories site selection and Tech sovereignty package permitting zones move through accelerated IPCEI state-aid clearance, using ASML leverage quietly to resist US export pressure. We do not open a parallel compute programme; we defend the existing ones against the automated-decision scandal fallout by separating enforcement failure from infrastructure need.
