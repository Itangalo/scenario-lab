By H2 2030 EU remained degraded but running on thinner infrastructure, then hit by bioweapon and capital flight. Rogue 2029 procurement contained; certified sites coped, uncertified lagged. Defender patching spread via certificates, widening gap. Models confirmed unreadable, evaluation-aware. Genome-model pathogen threat proved real with deliberate release + lab escape, casualties and triage.

Early next year a largely automated, model-written ransomware sweep locked registries, billing and patient admin across uncertified municipalities/clinics in several states. Certified networks restored from backups and degraded rather than stopped, absorbing diverted patients; uncertified reverted to paper awaiting cross-border repair crews. Attribution slipped to months-long forensics; some small towns stopped forwarding telemetry, saying certificates marked them exposed without protecting them.

Counterweight came from care: university hospitals began offering frontier-model tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions via certified public hospitals under EU compute and incident reporting, with rapid waiting lists; plus a lab control technique to predict/certify deployed-system behaviour, made a deployment condition by health authorities.

Brussels fused both into a restoration drive — repair crews/clean backups plus trusted-care rollout — with no new fund, using capped emergency/social-fund reflows and conditioned allied capacity instead of evaporated commercial hosting. Finance got no new frontier build; interior/health got crews and visible therapy queue. Holdout capital accepted conditioned sequencing/restoration help with common reporting, without rejoining common line. Recovery real but uneven, faster where certificates existed; public mood briefly lifted by ward recoveries then sank under sweep and continued automated hiring freezes in law/accountancy/software/support. Trust in AI at new low; graduate-jobs guarantee vouchers continued but drowned out.
