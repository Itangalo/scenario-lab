**Uncertified Town Repair and Trust Compact**
It sends no-strings repair crews, offline backups and basic hardening to boycotting municipalities and clinics while pausing certificate penalties and creating an amnesty telemetry channel, to restore services and bring incident reporting back.
This and now because the telemetry boycott blinds defenders amid a mutating sweep, and with sentiment at 14 and capital at 31 we cannot coerce compliance — we must earn it by protecting first, in line with holding social absorption through ungovernable diffusion.

## Priority
Uncertified Town Repair and Trust Compact — because without telemetry from hit towns we cannot see the next sweep, and without visible repair we cannot hold our absorption commitment.

## In practice
We act through ENISA and DG CNECT under the Shield and NIS2 bases, with Interior and Health Council backing. Cross-border IT mutual-aid teams and clean-backup kits go first to boycotting utilities and clinics with no certificate precondition; ESF+ reflows cover overtime and paper-to-digital restoration. Certificate deadlines are frozen for participants for this turn.

In parallel we open an amnesty reporting channel via national CSIRTs: anonymised incident feeds with no non-compliance flagging, co-signed by mayors' associations, to restart telemetry flows. Trusted-care rollout continues in certified hospitals, but crews and communications priority this semester is the uncertified periphery that stopped forwarding data.
