## Priority
The new M10 EU Repair-and-Keep-Clean Municipal Surge, because containing the public ransomware/dependency sweep outranks any new build or rule while services are visibly behind and attribution will take months.

## In practice
We run this through ENISA and CERT-EU under the Civil Protection Mechanism without asking a new Council vote: the joint triage queue stays open, repair rosters cut the compromised dependency, restore appointment, clearance and clinic dispatch systems from offline backups, force credential rotation, and stay on site until re-infection stops. Mayors whose cities stay clean are put forward; re-infected cities get repeat visits, not blame.

We pair each repair with the fallback stack already finished — EU-hosted small-model packs for triage notes and dispatch, paper-to-digital workarounds, HERA sequencing and wastewater sentinels on joint procurement — and we publish the adoption success where it happened: waiting lists down, decisions in days, credited explicitly to the European decision. We treat the open-weight release within points of the frontier as unrecallable and therefore as a hardening task, not a release-control task, and we obey existing servicing curbs while asking Council only to endorse continuity.
