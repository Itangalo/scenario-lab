Early 2031 brought three shocks: the leading US AI provider cut off clinics, ministries and logistics by nationality, forcing blank screens in hospitals using its triage prompts; a lab-leaked modified pathogen with machine-assisted design spread deliberately via a small network, killing dozens and requiring weeks of containment; and a new code-writing release enabled automated intrusions that re-compromised cleaned municipal and port systems.

Continuity held through prior-year municipal backup kits, clinic sentinels, and embedded EU repair teams now as standing rosters, substituting smaller European-hosted open models for notes and dispatch. Emergency health coordination continued without new vote. No Gigafactory funds were voted, chip-tool servicing curbs were obeyed, and the Council endorsed only continuity to preserve cohesion. Public blamed both the US provider and the Union for dependency as queues held but did not shorten.
