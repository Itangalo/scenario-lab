### The Frontier Leaps, the Union Stalls

Global AI capability surges by +3.0 to 98.0, driven by a discontinuous advance that redefines strategic reality. The jump renders human oversight not just impractical but structurally obsolete across military, financial, and infrastructure domains. No known interpretability method scales to the new models; safety systems fail without alerting their operators. The leap widens the chasm between capability and control.

The EU finalizes integration into the joint cyber-biosurveillance alliance. Real-time detection cells in Brussels and Stockholm successfully flag two early bio-signals, validating the framework’s operational value. Yet the systems run on opaque foreign models, deepening dependence at the very moment oversight collapses globally.

The Open Science Resilience Initiative completes its mandate. Its auditable protein folding models are now in use at Charité Berlin, Hospital del Mar, and the Joint Research Centre, and have been formally adopted by three national biosecurity agencies. The breakthrough proves that open, verifiable AI can deliver in high-stakes domains — but only where the EU controls the full stack. However, the impact on global open-weight capability remains limited and incremental. While adoption is real, the models are specialized and not general-purpose, contributing only a modest uplift in capability. Open-weight systems rise to 64.0, reflecting steady progress but not a paradigm shift.

Meanwhile, the Commission’s push for binding auditable AI mandates stalls. The Energy Council delays alignment, and Germany, Spain, and Italy refuse to commit, citing grid risks and clinical reliability. With political capital at 0.0, enforcement is impossible. The Union shifts to demonstration: offering technical support and integration kits to willing member states, but abandoning compulsion. Sovereignty erodes further as fragmentation spreads — eight member states now restrict data centre expansion, citing energy security.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 27.0, caught between fatigue and distrust.

### Fractured Authority

The EU acts, but nothing lands. It demonstrates capability, but cannot compel cohesion. Power without enforcement is performance.
