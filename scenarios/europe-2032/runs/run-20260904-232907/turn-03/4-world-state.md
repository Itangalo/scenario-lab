### Frontier Stalls, But Progress Persists  
Despite signs of a capability plateau—underwhelming releases and falling prices—AI development continues. Efficiency gains and algorithmic refinements push `ai_capability` up by 1.0 to 55.5. The frontier slows, but does not stop. Open-weight models benefit, with offensive tools and research agents now widely accessible, lifting `openweight_capability` to 43.0.

### Sovereignty Gains Modestly Amid Delayed Implementation  
The Emergency Sovereign Compute Directive formally passes this turn, marking the EU’s most ambitious step yet toward structural autonomy. As a high-cost category 4 measure, it requires at least two full turns for full implementation and cannot take full effect this turn. The directive enters its first phase of rollout, with procurement and infrastructure planning now underway, but compute reservation remains partial. No sovereignty contribution is realized yet from this measure. Ongoing progress from InvestAI Gigafactories provides +1 to `eu_ai_sovereignty`, offset by −1 due to rising `ai_capability`, resulting in a net increase of +1 to 20.0.

### Political Capital Drains Under Burden  
Despite legislative momentum, `eu_political_capital` falls to 25.0, weighed down by a −10 portfolio charge: −3 each for Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty, Emergency Compute, and Resilience Surge, plus −1 for priority designation. No recent incident justifies a proposal bonus, and no legitimacy lending occurs. The cost of ambition mounts, even as major initiatives advance.

### Safety and Sentiment Dip  
With no safety measures landing on deployed systems, `ai_safety` declines to 26.0 amid rising capability. Public sentiment softens to 38.0 as moratorium campaigns gain traction and worker displacement fears grow, though no major backlash crystallizes this turn.
