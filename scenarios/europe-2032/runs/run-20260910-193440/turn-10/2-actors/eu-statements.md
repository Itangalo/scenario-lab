# Statements: The European Union (turn 10)

## Ledger

- `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing.
- `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.
- `two_year_commitment` (commitment): Keep essential European services, livelihoods and democratic trust functioning through AI-driven disruption on resources Europe itself controls
- `sovereignty_through_sufficiency` (position): Independent frontier leadership is no longer attainable in the next two years after US nationalisation, EU access denial and stalled build-out; sovereignty now means assured sufficiency for hospitals, ministries, power, water and basic white-collar continuity on EU-controlled compute and models
