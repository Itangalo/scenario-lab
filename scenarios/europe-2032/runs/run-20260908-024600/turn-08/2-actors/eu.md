## New measure  
**Pilot Transitional AI Adjustment Fund**  
Launch a time-limited, targeted wage insurance and retraining programme for workers displaced by AI in legal, accounting, and administrative roles, with co-financing from firms that automate and mandatory redeployment pathways.

- Category: 7 (Labour and social protection)  
- Size: small  
- Finishes on turn: 10  
- Targeted effect: Stabilise public_sentiment by 5–8 points; reduce strike risk and institutional disruption; begin building institutional capacity for AI-driven labour transitions  
- Applies to: Member states (implementation), ETUC, professional associations, and firms above 250 employees in legal, accounting, and public administration sectors  

## Priority  
**Deploy Grid-Ready Compute Corridors** — because the legal authority is now asserted, the funding is allocated, and the only thing standing between the EU and sovereign compute is physical deployment — and without progress by mid-2030, we will have missed the window to close the sovereignty gap before openweight capability reaches 60.0.

## In practice  
This turn, we are forcing movement where money and law have stalled. The EAIA, backed by the Article 114 TFEU override, is now issuing binding directives to grid operators in Belgium, Poland, and Finland: they must submit binding grid upgrade plans by Q3 or forfeit access to the €40bn Compute Corridors fund. ENISA is fast-tracking security certification protocols — training run logging, weight custody chains, and tamper-proof audit trails — which will now gate disbursement. This is no longer a negotiation. The Gigafactories are built. The law is clear. The money is real. Now the wires must carry power.

At the same time, we cannot ignore the rising social cost. The strikes in legal and accounting sectors are not just protests — they are signals of systemic fracture. We are launching the pilot Transitional AI Adjustment Fund with €8bn in initial allocations from the European Social Fund and national co-financing. It will offer 70% wage insurance for displaced junior professionals for up to 18 months, paired with retraining in AI-augmented roles — legal tech oversight, audit validation, compliance engineering — and mandatory redeployment quotas for firms that adopt AI at scale. We are negotiating with the ETUC and national bar associations this week in Brussels, offering access to sovereign AI tools for union-led transition planning in exchange for a pause in industrial action. This is not charity. It is strategic stabilisation.

We are also advancing the Secure Sovereign Lithography Initiative — but not as a new measure. It remains in exploratory drafting, with technical working groups now meeting in Eindhoven under the Commission’s Semiconductor Task Force. We are not launching it this turn because we cannot credibly fund two Category 4 megaprojects at once, and because the world has made clear that physical deployment of compute is the immediate bottleneck, not future chipmaking. We are holding the concept in readiness, but our capital, our attention, and our credibility are tied to getting real systems online — now.
