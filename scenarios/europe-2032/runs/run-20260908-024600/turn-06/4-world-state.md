### US Posture Set: Alliance Begins

The new U.S. administration officially assumes office, confirming a posture of **ALLIANCE**. Early coordination begins on joint safety evaluations and structured access to frontier systems for vetted EU institutions. Shared export control templates are circulated, and a bilateral working group on compute access is scheduled for July.

### Legal Wins Land, But Steel Doesn’t Follow

The EAIA Fast-Track Mandate officially completes, streamlining regulatory approval for qualifying AI infrastructure projects. However, physical deployment remains stalled: no grid commitments are secured, and regional opposition in Poland and Finland delays site agreements. The InvestAI Gigafactories and Mandatory Red Teaming measures, while referenced in policy discussions, were initiated in earlier turns and are not newly completed; their implementation remains partial and off-schedule.

### Investment Collapse Reshapes the Landscape

Private capital flees the AI sector globally. Major U.S. labs downsize training runs; European developers cancel expansion plans. While this slows global capability growth, it eliminates hoped-for private co-investment in sovereign infrastructure. The EU becomes the sole remaining funder of scale, straining its fiscal capacity.

### Labour Displacement Deepens Public Anxiety

Visible job losses in law, accountancy, and software trigger media storms across member states. Entry-level hiring freezes are widespread. Public sentiment sours further, with protests outside tech hubs and data centres.

### Middle Power Coalition Strengthens Leverage

A coalition of mid-sized democracies—including South Korea, Netherlands, and Taiwan—forms a coordination framework on export licensing and compute bargaining. Though non-binding, it enhances the EU’s leverage in negotiations with suppliers.

### One New Measure: Grid-Ready Compute Corridors

The EU proposes Deploy Grid-Ready Compute Corridors, targeting fast-tracked zones in Belgium, Poland, and Finland. Funding would be tied to grid readiness and security certification, enforced by the EAIA. However, with no secured budget reallocation, no new appropriations, and ongoing regional resistance, the initiative remains in early proposal stage. The lack of a credible funding pathway prevents any meaningful political momentum, and no proposal bonus is realized. The measure is under review for potential inclusion in the next fiscal envelope, but no commitment is made this turn.
