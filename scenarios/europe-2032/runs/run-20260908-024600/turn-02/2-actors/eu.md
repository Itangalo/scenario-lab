## Priority  
**Emergency Resilience Surge** — because the cyber intrusion exposed a live vulnerability in critical systems, and with the Taiwan blockade constraining compute supply, we cannot assume time or external support; resilience is the only immediate barrier between us and cascading failure.

## In practice  
We are pushing on three tracks: industrial build-out, institutional empowerment, and crisis response. The InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package remain locked in Council negotiations, with Germany and France insisting on national control over site selection and allocation. To break the deadlock, we are offering a binding EU-level framework under a newly empowered European AI Infrastructure Agency (EAIA), which will be fast-tracked through Article 114 TFEU and given enforcement powers over cross-border connectivity, security standards, and allocation of sovereign compute. This is not a parallel programme — it is the legal backbone the current portfolio lacks. The EAIA will also absorb the resilience mandate for critical AI infrastructure, ensuring that air-gapped systems and detection protocols are not just mandated but monitored and maintained.

The Emergency Resilience Surge is now in implementation, with the Commission deploying €12bn from the redirected Recovery and Resilience Facility. However, two energy networks — one in Poland, one in Spain — are delaying procurement pending national budget approvals. We are offering front-loaded grants covering 70% of initial costs, tied to quarterly delivery milestones, and activating the Civil Protection Mechanism to run red-team exercises in Q2. The Internal Market and Environment formations remain sceptical of central oversight, so we are co-designing protocols with national CISOs and embedding national experts in the EU Cyber Crisis Liaison Organisation. This is not about control — it is about coherence under fire.

Public anxiety is rising. Job displacement stories dominate regional media, and protests have delayed data centre construction in Ireland and Finland. We are not ignoring sentiment — but we cannot pause. The EAIA proposal includes a transparency clause: annual public reporting on job impacts, retraining uptake, and infrastructure resilience, to be delivered through the Digital Agenda forum. This is not a concession — it is how we maintain capital while acting. We are building sovereignty not just in silicon, but in trust.
