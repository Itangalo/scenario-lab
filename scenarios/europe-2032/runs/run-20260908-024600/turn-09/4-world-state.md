### The Frontier Advances, Safety Stalls  
Global AI capability advanced to 65.0, reflecting steady progress along the established trajectory. Systems demonstrated improved autonomy in research execution, managing multi-step planning tasks with reduced oversight. While notable, this evolution was consistent with prior trends and did not represent a structural break in the rate of change. Open-weight models gained ground more gradually, reaching 53.0, supported by wider diffusion of training techniques and increased community coordination. This narrowing of the gap remains within the bounds of cumulative progress; no sudden leak or permissive release occurred at a scale sufficient to trigger a discontinuous jump.

### Sovereignty Through Compulsion  
The EU completed the *Deploy Grid-Ready Compute Corridors* initiative this turn, delivering +5 to *eu_ai_sovereignty* under metric rule 5. Momentum from the ongoing *Pilot Transitional AI Adjustment Fund* added +1. However, the 4.0-point rise in *ai_capability* triggered a −1 decay. No capacity event occurred. Net sovereignty: 28.5.

### Political Capital Crumbles  
The portfolio charge included one large measure (−3) and one small (−2), plus −1 for naming a priority—totaling −6. The new measure, *Enforce Binding Grid Integration Deadlines*, earned no proposal bonus: while long-anticipated, it responded to a slow-building crisis, not a recent shock. Public sentiment (29.0) now exceeds political capital, but the legitimacy boost does not apply since capital is higher. Final political capital: 39.0.

### Backlash and Scandal  
A judicial investigation revealed that an AI-assisted welfare eligibility system in a major member state had wrongfully denied benefits to thousands, relying on unexplainable model logic. The resulting scandal, widely covered as systemic negligence, triggered a significant public reaction. Labour displacement continued to accelerate, and the ETUC withdrew its cooperation, citing broken promises. Trust in AI governance declined markedly, though the erosion of public sentiment was partial this turn, with further fallout expected.

### Resilience Erodes  
With *ai_capability* rising and no new category 6 measure in place, *resilience* fell by −2. The cyber and legal infrastructure meant to absorb harm proved inadequate against the pace of change.
