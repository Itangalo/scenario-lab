## In practice
This turn, we are executing on the closure of three major measures. The European AI Infrastructure Agency’s Fast-Track Mandate has been formally adopted under Article 114 TFEU, with the Council accepting the compromise: national governments retain veto power over siting, but once a location is chosen, connectivity, security, and access standards are binding and enforced by the Commission. We are now deploying the first €30bn tranche of the sovereign compute bond, tied to milestones in grid readiness and physical security certification, to break the deadlock. ENISA has delivered the technical annexes for weight custody and training run logging, ensuring that any system trained on EU soil falls under enforceable jurisdiction — a condition now baked into funding disbursement.

Simultaneously, the Mandatory Pre-Release Red Teaming framework has been finalized as a delegated act. The first pilot, conducted on a 54.0-capability model, has yielded test protocols that Fraunhofer and CNRS are now scaling. Public summaries will be released next month to build credibility, though we are holding back on full transparency pending a compromise with industry over IP protection. The carve-out for startups below €500M valuation, conditional on third-party sponsorship, has been accepted by the EP, easing concerns about innovation flight.

The export control measure has cleared its final legal hurdle under the Dual-Use Regulation. ASML, Infineon, and STMicroelectronics have co-designed the technical thresholds, and we have filed the implementing act. While U.S. cooperation remains limited, the unilateral authority is now in place. The immediate effect is a freeze on openweight_capability at 46.5 — just below the unsupervised agent threshold — but the backlash from German industry and French tech leaders persists. We are now in damage control: offering compliance support and reciprocal access pathways to maintain cohesion. The real test comes next turn, when we must convert legal authority into physical capacity — and that depends entirely on whether we can get shovels in the ground.
