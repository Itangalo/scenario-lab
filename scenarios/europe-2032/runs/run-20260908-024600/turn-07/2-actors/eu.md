## In practice  
This turn, we are finalising the transition from investment to delivery. The InvestAI Gigafactories programme concludes with the disbursement of its final tranche, but no new sovereign compute has come online — the bottleneck is no longer money, but land, grid, and political will. We are now leaning fully on the EAIA to enforce the Grid-Ready Compute Corridors, deploying its Fast-Track Mandate authority to override regional objections in Poland and Finland under Article 114 TFEU, treating these sites as strategic infrastructure. The first €40bn of corridor funding is tied to binding commitments: grid operators in Belgium and Poland must present signed upgrade plans by Q4, or lose access. Security certification by ENISA, including weight custody and training run logging, is now a condition of disbursement — no jurisdiction, no euros.

We are also acting on the emergent ASML investment push, converting quiet momentum into a formal initiative. The Secure Sovereign Lithography Initiative launches with €30bn in initial funding from the European Defence Fund, the InvestEU programme, and national contributions from the Netherlands and Germany. The European Semiconductor Sovereignty Consortium will be hosted in Eindhoven, with Fraunhofer, CEA-Leti, and IMEC as core partners, and co-development pathways opened to South Korea and Taiwan under the mid-power framework. The U.S. has signalled tolerance, but we are framing this as resilience, not rivalry — a move to stabilise supply, not to weaponise it. This is not a moonshot; it is a survival shot.

Meanwhile, the labour crisis deepens. We are holding emergency talks with the ETUC and professional associations in law and accounting, offering a pilot for a **Transitional AI Adjustment Fund** — not yet a measure, but a signal. It would pair wage insurance for displaced juniors with retraining in AI-augmented roles and incentives for firms to redeploy, not just replace. Public sentiment remains fragile, and we cannot afford to let innovation and stability become opposing poles. But we cannot pause — only pivot.
