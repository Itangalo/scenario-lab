### The Frontier Presses Forward

AI capability advances to 56.5 as leading labs achieve tighter integration between planning and execution modules, enabling more persistent agent behaviour. ### Resilience Gains from Completed Surge

The Emergency Resilience Surge concludes, with air-gapped systems operational in three major grid operators and red-teaming protocols now embedded under ENISA’s authority. Though delayed in Poland and Spain, the partial rollout delivers tangible hardening, lifting resilience to 45.0. The new standing unit begins quarterly reporting and integrates national CISOs, marking a shift from crisis response to sustained coordination.

### Sovereignty Stagnates Amid Political Friction

The European AI Infrastructure Agency (EAIA) proposal advances under Article 114 TFEU, but Germany and France insist on retaining national veto power over site selection. The compromise—binding standards and cross-border pooling, but national control over location—passes legal scrutiny, yet delays full implementation. With no new sovereign compute online and capability rising, eu_ai_sovereignty slips to 20.0.

### A New Push for Safety, But No Immediate Gain

The EU launches its Mandatory Pre-Release Red Teaming initiative, framing it as a market access condition under the AI Act. Developers express concern, particularly U.S.-based firms, but the measure is still in flight—no safety improvements land yet. ai_safety remains stagnant at 29.0, reflecting the lag between policy and enforcement.
