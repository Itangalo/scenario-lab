## In practice  
We are acting on two fronts: one industrial, one existential. On the industrial front, the InvestAI Gigafactories and Tech Sovereignty Package are moving through the European Parliament under the Digital Agenda legal base, with the Council’s Competitiveness formation leading negotiations. Member states are divided — Germany and France push for national champions, while the Nordics and Benelux demand open access and cross-border interconnection. We are offering a compromise: gigafactory sites will be co-invested with national governments, but legal anchoring and compute allocation will be under EU control via a new European AI Infrastructure Agency, modelled on ENISA but with enforcement powers. The Commission is fast-tracking the agency’s mandate under Article 114 TFEU, but resistance remains from capitals fearing loss of autonomy.

On the existential front, the cyber_test_shot changes everything. The intrusion was not disruptive, but its stealth and coordination — across continents, sectors, and jurisdictions — reveal a new class of AI-driven threat that bypasses current detection. With openweight_frontier_release now placing offensive cyber tools in distributed hands, and the Taiwan blockade cutting off future compute upgrades, we cannot wait for long-term sovereignty to deliver safety. The Emergency Resilience Surge is being launched under the NIS2 Directive, with binding requirements for energy transmission operators, water utilities, and critical port authorities to implement AI-augmented intrusion detection, air-gapped control systems, and cross-border incident response protocols by Q1 2027. We are deploying €12bn from the Recovery and Resilience Facility, redirected via an urgent amendment, and activating the Civil Protection Mechanism to coordinate red-team exercises across the affected sectors. The Council’s Internal Market and Environment formations are resisting the cost and scope, but the recent near-miss has shifted sentiment — we are offering co-design of the protocols to secure buy-in, and front-loading funding to ease the burden. This is not a permanent fix, but it is the only shield we can raise before the next strike lands.
