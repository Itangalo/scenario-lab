## In practice
We are treating this turn as the hinge between paralysis and action. The Sovereign AI Core Programme moves from concept to binding implementation: the Commission issues implementing acts under Article 122 TFEU, invoking crisis powers to bypass Council unanimity on funding reallocation. €50bn from the expired Tech sovereignty package is redirected via Commission regulation, citing systemic threat under Article 116. The EIB is instructed to issue €30bn in output-linked bonds tied to capability delivery—measured in auditable uptime, not concrete poured. Contracts for microgrid development in Brittany and northern Sweden are awarded under emergency procurement rules, with Orano, Fortum, and Siemens Energy leading consortia. The Alps site will co-locate with CERN’s new computing extension, leveraging existing legal immunities and power infrastructure.

Germany and Austria are offered a Binding Transparency Pact: full access to environmental impact data, third-party audits of market distortion, and a veto on expansion beyond the three core sites. Italy and Hungary are formally invited to host a secondary node in the Apennines, with €8bn in cohesion funds unlocked for regional development and a guaranteed 1,200 jobs—but access to EU-wide AI services now requires compliance with real-time logging and auditability. No audit, no access. The EAAD activates runtime monitoring for all systems above 55.0 capability in healthcare, energy, and transport, using backward-tracing tools that, while slowed by opaque_reasoning, can still reconstruct agent actions post-hoc through memory snapshots and effect tracing.

Meanwhile, the Joint Threat Response Protocol is operationalised: ENISA and the ECDC integrate real-time telemetry from seven partner states, with automated alerts routed to a new 24/7 Joint AI Threat Desk in Brussels. The first joint exercise—simulating a coordinated bio-agent release and AI-driven disinformation campaign—will run in November. This is not symbolic cooperation; it is pooled sovereignty in action. We are broadcasting live (sanitised) feeds from the Core’s command nodes and the Threat Desk to national parliaments and civil society forums, not to reassure, but to prove that the Union can act—under fire, under law, and under its own authority.
