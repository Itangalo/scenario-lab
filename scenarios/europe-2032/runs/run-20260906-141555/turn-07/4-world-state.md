### The Hinge That Creaked

The EU attempted to force the hinge of action open — and it moved, slightly, under strain.

**Sovereign Core: Crisis Powers Tested**  
The Commission invoked Article 122 TFEU to redirect €50bn and launch construction in Brittany and northern Sweden. Emergency procurement bypassed normal tenders, but Germany and Austria filed an urgent reference with the CJEU, calling the move unconstitutional. Work began on paper, but ground-breaking is frozen pending legal review. Orano and Fortum mobilised teams but await clearance. The Alps site remains entangled in CERN coordination delays. Sovereignty gains were blocked; instead, capability growth outpaced control, and legal resistance intensified the perception of overreach.

**Joint Threat Response: First Real Step**  
ENISA and ECDC integrated real-time telemetry from seven partners. The Joint AI Threat Desk went live, and a November exercise was scheduled. This tangible step in shared defence boosted resilience despite opaque reasoning slowing post-incident tracing. A small but meaningful gain landed.

**Political Capital: Costly Momentum**  
Three large measures remain in flight, including the newly activated Core Programme (−3). The priority added −1. Total portfolio charge: −10. However, the *completion* of InvestAI Gigafactories delivered +4, and the new Joint Threat Response Protocol earned a proposal bonus (+3) for answering the recent cyber incident directly. Net change: +7 −10 = −3 → capital rose from 15.0 to 17.0.

**Public Sentiment: Faint Signal**  
Visible movement on resilience and the threat desk marginally improved public mood (+2), though job losses in tech and bio sectors kept sentiment low.

**Safety Erosion**  
With `opaque_reasoning` confirmed this turn, interpretability-based oversight collapsed. No new safety measure compensated. AI Safety dropped sharply (−5).

**Capability Advance**  
AI capability rose +1.5, driven by self-improving logistics agents and bio-design tools nearing experimental validation.
