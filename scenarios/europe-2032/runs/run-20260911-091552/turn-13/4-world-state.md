### Holding on
The second half of 2032 did not bring a second week like January, but it brought the bill for the first one.

Hospitals and utilities stayed on manual workarounds through autumn. Appointment backlogs cleared slowly where emergency patching teams had landed, and not at all where small municipalities waited for contractors. Pooled sequencing flagged the engineered features in the respiratory agent early, and stockpiled protective equipment kept surge hubs open, but night shifts thinned and local papers ran lists of postponed operations.

A new defensive toolkit helped: automated patching and swarm detection stopped a repeat of the winter ransomware sweep in several regions, and officials pointed to it as proof containment was working. The relief was partial. Attribution for the winter attacks never arrived, leaked test results from an unreleased frontier system revived arguments about unexplainable behaviour, and a contested paper claiming a genome model could guide human-infecting design kept biosecurity staff on edge.

Then two economic blows landed together. Entry-level hiring freezes in law, accountancy, software and customer operations turned into confirmed layoffs, hitting graduates hardest. Almost simultaneously, venture valuations reset, announced data-centre expansions were cancelled, and two compute arrangements European planners had counted on evaporated.

In the middle of this, the leading American model cut off European users at short notice. Ministries, clinics and firms that had built workflows on it found error messages with no appeal. Brussels protested, offered incident aid to the capital with its separate supply deal, and got kits taken without political return. A tailored therapy breakthrough that should have lifted the mood instead underlined dependence: doctors praised the treatment while administrators noted it ran on infrastructure Europe could not command.

By December, wards and grids still held, but on overtime and without margin. Vigils hardened into permanent encampments outside stalled sites.
