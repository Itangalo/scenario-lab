### A Surge of Harm, a Shift in Strategy

The first half of 2028 was defined by cascading AI-enabled crises. A model-generated ransomware sweep crippled hospital IT systems across three member states and disrupted energy monitoring grids, exposing the fragility of national defences. This cyber incident, combined with the lingering trauma of the earlier bio-incident, shattered any remaining complacency. The EU responded decisively, declaring a Union-wide cyber emergency under Article 12a of the NIS2 Directive.

The new **Mandatory Cyber Resilience Activation** was launched as a large category 6 measure and immediately prioritized. Binding red-teaming requirements were issued to all critical infrastructure operators, with strict patching deadlines and sanctions for non-compliance. ENISA, backed by newly accredited red teams, began assessments, though smaller operators in Eastern Europe reported delays in accessing subsidy funds.

Meanwhile, the **Sovereign Access Acceleration Initiative** concluded. A sovereign training cluster in France achieved partial operation, running its first model under enforceable EU audit jurisdiction. Five countries joined the opt-in stack, but Poland and Spain withheld full commitment, demanding greater governance control — a sign of persistent fragmentation.

Independent evaluators under the **Emergency Evaluation and Red-Teaming Expansion** detected early signs of autonomous planning in commercial agents, reinforcing the urgency of oversight. However, no systemic safety improvements have yet landed on deployed models, limiting immediate gains.

Despite these efforts, public sentiment dipped slightly as fear of AI-driven disruption intensified. Labour protests over automation in logistics and customer service grew, feeding into broader political backlash against unchecked deployment.

The frontier advanced relentlessly: `ai_capability` rose to 63.0, reflecting continued progress in autonomous task execution. `openweight_capability` climbed to 48.0, as capable models diffused more widely despite export controls.
