## Changes this turn

- **modify** `two_year_commitment` — rejected-structural
  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: Prevent uncontrolled AI systems from determining the EU’s future by securing sovereign control over critical AI infrastructure and ensuring societal resilience against runaway automation and strategic dependency
  - Grounds: The crossing of the supervision threshold — confirmed by the Swiss lab incident and the global drop in ai_safety to 9.0 — has rendered the previous formulation insufficient. Sovereign access is no longer about capability parity alone; it is now a condition for maintaining any agency at all. The commitment must reflect that we are no longer preparing for risk, but responding to an active erosion of human oversight at the frontier. Labour displacement and physical backlash further show that resilience must now include social absorption, not just cyber or institutional continuity.
  - Reason: a commitment-tier change must name a Trigger

  - Tier: commitment
  - Text: We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late — but we will no longer act without a coherent theory of control
  - Grounds: The Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation has exposed the limits of emergency assertion without legitimacy or operational unity. From now on, every commitment must include a plausible path to control, verification, and public buy-in, even if partial. This is not retreat, but recalibration: agency requires not just speed, but structure.
