- modify `rebuild_public_legitimacy` (commitment): Restore the EU’s authority to act in AI by visibly re-establishing public trust through enforceable accountability, redress, and inclusive governance in public-sector AI  
- Grounds: The cancellation of the Public Redress and Co-Design Framework has not been read as realism but as retreat. Public sentiment has cratered to 15.0, with protests now targeting data centres and AI-enabled public services. No measure currently in flight delivers visible benefit to citizens. The legitimacy deficit is no longer political but existential: without public tolerance, even defensive sovereignty becomes ungovernable. We must now shift from preserving capability in isolation to re-linking it to public purpose—starting not with grand promises, but with survival.

- reclassify `defend_sovereign_capacity` to identity  
- Trigger: The emergent Industrial Sovereignty Consortium now represents the only functioning node of EU-aligned AI development. Its survival is no longer a policy option but a precondition for any future action.  
- Grounds: With `eu_ai_sovereignty` at 9.0 and external control tightening, the consortium is not a supplement to sovereignty—it *is* sovereignty. Its members operate under shared safeguards, pooled compute, and EU-anchored testbeds. If this network collapses, there will be no domestic base left to defend. We are no longer choosing to defend sovereign capacity—we are defined by it.

- modify `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong sometimes as the price of not being late.  
- Grounds: Our last commitment to act under uncertainty led us to anchor the consortium—but that decision now feels less like initiative and more like survival. The world is moving faster than our instruments can track: `ai_capability` at 70.0 with `ai_safety` at 11.0 means systems are already outpacing human oversight. We must now refine what "acting" means: not just moving early, but moving *coherently*, even when the full path is invisible. The price of being wrong must now include the cost of fragmentation.

## New measure

Establish a time-limited, EU-coordinated transition mechanism for workers displaced by AI in professional services, combining wage insurance, accelerated retraining in resilient sectors, and civic engagement roles in AI oversight and public redress.

**Priority:** Launch Emergency Labour Absorption and Civic Stabilisation Programme  
We are pushing this hardest because the collapse in public sentiment to 15.0 has frozen our political capital at 0.0—without public tolerance, even the most urgent sovereignty measures become impossible to sustain, and the only path back to agency runs through stabilising the social ground beneath us.

## In practice

This turn, we are acting from the edge of institutional freefall. The Council has no bandwidth for long-term bets, the Commission no credibility to spend, and the public sees AI not as infrastructure but as assault. We cannot rebuild trust through declarations—we must *do* something that lands. The Emergency Labour Absorption and Civic Stabilisation Programme is not a return to idealism. It is triage. We are activating the European Social Fund’s crisis clause, repurposing unspent cohesion funds, and working with member states to fast-track wage insurance for professionals displaced in law, accountancy, and administration—roles now vanishing under AI-driven automation. The retraining track focuses on cyber monitoring, biosecurity response, and AI audit support: skills that feed back into resilience and oversight. Crucially, we are also creating 10,000 civic roles in public-sector AI redress panels—citizen stewards who review denials, flag bias, and report systemic failures. This is not symbolic inclusion. It is a bid to re-anchor AI governance in lived experience, to show that the Union still serves people, not just systems. We are offering national governments co-funding and flexibility—anything to get this moving before the next wave of job losses hits. If we do not act now, there will be no public left to govern.
