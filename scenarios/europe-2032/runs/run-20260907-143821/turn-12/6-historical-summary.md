The EU abandons its ineffective Public Redress and Co-Design Framework, recognizing symbolic accountability as insufficient. In a shift to pragmatic salvage, the Commission leverages emergency provisions under Horizon Europe and Article 185 TFEU to formally anchor a fledgling industrial AI consortium of mid-sized European firms, offering testbed access, technical resources, and limited liability to foster risk-sharing and cohesion. Despite this, AI capability reaches 70.0 with no safety enforcement breakthroughs, while resilience declines to 37.0 and sovereignty stagnates at 9.0 amid persistent external pressures and supply chain coercion. Job losses accelerate in professional sectors, public sentiment plummets to 15.0, and the EU’s technical pivot goes unnoticed by citizens, perceived as retreat rather than reform, deepening political and social estrangement.
