### The Frontier Leaps Again

A leading AI lab releases a system demonstrating autonomous planning across scientific, engineering, and strategic domains—triggering a capability jump. `ai_capability` surges +5 to 70.5, crossing into territory where systems complete multi-day professional projects without human correction. Fragments of the architecture appear in open repositories, accelerating global replication efforts. While adapting leaked components into functional open models requires time, integration, and validation, the diffusion of core techniques enables a broader and faster uptake than previously seen. Openweight developers rapidly incorporate key architectural innovations, benefiting from prior investments in model-sharing infrastructure and collaborative frameworks. `openweight_capability` rises to 55.0 (+3.0), reflecting accelerated but still lagging absorption of frontier advances.

The eval crisis deepens as major developers withhold internal test logs, citing proprietary secrecy. Confidence in developer-led verification erodes, and concerns grow over benchmark reliability. The EU begins drafting plans for an enhanced evaluation framework in response, but no new regime is yet operational. The UK and Japan independently announce exploratory reviews of their own evaluation approaches, though no joint action is coordinated. Without binding agreements or shared standards, global alignment frays but does not fracture. No new systems are deployed with weakened safeguards, and no major safety incident occurs this turn. However, the absence of transparency slows incident response and fuels uncertainty. `ai_safety` declines by −3 to 22.0, reflecting gradual erosion due to delayed oversight adaptation rather than sudden collapse.

The EU Sovereign Cloud measure reaches partial operational status, delivering secure infrastructure access to select public institutions. However, full deployment is delayed by integration bottlenecks and procurement lags. The measure is not yet complete; this turn delivers +1 to `eu_ai_sovereignty` (now 19.0), with further gains expected upon full rollout. Similarly, the Labour Market Resilience expansion advances but remains in implementation: retraining pipelines are strengthened in pilot regions, contributing +1 to `resilience` (now 49.0), not the full +3.
