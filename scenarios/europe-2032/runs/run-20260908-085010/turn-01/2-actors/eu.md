## New measure
### Emergency Resilience Surge for Critical Infrastructure  
A six-month programme to harden electricity, water and port operators against AI-driven cyber intrusion, using newly available interpretability tools and threat intelligence from the cyber_test_shot.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 2`  
`Targeted effect:` resilience +15, ai_safety +5 (indirectly via faster detection and containment)  
`Applies to:` EU member states with critical infrastructure exposed in cyber_test_shot

## Priority  
**Emergency Resilience Surge for Critical Infrastructure** — because the cyber_test_shot proves that detection failed and the window between compromise and catastrophe is closing; this must move faster than normal cycles allow, even at cost to longer-term industrial plans.

## In practice  
We are activating Article 12 of the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) to mandate immediate cyber hardening across designated operators of essential services, starting with energy transmission, water utilities and major ports. The ENISA rapid response unit is being redeployed to lead a task force with national CERTs, using classified insights from the autumn intrusion to simulate and patch known attack vectors. We are fast-tracking procurement under the Crisis Response Protocol to deploy new monitoring stacks incorporating the safety_breakthrough in agent behaviour prediction — specifically, the activation-heatmap correlation method now adopted by Anthropic and DeepMind — into live environments within 90 days.

At the same time, we are pressing the Council’s Competitiveness formation to unlock emergency funding from the Digital Europe Programme and the Recovery and Resilience Facility, repurposing €8.3bn in unused allocations. Resistance is expected from member states citing cost and sovereignty, particularly in Central Europe, where some operators still treat AI risk as theoretical. To overcome this, we are offering full EU co-financing and liability shielding for any incident discovered during the transition period, conditioned only on compliance with the new baseline standards by end-Q1 2027. The Commission will chair weekly coordination calls with CISOs of affected operators, making anonymized threat telemetry available in near real-time — a first for cross-border operational security sharing. This is not about building capacity for the future; it is about preventing collapse in the present.
