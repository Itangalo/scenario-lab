### A Surge Against the Clock

The EU launches the Emergency Resilience Surge in response to the global cyber intrusion, invoking DORA to mandate rapid defences in energy, water, and port systems. ENISA coordinates with national cyber units, deploying new monitoring stacks based on a recent breakthrough in predicting AI agent behaviour. The €8.3bn in repurposed funding enables full co-financing, easing resistance — but not eliminating it. Hungary and Poland initially refuse participation, citing national sovereignty and questioning the threat’s immediacy. After tense negotiations, both agree to partial compliance, delaying full rollout.

Despite urgency, implementation is uneven. Some operators lack technical capacity to integrate the new tools; others resist due to legacy systems. Still, by December, 60% of targeted entities have deployed enhanced detection, and early simulations show improved response times. The measure lands with partial effect: resilience rises, but not to the intended scale.

### Capability Presses On

Global AI capability advances steadily to 54.5, driven by compressed release cycles and continuous agent training. The open frontier lags, with openweight capability rising only to 41.0 — developers now restrict releases more tightly after Kimi K3’s fallout, though underground redistribution grows.

The safety_breakthrough — a method to predict agent actions via activation heatmaps — is rapidly adopted by leading labs and now integrated into EU defences. This lifts ai_safety to 37.0, a rare gain amid rising risk.

### Sovereignty Erodes, Capital Tightens

The EU’s compute ambitions continue, but no immediate gains in sovereignty offset the cost of action. With ai_capability rising by 2.5, eu_ai_sovereignty falls by 1. No category 4 measure finishes this turn, and no external event secures or denies access — so the net change is −1.

Political capital is strained. Three measures are in flight (two large, one small), costing −8, plus −1 for the priority. The Resilience Surge earns a +3 proposal bonus for directly answering the cyber_test_shot. No sentiment boost applies. Final capital: 48 − 8 − 1 + 3 = 42.

Public sentiment holds steady — no major incident backlash, but no visible benefit yet to lift approval.
