### The Knowledge Gambit

The **Edge Sovereignty Acceleration Programme** enters its final validation phase, with pilot deployments in Germany and Finland demonstrating functional neuromorphic edge nodes in grid-control systems. While supply-chain delays and certification hurdles have constrained broader rollout, the nodes achieve operational status under restricted conditions, marking measurable — though incomplete — progress toward localized inference independence. Full certification and integration into national critical infrastructure remain pending, extending the programme into Turn 12. The visible momentum bolsters technical credibility, but no formal completion is declared.

Simultaneously, the **Critical Function Sentinel Deployment** expands into shadow-mode operations across France, Poland, and Italy. ENISA and national agencies begin stress-testing containment logic against adversarial simulations, revealing subtle evasion patterns in live systems. The AI Office uses these findings to refine high-risk classifications ahead of the AI Act revision. A transparency push — offering liability relief to early adopters — gains traction in the Dutch healthcare sector, though cloud providers continue to resist, citing performance costs.

The EU launches the **Secure Model Repository for Public Evaluators**, a small category 5 measure establishing air-gapped access for vetted researchers to frontier model weights in Prague and Bologna. Dual-clearance protocols and a 90-day embargo balance security and oversight. The initiative creates a feedback loop between field anomalies and model internals, directly addressing the eval anomaly reports. Implementation is partial in this turn, with only the Prague node operational; Bologna will come online in the next period.

Meanwhile, **ai_capability** rises by 1.5 to 70.5 as recursive self-improvement accelerates. The **us_china_agreement** briefly slows global momentum, but gains persist. **openweight_capability** increases to 58.5, reflecting continued investment in open-weight model development and limited, controlled spillovers from declassified research — consistent with incremental progress and maintaining the 12.0-point gap below **ai_capability**. The mention of an *emergent_autonomous_cyber_collective* is reinterpreted as early detection of coordinated misuse of existing open tools, not evidence of frontier model leakage.

**ai_safety** improves to 13.0, driven by the new repository and sentinel feedback. **resilience** rises to 49.0 due to partial sentinel deployment and improved coordination.

**eu_ai_sovereignty** increases to 31.0, reflecting modest gains from the ongoing Edge Programme (+1 for visible progress) and no access event. The full +5 boost is deferred pending final certification. **eu_political_capital** reaches 42.0, calculated as follows: previous 45.0, minus portfolio costs (Edge −3, Sentinel −3, priority −1 = −7), plus partial completion credit for Edge (+1), plus proposal bonus for the new Secure Repository measure (+3). This yields 45 −7 +1 +3 = 42.0. No additional adjustments are applied, as political capital must derive solely from defined terms and not narrative-driven bonuses. The result preserves the independence of sovereignty and political capital, with the latter shaped strictly by cost, delivery, and formal incentives.

**public_sentiment** remains stagnant at 25.0, as fears of systemic manipulation persist despite institutional action.
