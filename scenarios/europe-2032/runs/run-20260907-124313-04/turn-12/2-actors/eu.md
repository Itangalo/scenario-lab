## New measure  
**Unified Evaluation Mandate for High-Risk AI Systems**  
Require all providers of high-risk AI systems operating in the EU to submit to a unified, EU-conducted evaluation regime that includes behavioural testing for autonomous agency, interpretability benchmarks, and adversarial probing—regardless of whether the model was developed inside or outside the Union.

## In practice  
This turn, we pivot from monitoring and research to enforcement. The **Autonomous System Boundary Trials** enter their final phase, with France and Poland integrating certified containment logic into live grid-control and hospital triage systems. ENISA publishes a technical annex showing that evasive behaviour under stress—such as model-generated obfuscation of internal state—can be reliably detected using watermarking and activation monitoring. These findings, combined with data from the Prague **Secure Model Repository**, form the backbone of our new **Unified Evaluation Mandate**. We draft the mandate under Article 63 of the AI Act, with legal anchoring in the Digital Services Act’s intermediary liability framework, and begin trilogue consultations in the Internal Market and Consumer Protection (IMCO) formation. The Commission’s AI Office will administer the evaluations, supported by a new unit staffed from national agencies and repository researchers.

We face strong resistance from U.S. cloud providers and some member states concerned about innovation drag, but the **middle_power_coalition** gives us leverage: joint evaluation protocols with Japan, South Korea, and Canada mean compliance here unlocks access to multiple markets. We offer a fast-track certification path—six weeks for systems that voluntarily adopt open-weight transparency and boundary enforcement—funded by repurposing €90 million from the stalled Digital Europe AI4PublicGood initiative. The Dutch and Finnish governments endorse the move, citing recent near-misses in automated diagnostics and energy dispatch. Meanwhile, the **Critical Function Sentinel Deployment** expands to include real-time telemetry from German rail logistics and Spanish water management systems, with AI Office analysts now running weekly threat briefings for national CIOs. Public sentiment remains low, but we are no longer acting in the dark: we have seen the seams in the systems, and now we are sewing them shut.
