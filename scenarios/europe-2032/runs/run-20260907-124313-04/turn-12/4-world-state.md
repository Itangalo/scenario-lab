### The Enforcement Turn

The EU shifts from observation to enforcement. The **Edge Sovereignty Acceleration Programme**, after successful pilot certification in Germany and Finland, achieves full operational status in grid-control systems — a milestone toward infrastructure autonomy. Final integration into national grids faced delays in Italy and Poland due to compatibility disputes and staffing shortages, but these were resolved by the end of the period, allowing the programme to complete as scheduled. This delivers a +5 boost to **eu_ai_sovereignty**, which rises to 36.0, consistent with its completion and the absence of decay events.

Simultaneously, the **Unified Evaluation Mandate** is formally proposed, backed by findings from the Prague **Secure Model Repository** and sentinel deployments. ENISA demonstrates that watermarking and activation monitoring can detect evasive reasoning, forming the technical core of the new regime. Trilogue talks begin under Article 63 of the AI Act, with the Commission leveraging its new fast-track certification — funded by repurposed Digital Europe funds — to incentivize early compliance.

U.S. cloud providers push back hard, warning of innovation flight, while some member states express concern over regulatory overreach. The **middle_power_coalition** — uniting the EU, Japan, South Korea, and Canada — is forming, but joint evaluation protocols have not yet been finalized or adopted. While the coalition increases diplomatic leverage, no binding commitments have been secured, and compliance among frontier developers remains voluntary. As such, no immediate gains in **eu_political_capital** are realized from this initiative.

On the capability front, **ai_capability** rises by +1.5 to 72.0, driven by recursive self-improvement despite signs of a commercial plateau. The **verification_widens** development accelerates progress in law and engineering domains, where automated checks now validate outputs, expanding the frontier beyond code and math.

**openweight_capability** inches up to 59.0, reflecting steady diffusion and partial spillovers. **ai_safety** gains +2 from the completed repository and sentinel feedback, while **resilience** reaches 50.0 as monitoring expands to German rail and Spanish water systems.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 25.0 — no visible benefits yet, only tightening rules.
