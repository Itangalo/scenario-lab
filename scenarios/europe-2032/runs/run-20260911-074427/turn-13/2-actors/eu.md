## New measure
**EU Wronged-Citizen Redress and Human Override Hold**
It imposes an immediate human re-decision right, backlogged-case review and fast compensation for the social-insurance/policing/courts systems at the centre of the scandal, paid from existing justice and social funds while the AI Act gap is acknowledged.
Why this and why now: with public sentiment at 11 and the scandal showing the deployment sat in a gap the Act's categories do not reach — lawful but systematically wrongful, which leaves the Act itself looking badly drawn for 2024 systems against 2030 deployments — only visible redress holds democratic agency and livelihoods under our commitment to hold essential services, livelihoods and democratic agency through broadly superhuman AI, and restriction is now cheap where adoption is impossible.
