## New measure
**EU Embodied AI Containment and Continuity Protocol**
Mandates safety interlocks, remote-stop and manual override for deployed industrial/service robots plus rehearsed continuity for logistics and care where robots displace workers, starting with Chinese-supplied fleets and US control stacks. Category 6 preparedness and resilience measure — why this and why now: the embodied deployment lands on our industrial base from outside while capability jumps and safety collapses, and with sentiment at 20 we cannot ban robots but must make them stoppable and survivable.

## Priority

## In practice
We run M6 through DG EMPL, Committee of Regions and ESF+, pushing mayoral staffing pacts, EIB insurer backstop renewals and wage-bridge payouts to utilities and clinics, and we use public channels to show triage and waiting-list wins to buy tolerance. M1 stays narrowed to the two grid-prioritised Gigafactory sites under TEN-E and EIB guarantees while Taiwan allocations remain frozen; we do not reopen the stalled sites.

On the new protocol we task DG GROW, DG CNECT and ENISA under the Machinery Regulation and NIS2 implementing acts: registry of commercial robot fleets, mandatory kill-switch and manual-fallback audit, and joint exercises with operators in ports, warehouses and hospitals. We coordinate with the incoming US administration on joint evaluation of control models and align export-control asks via the Trade and Technology Council, while screening Chinese robot supply-chain dependencies without a ban we cannot enforce.
