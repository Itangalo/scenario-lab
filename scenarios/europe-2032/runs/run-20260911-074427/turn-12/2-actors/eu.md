## New measure
**EU Degraded-Service and Graduate Livelihood Hold**
It keeps hospitals, ports and energy operators on exercised manual-fallback playbooks and extends the finished placement corps wage-insurance on care-maintenance while freezing frontier automation approvals, using existing resilience and labour funds.
Why this and why now: with opaque models unreadable, entry-level hiring frozen and ASML coercion exposing our chokepoint, the only hold we can afford under our commitment to hold essential services and livelihoods through superhuman AI is degraded-not-stopped services plus visible jobs protection we do not have to build anew.

## Priority
M12 EU Degraded-Service and Graduate Livelihood Hold — because with no measures in flight and capital at 4 it is the only instrument that directly serves the two-year commitment to hold essential services and livelihoods.

## In practice
We act by Council conclusions and Commission implementing guidance, not new law: ENISA and ECDC/HERA keep the joint telemetry and sample feeds flowing to hospital and port operators, with monthly fallback exercises paid from the existing civil protection and EU4Health envelopes. DG EMPL extends ESF+ clearance for the few thousand Corps placements on care-and-maintenance while EPSCO holds the freeze on new public-sector automation approvals until redress logging lands.

We name dependence to contain it: US therapies stay limited to Lyon, Porto, Gdansk secured capacity, ASML servicing cuts are handled in the Trade Council via the Anti-Coercion Instrument consultation without a new fund, and blockade surcharges stay under existing continuity coordination — vendors angered by the freeze are offered places in the fallback exercises rather than new approvals.
