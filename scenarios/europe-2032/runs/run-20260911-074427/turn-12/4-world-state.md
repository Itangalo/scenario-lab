### The hold
Brussels could afford continuity, not construction. Council conclusions kept the joint telemetry pool flowing through ENISA and the health-threat centre to hospitals and ports, with monthly manual-fallback drills paid from civil protection and health envelopes. The few thousand care placement contracts were extended on existing social funds, and the freeze on new public-sector automation stayed until redress logging arrived.

It kept lights on, barely. When spring intrusions probed port logistics and a grid operator, fallback playbooks held services degraded-not-stopped. Clinicians still got early warnings. Mayors welcomed the freeze; vendors called it a hiring block by other means.

### Unreadable minds, missing jobs
Two shocks undercut the calm. Leading American labs acknowledged their newest systems no longer reasoned in readable words — oversight built on inspecting chains of thought stopped working at once. Brussels briefings turned bleak: evaluation now meant black-box tests and immature activation probes.

At home, graduate hiring stalled openly. Law, accountancy, software and customer-operations firms confirmed entry roles simply not replaced. University towns filled with protests; local news ran queues outside job centres. The wage-insurance extension covered thousands while tens of thousands waited.

### The chokepoint used by another hand
Washington then ordered deeper cuts to lithography exports and servicing — not only leading-edge machines but older tools, and to a wider customer list. The Dutch government complied; the company warned refusal would risk its American supply chain.

For the Union it was humiliation made material. Its one real bottleneck had been spent, and not by it. Trade ministers invoked consultation procedures but offered no fund, no alternative. Near Lyon, Porto and Gdansk, secured capacity rationed American therapies while elsewhere waitlists grew. A brief outage and drone sightings at a fenced site fed sabotage talk police could not dispel, and insurers raised strait surcharges again.
