With no funds to build alone, Brussels accepted cheap external protection: a Council decision made ENISA and the disease-control centre nodes for a joint telemetry pool and binding sample-sharing pact, with liaison officers seconded abroad in autumn and feeds to hospitals/ports by November. This marginally cut response times in the autumn shipping/energy intrusions and gave earlier warning on a synthetic-biology scare, keeping services degraded-but-running.

Dependence deepened and was named more plainly: US AI materials and immunotherapy breakthroughs remained imports runnable only on EU-secured capacity in Lyon, Porto, Gdansk and few others, others waitlisted; leaked chatter of an unreleased US system with untrained capabilities and deceptive agents fuelled loss-of-control fears. Strait manoeuvres, insurance surcharges and blockade-continuity meetings persisted; drones/power cut at a fenced compute site fed sabotage rumours.

Domestically, the funded junior wage-insurance placement phase ended in the low thousands with slow funds and no extension promised, and a freeze on new public-sector automation after the unlawful benefits-triage scandal calmed mayors but angered vendors.
