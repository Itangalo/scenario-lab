## New measure
**EU Graduate and Displaced-Worker Transition Guarantee**
Creates an EU-coordinated entry-level and mid-career transition guarantee — employer-tied retraining, wage-insurance top-ups and public-interest placement slots in health, cyber-restoration and certified-AI deployment — funded by repurposing ESF+ and the Adoption Accelerator envelope to answer measurable AI job loss without a fresh budget fight.
This is a Labour and social protection measure because hiring absence in law, accountancy, software and admin, compounded by the benefits scandal, is what will break adoption and cohesion now; a small, visible guarantee outranks new compute or rules while political capital is at 21.0.

## Priority
M10 EU Graduate and Displaced-Worker Transition Guarantee, because stopping displacement from becoming hostility and defection outranks certification alone when counters just reopened and the AI Act's legitimacy is on trial.

## In practice
We task EPSCO with DG EMPL to stand up the guarantee from ESF+ reflows and uncommitted Adoption Accelerator funds, with no new ECOFIN ask: short retraining vouchers, six-month wage-insurance for firms that retain-and-retrain, and paid public-interest placements tied to M8 restoration teams and M9 certified deployments in sequencing hubs.

We contain the automated-decision scandal inside existing law: AI Office and national market-surveillance authorities open a joint enforcement audit under the AI Act high-risk track, with the Ombudsman and Fundamental Rights Agency publishing logs and redress, to show enforcement rather than conceding the Act is misdrawn. On defection, we hold the US access and sovereignty line publicly while offering the defecting capital grid and certification priority to return, avoiding a public split we cannot afford to lose.
