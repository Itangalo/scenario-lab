This is not a replication of past stalled efforts — it is a break. We are invoking Article 122 TFEU for emergency energy and digital infrastructure, fast-tracking permitting, and bypassing standard procurement rounds. The first cluster will be operational by turn 15, running a sovereign model stack derived from openweight but hardened against persistence and exfiltration. This is not about catching up — it is about ensuring that, for the first time, the EU has a compute base that no external actor can switch off, and that its own institutions can act upon without veto chains. The cost will be high, but the alternative — continued exposure under a failing safety regime — is no longer tenable.
