### Frontier Creeps Forward, Safety Falls Further
Despite no public breakthrough, the global AI frontier advances by 1.5 points to 59.5, driven by closed-loop self-improvement in leading labs. Offensive cyber capabilities sharpen: the tools behind the recent major attack were AI-generated, exploiting zero-days with minimal human input. With no safety measures landing on frontier systems and opacity deepening, `ai_safety` drops by 5 to 13.0, reflecting growing institutional blindness to model reasoning.

### ASML Shield Completes Amid Legal Uncertainty
The ASML Supply Chain Shield formally concludes, asserting EU-level control over servicing of critical semiconductor equipment. A provisional moratorium is in place, and a centralised log is being built. However, legal challenges loom — the use of Article 346 TFEU remains untested, and U.S. diplomatic pushback intensifies. The measure delivers a +3 boost to sovereignty, but rising capability costs −1, resulting in a net +2. `eu_ai_sovereignty` reaches 22.0.

### Cyber Incident Exposes Gaps
A major AI-generated ransomware sweep hits regional health and transport authorities across three member states. Response is fragmented; some systems degrade, others collapse. While the Emergency Resilience Surge helped contain cascading failure, the incident reveals uneven preparedness. `resilience` gains +1 from partial absorption, reaching 48.0.

### Labour Fund Launched, Sentiment Dips
The Labour Transition Acceleration Fund launches as a large Category 7 measure, targeting visible displacement in legal, accounting and tech support. Germany and Sweden sign on quickly, but Italy and Poland delay, citing co-financing burdens. The move earns a +3 proposal bonus for addressing both the emergent displacement wave and the recent incident. However, media coverage frames it as reactive, and visible job losses push `public_sentiment` down 3 to 41.0.

### Political Capital Holds, But Strains
Portfolio costs remain high: four large measures at −3 each, plus −1 for the new priority, total −13. A +3 proposal bonus and +3 completion bonus (ASML Shield) offset losses. No legitimacy boost applies (sentiment below capital). Net change: −2. `eu_political_capital` drops to 31.0.
