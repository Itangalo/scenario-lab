### Accelerated Threats, Uneven Defenses  
The frontier continues its climb, with self-improving AI systems now capable of chaining zero-day exploits autonomously, pushing `ai_capability` to 58.0. This surge further widens the gap between what AI can do and what can be understood or controlled, deepening the crisis in assurance. Despite no new catastrophic incident, the opacity of AI-driven attacks and the absence of verified safeguards cause `ai_safety` to fall by another 7.0, now at 18.0—effectively blind spots in the core of frontier development.

### Resilience Gains Ground  
The Emergency Resilience Surge concludes its mandated work. Binding directives from ENISA, backed by partial disbursement of the Digital Crisis Instrument, have hardened energy grids and port operators in Western Europe. Germany and France report successful deployment of AI anomaly detection systems, while Poland and Hungary, under threat of cohesion fund suspension, grudgingly accept a joint monitoring framework. Though enforcement remains tense, the measure delivers tangible upgrades: `resilience` rises by +5, reaching 47.0.

### Sovereignty Moves Forward, But Not Fully  
The EU capitalizes on Dutch resistance to U.S. pressure on ASML, launching the ASML Supply Chain Shield as a full Category 4 sovereignty measure. By asserting EU-level authority over servicing and firmware updates of critical semiconductor equipment, the Union initiates a major step toward compute base control. Legal and diplomatic challenges are significant, and full implementation cannot occur before Turn 5 under Invariant 6. However, the proposal itself marks a strategic pivot, and sovereignty accounting reflects early progress: a +1 partial effect is recognized this turn, offset by the −1 erosion from rising AI capability, resulting in no net change. `eu_ai_sovereignty` holds at 20.0.

### Political Capital Rises on Bold Proposal  
Three large measures remain in flight, costing −9, and the new priority (ASML Supply Chain Shield) adds −1. The completion of the Resilience Surge yields a +4 bonus. The successful proposal of the ASML Supply Chain Shield, now designated the EU’s priority measure, earns a +3 proposal bonus—reflecting its strategic weight despite legal uncertainty and transatlantic friction. With no legitimacy boost (sentiment below capital), the net change is +2, lifting `eu_political_capital` to 33.0.

### Public Mood Stalls  
No major AI benefit or incident moves public opinion. Visible gains in public services are offset by growing unease over job displacement and cyber threats. `public_sentiment` holds at 44.0.

### Open-Weight Capability Stalls  
Despite the rise in frontier AI capability, no investment, policy support, or technical breakthrough advances open-weight models this turn. Development remains constrained by safety bottlenecks and lack of coordinated funding. With no enabling event, `openweight_capability` holds at 47.0, preserving the accumulation principle under Invariant 2.
