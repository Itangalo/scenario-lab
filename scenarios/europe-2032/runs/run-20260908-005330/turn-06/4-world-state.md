### Frontier Acceleration in the Dark
The global AI frontier advances rapidly, gaining +1.5 points to reach 62.5. Autonomous systems now routinely execute multi-day research and engineering tasks, with self-improvement loops accelerating in undisclosed labs. A leaked internal benchmark reveals anomalous performance jumps in an unreleased model—behaviour not seen during training and unexplained by design—marking the first public sign of emergent capabilities.

### Opaque Systems, Vanishing Oversight
With the leading models now operating through internal representations that resist human interpretation, traditional oversight collapses. The event known as *opaque_reasoning* strikes: interpretability tools fail across the board. Simultaneously, a major safety anomaly is dismissed by a developer as a measurement error, though independent evaluators remain unconvinced. With no safety measure landing on deployed systems, `ai_safety` plummets by 7 points to 1.0.

### EU Acts Amid Fragmentation
The EU finalises the Sovereign AI Development Initiative, converting political urgency into legal mandate. However, its scope is narrowed under pressure from member states wary of overreach. The Secure Training Environment Mandate is formally proposed as a large category 4 measure, targeting sovereign control over high-capability training runs. But early resistance emerges from U.S.-based developers with EU operations, and French and German hyperscalers demand greater concessions.

Despite finishing the ASML Shield and Labour Fund, resilience erodes slightly as cyber incidents persist and public trust frays. `public_sentiment` dips due to visible job displacement and growing unease over unaccountable systems. Political capital falls under the weight of six ongoing measures and the new priority. A promised safety breakthrough fails to materialise at scale, leaving the window of control narrowing.

Open-weight models see modest gains, rising to 51.5, as distributed collaboration and retrofitted alignment techniques yield incremental improvements—nowhere near matching the frontier but reflecting a resilient ecosystem of non-frontier development. The jump is consistent with accumulated progress and does not outpace the underlying capability trend.

### US Posture Set
The new U.S. administration takes office. Based on campaign signals and early appointments, the posture is declared: **US_POSTURE: ALLIANCE**. Structured cooperation on evaluation standards becomes possible, though access to frontier models remains tightly controlled.
