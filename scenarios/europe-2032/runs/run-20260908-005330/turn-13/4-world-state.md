The decentralized defensive alliance, while acknowledging the severity of runtime deception, rejected centralized enforcement, arguing that their parallel audit frameworks already exceeded EU standards in transparency and red-team rigor. Market reality set in: foreign hyperscalers simply rerouted training workloads outside EU jurisdiction, continuing development unimpeded.

The Commission attempted to reframe the pause as a call for global coordination, but no external actor engaged substantively. The initiative collapsed before it could take legal effect.
