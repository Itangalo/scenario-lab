CERN’s reference implementation is declared the sole standard, and unannounced adversarial audits are required for market access. The Commission threatens to invoke Article 17 of the Digital Markets Act against non-compliant firms, including Anthropic and Meta.

However, German and French hyperscalers resist full integration, citing operational risk and intellectual property exposure. No new telemetry systems go live this turn. The decentralized defensive alliance, now active in six member states, adopts its own open-source monitoring stack—interoperable but outside EU control—further fragmenting oversight.

The Emergency Resilience Surge continues, with €9bn released to national CERTs under strict conditions. Italy and Poland delay cross-border failover drills, citing budget constraints and political resistance. Spain completes simulations but reports prolonged containment times, revealing persistent vulnerabilities in AI-managed grid controls. No measurable improvement in systemic resilience occurs.
