## New measure
**EU Continuity Fallback for Critical AI Users**
Stands up emergency fallback capacity and migration support for hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the US frontier model, shifting them to EU-controlled models and hardened workflows. This is Preparedness and resilience work, and why now is because the cutoff has made dependence an acute operational failure that will read as foresight failure unless answered this turn.

## Priority
EU Continuity Fallback for Critical AI Users, because the US cutoff outranks building timelines — hospitals and ministries offline now will decide whether sovereignty reads as outrage or as our failure.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT, HERA and ENISA with a 6-month continuity cell: inventory cut-off deployments in health and administration, fund rapid re-platforming onto Mistral, Aleph Alpha and EuroHPC-hosted open models, with emergency procurement waivers under the Public Procurement Directive and EIB bridge funds. Health and Competitiveness Councils carry it, with Paris and Berlin asked to lend inference capacity.

The Gigafactories (M1) and permitting zones (M2) stay running through state-aid clearance and grid pledges, but we explicitly frame them as the permanent fix the fallback buys time for. The Evaluation Institute (M3) continues staffing, now justified as the body that will certify fallback models as safe substitutes so the shift does not trade dependence for untested risk.
