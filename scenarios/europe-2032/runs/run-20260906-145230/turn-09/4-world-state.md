### Sovereignty Through Constraint

The EU finalises the **Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield** directive, securing qualified majority support in the IMCO Council after last-minute concessions on liability and InvestAI access. ENISA rolls out Version 2 red-team scenarios featuring swarm tactics, and live simulations across rail and grid systems expose critical gaps in cross-border escalation protocols. The **Cross-Border AI Incident Response Network** is declared operational, with ten national CERTs now bound to its activation framework—a milestone, though coordination under stress remains untested.

Amid rising capability, the Commission launches the **Open-Weight Capability Threshold Regulation**, aiming to cap open releases at 52.0 to prevent autonomous agents from becoming persistent and uncontainable. Framed as a resilience measure, it proposes binding limits on model weights, enforced via DSA safe-harbour conditions for hosting platforms. Early technical work begins at ETSI to develop capability proxies for upload screening. Yet backlash emerges from open-source communities and digital rights advocates, who denounce the move as censorship in disguise.

### Capability Creeps, Safety Crumbles

`ai_capability` advances to 63.5, continuing its slow climb in the terminal zone as labs refine agent autonomy. No safety safeguards land on deployed systems; the gap widens further. The absence of monitoring tools for agent memory and planning leads to a sharp drop in `ai_safety`, exacerbated by growing opacity in frontier models.

`openweight_capability` rises to 50.5, reflecting diffusion of older frontier models despite no new open release. The proposed cap has no immediate effect—compliance is distant, enforcement unproven, and the global open ecosystem remains beyond EU jurisdiction.

`resilience` gains +3: +2 from the completed Incident Response Network and +1 from partial early effect of the Cyber Shield. However, `eu_ai_sovereignty` falls to 30.0, down −1 as capability growth outpaces sovereign gains. The new restriction does not yet offset dependence.

`eu_political_capital` drops to 12.0 under a crushing portfolio charge and no finishing bonus. Public sentiment declines as fears of overreach mix with continued job displacement in administrative sectors.
