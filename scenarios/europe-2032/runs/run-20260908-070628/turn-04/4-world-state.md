### Capability Creeps Higher  
AI capability advances by +1.5 to 58.0, as frontier labs achieve stable multi-day autonomous operation in controlled environments. Architectural refinements and training compute gains drive progress, narrowing the gap to full multi-week autonomy. The closed frontier pulls ahead slightly, but open models remain close behind.

### Open Frontier, Growing Risk  
With `ai_capability` at 58.0, `openweight_capability` rises to 55.0—halfway between its prior value and the closed frontier. The gap reflects growing caution among open-weight developers, but several high-impact models are released under permissive licences, including one fine-tuned for protein design. While not weaponised, its capabilities reignite biosecurity concerns within EU health agencies.

### Safety Gains, But Slowly  
The new **Accelerated Evaluation Capacity** measure launches, aiming to build sovereign technical muscle for red-teaming open models. However, initial coordination is patchy: Poland and Hungary resist binding commitments, and private labs in France and Sweden express concern over duplication. Still, the measure’s establishment of public evaluation nodes delivers a modest +1 boost to `ai_safety`, reaching 34.0. This falls short of projections, but avoids regression amid rising capability.

### Resilience Holds  
The **Cyber Resilience Emergency Directive** completes this turn, hardening critical infrastructure across energy and transport sectors. Automated patching and anomaly detection systems are now operational in 18 member states. No major incidents occur, and `resilience` remains stable at 42.0—no drop from capability growth, but no bonus yet, as the measure’s full effects will take time to manifest.

### Sovereignty Inches Forward  
`eu_ai_sovereignty` rises to 25.0: +1 from ongoing **InvestAI Gigafactories**, +1 from **Tech Sovereignty Package**, and +1 from the new evaluation network’s compute footprint. The **Sovereign Access to Frontier Models** initiative remains on track, but delivers no additional gain this turn. No external access events occur.

### Political Capital Erodes  
Five large measures now in flight cost −15; the priority adds −1. Despite the completion of the Cyber Directive (+3), political capital drops to 19.0. Public sentiment remains stagnant at 33.0—neither scandal nor visible benefit shifts perception. The new evaluation programme, while technically sound, is seen as bureaucratic, drawing criticism from both open-science advocates and industry.
