### Capability Surge, Safety Erosion  
AI capability advances by 1.5 points to 55.0 as labs leverage new training efficiencies and denser architectures. However, with no safety measures yet operational on frontier systems, and the open-weight frontier gaining ground rapidly, `ai_safety` drops by 3 to 28.0. The gap between what AI can do and what anyone can control continues to widen.

### Open Release Accelerates Diffusion  
An open-weight model with significant strategic capabilities is released, demonstrating strong performance in cyber and bio design tasks. While not yet matching the closed frontier, its architecture enables rapid fine-tuning and adaptation. Downloads exceed 100,000 in the first two weeks, and replication clusters emerge across academic and activist networks. This surge brings `openweight_capability` to 53.0, reflecting its rapid catch-up within the 2.0-point threshold of the closed frontier under diffusion dynamics. Full parity remains a threshold not yet crossed, but the narrowing gap intensifies concerns about uncontrolled proliferation.

### EU Launches Sovereign Access Initiative  
The European Commission proposes the Sovereign Access to Frontier Models programme, aiming to deploy high-capability open models on EuroHPC-controlled infrastructure. The plan is announced with a proposed €8bn reallocation and expanded EuroHPC mandate, backed politically by France, Germany, and the Netherlands. However, the initiative remains in early development: no funding has been formally approved, co-financing negotiations are only beginning, and Poland and Sweden voice strong opposition to centralisation and regulatory overreach. Civil society groups warn of unaccountable AI use under the guise of security. Due to these unresolved hurdles, the measure is not yet operational and does not contribute to sovereignty this turn. It also does not yet incur a full portfolio charge, as implementation has not meaningfully begun.

As a result, only the two existing large in-flight measures — InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech Sovereignty Package — contribute to sovereignty this turn. However, both remain in mid-implementation with no nearing completion, so each contributes +0. With no decay (as `ai_capability` rose by less than 2), `eu_ai_sovereignty` remains flat at 22.0.

### Resilience Efforts Stumble  
The Cyber Resilience Emergency Directive, meant to harden critical infrastructure, faces implementation delays. Eastern member states resist binding red-teaming mandates, citing cost and sovereignty. As a result, resilience dips to 39.0, penalised for rising AI capability without effective countermeasures.

### Political Capital Erodes Further  
The EU advances three large in-flight measures: InvestAI Gigafactories, Tech Sovereignty Package, and Cyber Resilience Emergency Directive. The newly announced Sovereign Access Initiative is in pre-implementation and does not yet count as an active measure in the portfolio. The three active measures each carry a −3 portfolio charge, and the priority designation on Cyber Resilience adds −1, for a total charge of −10. A modest proposal bonus of +3 is earned for responding directly to the openweight release. Public sentiment remains above political capital, lending +2. This results in a net change of −5, bringing `eu_political_capital` down to 33.0. Public sentiment softens slightly due to visible action but remains low at 35.0.
