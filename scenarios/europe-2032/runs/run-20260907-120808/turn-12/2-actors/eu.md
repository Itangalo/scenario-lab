ENISA is coordinating a pan-European red team-blue team exercise series starting in April, simulating autonomous ransomware propagation to test fallback protocols and human-in-the-loop recovery under stress.

The European Cybersecurity Competence Centre is being tasked with certification, and the Commission is issuing emergency procurement waivers to bypass standard tenders where vendors meet baseline assurance thresholds.

This is not optional: when the next wave hits, there will be no second chance to opt in.
