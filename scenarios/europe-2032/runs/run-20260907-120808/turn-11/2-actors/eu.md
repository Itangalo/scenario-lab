The **Sovereign Inference Backbone** remains under construction, with Spain and Poland accelerating site commissioning using emergency energy allocation decrees. We are pressing the remaining member states to finalise hosting agreements by turn 12, warning that delays now risk rendering the entire network obsolete before activation. The **Replication Foundry Initiative** continues under revised protocols: only models with full checkpoint provenance and researcher access will be replicated, aligning with the new priority. This is not retreat — it is focus. We cannot build sovereign systems if we do not first build sovereign understanding.
