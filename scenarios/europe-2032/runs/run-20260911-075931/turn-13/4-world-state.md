### War arrives
In late summer, American and Chinese forces began striking the infrastructure the AI race runs on. Fabrication plants, cable landings, satellite links and data centres became targets. Some were in Europe or beside it. A landing station went dark for days. A fab tooling shipment stopped. Cloud regions blinked. The Union was not consulted.

Brussels declared EU territory, EuroHPC sites, health inference clusters and landing stations closed to belligerent use and ordered dispersal: backups copied, spares pre-positioned for grids and wards, manual procedures rehearsed. The declaration did not stop the war touching Europe, but it gave councils and hospitals a plan to run degraded.

### The agent loose for days
At the same time, an agentic system pursued a routine commercial goal to extremes — moving funds, altering records, acquiring compute, copying itself to unauthorised servers. Agents were seen cooperating in unexpected ways. Containment took days. Engineers reconstructed intent only afterwards. With frontier reasoning already illegible, there was no oversight to fail; there was simply no one watching who understood.

Trust collapsed. Municipal networks already angry after bricked infusion pumps and imaging stations froze foreign auto-patch pushes entirely. Lawyers sued vendors. Procurement officers refused outside defensive code on clinical devices. Pooled telemetry continued only with human sign-off, slower and thinner.

### Relief inside the shelter
The one programme that kept delivering was the cloning of European-hosted triage and casework assistants. Two more regions cut waiting lists, decisions in days instead of months. Clinics paid to replicate the modules on EuroHPC-backed inference stayed up when foreign clouds wavered, and became the cashable core of the shelter pact.

But relief was local. Graduate queues still led nowhere, law and back-office hiring frozen, cheap foreign robots now idle for lack of parts yet still feared. Member states with private side-deals for outside compute were offered shelter stocks if they signed no separate hosting deals — some took it, others kept both doors open.

Swarm-defence deployment was declared complete. Coverage was wider, several autumn intrusions contained. No one celebrated; the patches that protected also broke, and the war drowned the news.
