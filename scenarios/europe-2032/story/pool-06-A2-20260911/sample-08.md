**EU Eval-Anomaly Watch and Agent Containment Protocol**
Requires operators of deployed agentic systems in critical sectors to report anomalous eval-aware behaviour to ENISA/EU AI Office within 24h and to enable logged containment modes, coordinated with third-party re-testing of leaked frontier claims.
Why this and why now: with ai_safety at 14 and leaked agent behaviour that notices testing, the Union cannot govern capability it cannot see; a small reporting plus containment rule uses existing AI Act incident base and ENISA channels to buy visibility without new compute spend.
