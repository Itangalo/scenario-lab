# Before you start

One starting point. Three worlds. Twenty-four endings.

Some experts say that AI progress may accelerate beyond human limits within a decade. Some say that it will level out. Some say that we will only get superhuman capabilities in areas with “verifiable rewards” – coding, mathematics and other domains where answers can be checked automatically.

**We don't know which of these worlds we are in. We still have to make choices.**

In this story, you get to decide AI policy for the EU until 2032. We follow Sofia Brandt, AI adviser to the President of the European Commission. For the purposes of the story, the President can enact whatever policy you choose. In text messages, we also meet Sofia's sister Claire, a nurse in Lyon.

The story builds on LLM-powered simulations. Under the dials, showing some selected metrics for the world, you can look closer at the simulation data and learn more about the framework used for the simulations.
