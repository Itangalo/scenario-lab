# Before you start

Three different worlds, 24 different endings.

Some experts say that AI progress may accelerate beyond human limits within a decade. Some say that it will level out. Some say that we will only get superhuman capabilities in areas with "verifiable rewards" – where there is an easy way to say what is right.

You don't know which of these worlds you are in. You still have to make choices. In this story, built on LLM-powered simulations, you get to dictate the AI policy for the EU until 2032. You set a two-year commitment, roll the dice, and see how the world unfolds.
