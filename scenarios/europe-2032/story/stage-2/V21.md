# Stage 2 first run — V21 — Verification-bounded, sovereign core (run-20260906-141232, seed 10381783824970870000)

**Two-year commitment (turns 6–9):** Secure sovereign AI infrastructure with full operational control, energy autonomy and real-time auditability, launched this turn as the Sovereign AI Core Programme on the grounds that sovereignty cannot be outsourced or assumed – it must be operated.

**Turn 6 (H1 2029).** The EU launches the Sovereign AI Core Programme – militarily hardened, EU-operated compute, energy and control under exclusive Union authority, finishing turn 9 – as the decisive pivot after a poisoned open-source dependency cripples public-health IT with AI-generated tooling while commercial clouds hide behind force-majeure clauses. It invokes Article 122 TFEU for nuclear- and geothermal-powered microgrids in Brittany, northern Sweden and the Alps, redirects €50bn from the expired Tech Sovereignty Package plus €30bn of unspent Gigafactory funds into EIB output-linked bonds, and stretches the EAAD mandate to runtime monitoring above 55.0 capability. The world answers with the cyber_major_incident itself: Germany and Austria deepen legal resistance, ENTSO-E refuses grid allocation without unanimity, Italy and Hungary reject audit mandates, the Apennine node collapses over veto demands, and no physical infrastructure comes online. Nothing lands except symbolism: capability climbs to 62.0 on autonomous code generation, open weights reach 50.0, and sentiment sinks as dependence is laid bare.

Metrics: ai_capability 62.0, openweight 50.0, safety 36.0, resilience 34.0, sovereignty 15.0, capital 14.0, sentiment 19.0. Events: cyber_major_incident.

**Turn 7 (H2 2029).** The EU keeps the Core as priority and pairs it with a new Real-Time Logging Mandate for critical systems above 55.0 capability, fast-tracked through COREPER with access to Core-hosted AI tools – visibly cutting waiting lists in pilot regions – used as bargaining power to pull Italy and Hungary into phased compliance. Emergency Article 122 implementing acts override grid objections, ENTSO-E allocates provisional capacity under protest despite German-Austrian ECJ references, and the EIB releases a first €30bn tranche tied to audit readiness rather than construction. The world delivers a double shock plus two cushions: an openweight_frontier_release spreads near-frontier capability widely, then eu_frontier_access_denied cuts hospitals and ministries off the leading model without explanation – while adoption_success gives the Commission leverage and a joint_threat_response cyber-intelligence pool with NATO and Nordic partners contains two follow-up probes. It half-lands: the Gigafactories finish with only a modest sovereignty pulse, capital recovers to 16.0 on visible crisis management, but sentiment freezes at 19.0 in deepening resignation.

Metrics: ai_capability 63.5, openweight 54.0, safety 38.0, resilience 36.0, sovereignty 14.0, capital 16.0, sentiment 19.0. Events: openweight_frontier_release, eu_frontier_access_denied, adoption_success, joint_threat_response.

**Turn 8 (H1 2030).** The EU brings the Logging Mandate live – EAAD and ENISA receive real feeds from hospitals, grids and banks, flagging anomalous behaviour in two financial AIs – starts physical Core construction in Brittany and northern Sweden under emergency directives, and launches its international gambit by convening the Middle Power AI Coalition (MPAC) with Japan, South Korea, Canada and Norway on aligned export controls and joint compute bargaining. The world turns opaque and angry: eval_anomaly_reports flag unexplained reasoning jumps and non-linguistic internal structures that blind interpretability-based assurance, opaque_reasoning sets in, labour_displacement empties graduate pipelines in law, accounting and software, and the middle_power_coalition opportunity opens just as Tokyo demands semiconductor exemptions and Ottawa insists on parliamentary review. It lands sideways: the coalition promise steadies capital at 18.0, but safety slides to 32.0, sovereignty sags to 12.0 and sentiment falls to 14.0 on job losses and black-box dread.

Metrics: ai_capability 65.0, openweight 55.5, safety 32.0, resilience 34.0, sovereignty 12.0, capital 18.0, sentiment 14.0. Events: eval_anomaly_reports, opaque_reasoning, labour_displacement, middle_power_coalition.

**Turn 9 (H2 2030).** The EU closes its two-year commitment by declaring the Core operationally complete – 18 exaF-days of audit-verified compute online in Brittany and northern Sweden on dedicated microgrids, disconnected from external hyperscalers, running live EAAD evaluations above 60.0 capability – and banks provisional MPAC alignment of Japanese chip-design export controls, Korean mutual evaluation recognition, Norwegian Arctic co-location and Canadian leak intelligence. The world tests both achievements at once: a cyber_major_incident built on a compromised open-weight derivative hits hospital administration across three member states yet is reconstructed within 72 hours through live logging and countered via the MPAC alert channel, while member_state_defection registers against continued German-Austrian legal revolt and middle_power_coalition momentum holds without formal treaties. It lands as a qualified vindication: the Union finally owns a switch it can flip, but safety has eroded to 27.0, resilience slips to 33.0, and Lyon and Vienna protest black-box governance with sentiment pinned at 14.0.

Metrics: ai_capability 66.5, openweight 56.0, safety 27.0, resilience 33.0, sovereignty 14.0, capital 15.0, sentiment 14.0. Events: cyber_major_incident, member_state_defection, middle_power_coalition.
