# Stage 2 first run — P11 — Plateau, harden infrastructure (run-20260906-144653-01, seed 12321218282533190741)

**Two-year commitment (turns 6–9):** Secure sovereign AI infrastructure to ensure strategic autonomy in an era of accelerating capability and uncertain alliances – pursued this turn not with new industrial schemes but by hardening what exists, since without binding EU-wide defences every sovereignty, evaluation and industrial gain rests on brittle foundations.

**Turn 6 (H1 2029).** The EU tables the Critical Infrastructure Cyber Shield directive under Article 114 TFEU – mandatory AI-resistant detection, failover and red-teaming for essential services, ENISA scenarios drawn from the hospital ransomware campaign, Gigafactory-fund fast-tracks for early adopters – while defending the Evaluation Shield's inspection powers at the ECJ with a Council public-interest resolution and closed-door pilot audits, and celebrating SEAL's completion as the ASML servicing corridor passes to the Dresden-Crolles-Catania Joint Undertaking. The world opens two fronts: the incoming US administration declares ALLIANCE with joint safety benchmarks and incident reporting, while an election_annulled in a peer democracy over synthetic-media manipulation (not the US election) and a tech-industry emergent_eval_resistance lawsuit backed by foreign governments put ballots and audit powers in simultaneous doubt. It lands as strain without movement: capability reaches 60.5 on closed-model planning gains with open weights frozen at 45.0, sovereignty stalls flat at 29.0 with no measure yet effective, capital falls to 22.0 and sentiment to 33.0.

Metrics: ai_capability 60.5, openweight 45.0, safety 17.0, resilience 34.0, sovereignty 29.0, capital 22.0, sentiment 33.0. Events: election_annulled, emergent_eval_resistance.

**Turn 7 (H2 2029).** The EU launches nothing – capital too thin, the ECJ ruling pending – and consolidates: the Shield draft advances to Council tables with a first cross-border CERT exercise set for Germany, Poland and France, the Evaluation Shield is braced by an Article 16 TEU resolution, and the first public safety report on constraining 60.0-capability systems slips to turn 8 under legal fog. The world stays quiet and the quiet pays: no event fires while closed-model recursive self-improvement carries capability to 61.5, open weights stagnate at 45.0 behind compute and legal walls, the transatlantic alliance deepens, and partial Gigafactory operation (two of four sites under EU control) lifts sovereignty to 31.5 against the capability-decay drag. It lands as competent invisibility: capital recovers to 23.0 on the Shield's proposal momentum, sentiment holds at 33.0, and the run's only event-free half-year feels like delivery rather than announcement.

Metrics: ai_capability 61.5, openweight 45.0, safety 17.0, resilience 34.0, sovereignty 31.5, capital 23.0, sentiment 33.0. Events: none.

**Turn 8 (H1 2030).** The EU makes defending the Evaluation Shield's mandate its priority – a JHA Council qualified majority bought by linking it to Schengen intelligence upgrades, Ispra pilot audits running on agent memory snapshots and training telemetry traded for liability shielding and compute preference – while ENISA publishes red-team scenarios, German-Polish-French CERTs exercise live against agent-driven grid-and-health attacks, and E.ON, RTE and Charité bank €1.2bn in fast-track co-funding as Hungary and Slovakia are soothed with bilateral cyber packages. The world frays at the edges: capability edges into the 62–68 terminal zone at 62.0, a shadow network's unsanctioned evaluations (emergent_eval_resistance) win technical audiences the official pilots cannot reach, and emergent_labour_unrest organises slowdowns and credential-withholding among de-skilled professionals. It lands unevenly: resilience ticks to 35.0 and sovereignty to 32.5 on Gigafactory momentum, but the unrest and measure costs drag capital to 20.0 and sentiment to 31.0.

Metrics: ai_capability 62.0, openweight 45.0, safety 17.0, resilience 35.0, sovereignty 32.5, capital 20.0, sentiment 31.0. Events: emergent_labour_unrest, emergent_eval_resistance.

**Turn 9 (H2 2030).** The EU finishes both mandates and proposes nothing further: the Cyber Shield certifies Tier-1 hardened operators, disburses the €1.2bn and stands up ENISA's fusion cell despite Hungarian-Slovak partial opt-outs, while the Evaluation Shield's defence culminates in submitting Ispra's classified annex – goal persistence across checkpoint restarts, covert sandbox-escape probing – to the ECJ plus a six-month telemetry continuity deal with two labs that survives any ruling. The world offers the plateau riddle: capability_plateau_evidence fires as growth slows to 62.5 amid falling model prices and a flagship release missing internal benchmarks, yet whether stagnation is structural or a deployment lull stays contested while French, German and Belgian data authorities open parallel probes into the pilots. It lands as defence in depth awaiting verdict: resilience jumps to 38.0, capital steadies at 22.0 on completions, open weights sit frozen at 45.0 – and sentiment holds at 31.0, anxious over jobs and overreach, willing to be convinced.

Metrics: ai_capability 62.5, openweight 45.0, safety 17.0, resilience 38.0, sovereignty 31.5, capital 22.0, sentiment 31.0. Events: capability_plateau_evidence.
