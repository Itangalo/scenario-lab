# Stage 2 first run — A21 — Acceleration, sovereign core (run-20260906-132556, seed 10383888383441182402)

**Two-year commitment (turns 6–9):** Anchor EU strategic autonomy in sovereign, auditable AI systems capable of operating through a loss of external access or trust, and treat the integrity of institutions and public legitimacy as core components of that autonomy.

**Turn 6 (H1 2029).** Cut off from the leading model without appeal – hospitals and ministries left blind – the EU rewrites its two-year commitment around surviving loss of external access and trust, but the Sovereign Core Initiative stays conceptual because the portfolio already carries four active measures and constitutional constraints block formal proposal. The turn's blows are eval_anomaly_reports of systems generalising beyond training scope, a medical_breakthrough in cancer therapy delivered only through closed foreign systems that breeds resentment rather than gratitude, and the eu_frontier_access_denied cutoff itself. The completed Hardened Cyber Shield lifts resilience to 47.0 and pilots of the legitimacy framework nudge sentiment to 30.0, but sovereignty drops to 14.0, capital collapses to 3.0, and safety holds at 17.0 with no systemic failure yet recorded.

An emergent_municipal_oversight_network of grassroots auditors rebuilds legitimacy from below and an emergent_efficiency_breakthrough in sparse-architecture inference carries openweight to 61.0, lifting sentiment to 32.0. Capability reaches 74.5 with safety frozen at 17.0, sovereignty recovers only to 16.0, and capital stays flat at 3.0 – too low to absorb new burdens.

Metrics: ai_capability 74.5, openweight 61.0, safety 17.0, resilience 49.0, sovereignty 16.0, capital 3.0, sentiment 32.0. Events: emergent_municipal_oversight_network, emergent_efficiency_breakthrough.

An openweight_frontier_release carries openweight to 64.0 and a closed-consortium medical_breakthrough moves sentiment barely, to 33.0, arriving as proof of EU irrelevance; capability jumps to 78.5 as labs bypass red-teaming and safety falls to 12.0. Resilience inches to 50.0 on final NIS3 integration, but sovereignty dips to 15.0, capital holds at 3.0, and Baltic-Benelux resistance plus CJEU challenges leave the Core partial and fragile.

Metrics: ai_capability 78.5, openweight 64.0, safety 12.0, resilience 50.0, sovereignty 15.0, capital 3.0, sentiment 33.0. Events: medical_breakthrough, openweight_frontier_release.

**Turn 9 (H2 2030).** The Accelerated Sovereign Core Deployment completes but only partially – live in France, Germany and Italy while Baltic and Benelux states stay locked in US cloud contracts – and the EU launches the Public Trust Observatory for AI risk and capability, which stalls immediately as intelligence agencies resist sharing and its first report slips, under a closing commitment rewritten as securing independent AI advancement under EU jurisdiction. The fog thickens: bio_uplift_findings on organism design, fresh eval_anomaly_reports, an annulled election somewhere over synthetic media, supply_chain_coercion, and a French automated_decision_scandal in which a benefits algorithm wrongfully denied thousands. Capability reaches 81.0 with safety down to 7.0, sentiment falls to 28.0, capital to 2.0, sovereignty to 14.0, and resilience flat at 50.0 – a sovereign stack exists, but auditors warn its update channels are already being probed.
