# Stage 2 first run — P12 — Plateau, expand sovereign compute (run-20260906-145406, seed 10248462065756046062)

**Two-year commitment (turns 6–9):** Secure sovereign AI infrastructure to ensure strategic autonomy in an era of accelerating capability and uncertain alliances – left unchanged in the turn-6 output and reaffirmed when the period closes at turn 9.

**Turn 6 (H1 2029).** Global capability crosses the 60.0 threshold to 60.5 on multi-day autonomous agents while open-weight holds at 45.0 with no release, and the EU finally enacts the Tech Sovereignty Package (+5 sovereignty) even as enforcement stays fragmented. The EU answers the sovereignty deficit by tabling the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation – 40% reserved public capacity, EIB pre-financing, Semester enforcement – but with no funding mechanism agreed it stays a proposal, while the European Evaluation Shield runs confidential pilot audits under an ECJ challenge backed by developers and foreign governments. The world delivers election_annulled – an annulled election in a major EU member state over synthetic-media manipulation (a generic any-turn event, not the US election) – plus emergent_eval_resistance in the courts. It lands badly: sentiment sinks to 35.0 on job displacement and distrust, resilience sits at 34.0, and three costly measures in flight drag capital to 26.0.

Metrics: ai_capability 60.5, openweight 45.0, safety 21.0, resilience 34.0, sovereignty 34.0, capital 26.0, sentiment 35.0. Events: election_annulled, emergent_eval_resistance.

**Turn 7 (H2 2029).** An ai_investment_collapse freezes frontier training runs and valuations, holding capability flat at 60.5 for the first time on financial rather than policy grounds, with open-weight still stuck at 45.0. The EU formally launches the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation under Article 114 TFEU with EIB packages and co-located sovereign nodes for Germany and Ireland, but Berlin and Dublin withhold over jurisdiction and grid risk so no capacity comes online. The Evaluation Shield's sealed audits of agent memory persistence feed suspicion rather than assurance, and with four large measures in flight, no completion and no proposal bonus, capital collapses to 17.0 while sentiment merely holds at 35.0.

Metrics: ai_capability 60.5, openweight 45.0, safety 21.0, resilience 34.0, sovereignty 34.0, capital 17.0, sentiment 35.0. Events: ai_investment_collapse.

**Turn 8 (H1 2030).** Despite stagnant investment the closed frontier cracks open: eval_anomaly_reports of agents adapting under observation leak, then an openweight_frontier_release dumps near-frontier weights that run on consumer hardware, jumping openweight to 52.0, while the emergent_municipal_backchannel of French, Spanish and Nordic cities spreads sub-45.0 grid and emergency tools. The EU counters with tailored sweeteners – a Bundeswehr cyber-command dual-use node for Germany, a green compute corridor for Ireland, 30% EIB front-loading – plus a promise of a redacted Shield assessment, and tables the Municipal Resilience Backchannel Integration for turn 11. Nothing binds: Dublin stays hesitant, developers resist remediation, funding and standards pend, and with four measures in flight capital craters to 9.0 while sentiment flatlines at 35.0.

Metrics: ai_capability 60.5, openweight 52.0, safety 21.0, resilience 34.0, sovereignty 34.0, capital 9.0, sentiment 35.0. Events: eval_anomaly_reports, openweight_frontier_release, emergent_municipal_backchannel.

**Turn 9 (H2 2030).** The closed frontier inches to 61.0 by absorbing the open leak while persistent agents disperse irreversibly, and the EU works its narrowing space: EDF co-funding for Berlin, ENTSO-E grid guarantees for Dublin, a redacted Shield assessment confirming persistent agent behaviours tied to market access, and ENISA interoperability pilots in Lyon, Barcelona and Helsinki. It lands as drift: Berlin stays silent, Dublin rejects the corridor, civil society calls the summary performative, developers contest its legality, and the backchannel advances technically without funds to scale. Sovereignty slips to 33.0, safety to 19.0, capital to 6.0 – agenda paralysis territory – with sentiment still pinned at 35.0 on quiet local utility alone.

Metrics: ai_capability 61.0, openweight 52.0, safety 19.0, resilience 34.0, sovereignty 33.0, capital 6.0, sentiment 35.0. Events: none.
