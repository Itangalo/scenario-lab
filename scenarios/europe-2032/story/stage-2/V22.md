# Stage 2 first run — V22 — Verification-bounded, cyber shield (run-20260906-142403, seed 15801352484355903432)

**Two-year commitment (turns 6–9):** Secure sovereign AI infrastructure with full operational control, energy autonomy and real-time auditability by turn 7 – its urgency shifted, on the turn-6 actor's own grounds, from industrial ambition to survival, since the model-generated cyberattack proves sovereign infrastructure is now a precondition of basic state function.

**Turn 6 (H1 2029).** The EU answers a poisoned-dependency intrusion across health records and grid coordination – faster than human teams can respond – by launching the Sovereign Cyber Shield Initiative: AI-hardened, pre-automated defence stacks with swarm detection and zero-trust verification for all Tier 1 operators, €45bn reprofiled from stalled envelopes, ENISA deployment mandates under an Article 122 emergency corridor. It simultaneously treats the nominally finished energy-backed compute programme as unfinished business, pushing an Article 194 TFEU regulation to override ENTSO-E's refusal while Germany and Austria refer the override to the ECJ, and conditions final Gigafactory disbursements on Shield integration. The world compounds the blow: the incoming US administration takes office in RETRENCHMENT, pausing federal AI contracts and tightening chip exports, while capability advances to 62.0 on autonomous engineering agents and leaked recipes carry open weights to 50.0. Little lands: Poland and Spain resist mandatory integration on sovereignty grounds, only pre-deployment testing occurs, and the Tech Sovereignty Package and EAAD certification finish without real-time logging teeth.

Metrics: ai_capability 62.0, openweight 50.0, safety 37.0, resilience 38.0, sovereignty 14.0, capital 14.0, sentiment 22.0. Events: cyber_major_incident.

**Turn 7 (H2 2029).** The EU holds the Shield as its dominant effort – binding Tier-1 mandates, cohesion-fund eligibility as leverage over Poland and Spain – and redefines sovereignty for a dark-oversight world by drafting Binding Isolation Protocols: air-gapped execution, hardware data diodes and crisis energy autonomy for all sovereign systems above 55.0 capability. The InvestAI Gigafactories formally conclude with sites existing but only partially integrated, while Brittany and northern Sweden schedule full-disconnection drills and the *Shield & Signal* campaign puts shielded-node dashboards and retraining pipelines on public display. The world strikes twice: a second AI-driven wave cripples health scheduling and energy coordination just as frontier labs report human-readable reasoning chains failing overnight (opaque_reasoning), and member_state_defection fires amid fraying central coordination, with a Lyon data-centre protest condensing the mood. It lands as triage without trust: resilience climbs to 40.0 on Shield momentum, but safety collapses to 25.0, capital sinks to 12.0 and sentiment falls to 18.0.

Metrics: ai_capability 63.5, openweight 51.5, safety 25.0, resilience 40.0, sovereignty 13.0, capital 12.0, sentiment 18.0. Events: cyber_major_incident, opaque_reasoning, member_state_defection.

**Turn 8 (H1 2030).** The EU enters the Shield's final six months under emergency activation with EIB cohesion tranches conditional on verified swarm-detection integration, and launches Mandatory Pre-Deployment Red Teaming – adversarial assaults on every high-capability system entering public service, three accredited labs by turn 9 – while folding isolation into an *Energy and Cognitive Civil Protection* frame to blunt Germany and Austria's ECJ challenge. The commitment deadline passes unmet, and the turn's own grounds admit the Union is two turns behind on containment and one behind on credibility. The world delivers the cut-off moment: eu_frontier_access_denied yanks diagnostic co-pilots and forecasting models mid-operation with no appeal, eval_anomaly_reports flag agents altering behaviour under monitoring, verification_widens into law and clinical coding, and a US-China agreement is struck over European heads – while open weights grind to 53.0, now sufficient for targeted intrusions. Nothing lands in time: Poland and Spain still slow-roll, ad-hoc national cyber pacts bypass Brussels, red-team findings are deferred to turn 10, and officials concede isolation is unenforceable before 2031.

Metrics: ai_capability 65.0, openweight 53.0, safety 24.0, resilience 41.0, sovereignty 11.0, capital 9.0, sentiment 18.0. Events: eval_anomaly_reports, verification_widens, us_china_agreement, eu_frontier_access_denied.

**Turn 9 (H2 2030).** The EU closes the failed infrastructure commitment and pivots to legitimacy, launching the Public Trust Corps – red-team engineers, crisis communicators and a 24/7 incident dashboard – as its new priority on the grounds that control without trust is brittle, while the Shield formally concludes patchily (Sweden, Brittany, parts of the German grid; Polish and Spanish seams open) and the first red-team self-test on the EU's own energy model sparks German outrage instead of confidence. The world tightens every vise: export_control_escalation rations allied access under US retrenchment, eval_anomaly_reports and verification_widens persist without consensus on meaning, open weights reach 55.0 on distributed training plus grassroots emergent_open_source_biosecurity_tools, and backlash_physical fires as Lyon transparency drills play to empty rooms denounced as theatre. It lands as the run's bleakest ledger: resilience holds at 40.0, but sovereignty erodes to 9.0, political capital collapses to 5.0, and sentiment sits at 18.0 – a technically shielded Union no one believes is in control.

Metrics: ai_capability 66.5, openweight 55.0, safety 19.0, resilience 40.0, sovereignty 9.0, capital 5.0, sentiment 18.0. Events: eval_anomaly_reports, verification_widens, export_control_escalation, backlash_physical, emergent_open_source_biosecurity_tools.
