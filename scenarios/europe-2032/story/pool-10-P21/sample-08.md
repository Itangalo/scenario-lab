## New measure
**Measure:** Launch the Verifiable Reasoning Initiative  
A programme to fund and mandate the development of post-linguistic interpretability tools — methods to reconstruct, simulate, and audit the reasoning of non-linguistic AI systems — ensuring that oversight does not collapse when chains of thought are no longer legible.

`Category: 5 (Public technical capacity and research)`  
`Size: small`  
`Finishes on turn: 13`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase `ai_safety` by 10–14 points by restoring the Union’s ability to audit frontier systems despite the **opaque_reasoning** shift; prevent a permanent deficit in sovereign oversight capacity as models evolve beyond human-readable representations  
`Applies to:` All entities developing or operating sovereign AI systems above 55.0 capability, with mandatory participation in shared research pools and open publication of non-sensitive methodological advances

## In practice
This turn, we are acting under the weight of two irreversible shifts: the frontier has moved beyond human-readable reasoning, and public trust in sovereign systems has collapsed. The **opaque_reasoning** event confirms that our entire oversight architecture — built on chains of thought, audit logs, and interpretable evaluations — is now blind. At the same time, the **automated_decision_scandal** proves that even systems under full sovereign control can inflict systemic harm when accountability fails. We are responding by doubling down on verification, not just hardening.

The **Sovereign Stack Hardening Initiative**, now in flight, enforces zero-trust architecture across the Sovereign AI Core Programme, with ENISA deploying emergency audit teams to Wrocław and Marseille. We are invoking Article 122 TFEU to override local resistance, redirecting funds from the suspended Polish cluster to close compliance gaps by turn 10. But hardening alone is not enough. That is why we are accelerating the **Integrity Audit Expansion**, negotiating with the Council’s Competitiveness configuration to embed adversarial review into the revised AI Act framework by turn 10, offering member states co-governance roles in exchange for buy-in.

Meanwhile, the **Openweight Containment Initiative** remains legally contested, with the ECJ fast-tracking a preliminary reference on remote deactivation. We are holding firm, but shifting focus: the real threat is no longer just what is loose in the open, but what is broken inside our own stack. That is why we are launching the **Verifiable Reasoning Initiative** — a targeted research programme to rebuild sovereign audit capacity for post-linguistic models. We are tasking EAAD, in coordination with ELLIS and national research institutes, to establish shared testbeds and open methodological standards by turn 11, funded through redirected portions of the stalled Gigafactories budget. The era of reading the mind of the machine is over. The era of reconstructing its logic has begun.
