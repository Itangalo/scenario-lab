## In practice
We run this as a NIS2 Article 11/12 emergency tasking: ENISA and CERT-EU triage the ransomware / dependency compromise, push hardened configs, offline backups and pre-imaged rebuilds to the affected municipalities, clinics and utilities, with daily technical notes to blunt attribution delay.

We fund islanding-style cyber kits and joint municipal recovery teams from repurposed cohesion and Digital Europe envelopes via the Competitiveness and TTE Councils, asking data-centre operators curtailed last winter to lend clean compute and staff for rebuilds in exchange for priority reconnection, and asking the breakaway state for incident telemetry rather than coercion.
