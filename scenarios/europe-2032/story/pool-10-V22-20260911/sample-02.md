## New measure
**Municipal Recovery and Transition Bridge**
Puts cyber first-aid teams and offline fallback into hit municipalities and hospitals while paying wage-bridge and rapid retraining for workers displaced from checkable-output jobs.

This and now because the automated attack lands on services already degraded by two winters of load-shedding, and narrow displacement is destroying the last tolerance for any EU direction. It is category 7 Labour and social protection with a category 6 Preparedness and resilience execution arm, small and fundable from repurposed cohesion and ESF+ without new builds.

## In practice
We run this as a Civil Protection + Employment Council joint track: ENISA and CERT-EU deploy municipal incident-response pools to the cyber-hit public services, with the islanding spares, offline clinical folders and daily technical notes from M6-M7 reactivated, and large compute curtailed only to protect hospitals and water.

EPSCO repurposes ESF+ and cohesion relief into a six-month wage-bridge and tier-one support/coding retraining voucher paid by automating employers where identifiable, fronted by mayors and clinics. We ask US volume-licence access for defenders while rationing lasts, and do not coerce the breakaway state for balancing power.
