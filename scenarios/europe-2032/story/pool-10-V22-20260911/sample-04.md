## New measure
**Municipal Cyber Shield and Entry-Level Transition Fund**
Patches the ransomware/dependency blast radius in municipalities, clinics and mid-size industry with enforced backups and offline fallback, while paying wage-insurance and retraining for displaced junior coders, analysts and support staff.

This and now because the large automated attack and the narrow but real labour displacement have fused into one legitimacy crisis — brittle local services plus unrecovering entry hiring — and with no measures in flight this is the only executable answer under Preparedness and resilience paired with Labour and social protection.

## In practice
We run this as a Civil Protection + Employment Council joint track: ENISA and CERT-EU push mandatory offline backups, patched dependencies and islanding procedures to the exposed municipalities and health networks hit this turn, funded by repurposed cohesion and ESF+ envelopes, with daily public incident notes to show defenders catching up.

DG EMPL with national PES pays wage-insurance and fast retraining vouchers tied to automating employers in routine coding, analysis and tier-one support, while we freeze any new large sovereignty spend and ask TSOs to hold the load-shedding pact without new builds. We seek US volume-licence continuity for chips but do not stake the turn on it.
