## New measure
**Municipal Cyber Shield and Displaced Checkable-Work Transition**
Hardens municipal clinics, utilities and public services against model-generated ransomware/dependency attacks while moving displaced junior coders, analysts and support staff into retraining and wage-bridged roles.

This and now because the major automated incident plus capability jump in verifiable domains hits exactly the brittle municipalities and the checkable jobs already shedding, and with sovereignty at 17 and capital at 10 only a small, visible absorb-and-cushion move can stop rationing anger turning to hostility.

## Priority

## In practice
We run this as ENISA and CERT-EU led hardening: emergency patching, offline backups, dependency triage and exercised failover for the hit municipalities, hospitals and utilities, funded by repurposed cohesion and Digital Europe envelopes via the Competitiveness Council, with daily technical notes to show defenders catching up.

Alongside, DG EMPL with national PES launches a narrow flexicurity window for routine coding, analysis, documentation and tier-one support: wage insurance, rapid retraining into cyber-hygiene, care-admin and grid-tech roles tied to the Shield rollout, offered to mayors and unions as concrete placements instead of relabelled relief.
