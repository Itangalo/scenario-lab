## Priority
Municipal Cyber Hardening and Displaced-Worker Bridge, because with no measures in flight it is the only instrument containing the concurrent cyber and jobs shocks that will otherwise finish off political capital and public tolerance.

## In practice
We run this as a DG CNECT-ENISA and DG EMPL joint operation: CERT-EU pushes emergency patching, offline backups and dependency isolation to affected municipalities and health providers under NIS2 Article 11, funded by repurposed cohesion and Digital Europe money, with daily technical notes to avoid repeat rationing narratives.

In EPSCO and with national employment agencies we activate ESF+ wage-bridge and fast retraining slots targeted at routine coding, standardised analysis and tier-one support where hiring has frozen, asking automating employers to co-fund via social dialogue. We seek US volume-licence continuity for allied buyers to keep patching tooling flowing, without staking new sovereignty builds this turn.
