## New measure
**EU Entry-Level Transition and Municipal Response Corps**
Pays wage-insurance, retraining stipends and municipal hiring for routine coders, analysts and tier-one support displaced by verifiable-domain automation, while staffing cyber-hygiene and continuity teams for the ransomware-hit public services.

This and now because labour displacement is narrow but real and politically explosive at sentiment 2, and the major automated cyber incident has left defenders visibly behind — a Labour and social protection (7) small measure answers both the job shock and the absorption failure without pretending to rebuild frontier sovereignty we cannot fund at capital 10.

## Priority
EU Entry-Level Transition and Municipal Response Corps, because with capital at 10 and sentiment at 2 nothing else survives if displaced workers and ransomed municipalities see no Union answer this winter.

## In practice
We run this through DG EMPL and DG CNECT under the ESF+ and repurposed cohesion relief already used for the load-shedding pact: employer-paid transition levy on automating firms funds 12-month wage insurance plus fast retraining vouchers for entry coding, analysis and support roles, with hiring preference into municipal CERT-aide, offline-systems and clinic-admin corps.

ENISA and CERT-EU pair the Corps with the hit public services: dependency patching, clean backups, paper-to-digital re-entry, coordinated with TSOs to keep large compute curtailment from recurring. ECOFIN approves as emergency reallocation, not new money; mayors get staff not relabelled funds, unions get income bridge, and we ask no Council vote on sovereignty or export-control alignment we cannot win now.
