## New measure
**Municipal Cyber Repair and Displaced-Worker Redeployment Corps**
Puts displaced junior coders, analysts and support staff on paid municipal repair teams to clean up the automated attack, harden clinics/municipalities, and keep offline services running.

This and now because the cyber major incident lands on brittle municipalities while labour displacement is narrow, checkable-output work that maps directly onto repair — a cheap, visible absorption that answers both events within the new survivability commitment. It is category 7 Labour and social protection, small and executable via ESF+/Cohesion repurposing and ENISA playbooks without new builds.

## Priority
Municipal Cyber Repair and Displaced-Worker Redeployment Corps, because with no measures in flight it is the only instrument spending capital, and visible cleanup plus paychecks is what can lift capital from 10 before export-control rationing bites.

## In practice
We run this as an EPSCO plus JHA emergency line: DG EMPL repurposes ESF+ and unspent cohesion relief into six-month municipal contracts, ENISA/CERT-EU ships a single hardened rebuild image and incident-reporting template, and mayors in the blackout and ransomware-hit communes get teams first.

We ask TSOs and data-centre operators for curtailed-compute log access to trace the model-generated tooling, and we offer the US volume-licence channel cooperation on dependency forensics in exchange for keeping allied compute flowing. Success is measured in clinics back online, dependency patched, and displaced workers hired — reported in the same daily note voters already read.
