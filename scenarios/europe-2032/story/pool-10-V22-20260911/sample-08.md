Puts joint ENISA-CERT-EU rapid recovery teams and EU-funded wage-insurance plus retraining for routine coding, analysis and support roles into the municipalities hit by the automated attack and hiring freeze.

This and now because the cyber major incident and the verifiable-domain capability jump have made defence visibly behind while open weights at 64 put the tooling everywhere, and with no measures in flight and capital at 10 only a small, fast, visibly helpful Category 6 Preparedness and resilience effort with a labour-protection edge can stop further collapse.

## Priority
Municipal Cyber Recovery and Displaced-Worker Transition Corps, because it is the only measure in flight and it directly serves the new two-year commitment to absorb shocks visibly.

## In practice
We run this through Civil Protection Mechanism and ENISA with CERT-EU lead: pre-committed mutual-aid teams for ransomware / dependency-compromise triage in public services, hospitals and municipalities, with spares, clean backups and offline procedures from the finished continuity kits, plus daily technical notes to show defenders catching up.

We pair it with DG EMPL and ECOFIN using reshuffled ESF+ and cohesion money: wage insurance and 6-month retraining placements tied to employers restructuring entry-level coding, analysis and tier-one support, administered by public employment services. We ask Council JHA and EPSCO for a joint mandate, offering mayors real recovery staff and funds instead of relabelled relief, and we do not open a new compute or coercion fight this turn.
