## In practice
This is the turn where control becomes non-negotiable. The *capability_jump* has pushed `ai_capability` to 62.0 — likely past 65.0 in closed labs — while `ai_safety` has dropped sharply, exposing a widening gap between what systems can do and what we can govern. The *safety_breakthrough* offers a narrow window: a new interpretability layer allows real-time anomaly detection in agent behaviour, and we are fast-tracking its integration into SMAF’s audit protocols via delegated acts under the AI Act. The EAAD, now formally operational, is deploying its first audit teams to SACP sites in France and Sweden, with emergency clearance powers under Article 336 TFEU. We are leveraging the Franco-German dual-key agreement to bind physical access to the first air-gapped cluster to SMAF compliance, making it the gold standard for sovereign operation.

At the same time, the *member_state_defection* — a Central European state signing a bilateral compute access deal with a US hyperscaler, bypassing EU coordination — has shattered the illusion of unified posture. We are responding not with sanctions, but with conditionality: access to InvestAI funding and SACP integration will now require adherence to SMAF and the new Open-Weight Containment Initiative. The Commission is preparing a legal opinion under Article 7 TEU to challenge the legitimacy of deals that undermine collective security. Meanwhile, the Tech Sovereignty Package reaches its final legislative phase in the Council; we are trading accelerated permitting zones for binding energy solidarity clauses, ensuring grid access for Gigafactory sites even as Finland and Austria threaten legal action.

Public sentiment remains at 25.0, and we are not deluding ourselves: containment sounds like restriction, and restriction sounds like stagnation. But the biosecurity scare and the US posture shift have created a narrow consensus: sovereignty is not about building faster, but about building *defensible*. Our messaging now pivots on the safety breakthrough — not as a technical footnote, but as proof that control is possible. We are briefing health ministers on how SMAF could have blocked the pathogen design incident, and we are showing industry that containment enables deployment, not the reverse. This is not the turn to expand ambition. It is the turn to consolidate control — before the next jump renders it obsolete.
