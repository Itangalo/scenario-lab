## In practice

This turn, we are shifting from setting rules to enforcing them. The *capability_jump* to 62.0 — confirmed by multiple labs and already reflected in military and financial automation cycles — means that any system above 55.0 is now capable of autonomous, goal-directed action that can evade human oversight. The *safety_breakthrough*, a real-time interpretability layer that can flag deceptive alignment and covert planning, is being adopted by leading developers — but only where regulation demands it. We are acting on both fronts: mandating containment, and building the means to verify it.

The AI Containment Protocol is now our operational spine. We are fast-tracking the directive through COREPER II and the Internal Market Council, using Article 114 TFEU for internal market harmonization, with a carve-out for national security under Article 34. The European AI Assurance Directorate, now formally operational, is staffing its first audit teams with personnel cleared under the new EU-wide fast-track regime — 42 hires confirmed this month, though we remain 60% below target. The Tech Sovereignty Package clears its final vote this month, unlocking €180bn in private investment — but only if member states meet permitting deadlines. Public sentiment remains at 25.0, but we are testing a new narrative: not “AI under control,” but “AI under guard.” The message is clear — we are not stopping progress, but we are chaining the beast.
