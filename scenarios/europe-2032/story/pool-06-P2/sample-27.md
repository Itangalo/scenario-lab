## In practice

This turn, we are shifting from framework-building to operational enforcement. The *capability_jump* has vaulted global AI capability into a regime where systems can execute multi-day strategic projects without supervision, and the contested bio-design incident proves that the knowledge frontier is already spilling into dangerous domains. At the same time, the *safety_breakthrough* — a real-time interpretability method adopted by leading labs — gives us a tool we did not have six months ago: the ability to certify not just what a model outputs, but how it thinks. We are not waiting.

The AI Containment Protocol moves from proposal to binding directive this turn. We are invoking Article 114 TFEU for internal market harmonization, with a recital citing Article 12 of the Treaty on European Union (TEU) to justify emergency powers. The Council’s Internal Market and General Affairs formations will vote under urgency procedure. France and Germany have agreed to dual-key hosting for the first air-gapped cluster in Strasbourg, with physical access split between the EAAD and a joint Franco-German command. ENISA is being tasked with drafting the technical specifications for real-time anomaly detection, to be integrated into the Protocol by turn 7.

The Real-Time Assurance Layer (RTAL) is our next step: a sovereign, open-source stack that embeds the *safety_breakthrough* into every high-capability system we operate. It will run in parallel with the Containment Protocol, providing not just isolation, but insight. We are allocating €4.2bn from the Digital Europe Programme and Horizon Europe to fast-track its development, with a core team drawn from the newly operational EAAD, Fraunhofer, and INRIA. Deployment will be mandatory for all SACP systems by turn 8.

Public sentiment remains a drag at 25.0, and the *member_state_defection* — Poland’s unilateral deal with a US hyperscaler for preferential AI access — threatens cohesion. We are responding not with sanctions, but with leverage: offering Poland guaranteed access to RTAL-secured systems and a seat on the EAAD’s audit panel in exchange for alignment. The message is clear: sovereignty is not isolation, but control. And control is the only path to trust — both from our institutions, and from our people.
