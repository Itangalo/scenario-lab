## In practice

We are acting on three fronts: containment, cohesion, and resilience. The *capability_jump* — a new model from a US lab demonstrating autonomous multi-week research cycles and self-modifying agent architectures — has moved the frontier to 69.0. This is not incremental; it is a rupture. The *safety_breakthrough* — a new interpretability method allowing real-time tracking of emergent goals in high-dimensional latent space — offers hope, but it is not yet deployable at scale. We cannot wait. The *member_state_defection* — Hungary’s secret agreement with a US cloud provider to host unlicensed frontier models under Budapest’s sole jurisdiction — proves that our legal framework is only as strong as its weakest link.

We are fast-tracking the AI Containment Protocol through the Council’s Internal Market and Telecommunications formation, using Article 114 TFEU as legal basis. The Commission is drafting delegated acts to apply it immediately to SACP systems and to any model above 55.0 operating on EU soil. France and Germany have agreed to host the first air-gapped cluster under dual-key control, with physical access logs shared with the EAAD. We are also invoking the Anti-Coercion Instrument to signal that bilateral deals undermining EU-wide AI control will trigger economic countermeasures.

At the same time, we are launching the Resilience by Design Initiative to close the gap left by CIRS. ENISA will issue binding AI-resilience standards for all critical infrastructure procurement by turn 7, requiring real-time anomaly detection, automatic patch validation, and cross-border failover. We are redirecting €30bn from the InvestAI fund to subsidize compliance, particularly in Eastern member states. The goal is not just to patch systems, but to rebuild them so that AI-enabled attacks fail by design, not by chance.

Public sentiment remains at 25.0, and we are not deluding ourselves: this is a moment of crisis, not confidence. But we are not asking for trust — we are enforcing necessity. The narrative is shifting: sovereignty is not about building faster, but about holding together. We are not just containing AI — we are containing the chaos that follows when power outruns control.
