## In practice
This turn, we are acting on the conviction that control must now precede capability. The *capability_jump* has pushed global AI to an estimated 65.0, far beyond the 62.0 we entered the half-year with — a leap that outpaces safety, integration, and institutional readiness alike. The *safety_breakthrough* in interpretability offers a rare foothold: for the first time, we can predict certain failure modes before deployment, and we are fast-tracking its adoption across SACP and EAAD. But the *member_state_defection* — Poland’s side deal with a US hyperscaler for priority access to frontier models — proves that without enforceable containment, even our own members will race toward dependency, not sovereignty.

We are treating the AI Containment Protocol as non-negotiable. The Commission is drafting implementing acts under Article 16 of the AI Act, to be adopted by COREPER in May, with enforcement tied to EU funding access and critical infrastructure licensing. France and Germany have agreed in principle to host the first two air-gapped clusters under dual-key control, but we are still negotiating the chain of escalation: who triggers the human-in-the-loop override, and on what evidence? We are tasking ENISA with drafting the anomaly detection standards, using the safety breakthrough as a baseline for certified predictability. Meanwhile, the Openweight Watch Programme begins as a joint effort between EAAD, Europol’s Cybercrime Centre, and the European Bioinformatics Institute, with seed funding from the Digital Europe Programme. We are deploying crawlers to monitor Hugging Face, GitHub, and decentralized inference markets for models approaching 50.0, and preparing emergency blocking powers under the Digital Services Act framework.

Public sentiment remains at 25.0, and we are not under illusions: containment sounds like restriction, and sovereignty sounds like cost. But after the bio-uplift findings and the US posture shift, we can no longer afford to wait for consensus. The Gigafactories in Sweden and Brittany are progressing, but the grid strain is triggering local backlash — we are responding with binding solidarity clauses and a new Just Transition AI Fund under the European Semester. Our political capital is at 17.0, barely above paralysis, but we are spending it because the alternative is irrelevance. This is not the turn for compromise. It is the turn to lock the doors before the storm arrives.
