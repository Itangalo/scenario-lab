## In practice
We are treating this turn as the pivot from reactive containment to systemic resilience. The *capability_jump* — confirmed at +5.2 on the index — means the most capable systems are now completing multi-day scientific and engineering projects end to end, displacing not just junior staff but entire research workflows. Worse, the *member_state_defection* — Austria’s quiet deal with a US hyperscaler for tiered access to frontier models — proves that without enforceable safeguards, sovereignty is a slogan, not a reality. We cannot match the frontier if access is rationed, so we must ensure that what we build is under our own lock and key.

The *safety_breakthrough* changes everything: a new interpretability method from a joint ETHZ-CEA team allows us to certify model behaviour in high-risk domains before deployment, not after. This is not a lab curiosity — it is being integrated into the EAAD’s audit toolkit this quarter. But breakthroughs decay if not institutionalised. That is why we are pushing the AI Containment Protocol with full force: we are drafting implementing acts under Article 114 TFEU, mandating air-gapped operation, dual-key access, and real-time anomaly detection for all sovereign systems above 55.0. The German and French technical teams are already aligning their cluster designs to the protocol; we are offering Spain and Poland early access to the Swedish Gigafactory in exchange for compliance. This is not optional: the next model trained under SACP will be subject to SMAF *and* the Containment Protocol, no exceptions.

At the same time, the *bio_uplift_findings* demand more than evaluation — they demand rehearsal. That is why we are launching the EU Biosecurity Red-Teaming Network: a distributed offensive capability modelled on ENISA’s cyber exercises, but for life sciences. Coordinated by the ECDC and funded through Horizon Europe, it will simulate AI-driven pathogen design and delivery vectors, testing both technical defences and institutional response. We are facing down resistance from research universities who fear overreach, but we are offering them autonomy in scope and methodology — what we cannot offer is complacency. The US may be weaponizing access, and the code may be leaking, but we will not let Europe become the lab where the next crisis starts.
