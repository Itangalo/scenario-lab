## In practice
We are acting on the conviction that control, not access, defines power in this new phase. The **capability_jump** has rendered our current sovereign development timelines strategically insufficient — the frontier is now at 62.0, and no EU-led system will reach that level before turn 8. Worse, a northern member state has signed a bilateral agreement with a US hyperscaler for early access to next-generation models, bypassing EAAD evaluation and SMAF compliance. This is not just a breach of solidarity — it is a systemic vulnerability. We respond by hardening the perimeter: the AI Containment Protocol moves from proposal to implementation, with the Commission drafting implementing acts under Article 16 TFEU for emergency health and security powers, to be adopted by the Council in April. France and Germany have agreed to host the first two air-gapped nodes, with dual-key access protocols finalised by the Joint Research Centre by June.

The EAAD, now formally operational, inherits the audit mandate from SMAF and expands it to cover the Containment Protocol. We are fast-tracking 50 senior technical auditors through DG HR, with security clearances expedited via the Franco-German bilateral channel. The first red-team exercise on a sovereign testbed runs in May, simulating an internal agent escalation under high-latency human override. Meanwhile, the Openweight Containment Initiative is being negotiated through the Digital Services Act review process — we are amending Article 28 to include capability-based distribution controls, framing it as a security necessity, not a restriction on innovation. Resistance from open-source communities is expected; we are offering co-design of the kill-switch protocol through the EU Open Science Cloud to buy legitimacy.

Public sentiment remains at 25.0, and we are not deluding ourselves: this is a moment of crisis management, not public mobilisation. We are not launching new campaigns. Instead, we are briefing national parliaments directly on the biosecurity findings and the member-state defection, using classified annexes to build consensus. The message is stark: without containment, sovereignty is a fiction. The InvestAI Gigafactory in Sweden breaks ground next month, but we are no longer selling it as a race to capability — we are selling it as the foundation of a sovereign kill switch. This is not the future we wanted. It is the one we must build.
