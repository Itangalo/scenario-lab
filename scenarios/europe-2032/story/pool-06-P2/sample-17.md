## In practice
This turn, we are acting on the conviction that control is no longer a supplement to capability — it is its precondition. The *capability_jump* has vaulted the frontier to 62.0, and while the *safety_breakthrough* gives us new tools to monitor and interpret model behaviour, it also exposes how far behind we are in applying them at scale. The US is locking down its frontier labs, and China is accelerating its own sovereign stack — we cannot afford to be the open flank. Our response is tripartite: contain, secure, and consolidate.

First, we are fast-tracking the AI Containment Protocol through COREPER II and the Internal Market Council, using the Article 114 legal base to establish harmonized operational standards for all systems above 55.0. The protocol mandates air-gapped execution, multi-party control (with at least one EU-nominated observer), real-time anomaly logging, and a legally binding human escalation chain. The *safety_breakthrough* — a certified interpretability module now adopted by DeepMind and Anthropic — will be integrated into the protocol’s monitoring stack, giving us a rare moment of alignment between public oversight and private innovation. We are offering labs that comply early a fast track to SACP participation and access to InvestAI compute, but the deadline is firm: full compliance by turn 7.

Second, we are launching the Openweight Containment Initiative to close the back door. Openweight_capability stands at 48.0, but with the latest optimizations, several open models are nearing 50.0 on consumer hardware. We cannot allow the EU to become a sanctuary for uncontrolled deployment. Through ENISA and national agencies, we are requiring all cloud and hosting providers to implement capability screening for model uploads and training jobs, using watermarking and hash-matching tools developed under the EAAD. Providers that fail to comply will face disconnection from EU energy and network infrastructure under revised NIS2 enforcement powers. This is not a ban — it is a containment regime, and we are working with open-research communities to preserve legitimate access under audit.

Third, we are consolidating our sovereign stack. The first InvestAI Gigafactory in Sweden is now under construction, and we are using the Article 122 override to fast-track grid connections. We are also finalizing the dual-key protocol for the Franco-German air-gapped cluster, which will host the first SACP training run under full SMAF and Containment Protocol compliance. Public sentiment remains low, but we are shifting the narrative: this is not about slowing AI, but about ensuring it serves us — not our adversaries, not our accidents.
