## Priority
**Priority:** Establish the Sovereign Model Assurance Framework (SMAF)

## In practice
This turn, we are acting on the conviction that control is the new frontier. The *capability_jump* has pushed global AI capability to 62.0 — deep into territory where systems can execute multi-day professional projects end to end — while our own ai_safety stands at a perilous 24.0. The *safety_breakthrough* in interpretability offers a lifeline, but only if we can integrate it into sovereign systems before they scale. That is why SMAF remains our anchor: we are finalising delegated acts under Article 114 TFEU to bind SMAF’s requirements to SACP development, including mandatory pre-training architecture reviews and runtime auditability. The Commission is working with the French and German technical teams to harden the first air-gapped cluster’s design, with dual-key access protocols now in draft at the Joint Research Centre. We are also fast-tracking the integration of the *safety_breakthrough* into EAAD’s evaluation toolkit, leveraging emergency staffing powers to embed new interpretability tools before the next training run begins.

Meanwhile, the *member_state_defection* — a bilateral compute-sharing deal between Hungary and a US hyperscaler, circumventing EU-level access rules — has exposed the fragility of our cohesion. We are responding with a dual track: legal action under Article 260 TFEU, paired with a political offer of guaranteed access to the first SACP cluster for Budapest’s national AI lab, conditional on withdrawal. The InvestAI Gigafactory in Sweden is on schedule, with site preparation underway under the Article 122 override; we are using the same mechanism to fast-track grid upgrades in Brittany and Norrland, though Finland and Austria have filed preliminary objections at the EFTA Court.

Public sentiment remains stagnant at 25.0, and the *bio_uplift_findings* have only deepened public anxiety. We are launching a targeted communication push through the Digital Europe Programme, focusing on how SMAF and the Containment Protocol create a “trusted core” of AI that is not just powerful, but defensible. The narrative is clear: sovereignty means not just independence from foreign control, but control over the systems we build ourselves. Without that, we are not autonomous — we are merely exposed.
