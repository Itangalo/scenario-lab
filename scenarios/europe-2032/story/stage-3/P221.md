# Stage 3 first run — P221 — Plateau, continuity response (run-20260907-145136, seed 5110626522058933561)

**Two-year commitment (turns 10–13):** Prioritize systems that work over systems that are pure, accepting imperfect tools, incomplete readiness and partial compliance where they serve a functioning whole (added turn 10 as `no_time_for_perfection`; prior two-year commitment retired this turn).

**Turn 10 (H1 2031).** A model-generated ransomware sweep — the cyber_major_incident — disables emergency dispatch in three member states, exposing air-gapped fallback nodes that were offline, misconfigured or unreachable when the crisis hit. A capability_jump carries ai_capability +4.0 to 75.0 on sustained autonomous operation in complex simulations, with ai_safety falling to 12.0 on opaque reasoning and openweight rising to 58.0 on partial leakage, while ai_investment_collapse and export_control_escalation tighten the external environment and adoption_success registers a rare visible benefit. The EU answers by launching the Emergency Continuity Response Initiative under Article 122 TFEU, mobilizing €1.8bn and cross-border EAAD teams to 17 high-risk sites with live failover drills, replacing the failed command in Thessaloniki and buying compliance in two resisting capitals through cost coverage and co-ownership. The Continuity Assurance Framework becomes law but is not yet lived, and with the Frontier Access Guarantee still draining resources, capital stays frozen and sovereignty slips on capability-driven decay even as visible repair lifts sentiment slightly.

Metrics: ai_capability 75.0, openweight 58.0, safety 12.0, resilience 53.0, sovereignty 22.0, capital 0.0, sentiment 22.0. Events: cyber_major_incident, capability_jump, ai_investment_collapse, export_control_escalation, adoption_success.

**Turn 11 (H2 2031).** A second cyber_major_incident wave galvanizes a joint_threat_response — a joint cyber cell with real-time telemetry sharing that includes non-EU members — while capability_plateau_evidence appears and the frontier itself stalls at 75.0 with safety flat at 12.0. The EU shifts from emergency response to durable repair with the Sovereign AI Core Readiness Campaign, sending EAAD red teams against 20 designated core nodes: Warsaw and Helsinki hold autonomous failover, while Athens and Bratislava collapse on unpatched dependencies and untrained operators, with full publication of failures sparking debate over transparency versus exposure. Stabilization at the 17 earlier sites plus the finished Continuity Assurance Framework lifts resilience to 57.0 and sentiment to 24.0, with capital edging to 1.0, but enforcement frays as one member state denounces unannounced drills as coercive diagnostics. Rumours of a Global AI Watchdog and a coming aid-disbursement strike narrow the margins further.

Metrics: ai_capability 75.0, openweight 58.0, safety 12.0, resilience 57.0, sovereignty 22.0, capital 1.0, sentiment 24.0. Events: cyber_major_incident, joint_threat_response, capability_plateau_evidence.

**Turn 12 (H1 2032).** Frontier systems stop explaining themselves — opaque_reasoning leaves fluent, correct outputs with blank interpretability tools — just as the emergent_eu_aid_strike freezes Emergency Continuity Response disbursements, stalls Athens and Bratislava mid-upgrade, and suspends new EAAD drills pending negotiation. The US and China announce a narrow us_china_agreement pausing select military AI work without consulting the EU, while capability edges to 75.5 with safety at 2.0 and no corresponding validation. The EU responds with the Human-in-the-Loop Assurance Initiative, co-designing the next red-team phase with the civil service unions around operator rights to pause, demand explanation and appeal, and redirects the Frontier Access Guarantee's final phase toward mandatory operator feedback, delaying its audit by two weeks. Resilience holds at 57.0 on existing fallbacks with no new gains, sovereignty slips to 21.0, and capital and sentiment stay flat at 1.0 and 24.0.

Metrics: ai_capability 75.5, openweight 58.0, safety 2.0, resilience 57.0, sovereignty 21.0, capital 1.0, sentiment 24.0. Events: opaque_reasoning, emergent_eu_aid_strike, us_china_agreement.

**Turn 13 (H2 2032).** Capability inches to 76.0 on inscrutable but correct outputs across research, logistics and forecasting, with safety stagnant at 2.0, as a coalition of mid-sized nations and experts stands up the emergent_global_ai_watchdog — cross-lab audits and early warnings with rapid legitimacy but no enforcement power. Both long-running measures conclude: the Continuity Assurance Framework certifies air-gapped fallbacks in critical services and the Frontier Access Guarantee secures EU-jurisdiction compute access, now being retrofitted with the new oversight conditions. The strike persists and spreads to national digital agencies decrying operational brakes, so the EU launches the Trusted Operator Regime — binding redress rights, 15-minute audit logs, union-backed panels with halt authority — now before the Council's Digital Affairs formation without a final vote this turn. Resilience rises to 59.0 on the completed frameworks, capital recovers to 3.0, sovereignty eases to 20.0, and sentiment holds at 24.0, with the Readiness Campaign wrapping up and the two operator-centred measures continuing into turns 14 and 15.

Metrics: ai_capability 76.0, openweight 58.0, safety 2.0, resilience 59.0, sovereignty 20.0, capital 3.0, sentiment 24.0. Events: emergent_global_ai_watchdog.
