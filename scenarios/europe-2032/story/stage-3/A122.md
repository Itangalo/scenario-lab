Sovereignty eases to 18.0 on capability decay plus the access hit, resilience holds at 55.0, capital at 1.0 and sentiment at 27.0, while backlash_physical spreads through data-centre occupations and grid sabotage in two countries and the Shield build and fallback drafting carry on into the next turns.
