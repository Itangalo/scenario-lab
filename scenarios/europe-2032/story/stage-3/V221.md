Poland and Spain keep resisting full Sovereign Cyber Shield integration, and sentiment and capital sink toward paralysis.

The Commission's bid to give the Accountability Boards binding halt powers under Article 114 TFEU stalls against Polish and Spanish resistance, and protests widen from benefits to migration-processing systems.
