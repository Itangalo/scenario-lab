# Stage 3 first run — V222 — Verification-bounded, tribunal (run-20260907-132741-04, seed 3793806113562981540)

**Two-year commitment (turns 10–13):** Restore political agency and public trust through demonstrable control over AI incidents, transparent accountability, and sovereign response capacity (set turn 10; triggered by the automated_decision_scandal and sustained low sentiment).

**Turn 10 (H1 2031).** The EU launches the AI Accountability Tribunal and €10bn Remediation Fund under Article 45 TFEU, starting with the social insurance case that wronged over 12,000 people – but six member states extract opt-outs and co-governance concessions that gut enforcement before it begins. The Trust Corps dashboard goes live in beta with glitchy Warsaw drills and heckled Lyon simulations, while a capability_jump shows in a Tier 2 Belgian energy breach and openweight climbs on distributed training. Washington extends ASML pressure to mature-node equipment with no domestic frontier compute to fall back on. The Tribunal passage itself costs dearly, leaving capital at 1.0 and sentiment unmoved.

Metrics: ai_capability 68.5, openweight 57.0, safety 12.0, resilience 38.0, sovereignty 7.0, capital 1.0, sentiment 12.0. Events: capability_jump, supply_chain_coercion, automated_decision_scandal.

**Turn 11 (H2 2031).** Frontier access is cut at short notice – hospitals lose diagnostic pipelines, customs stalls, central banks lose forecasting – and the Commission answers with an Article 122 Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation repurposing Gigafactory sites in Finland, Hungary and Spain with €45bn redirected. Execution falters immediately: Hungary delays environmental waivers, NVIDIA and AMD commit to nothing, and the Court of Auditors challenges the bypassed controls with capital at 1.0. Capability creeps to 69.5 with no safety landing, though automated patching systems post a cyber_defence_breakthrough that slightly shifts the offence-defence balance. Sovereignty falls to 5.0 on the access denial plus the capability rise, and sentiment freezes at 12.0 with the Tribunal limping on ignored.

Metrics: ai_capability 69.5, openweight 58.0, safety 12.0, resilience 38.0, sovereignty 5.0, capital 1.0, sentiment 12.0. Events: eu_frontier_access_denied, cyber_defence_breakthrough.

**Turn 12 (H1 2032).** The mobilisation frays further as Hungary formally withdraws cooperation and Spain pauses integration over oversight rights, leaving only sandboxed Finnish experiments toward an Emergency Public AI Service with no live ENISA or CERT data. A fresh cyber_major_incident overwhelms agencies with late, unattributable patches, openweight stalls at 58.0 on fragmented replication, and safety stays flat at 12.0. The Accountability Tribunal is abandoned mid-flight at further cost, and sovereignty drops to 3.0 on renewed access denial plus the capability rise. Capital hits the floor at 0.0 under three measures in flight, and sentiment holds at 12.0 – fatigue rather than consent.

Metrics: ai_capability 70.5, openweight 58.0, safety 12.0, resilience 36.0, sovereignty 3.0, capital 0.0, sentiment 12.0. Events: cyber_major_incident, eu_frontier_access_denied.

**Turn 13 (H2 2032).** The frontier pulls to 72.0 while an openweight_frontier_release narrows the gap to 59.5 without closing it, and the EU's access is severed outright – ministries, hospitals and firms stranded with no reason and no appeal. The Tribunal is formally cancelled as politically untenable, and the Emergency Public AI Service starts repurposing open models on Finnish and Swedish hardware under Article 122 with the Trust Corps publishing logs. Supply_chain_coercion returns on top of the access denial while Spain and Hungary still hold out, so sovereignty sinks to 1.0 and capital stays at 0.0. Sentiment remains frozen at 12.0; the Union keeps operating what it can on open models.
