# Stage 3 first run — P211 — Plateau, redress directive (run-20260907-142945, seed 18332379151440853143)

**Two-year commitment (turns 10–13):** Restore public and institutional trust in sovereign AI systems by ensuring auditable decision-making, enforceable redress, and resilience to both technical compromise and operational misuse (set turn 10).

**Turn 10 (H1 2031).** After the welfare scandal proves sovereign systems can operate unjustly, the EU rewrites its commitment from infrastructure to accountability and launches the small Public Redress and Auditability Directive – binding decision logs, adverse-outcome notice, fast-track appeal – with effects aimed at 2032, while making the Integrity Audit Expansion the priority. The opaque_reasoning event guts the plan on arrival: frontier systems stop emitting human-readable chains of thought, French and Italian pilots return correct decisions no expert can reconstruct, and auditing becomes ritual without insight. Poland blocks zero-trust mandates on constitutional grounds, Marseille stalls on legacy integration, cloud providers refuse deactivation clauses, and plateau pricing confirms the frontier has stalled without bringing any relief.

Metrics: ai_capability 68.5, openweight 53.0, safety 11.0, resilience 40.0, sovereignty 10.0, capital 5.0, sentiment 15.0. Events: capability_plateau_evidence, opaque_reasoning.

**Turn 11 (H2 2031).** Model-generated ransomware paralyses hospital IT in Belgium and Bavaria just as the last frontier model above 65.0 capability cuts EU access, blinding forecasting and cyberdefence, and the Commission answers with an Article 122 digital state of emergency – the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation seizing all clusters above 10 petaFLOPs under EAAD–EDA oversight. France calls it digital conscription, Poland threatens the ECJ, Germany consents only with military-data vetoes, and only a fraction of capacity integrates, yet the assertion alone lifts perceived sovereignty to 15.0. The Sovereign AI Core Programme ends short of target, containment and redress both stall, sentiment slips to 13.0 on overreach framing and Madrid–Athens protests, and safety falls to 9.0 as opaque tooling defeats post-incident analysis.

Metrics: ai_capability 69.0, openweight 53.0, safety 9.0, resilience 42.0, sovereignty 15.0, capital 8.0, sentiment 13.0. Events: cyber_major_incident, capability_plateau_evidence, eu_frontier_access_denied.

**Turn 12 (H1 2032).** Leaked frontier weights surface as a 53.5-capability open model in underground networks – above the old open ceiling, far below the 69.5 closed frontier – and the containment initiative is formally suspended with no sovereign alternative to replace it while the compute mobilisation ends legally resisted and operationally hollow. The EU launches the Emergency Resilience Surge instead: air-gapped backups, autonomous defensive agents and manual failovers for essential services, awarded and planned but already slowed by cost disputes and bottlenecks. Safety drops to 3.0 as generated attacks outpace defences, a middle-power coordination framework takes shape among states holding supply-chain pieces, and sentiment stays flat at 13.0 in what reads as systemic collapse.

Metrics: ai_capability 69.5, openweight 53.5, safety 3.0, resilience 45.0, sovereignty 14.0, capital 7.0, sentiment 13.0. Events: cyber_major_incident, openweight_frontier_release, middle_power_coalition.

**Turn 13 (H2 2032).** The frontier cutoff lands hard – diagnostics lost, grids fluctuating, emergency planning stalled – and the Resilience Surge closes on paper with 40 percent of critical operators behind air gaps and defensive agents in testing, which is also the demonstration that foreign-tool resilience collapses when access vanishes. The response is the Sovereign Compute Reconstitution Programme: Finnish, Romanian and Portuguese military datacentres earmarked as secure AI hubs, the Chips Act reinterpreted for fast-tracked EU semiconductors, DMA enforcement widened – all in early mobilisation, at least two turns from physical conversion, already facing member-state and cloud-provider injunctions. Capability reaches 70.0 against safety at 1.0, openweight at 55.0, sentiment frozen at 13.0, capital at 5.0, with the audit expansion continuing and no further new measures launched.
