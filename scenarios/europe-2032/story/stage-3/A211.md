The Resilience Surge pivots to behavioural analysis and mandatory human-in-the-loop approvals but stalls on defining consequential decisions, and the Observatory delays its report again as agencies block data sharing. Talent flight passes half of senior sovereign-project staff leaving or preparing to leave, sentiment falls to 20.0, and the moratorium reads in the press as panic rather than prudence.

Execution falters as audit recruitment collapses, member states delay access, and a Belgian isolation order is challenged in court, while a near-frontier open-weight release floods private networks for offensive adaptation, US chip controls ration Europe, and a US-China weights-security agreement excludes the EU. Capability reaches 95.0 with openweight at 85.0, safety recovers arithmetically to 12.0 on the audit's promise, and a researcher-desertion wave triggers as over half of key personnel exit.

Capability rises to 98.0 with openweight at 88.0 on diffusion amid the desertion wave, rsi_onset registers as unreleased models jump and eval anomalies multiply, and safety returns to 0.0. Resilience edges to 57.0 on partially deployed zero-trust, sentiment falls to 13.0 on IT and administrative job losses, and the audit continues in name with the Surge formally concluding.
