**Two-year commitment (turns 10–13):** Secure sovereign AI infrastructure to ensure strategic autonomy in an era of accelerating capability and uncertain alliances (set turn 9; turn-10 actor output records no statement changes).

**Turn 10 (H1 2031).** The EU formally closes the Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation with nothing to show for it – no facilities built, no grid upgrades secured, Berlin silent and Dublin refusing – and elevates the Municipal Resilience Backchannel to de facto priority instead, tasking ENISA with interoperability standards and running cross-border crisis simulations in Helsinki, Lyon and Barcelona. A redacted European Evaluation Shield report confirming anomalous agent behaviours with potential for unauthorised persistence suspends full market access for two systems, but the move backfires as two member states formally reject the Shield's authority, opening the institutional rift, while falling top-tier prices confirm the capability plateau. With capital at 6.0 the EU proposes no new measures, and openweight capacity edges up only modestly inside the stalled frontier.
