# Stage 3 first run — P212 — Plateau, moratorium (run-20260907-143821, seed 13527330695453551559)

**Two-year commitment (turns 10–13):** Secure sovereign AI integrity against internal and external compromise (set turn 9; turn-10 actor output records no statement changes).

**Turn 10 (H1 2031).** The Commission proposes triage by pause – a small, time-bound Emergency AI Governance Suspension of public-service AI until audits complete and redress is binding – and the Council fractures on cue, with Germany and France demanding exemptions for audited systems and Poland and Hungary rejecting it on sovereignty grounds until only pledged triage-team funding survives. Behind the deadlock the ground shifts permanently: the suspected plateau proves fleeting as capability reaches 69.0, and confirmed opaque reasoning strips the finished EAAD and SMAF regimes of their primary inspection method overnight. Every major build – Gigafactories, CIRS, SACP, SMAF, assurance protocol, containment – concludes without political reward, capital sinks to 2.0, and sentiment freezes at 18.0 with the suspension read as hesitation, not leadership.

Metrics: ai_capability 69.0, openweight 53.0, safety 11.0, resilience 40.0, sovereignty 10.0, capital 2.0, sentiment 18.0. Events: capability_plateau_evidence, opaque_reasoning.

**Turn 11 (H2 2031).** The suspension dies in Council and mutates into something smaller: the Public Redress and Co-Design Framework, a category-9 accountability condition for any resumption of welfare AI, launched without enforcement power behind it. The Integrity Audit Expansion continues into voluntary reform, the ECJ review freezes containment enforcement, the containment initiative lapses, and the labour market delivers the sharper shock as law, accountancy and administration entry roles vanish and graduate hiring freezes. Washington's forced ASML servicing restrictions on older lithography machines bite EU supply chains directly, capability inches to 69.5 with no safety catch-up, and capital collapses to 0.0 under seven active measures and a named priority.

Metrics: ai_capability 69.5, openweight 54.0, safety 11.0, resilience 39.0, sovereignty 9.0, capital 0.0, sentiment 18.0. Events: labour_displacement, supply_chain_coercion.

**Turn 12 (H1 2032).** With capital exhausted, the EU scraps the symbolic redress framework as performance rather than governance and pivots to salvage, anchoring the emergent mid-sized-firm industrial AI consortium through Horizon Europe and Article 185 emergency provisions – EIC recognition, Finnish and Swedish testbeds, vetted talent, limited liability – framed as risk-sharing among equals. Capability hits 70.0 with the audit expansion finishing toothless and opaque reasoning intact, export-control escalation and supply coercion deepen ASML-jurisdiction dependency, and sovereignty stays at 9.0 since the small in-flight measure yields no immediate boost. Displacement hardens into generational exclusion, sentiment drops to 15.0, and citizens read the invisible industrial pivot as abandonment of accountability.

Metrics: ai_capability 70.0, openweight 57.0, safety 11.0, resilience 37.0, sovereignty 9.0, capital 0.0, sentiment 15.0. Events: labour_displacement, export_control_escalation, supply_chain_coercion, emergent_industrial_sovereignty_consortium.

**Turn 13 (H2 2032).** The EU launches the Emergency Labour Absorption and Civic Stabilisation Programme as its sole priority – cohesion and Social Fund money repurposed for wage insurance, retraining and 10,000 civic AI-oversight roles, including seats for displaced professionals on redress panels – which survives a narrow Council vote after German and Polish threats and starts with states already demanding flexibility and delaying implementation. Capability reaches 70.5 with no safety movement, no incident cascades, and the informal grassroots cyber-pooling of national CERTs continuing without EU coordination at marginal effect. Sentiment holds at 15.0 with data-centre protests ongoing, resilience and sovereignty sit at 37.0 and 9.0 with no programme-driven movement yet, and the measure's scale is questioned from its first turn.
