## New measure
**EU Entry-Work Transition Guarantee**
Pays wage-insurance, paid retraining placements and public-service apprenticeships for displaced graduates and junior staff in automated clerical and support occupations, funded by a levy-contribution from large automating employers.
This and now because graduate hiring has frozen while essential-services trust collapses at capital 12: without a livelihood instrument the two-year commitment to hold services and livelihoods fails on its second half, and a small category 7 flexicurity measure is the only fundable answer before unions and youth resentment block recovery.

Labour ministers get an immediate instrument to show, unions get a seat on allocation, and employers get flexibility in exchange for contribution.
