## Priority
The new Graduate Transition and Wage Insurance Bridge, because without visible income and placement for cohorts with nowhere to go the Cyber Recovery Pact restores systems no one trusts us to govern.

## In practice
We run the Bridge through DG EMPL and EPSCO on an ESF+ top-up plus SURE-style loans: national public employment services register 2029-2031 graduates in the four frozen occupations, pay 6-month wage insurance for on-the-job retraining placements, and require participating firms to report automated entry-role displacement to keep eligibility. Labour ministers who demanded flexicurity get the fund; finance ministers get employer co-pay and a sunset at 12.

We keep M9 as the running recovery cell under DG CNECT/ENISA — pooling clean backups, EuroHPC rebuild time, and rehearsed manual fallback for health, water and energy — and we seek entry to the joint threat-response telemetry pool on the back of that contribution, without opening a second instrument this turn. Therapy delivery continues on human-checked protocols to stop further trust loss while opaque, self-training systems are handled by ring-fencing, not by new EU evaluation we cannot staff at capital 12.
