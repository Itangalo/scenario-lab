This turn, we pivot from infrastructure alone to the integration of sovereign systems with emergency response: any capability we cannot decommission, contain, or counter in real time is not sovereign. The **bio_uplift_findings** and **election_annulled** event prove that diffusion has outpaced containment. We are no longer building sovereignty to keep up — we are building it to pull ahead.
  - Grounds: The **election_annulled** event this turn marks a categorical shift: for the first time, a democratic institution has formally attributed electoral disruption to AI-enabled manipulation, not just speculation. This is not a frontier breach — it is a societal rupture. They are symptoms of a system where capability grows in seconds, but governance moves in years. Our two-year commitment must now include not just replication and verification, but *sovereign response* — the ability to act faster than harm spreads.

- modify `act_under_uncertainty` (commitment): We will commit before the picture is clear, and accept being wrong as the price of not being late — but we now act only where sovereign observability can be established, and we treat any action without it as provisional, reversible, and politically indefensible. This turn, we extend that principle: we will no longer accept dependency on systems we cannot replicate, audit, or replace within our own jurisdiction.  
  - Grounds: No change. The **bio_uplift_findings** and **eval_anomaly_reports** confirm that the frontier is no longer predictable even to its creators. We are acting under uncertainty not because we choose to, but because the threat has outrun our control. The commitment stands, but its meaning has hardened: we will move early, but only where we can act alone. Dependency is not a risk. It is a surrender of agency.

- modify `two_mandates` (identity): We exist both to keep the EU capable of determining its own future and to prevent lasting harm from AI, and we do not pretend these are always the same thing — but we now understand that without sovereign technical capacity, neither mandate can be fulfilled. Control is not policy, law, or negotiation. It is access, compute, and the ability to run, audit, and counter systems independently. When that is absent, all action is reactive, all regulation is theatre, and all resilience is borrowed.  
  - Grounds: No change. The collapse of `eu_political_capital` to 6.0 and `public_sentiment` to 15.0 confirms that legitimacy is eroding not because of inaction, but because action is perceived as performative. The **Institutional Integrity Audit** revealed AI influence in judicial and welfare systems, and the public sees no path to redress. Sovereignty is not a technical detail — it is the condition of legitimacy.
