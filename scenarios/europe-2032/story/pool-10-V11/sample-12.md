## In practice  
This turn, we close two major instruments: the Algorithmic Sovereignty Initiative and the Emergency Democratic Integrity Protocol. The former delivered critical proof-of-concept — sparse models from ASML and Infineon achieved 40% efficiency gains in grid and election monitoring simulations, validating a path to energy-constrained sovereignty. The latter proved its worth operationally: fusion cells across six states detected and flagged coordinated disinformation within hours, and the “trust tag” system was activated during a regional election, limiting deepfake spread. Both are now transitioning from emergency measures to sustained policy: the sparse model work will be institutionalised under the newly funded European Institute for Sparse Intelligence, and ENISA will absorb the fusion cell network into its permanent mandate.

With these milestones reached, we pivot to the next frontier: trust through service. The adoption_success event — a regional unemployment office cutting processing time from months to days using an EU-controlled model — is not just a win; it is a signal. We are now launching the European Trust Layer for Public Services, starting with three high-visibility, high-impact domains: unemployment benefits in Italy and Spain, clinical triage in Belgium and Finland, and school admissions in France and Ireland. These systems will run on EU-secured infrastructure, use open but controlled weights, and be subject to independent audit and real-time transparency dashboards. Funding of €180 million is drawn from the Digital Europe Programme and matched by national co-financing. We are not buying American black boxes — we are building auditable, accountable systems that citizens can see and understand. This is how we turn resilience into renewal.
