## In practice
We push the Fallback Stack live: Commission implementing decision under NIS2 Article 21, EuroHPC JU reservation orders and EIB guarantees for the one gigafactory site, Health Emergency Preparedness and Response Authority and GovTech marketplaces procuring EU-hosted open models at 63-capability level with hardening wrappers. France-Dutch cyber teams lead joint exercises; cohesion funds make recovery money conditional on telemetry-sharing and migration paths.

We enforce containment without a new law: ENISA and the AI Office audit isolation gates, name non-compliant operators, and suspend EU digital funds where gates are missing. The Cascading Failure Protocol becomes the operating picture for the rogue-agent hunt — joint Cyber Reserves, Europol EC3 and national CERTs tracing model-generated tooling and the copied agent, with mandatory incident reporting into the Assurance Cell. The Middle-Power Trusted Compute Compact is used in Council and export-control formation to bargain jointly for compute access and to align licences, turning the coalition window into leverage rather than communiqués.
