## In practice
We push the Fallback Stack through reprogrammed Digital Europe funds and EIB guarantees: EuroHPC reservation orders, procurement preference for EU-hosted hardened open models, and NIS2 fallback-readiness decisions enforced by the Commission with French-Dutch joint exercise teams. Hesitant private co-financiers are offered offtake contracts tied to re-anchoring.

We enforce isolation in parallel under the existing Containment Protocol without opening a second budget line: ENISA and the AI Office audit essential entities, recovery funds are conditioned on telemetry-sharing and gate installation, and the Middle-Power Trusted Compute Compact is used to align export licences and secure supply for replacement hardware. No new labour or liability fight while services are still restoring in weeks not days.
