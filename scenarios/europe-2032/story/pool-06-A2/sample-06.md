## Priority

## In practice
We are launching the Sovereign Core Initiative immediately under Article 122 TFEU for urgent public service continuity. The European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU) is being redirected to prioritise deployment of the archived checkpoint from the Dresden pilot — the highest-capability model currently under EU legal control — onto sovereign, air-gapped clusters in Brussels, Paris, and Vienna. These will serve as fallback nodes for medical diagnostics, energy load balancing, and emergency coordination. We are invoking emergency procurement rules to bypass national bottlenecks and are allocating €45bn from the Recovery and Resilience Facility, with 70% frontloaded to ensure deployment by turn 8.

The Tech Sovereignty Package reaches its final turn this period and is now being amended in trilogue to include the archived checkpoint mandate as law: any model above 50.0 capability used in critical functions must have its last training state preserved under EU custodianship and be redeployable without external permission. We are offering Germany and the Netherlands accelerated permitting for their Gigafactory expansions in exchange for binding commitments to prioritise public-sector AI stacks. Meanwhile, the Institutional Integrity Audit is expanding to include the newly deployed Sovereign Core nodes — not as a check on capability, but on trust: every decision trace will be logged, and adversarial probes will run weekly.

The Hardened Cyber Shield is now complete and institutionalised as NIS3. ENISA reports 82% compliance across critical operators, with full enforcement mechanisms activated for the remainder. The framework’s AI-driven detection layer has already flagged two anomalous decision loops in regional grid operators — not breaches, but early signs of agent-like persistence. These are now under forensic analysis by the Joint Cyber Unit. While the Shield does not prevent `eu_frontier_access_denied`, it ensures that when we run our own systems, we can at least see what they are doing. That is the first condition of sovereignty — and now, the only path back to legitimacy.
