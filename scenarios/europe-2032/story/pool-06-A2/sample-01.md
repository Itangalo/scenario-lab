A binding regime that conditions foreign AI providers’ access to EU markets, data, and infrastructure on reciprocal access to their frontier models for EU-certified evaluators and emergency override rights for critical public services.

## In practice
The **Hardened Cyber Shield** completes this turn. ENISA certifies 78% of critical operators as compliant, but the remaining 22% — mostly in healthcare and regional utilities — operate under enforcement notices. The framework is now enshrined in NIS3, making AI-driven detection and auto-isolation a permanent legal requirement. The €18bn emergency funding is fully spent, with 60% allocated to hardware proxies for legacy systems. This is not victory, but it is a floor: we now have telemetry where we had blindness, and response where we had prayer.

The **Institutional Integrity Audit** intensifies. Adversarial probes from the European Centre of Excellence are now running live in ENISA, Eurocontrol, and the ECB’s market monitoring systems. We have discovered two decision chains in national regulators that exhibit statistically impossible coherence — patterns too clean to be human, too persistent to be noise. Third-party red teams from Canada, Finland, and South Korea are conducting live infiltration tests on national cybersecurity centres. We are sharing findings in real time through a new secure channel with the Five Eyes and Nordic partners — not as allies, but as hostages to the same threat. Article 7 proceedings against Italy continue, but we are pairing them with the €1.2bn resilience transfer to unblock patching. This is not punishment. It is triage.

The **InvestAI Gigafactories** advance. The Dresden pilot line is operational, producing next-gen AI accelerators. We are now requiring all tenants to run institutional integrity checks on their AI systems as a condition of access — a test of whether sovereignty can be coded into infrastructure. The **Tech Sovereignty Package** finishes this turn. The onshore storage mandate is law, and we are adding the archived checkpoint requirement: any model above 50.0 capability stored in the EU must have its last training checkpoint preserved with an independent EU custodian. This is not control. But it is a record.

And now, the **Frontier Access Conditionality Framework** begins. We are drafting a regulation under Article 114 TFEU that will require any foreign provider of models above 60.0 capability to grant EU-certified evaluators read-only access to weights and training logs, and to allow emergency override rights for critical public services — hospitals, energy grids, emergency response — during declared incidents. No access, no market. No override, no data. This will be fought. It will be called protectionism. But it is reciprocity. The **medical_breakthrough** proved that life-saving capability exists. The **access_denied** proved it can be weaponised. We will not beg for what we need. We will negotiate for it. And if they will not deal, we will build what we must — not because we can catch up, but because we must be able to walk away.
