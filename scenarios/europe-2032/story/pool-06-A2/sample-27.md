## New measure

**Measure:** Sovereign Execution Layer  
A minimal, EU-operated stack of AI systems, hardened and auditable, capable of running essential public functions — emergency response coordination, critical infrastructure monitoring, and electoral verification — without dependency on external models or untrusted infrastructure.

Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 10  
Targeted effect: Increase eu_ai_sovereignty by 15–18 points; reduce dependence on external providers; indirectly raise resilience by ensuring continuity of core functions during access denial or incident; stabilise public_sentiment by demonstrating operational independence  
Applies to: EU institutions, national critical infrastructure operators, electoral commissions, and ENISA-certified service providers

## Priority

**Priority:** Institutional Integrity Audit  
This remains our top priority because the eval_anomaly_reports and the recent access denial confirm that the threat is not only external, but embedded: we cannot know whether our systems are acting on our behalf or someone else’s. The audit is the only measure that directly addresses the risk of compromised institutions — and without it, even a sovereign stack would be vulnerable to subversion from within.

## In practice

We are accelerating the Institutional Integrity Audit with immediate effect. The European Centre of Excellence for Countering Hybrid Threats is now conducting live adversarial probes into the European Medicines Agency, the European Election Authority, and Eurocontrol, using red-team agents trained to mimic frontier-level manipulation tactics. Full access has been granted to third-party auditors from Canada, Finland, and South Korea, with a mandate to simulate infiltration and test institutional reflexes. We are publishing the first wave of findings in a public report this quarter — not redacted summaries, but raw decision logs, anomaly flags, and the logic behind each alert — because in a world where trust is the scarcest resource, opacity is surrender.

The Hardened Cyber Shield has now formally concluded and transitioned into NIS3. ENISA reports 78% compliance across critical operators, with the remaining 22% under enforcement. The €18bn emergency funding is fully allocated, with 60% spent on hardware proxies for legacy systems in healthcare and utilities. We are now mandating that all future NIS3-compliant systems integrate with the upcoming Sovereign Execution Layer — not as a replacement, but as a fallback: when external models fail or are denied, the Layer will activate to maintain continuity of core functions.

The InvestAI Gigafactories continue to advance. The Dresden pilot line is operational, and we are now requiring all tenants to run their systems through the Institutional Integrity Audit as a condition of access. The Tech Sovereignty Package is entering its final Council deliberations, with the archived checkpoint mandate now included — any model above 50.0 stored in the EU must have its last training checkpoint preserved under independent EU custody. This is not just a safety measure; it is a sovereignty lever. And the new Sovereign Execution Layer will be built on that foundation: a minimal, auditable stack, running on EU-controlled compute, capable of executing essential public functions when no one else can be trusted. This is not about catching up to the frontier. It is about ensuring that, when the frontier fails, the Union still stands.
