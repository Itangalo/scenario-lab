## In practice

We are launching the Sovereign AI Stack Acceleration with immediate legal effect. The European Commission is invoking Article 114 TFEU to establish a new regulatory framework: the **Sovereign AI Infrastructure Act**. This will mandate that all AI systems used in critical infrastructure, public administration, and national security must be trained on EU-anchored compute, using chips fabricated under EU jurisdiction or allied control, and that all training checkpoints above capability 50.0 must be archived with the newly established **European AI Custodian Authority** in Luxembourg. We are redirecting €45bn from the Digital Europe Programme and the Recovery and Resilience Facility to fast-track three sovereign fabs — in Dresden, Grenoble, and Vilnius — with ASML, Intel, and GlobalFoundries under binding contracts that include IP transfer and audit rights.

The **Tech Sovereignty Package** reaches its final turn this period. While it has mobilised some private capital, its voluntary approach has failed to secure control. We are therefore converting it from incentive to mandate: the new Act supersedes its permitting zones with enforceable siting rights and grid access guarantees, and adds penalties for non-compliance. The Council’s Competitiveness Working Party has raised concerns about market distortion, but we are offering a carve-out for SMEs and a transition period for existing contracts — not as compromise, but as triage. This is not about competition; it is about survival.

The **Institutional Integrity Audit** continues, but its findings are now feeding into the new Stack framework: compromised decision chains are not just a risk to governance, but a design flaw in dependency. We are expanding the audit’s scope to include all foreign-hosted AI services currently used by EU institutions, with a deadline for migration to sovereign infrastructure by turn 10. The **Hardened Cyber Shield** is now law under NIS3, but its effectiveness depends on systems that can be trusted — and we no longer trust systems we do not control. The shield holds, but the ground beneath it is shifting. Only sovereignty can be the new foundation.
