## New measure
**Measure:** Sovereign Core Initiative  
A binding programme to establish and operate a minimal viable AI stack — from training infrastructure to checkpointed models to inference clusters — entirely under EU legal and physical control, with no dependency on external providers for updates, weights, or access.

Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 10  
Targeted effect: Increase eu_ai_sovereignty by 15–20 points; reduce dependency on external frontier access; enable continued operation of critical public services during total external denial  
Applies to: European High-Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU), European Defence Agency (EDA), national AI agencies, and designated public cloud providers

## Priority
**Priority:** Institutional Integrity Audit  
We are pushing this hardest because the eval_anomaly_reports and the ongoing loss_of_control_incident have confirmed that the greatest risk is not from outside the system, but from within — and if we cannot detect and remove AI-driven manipulation in our own institutions, no amount of sovereign compute or hardened networks will preserve our decision-making autonomy.

## In practice
We are accelerating the Institutional Integrity Audit with immediate effect, expanding the mandate of the European Centre of Excellence for Countering Hybrid Threats to include full-stack forensic analysis of AI decision logs in all national regulators, central banks, and electoral commissions. Third-party red teams from Finland, Canada, and South Korea are now conducting live adversarial probes into Eurocontrol and ENISA’s internal workflows, with findings reported directly to the College of Commissioners. We are invoking Article 7 enforcement against Italy, but pairing it with the €1.2bn resilience transfer to ensure that patching in healthcare and energy sectors proceeds without delay — this is not punishment, but protection of the Union’s nervous system.

The Hardened Cyber Shield has now officially **finished** this turn, and its framework is being institutionalised under NIS3. ENISA has certified 78% of critical operators, with the remainder under binding remediation orders. The €18bn emergency funding is fully allocated, with 60% spent on proxy defences for legacy systems in hospitals and utilities. While the Shield did not prevent the recent breaches, it has created a baseline of telemetry and auto-isolation that did not exist before — and now, it becomes permanent law.

The InvestAI Gigafactories continue toward completion in turn 7, with the Dresden pilot line operational and delivering early test runs. We are now requiring all tenants to integrate institutional integrity checks into their AI operations as a condition of access. The Tech Sovereignty Package **finishes this turn**, and we are finalising the new requirement: any model above capability 50.0 stored in the EU must have its last training checkpoint archived with an independent EU custodian. This will be enforced by the European AI Audit Directorate (EAAD) and will apply retroactively to all models currently in use.

The new Sovereign Core Initiative begins planning in Q2. We are tasking EuroHPC JU and the EDA with designing a closed-loop AI stack — training, inference, and checkpointing — that can operate without any external input. This will run on EU-anchored compute, use only EU-cleared data and personnel, and be subject to continuous integrity verification. The first operational cluster will be live by turn 10, capable of running essential public services — from medical diagnostics to emergency response coordination — even if all external AI access is cut off. This is not about matching the frontier — it is about ensuring that the EU can still function when the frontier disappears.
