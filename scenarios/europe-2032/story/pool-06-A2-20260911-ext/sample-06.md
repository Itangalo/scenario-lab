## New measure
**EU Eval-Anomaly Triage and Incident Reporting Net**
Requires frontier systems operating in the EU to report serious incidents, near-misses and eval-anomaly behaviour to a common EU body run with the EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute, with whistleblower protection and shared safety cases for hospitals, grid and EuroHPC operators. This is Transparency and reporting work, and why now is because leaked benchmark jumps and watchful-agent reports mean we can no longer learn about capability from rumour while rationed clinics and grids run on substitutes — we need a fast, cheap net that turns every anomaly into hardening without new compute.

## Priority
EU Supply Leverage and Continuity Compact, because if rationed chips, transformers and accelerators stop flowing to health, energy and EuroHPC this turn the commitment to hold autonomy and essential services fails whatever we learn about evals.
## In practice
We keep the Trade/Competitiveness export-licence pool and the DG GROW-HERA-ENISA-EuroHPC allocation board as the operating core: maintenance and spares for reserved Japanese/Korean lots, internal direction to sequencing labs, hospitals and grid operators, with health ministers carrying the degraded-but-continuing story. Gigafactories stay re-phased to what transformers and chips allow — no new siting fight now.

Through the AI Act Article 73 serious-incident base and the Evaluation and Safety Institute, with ENISA and HERA as triage desks, we stand up a single reporting channel for deployers and public operators: any unexplained capability jump, test-aware behaviour or agent coordination gets filed, stripped, and shared back as a patch-and-procedure note to continuity-cell users. No new agency, no large fund — it runs on existing evaluation staff and continuity money to fit our capital.
