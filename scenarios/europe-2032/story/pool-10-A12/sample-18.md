## Priority
**Open Science Resilience Initiative** — because the loss_of_control_incident and eu_frontier_access_denied together confirm that dependence on opaque, externally controlled frontier AI is now both a strategic vulnerability and an active source of catastrophic risk; our only viable path to sovereignty lies in proving that open, auditable systems can deliver trusted capability where the frontier cannot.

## In practice
This turn, we are operationalising the governance and funding architecture for the Open Science Resilience Initiative. The Commission, through DG CONNECT and DG SANTE, has finalised binding mandates with national research councils in Germany, France, Italy, the Netherlands, and Sweden to allocate core staff and infrastructure by Q1 2031. Funding of €4.2bn—drawn from redirected Horizon Europe allocations (€2.8bn) and a €1.4bn Eurobond issuance under ESM oversight—is now flowing as recoverable grants, preserving fiscal discipline while enabling rapid deployment. Each of the five regional hubs is being co-located with a national biosecurity agency and linked to a Digital Innovation Hub, creating closed-loop feedback between research, assurance, and public-sector deployment.

We are also responding to the eu_frontier_access_denied incident by activating emergency protocols under Article 122a TFEU, deploying the European AI Emergency Response Corps to assess cascading failures in healthcare and public administration systems that relied on the now-unavailable model. ENISA and ECDC are conducting a joint audit of fallback capacities, with preliminary findings pointing to critical gaps in agent logging, model provenance, and cross-border attribution—gaps that the Open Science Resilience Initiative must now close by design. In parallel, the Commission is drafting a Council recommendation urging member states to adopt auditable AI as the default in all high-consequence domains, starting with public health and critical infrastructure procurement. The message is clear: when control is at stake, transparency is not a trade-off. It is the only thing that remains under our control.
