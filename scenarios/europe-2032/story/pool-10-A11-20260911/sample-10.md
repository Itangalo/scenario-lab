**EU Wartime Continuity and Critical Systems Shield**

## Priority
EU Wartime Continuity and Critical Systems Shield — because without kept-running services through great-power war and sabotage, the supply coalition and Guarantee have nothing to stand on.

## In practice
We run the Shield through the European Council and Energy and Telecoms Councils under Article 222 solidarity, tasking ENISA, ACER and HERA with exercised blackout, cable-cut and cloud-loss drills, emergency grid-connection protection for the two gigafactory plots, and guarded corridors for therapy doses and port logistics. Funding comes from repurposed ESF+ and Civil Protection Mechanism top-ups, with mayors and works councils offered protection and bridge pay in exchange for ending blockades and site security cooperation.

On existing books we keep the Middle-Power Supply and Evaluation Coalition as our external lifeline, using pooled bargaining to keep California-queued therapies and frontier access flowing during the war and to press re-entry of the defecting capital, while DG EMPL holds the Transition Guarantee on income support as graduate hiring freezes and DG SANTE keeps triage procurement on EU-accessible certified systems only.
