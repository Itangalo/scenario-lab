## Priority
EU Wartime Continuity Shield — because under bombardment risk and blackouts no supply club, Guarantee or therapy procurement survives if services stop.

## In practice
We run the Shield through the Energy and JHA Councils with ENISA, Europol and the Civil Protection Mechanism, using Article 222 solidarity and TEN-E emergency procedures: military-guarded perimeters for the two gigafactory grid works and landing stations, mandated spares and fuel for hospitals and telecoms, and DG SANTE ring-fencing of therapy doses and triage compute from hourly foreign billing. We ask Berlin and Rome to trade blockade enforcement for bridge-pay acceleration, and the defecting capital to place its bilateral capacity inside the common repair pool.

We keep the Middle-Power Coalition (M10) alive as our supply arm, asking it only for pooled spares, re-routed chips and mutual test recognition for wartime systems, not new frontier access. DG EMPL keeps the finished Guarantee paying out through national schemes for displaced graduates and logistics workers, because hiring absence now reads as abandonment under war.
