# The chokepoint, used by someone else

> **February 2028.** Washington orders a further cut in lithography exports and servicing to China, reaching beyond leading-edge tools to the older machines used for ordinary chips, and in harder drafts to other customers.

The lever is American technology embedded in the Dutch supply chain. For the company in Veldhoven, refusal looks unviable, and it complies. In Brussels the sting is political. The Union's one real bottleneck has been used, by someone else.

Sofia's note documents the order for a possible joint response under the Anti-Coercion Instrument, and does not recommend triggering it. With the funds exhausted and the capitals divided, a rupture with Washington is judged unaffordable. Paris and The Hague warn openly against escalation. The eastern capitals call the inaction a humiliation.

The grid and public-services protection continues under emergency mandates, still the President's priority. The transmission operators are re-audited, the passwords re-rotated, and the Union-funded teams stay in the hit municipalities, hospitals and ports. Officials declare the worst cascading paths closed, again. The operators say what they said before: audits on paper, legacy controllers, missing spares and unpatched hospital networks, unchanged. The energy ministries bridge the bills month to month.

> **March 2028.** The EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute formally opens, with mandatory reporting of anomalies after the autumn's leaked benchmarks.

It gains paper, and no access to unreleased systems. The laboratories join a joint review without pausing a single deployment.

The gigafactory sites in Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm and Warsaw are kept reserved, their permits and grid connections preserved, and unfunded. The bankers have stayed away since the crash. A promised acceleration of the protection effort, named as the priority, produces no acceleration at all: without money or cohesion the timetable does not move, and the Council corridors notice, bitterly.

By June the power-price protests flare again around two sites, and the polls show dependence itself as the story. The Union has conserved itself, and shrunk.
