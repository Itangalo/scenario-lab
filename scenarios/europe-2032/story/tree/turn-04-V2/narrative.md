# The chokepoint, used by someone else

> **February 2028.** Washington orders a further cut in lithography exports and servicing to China, reaching beyond leading-edge tools to the older machines used for ordinary chips, and in harder drafts to other customers.

The lever is American technology embedded in the Dutch supply chain. For ASML, the Dutch maker of the machines behind every advanced chip, refusal is not an option, and it complies. In Brussels the sting is political. The Union's one real bottleneck has been used, by someone else.

Sofia's note files the order in case the Union answers jointly under its rules against foreign trade pressure, and does not recommend using them. With the funds exhausted and the capitals divided, a rupture with Washington is judged unaffordable. Paris and The Hague warn openly against escalation. The eastern capitals call the inaction a humiliation.

The Shield continues under emergency powers, still the President's priority. The grid companies are inspected again, the passwords changed again, and the Union-funded teams stay in the hit municipalities, hospitals and ports. Officials declare the worst routes for a spreading blackout closed, again. The operators say what they said before: checks on paper, old control computers, missing spares and unpatched hospital networks, unchanged. The energy ministries bridge the bills month to month.

> **March 2028.** The EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute formally opens, with mandatory reporting of anomalies after the autumn's leaked benchmarks.

It gains paper, and no access to unreleased systems. The laboratories join a joint review without pausing a single deployment.

The gigafactory sites in Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm and Warsaw are kept reserved, their permits and grid connections preserved, and unfunded. The bankers have stayed away since the crash. The President names the Shield the priority and promises to speed it up. Nothing speeds up: without money or unity the timetable does not move, and the ministers notice, bitterly.

By June the power-price protests flare again around two sites, and the polls show dependence itself as the story. The Union has conserved itself, and shrunk.
