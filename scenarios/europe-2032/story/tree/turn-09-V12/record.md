## World state

### Containment holds, trust breaks
The rapid containment protocol finished in time to matter. Clinics and town halls that had joined the telemetry and locked-backup scheme isolated infected segments, cut cloud links on playbook, and restored from clean copies within hours. Auditors called it the reason a bad autumn did not become a cascading winter.

Everything else slipped. With advanced chip slots still cancelled and foundry letters still citing force majeure, the joint licensing offer to The Hague, Tokyo and Seoul stalled. Partners asked for binding lithography controls first, quotas later. The gigafactory site stayed fenced and idle. Insurers welcomed the liability clarification draft but did not resume cover, waiting for law, not promise.

Then finance fled. Valuations in AI reset violently; announced data-centre expansions were cancelled outright, not delayed. Two co-location options Brussels had counted on for anchored capacity evaporated. American labs slowed training plans; second-tier builders talked openly of catching up.

At home, two opposite stories collided. In cooperating municipalities, audited triage assistants cut waiting lists and administrative decisions that took months came back in days. Nurses named the system as European, and voters noticed.

In the same weeks, an ombudsman and then a court found a benefits and policing support system had systematically harmed people for months. Caseworkers had approved machine-ranked queues at forty seconds a case, logs were written and never read, conformity papers were in order. Opposition and press did not argue the Act was broken in enforcement — they argued the deployment had never been high-risk at all under its categories. Everything done had been lawful. That reading stuck.

The Commission froze the system, published logs, promised workload limits, independent re-checks and a gap-closing implementing act, and launched audited roll-outs of the working triage tools. But coverage framed it as lawful harm, and restriction became the cheap position. Deployments froze beyond insurance.

Brussels ended the year with containment proven, hardware missing, and legitimacy split between visible benefit and lawful scandal.
