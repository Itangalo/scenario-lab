# The sweep

At 07:45 on 11 February 2031, the attack comes as warned. It is not a single intrusion but a wave: machine-written ransomware and a poisoned software library spreading through municipal IT, clinic booking systems and two grid operators. Screens freeze, backups are wiped, and the patch notes arrive faster than staff can read them.

Sofia's recovery corps is still hiring when it hits, and it goes out anyway. Three mobile corps drawn from national response units and contractors deploy to the worst-hit cities. They isolate servers, push vendor patches paid for in advance from emergency funds, and fall back on the paper procedures and offline folders left over from the winter blackouts. The President puts the Commission's standing behind them. Where they arrive, water and hospitals stay up. Where they have not yet arrived, services stay down for days.

> **Sofia:** The corps is on its way.

Brussels publishes daily technical notes and opens a single claims window for energy relief and cyber repair. The mayors say it is the same envelope, renamed twice. The data-centre operators who were curtailed for the grid in the winter are now asked to lend clean capacity for the recovery.

> **March 2031.** An American laboratory demonstrates systems that write and break code, prove theorems and plan intrusions markedly better than before.
>
> **April 2031.** Washington tightens chip and model controls. Allies keep volume licences while others are cut off, and European buyers keep their access on paper, at higher prices and with new conditions.
>
> **May 2031.** Graduate hiring in routine coding, analysis and support collapses. Firms say entry-level drafts are now written by machines and checked by seniors.

The specialists argue over whether the demonstration is general progress. The security teams feel the difference within weeks. The unions call the hiring collapse a white-collar freeze; the ministers call it transition.

Voters see rationed power, ransomed town halls and vanishing first jobs, and blame the machines. Trust falls to nothing.
