# The corps goes in

The spring begins with two shocks at once.

At 22:40 on 3 March 2031, the health authorities confirm what clinics in three member states have been reporting for a week: clusters of severe atypical pneumonia. Sequencing shows an engineered agent, and the investigators find traces of machine assistance in its design. Quarantine wards fill, contact teams deploy, and containment stretches for weeks.

The corps Sofia recommended in the winter, for a threat that was then only a paper, goes in the next morning. It runs under the existing health-emergency and civil-protection law, with no new vote: funded overtime for clinic staff, expanded laboratory surveillance, and the sequencing and wastewater sentinels wired into case-finding. The President puts the Commission's standing behind it.

> **March 2031.** In the same weeks, access to the leading American model goes dark for European users. Hospitals, ministries and logistics firms that had built triage helpers, dispatch copilots and port paperwork tools on it get error messages, with no reason and no appeal.

Staff fall back on older European-hosted open models, and on paper. What prevents collapse is work already done. The municipal backup kits, the offline triage procedures and the upgraded monitors, installed last year under emergency health powers, carry the case-finding when the networks jam. The Union's cyber and civil-protection teams stay embedded in town halls and hospitals, rebuilding from offline copies and keeping emergency dispatch on workarounds. The corps adds a cleared list of fallback models to run on European soil, paid for with reprogrammed digital funds. The mayors credit the crews, even as the queues lengthen.

No new factory money is voted, and the sites are kept warm. No retaliation is attempted, for the cut-off or for the extended curbs on servicing chipmaking tools.

> **May 2031.** A sharp advance in code-writing and intrusion tools is demonstrated abroad.

It makes defence harder within weeks, and machine-written break-ins continue against clinics and ports. The public mood, exhausted by the outages and now frightened by the outbreak, turns to open hostility in places. That services degraded rather than stopped is noticed. The gratitude is thin against the fear and the fatigue.
