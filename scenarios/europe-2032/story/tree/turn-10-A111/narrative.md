# Legitimate targets

> **17 February 2031, 04:10.** American and Chinese forces are striking each other's satellites and undersea infrastructure, defence officials in several capitals confirm. Each side says the other was months from a lead that could never be caught.

Within weeks both capitals have declared fabrication plants, cable landings, satellite ground stations and data centres legitimate targets. Two cables are cut in the Atlantic. A fab yard in the Pacific is hit. Power and parts markets panic.

The College meets that afternoon, and again that night. By morning the Union has declared itself neutral and offered its ports, cleanrooms and test sites as neutral ground, on condition that both sides leave European sites alone. Neither capital gives a firm answer.

The continuity shield Sofia recommended in the winter becomes a wartime instrument in a week. An emergency joint session of energy and defence ministers puts guarded repair battalions on the two gigafactory grid connections, gives hospitals and certified clinical computing first call on power, and lines up failover for cables and data centres that are hit. It is paid from the envelopes that already exist. Nobody asks the finance ministers for new money, because everybody knows the answer.

Europe is hit anyway. A drone damages a transformer in the Netherlands. Debris warnings go out over the Mediterranean. Medical inference is rerouted in a hurry, and hospitals flicker.

> **March 2031.** Night-time arson and blockades spread from robot pilot sites to the grid-connection works for the gigafactories. Construction halts for weeks.
>
> **April 2031.** A new freely downloadable model matches last autumn's frontier. Municipal IT departments are running it on local servers while national regulators are still reading the licence.
>
> **May 2031.** The Transition Guarantee closes, described as a modest success. Bridge pay reached a fraction of applicants before finance ministers froze the top-ups. Mayors call it burial money.

Power priority for certified clinics holds in most places. The therapy queues lengthen anyway, because the licensed doses and scheduling hours are rationed from abroad. The middle-power club meets in emergency session, pools its orders for spare parts, and gets no vendor to reroute a chip. The capital that walked keeps its separate pact and asks to be exempted from wartime solidarity.

The frontier laboratories go dark, or under military contract. What little leaks suggests the training never stopped.
