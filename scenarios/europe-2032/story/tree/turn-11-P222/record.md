## World state

### Listed to unlisted
The black-start network formally completed in autumn: tested islanding plans, manual fallback binders and reimbursement rules in every member state. On paper, restoration by hand was now Union doctrine.

On the ground it was already being unmade. Arson and intimidation against the guarded depots spread through the second half of the year — stores torched, drivers threatened, several municipalities quietly pulling their spares off the European listing and back into off-books pools. Brussels answered by un-listing: dispersed micro-stores held by towns, hospitals and grid operators, reimbursed flat, guarded only on request, with no public map. Some holders returned. Others kept two sets of books, convinced any list was a target list.

Washington tightened export licensing again, and under its country-tier system allied buyers stayed inside but on volume quotas. For Europe that meant licences without volume: chips and model access metered, Dutch transit complied under protest, and with private AI capital collapsed there was little to buy in any case. Taipei's exercises and rising shipping insurance hardened the sense of a strait that could close.

### The wrongful queue
Then the scandal broke. An ombudsman and then a court found a social-insurance and policing support system had systematically wronged thousands — reduced benefits, flagged families — with human oversight reduced to seconds per case and logs generated but never read. The Commission suspended the system, opened coordinated enforcement with the AI Office and supervisors, published logs and promised case review and back-pay. It argued the system was high-risk and obligations had been breached on paper.

The public heard the second reading: lawful throughout, badly drawn law. Health-scheduling tools that still cut waits from months to days could not separate themselves from the story. Depot guards were heckled as enforcers of the same machine administration. Vandalism of grid kits returned.

Washington and Beijing announced a limited risk agreement on weights security and dangerous design tools, with thin verification. Brussels was informed afterwards. A new open-weight release, near the closed frontier, was downloaded hundreds of thousands of times in its first week. The common line held, thinner and unmapped.
