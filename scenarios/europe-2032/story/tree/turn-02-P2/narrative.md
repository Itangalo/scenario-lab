# Grids first

The spring belongs to the grid operators. After the autumn's discovery of intruders who had lived inside the networks for weeks, Sofia's first new measure is not the Institute she recommended – that is still hiring – but the grid shield, a programme hardening the power grids, and with the new year the President moves political capital onto it. ENISA pushes through emergency standards for segmentation – sealing networks into sections an intruder cannot cross – and the two grid operators that were hit, along with their peers in the five states hosting the planned gigafactories, are ordered to install network monitoring and pool their telemetry: the breaker logs of every switch.

> **March 2027.** Energy ministers endorse the mandate.

On paper it moves fast. On the ground the suppliers slip, the data formats clash, and eastern operators hold out for EU regional-development money before they sign anything.

The gigafactories and the tech sovereignty package for data centres grind on. Digital officials advance site selection and the fast-permit zones, but land deals stall over grid pledges, the financing waits on the long-term budget, and no EU borrowing is tabled. The Institute, hiring people to test cyber tools, has no independent results yet.

> **April 2027.** Washington tightens chip and model export licences again. Early drafts suggested volume licences for allied buyers. The final text rations allies alongside adversaries, capping high-end shipments and access to cloud computing.

European buyers face queues and paperwork. Talks linking cooperation on the factories to better terms produce communiqués, not licences. Using ASML's machines as leverage is discussed in Brussels, and not used.

The evening news ties the autumn's intrusions to freely available models, and councils in one candidate region freeze permit hearings after residents cite the strain on power and water. There is no blackout; the grid stays quiet. But the costs of containment and the delayed investment leave public sentiment more anxious than before.
