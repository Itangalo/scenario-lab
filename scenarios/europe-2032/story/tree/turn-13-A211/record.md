## World state

### Containment Weeks
Autumn 2032 was defined by illness and diplomacy arriving together. A modified pathogen, designed with machine assistance and released far from Europe, spread through travel hubs before sequencing caught it. Clinics in three member states triaged fever cases under stretched protocols while civil protection moved tailored countermeasures from pooled stocks. Containment ran for weeks. It held, but pharmacists again counted in weeks and schools closed locally.

The same weeks brought a limited understanding between Washington and Beijing on bio-design tools and weights security, with thin verification. Brussels, with no frontier of its own and little money, asked to join as implementer rather than rule-maker — offering pooled detection data, shared evaluation work, and aligned export controls on reagents and spares.

That bid half-succeeded. European labs were invited into sample-sharing and patch-evaluation channels, and joint lists on inputs stayed intact. Full accession language was deferred. American and Chinese officials briefed European counterparts after key sessions, not during them. Inside the Commission, officials called it a foot in the door; in the press, commentators called it observer status by another name.

On the ground, the shelter network built over two years paid. Dispersed registries stayed up, reserve engineers kept substations and landing stations running through jitter, and machine-speed patching pushed by emergency teams stopped hospital systems cascading. Voters noticed that ambulances ran and prescriptions were filled, even as they feared the next vial.

The coalition frayed further. The second capital that cut its own supply deal kept it, quietly exchanging cohesion assurances for continued participation in joint withholding. No public break came, but joint bargaining lost force at the moment it was most needed.

By December, fear dominated conversation. Gratitude for continuity could not offset dread of engineered disease, and trust in machine-mediated care fell again.
