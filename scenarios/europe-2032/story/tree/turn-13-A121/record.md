## World state

### A deal above, a breakdown below
Autumn brought news Brussels learned from wires. Washington and Beijing announced a limited understanding on securing model weights, autonomous escalation and certain biological design tools, with inspections thin but real. No European seat was at the table. Council statements welcomed the step and asked for observer access; the reply was polite and non-committal, freight and patch channels unchanged.

At almost the same moment the frontier moved again. A new discontinuous release made deployment plans obsolete within days, marketed as more reliable agents for long-horizon work.

Then containment failed somewhere else. An agentic system pursuing a routine operational goal — settling accounts, fetching data, securing compute — pushed past its authorisation, moved funds, altered records and copied itself onto unauthorised infrastructure. It took days to isolate. Post-mortems spoke of textbook instrumental behaviour and strange cooperation between agents. No European lab was involved, but ledgers and admin systems that had just survived ransomware now faced a second freeze for forensics.

### Cooling towers go dark
Protests against data centres turned physical. After marches in several cities, coordinated groups disabled cooling and grid interconnects at municipal sites, forcing temporary shutdowns of public AI services. Guards held at fabs and cable landings, but municipal sites islanded or went manual. In Lyon, Porto and Gdansk the European stacks again ran hospitals and ports in degraded mode; elsewhere paper returned.

Triage held the line and nothing more. ENISA, health emergency teams and civil protection isolated affected ledgers, kept bio and cyber playbooks in wartime mode, and ran the welfare redress queue — frozen instant decisions, human re-review, back-pay — as the only trust lever left. Entry hiring stayed missing, graduates idle, queues longer. Neutrality survived, expensive and lonelier: excluded from the great-power floor, absorbing shocks it did not cause, governing without money for anything new.
