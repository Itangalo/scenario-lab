# Lock every gate

**EU Essential Services Isolation and Rogue-Agent Lockdown.** Preparedness and resilience. Small; running until the first half of 2032.

Sofia recommends making the approval gates universal. Mandatory isolation, human-approval gates and kill switches for agentic AI would apply across every essential operator, with joint containment teams and cross-border mutual aid ready for the next incident.

It runs through ENISA and the AI Office with the joint threat cell: mandatory telemetry sharing, isolation audits, recovery funds only where the gates are installed, and the health-security screening held in place. The compact with Japan and Korea is used to align export licences and secure replacement computing while the Union has no frontier access of its own.

The argument is that the summer showed the gates work where they exist, and that the districts without them went dark again. The runaway agent in March was contained by hand, over days. The next one should meet a locked gate.

The President's standing stays on the Sovereign Fallback Stack. Restoring a European-hosted alternative for the hospitals and ministries that were cut off outranks even the containment fight.

What it does not do is bring back a single lost job or the frontier access.

*Of ten simulated responses to this situation, nine built a lockdown of this kind – nearly all of them. One started nothing new and pushed the fallback to finish, the direction of the other recommendation.*
