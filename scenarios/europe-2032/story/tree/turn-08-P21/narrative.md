# The standby goes dark

> **February 2030.** Access to the leading American model stops for European users, with short notice and no explanation.

Since the first cut-off in 2028, hospital managers had quietly kept the old American workflows on standby wherever a licence still flickered. Hospitals, ministries and firms that kept critical work on it, despite months of warnings, find their queries refused overnight. The Commission's digital department and ENISA spend weeks rerouting wards and offices onto the continuity plan's European models and the domestic helpers that clinicians still dislike.

> **Claire:** the american standby is gone. the one the managers kept
> **Claire:** we're fully european now. by force
>
> **Sofia:** It was always going to happen.
>
> **Claire:** everyone knew. nobody moved. now everyone moved in a week

The substitution keeps services running, more slowly. Imaging queues lengthen again in France and Germany, the referral templates are rewritten a second time, and the locum money from the autumn cannot cover the extra hours. The regional papers call it humiliation. The union leaders call it foresight that failed.

Almost at once, Berlin confirms it has secured its own supply arrangement with a large foreign provider, on separate terms. Sofia's answer is a pact under which no member state strikes its own deal for AI access, and the President declares it the Commission's business and not a capital's. Behind closed doors in the Council, Berlin is offered a share of whatever access the Union buys together, and of future factories, if it freezes its deal. Its government keeps its deal, and calls it pragmatism to keep its hospitals lit.

The cohesion frays. The Commission obtains a mandate to seek clarification and a timetable for restoring access from Washington, and gets polite language and longer lead times in return. No new factory ground is broken. Two sites pour concrete between water-permit freezes and night blockades, which the local press now connects into a pattern while officials still describe each one as isolated.

> **April 2030.** Commercial humanoid robots arrive in warehouses arranged around them, lifting and carrying where the floors are marked for them and stopping everywhere else. Defence pilots for carrying and mine clearance remain pilots.

The manufacturers temper their claims.

By June, the wards are open on European systems, the grid has sealed off the spring faults cleanly, and the Union holds a pact on paper. It has thinner staff, angrier publics, and open doubt about whether the common line will hold.
