# The watch floor

> **February 2031.** Allied capitals invite the Union to pool live intrusion data and coordinate its response across borders.

The invitation arrives while the clinics are still running isolation drills, and the ministers accept it. Liaison officers from the Union's computer emergency team move onto the shared watch floor, and the cooperating municipalities wire their locked backups and cut-link playbooks into the common feed.

> **Claire:** we're on the shared feed now
> **Claire:** our IT guy calls the watch floor the aquarium. he's never seen it
>
> **Sofia:** It's a room with a lot of screens.
>
> **Claire:** so an aquarium

It works, narrowly.

> **April 2031.** A spring wave of extortion software probes municipal systems in three countries.

It is spotted faster, because the attribution arrives with the telemetry. The cooperating towns isolate and restore in hours. The ones that did not join stay dark longer, and the divide hardens into routine: protected inside, exposed outside.

Nothing else comes back. The licensing talks with the Dutch, Japanese and Korean partners stay stuck on their preconditions about binding controls on advanced lithography, and quotas are not even discussed. The fenced gigafactory site stays fenced.

> **June 2031.** The Chips-for-Compute and Civic Liability Pact is declared complete.

The insurers study it, welcome its language on cover for audited deployments, and still decline to underwrite new roll-outs until Parliament turns the promise into a statute.

At home, the trust repair that the President has put the Commission's standing behind stalls halfway. The frozen benefits and policing system stays frozen, its logs are published with a window for redress, and workload limits and independent re-checks are imposed on paper. The audited triage assistants in health administration keep cutting queues, and the local press credits them. National coverage keeps the other frame: what harmed people for months was entirely lawful. Opposition mayors refuse to expand triage, citing the court findings and the missing insurance, and the Commission's money sits unspent for lack of eligible sites.

Brussels ends the half-year more connected abroad, still without capacity at home, and still arguing that lawful does not mean trustworthy.
