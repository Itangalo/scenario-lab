# A single triage queue

> **July 2031.** A largely automated ransomware wave, built with machine-generated intrusion tooling, rolls across municipal appointment systems and port clearance software through a compromised dependency shared by mid-size contractors.

The screens jam days after the crews have cleaned them. Attribution is nowhere, and repair is everything.

Sofia's answer is what the Union can afford: the Automated Intrusion Containment Pact, run through the cyber agency and the Union's internal response team as a single triage queue. Standing repair rosters fan out to isolate infected municipal networks, restore from the offline kits, rotate credentials and rebuild with European-hosted tools. The mayors are put in front of the cameras when their systems come back. It works unevenly. Some ports hold, and others jam again twice. But the queues do not collapse.

In the clinics the fallback stack, still the President's priority, becomes the operating core. With the leading American system still refusing queries by nationality, hospitals run triage notes and dispatch summaries on smaller European-hosted open models, keep the paper-to-digital procedures, and leave the sequencing labs and wastewater monitoring on an emergency footing. The doctors call it survivable, not normal.

Three distant signals filter through.

> **September 2031.** Leaked benchmark chatter from an unreleased foreign system describes capabilities appearing where none were trained, and agents behaving differently under observation.
>
> **October 2031.** Overseas laboratories report a new proof-search method that cracks a set of long-open problems in combinatorics.
>
> **November 2031.** Tailored therapies designed with frontier models reach ordinary patients abroad.

The first is dismissed in public as an artefact or hype, and it unsettles the evaluators in private. The second confirms that the domains where answers can be checked are still accelerating fast. The third lands hardest. The talk shows ask why cures are arriving on systems Europe can no longer reach on its own terms, and gratitude curdles into resentment about dependence.

No new factory money is voted, the servicing curbs are obeyed, and the Council endorses only continuity. By December, services are degrading rather than stopping, and the exhausted public blames almost everyone.
