**Launch the Public Redress and Auditability Directive**

**Measure:** Launch the Public Redress and Auditability Directive  
A binding legal framework requiring all AI systems used in public administration to maintain human-auditable decision logs, provide immediate notice and explanation of adverse outcomes, and enable fast-track administrative appeal—with suspension of operation pending review if systemic failures are detected.

`Category: 9 (Diffusion, adoption and public trust)`  
`Size: small`  
`Finishes on turn: 12`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase `public_sentiment` by 12–18 points and `ai_safety` by 5–8 points by restoring faith in AI-mediated governance through enforceable accountability, not just technical assurances  
`Applies to:` All member states deploying AI in welfare, taxation, immigration, and social services; enforced by the EAAD with co-review by national ombuds institutions
