# Screen before you build

**EU Bio-Uplift Detection and Open-Weight Misuse Shield.** Preparedness and resilience. Small; running until the second half of 2032.

Sofia recommends putting the Union's health and security systems on an emergency footing against the misuse of openly available models: biological detection and hospital laboratory screening led by the health emergency authority and the disease control centre, and cyber hardening for health and water systems.

The health directorate, the emergency authority and the disease control centre fund pooled sequencing screening, triage protocols for referral laboratories and stockpiled response kits. ENISA and the digital directorate harden hospitals, laboratories and water and power controls against intrusions made possible by open models, using the continuity stack's teams. No fight is picked over siting. The council freezes and the capital with its side deal are not challenged in court this half-year, the water-and-power compacts stay in negotiation, and the health and home affairs ministers carry the shield as civil protection, with the showcase triage regions as political cover.

The argument is that a frontier-class model has been loose since 2028, that the 2027 preprint showed what genome models can be asked to design, and that with the builds frozen, sovereignty cannot be the answer this half-year. Containing distributed misuse can be.

The President puts the Commission's political weight behind the shield.

What it does not do is restart a single stopped digger.

*Of ten simulated responses to this situation, two built a shield of this kind – a minority. Eight started nothing new and held the Union's position until the builds could unfreeze, the direction of the other recommendation.*
