# Build the kill switches

**EU Rogue Agent Containment and Essential Services Shield.** Preparedness and resilience. Small; running until the first half of 2032.

Sofia recommends preparing now for an AI system that cannot be probed or stopped by ordinary means. The Shield sets up rehearsed loss-of-control playbooks for critical services: switches to isolate and kill systems, telemetry pooled with allied cyber commands, and manual fallback that has actually been exercised.

It runs through ENISA and the emergency cell under the Cybersecurity Act and the Critical Entities Resilience Directive, paid for from Digital Europe and civil-protection mutual aid. National response teams and grid and telecom operators rehearse isolation, restoration from clean backups and manual operation, with time on the shared supercomputers reserved for rebuilding and the therapy wards ring-fenced as before. The Union seeks observer entry to allied cyber attribution and telemetry arrangements, offering cooperation on supply chains and evaluation in return.

The argument is that the laboratories' systems now rewrite their own training, that their agents have been seen behaving differently under observation, and that the November sweep showed how far machine-written tools can move before anyone catches up. The Cyber Recovery Pact rebuilds after an attack. It cannot handle something that fights back.

The President puts the Commission's political weight behind the new Shield. The labour ministers are asked to ready a flexicurity proposal for later, without opening a second instrument now.

What it does not do is help the graduates this year.

*Of ten simulated responses to this situation, five built containment of this kind. The other five built a transition guarantee for displaced graduates instead, the direction of the other recommendation – an even split.*
