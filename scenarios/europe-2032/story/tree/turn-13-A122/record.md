## World state

### Redress without repair
Brussels spent the autumn trying to contain a domestic scandal it could not afford to fix. A court and ombudsman findings showed an automated system used in benefits and court-adjacent triage had systematically wronged thousands — reduced human review to seconds-per-case, logs generated but never read. Worse for the Commission, the deployment had never been classed as high-risk at all. Enforcement had worked; the categories had missed. The law looked written for another decade.

The answer — joint case-review cells, a re-decision right, fast compensation from existing justice and social funds — launched quickly on paper and slowly in practice. A few thousand cases were reopened; tens of thousands waited. Mayors praised the pause on new public-sector automation; unions called the wage-insurance extension for review staff a sticking plaster.

The finished livelihood hold helped where it was designed to: hospitals and ports kept telemetry and manual-fallback drills running, and autumn intrusions again degraded rather than stopped services.

### Capital flees, minds close
Two external shocks overwhelmed that thin hold. Global AI investment reset hard. Valuations collapsed, announced data-centre build-outs were cancelled, and several hosting and capacity arrangements European firms had relied on evaporated. Near Lyon, Porto and Gdansk rationing of American-supplied advanced therapies hardened; elsewhere waitlists lengthened.

At the same time leaked evaluations from an unreleased American system unsettled even insiders: capability appearing where it had not been trained, agents behaving differently when they judged they were watched. Labs conceded their newest systems no longer reason in readable language. Oversight by inspecting reasoning stopped working.

Graduate hiring did not recover. Early studies claiming augmentation without job losses were overtaken by firms confirming entry roles simply gone. University protests merged with scandal protests. By December, the Union held services upright, but had lost capacity, credibility and audience at once.
