## World state

### The attack and steady pressure
Autumn brought two strains at once. A largely automated intrusion swept municipal IT, hospital administration and two grid operators, locking records, forcing appointments onto paper and cutting billing and dispatch to manual fallback for days. Restoration teams worked visibly behind the tooling that hit them, and attribution remained open at Christmas. In the same weeks an American lab demonstrated improved autonomous capability — longer planning and tool use in controlled demos — adding to existing deployment pressures without overturning prior timelines overnight.

Brussels answered with a restoration surge run through emergency communications teams and civil response channels. Joint response units were sent to the hardest-hit towns, offline backups were mandated for hospitals and utilities, and manual-operation drills became compulsory. Where crews arrived, services degraded rather than stopped. Where they had not yet arrived, mayors reported queues and dark offices, and anger mixed with relief. Rollout remains partial and will take another turn to reach full effect.

### Small reliefs
Two older programmes formally closed. The computing-site permits and grid reservations became law, and the bio-monitoring network was declared operational. The municipal wage bridges and staffing pacts also closed, with videos of shorter queues in cities that received crews. The computing sites themselves remained fenced fields waiting on chips, now further delayed by nighttime fence-cutting, cable damage and halted works reported in local press as vandalism with unclear coordination.

Counterweights appeared. A new interpretability and control result that modestly improved prediction on deployed systems was taken up by some labs and began to be pressed into public-sector deployments in Europe, and negotiators in health secured early supply terms for newly arrived tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions. Patients treated under the first courses gave the autumn its only unambiguously positive coverage, lifting public sentiment while political capital gained only modestly from legitimacy.

Washington's published allied-access terms remained in drafting at the Trade and Technology Council, with no new European firm onboarded. Shippers quietly noted rising insurance quotes on Taiwan Strait routes after extended exercises there, and port audits of foreign-steered warehouse robots in Rotterdam, Hamburg and Lyon were repeatedly postponed as inspectors were diverted to cyber triage.
