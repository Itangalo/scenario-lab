# Know what you are dealing with

**Your commitment for the next two years: secure foundational AI capacity under EU control to anchor sovereignty and risk resilience.**

You cannot govern what you cannot inspect. You establish the European AI Assurance Directorate, housed inside ENISA at first and standing on its own by the second half of 2028, with a mandate sharper than the AI Act it is meant to operationalise: red-team evaluation of any frontier model before it may be deployed in the Union, and the legal authority to delay a release. A regulation under Article 114 gives it access to model weights, training-data summaries and internal logs during evaluation windows, and it reaches any model deployed here regardless of where it was built. Funding comes from a levy on non-EU hyperscaler revenue in the single market — you expect the legal challenge, and intend to defend it as proportionate.

What you will spend your standing on, though, is not the Directorate. It is the InvestAI Gigafactories fund you inherited: activated through the EIB and the Recovery and Resilience Facility, five sites where land, power and cooling are already part-secured, 15GW of planned grid capacity reserved with ENTSO-E. Without the metal, no evaluation regime holds.

This buys you sight, and standing to say no. It buys no capacity of its own, and the Directorate cannot act alone for two years.

*Thirty independent simulations of this decision were run. Five answered with evaluation and oversight, as this one does — behind industrial capacity at twelve and public technical capacity at ten.*
