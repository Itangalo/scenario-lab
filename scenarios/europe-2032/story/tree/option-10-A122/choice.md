# Let the Corps land

**No new measure.** The Union runs what is already on the books.

Sofia recommends starting nothing new. The Corps should land, and the Commission should rebuild its political capital, before anything else is attempted.

The EU's Social Fund for jobs and training pays for the Corps's placements in hospitals, municipalities and the guarded build sites. The Corps's wage insurance requires common purchasing terms, to keep Czechia's side deal in check. The joint response teams and ENISA keep hospitals and ports degraded rather than stopped, and civil protection keeps guarding the fenced sites and the chip installations. If something goes wrong in the Union's own AI-supported services, the answer will be an ombudsman-led redress and a narrow patch to the Act, not a broad restriction nobody could enforce.

The argument is that with the Commission close to spent, visible jobs and services that keep running outrank any new build.

The President's political capital stays on the Corps.

What it does not do is prepare anything for what comes next.

*Of ten simulated responses to this situation, three chose to wait like this – a minority. Seven built redress and oversight for AI-supported public decisions, the direction of the other recommendation.*
