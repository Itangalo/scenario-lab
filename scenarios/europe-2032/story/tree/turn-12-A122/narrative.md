# Allocations

The first half of 2032 does not feel like recovery. Freight on the Far East routes is still prohibitive, cable crews work under guard, and the Continuity Command keeps hospitals and ports on satellite backup and rationed power.

> **February 2032.** An American laboratory under federal control releases a discontinuous advance. Benchmarks are rewritten overnight.

Brussels receives the technical summary through the queue between states, days after others have acted on it. Behind it, distilled versions of the previous generation spread to private servers everywhere, beyond any recall, and no evaluation regime can see inside the newest closed systems.

> **March 2032.** Automated patching and detection of swarm behaviour close a whole class of intrusion for the first time in years.

ENISA rushes the tooling to hospitals, ports and grid operators under the Command, and intrusion rates fall wherever it lands.

> **April 2032.** Tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions are demonstrated in American clinics.

The therapies reach Europe as allocations, not products. Doses and access to the grid-dispatch models are bargained for lot by lot. The editorials that praised a cure in April denounce dependence by June. Graduate hiring stays frozen, and the Corps's placements in town halls cannot cover the gap.

> **Claire:** we got twelve doses this month
> **Claire:** twelve. for the whole region
>
> **Sofia:** It will be more next month.
>
> **Claire:** is that a promise or a hope
>
> **Sofia:** It's a queue.

So Sofia recommends that the Union stop queuing alone. The Middle-Power Continuity and Access Pact goes through the trade ministers, with the Anti-Coercion Instrument's preparatory track behind it: aligned export licences, pooled evaluation, and a single common queue for medical and grid models. The eastern capital's side deal is folded in as its contribution to a coalition stockpile rather than punished. Paris and Berlin accept the compromise, because it gives them the joint bargaining they lacked. The eastern capital calls it vindication.

The licences flow slightly faster. The capacity does not. By June the Union has leverage it did not have in December, and very little relief to show for it.
