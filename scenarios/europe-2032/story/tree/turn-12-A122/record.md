## World state

### A jump in the middle of the war
The first half of 2032 did not feel like recovery. Freight remained prohibitive on Far East routes, cable repair crews worked under guard, and the Continuity Command kept hospitals and ports on satellite backup and rationed power. Into that scarcity came a discontinuous advance from an American lab under federal control: benchmarks rewritten overnight, planning documents obsolete within days. Brussels received the technical summary through the state-to-state queue, days after others acted on it.

Open weights crept closer behind, as distilled versions of the prior generation spread to private servers everywhere. No authority could recall them, and no evaluation regime could see inside the newest closed systems.

### Medicines that arrive as a queue
The same quarter brought tailored therapies for previously untreatable conditions, demonstrated in American clinics, and a genuine defensive advance — automated patching and swarm-behaviour detection that closed a whole class of intrusion for the first time in years. ENISA rushed the patching tooling to hospitals, ports and grid operators under the Continuity Command, and intrusion rates fell where it was deployed.

But the therapies arrived in Europe as allocations, not products. Doses and model access for grid dispatch were bargained lot by lot. Editorials that had praised a cure in February denounced dependence by April. Graduate hiring stayed frozen across law, accountancy, software support and administration, and placement corps in town halls could not cover the gap.

### The coalition becomes the policy
Faced with queuing, the Union folded its eastern bilateral stockpile deal into a joint front with other supply-chain holders — aligned export licences, pooled evaluation, a common queue for medical and grid models. Paris and Berlin accepted the compromise; the eastern capital called it vindication. Licences flowed slightly faster, though capacity did not.

Services held where the Command reached and frayed where it did not. By June the Union had leverage it lacked in December, but little relief to show for it, and public mood remained bleak.
