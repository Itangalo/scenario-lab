# Push the defence where the drills failed

**EU Defensive Breakthrough Deployment and Bio-Detection Sprint.** Preparedness and resilience. Small; running until the first half of 2032.

Sofia recommends taking the defensive tools that worked in the certified hospitals and pushing them into every place the drills failed – uncertified municipal utilities, small hospitals – while standing up rapid biological detection before anyone needs it.

It runs through ENISA and the health emergency authority, with mandates from the Health and Telecoms Councils, on the existing Shield budgets plus repurposed Digital Europe money. ENISA pushes automated patching and swarm detection to the national response teams, municipal utilities and certified hospitals, and requires the containment protocol to be exercised again wherever last year's drills failed. The health emergency authority contracts hospital laboratories and detector sites for wastewater and clinical sequencing, linked to the compact's swap of incident data for shared black-box test results. At home the spending line holds: the gigafactory concrete and Shield certificates continue on old money, and the labour ministers finish their unfunded transition plan, so that it can be funded if the Commission's standing recovers. The holdout capital is offered priority access to the new defensive tooling, and pooled hosting, if it aligns its export criteria and unwinds its side deal.

The argument is that the detectors are the first defensive gain in years and should reach the places that need them most, and that genome models have been designing working viruses since 2026 while DNA screening stays voluntary. Detection has to come before any recipe spreads.

The President puts the Commission's political weight behind the sprint.

What it does not do is put money on a single empty graduate desk.

*Of ten simulated responses to this situation, eight built a defence and detection sprint of this kind – the large majority. Two funded a transition guarantee for displaced entry-level workers instead, the direction of the other recommendation.*
