# Containment wards

At 04:15 on 21 February 2031, a sequencing laboratory in the early-warning network flags an agent that should not exist. It is a modified pathogen, designed with help from openly available models, and before border tracing catches up it has made hundreds of people ill in two member states.

Quarantine lines, isolation wards and the distribution of countermeasures run for weeks through the EU health agencies. Cross-border transfers pull the most severe cases off paper wards that are already overflowing.

Sofia's treatment surge, adopted in the winter, lands in the middle of the crisis, and the President puts the Commission's standing behind it. Overtime funds, double-check teams and repurposed channels for moving patients keep the hospitals open, and joint procurement fills some gaps in the stocks. But staff exhausted by a year of manual fallback now face protective protocols on top of their backlogs. Errors fall in the biocontainment units. Everywhere else, appointments slip further, and walkouts spread.

> **Claire:** we're a transfer hospital now. patients come in by ambulance from two countries
> **Claire:** full suits for eight hours. then the backlog. then home
>
> **Sofia:** The surge is paying for the overtime.
>
> **Claire:** the surge doesn't wear the suit

> **March 2031.** The leading developers confirm that their newest systems no longer reason in readable language. They present it as a gain in performance.

Overnight, monitoring of the models' reasoning, the audit logs and the Union's planned third-party checks lose their object. Brussels concedes it cannot govern the frontier this half-year. No new AI law is tabled. The factory payments stay frozen, with the shipments still quarantined and the backup site still blocked, and the joint export stance stays a position on paper. The large member state's separate supply deal keeps operating in parallel.

The public judgement is harsh. Gratitude towards the containment nurses sits alongside anger over closed clinics, confusing guidance, and the sense that dangerous capability is everywhere and control nowhere. Ministers can point to no cascading failure – the grids held, and the wards held – but restoration no longer counts as success.
