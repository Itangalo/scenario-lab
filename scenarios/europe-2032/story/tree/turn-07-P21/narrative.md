# Triage first

Brussels spends the autumn paying to reopen wards rather than breaking new ground. Sofia's note becomes the EU Hospital Helper Fix and Triage Continuity Fund, and the President puts the Commission's standing behind it. Emergency health money buys locum shifts and overtime in France, Germany, the Netherlands and Poland, while joint procurement teams rewrite the referral templates on the European-hosted helpers that clinicians rejected.

> **Claire:** a locum from rotterdam covered my nights this week
> **Claire:** first full sleep in a month. i dreamed about nothing. it was wonderful
>
> **Sofia:** That's the fund.
>
> **Claire:** tell the fund i love it

In several hospitals it works. Triage desks are staffed again, discharge backlogs clear, and walkout committees in two countries sign returns hospital by hospital. In others it stalls. The latency fixes help with text summaries and not with imaging queues, and union representatives accuse the Commission of papering over understaffing with software patches. ENISA's kits, extended to hospital IT, find more blind spots than they close.

What holds is the grid. The hardening programme finishes, on paper and in the control rooms. Thanks to the segmentation kits and the live cut-off exercise, the autumn phishing waves and a substation fault in November degrade service rather than stop it: operators island cleanly where a year earlier they would have cascaded. The engineers call it the quiet success of the half-year.

The groundbreakings do not stay quiet. Two sites pour foundations, and the local councils in both regions freeze their water permits for weeks after protests link the builds' demands for power and water to failures in care.

> **November 2029.** Night-time blockades at a substation, and graffiti along a site fence, are reported as isolated incidents.

The regional papers begin to connect them. No new siting is attempted, and the Commission freezes that fight.

Washington offers no relief. The talk of tiered access hardens into procurement practice: European buyers are quietly quoted longer lead times, and the substitution with weaker domestic models goes on. The frontier creeps forward, and the open weights follow it, beyond recall.
