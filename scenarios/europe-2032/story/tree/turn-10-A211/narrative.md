# Shelter under fire

> **9 January 2031, 23:50.** American and Chinese forces exchange strikes on orbital links, undersea cables and fabrication plants.

Within days a cable landing station on the Atlantic and a power feed for a compute hall in southern France are damaged as collateral. Sofia's shelter, drafted for a war nobody wanted to name, goes out the next morning with almost no changes. The Union declares non-belligerency, denies offensive use of any infrastructure on its soil, and orders the backups dispersed.

The declaration does not take Europe off anyone's targeting maps. Insurance for shipping and chip supplies spikes, and two accelerator deliveries are cancelled. The reserve teams that had spent two years restoring town-hall registries are reassigned to guard grid switchgear and hospital power.

The Transition Shield's wage top-ups keep paying, late but steady. Nurses keep the triage assistants running on local servers whenever the links jitter.

> **February 2031.** Tailored therapies for previously untreatable blood and immune diseases reach ordinary clinics in the United States, and weeks later, in licensed batches, in Shanghai.

European health ministers queue to negotiate licences to manufacture the therapies on EU-hosted infrastructure, pooling sequencing reagents through the health emergency apparatus. In March a few thousand patients in Denmark, Spain and the Netherlands receive the first courses, and the evening news shows remissions nobody had dared to promise.

The gratitude is real, and so is the sting. Every dose depends on a production line across a war zone and on design work no European laboratory can reproduce. Pharmacists count their stocks in weeks, not months.

By June the lifelines hold, barely. The queues are shorter where European software runs the services and longer where imports have stalled. The factory halls stay empty and graduate hiring stays frozen, and the nightly maps of naval movements have replaced the pandemic maps. Voters credit Brussels for keeping the hospitals lit and the registries open while the great powers fight. They also ask, openly, how long shelter can last when the cables, the chips and the medicines all come from somewhere else.
