# The war nobody voted for

> **11 February 2031, 03:15.** A fabrication plant across the Pacific is struck. Within hours a satellite constellation goes dark and cables are cut in two seas. American and Chinese forces are at war.

In February the war is far away. By March it is not. Airspace closes, power and data are rerouted in a hurry, and debris and sabotage scares end in a fire at a logistics hub near Rotterdam. The conflict arrives on the Council's agenda as a question of survival.

Sofia's shelter goes out as drafted. The Union declares non-belligerency: no Union territory, ports, fabs or data centres for any targeting use, orders to protect airspace and cables, and the reserve teams dispersed again. The declaration does not stop the effects. Cloud outages roll through registries and hospitals for days at a time. American frontier systems that European clinics and grid operators had quietly relied on become unreachable or restricted overnight. The empty accelerator halls now read less as an embarrassment than as an exposure.

> **April 2031.** Tailored therapies for several previously untreatable conditions reach ordinary clinics abroad.

The first European doses come through foreign platforms. Then the conditions Sofia wrote into the care network start to work. After emergency verification by the medicines agency, the therapies are delivered through Union-run hospitals in the pilot networks, on infrastructure the Union hosts.

The nurses keep the wards open with triage assistants and pooled sequencing while the systems flicker around them, and the wage top-ups keep flowing to the restoration crews.

It is not enough to lift the mood. Graduate hiring stays frozen, the marches at the warehouses continue, and the nightly news pairs pictures of cures delivered with pictures of darkened town halls. Voters see a Europe that keeps care running under the threat of bombardment, and also a Europe that cannot keep its lights on without permission from the belligerents. The blame points outward. The cohesion thins anyway, as coastal and eastern states demand air defence and cable patrols the centre cannot fund.
