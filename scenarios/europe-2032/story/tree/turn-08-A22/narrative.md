# The treatment that worked

> **February 2030.** Individually tailored therapies designed with frontier models turn a handful of previously untreatable conditions into treatable ones.

The breakthrough comes from outside the Union. For families in Lyon, Milan and Rotterdam it is not abstract. The oncologists talk about remissions where none had been expected.

> **Claire:** a kid on our ward. remission
> **Claire:** the whole floor clapped. even the porters
>
> **Sofia:** I'm glad.
>
> **Claire:** it came from america. we clapped anyway

Sofia's note that month is the first new measure she has recommended in over a year, and the President backs it among the Commissioners and in public. Her therapies programme makes the delivery European, even where the design is not. Health officials and emergency planners steer supplies to the wards giving tailored therapies, review teams approve treatment protocols with human checks built in, and the EU's medicines agency opens a faster but audited route. Mayors who refused new automation after the welfare scandal are offered published logs and a right of recourse for patients.

The queues do not disappear, because the same rationed hardware runs the planning. But wards that had been treating at lower throughput begin treating at something closer to normal again. Trust lifts, measurably. The polls still show resentment over the empty factories and the benefits scandal, but for the first time in a year the coverage pairs AI with a cured child rather than a cut benefit.

> **June 2030.** The supply compact completes its term. One small memory shipment clears; another large lot is bid away.

The maintenance clearances hold, and the allocation board keeps the clinics and grid operators from failing. Ministers stop calling the Paris and Warsaw halls gigafactories. They call them reserved capacity now. The local papers notice the change of word.

The Institute re-runs its checks on the substitutes and keeps them approved for clinical use with human oversight. The review of public-sector systems grinds on, and no new law is opened. That restraint angers the campaigners, and spares the Commission a fight it could not fund.

By June the Union is still dependent and still degraded, and delivering a visible cure on borrowed machines.
