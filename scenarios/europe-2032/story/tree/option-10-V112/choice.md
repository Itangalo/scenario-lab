# Finish the fallback

**No new measure.** The Union runs what is already on the books.

Sofia recommends opening no second front while the Sovereign Fallback Stack is still landing. With the Commission's standing low and public trust lower, a new instrument could not be funded.

The Union holds the line on what it has. ENISA, the AI Office and the bio-surveillance cell enforce the existing isolation and approval gates and the continuity backstop across essential entities, with recovery funds conditional on sharing telemetry and being ready to fall back. The French and Dutch joint containment teams are extended to newly hit districts. The fallback is pushed to finish: reserved supercomputing and space at the one building gigafactory for public-interest inference, procurement of hardened European-hosted open models, and migration paths checked in autumn exercises. The compact with Japan and Korea bargains jointly for chips and access, and hesitant private co-financiers are offered guaranteed offtake if they anchor in the Union.

The argument is that the hospitals and ministries that were cut off need somewhere to go before anything else, and that the Union cannot fight on two fronts with the standing it has left.

The President's standing stays on the Sovereign Fallback Stack.

What it does not do is extend the gates to anyone who has not installed them already.

*Of ten simulated responses to this situation, one chose to wait like this – the only one. Nine built a lockdown for agentic AI across essential services, the direction of the other recommendation.*
