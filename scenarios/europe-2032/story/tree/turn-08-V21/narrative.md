# Tape and sentinels

> **February 2030.** Another large, largely automated assault sweeps public services: appointment systems, dispatch fallbacks, port releases.

The defenders are visibly behind, and the investigators warn that attribution will take months. Clinics run on paper, and town halls queue citizens at temporary desks. The emergency repair cell stays embedded under crisis rules, restoring offline backups and pushing out the automated patching and swarm detection.

The repair cell completes its term this spring, and it matters. In many places systems degrade rather than stop outright, and the mayors credit the crews who stayed until services held.

> **March 2030.** A contested genome-model paper claims that a non-expert could reach a design for an organism able to infect humans with a model's help.

The argument stays inside the biosecurity community. Inside the Commission it supplies the legal basis Sofia needs. Her note pairs the continuity work with a Municipal Continuity and Bio-Sentinel Surge: hardened backup kits for the hardest-hit communes, and upgraded sequencing and wastewater sentinels in clinic laboratories, run through the health emergency channels without a new vote.

Everything else frays.

> **April 2030.** Washington compels further cuts to the servicing of Dutch lithography equipment, reaching back to the older machines used for ordinary chips. The company complies.

The Union's one chokepoint is used again, and not by the Union. The capital with its own access arrangement defends it as pragmatism, undercutting the joint licensing front that the External Action Service is trying to hold with solidarity repair aid. No retaliatory export action follows, only a political protest.

No new building money is voted. The permitted factory sites are kept warm while the grid-connection works draw angry gatherings – rumours and single-source photos rather than a confirmed blockade, but enough to slow the contractors. Citizens feel the outages and not the productivity gains in offices. Trust falls further.
