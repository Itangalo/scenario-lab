## World state

### Holding the line with tape and sentinels
The first half of 2030 brought no respite. A large, largely automated assault swept public services again — appointment systems, dispatch fallbacks, port releases — with defenders visibly behind and investigators warning attribution would take months. The tooling, analysts said, was machine-written. Clinics ran on paper, town halls queued citizens at temporary desks, and the emergency repair cell deployed under crisis rules stayed embedded, restoring offline backups and pushing automated patching and swarm-detection software adopted from abroad.

That repair presence is what finished this turn, and it mattered. Systems degraded rather than stopped outright in many places, and mayors credited crews who stayed until services held. Brussels paired the continuity work with a new municipal and bio-detection surge: hardened backup kits for the hardest-hit communes and upgraded sequencing and wastewater sentinels in clinic labs, run through health emergency channels after a contested genome-model paper claimed a non-expert could reach a human-infecting design with model help. The argument stayed inside the biosecurity community, but inside the Commission it supplied legal base for enhanced reporting without a new vote.

Everything else frayed. Washington compelled further cuts to servicing of Dutch lithography equipment, reaching back to older machines used for ordinary chips, and the company complied — the Union's one chokepoint used, and not by the Union. A capital that had signed its own access arrangement defended it as pragmatism, undercutting the joint licensing front the External Service tried to hold with solidarity repair aid. No retaliatory export action followed, only a political protest.

No new build money was voted. Permitted factory sites were kept warm while grid-connection works drew angry gatherings, still rumour and single-source photos rather than confirmed blockade, but enough to slow contractors. Citizens felt outages, not office productivity gains. Trust fell further.
