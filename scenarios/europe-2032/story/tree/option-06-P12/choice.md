# Expand the sovereign compute

**Emergency Sovereign Compute Mobilisation.** Sovereignty and industrial capacity. Large; running until the second half of 2029.

You read the same two events differently. The deficit is not only in chips or models but in physical and legal control of the infrastructure that runs them — and without compute that no external actor can throttle or surveil, evaluation and resilience both remain contingent on goodwill you cannot assume.

So you fast-track permitting, grid access and state aid approval for EU-controlled facilities that commit to reserving at least forty per cent of capacity for public safety, resilience and sovereign industrial use. The legal basis is Article 114 plus the dual-use logic your lithography corridor established. The investment bank pre-approves financing for operators accepting EU jurisdiction, open architecture for public audit, and that reserved share — red-teaming, crisis simulation, continuity-of-government services. Permitting is enforced through the European Semester, with non-compliant member states facing adjusted recovery disbursements. Germany and Ireland, hesitant until now, are offered co-location of Union-level sovereign nodes in exchange for dropping local objections.

Not industrial policy. Infrastructure for survival.

*Of ten simulated responses to this situation, three answered by building, against seven that hardened. This is the minority pole.*
