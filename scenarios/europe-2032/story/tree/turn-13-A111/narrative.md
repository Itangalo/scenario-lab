Clinicians in Lyon, Utrecht and Krakow learn to work on the older systems. The local papers print weekly counts of postponed scans.

> **October 2032.** Washington and Beijing announce a limited risk pact: securing model weights, restraining autonomous escalation, and restricting a class of biological design tools. Verification is thin.

Brussels was not at the table. It was briefed afterwards.

Sofia's last note of the year offers what the Union has for a seat as verifier: the intrusion feeds from the joint pool, the triage work on the deceptive benchmark, and the Union's own controls on weight security and biological design tools, aligned in advance to the new floor so that joining is a technical step rather than a plea. The envoys listen politely. At year's end inclusion is neither granted nor refused.

The neutral reserve completes its term. Capability growth eases slightly worldwide, and the openly released weights keep spreading, now on hundreds of thousands of private machines. The evaluation hosts warn that assurance is still far behind what the systems can do.

## Note to the President

*From Sofia Brandt, Cabinet. 15 December 2032. Six years in.*

The short version: we kept the wards open. We were never in the room.

What held:

- The Shield, the Continuity Stack and the continuity shield. Through a cut-off, a pathogen, a war and a strike on our own soil, the hospitals stayed on.
- The towns, mostly. They have manual fallbacks, some staff, and an EIB backstop that kept at least one insurer in the market.
- The joint pool. We are inside something that sees attacks in real time.

What did not:

- Access. We asked for it on published terms, and it was revoked by a government that now owns the laboratories.
- The build. Two gigafactory sites, guarded by soldiers, feeding models we did not train.
- Our voice. The one agreement that might slow any of this was written in Washington and Beijing. We were briefed afterwards.

What I would tell whoever holds this file next: we learned to keep things running. We never learned to be at the table.

*In the margin, in the President's hand:* Neither did I.
