# Stop buttons

> **20 January 2029.** The new US administration takes office. Its first statement on technology promises allies "structured access on published terms" and joint testing of models.

> **March 2029.** An American laboratory demonstrates a system that plans, writes code and operates tools for hours without pause.

Within weeks its control software is steering warehouse fleets and port handlers ordered from Chinese factories. European manufacturers who waited months for accelerators can buy a capable robot the same day – with a foreign update server attached.

The Commission does not try to ban what the towns are already buying. Sofia's Protocol sends inspectors to catalogue the fleets in Rotterdam, Hamburg and Lyon. They demand physical stop buttons and manual driving modes, and rehearse what a hospital or a food depot does when its carriers freeze. The port operators comply on paper. Many smaller logistics firms keep the machines running between audits. A stop test in one depot shuts down sorting for an afternoon, and the video goes round the building twice.

> **Claire:** we had a robot drill today
> **Claire:** all the carriers froze in the corridor at once
> **Claire:** we pushed them. they're heavier than they look
>
> **Sofia:** That's the point of the drill.

> **Claire:** i know. my back doesn't

The President's standing goes on the towns. Wage bridges reach utility crews and care assistants in several regions, and mayors post videos of shorter hospital queues. Where the crews arrive, the boil-water notices end. Where they do not, mostly in smaller towns in the east and south, the Trust Pact looks like posters, and the anger at foreign robots taking warehouse shifts deepens.

> **June 2029.** The tech sovereignty package closes, its permits and grid reservations now law. The bio-monitoring network, stockpiles and rationing rules are declared operational.

Engineers welcome both. Voters see little change. The computing sites in question are two fenced fields waiting on chips, and a monitoring network does not staff a clinic.

In Washington, the new team's negotiators speak warmly at the Trade and Technology Council about aligned controls and published terms. No European firm receives new access this spring. The terms are still being written.
