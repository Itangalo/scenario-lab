# Harden what must not fail

**EU Critical Services Shield.** Preparedness and resilience. Large; running until the first half of 2028.

Sofia recommends a two-year commitment to securing independent capacity and resilience, so that the Union can withstand coercion and absorb the harm AI will do – and starting with what the test shot showed.

The Shield sets baseline standards for energy, telecoms, health and finance, runs joint exercises across borders, and upgrades detection, including for biological threats. It begins with mandatory incident-reporting drills for the operators of essential services and pooled purchasing of detection tools, run through ENISA and the health emergency authority and paid for from the Civil Protection Mechanism. Interior ministers who resist the new obligations are offered EU money for hardening, in exchange for drilled continuity plans.

The argument is that nobody in Europe controls the frontier, and the tools that probed the grids are already downloadable and beyond recall. What the Union can control is how much breaks. Resilience is within reach; the models are not.

The President's standing, meanwhile, goes on the gigafactories. Without compute of its own, the Union stays one American order away from being switched off, and every other measure stays hostage to that.

What it does not do is tell anyone what the models themselves can do.

*Of thirty simulated responses to this situation, sixteen answered with preparedness and resilience, as this one does. The other fourteen built public technical capacity – evaluation and research, the direction of the other recommendation.*
