### Concrete and Chips

### Washington Steps Back

Washington turned inward, frontier work slowed for lack of permission rather than power, and Beijing's humanoids kept arriving in Rotterdam and Duisburg through intermediaries. Unions screened footage of machines stacking and carrying, and asked what retraining was for.

By June clinics ran, rationed but trusted, and voters resented the wait all the same.
