## The roads not taken

You can now go back to any of your decisions and take the recommendation you turned down. Pick one below to jump to it. The story then continues from there as if you were reading it for the first time, and everything you read after that point is replaced.

A few things are worth knowing before you compare:

- **The dice are re-rolled.** The random events are different on different branches. A scientific breakthrough may appear in late 2029 on one branch, while another has a crisis in Taiwan. This is usually not because of your choices. Probabilities may be affected by what you do, but the dice rolls dominate. (For technical reasons, the branches share events for the first 6 months after your second and third choice, though.)
- **The underlying physics is unchanged.** The general trends for AI progress stay the same on all branches you can reach from here. Reload the page, or hit "Start over", and you may end up in a world with different physics for AI progress.
