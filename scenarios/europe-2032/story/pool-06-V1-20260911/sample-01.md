## In practice
We task AI Office with ENISA and the Joint Research Centre to run the regime: mandatory pre-registration and sandboxed black-box tests for frontier deployments in energy, health, finance and telecoms, using the Incident Continuity Backstop's telemetry conditionality as enforcement — no test access, no recovery cover. Vetted researcher access and weight-security audits are folded in, not built separately.

M1 Gigafactories stays alive by mediation and grid priority, not new cash, and the M7 Trusted Compute cell is used to contain the defection: we offer the defecting member state joint accelerator procurement through the cell rather than public punishment, while making clear Anti-Coercion Instrument cover depends on holding the common screening line. M2/M6 finished effects — permitting zones and shared bio-cyber telemetry — are what we test against.
