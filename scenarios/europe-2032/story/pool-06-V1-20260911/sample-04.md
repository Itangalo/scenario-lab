## New measure
**EU Opaque-Model Containment and Black-Box Verification Regime**
It imposes deployment containment, staged rollout with kill-switches, and mandatory black-box red-teaming plus activation-probe access for any opaque frontier-class model deployed in the EU, public or open-weight-derived. Why this and why now: opaque reasoning just killed our only working oversight tool while a verifiable-domain capability jump and a near-frontier open release put attacker-grade cyber/code capability on private hardware; without containment that does not need readable reasoning, the next incident cascades.
