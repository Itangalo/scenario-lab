## In practice

We execute with existing M10-M11 money: Transition Shield wage top-ups keep reserve teams from Estonia, France, Germany paid for cable-landing and grid-switchgear repair, Bio-Detection seed contracts convert to wartime bio-watch, and the alloy switchgear orders are prioritised to grid nodes. We ask Washington and Beijing for deconfliction of EU sites via member-state embassies, offering continued supply-chain neutrality, not alignment, to both.
