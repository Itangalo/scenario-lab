## Priority

## In practice
We act through the European Council declaration of non-belligerence and the Civil Protection Mechanism plus ENISA/ECDC/HERA: declare EU civilian infrastructure off-limits, demand no hosting of offensive AI targeting from EU soil, and disperse clean registry images, hospital paper-fallback kits, and pooled sequencing reagents away from likely target corridors.

We fund from existing ESF+, EU4Health and Connecting Europe envelopes, with reserve teams from Estonia, France and Germany extended on wartime footing and wage top-ups protected for hospital, registry and grid staff. We tell Washington and Beijing plainly: Europe will not be a launchpad, will not surrender its cables and fabs without consequence, and will keep medical breakthroughs accessible on European terms rather than as dependence leverage.
