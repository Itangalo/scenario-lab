## New measure
**EU Wartime Infrastructure Shield and Neutral Continuity Regime**
Declares EU territory, cables, fabs and data centres neutral protected infrastructure under preparedness powers, with dispersed backups, military-threat hardening and non-belligerent access rules to keep registries, care and power running through US-China strikes. This is preparedness and resilience work, and why now is because great-power war makes European sites targets and with capital at 15 and safety at 1.5 only a visible keep-services-alive shield preserves any room to act.

## Priority
EU Wartime Infrastructure Shield and Neutral Continuity Regime, because under direct war exposure preventing cascade failure of power, registries and hospitals outranks all other work and is the precondition for the two-year commitment to hold.
## In practice
We act through the European Council emergency conclusions and FAC/Defence plus JHA: invoke solidarity clause and civil protection mechanism, task ENISA, ECDC/HERA and EU SATCEN with dispersed clean images, pooled sequencing reagents and cable/data-centre guarding, funded from existing ESF+, EU4Health and CEF reprogramming with no new large pledge.

We keep M10 wage top-ups and M11 bio-detection running as the daily face — reserve teams, triage assistants limited to sorting/recall, staffing guarantees — while signalling strict non-belligerence, denying targeting use of EU soil and offering medical/humanitarian continuity to both sides to deter strikes on European nodes. Message: Europe stays out, Europe stays on.
