## Priority
EU Wartime Continuity and Shelter Compact, because under direct kinetic/cyber threat to European infrastructure outranking build-out, holding power, care and registries alive is the precondition for the two-year commitment.
## In practice
We execute through the European Council emergency formation and PSC with HERA/ECDC/ENISA: declare critical-site protection, disperse registry/health backups across three zones, extend Estonian-French-German reserve teams to cable landing, grid switchgear and hospital logistics, and pool sequencing reagents under health-emergency powers.

We fund from existing ESF+, EU4Health and Connecting Europe envelopes with no new large pledge: fast-track Shield wage top-ups for essential staff, ration Paris/Warsaw power to triage assistants and detection, and message neutrality-plus-protection — Europe not a battlefield, but defended — to hold cohesion while medical breakthrough pilots are kept on EU-run models where possible.
