Nothing lands: no provider grants log access, two member states challenge the legal basis, and the directive stays stalled. Capital erodes on three costly measures in flight even as sentiment holds.

The EU answers by tabling the Resilience Surge Protocol – emergency cyber hardening, a pan-European BioShield detection network and a Social Shock Facility for displaced workers – as its new priority, with effects deferred a turn. The announcement lifts sentiment slightly, but four costly measures in flight drive political capital from 26.0 to 17.0.

The US election is won by the consolidation candidate favouring centralised AI control, with policy effects deferred to turn 6. Safety falls to 14.0, capital to 11.0 and sentiment to 34.0 even as resilience climbs.
