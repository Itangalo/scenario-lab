## New measure
None this turn.  
We are waiting for a restoration of minimal political capital before initiating any new measure, as the current portfolio cannot be advanced without it.

## Priority
**Priority:** Establish the Continuity Assurance Framework  
We are prioritizing this because it is the last remaining instrument through which the Union can demonstrate that its sovereignty means something during a crisis — and because without operational continuity, all other ambitions collapse into abstraction.

## In practice
This turn, we are fighting to salvage credibility from the wreckage of overreach. The Continuity Assurance Framework is law, but in Thessaloniki, Maastricht and Kiruna, the air-gapped nodes are offline, untested, or incompatible with live systems. The cyber_major_incident — a model-generated ransomware sweep that crippled hospital scheduling in three member states — exposed the fatal gap: we legislated continuity, but did not ensure it worked. The EAAD has issued emergency directives under Article 122 TFEU to compel quarterly failover drills, but two member states refuse to participate, citing national competence over health infrastructure. We are not sanctioning them — we cannot afford the split — but we are offering targeted Digital Europe Programme grants to fast-track integration, conditioned on quarterly verification by EAAD technical teams.

Behind the scenes, the Commission is negotiating with national CIOs to accept a phased but binding compliance pathway: full certification by turn 11, with interim status granted only to systems that pass unannounced drills in at least two of the three critical domains — health, energy, transport. We are reallocating the remaining €1.1bn of the €3.2bn package to municipalities that commit by June, with front-loaded disbursements to signal seriousness. But without political capital, we have no leverage, no speed, and no margin for error. This is not about building new systems — it is about making the ones we have actually function before the next shock hits. If we fail, the Union will not fall to an attack — it will dissolve into irrelevance, one untested backup node at a time.
