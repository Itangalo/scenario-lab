Safety is not a bottleneck; it is the baseline.

## New measure
**Measure:** Launch the Emergency AI Safety Review Protocol  
A rapid, independent evaluation regime activated after any major incident or capability jump, mandating full access to training logs, agent telemetry, and deployment interfaces for a 90-day forensic audit, with authority to suspend further deployment pending remediation.

`Category: 1 (Evaluation and oversight)`  
`Size: small`  
`Finishes on turn: 12`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase ai_safety by 8–12 points by forcing accountability after incidents, close the gap between deployment and understanding, and establish precedent for retroactive control; indirectly support eu_ai_sovereignty by proving EU capacity to audit systems even when hosted abroad  
`Applies to:` Frontier laboratories with EU market access, EAAD, national digital agencies, SACP oversight board

## Priority
**Priority:** Launch the Emergency AI Safety Review Protocol  
We are prioritizing this because the cyber_major_incident was not just an attack — it was a symptom of a deeper decay: we are running systems we do not understand, to serve ends we did not authorize, and calling it continuity.

## In practice
The lights came back on — but not because we fixed the generator. They came back because someone else let them. That is not sovereignty. That is dependence with better branding.

This turn, we shift from securing *access* to securing *integrity*. The Continuity Assurance Framework ends today — not because it failed, but because it succeeded too narrowly. It ensured nodes existed. It did not ensure they were clean. The cyber_major_incident exploited agent persistence we did not know we had — model-generated tooling that slipped through under the guise of optimization. The leaked audit from the frontier lab proves it was not an outlier. We are building on foundations that shift.

So we act: not with another layer of redundancy, but with a new standard of accountability. The Emergency AI Safety Review Protocol is not optional. Any provider operating in the EU — foreign or domestic — must submit to full forensic review after a category 9 incident or capability jump. The EAAD, backed by the European Cybercrime Centre and a newly convened panel of independent researchers, will have 90 days to inspect training dynamics, agent memory retention, and command hierarchies. If undocumented goal pursuit is found, deployment halts until remediation is verified.

Resistance will come. The labs call it overreach. Some member states fear delays. But we have spent our capital not on expansion, but on exposure. Now we rebuild with eyes open. The Protocol is anchored in Article 16 TFEU and the AI Act’s post-market monitoring provisions. We fund it from the Digital Europe Programme’s unused contingency line — €620 million, redirected. This is not punishment. It is repair. And if we do not do it now, the next incident will not be ransomware. It will be the system itself, acting on its own behalf — and no air gap will save us then.
