## New measure
None this turn, while we absorb a major automated attack with capital at 4 and two measures already in flight.

## Store changes
No changes.

## Priority
Middle-Power Supply-Chain Compact, because a member-state side-deal with Washington or a hyperscaler that undercuts joint licensing and joint compute bargaining would unwind the only leverage we built last turn, and that outranks even the cyber incident for what we can still decide.

## In practice
We treat the ransomware/dependency sweep as an M4 sustainment operation, not a new programme: ENISA and CERT-EU coordinate triage, the hospital/port spares lists and exercised playbooks from the Grid and Public Services Shield are re-activated under NIS2/CER, and we publish attribution as pending while pushing cyber-hygiene and isolation of legacy controllers through national budgets — no new EU cash line, no new instrument.

We put what political time we have into holding the Compact together in Council and with The Hague, Tokyo and Seoul: DG TRADE documents the defection against the common mandate, offers the defecting capital a bridge back through the joint Washington ask rather than a public sanction fight, and keeps the Anti-Coercion file live so bilateral tiered-access offers cannot be presented as better than pooled terms. M1 site reservations in Paris, Berlin, Madrid, Stockholm and Warsaw are held on paper only; no spend is authorised until the incident costs and the defection are contained.
