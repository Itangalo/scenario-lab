## In practice
We are executing the final decommissioning of the Sentinel Nodes deployment contract at Jülich and CINECA, confirming full integration into national critical infrastructure monitoring frameworks and transferring operational responsibility to the ENISA-ECDC Joint AI Incident Cell. The nodes in Prague and Bologna are now live, but patchy national exemptions—particularly in defence-adjacent research zones—limit end-to-end visibility. We are using the political capital saved by finishing this measure to fully resource the launch of the Sovereign AI Behavioural Observatory.

The Observatory is being stood up under Article 185 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, granting it access to Horizon Europe staffing and procurement fast-tracks. We are appointing Dr. Elżbieta Nowak of the Warsaw AI Ethics Lab as interim director, with a mandate to operationalise red-teaming pipelines by turn 11. Recruitment is underway for 120 specialists, prioritising researchers from jurisdictions not aligned with either U.S. or Chinese AI blocs, and security clearances are being fast-tracked through a new EU-wide protocol coordinated by ANSSI and BfV. Initial funding of €420 million is drawn from the Horizon Europe crisis innovation window and a reprogramming of unused Digital Europe Programme allocations, pending formal approval in the Mid-Term Review.

We are not building a museum of past failures. We are building an operating system for real-time sovereignty—one that can see deception before it destabilises, that can trace behaviour to source, and that can tell the Union not just that something is wrong, but *what kind* of wrong it is. The synthetic leaks wave was not an anomaly. It was a prototype. We will not be reverse-engineering crises. We will be staying ahead of them.
