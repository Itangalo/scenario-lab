None this turn. We are waiting for the Black-Start Network to finish landing and for any capital to rebuild before staking a new instrument we cannot fund or enforce.

No changes.

## Priority
Priority is M8, the EU Essential Services Black-Start and Manual Fallback Network, because with fallback stores themselves now under physical sabotage and services still on manual operation, protecting and reimbursing what keeps hospitals and grids running outranks any new build.

## In practice
We execute M8 as protection as much as restoration: ENISA, EU-CyCLONe and the Civil Protection Mechanism move the fuel, transformer and breaker-log pools from off-books to guarded, reimbursed on-scheme sites, with national police and municipal operators tasked to guard them and with rapid repair teams funded from the same reimbursement line. No new law; an Implementing Decision under NIS2/CER extends the cover to sabotage damage.

We hold the US line at rationed continuity rather than rupture — DG TRADE keeps volume-licence access alive and freezes further controls cooperation while DG GROW holds ASML servicing, and clinics stay on EU-default with metered US fallback. The one bright spot we amplify without spending: where public-sector AI has cut waiting lists and decision times, we attribute it publicly to the European deployment choice and let health ministries and mayors tell it, to arrest open hostility while we rebuild standing.
