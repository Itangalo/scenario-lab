## New measure
**Local Guardians for Fallback Stores**
It puts the off-books generator pools, transformer stores and breaker-log sites under joint municipal-police and community stewardship with lighting, access control and paid local guardian roles, so sabotage does not take down the manual fallback.
Why this and why now: the emergent sabotage wave directly targets what M8 is formalising, and with capital at 3 and sentiment at 10 we cannot restore by hand if the hand-tools are burned — protection through local ownership is the cheapest way to hold services together and turn hostile communities into defenders.

## In practice
We execute M8 as operations: ENISA/EU-CyCLONe keeps compromised dependencies offline, TSOs/DSOs run segmented manual operation, and DG ECHO reimburses fuel/transformer pooling brought on-scheme via the Civil Protection Mechanism. The Guardians layer is added by Implementing Decision under CER/NIS2: mayors get a micro-grant line for fencing, lighting and inventory, local police add stores to patrol rounds, and guardian stipends go to municipal staff and vetted volunteers.

We use the adoption success we have: DG DIGIT and health ministries publish the waiting-lists-fallen, decisions-in-days figures clinic-by-clinic as European-default results, paired with guardian sign-ups, to give hostile publics a visible benefit and a role. On Washington quotas we hold: no new ASML concession, DG TRADE takes sympathy spares from Tokyo/Seoul where offered, and we use the investment-collapse price drop to option cancelled power and site reservations for later, not to start a new build we cannot fund.
