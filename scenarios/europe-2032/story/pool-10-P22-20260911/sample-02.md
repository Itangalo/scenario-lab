## Priority
Priority is M8, the Black-Start and Manual Fallback Network, because the emergent sabotage is now physically targeting exactly those generator pools, transformer stores and breaker-log sites and losing them loses restoration itself.

## In practice
We put M8 on a protection footing. Under CER/NIS2 and the Civil Protection Mechanism, ENISA, Europol and national police coordinate site security for pooled spares and manual-operation points, move inventories off published lists, split stocks across more locations, and reimburse municipalities and DSOs for guarding, relocation and repair — paid from existing M8 and UCPM lines, no new fund this turn.

We use the one bright spot — the public-sector adoption success with falling waiting lists and faster decisions — visibly and locally: mayors, hospitals and schools front the results as European-run services kept alive by hand when automated systems failed, to separate essential fallback from the hostile AI story and to slow vandalism. On Washington quotas and the investment collapse we hold position only: no new chip bid, DG TRADE keeps Japan/Korea servicing talks warm while distressed compute and talent are mapped for a later cheap buy, because with capital at 3 we cannot start what we cannot finish.
