## New measure  
**Launch EU Sovereign Compute Initiative**  
Establish a legally anchored, EU-controlled high-performance compute infrastructure for training and running advanced AI models, insulated from external access restrictions.  
Category: 4 (Sovereignty and industrial capacity)

## Priority  
**Priority: Launch EU Sovereign Compute Initiative**  
This is the foundational lever without which no other measure—on safety, resilience, or fairness—can be enacted on our own terms; we cannot regulate, defend, or innovate with systems we do not control.

## Actions  
Initiate emergency planning for the EU Sovereign Compute Initiative, convening a task force of member state energy and digital ministers, ENISA, EIB, and selected national labs to assess optimal siting, energy resilience, and procurement pathways for exascale-class infrastructure; the task force will report within six weeks with binding recommendations for pilot site selection and governance structure.  

Redirect €12 billion from the stalled InvestAI Fund’s Gigafactory allocation toward the first tranche of sovereign compute procurement, conditioning disbursement on full legal anchoring within EU jurisdiction and redundancy against single-point-of-failure in energy and connectivity; this repurposing will be presented to the European Parliament as a matter of strategic urgency under the revised Industrial Policy Emergency Framework.  

Begin negotiations with ASML, selected national energy providers, and semiconductor startups to secure priority access to next-generation lithography services and co-locate chip assembly with planned compute nodes, leveraging the EU’s chokepoint in EUV technology as counterweight to external dependencies; these talks will be framed as reciprocal—access for security.  

Fast-track legal drafting of a Sovereign AI Infrastructure Act to establish the governance, access controls, and red-line usage prohibitions for the compute platform, ensuring it serves public research, critical infrastructure defence, and regulatory evaluation—never commercial dominance—thereby aligning sovereignty with legitimacy and public consent.
