## In practice
We push the Resilience Shield through DG CNECT and HERA with the Health and Telecoms Councils, using the Civil Protection Mechanism and ENISA as operators, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and EU4Health money plus member-state co-finance. We meet municipal and hospital resistance over mandatory hardening standards with EU-paid incident-response teams and joint procurement.

We keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the tech sovereignty package moving on their long tracks: site selection and grid connection for four to five gigafactories, accelerated-permitting zones, and negotiation on private data-centre capital, using ASML leverage quietly to hold US pressure off. We do not open a parallel compute programme; broadening happens inside those lines.
