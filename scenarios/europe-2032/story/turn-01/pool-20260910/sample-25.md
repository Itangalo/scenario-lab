## In practice
We push the Gigafactories selection through DG CNECT and the EuroHPC JU this turn: final sites, power reservations with TSOs, and EIB de-risking to lock private co-finance, trading accelerated permitting zones for member-state agreement to EU legal anchoring of capacity. The tech sovereignty package runs alongside via the Competitiveness Council, using Important Project of Common European Interest state-aid clearance to keep France, Germany and the Nordics aligned while offering cohesion-funded grid upgrades to the sceptics.

At the same time we table the AI Act implementing regulation for the Evaluation Authority under Article 114, housed initially in the AI Office with seconded national experts and compute quota from EuroHPC, with a mandate to demand model and scaffold access for systemic-risk tests and to publish incident findings. ASML and export-control alignment are handled quietly with Washington and The Hague to buy time: no confrontation while the June-controls lesson is fresh, but a clear linkage that supply-chain cooperation requires predictable US access terms.
