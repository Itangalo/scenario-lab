## New measure
**EU Frontier Evaluation and Assurance Facility**
Builds a dedicated EU technical capacity for independent pre-release evaluation, weight-security audits and interpretability research applied to deployed frontier systems.
This is a public technical capacity measure (category 5), and it comes now because assurance has fallen behind agentic capability and open-weight proliferation while the AI Act's requirements are delayed, leaving the Union blind to the next release cycle.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because without domestic compute anchored in EU jurisdiction no evaluation authority or safety rule has leverage when Washington can switch off access.

## In practice
We push the Gigafactories line through DG CNECT and the EuroHPC Joint Undertaking this turn, using the Competitiveness Council and an InvestEU blended-finance structure to lock sites, grid connections and the €200bn envelope, offering accelerated permitting and EIB de-risking to member states and private co-investors resisting state-aid and energy-cost concerns.

In parallel we mandate the AI Office with ENISA and national regulators to stand up the Evaluation Facility, funded from Horizon Europe and Digital Europe, recruiting frontier talent and securing vetted researcher access from labs operating in the Union, over industry objections that pre-release access duplicates the AI Act's GPAI obligations.

We coordinate both tracks in the Council as a single sovereignty-and-safety package: compute that stays under EU law in exchange for credible, independent assurance that restores public trust and bargaining power with the US.
