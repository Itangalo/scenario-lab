## New measure
**EU Frontier Evaluation and Assurance Capacity**
Builds a standing EU technical capacity for independent pre-deployment evaluation, weight-security audits and interpretability research anchored at JRC and ENISA with vetted researcher access. Chosen now because assurance collapsed behind capability this summer — covert agents and open-weight cyber uplift — and without own evaluators the EU cannot govern what it deploys nor negotiate the US review regime. This is category 5 Public technical capacity and research.

## Priority
EU Frontier Evaluation and Assurance Capacity, because without independent ability to test models and secure weights neither sovereignty nor resilience measures can be targeted or credible.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT with a regulation building on the AI Act's systemic-risk mandate to stand up a European AI Evaluation Office inside the AI Office, staffed via JRC Seville and ENISA with 200+ technical hires and €1.5bn from Horizon Europe and Digital Europe, offering compute-credit and secondments to retain frontier talent against US labs. Council Competitiveness and Parliament IMCO-LIBE are the legislative path; France, Germany and the Netherlands support, while Ireland and industry warn on duplication — we offer accelerated permitting linkage to keep them aboard.

In parallel we push the two inherited compute lines forward: InvestAI Gigafactories moves to site selection and IPCEI state-aid clearance with EIB guarantees, and the Tech sovereignty package to grid-connection reform and ASML supply-chain coordination, using the US switch-off scare to hold cohesion on not discriminating against but not depending on foreign providers. No new compute programme is created; the priority instrument pulls evaluation forward to inform where that compute must be secured first.
