In parallel we stand up evaluation capacity now: mandate the AI Office with JRC and ENISA support to contract compute and hire frontier talent, negotiate structured access with Anthropic and other labs under the AI Act GPAI provisions, and fund interpretability and agent-behaviour research through Horizon Europe. This creates the technical staff and legal access that lets postponement to 2027-2028 be reversed in practice and gives us evidence for the next controls fight.
