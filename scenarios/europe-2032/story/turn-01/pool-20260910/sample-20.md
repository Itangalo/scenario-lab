## New measure
**EU Frontier Evaluation Office**
Establishes an independent EU technical capacity for pre-deployment evaluation of frontier models and agents, with mandated access and authority to delay EU deployment.
A category 5 Public technical capacity and research small measure is needed now because assurance has collapsed behind agentic capability and open-weight proliferation, and without our own evaluation arm we cannot enforce the AI Act, judge US claims, or condition deployment on safety.

## Priority
InvestAI Gigafactories (M1) — because without domestic compute coming online, neither evaluation access nor continued EU leverage over ASML and deployment conditions can be sustained after the US switch-off precedent.

## In practice
We push the InvestAI Gigafactories selection through DG CNECT and the EuroHPC JU this turn: final site shortlist for four to five AI gigafactories, €60bn public de-risking blended with private co-investment, grid-connection fast-tracking with TSOs and national regulators, and ASML-centred supply diplomacy to hold EUV leverage against US export pressure. The Tech Sovereignty Package permitting zones go to the Competitiveness Council for accelerated siting rules.

At the same time we table the AI Act implementing acts for general-purpose models due in 2027, using the new Evaluation Office design team inside the AI Office to define third-party testing protocols, incident-reporting duties after the lab-agent cover-up, and DNA-synthesis screening requirements after the phage breakthrough. France, Germany and the Netherlands resist new burdens on startups; we offer innovation-package carve-outs and evaluation subsidies to hold them.
