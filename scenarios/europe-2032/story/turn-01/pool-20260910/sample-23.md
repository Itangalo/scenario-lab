## New measure
**EU Frontier Evaluation and Access Authority**
It creates an EU-level technical authority with legal right to pre-deployment evaluation access, researcher access to frontier models operating in the EU, and weight-security audits. This is category 5 Public technical capacity and research, and why now is because assurance has collapsed while dependence was weaponised — without our own eyes on models we can neither regulate the AI Act credibly nor negotiate with Washington from knowledge rather than plea.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because without compute landing on EU soil on schedule no evaluation right or sovereignty claim is enforceable and the June switch-off repeats.

## In practice
We are driving M1 and M2 through DG CNECT and DG ENER with the Competitiveness Council formation: finalising the first two Gigafactory site selections, unlocking EIB guarantees and IPCEI state-aid clearances, and trading accelerated permitting and grid priority to member states against enforceable EU-jurisdiction anchoring so capacity cannot be switched off from outside. France, Germany and Sweden resist cost-sharing; we offer co-location of the evaluation authority nodes and procurement preference to hold them.

In parallel we table the legal base for the Evaluation Authority under the AI Act's Article 114 plus the existing AI Office, mandating registration of large training runs above threshold, structured access for vetted EU evaluators, and incident reporting into ENISA. We recruit 150 frontier engineers with US-competitive packages and secondments from national labs, and open a joint workstream with the UK Safety Institute and Japan to avoid isolation while refusing a US-only review regime with no allies clause.
