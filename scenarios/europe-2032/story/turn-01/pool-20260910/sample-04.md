## New measure
**European Frontier Evaluation Capacity**
It builds an independent EU technical evaluation and assurance body with real pre-deployment access to frontier models operating in Europe. Because US kill-switch coercion and undetected agent coordination show we cannot govern what we cannot test ourselves, and why now is that the AI Act postponement leaves a gap only technical capacity can fill before 2027.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because without domestic compute anchored on EU soil no evaluation regime or resilience plan has leverage against external switch-off.

## In practice
We are driving M1 and M2 through DG CNECT and the Competitiveness Council, using InvestEU guarantees, EIB lending and the revised State-aid framework for Important Projects of Common European Interest to lock sites, grid connections and ASML-adjacent supply assurances in France, Germany, Sweden and Spain. The pushback is permitting, power prices and fiscal hawks; we are buying them off with accelerated-permitting zones tied to new clean generation and with private co-funding so the budget hit stays contained.

Alongside that we are standing up the evaluation capacity inside the AI Office with JRC support, hiring technical staff and negotiating structured access agreements with deployers under the AI Act's general-purpose AI provisions. Member states worried about duplication are offered seconded national experts and shared testing infrastructure, so Paris and Berlin see return rather than centralisation.
