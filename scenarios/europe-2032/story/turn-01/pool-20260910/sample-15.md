## In practice
We are pushing the Shield through the Competitiveness and JHA Councils together on an Article 114 plus Civil Protection Mechanism base, led by ENISA, ECDC and the new AI Office incident cell, funded by reprogramming Digital Europe and EU4Health with member-state co-finance. Finance ministries resisting new money are offered CER/NIS2 compliance credit, while municipalities and hospitals get direct hardening grants tied to joint exercises this autumn.

At the same time we keep the two inherited compute lines moving without reopening them: InvestAI Gigafactories stays in site selection and power reservation with EIB guarantees, and the Tech Sovereignty Package stays in permitting-zone transposition and private-capital pledges. On Washington we do not retaliate now but quietly prepare an Anti-Coercion dossier and demand allied consultation on the review regime, to buy space for both tracks.
