## New measure
**EU Critical Services Resilience Programme**
Hardens energy, telecoms, health and finance against AI-enabled cyber disruption and builds EU-wide bio-detection and incident response exercises.
This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and why now is that the summer lab incident, Kimi K3 open-weight cyber uplift, and the US switch-off order show harm arrives before sovereignty does, so resilience must land while compute is still being built.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because without domestic compute anchored in EU jurisdiction no evaluation, restriction or negotiation has leverage after the June US controls.

## In practice
We push the InvestAI Gigafactories selection through DG CNECT and the EIB this turn: finalising the four to five site shortlist, locking IPCEI state-aid clearance in the Competitiveness Council, and tying electricity grid priority via the Trans-European Energy Networks to silence member states competing to host. The Tech sovereignty package permitting zones go to co-legislators under the Emergency Instrument legal base to compress timelines, with France, Germany and the Nordics offered first grid allocation to hold cohesion.

Alongside that we launch the Resilience Programme through ENISA, HERA and the Health Emergency Preparedness Authority: baseline cyber-hardening standards for operators of essential services under NIS2 implementing acts, funded from Digital Europe, plus joint cross-border exercises in autumn and pooled sequencing-based bio-detection procurement. We frame it as protection of citizens, not AI regulation, to keep public sentiment and avoid reopening the AI Act fight.
