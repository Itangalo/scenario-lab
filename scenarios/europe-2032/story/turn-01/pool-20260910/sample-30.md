## New measure
**EU Frontier Evaluation and Assurance Authority**
Builds an independent EU technical capacity to evaluate frontier models, audit weight security, and gate EU deployment on safety findings.
This first because without our own evaluation capability we cannot govern what we deploy nor bargain with Washington, and the June switch-off showed negotiated access without verification is not sovereignty.

## Priority
EU Frontier Evaluation and Assurance Authority, because credible evaluation is the precondition for both enforcing the AI Act and negotiating an allies clause with the US review regime.

## In practice
We task DG-CNECT with a regulation under Articles 114 and 173 to stand up the Authority inside the AI Office, with seconded staff from national AI Safety Institutes, ENISA and JRC, funded by reprioritised Horizon Europe and Digital Europe envelopes. Its first mandate is mandatory pre-deployment evaluation access for general-purpose models placed on the EU market and a weight-security audit of providers serving EU critical sectors.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the tech sovereignty package moving: accelerated-permitting zones in the Competitiveness Council, grid-connection commitments from TSOs, and structuring the €200bn private vehicle via EIB guarantees. We tell Member States and industry plainly that evaluation gates will apply to gigafactory customers as well — sovereignty without assurance will not hold public consent.
