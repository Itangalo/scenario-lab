## New measure
**EU Frontier Evaluation and Assurance Capacity**
Builds an independent EU technical capacity to evaluate frontier models and open-weight releases for dangerous capabilities before deployment.
This is category 5 Public technical capacity and research, and it must come now because without our own evaluation capability we cannot enforce the AI Act, judge US assurances after the Mythos switch-off threat, or decide what open-weight risk we can live with.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because without domestic compute landing no evaluation authority or rule has leverage and the June switch-off threat repeats.

## In practice
We are driving M1 and M2 through DG CNECT and the Competitiveness Council on an Article 173/182 legal base, using InvestAI guarantees and EIB co-financing to de-risk the four to five gigafactory sites, while pressing grid operators and permitting authorities in France, Germany, Spain and Nordics to deliver power and land. France and Mistral push for EU preference, Germany demands state-aid discipline, and we buy them off with shared site selection and open access for EU startups.

Alongside that we stand up the new evaluation capacity inside the AI Office with the Joint Research Centre, hiring 80-100 frontier evaluators and securing pre-release model access agreements, and we start structured information-sharing with the UK AI Safety Institute and US review regime to avoid being blind-sided again. ASML export pressure is handled in the Foreign Affairs Council via coordinated export-control dialogue, linking lithography leverage to an allies clause on model access.
