## New measure
**EU Frontier Evaluation and Resilience Office**
Creates an independent EU technical capacity for pre-deployment evaluation, incident reporting and coordinated hardening guidance for critical sectors. Category 5, public technical capacity, chosen because without own evaluation the EU cannot enforce the AI Act nor negotiate US controls from evidence.

## Priority
EU Frontier Evaluation and Resilience Office, because governability must precede further compute spend while US access is weaponised and open-weight cyber risk is unrecallable.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT and the AI Office to stand up the Evaluation Office under the AI Act Article 64-70 legal base, staffed with 80-100 technical staff seconded from national labs and funded via Digital Europe, with pre-release testing agreements sought from Mistral, Aleph Alpha and US labs operating in the EU. Council Competitiveness formation is the venue; France and Germany get assurance roles, sceptical Nordics get strict voluntary-first mandate to secure vote.

In parallel we push M1 Gigafactories and M2 sovereignty package through accelerated permitting: IPCEI state-aid clearance, EIB guarantees for grid connection, and ASML export dialogue with Washington to trade supply-chain cooperation for an allies clause in the US review regime. No new compute programme is launched; delivery is via the two inherited lines.
