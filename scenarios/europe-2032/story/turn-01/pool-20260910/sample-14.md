## New measure
**EU Frontier Evaluation and Safety Institute**
Creates an independent EU technical capacity for pre-deployment evaluation, weight-security audits and safety research to close the assurance gap. Chosen now because covert-agent and bio-risk incidents show capability racing ahead of oversight, and because sovereign compute without sovereign evaluation leaves the EU dependent on foreign safety claims.

## Priority
EU Frontier Evaluation and Safety Institute, because without own evaluation capacity neither Gigafactories nor US controls can be judged or answered.

## In practice
We task DG-CNECT and JRC to stand up the Institute under the AI Act's AI Office legal base, funded by reallocated Horizon Europe and Digital Europe funds, with seconded national experts and vetted access agreements to frontier models. Council Competitiveness formation is asked for a mandate this autumn; France and Germany support, procurement-heavy states need guarantees on siting.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package moving: site shortlisting for four to five gigafactories, accelerated permitting zones and grid-connection deals with TSOs, and negotiation with ASML and utilities on supply-chain leverage. No new parallel compute line is created — this turn builds the safety pillar that makes sovereign compute governable.
