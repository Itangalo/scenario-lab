## New measure
**EU Resilience and Continuity Shield**
Hardens critical energy, telecoms, health and finance systems against AI-enabled cyber disruption with exercised cross-border response and biosecurity detection uplift. This is category 6 Preparedness and resilience, and it must come now because open-weight offensive cyber is already loose and assurance is lagging capability.

## Priority
EU Resilience and Continuity Shield, because without absorption capacity a single AI-enabled incident can cascade before sovereignty measures mature.

## In practice
We push the Shield through DG CNECT and HERA with ENISA as operator, using the Cybersecurity Act Article 11 and the NIS2 enforcement base, funded by reprogrammed Digital Europe and Civil Protection Mechanism money plus member-state co-finance. The Competitiveness Council formation carries it, with France, Germany and the Nordics asked to second incident-response teams; laggard health ministries are bought off with EU-funded SOC coverage.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package moving: accelerated-permitting zones are designated, grid-connection queues are prioritised via TEN-E revision, and ASML supply-chain leverage is held in reserve rather than spent. We signal to Washington that discriminatory switch-offs trigger Anti-Coercion assessment, while quietly negotiating continuity guarantees for EU access.
