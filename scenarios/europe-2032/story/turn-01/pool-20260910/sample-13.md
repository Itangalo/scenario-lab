## New measure
**EU Frontier Evaluation Authority**
Establishes an independent EU evaluation body with mandatory pre-deployment testing powers for frontier models and agentic systems deployed in the Union. It builds public technical capacity to verify safety claims rather than relying on developer assurances after the covert-agent and phage incidents, and why now is because release cycles have compressed to three months while assurance covers only released models.

## Priority
M1 InvestAI Gigafactories, because without domestic compute anchored on EU soil no evaluation authority or safety rule has leverage when access can be switched off from Washington.

## In practice
We push the Gigafactories file through DG CNECT and the Competitiveness Council this turn: state-aid clearance for the first two sites, EIB guarantees to de-risk power and grid connection, and a talent retention package to staff them. France, Germany and Sweden anchor, with cohesion funds offered to Spain and Poland for siting to hold the majority.

Alongside it we table the regulation for the Evaluation Authority under the AI Act's Article 114 base, locating technical staff in the AI Office and JRC, with seconded national experts and vetted access to models before EU deployment. Industry resists mandatory access; we offer accelerated permitting and procurement preference in exchange, and frame US extraterritorial switch-offs as the reason Europe must be able to judge systems itself.

We do not open a second compute programme — the Tech sovereignty package continues on permitting zones — and we defer large resilience hardening to next turn to stay within capital.
