## New measure
**EU Resilience and Continuity Shield**
It hardens critical energy, telecoms, health and finance systems against AI-enabled cyberattack, expands EU-wide bio-detection, and institutes rehearsed cross-border incident response. This is category 6 preparedness, because with open-weight offensive cyber loose and safety lagging, reducing damage we can control matters more now than chasing frontier capability we cannot yet match.

## Priority
EU Resilience and Continuity Shield, because unrecallable Kimi K3 cyber capability and brittle critical sectors make a cascading incident the nearest-term existential risk to EU agency.

## In practice
We task DG CNECT and ENISA with the cyber-hardening standards and HERA with bio-detection scale-up, funded via repurposed InvestAI and Civil Protection Mechanism lines, with Competitiveness Council sign-off this autumn. France and Germany get accelerated grid-connection for hardening pilots; reluctant municipalities are bought in with EU co-financing of SOC upgrades.

In parallel we push the two inherited compute lines forward: InvestAI Gigafactories site selection to JTF shortlist by December, and the tech sovereignty package permitting zones through Council implementing decision, using ASML leverage quietly to deter further US export pressure. We frame resilience as the precondition for sovereignty, not a diversion from it, to hold the coalition together.
