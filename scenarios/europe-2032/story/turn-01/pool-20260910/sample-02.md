## New measure
**EU Resilience Shield**
Builds EU-wide hardening for AI-enabled cyber and bio incidents: baseline cyber-hardening of critical services, EU bio-detection network, and exercised cross-border contingency plans.
This first because open-weight offensive cyber is already loose and assurance is falling, while our compute build-out takes years; resilience is within our control now and buys room for sovereignty to land.

## Priority
EU Resilience Shield, because distributed Kimi K3-level cyber capability and AI-designed phages create immediate unrecallable harm that gigafactories cannot answer in time.

## In practice
We push the Resilience Shield through DG HOME and DG SANTE with HERA and ENISA as operators, using the Civil Protection Mechanism and an Article 122 emergency tranche to fund hospital and municipal cyber-hardening and a federated bio-surveillance upgrade. We meet interior and health ministers in the Justice and Home Affairs Council to lock in mandatory incident reporting and joint exercises for autumn 2027, offering cohesion co-financing to reluctant Eastern and Southern states.

At the same time we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the tech sovereignty package moving: DG COMP clears state-aid for the first two gigafactory sites, DG ENER negotiates grid connections, and we use the Chips Act successor and EIB guarantees to hold private data-centre capital. We link both tracks by requiring hardened public procurement and EU-controlled hosting for sensitive workloads, so resilience spending builds demand for sovereign compute.
