## Priority
EU Critical Shield and Bio-Cyber Resilience Programme, because without absorption capacity the next leaked capability cascades before Gigafactories deliver.

## In practice
We push the Critical Shield via a joint DG CNECT–HERA–ENISA instrument, using the Civil Protection Mechanism and an Article 122 emergency resilience envelope to fund cyber-hardening audits, backup power and comms, and hospital and municipal patching, with NIS2 enforcement as the legal base in Competitiveness and JHA Councils. Member states resisting EU intrusion on health and telecoms are bought with 100% EU funding for detection nodes and mutual-aid guarantees.

In parallel we keep InvestAI Gigafactories and the Tech sovereignty package moving: DG COMP state-aid clearance for first two gigafactory sites, accelerated permitting zones through the EIA derogation debate, and grid-connection deals with TSOs, while talking to ASML, Siemens Energy and Nordic hydro hosts to lock power and chips. We do not open a second compute programme; we staff what is already promised.
