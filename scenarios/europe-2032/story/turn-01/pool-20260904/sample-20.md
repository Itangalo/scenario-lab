This measure is foundational to our two-year commitment — without sovereign compute, every other instrument is conditional on permission we do not control, and the current US precedent proves that access can be revoked overnight.

## In practice  
We are moving immediately on the legal and financial architecture for InvestAI Gigafactories, convening the EIB, national development banks, and selected member states under the legal base of Article 114 TFEU to establish a dedicated vehicle for co-investment in EU-anchored compute. The first site selection will be fast-tracked through the proposed accelerated-permitting zones from the Tech sovereignty package, with grid upgrades coordinated through ENTSO-E and national regulators. Resistance from member states wary of centralisation is being met with binding commitments on regional distribution and local ownership stakes, while energy concerns are addressed by pairing each site with a renewable package under the Net-Zero Industry Act.

Simultaneously, we are launching the first phase of our public technical capacity build-out: the European AI Evaluation Agency (EAIEA) will be established as a decentralised competence, hosted initially within the Joint Research Centre but with a mandate to grow into an autonomous body by turn 4. This new measure will grant it legally enforceable access to model weights and training data for all general-purpose AI systems above a defined capability threshold operating in the EU. Access will be conditional on deployment authorisation, and evaluations will focus on unauthorised agent coordination, misuse potential, and weight security. We are negotiating with national cybersecurity agencies and academic consortia to staff the first red-team units, with initial funding drawn from the Digital Europe Programme and Horizon Europe safety research envelopes.

The US action on Anthropic’s models has made clear that reliance on foreign-controlled frontier AI is not a policy choice — it is a vulnerability. Our industrial measures are not about catching up in a race we are losing; they are about ensuring that when the next crisis hits, the EU can assess, act, and defend without asking permission. That is the substance of sovereignty, and it begins with compute and competence we control.
