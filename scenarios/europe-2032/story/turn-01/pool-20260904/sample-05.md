## New measure  
**Measure:** Launch EU Evaluation Corps  
Build a permanent, independent technical agency capable of pre-release evaluation and continuous monitoring of frontier models under development in the EU and beyond.

`Category: 5 (Public technical capacity and research)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 6`  
`Targeted effect:` Raises `ai_safety` by enabling real oversight; supports `eu_ai_sovereignty` by building sovereign technical judgment. Indirectly improves `resilience` and lowers pressure on `public_sentiment` by grounding regulatory decisions in evidence.  
`Applies to:` EU jurisdiction, with access rights negotiated for systems used by EU entities or operating across its markets.

## Priority  
**Priority:** InvestAI Gigafactories — because without sovereign compute, every other measure depends on permission from powers whose interests do not align with ours, and this turn’s US action against Anthropic proves that dependency is already strategic vulnerability.

## In practice  
This turn, we begin operationalising the InvestAI Gigafactories programme by convening EIB, national development banks, and ENTSO-E to identify grid-ready sites in Finland, Sweden, and northern Spain where cooling and renewable supply allow rapid scaling. We issue the first call for proposals under the €200bn envelope, tied to binding commitments on power purchase agreements and workforce training partnerships with local universities. Resistance from regional authorities over land use and energy allocation is expected; we counter with guaranteed reinvestment in local infrastructure and a share of jobs reserved for host communities. The legal basis rests on Article 118 TFEU for industrial policy and delegated powers under the Net-Zero Industry Act.

Simultaneously, we launch the scoping phase for the EU Evaluation Corps, tasking BEREC with organisational design and DG CONNECT with technical specifications. We open negotiations with CERN, ESA, and national cybersecurity agencies to second staff and repurpose secure facilities for red-teaming and interpretability work. Funding will draw from Horizon Europe and the Digital Europe Programme, but we prepare a supplementary proposal for mid-term review if matching contributions lag. The aim is not just assessment, but *anticipation* — building institutional intuition about agent drift, emergent coordination, and model autonomy that today exists nowhere outside the labs themselves. This is not duplication of the AI Office, but augmentation: an embedded technical corps with clearance, continuity, and competence.
