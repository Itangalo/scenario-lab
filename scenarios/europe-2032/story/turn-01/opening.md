# Turn 1 — the shared frame

Turn 1 is identical in every arm and on every branch. Two things are fixed here and are not the reader's to choose: the event, and what the Union already carries.

The event is `cyber_test_shot`, pinned by `pin-turn-1.py` rather than drawn. The world state is the scenario's own opening description, seeded from `background/context.md`.

What the Union carries into the turn is the portfolio below. It is inherited rather than chosen — two programmes already running (seeded at turn 0) when the actor takes over. Under framework custody the options do not restate it; each option carries a `Store changes` command adding its own measure instead.

## Portfolio
- ``InvestAI Gigafactories (category 4, costs 3 per turn, started turn 0, finishes on turn 7): €200bn fund for four to five sites``
- ``Tech sovereignty package (category 4, costs 3 per turn, started turn 0, finishes on turn 6): Targets €200bn of private capital for AI data centres by 2036 and proposes accelerated-permitting zones``

## What the reader chooses

Everything else in the turn-1 response — the two-year commitment, the new measure, the priority and the prose — belongs to the option, because the two-year commitment is named in turn 1 and it is the thing the first choice decides.

- [`option-02-1.md`](option-02-1.md) — **EU Critical Services Shield.** Baseline standards, joint exercises and detection for energy, telecoms, health and finance. Category 6, large, finishes turn 4. Answers the test shot by hardening what must not fail.
- [`option-02-2.md`](option-02-2.md) — **EU AI Evaluation and Safety Institute.** Independent frontier evaluation, weight-security audits, interpretability funding. Category 5, large, finishes turn 4. Buys sight before shock.

Each option file is a complete, valid turn-1 actor response, so it can be passed to `pin-turn-1.py` as it stands.
