## New measure
**EU Embodied Autonomy Containment and Shopfloor Continuity Pact**
A preparedness and resilience pact that puts armed/logistics-robot incident playbooks, manual-fallback staffing and SME supplier continuity credit behind the robotics shock hitting EU industry from outside. Category 6 preparedness and resilience — why this and why now: the capability jump plus commercial robots landing on hostile public sentiment and brittle small utilities means displacement and sabotage arrive together, and the US access offer risks hollowing home-build urgency unless we show protection first.

## Priority
M6 EU Municipal Trust and Transition Pact — because with political capital at 14 and sentiment at 20 nothing else lands unless small-town staffing, insurer cover and visible waiting-list wins hold first, outranking even the new robotics shock under our commitment to trusted capacity and resilience.

## In practice
We run M6 hard through DG EMPL, DG REGIO and the Committee of Regions: ESF+ wage-bridges, HERA/ rescEU-funded locum pools for clinics and water operators, EIB backstop to keep reinsurers in small-utility risk, and ENISA's rewritten manual-fallback playbooks drilled not just published. Mayors who staff get cash; public channels carry only triage and maintenance wins that already happened.

On M1 we hold the line on the two advanced Gigafactory sites with TEN-E grid priority and EIB guarantees while Taiwan allocations stay frozen, coordinating lithography holds in Council via the Anti-Coercion Instrument and preparing joint-evaluation terms for the incoming US administration without trading away build-at-home. The new Pact is stood up via Civil Protection Mechanism and DG CNECT/DG MOVE with industry: no-armed-retrofit procurement clauses, geofenced trials for logistics robots, and working-capital lines for SME suppliers hit by Chinese robot undercutting and US control-model licensing.
