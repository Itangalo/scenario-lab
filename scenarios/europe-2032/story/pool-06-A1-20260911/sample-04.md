**EU Embodied Deployment Shield**
Extends the Critical Services and Bio-Shield playbooks to commercially deployed robots: mandatory incident reporting and manual-fallback for logistics, care and municipal fleets, cyber-hardening for fleet operators, and EU-preference procurement to keep a domestic robotics base alive. Category 6 preparedness and resilience measure — why this and why now: embodied AI hits the EU industrial base from outside via Chinese hardware and American models within this turn, while the capability jump obsoletes prior timelines, so absorption and supply-chain defence outrank new frontier promises.
