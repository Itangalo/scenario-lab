## New measure
**EU Embodied-AI Industrial Hold**
Screens and hardens deployment of foreign-controlled industrial robots in EU factories and logistics while funding EU-controlled control-stack retrofits and manual-fallback drills. Category 6 preparedness and resilience measure — why this and why now: embodied deployment lands directly on the EU industrial base we still lead in via Chinese hardware and American models, and with a capability jump plus safety at 18 we cannot govern the models so must blunt cascade and coercion at the point of deployment.

## Priority
M6 EU Municipal Trust and Transition Pact — because with political capital at 14 and sentiment at 20 nothing else lands if small towns and clinics keep rejecting the playbooks.

## In practice
We run M6 through DG EMPL, the Committee of Regions and ESF+, keeping wage bridges and staffing pacts for utilities/clinics flowing and using EIB backstop to keep insurers in small-utility cover; mayors get the triage waiting-list wins to show. M1 stays narrowed to the two grid-prioritised Gigafactory sites under TEN-E while Taiwan allocations are frozen, no new compute line opened.

On the new Hold we task DG GROW and DG CNECT with an implementing act under the AI Act plus NIS2: inventory of Chinese-built/American-model robots in critical manufacturing, procurement preference for EU-controllable stacks, and ENISA-led isolation and manual-fallback exercises with unions and employers. We coordinate with the incoming US administration on joint evaluation access to ease frontier access without trading away the home-build case.
