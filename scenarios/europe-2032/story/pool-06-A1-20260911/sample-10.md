## New measure
**EU Embodied-AI Containment and Continuity Protocol**
Extends the Shield playbooks to commercially deployed robots — mandatory kill-switches, manual override and maintenance fallback in critical sites, plus procurement preference for EU-serviceable systems. Category 6 preparedness and resilience measure — why this and why now: the capability jump plus Chinese-built, US-modelled robots land directly on EU industry and municipalities already brittle from the autumn failures, and with capital at 14 we can only afford a small extension of what is built, not a new build.

## Priority
M6 EU Municipal Trust and Transition Pact — because with sentiment at 20 and capital at 14, holding small-town staffing and insurer cover is the only thing that keeps any other shield enforceable through the robot wave.

## In practice
We run M6 through DG EMPL, the Committee of Regions and ESF+, keeping wage-bridges and utility staffing pacts funded and the EIB insurer backstop extended to robot-related downtime, while pushing triage and waiting-list wins to blunt hostility. ENISA and HERA stretch M5/M3 playbooks to embodied systems via implementing acts: isolation with manual chlorination and grid fallback now covers robotic maintenance ports, no new directive needed.

On M1 we hold the two grid-prioritised Gigafactory sites under TEN-E and EIB guarantees and use the new US coalition opening in the Trade and Technology Council to negotiate published allied-access terms as a bridge, explicitly ring-fenced so it does not replace the home build. Lithography coordination continues via the Anti-Coercion Instrument.
