## The simulation ends here

The story has reached a catastrophic event. It doesn't necessarily mean that the world ends here, but this story does. The scenario underpinning this story is not written to simulate a world after a catastrophe, and any continued simulations would continue as if this were business as usual. It would not be, so the story stops.

**This is most likely not caused by your choices.** Catastrophes like this one are driven mainly by forces the Union does not control: how fast AI advances, the rivalry between the United States and China, and chance. Some of the Union's measures can lower the odds of certain disasters, but no choice in this story decides whether one happens. Your last choice in particular came too late to cause it.

If you start over, the years may go very differently.
