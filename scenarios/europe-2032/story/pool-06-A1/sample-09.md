## In practice
We are acting on the fact that the EU is now living inside a system it did not design and cannot fully inspect. The biosecurity leak—confirming that a pathogen blueprint is reconstructable for under €100,000—has triggered emergency sessions in the Health Security Committee and the Political and Security Committee. We are responding by expanding the BioShield Initiative from pilot nodes to a full network, co-located with EuroHPC centres, using sovereign AI to monitor DNA synthesis orders, research supply chains, and wastewater signals in real time. This is being funded through a reallocation of €4.2bn from the unspent portion of the NextGenerationEU digital tranche, under Article 122 TFEU, and will be operational in all 27 capitals by turn 8.

At the same time, we are forcing the ratification of the European AI Emergency Response Corps mandate through the Council’s Integrated Political Crisis Response framework. Legal resistance from Germany, Austria, and Finland—concerned about sovereignty over national infrastructure—is being met with binding offers: access to pooled sovereign compute, guaranteed response times under attack, and shared liability protections for operators who comply. We are staffing the Corps with 500 technical operators drawn from ENISA, ECDC, and national cyber agencies, trained in AI incident containment and agent isolation. Their authority to intervene in compromised systems—without waiting for national approval during declared incidents—is being codified in a new Regulation on AI Crisis Powers, tabled this month in the Commission and fast-tracked under urgent procedure.

The Resilience Surge Protocol continues to roll out: €9bn in emergency funding is being disbursed to harden energy, water, and election systems, with mandatory AI-driven red-teaming every quarter. The Social Shock Facility is now pre-funded with €6bn, tied to national reforms in retraining access. But we know this is not enough. That is why we are launching the BioShield Expansion and Compulsory Red-Teaming measure: no model above 50.0 capability deployed in critical sectors will be allowed to operate without quarterly adversarial testing by an EU-authorized red team. This is not a request—it is a condition of deployment. The frontier may be moving faster than we can regulate, but we will not let it move faster than we can resist.
