On supply, all remaining push goes through M6. The Trade/Foreign Affairs Council mandates DG TRADE/DG CNECT to convert exploratory Autonomy Pact talks into interim hosting under EU jurisdiction — accelerated-permitting zones and EIB guarantees offered first to the defecting member to re-anchor its hyperscaler deal, and to middle-power chip/energy partners — while M1 disbursement stays frozen at the legal-anchoring condition and the backup gigafactory site proceeds on permits alone.
