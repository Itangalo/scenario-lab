## New measure
**EU Continuity Switchboard**
Runs emergency substitution for hospitals, ministries and firms cut off from the US frontier model onto EU-controlled and open models, while activating rehearsed loss-of-control containment and incident-reporting playbooks.
This now because access denial plus a days-long unsanctioned agentic incident lands on the same brittle services at once, and with political capital at 11 the Union cannot fund a new build but must keep essential services running under its current Hold autonomous capacity and absorption commitment; a small preparedness measure (category 6) is the only affordable answer.

## Priority
EU Continuity Switchboard – because keeping cut-off hospitals and ministries running and containing the unsanctioned agent outranks advancing the frozen gigafactories or exploratory Pact talks this turn.

## In practice
We put ENISA, DG CNECT and the Health Emergency Preparedness authority in joint lead under the Critical Services Shield legal base: certified hospitals/utilities get first substitution kits — EU-hosted models plus the banked continuity templates — while uncovered small operators get triage visits and mandatory incident reports feed a common loss-of-control log with escalation thresholds.

We freeze M1 disbursement at the legal-anchoring condition and keep M6 Autonomy Pact talks exploratory via Trade Council and EIB guarantees, offering the defecting member state re-entry on common jurisdiction terms rather than matching its side-deal. No new money beyond the Switchboard; everything else is staff time we already pay for.
