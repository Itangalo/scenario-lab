## New measure
**Measure:** Launch Sovereign Resilience Stack  
Deploy a unified, EU-operated technical and governance layer across critical infrastructure—finance, energy, health—integrating sovereign compute, real-time model logging, and automated cyber response into a single auditable stack.

Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 8  
Targeted effect: Increase resilience by 20–25 points by turn 8, close the enforcement gap in EAAD oversight, and raise eu_ai_sovereignty by 10–15 points by anchoring control in EU-run systems  
Applies to: Member states, ENISA, EAAD, national CERTs, critical infrastructure operators, InvestAI Gigafactory sites

## Priority
**Priority:** Launch Sovereign Resilience Stack  
Because the cyber_major_incident has revealed that our sovereignty is hollow without resilience, and our resilience is blind without control—this measure integrates both into a single operational reality, making the infrastructure we are building actually defensible and accountable, not just powered.

## In practice
We are treating this turn as the pivot from infrastructure promises to operational sovereignty. The cyber attack—its scale, speed, and model-driven nature—has shattered any illusion that compute without control is power. It is exposure. The Secure Energy-Backed Compute Sovereignty programme ends this turn with partial implementation: 5GW was declared, but only 1.8GW is physically ring-fenced and sovereign, concentrated in Sweden and France. Germany and Austria continue to challenge the legality of Commission action under Article 122 TFEU, and ENTSO-E remains unwilling to bind grid operators without unanimity. We accept that energy-backed compute cannot be achieved through directive alone—it must be built, hardened, and defended as a system.

Accordingly, we are launching the Sovereign Resilience Stack as a new large-scale measure under Category 6. This is not an add-on—it is the integration of everything we have built: the InvestAI Gigafactories will host the stack’s core nodes; the EAAD’s logging mandate will be embedded directly into the software supply chain of critical systems; ENISA will operationalise automated patching and swarm detection using sovereign models running on sovereign hardware. We are directing the EIB to reprofile €45bn from the now-completed Tech Sovereignty Package into this effort, prioritising microgrid-isolated compute clusters co-located with nuclear and geothermal sources. Legal authority will be established through a Council Implementing Regulation under Article 122 TFEU, bypassing COREPER II gridlock, with enforcement tied to access to EU funding and public procurement.

Italy and Hungary’s resistance to real-time logging cannot stand. We are offering them a binding seat on the EAAD’s technical oversight panel and co-development rights on the Stack’s monitoring interface—but access to EU-wide services will now require full auditability. No logging, no access. On the public front, we are launching a crisis communication campaign: “One System, Under Our Control,” showing citizens how the Stack will detect attacks, isolate failures, and keep hospitals, banks, and grids running. This is not just defence—it is demonstration of sovereignty in action. Without it, we are not protecting the Union—we are managing its dependence.
