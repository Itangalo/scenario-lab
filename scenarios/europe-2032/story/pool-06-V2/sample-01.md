## In practice  
We are treating this turn as the pivot from infrastructure promise to operational reality. The cyber_major_incident—its speed, automation, and use of model-generated exploits—has shattered any illusion that sovereignty can be deferred. We are now directing the EIB to reallocate €45bn from the expired Tech sovereignty package and the stalled SCAP envelope into the immediate deployment of the Sovereign AI Stack Pilot. This will fund the integration of nuclear microreactors in Brittany and geothermal in northern Sweden with the first two InvestAI Gigafactories, ensuring not just power but fuel stockpiling and microgrid isolation. These sites will host the first sovereign model weights—derived from pre-60.0 open but now secured and hardened—running under EAAD certification and ENISA red-teaming, with real-time logging now mandated via a fast-tracked AI Act amendment under Article 114, bypassing COREPER II through qualified majority in the Council.

The EAAD is being empowered to issue emergency deployment certificates for models below 55.0 capability that meet the new auditability standard, enabling rapid onboarding for cyber defence and medical triage systems. We are instructing ENISA to operationalise the Emergency Resilience Corridors by turn 6, integrating the Sovereign AI Stack into Tier 1 critical operators for automated patching and swarm detection. Italy and Hungary are being offered co-leadership in the pilot’s healthcare module and direct access to the sovereign stack for national CERTs—concrete inclusion in exchange for dropping resistance. Public sentiment remains at 24.0, and we are launching a visible “Shield & Serve” campaign to show the Union deploying AI *for* protection, not just displacement. If we cannot demonstrate control, we will lose both trust and territory.
