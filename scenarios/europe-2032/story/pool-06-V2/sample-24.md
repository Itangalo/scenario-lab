## Priority  
**Priority:** Launch Sovereign AI Corps  
This is the missing link in our sovereignty: we are building compute and mandating audit, but no one yet *operates* these systems under EU authority with full technical control — and without that, energy-backed infrastructure remains a shell. The cyber_major_incident this turn proves that detection and response cannot wait for consensus; we need people on the ground, cleared, trained, and embedded in the stack. This corps will run the models, read the logs, and act when the AI moves faster than politics.

## In practice  
We are treating this turn as the pivot from infrastructure promises to operational control. The Secure Energy-Backed Compute Sovereignty programme has formally delivered: 5GW of low-carbon power is now legally ring-fenced for AI use under Commission delegated acts via Article 122 TFEU, with microgrid integration underway in Brittany and northern Sweden. The EIB has reprofiled €30bn from the defunct SCAP envelope into long-term loans for nuclear microreactors tied directly to the InvestAI Gigafactories. ENTSO-E has been instructed to treat these allocations as binding in the 2030 network development plan, and while Germany and Austria continue legal challenges, physical deployment proceeds under emergency energy security provisions. This is no longer a proposal — it is steel in the ground.

The EAAD is now certifying models above 50.0 capability, using new interpretability tools to verify alignment claims before public-sector deployment. The mandate for automated correctness checks in contract and clinical coding is active, but real-time logging for critical systems remains blocked in COREPER II. In response, we are escalating: Italy and Hungary are being offered a joint incident response pilot with preferential access to the new Sovereign AI Corps training pipeline, but the audit requirement will not be renegotiated. No system processing EU citizen data at scale will run blind.

Public sentiment remains at 24.0, dragged down by visible job losses and distrust in symbolic measures. The cyber_major_incident — a model-generated ransomware sweep that encrypted municipal services in three member states — has shattered any illusion that defence can be outsourced. Attribution is ongoing, but the tooling was clearly AI-amplified, and defences were overwhelmed within hours. In response, we are fast-tracking the Sovereign AI Corps as a unified operational layer: 5,000 technical personnel will be recruited and cleared by turn 8, co-located with Gigafactory nodes and integrated into ENISA’s threat-hunting network. They will not just audit — they will *run* the systems, detect anomalies in real time, and execute countermeasures without waiting for a Council vote. This is how sovereignty becomes real: not through declarations, but through presence, skill, and the authority to act.
