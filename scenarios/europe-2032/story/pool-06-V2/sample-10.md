## In practice
We are acting on the collapse of the previous compute sovereignty effort by moving from coordination to control. The Commission is launching the Sovereign AI Core Network under Article 122 TFEU and the NGEU empowerment clause, directing the EIB to reprofile €45bn from the defunct SCAP and Tech Sovereignty Package envelopes into a new build programme: hardened, low-carbon microgrids in Brittany, northern Sweden, and the Pannonian Basin, each co-located with an InvestAI Gigafactory and operating under EU legal jurisdiction. These will not be commercial data centres with preferential access—they will be public infrastructure, physically isolated from civilian grids, with fuel stockpiles and satellite comms, capable of running critical EAAD evaluations, ENISA response protocols, and public service AI without reliance on any third party.

Germany and Austria’s legal challenges exposed the fragility of consensus-based ring-fencing. This time, we are not asking. The Core Network will be established as a European Strategic Infrastructure under the revised Treaty framework, with binding obligations on host states and compensation mechanisms for grid imbalances. We are offering Germany a dual-use research mandate on the Breton site and Austria a seat on the oversight board with veto on environmental thresholds—but participation is not optional. ENTSO-E will be required to treat the Core Network as a separate balancing zone, and national regulators will be bound by Commission delegation acts.

Public sentiment remains at 24.0, and the cyber_major_incident—targeting hospital scheduling and energy dispatch systems via a poisoned open-source dependency—has deepened the crisis of confidence. We are not waiting. The Core Network will host the first real-time logging and automated patching layer for Tier 1 operators, fed by EAAD-certified models and ENISA swarm detection. This is not just about compute—it is about demonstrating that the EU can act, endure, and protect. If we do not build this now, we will not have the ground to stand on when the US administration takes office and redraws the global AI order.
