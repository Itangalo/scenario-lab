The EAAD is now operational, having certified its first models above 50.0 capability using new interpretability tools. We are no longer negotiating access—we are building it. This is not optional compliance; it is architectural enforcement. Any AI system in a critical public function must run on this chain to be deployed. We are instructing the Commission to draft a delegated act under the AI Act to mandate this by turn 7, with phased enforcement starting in finance and energy.
