## New measure
**Measure:** Launch Sovereign Resilience Integration Programme  
Establish an integrated, EU-operated cyber resilience layer for sovereign AI infrastructure, combining automated threat detection, pre-authorised countermeasures, and real-time auditability across energy, finance, and health systems.

`Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)`  
`Size: large`  
`Finishes on turn: 8`  
`Targeted effect:` Increase resilience by 20–25 points by turn 8, close the enforcement gap in critical infrastructure logging, and reduce the mean time to contain AI-enabled attacks from days to hours  
`Applies to:` Member states, ENISA, EAAD, national CERTs, critical infrastructure operators, InvestAI Gigafactory operators

## Priority
**Priority:** Launch Sovereign Resilience Integration Programme  
This is no longer a choice between sovereignty and resilience—the cyber_major_incident has fused them: without resilience, sovereign infrastructure is just a more expensive target. This measure ensures that the compute we are building under the Gigafactories and energy-backed programmes is not only independent but survivable, with integrated defences that operate at AI speed and are legally empowered to act.

## In practice
We are treating the cyber_major_incident as a system shock, not an outlier. The attack—using AI-generated exploit chains against public services—has exposed a fatal gap: we have been building sovereign infrastructure as if it were only a matter of energy and ownership, but not of operational endurance. The Commission is now invoking Article 4 of the Cyber Resilience Act, in conjunction with the recently strengthened ENISA mandate, to establish a unified command layer across Tier 1 critical operators. This will integrate the InvestAI Gigafactories into a sovereign resilience network, requiring all operators to adopt ENISA-certified AI-driven intrusion detection systems with pre-authorized response protocols, including automated patching and traffic isolation.

We are directing the EIB to reprofile €25bn from the now-completed Tech sovereignty package into this new programme, funding both the integration layer and the auditability stack required for EAAD oversight. The logging deadlock in COREPER II will be broken by attaching compliance to access to EU cohesion funds—Italy and Hungary will be offered co-development roles in the detection stack and fast-tracked retraining programmes for displaced public-sector workers. Germany and Austria will be assured that microgrid isolation preserves energy market integrity. The EAAD will be empowered to conduct red-team evaluations of the entire system by turn 7, with results binding on operation.

Public sentiment remains at 24.0, and the perception of AI as a source of harm is deepening. We are launching a visibility campaign around the Sovereign Resilience Integration Programme, framing it as the first tangible shield for citizens and institutions. The message is clear: sovereignty is not just about who owns the servers, but whether they keep running when the lights go out. This is the pivot—from symbolic assurance to operational endurance. Without it, no amount of compute will save us.
