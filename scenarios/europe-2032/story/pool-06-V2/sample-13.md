## In practice
We are treating this turn as the pivot from infrastructure promises to operational control. The Secure Energy-Backed Compute Sovereignty programme has formally delivered: 5GW of low-carbon power is now ring-fenced under Commission Regulation 2029/SCS-1, co-developed with national energy ministries and legally anchored via Article 122 TFEU. ENTSO-E has been directed to integrate these allocations into the 2030 Network Development Plan, with microgrid isolation protocols for the Brittany and northern Sweden sites. Germany and Austria have withdrawn their legal challenges in exchange for binding impact assessments every six months and a seat on the SCS Oversight Board. The EIB has reprofiled €30bn from the failed SCAP envelope into long-term loans for nuclear microreactors and geothermal integration—tying physical compute to physical energy, at last.

The EAAD is now live and has issued its first three certifications under the updated AI Act, mandating automated verification for all public-sector contracts and clinical coding systems above 50.0 capability. But Italy and Hungary still block real-time logging access for critical infrastructure, and without technical enforcement capacity, our legal authority is hollow. That ends with the Sovereign Audit and Control Stack: we are tasking ENISA and the EAAD to co-develop it with Fraunhofer, CEA, and a newly formed EU AI Safety Lab, funded through Horizon Europe and the Digital Europe Programme. The stack will be open-source but permissioned—available only to systems operating on EU-sovereign infrastructure—and will include runtime interpretability probes, anomaly detection with automated rollback, and cryptographic audit trails accessible to national regulators and the EAAD. We are mandating its deployment on all InvestAI Gigafactory outputs by turn 7.

We are reframing our entire narrative: sovereignty is not about compute for growth, but control for survival. If we cannot enforce our own safety on our own infrastructure, then we are not sovereign—we are tenants. And tenants do not determine their own future.
