## Priority
**Priority:** Accelerate Sovereign Audit Infrastructure  
Because assurance without access is ritual, not control — and because this turn’s cyber_major_incident proved that even second-tier actors now wield model-generated offensive tools beyond our ability to trace or stop — we must shift from certifying models at release to auditing cognition in operation. The Secure Energy-Backed Compute Sovereignty programme has laid the physical foundation; now we must make it intelligent, transparent, and defensible. This measure builds the technical backbone that turns sovereign infrastructure into sovereign understanding.

## In practice
We are treating this turn as the pivot from hardware to insight. The completion of the Secure Energy-Backed Compute Sovereignty programme gives us 5GW of ring-fenced low-carbon power — now legally anchored through Council Regulation under Article 122 TFEU despite German and Austrian challenges, which will be addressed through joint monitoring but not delay deployment. The EIB has reprofiled €30bn from SCAP into long-term loans for microreactors in Brittany and geothermal in northern Sweden, directly tied to InvestAI Gigafactory operations. These sites will host not just compute, but the first wave of sovereign audit infrastructure: secure enclaves running open-weight variants of frontier models under full interpretability stacks, developed in partnership with the EAAD and vetted researchers.

The cyber_major_incident — a widespread ransomware sweep across regional health administrations, exploiting an AI-generated zero-day in a legacy patient management dependency — has crystallised the stakes. Attribution remains uncertain, but forensic analysis confirms the exploit chain was autonomously designed and tested. ENISA’s preliminary report shows no existing detection system caught it before deployment. We are therefore fast-tracking integration between the EAAD’s new certification regime and real-time logging mandates: all models above 50.0 capability used in Tier 1 services must now stream anonymised attention weights and action rationales to sovereign audit nodes. Italy and Hungary have been offered opt-in technical assistance and observer status on the EAAD board; refusal will mean exclusion from EU-wide procurement contracts.

Public sentiment remains at 24.0, and trust in institutions is fraying. The failed Emergency Resilience Corridors campaign showed that preparedness must be seen to protect — not just promised. We are therefore launching a visible pilot: the first three regional hospitals restored after the attack will be rebuilt as fully auditable AI environments, with live dashboards showing model decisions, override logs, and security patches deployed by swarm detection. This is not symbolic. If we cannot demonstrate that the Union sees what its systems do, no amount of resilience will restore legitimacy — and no amount of compute will remain ours to command.
