## New measure
**Measure:** Unify Sovereign AI Command and Response  
Establish a single operational authority with legal mandate to coordinate sovereign AI infrastructure, real-time audit, and crisis response across energy, compute, and critical services, integrating EAAD, ENISA, and national CERTs into a unified command structure with binding authority over Tier 1 systems.

Category: 6 (Preparedness and resilience)  
Size: large  
Finishes on turn: 8  
Targeted effect: Increase resilience by 10–15 points by turn 8, close the enforcement gap in auditability, and reduce response latency to AI-driven incidents by institutionalising cross-domain command

## Priority  
**Priority:** Unify Sovereign AI Command and Response  
This is no longer about building capacity—it is about making what we have *work*. The cyber_major_incident revealed that fragmented oversight, delayed protocols, and uncoordinated sovereignty are not weaknesses but systemic failure points. Without a single command, our energy-backed compute, our assurance regime, and our resilience plans remain siloed and ineffective. This measure is the necessary integration of our two-year commitment into a functioning whole.

## In practice  
We are treating this turn as the moment the cost of fragmentation became undeniable. The cyber_major_incident—automated, widespread, and exploiting open-weight tooling—has hit public services across three member states, disrupting hospital scheduling, tax processing, and regional grid monitoring. Attribution is stalled, but the attack vector was clearly AI-generated and self-propagating. ENISA’s reports confirm that existing protocols failed to detect or contain it in time. This is the breaking point: we can no longer afford parallel tracks.

We are now launching the Unify Sovereign AI Command and Response measure under Article 175 TFEU (civil protection) and Article 4(2) TEU (security coordination), with the Commission proposing a Council Regulation to establish a Joint AI Crisis Authority (JACA). This body will integrate EAAD’s audit mandates, ENISA’s cyber response, and national CERTs into a single operational command with pre-delegated authority to act during AI-driven incidents. It will have binding access to real-time logs from all Tier 1 critical systems, including those running on sovereign compute, and will report directly to the European Council during emergencies.

The EIB is redirecting €12bn from the now-completed Tech sovereignty package into JACA’s integration layer—secure comms, shared AI monitoring stacks, and joint training exercises. Germany and Austria are being offered veto-proof observer status on JACA’s oversight board in exchange for dropping legal challenges to the energy ring-fencing, now reclassified as critical infrastructure under the new framework. Italy and Hungary are being presented with a binary: full audit access or exclusion from EU-wide AI procurement. We are not asking for trust—we are enforcing interoperability. The public will be informed not through another campaign, but through the visible activation of JACA within 90 days. If we cannot act as one, we will not survive as one.
