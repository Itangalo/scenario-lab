## New measure
**EU Automated Decisions Redress and Audit Fix**
Creates an EU-level rapid redress, logging-review and conformity re-check for AI-supported welfare, policing and court-assistance systems after the ombudsman scandal, paying back the wronged and re-imposing meaningful human review. Category 2 transparency and reporting measure — why this and why now: with capital at 9 and sentiment at 20 the scandal will otherwise collapse what remains of consent for the Corps and Dividend, and only a visible fix that admits breach and pays redress can keep adoption politically possible.

## In practice
We run the Corps as the main spend through DG EMPL, ESF+ top-ups and EPSCO: standardise 12-month contracts, push funds directly to municipalities, hospitals and guarded build sites in Lyon, Porto, Gdansk and dark-map university cities, and condition any Dividend follow-on on common procurement terms. Mayors get slots, firms get wage-insurance only if they retain-retrain alongside AI.

We run the Redress as enforcement, not new AI law, through DG JUST, FRA and national ombudsmen under the AI Act high-risk Annex III base: freeze the impugned queue-approval deployments, order human-review time minima and log re-reads, fund legal aid and back-payments from the deployers, and publish the audit trail. Where the gap reading holds we table a narrow delegated fix to close it, without reopening the Act.

We absorb the other shocks without new instruments: celebrate the research and medical breakthroughs through Horizon and HERA procurement on EU-anchored capacity only to limit dependence resentment, and trigger Bio-Shield/Continuity Stack stock and Strait-rerouting rehearsals for the Taiwan precursor via the Integrated Political Crisis Response, with no new measure this turn beyond the two on the books.
