## In practice
We run what is already on the books and nothing else. DG EMPL pushes the ESF+ clearances for M10 through EPSCO by emergency written procedure, mayors get the next tranche of 12-month placements in wards where graduate unemployment is darkest, and wage-insurance is paid only on common procurement terms to hold the line against the hyperscale side-deal split. NIS2 joint teams and ENISA keep hospitals, ports and the Lyon/Porto/Gdansk Dividend modules degraded-not-stopped on EU-anchored capacity, with civil-protection guarding of fenced sites and grid-reserved chips as routine policing, not a new programme.

This turn forces us to name what happened. The automated welfare-fraud scoring scandal in two member states — confirmed by ombudsmen to have systematically cut benefits — sat in a gap the AI Act's Annex III categories do not reach for 2030 agentic deployments, so everything done was lawful: enforcement intact, the Act itself now looking badly drawn, and restriction cheap while adoption is politically toxic.
