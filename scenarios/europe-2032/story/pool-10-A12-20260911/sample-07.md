## In practice
We run the Corps as the survival line through DG EMPL and EPSCO: ESF+ top-ups cleared by emergency procedure, wage-insurance paid directly to SMEs retaining juniors, and 12-month placements concentrated in Lyon/Porto/Gdansk clinics, hospitals and guarded build sites where Dividend modules already cut queues, with mayors given slot quotas to stop the ward-by-ward lottery.

We run the Redress as enforcement repair through DG JUST, the AI Office and national ombudsmen under AI Act Articles 59/65: freeze the offending social-insurance system to meaningful human review with time-per-case standards, publish the logs that were never read, and pay arrears and compensation from the deployer's conformity liability, while classifying the gap deployment as high-risk in practice. No new frontier build is announced; chips on paper are pushed into racks under civil-protection guard, allied-access stays warm, and the US materials/physics result and foreign-model medical advance are procured only on EU-anchored terms to avoid a further dependence shock.
