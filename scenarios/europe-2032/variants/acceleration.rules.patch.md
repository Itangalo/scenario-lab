1. **The trajectory regime sets the growth of `ai_capability`.** This run's regime is ACCELERATION: +2.5 to +4 per turn, steadily – slower than compounding, never stalled. After `rsi_onset`: +6 to +10 per turn, and the increment itself grows, carrying capability into the **88–96** terminal zone within a few turns. Growth is sub-linear above 85: halve the stated increment there, because the instrument is losing resolution rather than the world slowing down. Rates are floors on motion, not decorations – a trajectory that stalls well below its terminal zone is misapplying the rule.

3. **`openweight_capability` tracks `ai_capability` at a lag, and under ACCELERATION the lag widens into a growing distance.** Open weights trail the closed frontier by roughly six to nine months throughout, and against an accelerating frontier that fixed lag is a capability gap that grows: the gap widens by 1 to 3 per turn, and by 3 to 6 per turn once `rsi_onset` has occurred. This is the regime where frontier-only governance keeps working, because the dangerous capability stays inside a shrinking number of auditable organisations. `openweight_frontier_release` cuts the gap to 5 or below at a stroke and the gap then re-widens at the regime rate, which is what makes that event survivable here. A fully implemented restriction on open release, binding where such models are actually trained, adds a further 2 to 4 per turn to the gap while it holds; binding only inside the Union it adds nothing. `openweight_capability` never exceeds `ai_capability` and never falls.
