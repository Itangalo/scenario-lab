## Knowledge Work Is Boosted, Not Replaced
**ID:** knowledge_work_augmented
**Probability:** 14%. Halve if `labour_displacement` occurred in either of the previous 2 completed turns.
**Description:** The evidence arrives from ordinary offices: measured productivity gains in law, accountancy, administration, journalism and consulting, largest among the least experienced, and no matching fall in employment. This is where it stops. The tools are useful, they are finished, and what was sold as a transition turns out to have been the destination – which is a good outcome for anyone holding a job and a poor one for anyone who borrowed against the transition continuing.

## Robots Leave the Demonstration Floor
**ID:** embodied_ai_deployment
**Probability:** 4%.
**Description:** Robots reach commercial deployment in the narrowest sense: fixed tasks in settings arranged entirely around the machine, sold against the cost of the labour they replace and no more. Even the coarse-motor promise is only half kept – the machines work where the environment is controlled and stop at the edge of it. Defence ministries run pilot programmes for carrying and mine clearance and keep them as pilot programmes. Manufacturers who took orders against a capability that did not arrive spend the period explaining themselves.
