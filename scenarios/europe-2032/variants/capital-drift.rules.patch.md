# Metric rules patch — capital drifts toward legitimacy

Replaces rule 11 wholesale. Everything else is inherited from the verification-bounded arm.

11. **`eu_political_capital` recovers from results, erodes from their absence, and settles toward what the public will bear.**
    - a measure reaching its finishing turn: +4 to +8
    - a measure in flight or finished that visibly blunts an incident when it lands: +1 to +7, judged the same way as rule 13 — the bigger the event and the larger the measure, the bigger the gain. This is the mirror of rule 13: that one pays for acting after the harm, this one for having already acted.
    - a measure abandoned, a deadline missed, a proposal publicly defeated: −5 to −12
    - nothing in flight: −3
    - a live class of harm sitting unaddressed: a further −2
    - **drift toward legitimacy: each turn, move `eu_political_capital` 1 to 2 points toward `public_sentiment`, and never past it.** Above sentiment it sags, below it recovers, and where the two are within a couple of points nothing happens. Apply it after everything else, as the last thing that touches the metric.

    There is still no return to 48 for its own sake. What a Union out of capital can recover is what its publics will lend it, slowly, and only by holding them: the drift is toward sentiment wherever sentiment happens to be, which in a bad run is not far.
