## Strategic situation

- Frontier AI development is capital-intensive (data centres, chips, power); only large firms and states play unless diffusion happens [source: ai-wargames-survey in-repo, 2026-09-23].
- The frontier is concentrated: a few early movers hold most power over how AI impacts society, driven by talent concentration and, increasingly, compute (GPU acquisition races, Nvidia dominance via software stack, talk of trillion-dollar clusters) [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- Private labs race ahead of governments. Information asymmetry is structural: states and the public constantly deal with the impacts of the last generation while labs build the next [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- Compute is contentious. In about a third of recent games, compute is the leading factor in China's actions toward Taiwan, including military use of hard power to seize TSMC facilities; this sets US/Western labs back via chip limits and can help the PRC if production resumes [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- Global stability opens at 7 out of 10 and typically falls to 2–3 by turn 4, driven by unmitigated AI concerns and US–China adversarial actions [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- US presidential elections occur every 4 years (turns covering Nov 2028 and Nov 2032 in this scenario); outcomes are dice-influenced with context (unrest, economy, interference). Democrat-to-Republican transitions are usually very disruptive to trust and agreements [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- AI is a political issue from turn 1–2 but rarely the determinative election issue until late game [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].

## Technology

- Two-lane tech tree, four levels per lane: one lane for language/world-modelling advances, one for reinforcement learning in increasingly complex environments [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- Level 4 basic R&D unlocks radically transformative AI (RTAI): either agentic goal-driven AGI or Drexlerian Comprehensive AI Services [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- Predefined "concerns": negative consequences of AI applications that player actions can mitigate; a development–concern cycle repeats most games with companies outpacing governance [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- AI R&D capacity (researchers, data, compute) is the key team attribute; talent prefers top labs, reinforcing concentration [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- AI safety research exists as allocatable R&D but lags capabilities; coordination on it is rare and late [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].

## Institutions

- Complete nationalisation is acceptable in China but not in the West; US nationalisation attempts face internal-stability checks (Congress opposition) or soft-power legal challenges from the company [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- US public–private partnerships (PPPs) are the common mid/late-game form; firms usually retain decision power over final RTAI deployment while the state holds hard power for defence [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- The US rarely invokes the Defense Production Act; firms' soft and economic power lets them avoid it [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- Verification regimes for slowing/joint development exist as a mechanic; defection checks are resolved in secret and usually produce at least one defector from a resource-rich team [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].
- Game assets and IP transferred in 2024 to Technology Strategy Roleplay, UK charity no. 1200928 [source: Gruetzemacher et al., arXiv 2410.03092, retrieved 2026-09-23].

## Starting assumptions for 2026-01 [assumption]

- No RTAI deployed; both blocs sit at tech-tree level 1–2 with level-2 applications proliferating and first serious safety debates underway.
- No standing US–China AI treaty; baseline agreement_strength near zero; a thin layer of track-two safety dialogue exists.
- TSMC produces the leading edge; no blockade in force at start.
- Sitting US administration is Democratic-leaning for continuity purposes [assumption]; the 2028 election is the first scheduled disruption point.
