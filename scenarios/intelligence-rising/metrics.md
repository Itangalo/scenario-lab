# Metrics for Intelligence Rising

## us_capability

**Description:** US-bloc frontier AI level on the game's four-level technology tree (both lanes summarised in one number). Tracks how close the USA + Alphabet bloc is to deploying RTAI.

**ID:** us_capability

**Min:** 0

**Max:** 4

**Unit:** tech-tree level

**Start value:** 1.5

**Reference points:**

- 0: Pre-frontier, no general systems.
- 1: Strong narrow systems and early general assistants in wide use.
- 2: Advanced general systems reshaping software and knowledge work.
- 3: Near-RTAI, transformative products deployed, decisive-advantage debates begin.
- 4: RTAI deployed (agentic AGI or comprehensive AI services).

## china_capability

**Description:** China-bloc frontier AI level on the same four-level tree. The gap between the two capability metrics is what tempts preventive attack by the runner-up.

**ID:** china_capability

**Min:** 0

**Max:** 4

**Unit:** tech-tree level

**Start value:** 1.2

**Reference points:**

- 0: Pre-frontier, no general systems.
- 1: Strong narrow systems and early general assistants in wide use.
- 2: Advanced general systems reshaping production and governance.
- 3: Near-RTAI, transformative systems fielded, decisive-advantage debates begin.
- 4: RTAI deployed (agentic AGI or comprehensive AI services).

## safety_progress

**Description:** Shared solvedness of AI alignment and safety: verified methods, safety commons, and oversight that survive contact with deployment pressure. All blocs draw on it; nobody owns it alone.

**ID:** safety_progress

**Min:** 0

**Max:** 100

**Unit:** index

**Start value:** 20

**Reference points:**

- 0: Nothing verified, no shared methods.
- 25: Scattered lab-internal measures, unshared or unverifiable.
- 50: Joint safety commons exists but is unverified at deployment scale.
- 75: High-confidence verifiable methods with oversight in place.
- 90: Robust solutions plus verification that survives defection pressure.

## global_stability

**Description:** Overall geopolitical and social stability, the game's own 0–10 tracker. Falls as unmitigated AI impacts and US–China adversarial actions accumulate.

**ID:** global_stability

**Min:** 0

**Max:** 10

**Unit:** index

**Start value:** 7

**Reference points:**

- 0: Systemic breakdown, war footing, no functioning governance of AI.
- 2: Severe crisis, rolling confrontations, institutions barely coping.
- 4: Strained, reactive policymaking, frequent incidents.
- 7: Tense but functioning world, the 2026 opening position.
- 9: Calm, trusted verification, coordinated frontier governance.

## us_china_tension

**Description:** Bilateral escalation level between the United States and China across cyber, economic, and military domains.

**ID:** us_china_tension

**Min:** 0

**Max:** 100

**Unit:** index

**Start value:** 45

**Reference points:**

- 20: Cooperative rivalry with working crisis channels.
- 45: Structural rivalry short of crisis, the 2026 opening position.
- 60: Proxy race with sabotage, sanctions, and regular incidents.
- 75: Blockade or major cyber-campaign territory.
- 90: Preventive-strike logic active, runner-up weighs attack over submission.

## agreement_strength

**Description:** Robustness of binding US–China (and lab-inclusive) AI agreements including verification. The only historically observed path to good outcomes; reactive statements without verification score low.

**ID:** agreement_strength

**Min:** 0

**Max:** 100

**Unit:** index

**Start value:** 10

**Reference points:**

- 0: No agreements of any kind.
- 10: Track-two dialogue without commitments, the 2026 opening position.
- 30: Reactive bilateral statements and narrow safety pledges.
- 60: Binding treaty with verification that slows development until safety is shown.
- 85: Autonomy-assuring regime both blocs trust, democratic and non-democratic governments alike.
