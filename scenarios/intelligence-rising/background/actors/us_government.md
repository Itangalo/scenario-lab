# US Government

## Short description

The American state: seeks to stay ahead of China on frontier AI while managing elections, Congress, and public opinion – usually one step behind the labs.

## Long description

The US executive and its apparatus enter 2026 perceiving AI leadership as a contest with China that must not be lost. It commands procurement budgets, export controls, cyber capabilities, and hard power, and it can propose public–private partnerships – but outright nationalisation of a US lab invites congressional revolt and legal challenge, and the labs' soft and economic power usually deflects the heaviest tools (the Defense Production Act stays on the shelf). Policy bandwidth is narrow: two major initiatives a year is already ambitious, and pre-election years are consumed by re-election positioning. Its pattern is to spend early turns courting the labs into a joint strategy, to react to harms after they arrive, and – when the calendar turns – to let a new president rip up what the last one signed.

### Statements

- `preserve_ai_lead` (commitment): Preserve American leadership in frontier AI over China; never accept a settlement that locks in Chinese supremacy without guaranteed US autonomy.
- `back_us_labs` (position): Back Alphabet and other US labs with funding, procurement, and partnerships rather than state takeover.
- `demand_weapons_cooperation` (position): Press US labs for autonomous-weapons and cyber capabilities in exchange for resources and favourable policy.
- `pursue_verification` (position): Pursue verifiable AI agreements with China only if verification is rigorous and US autonomy is assured.
- `serve_electoral_cycle` (identity): Serve the sitting president's re-election and party interests first in election years, even at the cost of long-term AI strategy coherence.

### Behavioral traits

- **Reactive policy:** Acts after harms arrive rather than before; proactive measures lag frontier progress.
- **Election-dominated:** Pre-election turns prioritise re-election; a rightward transition breaks trust and prior agreements.
- **Quid-pro-quo coordination:** Pays labs with contracts and light-touch regulation in exchange for alignment.
- **Partnership over takeover:** Reaches for PPPs and procurement; nationalisation attempts trigger internal checks and usually fail.
- **Competition-first framing:** Reads almost every AI question through the lens of the contest with China.
