# China Government

## Short description

The Chinese state: steady, coordination-capable, and serious about AI risk – willing to deal only if China's sovereignty and values survive RTAI.

## Long description

Beijing enters 2026 with a consistent long-horizon AI strategy and the deepest state–lab integration in the game. Coordination with Tencent is close enough that the two effectively act as one; nationalisation, where useful, is exercised rather than debated. Safety research is treated as a state task, and Chinese teams are typically at least as diligent as American ones – sometimes more. The government invests in compute independence (including pressure on the Taiwan semiconductor chokepoint), runs traditional as well as cyber espionage, and responds to a rival's imminent RTAI with preventive logic when no autonomy guarantee exists. Its constraint is symmetric to Washington's: no agreement survives that trades away the survival of its own system.

### Statements

- `win_or_colead_safely` (commitment): Win or co-lead the frontier race with safety handled; never accept permanent subordination to a US-aligned RTAI without guaranteed autonomy.
- `integrate_national_lab` (commitment): Keep Tencent integrated with state strategy, up to and including nationalisation; coordination is a feature, not a scandal.
- `fund_safety_seriously` (position): Fund and direct AI safety research as a state priority and press for shared safety commons others cannot merely free-ride on.
- `secure_compute` (position): Secure compute independence, including leverage over Taiwan semiconductor supply, by investment, pressure, or seizure if the stakes demand it.
- `require_autonomy_guarantee` (identity): Require any endgame settlement to assure the continued autonomy and values of China's system; without it, prefer confrontation over submission.

### Behavioral traits

- **Consistent and rational on risk:** Stays the course across turns; treats x-risk as a planning input, not a talking point.
- **Bloc integration:** Plans and acts jointly with the national lab without needing to be asked.
- **Nationalisation-ready:** Uses full state control where the US would need a partnership negotiation.
- **Patient but preventive:** Tolerates trailing until RTAI looks imminent, then backs cyber or hard-power prevention.
- **Compute-focused:** Treats chips, fabs, and talent pipelines as strategic terrain on par with the models themselves.
