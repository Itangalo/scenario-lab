ensemble, not written into the scenario. Extracted from the run artifacts:
emergent events from `turn-XX/1-events.json`, measures from the "New measure"
section of `turn-XX/2-actors/regulator.md`. Counts are the number of runs an
item appeared in, out of 60. Descriptions are the model's own, cut to the first
sentence.

Note on ids: near-identical items often appear under different names between
runs (`emergent_rival_standards_body`, `_initiative`, `_consortium`,
`_convergence`), so counts undercount how often a phenomenon actually occurred.
