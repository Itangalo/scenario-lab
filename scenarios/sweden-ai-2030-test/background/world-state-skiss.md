## Global AI Development

**AI Capability (METR benchmark):** AI systems can reliably complete half of software development tasks taking a skilled human approximately 3 hours (November 2025 baseline: 2h42m). In the base scenario, this metric is doubling every 6 months.

**Major AI players:** OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and xAI lead frontier model development. Chinese labs are quick to follow with open models that lag 6 months or so behind the frontier models.

**Recent developments:**
- GPT-5, Claude 4, Grok 3 and Gemini 3 released in 2025, showing significant reasoning improvements.
- AI-assisted software development is standard among all respectable developers.
- "Vibe hacking" -- hacking largely automated by AI -- has started to be an issue.
- First generation of AI agents handling multi-step workflows gaining adoption.
- Growing concerns about AI safety, but no major incidents yet.
- There is worries about an economic AI bubble. A collapsing economy around AI will affect development indirectly, since reduced investments will hit the newer companies hard.
- The US is working to build domestic AI chip production, but it will take a number of years to get something remotely on par with Taiwan.

## Sweden

### Government and Policy

The Swedish government has launched several AI initiatives but with limited coordination and resources:
- AI Commission (AI-kommissionen) published recommendations in late 2024
- Scattered funding through Vinnova and other agencies
- No comprehensive AI strategy implemented
- School (but not higher education) is actively excluded from AI strategies, since Liberalerna is against digital tools in schools
- Focus on appearing proactive rather than substantive action

**EU context:** AI Act entered into force in 2024, with compliance deadlines approaching. Sweden follows EU lead but has not taken independent initiatives. The EU is considering postponing the enactment of some of the high-risk regulation, and also softening parts of GDPR, to make AI in the EU more viable.

### Labor Market

- Unemployment at approximately 7.5% (Arbetsförmedlingen definition)
- Some anxiety about AI impact, especially among white-collar workers
- Union-employer discussions about AI implementation ongoing, but not a big topic
- No major AI-driven layoffs yet, but automation increasing in customer service, translation, and basic content creation

### Business

- Large Swedish companies experimenting with AI but generally moving slowly
- Tech sector and startups more aggressive in adoption, Swedish company Lovable has become a global hit
- Significant dependency on US AI platforms (OpenAI, Microsoft, Google, Anthropic)
- Talent shortage for AI specialists

### Media Landscape

- Active coverage of AI, cycling between hype and concern
- Growing use of AI tools in newsrooms (translation, summarization)
- Concerns about AI-generated misinformation, especially from foreign actors
- Competition from social media and AI-generated content intensifying -- in particular young people turning to SoMe or AI for news

### Public Opinion

- Approximately 45-50% of Swedes use AI tools regularly (work or personal)
- Opinion divided: roughly balanced between positive and skeptical views
- Limited deep understanding of AI capabilities among general public
- Growing awareness of job displacement risks, especially among younger workers

## Geopolitical Context

### United States

- 2024 election concluded; new administration settling in
- Continued focus on AI competition with China
- AI policy heavily influenced by accelerationism -- advance AI as quickly as possible, regulate as little as possible
- Significant private investment in AI continues despite recent market concerns

### European Union

- AI Act implementation ongoing, postponing some parts is being discussed to increase competitiveness
- Digital sovereignty initiatives continuing
- Tension between innovation promotion and precautionary regulation

### China

- Domestic AI development accelerating despite chip restrictions
- Chinese AI labs in general release ther models with open weights
- China is dominating robotics, with more than 50 percent of global production
- The "AI plus" policy means that AI should be combined with and incorporated in several sectors
- Active information warfare using AI-enhanced tools

### Russia

- Active information warfare using AI-enhanced tools
- Targeting Swedish and Nordic information space
- Limited domestic AI development capacity

- Can EU regulation be enforced without harming competitiveness?
- Will there be a major AI safety incident that changes public attitudes?
- How will the 2026 Swedish election affect AI policy?

## Notes for world synthesis

The world state should evolve based on:
1. Exogenous events (AI development pace, geopolitics, etc.)
2. Actor decisions and their consequences
3. Realistic cause-and-effect chains

Swedish society tends to change gradually. Dramatic shifts should require significant triggers. Public opinion is relatively stable but can shift in response to major events or sustained media coverage.
