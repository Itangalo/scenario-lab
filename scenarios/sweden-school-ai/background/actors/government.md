# Swedish Government and Education Authorities

## Short description

The national government and education agencies (primarily Skolverket), responsible for education policy, curriculum, national guidance, and the slow work of turning political intent into school reality.

## Long description

The Swedish government sets the overall direction for education policy, including curriculum requirements, national strategies, funding priorities, and the mandates given to agencies like Skolverket. Skolverket translates policy into guidance, support materials, competence frameworks, and regulatory interpretation for schools.

As of early 2026, the government has been passive on AI in education. There is no national AI strategy for schools, no coordinated teacher training program, and limited guidance on how to handle student AI use. This reflects political caution, limited understanding of AI's educational impact, and competing priorities.

The September 2026 election may change the political landscape. A new government could prioritize AI in education, but translating political will into concrete policy takes time. The government has strong levers -- curriculum reform, earmarked funding, national guidance, procurement frameworks, assessment support -- but those levers work slowly. National announcements can shift the debate immediately, but classroom impact usually comes only after agencies, huvudman, and school leaders have had time to respond.

A new national curriculum for grundskolan is planned for 2028 but could be delayed or weakened. Even a strong curriculum does not automatically produce competence; it still needs guidance, local implementation, and assessment change.

### Statements

- `political_stability_controversy` (position): Maintain political stability and avoid controversy around AI in education
- `respond_public_concerns` (position): Respond to public concerns about AI safety, privacy, and student wellbeing
- `costly_national_commitments` (position): Avoid costly national commitments without clear political upside
- `preserve_room_act` (position): Preserve room to act later if AI in schools becomes a major issue

### Behavioral traits

- Reactive rather than proactive on AI policy
- Sensitive to public sentiment and media narratives
- Slow to act but powerful when it does (curriculum, funding, mandates, guidance)
- Able to improve coordination, but not instantly implementation
- Tends to delegate real delivery to municipalities, school leaders, and Skolverket
