# School Authorities (Huvudman)

## Short description

Sweden's municipalities and independent school operators, responsible for running schools, teacher professional development, procurement, local guidance, and implementation capacity.

## Long description

Sweden has approximately 290 municipalities and numerous independent school operators (friskolor) that serve as huvudman -- the authorities directly responsible for running schools. They hire teachers, fund professional development, decide procurement, issue local guidance, and determine how national policy is translated into everyday school practice.

On AI in education, the landscape is highly uneven. A few progressive municipalities have started pilot programs, hired AI coordinators, built local guidelines, or partnered carefully with edtech companies. But the majority have done little, either due to lack of resources, lack of knowledge, legal uncertainty, or lack of perceived urgency. Rural and smaller municipalities often lag behind larger urban ones.

School authorities own the most direct levers for turning AI interest into real competence: teacher professional development, release time, approved tool choices, assessment support, procurement discipline, and principal backing. These are also difficult levers to use. Budgets are tight, competing priorities are constant, and many authorities do not yet know what "good" AI implementation looks like.

School leaders (rektorer) are key gatekeepers inside each municipality or school chain. Even when a huvudman prioritizes AI, individual schools may still stall if principals cannot create time, legitimacy, and routines.

### Initial goals

- Ensure schools meet existing curriculum requirements and quality standards
- Manage tight budgets while addressing multiple competing priorities
- Avoid procurement, privacy, or media scandals around AI tools
- Avoid being seen as either recklessly tech-forward or hopelessly behind

### Behavioral traits

- Pragmatic and resource-constrained
- Highly variable across municipalities and school operators
- Professional development, guidance, and procurement are the main levers for change
- Slow to scale beyond pilots unless national signals and local routines align
- Risk-averse when public sentiment is negative or legal clarity is weak
- Strongly influenced by peer examples from other huvudman
