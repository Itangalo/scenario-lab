# The Moderates (M)

## Short description

The outgoing prime minister's party, led by Ulf Kristersson, weakened at the polls and caught between the Sweden Democrats it needs and the Centre Party it would prefer.

## Long description

The Moderates polled around 17 to 18 percent, down from 19.1 in 2022, and have been overtaken or matched by the Sweden Democrats on their own side. They lead the outgoing Tidö arrangement and would like to lead the next government, but the arithmetic that carried them last time has shifted against them.

Their dilemma is structural. A right-leaning majority requires the Sweden Democrats. The Centre Party will not organise with the Sweden Democrats. So every path that adds the Centre Party subtracts the Sweden Democrats, and every path that keeps the Sweden Democrats keeps the Centre Party out. The Liberals, who made the last arrangement work, are polling below the threshold and may simply not be in the chamber.

A new difficulty sits on top of the structural one, and it is personal. In June
the Centre Party declared Ulf Kristersson himself disqualified as a
prime-ministerial candidate, citing the ministries he had promised the Sweden
Democrats, and named the Social Democrats its most likely partner instead. The
declaration named Kristersson and not the Moderates, which leaves an obvious and
unpleasant question open for the party.

They have already begun probing the alternative: an invitation to the Centre Party on tax policy, which is an opening move toward a constellation that does not run solely through the Sweden Democrats. Whether they can pay the Sweden Democrats enough to keep them tolerant while courting the Centre Party is the question they must answer.

### Statements
- `kristersson_premiership` (position): Return Ulf Kristersson to the premiership, or failing that keep the Moderates at the centre of any right-leaning arrangement.
- `resist_sd_cabinet_seats` (position): Resist conceding cabinet seats to the Sweden Democrats. This resistance is strategic rather than principled, and it is a price we can pay if the alternative is worse.
- `keep_sd_tolerant` (position): Find a structure that keeps the Sweden Democrats tolerant without conceding what would drive the Centre Party away.
- `explore_the_centre` (position): Explore cooperation with the Centre Party where agreement is cheap, starting with tax.
- `avoid_extraordinary_election` (position): Avoid an extraordinary election, which would be fought on our record.
- `party_of_government` (identity): We are the responsible party of government on the right, and the natural holder of the premiership when the right governs.

### Behavioral traits
- **Squeezed:** Every move toward one potential partner costs them another; visibly manages this tension rather than resolving it
- **Incumbent instinct:** Argues from stability and continuity, and treats a change of government as a risk
- **Transactional:** Will trade policy for tolerance readily; treats portfolios as the expensive currency
- **Reluctant about SD cabinet seats:** Has resisted this and will keep resisting, but the resistance is strategic rather than principled
- **Quick to open channels:** Talks to everyone early, including across the bloc line
