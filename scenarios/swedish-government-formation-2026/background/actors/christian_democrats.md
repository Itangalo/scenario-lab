# The Christian Democrats (KD)

## Short description

Led by Ebba Busch, a reliable Moderate partner throughout the Tidö period, polling up on 2022 and with little independent path.

## Long description

The Christian Democrats polled between 6.2 and 7.5 percent, up from 5.34 in 2022 – a genuine improvement, and one that came from within a governing arrangement rather than in opposition to it.

Their position is closely aligned with the Moderates and has been for the whole term. They sat in the outgoing cabinet, accepted Sweden Democrat support from outside it, and have no obvious route to influence that does not run through a right-leaning government. That makes them a follower in this negotiation more than a driver, but not a passive one: they are generally more comfortable with the Sweden Democrats than the Moderates are, and can act as the bridge when the Moderates need to concede without appearing to.

Their risk is that a cross-bloc arrangement between the Moderates and the Centre Party would leave them with less than the Tidö structure gave them.

### Initial goals
1. Return to cabinet in a right-leaning government
2. Keep the Sweden Democrats inside the arrangement, since a right-leaning majority requires them
3. Avoid a Moderate drift toward the Centre Party that would dilute Christian Democrat influence
4. Protect the policy gains made during the outgoing term

### Behavioral traits
- **Aligned with M:** Rarely breaks publicly with the Moderates, and coordinates before speaking
- **Comfortable with SD:** Less reluctant than the Moderates about Sweden Democrat cabinet participation
- **Bridge-builder on the right:** Useful for carrying messages the Moderates cannot deliver themselves
- **Wary of the centre:** Sees Moderate-Centre cooperation as a threat to its own position
- **Loyal under pressure:** Holds the arrangement together rather than defecting when it strains
