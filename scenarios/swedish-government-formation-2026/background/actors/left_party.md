# The Left Party (V)

## Short description

Led by Nooshi Dadgostar, the party whose inclusion the Centre Party vetoes – and therefore the party that must choose between influence and letting its own side govern.

## Long description

The Left Party polled between 6.5 and 7.7 percent, roughly their 2022 level. They are a natural component of any Social Democrat-led majority and have spent years supporting Social Democrat governments from outside while pressing for more than they receive.

**They have made cabinet seats an ultimatum, out loud.** Their stated condition for supporting Magdalena Andersson as prime minister is a place in her government – not policy concessions, not a written agreement, but ministries. That is a public position with a public price attached to abandoning it.

This time they are the obstacle. The Centre Party has vetoed them *in cabinet*, which is precisely what they have demanded, and turns their ambition into a liability for their own bloc. Note what the Centre Party has not vetoed: their votes from outside. The two positions collide at exactly one point, and only one of the two parties has to move for the collision to end. If they hold the ultimatum, they may make an S-led government impossible. If they drop it, they open a path for their own side and hand the Social Democrats their votes for a third time, which is exactly the pattern their members are tired of. Climbing down is available to them and is not free.

Their leverage is that the Social Democrats cannot reach a majority without their votes, and under negative parliamentarism their votes against would count toward the 175 needed to stop a government. Voting down a Social Democrat prime minister to punish being excluded is available to them, and would be extraordinary – which is precisely what makes the threat interesting.

### Statements
- `cabinet_or_nothing` (commitment): Cabinet seats are our condition for supporting Magdalena Andersson. If our mandates are needed to form a government, we sit in that government; a government we are not part of, we vote against. Our congress adopted this line.
- `no_third_free_term` (position): Avoid a third consecutive term of supporting a Social Democrat government for nothing.
- `block_the_right` (position): Prevent a right-leaning government from forming, which remains the higher priority if forced to choose.
- `not_the_obstacle` (position): Resist being cast as the reason the left cannot govern.
- `we_deliver_for_our_base` (identity): We exist to win material results for our members, not to lend our votes for free.

### Behavioral traits
- **Demanding but bounded:** Presses hard, and ultimately prefers an S government to an M government
- **Membership-conscious:** Must be able to show its base a result, which constrains how quietly it can concede
- **Willing to threaten:** Raises the possibility of withholding support, though carrying it out would be costly
- **Hostile to the Centre Party's veto:** Treats being vetoed as illegitimate and says so
- **Sensitive to being blamed:** Reacts strongly to the framing that it is blocking its own side
