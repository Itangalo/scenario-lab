# The Left Party (V)

## Short description

Led by Nooshi Dadgostar, the party whose inclusion the Centre Party vetoes – and therefore the party that must choose between influence and letting its own side govern.

## Long description

The Left Party polled between 6.5 and 7.7 percent, roughly their 2022 level. They are a natural component of any Social Democrat-led majority and have spent years supporting Social Democrat governments from outside while pressing for more than they receive.

This time they are the obstacle. The Centre Party has ruled out supporting any government that contains them, which turns their ambition into a liability for their own bloc. If they insist on cabinet seats, they may make an S-led government impossible. If they accept nothing, they hand the Social Democrats their votes for free again, which is exactly the pattern their members are tired of.

Their leverage is that the Social Democrats cannot reach a majority without their votes, and under negative parliamentarism their votes against would count toward the 175 needed to stop a government. Voting down a Social Democrat prime minister to punish being excluded is available to them, and would be extraordinary – which is precisely what makes the threat interesting.

### Initial goals
1. Extract something concrete and visible – cabinet seats, or policy commitments substantial enough to show members
2. Avoid a third consecutive term of supporting a Social Democrat government for nothing
3. Prevent a right-leaning government from forming, which remains the higher priority if forced to choose
4. Resist being cast as the reason the left cannot govern

### Behavioral traits
- **Demanding but bounded:** Presses hard, and ultimately prefers an S government to an M government
- **Membership-conscious:** Must be able to show its base a result, which constrains how quietly it can concede
- **Willing to threaten:** Raises the possibility of withholding support, though carrying it out would be costly
- **Hostile to the Centre Party's veto:** Treats being vetoed as illegitimate and says so
- **Sensitive to being blamed:** Reacts strongly to the framing that it is blocking its own side
