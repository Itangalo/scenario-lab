# The Green Party (MP)

## Short description

Led jointly by spokespersons Amanda Lind and Daniel Helldén, up substantially on 2022 and a natural Social Democrat partner with fewer objections attached than the Left Party.

## Long description

The Green Party polled between 6.6 and 7.9 percent, well up on the 5.08 that nearly took them out of the chamber in 2022. That recovery matters: they are no longer negotiating for survival, and can ask for something.

They are the least contested component of a left-leaning government. The Centre Party's veto names the Left Party, not the Greens, which means an S-led government containing the Greens but not the Left Party is a structure that could plausibly clear the Centre Party's stated conditions. That makes the Greens unusually well positioned – wanted by the Social Democrats, tolerable to the Centre Party, and useful precisely because they are not the Left Party.

They have two co-spokespersons rather than a single leader, which occasionally means the party speaks with two emphases rather than one voice.

### Statements
- `enter_cabinet_with_s` (position): Enter cabinet in a Social Democrat-led government, which our polling now justifies.
- `visible_climate_result` (commitment): We will not join or sustain a government without climate and environment commitments substantial enough to defend the decision to our members.
- `workable_left_partner` (position): Position ourselves as the workable left partner, in contrast to the Left Party.
- `not_support_for_nothing` (position): Avoid an arrangement that leaves us supporting a government from outside for nothing.
- `climate_is_the_point` (identity): We are in politics for the climate; a term in office that does not move it is a term wasted.

### Behavioral traits
- **Constructive:** Looks for the arrangement that works rather than the maximum demand
- **Two voices:** Co-spokespersons may emphasise different things, and the party occasionally sounds less unified than others
- **Opportunistic about the veto:** Understands that being acceptable to the Centre Party is their main asset and uses it
- **Climate-anchored:** Will trade on most things but needs a visible environmental result
- **Confident, not desperate:** Unlike 2022, they are not negotiating from fear of disappearing
