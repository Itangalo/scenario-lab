# World State at Election Night, 13 September 2026

## The Election

Sweden's general election falls on the second Sunday of September, which in
2026 is **13 September** `[source: Swedish electoral calendar, verified by
weekday computation 2026-08-22]`. The riksdag has **349 seats**; **175** is an
absolute majority `[source: Regeringsformen, via regeringen.se, retrieved
2026-08-22]`.

A preliminary count is available on election night, but the **final count**
(often called the Wednesday count) can move parties across the 4% threshold and
reallocate seats `[model]`. Formation bargaining therefore begins under
arithmetic that is not yet fully settled — a fact worth modelling as an early
event rather than a certainty.

## The Outgoing Situation

The government going into the election rests on the Tidö arrangement: M, KD and
L in cabinet with SD as a support party outside it `[source: Nationalekonomiska
Föreningen, "Minoritetsregeringar och regeringsfrågan inför valet 2026",
retrieved 2026-08-22]`.

## Government Formation Procedure

Sweden uses **negative parliamentarism**. A prime-ministerial candidate proposed
by the Speaker is elected unless an absolute majority — at least 175 members —
votes against `[source: regeringen.se, retrieved 2026-08-22]`. A government
therefore does not need a majority *for* it, only the absence of a majority
*against* it. This makes abstention a decisive currency and is the single most
important mechanic in this scenario.

Sequence `[source: regeringen.se and Regeringsformen ch. 6, retrieved
2026-08-22]`:

1. After an election a vote is held on whether the sitting prime minister may
   remain, no later than two weeks after the election (RF 6:3).
2. The Speaker holds consultations (sonderingar) with party leaders, then
   proposes a candidate to the riksdag.
3. If the Speaker's proposal is rejected **four times**, an extraordinary
   election is called — unless an ordinary election is due within three months.

The Speaker is elected by the incoming riksdag, so which side controls that
office is itself a consequence of the result `[model]`.

## Historical Pacing

The 2018 formation took **134 days**, the longest in modern Swedish history
`[model]`. That sets the horizon: 20 weekly turns covers even that case.
