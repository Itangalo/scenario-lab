# Metric Rules for Librar Labs – US Market Opportunities

These rules govern how metrics influence each other and evolve across turns. Apply them as quantitative guardrails when evaluating simulation outcomes.

1. **Market reach and competitive pressure reinforce each other.** As librar_us_market_reach grows past 5%, competitive_pressure rises by 5–10 points (incumbents respond). If competitive_pressure exceeds 65, librar_us_market_reach growth slows by 30–50% unless Librar secures strategic partnerships or differentiating events occur.

2. **Digital readiness gates adoption speed.** When school_digital_readiness is below 40, librar_us_market_reach can grow by at most 1 percentage point per turn regardless of other factors. Each 10-point increase in school_digital_readiness above 40 allows proportionally faster adoption.

3. **Funding amplifies adoption.** When us_education_funding rises above 60, the effective adoption rate for librar_us_market_reach increases by 50%. When us_education_funding falls below 30, adoption nearly stops (schools are in budget freeze).

4. **Content integration and platform events are linked.** If a major digital platform enters the school library market, content_platform_integration jumps by 15–25 points in the following turn. If an audiobook or podcast adoption surge occurs, content_platform_integration rises by 10–15 points over the next two turns.

5. **Literacy evidence feeds funding and adoption.** Each 20-point increase in literacy_impact_evidence above 20 adds 5 points to us_education_funding (evidence-based policy effect) and reduces buyer skepticism, adding 0.5–1 percentage point to librar_us_market_reach per turn.

6. **Librarian shortage is a tailwind for Librar specifically.** When the US school librarian shortage becomes acute, librar_us_market_reach growth rate increases by 25% for the following 3 turns, as underfunded schools seek no-expertise-required solutions.

7. **Competitive pressure moderates itself.** Competitive pressure above 70 tends to stabilize as incumbents consolidate rather than keep escalating spend. If librar_us_market_reach is below 5% when pressure is above 70, incumbents may lose interest in Librar as a threat, causing pressure to fall 5–10 points.

8. **Content integration is slow to move without catalysts.** Absent specific triggering events, content_platform_integration changes by at most 3–5 points per turn in either direction.

9. **Funding is sensitive to political cycles.** Without literacy crisis events or evidence-based policy wins, us_education_funding drifts toward 40–50 (baseline). School budget cuts reduce it by 10–15 points; E-Rate expansion raises it by 15–20 points; a federal literacy crisis declaration raises it by 20–30 points.

10. **Market reach above 20% triggers network effects.** Once librar_us_market_reach crosses 20%, district-to-district word-of-mouth and consortium purchasing become significant, adding 1–2 extra percentage points of growth per turn even without new events.
