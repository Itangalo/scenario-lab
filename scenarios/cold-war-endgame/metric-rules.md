# Metric Rules (Turn 0 – Initial)

## Rules

1. soviet_stability tends to decline slowly each turn as structural problems accumulate. The decline accelerates if soviet_economic_output falls below 80 or if reform_momentum rises above 50, because both conditions erode institutional loyalty.
2. east_bloc_cohesion follows soviet_stability downward but with a delay of roughly 1 turn. A sharp drop in soviet_stability (more than 10 points in one turn) should produce a larger-than-usual drop in east_bloc_cohesion the following turn.
3. If the Soviet reformers explicitly signal they will not intervene militarily in Eastern Europe, east_bloc_cohesion should drop sharply (10–20 points) as satellite states realize the threat is gone.
4. us_soviet_tension decreases when both superpowers pursue diplomatic engagement or sign arms agreements. It increases if either side takes confrontational actions, engages in proxy conflicts, or if a crisis event occurs.
5. soviet_economic_output declines slowly under the baseline scenario (roughly 2–4 points per turn) reflecting systemic stagnation. Reform disruptions can accelerate the decline in the short term. Successful reform or Western economic aid can slow or reverse the decline, but the effect takes 2–3 turns to materialize.
6. reform_momentum increases when Gorbachev pushes glasnost/perestroika, when opposition movements gain concessions, or when reform succeeds in one country and inspires others. It decreases if a crackdown occurs or if reform efforts visibly fail.
7. If reform_momentum exceeds 60 while soviet_stability is below 50, the situation becomes unstable: further changes in any metric tend to be larger and less predictable, representing loss of central control.
8. If east_bloc_cohesion drops below 30, reform_momentum should receive a boost (5–10 points) from the demonstration effect — success in one country emboldens others.
9. Low us_soviet_tension (below 40) gives the Soviet reformers more room to maneuver, which can accelerate reform_momentum by 3–5 additional points per turn.
