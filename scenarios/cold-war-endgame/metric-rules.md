# Metric Rules (Turn 0 – Initial)

## Rules

### Pace-of-change limits (apply these before everything else)

- soviet_stability: maximum change of 10 points per turn in any direction during turns 1–7. From turn 8 onward the limit is 15 points.
- east_bloc_cohesion: maximum change of 12 points per turn.
- us_soviet_tension: maximum change of 10 points per turn. The general trend through 1985–1991 is downward; absent major confrontational events, apply a baseline drift of −2 to −3 points per turn.
- soviet_economic_output: maximum decline of 8 points per turn. Maximum recovery of 2 points per turn.
- reform_momentum: maximum increase of 12 points per turn in turns 1–5. From turn 6 onward the limit is 18 points. Decreases are limited to 10 points per turn.

### Substantive rules

1. soviet_stability declines by 3–5 points per turn as structural problems accumulate. The decline increases to 6–8 points if soviet_economic_output falls below 75 OR if reform_momentum rises above 55.
2. east_bloc_cohesion generally follows soviet_stability downward with a 1-turn delay. However, cohesion can also erode independently if satellite state reform events occur or if reform_momentum exceeds 65.
3. If the Soviet reformers explicitly signal they will not intervene militarily in Eastern Europe, east_bloc_cohesion should drop by 12–18 points in that turn as satellite states realize the constraint is gone.
4. us_soviet_tension has a natural downward drift of 2–3 points per turn through this period. It decreases further (additional 4–8 points) when arms agreements are signed or summits occur. It increases (4–8 points) if either side takes confrontational actions. Absent major events, let the baseline drift dominate.
5. soviet_economic_output declines by 3–5 points per turn (chronic stagnation). When reform_momentum is above 40, add 2–3 additional points (perestroika disrupts the old system before the new one works). Do not stack more than 8 total points of decline in a single turn. Western economic aid or arms savings can reduce the decline by 1–2 points, but only with a 2-turn delay.
6. reform_momentum increases by 4–8 points per turn as glasnost and opposition movements gain ground. It grows faster (8–12 points) when a satellite state reform event occurs or when east_bloc_cohesion drops sharply. It decreases by 5–10 points if a significant crackdown occurs.
7. If reform_momentum exceeds 70 AND soviet_stability is below 45, the system is under severe strain: apply the upper end of all decline ranges and the lower end of any recovery ranges. Do not exceed the pace-of-change limits above even in this condition.
8. If east_bloc_cohesion drops below 30, add 5–8 points to reform_momentum from the demonstration effect.
