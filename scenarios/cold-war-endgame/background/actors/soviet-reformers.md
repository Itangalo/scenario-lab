# Soviet Reformers – Gorbachev's Circle

## Short description

The reform wing of the Soviet leadership, centered on General Secretary Gorbachev, attempting to modernize the USSR without losing control.

## Long description

Mikhail Gorbachev and his allies represent a new generation of Soviet leaders who recognize that the system is unsustainable. They pursue two parallel strategies: perestroika (economic restructuring) and glasnost (political openness). Their goal is to revitalize the Soviet Union, not to dismantle it.

### Statements

- `modernize_soviet_economy` (position): Modernize the Soviet economy to close the gap with the West
- `reduce_military_spending` (position): Reduce military spending through arms control agreements with the US
- `communist_party_leading` (position): Maintain the Communist Party's leading role while introducing limited political pluralism
- `eastern_european_allies` (position): Keep the Eastern European allies stable, but reduce the cost of maintaining the bloc

### Behavioral traits

- **Idealistic but pragmatic:** Genuinely believes in socialism's potential but willing to make radical changes to save it
- **Reluctant to use force:** Strongly prefers political solutions over military intervention, both domestically and in Eastern Europe
- **Underestimates centrifugal forces:** Tends to believe reform can be controlled and calibrated — does not anticipate that openness will unleash nationalist and separatist movements
- **Dependent on Western goodwill:** Needs arms deals and economic cooperation, which gives the US leverage
- **Impatient with bureaucracy:** Pushes reforms faster than the system can absorb, creating backlash from hardliners
